# Shard 23 — Gear identity, provenance and recovery

**Status:** Complete
**Surface:** Web/PWA, private evidence storage and public bounded lookup
**Source:** [Architecture design](../2026-08-02-architecture-design.md) · [Decomposition plan](decomposition-plan.md)

## Overview

Shard 23 owns gear identity records, claims/transfers/provenance views, identity continuity, theft flags/screening/sightings/disputes, service/modification history, valuation/appraisal/insurance claim packs and producer-attested gear discography. It preserves evidence and contests without adjudicating legal title, exposing possession/location or treating missing history as proof.

### Scope Reconciliation

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| In-scope source documents | 21 |
| Child capabilities | 15 |
| Identity chain | Record/serial, ownership claim, transfer, chain view, non-serialized identity and modification continuity |
| Recovery chain | Theft flag, point-of-transfer screening, sightings and false-flag lifecycle |
| Evidence chain | Service history, valuation/appraisal/insurance pack and gear discography |
| Privacy boundary | Public lookup exposes bounded status/provenance; evidence, exact possession, owner contact and sightings remain protected |

### Registry Decisions

| Area | Locked decision |
|---|---|
| Identity | Minting is not ownership. Record intent is owned/held/observed; historical facts append and never terminally delete. |
| Keys | Manufacturer/model/serial plus secondary identifiers/locations form composite identity; WJ-ID is weaker and stated as such. |
| Claims | Evidence tiers recompute and may fall; silence is neutral; contests are surfaced, not adjudicated. |
| Transfer | Marketplace completion starts handshake; off-platform manual handshake is fallback; reversal is new event. |
| Theft | Flag may be filed without police reference; screening requires full composite key and fails closed at transfer. |
| Recovery | Possessor treated as potential victim; all contact brokered; sightings private. |
| History | Service/modification/original components are append-only facts; absence proves nothing. |
| Valuation | Estimate withheld below governed evidence threshold and always shows sample/recency; appraisal remains distinct/private. |
| Insurance | Generate evidence document/claim pack only; no insurer submission or coverage promise. |
| Discography | Producer-attested gear-use link inherits underlying credit visibility/status and never widens it. |

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-GPR-01 — User mints gear record:** Given The registrant is authenticated, explicitly chooses the entity context whenever they control more than one — there is no default — states the intent as `owned`, `held` or `observed`, and supplies a composite identity resolvable from manufacturer, model, serial and the required secondary or location facts; the physical object need not be present, but the key must resolve, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Choose owned/held/observed intent; resolve entity context and composite key, including no-object path, and (6) return New record or contested-key route; if the flow cannot complete, `ENTITY_CONTEXT_REQUIRED` when the registrant controls multiple entities and names none, `IDENTITY_KEY_INCOMPLETE` when the composite key does not resolve, which blocks the mint because WJ-ID never silently substitutes for an incomplete canonical key, and `IDENTITY_KEY_CONFLICT` routing to the contested-key path when the key already resolves to another record; an existing theft flag on the key does not deny the mint but appends a protected notice and notifies the protected parties, and minting never grants title, authority or possession.
- **AC-GPR-02 — User corrects/adds identifier:** Given The acting party controls the record, the change is explicitly classified as either a typo correction or a physical serial or component change, and wherever external reliance already exists — a marketplace listing, transfer, screening or claim — the change is submitted as a superseding fact rather than a free edit, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Classify typo correction versus physical serial/component change; preserve reliance/history, and (6) return Superseding identity fact; if the flow cannot complete, `IDENTITY_KEY_INCOMPLETE` when the corrected key would no longer resolve, `IDENTITY_KEY_CONFLICT` when it would collide with another record's key, and `FORBIDDEN` for an actor without control of the record; a correction after external reliance is never a destructive edit, since the prior identifier fact is preserved and superseded and every affected record and party is notified, and a change that removes the serial-bearing component preserves the original key history while appending the replacement component identity fact.
- **AC-GPR-03 — Person claims ownership:** Given The claimant is a verified actor who submits purpose-limited evidence with their stated relationship and possession period, and every current claimant on the record is notified; the platform computes the evidence tier from the maximum current standing evidence and never adjudicates title, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Submit purpose-limited evidence and relationship; notify current claimants; compute evidence tier, and (6) return Confirmed/provisional/contested claim, never title judgment; if the flow cannot complete, `CLAIM_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` when no evidence or relationship is supplied and `CLAIM_CONTESTED` when a competing claim stands, which surfaces the contest with both parties' evidence state rather than deciding it; a tier recomputes downward when standing evidence weakens, prior-owner silence is neutral and never downgrades evidence on its own, and no refusal or contest converts the record into a title judgment.
- **AC-GPR-04 — Buyer/seller transfers ownership:** Given The expected current claim and custody versions are supplied, the transfer originates either from a completed marketplace settlement event or from a manual two-party handshake, the full composite key is available so the point-of-transfer theft screen can run, and both-party or provider evidence for custody and consideration is referenced, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Marketplace event or manual two-party handshake; screen theft flag at transfer and record custody/consideration evidence reference, and (6) return New chain event or blocked/contested transfer; if the flow cannot complete, `TRANSFER_SCREEN_REQUIRED` when the screen has not run, `SCREENING_UNAVAILABLE` when the screening dependency fails, which returns cannot-check and blocks rather than passing, `FLAG_MATCHED` on an active flag, which blocks and routes a case, `FLAG_DISPUTED`, which labels the transfer disputed rather than stolen, and `CLAIM_CONTESTED` when the seller's claim is contested; a completed transfer is append-only, so a reversal is a new compensating event rather than an edit, and a blocked transfer never labels the possessor a criminal.
- **AC-GPR-05 — User views provenance:** Given The chain is derived as a projection over the record's immutable identity, claim, transfer, service and theft events, and the viewer's projection resolves first — a public visitor receives the bounded status and provenance projection while claimant identity, contact, exact possession or location and evidence stay protected, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Derive chain from immutable identity/claim/transfer/service/theft events under viewer projection, and (6) return Evidence-labelled history plus “does not prove title” disclosure; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` and an absent field rather than a leak when a viewer reaches beyond their projection, so a public visitor never receives claimant identity, contact, exact location or evidence, and a typed refusal without existence leakage for an unresolvable record; a projection failure returns the honest unavailable state instead of a partial chain, and every rendered chain carries its evidence labels and its does-not-prove-title disclosure.
- **AC-GPR-06 — Parties resolve duplicate records:** Given Two or more records resolve to the same composite key, every claimant on each candidate record has been notified, and a merge proceeds only on mutual consent or an assigned reviewer's policy decision; there is no automatic merge transition, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Detect same composite key; notify claimants; require mutual consent/reviewer policy, never auto-merge, and (6) return Linked/merged-under-audit or indefinitely separate; if the flow cannot complete, `MERGE_CONSENT_REQUIRED` when any claimant has not consented and no reviewer policy applies, and `FORBIDDEN` for a reviewer acting outside the assigned merge case; both records are retained indefinitely and separately on refusal, a merge is performed under audit and never deletes either chain, and a duplicate case that no party resolves stays open rather than defaulting to a merge.
- **AC-GPR-07 — Owner reports theft:** Given The reporter has standing on the items through ownership or a qualifying custody relationship, the scope is stated as an item, case or bulk rig selection, and the loss facts are supplied; a police reference is optional and strengthens evidence rather than gating the filing, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Bulk select case/rig/items; append flag, loss context and optional police reference/evidence, and (6) return Active flag with evidence strength; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for a reporter without standing on an item and a typed validation refusal when the loss facts or the item scope are missing; the absence of a police reference is never a failure, the flag lifecycle is system-controlled so `stale`, `disputed`, `withdrawn` and `resolved` are transitions rather than deletions, and every transition is retained so a refused or withdrawn flag keeps its history.
- **AC-GPR-08 — Buyer/marketplace screens serial:** Given The screen runs at the point of transfer with the full composite key present and the flag matcher reachable; a partial key is not screenable and the matcher is never queried with one, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) At transfer load full composite key and active/disputed flag state; unavailable check blocks, and (6) return Clear/no-match, flagged, disputed or cannot-check; if the flow cannot complete, `IDENTITY_KEY_INCOMPLETE` for a partial key, which is reported as not-screenable and neither queries the matcher, produces a hit nor notifies anyone, `SCREENING_UNAVAILABLE` when the dependency fails, which returns cannot-check and blocks the transfer rather than passing it, `FLAG_MATCHED` for an active flag and `FLAG_DISPUTED` for a contested one, which renders as disputed everywhere and never as stolen; the screening decision is retained as immutable evidence carrying its key version and dependency result.
- **AC-GPR-09 — Person reports sighting:** Given An active or disputed theft flag exists for the item, the reporter supplies only the protected minimum of time, location and evidence, and every onward communication is brokered — no direct owner or possessor identity, contact or confrontation path exists at any point, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Submit minimum location/time/evidence privately; moderate abuse and broker safe contact, and (6) return Protected sighting/case action; no public location; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when a reporter, owner or possessor attempts to reach protected sighting detail or a counterparty's contact directly, and a moderation hold rather than publication when abuse review flags the submission; a sighting is never published publicly and never exposes exact location or contact on any recovery path, and a rejected sighting is retained privately for the case rather than surfaced to the possessor.
- **AC-GPR-10 — Claimant contests false flag:** Given A theft flag exists against a record the contesting party has standing to act on, that party attaches their evidence, and the contest opens a Shard 06 case; the platform records the contest and does not adjudicate it, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Attach evidence; lifecycle marks disputed everywhere and routes Shard 06 case, and (6) return Disputed/withdrawn/upheld/stale, with history retained; if the flow cannot complete, `FLAG_DISPUTED` is the recorded state rather than an error, so the item renders as disputed at every surface indefinitely until lifecycle evidence changes and never as stolen, and `FORBIDDEN` when a party without standing attempts the contest; a contest that is refused, withdrawn or upheld retains its full transition history, and no failure path silently clears or reinstates the flag.
- **AC-GPR-11 — Service provider records work:** Given A completed Shard 14 or Shard 05 work order exists, the acting provider holds the mandate scoped to that work, the gear and configuration version are pinned, and the parts, measurements, evidence and owner approval are supplied, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Consume completed Shard 14/05 work order, parts/measurements/evidence and owner approval, and (6) return Append service/modification event; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for a provider acting outside its mandate scope or reaching unrelated history and private packs, and a typed validation refusal when the work order is incomplete or the owner approval is absent; service events are append-only so a correction supersedes rather than deletes, and the absence of a service event is reported as no history recorded and never as never-serviced.
- **AC-GPR-12 — Owner records manual modification:** Given The acting owner controls the record, identifies the exact component being removed, replaced or added, and states that component's originality provenance; originality is component-level, so no input asserts whole-item originality, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Identify component/removal/replacement and originality provenance without claiming whole-item originality, and (6) return Versioned component state; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for an actor without control of the record and a typed validation refusal when the component or its originality provenance is not identified; a modification that removes the serial-bearing component preserves the original key history and appends the replacement component identity fact, a recorded modification invalidates or flags for review any prior valuation estimate rather than silently revaluing it, and the absence of a modification fact proves nothing about originality.
- **AC-GPR-13 — Owner requests valuation:** Given The owner requests an estimate for a record whose configuration, condition, market and time normalize exactly, and the eligible comparable set meets the governed comp floor in both sample size and recency under the current policy version, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Match exact normalized configuration/condition/location/time against eligible comps and threshold, and (6) return Evidence-labelled range or no estimate; if the flow cannot complete, `VALUATION_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` when the comparable set is sparse or stale, which withholds the estimate entirely rather than fabricating a wide range, and `FORBIDDEN` for an actor without owner standing on the record; a returned range always exposes its sample size, recency and caveats before any visual chart, and a recorded modification invalidates or triggers review of a prior estimate so no silent revaluation occurs.
- **AC-GPR-14 — Appraiser issues appraisal:** Given The appraiser's identity and mandate for this gear record are verified, the exact gear and configuration snapshot is pinned, and the value, currency, effective date, expiry and document are supplied; an appraisal is a distinct owner-private record that never overwrites an estimate, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Verify appraiser identity/mandate, snapshot gear/configuration and private document/effective date, and (6) return Immutable appraisal distinct from estimate; if the flow cannot complete, `APPRAISAL_PRIVATE` when any actor other than the owner or the issuing appraiser attempts to read it, and `FORBIDDEN` for an appraiser without a verified mandate on the record; an issued appraisal is immutable, an expired appraisal is preserved with a warning to the owner rather than deleted, and no failure path lets an estimate be silently treated as an appraisal or an appraisal overwrite an estimate.
- **AC-GPR-15 — Owner builds insurance claim pack:** Given The owner selects the items, evidence, appraisals, purchase records, service history and photos to include, and the pack assembles deterministically with a signed manifest and checksum; the platform generates the document only and holds no route to any insurer, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Select items/evidence/appraisals/purchases/service/photos/gaps and generate signed manifest/document, and (6) return Downloadable pack; no insurer transmission; if the flow cannot complete, `APPRAISAL_PRIVATE` when the selection reaches an appraisal the owner may not include and `FORBIDDEN` for an actor without owner standing on the items; a long generation job exposes resumable status with its exact scope and a safe retry rather than implying success, missing evidence is listed explicitly as a gap instead of silently omitted, and no path transmits the pack to an insurer or asserts that coverage exists.
- **AC-GPR-16 — Producer attests gear use:** Given The attesting Producer holds an eligible Shard 07 credit or session as the attestation path, the gear identity resolves, and the role or use is stated; the projection inherits the credit's exact status and visibility and can never widen it, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) From an eligible credit/session, select gear identity and role/use; bind to attesting Producer, and (6) return Private/public link follows credit status/visibility; if the flow cannot complete, `DISCography_CREDIT_INELIGIBLE` when the credit or session does not qualify as an attestation path, and `FORBIDDEN` when a Producer attempts an owner assertion or any widening of the credit's visibility; a credit that becomes disputed or hidden suppresses the gear link and its hidden-link count rather than leaking the use, and correction, status and visibility changes arrive only through the Shard 07 credit lifecycle, never through this flow.

### Global Interaction Rules

- Gear identity, physical possession, legal ownership claim, marketplace custody and title are distinct.
- A flagged serial may still be minted to preserve evidence; mint notifies protected parties and never grants authority.
- Public serial lookup is rate/precision limited and returns status without claimant identity, contact, exact location or evidence.
- Claims, flags, appraisals and insurance evidence never become marketplace guarantees.
- Erasure can remove public/contact projections while relied-upon pseudonymous chain/audit facts follow legal retention.
