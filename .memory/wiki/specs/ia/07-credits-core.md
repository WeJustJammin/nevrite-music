## Overview

Shard 07 owns contribution truth: immutable credit records, session-time capture, claims, attestations, provenance derivation, corrections, disputes and role/instrument vocabulary. A credit says who contributed what to which recording/composition and with what evidence. It never implies ownership, split percentage, royalty, legal status, popularity or platform trust.

## Features

- **02.01 Credit Graph & Discography** — one party/role/work credit, public role-grouped discography, bounded traversal, non-automatic merges, confidentiality/embargo and superseding corrections.
- **02.02 Session Capture** — temporal roll call, per-part contribution log, non-blocking close prompt and consent-respecting attendance evidence.
- **02.03 Claiming & Cold-Start Seeding** — source-marked imports, precise claim suggestions and witness-led claim adjudication.
- **02.04 Attestation & Credit Confidence** — bounded asks, independent evidence, derived rung/internal score, immutable retractions and score-only collusion demotion.
- **02.05 Credit Dispute Resolution** — shared case-engine disputes, participant-only contest state, direct/witness/adjudicated ladder and joint outcomes.
- **02.06 Credit Role & Instrument Taxonomy** — DDEX-anchored role core, separate functional instruments, bounded aliases, pending terms and immutable assertion-time resolution.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-CRD-01 — Assert credit:** Given Actor is an authorized participant on the target work with a current acting context; the target work, the party or shell, the scope and the confidentiality input all resolve; the role resolves exactly or by alias, or commits as a bounded screened literal with a requester-scoped pending alias, since fuzzy candidates are offered and never auto-selected, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Authorized participant names one party, one canonical/literal role, recording/composition scope, contribution date, asserter and visibility, and (6) return Immutable credit version created; no rights/split inference; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` on an unresolvable work, party or scope, `FORBIDDEN` when the actor is not an authorized participant, `ACTING_CONTEXT_STALE` on a moved acting context, `IDEMPOTENCY_MISMATCH` on a replayed key carrying a differing payload. A concurrent assertion on the same party/role/work/scope key converges on the one active record and accretes the new evidence and asserter reference instead of creating a duplicate public row. `TAXONOMY_UNAVAILABLE` never blocks this flow — the literal commits with a pending alias — and no refusal or success path creates a right, split, payment or ownership row.
- **AC-CRD-02 — View work ledger:** Given Authenticated viewer holds a read authority over the work as credited party, session participant, Producer or session owner, work/release owner, or case-scoped dispute reviewer; confidentiality and row-level authorization are applied before selection so the projection and its count are computed from the same authorized set, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Viewer receives all authorized records including qualified/not-final and participant-visible contest states; billing order is separate owner assertion, and (6) return Viewer-relative projection and count agree; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` where no read authority holds, and `ACTING_CONTEXT_STALE` on a moved acting context, which the viewer re-resolves and retries. Records the viewer may not see are absent from both the rows and the count rather than marked hidden, so no count, status or error discloses their existence. A read mutates no ledger record and a projection failure renders the ledger unavailable rather than serving a partially authorized subset.
- **AC-CRD-03 — View public discography:** Given Any viewer, authenticated or not; the requested party page resolves and each record's public eligibility is computed from confidentiality plus release or lift plus page curation, with authorization applied before grouping, counts, ordering, pagination, cache keys and search, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Public records group by role family with provenance sentence/rung and bounded pagination; embargo/confidential records leave no observable trace, and (6) return Cacheable viewer-safe projection rendered; if the flow cannot complete, An embargoed or confidential record produces the 404-equivalent `EMBARGOED_NOT_FOUND` behaviour with counts, cache and search left invariant — never a 403 and never an existence leak through a differing total. A permissive publication that has not yet converged renders version-labelled with its freshness, while a restrictive change purges the public projection within 60 seconds, so the cache never outlives a confidentiality decision.
- **AC-CRD-04 — Traverse graph:** Given The viewer's tier resolves before the walk: a Fan is limited to work-to-party walks while a Professional query may follow the allowed work-party paths with tier weighting; local density meets the configured floor; and every hop's endpoint is independently viewer-authorized, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Professional query follows allowed work-party paths with tier weighting, density floor and visible path explanation; Fan is limited to work↔party walks, and (6) return Bounded result/cursor or explicit sparse-data degradation; if the flow cannot complete, Below the density floor the query degrades or disables explicitly rather than returning a thin result that could be differenced into private relationships, and `RATE_LIMITED` on traversal-budget exhaustion returns the bounded cursor for resumption. Unauthorized endpoints are absent from paths rather than reported as blocked, so no enumeration is possible, and every returned path carries its visible explanation rather than an opaque score.
- **AC-CRD-05 — Merge duplicate party shell:** Given A merge proposal names the candidate party or shell IDs with its evidence and re-point manifest, and a knowledgeable party with standing over those candidates reviews it. For people this precondition can be satisfied only by a human decision — no system worker, heuristic or import path may satisfy it, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Knowledgeable party reviews proposal; people never auto-merge; rejection records permanent negative assertion, and (6) return Re-point plan commits without changing credit confidentiality; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` on any attempt to auto-merge people or to merge without a knowledgeable party's confirmation, and `VERSION_CONFLICT` when a candidate moved during review. Rejection is not an error: it commits a permanent negative assertion that suppresses re-proposal. Each merged credit keeps its own visibility and history while viewer projections re-derive, and a merge that would exceed the discography pin cap unions both pin sets past the cap and prompts re-curation rather than dropping a pin.
- **AC-CRD-06 — Set/lift embargo:** Given Setting requires visibility authority over the record and inherits the strictest allowed default down project, Producer, room, embargoed. Lifting requires one of exactly three grounds — a release event, verified public evidence that has survived the 72-hour objection window, or an authorized manual lift — because public reachability is evidence and never authorization, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Session capture inherits strictest allowed default; verified public-release evidence may trigger objection window; lift is one-way except retracted-release window, and (6) return Visibility version and participant notices commit; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` where the actor cannot lift another party's confidentiality alone, `VALIDATION_FAILED` where no lift ground is present. A timely closed-ground objection pauses the lift at the status quo and opens a Shard 06 case after platform re-verification, with repeated evidence remaining admissible. A lift is one-way except inside the seven-day retracted-release recovery window, participants and the credited party retain scoped read and export throughout, and the exposure window persists as a permanent audit record.
- **AC-CRD-07 — Propose correction:** Given Actor holds correction authority over the target record, the change is expressed as a superseding proposal rather than an in-place edit, the required approver set resolves, and the change is not a party change — a party change routes to claim transfer or dispute and has no valid precondition here, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Authorized actor proposes superseding party/role/work/qualifier change; required parties agree or proposal escalates after bounded reminders, and (6) return Successor or linked dispute; original remains immutable; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` on an in-place edit attempt or a party change submitted as an ordinary amendment, and `DISPUTE_REQUIRED` where agreement fails and the change must route to the Shard 06 case engine. Required non-response escalates on day 14 after day-3 and day-10 reminders and never auto-applies; a record with no available approver enters `correction_blocked` and stays readable. The original is immutable throughout, and a material party, role or work change invalidates supporting attestations while instrument, qualifier and taxonomy completion do not.
- **AC-CRD-08 — Run session roll:** Given Actor is the session owner or a delegate; the session exists in Shard 09 and is not yet closed; the provisional roll is pre-seeded owner-only from bookings, project collaborators and prior personnel, none of which counts as presence until the owner or delegate confirms it, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Owner/delegate confirms pre-seeded people/shells/entities, capacities and observed/inferred intervals; presence never creates credit, and (6) return Versioned temporal roll persists offline conflicts; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for an actor who is neither owner nor delegate, and `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a moved roll version. Offline edits do not fail: the merge is additive, so additions win removals, capacities union, intervals widen and are marked conflicted, and the conflict is offered for resolution at close without blocking it. Inferred endpoints never corroborate an interval, and no roll state on any path creates a credit.
- **AC-CRD-09 — Log contribution:** Given A session roll exists and the named subject is on it; Self may attribute Self while a Producer or delegate may attribute any roll party; the row carries exactly one role plus zero or more instruments, a visibility intent, and the actual human asserter rather than the device or account used, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Self attributes self; Producer/delegate may attribute any roll party; one role per row, multi-valued instruments, visibility intent and asserter recorded, and (6) return Append-only contribution claim committed; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when a non-Producer attributes another party or the named subject is not on the roll, and `VALIDATION_FAILED` on more than one role in a single row. A committed contribution log is append-only and immutable, so an error is corrected by a superseding qualifier or amendment and never by deletion; a replaced or non-final part survives as a qualified record rather than disappearing.
- **AC-CRD-10 — Close session:** Given The session reaches wrap or six hours of inactivity, and a contributor delta or Producer reconciliation set is computable from the committed roll and contribution log; no credit, split or attestation confirmation is a precondition, because close never waits for one, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Wrap fires contributor delta cards and Producer reconciliation independently; never blocks close; silence is not refusal, and (6) return Stable prompt issue IDs and passive follow-up state recorded; if the flow cannot complete, A prompt-issuing failure never blocks or reverses the close: issue is idempotent on the stable issue ID, a duplicate issue is a no-op and the delta is reissued on retry. Silence and skipped prompts carry no refusal semantics and are never recorded as negative. An incorrect auto-close is reopened within 24 hours with the event history preserved and only the changed prompt delta reissued.
- **AC-CRD-11 — Capture attendance evidence:** Given The session exists and the participant has consented to the specific byproduct signal being attached, with consent recorded per signal; the evidence attaches to the session as a reference and never to a credit, so no credit, rung or prompt depends on it, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Optional byproduct signals attach to session; declining is consequence-free; human evidence outranks device conflict, and (6) return Evidence reference recorded without gating credit; if the flow cannot complete, Declining or withdrawing consent is consequence-free, is never surfaced as a refusal and never gates a credit, a rung or a prompt. Where a device signal conflicts with a human record the human record outranks it and the conflict is recorded rather than silently resolved. A capture failure records the absence of evidence and never lowers an already-derived rung.
- **AC-CRD-12 — Import external credit:** Given A system worker with service identity holds a licensed source row and its permanent source hash; identities and taxonomy terms resolve by exact match only, because import may never fuzzy-resolve; and the candidate review queue exists to receive the row, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Source row remains source-marked, exact-resolves identities/taxonomy where possible, queues candidate review and never publishes automatically, and (6) return Candidate, source hash and review state recorded; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` on a missing or non-reproducible source hash. An identifier or role that does not resolve exactly leaves the candidate unresolved in the review queue rather than guessing a match; the candidate is capped below every captured tier and no import path writes to a public page. A rejected candidate persists as a permanent negative assertion so the same source row is not re-proposed.
- **AC-CRD-13 — Claim unclaimed credit:** Given The target credit is attached to an unclaimed shell rather than a resolved party, and the claimant is a verified person whose identity evidence reference resolves. Identity verification is the precondition for linking the record, never for confirming the contribution, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Verified person claims a shell-linked record; verification proves identity, not contribution; first claimant remains during contest, and (6) return Claim state/party link or witness request created; if the flow cannot complete, `CLAIM_CONFLICT` when a second verified person claims the same shell-linked record: the first claimant remains attached during the contest and the case routes to a witness request or Shard 06 adjudication rather than reassigning silently. `FORBIDDEN` on a claim against an already-resolved party. An unresolvable claim returns the credit to its original shell, and no claim state on any path raises the provenance rung.
- **AC-CRD-14 — Request attestation:** Given The requester is the credited party, the session owner or the close workflow; the proposed attester overlapped the session, is a present independent human, and is independent of the credited party and the asserter including through entity membership; there is no active block and no open dispute between them; the context floor of work, date and another named present party is satisfied; and the requester's cadence budget has room, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Eligible credited party/session owner/close prompt asks a present independent human using canonical claim and bounded optional note, and (6) return One pending ask per credit/attester with cadence budget; if the flow cannot complete, `ATTESTATION_INELIGIBLE` where the attester is not independent, was not present, or where a block or open dispute exists, and `RATE_LIMITED` where the cadence budget, recipient outstanding limit, rolling limit, mute or dormancy state is exhausted. Concurrent requests for the same credit and attester collapse to one pending ask rather than duplicating, and no refusal reveals the attester's block, dispute or mute state beyond the typed reason.
- **AC-CRD-15 — Answer/retract attestation:** Given A delivered request is outstanding for this attester and the claim hash shown to them still matches the credit's current claim hash; a retraction additionally requires an existing answer authored by the same attester and a stated reason, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Attester confirms/refuses/does-not-know; refusal remains unattributed; retraction requires reason and preserves original, and (6) return Immutable evidence edge and derived-state refresh queued; if the flow cannot complete, A changed claim hash invalidates the ask rather than the answer: the prior answer stays historical but no longer supports the new claim, and a fresh request is issued. Refusal and don't-know are recorded with the attester's identity and any reason kept private and publicly indistinguishable from unanswered, so no retaliation surface exists. A retraction appends and preserves the original edge, and the rung and score re-derive from the remaining eligible evidence with no public negative marker.
- **AC-CRD-16 — Derive provenance:** Given A system worker with service identity holds the credit's current eligible evidence set and its hash plus the configured algorithm version; self-assertion, claim state, imported agreement, attendance and popularity may be present in that input but none of them is an eligible promoter of the rung, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Current eligible evidence computes categorical rung plus internal score; score cannot cross rung; failure renders unavailable, not lowest, and (6) return Versioned derivation and explanation stored; if the flow cannot complete, Derivation failure emits an explicit unavailable reason and removes the label — it never substitutes the lowest rung and never silently retains a stale higher tier — and retry is idempotent on the evidence-set hash. Ring detection demotes the internal score only and can never move the categorical rung, and the internal score never appears in any projection, export or event.
- **AC-CRD-17 — Contest credit:** Given The actor is a participant on the contested credit as credited party, session participant, Producer or session owner, or work/release owner, and the Shard 06 case engine is reachable so the contest can be committed together with its case link, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Participant marks dispute; public line remains at existing tier without marker; participant view shows contest and evidence ladder, and (6) return Shard 06 case link and contest state committed; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for a non-participant and `DISPUTE_REQUIRED` where the case link cannot be established, in which case no contest state is committed at all. A contest never suppresses: the public line stays at its existing derived tier with no public marker, the marker is participant-visible only, and an unresolved contest leaves the credit attached and visible with zero weight in derived discovery.
- **AC-CRD-18 — Resolve pending taxonomy alias:** Given A `pending_role_alias` exists in an unresolved state with its retained literal and requester scope, and the acting taxonomy admin holds the reviewed vocabulary capability that also governs `vault_role_class` assignment, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Admin promotes/maps/rejects bounded literal; first mapping completes resolution without changing claim identity or invalidating attestations, and (6) return Canonical link/version added; retained literal preserved; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for any actor without the reviewed taxonomy capability and for any attempt to edit a credit claim, derive a right or retroactively rewrite an assertion through this flow. `VERSION_CONFLICT` where the pending alias was already resolved — the first mapping wins and completes resolution while later attempts are no-ops rather than re-resolutions. Deprecation never rewrites a committed credit, since credits pin their role version and retained literal, and completing a mapping does not invalidate supporting attestations.
- **AC-CRD-19 — Curate discography page:** Given The acting party is the credited party whose own page this is, or after death the Shard 01 estate governance authority; the submitted curation is within the caps of at most three primary role families and at most six pins per family with unique pin rank; and every pinned credit is already visible on that page, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Credited party sets page curation `listed`/`unlisted`, designates at most three primary role families, orders families and pins at most six highlight credits per family in their own order; curation never adds, edits or reorders a credit, never changes record confidentiality, never alters the work ledger and never invokes computed prominence, and (6) return Curation version commits; caps enforced on write and never on read; concurrent edits resolve last-write-wins scoped per role family; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` where a cap or pin-rank uniqueness is violated, because caps are enforced on write and never on read, and `FORBIDDEN` where the actor is neither the credited party nor the estate authority. Concurrent edits from two devices resolve last-write-wins scoped per role family and never per page, so edits to different families both survive. A refused curation changes no credit, no record confidentiality and no work ledger entry, and curation never invokes computed prominence to fill a gap.

### Global Interaction Rules

- Commands carry `actor_person_id`, `acting_party_id?`, `acting_context_version`, `idempotency_key`, `expected_version?` and `request_id`.
- Credit, work, session, party, role and instrument use canonical IDs; all edits are new versions/supersession.
- Confidentiality is enforced in query/RLS and every derived count/search/cache/export, never as a final render filter.
- Claiming, self-assertion, imported agreement, attendance and popularity never independently raise provenance rung.
- Contest marks but does not suppress; confidentiality still wins over citation, correction redirects and public discoverability.

## Accessibility

- Roll, contribution grid, close cards, discography, evidence timeline and taxonomy picker are keyboard complete with semantic table/list alternatives.
- Provenance, contest, qualifier and visibility states use text plus icon/structure; color never carries meaning alone.
- One-tap confirmation has an equivalent labeled control, visible claim summary and undo/retraction route; silence is never visually marked negative.
- Embargo/non-disclosure screens do not announce hidden counts or existence to assistive technology.
- Offline/conflict status is announced politely; preserved edits and exact changed rows are focus-targeted after reconciliation.
- Graph traversal has a linear path list and explanation; no force-directed canvas is required to understand results.

## Edge Cases

| Case | Required result |
|---|---|
| Concurrent same credit assertions | Unique active key converges; evidence/asserters attach without duplicate public row. |
| Offline roll add/remove conflict | Add wins, intervals union/conflicted, close offers resolution without blocking. |
| Party removed with contributions | Explicit reassign/remove proposal; immutable committed contribution not silently deleted. |
| Session auto-closes incorrectly | Reopen within 24h, preserve event history and reissue only changed prompt delta. |
| Claim changes after ask | Prior answer stays historical but no longer supports new claim; re-ask required. |
| Attestation refusal/retraction | No public negative marker; rung/score re-derive from remaining evidence. |
| Derivation worker fails | Provenance renders unavailable distinctly; never defaults to lowest or stale higher tier without label. |
| Embargoed record requested publicly | 404-equivalent and invariant counts/cache/search; no 403/existence leak. |
| Public-release evidence contested | Pause lift at status quo and use Shard 06 case; repeated evidence remains allowed. |
| Person merge uncertain | Never automatic; rejection persists as negative assertion. |
| Taxonomy unavailable/new term | Commit bounded literal and pending alias; credit flow never blocks. |
| Contest remains unresolved | Credit stays attached/visible per existing policy, zero weight in derived discovery, participant marker only. |
| Party merge leaves more than six pins in a family | Pin sets union past the cap; every merged pin renders and the survivor is prompted to re-curate. Curation is never silently dropped. |
| Owner curates on two devices at once | Last-write-wins scoped per role family, never per page; concurrent edits to different families both survive. |
