# Shard 37 — Fanbase and direct-to-fan

**Status:** Complete
**Surface:** Responsive web/PWA
**Source:** [Architecture design](../2026-08-02-architecture-design.md) · [Decomposition plan](decomposition-plan.md)

## Overview

Shard 37 owns the private fan-to-entity relationship, consent and preferences, segmentation, direct messaging intent, entity storefront, single-payee memberships and patronage, fan library, follows, gig alerts and private demand signals. It consumes identity from [[specs/ia/01-identity-authority|Shard 01]], platform delivery primitives from [[specs/ia/00-infrastructure|Shard 00]], moderation from [[specs/ia/06-trust-safety|Shard 06]], catalog objects from [[specs/ia/22-release-distribution|Shard 22]] and ticket/show facts from [[specs/ia/35-ticket-products-sales|Shard 35]].

### Scope Reconciliation

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| In-scope source documents | 40 |
| Child capabilities | 27 across 20.01–20.07 |
| Audience boundary | One private relationship per fan and artist entity; observations are append-only and imports never manufacture consent |
| Consent boundary | Fan-authored, channel-and-purpose-specific, strict opt-in; suppression and latest fan instruction always win |
| Intelligence boundary | Explainable live segments and confidence-gated affinity bands; no opaque consequential scoring |
| Commerce boundary | Entity-scoped storefront and single-payee settlement only; no marketplace, escrow or collaborator payout under counsel gate B3 |
| Messaging boundary | Composer, audience resolution and compliance controls may ship; bulk delivery remains disabled until sender and operations gates pass |
| Discovery boundary | Browser-local follows remain local; durable alerts require verified email and explicit consent under B4 |
| Privacy boundary | Fan identities and cross-artist behavior are never exposed; sparse demand/analytics remain disabled under counsel gate B2 |

### Architecture Decisions

| Area | Locked decision |
|---|---|
| Fan relationship | Canonical key is `(fan_party_id, artist_entity_id)`. Strong identifiers may merge observations automatically; weak matches remain quarantined. Account closure severs identity without deleting lawful transactional evidence. |
| Consent | Append-only ledger per entity, channel and purpose. Latest fan-authored timestamp wins; owner/admin cannot grant, restore or bypass consent. Ledger uncertainty fails closed. |
| Preferences | Passwordless signed preference access supports unsubscribe without login. Global marketing opt-out stops all marketing, not required transactional/service notices. Re-grant requires a fresh affirmative act. |
| Imports | Verified entities may import one-way source records with asserted provenance. Import creates an unmarketable quarantined relationship until an independently valid consent exists. Suppression always wins. |
| Entity succession | Audience belongs to the artist entity, not an acting person. Reparenting requires recorded controller change, fan notice and policy approval; marketing fails closed pending re-permission when controller status changes. |
| Segments | Segments are versioned live predicates; campaign recipient snapshots are immutable. Contactability is resolved again at dispatch. |
| Affinity | Rule-based bands weight attended events above spend, include recency decay and expose factors to authorized entity staff. Low-confidence bands are withheld and never fan-visible or sole eligibility gates. |
| Messaging | Object-first block composer produces separate channel renderings. One campaign delivers at most once per fan, using fan channel preference; no silent fallback. In-flight campaign revisions are immutable. |
| Delivery launch | Bulk email, SMS, push or equivalent marketing broadcast is disabled until managed sender, numeric bounce/complaint thresholds, ramp curve, domain policy and blocklist incident runbook are approved. SMS additionally requires jurisdictional counsel approval. |
| Storefront | Store-scoped cart per selling entity and fulfillment group; ticketing remains separate. Minimal fan record and verified delivery email support purchase without forced account creation. Products reference canonical catalog and rights facts. |
| Products | Shared product shell delegates kind-specific data for digital, own-stock physical and POD merchandise. Finite variants reserve stock for 15 minutes; price holds 30 minutes; money uses integer minor units and immutable sale currency. |
| Money | Paid-net is the versioned platform basis term. V1 supports one compliance-cleared payee only. Multi-party plans, held collaborator funds, rolling reserves, campaign custody and venue merch splits are disabled under B3. |
| Membership | Artist-entity memberships only at consumer launch. Cancellation is immediate and symmetric. Billing outage never revokes existing access. Content permanently granted while active survives lapse; continuing vault access does not. |
| Patronage | Tips grant no entitlement, route to one payee, remain private to the artist and pass moderation. Paid crowdfunding and preorder fund custody are disabled; non-monetary campaign pages may collect interest only. |
| Virtual events | Membership listening parties/livestreams use a provider-neutral access envelope, no v1 live chat, immediate access for mid-event joins and no mid-event ejection after lapse. Streaming transport remains replaceable. |
| Fan experience | Follows begin browser-local with no alert delivery. Verified email plus explicit gig-alert consent creates a durable follow. Alerts cover announced first-party shows at on-sale and deduplicate by event. Listening imports are one-time, fan-initiated snapshots, never ongoing surveillance. |
| Library | Paid and permanently granted digital entitlements survive delisting, entity departure and campaign failure. Downloads are DRM-free, format-agnostic and unmetered as a right, with abuse-safe queue pacing. |
| Demand | Raw fan demand is private to the fan. Artist demand maps, low-count exports and promoter/operator analytics do not ship until B2 review approves numeric floors and enforcement. |
| Configuration | Holds, fees, limits, thresholds, radii, quiet hours, decay rules, grace periods and retention windows are typed, versioned settings with safe defaults and audit history; none are hardcoded in application logic. |

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-37.01 — Observe fan relationship:** Given Valid first-party source event, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Observe fan relationship, and (6) return Immutable observation appends; resolver creates/links relationship; if the flow cannot complete, Weak identity quarantines; event is never dropped.
- **AC-37.02 — Grant or withdraw consent:** Given Fan-controlled verified channel or signed preference token, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Grant or withdraw consent, and (6) return Ledger event appends and effective contactability recomputes; if the flow cannot complete, Ledger unavailable fails closed; withdrawal wins dispatch race.
- **AC-37.03 — Manage preferences:** Given Valid fan session or single-purpose signed token, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Manage preferences, and (6) return Channel, purpose, frequency and location settings change additively; if the flow cannot complete, Token cannot enumerate other artists or purposes.
- **AC-37.04 — Import audience:** Given Verified entity mandate and provenance declaration, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Import audience, and (6) return Rows validate, quarantine and resolve without consent mutation; if the flow cannot complete, Invalid/stale/weak rows stay isolated with row-level report.
- **AC-37.05 — Transfer entity stewardship:** Given Approved controller-change workflow, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Transfer entity stewardship, and (6) return Relationship records reparent atomically; fans receive notice; if the flow cannot complete, Marketing remains suppressed until controller-change policy passes.
- **AC-37.06 — Define segment:** Given Authorized entity actor and versioned predicate schema, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Define segment, and (6) return Live segment saves with count range and factor explanation; if the flow cannot complete, Unsupported/sparse predicate refuses disclosure.
- **AC-37.07 — Grant presale/benefit:** Given Explicit criteria and entitlement definition, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Grant presale/benefit, and (6) return Eligibility snapshots idempotently; mechanism delegates to owner shard; if the flow cannot complete, Affinity alone cannot deny; oversubscribed grants are blocked.
- **AC-37.08 — Compose campaign:** Given Authorized entity actor and supported source object, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Compose campaign, and (6) return Channel-specific immutable revision previews recipients, cost and compliance; if the flow cannot complete, Missing required block, legal footer or delivery gate blocks send.
- **AC-37.09 — Schedule/dispatch campaign:** Given Approved delivery capability and audience snapshot, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Schedule/dispatch campaign, and (6) return Fan preference selects one channel; consent rechecked immediately before dispatch; if the flow cannot complete, Unknown consent/reputation throttles or suppresses; no fallback.
- **AC-37.10 — Cancel campaign:** Given Campaign has undispatched timezone buckets, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Cancel campaign, and (6) return Remaining buckets cancel; prior deliveries remain auditable; if the flow cannot complete, In-flight transport may complete but dedupe prevents retry duplicate.
- **AC-37.11 — Browse storefront:** Given Published entity store and product projection, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Browse storefront, and (6) return Fan sees availability, lock state, credits, all-in price and fulfillment facts; if the flow cannot complete, Stale stock becomes unavailable without hiding product.
- **AC-37.12 — Reserve and checkout:** Given Valid product revision, stock and one-payee route, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Reserve and checkout, and (6) return Inventory/price holds commit to order; fan relationship observation appends; if the flow cannot complete, Expired hold re-prices visibly; uncertain payment reconciles before retry.
- **AC-37.13 — Fulfill digital purchase:** Given Settled order and encoded asset, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Fulfill digital purchase, and (6) return Permanent entitlement enters library and receipt records terms; if the flow cannot complete, CDN failure preserves entitlement and offers retry.
- **AC-37.14 — Fulfill physical/POD order:** Given Settled order and accepted provider/artist route, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Fulfill physical/POD order, and (6) return Tracking and lead-time state update without platform stock custody; if the flow cannot complete, Provider uncertainty remains explicit; refund path remains available.
- **AC-37.15 — Join/cancel membership:** Given Published non-empty tier and valid one-payee billing, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Join/cancel membership, and (6) return Membership and benefit state append; cancellation stops renewal immediately; if the flow cannot complete, Billing outage preserves current access; failed renewal enters grace state.
- **AC-37.16 — Publish vault item:** Given Authorized artist actor, collaborator release authority and non-empty tier, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Publish vault item, and (6) return Item grants snapshot entitlements to active tiers; if the flow cannot complete, Missing consent/asset blocks publication; raised tier never revokes prior grant.
- **AC-37.17 — Tip artist:** Given Fan, one-payee route and moderated optional message, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Tip artist, and (6) return Non-entitling patronage receipt and private message append; if the flow cannot complete, Blocked parties cannot bypass block by paying; payment ambiguity reconciles.
- **AC-37.18 — Publish campaign interest page:** Given Authorized artist actor and no payment/custody action, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Publish campaign interest page, and (6) return Non-monetary goal, updates and private interest signals publish; if the flow cannot complete, Pledge/capture/release controls remain absent while B3 gate is closed.
- **AC-37.19 — Join virtual fan event:** Given Active/grace membership or newly settled join, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Join virtual fan event, and (6) return Access lease admits fan; mid-event join works immediately; if the flow cannot complete, Lapse during event does not eject; blocked/moderated access remains enforceable.
- **AC-37.20 — Follow/unfollow artist:** Given Browser or authenticated fan context, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Follow/unfollow artist, and (6) return Local follow toggles instantly; verified consent may upgrade it to durable; if the flow cannot complete, Unfollow suppresses re-import and cancels pending marketing alerts.
- **AC-37.21 — Dispatch gig alert:** Given Durable follow, location, consent and announced first-party on-sale event, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Dispatch gig alert, and (6) return One event-keyed alert explains artist, radius and reason; if the flow cannot complete, Missing location surfaces partial state; stale event cancels alert.
- **AC-37.22 — Use fan library:** Given Fan owns entitlement, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Use fan library, and (6) return Stream/download/export returns owned artifact plus manifest; if the flow cannot complete, Unknown ownership returns 404; throttling queues rather than denies.
- **AC-37.23 — Import listening history:** Given Fan uploads supported export or grants scoped one-time source read, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Import listening history, and (6) return Suggestions render for explicit confirmation; no auto-follow; if the flow cannot complete, Unmatched artists remain private informational demand.
- **AC-37.24 — Request a show:** Given Fan has location and artist context, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Request a show, and (6) return Private non-binding request appends and can be withdrawn; if the flow cannot complete, No public count or commitment language; B2 blocks aggregate disclosure.

## Data Models

| Entity | Key relationships and constraints |
|---|---|
| `fan_relationship` | Unique `(fan_party_id, artist_entity_id)`; lifecycle active/severed/deidentified; never contains effective consent flags |
| `fan_observation` | Immutable source fact linked to relationship or quarantine; source event ID unique per producer |
| `identity_resolution_case` | Strong/weak evidence, candidate set and human-safe resolution state; no artist-facing cross-entity matches |
| `consent_event` | Append-only fan instruction by entity/channel/purpose; supersession derived, never updated in place |
| `fan_preference` | Frequency tier, channel order, locale and coarse geo; optimistic revision and explicit provenance |
| `audience_import` / `import_row` | Entity-scoped batch, asserted provenance, row quarantine and irreversible suppression checks |
| `segment_definition` | Versioned typed predicate and explainability metadata; memberships in a segment are computed, not stored |
| `affinity_projection` | Entity-scoped rule version, band, confidence and factor contributions; disposable projection |
| `campaign` / `campaign_revision` | Stable campaign identity and immutable typed content/audience revisions; schedule references one revision |
| `recipient_snapshot` / `delivery_attempt` | Immutable intended audience and one deduped outcome per fan; separate suppression reason and provider receipt |
| `store` / `product_listing` / `product_variant` | One entity store; delegated kind payload; listing and inventory revisions; sold-out remains visible |
| `cart` / `reservation` / `order` / `order_line` | One seller/fulfillment scope; immutable price/terms snapshots; payment and fulfillment states are orthogonal |
| `membership_tier` / `membership` | Versioned benefits and hard capacity; active/grace/cancelled/ended state with no destructive history |
| `vault_item` / `benefit_grant` | Source asset, collaborator authority and tier rules; immutable per-fan grant snapshot |
| `tip` | One-payee payment, no entitlement relation, optional moderated private message |
| `interest_campaign` / `campaign_interest` | Non-monetary goal/update shell and private fan signal; no pledge, fund or tranche relation while gated |
| `virtual_event` / `event_access_lease` | Provider-neutral event metadata, qualifying tier and non-revoking admission lease; no v1 chat model |
| `follow` / `alert_subscription` | Local follow has anonymous browser key; durable follow links verified fan plus consent and geo |
| `fan_entitlement` | Fan/source/artifact/grant permanence and available formats; independent of current listing availability |
| `listening_import` / `follow_suggestion` | Scoped source snapshot and fan-confirmed result; rejected/unfollowed suggestions stay suppressed |
| `demand_signal` | Private fan/entity/coarse-geo fact; aggregate projections absent until counsel-approved B2 policy |

Canonical facts use Supabase PostgreSQL with row-level security. Raw contact details, signed preference tokens and import payloads remain in restricted PII schemas; artist-facing projections use opaque relationship IDs. Campaign renderings and receipts retain the policy/terms versions needed to reconstruct a send or sale. Erasure severs direct identifiers while preserving legally required orders, consent evidence and entitlement facts under the retention schedule.

### Typed Field and Cardinality Registry

Field typing is deterministic: `*_id: uuid`, `*_at: timestamptz`, `*_date: date`, `*_minor: bigint`, `*_count: integer`, `currency: char(3)`, `is_*|has_*: boolean`, `state|status|type|kind|class: closed enum`, `version: bigint`, ratios `numeric(9,6)`, checksums `text`, and URLs `text`. Every named contract field uses this registry unless its contract declares a stricter type.

- **`fan_relationship`:** required core fields `id: uuid`, `owner_id: uuid`, `state: closed enum`, `version: bigint`, `created_at: timestamptz`, `updated_at: timestamptz`; domain fields are the named keys in Contracts and the model row above using the deterministic registry; cardinality is N:1 to its owner/aggregate and 1:N to additive events, revisions or evidence unless the row declares uniqueness. Constraints/relationships: Unique `(fan_party_id, artist_entity_id)`; lifecycle active/severed/deidentified; never contains effective consent flags.
- **`fan_observation`:** required core fields `id: uuid`, `owner_id: uuid`, `state: closed enum`, `version: bigint`, `created_at: timestamptz`, `updated_at: timestamptz`; domain fields are the named keys in Contracts and the model row above using the deterministic registry; cardinality is N:1 to its owner/aggregate and 1:N to additive events, revisions or evidence unless the row declares uniqueness. Constraints/relationships: Immutable source fact linked to relationship or quarantine; source event ID unique per producer.

RLS keys every entity-managed record by entity and mandate. Sensitive reads require purpose-bound audit context. ## Accessibility

- All consent, unsubscribe, follow, checkout, membership cancellation and campaign controls are complete by keyboard with visible focus and no drag-only operation.
- Consent choices use explicit labels for entity, channel and purpose; no preselected marketing checkbox, color-only state or bundled acceptance.
- Screen-reader live regions announce cart expiry, stock conflict, send suppression totals, membership state and download queue progress without exposing other fans.
- Composer blocks preserve semantic heading/list/link structure across channel previews; compliance text cannot be hidden or visually de-emphasized below readable contrast and size.
- Countdown timers expose absolute expiry time and do not auto-submit; users receive warning and a non-destructive refresh path.
- Store, library and preference views meet WCAG 2.1 AA at 320 CSS pixels, 200% zoom, reduced motion and high-contrast modes.
- Gig-alert location entry accepts manual place/postcode input; geolocation permission denial never blocks follow creation.
- Charts and segment summaries provide equivalent textual ranges and factor tables; sparse values remain omitted rather than represented ambiguously.
- Media items include captions/transcripts when speech is material; virtual-event access never depends solely on live audio.
- Normal web/PWA reads target p95 ≤2 seconds; slower projections render named freshness/loading states and never fake complete data.

## Edge Cases

- Two observations with the same verified email merge only after identity policy confirms a strong key; conflicting account evidence opens a resolution case and preserves both originals.
- Consent withdrawal committed after campaign snapshot but before transport handoff suppresses delivery; after provider acceptance it records the race and blocks every future send.
- Preference token replay is idempotent, expires by purpose and cannot reveal whether unrelated artist relationships exist.
- Importing a suppressed address neither reactivates contactability nor tells the artist that the address belongs to an existing fan.
- Entity controller change freezes marketing while transactional order/membership notices continue from the named service controller.
- Simultaneous segment edits use expected revision; send snapshots retain the exact accepted predicate version.
- Affinity confidence drops when source data is stale or sparse; projection becomes unavailable rather than silently changing eligibility.
- Overlapping campaigns compete against fan frequency policy at dispatch; scheduling first never reserves a fan's attention.
- Provider timeout after send/payment acceptance enters reconciliation; retries reuse the same dedupe/idempotency key.
- Concurrent finite-stock carts reserve atomically; expired holds release once and a late payment creates a reviewed exception, never negative stock.
- Product edits during checkout do not alter held price/terms; material fulfillment change requires reconfirmation or refund.
- Membership capacity is consumed only after payment confirmation; simultaneous last-slot attempts resolve deterministically.
- Billing outage extends access through grace; artist/entity termination stops future billing and preserves permanent grants.
- A blocked fan cannot purchase a tip message to bypass a communication block; the payment is refused before capture.
- Unfollow during queued alert processing suppresses dispatch; later listening import cannot recreate the follow.
- Multi-artist event creates one alert listing followed acts; cancellation before dispatch invalidates the candidate.
- Media/CDN outage displays entitlement as owned and unavailable, never revoked; export retries preserve manifest checksum.
- Legal takedown moves an artifact to `withheld_legal_review` with explicit support/refund state, not deletion disguised as non-ownership.
- Location absence is visible in follow and demand views; no inferred precise location is persisted from IP address.
- Paid crowdfunding, multi-party settlement, sparse demand maps and bulk broadcast return stable policy-disabled errors until their gates are approved.
