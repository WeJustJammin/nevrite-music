## Overview

Shard 15 owns teacher tuition facets, lesson series/credits, cancellation, safeguarded delivery, teacher discovery/trials, curriculum/assignments/practice, group teaching, mentorship and curated learning paths. It separates educational participation from credentials, credits, public reputation and automated assessment. Consumer launch is adults-only; minor registration/teaching and all guardian/chaperone flows remain fully specified but disabled until the safeguarding/minor gate is approved as a whole.

## Features

- **06.01 Lesson Booking, Packages & Delivery** — recurring series, teacher-scoped lesson credits, frozen cancellation policy and safeguarded session record.
- **06.02 Teacher Discovery, Profiles & Trials** — identity facet, separate evidence kinds, published transparent matching and bounded trials.
- **06.03 Curriculum, Assignments & Practice** — optional plan instances, timestamped feedback, non-coercive practice capture/tools and teacher-approved progress reports.
- **06.05 Group Lessons, Workshops & Masterclasses** — viability/roster/safeguarding sibling lifecycle.
- **06.06 Mentorship Programmes** — scarce fixed-term relationship without lesson credits/cancellation/curriculum inheritance.
- **06.07 Learning Paths** — self-paced curation over existing content with total cost disclosure.

### Delivery Phases

| Phase | Enabled boundary |
|---|---|
| Adult consumer launch | Adult teacher/student profiles, discovery, booking/credits, remote/in-person lessons, assignments/practice, adult groups/mentorship/paths |
| Minor activation | Entire approved age/guardian/vetting/chaperone/recording/feedback/privacy control set; no piecemeal feature flags |
| Deferred | Automated skill assessment, participation grading, trust scores, platform-wide lesson credits and guaranteed teacher feedback SLA |

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-EDU-01 — Publish tuition facet:** Given Authenticated adult teacher owns the target tuition facet under current acting context, a rate card line and availability rule exist, and Shard 02 evidence blocks are read-only projections, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Teacher explicitly publishes self-authored fields, structured rate/availability/intake and read-only evidence blocks; contact data blocked, and (6) return Facet version publishes after rate/availability/age-range gates; if the flow cannot complete, Missing rate, availability or age-range gate returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` and the facet stays unpublished; a known minor teacher returns `AGE_GATE_DISABLED`; a stale facet revision returns `VERSION_CONFLICT` and the teacher republishes from current state; expired credential or vetting retracts the evidence projection and the under-18 range while the eligible adult facet remains published.
- **AC-EDU-02 — Discover teacher:** Given Authenticated adult student supplies instrument, level, age band, mode, language and availability window, and blocks, restrictions and safeguarding predicates have already removed ineligible teachers from the pool before ranking, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Student states instrument/level/age/mode/language/window; hard filters precede transparent weighted ranking and partitioned bookability, and (6) return Small ranked list with reasons/missing inputs; if the flow cannot complete, Invalid or missing filter input returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` naming the field; an empty pool widens progressively and explicitly rather than returning bare silence; missing or degraded evidence is reported as a result reason and never removes a teacher for absent evidence, and no refusal path exposes a rank integer or comparative position.
- **AC-EDU-03 — Book trial/lesson:** Given Authenticated adult student selects a bookable occurrence on a published rate card line whose exact cancellation policy version, credit or money value and safeguarding profile have been displayed, and a trial additionally requires that no trial already exists for this teacher-student pair, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Student selects occurrence/series/rate line/mode; exact policy, credit/value and safeguarding profile shown, and (6) return Booking/reservation/payment or credit hold commits; if the flow cannot complete, A known minor participant returns `AGE_GATE_DISABLED` before any booking record exists; a failed safeguarding predicate returns `SAFEGUARDING_FAILED` without leaking the reason; insufficient entitlement returns `CREDIT_UNAVAILABLE`; a rate or policy version that changed since display returns `POLICY_CONFLICT` or `VERSION_CONFLICT` and the student re-confirms current terms; a replayed idempotency key returns the original booking instead of a second one.
- **AC-EDU-04 — Purchase lesson pack:** Given Authenticated adult purchaser selects a teacher or academy-scoped rate line carrying a current price, cancellation policy, currency, tax, FX and revenue-share snapshot, and auto-renew is off unless explicitly opted in, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Purchaser buys teacher-scoped rate-line credits with pinned currency/tax/FX/revenue share/policy; auto-renew opt-in only, and (6) return Credit ledger and liability evidence commit; no teacher earning yet; if the flow cannot complete, Validation, authority or pinned-policy mismatch returns a typed refusal before any provider effect, leaving no ledger entry and no charge; a duplicate idempotency key returns the original purchase; a rate-line or policy version change during checkout returns `POLICY_CONFLICT` for re-confirmation; an unused pack that later expires converts to redeemable residual purchase value rather than being destroyed or paid to the teacher.
- **AC-EDU-05 — Cancel/no-show/make-up:** Given The actor is the booking's student, purchaser or teacher, the occurrence is in `booked` or `room_open` and not yet closed, and the booking-pinned cancellation policy version and its exact credit consequence have been shown; cancellation is no longer available once joint presence has begun, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Actor sees exact credit consequence before action; policy at booking governs; evidence/ordering handles cancellation/no-show race, and (6) return Settlement event/credit return-burn/make-up grant commits; if the flow cannot complete, A settlement write failure leaves the lesson `booked` with no partial credit movement and retries under the same idempotency key; a cancellation received before the no-show commit wins as a late cancel; a no-show cannot commit before scheduled end and is vacated by five minutes of joint presence; missing in-person delivery evidence after seven days returns the credit rather than burning it; a competing settlement returns `VERSION_CONFLICT` to the loser.
- **AC-EDU-06 — Join lesson room:** Given An authenticated identity holds a named participant or consented observer role on the occurrence, the occurrence is in `booked` or `room_open`, and the occurrence's current safeguarding profile is re-evaluated server-side at join time, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Identity-bound participants pass current safeguarding gate; no anonymous/link/dial-in join, and (6) return Presence/session grant or actionable fail-closed denial; if the flow cannot complete, An unauthenticated, bearer-link, dial-in or anonymous joiner returns `ROOM_IDENTITY_REQUIRED`; a failed safeguarding predicate returns `SAFEGUARDING_FAILED` and fails closed with an actionable denial that does not disclose the participant list; a known minor under the adult launch profile returns `AGE_GATE_DISABLED`; no denial creates presence, attendance or delivery evidence.
- **AC-EDU-07 — Deliver/close lesson:** Given The occurrence reached `room_open` with server-stamped joins and leaves recorded, or an in-person delivery assertion exists, and the acting teacher is the assigned teacher for the relationship; the delivery predicate is evaluated only against server presence, never inferred before a human joins, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Server-stamped concurrent presence evaluates delivery; teacher captures session record and next assignment, but write failure does not block earned outcome, and (6) return Delivery fact and education record/outbox commit; if the flow cannot complete, Concurrent presence below fifty percent of scheduled duration returns `DELIVERY_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` and classifies the occurrence as `partial`, `no_show` or `no_fault` rather than `delivered`; a failed session-record write does not block the earned delivery or credit outcome and opens a private teacher quality task for retry; an absent in-person assertion after seven days returns the credit.
- **AC-EDU-08 — Convert trial:** Given A `trial_relationship` exists for this teacher-student pair and its trial occurrence has closed, so the end-of-room conversion prompt is offered; no rating, message or outcome declaration is required from either party for the flow to be legal, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) End-of-room prompt offers series; walking away needs no message/rating; one bounded 48h nudge only, and (6) return Conversion/quiet-expiry state commits; if the flow cannot complete, Declining or ignoring the prompt is a valid terminal outcome requiring no message or rating; at most one nudge is sent 48 hours after the trial and the relationship goes silent at seven days into quiet expiry; a duplicate conversion attempt returns the existing series rather than creating a second one.
- **AC-EDU-09 — Create curriculum/path:** Given An authenticated teacher holds a current teacher-student relationship version and references a curriculum template version that exists; instantiation is optional, so the flow is legal only where the teacher chooses to create a plan instance, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Teacher instantiates optional versioned curriculum; learner may proceed/self-place without behind-schedule state, and (6) return Plan instance/version relation commits; if the flow cannot complete, A missing or unreadable template version returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` and no instance is created; an expired relationship returns `FORBIDDEN`; a concurrent instance edit returns `VERSION_CONFLICT`; later template edits never retroactively mutate a live instance, and a learner who proceeds or self-places is never placed in a behind-schedule state.
- **AC-EDU-10 — Assign/submit work:** Given Assignment requires the authoring teacher to hold a current teacher-student relationship and to name a medium with an optional reference; submission requires the student to own that assignment and to record a take within the ten-minute in-room or twenty-minute external duration cap, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Teacher defines medium/reference; student submits optional resumable take; no grade/overdue/quota display, and (6) return Assignment/take/version/outbox state commits; if the flow cannot complete, An over-length external take is rejected before transfer and an in-room take auto-stops at ten minutes; a failed upload returns `UPLOAD_FAILED`, preserves the take locally and retries at 2s, 8s and 32s before moving to a persistent outbox that survives to the next open and never expires; resubmission creates a new immutable take and destroys neither the earlier take nor its annotations.
- **AC-EDU-11 — Annotate feedback:** Given The authoring teacher holds a current teacher-student relationship, the target take is inside the teacher's unexpired access window, the declared feedback stance and its cap still permit this annotation, and the async channel passes the lesson-level safeguarding profile, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Authorized teacher records time/range comments under declared stance; async channel uses lesson-level safeguarding, and (6) return Feedback version/access-retention state commits; if the flow cannot complete, A stance of `in_lesson_only` or an exhausted weekly cap returns `FEEDBACK_NOT_INCLUDED` and states the stance rather than implying a service failure; an expired teacher access window ninety days after the last delivered lesson returns `FORBIDDEN` while the student keeps their own material; a bar or beat anchor without a declared click or tempo falls back to the timestamp anchor; no platform feedback SLA is promised or owed.
- **AC-EDU-12 — Practice:** Given The authenticated student records their own practice, optionally referencing one of their own assignments; every tool is optional and the shard states no further gate, so the flow is legal with or without a lesson, assignment or network connection, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Student uses optional timer/click/tuner/drone/recording; logging is byproduct, offline/manual backfill allowed, streak optional, and (6) return Private practice event/log version commits; if the flow cannot complete, An offline or failed sync preserves the event locally and backfills on reconnection, and the student may enter or correct a duration manually; a named practice log requested by a guardian, teacher, operator or public projection is denied by row-level policy; a broken streak never blocks, shames or penalises the student and carries no evidentiary meaning.
- **AC-EDU-13 — Issue progress report:** Given The authenticated teacher holds a current relationship with the student, coverage evidence for the reporting interval exists, and the teacher has explicitly reviewed and approved the draft; the report is optional and no report exists without that approval, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Teacher reviews coverage evidence and explicitly approves optional report; platform never measures musicianship, and (6) return Approved report version and audience commit; if the flow cannot complete, Any attempt to publish without explicit teacher approval returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` and no report version is created; missing coverage evidence is rendered as a stated gap rather than inferred; no path may substitute a skill rating, grade or musicianship measure for the missing fact; a concurrent approval returns `VERSION_CONFLICT`.
- **AC-EDU-14 — Run group class:** Given The authenticated organizer has frozen roster composition, minimum viable enrollment, schedule, refund terms and the group safeguarding profile, and the class is in `enrolling`; activation is legal only once enrollment reaches the declared viability threshold, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Organizer publishes roster composition/viability/refund/safeguarding; class proceeds only at threshold, and (6) return Cohort/occurrences/refund or delivery state commits; if the flow cannot complete, A class below its viability threshold at the decision point moves to `cancelled_refunded` and refunds every participant rather than silently running a materially different cohort; the group safeguarding profile can never be relaxed to 1:1 controls; a concurrent enrollment past capacity returns `VERSION_CONFLICT` to the loser with no double seat.
- **AC-EDU-15 — Run mentorship:** Given Both mentor and mentee are authenticated adults, the mentorship is `proposed` with an explicit fixed term, end, goals and check-in cadence, and the mentor's scarce capacity snapshot of an expected one to three mentees still has an open slot, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Mentor/mentee enter fixed-term scarce-capacity programme with goals/check-ins/end; no lesson-credit/cancellation/curriculum semantics, and (6) return Mentorship agreement/state/check-ins commit; if the flow cannot complete, A mentor at capacity returns `FORBIDDEN` and the proposal stays unaccepted; any attempt to apply lesson-credit, cancellation-policy or curriculum semantics returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` because those relations do not exist for a mentorship; the term closes into a deliberate `completed` or `cancelled` state with retained history and never auto-renews into an indefinite relationship.
- **AC-EDU-16 — Follow learning path:** Given The authenticated learner has been shown the path's total cost and requirements from the current cost version before enrolling, and every curated unit reference still resolves; self-placement at any unit is permitted with no prerequisite gate, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Learner self-places and consumes existing units with upfront total cost/requirements, and (6) return Path enrollment/progress references commit; if the flow cannot complete, A referenced unit that has been removed or become unavailable is reported as an explicit gap and the disclosed total is recomputed rather than silently substituted; a cost version change between disclosure and enrollment returns `POLICY_CONFLICT` and the learner re-confirms the current total; a duplicate enrollment returns the existing enrollment.

### Global Interaction Rules

- Commands carry `actor_person_id`, `acting_party_id?`, `acting_context_version`, `idempotency_key`, `expected_version?`, `request_id` and teacher-student relationship version.
- Consumer launch rejects known under-18 participants before booking/profile publication; minor-specific records cannot be enabled independently.
- Lesson credits are teacher/academy + rate-line scoped units, not money or platform currency. Undelivered value remains with platform provider ledger.
- Lesson attendance/delivery, session record, practice, assignment, feedback, credential and public reputation are separate facts.
- Blocks/restrictions and safeguarding predicates override discovery, booking, room, assignment, feedback, group and mentorship access without leaking reasons.
- Education data never creates Shard 07 professional credit, automated skill rating or public participation score.
