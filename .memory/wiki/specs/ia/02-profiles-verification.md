## Overview

Shard 02 turns Shard 01 parties into truthful public/protected representations, provides bounded account-free shadow-party notice and remedy, governs reversible ownership claims and contests, composes fact-level provenance into profiles/portfolios/EPKs, records issuer-backed professional credentials, and classifies marketplace sellers. It never treats public text, a provider login, a registry identifier, or a self-declaration as authority or verification.

## Features

- **01.05 Profile Claiming & Ownership Verification** — shadow-party creation by reference, bounded invitation, tiered claim proof, provisional control, contests, transfer, suppression, and correction without forced signup.
- **01.06 Portfolio, Media Reel & EPK** — one fixed profile spine, fact-level provenance, credit-backed identity portfolio, rights-governed reel, live per-pitch EPK, and accessible derived export.
- **01.07 Professional, Union & Credential Verification** — issuer/method/evidence/expiry-aware credential records whose domain requirements remain with consuming domains.
- **01.08 Trader vs Private Seller Classification** — party-level situational declaration, mismatch review, jurisdiction rule-pack disclosure, and fail-closed listing eligibility.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-PRF-01 — Create shadow by reference:** Given A qualifying domain command from an authorized creator names an absent party with structured role or instrument plus a source domain and entity reference; that source-domain, entity and party binding must be unique, and creation itself is never quota-blocked, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) A qualifying domain command names an absent party and atomically creates/reuses an inert Shard 01 party plus structured context; creation itself is never quota-blocked, and (6) return Original domain fact commits even if matching/invitation fails; if the flow cannot complete, An abusive or mixed-script name still creates an inert flagged record with no index and no public page, because structured fields rather than free text carry the capture; a repeated source binding or idempotency key returns the existing shadow rather than a second party; a failure in matching or invitation never rolls back the original domain fact.
- **AC-PRF-02 — Match possible duplicate:** Given A newly captured or existing party reference is available to match against; matching is advisory and never a uniqueness constraint, and it must not block the submitting command, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Return suggestions within 400ms; never block submit; continue matching after commit and offer merge/claim later, and (6) return No silent merge or dropped capture; if the flow cannot complete, Exceeding the 400ms suggestion budget returns no suggestion rather than blocking submit, and matching continues after commit to offer merge or claim later; two shadows that are likely the same party are suggested for merge and both are preserved until an approved claim or merge, so no silent merge, dropped capture or automatic authority occurs.
- **AC-PRF-03 — Dispatch invitation:** Given The shadow has a contact route with no active outreach or both-scope suppression, the creator has remaining dispatch budget for their standing, and the attempt sits within the lifetime maximum of six on the day 0, 3 and 14 schedule plus one extra per new unrelated attester; a known or suspected minor context blocks dispatch entirely, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Apply creator-standing dispatch budget; queue excess; send day 0/3/14, one extra per new unrelated attester, lifetime maximum six, and (6) return Notice reveals attester, count, instrument/role—not work titles or portfolio; if the flow cannot complete, Exceeding the dispatch budget queues transparently and never rejects the capture or accuses the creator; a suppression, a not-you response, a completed claim or the lifetime cap stops every future attempt; a failed send stays retryable, and no notice ever reveals work titles or portfolio content.
- **AC-PRF-04 — Account-free suppress/correct:** Given The requester demonstrates control of the contacted route, or supplies protected case evidence, for a shadow that exists; account creation is explicitly not required, and possession of the invitation link alone is not control, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Verify control of the contacted route or protected case evidence without requiring account creation, and (6) return Future outreach/public release is blocked; underlying third-party fact remains reviewable; if the flow cannot complete, An unverified or mistyped route stops all outreach without login but grants no control and never claims the party; the underlying third-party fact remains protected and reviewable rather than deleted, and suppression is recorded as a protected lookup, never as a public registry of non-users.
- **AC-PRF-05 — Start/resume claim:** Given The claimant authenticates or creates an account — the non-expiring link is only a pointer and never authenticates — and the target party must not be suppressed for claim contact, frozen, merged without a redirect, or under an incompatible case; at most one active self claim exists per person per target, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Non-expiring link is only a pointer. Authenticate/create account, load current party/claim state, and offer fastest eligible fresh proof, and (6) return Claim remains resumable indefinitely; no link authenticates; if the flow cannot complete, A target merged mid-flow redirects the claim to the survivor and re-runs the proof, visibility and conflict checks; an unavailable proof provider leaves the claim open and offers another method with no downgrade and no hidden denial; the claim stays resumable indefinitely and link possession never grants control.
- **AC-PRF-06 — Complete claim proof:** Given A fresh single-use challenge exists — six digits, at most five attempts, expiring in 15 minutes and stored hashed — the offered method's adapter is configured and healthy for Tier A, and any Tier B or C attester is an independent human who is not the claimant through any acting party, not a same-organization co-member, not in a mandate relation and not from the same project or session, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Evaluate Tier A/B/C, independence, self-attestation, current party state, and contest status, and (6) return Full or provisional control is attached to the existing party; history never migrates to a new party; if the flow cannot complete, A burned, expired or over-attempted challenge grants nothing and a new challenge must be issued; a failed independence check rejects that attester rather than the claim; a Tier C ring confirming each other reaches provisional control only, and full control still requires independent Tier A or full Tier B evidence; every grant attaches to the existing party so history never migrates to a new party.
- **AC-PRF-07 — Convert provisional claim:** Given A provisional claim exists with no open contest, and either its entity-specific reversibility window has elapsed — 14 days for an organization, 30 days for a person claim — or stronger independent Tier A or full Tier B proof has been supplied, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Wait the entity-specific reversibility window or accept stronger independent proof; notify prior attesters on grant, and (6) return No open contest; ownership becomes full and provisional restrictions lift; if the flow cannot complete, An open contest blocks conversion and freezes control until that contest resolves; without an elapsed window or stronger proof the claim stays provisional and its denied capabilities stay denied — no transfer, payout change, signing, rights assignment, new durable obligation, authority grant or private export; a reversal restores the prior ownership period through a compensating command and never deletes case evidence or attributed actions.
- **AC-PRF-08 — Contest ownership:** Given The contestant is eligible to challenge this target, no contest is already open for that target and person, and the contestant is within three attempts per target per rolling 90 days; there is no fee, and both sides retain their prior operational access while the contest is open, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Notify both sides, preserve current operation, collect comparable proof for 14 days, and block transfer, and (6) return Strong uncontested challenger evidence may win reversibly; credible conflict freezes control and routes a `CaseKind = ownership` case to Shard 06 (TSE-01 intake, TSE-13 resolution); if the flow cannot complete, A fourth attempt inside 90 days routes to Shard 06 review rather than opening a contest; an incumbent who ignores the 14-day response window loses only to full-grade independent challenger evidence while weaker evidence freezes instead; a credible two-sided conflict freezes control and routes a `CaseKind = ownership` case to Shard 06 through `CreateReport` (TSE-01) for resolution under TSE-13 — there is no default winner and the platform never guesses.
- **AC-PRF-09 — Consensual transfer:** Given The actor is the current full owner, no contest is live on the target, the named recipient accepts, and that recipient completes a step-up before the acceptance commits, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Current full owner names recipient; recipient accepts after step-up; live contest blocks transfer, and (6) return Org reversibility 14 days, person claim 30 days; 30-day public changed-hands marker; if the flow cannot complete, A live contest blocks transfer outright, a provisional owner cannot transfer at all, and a stale acceptance against a changed ownership version returns a typed conflict; a reversal inside the applicable window restores the prior ownership period through a compensating command and never deletes case evidence or attributed actions.
- **AC-PRF-10 — Compose public profile:** Given The target party has a public projection to compose — an unclaimed shadow has none — and every candidate fact passes the viewer-safe projection checks for source version, provenance, visibility, embargo, listing, dispute and party lifecycle, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Build Header → Now → Record → Detail from viewer-authorized facts; omit truly empty sections and render loading/denial/error distinctly, and (6) return Publication projection carries immutable version/cache key; if the flow cannot complete, A denied, errored or timed-out source renders its explicit state and never an empty or zero result, so absence is never inferred and existence is never leaked; private aliases are excluded before totals, collaborators, ranges, filters and counts are computed, so a hidden alias produces no observable variation; legal identity and trader address are structurally absent from the projection.
- **AC-PRF-11 — Edit asserted profile:** Given The actor is the subject or holds a current mandate covering profile edits, the named section is an asserted section, and the command supplies that section's expected version; the author human and acting party are recorded on the revision, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Authorized subject/mandate edits one section with expected version; author human and acting party are recorded, and (6) return Attested facts, layer order, and reserved provenance visuals cannot change; if the flow cannot complete, An attempt to change an attested fact, the fixed layer order or reserved provenance visuals is refused rather than partially applied; a stale expected version returns a typed conflict, and activating a revision archives the prior active revision atomically so no lost update occurs; user content can never emit HTML, badge glyphs, CSS, scripts, active URLs or verification styling.
- **AC-PRF-12 — Curate portfolio/reel:** Given The actor controls this party's own projection, each selected clip references a credit plus governed media carrying a valid ownership, licence or provider-publication rights basis, and the role travels with the clip; emphasis and unlisting act only on this party's page, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Subject changes emphasis/listed state and selects only rights-eligible governed media; role travels with each clip, and (6) return Credit graph remains unchanged; every clip exposes rights basis and provenance; if the flow cannot complete, A clip without a rights basis is rejected for publication while its credit stays visible, and an approved governed link or embed or the rights workflow is offered instead; a takedown revokes the item; unlisting removes a credit from this party's page only and leaves the source ledger and co-contributor pages unchanged; no completeness score, career timeline density or aggregate provenance score is produced.
- **AC-PRF-13 — Generate/share EPK:** Given The actor controls the party, each included private alias is deliberately confirmed for this specific send after a forwardability warning, each included member credit carries active member consent naming the band and the use, and contact or rate fields are drawn only from approved values; the share token is at least 128 bits and defaults to 90 days, configurable to at most 365, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Select recipient/purpose, public facts, optional consented member/private-alias facts, and approved contact/rate fields, and (6) return Revocable 90-day recipient link and optional timestamped accessible PDF are created; if the flow cannot complete, Consent is fail-closed, so a missing or revoked alias confirmation or member consent omits that fact from the live EPK and raises a material-change notice to the sender rather than an implicit aggregation; the token grants view of the selected projection only and is not authentication, so a forwarded link works for a stranger and reveals only selected data; an expired or revoked share stops rendering while any previously exported PDF remains a timestamped snapshot.
- **AC-PRF-14 — Record/verify credential:** Given The subject party is named, the credential type exists as an approved jurisdiction-profile registry entry — US is the only authored launch profile — and issuer, identifier, issue and expiry dates and evidence satisfy that entry's schema; a `verified` outcome additionally requires current evidence from a configured adapter or an MFA-authenticated reviewer holding the named capability, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Validate jurisdiction-profile type, issuer, subject, dates, and evidence; verify by adapter or protected review, and (6) return State/method/expiry are visible; failure never renders verified; if the flow cannot complete, An unknown or unapproved jurisdiction type renders unknown and fails any hard gate rather than borrowing another regime's names or behavior; adapter outage keeps prior evidence until policy expiry and never renders verified on failure; expiry is deterministic from the recorded expiry date, fires an event and leaves the credential visible as expired while the consuming domain applies its own predeclared block, warn or notify policy.
- **AC-PRF-15 — Declare trader status:** Given The party is at its first sale in the relevant jurisdiction, a current counsel-approved rule pack exists for that jurisdiction, and the exact fields that will become public are disclosed before submission; the seller answers situational questions and never directly chooses the legal label, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) At first sale, ask situational jurisdiction questions and disclose exactly what will become public before submission, and (6) return Approved rule pack resolves `private|trader|undetermined|review_required`; listing gate rechecks; if the flow cannot complete, A missing or expired rule pack yields undetermined and blocks listing publication with no generic or invented legal copy; undetermined and review-required fail the commerce gate closed, and the listing gate rechecks classification and disclosure currency for both seller and buyer jurisdiction at publish time.
- **AC-PRF-16 — Detect classification mismatch:** Given A durable business signal — active-listing count, repeat-category velocity, buy-and-relist pattern or bounded revenue concentration — has fired against a party that already holds a classification, and the signal carries bounded metrics only, never raw buyer or content data, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Durable business signals trigger `review_required`; pause public listings and require re-declaration/review without silently calling the party a trader, and (6) return Audited disposition restores a valid classification or keeps listings blocked; if the flow cannot complete, The signal triggers review but never self-adjudicates legal status, so the party moves to review-required and is never silently called a trader; public listings pause while drafts and orders are preserved, and listings stay blocked until an audited disposition restores a valid classification through re-declaration or review.

### Global Interaction Rules

- Shard 00 governs boundary validation, idempotency, version conflicts, object/media handling, jobs, errors, audit, and events; Shard 01 governs party, acting context, mandate, legal identity, and external identifiers.
- A shadow is always a subject and never an agent. It has no public/search-indexed portfolio before claim or explicit consent plus approved release controls.
- Provisional control may perform only actions the platform can fully reverse within the claim window; it cannot sign, transfer, redirect payout, dispose of identity, publish private evidence, or create durable rights facts.
- Public profiles never read legal identity. Trader-required legal disclosures live on governed commerce listings unless an approved jurisdiction rule explicitly requires another surface.

## Contracts

### Claim Proof and Contest

| Contract | Locked rule |
|---|---|
| Tier A | Independent control: matching own-domain challenge, provider-verified business OAuth, one-party DSP OAuth, postal challenge (30 days), or callback to public business number. Grants full control. |
| Tier B | Fresh challenge to attester-designated route. One independent designation grants provisional; ≥2 independent same-route designations grant full. |
| Tier C | Counter-attestation by eligible people who credited the party. One or many confirmations can grant provisional only; independent proof is required for durable full control. |
| Independence | Attesters are different humans, not same org co-members, not in a mandate relation, not from the same project/session, and never the claimant through any acting party. |
| Challenge | Email/voice code is six digits, single-use, maximum five attempts, expires in 15 minutes; storage is hashed; successful/expired use burns it. |
| Contest | One open contest/target/person; three attempts/target/rolling 90 days then Shard 06 review; no fee; 14-day response; no default winner. |
| Frozen operation | Existing committed obligations may be serviced through operate-only custody; no new obligations, transfer, ownership edits, or payout redirection. Custom escrow remains disabled. |

### Profile, Portfolio, and EPK

| Contract | Locked rule |
|---|---|
| Provenance states | `asserted\|attested\|confirmed_assertion\|creator_asserted\|disputed`; state belongs to each fact, not its section. Public never reveals attester identity; evidence class/count may render. |
| Composition | Fixed Header → Now → Record → Detail. Record is one reverse-chronological/filterable body of work; Detail is sectioned by heterogeneous facet/type. No viewer-adaptive order or user theming. |
| Portfolio | Query over Shard 02/credit graph, never stored document. Human aggregation includes only publicly linked aliases; all derived totals/collaborators are recomputed from that visible set. |
| Curation | Emphasis orders; `Unlisted` removes a credit from this party page only; neither edits the source ledger. No completeness score, career timeline density, or aggregate provenance score. |
| Reel | Credit proves participation, not media rights. Item requires governed media with owner/license/public-embed basis; role and credit reference travel with clip; takedown revokes item. |
| EPK | Live generated view per pitch. Share wrapper contains public facts by default; private aliases/member credits require explicit per-send consent/inclusion. Sender sees material-change notices. |
| EPK export | Optional PDF is a timestamped accessible snapshot with canonical live-link, source/version list, and “current as of” notice; never replaces live model. |

## Accessibility

- Profile provenance states use text, semantics, and icons; color alone never distinguishes asserted, attested, disputed, provisional, expired, or trader states.
- Claim/contest steppers expose current step, alternatives, countdowns, proof requirements, and resumable status; code inputs support paste, one-time-code autocomplete, and non-destructive errors.
- Empty, loading, denied, and failed sections remain distinct; a failed portfolio section never disappears as if the career were empty.
- Fixed profile layers use landmarks/headings; record filters are keyboard operable, preserve focus, announce result counts, and expose an unfiltered route.
- Reel media supplies captions/transcripts where applicable, accessible controls, no autoplay, reduced motion, and rights/takedown status.
- EPK links and PDFs meet WCAG 2.2 AA, reflow/reading-order requirements, descriptive link text, tagged headings, image alternatives, and visible generated/current timestamps.
- Trader/legal disclosure names fields and consequences before consent, requires an explicit confirmation, and restores focus/context after step-up.

## Edge Cases

| Case | Required result |
|---|---|
| Capture names abusive/mixed-script party | Create inert flagged record; no index/public page; structured fields prevent free-text spam. |
| Invitation route typo | “Not you” stops all outreach without login; link never claims; fact remains protected/reviewable. |
| Dispatch budget exceeded | Queue transparently; never reject capture or accuse creator. |
| Two shadows likely same party | Suggest merge; preserve both until approved claim/merge; no automatic authority. |
| Claim proof provider unavailable | Claim stays open; offer another method; no downgrade or hidden denial. |
| Tier C ring confirms each other | Eligible only for provisional control; no full ownership/public trust/routing without independent proof. |
| Claim target merges mid-flow | Redirect claim to survivor and re-run proof/visibility/conflict checks. |
| Incumbent ignores contest | After 14 days, full-grade independent challenger evidence may win reversibly; weaker evidence freezes. |
| Both claimants have credible proof | Freeze ownership, preserve operate-only committed work, route Shard 06; platform does not guess. |
| Frozen party has pending payment | Use provider/local reconciliation and manual case; no custom escrow representation or payout redirection. |
| Profile source fact is denied/errored | Render denial/error explicitly; never infer absence or leak existence. |
| Private alias would change a total/collaborator edge | Recompute only visible-set projection; no observable variation from hidden alias. |
| Subject unlists credit | Remove from their page only and explain source ledger/co-contributor pages remain. |
| Reel clip lacks rights basis | Reject publication; credit remains visible; offer governed link/embed or rights workflow. |
| EPK is forwarded | Works for stranger; reveals only selected data; recipient label is not an access guarantee. |
| EPK source changes after send | Recipient sees current fact; sender receives material-change notice; old PDF remains timestamped snapshot. |
| Credential expires during active booking | State becomes expired and event fires; consuming domain applies its predeclared policy. |
| Jurisdiction credential type unknown | Render unknown/unverified and fail any hard gate; never substitute another regime. |
| Trader mismatch signal fires | Pause listings, set review-required, preserve drafts/orders, and require review/re-declaration. |
| Counsel-approved trader disclosure absent | Commerce listing activation remains disabled; no generic or invented legal copy. |
