# Shard 21 — Specialized clearances and licensing

**Status:** Complete
**Surface:** Web/PWA with provider-gated whitelist, fingerprint and dataset adapters
**Source:** [Architecture design](../2026-08-02-architecture-design.md) · [Decomposition plan](decomposition-plan.md)

## Overview

Shard 21 owns sample/interpolation/remix clearance, creator micro-licences and Content ID claim recovery, AI-training consent/corpus manifests/compensation records, cover compulsory-mechanical guidance, print/lyric licensing and the explicit exclusion of grand-rights automation. It composes Shard 20 scope/instrument gates and never converts machine suggestions, possession, silence, subscription state or statutory status into authority.

### Scope Reconciliation

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| In-scope source documents | 21 |
| Child capabilities | 15 |
| Sample chain | Declaration, instant/negotiated clearance, interpolation, remix/stem/bootleg |
| Creator chain | Flat catalogue, verified whitelist, claim release and perpetual issued grant |
| AI chain | Opt-in registry, immutable corpus manifest and manifest-based compensation record |
| Statutory/special rights | Cover mechanical, print/lyrics and grand-rights exclusion |
| Provider boundary | Fingerprint, Content ID/whitelist, corpus delivery and payout adapters remain disabled until reviewed Phase-2 integration/evolution |

### Specialized Decisions

| Area | Locked decision |
|---|---|
| Samples | Human declares; machine only suggests/measures. Missing declaration does not block release, but unresolved truth blocks scopes requiring clearance. |
| Interpolation | Composition side only; plain-language replay declaration; no machine backstop. |
| Remixes/stems | Stem possession is not grant authority; independent exploitation is explicit; bootleg path means retroactive legitimisation only. |
| Micro-licence | Flat listed price, no negotiation; purchase completes only after issued licence and confirmed whitelist capability. |
| Persistence | Issued creator licence/whitelist survives subscription cancellation; subscription affects future purchases only. |
| AI | Share-level opt-in; silence/refailure/refusal excludes. Shipped corpus/manifest immutable; withdrawal cannot undo shipped models. |
| Compensation | Allocate by disclosed manifest rule, never inferred influence; small amounts remain exact and accrue without forfeiture, but payout remains B3-gated. |
| Covers | Statutory eligibility/identification flow; owner policy/veto does not create a right to refuse a valid compulsory route. |
| Grand rights | WONT in current product; never auto-approved or encoded as ordinary media scope. |

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-SPL-01 — Contributor declares sampled material:** Given Authenticated contributor acts on their own Shard 09 contribution, the enumerative prompt and its prompt version are supplied, recording and composition sides and the known/unknown source state are stated per contribution, per-field human/machine provenance is recorded with the human as the only author of the declaration, and any current declaration version is supplied so a change supersedes rather than overwrites, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Enumerative versioned prompt records contribution, recording/composition sides, known/unknown source and human/machine provenance, and (6) return Current declaration plus immutable superseded history; if the flow cannot complete, `DECLARATION_REQUIRED` when the enumerative prompt answer or its prompt version is absent, a typed validation refusal for a malformed side or provenance field, `FORBIDDEN` when declaring on another owner's contribution or source, and a typed revision conflict to the loser of a competing expected revision with no duplicate effect; a truthful declaration is never refused to simplify downstream arithmetic, an unknown source persists as `unidentified` indefinitely rather than erroring, and a refused write leaves the current declaration and its immutable superseded history untouched.
- **AC-SPL-02 — System suggests sample identity:** Given A fingerprint or local identity adapter job runs under a single scoped service principal, or a contributor reviews suggestions on their own declaration; an existing contribution declaration is the target; and provider absence is representable so a missing adapter is shown honestly rather than assumed available, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Provider/local adapter returns candidates/prominence measurements; human confirms/rejects without automatic merge, and (6) return Suggestion or honest no-machine state; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for a service principal reaching beyond its single fingerprint job scope or a contributor reading another owner's declaration, a typed refusal for a malformed candidate payload, and the honest no-machine state rather than a candidate whenever the provider is unavailable or returns an incorrect match; a rejected candidate is retained as evidence, the human declaration continues unchanged, and no failure path auto-declares, auto-notifies, auto-merges or auto-clears.
- **AC-SPL-03 — Owner requests instant sample clearance:** Given A work or master owner or admin acts under standing on the declaring work, a current sample declaration exists with an identified source on every required side, the source owner's instant policy and the Shard 20 scope/gate evaluation are readable, and the requested scope and terms fall inside that listed policy, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Evaluate policy then complete source scope/consent/terms; any failure falls to negotiation, and (6) return Issued clearance or negotiation route, never false clear; if the flow cannot complete, `DECLARATION_REQUIRED` when no current declaration exists, `SOURCE_UNIDENTIFIED` when a required recording or composition side has no identified source, `CLEARANCE_REQUIRED` when the Shard 20 scope gate is unmet, and `FORBIDDEN` for an actor without standing on the work; any instant-policy failure falls through to the simultaneous negotiated route rather than issuing a false clear, and no clearance state advances on refusal.
- **AC-SPL-04 — Owners negotiate sample terms:** Given The instant route failed or was ineligible, every source owner on every required side is resolvable and routed simultaneously, the proposed terms state fixed fee, revenue share or an explicit ordered combination rather than a single price field, and the current clearance-request version is supplied, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Route all source owners simultaneously; unanimous consent; validate fee/revenue-share obligation stacking, and (6) return Complete instrument or no clearance; if the flow cannot complete, `UNANIMOUS_CONSENT_REQUIRED` when any routed source owner refuses or has not yet consented — a decline yields no clearance and the instant path can never override it — `OBLIGATION_STACK_INVALID` for an unordered or otherwise invalid fee and revenue-share stack, `FORBIDDEN` for any attempt to override co-owner or source consent, and a typed revision conflict to the loser of a concurrent edit; a refused negotiation leaves the request open with no partial instrument issued.
- **AC-SPL-05 — Creator declares replay/interpolation:** Given The declaring contributor acts on their own contribution, has answered the plain-language replay question affirmatively, no original source audio is present in the contribution, and the referenced work's composition owners are resolvable; no machine backstop substitutes for the human answer, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Ask whether they replayed recognizable musical material; resolve composition owners only, and (6) return Composition clearance route with explicit master-not-cleared warning; if the flow cannot complete, `SOURCE_UNIDENTIFIED` when composition owners cannot be resolved leaves the declaration truthful and the clearance route unavailable rather than fabricated, `FORBIDDEN` when declaring for another contributor's contribution, and reclassification as a sample requiring both recording and composition sides whenever the declaration includes original source audio, instead of proceeding on the composition-only route; the master-not-cleared warning is never suppressed on any recovery path.
- **AC-SPL-06 — Rights holder grants remix/stem use:** Given The granting party holds Shard 10 registry authority over the source master independent of any asset possession, the exact source assets are named and readable, and the remix, stem or bootleg route is stated together with an explicit exploitation and derivative scope, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Verify Shard 10 authority, exact source assets and exploitation/derivative scope, and (6) return Authorized instrument; no declaration/encumbrance for permitted use; if the flow cannot complete, `STEM_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED` when the granting party merely possesses the stems, since possession never creates standing and no grant or listing is created, a typed validation refusal for unnamed source assets or an absent exploitation or derivative scope, and `FORBIDDEN` for an actor without registry authority; a refused grant leaves no declaration or encumbrance on the source, and a bootleg route is only ever retroactively legitimised by the authorized rights holder, never approved by the uploader.
- **AC-SPL-07 — Owner opts work into creator catalogue:** Given A work or master owner or admin holds standing on the share being listed, the requested terms fit entirely inside the fixed non-negotiable template — flat listed price, currency, channel and use scale — and the work resolves to a single eligible payee path under the B3 gate, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Validate fixed template containment, price, channel/use scale and single-payee path, and (6) return Active listing or human-clearance fallback; if the flow cannot complete, `TEMPLATE_SCOPE_MISMATCH` when the requested scope falls outside the fixed template, `MULTIPAYEE_DISABLED_B3` when the work resolves to more than one payee, `FORBIDDEN` for an actor without owner standing on the share, and a typed revision conflict on a competing listing edit; a refused listing never activates, the request routes to the human clearance fallback, and any existing listing is left unchanged.
- **AC-SPL-08 — Creator buys micro-licence:** Given An authenticated creator licensee targets an active listing, channel control is proved by an approved provider OAuth grant distinct from login, the requested scope matches the listed flat template and price, the subscription-derived purchasing benefit is currently active, and the listing's Shard 20 single-payee clearance path is eligible, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Verify buyer/licensee/channel OAuth, scope/price/clearance; issue instrument and request whitelist, and (6) return Complete only after confirmed whitelist, else pending/failed recovery; if the flow cannot complete, `CHANNEL_PROOF_REQUIRED` when the channel OAuth grant is missing or expired, `TEMPLATE_SCOPE_MISMATCH` for a scope outside the listing, `CLEARANCE_REQUIRED` when the listing's clearance is unmet, `MULTIPAYEE_DISABLED_B3` for an ineligible payee path, and `WHITELIST_UNCONFIRMED` while the provider write is `pending` or `failed`, so the purchase never reaches complete on an unconfirmed whitelist; a confirmation arriving after local timeout reconciles to the same terminal state without issuing a duplicate instrument, and a payment that succeeds against a failed whitelist leaves the purchase unfulfilled and routes to void, refund or recovery according to the committed transaction rather than a silent licence-only state.
- **AC-SPL-09 — Creator reports Content ID claim:** Given The creator licensee holds an issued instrument whose scope covers the claimed content, the claim, content and channel references resolve and bind to that instrument and to the licensee's own proved channel, and the instrument's whitelist state is `confirmed` or `reconciling`, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) One “I have a licence” action loads instrument/channel/content/claim and submits release, and (6) return Released, correct-claim explanation or escalated relanding case; if the flow cannot complete, `CLAIM_REFERENCE_INVALID` when the claim, content or channel does not bind to the held instrument, `WHITELIST_UNCONFIRMED` when the underlying provider write is not confirmed, and `FORBIDDEN` when reaching another licensee's channel or claim; a claim that is correct against uncovered use is explained plainly and no dishonest release is submitted, and a claim that relands after release escalates to the claim and dispute reviewer carrying the prior provider receipts instead of retrying indefinitely.
- **AC-SPL-10 — Subscriber cancels:** Given An authenticated subscriber cancels their own subscription, and the subscription grant history is scoped to future-purchase capability only, never to ownership of an issued instrument or whitelist entry, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Disable new purchasing benefit; preserve issued instruments/whitelists; failed cascade favors persistence, and (6) return Future capability off, grants unchanged; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when cancelling another party's subscription and a typed refusal for a malformed cancellation; a failed downstream provider cascade resolves in favour of persistence, so issued instruments and confirmed whitelists remain valid while reconciliation retries idempotently, and no failure path revokes a licence, a whitelist entry or a claim-release case.
- **AC-SPL-11 — Owner sets AI-training decision:** Given The acting party is the verified current owner of the exact share being decided, the decision names the exact work, share, data use and corpus purpose and passes the Shard 20 policy and consent gate, the current consent version is supplied, and the state before any explicit decision is `refused` because silence is refusal, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Share/work/data-use/corpus-purpose opt-in through Shard 20 policy/consent; silence is refusal, and (6) return Versioned consent/refusal with effective time; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when the acting party is not the current share owner, a typed validation refusal when the data use or corpus purpose is not exact, and a typed revision conflict to the loser of a concurrent decision; a share transfer supersedes prior consent and resets the acquirer to `refused`, and a failed or refused decision leaves the state `refused`, never inferring consent from broad media rights, silence, a provider failure or a sold share.
- **AC-SPL-12 — Dataset buyer assembles corpus:** Given An authorized dataset buyer acts on a corpus in `draft` or `ready` that has not shipped, the proposed manifest is frozen with its Shard 20 instruments pinned, every item carries current full clearance and a current `opted_in` consent, and affected owners have been notified before ship, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Freeze proposed manifest; require full clearance/current opt-in on every item and notify owners before ship, and (6) return Immutable shipped corpus or blocked assembly; if the flow cannot complete, `AI_OPT_IN_REQUIRED` when any item lacks a current opt-in and `CORPUS_CLEARANCE_INCOMPLETE` when any item's clearance is incomplete, so the corpus holds at `clearance_blocked` and does not ship, plus `FORBIDDEN` when the buyer reaches raw refusals, undeclared works or influence attribution; an owner who is silent or whose withdrawal races the freeze has that item excluded rather than included, and a failed ship leaves the manifest editable in draft with no partial delivery evidence, since only a shipped manifest is immutable.
- **AC-SPL-13 — Owner withdraws AI consent:** Given The acting party is the verified current owner of a share whose consent state is `opted_in`, the withdrawal names the exact share and its current consent version, and the irreversible shipped-use disclosure has been presented before confirmation, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Exclude from unshipped corpora; explain shipped corpus/model irreversibility and preserve visible history, and (6) return Future exclusion plus immutable shipped-use register; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when withdrawing for a share the actor does not currently own, a typed validation refusal when the exact share or its current version is absent, and a typed revision conflict when the consent moved first; withdrawal can never undo a shipped corpus or a trained model, so the shipped manifest and model-use register stay visible, immutable and append-only, and a failed withdrawal leaves the prior `opted_in` state intact rather than silently excluding future corpora.
- **AC-SPL-14 — System records training compensation:** Given A shipped immutable corpus manifest, its disclosed allocation rule and recorded deal proceeds all exist, every manifest item resolves to an entitled party, and the allocation runs as a scoped corpus service-principal job that reads the disclosed rule only and never estimates model influence, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Apply disclosed manifest allocation to deal proceeds; preserve exact small entitlement and B3-disabled payout state, and (6) return Traceable accrual record, never influence claim; if the flow cannot complete, `CORPUS_CLEARANCE_INCOMPLETE` when the referenced manifest is not a shipped immutable manifest, a typed validation refusal when the disclosed allocation rule or the deal proceeds are absent, and `MULTIPAYEE_DISABLED_B3` for any payout attempt while multi-party payout is disabled — the accrual record still commits with exact entitlements; a small entitlement is never rounded to zero, forfeited below a threshold or converted into a platform-held wallet or escrow balance, and a refused allocation leaves prior allocations unchanged and asserts no model influence.
- **AC-SPL-15 — User clears cover/print/grand-right use:** Given The actor supplies the intended use with its work, release and territory and, for a cover, the recording's faithfulness to the underlying composition, and classification runs before any Shard 20 clearance route is requested so no route is inferred from ordinary media scope, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Classify use: valid cover statutory route, negotiated print/lyric route, or unsupported grand rights, and (6) return Correct route, blocked gap or explicit out-of-scope; if the flow cannot complete, `STATUTORY_ROUTE_INELIGIBLE` when a cover materially changes melody or lyrics or the territory and eligibility facts do not support the compulsory route, in which case the case routes to negotiated derivative clearance rather than issuing a statutory route, `GRAND_RIGHTS_UNSUPPORTED` as an explicit out-of-scope WONT for any dramatic or grand-rights use with no auto-approval and no ordinary-scope substitution, and a typed validation refusal when territory or use facts are missing, reported as a blocked gap rather than guessed; an owner policy or veto never overrides a valid compulsory route, and the platform never generates a score or transcription in place of a supplied authorized print or lyric artifact.

### Global Interaction Rules

- Declaration, detected suggestion, upstream licence, downstream clearance, issued instrument and public disclosure are separate records.
- A true declaration is never rejected to simplify downstream arithmetic; “unidentified source” remains first-class indefinitely.
- External provider success is confirmed/reconciled; fire-and-forget whitelist, fingerprint, claim release or corpus delivery is forbidden.
- AI consent cannot be inferred from broad media rights and does not transfer with a sold share.
- Grand rights and fan transcription/score generation are not hidden generic-scope cases.
