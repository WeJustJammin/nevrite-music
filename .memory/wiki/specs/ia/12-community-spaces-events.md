## Overview

Shard 12 owns evidence-grounded scenes, craft discussion, contests, informal local-event discovery, professional listening rooms and bounded conference networking. It does not create authority from membership, enumerate local people, fabricate cold-start activity, infer correctness from reputation, transfer submission rights silently, own realtime room transport or permanently relax reachability.

## Features

- **03.06 Scenes & Communities** — offered overlapping memberships, evidenced density, density-gated stewardship and fact-only premises/event seeding.
- **03.07 Forums & Craft Q&A** — craft discussion without participation reputation; credentials are context and Fans are read-only.
- **03.08 Contests, Challenges & Beat Battles** — frozen briefs/eligibility, rights-safe deliberate submissions, craft-scoped judging and pre-funded/specific prizes.
- **03.09 Local Jam & Open Mic Discovery** — freshness-labelled discovery of informal recurring opportunities without owning event recurrence.
- **03.10 Peer & Scene Listening Rooms** — professional-only, scene/peer-scoped use of shared room transport/presence.
- **03.11 Conference & Industry Event Networking Mode** — attendee-only temporary reachability relaxation with durable consented relationship follow-up.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-SPC-01 — Discover/join scene:** Given Authenticated party confirms an `offered` membership derived from its own evidenced work location or labelled profile fallback; no Shard 06 restriction applies and joining is never automatic, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) System offers resident/visiting membership from evidenced work location or labelled profile fallback; user confirms; never automatic, and (6) return Membership version and evidence/expiry commit; if the flow cannot complete, Missing evidence, absent confirmation or an active restriction returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` or `FORBIDDEN` with no membership written; unavailable scene metrics render unknown rather than zero, and the actor may retry or join manually.
- **AC-SPC-02 — Leave/dismiss scene:** Given Actor holds the target membership — `active` or `dormant` to leave, `offered` to dismiss — and supplies its `expected_version`, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Leaving writes permanent anti-reinference signal; dismissing offer only suppresses current offer, and (6) return Membership/offer state updates without notification; if the flow cannot complete, Acting on another party's membership returns `FORBIDDEN` and a superseded revision returns `VERSION_CONFLICT`; membership and offer state are unchanged, no member is notified, and manual rejoin stays available because the negative signal blocks only automatic re-inference.
- **AC-SPC-03 — View scene:** Given Any authenticated viewer, including Fan, reads a scene; no membership, stewardship or authority is required and no enumerable roster may be requested, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Viewer sees evidence-based density, aggregate aliveness and bounded non-exhaustive samples; roster cannot enumerate, and (6) return Viewer-safe projection and freshness labels returned; if the flow cannot complete, Roster enumeration returns `FORBIDDEN` and is never satisfied by paging; unavailable density or graph metrics return unknown/unavailable with freshness labels, never zero or no community, and the viewer may retry.
- **AC-SPC-04 — Steward scene:** Given Actor holds an active `scene_stewardship` inside its capability scope and start/expiry window, and the scene has passed the approved density and evidence threshold, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Eligible evidence-backed local role performs bounded curation/reporting after density gate; cannot remove members or intercept safety escalation, and (6) return Steward action/audit or safe denial; if the flow cannot complete, Below-threshold density, expired or out-of-scope stewardship, or an attempt to remove members, adjudicate reports or alter graph evidence returns a safe denial — `FORBIDDEN`, or `MEMBERSHIP_NOT_AUTHORITY` where membership was the claimed basis — with canonical state unchanged; safety matters route to the Shard 06 escalation path the steward cannot intercept.
- **AC-SPC-05 — Seed/claim premises/event:** Given Seeding requires a non-residential premises/event carrying two independent agreeing sources or one first-party fact with recorded provenance; claiming requires a verified Operator acting on a `derived_unclaimed` record, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) System publishes corroborated non-residential premises/events as derived/unclaimed; verified claimant takes forward-looking control only, and (6) return Seed/claim lifecycle and provenance commit; if the flow cannot complete, A residential subject returns `RESIDENTIAL_SEED_PROHIBITED` and is suppressed regardless of corroboration; insufficient corroboration leaves the record unpublished and unindexed; a conflicting source refresh notifies the claimant and never overwrites a claimed record; an unverified claimant returns `FORBIDDEN` and may complete verification then retry.
- **AC-SPC-06 — Ask/answer craft question:** Given Authenticated professional party posts in a readable, unrestricted thread; Fans are read-only and no reputation, credential level or scene membership is consulted for permission, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Professional actor posts bounded question/answer; credentials shown as context only; Fan reads but cannot post, and (6) return Thread/revision/moderation state commits; if the flow cannot complete, A Fan or restricted actor returns `FORBIDDEN` without leaking the moderation reason; invalid or oversized content returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` before any thread mutation; a concurrent edit returns `VERSION_CONFLICT` with post history preserved, and the author may revise and resubmit.
- **AC-SPC-07 — Publish contest brief:** Given Contest organizer acts on a `draft` contest that has received no submission, supplying exact eligibility, judging, rights, unused-submission handling, deadline and a funded or specifically deliverable prize, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Organizer defines eligibility, judging, rights, submission use, deadlines and funded/specific prize; rights check required, and (6) return Immutable published brief version created; if the flow cannot complete, A failed rights check returns `RIGHTS_CHECK_FAILED` and blocks publication; an unfunded cash or "exposure" prize returns `PRIZE_UNFUNDED`; an edit after the first submission returns `BRIEF_FROZEN` and must use the visible versioned cancellation/republication policy; no brief version is created and the organizer may correct and republish.
- **AC-SPC-08 — Submit contest entry:** Given Professional entrant performs a deliberate submit against a `published`/`accepting` contest before its deadline, pinning the frozen brief version and an asset version it is entitled to submit; upload or posting alone never enters, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Entrant deliberately submits under frozen brief; intake evaluates/fixes eligibility where possible and pins asset/version, and (6) return Submission/eligibility/evidence state commits; if the flow cannot complete, Eligibility intake cannot fix returns `ELIGIBILITY_FAILED` naming the failing criterion and remediation before any submission is recorded; a contest paused for failed prize funding accepts no new entry; failures leave no partial submission, a repeated idempotency key returns the original submission, and the entrant may correct and resubmit while the deadline holds.
- **AC-SPC-09 — Judge/award contest:** Given Actor holds a `judge_appointment` whose craft scope covers the assigned submission with conflicts disclosed, the contest is in `judging` under a frozen brief, and awarding additionally requires re-checked winner eligibility and a funded or specific prize, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Craft-scoped judges disclose conflicts, score/reason under brief; no peer voting; winner eligibility rechecked, and (6) return Reasoned verdict and prize instruction commit; if the flow cannot complete, Unassigned, out-of-scope or undisclosed-conflict judging returns `FORBIDDEN` and an unreasoned verdict returns `VALIDATION_FAILED`, with no verdict written; a failed winner recheck returns `ELIGIBILITY_FAILED` and an unfunded prize returns `PRIZE_UNFUNDED` with no award instruction; a conflict found after the verdict preserves verdict history, invalidates or re-runs judging under brief policy and notifies entrants; peer or public votes are never accepted as input.
- **AC-SPC-10 — Confirm local jam/open mic:** Given Actor is the listing Operator, producing `confirmed`, or a first-hand attendee, producing `attendee_reported`, acting on an existing listing, and the assertion names source, evidence class and observed-at, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Operator/attendee confirms or flags informal listing attributes/freshness; stale stays visibly unconfirmed, and (6) return Freshness assertion and listing projection update; if the flow cannot complete, A missing source, evidence class or observed-at returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` and freshness is unchanged; a non-Operator claiming Operator confirmation returns `FORBIDDEN` but may still file an attendee report; an aged-out listing renders `stale`/`unconfirmed` rather than being silently removed or shown as current, so any eligible actor may refresh it later.
- **AC-SPC-11 — Join peer listening room:** Given Professional party whose scene/peer eligibility satisfies the target `listening_room_scope` inside its active window, with shared Shard 00 room transport available and no Shard 06 restriction in force, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Eligible professional enters scene/peer-scoped room through shared transport; room policy and moderation enforced server-side, and (6) return Participation grant/state without Fan access; if the flow cannot complete, Fans and out-of-scope actors return `ROOM_SCOPE_DENIED` without leaking room membership; a restriction arriving mid-session revokes the interaction route immediately without reason leak; degraded transport preserves room scope and moderation and exposes a degraded mode plus a non-audio participation route, never a broadened audience, and the actor may rejoin when transport recovers.
- **AC-SPC-12 — Enable conference mode:** Given Actor holds a verified attendee record for a specific event and activates inside that event's declared window with an explicit contact scope and an announced absolute end time; existing blocks and restrictions remain in force, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Verified attendee opts into bounded event window and contact scope; reachability reverts automatically at end, and (6) return Attendee grant/expiry and persistence choices commit; if the flow cannot complete, Unverified attendance, an unknown event or activation outside the window returns `FORBIDDEN` or `VALIDATION_FAILED` and no reachability changes; a scope that would override an existing block is refused without leaking the block; at the announced end the grant expires automatically and reachability reverts, so lasting contact requires an explicitly persisted relationship through SPC-13.
- **AC-SPC-13 — Persist event relationship:** Given An explicit participant action — connect, follow or save private contact — is invoked through the owning Shard 11 flow; the conference grant supplies context only and proximity or co-presence alone is never sufficient, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Participants explicitly connect/follow/save private contact through owning Shard 11 flows; proximity alone creates nothing, and (6) return Typed downstream edge/contact command records; if the flow cannot complete, Without an explicit participant action nothing is created and no edge exists; a Shard 11 refusal for restriction, block or reachability is returned as its typed error unchanged with no local shadow copy written, and the actor may retry through the owning Shard 11 surface.

### Global Interaction Rules

- Commands carry `actor_person_id`, `acting_party_id`, `acting_context_version`, `idempotency_key`, `expected_version?` and `request_id`.
- Shard 06 restrictions/moderation override membership, posting, entry, judging, rooms and networking without leaking reasons.
- Scene membership grants no ranking boost, stewardship, authority, contact access or event eligibility.
- Location is declared/evidenced record data; never device-derived. Residential addresses and seeded person profiles are prohibited.
- Terms, rights, judging and prizes freeze at first contest submission; later correction requires visible versioned cancellation/restart policy.
- Every freshness/verification claim names source, evidence class, observed-at and expiry; stale/unknown never renders confirmed.

## Accessibility

- Scene density/thinness uses text and evidence explanations, not maps/color alone; membership offers state exact basis and expiration.
- Non-enumerable scene samples are presented as examples, never implied complete rosters.
- Forum and contest forms are keyboard complete with preserved drafts, semantic rubrics and clear frozen-term references.
- Eligibility/right/prize failures identify consequence and remediation before submission; focus moves to exact field.
- Listening rooms expose captions/text chat/participant controls through shared accessible transport and provide non-audio participation route.
- Conference expiry and reachability scope are announced before activation and remain visible with absolute end time.

## Edge Cases

| Case | Required result |
|---|---|
| User relocates | New scene membership adds; old does not auto-remove. |
| User explicitly leaves | Permanent negative signal prevents re-inference; manual rejoin remains possible. |
| Scene has little evidence | Render thin honestly; no fabricated members/events or inflated density. |
| Scene graph/metrics unavailable | Unknown/unavailable, never zero or no community. |
| Seed source refresh conflicts with claimant | Notify claimant; never overwrite claimed record. |
| Residential venue appears in source | Reject/suppress regardless corroboration. |
| Contest receives first submission | Freeze exact brief; amendments require visible cancellation/republication policy. |
| Prize funding fails | Contest cannot state/publish cash prize; existing contest pauses before new submissions. |
| Judge conflict found after verdict | Preserve verdict history, invalidate/re-run under brief policy and notify entrants. |
| Informal listing ages out | Mark stale/unconfirmed; do not silently remove or show current. |
| Listening room transport degrades | Preserve room scope/moderation; expose degraded mode, never broaden audience. |
| Conference ends | Reachability reverts automatically; only explicitly persisted Shard 11 edges/contacts remain. |
| Block/restriction arrives mid-event | Revoke interaction route immediately without reason leak. |
