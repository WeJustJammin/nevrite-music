## Overview

Shard 14 owns craft-service listings, exact quotes, requirements-gated engagements, milestones/revisions/change orders, delivery/acceptance, single-payee settlement instructions, multi-party supply composition, rights elections/execution and custodial service evidence. Listing is a template, quote is the offer, accepted quote is scope of record and downstream rights/credit/payment actions use only frozen agreement and artifact data.

## Features

- **05.01 Service Listings & Pricing** — curated craft taxonomy, tier/package/add-on templates, shape-specific pricing, private benchmarks, service mode/SLA and fact-derived capacity/liveness.
- **05.02 Quotes, Scope & Contracting** — immutable expiring quotes, structured terms/diffs, NDA/anonymity and union/session contract facts.
- **05.03 Engagement Lifecycle** — frozen requirements, milestones, revisions/change orders, retainers, cancellation/abandonment and recall.
- **05.04 Delivery, QC & Acceptance** — complete artifact delivery, technical QC, previews and explicit source/session handover.
- **05.05 Multi-Party Supply** — consented deps, fixers, subcontracting and bundle composition, subject to B3 payout gate.
- **05.06 Rights, Warranties & Transfer** — explicit rights postures, points, atomic execution, source/originality and human-performance declarations.
- **05.07 Custodial & Physical Services** — repair/inspection micro-lifecycles and mutual custody/condition evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-SRV-01 — Publish service listing:** Given Seller holds acting authority on a listing in `draft` with exactly one primary craft from the curated taxonomy, a legal pricing model with minimum, currency and tax, structured exclusions and add-ons, and — for every tier — both a master and a composition posture with all their required parameters and recorded posture-determining facts, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Seller chooses immutable craft, tiers, deliverables/exclusions, pricing shape, mode, SLA, capacity and explicit master/composition rights posture, and (6) return Atomic listing version publishes after taxonomy/gate validation; if the flow cannot complete, A missing or partial posture leaves the tier `unelected` and blocks publish with `LISTING_GATE_FAILED` naming the missing parameter; an uncurated craft, absent pricing minimum, currency or tax, or free-text posture returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` and publishes nothing; changing craft on a live listing is refused and requires archiving and creating a new listing; a superseded listing version returns `VERSION_CONFLICT`, and the seller may correct and republish because issued quotes are unaffected.
- **AC-SRV-02 — Browse/request quote:** Given Buyer reads a `live` listing and supplies the actual job inputs its pricing model requires; the request pins buyer, seller and context versions and the seller's own model renders without normalization against other sellers or private benchmarks, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Buyer selects actual job inputs; engine renders seller model or quote-required without normalizing models/benchmarks, and (6) return Quote request with buyer/seller/context versions commits; if the flow cannot complete, Missing or malformed job inputs return `VALIDATION_FAILED` and write no request; inputs the model cannot price render as quote-required rather than a fabricated or normalized number; private rates and benchmarks never appear in the refusal; a stale listing version returns `VERSION_CONFLICT` and the buyer may re-read the current listing and re-request.
- **AC-SRV-03 — Issue/reissue quote:** Given Seller holds acting authority on a quote in `requested` or `drafting` and supplies an ordered price evaluation plus exact scope, artifacts, requirements, revision allowance, recall allowance, rights election, kill schedule, anonymity and mandatory expiry; recall terms either copy the current versioned craft seed — mastering `2/30`, mixing or production `2/60`, other recall-capable crafts `1/30` — or identify the seller override author and satisfy count `0..5`, with days `0` only for count zero and otherwise `7..90`, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) freeze all ordered terms including recall policy version/origin/author/count/window; reissue creates a new version/diff and separately marks a changed recall allowance as material, and (6) return the issued binding offer version and evidence commit; if the flow cannot complete, invalid recall pairing returns `RECALL_TERMS_INVALID`; a missing expiry, unstructured term or `unelected` rights posture returns `VALIDATION_FAILED`; no version may issue during acceptance (`VERSION_CONFLICT`). Later listing, craft-policy, rate or capacity changes never alter or void an issued quote.
- **AC-SRV-04 — Accept quote:** Given An eligible buyer authority acknowledges each material term of the exact issued quote version — including its recall count/window whenever changed from the prior version — before expiry, the seller is not suspended, no acceptance is in flight, a distinct authorized human represents the buyer side, a verified guardian co-signatory exists for a known under-18 party, and provider payment authorization succeeds in the contract currency, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) confirm the full exact version and create one engagement/payment authorization carrying immutable recall policy/origin/author/count/window terms, and (6) return exactly one engagement referencing the accepted quote; if the flow cannot complete, missing recall acknowledgement returns `VALIDATION_FAILED`, expired quote returns `QUOTE_EXPIRED`, same-human control returns `SELF_ACCEPTANCE_FORBIDDEN`, and provider failure returns `PAYMENT_AUTH_FAILED` while leaving the quote issued. Acceptance compare-and-set prevents duplicate engagement; live policy/listing edits cannot move the accepted recall terms.
- **AC-SRV-05 — Satisfy requirements gate:** Given Engagement is in `requirements` with its typed checklist frozen from the accepted quote, and the buyer supplies each item as a typed value or a Shard 09 project attachment; off-platform links are not an accepted source for file requirements and new items require a change order, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Buyer supplies typed complete items or Shard 09 project attachments; observations never verdict; bounded rejection/deadlock, and (6) return Gate passes atomically or exact buyer/deadlock state commits; if the flow cannot complete, An incomplete or wrongly typed set returns `REQUIREMENTS_INCOMPLETE` naming the exact gaps and passes nothing because the gate is all-or-nothing; the seller receives mechanical measurements and never a verdict; the third rejection round enters no-fault deadlock with full return and no kill fee rather than a fourth attempt, and an upstream `satisfied_by` engagement that is still on time suppresses nudges and fault until it fails or runs late.
- **AC-SRV-06 — Start/pause SLA:** Given The clock starts only at the instant all requirements pass atomically; a pause requires a named response the buyer owes and a resume requires that awaited act to occur, with every event attributed to an actor and cause, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Clock starts only after requirements pass, pauses when buyer owes response and records contestable attributed events, and (6) return Absolute due instant/version updates; if the flow cannot complete, A start before the gate passes, or a pause without a named awaited act, returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` and leaves the due instant unchanged; every clock event is contestable and a contest is recorded against that event rather than silently rewriting the due date; a requirement replacement after pass restarts the clock only if the seller elects it, otherwise the original due instant stands.
- **AC-SRV-07 — Deliver milestone:** Given Engagement is active on a quote-declared milestone whose predecessors are satisfied, and the seller submits that milestone's complete artifact set with its required declarations for QC and integrity evaluation, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Seller submits complete milestone artifact set; QC/integrity evaluates; accepted milestone releases tranche/credit, not final rights, and (6) return Milestone delivery/acceptance evidence commits; if the flow cannot complete, A partial artifact set stays `draft` and returns `DELIVERY_INCOMPLETE`; a mandatory QC failure returns `QC_FAILED` and is not a delivery, while a QC engine outage fails open with a visible unverifiable flag and a watermark outage fails closed to streaming-only; milestone acceptance releases only its tranche and stage credit — final rights stay absent until SRV-16 — and the seller may correct and redeliver.
- **AC-SRV-08 — Request revision:** Given A delivery is published and inside its note window with allowance remaining, the buyer batches at least one note anchored to the exact artifact version, and the round freezes on send, pausing buyer-wait and resuming the seller clock, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Buyer batches notes within window against exact artifact; allowance decrements on valid redelivery; empty/identical cycles consume nothing, and (6) return Round/note/resolution state commits; if the flow cannot complete, A round opened with no note, against a superseded artifact, or after the window closes returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` or `REVISION_RACE` and consumes no allowance; the seller cannot redeliver before the freeze; an identical or rejected redelivery refunds the allowance so only a valid different-file redelivery decrements it; a revision received through the accept deadline plus 120 seconds wins over auto-accept, and any later request is a recall rather than a revision.
- **AC-SRV-09 — Accept change order:** Given A request is classified out of scope against the accepted quote and presented as a priced mini-quote with its own expiry, payment delta and allowance delta defaulting to zero; an eligible buyer authority accepts it before that expiry, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Out-of-scope request becomes priced expiring mini-quote/payment top-up and optional allowance delta, and (6) return Accepted change order versions engagement scope; if the flow cannot complete, An expired change order returns `QUOTE_EXPIRED` and a failed top-up returns `PAYMENT_AUTH_FAILED`, leaving engagement scope at its prior version; a pending change order never pauses the auto-accept window, so the buyer must still act on the delivery; the private assistant may suggest a classification but never rules for the buyer, and a contested classification routes to the Shard 06 dispute path.
- **AC-SRV-10 — Deliver final work:** Given Engagement has reached final delivery, the seller publishes the complete frozen artifact set with digests, the required source, AI and human-performance declarations are present and append-only, and seller payout readiness is verified at delivery, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Seller publishes complete frozen artifact set plus source/AI/human-performance declarations and payout-readiness result, and (6) return Delivery starts fixed acceptance window; if the flow cannot complete, An incomplete set returns `DELIVERY_INCOMPLETE` and remains `draft`; a mandatory QC failure returns `QC_FAILED` and is not a delivery; missing declarations block publication, and a false declaration later routes a Shard 06 integrity case because the platform detects nothing itself; failed payout readiness is surfaced and notified through the window instead of silently blocking acceptance, and the seller may remedy and republish.
- **AC-SRV-11 — Accept/auto-accept:** Given A delivery is published with its fixed acceptance window running — a 3-business-day floor and 30-calendar-day ceiling — and either an eligible non-seller buyer authority accepts explicitly, or the auto-accept job fires after the window and 120-second grace having rechecked that no timely revision, extension or retraction exists, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) accept explicitly or automatically with revision timestamp winning ties, then activate the accepted quote's recall allowance from the actual acceptance `committed_at` instant, and (6) return payment release, rights execution, credit emission and recall-allowance activation all committed or all rolled back; if the flow cannot complete, seller acceptance returns `SELF_ACCEPTANCE_FORBIDDEN`; a timely revision cancels auto-accept and no recall clock starts; retry atomic legs at 2s/8s/32s, then roll all back and page on persistent failure. Count zero produces a closed zero-day allowance; positive count computes exclusive `ends_at = starts_at + window_days` and explicit/auto acceptance use identical clock semantics.
- **AC-SRV-12 — Cancel/abandon/release:** Given An authorized party acting under the same mandate class as quote acceptance chooses one distinct exit kind — mutual release requires both parties — and for abandonment the timer started at the awaited act, was reset only by that act, and any single consented extension has elapsed, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Authorized party chooses distinct exit; settlement derives consumed capacity/work, fault, quote kill schedule and rights disposition, and (6) return Four-leg settlement instruction and terminal state commit; if the flow cannot complete, A delivery received before the cancellation wins and the exit is refused; an unauthorized party or a one-sided mutual release returns `FORBIDDEN` with the engagement unchanged; automatic abandonment settles on the kill schedule and never the full amount, seller fault yields refund plus cover with no reverse kill fee, a points-only exit executes rights disposition and invents no cash debt, and repeated seller rejection caps the fee at zero and routes review.
- **AC-SRV-13 — Open recall:** Given Engagement is terminal and has a quote-pinned positive recall allowance; a buyer request commits with `starts_at <= requested_at < ends_at`, cites the accepted delivery/artifact and has a distinct idempotency key, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) record the request without consuming allowance; on seller acceptance of a timely in-scope request, compare-and-set one remaining count and assign the next ordinal, while decline/duplicate/out-of-scope/withdrawal consumes nothing, and (6) return typed request, allowance and task state while preserving terminal engagement, released payment and transferred rights; if the flow cannot complete, zero/exhausted allowance returns `RECALL_ALLOWANCE_EXHAUSTED`, late request returns `RECALL_WINDOW_CLOSED`, and a final-count race returns `RECALL_ALLOWANCE_CONFLICT` to the losing acceptance without a negative count. A timely request remains reviewable after window expiry; recall never reopens acceptance or payment/rights/credit.
- **AC-SRV-14 — Substitute supplier:** Given The substitute is eligible and, where the engagement is identity-based, the buyer has explicitly approved the named dependency or subcontractor; facility or channel-based engagements may proceed on a pre-authorized profile, and the actual performer is the credited party either way, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Buyer explicitly approves eligible dep/subcontractor where identity-based; actual worker receives credit; race respects first delivery/approval, and (6) return Substitution/engagement/credit facts update; if the flow cannot complete, An unapproved identity-based substitution returns `FORBIDDEN` and, where the seller refuses or fails to perform, the exit settles as seller fault; a delivery recorded before the approval belongs to the original supplier and one recorded after belongs to the actual worker, resolving the race by recorded order rather than claim; an agency or fixer may take commission but never the credit.
- **AC-SRV-15 — Compose fixer/bundle:** Given Buyer is shown the full composition — every stage engagement plus the fixer engagement, each counterparty, the composed title chain and the payout plan — before any commitment; recording the composition is permitted, but activating multi-payee release additionally requires the B3 counsel and payment capability, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Buyer sees N+1 engagements/stages, counterparties, title chain and payout plan; B3 gate controls multi-payee activation, and (6) return Composition record or counsel-gate denial; if the flow cannot complete, Requesting multi-payee activation or release records the composition and returns `COUNSEL_GATE_DISABLED` until B3 activation — the gate is checked at both composition activation and release and no role overrides it; an undisclosed counterparty or a title-chain gap returns `VALIDATION_FAILED`, and each stage meanwhile remains a single-payee engagement carrying its own credit.
- **AC-SRV-16 — Execute rights posture:** Given Final acceptance is committing and the frozen quote rights elections plus artifact digests are readable; execution runs as one leg of the atomic acceptance and reads only the live Shard 10 aggregate allocation, never a live reinterpretation of the listing or posture, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Final acceptance applies frozen master/composition elections against Shard 10 aggregate allocation; no live reinterpretation, and (6) return Rights instruments/title events and credit claim commit atomically; if the flow cannot complete, A co-ownership share failing validation against the Shard 10 aggregate for the recording, or any instrument the registry rejects, returns `RIGHTS_EXECUTION_FAILED` and rolls back the payment and credit legs with it after the 2s/8s/32s retries, preserving the delivery and paging a human; a `creates_none` election writes no composition instrument and is still a valid completed execution; the credit leg emits to Shard 07 regardless of posture, so a buyout moves money and never attribution.
- **AC-SRV-17 — Run repair service:** Given A custodial engagement exists and both parties have confirmed the structured intake condition, media and declared-value consequence; the estimate separating labour and parts is approved before payment authorization — never at booking — and every handoff records from, to, time and mutual condition, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Custody intake condition → assessment/estimate → approval/payment authorization → work → mutual return condition, and (6) return Repair job/custody chain/condition evidence commits; if the flow cannot complete, Work exceeding the approved estimate scope requires a new approval and otherwise returns `VALIDATION_FAILED`; a one-sided condition record leaves the handoff pending rather than transferring custody; the return is compared against the estimate and condition chain rather than original appearance, and a disagreement opens the Shard 06 evidence case while fee escrow is never represented as cover for the item's value.
- **AC-SRV-18 — Complete inspection:** Given The assigned inspector passes the graph conflict check against both parties and submits the category structured template in full; payment is owed on report delivery regardless of the finding or whether the underlying transaction proceeds, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Independent inspector submits structured template and is paid for report delivery regardless outcome, and (6) return Immutable report/conflict check/payment instruction commits; if the flow cannot complete, A failed conflict check returns `FORBIDDEN` and accepts no report; an incomplete template returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` and the report stays unsubmitted; once submitted the report is immutable, so a correction is a superseding report rather than an edit, and no finding or payment may be made conditional on the transaction closing.
- **AC-SRV-19 — Record damage claim:** Given A party to the custody chain files against an existing mutual handoff condition record, comparing it with the approved estimate scope and the declared value; the claim is an evidence record only, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Party compares mutual handoff conditions against approved estimate and declared value; Shard 06 dispute path handles contest, and (6) return Claim/evidence case link without insurance promise; if the flow cannot complete, A claim without a mutual condition record at both ends returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` because there is nothing to compare; the claim never asserts insurance, coverage or a payout entitlement and fee escrow is never represented as item cover; a contested claim opens the Shard 06 dispute case, which owns adjudication, and the condition chain is preserved intact for it.

| SRV-19 | Record damage claim | A party to the custody chain files against an existing mutual handoff condition record, comparing it with the approved estimate scope and the declared value; the claim is an evidence record only. | Party compares mutual handoff conditions against approved estimate and declared value; Shard 06 dispute path handles contest. | Claim/evidence case link without insurance promise. | A claim without a mutual condition record at both ends returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` because there is nothing to compare; the claim never asserts insurance, coverage or a payout entitlement and fee escrow is never represented as item cover; a contested claim opens the Shard 06 dispute case, which owns adjudication, and the condition chain is preserved intact for it. |

### Global Interaction Rules

- Commands carry `actor_person_id`, `acting_party_id`, `acting_context_version`, `idempotency_key`, `expected_version?`, `request_id` and quote/engagement version.
- Listing edits never change issued quotes; quote acceptance freezes scope, requirements, artifacts, revision allowance, rights, kill schedule and payout basis.
- Craft service excludes time/space rental and teaching; taxonomy maps to Shard 07 roles but never extends them.
- Money, rights and credits are separate transaction legs with explicit all-or-none coordination at acceptance milestones named by contract.
- Platform describes evidence/terms and computes arithmetic; it never promises artistic quality, leak prevention, insurance, legal effectiveness or union enforcement.
- Known under-18 professional transactions require verified guardian co-signatory; otherwise blocked under launch age policy.

## Edge Cases

| Case | Required result |
|---|---|
| Listing changes after quote | Issued quote unchanged and still valid unless closed void condition occurs. |
| Same human controls buyer/seller | Different authorized buyer human required; single-authority entity cannot accept. |
| Payment authorization races quote expiry | Serializable accepted/expired result; at most one engagement. |
| Requirements reject three times | Deadlock, full return, no fault/kill fee; Shard 06 only if contested. |
| Upstream requirement engagement in flight | Suppress nudges/fault while on time; resume if failed/late. |
| Revision/auto-accept race | Valid revision through deadline+120s wins; auto-accept never backdated. |
| QC engine unavailable | Fail open with visible unverifiable flag; watermark engine fails closed to streaming-only. |
| Acceptance atomic leg fails | Retry 2s/8s/32s then rollback all legs and page human. |
| Seller/buyer identity overlaps after engagement | High-consequence action rechecks authority/self-dealing; existing record remains audited. |
| Multi-party payout requested | Record composition but return `COUNSEL_GATE_DISABLED` until B3 activation. |
| Delivery arrives as substitution approved concurrently | Delivery before recorded approval belongs original; after approval actual worker. |
| Custody item damaged | Preserve mutual condition chain; fee escrow never represented as item coverage. |
| Seller cancels | Refund/cover/reputation facts; no reverse kill-fee obligation. |
| Points-only exit | Rights disposition only; no manufactured cash settlement. |
| Seller and buyer are the same human | Election allowed; execution writes no self-referential grant. |
| Co-owner shares across sellers exceed the buyer's holding | Blocked at execution against Shard 10's aggregate for the recording; never a false green at election time. |
| Listing posture edited while a quote is outstanding | The issued quote is untouched; no mutation upstream of an issued quote may alter that quote's posture. |
| Recall craft policy changes after quote issue | Issued and accepted quote values, engagement snapshot and allowance remain unchanged; only a newly issued quote uses the new policy version. |
| Quote sets zero recalls with positive window, or positive count outside 7–90 days | Return `RECALL_TERMS_INVALID`; issue no quote. Zero requires zero days; count is always `0..5`. |
| Explicit and automatic delivery acceptance replay | One acceptance and one allowance exist. Both use actual first `committed_at`; replay returns identical start/end and never extends the window. |
| Two timely recalls compete for final count | Requests may coexist; seller acceptance compare-and-set yields one ordinal/winner and `RECALL_ALLOWANCE_CONFLICT` for the loser. No negative count or duplicate consumption. |
| Timely recall reviewed after window expiry | Preserve eligibility from request timestamp; seller may accept later and consume count. A request at or after exclusive end returns `RECALL_WINDOW_CLOSED`. |
