## Overview

Shard 06 owns the protected case system for reports, moderation, sanctions, appeals, fraud review, transaction disputes, copyright/authenticity enforcement, personal-safety response, identity/ownership disputes, illegal-content/legal process, and immutable evidence. It separates allegation from finding, advisory signals from decisions, access sanctions from ownership truth, and ordinary case access from restricted preservation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-TSE-01 — Submit safety report:** Given Actor holds Reporter/claimant standing with a current acting context; the target object or profile is addressable and the submitted reason is valid for that target kind at the pinned reason-registry version; anonymous mode is available only where lawful for that target; the case does not yet exist beyond `draft`, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Reason derives from target type; anonymity allowed where lawful; per-reporter throttling admits but deprioritizes excess; replay is idempotent, and (6) return Case and intake snapshot commit atomically; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` on an unaddressable target or a reason invalid for the target kind, `UNAUTHENTICATED` or `ACTING_CONTEXT_STALE` on stale identity, and `IDEMPOTENCY_MISMATCH` when a replayed key carries a differing payload while a matching key returns the prior case unchanged; the refusal discloses no target existence and commits no partial case, and the reporter may correct the reason or target and resubmit. Excess per-reporter volume never refuses intake — it reweights routing and raises a fraud-review signal after admission.
- **AC-TSE-02 — Submit legal/DMCA notice:** Given Actor is an identified claimant with protected legal identity — this path admits no anonymous submission; the asserted regime's full attestation set is presented at its pinned policy version; the target asset is addressable and the claimant supplies the claimant/asset/infringement-event triple that forms the strike unit, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Identified claimant completes regime attestations; incomplete notice stays draft and cannot remove content or increment strikes, and (6) return Validated notice, attestation and capture intent exist; if the flow cannot complete, An incomplete or unattested notice returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` and the case stays `draft`: no content is removed, no availability action runs and no repeat-infringer strike is counted; `POLICY_VERSION_INVALID` where the attestation set does not match the pinned regime version. The claimant completes the missing attestations and resubmits against the same draft, and a duplicate notice on an already-counted strike unit cannot inflate the ledger.
- **AC-TSE-03 — Route and claim case:** Given Case is `received`, `triaged` or `queued` with a resolved severity and no active lease; the claiming staff actor holds the queue capability for that lane, has exposure budget remaining and has no conflict with any case party, target or mandate; an S0 case is claimable only from its isolated queue, which has no general-queue fallback, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Reason, object type, severity and remaining time select a weighted-fair lane; staff claims an exclusive expiring lease, and (6) return Lease/version recorded without resetting clocks; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` on a missing capability, exhausted exposure budget or a conflict; `VERSION_CONFLICT` when another reviewer wins the compare-and-set on the same `case_id/version`; `CASE_LEASE_LOST` where a lease is already active. A refused or expired claim preserves the policy clock and the original deadline, returns the case to its weighted-fair lane for reclaim, and never weakens the safety-of-person capacity floor to make room.
- **AC-TSE-04 — Review case:** Given Case is `claimed` or `reviewing` under the acting reviewer's own unexpired lease; the exact policy rule version for the case reason is published and effective; the reviewer's exposure controls, conflict and mandate checks pass; and the minimum safe projection renders the mutable target beside the immutable intake snapshot, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Minimum safe projection, exposure controls, conflict/mandate checks and exact policy version required; allegation remains distinct from finding, and (6) return Proposal or no-action result written; if the flow cannot complete, `CASE_LEASE_LOST` when the lease expired or was reassigned, which preserves the reviewer draft outside canonical state and retains the original deadline; `POLICY_VERSION_INVALID` when no rule version is effective for the reason; `FORBIDDEN` on a conflict surfaced mid-review, which routes to recusal or escalation. No proposal is written on refusal, the allegation record is untouched, and reporter identity, detection method and sealed evidence stay omitted from every refusal.
- **AC-TSE-05 — Apply sanction/takedown:** Given A `case_decision` sits in `awaiting_control` with its required control satisfied, a cited effective policy rule version, an evidence inventory, a consequence preview and the expected case and target versions; the requested `Decision` and `ScopeType` map to a permitted `SanctionRung` under `DecisionRungMap`; an entity-scope action additionally requires a resolved responsible actor and mandate from the Shard 01 snapshot, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Narrowest scope only; access/privileges may change, never ownership/credits; expected version and idempotency required, and (6) return Sanction, audit and required SoR commit atomically; if the flow cannot complete, `CONTROL_REQUIRED` when concurrence or the compensating control is unsatisfied, `SANCTION_CLASS_GATED` for a rung-5 request which routes to the Payments risk reserve instead, `VERSION_CONFLICT` when a version moved or another enforcement already won the subject-target-policy tuple, and `FORBIDDEN` on unresolved actor or mandate at entity scope. A failed Statement of Reasons or audit write rolls the whole enforcement back so no sanction activates; ownership, confirmed credits, splits and export rights are unreachable from this flow in every outcome.
- **AC-TSE-06 — Concur or reaffirm:** Given A proposed decision sits in `awaiting_control` with an unmet control requirement computed from severity and rung; for concurrence the acting reviewer is a human, authorized, unconflicted and distinct from the original decider; for the solo-team path the separate-sitting cooling-off interval has elapsed, except on urgent S0/S1 where the same compensating controls apply without the delay, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) From second moderator onward, S1 and rung ≥6/indefinite require another human; solo uses compensating controls and cooling-off except urgent S0/S1, and (6) return Actual control used is immutable; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when the actor is the original decider or the control is offered by model output rather than a human, `CONTROL_REQUIRED` when the cooling-off interval has not elapsed on the solo path, and `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a moved decision version. The decision stays in `awaiting_control` with no sanction active, and the recorded control kind is never rewritten to claim a control that was not actually used.
- **AC-TSE-07 — Appeal decision:** Given An active decision is in `decided` state, the appellant is an eligible enforcement subject or their authorized party, the appeal window under the pinned policy version is open, and the assigned reviewer is a human distinct from the original decider; where the ordinary appeal surface is not yet enabled for the phase, reversibility remains reachable through the same route, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Eligible subject appends evidence; independent human review applies when surface enabled while reversibility always remains possible, and (6) return Per-item outcome and correction recorded; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when the appellant is not the enforcement subject or the assigned reviewer is the original decider, `VALIDATION_FAILED` on a closed appeal window, `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a moved decision. Supplement material is a sealed append that never mutates the original snapshot or evidence; a partially failed multi-scope reversal leaves the decision active with per-item compensating commands retried to idempotent convergence, and the appellant sees a truthful partial-restoration state until it converges.
- **AC-TSE-08 — Restrict another user:** Given Authenticated consumer actor with a current acting context and an addressable subject; no case, finding, severity, policy version or moderator involvement is required and none is created — the edge is private actor-to-subject and non-discoverable, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Private block/restriction is immediate with no case requirement and propagates deny-first, and (6) return Effective edge version returned; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` on an unaddressable subject and `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a concurrent edge change, resolved against the returned effective edge version. Partial propagation is deny-first at the source: the edge is effective immediately, stale downstream surfaces must not expose content, and retry plus alert drives convergence. The subject is never notified, the edge stays non-discoverable, and no refusal reveals that an edge exists.
- **AC-TSE-09 — Evaluate advisory signal:** Given A system worker with service identity evaluates one named action event or object under a named, versioned rule, model or provider; a rule affecting live routing has already completed shadow mode; the subject is an action or an object and never a person, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Rule/model/provider scores action or object, never person; may prioritize review but cannot sanction, remove or notify, and (6) return Signal and disposition linked without becoming finding; if the flow cannot complete, `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` or a classifier/matcher outage fails open — the case continues through the ordinary unscored flow with the gap logged, and no removal, notice, priority claim or finding is fabricated to fill it. `VALIDATION_FAILED` where the proposed subject is a person. A recorded signal that is never dispositioned lapses at its stated expiry, and no failure path lets a signal reach an enforcement command.
- **AC-TSE-10 — Open transaction dispute:** Given A transaction exists at a readable version in its owning commerce shard, the filer holds a claimant role on it under a current mandate, the filing window under the effective remedy-policy version is open, and the contemporaneous evidence manifest for that transaction is already sealed and retrievable, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Authorized party files against transaction; remedies derive from type; contemporaneous evidence is retrieved, not reconstructed, and (6) return Parties, remedies and deadline freeze; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` on an unknown transaction or a closed filing window, `FORBIDDEN` when the filer holds no claimant role or the mandate has lapsed, `POLICY_VERSION_INVALID` when no remedy policy is effective for the transaction type. Nothing freezes on refusal. Evidence missing from the sealed manifest is reported as an evidentiary gap and may never be reconstructed after the fact by the filing itself.
- **AC-TSE-11 — Mediate or adjudicate:** Given A dispute is open with its parties, transaction version, remedies, deadline and evidence manifest already frozen; settlement additionally requires every binding party to hold a current mandate and to have signed the exact proposal terms; adjudication additionally requires an unconflicted adjudicator and pinned evidence-weight and protection-rule versions, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Settlement is party agreement, not platform finding; adjudication uses disclosed evidence weights and protection rules, and (6) return Signed settlement or reasoned outcome reconciles; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` when proposal terms are inexact or a signature is missing, `FORBIDDEN` on a lapsed or absent binding mandate, `VERSION_CONFLICT` when the dispute version moved. A settlement that cannot bind every party leaves the dispute open and creates no finding and no protection payout; an externally arriving chargeback reconciles into the same canonical dispute rather than opening a second one.
- **AC-TSE-12 — Process DMCA counter-notice:** Given A validated notice has already produced an availability action against the subject's asset; the actor is the identified affected subject, has been shown the address and jurisdiction disclosure consequences before signing, and presents the complete signed 512(g) statement set, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Subject sees disclosure consequences and signs `512(g) statements; restoration timer starts only when complete, and (6) return Delivery evidence and deadline recorded; if the flow cannot complete, An incomplete counter returns `VALIDATION_FAILED` and starts no restoration timer — the availability action stands and the subject may complete the statements and resubmit. `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` on undeliverable claimant notice retries with the delivery evidence recorded; a notice demonstrably undelivered cannot finalize termination. Every transition preserves the notice and counter-notice evidence, and no disclosure consequence is applied before the subject has seen it.
- **AC-TSE-13 — Resolve identity/ownership case:** Given A case of kind `impersonation` or `ownership` is under review with the Shard 01 party, alias, credit, membership and mandate snapshot resolvable at an exact version, and the outcome sought is scoped to platform access and privileges only; possession of a credential is evidence in the case and never satisfies the ownership precondition, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Shard 01 party, alias, credit, membership and mandate truth controls; credential possession is not ownership, and (6) return Scoped outcome preserves ownership records; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` where the requested outcome would mutate ownership, confirmed credits or splits, which Shard 06 cannot write; `ACTING_CONTEXT_STALE` or `VERSION_CONFLICT` when the Shard 01 snapshot moved, requiring re-resolution before any decision. Unresolved actor or mandate ambiguity blocks collective or entity-level action and routes to recusal or escalation. Ownership records survive every outcome, including termination, and stay exportable.
- **AC-TSE-14 — Handle safety/crisis intake:** Given For harassment or doxxing, the case is admitted to the protected lane with reviewer exposure controls in place and reporter identity sealed. For crisis, the intake itself is the only precondition: no classification, policy version, queue position, severity or reviewer authority is required for a resources-only response, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Harassment/doxxing uses protected lane; crisis resources bypass classifier/ladder/SoR/appeal and never sanction person in crisis, and (6) return Safety action or resources-only completion recorded; if the flow cannot complete, The protected lane refuses with a typed reason that never discloses the reporter, the detection method or the reviewer. The crisis path has no refusal that withholds resources — classifier, ladder, Statement of Reasons and appeal are bypassed by design, the person in crisis is never sanctioned by this flow, and `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` on any advisory dependency still delivers the resources-only completion.
- **AC-TSE-15 — Receive legal process:** Given The request arrived through the documented intake channel, since v1 exposes no self-service portal and makes no 24/7 or emergency guarantee; requester identity, instrument authenticity, jurisdiction, authority and scope are verified and any nondisclosure prohibition is recorded; any release additionally requires counsel-authorized capability, step-up proof, a minimization manifest and the expected request version, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) V1 verifies requester, authority, scope and prohibition; overbroad requests narrow/refuse; user notified unless barred, and (6) return No disclosure before legal authorization; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` or `STEP_UP_REQUIRED` without counsel-authorized capability and step-up, `COUNSEL_GATE_DISABLED` where no gate record exists, `DISCLOSURE_PROHIBITED` where an order bars notifying the user. An overbroad request is narrowed or refused with the reason recorded; an urgent after-hours request is logged as intake only with no improvised disclosure; nothing is released before legal authorization, and release is limited to manifest-listed fields through an expiring transfer that grants no search or database access.
- **AC-TSE-16 — Capture evidence:** Given A source-domain event is committing in its own transaction and carries the field and blob manifest, so the capture intent is written atomically with it; the worker holds service identity and a stable idempotency key; referenced media already sits in immutable storage. The user-facing transaction never waits on capture delivery, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Source event atomically writes capture intent; worker snapshots fields and hashes media; terminal failure is explicit, and (6) return Sealed entry or `capture_failed` marker exists; if the flow cannot complete, Retries reuse the stable idempotency key; exhaustion writes an explicit `capture_failed` terminal reason with `CAPTURE_FAILED`, alerts the bundle owner and exposes a truthful safe warning — no evidence is fabricated and `capture_failed` attaches to a live case rather than closing it. A worker crash after the source commit leaves the atomic capture intent intact for idempotent retry, and purged referenced media leaves an immutable tombstone and an explicit evidentiary-degradation note rather than a reused locator.
- **AC-TSE-17 — Place/release legal hold:** Given The acting custodian holds the counsel-authorized capability granted through Shard 05 with step-up proof, and states a basis code, an object manifest and a release condition; a release additionally requires the hold to be `active`, its stated release condition met and the expected hold version supplied, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Counsel-authorized actor states basis, scope and release condition; hold supersedes destructive clocks, and (6) return Hold/release version and manifest commit; if the flow cannot complete, `COUNSEL_GATE_DISABLED` or `FORBIDDEN` without the counsel-authorized capability, `STEP_UP_REQUIRED` without step-up proof, `VALIDATION_FAILED` on a missing basis, manifest or release condition, `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a moved hold version. Release deletes nothing by itself — it re-runs every remaining retention clock and re-evaluates deletion eligibility — and while active the hold contributes an unbounded clock that supersedes destructive timers, including an intersecting erasure request, which anonymizes only where lawful.
- **AC-TSE-18 — Assess domain-launch risk:** Given A domain or release is pending launch and not yet enabled; the acting owner holds the Shard 05 launch-gate capability and can record the harms, controls, gaps, supporting evidence and an explicit disposition against that specific domain and release, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Before launch, owner records harms, controls, gaps, evidence and disposition; calendar-only review is insufficient, and (6) return Assessment gates launch configuration; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` when harms, controls, evidence, gaps or disposition are absent — a calendar-only or date-stamped review is rejected as insufficient and the launch configuration stays gated. A refused assessment enables nothing; the Shard 05 gate reads the recorded disposition rather than the existence of an assessment, and a later assessment supersedes rather than edits the prior record.
- **AC-TSE-19 — Submit trusted-flagger notice:** Given An active `trusted_flagger_grant` exists for the submitting entity with its recorded basis, scope and granted SLA profile; every submitted item targets public content inside that scope; a bulk submission carries a per-item idempotency key; a throttled or suspended lane no longer satisfies the shortened-SLA precondition and the entity falls back to ordinary reporting, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Granted per-entity status shortens the report SLA and admits idempotent bulk API submission per item; the lane covers public content only, never private messages, and never bypasses SoR or appeal. Each per-item outcome writes back to the flagger accuracy ledger, and (6) return Per-item cases created at the granted SLA; accuracy-ledger entries committed; duplicate batch replay creates no additional case; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for items outside the grant scope or targeting private messages, and `VALIDATION_FAILED` on a missing per-item key. A partially failed batch is retried idempotently per item and creates no duplicate case; a batch exceeding review capacity takes its weighted-fair lane share without touching the safety-of-person capacity floor. Where the flagger's claim contradicts the provenance graph the notice does not win by default and the platform record is cited. Sustained inaccuracy throttles then suspends the lane by a reasoned, appealable decision, never silently.
- **AC-TSE-20 — Evaluate message-safety signal:** Given A message is in flight on a thread and only metadata and pattern features are available for evaluation; content inspection becomes legal to attempt only after a thread participant — never a non-participant — has reported that thread, and the evaluation runs off the send path, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Metadata/pattern signals only by default (new account + payment instruction + off-platform contact + urgency); content inspection escalates only on a participant report. Warning is inline, dismissible and never blocks the send, and (6) return Recipient warning surfaced or thread delivered clean; evaluation never delays delivery; if the flow cannot complete, `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` or any evaluation unavailability fails open: the gap is logged and the thread is delivered unwarned, never blocked and never delayed. A non-participant report is rejected outright. The warning is inline and dismissible, so a dismissed or unseen warning has no further recovery path and the send always completes; absent a report no message content is retained.
- **AC-TSE-21 — Defend card testing and triangulation:** Given An authorization attempt reaches the payment rail with device, session and card-BIN-spread windows computable, and velocity is evaluated there before any risk score is consulted; digital fulfilment additionally requires settlement confirmation. A triangulation payout hold requires an indicator set that stands without price and a human reviewer to receive the case, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Card-testing velocity per device/session/card-BIN-spread throttles then blocks at the rail before any score is consulted; digital fulfilment never precedes settlement confirmation. Triangulation indicators produce a payout hold plus review, never an auto-block, and below-market price is never a signal on its own, and (6) return Rail block recorded, or hold plus review case opened with the indicator set; if the flow cannot complete, A velocity breach throttles then blocks at the rail with the decision recorded, and fulfilment is withheld until settlement confirms. Per-device velocity is blind to a distributed botnet, so the aggregate BIN-level window is the required control, while a legitimate spike of many distinct buyers, devices and funding sources is the inverse fingerprint and must not throttle. Triangulation never auto-blocks: the seller keeps account access under a payout hold with a review case open, a spotless reputation cannot veto the finding, and a below-market price alone is refused as a basis.
- **AC-TSE-22 — Adjudicate review integrity:** Given Fraud-linkage-graph evidence exists for the role under examination and hashes to a recorded manifest; a removal additionally requires a cited effective rule version and a deliverable Statement of Reasons route to the review author. Review data is never a valid detection input, and a finding in one role is never a precondition in another, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Detection runs on the fraud linkage graph, never on review data. Removal is an enforcement action requiring a cited rule, a Statement of Reasons to the review author and an appeal. Reputation and integrity are per-role; a ring in one role never contaminates another, and (6) return Review-removal decision recorded with cited rule, SoR and appeal route; per-role scope preserved; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` where the evidence is drawn from review data rather than the linkage graph, `POLICY_VERSION_INVALID` without a cited effective rule, `CONTROL_REQUIRED` where the Statement of Reasons or appeal route is unavailable — in each case no review is removed. Every removal delivers the Statement of Reasons and an appeal to the author, a reversal restores the review per item, and per-role scope is preserved so no reversal or finding crosses a role boundary.
- **AC-TSE-23 — Review authenticity or counterfeit claim:** Given One of three claim paths opens the case against an addressable listing or item — a buyer post-purchase dispute, a brand notice, or platform listing-time detection; a brand claim additionally requires the trademark-exhaustion check to have been evaluated before any seller-facing action. Authenticity claim windows extend beyond the ordinary dispute window, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Three claim paths converge (buyer post-purchase dispute, brand notice, platform listing-time detection) and must never yield contradictory seller reasons. Trademark exhaustion is checked before any brand claim is actioned. Misrepresented provenance is adjudicated against the Shard 23 registry chain, not against detection; innocent misrepresentation is a dispute, not an offence, and (6) return Single reconciled outcome per listing with the claim path and exhaustion check recorded; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` where the registry chain does not resolve, in which case the claim is adjudicated as unproven rather than decided against detection output; an exhausted trademark refuses the brand claim before any seller-facing action is taken. Where two paths would produce different seller reasons they reconcile to one outcome per listing rather than issuing contradictory reasons, and innocent misrepresentation resolves as a dispute remedy rather than an enforcement offence.
- **AC-TSE-24 — Respond to pre-release leak:** Given The unreleased asset was watermarked per recipient at transfer and each access was logged as a consequential audited action; a leak report supplies a copy that can be matched against the platform's own reference; and the asset's release state at match time is resolvable, because it changes what an identical match means, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Every delivery of an unreleased asset is watermarked per recipient at transfer and logged as a consequential action. A leak report matches the copy against the platform's own reference, extracts the watermark, traces the access log and produces a finding. The asset's release state changes the meaning of an identical match, and (6) return Forensic finding with watermark identity and access trace, or explicit no-attribution result; if the flow cannot complete, An unwatermarked, degraded or unmatchable copy yields an explicit no-attribution finding rather than a guess, and an incomplete access trace is stated as a gap in the finding. A match against an already-released asset is recorded as a non-event rather than escalated as a crisis. The platform supplies evidence for the rights holder's external notice and never hosts the leaked copy itself.
- **AC-TSE-25 — Attach meetup safety layer:** Given A meeting is being arranged between two parties and neither holds an active restriction edge against the other, since a restricted party cannot arrange the meeting at all rather than merely being unable to message; the controls are offered at the arrangement moment rather than from settings, and every one of them is optional, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Offered at the moment the meeting is arranged, never buried in settings: suggested public exchange location, optional check-in/check-out timer, share-my-meetup with a trusted contact. ### Global Interaction Rules

- Every command carries `actor_person_id`, `acting_party_id?`, `acting_context_version`, `idempotency_key`, `expected_version?`, `request_id` and required step-up proof.
- Canonical writes use domain services plus protected PostgreSQL RPC/RLS; Realtime, queues, search and analytics are projections or hints.
- Intake captures target, acting context, policy/routing versions and minimal immutable snapshot; later edits never rewrite allegation.
- Volume, badge, persona, entity membership, price, genre, protected trait and role diversity never independently establish guilt or priority.
- Advisory classifiers/matchers fail open; prohibited-item checkout and approved payout screening fail closed; counsel-gated automation remains disabled.
- User status exposes safe projection only; reporter identity, detection method, reviewer identity, legal prohibitions and restricted evidence stay sealed.

## Access Control

| Actor/capability | Permitted | Explicitly denied |
|---|---|---|
| Reporter/claimant | Create applicable report/notice; opaque receipt and safe own status | Queue, other reports, signals, reviewer identity, sealed evidence |
| Case party | Own disclosed material, typed response/appeal, authorized settlement | Mutate snapshots/findings; counterparty-private/restricted evidence |
| Public user | Published policy and aggregate transparency | Allegations, case existence, safety/legal records, low-count analytics |
| Moderator | Assigned safe projection; review/propose within capability/conflict rules | General DB/search, self-concurrence, restricted evidence, ownership mutation |
| Independent reviewer | Concur/appeal when distinct and authorized | Bypass policy/SoR, alter evidence, decide conflicted case |
| Fraud/safety specialist | Scoped queue/signals for assigned purpose | Marketing reuse, unrelated profiling/enforcement |
| Legal/counsel | Verify process, authorize disclosure/hold/gates | Unlogged disclosure, blanket access, retroactive policy change |
| Break-glass custodian | Time-boxed restricted validation with step-up and dual evidence | Export/copy/preview/party disclosure/persistent access |
| System worker | Idempotent route/capture/project/notify with service identity | Human judgment absent approved automatic legal path |
| Platform administrator | Capability/configuration/health | Decide case by admin status or weaken floors through settings |
| Trusted flagger | Submit notices in the granted scope; read own accuracy record and lane decisions | Private messages, queue visibility, other flaggers' records, any content outside the grant scope |
| Meetup participant | Own arrangement safety record and trusted-contact share | Counterparty's record |
