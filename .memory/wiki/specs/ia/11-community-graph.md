## Overview

Shard 11 owns entity follows, supplementary professional connections, evidence-based endorsements, actionable activity feed, collaborator discovery, derived/citable two-hop collaboration paths, double-opt-in introductions and a strictly private personal rolodex. Social edges never grant contact consent, project access, authority, rights or trust. Private CRM records never influence shared ranking, recommendations or other users' experiences.

## Features

- **03.01 Connections, Follows & Endorsements** — acting-entity follows, contextual professional requests and evidence-based/hideable endorsements.
- **03.02 Activity Feed & Ranking** — typed domain events, actionability-first/evidence-first ranking, explicit controls and subordinate native posts/reactions.
- **03.03 Collaborator Discovery & Matchmaking** — evidence-ranked search, explainable fit, expiring role-specific appetite and term-explicit collaboration calls.
- **03.04 Warm Intros & Collaboration Graph** — derived citable paths, broker-first double opt-in, evidenced referrals and density-aware reachability.
- **03.05 Private Rolodex & CRM** — owner-isolated shadow contacts, private notes/tags/lists and author-only reminders.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-COM-01 — Follow/unfollow entity:** Given An explicit acting entity is selected, because every follow is a directed entity-to-entity edge and states are never unioned across a person's acting contexts; the target party exists and is neither suspended nor deleted, no block or Shard 06 restriction stands between the parties, and any per-edge `FollowAlertScope` above local-only requires a verified email and explicit consent under B4 — no target approval is required, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Explicit acting entity follows without approval; bounded alert scope; unfollow never notifies and follow gives no contact consent, and (6) return Edge version/local state and safe counts update; if the flow cannot complete, `BLOCKED_ROUTE` when a block or restriction stands between the parties, returned without disclosing that it exists, `RATE_LIMITED` on abuse-rate breach, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for an alert scope outside `FollowAlertScope` and `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a competing edge change; without verified email and consent the edge stays browser-local and is truthfully labelled rather than creating a durable alert, an unfollow never notifies the target, and suspended or deleted endpoints simply drop out of safe counts.
- **AC-COM-02 — Request professional connection:** Given The acting context is a professional party because a Fan acting context has no connect surface, the mandatory contextual note is supplied, the target's reachability policy admits a direct request from this sender class, no block or restriction stands between the parties, and the sender is inside its request rate budget, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Non-Fan actor sends context-specific note under reachability/rate rules; acceptance never auto-follows, and (6) return Request/edge state and neutral expiry commit; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for a Fan acting context, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for a missing contextual note, `BLOCKED_ROUTE` when a block or restriction stands between the parties — returned without exposing the reason — and `RATE_LIMITED` when the sender's budget is exhausted; an unanswered request reaches a neutral expiry that carries no blame signal, and acceptance creates only a supplementary edge that never auto-follows and never becomes a source for the derived collaboration path.
- **AC-COM-03 — Endorse collaborator:** Given The acting context is a professional party, an eligible verified collaboration between endorser and endorsee exists in Shard 07, Shard 09 or booking evidence, the claim names a skill or reliability attribute that evidence supports — an Operator may endorse booking-evidenced reliability only, never craft — and the basis is displayable to viewers, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Actor selects skill/reliability claim backed by eligible verified collaboration; basis is displayed; endorsee may hide, and (6) return Endorsement/basis/visibility version commits; if the flow cannot complete, `EVIDENCE_INELIGIBLE` when no eligible verified collaboration backs the claim or when an Operator claims a craft attribute, `FORBIDDEN` for a Fan acting context, `BLOCKED_ROUTE` when a block stands between the parties and `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a competing endorsement edit; private CRM notes can never supply a basis, and an endorsee hiding an endorsement is silent and changes visibility only — the endorser's statement and its evidence stay intact.
- **AC-COM-04 — Read activity feed:** Given The viewer is authenticated in a resolved acting context, each candidate event is eligible under its own source domain's policy, and viewer authorization, blocks and restrictions, acting-party scope and feed preference are all re-checked at read time before candidacy, with cache keys carrying the viewer party plus policy, block, suppression and projection versions, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Viewer receives typed eligible events ranked for actionability, evidence, proximity and geography; alert events bypass rank, and (6) return Cursor/source versions and reason labels returned; if the flow cannot complete, `ACTING_CONTEXT_STALE` when the acting context has moved and the authorization recheck invalidates the cache, and `FORBIDDEN` for a viewer outside the requested scope; a retracted or cancelled source event renders as a visible amendment or tombstoned removal under its source policy rather than a silent rewrite, an unavailable ranking input is labelled unknown or degraded instead of being treated as zero, and no response exposes a numeric score, a hidden alternative or the existence of a muted source.
- **AC-COM-05 — Mute/reduce feed source:** Given The viewer is authenticated in a resolved acting context and names an existing entity, event type or domain to mute or a control to adjust; the preference is viewer-private, so no consent, relationship or notice from the muted party is required, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Viewer silently mutes entity/type/domain or adjusts controls; muted party never learns, and (6) return Viewer-private preference version applies; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for an unknown entity, type or domain and `VERSION_CONFLICT` when a competing preference edit committed first; a refused change leaves the prior preference version in force, and on no failure path is the muted party notified, shown a count or otherwise able to learn that a mute exists or was attempted.
- **AC-COM-06 — Publish/react to native post:** Given For authoring, the acting context is a professional persona, the content is inside the bounded post limits and it passes moderation intake; for reacting, any acting context including Fan may react to a post it is authorized to read. Either way posts stay structurally subordinate to evidenced domain events in ranking, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Professional persona authors bounded post; Fan may read/react only; structured events outrank posts, and (6) return Post/reaction state with moderation linkage commits; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when a Fan acting context attempts to author, `VALIDATION_FAILED` when content exceeds the bounded limits, `BLOCKED_ROUTE` when a block or Shard 06 restriction stands between the actor and the post's author and `RATE_LIMITED` on abuse-rate breach; a moderated or removed post keeps its moderation linkage rather than vanishing silently, and no failure here promotes a post above evidenced domain events.
- **AC-COM-07 — Search collaborators:** Given The searcher is authenticated in a resolved acting context, the query states its role, evidence needs, remote or in-room mode as the primary axis, geography and feasibility, and the candidate set is drawn only from public or shared role, provenance, appetite, remote-capability and geography projections — private notes, shadow contacts, CRM tags, message content, protected traits and undisclosed availability are forbidden inputs, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Query includes role, evidence needs, remote/in-room, geography and feasibility; results explain match and degrade honestly, and (6) return Bounded authorized result/cursor plus reasons; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for a query missing its role or its remote/in-room axis and `FORBIDDEN` for a scope the searcher may not read; a candidate backed only by self-tags returns explicitly labelled as degraded rather than ranked as evidenced, a missing ranking input appears in the explanation instead of being silently treated as zero or negative, disappearing evidence re-ranks or degrades the result and is never cached as a durable trust claim, and the response carries reasons rather than a numeric score.
- **AC-COM-08 — Set open-to status:** Given The acting party opts in explicitly, because silence is the default and appetite is never inferred; each signal names its role version, mode, geography or scope and an expiry, and the party is authorized to publish an appetite signal for that role, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) User opts into one or more role-specific appetite signals with expiry and scope; silence is default, and (6) return Signal/version/expiry task commits; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for a signal without a role, scope or expiry, `FORBIDDEN` for a party that may not publish for that role and `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a competing signal edit; a paused or expired signal drops out of discovery, and if the expiry task itself fails the signal is still excluded from search because a stale status never remains searchable.
- **AC-COM-09 — Publish collaboration call:** Given The acting party owns the call and may publish one, the call states its role, scope, split, unpaid or credit-only terms, its unused-submission handling policy and its expiry — all mandatory — and those terms and unused-submission consequences are presented before any responder uploads, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Owner provides role, scope, split/unpaid/credit-only terms, unused-submission handling and expiry, and (6) return Moderated call version publishes; if the flow cannot complete, `VALIDATION_FAILED` when terms, unused-submission policy or expiry are missing, `FORBIDDEN` for a party that does not own the call and `BLOCKED_ROUTE` when Shard 06 restricts the actor or the content; a call held or rejected by moderation does not publish and solicits no responder, and no failure path lets a published call bypass its stated terms or its safety review.
- **AC-COM-10 — Accept call responder:** Given The actor is the call owner, the call is still active rather than paused, expired or closed, exactly one response is being accepted, and typed Shard 09 project and Shard 10 split setup commands are available to initiate — the submission itself transferred no rights, so acceptance is the first point at which downstream ownership work begins, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Authorized owner accepts one response and initiates typed Shard 09 project/Shard 10 split setup; submission itself transfers no rights, and (6) return Acceptance command set and audit commit; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for an actor who does not own the call, `VALIDATION_FAILED` when the call has expired or closed or when more than one response is accepted at once and `VERSION_CONFLICT` when a competing acceptance committed first; if the downstream Shard 09 or Shard 10 setup commands cannot be issued the acceptance confers no rights by default — the submission still transfers nothing and the command set is retried idempotently from the recorded audit entry.
- **AC-COM-11 — Find intro path:** Given The query is ego-rooted at the requester's own party and names a specific target, because an arbitrary third-party traversal between two other people is refused server-side; search depth is at most two hops, every candidate edge is built from session or attestation evidence between claimed active human parties with second-human confirmation, every intermediary is a claimed active human while entities and unclaimed nodes may be terminals only, and current blocks, endpoint suppressions and evidence citability are resolved at request time rather than served from cache, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Actor asks ego-rooted path to target; system returns citable ≤2-hop path or unknown/no-path-within-range, and (6) return Fresh path-evidence snapshot returned; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when the query is not ego-rooted, `PATH_UNCITABLE` when an edge cannot be explained to the requester without leaking private context and `BLOCKED_ROUTE` when a block or restriction cuts the route; a dependency timeout or missing projection returns `unknown` and never a no-path answer, an exhaustive authorized search returns `no_path_within_intro_range`, and edge age is disclosed in the path reason rather than decayed or weighted.
- **AC-COM-12 — Suppress graph edge:** Given The actor is one of the derived edge's two human endpoints, since no third party, entity or administrator may suppress it, and the edge exists in the derived collaboration graph; the underlying evidence and credit records are neither inputs to nor targets of this decision, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Either human endpoint silently makes derived edge non-traversable; evidence/history remains private, and (6) return Suppression version immediately affects paths; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for an actor who is not an endpoint of the edge, `VALIDATION_FAILED` when the named edge does not exist in the derived graph and `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a competing suppression change; a refused suppression is as silent to the other endpoint as a successful one, the underlying evidence and history remain private and intact, credits are never affected, and because the suppression version is part of the path cache key an accepted change invalidates cached paths immediately.
- **AC-COM-13 — Request warm intro:** Given A citable broker path to the target exists and the chosen broker is eligible, the requester supplies a specific ask because a generic request is not accepted, the requester is inside its dedicated intro-request rate budget and the broker is inside its inbound cap, and the target's reachability resolves to `intro_required`, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Requester states specific ask to eligible broker; target is not contacted until broker consents, and (6) return Broker request with expiry/rate budget commits; if the flow cannot complete, `RATE_LIMITED` when the requester's dedicated intro budget is exhausted, `BROKER_CAP_REACHED` when the broker's inbound cap is full, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for a missing specific ask, `PATH_UNCITABLE` when the broker path cannot be cited and `BLOCKED_ROUTE` when the route is closed — none of which reveal a target refusal or block; on every failure path the target receives no event, message or count, and a request the broker never answers reaches a neutral, blameless expiry.
- **AC-COM-14 — Broker introduction:** Given The actor is the named broker on a request that is still awaiting a broker decision; an acceptance may create the target invitation with minimum disclosure while the channel itself opens only on the target's own acceptance, and the broker may attach an optional note without any obligation to forward the requester's pitch or to warrant either party, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Broker accepts/declines silently; on accept opens scoped channel with optional note; no pitch forwarding obligation, and (6) return Channel invitation and disclosure evidence commit; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` for an actor who is not the named broker, `VALIDATION_FAILED` when the request has already expired, been revoked or been answered and `BLOCKED_ROUTE` when a block or revocation closes the route at any stage; a decline or an ignored request stays reasonless and blameless to the requester, a target that does not accept leaves the channel unopened, and no failure explains which control fired.
- **AC-COM-15 — Evaluate reachability:** Given Both a sender and a target party resolve, and the evaluation reads the target's current `reachability_policy_version` together with block and Shard 06 restriction state, compliance and minor policy, graph density and the sender's class; the verdict is computed before any route is offered to the sender, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Sender-target policy resolves direct, intro-required or unavailable using density/compliance/block context, and (6) return Safe route returned without exposing refusal reason; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when the caller may not evaluate for this sender and `BLOCKED_ROUTE` when the route is closed — both surfaced to the sender as one unified unavailable state that reveals no block, refusal, reason or count; where the graph is too sparse for a citable broker path reachability stays permissive under the approved density rule rather than fabricating a warm path, and a policy input that cannot be read is labelled unknown or degraded rather than silently resolving to `direct`.
- **AC-COM-16 — Create/reconcile shadow contact:** Given The actor is the CRM owner acting for itself and the record is scoped to that owner person and party; a reconciliation additionally requires the owner to confirm an explicit canonical target, because there is no email or name auto-merge, no cross-owner deduplication and no subject-visible step at any point, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Owner records off-platform person privately and may later confirm reconciliation; never automatic or subject-visible, and (6) return Owner-scoped record/merge provenance commits; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when an actor reaches for another owner's CRM, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for a reconciliation without an owner-confirmed canonical target and `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a competing record edit; a failed reconciliation leaves the contact private with its reminders, tags and notes still pointing at the shadow record because the re-point is one owner transaction, two owners' records for the same human never merge or co-reference, and the subject receives no signal on any path.
- **AC-COM-17 — Add private note/tag/list:** Given The actor is the CRM owner acting for itself on its own contact record, the content is inside its bounded length, and it passes B5 content validation, which prohibits special-category data and unverified allegations until a narrower policy is counsel-approved; storage is owner-scoped and encrypted by construction, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Owner stores bounded private relationship context; prohibited content validation applies; no shared computation reads it, and (6) return Encrypted owner-only version commits; if the flow cannot complete, `CRM_CONTENT_PROHIBITED` when B5 validation rejects special-category data or an unverified allegation, routed to the content-policy explanation and never indexed, logged or shared as raw note content, `FORBIDDEN` when an actor reaches for another owner's CRM and `VALIDATION_FAILED` when the content exceeds its bounds; a rejected note leaves no shared artifact because notes are non-shareable by schema and API and are structurally excluded from feed, search, ranking, endorsement, referral and safety inputs, and logs carry only opaque record IDs and policy outcomes.
- **AC-COM-18 — Schedule follow-up:** Given The actor is the reminder's author acting for itself, the reminder is tied to a contact inside that owner's own CRM, and a due time or recurrence is supplied; delivery targets the author alone, so no consent, relationship or reachability check against the contact applies, when the actor invokes this flow, then the system MUST (1) validate inputs, (2) authenticate and resolve acting context, (3) authorize, (4) enforce revision and idempotency, (5) Owner creates private reminder tied to contact; reconciliation moves reference; only author is notified, and (6) return Reminder state/delivery evidence commits; if the flow cannot complete, `FORBIDDEN` when an actor schedules against another owner's contact, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for a missing or unparseable due time or recurrence and `VERSION_CONFLICT` on a competing reminder edit; a delivery failure records the delivery evidence and leaves the reminder due for retry rather than marking it delivered, and on no path — scheduled, due, delivered, snoozed or cancelled — does the reminder signal the contact.

### Global Interaction Rules

- Commands carry `actor_person_id`, `acting_party_id`, `acting_context_version`, `idempotency_key`, `expected_version?` and `request_id`.
- Follow/connection/endorsement/collaboration edges connect explicit parties/entities; UI never unions states across acting contexts.
- Blocks and Shard 06 restrictions override follow, discovery, path, intro, call, post and contact routes without leaking existence/reason.
- Counts, candidate sets, paths and explanations are computed after visibility/suppression/block authorization.
- Ranking uses allowlisted shared evidence only. Private notes, shadow contacts, CRM tags, message content, protected traits and undisclosed availability are forbidden inputs.
- Missing graph/search inputs are labelled unknown/degraded; absence never means no relationship, no interest or poor fit.
