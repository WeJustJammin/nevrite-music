## Overview

The ways a digital good can be paid for, and how those ways interact. Music software has evolved
an unusually rich set — perpetual purchase, subscription, credit economies, rent-to-own, upgrades,
crossgrades and loyalty credit — because the products are expensive, the buyers are
capital-constrained, and vendors need recurring revenue from a catalog people buy once. Each model
changes what the entitlement (14.02.01) *is*, not merely what it costs.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: the models are not independent price tags — they interact, and the
interactions are where the real behaviour lives. Rent-to-own payments must count toward perpetual
ownership; upgrade pricing depends on what you already own; a bundle containing something you own
must discount or it insults the buyer; loyalty credit spans all of them. Five parallel pricing
features with no shared state would produce five wrong answers to "what do I pay?"

| D-01 | Candidates 17 and 18 merged into one sub-domain | Candidate 17 ("Perpetual, Subscription, Credit Economy & Rent-to-Own") and 18 ("Bundles, Sales, Upgrade/Crossgrade & Loyalty Credit") were split by the sweep along a line that does not survive contact: they share one question ("what does this buyer pay for this product?") and one state (what the buyer already owns). Keeping them apart would split the state that makes either work. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 classification |
| D-02 | Candidate 18 split into two features (14.07.04 bundles/sales, 14.07.05 upgrades/crossgrades) | Bundling is a *packaging* mechanic driven by what's in the basket; crossgrading is an *entitlement* mechanic driven by what's in the library — including a competitor's product we cannot see. Different inputs, different failure modes, different data. One node would hide the crossgrade's central problem (Q-03). | Agent, Node Classification Gate |
| D-03 | Monetisation model is a property of the **entitlement** (14.02.01), not just the listing's price field | Per 14.02.01 D-01, the entitlement record is primary. The model determines what the entitlement *is*: whether it expires, whether it converts, what version range it covers, and what happens on non-payment. A price field cannot express "12 payments then perpetual". | Agent, classification reasoning |
