## Overview

Undoing a digital sale — which is not possible. A returned amp comes back; a downloaded plugin
does not, and the buyer has it forever whatever we decide. EU and UK consumer law meets this with
a specific instrument: the buyer's 14-day withdrawal right can be **waived**, but only if they
expressly consent and acknowledge the loss *before* the download begins. That waiver moment is a
legal gate sitting directly on the delivery path, and it is the reason this is a sub-domain rather
than a refund button.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three capabilities in a chain. The waiver is captured pre-download or
the refund position is legally indefensible; adjudication decides an irreversible case using what
the buyer was actually told; revocation executes a clawback against an entitlement the buyer may
have already activated. Each is meaningless without the others.
