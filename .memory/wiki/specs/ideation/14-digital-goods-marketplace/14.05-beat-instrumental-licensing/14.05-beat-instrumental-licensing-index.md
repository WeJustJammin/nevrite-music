## Overview

Selling instrumentals to artists — the BeatStars/Airbit model: a beat listed with a player and a
cart, offered at tiered lease prices (MP3 lease, WAV lease, trackout, unlimited) and an exclusive
buyout that removes it from sale. It looks like a digital goods sale and behaves like a
**publishing deal**: what is transacted is not a file but a bundle of rights with stream caps,
term limits, and — at the exclusive tier — an actual transfer of copyright.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: four capabilities that only work in combination. The tiered catalog
is meaningless without lease lifecycle (a lease with no expiry is an unlimited licence sold at
lease prices); exclusivity is meaningless without auto-delist and a rule for existing leases; the
tagged preview is what makes the whole model safe to list publicly. Each depends on the others.

**Placement caveat (inherited from the domain index)**: the ratified map places beats here rather
than in Music Licensing (11) because they are "bought in a store with a player and a cart", while
acknowledging it is "genuinely both". Classification has strengthened the objection rather than
resolved it — see Q-01.

| Q-01 | **Does this sub-domain belong in 14 at all?** The domain index concedes it is "genuinely both" 14 and 11 (Music Licensing), placing it here on the *shopping surface* argument. But classification found that its actual objects are rights, not files: an exclusive sale transfers copyright (domain 09), a lease is a licence with stream caps (domain 11), and expiry obligates a *released record* rather than a download. The shopping surface is real but shallow — a cart is a cross-cut. Recommend the owner reconsider: it may be that 11 owns the rights model and 14 owns only the storefront. | User | `/ideate-validate` |
| Q-02 | When an exclusive is sold, do existing leases survive to their expiry? Industry practice says yes; the buyer paying for exclusivity usually believes otherwise. Whichever way it resolves, both parties must be told *before* the transaction. | User | `/ideate-validate` |
