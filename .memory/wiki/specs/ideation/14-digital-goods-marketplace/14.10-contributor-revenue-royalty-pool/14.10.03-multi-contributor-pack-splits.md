# Feature: Multi-Contributor Pack Splits & Use Consent

> **Number**: 14.10.03
> **Parent**: [14.10 Contributor Revenue & Per-Download Royalty Pool](./14.10-contributor-revenue-royalty-pool-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17
> **Origin**: **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's 24 candidates.

## Overview

Who gets paid when a pack has several makers. Candidate 20 says "contributor", singular — but a
sample pack is routinely a drummer who played it, an engineer who recorded it, and a producer who
edited and assembled it. The pool computes what a pack earned; it cannot pay a pack, only people.
Between those two facts sits a split, and if it does not exist the money goes to whoever happened
to click upload.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **The contributor this protects** — the drummer whose kit samples are in a pack a friend assembled, who currently gets a verbal "I'll sort you out" and nothing else. They propose, review, and agree their share, and they see what the pack earned so their share is verifiable rather than asserted. |
| Producer | Full | Usually the **pack owner** who assembles, uploads and proposes the split — and holds the power in the negotiation. Personas.md names their worst accidental behaviour precisely: closing a project without capturing splits because "we'll do it later". This feature exists to make the lazy path the correct path. |
| Operator | None | No column. Studios do not contribute to sample packs as a rights-holding party. Explicit absence. |
| Fan | None | Not on the fan surface. Personas.md: the Fan "will never file a split sheet". |

## Behavior

A pack carries a contributor gate with two independent records for every named contributor:
their **split** (percentage and agreement state) and their **use consent** (`pending`,
`confirmed`, or `refused`) to include and sell their contribution in this sample
pack. Both are proposed at submission (14.08.01) — while the pack is being made and everyone is
still friendly — and each contributor responds separately.

All required use consents must be `confirmed` before publish. A use refusal blocks publish;
permission is not a debt that can be held and settled later. The split is **frozen per accounting
period** (CX-02): changing it mid-period would retroactively rewrite who earned what. An unagreed
split initially holds payout, not publish. After D-07's 30-calendar-day response period, already
agreed shares pay and only the unresolved portion remains in a non-forfeitable held-funds record.

Splits produce sub-penny arithmetic at pool rates (CX-03) — a three-way split of £0.03 is where
rounding stops being a detail.

### Happy Path

1. Producer assembles a pack from a session with a drummer and an engineer.
2. At submission (14.08.01) they name contributors, propose shares (50/30/20), and ask each person to consent to the intended sample-pack use.
3. Each contributor confirms the use separately from their percentage — while the work is fresh and the relationship is good.
4. All required use consents are confirmed, so the pack can publish. If the split is still unagreed, earnings initially hold; after D-07's 30-calendar-day response period, agreed shares pay and only the unresolved portion remains held.
5. Downloads accrue (14.10.02); at period close the accrual is valued (14.10.01) and split.
6. Each contributor receives an evidentiary statement (14.10.02 D-01) and their payout.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Contributor does not answer the use-consent request | We have no permission to include their contribution in the pack | Publish stays blocked until the contributor confirms or the contribution is removed |
| Contributor confirms use but never confirms the split | The pack may publish; the percentage remains unresolved | Payout holds during D-07's 30-calendar-day response period. Then agreed contributors are paid their confirmed shares and only the unresolved portion remains in a non-forfeitable held-funds record with a claim path |
| Contributor refuses the intended use | A percentage cannot substitute for permission | Publish is blocked; the producer removes the contribution or resolves consent before resubmitting |
| Pack published with no split declared | Defaults to 100% uploader, silently | **Must not be silent.** A single-contributor pack is a legitimate 100% claim, and it must be *claimed*, not defaulted into |
| Contributor disputes their share after publish | Frozen per period; changes apply forward | Past periods stand; disputes route to 24 |
| Contributor's share is £0.004 this period | Sub-penny; rounding is a material term (CX-03) | Minimum payout thresholds and accumulation |
| Pack owner names a contributor who denies involvement or refuses the use | An attributed and permission claim about another person | The contributor is not treated as agreed; the pack cannot publish with their contribution |
| Contributor leaves the platform / account deleted | **P-11 (D-05):** their **confirmed split row survives unchanged** — it is never zeroed, redistributed among the remaining parties, or forfeited to the seller or platform. The unpayable-balance mechanics are out of scope: this file is decoupled from the pool (MoSCoW), and pool funding and download accrual are **WONT**, so per-download accrual does not exist to resolve. What remains is the direct-sale split, where a departed party's share is owed from a sale that already happened and is handled by the ordinary unpayable-balance path, not by altering the split. | The split still shows their row and share; the balance shows as unpayable pending a claim, never silently reassigned |
| A pack contains samples from *another* pack | The upstream vendor is arguably a contributor whose share nobody declared — the split covers only the assembler's named parties, so the pool would pay the derivative pack in full while the source vendor earns nothing (14.02.05 DT-03's chain, arriving where there is money to divide) | At submission the assembler is prompted to declare upstream-pack provenance; a declared source vendor is surfaced as a proposed contributor on the split, subject to the same confirmation flow. Whether an unlicensed-for-resale upstream sample blocks payout entirely is a licensing-terms question — `[PENDING — /write-be-spec: chain-of-rights resolution against 14.02.05 terms registry]`. Recommend the split *reads* 02/09 machinery (DT-03) rather than re-deriving the chain here. Tracked as Q-02 |
| Split proposed after the pack is earning | Late splits are exactly what the platform exists to prevent | Allowed, but the friction should be visible — it is the reconstruction failure in miniature |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Split fetch | Skeleton |
| Empty | No split declared | Submission gate: "Who made this? Claim 100% or add contributors" — never a silent default |
| Populated | Required use consents and split agreed | Named parties, shares, split agreement dates, use-consent evidence |
| Error | Contributor-gate fetch failed | Retry; no consent state is assumed. A known unagreed split alone holds payout, not publish. |
| Partial | Use consents confirmed; split awaits confirmations | "2 of 3 shares confirmed — the pack may publish; earnings hold for 30 days, then confirmed shares pay while the unresolved portion remains held" |
| Blocked | A required use consent is pending or refused | "Publish blocked — every contributor must approve this sample-pack use" |
| Frozen | Period closed | The period's split is immutable; edits apply forward only |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A contributor is a person — the pack's uploader | Candidate 20's own wording ("Per-Download Royalty Pool for **Contributor**s" with a single payee implied), and how every sample store models it | ❌ REJECTED — **packs are made by several people, and every store's single-payee model is precisely why session players get nothing from them.** A drum library is a drummer who played it, an engineer who recorded it, and a producer who edited and assembled it. Splice pays the account that uploaded; the drummer gets a verbal "I'll sort you out" and, usually, nothing — because the split was never written down and the platform never asked. That is *the exact failure this entire project exists to fix* (D-18: facts of the work never recorded, reconstructed later from memory, contested when valuable), reproduced inside our own marketplace, on money we ourselves are distributing. Building a pool with a single payee would mean WeJammin becoming the thing it condemns while holding the money. And the fix is nearly free here: the pack is being assembled *on the platform*, at submission, while everyone is present and friendly — the exact conditions personas.md says the Producer needs, and the exact moment they otherwise skip. |
| DT-02 | Splits should be optional so vendors aren't blocked from publishing | The pragmatic instinct — friction at submission loses supply, and supply is the store's whole problem (14.08.01 DT-02) | ❌ REJECTED — and the resolution is the sharpest available statement of what "capture at source" means. Optional splits will not be used: personas.md names the Producer's worst accidental behaviour as *"closes a project without capturing splits ('we'll do it later') — the exact failure the platform exists to prevent, committed by the persona best placed to prevent it"*, and concludes that **the design must make the lazy path the correct path**. Optional makes the lazy path the wrong one. But *blocking publish* is the wrong lever too — it costs supply the store cannot afford, and it punishes a solo creator for the multi-contributor case. The right lever is subtler: **publish freely, block the payout**. Money is the only thing that reliably makes people finish paperwork, it arrives after the pack is already live and earning (so no supply is lost), and it means the uncomfortable conversation happens once, early, with a number attached. Note what this does *not* fix — a pack that never earns never gets a split, so the record we most want is captured only where money appeared. That is an honest limit of the mechanism, worth naming rather than papering over. |
| DT-03 | This is a marketplace payout feature | Its position in domain 14, under a royalty pool, next to a rate model | ❌ REJECTED — **it is a split sheet, and the platform already has one.** The object here is identical to what domains 02 (Credits) and 09 (Rights) exist to hold: named people, agreed percentages, a work, captured at creation, timestamped, evidentiary. Building a second, parallel split system inside a marketplace would mean the drummer's 30% of a sample pack lives somewhere entirely different from their 30% of a record — two vocabularies, two agreement flows, two sources of truth about the same human's income, and no way to answer "what am I owed" in one place (which is domain 23's whole job). This should almost certainly **read** 02/09's split machinery rather than define its own. It also lands the strongest strategic point in the domain: the reason WeJammin can do multi-contributor packs and Splice cannot is that we are already where the work happened and already hold the credits — D-18's causal link, arriving in a marketplace nobody expected it to touch. Escalated as Q-03: the boundary is an owner's call, but a second split system is a mistake either way. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The contributor gate is proposed at submission; each named contributor separately confirms their split and intended use | Capture while the work is fresh and everyone is friendly — the platform's founding argument, applied to both money and permission. |
| D-02 | Publish is **not** blocked on an unagreed split; payout initially holds, then D-07 pays confirmed shares and holds only the unresolved portion | Per DT-02 and owner ratification B1. Blocking publish costs supply; an initial hold makes the paperwork visible without allowing one non-responder to freeze everyone else's earned money. This does not override the separate use-consent gate in D-06. Honest limit: a pack that never earns never gets a split. |
| D-03 | A single-contributor pack must **claim** 100%, never default to it silently | A silent default is how the drummer gets nothing without anyone deciding that they should. |
| D-04 | The split is frozen per accounting period; edits apply forward only | Per CX-02. Retroactively rewriting a closed period is the reconstruction failure with our own money. |
| D-05 | **P-11:** a departed or erased contributor's **confirmed split row survives unchanged** — never zeroed, redistributed, or forfeited | Redistribution and forfeiture are both foreclosed by locked decisions elsewhere: `09.02.04` D-14 ("an amendment **cannot produce a 0% row**; zeroing a party is removal-by-agreement") makes silent reassignment the removal-without-consent loophole, and `10.04.03` D-01 with `royalties-collections-index.md` D-09 forbid unpayable money becoming platform float, revenue or a rounding sink. **Scope note:** the P-11 question as filed asked about an *accruing* share, but pool funding (`14.10.01`) and download attribution/accrual (`14.10.02`) are **WONT** — per-download accrual is not being built, and this file is explicitly decoupled from the pool. So there is no accruing balance to escrow; what exists is a direct-sale share already owed, which the ordinary unpayable-balance path handles without touching the split. GDPR erasure versus payout retention is a separate security decision (Q-04), not a split-policy question. |
| D-06 | A contributor's **use consent** is separate from the split. Every named contributor must confirm the intended sample-pack use before publish; refusal blocks publish. | Per 14.04.01 D-10. A percentage answers who receives money; it cannot grant permission to sell a contribution. A pending or refused use-consent state therefore blocks publication, while an unagreed split remains a payout-only hold under D-02. |
| D-07 | After **30 calendar days from the recorded split request**, pay each already agreed share and retain only the unresolved portion in a non-forfeitable held-funds record with a claim path | Owner ratification B1 (2026-08-02). Silence never creates consent, a replacement split, forfeiture, redistribution, or platform revenue. The response period concerns interim payout only; the proposal and evidence remain durable. |
