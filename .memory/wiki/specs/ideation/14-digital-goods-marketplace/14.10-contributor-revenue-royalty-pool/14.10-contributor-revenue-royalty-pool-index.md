## Overview

Paying the people whose sounds get used, when nobody bought their pack. Under a subscription and
credit model (14.07.02) the buyer pays a monthly fee and spends credits on individual sounds —
so there is no sale to attribute revenue to. Instead, a pool funded by subscription revenue is
distributed across contributors according to what was downloaded. It is the Splice model, and it
is a **royalty system** wearing a marketplace's clothes.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three capabilities in a chain that only works whole. The pool's
funding and rate model decides what a download is worth; attribution decides whose download it
was; and — the part nobody in the candidate list noticed — a pack made by three people needs a
split before any of it can be paid out. Without the split, the pool computes a correct amount and
sends it to the wrong person.

## Open Questions

| # | Question | Owner | Deferred To |
|---|----------|-------|-------------|
| Q-01 | **Does this sub-domain exist at all?** It is entirely downstream of 14.07.02 (subscription & credits). If the store sells packs perpetually — which is what creator content sells as, and what index Q-01 of 14.07 recommends at launch — there is no pool, because every download already has a sale attached. This whole sub-domain is contingent on a monetisation decision nobody has taken. | User | `/ideate-validate` |
| Q-02 | Per-download rate: fixed, or a share of pool revenue divided by total downloads? Fixed is predictable for contributors and an unbounded liability for the platform; pro-rata is safe for the platform and means a contributor's earnings fall when *other* packs get popular. | User | `/ideate-validate` |
| Q-03 | Per 14.07 Q-03: personas.md says the Musician "cannot afford per-tool subscriptions across six products". If the platform declines to sell subscriptions on those grounds, this sub-domain has no funding source and does not exist. | User | `/ideate-validate` |
