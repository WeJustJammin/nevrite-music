## Overview

Getting the bytes to the buyer and keeping them reachable forever. Digital music goods are
unusually hostile to naive delivery: a sample library is routinely 40–100 GB, an update can break
a session that was mixed against the old build, and a buyer who bought a plugin in 2019 expects
the 2019 version to still be downloadable in 2029 because their project needs *that* build. This
sub-domain owns transfer, versions, the permanent library, and the watermark stamped on the way out.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: five capabilities over one lifecycle. Delivery moves bytes;
versioning decides *which* bytes and is the reason the archive exists; the library is the
permanent index of what you may fetch; asset organisation is what makes 100 GB of loops usable;
watermarking is a delivery-time operation that only exists because delivery is the last point of
control.
