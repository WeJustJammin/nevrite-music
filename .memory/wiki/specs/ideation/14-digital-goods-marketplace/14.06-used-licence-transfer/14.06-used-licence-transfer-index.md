# Used Licence Transfer & Resale — Index

> **Level**: sub-domain

> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

A second-hand market for software licences. The owner's directive for gear (D-05) says "new **and
used**"; applied to digital goods that means a producer who no longer uses a plugin should be able
to sell their licence to someone who does. The market wants this badly — it is the single most
requested and least served thing in plugin retail — and it is nearly impossible, because whether a
licence *may* be transferred is decided by the vendor, differently for every vendor, and often not
at all.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three capabilities that are useless apart. The policy registry
decides whether a transfer is permitted at all; the escrow makes a transfer between two strangers
safe; the hardware-bundle case is a genuinely different transaction that spans domain 13. Without
the registry the escrow brokers void transfers; without the escrow the registry is a database
nobody acts on.

**Domain index framing**: this is called out as "the sharpest wedge here" and "technically
impossible without a per-vendor transfer-policy registry". Classification agrees with the second
half and has doubts about the first — see Q-01.

| D-02 | Kept in domain 14 rather than routed to 13, despite 14.06.03 spanning both | The transferable object is a *licence* (14.02.01), and every hard part — policy, escrow, revocation, re-issuance — is licence machinery. Domain 13 owns the hardware listing and triggers the bundle; 14 owns the licence transfer. Recorded as a cross-domain touchpoint rather than a shared node. | Agent, Node Classification Gate |
| D-03 | Transfer is modelled as a **change of holder on an existing entitlement**, not as a re-sale creating a new one | Per 14.02.01 D-01, the entitlement record is primary. A transfer that mints a fresh entitlement loses the licence's history — which version range it covers, what terms it was bound under (14.02 CX-02), what it originally cost. The buyer of a used licence inherits the seller's bound terms, and that inheritance is the entire legal basis of the transaction. | Agent, classification reasoning |
