# Feature: Offline Activation & Third-Party DRM Bridges

> **Number**: 14.02.03
> **Parent**: [14.02 Licensing, Activation & Entitlement](./14.02-licensing-activation-entitlement-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Two escape hatches from the assumption that the buyer's machine can reach WeJammin. **Offline
activation** is a challenge/response flow for machines that are deliberately or unavoidably
disconnected — the air-gapped studio, the mix room with no wifi, the touring rig on a hotel
connection. **DRM bridges** are integrations with the licence systems the industry already runs
on (PACE/iLok, Steinberg, Native Access, Waves), where WeJammin does not enforce the licence at
all but resells someone else's.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Rarely needs offline activation (a laptop is usually online) and may already hold an iLok without knowing what it is. Mostly experiences this feature as "the code went to my iLok instead of here", which is confusing unless explained at purchase. |
| Producer | Full | **The persona this exists for.** Personas.md is explicit: their work happens inside a DAW, not a browser. A commercial mix room may be off the network by policy; an AAX plugin for Pro Tools is PACE-managed by industry default. They generate the challenge on the offline machine, carry it to a connected device, and return the response. |
| Operator | Full | Air-gapped or restricted-network rooms are a real studio configuration (client confidentiality, stability). Needs offline activation per room and needs it to survive staff who don't understand it. |
| Fan | None | Not on the fan surface. No software, no activation, no iLok. |

## Behavior

**Offline activation**: the machine emits a challenge (fingerprint + entitlement reference); the
holder carries it to any connected device; WeJammin returns a signed response valid only for that
machine; the machine consumes it. A seat is spent (CX-01) exactly as in the online path — the
seat ledger cannot fork.

**DRM bridges**: for products managed by a third-party system, issuance (14.02.01) deposits an
activation code into that system rather than minting an enforceable WeJammin licence. WeJammin
becomes a **reseller**, not an authority — with the consequences recorded in CX-05 and R-02:
blacklisting (14.02.04) does not reach these products, and revocation (14.09.03) requires the
third party's cooperation.

### Happy Path — Offline Activation

1. Holder installs on an offline machine; requests offline activation.
2. Machine displays/exports a challenge (a code or file).
3. Holder opens WeJammin on a connected device, enters the challenge against the entitlement.
4. System validates seats, binds the fingerprint, returns a signed response.
5. Holder carries the response back; the machine consumes it and authorises.

### Happy Path — DRM Bridge

1. Buyer purchases a bridged product; checkout states clearly that the licence will be deposited into iLok.
2. Buyer supplies (or links) their third-party account identifier.
3. On payment, WeJammin deposits the code with the third party.
4. Buyer manages the licence in that system from then on — WeJammin's library (14.03.03) shows it as externally managed.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Buyer has no iLok account for a PACE-managed product | Purchase must not complete into a void | "This product needs a free iLok account — create one, then link it" *before* payment |
| Offline response carried to the wrong machine | Response is fingerprint-bound; rejected | "This authorisation was issued for a different computer" |
| Offline machine's seat can never be self-released (it can't call home) | Stranded seat, worse than the online case | Self-service reclaim (14.02.02 D-02) must cover offline machines — an offline seat with no reclaim is a permanent loss |
| Third-party DRM provider is down at purchase | Payment taken, deposit fails | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — the payment/deposit atomicity problem, harder than 14.02.01's because the second system isn't ours |
| Refund of a bridged product (14.09) | WeJammin requests revocation from the third party; the request's outcome does not gate the money | **RESOLVED — see Q-02.** The refund is granted where the statutory floor requires it: "a failed clawback must never hold the buyer's money hostage" (14.09.02 D-03). Only the discretionary policy above the floor may be stricter for bridged products, and it must be disclosed pre-purchase (D-01) |
| Third-party account linked to a different person than the buyer | Effectively an undeclared gift/transfer, bypassing 14.06 — and the one path that can hand a licence to a third party without the platform recording the divergence | Open by nature, now tracked rather than deferred to a closed stage. Settled already: purchaser and holder are separate principals (14.02.01 D-05), so the divergence is expressible; at launch, absent a gift flow, "checkout sets holder = purchaser and no gift path is offered" (14.02.01 gift edge case); and a deposit is irreversible in a system WeJammin does not control (D-01), so whichever rule is chosen has to bite **before** the deposit, not after. Still open: whether depositing into a third party's account is permitted, blocked at checkout, or treated as a transfer under 14.06.01's policy registry. Deferral re-pointed from the completed `/ideate-discover` Step 5 to `/create-prd` — Q-04 below |

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|

| Empty | Bridged product, no external account linked | "Link your iLok account to receive this licence" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A DRM bridge is an integration — a connector to build | The candidate's framing ("Third-Party DRM Bridges"), which reads as an engineering task | ❌ REJECTED — it is a **commercial relationship wearing an integration's costume**, and that reframing changes its MoSCoW entirely. PACE does not expose a self-serve reseller API to anyone who wants one; becoming an authorised iLok reseller is a business-development negotiation with revenue terms, not a sprint. This lands on the same rock as 14.01.01 DT-02: the products that need iLok are third-party commercial plugins from vendors who have no reason to list on a new store, so bridging is *only* needed for inventory the store cannot obtain at launch anyway. The two facts compound — this cannot be built early and does not need to be. Proposed `wont` for now, and the reasoning should be recorded so it isn't rediscovered as a surprise blocker later. |
| DT-02 | Offline activation is a niche edge case for a handful of paranoid studios | The volume intuition — most machines are online | ❌ REJECTED — it is a **direct consequence of the personas as written**, not an edge case. The Producer's defining constraint is that work happens in a DAW in a room; the Operator's inventory *is* rooms. Commercial mix rooms run off-network for stability and client confidentiality routinely. But the sharper point: offline activation and the fail-open/fail-closed question (14.02.02 DT-01) are the **same decision viewed twice**. If activation fails open with a generous grace window, offline activation is nearly unnecessary — the machine simply never checks in and keeps working. If it fails closed, offline activation becomes mandatory infrastructure. So this feature's existence is downstream of 14.02.02 Q-01, and the two must be decided together rather than separately. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Bridged products are marked as externally managed throughout the buyer's library and at checkout | Per DT-01/CX-05 — the buyer must know before paying that refunds, transfers and revocation may not behave like a normal WeJammin purchase. Silently selling a different product shape is a dispute generator. |
| D-02 | Offline activation spends a seat through the same ledger as online activation | The seat ledger cannot fork. Two counters for one allowance is an arithmetic bug waiting to happen. |
