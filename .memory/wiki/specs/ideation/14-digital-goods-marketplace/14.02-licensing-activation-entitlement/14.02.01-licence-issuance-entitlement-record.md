# Feature: Licence Issuance & Entitlement Record

> **Number**: 14.02.01
> **Parent**: [14.02 Licensing, Activation & Entitlement](./14.02-licensing-activation-entitlement-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The record of what a buyer acquired — created at purchase, and the object every other feature in
this sub-domain reads or mutates. It is the thing that makes a digital sale different from a gear
sale: buying a cable transfers an object, buying a plugin creates an *ongoing relationship* with
a stated scope, a seat allowance, bound terms, and a revocability that a cable does not have.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Holds entitlements for everything they've bought; sees them in the library (14.03.03). As a vendor of their own packs, their sales *mint* entitlements they can see in aggregate but not individually inspect (a buyer's licence is not the vendor's business beyond its state). |
| Producer | Full | Same as Musician but at volume, and the persona most affected by the record's scope fields — a producer needs to know whether a pack's entitlement permits commercial release before they use it on a paid client's record. |
| Operator | Full | Entitlements bought by a studio should belong to the **studio**, not the engineer who clicked buy — the engineer leaves, the plugins stay. This is unresolved (sub-domain Q-03) and is a real blocker for the Operator persona rather than a nicety. |
| Fan | Read-only | The only touchpoint: music bought from an artist page (domain 20) also mints an entitlement, because the delivery/library machinery is shared (see domain index). The fan never sees the word "entitlement" — they see "my purchases". No store access, no seats, no terms UI. |

## Behavior

At successful payment, an entitlement is minted binding: buyer (or org — Q-03), product, version
range covered, monetisation model (14.07), seat allowance, terms version (14.02.05, frozen per
CX-02), and state (`active` / `expired` / `revoked` / `suspended`). Issuance is atomic with the
payment cross-cut — a payment without an entitlement is a support ticket, an entitlement without
a payment is theft.

For products bridged to third-party DRM (14.02.03), issuance instead deposits a code into that
system and the local record becomes descriptive rather than authoritative (CX-05).

### Happy Path

1. Buyer completes checkout via the platform cart/payments cross-cut.
2. Payment confirms; issuance mints the entitlement in the same transaction.
3. The terms version displayed at checkout is frozen onto the record (CX-02).
4. Seat allowance is set from the product's monetisation model.
5. Entitlement appears in the buyer's library (14.03.03); downloads unlock (14.03.01) — gated on the withdrawal waiver (14.09.01).
6. If the product needs activation, a serial/authorisation is available to the holder.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Payment succeeds, issuance fails | Must not leave a paid buyer with nothing; issuance retried, or payment reversed. Never silently drop. | "Purchase confirmed — preparing your licence" then resolution; never a blank library |
| Buyer already owns the product | Duplicate purchase blocked before payment, or converted to a gift/seat top-up | "You already own this — add seats instead?" |
| Buyer buys during a trial (CX-06) | Existing trial entitlement upgraded in place, not duplicated | Nothing changes in their DAW — it just stops expiring |
| Purchase for an org where buyer is an individual | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — blocked on Q-03 | `[PENDING]` |
| Buyer's account is deleted under GDPR erasure | Entitlement is a contractual obligation to a vendor; erasing it destroys the vendor's sale record and the buyer's rights proof | `[PENDING — /create-prd-security]` — sub-domain Q-02, genuinely unresolved |
| Gift purchase (buyer ≠ holder) | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` | `[PENDING]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Library fetch | Skeleton rows |
| Empty | No purchases | Library empty state with store CTA |
| Populated | Entitlements exist | List with product, model, seats used/total, terms link |
| Error | Fetch failed | Retry; cached list if available — a buyer must never see "you own nothing" on a transient error |
| Partial | Issued but bridged to external DRM | Record shows "managed in iLok" rather than a serial we control |
| Revoked | Refund (14.09.03) or blacklist (14.02.04) | Entitlement shown as revoked with reason and appeal route |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | An entitlement is a licence *key* — a string the buyer copies into a plugin | The candidate's framing ("Licence Issuance, Delivery"); how a 2005 software store worked | ❌ REJECTED — the key is an *artifact* of one enforcement strategy, not the thing being sold. Most of this store's realistic inventory (DT-02 in 14.01.01: sample packs, presets, beats, templates) has **no key at all** — there is nothing to type a serial into; a WAV is a WAV. Yet those purchases absolutely have an entitlement, and it is the most consequential one on the platform because its *terms* govern whether a released record is clearable (14.02.05). Modelling entitlement as key makes the key-less majority of the catalog unmodellable and quietly drops the domain's only link to the thesis. The record is primary; the key, where one exists, hangs off it. |
| DT-02 | An entitlement belongs to a person | The default; every consumer store does this | ⏳ DEFERRED — escalated to sub-domain Q-03. A domain expert would immediately object: **studios buy plugins, and studios are not people.** The Operator persona's whole economic unit is a business whose tools must outlive its staff; personas.md also establishes bands/labels/studios as first-class *entities* (domain 01), not sugar over a user account. A person-only entitlement means every studio purchase is held hostage by whichever engineer's account it landed in — the exact "proof evaporates when the person moves on" failure the project exists to fix, reproduced in the commerce layer. Not an agent call (it's architecture with product consequences); routed to `/create-prd-architecture` with a strong recommendation that entitlements bind to a principal that may be a person **or** an org. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The entitlement record is primary; the licence key is an optional artifact of it | Per DT-01. Most realistic inventory has no key; a key-centric model cannot represent it. |
| D-02 | Issuance is atomic with payment capture | A payment without an entitlement, or vice versa, are both unacceptable failures with no clean recovery. |
| D-03 | Terms version is frozen onto the record at issuance, not referenced live | Per CX-02 — the buyer's rights cannot be retroactively edited by the vendor. Also a legal requirement, not just a design choice. |
