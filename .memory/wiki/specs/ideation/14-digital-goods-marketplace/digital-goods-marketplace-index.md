## Overview

Multi-vendor sale and delivery of DAW plugins, sample packs, presets, beats, MIDI, stems and project templates — with licensing, activation, compatibility, updates, and the buyer's permanent library.

**Why this is a top-level domain**: Explicit owner directive (D-06: 'digital plugins for DAWs'). Shares a checkout with physical gear and essentially nothing else. Everything below the cart differs: no shipping but licence keys and machine seats; no condition but a format×OS×DAW×host-dependency matrix; no inventory limit but piracy, revocation, code signing and malware; refunds that cannot be un-given and collide with EU withdrawal law. Plugin Boutique, Splice, ADSR and Loopmasters exist as separate businesses from Reverb for exactly this reason. The used-licence transfer market the owner's 'new or used' directive implies is the sharpest wedge here and is technically impossible without a per-vendor transfer-policy registry. Known seam: Fanbase & D2F's digital music sales reuse this domain's download/library delivery machinery. The split is by destination per the map's own principle — a producer buying a sample pack is in the store; a fan buying an album is on the artist's page — and the shared delivery layer is an acknowledged dependency rather than a duplicated build. Beat licensing is placed here (bought in a store with a player and a cart) rather than in Music Licensing; see coverage notes — it is genuinely both.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies domain status):

- digital listings & compatibility matrix
- licence issuance, activation & seats
- download/version delivery & library
- sample/preset/beat catalogs with musical metadata
- vendor build submission & QA
- monetisation models (perpetual, subscription, credits, rent-to-own)
