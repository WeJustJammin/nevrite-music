## Overview

The catalogs whose product is **content rather than software** — sample and loop packs, preset
and patch banks, and DAW project templates. They differ from a plugin listing on every axis that
matters: they have musical metadata (BPM, key, instrument) instead of a format matrix, they are
auditioned rather than trialled, they are usually key-less (nothing to activate), and they depend
on *other products* to be usable at all — a Serum bank without Serum is a folder of noise.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three catalogs plus the index that makes them findable, and they
genuinely interact — a project template's manifest references sample packs and presets in the same
catalog, a preset bank depends on a plugin via the same dependency graph, and all three are
searched with one musical vocabulary. Treating them as three unrelated listings loses the thing
that makes them a store rather than three stores.

**Strategic note (see 14.01.01 DT-02)**: this sub-domain is the store's *plausible* inventory.
Commercial plugin vendors require a business-development motion WeJammin cannot run at launch;
sample packs, presets, beats and templates are made by the platform's own users, who are already
here. If domain 14 ships, it probably ships as this.

Modelling it as a per-catalog property means building it three times and never searching across them. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` |
