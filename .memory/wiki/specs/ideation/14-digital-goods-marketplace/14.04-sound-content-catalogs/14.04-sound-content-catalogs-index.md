## Overview

The catalogs whose product is **content rather than software** — sample and loop packs, preset
and patch banks, and DAW project templates. They differ from a plugin listing on every axis that
matters: they have musical metadata (BPM, key, instrument) instead of a format matrix, they are
auditioned rather than trialled, they are usually key-less (nothing to activate), and they depend
on *other products* to be usable at all — a Serum bank without Serum is a folder of noise.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three catalogs plus the index that makes them findable, and they
genuinely interact — a project template's manifest references sample packs and presets in the same
catalog, a preset bank depends on a plugin via the same dependency graph, and all three are
searched with one musical vocabulary. Treating them as three unrelated listings loses the thing
that makes them a store rather than three stores.

**Strategic note (see 14.01.01 DT-02)**: this sub-domain is the store's *plausible* inventory.
Commercial plugin vendors require a business-development motion WeJammin cannot run at launch;
sample packs, presets, beats and templates are made by the platform's own users, who are already
here. If domain 14 ships, it probably ships as this.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |

| D-01 | Candidates 10, 11, 12 grouped as one sub-domain rather than three features under the domain | They interact through the dependency graph (a template requires the presets and packs it was built with) and share one musical vocabulary. Splitting them across the domain root would leave three catalogs with no stated relationship and no shared index — three stores in a trenchcoat. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 classification |
| D-02 | Separated from 14.01 (Catalog & Compatibility) rather than merged | 14.01 answers "will this run on my machine" for any product. This sub-domain answers "what does this sound like and will it fit my track" for content. Different question, different metadata, different buyer moment. A WAV pack has no format matrix; a compressor has no key. | Agent, Node Classification Gate |
| D-03 | 14.04.04 (Musical Metadata Index) added — not in the candidate list | Candidate 10 names BPM/key as a *property of sample packs*. But the same vocabulary indexes presets (by synth, character), templates (by genre, tempo), and — critically — the buyer's own asset library (14.03.04). One index, four scopes. Modelling it as a per-catalog property means building it three times and never searching across them. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` |
