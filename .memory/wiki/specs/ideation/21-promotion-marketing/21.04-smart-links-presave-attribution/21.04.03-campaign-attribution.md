# Feature: Campaign Attribution

> **Number**: 21.04.03
> **Parent**: [Smart Links, Pre-Save & Attribution](./21.04-smart-links-presave-attribution-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Which campaign, which channel, which post brought this person here. The honest answer stops at the
click — everything past it happens inside a DSP that reports nothing back at that resolution.
This feature's design problem is not measurement; it is refusing to manufacture the causal story
that every competing product cheerfully sells.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Tags links per channel/campaign; sees clicks by source, honestly, with the uncertainty visible rather than smoothed away; sees where the observable chain **ends** and inference begins, stated plainly. Makes real spending decisions on this, which is precisely why it must not flatter. |
| Producer | None | Not their artefact. |
| Operator | None | Ticket-sale attribution — the one case in this platform where the conversion *is* observable, because we would own the transaction — lives in 21.08.03. |
| Fan | None | Not a participant, and their click being tracked is a privacy fact rather than a neutral one. Under D-13, consumer-scale fan traffic brings statutory duties; a click log is personal data. |

## Behavior

**What we can honestly observe**: the click on our own link, its source tag, its rough geography,
and — if it happens on our surface — the pre-save authorisation. That is first-party data about
our own property and it is genuinely ours.

**What we cannot observe**: whether that person streamed the track, how many times, whether they
came back, whether they bought a ticket six months later. Those events happen inside Spotify,
Apple and Meta, none of whom return click-level provenance to anybody. Post-ATT and post-cookie,
even the ad-platform side of the chain is modelled rather than measured.

**So the design principle is that the report must show where the truth stops.** A funnel diagram
running click → stream → fan is a drawing of something nobody has ever measured. Every incumbent
draws it anyway.

**The uncomfortable consequence**: this feature is honest and therefore looks worse than every
competitor's. That is a product risk and the owner should know it (Q-01), because "our analytics
are less impressive because they are true" is a hard sell that only pays off after someone has
been burned.

### Happy Path

1. Musician creates links per channel with source tags — announce email, Instagram bio, the paid ad, the premiere article.
2. Links are shared; clicks arrive and are attributed to their source inside the campaign's window.
3. Musician sees clicks by source, with bot/invalid traffic filtered and the filtering disclosed.
4. The report states plainly where observation ends: "Post-click activity happens on the DSP and is not reported back. Stream figures below are DSP-reported totals, correlated in time, not attributed."
5. Musician draws their own conclusion — which is a better conclusion than a fabricated one.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Musician asks "did the ad drive streams?" | Nobody can answer this. The honest answer is a correlation with its confounders named | "Streams rose 40% in the week the ad ran. The release also came out that week. We cannot separate these." — this is more useful than a confident false number, and it will feel like less |
| Same fan clicks from three sources | Multi-touch attribution is a modelling choice presented as a fact | Show the touches; do not invent a credit-allocation model and present its output as measurement |
| iOS/ATT and cookie deprecation break the ad-platform chain | Ad platforms report modelled conversions, not measured ones | Never re-present a modelled number as measured. If the source is a model, say so |
| DSP reports totals but not provenance | The universal case | Correlate in time; label it correlation; never join it to a click |
| Bots inflate clicks | The only first-party number we have becomes untrue | Filter, and disclose the filtering — an unexplained drop in "real" clicks reads as a bug |
| Attribution window closes; clicks keep arriving | A live link two years on still logs clicks | Window close is a lifecycle event (CX-04); late clicks must be neither silently counted nor silently dropped |
| Artist compares our honest numbers to an incumbent's confident ones | Ours look worse. Theirs are not better; they are less careful | The product risk. See Q-01 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| No data | Links live, no clicks | Empty state |
| Collecting | Clicks arriving | Clicks by source, in-window |
| Reported | Window closed | Final in-window figures with the observability boundary stated on the report itself |
| Out of window | Clicks after close | Recorded separately; not folded into campaign figures |
| Degraded | Filtering removed a large share | Disclosed — a silent drop looks like a bug and destroys trust in the whole report |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Attribution past the click is **not measurable by anyone**, so any product claiming click→stream causation is modelling and presenting the model as measurement | Reasoning about the actual data availability: DSPs report aggregate totals to artists and never click-level provenance; ATT and cookie deprecation broke the ad-platform side; there is no join key between "this person clicked" and "this person streamed" that anyone possesses | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it forces the design: the report must display where truth stops. This is not fastidiousness. `meta/problem-statement.md`'s entire thesis is that the industry's failure is *reconstruction presented as record* — "they are asking people to remember, and memory is contested, lossy, and self-interested." A fabricated attribution funnel is exactly that failure in a new costume: an inference presented as a fact, in a product whose reason to exist is that facts and claims are different objects. We would be committing our own root cause. |
| DT-02 | We should build multi-touch attribution because artists expect it and competitors have it | Competitive-parity instinct | ❌ REJECTED — multi-touch attribution is a *credit-allocation model*, not a measurement, and its outputs are determined by the model's assumptions rather than by anything that happened. Shipping it would mean shipping a number whose value we chose, in a UI that implies we observed it. Artists then spend real money against it. The honest alternative — show the touches, decline to allocate credit — is less satisfying and does not lie. |
| DT-03 | The **pre-save count is a vanity metric**, and the metric that would matter (pre-save → release-day stream conversion) is unobservable to us | Reasoning about what the number actually means. A pre-save is an intention recorded weeks early; conversion depends on token survival (21.04.02 DT-02), on the DSP's write succeeding, and on the fan actually listening — and we observe only the first two | ✅ CONFIRMED — with a sharp consequence for 21.04.02: the pre-save count we report should be **net of known failures**, because the gross figure includes fans who never received the record. That is the one honest improvement available on this metric, and it is available precisely because we chose to count failures instead of absorbing them. Note the pattern: the two features' honesty decisions compound. Recorded as a rejected pair in the sub-domain CX because the absence of a measurable loop is the finding. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **21.04.01** and **21.04.02** — the click and the authorisation are the only first-party events; see [CX-03](./21.04-smart-links-presave-attribution-cx.md).
- Touches **22 Analytics & Market Intelligence** ([../../22-analytics-market-intelligence/](../../22-analytics-market-intelligence/)) — the boundary: 21 owns campaign-attributed clicks, 22 owns audience and market truth. DSP-reported totals are 22's, correlated here, never joined.
- Touches **21.05 Paid Promotion** ([../21.05-paid-promotion/](../21.05-paid-promotion/)) — ad platforms report modelled conversions; the label must survive the import.
- Touches **21.08.03 Ticket-Sale Attribution** ([../21.08-event-tour-marketing/21.08.03-ticket-sale-attribution.md](../21.08-event-tour-marketing/21.08.03-ticket-sale-attribution.md)) — the one genuinely observable conversion in the platform, because we would own the transaction.
- **Not-product**: click-event pipeline, bot/invalid-traffic filtering → `/create-prd`.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Attribution is honest to the click; everything past it is labelled correlation | See DT-01. Fabricating it would commit the platform's own diagnosed root cause. |
| D-02 | No multi-touch attribution model | See DT-02. A chosen number presented as an observed one. |
| D-03 | Pre-save counts are reported net of known failures | See DT-03. The one honest improvement available, enabled by 21.04.02 D-02. |
| D-04 | Modelled numbers imported from ad platforms keep their label | A model's output re-rendered as measurement is a lie we did not even author. |
