## Overview

The campaign's public instruments: a link that routes a listener to their own DSP, a pre-save that
converts intent into a release-day action, and the attribution that tries — honestly — to say what
worked. These are the highest-frequency, most-demanded and most commodity artefacts in the whole
domain; every artist already uses one of the incumbents, which is exactly why absence here is felt
immediately.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: a pre-save is a smart link in a pre-release state
that mutates into a stream link at release; attribution rides on both; and lifecycle governs when
each one is born, changes meaning, and dies. Four capabilities that transform into each other
rather than merely coexisting.

**The boundary against domain 20** (from the domain index, and load-bearing): smart links are
**campaign instruments** with an attribution window and a lifespan. The artist microsite is
**permanent owned infrastructure** and stays in Fanbase (20). A verifier argued these are the same
artifact; they are adjacent, but a campaign artifact and a permanent home differ in lifecycle,
ownership and purpose. **Consequence**: "Link-in-Bio" from sweep candidate 07 is dropped from this
sub-domain and routed to 20 — a link-in-bio page is a permanent home, not a campaign instrument.
See Q-01.

> **Note on the Fan's access — the first real Fan presence in this domain.** Everywhere else in 21
> the Fan is the audience of an artefact. Here they are an **actor**: a pre-save is something the
> Fan *does*, authorising a third party to write to their music library, and per D-11 they are a
> first-class user rather than a CRM record. That makes their consent, their comprehension of what
> they granted, and their ability to revoke a first-class concern — not a footnote. `Fan: Full` on
> 21.04.02 means full agency **over their own authorisation**, not access to the artist's tool.
> The `👁️ Read-only` on links means they are the recipient of the artefact.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | **Link-in-bio is routed OUT** to domain 20 | The domain index's own boundary test: campaign instrument (lifespan, attribution window) vs permanent owned infrastructure. A link-in-bio page is the artist's permanent front door — it outlives every campaign and is the artist's home, which is Fanbase's definition. Keeping it here because sweep candidate 07 bundled it would have imported a permanent artefact into a sub-domain organised entirely around ephemerality. | Agent, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | The Fan is a **first-class actor** in 21.04.02, not an audience | D-11 makes fans users, not CRM records. A pre-save is a consent grant with library-write scope; treating the granting party as a passive audience is how pre-save consent got as ugly as it is across this category. | Agent, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | Attribution is modelled as **correlational and labelled as such** | See 21.04.03. Post-ATT and post-cookie, plus DSPs never exposing click-level stream provenance, mean nobody can honestly say "this ad drove this stream." Building a UI that implies causation would make us liars in the one domain where the platform's whole pitch is that facts are verified rather than claimed. | Agent, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
