## Overview

Reaching people who don't know you yet — playlist and curator pitching, press and radio outreach, release campaign planning, smart links and pre-save, paid ads and creator seeding, and coverage tracking.

**Why this is a top-level domain**: The acquisition function, distinct from Fanbase & D2F (selling to people who already care) and from Analytics (measuring). Three roles — publicist, plugger, playlist pitcher — share one shape: a contact database, a pitch, an embargo, a result, all currently living in a personal spreadsheet that walks out the door when the person leaves. The release campaign planner is the connective object: labels run this as 'the release grid' and no indie tool provides it, yet backward planning is what prevents a specific, common, unrecoverable loss (missing the DSP editorial pitch window forfeits Release Radar for the cycle). Requires explicit design against payola: paid guaranteed placement violates DSP terms and gets tracks pulled, so this must be pay-for-consideration, never pay-for-placement. Boundary against Fanbase clarified: smart links are campaign instruments with attribution windows and a lifespan; the artist microsite is permanent owned infrastructure and stays in Fanbase. A verifier argued these are the same artifact — they are adjacent, but a campaign artifact and a permanent home differ in lifecycle, ownership and purpose.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies domain status):

- release campaign planning & deadlines
- curator/press/radio pitching
- contact CRM & pitch tracking
- smart links, pre-save & attribution
- paid ads & creator seeding
- coverage capture → EPK
