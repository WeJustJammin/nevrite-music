## Overview

Reaching people who don't know you yet — playlist and curator pitching, press and radio outreach,
release campaign planning, smart links and pre-save, paid ads and creator seeding, and coverage
tracking.

**Why this is a top-level domain**: The acquisition function, distinct from Fanbase & D2F (selling
to people who already care) and from Analytics (measuring). Three roles — publicist, plugger,
playlist pitcher — share one shape: a contact database, a pitch, an embargo, a result, all
currently living in a personal spreadsheet that walks out the door when the person leaves. The
release campaign planner is the connective object: labels run this as 'the release grid' and no
indie tool provides it, yet backward planning is what prevents a specific, common, unrecoverable
loss (missing the DSP editorial pitch window forfeits Release Radar for the cycle). Requires
explicit design against payola: paid guaranteed placement violates DSP terms and gets tracks
pulled, so this must be pay-for-consideration, never pay-for-placement. Boundary against Fanbase
clarified: smart links are campaign instruments with attribution windows and a lifespan; the
artist microsite is permanent owned infrastructure and stays in Fanbase. A verifier argued these
are the same artifact — they are adjacent, but a campaign artifact and a permanent home differ in
lifecycle, ownership and purpose.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies domain status):

- release campaign planning & deadlines
- curator/press/radio pitching
- contact CRM & pitch tracking
- smart links, pre-save & attribution
- paid ads & creator seeding
- coverage capture → EPK

### What the breadth pass found

Three things materially changed the picture, and they are recorded as D-02, D-03 and D-04 below:

1. **The domain has a real thesis connection, and it is not where anyone would look.** Verified
   coverage (21.07) and the auto-populated EPK (21.09) are provenance artefacts — a press quote is
   normally an *assertion* made at the moment it is most valuable and most self-interested, which
   is `meta/problem-statement.md`'s root cause verbatim. WeJammin can verify it because it sent the
   pitch and holds the link, and can populate the EPK's credits because it was in the session.
   This is the answer to "why does a rights platform have a marketing domain?"
2. **The gatekeeper is not a persona**, and this is a structural gap, not a detail. A curator,
   journalist, radio programmer or DSP editor is none of Musician/Producer/Operator/Fan — yet the
   entire domain is built on reaching them, and the directory's value depends on their goodwill.
   See Q-01.
3. **Two candidates were mechanisms in disguise** (the embargo and paid-endorsement disclosure) and
   have been routed to cross-cuts rather than built here — where each would have been rebuilt four
   more times, inconsistently, and the inconsistency is where the failures live.

> **Reading this matrix** — the shape is a finding, not an artefact of laziness:
>
> - **Musician is Full everywhere.** Domain 21 is a Musician-lens domain end to end. Per
>   `meta/personas.md`, personas are lenses on behaviour — a Producer or Operator who pitches their
>   own record is acting in the Musician lens while they do it.
> - **Producer's `👁️` rows are narrow and real, never courtesy access.** They see the deadline that
>   binds them (21.01), the embargo they could break with one studio photo (21.02.05), coverage that
>   names them (21.07), and their credit on an EPK before it reaches press at scale (21.09). Every
>   one is a constraint or a reputational fact, never a marketing view — and per `meta/personas.md`
>   they will not open a marketing surface, so these reach them in project context.
> - **Operator is `❌ None` across the release cycle and `✅ Full` in 21.08.** Release marketing is
>   the artist's job; a venue marketing its own perishable Tuesday is a first-class marketer. This
>   asymmetry is why 21.08 is a separate sub-domain (D-02 there).
> - **Fan is `✅ Full` in 21.04** — the only place in the domain where they act rather than receive.
>   A pre-save is a consent grant with library-write scope, and D-11 makes them a user, not a
>   resource. `Full` means full agency over *their own authorisation*, not the artist's tool.
> - **Fan `❌ None` conceals real interests** in 21.05 (they are the ad target, built from their own
>   fan-graph data) and 21.08 (they are geo-retargeted). Those are access facts, not interest facts,
>   and D-13's statutory duties attach to both.
