# Feature: Campaign Press Kit (EPK) & Asset Pack

> **Number**: 21.09
> **Parent**: [Promotion & Marketing](./promotion-marketing-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The thing every pitch attaches and every gatekeeper asks for: bio, photos, the music, verified
credits, press quotes, contact. "Send me the EPK" is the standard first reply to any press pitch,
and building one is the tedious, recurring, always-out-of-date task that every artist does badly.
**This appeared on no candidate list** — a Deep Think addition — which is remarkable given the
domain's own rationale ends with "coverage capture → EPK".

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Builds a campaign EPK — mostly by *not* building it, since the credits (02), the release (12), the coverage quotes (21.07) and the assets (21.01.02) are already in the platform. Chooses what to include, shares a link, sees who opened it. Keeps versions, because the EPK for the album is not the EPK for the tour. |
| Producer | Read-only | **Verifies their own credit on it.** An EPK is where a mis-credit gets propagated to press at scale — the journalist copies it, and the wrong producer name enters the public record permanently. They can see EPKs naming them and dispute through 02. This is the second of only two genuine Producer stakes in domain 21 (with 21.07). |
| Operator | Read-only | **Venues request EPKs when booking.** This is real Operator behaviour that surprised me: the press kit is not only a press artefact — it is a booking artefact, and the Operator is a consumer of it via a shared link. See DT-03. |
| Fan | None | Not fan-facing. The public artist page (01/20) is the fan's surface; an EPK is a professional document with contact details and unreleased assets in it, and confusing the two is how unreleased music leaks. |

## Behavior

An EPK is a **campaign artefact**, not a profile — versioned, scoped to a release or a tour,
shared by link, with a lifespan. It sits on the same side of the domain index's boundary as the
smart link: campaign instrument, not permanent home. The permanent artist page belongs to 01/20.

**It writes itself, and that is the point.** Every component already exists in the platform: the
bio and photos (01), the release and the music (12), the credits (02 — *verified*, not typed), the
press quotes (21.07 — verified, with live sources), the assets (21.01.02). The artist's job
collapses from assembly to selection.

This is the domain's densest expression of the consolidation thesis. Not because an EPK builder is
clever — several exist — but because ours is populated by facts the platform captured at source
rather than fields the artist retyped from memory into a template. The credits are right because
the Producer confirmed them in the session. The quotes are real because we sent the pitch and hold
the link.

### Happy Path

1. Musician creates an EPK for the campaign.
2. It populates: bio and photos from 01, release and audio from 12, credits from 02 marked verified, press quotes from 21.07 marked verified, assets from 21.01.02.
3. Musician selects and orders — the work is curation, not data entry.
4. Shares a link with a press pitch (21.02.03) or a booking enquiry (17).
5. Recipient opens it; sees the music, the story, and credits/quotes that are checkable rather than asserted.
6. Musician sees it was opened — a weak but real signal that the pitch landed somewhere.
7. Next campaign: a new version. The old one still resolves, because it is in a journalist's inbox forever.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Credits not yet confirmed in 02 | The EPK cannot claim credits the credit graph has not confirmed — that would be manufacturing the exact thing we verify | Blocked on the unconfirmed credit, with a link to 02. Or included as **claimed**, visibly distinguished. Never silently rendered as verified |
| A credit is disputed after the EPK was sent | The wrong name is already in a journalist's inbox and possibly in print | The escaped-item problem again (21.01.04). Versioning limits the blast radius; nothing recalls it |
| EPK contains unreleased audio and the link is forwarded | Press kits leak. A journalist forwards it; the link is public; the record is out before the premiere | Embargo (21.02.05) governs the content; link controls govern the artefact. See Q-02 — this is the leak vector nobody plans for |
| Old EPK version still circulating with stale info | Journalists keep emails for years | Old versions must resolve, not 404 (same logic as 21.04.04 DT-01) — but should show they are superseded |
| Artist wants a quote we cannot verify | Their EPK, their call | Included, marked **claimed** (21.07 D-01). The distinction is the feature; hiding it would be the failure |
| Operator requests an EPK for a booking | Real behaviour, different need — they want the live show, not the record | See DT-03 and Q-01. A press EPK is a bad answer to a booking question |
| Photos are not cleared for press use | Photographer credit and licence are real obligations artists routinely ignore | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — ties to 09. A press kit distributing uncleared photos is a rights problem we would be facilitating |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **The EPK is the domain's densest thesis expression**, because it is the artefact where verified credits and verified coverage are consumed by an outsider at the moment they matter most | Reasoning about what an EPK actually is: a document of claims, assembled from memory, at the moment those claims are most valuable and most self-interested — sent to someone with no way to check any of it. That is `meta/problem-statement.md`'s root cause verbatim, in a marketing artefact. And per D-18, consolidation is what makes capture possible: we hold the credits because we were in the session, and the quotes because we sent the pitch | ✅ CONFIRMED — an EPK whose credits are verified because the Producer confirmed them in the session, and whose quotes are verified because we sent the pitch and hold the link, is an artefact no competitor can manufacture retroactively. Jaxsta cannot build it (no daily hook, no session presence). Buffer cannot build it (no credit graph). It requires having been in the room, which is exactly D-18's claim. This is the clearest single answer to "why does a rights platform have a marketing domain?" |
| DT-02 | The EPK belongs to domain 01 or 20 as part of the artist's profile | Reasonable structural instinct — it *looks* like a profile page, and the domain index already sent the microsite to 20 | ❌ REJECTED on the domain index's own boundary test: campaign instrument vs permanent infrastructure. An EPK is versioned per campaign, scoped to a release or tour, shared by link, contains unreleased material, and has a lifespan — every property of a campaign artefact and none of a permanent home. The album EPK is not the tour EPK. It is also not fan-facing: it holds contact details and unreleased assets, and conflating it with the public profile is precisely how unreleased music leaks. The permanent public page stays in 01/20; this stays here. Same test, opposite answer to link-in-bio (21.04 D-01) — which is evidence the test works. |
| DT-03 | The EPK is a **booking** artefact as much as a press artefact — venues ask for one | Reasoning about the Operator's Role Lens, which I expected to be `None` and was not. `meta/personas.md` has the Operator "advancing the show" and evaluating whether an act fits their room; the thing they ask for is a press kit | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it surprised me — but the useful part is what it reveals: a press EPK is a *bad* answer to a booking question. A promoter wants live footage, draw in this city, past venues played, tech requirements. A journalist wants the story, the record, the quotes. Same artefact name, different documents. This suggests the EPK is polymorphic (press / booking / sync), mirroring the campaign object's polymorphism (21.01 Q-02) — and the booking variant would draw on 17/18 data this domain does not own. Recorded as Q-01 rather than built, because it may belong to 17 rather than here. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The EPK is a campaign artefact and stays in 21 | See DT-02. Versioned, scoped, link-shared, has a lifespan, contains unreleased material, not fan-facing. |
| D-02 | It auto-populates from platform data; the artist's work is curation | See DT-01. The dividend of having been in the room. |
| D-03 | Unverified credits are blocked or marked claimed, never rendered as verified | Manufacturing verification inside the verification feature would be self-defeating. |
