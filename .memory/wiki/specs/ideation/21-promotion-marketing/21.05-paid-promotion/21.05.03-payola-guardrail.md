# Feature: Payola Guardrail & Paid-Consideration Policy

> **Number**: 21.05.03
> **Parent**: [Paid Promotion](./21.05-paid-promotion-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The line between buying attention and buying placement, enforced structurally rather than
explained. The domain index made this a condition of the domain's existence: "paid guaranteed
placement violates DSP terms and gets tracks pulled, so this must be pay-for-consideration, never
pay-for-placement." The penalty for crossing it lands on the artist's track, not on the seller and
not on us — which is why this cannot be left to the artist's judgement.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | **Cannot configure it — that is the definition of a guardrail.** Sees the policy stated plainly, sees *why* a specific action was blocked in concrete terms ("this is pay-for-placement; DSPs remove tracks for this"), and sees the distinction between the three things they cannot currently tell apart: buying attention, buying an endorsement, buying a slot. |
| Producer | Read-only | Bound whenever they are compensated to promote something — a plugin demo, a gear endorsement. The policy is visible to them because a rule nobody can read cannot be complied with. |
| Operator | Read-only | Bound too. A venue boosting an event post, or paid to feature a promoter's show, is inside disclosure law. Their exposure is real and currently invisible to them. |
| Fan | Read-only | **The party the disclosure half exists to protect.** They are entitled to know a post was paid for. A guardrail whose beneficiary cannot read it is a guardrail we are asking to be trusted on — and per D-11 fans are users, not a population we manage. |

## Behavior

The guardrail classifies what is being bought and rules on it:

| What is bought | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Attention (ad impression) | **Allowed** | Nobody is paid to endorse; it looks like an ad because it is one |
| Attention + endorsement (paid creator post, sponsored review) | **Allowed with mandatory disclosure** | A material connection exists; the audience must be told |
| Placement on a DSP playlist | **Blocked** | Violates DSP terms; playlists selling placement get purged and the *artist's* track can be caught in it |
| Airplay for payment | **Blocked — hardest line** | US law requires sponsorship identification for consideration given for broadcast; undisclosed pay-for-airplay carries statutory exposure, not merely contractual |
| Consideration (paying a curator to *listen*, no placement promised) | **Unresolved** | The model comparable platforms have used to survive; also the model that has drawn DSP sanction. See Q-01 |

**No override at any tier.** No payment, no seniority, no support ticket lifts a payola block. An
override would be an override with a guardrail-shaped label.

**A block must explain itself concretely.** A bare denial reads as arbitrary and sends the artist
to buy the same thing off-platform — which is the exact harm, achieved by our own opacity. The
explanation is the intervention.

### Happy Path

1. Musician attempts a spend (ad, seeding, playlist).
2. Guardrail classifies what is being bought, synchronously, before money moves.
3. Attention → allowed. Endorsement → allowed, disclosure requirement attached and non-removable. Placement → blocked with a concrete reason.
4. Allowed actions proceed with their obligations attached.
5. Blocked actions get a specific explanation — what this is, why DSPs sanction it, what the consequence to *them* would be.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| The same offer appears as a **service listing in domain 05** | The guardrail's back door (D-03, sub-domain index). Nothing in 05's listing flow knows about DSP terms | The policy must bind listing creation in 05. Otherwise this feature is a locked front door on an open house — see 21.02.02 DT-03 |
| Artist buys pay-for-placement off-platform | We cannot stop it, and their track gets sanctioned anyway | The only lever is that they understood what it was before they went looking. This is why the explanation is the product |
| Terms change; a running campaign becomes non-compliant | Do we re-evaluate live campaigns against changed rules? | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see Q-02. Non-trivial: retroactive blocking is hostile, letting it run is negligent |
| A "consideration" offer is placement with extra steps | The real-world case: pay for a listen, placement mysteriously always follows | The hardest classification problem here, and it is adversarial by construction |
| An artist is sanctioned by a DSP for something bought elsewhere | Not our doing; it is our user's disaster | `[PENDING]` — is there a duty to warn? A support path? |
| Our own platform features drift toward selling placement | The endogenous risk: a "featured artist" slot or promoted-listing revenue is placement-for-money on *our* surface | Not currently modelled and it should be. See Q-03 — this is the version of the problem we would inflict on ourselves |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Allowed | Buying attention | No friction; it is the clean case |
| Allowed with obligation | Buying an endorsement | Disclosure attached, non-removable |
| Blocked | Buying placement | Concrete explanation of what it is and what it would cost them |
| Unresolved | Consideration-shaped offer | Q-01 governs; currently no defined behaviour |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The guardrail's **explanation is the product**, not the block — because the artist can buy the forbidden thing elsewhere in thirty seconds and will, unless they understand what it is | Reasoning about the actual threat model. We are not a gate on the world; we are one of many places to buy playlist promotion. A bare "not permitted" teaches the artist that WeJammin is restrictive, not that pay-for-placement is dangerous — so they buy it on the open web and get their track pulled anyway, having learned only to resent us. `meta/personas.md` names the Musician's worst *accidental* behaviour as signing something they don't understand | ✅ CONFIRMED — the block prevents nothing on its own; the explanation prevents the harm. This inverts the feature: it is educational infrastructure with an enforcement mechanism, not the reverse. And it is why 21.05.03 is proposed `must` despite sitting inside a `could` sub-domain — the explanation has value even if we never build a single paid-promotion feature. |
| DT-02 | The disclosure mechanism belongs to domain 21 | Initial framing — it arrived bundled with sweep candidate 12, next to paid promotion | ❌ REJECTED for the *generic* half. Material-connection disclosure applies wherever compensated endorsement happens on the platform: a paid gear review (13), a sponsored plugin demo (14), a paid service testimonial (05), a paid fan post (20). Building it inside 21 means four inconsistent reimplementations, and the inconsistency is where the violations live. **Routed as the `Sponsorship & Paid-Endorsement Disclosure` cross-cut.** What stays here is the music-specific payola policy — DSP terms, radio statute — which is real domain knowledge and belongs nowhere else. Note the shape: this is the second time in this domain that a mechanism arrived dressed as a feature (cf. 21.02.05 DT-02, the embargo). |
| DT-03 | The most dangerous payola risk is our users buying it elsewhere | The obvious threat model, and the one the domain index framed | ❌ REJECTED as *the most dangerous* — it is the most likely, but not the worst. The worst is **endogenous**: WeJammin itself selling placement on its own surfaces. A "featured artist" slot, a promoted listing, a paid boost in discovery — every marketplace builds these, they are the obvious revenue, and they are pay-for-placement with our own name on it. We would not be violating a DSP's terms, so nothing would block it; we would simply be doing on our surface the exact thing this feature exists to say is wrong. And the argument for it will be made by someone reasonable, in a growth meeting, in eighteen months. Recorded as Q-03 so that when it is proposed, the contradiction is already on the record and has to be argued past rather than around. |
