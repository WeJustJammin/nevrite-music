## Overview

A rights record that only its owner can see is a diary. This sub-domain is where the registry becomes
**usable by other people**: the identifiers that let external systems refer to a work at all, the
evidence that a creation existed on a date, the statutory filing that makes it enforceable, and the
public lookup that answers the industry's most-asked and least-answerable question — *who owns this?*

The strategic framing matters. `meta/problem-statement.md` names Jaxsta and Muso.AI as the failure mode:
a credits database with no daily hook, built by reconstruction, asking people to remember. WeJammin can
build the same public surface from a fundamentally different substrate — records that were consented at
creation by the people in the room. **Same product, different truth.** Which is only true if the lookup
exists; without it, the provenance half of D-18 has no demand side.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: identifiers are the keys the lookup resolves, timestamping
produces the evidence that registration files, and registration status is a fact the lookup surfaces.
Four capabilities, each consuming another's output.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Sweep candidate 21 (Proof-of-Creation Timestamping **& Copyright Office Registration**) **split into two features** | They are opposite in every dimension that matters: timestamping is free, instant, platform-owned, evidentiary, and universal. Registration is paid, slow (months), externally filed, statutory, and jurisdiction-specific — in the US it is a precondition of suing at all and unlocks statutory damages. One is a byproduct of using the platform; the other is a form-filling integration with a government office. A single node would have had to be both. | Node Classification Gate; `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | Identifier issuance narrowed to **works and recordings only** (ISRC/ISWC/UPC/GRid) | Inherited from the domain's boundary fix: IPI is an Interested **Party** identifier and belongs to [domain 01](../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/) with identities, not here with works. | Domain index; 14-lens sweep ratification (D-16) |
| D-03 | The feature is **"issuance & reconciliation"**, not issuance | An honesty correction to the candidate's framing. WeJammin can genuinely *issue* ISRCs (obtain a registrant code, allocate sequentially) and UPCs. It **cannot issue ISWCs** — those are allocated by a CISAC society when a work is registered, which is [domain 10](../../10-royalties-collections/)'s surface. For ISWC the platform captures and reconciles an identifier assigned elsewhere. Claiming otherwise would promise something undeliverable. | Deep Think; `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-04 | The public lookup is the **demand side of the thesis** | D-18 says provenance is why users cannot leave. But a record's value to its owner is proportional to how much *other people* rely on it — a credit nobody can verify does not win the next job, which is precisely the Musician persona's stated success criterion ("credits are cited rather than claimed"). Without a public surface, the provenance half is a private archive. | Deep Think; D-18; `meta/personas.md` |

## Open Questions

| # | Question | Owner | Deferred To |
|---|----------|-------|-------------|
| Q-01 | **[OWNER]** **What is public?** Names and roles are already public on any released record. Percentages and deal terms are commercially sensitive and their exposure could damage users badly (a co-writer discovering their split is worse than a peer's; a label seeing an artist's publishing position before a negotiation). Where is the line, and does the owner get to move it? The sub-domain's largest product decision. Same call as [domain 09 Q-06](../rights-ownership-index.md); [09.06.04 Q-01](./09.06.04-public-rights-lookup.md) is its per-feature instance. | User | `/create-prd` |
| Q-02 | **[OWNER]** Does WeJammin **buy an ISRC registrant code** and become an issuing body? It is cheap and it makes the platform infrastructure rather than a client — with a permanent obligation, since allocated ISRCs must never be reused or orphaned even if the platform dies. The same call is already queued at [12.07 Q-01](../../12-release-distribution/12.07-identifier-assignment-at-delivery.md) → `/create-prd` and restated at [domain 09 Q-10](../rights-ownership-index.md); it must be answered once, not three times. | User | `/create-prd` |
| Q-03 | **[OWNER]** Is the public lookup **open to the internet** (SEO, unauthenticated, the Jaxsta-shaped play) or gated to platform users? Open maximises reliance and therefore the record's value; it also exposes the data to scraping by exactly the AI companies [09.05.01](../09.05-ai-voice-likeness-consent/09.05.01-ai-training-consent-registry.md) is trying to hold to account. A competitive-position call, not an access-control detail — the exposure model follows the answer, not the reverse. | User | `/create-prd` |
