# Feature: Split & Ownership Dispute Cases

> **Number**: 09.04.02
> **Parent**: [Rights Conflicts & Disputes](./09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

When two parties genuinely disagree about who owns what, this is the record of that disagreement: who
claims what, on what evidence, what happened, and what the ledger looks like afterwards. It is the
rights-specific half of a dispute — the case machinery itself (submission, evidence, arbitration
workflow, resolution) is a cross-cut mechanism owned by [domain 24](../../24-trust-safety-disputes/).

What is domain-owned here and cannot live in 24: **what constitutes a rights dispute** (competing claims
over the same right), and **what a resolution does** (corrects a ledger — not refunds a transaction).
Domain 24 owns the courtroom; domain 09 owns the cause of action and the judgment's effect.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Asserts a dispute over a share they believe is wrong; submits evidence (the session, the messages, the sheet they signed); sees the other party's position. Sees the platform's own record as evidence — which is the entire point: a WeJammin-native work has a consented split sheet with timestamps and attestations, so the dispute is usually short. |
| Producer | Full | Can be a party, and is more often the **witness**. Their attestation of what was agreed in the room is worth more than either claimant's assertion — `meta/personas.md` identifies this as their structural value ("their attestation is worth more than a self-claim, which is precisely what makes counter-attestation valuable"). A dispute is where that value is cashed. |
| Operator | None | Not a party to rights disputes. Explicit absence. |
| Fan | None | No visibility. Contested ownership is commercially sensitive and reputationally loaded. |

## Behavior

A dispute attaches to a **specific right and share** — not to a work. Disputing 25% of a publishing
share does not contest the recording, does not contest the other 75%, and does not stop the other
writers being paid (see [09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-cx.md](./09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-cx.md) CX-02).
Scoping matters: an unscoped dispute is a weapon.

Opening a dispute freezes the contested share ([09.04.03](./09.04.03-rights-freeze-royalty-escrow.md)).
That freeze is the dispute's only clock.

**The platform does not adjudicate** ([09.04 D-04](./09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-index.md)). It
holds the facts, presents both positions, freezes the money, and records the outcome — whatever produced
it. Which leaves the sub-domain's central unresolved question: how does a dispute *end* when both parties
dig in? See [09.04 Q-01](./09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-index.md).

### Happy Path

1. Writer A believes Writer B's claimed 30% should be 20%.
2. A asserts a dispute scoped to that share.
3. The contested 10% freezes; all other shares keep paying.
4. Both submit positions. The platform surfaces its own record: the consented sheet from 2027, the Producer's attestation, the session participants.
5. **The record settles it.** B sees the sheet they consented to and withdraws — the dispute ends in days rather than years, because the facts were captured when they were fresh and friendly.
6. Ledger amended by consent ([09.02.04](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.04-split-amendment-reconsent.md)); escrow releases; outcome recorded on the chain.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| No platform record exists (pre-WeJammin work) | The platform has nothing to contribute; the dispute is the industry's old problem, imported. | "No platform record for this period" — honest about the limit of what being-in-the-room bought. |
| Both parties dig in; nobody withdraws | Escrow accumulates on the contested share and does not release; the platform surfaces both positions and its own record but issues no ruling. The share stays frozen until one party withdraws, a court judgment is recorded, or an agreed end-mechanism is chosen. | "Awaiting resolution — frozen amount growing." The termination mechanism itself is `[PENDING — resolution of Q-01 at /ideate-validate]`: the sub-domain's central unsolved problem (escrow-forever vs mediation vs recorded court judgment) — see [09.04 Q-01](./09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-index.md) and Q-01/Q-02 below. |
| Disputant is disputing in bad faith to extract a settlement | Freeze is the leverage they are exploiting: strangle a co-writer's income until they concede. | Scoping limits the blast radius to the contested share. Beyond that, a bad-faith pattern is [domain 24](../../24-trust-safety-disputes/)'s problem, not a rights problem. |
| Dispute over a work that is a party's whole income | Even scoped, the freeze may be existential for them. | The reason freeze is share-scoped and never work-scoped. |
| Court judgment resolves it off-platform | Recorded as a chain event with the judgment as evidence. | Outcome recorded; platform did not decide, and says so. |
| Producer's attestation contradicts both parties | Extremely valuable. Recorded as evidence, weighted by nobody — the platform presents, it does not rule. | Attestation shown alongside both positions. |
| Party dies mid-dispute | Estate inherits the position ([09.03.04](../09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle/09.03.04-estate-succession-catalog-legacy.md)); if none exists, this collapses into [09.02.04 Q-01](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.04-split-amendment-reconsent.md). | Escrow continues. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The platform should **adjudicate** — it holds better evidence than any court will see, and it could resolve most disputes in an afternoon | It is tempting and it is nearly true: a consented split sheet with timestamps and a Producer's attestation *is* better evidence than a court reconstructs from memory | ❌ REJECTED — for the same reason [09.02.04](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.04-split-amendment-reconsent.md) DT-02 rejects the admin override, and the reasoning compounds. If WeJammin can rule on ownership, then **every record on the platform means something different**: it is no longer evidence of what parties agreed, it is evidence of what WeJammin currently says they agreed. That is the whole asset, devalued in one decision. It also makes the platform a defendant in every dispute it rules on, invites regulatory capture of the adjudication function, and puts a solo-operated company (`meta/constraints.md`) in the business of quasi-judicial decisions about other people's property. **The record is the product; the ruling is not.** The right move is to make the record so good that the dispute resolves itself — which is exactly what the happy path shows. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect the **Producer's attestation** to be a first-class evidence type, and the candidate list treats disputes generically | `meta/personas.md` says the Producer's attestation "is worth more than a self-claim, which is precisely what makes counter-attestation valuable" — but the sweep gave "Split & Ownership Disputes" as a bare noun with no notion of who witnesses what. A dispute between two claimants is symmetric and unresolvable; a dispute with a neutral witness who was in the room is usually neither. | ✅ CONFIRMED — attestation is a distinct evidence type, structurally different from a party's assertion. This is also where the Producer persona's whole strategic function pays out: the platform recruits them as the capture point, and the reason capture is worth anything is that their presence makes the record hard to argue with later. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The platform **never adjudicates** the merits | Ruling would change what every record on the platform *means* — from evidence of agreement to evidence of WeJammin's opinion — devaluing the whole asset (see DT-01). |
| D-02 | Disputes are scoped to a **specific right and share**, never to a work | An unscoped dispute is a weapon: it strangles uninvolved co-owners' income to create settlement pressure. |
| D-03 | **Producer attestation** is a distinct evidence type, structurally separate from a party's assertion | A witness who was in the room breaks the symmetry that makes two-claimant disputes unresolvable (see DT-02). |
| D-04 | The platform's own record is presented as evidence, **unweighted** | Presenting is not ruling. Weighting evidence is adjudication through the back door. |
