# Feature: Rights Freeze & Royalty Escrow

> **Number**: 09.04.03
> **Parent**: [Rights Conflicts & Disputes](./09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Money does not stop arriving because ownership is contested. While two people argue over a 25% share,
the streams keep playing and the royalties keep landing — and someone has to decide where they go. Pay
them out and you have handed cash to a party who may have to give it back, from money they have already
spent. Escrow holds the contested share until the dispute ends.

**This is a Deep Think addition — it was not on the candidate list.** The candidates model disputes as a
records problem. Without escrow, the whole sub-domain is a debate club: a dispute with no financial
consequence has no clock, and the party currently receiving the money has every incentive to stall
forever. Escrow is what makes the dispute cost both sides equally.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees that their contested share is frozen, how much has accumulated, and since when. Read-only is the whole design: **nobody can release their own escrow**, or the freeze would be a suggestion. Also sees — importantly — that their *uncontested* shares are unaffected and still paying. |
| Producer | Read-only | Same, where they hold a contested share or points. Read-only for the same reason. |
| Operator | None | No shares, no escrow. Explicit absence. |
| Fan | None | No visibility. |

## Behavior

A freeze is scoped to a **share**, never a work. Contest 25% of the publishing and that 25% escrows;
the other 75% pays out on schedule to people who are not arguing. Freezing a whole work would punish
bystanders and would hand a bad-faith disputant a weapon: strangle four co-writers' income to force one
of them to settle.

The escrowed money is held by the platform's financial layer, not by either party — which is precisely
why neither can touch it. Release requires the dispute to end
([09.04.02](./09.04.02-split-ownership-dispute-cases.md)); the platform does not decide the outcome, it
executes the recorded one.

**This feature touches real money, and that has consequences well outside domain 09.** Holding funds on
behalf of third parties pending a dispute is a regulated activity in most jurisdictions. `meta/constraints.md`
already carries `[PENDING]` compliance flags for KYC/AML and payments; escrow is one of the things that
makes them binding rather than theoretical, and it materially constrains the payments provider decision
that is still open in `/create-prd-stack`.

### Happy Path

1. Dispute asserted over a 25% publishing share.
2. That share is flagged contested; the other 75% is untouched.
3. Next royalty run: 75% distributes normally; 25% routes to escrow.
4. Both parties see the escrow accumulating — visible, and mounting, and costing them both.
5. Dispute resolves; ledger amended by consent.
6. Escrow releases to the correct party per the resolved ledger, with the accumulated amount intact.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Dispute never resolves | Escrow accumulates indefinitely. Nobody gets paid; the platform holds money it cannot release. | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`. Unresolved escrow is a genuine liability — see Q-01 and [09.04 Q-01](./09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-index.md). |
| Escrow accumulates for months with no activity | Staleness is itself a signal ([09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-cx.md](./09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-cx.md) CX-03). | Escalating prompts to both parties. Silence is the failure mode nobody gets notified about, because nothing changed. |
| Money arrives for a share frozen mid-run | Run binds to the state at its start; the freeze applies from the next run. | Statement names the effective date, same version-binding discipline as [09.02.04](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.04-split-amendment-reconsent.md). |
| Escrow hold fails (payment provider error) | Distribution of the contested share **halts**. It must never fall back to paying someone. | "Distribution held" — a failure to escrow is not permission to pay. |
| Party needs the money urgently (rent, genuinely) | The freeze does not care, and that is the hardest human moment in the domain. | The reason freezes are share-scoped: their other income is untouched. Cold comfort, and it is the honest answer. |
| Both parties agree to split the escrow to end it | A consented resolution like any other; the platform executes it without ruling. | Release on consent. Probably the most common real ending. |
| Dispute resolves in favour of a party who has since left the platform | Payout to a departed user. | Interacts with account closure and GDPR erasure ([09.01.02 Q-03](../09.01-rights-registry/09.01.02-ownership-ledger-validation.md)). `[PENDING]` |
| Escrowed amounts earn interest | Whose interest is it? Not a rhetorical question — regulators have opinions. | `[PENDING]` — see Q-02. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Freezing the whole **work** is simpler and safer than freezing a share | Simplicity, and an abundance-of-caution instinct | ❌ REJECTED — it converts a dispute into a **weapon**. If contesting any share stops the whole work paying, a bad-faith actor disputes a 2% claim and strangles four uninvolved co-writers' income until someone pays them to go away. The platform would be supplying the leverage. Share-scoping keeps the pressure on exactly the two parties who are arguing, which is also the only fair place for it. This is the same principle as [09.04.02 D-02](./09.04.02-split-ownership-dispute-cases.md), and it is worth stating twice because the "safer" instinct is strong and wrong. |
| DT-02 | Escrow is a **rights** feature | Its placement in this sub-domain | ⚠️ PARTIALLY CONFIRMED — the *trigger* and *scope* are rights facts (which share, contested by whom, released per which resolved ledger) and belong here. The *mechanism* — holding third-party funds pending a dispute — is a **regulated financial activity** and belongs to [domain 10](../../10-royalties-collections/) plus `/create-prd-stack`. This node deliberately owns only the rights half. Flagging it matters because escrow is the point where domain 09 stops being a records system and starts touching money, which activates the KYC/AML and payments-compliance items already `[PENDING]` in `meta/constraints.md` and constrains a stack decision that is still open. A records domain quietly implying a money-transmission requirement is exactly the kind of thing that should surface in ideation rather than in `/setup-workspace`. |
| DT-03 | A domain expert would expect escrow to have a **terminal state**, and neither the candidates nor [09.04.02](./09.04.02-split-ownership-dispute-cases.md) provides one | Every escrow in every other industry has an ending: a deadline, an arbitrator, a court, a default rule. Here, [09.04 D-04](./09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes-index.md) says the platform never adjudicates — which means, followed literally, escrow can run forever, and the platform accumulates other people's money indefinitely with no lawful path to release it. | ⚠️ CONFIRMED as a genuine hole, **unsolved and honestly flagged**. This is the direct consequence of the never-adjudicate principle, and it is the strongest argument against that principle anyone has made in this domain. Candidate directions, all flawed: a deadline with a default rule (the platform adjudicating by timeout, wearing a disguise); binding arbitration on sign-up (real, common, and a serious consumer-rights posture for a platform with Fans on it per D-11); escheatment to the state after N years (lawful, and it means nobody in music gets the money — reproducing the black box the domain exists to eliminate). Raised as Q-01. Guessing here would bury a values decision inside a schema. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Freeze is scoped to a **share**, never a work | Work-scoping turns a dispute into a weapon against uninvolved co-owners (see DT-01). |
| D-02 | **Nobody can release their own escrow** — read-only to all parties | A freeze anyone can lift is a suggestion. |
| D-03 | A failure to escrow **halts** distribution; it never falls back to paying | Paying out a contested share because the hold failed is the exact harm escrow exists to prevent. |
| D-04 | This node owns the **rights half** of escrow only; the fund-holding mechanism is routed out | Holding third-party funds is a regulated activity belonging to domain 10 and the stack decision (see DT-02). |
