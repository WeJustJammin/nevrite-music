## Overview

What happens when two people claim the same thing. This sub-domain detects conflicts (automatically,
before anyone notices), runs the rights-specific dispute, and — critically — decides what happens to
the **money** while ownership is contested.

Its strategic position is worth stating plainly. The industry's dispute tooling is entirely
*post-hoc*: a conflict surfaces when a royalty statement looks wrong, years after the facts, at which
point resolution means litigation. WeJammin's advantage is that it holds consented records from the
moment of creation, so most conflicts here should be **detected before they mature** — a duplicate
claim caught in week one is a conversation; the same claim caught in year four is a lawsuit.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: detection, dispute, and freeze are three capabilities that
chain directly into each other — detection opens a dispute, a dispute freezes the money, and the freeze
is what gives the dispute urgency. Each is meaningless alone.

## Open Questions

| # | Question | Owner | Deferred To |
|---|----------|-------|-------------|
| Q-01 | [OWNER] If the platform never adjudicates, **how does a dispute ever end** when both parties dig in? Options: escrow forever (honest, useless); platform-provided arbitration (a service, and a liability); a court judgment the platform records (correct, and out of reach for a £400 dispute). The sub-domain's central unresolved question. Every candidate costs something the owner must choose to spend — arbitration weakens D-04 (the sub-domain's spine), escrow-forever leaves users unpaid indefinitely — and no downstream stage can make that trade. Sits with [09.04.02 Q-01](./09.04.02-split-ownership-dispute-cases.md), [09.04.03 Q-01](./09.04.03-rights-freeze-royalty-escrow.md) and [09 Q-07](../rights-ownership-index.md). | User | `/create-prd` |
| Q-02 | [OWNER] Does a dispute make the work **unusable** (release-blocked, licence-blocked) or only unpaid (escrowed)? Blocking is the stronger lever to force resolution and it punishes the innocent co-owners hardest. A values trade, not a derivable fact: domain 12 already blocks a *pre-delivery* release on contested ownership ([12.01.01 D-07](../../12-release-distribution/12.01-release-builder/12.01.01-release-composition-sequencing.md)), but the live-release half is the same open owner call there ([12.05 Q-01](../../12-release-distribution/12.05-catalog-lifecycle-after-release/12.05-catalog-lifecycle-after-release-index.md), and domain 12's `[OWNER]` Q-04 hard-gate-vs-soft-gate, already queued at `/create-prd`). Sits with [09.04.03 Q-04](./09.04.03-rights-freeze-royalty-escrow.md). | User | `/create-prd` |
| Q-03 | Does escrow hold **real money** in a platform-controlled account? That is a regulated activity (see `meta/constraints.md` — KYC/AML and payments compliance are already `[PENDING]`) and it materially constrains the payments provider choice. | User | `/create-prd-stack` |
