# Feature: Producer Points & Producer Agreements

> **Number**: 09.02.02
> **Parent**: [Split Capture & Agreements](./09.02-split-capture-agreements-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

A producer's compensation is conventionally expressed in **points** — a royalty percentage off the
master, typically 2–5 for an independent producer, paid before or alongside the artist's share
depending on the deal. Points are *not* ownership: a producer with 3 points cannot license the master,
cannot block a sync, and does not appear in the chain of title. Colloquially they sound like a share,
which is why they are one of the most commonly disputed instruments in independent music.

This feature records the points and the agreement they sit in, at the same moment the split is captured
— which is the only moment the producer's arrangement is ever discussed out loud.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The artist paying the points. Sees exactly what the producer's arrangement costs them and — critically — sees it expressed as a reduction in what they *receive*, not in what they *own*. Consents to the points as part of the whole sheet. Sees whether points are recoupable against an advance, which is the difference between "you get 3% of the money" and "you get nothing until you have recovered my fee". |
| Producer | Full | The points holder. Proposes their own points during capture and — because they are proposing their own compensation while driving the flow — this is the exact position `meta/personas.md` flags as their intentional anti-persona risk ("assigns themselves a larger split than agreed"). The structural guard is full-ledger consent: their points are visible to everyone on the sheet, not just to them. |
| Operator | None | Operating a room does not earn points. The studio-takes-points-in-lieu-of-fee arrangement exists ([09.01.03](../09.01-rights-registry/09.01.03-master-rights-ownership.md)) but it is contracted as a work-for-hire or an ownership stake, not as producer points — points are compensation for *production*, which a room does not perform. Explicit absence. |
| Fan | None | No visibility. |

## Behavior

Points are an **encumbrance** on the master, recorded alongside the master ledger but never inside it
(see [09.01-rights-registry-cx.md](../09.01-rights-registry/09.01-rights-registry-cx.md) CX-04 and
[09.02-split-capture-agreements-cx.md](./09.02-split-capture-agreements-cx.md) CX-02). They carry:

- **Point count** — the royalty percentage.
- **Base** — points *of what*? Gross receipts, net receipts, or the artist's share. These produce wildly different money from the same number, and the ambiguity is the dispute. This is **not** the name-your-price **payout basis**, which is a platform-wide constant carried on no record at all ([09.02 D-05](./09.02-split-capture-agreements-index.md), [09.02.01 D-16](./09.02.01-moment-of-creation-split-capture.md)): that one says what a *split percentage* is applied to on a direct-to-fan sale; this one says which master revenue pool a *points* percentage is computed from. Different instrument, different number — and unlike the payout basis, this term is per-agreement and required.
- **Recoupability** — are they payable from the first dollar, or only after an advance/fee is recovered?
- **Reversion / sunset** — do they run forever, or for a term?

The agreement itself is a document; signing is the cross-cut e-signature mechanism, not a capability of
this feature.

### Happy Path

1. During capture ([09.02.01](./09.02.01-moment-of-creation-split-capture.md)), the Producer's contribution is marked as points rather than share-or-fee.
2. Points, base, and recoupability are entered. The base is a required choice, never a default — a defaulted base is a guess about money.
3. The platform shows the artist a plain-language consequence: "*3 points of net receipts, recoupable against a £2,000 fee*" alongside what that means at an illustrative revenue figure.
4. Points appear on the sheet everyone consents to.
5. On consent, the encumbrance attaches to the master; [domain 10](../../10-royalties-collections/) honours it in distribution order.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Producer is also a co-writer | Two separate stakes: points on the master, writer share on the publishing. Neither implies the other. | Both shown separately. Conflating them is how producers end up with publishing they did not negotiate. |
| Points base left unspecified | Blocked. "3 points" of an unspecified base is not a term, it is a future argument. | "Points of what? Gross, net, or artist's share" — required, unfilled. |
| Points exceed 100 | Blocked by validation. | Inline error. |
| Points + master ownership share for the same producer | Allowed and real (a producer who both owns part of the master and takes points), but flagged because it is unusual and frequently a data-entry error. | "This producer owns 20% *and* takes 3 points — is that right?" Confirm, don't block. |
| Producer takes points **and** a WFH fee for the same contribution | Blocked — mutually exclusive per contribution. Allowed across *different* contributions (WFH on engineering, points on production). | "Points and a buyout fee for the same work — pick one." |
| Recoupment never reached | Points remain unpaid indefinitely; not a defect. | Recoupment progress visible to both parties from [domain 10](../../10-royalties-collections/) — a producer waiting on recoupment currently has no way to see it at all. |
| Artist sells the master; points holder not consulted | Points survive the transfer as an encumbrance; the buyer takes subject to them. | Chain of title ([09.03.01](../09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle/09.03.01-chain-of-title-ledger.md)) shows the encumbrance travelling with the asset. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The **base** is a detail that can be defaulted (e.g. to net receipts) to reduce capture friction | Friction-reduction instinct, which this sub-domain otherwise obeys aggressively | ❌ REJECTED — and this is the one place the friction-reduction principle must yield. "3 points" of gross and "3 points" of net are different amounts of money from an identical-looking record, and the gap is the entire content of the classic producer dispute. A defaulted base means the platform silently invented a financial term on the user's behalf and put its authority behind it. Everywhere else this feature minimises questions; here it insists on one, because the alternative is a record that looks captured and is actually ambiguous — the worst possible outcome for an evidentiary product. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect **recoupment visibility**, and no candidate mentions it | The candidate list said "Producer Points & Producer Agreements" — the instrument, not its lifecycle. But points are usually recoupable against a fee, and a producer today has no way to know whether they have recouped; they ask the artist, who asks the distributor, who sends a PDF. The platform holds the revenue data ([domain 10](../../10-royalties-collections/)) and the agreement terms — it can answer a question that currently requires three phone calls. | ✅ CONFIRMED — recoupment status is part of this feature. It is also a clean example of the thesis: consolidation (holding the royalty data) makes a provenance record (the agreement) *useful* rather than merely archival. An agreement you can query beats an agreement you can retrieve. |
| DT-03 | Points should be stored as **literal "points"** because that is the industry's word (resolves Q-02) | The instrument is universally called "points" in deal language, and a naive model would store the integer the user typed | ❌ REJECTED — "points" is a *display* label, not a storage unit. A point has no fixed meaning until it is bound to a base (DT-01/D-01), so the only value that can be paid out is a **percentage of a named base**. Store the percentage + the base; render the word "points" in the UI because that is the vocabulary the user negotiates in. Storing "3 points" as a bare unit reproduces exactly the ambiguity D-01 exists to kill — the number would look captured and still be unpayable. Percentage-of-base is the canonical model; the label is cosmetic. |
| DT-04 | Points can be **assigned or sold** by the holder (a producer selling their royalty stream), so they need their own provenance thread (resolves Q-03) | Royalty-stream financing is real and growing at the independent tier; a producer waiting years on recoupment may sell the future stream for cash today. If the platform holds the encumbrance, it will be asked to record the sale | ✅ CONFIRMED — assignment is a real lifecycle event and the encumbrance must be transferable to a new payee without altering the master ledger or the original agreement's terms. The assignment creates its own chain-of-title entry ([09.03.01](../09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle/09.03.01-chain-of-title-ledger.md)) so provenance survives the sale: the record shows *who originally earned the points* and *who is owed the money now*, which are no longer the same party. The detailed transfer mechanics (partial vs whole assignment, notice to the artist, whether the artist can object) are `[PENDING — /write-be-spec]`; what is settled at Level 1 is that points are an assignable payee-bearing encumbrance, not a fixed bond to the producer. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Base is a required term** — never defaulted | Points of gross vs net vs artist's share are different money from an identical record. A default is the platform inventing a financial term (see DT-01). Unaffected by [09.02 D-05](./09.02-split-capture-agreements-index.md): that decision removes the **name-your-price payout basis** from every record because it is one platform-wide constant; the points base is a genuinely negotiated per-agreement term over a different number, and stays required. |
| D-02 | Points are an **encumbrance**, never a ledger share | A share would grant licensing, blocking, and chain-of-title standing the producer never bargained for. |
| D-03 | **Recoupment status is queryable** by both parties | The platform holds the revenue and the terms; answering "have I recouped?" is a consolidation payoff (see DT-02). |
| D-04 | Points and a WFH fee are **mutually exclusive per contribution**, combinable across contributions | Scoping to the person forces a false either/or on a common independent arrangement. |
| D-05 | Points are stored as a **percentage of a named base**; "points" is a display label only | Resolves Q-02. A bare "points" unit has no payable meaning until bound to a base — storing it as a unit reproduces the exact ambiguity D-01 kills (see DT-03). |
| D-06 | Points are an **assignable, payee-bearing encumbrance**; assignment creates its own chain-of-title entry | Resolves Q-03. Producers sell future royalty streams; the encumbrance must transfer payee without altering the master or the original terms (see DT-04). Transfer mechanics deferred to `/write-be-spec`. |
