# Feature: Moment-of-Creation Split Capture

> **Number**: 09.02.01
> **Parent**: [Split Capture & Agreements](./09.02-split-capture-agreements-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

**The thesis feature.** Everything in D-18 reduces to this screen: the platform is in the room because
of consolidation, and being in the room is what lets it ask "who did what, and what's the split?" while
everyone is present, friendly, and remembers. That question costs one signature today and a lawsuit in
three years. No competitor can retroactively manufacture the answer, because none of them were there.

The feature captures both ledgers (master and publishing), routes each contributor to share-or-fee,
collects consent from every party, and locks the result. Its design constraint is unusual and absolute:
**it must be faster than not doing it.** `meta/personas.md` names the Producer's worst accidental
behaviour as closing a project without capturing splits — committed by the persona best placed to
prevent it — and concludes: *the design must make the lazy path the correct path.*

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The contributor. Receives the sheet on their phone while still in the room, sees the **whole** ledger (not just their own line — see [09.01.02](../09.01-rights-registry/09.01.02-ownership-ledger-validation.md) DT-02), and consents or objects in one tap. Objecting is a first-class action, not a hidden one: an objection while everyone is present is cheap; the same objection in three years is litigation. Can also initiate capture themselves — the Producer is the *usual* driver, not the required one. |
| Producer | Full | The capture point. Prompted at session close from the session's participant list ([domain 07](../../07-music-projects-collaboration/)) — never a blank form. Assigns share-or-fee per contributor, enters percentages, sends for consent. Sees who has and has not consented, and can chase from the same screen. Cannot consent on anyone's behalf, and cannot mark the sheet done while it is `proposed` — which is the structural answer to their own anti-persona behaviour. |
| Operator | None | A venue or studio operator is not a party to a split by virtue of operating the room. The studio's engineer may be captured (as a Musician or Producer); the studio entity enters only via a work-for-hire it holds ([09.02.03](./09.02.03-work-for-hire-buyout.md)), which is a different feature. Explicit absence — no Operator surface. |
| Fan | None | No visibility. Splits are commercially sensitive and fans have no stake. This is the single clearest "different product wearing the same brand" boundary in the domain. |

## Behavior

Capture is triggered by an event in the work — a session closing, a stem delivered, a project marked
complete — not by a user deciding to go and file paperwork. **Nobody opens an app to file a split
sheet** (D-18); the flow must arrive at them.

The flow, per contributor: *were you here?* → *share or fee?* → *how much?* → *consent from everyone*.
Both ledgers are prompted in one pass ([09.02 D-04](./09.02-split-capture-agreements-index.md)) —
letting the Producer complete the master and defer publishing would let the flow report success while
producing the exact failure the domain exists to prevent.

The output is a `consented` ledger in [09.01.02](../09.01-rights-registry/09.01.02-ownership-ledger-validation.md),
plus a performer record in [09.01.05](../09.01-rights-registry/09.01.05-performer-neighbouring-rights.md),
plus credits in [domain 02](../../02-credits-attribution/) — three records, one prompt. That ratio is
the feature's whole design goal.

### Happy Path

1. Producer marks the session complete in [domain 07](../../07-music-projects-collaboration/).
2. The platform presents the session's participants — already known, because the platform booked the room, paid the engineer, and hosted the stems. **This is consolidation buying provenance.**
3. Producer marks each person share-or-fee, and proposes percentages. Suggested defaults come from the session's known facts (who was present, who is listed as a writer), never from an algorithm guessing at creative contribution.
4. Every contributor gets a notification on their phone, in the room. Each sees the full sheet.
5. Each consents in one tap, or objects with a reason.
6. All consented → ledger locks, credits publish, performer records confirm. The record is complete on day one.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| A contributor objects | Sheet stays `proposed`; nothing locks. Objection is routed back to the Producer with the reason. | "X objected: *reason*." This is a **success** state for the platform — the disagreement surfaced while it was cheap. Copy must not frame it as an error. |
| Contributor has no WeJammin account | Invited via email/SMS; can consent via a signed link without a full account. If account creation were required, capture would depend on universal adoption at the exact moment it must not (see [09.02 Q-03](./09.02-split-capture-agreements-index.md)). | "Waiting on 1 of 4 — invite sent." |
| Producer closes the session and skips capture | The flow is skippable — a modal nobody can dismiss trains people to dismiss modals. But the recording is permanently badged `no split agreed`, that badge is visible to every collaborator, and it release-blocks in [domain 12](../../12-release-distribution/). | "No split agreed" on the work, on every contributor's view, and at the release gate. Consequence, not obstruction. |
| Nobody consents; sheet sits `proposed` for months | Escalating, decaying chase. The sheet does not expire — a stale proposal is still evidence of what was proposed and when. | "Waiting on 3 of 4 since March." Visible on every party's dashboard. |
| Session had 14 people (orchestra, choir) | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — per-person consent does not scale to an ensemble; likely a bulk WFH path via a contractor. | `[PENDING]` |
| Split proposed while a sample is undeclared | Capture proceeds; the sample flag blocks release separately ([09.01.06](../09.01-rights-registry/09.01.06-sample-interpolation-provenance.md)). | Both facts shown; neither blocks the other's capture. |
| Producer proposes themselves a larger share than agreed | The named Producer anti-persona. Full-ledger consent means every contributor sees the Producer's line, not just their own. | The sheet is the disclosure mechanism. This is why per-row consent was rejected. |
| Contributor consents, then says they misunderstood | Routes to [09.02.04](./09.02.04-split-amendment-reconsent.md) — amendment, not deletion. | "Propose an amendment" — the consent is a fact; the disagreement is a new one. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Capture flow opened | Participant list skeleton — never a blank form |
| Empty | No contributors identified | Should be unreachable from a session close; the fallback is manual add, flagged as the degraded path |
| Populated (`proposed`) | Sheet sent | Full ledger + per-party consent state + chase action |
| Populated (`consented`) | All consented | Locked sheet, signed badge, timestamp, participant list |
| Partial | Some consented | "Waiting on N of M" with names; the old sheet governs nothing yet |
| Error | Send failed | Inline retry. **Never discards entered data** — this data is unrepeatable and re-entry means it will not happen. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Capture should be **mandatory** at session close — an undismissable gate | It is the thesis feature; the strongest possible enforcement seems obviously right | ❌ REJECTED — and this is the most important call in the feature. A gate you cannot dismiss trains users to route around the event that triggers it: producers will simply stop marking sessions complete, and the platform loses its single point of presence in the room. Worse, a forced sheet is a *coerced* sheet — signatures collected to escape a modal are exactly the evidentiary garbage the domain exists to replace. The correct design is **consequence, not obstruction**: skipping is allowed, permanently visible to every collaborator, and blocks release. Make the lazy path correct; do not make the correct path compulsory. |
| DT-02 | The platform can suggest splits from session data | Consolidation gives us who was present, for how long, on what | ❌ REJECTED for *creative* shares. Time in the room does not measure authorship — the person who wrote the hook in ten minutes did not contribute less than the engineer who sat for nine hours. An algorithmic suggestion would carry the platform's authority into a judgement it has no basis for, and would anchor negotiations toward a number the platform invented. Suggesting *who was present* is confirmed and valuable; suggesting *what they deserve* is rejected. |
| DT-03 | A domain expert would expect the capture surface to be **where the work is**, and the declared surface model may not reach it | `meta/personas.md`, Producer's unique constraint: *"Their work happens inside a DAW, not a browser. Any capture flow requiring them to leave the session and go fill in a web form will not be used."* The project is declared `single-surface` responsive web (D-02). | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED as an unresolved structural tension — escalated.** The thesis depends on being in the room; the persona file states the room is a DAW; the surface model contains only a browser. The mitigation drafted here (trigger from session close, consent on the contributor's phone) narrows the gap but does not close it for the Producer, who is the capture point. Raised as [09.02 Q-02](./09.02-split-capture-agreements-index.md) — this is a surface-model question, not a feature question, and it is the highest-stakes open item in the domain. |
| DT-04 | The dangerous failure is a **wrong** split | Natural framing — disputes are about wrong numbers | ❌ REJECTED — the dangerous failure is **no** split. A wrong split is visible, disputable, and fixable while people are reachable. An absent split is silent: the record ships, earns, and only becomes a problem once the money is worth fighting over and the relationships have soured. Every design trade in this feature resolves in favour of *getting something captured* over *getting it perfect* — hence skippable-but-consequential, hence objection-as-success, hence never discarding entered data on error. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Capture is **skippable, with consequences** — not mandatory | An undismissable gate makes producers stop marking sessions complete, costing the platform its presence in the room, and produces coerced signatures (see DT-01). |
| D-02 | The platform suggests **who was present**, never **what they deserve** | Presence is a fact the platform holds. Creative contribution is a judgement it has no basis for, and an anchor it should not set (see DT-02). |
| D-03 | Objection is a **first-class success state** | A disagreement surfaced in the room is the product working. Framing it as an error teaches people to avoid the flow. |
| D-04 | Consent is available **without an account**, via signed link | Requiring accounts makes capture depend on universal adoption at the one moment it cannot afford to. |
| D-05 | Errors **never discard entered data** | Capture data is unrepeatable; forcing re-entry means it does not happen. |
