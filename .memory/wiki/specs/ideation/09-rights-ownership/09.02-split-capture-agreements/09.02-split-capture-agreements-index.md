## Overview

**This sub-domain is the wedge.** D-18 says provenance is the reason users cannot leave, and provenance
has exactly one mechanism: capturing who owns what *at the moment it becomes true* — in the room, on
the day, while everyone is present and friendly. Everything else in Rights & Ownership stores,
validates, transfers, or defends a record. This is where the record is **created**, and if it fails,
nothing downstream has anything to work on.

It holds the split sheet itself, the two designations that decide what a contribution actually earns
(producer points; work-for-hire), and the amendment path that keeps a locked record correctable
without making it worthless.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: capture, points, work-for-hire and amendment are four
capabilities that interact directly — a work-for-hire designation nullifies a split claim, producer
points change what the master ledger means without changing its numbers, and amendment re-opens a
consent state the other three depend on being closed.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | **E-signature is a cross-cut mechanism, not a node here** | The sweep's candidate 02 was "Moment-of-Creation Split Agreement **& E-Signature**". Signing serves domains 05 (service contracts), 06 (lesson terms), 11 (licences), 13/14 (marketplace terms), 16/17 (venue and booking agreements) and 09 alike — it is a mechanism serving many domains, which the Node Classification Gate routes to CX. The **split sheet** is domain-owned; the **pen** is not. | Node Classification Gate; recorded in [../rights-ownership-cx.md](../rights-ownership-cx.md) |
| D-02 | **09.02.04 Split Amendment & Re-Consent added** — not on the candidate list | The candidate list treats capture as a one-shot event. In reality splits change constantly after signature: a late contributor, a corrected error, a sample declared afterwards. A ledger that cannot be corrected is not immutable — it is **wrong forever**, which is a worse product than no ledger at all, because people trust it. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | Work-for-hire proposed as **Must**, not Should | The most common session arrangement in music is "you get a fee and no share". If the capture flow cannot express that, it cannot capture the majority case correctly — it will record either a phantom owner or nothing. This is a correctness dependency of the wedge, not an enhancement. | MoSCoW proposal, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-04 | Capture writes **both ledgers** (master and publishing) in one flow, never one | The Producer anti-persona in `meta/personas.md` is someone who closes a project without capturing splits. A flow that lets them complete the master and defer publishing has satisfied its own success state while producing exactly the failure the domain exists to prevent. | Deep Think; `meta/personas.md` |
| D-05 | **This sub-domain defines no payout-basis field.** What a split's percentages are applied to on a name-your-price sale is a platform-wide constant — **paid-net** — stated as a versioned platform term and **printed on the capture screen beside the percentages**, never captured as a property of a sheet, an agreement or a rights record | Sub-domain level because it binds both capture and amendment and neither may author it: [09.02.01](./09.02.01-moment-of-creation-split-capture.md) prints it (D-16) and its versioning binds the split agreed that day (D-17); [09.02.04](./09.02.04-split-amendment-reconsent.md) cannot amend it (D-16). A field was disqualified rather than merely ranked lower: the locked obligation is that the payee is told the basis **before the split is agreed**, and a split is routinely agreed years before any listing exists, so no listing-time field could satisfy it — while a constant satisfies it by construction and spends nothing of 09.02.01's 8/90 design budget. **Accepted costs:** no per-record variation is expressible (a charity release paying on the ask cannot exist without reopening the decision), and the money rule lives in platform terms rather than an artefact each payee signed ([09.02.01 Q-08](./09.02.01-moment-of-creation-split-capture.md)). | Owner ratification of DQ-19.1 / DQ-19.2 / DQ-19.3, 2026-07-22; downstream [20.04.04 D-10 / D-11](../../20-fanbase-direct-to-fan/20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront/20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md) |
