## Overview

The authoritative record of who owns what — the work/recording duality, splits captured at the moment of creation, master and publishing shares, chain of title, term/territory/reversion, work identifiers, AI and likeness consent, and disputes.

**Why this is a top-level domain**: Absent from idea.md and the single largest omission relative to 'everything the music industry wants'. Rights are how music generates income, and mismanaged rights are the industry's defining pathology — the MLC launched holding hundreds of millions in unmatched mechanicals and global black-box estimates run to billions, all traceable to one root cause: nobody can answer 'who owns this?' in machine-readable form. The work-vs-recording duality is an axiom every other domain must inherit; getting it wrong is unrecoverable. The platform's structural advantage is unique and unrepeatable: it can capture the split sheet in the room, on the day, while everyone is friendly and the facts are fresh — the most consequential and most neglected artifact in music, and the origin of most of its litigation. Two boundary fixes: Band Name & Trademark Rights moved to Identity's Band & Ensemble Governance (a trademark is a different IP regime from copyright in works, and who owns the name at dissolution is determined by the partnership agreement, not the rights registry); and identifier issuance narrowed to works/recordings (ISRC/ISWC/UPC/GRid), since IPI is an Interested PARTY identifier and belongs to Identity. IMPORTANT: this domain is `core` on inference alone — no owner directive asks for it. See owner decision #2.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies domain status):

- work/recording ownership registry
- moment-of-creation split capture & e-sign
- chain of title, term, territory & reversion
- work identifier issuance
- AI/likeness consent declarations
- claim conflict detection & disputes
