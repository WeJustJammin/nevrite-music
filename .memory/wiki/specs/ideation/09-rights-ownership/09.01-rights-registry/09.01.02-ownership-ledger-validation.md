# Feature: Ownership Percentage Ledger & Share Validation

> **Number**: 09.01.02
> **Parent**: [Rights Registry](./09.01-rights-registry-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The ledger is the list of who owns what percentage of a rights object, and the validator is the engine
that refuses to let it be wrong. It enforces the arithmetic invariants of music ownership — a master
ledger totals 100%; a publishing ledger totals 200% in the conventional writer/publisher expression
(100% writer + 100% publisher) — and it holds the rounding, nesting, and effective-share rules that
every downstream payout depends on.

This feature is deliberately *only* arithmetic and structure. What the shares **mean** (a co-pub deal,
producer points, a buyout) lives in the sibling features. A validator that knows about deal semantics
becomes a business-rules engine nobody can reason about.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Enters or accepts their own percentage; sees a live running total with the remainder called out ("15% unallocated") rather than a silent invalid state. Sees their *effective* share when a publisher controls part of it — the number that actually predicts their income, not the nominal one. Cannot save a ledger that does not balance, and cannot unilaterally change a share someone has already consented to (that routes to [09.02.04](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.04-split-amendment-reconsent.md)). |
| Producer | Full | Drives the ledger during capture — usually typing other people's percentages, which is exactly why every entry requires that person's consent rather than the Producer's word. Sees both ledgers (master and publishing) with their different totals, and is warned when one is complete and the other untouched — the classic "we did the master, we'll do publishing later" abandonment. |
| Operator | None | A venue or studio operator has no share in a ledger by virtue of being an Operator. If a studio entity holds an ownership stake (via work-for-hire or a points-for-time arrangement), it appears here as a **named party** — an entity from [domain 01](../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/) — not by way of this persona. |
| Fan | None | No access. Percentage ownership is commercially sensitive; even the public lookup ([09.06.04](../09.06-rights-evidence-public-record/09.06.04-public-rights-lookup.md)) is expected to expose *who* without exposing *how much* — see that feature's open question. |

## Behavior

A ledger is a set of `(party, share, role, consent-state)` rows attached to one rights object. It has a
lifecycle state of its own — `unallocated` → `proposed` → `consented` → (`disputed` | `superseded`) —
and downstream domains gate on that state, not on the numbers.

The validator's rules:

- **Master ledger** sums to exactly 100%.
- **Publishing ledger** sums to exactly 200% under the writer/publisher expression, decomposing to 100%
  writer-share and 100% publisher-share. Where a writer is unpublished, they hold their own publisher
  share — the row is not absent, it is self-held. Absent rows are the origin of black-box money.
- **Rounding**: thirds are the common case (three co-writers). 33.33/33.33/33.33 does not total 100.
  The ledger must carry exact rational shares rather than rounded decimals, and surface the remainder
  explicitly. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` on the canonical representation.
- **Nesting**: a publisher controlling half of a writer's 25% is not a 12.5% row. It is a claim over a
  share. The ledger must hold both the nominal and the derived effective figure without either being
  a lossy computation of the other.

### Happy Path

1. Rights object is created; its ledger opens in `unallocated`.
2. During capture, parties are named and given percentages; the running total and remainder are always visible.
3. Validator confirms the invariant for that ledger's type; the ledger moves to `proposed`.
4. Each named party is asked to consent to **the whole ledger**, not just their own row — you cannot agree to your 25% without agreeing to the other 75%.
5. All parties consent; ledger moves to `consented` and is locked. Further change requires amendment ([09.02.04](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.04-split-amendment-reconsent.md)).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Shares total 97% | Save blocked. | "3% unallocated — who owns it?" with the remainder shown as a live figure, never as a validation error after the fact. |
| Shares total 105% | Save blocked. | "You've allocated 105%. Someone's share needs to come down." Highlights nothing automatically — the system must not choose whose. |
| Three-way equal split | Exact thirds retained internally; display rounds. | "33.33% each" displayed; internal exactness invisible unless a payout remainder makes it matter. |
| Party listed with 0% | Valid and important — a work-for-hire contributor or a non-featured performer is a real 0% party. | Row shown with "0% — paid by fee, not by share", linking to the WFH designation. |
| Publishing ledger with no publisher rows | Valid: unpublished writers self-hold their publisher share. | Rows auto-created as self-held with an explanation, rather than left blank. Blank is what creates unmatched money. |
| Party is an unregistered person (no account) | Ledger accepts them as an invited stub; state stays `proposed` until they consent. | "Waiting on 1 of 4" with a chase action. |
| A consented party later deletes their account | The consent is a historical fact and survives; the ledger does not revert. | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — interacts with GDPR erasure. See Q-03. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Ledger requested | Skeleton rows |
| Empty (`unallocated`) | Object created, no shares | "Nobody owns this yet" + CTA into capture — deliberately alarming copy |
| Populated (`proposed`) | Shares entered, validated, awaiting consent | Ledger with per-row consent status and "Waiting on N" |
| Populated (`consented`) | All parties consented | Locked ledger with a signed badge and timestamp |
| Error | Invariant violated | Inline remainder indicator; save disabled with the reason stated as a question, not a code |
| Partial (`disputed`) | Conflict raised ([09.04](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/)) | Ledger shown with a dispute banner; payouts frozen ([09.04.03](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/09.04.03-rights-freeze-royalty-escrow.md)) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Percentages can be stored as decimals like every other money-adjacent number | Default engineering instinct | ❌ REJECTED — three co-writers is the most common configuration in music and it is exactly the case decimals cannot represent. A 33.33% × 3 ledger totals 99.99% and either fails validation forever or silently loses a hundredth of every royalty cheque, compounding across a catalogue. Exact rational shares are required. This is the sort of decision that is trivial now and unfixable after a million rows. |
| DT-02 | Consent should be per-row — you approve your own share | Reasonable-sounding permission model | ❌ REJECTED — and this is the key insight of the whole feature. Your 25% is only meaningful relative to the other 75%. A per-row consent model lets a Producer show each contributor only their own line, and every contributor cheerfully approves a sheet that does not total what they think it totals. Consent is to the **whole ledger**, which matches the persona anti-pattern in `meta/personas.md` (a Producer pushing through a larger share "while contributors aren't paying attention"). |
| DT-03 | The dangerous state is an invalid ledger | Validation-centric framing | ❌ REJECTED — the dangerous state is an **absent** one. An invalid ledger is loud and blocked; an unallocated ledger is silent, and the music still gets released, streamed, and paid — into a black box. The MLC's hundreds of millions in unmatched mechanicals is entirely made of absent rows, not wrong ones. The design implication is that `unallocated` must be treated as an alarm state, not a neutral default — hence the empty-state copy. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Consent is to the **whole ledger**, never to a single row | Per-row consent is exploitable by exactly the persona anti-pattern the domain exists to prevent (see DT-02). |
| D-02 | Shares stored as **exact rationals**, not decimals | Three-way splits are the common case and are unrepresentable in decimal (see DT-01). Cheap now, unfixable later. |
| D-03 | Unpublished writers' publisher shares are **auto-created as self-held**, never left blank | Blank publisher rows are the mechanical origin of black-box money. The default must be "you hold it" rather than "unknown". |
| D-04 | The validator holds **arithmetic only**; deal semantics live in sibling features | Prevents the validator degenerating into an unreasonable-about business-rules engine. |
