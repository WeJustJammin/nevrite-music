## Overview

The authoritative store of *what exists* and *who owns it* — the two-object model (a composition/work
and a sound recording/master are separate copyrights with separate owners), the percentage ledger that
holds their shares, and the encumbrances attached to them. Every other sub-domain in Rights & Ownership
reads from or writes to this registry; every downstream domain that pays money, issues a licence, or
publishes a credit resolves against it.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: it contains at least four capabilities that interact with
each other — the work/recording object model, the share-validation engine, the master and publishing
share structures (which follow *different arithmetic*), and sample encumbrances that mutate shares in
the ledger. Changing any one of them changes the others.
