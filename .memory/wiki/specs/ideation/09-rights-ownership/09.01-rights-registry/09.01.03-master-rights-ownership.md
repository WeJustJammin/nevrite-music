# Feature: Master Rights Ownership

> **Number**: 09.01.03
> **Parent**: [Rights Registry](./09.01-rights-registry-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The recording side of the duality: who owns the master, in what proportion, and — the half everyone
omits — **what that ownership actually lets them do**. Master ownership is a single 100% pool, simpler
arithmetic than publishing ([09.01.04](./09.01.04-publishing-rights.md)) and more contested in practice,
because the master is the asset labels acquire and artists spend careers trying to get back.

The feature exists because **owning a master is not the same as controlling it**. An artist can own 100%
of a master and be unable to release it, unable to licence it for AI training, unable to re-record the
song, and unable to open the multitrack — each for a different reason, each recorded in a different
place. This feature is the one node that sees all of those reasons at once, so it is the only node that
can answer the question the owner actually has: *what can I do with this, and who is stopping me?*

That question is unanswerable by any point solution, because no point solution holds the ownership pool,
the distribution grant, the producer's points, the sampled-master obligation, the performers' consent,
and the session assets simultaneously. Per [D-18](../../ideation-index.md), this is the thesis in
miniature: consolidation is what makes the provenance record *useful* rather than merely archival.

## Role Lens

> Access is **persona × party-status**. The persona sets the ceiling; being a party to this master sets
> the floor. A Musician who is not a party to a given master sees nothing of it — the ✅ Full in the
> [sub-domain Role Matrix](./09.01-rights-registry-index.md) is the ceiling for a party, not a
> platform-wide read.

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The default owner. Names the master's owners and their percentages; sees whether they own it outright (the independent default), share it with a band entity, or have assigned it to a label. **Sees the Control Summary** — the per-action verdict table that names which recorded term blocks which action, rather than leaving them to infer it from a contract they may not hold a copy of. Sees their **effective net receipt** after the encumbrance waterfall (owning 100% and receiving 73.15% is the normal case, not an error). Proposes transfers and encumbrances; consents to the whole ledger, never to their own row alone ([09.01.02 D-01](./09.01.02-ownership-ledger-validation.md)). Can override a block, with attribution and notification, per D-06 below. |
| Producer | Full | Two distinct positions, and the file must never merge them. **As co-owner** (common in independent arrangements): identical access to the Musician, and the party who typically opens the master ledger during capture, since they create the recording record. **As a points-only holder** (the more common case): they hold a royalty participation and own 0% — see [09.02.02](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.02-producer-points-agreements.md). A points-only holder sees the **owner set** (who currently owes them, so they know who to chase and can tell when the debt moves on a sale) and their own encumbrance's terms and recoupment status — but **not the ownership split among the owners**, which is commercially sensitive and irrelevant to a claim that runs against the master rather than against any one owner. |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees master ownership **only** where the studio entity is itself a named party — the studio holding rights via work-for-hire over its staff engineer ([09.02.03](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.03-work-for-hire-buyout.md)), or the "studio takes points in lieu of a fee" arrangement (then it is an encumbrance holder and sees exactly what a points-only Producer sees). Read-only because the studio's stake **originates in an agreement, not in an edit to this ledger** — it is configured once at 09.02.03 and lands here as a consequence. Where no such agreement exists, the Operator sees nothing. Per the [domain Role Matrix](../rights-ownership-index.md), **no bespoke Operator surface is built here** — the studio arrives through the generic named-entity party path (see D-11). |
| Fan | None | No access. Ownership percentages and deal terms are commercially sensitive and asymmetrically damaging, and the Fan has no professional stake ([`meta/personas.md`](../../meta/personas.md)). The *derived* fact "released by X" is already public on any released record and may surface through the separate public projection at [09.06.04](../09.06-rights-evidence-public-record/09.06.04-public-rights-lookup.md) — subject to [domain Q-06](../rights-ownership-index.md). That is a different product wearing the same brand, not a filtered view of this one. |

## Behavior

A master record carries **three structurally different things** over one recording, and the whole feature
turns on not conflating them.

### 1. The ownership pool

A set of `(party, share, consent-state)` rows totalling exactly 100%, validated by
[09.01.02](./09.01.02-ownership-ledger-validation.md) (shares as exact rationals; consent to the whole
ledger; `unallocated` → `proposed` → `consented` → `disputed` | `superseded`). Parties are people **or
entities** from [domain 01](../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/) — the band-as-entity case matters
here, because a band owning a master collectively is a different fact from four members owning 25% each,
and the difference only becomes visible when the band dissolves.

Ownership confers exactly four things, and this list is the definition: the right to **exploit**, the
right to **licence**, standing in the **chain of title**, and a **vote** on actions requiring owner
consent. Anything that does not confer all four is not ownership.

**The consent rule is recorded, not inferred.** Jurisdictions disagree about what joint owners may do
alone — US co-owners may generally grant non-exclusive licences unilaterally subject to a duty to
account; several other regimes require unanimity for any licence. The platform does **not** pick a
jurisdiction's default on the parties' behalf (D-04, and see DT-10). The master records the rule the
parties agreed — `unanimous` | `majority-by-share` | `any-owner-non-exclusive` — and where they agreed
nothing, it defaults to **`unanimous`**, because unanimity is the only default that cannot strip someone
of a right they never agreed to give up.

### 2. Encumbrances — attach to the asset, travel with it

Things that constrain what an owner may do, or reduce what they receive, **without changing who owns
it**. The defining property: on a transfer, the buyer **takes subject to them** ([09.02.02 edge case:
points survive the sale](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.02-producer-points-agreements.md)).

| Encumbrance | What it does | Recorded at |
|---|---|---|
| **Exclusive grant** | A distribution or licence exclusive for a right-type, grantee, term and territory. Blocks the owner from exploiting into the same right × window × territory. | [09.03.02](../09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle/09.03.02-term-territory-reversion.md), surfaced here |
| **Royalty participation** | An off-the-top royalty: point count, **base** (required, never defaulted), recoupability, term. The holder is **not always a producer** — mixers, featured artists ("feat. X" is typically points, not ownership), and studio entities take them on the same instrument. | [09.02.02](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.02-producer-points-agreements.md) |
| **Sample master-use obligation** | A royalty or fixed sum owed to a sampled recording's owner. | [09.01.06](./09.01.06-sample-interpolation-provenance.md) |
| **Security interest** | The master pledged as collateral against an advance (catalogue financing). Constrains sale and may redirect receipts. | This feature — see Q-05 |

### 3. Covenants — bind a party, do **not** travel

Structurally different from encumbrances and the distinction is invisible until a sale, which is exactly
when it costs money (see DT-05). A covenant constrains a **person**, not an asset:

| Covenant | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Re-recording restriction** | The artist may not re-record the work for N years after term end. It does not attach to the old master (the label owns that) nor to the new one (which is clean) — it binds the artist. |
| **Exclusive recording services** | The artist may not deliver a master to anyone else during a term. Constrains *future* masters, not this one. |

The master view surfaces both encumbrances and covenants and **labels which is which**, because a buyer
takes the first and not the second, and no party can tell them apart from the face of the agreement.

### 4. The Control Summary — derived, never stored

The answer to "what can I do with this?" is **computed from the above and never asserted** (D-01 below).
Control is not a thing anyone owns; it is a consequence. A stored control record would need its own
authority and could disagree with the encumbrances it was supposed to summarise.

Each action gets exactly one of **three** verdicts — never two:

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Blocked by _\<named term\>_** | A recorded, consented term is unambiguously breached by this action, decidable by date arithmetic, territory set membership, and right-type match. Always names the term, the holder, and the expiry. |
| **No recorded obstacle** | Nothing in the record blocks this. **Never rendered as "clear", "allowed", or "free."** |
| **Unknown — terms not recorded** | The relevant term is absent, or its source is unreachable. |

The second and third verdicts are different, and keeping them different is the point — this is
[domain D-06 (*silence must read as silence*)](../rights-ownership-index.md) applied to the highest-stakes
question in the feature. Rendering "no encumbrances recorded" as "you are free to release" fabricates a
clearance nobody gave, and does it to the person the record exists to protect. **Degradation fails to
`Unknown`, never to `No recorded obstacle`** — a network failure reading domain 12's grants must not
manufacture a clean bill of health.

The actions evaluated:

| Action | Blocked by |
|---|---|
| Release / distribute | An exclusive grant covering the target territory in the target window ([12](../../12-release-distribution/)); `unallocated` or `disputed` ledger |
| Grant a non-exclusive master licence | The recorded consent rule; `disputed` ledger |
| Grant an **exclusive** master licence | An existing overlapping exclusive; the consent rule (unanimity under the default) |
| Clear a **sync** | Master clearance alone is insufficient — a sync needs **both sides**; publishing clearance is [09.01.04](./09.01.04-publishing-rights.md) / [11](../../11-music-licensing/) |
| Licence for **AI training** | Performers' NIL non-consent ([09.05.02](../09.05-ai-voice-likeness-consent/09.05.02-voice-name-likeness-rights.md)) — see DT-09 |
| Sell / assign | The consent rule; a security interest |
| Take down / withdraw | An exclusive grant's delivery obligations |
| Make a derivative (remix / edit) | The consent rule (a derivative requires the source master owners' authorisation — DT-11) |
| **Re-record the work** | A re-recording **covenant** on a *different* agreement, surfaced here though it attaches nowhere on this master |
| Mutilate / materially alter | A recorded moral-rights assertion ([09.03.06](../09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle/09.03.06-moral-rights-attribution.md)) — surfaced, never evaluated for integrity (09.03.06 D-02) |

**Every block is overridable** by an owner with an attested reason, recorded as a chain event and
notified to the encumbrance holder (D-06 below). The platform's record may be stale or incomplete; a
platform that can permanently bar an owner from their own asset on the strength of its own bookkeeping
has stopped holding facts and started adjudicating ([D-04](../rights-ownership-index.md)).

### 5. The encumbrance waterfall and effective net

Encumbrance burden **cannot be summed** — the bases differ (DT-03). Encumbrances tier by their recorded
base ([09.02.02 D-01](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.02-producer-points-agreements.md) makes
base a required term, which is the only reason this is computable at all):

```
gross receipts  →  net receipts  →  owners' share
```

**Invariant** (owned by this feature, not by the validator — [09.01.02 D-04](./09.01.02-ownership-ledger-validation.md)
scopes the validator to the ledger's own arithmetic): *within each tier, that tier's participations must
not exceed 100% of that tier's pool.* Cross-tier addition is invalid and is blocked as a category error,
not as a total.

Worked example — 3 points of gross to a producer, a 20%-of-gross sample master-use obligation, and 5
points of the owners' share to a featured artist:

| Tier | Burden | Remaining |
|---|---|---|
| Gross | 3% + 20% = **23%** ≤ 100 ✅ | 77% |
| Net | — | 77% |
| Owners' share | 5% × 77% = **3.85%** | **73.15%** |

The ownership pool splits **73.15% of gross**, not 100%. An owner holding 100% who receives 73.15% is
the normal case; the view states the arithmetic rather than letting the owner discover it on a statement.

### 6. Provenance class — how the record came to exist

A master ledger carries a provenance class, and the view never renders one as another (DT-08):

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `captured` | All parties consented at or near creation, in the room. The wedge. |
| `asserted` | Entered retroactively by one party; no counterparty consented, because there is none reachable or none joined. |
| `imported` | Ingested from a DSP, distributor or CMS feed; nobody on the platform attested to it. |

### Happy Path

1. Producer creates the recording record ([09.01.01](./09.01.01-work-recording-duality.md)); its master ledger opens `unallocated`, its publishing ledger opens alongside — **never one alone** ([CX-06](./09.01-rights-registry-cx.md)).
2. During capture ([09.02.01](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.01-moment-of-creation-split-capture.md)), the master ledger is presented beside the publishing ledger, each labelled with its own total (100% / 200%) so the two are visibly different objects rather than two tabs of one thing.
3. Parties and shares are entered; the independent default is 100% to the artist or the band entity. Running total and remainder are live.
4. The consent rule is presented with `unanimous` preselected; changing it is a deliberate act with its consequence stated ("*any owner could then licence this without asking you*").
5. The Producer's compensation is marked as **points, not share** — the flow refuses the word "share" for a points holder, since the colloquialism is the dispute (DT-01). Base is required.
6. Any other participations (featured artist, mixer, studio) are entered on the same instrument.
7. The waterfall is computed live and shown as **effective net per owner** — "you own 100%, you receive 73.15%" — before anyone consents, not after.
8. Every named party consents to the **whole** master ledger plus its encumbrance list. Provenance class is set to `captured`.
9. Ledger locks to `consented`. Further change routes to amendment ([09.02.04](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.04-split-amendment-reconsent.md)).
10. The master detail view shows ownership, the encumbrance/covenant list with the labels intact, the effective-net waterfall, the asset custody indicator (DT-07), and the **Control Summary** — the three-verdict action table.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Band owns the master as an entity | Supported — the band entity is the single 100% party. Member-level entitlement is governed by the band agreement in [domain 01](../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/), not here. | "Owned by *The Band* (entity)" with a link to the band's governance, making the member/entity boundary explicit rather than assumed. |
| Band dissolves | The rights registry does **not** decide the outcome; the band agreement does. | Master shows the band entity with a "governance in flux" pointer to domain 01. Deliberately no opinion — see Q-02 for the freeze-vs-live decision. |
| Artist owns 100% but cannot release | Correct and intended: an exclusive distribution encumbrance blocks it. | "**Blocked by** *Distribution exclusive to X — worldwide, until 2029-03-01*. You own 100%; this is why you can't release it here." Naming the term, the holder and the date is the whole point. |
| Artist owns 100%, no encumbrances recorded, wants to release | **Not** cleared. The record is silent, and silence is not permission. | "**No recorded obstacle** — WeJammin holds no distribution terms for this master. That is not the same as clear." (D-06.) |
| Domain 12's grant store is unreachable when the Control Summary renders | Verdict degrades to `Unknown`. Never to `No recorded obstacle`. | "**Unknown** — couldn't read distribution terms. Retry." Actions gated on that verdict stay gated. |
| Master assigned to a label | Ownership rows change; the transfer is recorded in [09.03.01](../09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle/09.03.01-chain-of-title-ledger.md). Encumbrances travel; covenants do not. | Transfer requires consent per the recorded rule; history visible forever; the buyer's "what you take" preview lists encumbrances **and** explicitly lists the covenants they do *not* take. |
| Re-recording made to escape an owned-elsewhere master | Fully supported as a rights object — new recording, same work, new 100% pool, clean of the old master's encumbrances. **But** the *right to make it* may be barred by a re-recording covenant on the original agreement. | New master's ledger is independent; the Control Summary on it surfaces "**Blocked by** *re-recording restriction — 2019 agreement with X, expires 2027-03-01*" — a constraint that attaches nowhere on this master and would be invisible to any tool holding only this master. |
| Remix of an existing master | New recording, new 100% pool, plus a **derivation link** to the source master. The remixer typically owns 0% and takes points. | "Derived from *[source master]* — authorisation required from its owners." The source's owners appear as a consent gate, not as owners of the remix. |
| Producer claims ownership; artist believes it was points | Ledger enters `disputed` via [09.04](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/); payouts freeze ([09.04.03](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/09.04.03-rights-freeze-royalty-escrow.md)). | Dispute banner. The most common master-side conflict, and the reason points and shares must be structurally different objects (DT-01). |
| Same person is an owner **and** a points holder on one master | Allowed and real. They are counted **once** as an owner for the consent rule; their points and their share are paid separately. | "You own 40% and hold 3 points" shown as two rows, not netted — because on a sale the share leaves and the points stay. |
| Self-producing artist takes points off their own 100% | Warn, do not block. It is arithmetically circular *today* and entirely rational *tomorrow* — the points survive a future sale of the master, so the artist keeps earning from a master they no longer own. | "You're taking 3 points from yourself. That's usually a mistake — but it isn't if you plan to sell, since the points would survive. Confirm?" |
| Encumbrance holder deletes their own points before a sale | Permitted — a holder may **release** their own encumbrance; recorded as an event, never as an erasure. | "Released by *[holder]* on *[date]*" persists in the history. |
| An **owner** attempts to delete an encumbrance | Blocked. Consented encumbrances are append-only: they expire by their own terms or are released by their holder. | "Only *[holder]* can release these points, or they expire 2031-01-01." Hiding a producer's points before a sale is exactly the attack this closes. |
| Minority owner (10%) adds an encumbrance to sabotage a release | Blocked under the default rule. Creating an encumbrance requires the same consent as granting the right it encumbers. | "Adding this needs all 3 owners' consent — your master's rule is *unanimous*." |
| Gross-tier participations sum to 105% | Blocked. | "Off-the-top claims on gross total 105%. Someone's has to come down." The system highlights nothing automatically — it must not choose whose. |
| Someone tries to sum 60 points of gross + 60 points of the owners' share and is blocked | **Not** blocked — this is valid. 60% of gross leaves 40%; 60% of that 40% is 24%; owners receive 16%. | The waterfall is shown tier by tier. A flat sum would have wrongly rejected a legal deal. |
| Two owners open competing transfer proposals simultaneously | Only **one** open ownership proposal per master. The second is refused at open, not at save. | "*Sam* opened a proposal 4 minutes ago. Review it, or ask them to withdraw." |
| A party amends the ledger after others have consented | **All** prior consents void. Consent is to the whole ledger; the whole ledger changed. | "This resets 3 of 4 consents. Continue?" — stated before the edit lands, not discovered after. |
| An encumbrance is added while an ownership proposal is in flight | The proposal's effective-net figures change under the consenters' feet. Prior consents void, same rule. | "The points on this master changed. Your consent has been reset — the numbers you agreed to have moved." |
| A party is an unregistered person with no account | Accepted as an invited stub; ledger stays `proposed`. | "Waiting on 1 of 4" with a chase action. |
| A consented owner is an **entity that has since dissolved** (label liquidated, company struck off) | Their share does **not** vanish — it is an asset of the liquidation and is commonly sold. Share marked `unreachable`; receipts to it accrue to escrow ([09.04.03](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/09.04.03-rights-freeze-royalty-escrow.md)); every action requiring their consent is blocked and names them. | "**Blocked by** *[Defunct Ltd] — 30% owner, unreachable*. Your master's rule is unanimous." The platform does **not** apply deemed consent — see [domain Q-07](../rights-ownership-index.md). |
| A consented owner dies | Routes to [09.03.04](../09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle/09.03.04-estate-succession-catalog-legacy.md). No probate here. | Share shown as in-succession; consent gate names it. |
| Ledger `consented`, but no session assets are linked or the multitracks sit with a party who is not an owner | Master shows an **asset custody indicator** (DT-07). The platform never auto-transfers files — that would be adjudicating a delivery dispute. | "You own 100%. **The multitracks are held by *[Producer]*.**" Naming it is the remedy; taking them is not. |
| Producer withholds stems as leverage in a payment dispute (`meta/personas.md` anti-persona) | Not resolved here. The custody indicator makes the fact **visible and dated**, which is the difference between a dispute about what happened and a dispute about what is happening. | Custody indicator plus a route to [09.04](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/). |
| A DMCA claim asserts master ownership contradicting a `consented` ledger | The ledger does not defend itself and the platform does not rule ([D-04](../rights-ownership-index.md)). It routes to [09.04.01](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/09.04.01-conflicting-claim-detection.md) and [domain 24](../../24-trust-safety/) — carrying the ledger as **evidence**, which is the entire value of it being `captured` rather than `asserted`. | Conflict raised; the ledger's provenance class and consent timestamps are surfaced to both sides. |
| Legacy master entered retroactively by one artist, no counterparties reachable | Accepted as provenance class `asserted`. It is **not** rendered with the signed badge a `captured` ledger earns. | "Asserted by *[artist]* on *[date]* — not consented by other parties." A self-claim about a 20-year-old master must never look like a split signed in the room (DT-08). |
| Two master records claim to be the same recording, with different ledgers | Duplicate detection routes to [09.04.01](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/09.04.01-conflicting-claim-detection.md). Ledgers are **reconciled, never concatenated** ([CX-01](./09.01-rights-registry-cx.md)). | "This looks like *[existing recording]*. Two ledgers disagree." |
| Master with **no linked work** (field recording, sound effect, unstructured improvisation) | The master pool is valid alone; it does not require a work. The general duality rule is [09.01.01](./09.01.01-work-recording-duality.md)'s ([09.01 Q-02](./09.01-rights-registry-index.md)) — this is only its master-side consequence. | No phantom publishing ledger is created, and no "incomplete" warning is shown for its absence. |
| Owner overrides a block ("I bought out of that deal, you just don't know it") | Permitted, attested, recorded as a chain event, and **notified to the encumbrance holder**. Never silent. | "You're overriding *Distribution exclusive to X*. X will be told. Give a reason." — see Q-06 on whether notification is a sufficient remedy for X. |
| Owner is also the sole performer and refuses AI-training consent, then licences it for AI training | No conflict: the same human wearing both hats consents once, in each capacity, and the record says which. | Both records shown; the multi-hyphenate is not asked twice for the same fact, but each fact is stored under the right head. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Master view requested | Skeleton: owner rows, encumbrance list, and a Control Summary placeholder that reads **neither** blocked nor clear |
| Empty (`unallocated`) | Recording exists, ownership unallocated | "**Nobody owns this master yet**" + capture CTA. Deliberately alarming — the absent ledger is the dangerous one, not the invalid one ([09.01.02 DT-03](./09.01.02-ownership-ledger-validation.md)) |
| Populated (`proposed`) | Shares + encumbrances entered, awaiting consent | Ledger with per-row consent status, live effective-net waterfall, "Waiting on N of M" |
| Populated (`consented`) | All parties consented | Locked ledger, signed badge with timestamp **and provenance class**, encumbrance/covenant list with labels, Control Summary, custody indicator |
| Partial (encumbrances unknown) | Ownership `consented`, no distribution terms recorded | Ownership shown; Control Summary reads `Unknown` for the affected actions with "Distribution terms not recorded" — **not** "no obstacle" |
| Partial (degraded) | An encumbrance source (12, 11, 09.03.02) unreachable | Affected verdicts read `Unknown` with a retry; unaffected verdicts render normally; nothing silently upgrades to clear |
| Error | Write failed | Inline retry; entered data preserved; consent state unchanged (a failed write must never look like a landed consent) |
| Disputed / frozen | Conflict raised via [09.04](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/) | Dispute banner; payouts frozen; Control Summary blocks every exploitation action naming the dispute, not the counterparty's argument |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Producer points can be modelled as a small ownership share (e.g. 3 points = 3%) | It is how they are colloquially discussed, and it would simplify the model to one pool | ❌ REJECTED — points are a **royalty off the top**, not an ownership interest. A producer with 3 points cannot license the master, cannot block a sync, does not vote on a sale, and is not in the chain of title. Modelling them as ownership grants all four by accident. This conflation is one of the most common real-world master disputes, so a platform whose thesis is provenance cannot afford to reproduce it in its schema. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect the **ownership vs control** gap to be surfaced explicitly — and nothing in the candidate list covers it | Artists routinely discover they own a master they cannot exploit. The candidate list had "Master Rights Ownership" as a bare noun; ownership without control is a half-truth that reads as a lie the first time it bites. | ✅ CONFIRMED — encumbrances are first-class and the Control Summary answers "what can I actually do with this?" rather than only "whose is it?". A consolidation payoff: only a platform that also holds the distribution and licence records can answer it at all. |
| DT-03 | Total encumbrance burden can be validated by **summing** the participations (e.g. "points must not exceed 100") | [09.02.02](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.02-producer-points-agreements.md) validates points ≤ 100 per agreement; the obvious generalisation is to sum them across the master | ❌ REJECTED — **the bases differ, so the numbers are not addable.** 3 points *of gross* and 60 points *of the owners' share* are not 63 of anything. Summing them rejects legal deals (60 gross + 60 owners'-share = 16% to owners, perfectly valid) and accepts illegal ones. The correct model is a **base-tiered waterfall** with a per-tier ≤100% invariant. Note the dependency: this is computable **only** because 09.02.02 D-01 makes base a required term — a defaulted base would have made the master's aggregate burden permanently unknowable, which is a cost of that default nobody costed. This feature is the only node that sees every encumbrance on one master, so the invariant is its job. |
| DT-04 | Encumbrances and covenants are the same kind of thing — both are "constraints on what you can do" — and belong in one list | They present identically in the UI and both answer "why can't I?" | ❌ REJECTED — they behave **oppositely at the only moment it matters**. Encumbrances attach to the asset and the buyer takes subject to them; covenants bind a person and do **not** transfer. Put them in one list and every sale preview is wrong in one direction or the other: either the buyer is told they inherit a re-recording restriction they don't, or the seller is told they shed one they can't. The distinction is invisible on the face of the agreements and visible only at transfer — which is precisely why the platform must hold it and the parties won't. |
| DT-05 | A re-recording is unconstrained because the new master is a new object with a clean pool | The obvious reading of the duality, and what this file said at `[SURFACE]` | ❌ REJECTED — **this file was wrong.** The new master is clean; the *right to make it* frequently is not. Re-recording restrictions are standard in record deals and are the reason the most famous re-recording campaign in the industry waited years to start. The restriction attaches to neither master — it binds the artist through an agreement about a *different* recording. So the Control Summary must surface a constraint that lives nowhere in this master's own record. No tool holding only this master could ever show it; a tool holding the artist's agreements can. |
| DT-06 | A performer's neighbouring-rights entitlement ([09.01.05](./09.01.05-performer-neighbouring-rights.md)) is an encumbrance on the master | Both are "someone gets paid because of this recording", and the master is where money-out is modelled | ❌ REJECTED — non-featured performer remuneration is paid **by the exploiter, via a society**, not out of the owner's receipts. Modelling it here would (a) wrongly reduce the owner's effective net, making every forecast wrong, and (b) wrongly imply the owner owes the drummer, inventing a debt between two parties who have no financial relationship. Consistent with [CX-04](./09.01-rights-registry-cx.md) and [R-01](./09.01-rights-registry-cx.md): they are disjoint records over one recording. The temptation is real and the error would be expensive. |
| DT-07 | A domain expert would expect the master record to answer **"do I have the files?"** — and nobody has mentioned it anywhere in the domain | "Give me my masters" is, in its original and literal sense, a demand for **tapes**. Ownership and possession are routinely severed: artists own masters whose multitracks sit on a producer's drive or a defunct studio's server. `meta/personas.md` names withholding stems as the Producer's intentional anti-persona behaviour. Every rights database in existence models the deed and ignores the asset. | ✅ CONFIRMED — the master carries an **asset custody indicator** derived from [domain 07](../../07-projects-collaboration/): `held on platform` / `held by [party]` / `no assets linked`. It **names** the gap and never closes it — auto-transferring files would be the platform adjudicating a delivery dispute (D-04). This is the sharpest available instance of DT-02's ownership-vs-control thesis, and an unusually clean consolidation payoff: only a platform that holds both the deed and the drive can even ask the question. |
| DT-08 | A master ledger is a master ledger — how it came to exist is metadata | Uniform rendering is simpler and every record is "the record" | ❌ REJECTED — **it poisons the asset.** WeJammin's entire claim is that its records are captured-at-source evidence (D-18). If a retroactive self-assertion about a 20-year-old master renders with the same signed badge as a split four people consented to in the room, then the badge means nothing, and every `captured` record is devalued by every `asserted` one standing next to it. Hence the **provenance class** (`captured` / `asserted` / `imported`), never conflated in display. Generalising this across the whole registry is a Step 6 cross-cut — here it is decided for the master. |
| DT-09 | Licensing the master for **AI training** is a decision the master owner can make alone — it is their recording | It is the intuitive reading of ownership, and it is how every AI-training licensing deal has been structured to date | ❌ REJECTED — and this is the **strongest available proof of DT-02**. [09.05.02](../09.05-ai-voice-likeness-consent/09.05.02-voice-name-likeness-rights.md) establishes that NIL survives a buyout; a voice is not conveyed by a master. So a 100% owner cannot licence a recording for voice cloning over the performers' non-consent — a session singer who owns nothing can block a deal the owner is otherwise free to make. This is [domain D-07](../rights-ownership-index.md) exactly: the candidate sweep saw rights through the owner's eye and lost every right belonging to a non-owner. It is also the most commercially live instance of ownership ≠ control today. |
| DT-10 | The platform should apply the **jurisdiction's default rule** for what joint owners may do alone | It is the legally correct answer, and the platform could infer jurisdiction from the parties' locations | ❌ REJECTED — inferring jurisdiction is an interpretation, and acting on it means the platform silently imports US co-ownership law onto a UK band and hands one member a unilateral licensing power their bandmates never agreed to. That is the platform ruling ([D-04](../rights-ownership-index.md)) and executing on an inference ([D-05](../rights-ownership-index.md)) in one move. The master **records the parties' agreed rule** and defaults to `unanimous` — the only default whose failure mode is "you have to ask" rather than "you were stripped." |
| DT-11 | A remix is an **alternate version** of the same master | It is the same song, often the same stems, and it feels like a variant rather than a new thing | ❌ REJECTED — a remix is a **new recording** (its own ISRC, its own owners, its own encumbrances) that is **derivative** of a source master. Model it as a version and it inherits the source's ledger, so the remixer either becomes an accidental co-owner of the original or is erased from their own work. Model it as an unrelated new master and the source owners' authorisation gate disappears. It needs both: a new 100% pool **plus** a derivation link that makes the source owners a consent gate on creation without making them owners of the result. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Points are encumbrances, not shares** | Modelling points as ownership silently grants licensing, blocking, and chain-of-title standing (DT-01). Generalised: the instrument is a **royalty participation**, not a producer-specific object — featured artists, mixers and studios hold the same thing. |
| D-02 | The master view surfaces **ownership and control together**, and control is **derived, never stored** | Ownership alone is a half-truth. A stored control record would need its own authority and could disagree with the encumbrances it summarises. Resolves Q-01 at the behavioural level. |
| D-03 | **Encumbrances attach to the asset and travel; covenants bind a party and do not** | The distinction is invisible on the face of the agreements and decisive at sale, which is the one moment nobody can afford it to be wrong (DT-04, DT-05). |
| D-04 | Encumbrance burden is a **base-tiered waterfall** with a per-tier ≤100% invariant — never a flat sum | Bases differ, so the numbers are not addable; a flat sum rejects legal deals and accepts illegal ones (DT-03). Computable only because 09.02.02 D-01 requires base. |
| D-05 | The Control Summary has **three verdicts**, and `No recorded obstacle` is never rendered as "clear" | [Domain D-06](../rights-ownership-index.md) — silence must read as silence — applied to the highest-stakes question in the feature. Degradation fails to `Unknown`, never to clear. |
| D-06 | **Every block is overridable** by an owner, attested, recorded, and notified to the holder | The platform's record may be stale. Permanently barring an owner from their own asset on the strength of its own bookkeeping is adjudication ([domain D-04](../rights-ownership-index.md)). Notification is what stops the override being a silent breach. |
| D-07 | The **joint-owner consent rule is recorded, defaulting to `unanimous`** — never inferred from jurisdiction | Inferring imports one country's law onto another's band and hands a member a power nobody granted (DT-10). `unanimous` fails toward "ask", not toward "stripped". |
| D-08 | The master carries an **asset custody indicator**; the platform names the custody gap and never closes it | Ownership and possession are routinely severed and no rights database models it (DT-07). Auto-transferring files would be adjudicating a delivery dispute. |
| D-09 | Master ledgers carry a **provenance class**; `asserted` never renders as `captured` | If a retroactive self-claim wears the same badge as a split signed in the room, the badge is worthless and every genuine record is devalued (DT-08). |
| D-10 | Band dissolution outcomes are **out of scope here** — deferred to domain 01 governance | A rights registry adjudicating partnership law is out of its lane; it records the outcome, it does not determine it. |
| D-11 | **No bespoke Operator surface is built in this feature** | The studio arrives as a generic named entity via 09.02.03 and reads through the encumbrance-holder or owner projection. Matches the [domain Role Matrix](../rights-ownership-index.md) finding that the Operator's explicit absence is the useful output. Resolves Q-03. |
| D-12 | A **remix is a new recording with a derivation link**, not a version of the source master | A version model makes the remixer an accidental co-owner or erases them; an unlinked model loses the source owners' authorisation gate (DT-11). |
