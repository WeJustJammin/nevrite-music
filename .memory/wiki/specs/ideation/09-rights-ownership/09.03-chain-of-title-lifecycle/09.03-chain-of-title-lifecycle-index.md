## Overview

Rights move and rights expire. This sub-domain holds everything that happens to ownership **after** it
is first established: the unbroken history of transfers (chain of title), the term and territory limits
on each grant, the two ways rights come back (contractual reversion and statutory termination), what
happens when an owner dies, when copyright finally runs out, and the author's rights that survive every
transfer regardless (moral rights).

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: these capabilities are densely interdependent. Term
determination decides whether public domain applies; reversion and termination are two different
mechanisms returning rights to two different people on two different clocks; succession transfers them;
and the chain of title is the ledger that records every one of those events. You cannot specify any one
of them without the others.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | **Reversion and termination kept as separate features** despite both "returning rights" | They are different instruments on different clocks with different eligibility. Reversion is **contractual** — it happens because a deal says so, and its terms are whatever was negotiated. Termination is **statutory** — a US Copyright Act right to undo a grant after ~35 years, which cannot be contracted away and operates on a fixed notice window. Merging them would produce a feature that is contractual and statutory at once, which is incoherent. | Node Classification Gate; `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | **Moral rights placed here**, not in the registry | Moral rights are defined by what they do to the *lifecycle*: they survive assignment. An author who sells 100% of their work keeps the right of attribution in most of Europe. That is a chain-of-title property — "this right did not transfer with the others" — not a share in a ledger. | Node Classification Gate |
| D-03 | **Public domain determination folded into copyright term**, not a separate node | Public domain is not an independent capability; it is the *terminal state* of a term calculation. A separate node would be a feature whose entire behaviour is "the other feature returned zero". | Merge; `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-04 | **Territory model flagged as the sub-domain's structural risk** | Term, reversion, termination, public domain and moral rights are *all* territory-varying, and they vary differently from each other. If [09.01](../09.01-rights-registry/)'s ledger has no territory dimension, every feature here degrades to a US-or-nothing approximation. | Deep Think; see Q-01 |
| D-05 | **CQ-09 v1 term/moral-right status covers `US`, `FR`, `DE`, and `GB` only** | `09.03.05` owns source-backed copyright-term/public-domain results; `09.03.06` owns source-backed moral-right applicability/waivability; `09.03.04` remains authority for estate/death facts. Missing facts and all other territories are explicit `unknown` / not determined. Economic transfers never move moral rights, and no result is global, legal clearance, ownership adjudication, or release authorization. | Owner decision + source-contract recheck, 2026-07-21 |
