# Feature: Moral Rights & Attribution Rights

> **Number**: 09.03.06
> **Parent**: [Chain of Title & Rights Lifecycle](./09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Moral rights are the author's rights that **do not transfer**. In France, Germany and much of Europe
they are perpetual and non-waivable: an author who sells 100% of their work keeps the right to be
**named as its author** (attribution) and to object to **derogatory treatment** of it (integrity). The
US grants no general moral rights in music, which is why the concept is invisible to most of the
industry's tooling — and why an artist discovers it only when a European use goes wrong.

For this domain the point is structural: moral rights are the reason a chain of title showing a full
assignment is **not** a complete picture. Something stayed behind.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The author. Sees which of their moral rights persist in which territories after a transfer, and that they cannot sign them away in the territories where they are non-waivable — regardless of what a contract they signed says. Asserts a moral-rights objection when a use misattributes or mutilates their work; that objection routes to [09.04](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/) and [domain 24](../../24-trust-safety-disputes/), not to a rights ledger, because the remedy is not a percentage. |
| Producer | Read-only | Sees moral-rights constraints attached to works they are handling — relevant when commissioning a remix, an edit, or a sync that alters the work. Read-only because they hold no moral right in someone else's composition; where they authored something themselves, they act as a Musician. |
| Operator | None | No authorship, no moral rights. Explicit absence. |
| Fan | None | No visibility. The **outcome** of the attribution right — the author's name being present on the work — is fan-facing via [domain 02](../../02-credits-attribution/), but the right itself is not a fan surface. |

## Behavior

Moral rights are recorded as a **persistence property** of an authorship relationship rather than as a
share. When a chain-of-title event transfers economic rights, the moral rights do not move — the chain
must be able to express "everything transferred except this" (see
[09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle-cx.md](./09.03-chain-of-title-lifecycle-cx.md) CX-05), or it silently
asserts a completeness it does not have.

The two rights behave differently:

- **Attribution** — the right to be named. Mostly satisfied *automatically* if [domain 02](../../02-credits-attribution/) is doing its job, since the credit travels with the work. This is a rare case where a legal right is discharged by a product working correctly.
- **Integrity** — the right to object to derogatory treatment. Cannot be automated. It is a judgement about whether a use harms the author's honour or reputation, and only the author can make it. The platform's job is to route the objection, not to evaluate it.

Territory is decisive again: non-waivable in France, waivable by contract in the UK, largely absent for
music in the US. A single global answer is wrong.

### Happy Path

1. Musician assigns 100% of a work's publishing to a publisher.
2. The chain records the transfer; the work shows the publisher as owner.
3. The work **also** shows: "Moral rights retained by X — attribution and integrity, non-waivable in FR/DE."
4. A licensee commissions a remix; the platform surfaces the retained integrity right before the licence is granted, not after the remix ships.
5. Attribution is satisfied automatically because the credit travels with the work.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Contract purports to waive non-waivable rights | Recorded as a term; the platform does **not** treat the right as gone. | "This agreement waives moral rights — not effective in FR/DE." Contracts routinely include waivers that do not work in the territories that matter most. |
| Author objects to a derogatory treatment | Routes to [09.04](../09.04-rights-conflicts-disputes/) / [domain 24](../../24-trust-safety-disputes/). The platform does not evaluate the merits. | Objection recorded; the use is flagged. Not a ledger correction — no percentage will fix it. |
| Work-for-hire author in a moral-rights territory | Buyout does not extinguish moral rights where they are non-waivable. | Retained rights shown despite the buyout — the same lesson as [09.02.03](../09.02-split-capture-agreements/09.02.03-work-for-hire-buyout.md) DT-02: the fee buys the copyright, not the fact. |
| US-only user | No general moral rights in music. | "Not applicable in your territory" — stated, not silently omitted. A user who ships to Europe needs to know it exists. |
| Attribution stripped downstream (a DSP drops the credit) | The right is engaged, and the platform can actually **detect** it — it knows the credit and can see the delivery. | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` on detection and remedy. Notable: this is a right whose breach nobody currently notices. |
| Author dead; moral rights perpetual in some territories | Estate asserts them ([09.03.04](./09.03.04-estate-succession-catalog-legacy.md)). | Estate shown as the asserting party. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This belongs in [domain 02 Credits & Attribution](../../02-credits-attribution/) — it is literally called the right of attribution | The name is a strong pull, and the outcomes overlap | ❌ REJECTED — a credit is a **product feature** (a name displayed next to a work); the attribution right is a **legal persistence property** (a right that survives assignment and cannot be contracted away in some territories). They meet at the outcome and diverge everywhere else: domain 02 knows nothing about waivability, territory, or the integrity right, which has no attribution content whatsoever. Placing it here keeps the lifecycle framing that actually explains it — *this right did not travel with the others*. Recorded as a CX to 02 rather than a merge. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect the **integrity right** to be the awkward one, and the candidate list ("Moral Rights & Attribution Rights") names only attribution | Attribution is tractable and largely automatable — the credit travels, the right is satisfied. Integrity is not: it asks whether a use is *derogatory*, which is a judgement about honour and reputation. | ✅ CONFIRMED, with the resolution being restraint. The platform must **route** integrity objections, never **evaluate** them. Two reasons: it is not competent to (no algorithm assesses derogatory treatment), and any attempt would put WeJammin in the position of ruling on whether an artist's work has been mutilated — an adjudication with no defensible basis. The product is the *channel* and the *record*, not the verdict. This is the same restraint as [09.03.04](./09.03.04-estate-succession-catalog-legacy.md) D-01 (probate is a court's job) and it is becoming the domain's pattern: hold the facts, route the judgements. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Moral rights are a **persistence property** of authorship, not a ledger share | The remedy for a breach is never a percentage; modelling them as shares would put them in the wrong machine entirely. |
| D-02 | The platform **routes** integrity objections; it never **evaluates** them | No algorithm assesses derogatory treatment, and adjudicating it has no defensible basis (see DT-02). |
| D-03 | A purported waiver is **recorded but not honoured** where the right is non-waivable | Contracts routinely waive rights that cannot be waived in the territories that matter. Honouring the contract's claim would propagate a false statement of law. |
| D-04 | Non-applicability is **stated**, not silently omitted | A US user who ships to Europe needs to know the right exists. |
