## Overview

The graph that connects parties to each other: who is a member of what, who represents whom, and what authority either relationship carries. A drummer is a member of two bands and a session player on a third; a manager acts for four artists; an agent books for a roster; a publisher administers a catalogue. This sub-domain owns the edges and the mandate attached to each — **who may do what on whose behalf** — while enforcement of that mandate at every call site belongs to the Roles & Permissions cross-cut.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not three features**: membership and representation are the same graph with two edge types, and mandate is an attribute of both. The domain overview names it as one capability — "the membership/representation graph (who is a member of what, with what mandate)". Splitting mandate from the edges it qualifies would put the qualifier in a different node from the thing it qualifies.

| D-04 | **The default mandate is ratified and is two models, not one**: a **band** peer-seeds every `confirmed` `permanent` membership edge with all seven activities capped at **USD 1,000 per act** (escalating above it to the uncapped owning-mandate holder), while **every other org type** — studio, venue, label, shop, agency — seeds nothing and runs on the creator's owning mandate plus explicit grants | Resolves 01.03.03 Q-01 and domain-index Q-03, the highest-stakes product decision in the sub-domain. Detail and reasoning live in 01.03.03 D-07..D-10; this row exists so the index does not still read as though the question were open. Two consequences reach outside this sub-domain: the seed includes `administer`, so the governance disclosure (01.04.01 D-04/D-05) must describe the same authority in the same words; and `touring`/`staff`/`honorary` members hold presence with no acting context until granted. | User ratification, DQ-02.4 / DQ-02.5 / DQ-02.6 / DQ-02.7 |
| D-05 | **Representation scope is two flat axes, conjoined at the call site**: an edge carries `activities` (the closed seven verbs — book / sign / spend / list / release / settle / administer) and `domains` (`live booking / recording / publishing / sync / merch`), and an action is authorised only when its verb is in the first **and** its domain is in the second. A **membership** edge carries the activity axis only and resolves to **all** domains | Detail and reasoning live in 01.03.02 D-05, 01.03.03 D-11 and 01.03.01 D-19; this row exists so the index does not still read as though representation scope were one list. Two flat lists are at most 7 + 5 = **12** plain-language statements per edge, not 35 grid cells, so 01.03.03 DT-02's rejection of a permission matrix stands and the seven-verb enum (01.03.03 D-01) is untouched. Both edge types therefore present one shape — `{activities, domains}` — to enforcement (CX-03), which is what D-01's merge requires. **Accepted cost**: territory, term and commission are edge-level and do not vary per domain; splitting them takes a second edge | User ratification, DQ-R2-01 |
