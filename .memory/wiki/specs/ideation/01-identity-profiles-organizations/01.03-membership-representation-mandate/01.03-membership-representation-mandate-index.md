## Overview

The graph that connects parties to each other: who is a member of what, who represents whom, and what authority either relationship carries. A drummer is a member of two bands and a session player on a third; a manager acts for four artists; an agent books for a roster; a publisher administers a catalogue. This sub-domain owns the edges and the mandate attached to each — **who may do what on whose behalf** — while enforcement of that mandate at every call site belongs to the Roles & Permissions cross-cut.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not three features**: membership and representation are the same graph with two edge types, and mandate is an attribute of both. The domain overview names it as one capability — "the membership/representation graph (who is a member of what, with what mandate)". Splitting mandate from the edges it qualifies would put the qualifier in a different node from the thing it qualifies.
