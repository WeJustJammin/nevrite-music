## Overview

The rules a band runs by, and what happens when it stops. Four interacting capabilities: the partnership terms and decision rules that say how the band decides things; who owns the name; who may touch the money; and what happens on dissolution to the name, the catalogue, the royalties and the people. In most jurisdictions a band with no written agreement is a **general partnership by default** — which means every band on the platform already has governance, imposed by law, that none of its members have read.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: the four capabilities are a closed loop. Decision rules determine who can change the treasury mandate; the treasury mandate determines who can spend on the name's trademark; name ownership is the single most contested asset at dissolution; and dissolution is decided by the decision rules. Specify any one alone and it contradicts the other three.

**Why it is here and not in domain 09**: governance decides *who may act for the band*; domain 09 decides *who owns the work*. Dissolution is where those meet, and this sub-domain owns the party side of that meeting — the ownership side stays in 09.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Governance is modelled for the **band** specifically, not for orgs generally | A limited company has directors and articles; a studio has an owner. Neither needs modelling — the law and the companies registry already do it. A band is the case where the legal default (general partnership) is invisible to its own members and catastrophic at dissolution. That gap is the product. | Agent, Deep Think DT-01 on 01.04.01 |
| D-02 | The platform **records and surfaces** governance; it does not adjudicate, register or enforce it | Trademark registration, partnership law and dissolution are legal services. The platform's contribution is that the facts were captured while everyone was still friendly — which is the thesis (D-18) applied to the band rather than to the session. | Agent, per `problem-statement.md` root cause |
| D-03 | Access here is by membership, not by persona | Consistent with `personas.md`'s "lenses on behaviour, not account types". A Producer in the band governs; a Producer who tracked the record does not — but does hold a stake at dissolution (their credits and splits ride on the catalogue). | Agent, Role Matrix reasoning |
