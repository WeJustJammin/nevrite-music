## Overview

The human at the centre of the platform: one account, several simultaneous professional roles, and several public names to perform under. This sub-domain owns the person record, the role-facet model that lets a drummer also be a mixer and a seller without choosing, the alias/stage-name model that credits and releases actually attach to, the acting-context switcher that decides *who* an action is taken as, and the separation between the legal identity contracts and payouts require and the public identity the profile shows.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities that interact continuously. Role facets determine which contexts the switcher offers; aliases determine which of those contexts can be credited or released under; the legal/public split determines what any of them may expose. Change one and the other three change behaviour.

| D-01 | Sweep candidates 01 (Multi-Role Professional Identity) and 09 (Act-As / Context Switching) merged into this sub-domain | Act-As is not a separate thing from multi-role identity — it is the runtime expression of it. | D-02 | Aliases are modelled here (person-side), not as an organisation type | An alias is a projection of a professional self, not an entity with members and a treasury.
