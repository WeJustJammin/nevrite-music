# Feature: Person Record & Multi-Role Facets

> **Number**: 01.01.01
> **Parent**: [Person Identity & Roles](./01.01-person-identity-roles-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The single human record every other party relationship on the platform hangs off, carrying a set of **simultaneous** role facets rather than one account type. A working musician who is also a mixer, a band member, a studio engineer and an occasional gear seller holds all five facets at once; the record never asks them to choose. Role facets are additive and reversible, and adding one unlocks surfaces without hiding the ones already in use.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Owns their record. Adds/removes role facets (performer, writer, engineer, mixer, teacher, seller, tech) at any time from a single settings surface — never a wizard that forks the account. Sees every facet's surfaces simultaneously in one navigation, not behind a mode toggle. |
| Producer | Full | Same record type as Musician — the persona difference is behavioural, not structural. Typically holds engineer + mixer + performer facets. Their facet set is what makes them offerable as a session owner in 07 and a service provider in 05. |
| Operator | Full | Holds a person record distinct from the venue/studio org they operate. Their personal facets (often engineer, sometimes performer) are separate from the org's capabilities — the venue's PA spec is not a fact about them. |
| Fan | Config | Has a person record with **zero professional facets by default** and never sees facet management until they ask for it. Adding any professional facet is the frictionless, non-destructive Fan→Musician upgrade path: nothing they already had (follows, tickets, purchases) is lost or migrated. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

A person record is created once, at signup, with a display name and no professional assertion. Role facets are self-asserted claims about what this person does — they are **not** permissions and **not** verified. A facet grants access to that role's surfaces (adding `seller` reveals listing creation in 13/14; adding `teacher` reveals 06's tutor surfaces) and makes the person discoverable in that capacity. Verification of a facet, where it exists at all, is a separate assertion handled by 01.07.

The record is the anchor for: memberships (01.03.01), aliases (01.01.02), claims (01.05), credits (domain 02), and the payable-party link that Payments owns.

### Happy Path

1. Fan signs up with an email and a display name. No role question is asked. They get a working account immediately.
2. Months later they want to list a cymbal. They hit "Sell something" from 13.
3. System detects no `seller` facet, and adds it inline — one confirm, no separate onboarding flow, no new account.
4. Their existing account gains selling surfaces. Their follows, tickets and purchase history are untouched and still visible.
5. Later they add `performer`. Both facets are now live at once; nothing is hidden to make room.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| User removes a facet that has live dependencies (open listings, an active teaching booking, a mandate) | Removal is blocked, not silently cascaded — deleting a facet must never delete the work done under it | "You have 3 active listings as a seller. Close or transfer them before removing this role." with links to each |
| User adds every facet at once to "see everything" | Allowed — facets are self-asserted, not gated. Surfaces get dense, which is a UI problem, not a validity problem | Full navigation; a nudge that unused surfaces can be hidden without removing the facet |
| Fan adds a professional facet, then regrets it | Facet removed; account returns to fan-shaped UI; no data loss either direction | Confirmation that nothing is deleted, only hidden |
| A facet is asserted falsely (claims `engineer`, has never engineered) | Nothing blocks it — the facet is a claim, not a credential. Cost of the lie is borne by 01.06.02 (an empty credit-backed portfolio) and 01.07 (no badge) | Their profile shows the facet with zero verified credits behind it |
| Two people share one account (a duo, a couple, a band's shared login) | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — an account with two humans breaks attribution at the root, which is a thesis-level failure, but the mitigation is unknown | `[PENDING]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Record fetch | Skeleton profile header |
| Empty | New account, no facets, no aliases | Fan-shaped account; a single low-pressure "Are you a musician?" affordance, dismissible permanently |
| Populated | 1+ facets asserted | Facet chips on the settings surface; each facet's surfaces live in navigation |
| Error | Record fetch failed | Error with retry; the account is never presented as facet-less on failure (that would silently hide the user's own surfaces) |
| Partial | Facets asserted, none verified, no credits behind them | Profile renders facets as claims — visually distinct from verified/credited ones (see 01.06.02) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The record needs a primary/dominant role for onboarding and defaults | Every competitor does this (SoundBetter asks "what do you do?", Reverb forks buyer/seller) | ❌ REJECTED — the Musician's Unique Constraint in `personas.md` is explicit: "Any UX forcing 'are you an artist OR a producer OR a seller?' fails them immediately." A "primary" role is that fork wearing a softer word. Defaults must come from observed behaviour, not a declared primary. |
| DT-02 | Role facets and permissions are the same thing | Naive reading — both look like "what you're allowed to do" | ❌ REJECTED — a facet is a self-asserted claim about a profession; a permission is an authorisation to act on a resource. Conflating them means anyone can grant themselves authority by ticking a box. Facets reveal surfaces; the Roles & Permissions cross-cut authorises actions. The seam is already drawn in the domain overview. |
| DT-03 | The Fan→Musician upgrade is the highest-leverage path in the domain and must be non-destructive | Fans outnumber professionals by orders of magnitude (D-13); the porous boundary is stated in `personas.md` ("may be a hobbyist musician too") | ✅ CONFIRMED — this is a growth loop nobody named in the sweep. If the upgrade requires a new account or loses history, the largest population on the platform is walled off from the professional side. Facets being additive to an existing record is what makes it free. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | No primary role, no account type, no onboarding fork | The multi-hyphenate constraint (`personas.md`) makes this a correctness requirement, not a preference |
| D-02 | Facets are self-asserted and unverified by design | Verification is 01.07's job. Gating facets on verification would make the platform unusable for the long tail who hold no union card |
| D-03 | Facet removal never cascades to delete work | Deleting a role must not delete the record of what was done under it — that is the exact evaporation the platform exists to prevent |
