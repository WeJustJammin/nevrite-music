## Overview

The surfaces a party is *seen* through: the profile page, the body of work behind it, and the press kit that gets sent to someone who has to decide whether to book, hire or sign. The domain overview calls the profile/EPK "the most-visited page type on any music platform and the artifact hiring decisions are made from" — this sub-domain owns it for people, aliases and organisations alike.

**Why this is the sub-domain where the thesis becomes visible**: `personas.md` gives the Musician's measurable success criterion as "credits are cited rather than claimed", and their pain as "every new client requires re-proving who they are and what they've played on". A portfolio assembled from other people's attestations — rather than typed by its subject — is what a Linktree, a PDF one-sheet and a Notes-app credit list structurally cannot be. Provenance is the wedge (D-18); this is where the user *sees* the wedge pay out.

**Why it is a sub-domain**: three capabilities in a dependency chain. Page composition decides what a profile is made of; the credit-backed portfolio supplies the part nobody can fake; the EPK packages both for someone who will never visit the page. Change what a credit means and all three change.

| D-01 | Sweep candidate 11 (Portfolio, Media Reel & EPK) expanded into three features, splitting out **Credit-Backed Portfolio** | The sweep treated portfolio and EPK as one presentational thing. But a portfolio built from *others' attestations* is categorically different from one typed by its subject — it is the thesis made visible, and it is the only part a competitor cannot manufacture retroactively. Collapsing it into "profile page" would bury the differentiator inside a layout concern. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
