# Feature: Profile Page Composition

> **Number**: 01.06.01
> **Parent**: [Portfolio, Media Reel & EPK](./01.06-portfolio-media-epk-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

What a party's page is made of, and who decides. One composition model serves a drummer, a band, a venue and a studio — the same artifact with different sections, driven by what the party *is* (01.01.01 facets, 01.02.01 types) and what it has *done* (credits, shows, listings). The page is the platform's most-visited surface and the artifact hiring decisions are made from, so composition is not a layout preference; it is what a stranger learns in eight seconds.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Composes their page. The sections that matter are mostly not theirs to write — credits, collaborators and history are supplied by the graph. What they control is emphasis and the human parts: bio, photo, what they want first. A multi-hyphenate's page has to show all their roles at once without becoming a list of everything. |
| Producer | Full | Same, but their page's job is different: it converts a client, so rates, room, discography and turnaround matter more than a bio. Their page is a sales surface where a Musician's is a credibility surface — one composition model, different section weights. |
| Operator | Full | Composes the venue/studio page, which is the most information-dense in the domain: capacity, gear, access, calendar, past shows. Serves two audiences at once — a Fan deciding whether to go, and a band deciding whether to play. The page must answer both without becoming two pages. |
| Fan | Config | A radically thinner page, if they get one at all (parent Q-01) — no professional sections, no credits, no rates. Whatever exists is configured, not composed. The Fan surface must be "a different product wearing the same brand" (`personas.md`), and the profile is where that is most tempting to violate. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

A page is composed of sections. Which sections are *available* is derived from the party's facets/types and from what data exists — a drummer with no listings has no "for sale" section, not an empty one. Which sections are *emphasised* is the user's choice.

Sections divide into two kinds, and the distinction is the point:

- **Asserted** — bio, photos, links, rates. The subject wrote them. Worth what any self-description is worth.
- **Attested** — credits, collaborators, shows played, verified badges. Other parties supplied them. Not editable by the subject.

The visual distinction between these two must survive every layout decision downstream. A page where a self-claimed credit looks identical to an attested one is a page that has thrown away the platform's only structural advantage.

The page renders for **unclaimed parties** too (parent D-03) — that is the claim bait.

### Happy Path

1. Musician's page exists from signup, with a name.
2. As facts accrue — a credit from a session, a band membership, a listing — sections appear on their own. The page fills up without them filling it in.
3. They add a photo and a bio: the parts the graph cannot supply.
4. They reorder to lead with what they want (production work first, drumming second).
5. A stranger lands and, in seconds, sees what they have actually done and who says so — not what they claim.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Empty profile — new user, no credits, no history | Honest emptiness. Not a fake-full page, not a "100% complete" nag. The empty state's job is to point at the one action that fills it (get credited) | A near-empty page and a route to the first real fact |
| Multi-hyphenate with five facets | The composition problem of the domain. Everything at once is a wall; a "primary role" is banned (01.01.01 DT-01) | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see Q-01; this is unresolved and important |
| Party is unclaimed but rich (a shadow with 30 credits) | Renders fully, marked unclaimed. Deliberate — this is the claim incentive (parent D-03) | "This profile hasn't been claimed. Is this you?" |
| Subject dislikes an attested fact (a credit on a record they would rather forget) | Cannot delete it — it is not theirs. Can dispute it (24) or de-emphasise it | Emphasis control, not edit control. See Q-02 |
| Venue page serving Fan and band simultaneously | Two audiences, one page. Sections are relevant to one or the other, and neither should wade through the other's | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — audience-adaptive composition is unmodelled |
| Legal name leaks into a section | Must be structurally impossible — no section reads the legal identity surface (01.01.04 D-01) | Never rendered |
| Party has aliases: which page is "the" page? | The human's page aggregates; each alias has its own. Which one a stranger lands on depends on how they arrived | See domain CX-04 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Page fetch | Skeleton with the header resolved first |
| Empty | No facts yet | Honest empty page + one route to a real fact |
| Populated | Facts accrued | Composed page, attested sections visually distinct from asserted |
| Error | Fetch failed | Error with retry. **Never renders a partial page as complete** — a page missing its credits misrepresents its subject |
| Partial | Some sections loaded, credits still resolving | Section-level skeletons; never a page that looks finished while its best content is missing |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The profile is a presentation layer over other domains' data, therefore not a real feature | The strongest argument against this sub-domain — a verifier already used a version of it against the whole domain | ❌ REJECTED — the domain overview settles the destination question ("the most-visited page type... the artifact hiring decisions are made from"), but there is a better reason. The page is where the platform *decides what verified means* to a stranger in eight seconds. That decision cannot live in 02 (which knows credits but not the person's other four facets) or in 16 (which knows rooms but not reputations). Composition is the only place the whole party is visible at once, and it is a product decision, not a rendering one. |
| DT-02 | Users should have rich composition control | The creator-platform instinct — musicians are expressive people and will want it | ⏳ SPLIT — control over *asserted* sections, none over *attested* ones. The distinction is the feature. A page where the subject can edit their credits is a Linktree with extra steps, and every competitor already has one. The value is precisely that some of the page is not theirs to write. How much control over the rest is a design question (parent Q-03) — but the line itself is not negotiable. |
| DT-03 | The visual distinction between asserted and attested content is the platform's entire differentiator, expressed in a layout decision | Reasoning from `personas.md`: the Musician's success criterion is "credits are **cited** rather than claimed" — that word is doing a lot of work | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is a constraint on `/create-prd-design-system`, not just this file. If a self-written bio and an attested credit render with the same visual weight, the platform has spent its whole thesis on a page that looks like every other music profile. The distinction has to be legible to a stranger who has never heard of us and will not read a legend. Nobody in the sweep named this; it is the point at which provenance either becomes visible or does not. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Sections divide into asserted (subject-written) and attested (graph-supplied); the subject cannot edit attested content | DT-02 — the value is that some of the page is not theirs to write |
| D-02 | The asserted/attested distinction must be visually legible to a stranger with no explanation | DT-03 — this is where provenance becomes visible or fails to |
| D-03 | Available sections are derived from facets/types and from data that exists; no empty scaffolding | An empty section is a nag; an absent one is honest |
| D-04 | One composition model for persons, aliases and orgs | Parent D-02 — three page systems would produce three answers to "what does verified mean" |
