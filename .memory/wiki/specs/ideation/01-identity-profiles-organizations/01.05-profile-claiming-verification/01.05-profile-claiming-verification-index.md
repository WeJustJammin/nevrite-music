## Overview

The machinery that lets a party exist before its owner does, and lets that owner take control of it later. Someone has to be creditable at the session without having an account; a venue has to be listed before its operator has ever heard of the platform. This sub-domain owns the creation of those unowned parties (shadow parties), the flow and proof methods by which a real human claims one, and what happens when two people claim the same thing.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities with a hard dependency chain and different trust models. Shadow party creation is deliberately *unauthenticated* (anyone can name anyone); claiming is a *proof*; contested claims are an *adjudication*. Each is a distinct failure surface — spam, impersonation, and land-grab respectively — and they interact: a bad shadow party makes a fraudulent claim easier, and a weak proof method makes a contested claim inevitable.

**Why it matters more than its size suggests**: `problem-statement.md` says the root cause is "absence at the point of truth". The Producer captures credits at the session — where half the room has no account. If capture requires signup, capture does not happen, and the thesis fails at its first contact with reality. Shadow parties are the precondition for the wedge; claiming is the growth loop that converts them.
