# Feature: Service Category Taxonomy & Attributes

> **Number**: 05.01.02
> **Parent**: [Service Listings & Music Pricing](./05.01-service-listings-pricing-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The controlled vocabulary that makes service listings findable and comparable: what crafts exist (mixing, mastering, session performance, topline, arrangement, orchestration, editing/tuning, sound design, repair, artwork, PR), and the facets that qualify them (instrument, genre, service mode, deliverable, tier of market, language, DAW). Without it a services marketplace degrades to full-text search over marketing copy, which is exactly why buyers on generic freelance platforms cannot find a metal mixer who works in Pro Tools.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Selects from the taxonomy when publishing a listing and when filtering as a buyer ("session cello · remote · classical/film · delivers stems"). Cannot mint new categories — a self-serve taxonomy fragments instantly ("mixing", "Mixing", "mix engineer", "MIXING & MASTERING"). May *propose* a missing craft, which enters a curation queue. |
| Producer | Read-only | Same as Musician, but exercises the deepest facets — DAW, console/summing, genre sub-tier, Atmos/immersive capability. The Producer is the persona most likely to hit a taxonomy gap, because production crafts subdivide further than any other (comping, tuning, Beat Detective/editing, programming are separate purchasable jobs). |
| Operator | Read-only | Uses only the venue/studio-attached branches — backline tech, in-house engineering, piano tuning, PA with operator. The bulk of the taxonomy is invisible to this lens. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees category names as public browse/SEO surfaces ("Mixing engineers in Manchester"). Facets are simplified or hidden — the Fan will not filter by DAW or summing bus. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None
>
> Note: no persona has Full/Config. The taxonomy is **platform-curated**, which is itself the design decision (see D-01). The curation surface is an Admin concern — routed to the Admin, Back-Office & Support cross-cut, and see `meta/personas.md` Q-02 (Admin is not one of the four personas).

## Behavior

The taxonomy is **faceted, not a tree**. A music service is described by several orthogonal axes at once and any single hierarchy forces a false parent choice: is "remote metal mix in Pro Tools" under Mixing → Metal → Remote, or Remote → Mixing → Metal? A tree makes one of those unreachable.

### Happy Path

1. Seller picks a craft; system reveals only the facets relevant to that craft (a luthier is never asked for a DAW)
2. System reveals only the pricing models legal for that craft (05.01.03) — points are not offered for guitar setups
3. Seller sets facet values; required facets are enforced, optional ones improve ranking
4. Buyer browses by any facet combination; results are comparable because the facets are shared, not free text

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Craft does not exist in the taxonomy | Seller submits a proposal to the curation queue; publishes under the nearest craft with a free-text qualifier in the interim | "We'll review 'stem separation' as a category. Listed under Editing for now." |
| Two facet values mean the same thing (genre drift between domains) | Resolved by the Canonical Data cross-cut, not locally | No user-visible difference — that is the point |
| Buyer filters to an empty result | Progressive relaxation: drop the least-selective facet first, tell the buyer which one was dropped | "No cellists in Leeds. Showing remote cellists — 34 results." |
| Taxonomy changes after listings exist | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — migration/remap policy for retired categories undecided | `[PENDING]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Populated | Normal | Faceted browse with counts per facet value |
| Empty (cold start) | Facet combination has no listings | Relaxed results + "be the first" seller CTA |
| Partial | New craft has facets not yet populated | Craft browsable, unpopulated facets hidden rather than shown at zero |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A hierarchical category tree is sufficient (the Fiverr/Reverb model) | Marketplace precedent | ❌ REJECTED — music services are irreducibly multi-axis (craft × instrument × genre × mode × deliverable × toolchain). Any tree makes one axis primary and the rest unreachable, and the axis buyers actually lead with varies by craft: a mix buyer leads with genre, a session buyer leads with instrument, a repair buyer leads with location. Faceted is the only shape that serves all three. |
| DT-02 | The genre and instrument facets should be owned by this domain | Locality assumption | ❌ REJECTED — a services-local genre list forks from the one used by Release & Distribution (12), Licensing (11) and the credit graph (02). Two genre vocabularies in one platform is the classic entity-resolution failure, and the **Canonical Data, Taxonomy & Entity Resolution** cross-cut exists precisely for this. This domain owns the **craft** axis; it *consumes* instrument and genre. This is the seam a later reader should watch. |
| DT-03 | The taxonomy is a discovery concern and therefore belongs in the Search & Discovery cross-cut | Node Classification Gate — "is this a mechanism?" | ❌ REJECTED — Search owns indexing, ranking and query; it does not own *what crafts exist in music*. That vocabulary is product content with real editorial consequences (deciding that "stem separation" is a craft creates a market; deciding that "AI mastering" is a craft is a values call). Precedent: domain 02 retains its own credit role/instrument taxonomy for the same reason. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The taxonomy is platform-curated, not seller-minted | Self-serve category creation fragments a vocabulary within weeks and destroys comparability, which is the only reason the taxonomy exists. Sellers may propose; a curation queue decides. |
| D-02 | This domain owns the **craft** axis only; instrument and genre are consumed from the Canonical Data cross-cut | Prevents a second genre vocabulary. Recorded as a live seam — see DT-02. |
