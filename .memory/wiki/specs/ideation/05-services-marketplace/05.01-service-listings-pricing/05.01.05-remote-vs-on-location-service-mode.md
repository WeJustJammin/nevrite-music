# Feature: Remote vs On-Location Service Mode

> **Number**: 05.01.05
> **Parent**: [Service Listings & Music Pricing](./05.01-service-listings-pricing-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Whether a service is delivered as files from wherever the seller is, or by a human physically present in a room. The distinction looks like a filter and is actually a fork in the whole engagement: an on-location service consumes a calendar rather than a queue, needs a room that someone must pay for, carries travel and per-diem and cartage as expenses rather than fees, and delivers *attendance* rather than a file.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Declares mode per listing — a session player commonly sells both ("remote, £150/song" and "in your room, £450/day + travel"). As a buyer, filters by mode; hiring a remote cellist and hiring a cellist to your tracking date are different purchases with different risk. |
| Producer | Full | Declares mode for production/engineering listings. The Producer's `Unique Constraint` in `meta/personas.md` bites hardest here: their work happens inside a DAW, so remote is their default mode, and an on-location engagement is the exception that pulls them into calendars, rooms and travel. |
| Operator | Config | The counterparty for on-location work, but reached through domain 16, not here. Configures whether their room accepts externally-booked on-location engagements and what the room's constraints are (load-in, curfew, backline, capacity). Does not see the engagement's commercial terms — only the room booking. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Service mode is a professional procurement attribute with no Fan surface. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

Mode is declared on the listing and cascades. Choosing on-location changes the pricing shape (day rate + travel + per-diem + cartage + buyout, versus a flat per-song fee), the scheduling model (a calendar slot from the Availability cross-cut, versus queue depth from 05.01.07), the requirements (a room, an address, a load-in time, versus stems), and the delivery artifact (attendance + the tracks recorded, versus a file).

**The room question is the hard part.** An on-location engagement happens *somewhere*, and someone pays for it. There are three shapes and the platform must know which:

| Shape | Room comes from | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer-supplied | Buyer's own room / already-booked studio | Buyer, outside this engagement |
| Platform-booked | A domain 16 booking created alongside the engagement | Buyer, as part of the engagement or separately |
| Seller-supplied | Seller's own room (a producer with a home studio) | Included in the seller's rate, or itemised |

### Happy Path

1. Seller declares mode on the listing; on-location reveals travel radius, travel/per-diem terms, cartage, and minimum block
2. Buyer orders/requests a quote for an on-location service
3. System requires the room shape to be declared (buyer-supplied / platform-booked / seller-supplied)
4. If platform-booked, the engagement links a domain 16 booking; the room's constraints (curfew, load-in, backline) are surfaced against the engagement schedule
5. Availability cross-cut holds the seller's date and the room's date together — a confirmed engagement with no room, or a room with no player, is a failure
6. Seller attends; delivery is attendance-based (05.04.01), not file-based

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Engagement confirmed, room booking then cancelled | The engagement's precondition failed. Must not silently continue. | "The room for {date} was cancelled. This engagement needs a room or a new date." → cancellation path (05.03.05) |
| Room booked, seller cancels | Room booking may be non-refundable — a cost the seller caused | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — whether the seller's kill fee covers the buyer's stranded room cost is undecided; see 05.03.05 |
| Buyer outside seller's travel radius | Listing not orderable at that address; quote path may still allow it with itemised travel | "{Seller} travels within 50 miles. Request a quote for further." |
| Travel/per-diem/cartage charged | These are **expenses**, not fees — reimbursements at cost | "Travel £120 (at cost, no platform fee)" — see DT-02 |
| Hybrid engagement (tracking on-location, mixing remote) | Two modes in one engagement | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — whether hybrid is one engagement with staged modes (05.03.02 milestones) or two linked engagements is undecided |
| Seller no-shows at the room | The costliest failure in the domain — room, buyer's time, other players all wasted | Dispute path (24) + emergency cover (05.05.01) |
| Buyer's address is a private residence | Safety consideration for a lone seller attending | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — routed to 24 / Safeguarding cross-cut |

### States

**Mode-declaration surface** (the toggle + conditional fields on the listing/quote form):

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Listing form opening; seller's saved travel/expense defaults fetching | Skeleton on the mode toggle and the conditional on-location fields |
| Empty | New listing, no mode chosen yet | Mode toggle defaults to Remote; on-location fields collapsed |
| Populated | Mode chosen | Remote → queue/per-song fields; On-location → travel radius, travel/per-diem/cartage, minimum block, room-shape selector revealed |
| Error | Save failed, or on-location chosen with no travel radius / no room shape | Inline field errors: "Add a travel radius before publishing an on-location listing" |

**Engagement lifecycle** (once a buyer orders an on-location service — the fork DT-03 describes):

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Remote | Remote listing ordered | Queue-based ordering, file delivery — no calendar, no room |
| On-location — room pending | On-location ordered, room shape not declared | Blocker: "Where is this happening?" — engagement cannot confirm |
| On-location — room confirmed | Room shape declared and (if platform-booked) held atomically | Schedule view showing seller hold + room hold together (DT-01) |
| On-location — room failed | Room booking cancelled/expired after confirmation | Alert on the engagement, cancellation path (05.03.05) offered |
| Hybrid | Order mixes on-location + remote stages | `[PENDING — Step 5 Must-depth or /write-be-spec]` — depends on Q-01 resolution |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **An on-location engagement is not complete without a room, and the platform must know who supplies it** | Reasoning about the intersection with domain 16: a confirmed engagement whose room evaporates is a confirmed engagement that cannot happen. Nothing in the candidate list connected the two. | ✅ CONFIRMED — the room shape must be a declared, required field on any on-location engagement, and a platform-booked room must be **held atomically with the seller's date**. Booking a player for Tuesday and a room for Wednesday is the exact double-booking-class failure `meta/personas.md` names as the Operator's costliest event, arriving from a domain the Operator does not control. |
| DT-02 | **Travel, per-diem and cartage are expenses, not fees, and must not carry the services take rate** | Domain expertise: nobody in music has ever accepted a commission on their own petrol. Charging ~15-20% (the Payments cross-cut's stated services rate) on a £120 train fare is the kind of detail that makes professionals take the deal off-platform — which `meta/personas.md` lists as the Musician's "worst intentional" behaviour, and which the platform would in this case have provoked. | ✅ CONFIRMED — expenses are a distinct line type: reimbursed at cost, zero take rate, receipt-backed, and separately visible in the buyer's total. Flagged to the Payments, Escrow & Payouts cross-cut, which currently describes a per-domain fee rate but not a zero-rated expense line. |
| DT-03 | On-location versus remote is just a listing filter | The candidate list's framing ("Service Mode" as an attribute) | ❌ REJECTED — it forks the engagement. Remote consumes queue depth (05.01.07); on-location consumes a calendar slot (Availability cross-cut). Remote delivers a file that can be QC'd (05.04.02); on-location delivers attendance, which cannot be auto-QC'd and needs the session attendance proof mechanism domain 02 owns. Remote failure is late delivery; on-location failure is a no-show with a stranded room. Treating this as a filter would produce an engagement lifecycle that silently misbehaves for half the domain's listings. It stays a feature *here* because it is declared on the listing, but the cascade is recorded as CX-04 in this sub-domain's CX file. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Room shape (buyer/platform/seller-supplied) is a required declaration on any on-location engagement | An engagement whose venue is undefined cannot be settled or rescued. See DT-01. |
| D-02 | Expenses (travel, per-diem, cartage) are a zero-take-rate line type, distinct from fees | See DT-02. Provokes off-platform leakage otherwise. |
