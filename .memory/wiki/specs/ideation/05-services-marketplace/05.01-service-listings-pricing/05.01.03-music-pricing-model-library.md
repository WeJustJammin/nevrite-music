# Feature: Music Pricing Model Library

> **Number**: 05.01.03
> **Parent**: [Service Listings & Music Pricing](./05.01-service-listings-pricing-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The set of pricing shapes music work is actually sold in — per-song, per-minute of finished audio, day rate, half-day, per-stem, per-track, retainer, points (a royalty percentage instead of cash), spec, and hybrids of these. This is the single feature that makes a generic freelance marketplace unusable to music professionals: Fiverr and Upwork model "a price", and a mastering engineer's price is not a number, it is a function of programme duration.

**Why this is thesis-critical**: the `points` model has no cash value at purchase time. It cannot be escrowed. It only exists as a *rights* term. That one fact links this pricing feature directly to the provenance wedge (D-18) — a points deal is a split sheet wearing a price tag.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Chooses the model that matches how they actually charge: a session drummer sells a day rate and a per-song rate simultaneously; a topline writer sells points-only or a fee-plus-points hybrid. Sees, as a buyer, a price they can compare against another seller *in the same model* — comparing a £400/song mixer to a £600/day mixer is the arithmetic the platform must do for them. |
| Producer | Full | The most complex user of this feature. Prices production as fee + points, mixing per-song with a per-stem add-on, mastering per-minute for film/TV and per-track for records. Elects points, which is where their cash price and their long-term royalty participation diverge (05.06.02). |
| Operator | Config | Restricted to the models their attached services use — hourly and day rate for a backline tech or in-house engineer. Points, per-song and per-minute are not offered under this lens. Note: room hire pricing (peak/off-peak, minimum block, overtime) is **not** here — it is domain 16's perishable-inventory pricing, which is a different problem. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees a rendered price on a public listing ("from £250 / song") and nothing else. The model machinery is never exposed — a Fan will not reason about per-minute versus per-track. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

Pricing is **polymorphic**: a listing declares one model, and the model defines the inputs required to render a number. A quote (05.02.01) or a direct order supplies those inputs and produces a cash figure — or, for points, produces zero cash and a rights term.

| Model | Renders from | Typical craft | Cash at purchase? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-song / per-track | count of songs | mixing, session performance | Yes |
| Per-minute of programme | duration of finished audio | mastering, film/TV, podcast | Yes |
| Day rate / half-day | days booked | session performance, on-location engineering, tracking | Yes |
| Hourly | hours | tech/repair labour, editing | Yes |
| Per-stem / per-part | count of stems or parts | session overdubs, editing/tuning | Yes |
| Retainer | period + allowance | ongoing mixing for a label, an artist's regular engineer | Yes (recurring — 05.03.04) |
| **Points** | % of master and/or publishing | production, topline | **No — zero cash; a rights term (05.06.02)** |
| Spec | nothing | speculative writing/production | No |
| Hybrid | fee + points, or fee + backend | production | Partial |

### Happy Path

1. Seller selects a craft; system offers only the models legal for that craft (05.01.02)
2. Seller configures the model's parameters (rate, minimum, unit)
3. System renders an example price against a typical job and shows it beside the benchmark band (05.01.04)
4. Buyer supplies their job's inputs (5 songs, 38 minutes, 2 days) via order or quote
5. System renders the number; a points or spec election renders £0 cash and surfaces the rights consequence prominently
6. Rendered number is snapshotted into the quote and becomes immutable (05.02.01)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Job inputs are unknown at purchase (song count TBC, duration TBC) | Model cannot render → direct order blocked, quote path forced | "This job needs a quote — we can't price it until we know the run time." |
| Points-only engagement | Zero cash means **nothing to escrow**. The engagement's protection is the rights instrument, not the money. | Prominent: "You are being paid in points, not cash. Your protection here is the signed split — not escrow." |
| Buyer wants a model the seller doesn't offer (buyer wants a day rate, seller sells per-song) | Quote path; the seller may render a bespoke model on the quote | Quote flow with a model-mismatch note |
| Duration/song count grows after acceptance (the album became 12 tracks, not 10) | Change order, not a re-render (05.03.03) | "That's outside the agreed scope. Raise a change order?" |
| Currency mismatch between buyer and seller | Localization, Currency & Timezone cross-cut renders; the escrowed figure is fixed in the seller's currency | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — FX-risk-bearer undecided |
| Union scale floor applies (05.02.03) | Rendered price is floored at scale; a below-scale render is blocked | "Below AFM scale for this session type. Minimum is {x}." |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Unrendered | Inputs not supplied | "From {floor}" or "Quote required" |
| Rendered | Inputs supplied | Concrete price with the arithmetic shown (5 songs × £300 = £1,500) |
| Rendered — zero cash | Points/spec elected | £0 + the rights term, with a warning |
| Blocked | Below a statutory or union floor | Floor explanation |
| Snapshotted | Quote issued | Immutable, with expiry (05.02.01) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Points cannot be escrowed, because there is no money at purchase time** | Reasoning about the intersection with the Payments/Escrow cross-cut: escrow requires a captured sum and a release trigger. A points deal captures nothing. | ✅ CONFIRMED — and this is structurally important. It means a points engagement's entire protection mechanism is the **rights instrument** (05.06.02 + Contracts & E-Signature cross-cut), not the money. The escrow-release↔rights-transfer leverage the domain rationale calls "the only leverage that exists at the only moment anyone is motivated" **does not exist for points deals** — the platform's strongest mechanism is absent from exactly the deals where exploitation is most common (unknown producer, points-only, no cash). Flagged to `/create-prd-security` and 24. |
| DT-02 | A single `price` column with a `unit` enum is sufficient | Schema-simplicity assumption | ❌ REJECTED — mirrors the D-14 reasoning that killed the marketplace merge. Per-minute needs a duration input the platform must obtain from the audio (Media Handling cross-cut can measure it); day rate needs a calendar (Availability cross-cut); points needs a rights instrument and a royalty registration downstream (09/10); retainer needs a subscription engine. These are not one column with a unit — they are different objects with different downstream systems. The model must be polymorphic. |
| DT-03 | Per-minute pricing can be rendered from the buyer's stated duration | Convenience assumption | ❌ REJECTED — mastering is priced on the *finished programme* duration, which the buyer routinely misstates (and which changes between the quote and the delivery when a track gets extended). The platform can measure the actual duration from the delivered/supplied audio via the Media Handling cross-cut and reconcile at acceptance. Self-reported duration is a dispute generator; measured duration is a fact. This is a small instance of the whole product thesis: be present when the fact becomes true. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Pricing models are polymorphic objects, not a price + unit enum | Each model has different required inputs and different downstream systems (calendar, rights registry, subscription engine). See DT-02. |
| D-02 | Craft constrains which models are legal | Prevents nonsense listings (points for a guitar setup) and keeps the seller's form short. |
