# Feature: Service Listings, Tiered Packages & Rate Cards

> **Number**: 05.01.01
> **Parent**: [Service Listings & Music Pricing](./05.01-service-listings-pricing-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The artifact a music professional publishes to sell their work: what craft they offer, at what tiers, for what price, with what included and excluded. It is the shopfront the buyer browses, compares and orders from, and the template every quote is rendered from. Without it the marketplace has nothing to transact against.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Publishes listings as a seller ("Session bass, remote, £150/song, 2 revisions, 5-day turnaround"; "Topline writing, points-only") and browses/compares listings as a buyer when hiring a mixer or a player. Runs several listings simultaneously across unrelated crafts — the multi-hyphenate case: the same account sells session drums, mixing, and gear repair, and each is a separate listing with a separate rate card and reputation. |
| Producer | Full | The heaviest seller. Publishes tiered production/mixing/mastering packages (e.g. Mix / Mix + Master / Mix + Master + Stems) with per-tier revision counts and deliverable specs. Also the heaviest buyer — hires players, vocalists and mastering engineers into their sessions from listings. |
| Operator | Config | Does not sell creative output under this lens. Configures listings for venue/studio-attached services only — in-house engineer, backline tech, piano tuning, PA hire with an operator. Room hire itself is **not** a listing here; it is perishable time inventory in domain 16. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees public service listings as SEO/browse surfaces (a producer's page says "takes work"). Cannot order under the Fan lens — a hobbyist who buys a mix is acting as a Musician and is onboarded into that lens. The explicit absence matters: the listing surface must not assume a professional viewer, because a large share of its inbound traffic is not one. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

A listing is a **template**, not an offer. It declares a craft (from 05.01.02's taxonomy), one or more tiers, a pricing model (05.01.03) per tier, an inclusion/exclusion scope, a deliverable spec, a revision allowance, a turnaround (05.01.06), a service mode (05.01.05), and a rights posture (05.06.01). A buyer either orders a tier directly (fixed-scope path) or requests a quote (05.02.01), which renders the listing's pricing model against their specific job.

The rate card is the seller-facing view of the same data across all their listings — one place to see and change every rate they charge.

### Happy Path

1. Musician/Producer selects a craft from the service taxonomy (e.g. Mixing → Rock/Metal → Remote)
2. System presents the pricing models legal for that craft (per-song, per-day, per-stem, points, hybrid) — see 05.01.03
3. Seller defines 1–3 tiers, each with price, inclusions, exclusions, revision count, deliverable spec, turnaround
4. Seller declares rights posture and source-file handover terms (05.06.01, 05.04.04) — the system requires an explicit election, no silent default
5. Seller declares the **requirements** a buyer must supply (stems naming convention, tempo map, reference tracks) — this becomes the gate in 05.03.01
6. System previews the listing as a buyer sees it, including the rendered price and the benchmark band (05.01.04)
7. Seller publishes; listing enters Search & Discovery cross-cut indexing and becomes orderable subject to capacity (05.01.07)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Seller edits price while a quote derived from this listing is live | Listing version increments; the issued quote is immutable and honours its own snapshot until expiry (see 05.02.01) | "2 open quotes were issued from the previous rate. They remain valid until {date}." |
| Seller edits price while an engagement derived from this listing is in flight | Engagement is unaffected — it references the accepted quote version, not the live listing | No buyer-visible change |
| Seller publishes a listing with no rights posture elected | Publish blocked | "Choose what the buyer owns when they accept. There is no safe default here." |
| Seller is at capacity (05.01.07) | Listing stays visible but is not orderable | "Booking again from {date}" + join-waitlist CTA |
| Seller's payout account is not onboarded (Payments cross-cut) | Publish allowed, order blocked | "Finish payout setup to accept orders." |
| Craft is custodial (repair/tech) | Listing routes to the custodial job flow (05.07.01), not delivery/acceptance — different lifecycle | Listing form swaps to intake/estimate fields |
| Listing describes a service already covered by a different domain (rehearsal room hire, a lesson) | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — boundary enforcement mechanism undecided; see Q-01 in domain index | `[PENDING]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Draft | Seller starts a listing | Editable form, "not visible to buyers" banner |
| Blocked | Missing rights posture / payout account / required taxonomy facets | Inline blockers list with the specific missing item |
| Published — Orderable | Complete + capacity available | Live listing with Order and Request Quote CTAs |
| Published — At Capacity | Capacity ceiling hit (05.01.07) | Live listing, Order disabled, waitlist CTA, next-available date |
| Paused | Seller pauses | Hidden from browse; existing engagements unaffected; direct link shows "not currently taking work" |
| Archived | Seller archives | Delisted; historical engagements retain their listing snapshot for evidence |
| Empty (seller view) | No listings yet | Onboarding CTA seeded from the seller's existing credits — "You have 34 mix credits. Sell mixing?" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A listing must carry the **requirements the buyer owes the seller** (stem naming, tempo map, reference), not just what the seller delivers | Domain expertise: the single largest cause of a stalled mix engagement is not the mix — it is the client sending 40 unlabelled stems with no tempo map three days late. Every mix engineer's website has a "how to send me your session" page; no marketplace models it. | ✅ CONFIRMED — requirements are declared on the listing and enforced as a gate in 05.03.01. This is why the turnaround clock cannot start at purchase (see 05.01.06 DT-01). |
| DT-02 | Tiers are enough; a listing does not need arbitrary add-ons | Fiverr/SoundBetter precedent — tiered packages plus extras | ❌ REJECTED — music services have orthogonal, priced add-ons that do not tier cleanly: stem delivery, Atmos mix, alternate/TV mix, instrumental, extra doubles, session file handover, buyout of a sample. Forcing these into tiers produces a 9-tier listing nobody reads. Add-ons must be first-class and separately priced. |
| DT-03 | One account = one listing per craft | Simplicity assumption | ❌ REJECTED — the multi-hyphenate (personas.md, the structural fact) routinely sells the same craft at different rates to different markets: a mixer charges a label rate and an indie rate; a session player charges a scale rate for union dates and a flat rate for demos. Listings must be many-per-craft-per-account, and the reputation must attach per-role (Reviews cross-cut already requires this), not per-listing. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A listing is a template; a quote is an offer; an engagement references the accepted quote | Prevents the "seller raised their rate mid-job" class of dispute entirely, and makes the listing freely editable without side effects. |
| D-02 | Rights posture must be an explicit election at publish time — no default | A work-for-hire default silently strips session players of rights; a co-ownership default leaves buyers unable to clear their own record. Neither is a safe silent default, so there is none. See 05.06.01 DT-01. |
