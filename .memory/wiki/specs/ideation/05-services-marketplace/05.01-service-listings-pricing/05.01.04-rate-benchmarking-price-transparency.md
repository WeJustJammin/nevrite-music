# Feature: Rate Benchmarking & Price Transparency

> **Number**: 05.01.04
> **Parent**: [Service Listings & Music Pricing](./05.01-service-listings-pricing-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Banded market rate data derived from real transactions on the platform — what a rock mix actually sells for at this tier, in this region, at this experience level — shown to sellers when they price and to buyers when they compare. It answers the question every working musician asks and nobody can answer honestly: *am I charging the right amount?*

The information asymmetry today runs entirely one way. Labels and established buyers know the market; the musician quoting their first professional mix does not, and undercharges by a factor of two or three for years.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Reports | Sees, while setting a rate, a percentile band for their craft × tier × region × genre ("Remote metal mixes with 10-30 credits sell for £250–£600; you're at £180"). Sees the same band a buyer does when evaluating an **issued** quote (D-05, 05.02.01 D-15) — never on a public listing, in browse, or in a buyer preview. Never sees an individual seller's rate history or an individual transaction. This is the persona the feature exists for — the "Primary Pain Point" in `meta/personas.md` is that nothing accumulates and every client requires re-proving worth; pricing blindness is the money-shaped version of that. |
| Producer | Reports | Same bands, plus the models a Producer prices in — points norms by tier ("3–5 points is typical for a production credit at this level"), which is the least-documented number in music and the one most exploited against new producers. |
| Operator | Reports | Sees bands only for venue/studio-attached services (in-house engineer day rate, backline tech hourly). Room-hire rate benchmarking is domain 16's — different inventory, different economics. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Rate data has no Fan use case and exposing a professional pricing surface to a consumer audience invites screenshot-driven "musicians are overpaid" discourse with no upside. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

Benchmarks are **banded aggregates, never individual rates**. A band is computed over a cohort defined by facets (craft, tier, region, genre, experience proxy) and is suppressed below a minimum cohort size. The experience proxy is the platform's unique asset: it can band by *verified credit count* (domain 02) rather than by self-declared seniority, which no competitor can do because no competitor has a verified credit graph.

The data source is **accepted engagements**, not listing prices. Listing prices are aspiration; accepted engagements are the market.

**Where the band renders is a surface question, not a role question** (D-05). Pre-transactional surfaces — the public listing, browse results, the buyer's preview of a listing — carry no band at all. A band beside a shopfront at browse scale lets anyone shop a whole cohort's position, and that is the undercutting spiral 05.01.01 D-05 and sub-domain CX R-04 exist to prevent. A **live quote** is the opposite shape: bilateral, post-request, and already disclosing the seller's exact figure to exactly one buyer. So once a quote is *issued*, the band renders to both parties (05.02.01 D-15). In between — the seller's pricing step, and a quote still in Drafting — it is seller-only.

### Happy Path

1. Seller opens the pricing step of a listing (05.01.03)
2. System computes the cohort band for their facets and their verified credit count
3. Band renders as a percentile range with the seller's proposed rate positioned in it
4. Seller adjusts, or proceeds knowingly
5. Buyer viewing an **issued** quote sees the same band, so the number is contextualised rather than negotiated blind (D-05). Nothing earlier carries one — not browse, not the public listing, not the buyer preview

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Cohort below minimum size (cold start — the normal case at launch) | Band suppressed; progressively relax facets (drop region, then genre) and label the relaxation | "Not enough local data. Showing UK-wide remote mixing: £200–£550." |
| Cohort still too small after relaxation | No band shown; do not fabricate | "We don't have enough data to benchmark this yet." — never an invented number |
| Cohort small enough to deanonymise an individual seller | Band suppressed regardless of relaxation | Same as above |
| Seller prices far below band | Show the band; do not block | "Most sellers with your credits charge 2–3× this." Advisory only — the price is the seller's decision. |
| Collusion to skew the band — two accounts record fake accepted engagements (a ring inflating or depressing rates for their cohort) | Aggregation weights toward engagements with a settled payment through the platform's rails and a delivered work record; off-platform or unpaid "accepted" engagements do not feed the band. Anomaly detection on cohort velocity flags rings for Trust & Safety (domain 24). | No user-visible artefact — the poisoned data simply never reaches the band. |
| Currency / region mismatch — cross-border cohort mixes £, €, $ engagements | Bands are computed and rendered in the viewer's listing currency; cross-currency engagements are normalised at booking-date FX before aggregation, never blended raw. | Band shown in one currency with a footnote: "Normalised from mixed-currency sales." |
| Stale band — a fast-moving cohort where the last engagement is many months old | Recency-weight the aggregate and expose the freshness; a band computed only from old data is labelled, not hidden. | "Based on sales up to Mar 2026 — the market may have moved." |
| Outlier transactions skew the band (one £20k label mix) | `[PENDING — Step 5 Must-depth or /write-be-spec]` — trimming/winsorising policy (trim %, IQR fence, or robust percentile estimator) undecided. Behaviour intent: outliers must not distort the band; exact estimator is a spec-stage statistical decision. | `[PENDING]` |
| Buyer opens a public listing, a browse result or a buyer preview | No band is computed for that surface at all. This is **scope, not suppression** (D-05) — the cohort may be enormous and the answer is still no band. | Nothing. Not even the "not enough data" copy: there is no absence to explain, because a band was never offered here. |
| Cohort below k on an **issued** quote | Suppressed for **both** parties, never for the buyer alone. A band the seller holds and the buyer does not is precisely the asymmetry 05.02.01 D-15 forbids — "symmetrically, or to neither". | Both sides: "We don't have enough data to benchmark this yet." |
| Points norms requested | Points bands are derived from elected points terms (05.06.02), not from cash | "3–5 points typical at this credit level." |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Cohort query in flight | Skeleton band, no numbers rendered yet |
| Empty | No accepted-engagement data exists for the craft at all (true cold start at launch) | "We don't have enough data to benchmark this yet." — an honest absence, never a fabricated number (D-04) |
| Populated | Cohort meets minimum size | Percentile band with the seller's proposed rate positioned in it |
| Relaxed | Cohort met only after dropping facets (region, then genre) | Band + explicit note of which facet was dropped ("Showing UK-wide, not local") |
| Suppressed | Cohort too small to reach minimum even after relaxation, or small enough to deanonymise an individual seller | Honest absence, no number — same copy as Empty |
| Error | Aggregation pipeline unavailable or the compute failed | "Benchmark unavailable right now." Pricing step stays fully usable — the seller can still set a rate; the band is advisory, never a gate. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Publishing rate benchmarks will collapse prices toward the floor — the Fiverr race-to-the-bottom, which the domain map explicitly cites as the Sonicbids/ReverbNation cautionary shape | Adversarial: what would a domain expert fear here that nobody mentioned? | ❌ REJECTED **as a reason to omit the feature**, ✅ CONFIRMED as a design constraint. The race to the bottom on Fiverr is caused by *ranking on price* and by exposing individual undercutting rates, not by publishing bands. Opacity does not protect musicians — it is precisely what lets an experienced buyer lowball someone quoting their first mix, because only one side of that table knows the market. The mitigation is structural: band the data (never expose individual rates), source it from accepted engagements rather than listing prices, band by **verified credit count** so the comparison is like-for-like, and never rank search results by price ascending. |
| DT-02 | Benchmarks are an Analytics concern and belong in the Analytics cross-cut or domain 22 | Node Classification Gate — "is this a mechanism?" | ❌ REJECTED, with a seam. The **Analytics Instrumentation & Per-Domain Reporting** cross-cut owns the pipeline; domain 22 owns external market intelligence. What is product-owned here is the *pricing decision surface* — the band rendered inside the pricing step at the moment the seller sets a number. A benchmark on a dashboard nobody opens changes no rates. The value is entirely in the placement, and the placement is this domain's. Cross-cut dependency recorded. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Bands only — never individual rates, never individual transactions | Individual exposure is what causes undercutting spirals and is a privacy exposure over a seller's commercial terms. |
| D-02 | Source is accepted engagements, not published listing prices | Listing prices are aspiration; accepted engagements are the market. This is the same "capture the fact where it becomes true" principle as the domain thesis. |
| D-03 | Band by verified credit count, not by self-declared experience | Self-declared seniority is unfalsifiable and would make the band meaningless. Verified credits are the platform's asset. |
| D-04 | Never suppress an honest absence with an estimate | A fabricated band would be used to set real prices. Cold start must say "we don't know yet". |
| D-05 | **The render surface is split at issue.** No band on pre-transactional surfaces (public listing, browse, buyer preview); seller-only in the pricing step and while a quote is in Drafting; **symmetric to both parties once a quote is issued** (05.02.01 D-15) | Ratified 2026-07-30 (run-3 owner queue, `05-services-marketplace#010`), resolving Q-03. The two rules that appeared to collide were argued against different threats: 05.01.01 D-05, sub-domain CX R-04 and domain CX R-06 all reason about a **public, browse-scale** surface, where a band beside a shopfront lets anyone shop the whole cohort's position. A live quote is bilateral, post-request, and already discloses the seller's exact figure to that one buyer, so the marginal exposure is small and the marginal fairness gain is large — the platform must not arm one side of a negotiation it also referees (05.02.01 D-15; the same principle as 05.06.01 D-10 and 05.01.03 DT-11). Does not touch D-01: what renders is still a band, never an individual rate. Raises the stakes on Q-01's *k*, which now gates a buyer-facing render. |

## Open Questions

| Q-01 | What is the minimum cohort size (k) before a band is shown — and, since D-05, **which k**? The band is now buyer-facing on an issued quote, and a band rendered to a buyer *beside one named seller's exact price* needs a larger k than a band rendered privately to the seller pricing their own listing. The buyer holds the figure and the cohort together, and at small k those two read as that seller's position — the individual exposure D-01 exists to prevent, reconstructed by the reader rather than published by the platform. So this is either two thresholds (a pricing-step k and a higher quote k) or one conservative k set by the quote case, and **the buyer-facing threshold is the binding one**.
