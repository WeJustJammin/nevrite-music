## Overview

What happens between "we have a deal" and "this is finished": the engagement's states, the gate that stops work starting before the seller has what they need, the staged structure of long jobs, the revision economics that decide when work stops, the recurring form of an ongoing relationship, the exits when someone walks away, and the tail after the money has moved. This is the sub-domain that makes escrow operable — an escrow with no defined lifecycle is a pot of money with no rule for releasing it.

**Why a sub-domain, not a feature**: the capabilities interact continuously and in both directions. Requirements gating starts the turnaround clock (05.01.06) and, when it never passes, becomes abandonment. A revision request pauses that clock and consumes an allowance whose exhaustion converts the next request into a change order — which re-prices, re-dates, and may re-scope the accepted quote. A milestone splits the escrow into tranches, each with its own cancellation arithmetic. None of these can be specified without the others.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | **Cancellation, Abandonment & Kill Fee added — not in the sweep candidate list** | Deep Think addition. The candidate list specified escrow, milestones, revisions and acceptance but never what happens when someone walks. Escrow cannot be operated without a defined cancellation outcome — every escrowed engagement that stalls becomes a support ticket resolved by a human's judgement, which does not scale and is not fair. Kill fees are standard practice in music and creative services and have no home in the candidate map. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | Post-Delivery Support is framed as the music-industry **recall**, not as generic warranty | A mix recall — reopening a delivered, accepted, paid mix because mastering revealed a problem — is a normal, expected event in music with a well-understood etiquette. Framing it as "support" imports SaaS assumptions that do not fit. See 05.03.06 DT-01, which contains the structural consequence: a recall must reopen a *closed* engagement without unwinding its rights transfer. | Agent, domain expertise |
| D-03 | Retainers use the Subscriptions & Entitlements cross-cut as their engine; what is domain-owned is drawdown | The recurring billing, proration, dunning and lapse behaviour are the cross-cut's. What no cross-cut models is drawing engagements down against an allowance ("4 mixes a month") and what happens to unused allowance. See 05.03.04 DT-01. | Agent, Node Classification Gate |
| D-04 | Custodial engagements (05.07) do **not** use this lifecycle | A repair goes intake → estimate → approve → work → return. There is no requirements gate, no revision allowance, no auto-accept window. Forcing both through one state machine produces states that are nonsense in one half. Recorded as CX-08 in the domain CX file. | Agent, Node Classification Gate |
