## Overview

What the buyer actually ends up owning, what the seller promises about what they delivered, and the moment those become true. This is where the domain's rationale — *"binding rights transfer and split execution to escrow release — the only leverage that exists at the only moment anyone is motivated"* — is either implemented or is marketing. It is the provenance half of D-18 living inside the consolidation half, which is the exact causal link the thesis rests on.

**Why a sub-domain, not a feature**: four interacting capabilities. The posture elected at quote time determines what the transfer executes. Points elected as a pricing model have no cash value and therefore no escrow — so their protection is the rights instrument, which makes them a rights object wearing a price tag. The warranties are conditions of the transfer, not decoration: a master assigned with an uncleared sample in it transfers a liability, not an asset. And the transfer itself is one leg of an atomic three-way commit with escrow and the credit graph.

> **Operator is None across the whole sub-domain, and the absence is structural.** An Operator sells time and space (`meta/personas.md`: "their inventory is time and space, not goods or labour").
