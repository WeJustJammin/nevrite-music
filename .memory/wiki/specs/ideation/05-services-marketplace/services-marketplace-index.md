## Overview

Buying and selling human music work — mixing, mastering, production, remote session performance, topline, arrangement, repair, artwork, PR — with scoped proposals, escrow, revision limits, delivery QC and rights transfer on acceptance.

**Why this is a top-level domain**: In-source and owner-directed (D-07). Distinct from the goods marketplaces because the unit is scoped human output: no catalog, no shipping, no condition — instead briefs, quotes, revision economics, taste disputes, and the ownership question of who holds the resulting master. Distinct from Venues/Studios/Spaces because a mix engagement is a project with milestones while a studio hour is perishable time-sliced inventory with buffers and overtime. SoundBetter (acquired by Spotify), AirGigs and Fiverr Pro validate it. The differentiator no competitor has: binding rights transfer and split execution to escrow release — the only leverage that exists at the only moment anyone is motivated. Music pricing (per-song, per-minute, day rate, per-stem, points) is what makes generic freelance platforms unusable to professionals. Boundary adjusted: buyer-initiated briefs/RFPs and blind auditions moved to Opportunities & Casting, which owns the post→submit→triage→decide workflow. This domain owns the seller's listing and the resulting engagement lifecycle.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies domain status):

- service listings & music pricing models
- quotes/proposals & scope
- order lifecycle with revisions
- delivery, QC & acceptance
- escrow release ↔ rights transfer
- deps, fixers & subcontracting

**Reading the matrix — the shape is the finding:**

- **Musician and Producer are Full everywhere.** This domain is the two of them transacting with each other, in both directions, often within one engagement. That is the multi-hyphenate structural fact (`meta/personas.md`) at its most literal — the same human sells a mix on Monday and buys session bass on Tuesday.
- **Operator is mostly absent, and the absences are load-bearing.** Their inventory is time and space, which is domain 16's model, not this one's. Where they do appear it is narrow and specific: they configure venue-attached services (05.01), see an on-location engagement's schedule and cancellation against their room booking (05.03, 05.05), and — the one exception — are **Full** on custodial services (05.07), where they are the industry's heaviest buyer and frequent seller of technical work. `meta/personas.md` describes them as "frequently both buyer and seller"; 05.07 is where that is literal.
- **Fan is None everywhere except a read-only view of public listings (05.01)** — the SEO/browse surface where a fan discovers that a producer takes work. A hobbyist who then buys a mix is acting as a Musician and is onboarded into that lens. The absence is not an oversight: `meta/personas.md` states the Fan "will not tolerate professional-tool complexity", and this domain is nothing but professional-tool complexity.
