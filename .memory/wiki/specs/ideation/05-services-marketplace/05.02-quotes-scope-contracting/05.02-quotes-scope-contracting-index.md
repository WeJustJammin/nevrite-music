## Overview

The step between "I might hire you" and "we have a deal": a bespoke offer rendered from the seller's listing against the buyer's actual job, negotiated to an agreed scope, gated by confidentiality where the work is secret, and floored by union scale where the session is a union session. Its output is the single artifact every downstream dispute in this domain refers back to — the **accepted quote version**, which is the engagement's scope of record.

**Why a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities that interact. An NDA must be signed before the buyer's material can be shown, which means confidentiality gates the *quote*, not the delivery. Union scale is a floor that overrides the seller's own rate and must be enforced before the quote renders. And ghost-production anonymity — a real, large market — collides directly with the platform's provenance thesis, which is not a detail a feature file can hold.
