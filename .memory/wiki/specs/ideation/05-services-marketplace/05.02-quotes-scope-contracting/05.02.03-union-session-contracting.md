# Feature: Union & Session Contracting

> **Number**: 05.02.03
> **Parent**: [Quotes, Scope & Contracting](./05.02-quotes-scope-contracting-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16
> **Scope note**: **Narrowed.** The sweep candidate read "Union & Session Contracting (scale, doubling, B-forms)". B-form and session reporting are domain 02's (`Union & Performer Session Reporting`). This feature owns the **contracting terms** only. See sub-domain D-01.

## Overview

The terms that apply when a session is a union session: scale minimums that override the seller's own rate, doubling fees when a player covers a second instrument, cartage, overtime, and new-use provisions that reach forward in time. A union date is priced by a rate card the platform does not own and cannot negotiate, and a quote that ignores it is not a discount — it is a violation that hurts the player.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The persona this protects. A union session player elects union terms on their listing; the platform floors any quote below scale and computes doubling, cartage and overtime rather than leaving the player to argue for them at the end of a long day. Sees their session's terms rendered in a form they recognise from paper. Their `Switching Trigger` — "losing money to a fact they couldn't prove" — is precisely a doubling fee nobody wrote down. |
| Producer | Config | Configures whether an engagement is a union date, which is a consequential election: it changes the price floor, the pension/health obligations, and the reporting duty downstream (domain 02). The Producer is usually the party who *knows* the session's status, which is why the election sits with them and not with the buyer. |
| Operator | None | Not visible. Studio-side union obligations (a signatory facility) are the Operator's own business and belong to domain 16 if anywhere; they do not surface in this engagement's terms. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

The platform is **not** a signatory to any collective bargaining agreement and cannot become one. What it can be is *accurate*: hold the scale tables as reference data, floor the rendered price (05.01.03), compute the derived fees (doubling, cartage, overtime) from facts the engagement already knows, template the correct instrument via the Contracts cross-cut, and emit the completed session's data to domain 02 for B-form reporting.

That framing is the whole feature: **a rate floor, a fee calculator, a contract template and a data feed** — not enforcement.

### Happy Path

1. Seller marks a listing as available for union dates and states their union/local
2. Buyer (or Producer) elects union terms when requesting a quote, and states the session type (master, demo, jingle, film/TV — each has its own scale)
3. System renders the price and **floors it at scale** for that session type and duration
4. Derived fees compute from engagement facts: doubling from the instruments credited, overtime from the session's actual length, cartage from the instrument
5. Quote issues with the union instrument attached; below-scale issue is blocked
6. Session happens; on acceptance the engagement emits the session data to domain 02, which owns the B-form/reporting act

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Quote below scale | Issue blocked | "Below AFM scale for a master session at this length. Minimum {x}." |
| Scale tables out of date | The platform is quoting a wrong floor — worse than quoting none, because it is trusted | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — sourcing and refresh of scale data is unsolved; see Q-01. **This is the feature's central risk.** |
| Player doubles (bass → upright) mid-session | Doubling fee derives from the credited instruments (domain 02), not from a manual claim | "You doubled on this date — +{x} added." — the player never has to ask |
| Session overruns | Overtime derives from actual session length | Requires attendance/session facts — see Cross-Cut Notes |
| New use (a demo becomes a master; a track lands in a film) | A payment obligation triggers *years later*, long after this engagement closed | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see DT-03 and Q-02 |
| Non-union player on a union date, or vice versa | A mixed session is a real and messy case | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| Jurisdiction has no equivalent union structure | Feature is inapplicable, not broken | Election unavailable; standard terms |
| Buyer elects union terms to look legitimate but is not a signatory | The platform floored the price and templated a document for a deal it cannot back | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see Q-03 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Not applicable | Non-union engagement (the default) | Feature absent |
| Elected | Union terms chosen | Scale floor active; union fields on the quote |
| Floored | Rendered price below scale | Price raised to scale with the reason shown |
| Blocked | Attempted below-scale issue | Explanation + minimum |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **The platform cannot be a signatory to a CBA, so it cannot enforce union terms — it can only be accurate about them** | Reasoning about what this feature can actually be, before deciding whether it should exist. | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is clarifying rather than deflating. Enforcement is not the gap. The gap is that scale, doubling, cartage and overtime are *knowable numbers* that working musicians routinely fail to collect because nobody computed them at the moment the session happened. The platform's honest role is a rate floor + a fee calculator + a contract template + a data feed to 02. That is a genuinely valuable product and it makes no claim it cannot keep. It also sets the boundary against 02 cleanly: this computes what is owed, 02 reports that it happened. |
| DT-02 | **Doubling, cartage and overtime should derive from engagement facts, not from a player's claim** | The whole product thesis (D-18) applied locally: be present when the fact becomes true rather than asking someone to remember it. | ✅ CONFIRMED — and this is the feature's best argument for existing at all. A doubling fee is owed because a bassist also played upright; that fact is already in the credit graph (02) as two instrument credits on one session. The platform can compute the fee from a fact it already holds, rather than relying on a player to invoice for it after a 10-hour day — which is exactly the situation in which musicians do not invoice for it. Same for overtime (session length) and cartage (instrument). This turns a compliance feature into money the player would otherwise have lost, which is the persona's stated `Switching Trigger`. |
| DT-03 | **New-use obligations reach forward in time and outlive the engagement — so this feature cannot be contained in the engagement lifecycle** | Adversarial: what does a domain expert expect here that nobody mentioned? | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real gap, and deliberately **not** solved here. A demo that becomes a master, or a track that lands in a film, triggers a payment to the original session players years after this engagement closed and was paid. Nothing in this domain's lifecycle can carry that — an engagement terminates. The obligation is a long-lived attachment to the *recording*, which is domain 09/10 territory, and its trigger (a new use) originates in domain 11 (Licensing) or 12 (Release). Recorded as Q-02 and flagged to those domains rather than invented here. This is also a strong secondary argument for the `could` MoSCoW proposal: the feature is not self-contained. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **05.01.03 Pricing Model Library** ([path](../05.01-service-listings-pricing/05.01.03-music-pricing-model-library.md)) — scale is a floor applied to the rendered price. The floor overrides the seller's own rate, which is the only place in the domain where that happens.
- Touches **05.02.01 Quotes** ([path](./05.02.01-custom-quotes-proposals-scope.md)) — the floor is enforced before issue.
- Touches **02 Credits & Attribution** ([path](../../02-credits-attribution/)) — **the boundary**: 02 owns `Union & Performer Session Reporting` (B-forms, SAG-AFTRA). This feature computes the terms; 02 reports the session. 02's instrument credits are also the input to the doubling calculation (DT-02), and its Session Attendance Proof is the input to overtime.
- Touches **09 Rights & Ownership** / **10 Royalties & Collections** ([path](../../09-rights-ownership/)) — new-use obligations attach to the recording, not the engagement (DT-03).
- Touches **01 Identity, Profiles & Organizations** ([path](../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/)) — 01 owns `Professional, Union & Credential Verification`; this feature consumes union membership status, it does not verify it.
- Consumes **Contracts & E-Signature** cross-cut — union instrument templating.
- Consumes **Canonical Data, Taxonomy & Entity Resolution** cross-cut — scale tables are reference data with a refresh problem (Q-01).
- Consumes **Localization, Currency & Timezone** cross-cut — union structures are jurisdiction-specific and mostly US/UK; most of the world has no equivalent.
- Consumes **Payments, Escrow & Payouts** cross-cut — pension/health contributions are additional payees on a session payment, which the cross-cut's multi-party split model should already cover.
