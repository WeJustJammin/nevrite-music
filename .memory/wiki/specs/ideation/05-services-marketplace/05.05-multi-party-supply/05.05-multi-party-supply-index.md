## Overview

Everything that happens when the seller is not one person. A dep covers a date the booked player cannot make; a fixer contracts a string section and is paid one fee; an agency sells its roster's work and takes a commission; a bundle assembles several independent sellers into one purchase. Four shapes, one structural fact: **the person the buyer paid is not (only) the person who did the work.**

That fact is what makes this a sub-domain rather than four scattered features, and it is what makes it dangerous to the platform's thesis. Every one of these shapes inserts a party between the money and the labour, and every one of them is a place where the credit graph can silently lose the humans who actually played.

Recorded rather than cut — the owner decides. | Agent, MoSCoW proposal |

| Q-01 | [OWNER] When a fixer or agency stands between the buyer and the players, **who is the credit graph's counterparty**? If the platform records the engagement's seller, a string section of twelve becomes one credit for a contractor who did not play a note.
