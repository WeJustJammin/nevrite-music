## Overview

The services where the seller takes physical possession of the buyer's property: a luthier refretting a guitar, a tech servicing a console, an inspector examining an amp before someone buys it. The work is still scoped human labour — which is why it is in this domain and not in Gear (13) — but everything around it inverts. There is no requirements gate, no revision allowance, no auto-accept window, no deliverable to QC and no rights to transfer. There is instead a two-way shipment, a chain of custody, an estimate the buyer approves before work begins, and a real risk that someone's £8,000 vintage instrument is damaged, lost, or trapped in a workshop that has closed.

**Why a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities that interact. An inspection report becomes a repair estimate. Both are governed by the same custody chain. A damage claim mid-repair changes the job ticket's outcome and the custody record is the only evidence of who held what, when, and in what condition.
