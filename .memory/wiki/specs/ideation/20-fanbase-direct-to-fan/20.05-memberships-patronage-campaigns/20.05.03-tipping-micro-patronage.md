# Feature: Tipping & Micro-Patronage

> **Number**: 20.05.03
> **Parent**: [Memberships, Patronage & Campaigns](./20.05-memberships-patronage-campaigns-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-22

## Overview

A fan giving an artist money for nothing in return — no tier, no entitlement, no obligation. It is the
lightest possible transaction in the domain and the only one where **buying nothing is the point**: a fan
who wants to say thank you without joining anything, at the moment they feel it. Tipping is the ungated
sibling of membership, and its value is precisely that it commits neither party to anything.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Turns tipping on, sets suggested amounts, sees tips arrive with whatever message came attached. Sees tips as fan observations, not anonymous income — a tipper is a fan record ([20.01.01](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.01-unified-fan-record.md)) and a strong signal. |
| Producer | Reports (as split payee) | If a tip is attached to a **work** rather than to the artist, the split may apply — see DT-02. If it is attached to the person, it does not. That distinction decides whether the drummer sees any of it, and it is not obvious. |
| Operator | None | Venues do not take tips from fans. |
| Fan | Full | Tips. Picks an amount, optionally says something. Gets nothing, deliberately, and that is why they will do it. |

## Behavior

A tip is a payment with no entitlement (`-cx.md` R-01 — giving tips entitlements would turn them into
micro-memberships and destroy the zero-commitment property that makes them work).

The design question that matters is **what a tip attaches to**:

| Attaches to | Consequence |
|---|---|
| The artist (a person/entity) | Money goes to them. Clean. The drummer sees none of it. |
| A **work** (this track, this record) | The split arguably applies — you are paying for the thing four people made. |
| A **moment** (a livestream, `-cx.md` CX-05) | The highest-conversion context; attribution is ambiguous. |

Nobody agreed a split on a gift, which is the same problem as name-your-price
([20.04](../20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront/20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront-cx.md) CX-03) in a purer and harder
form: at least NYP has a base price to reason from. A tip has nothing.

**NYP's half of that problem is now settled and tipping's is not, and the settlement does not reach here.**
The split basis is a platform-wide constant — paid-net — printed on the split-capture screen
([20.04.04](../20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront/20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md) D-10/D-11, ratified
DQ-19.1/19.2/19.3). That rule fixes **what a sale's percentages are applied to**; it says nothing about
**whether a tip is a sale at all**, which is this file's open question (Q-01) and is decided by what a tip
attaches to (D-03, DT-02), not by a basis. Under D-03 — tips attach to the person, splits never apply — a tip
never reaches a basis at all. Ratifying DQ-19.3 explicitly left D-03 untouched.

Tip **placement** on the artist's surfaces and the shape of the **suggested-amount ladder** are presentation
detail — `[PENDING — /write-fe-spec]`. Whether tip **messages are public, artist-only or optional** is an owner
ruling and is tracked as Q-02 (`/create-prd`), not as a layout question.

### Happy Path

1. Fan finishes listening to a record they love on an artist's page
2. Taps "buy them a drink"; picks £5; types *saw you in Leeds in 2019, this record got me through a bad year*
3. Payment via CX-M01; artist paid
4. Artist reads the message. **The message is worth more than the £5 and both of them know it**
5. Tip and message land on the fan's record; the fan is now known to be a real one

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Tip attached to a work with a split on file | Does the drummer get 10% of a gift? Nobody agreed a percentage of a tip. See DT-02. | **Answered by D-03** — tips attach to the person, so no split applies and the drummer receives none of it; the rule is **stated at split time** on the same split-capture screen [20.04.04](../20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront/20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md) D-10 uses. Owner confirmation of the underlying reading is Q-01 (`[OWNER]`, `/create-prd`). |
| Abusive message attached to a tip | **A tip is a delivery mechanism for a message that bypasses every block.** Someone can pay £1 to say something vile to an artist who has blocked them everywhere else. Fan anti-persona: *"harasses artists through open contact surfaces."* | **Answered by D-04** — "Messages respect blocks and route through moderation": a blocked sender's paid message never reaches the artist, and the message field is treated as a moderated channel by default, not an exception path. Adjudication and repeat-offender handling remain **24**'s. This is real and it is not an edge case; it is the predictable use. |
| Tiny tip (£0.50) | Payment fees eat most of it. Under some rails the artist nets pennies and it costs us more than we take. | Minimum, or accrual. |
| Fan tips instead of buying | Fine — arguably better for the artist (no fulfilment, no cut of goods). Only a problem if it cannibalises a purchase that would have been larger. | Nothing. |
| [OWNER] Refund request on a tip | It was a gift. Chargebacks on gifts are messy and the fan's bank will side with the fan. | `[PENDING — /create-prd]` — whether a gift is voluntarily refundable at all, and who absorbs a chargeback on one, is a platform policy call no later stage derives on its own. Not covered by [20.04.04](../20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront/20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md) D-08's reserve, which covers split payouts and a tip has no split (D-03). |
| Tip during a livestream | `-cx.md` CX-05. Highest conversion, and imports a culture (name-on-screen, tip-spam) that may be wrong for a listening party. See DT-03. | **Answered by D-05** — tipping stays available during an event, but with **no Twitch-style tip alerts by default**: no name on screen, no escalating-amount leaderboard. The tip reaches the artist quietly (DT-03 — "keeps the conversion and skips the casino"); the burden of proof is on adding the leaderboard, not on omitting it. |
| Money-laundering via tips | Untraceable value transfer with no goods and no delivery obligation is a structurally attractive laundering channel. Adjacent to KYC/AML, flagged `[PENDING]` in `meta/constraints.md`. | `[PENDING]` — `/create-prd-security`. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Tipping is a minor feature — a tip jar on a profile | Its size in the candidate list; it looks like a button | ⏳ **Confirmed as revenue, rejected as a signal.** The money is small and will stay small. But **the message is the product**: a tip carries a sentence from a fan who has never otherwise spoken to the artist, and that sentence is the most concentrated fan signal in the domain. It is also — see the edge cases — an unfiltered channel into an artist's attention that bypasses every block they have. So a "minor" feature is simultaneously the best fan-intent data we get and a harassment vector. Both of those are bigger than the money. |
| DT-02 | Tips go to the artist; splits do not apply | The obvious and probably correct default | ⏳ **Unresolved, and sharper than NYP.** If a tip attaches to a *work*, the fan is plausibly paying for the thing four people made — and the drummer on 10% has an argument. If it attaches to the *person*, they clearly do not. NYP at least has a base price to reason from ([20.04](../20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront/20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront-cx.md) CX-03); a tip has nothing but the fan's intent, which is unknowable. **My read: tips attach to the person and splits never apply** — a gift is a gift — but this must be *stated to the collaborator when they agree the split*, or the first person to notice a tipped record will feel cheated. That obligation now has an obvious home and a precedent: [20.04.04](../20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront/20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md) Q-01 closed by putting the sale basis on the **split-capture screen** as a stated platform rule (their D-10/D-11), which is exactly the surface and exactly the shape a "tips are outside this split" line needs. The precedent is the disclosure pattern, **not** the answer: whether tips are in or out of splits stays Q-01 here. |
| DT-03 | Livestream tipping should work like Twitch — alerts, names on screen, escalating amounts | The dominant precedent, and it demonstrably works | ❌ REJECTED for this audience, with moderate confidence. Twitch's tipping culture is a **status game between viewers**, and it works for streamers whose product is presence. Importing it into a listening party turns a room of people hearing a record into a leaderboard, and it changes what the artist is doing on stage — they start performing for tips mid-song. `-cx.md` CX-05 is right that the moment converts; that does not mean the mechanic should be copied. A quiet tip that reaches the artist afterwards keeps the conversion and skips the casino. Not certain — but the burden should be on adding the casino, not on omitting it. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Explicitly **not** entitlement-granting — `-cx.md` R-01. The zero-commitment property is the feature.
- Highest conversion inside **[20.05.06 Virtual Fan Events](./20.05.06-virtual-fan-events.md)** — `-cx.md` CX-05.
- Possibly touches **[20.04.04 D2F Revenue Split & Payout](../20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront/20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md)** — only if tips attach to works (DT-02). My reading is they do not.
- Writes to **[20.01.01 Unified Fan Record](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.01-unified-fan-record.md)** — a tipper is a known fan, and the message is the richest thing on their record.
- Feeds **[20.02.02 Superfan Score](../20.02-segmentation-superfan-intelligence/20.02.02-superfan-score.md)** — a costly voluntary act with nothing received is close to a pure devotion signal.
- Consumes **CX-M01 Payments, Escrow & Payouts** — micro-payment economics; fees can exceed the tip.
- Consumes **CX-M02 Tax & Fiscal Compliance** — is a tip income, a gift, or a donation? The answer differs by jurisdiction and it is not rhetorical.
- **Touches 24 Trust, Safety & Disputes** ([../../24-trust-safety-disputes/](../../24-trust-safety-disputes/)) — **the message field is a block-bypassing harassment channel.** Predictable, not hypothetical.
- Touches **CX-M06 Messaging & Contextual Inbox** — the message has to land somewhere, and it must respect the artist's blocks.
- Touches **CX-M31 Safeguarding & Minor Protection** — minors tipping; a child's pocket money reaching an adult stranger.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A tip grants nothing | `-cx.md` R-01. Entitlements would make it a micro-membership and destroy the zero-commitment property that makes people do it. |
| D-02 | The message is the product; the money is a byproduct | See DT-01. It is the most concentrated fan signal the domain collects. |
| D-03 | Tips attach to the person; splits do not apply — **and this is stated at split time** | See DT-02. My reading, needing an owner ruling (Q-01). The obligation to state it up front is not optional either way. **Untouched by the 20.04 basis ratification (DQ-19.3, 2026-07-22), which explicitly preserved this row**: that decision fixed the basis of a *sale* and left tips outside splits exactly as written here. The stating surface is the same one 20.04.04 D-10 uses — the split-capture screen. |
| D-04 | Messages respect blocks and route through moderation | The predictable use of a paid message field is to bypass a block. Designing as if it were rare is designing for the wrong user. |
| D-05 | No Twitch-style tip alerts by default | See DT-03. The burden of proof is on adding the leaderboard, not on omitting it. |
