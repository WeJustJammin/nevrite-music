# Feature: Membership Tiers & Benefits

> **Number**: 20.05.01
> **Parent**: [Memberships, Patronage & Campaigns](./20.05-memberships-patronage-campaigns-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Fans paying an artist monthly in exchange for being closer to the work: a ladder of tiers, each with benefits
— the vault, early access, members-only events, a name in the sleeve notes. Patreon proved that recurring fan
money now materially exceeds streaming for most independents, and Bandcamp subscriptions proved it works
natively inside a music store rather than on a separate platform the fan has to be sent to.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Defines tiers, prices, and what each unlocks. Sees members, MRR, churn, and — the number that actually predicts churn — **how long since they last gave members anything**. Sees who their members are as fan records ([20.01.01](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.01-unified-fan-record.md)), not as subscription rows. |
| Producer | None | No visibility. A producer's own membership would be the Musician lens. |
| Operator | None | Currently excluded — see index Q-04. Venue supporter schemes are real and growing (members' bars, priority booking, grassroots supporter clubs), so this `None` may be an under-scope rather than a boundary. |
| Fan | Read-only (manages own) | Joins, upgrades, downgrades, cancels — cancellation must be as easy as joining, or the churn you avoid becomes a chargeback and a complaint. Sees exactly what each tier unlocks *before* paying, and sees locked benefits as visible-and-locked ([20.05.02](./20.05.02-exclusive-content-vault.md)). |

## Behavior

Billing — recurring charges, dunning, proration, entitlement state — is **CX-M24** (index D-01). What lives
here is what a music membership *is*: a ladder of tiers, each granting entitlements that
[20.05.02](./20.05.02-exclusive-content-vault.md) and [20.05.06](./20.05.06-virtual-fan-events.md) check.

The thing that kills music memberships is not price and not churn mechanics. It is **the artist stopping**.
Month one is exciting, month four is silent, and the membership becomes a direct debit for nothing — at which
point the fan does not just cancel, they learn that memberships are a trick. The platform therefore has to
treat *artist* engagement as the health metric, not fan engagement. This is the inverse of every subscription
tool ever built, which watches the customer.

### Happy Path

1. Musician sets three tiers: £3 (vault + early listens), £10 (+ members-only livestream), £25 (+ name in the sleeve notes of the next record, limited to 100)
2. Fan joins at £10; CX-M24 handles billing; entitlement granted
3. Fan immediately gets vault access ([20.05.02](./20.05.02-exclusive-content-vault.md)) and appears on the next members-only stream's guest list ([20.05.06](./20.05.06-virtual-fan-events.md))
4. Artist posts a demo to the vault; entitled members are notified — the drumbeat that keeps it alive
5. Fan stays for two years because something keeps arriving

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Artist stops posting** | The real churn driver. The vault goes quiet, members drift, and the model is discredited for that fan across every artist. The platform must nudge the artist, not the fan. | Artist: "Your members haven't had anything in 6 weeks. 340 people are paying you £2,400 a month." |
| Tier deleted while members hold it | Cannot orphan them. Grandfather, migrate, or refund — a decision, never a silent drop. | Forced choice at deletion, with the member count in the sentence. |
| Fan downgrades mid-cycle | Access holds to period end, then narrows. They paid for the period. | Effective date stated. |
| Fan lapses; what happens to what they had? | The hard one (`-cx.md` CX-01 Q2). Content released *while* they were a member — is it theirs? Patreon says no; a fan's intuition says yes; a fan who loses a year of demos feels robbed. | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see [20.05.02](./20.05.02-exclusive-content-vault.md) DT-02. |
| Limited tier oversells (101st person takes the 100-slot sleeve-note tier) | Must not. It is a scarcity promise like a limited pressing. | Tier closes at cap, truthfully. |
| Card fails | CX-M24 dunning. Grace period, then narrow access. Cutting a two-year member off for a bank glitch is an unforced error. | Dunning + grace. |
| Artist quits the platform / dies | Members are paying for a relationship that no longer exists. Billing must stop, and someone has to decide. Bands end; people die; this is not hypothetical. | `[PENDING]` — Q-03. |
| Physical benefit (sleeve notes, a postcard) never delivered | A membership with a physical promise is a fulfilment obligation wearing a subscription — the [20.05.05](./20.05.05-backer-funds-fulfilment.md) hazard leaking into memberships. | `[PENDING]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Membership page opened | Tier skeleton |
| Empty | No tiers | Artist: the honest warning first — this only works if you keep feeding it. Fan: nothing; no membership exists. |
| Populated | ≥1 tier | Tier ladder, benefits, member counts where the artist chooses to show them (social proof cuts both ways at low counts) |
| Error | Billing provider down | Existing entitlements **hold**. Never revoke access because a payment processor is having an afternoon. |
| Partial | Fan is mid-dunning | Access retained during grace, with a quiet, non-punitive prompt |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This duplicates CX-M24 (Subscriptions & Entitlements) and should be dissolved | Node Classification Gate; the domain rationale already routed the billing engine to CX-M24 | ❌ REJECTED — CX-M24 is billing and entitlement mechanics serving 8 domains (plugin licences, pro subscriptions, venue plans). None of them model **what an artist owes a fan every month for the rest of the relationship**. A plugin licence is a transaction that completed; a music membership is an obligation that never completes. The domain-owned part is the ladder, the benefit model, the vault relationship, and the artist-side health metric — none of which a billing engine has an opinion about. Index D-01. |
| DT-02 | Churn is a fan-side problem to be solved with retention mechanics | Every subscription playbook | ❌ REJECTED, and it is the most important inversion in this feature. Music-membership churn is **caused by the artist stopping**, not by fans becoming fickle. Discounts, win-back emails and pause options treat the symptom; the disease is an empty vault. **The health metric is artist output, not fan engagement** — so the retention product is a nudge to the artist, which no subscription tool does because in every other market the supply side is a company, not a tired human on tour. |
| DT-03 | More tiers means more revenue | Standard pricing intuition — capture more willingness-to-pay | ⏳ **Leaning reject, unproven.** More tiers means more promises, and every promise is content the artist must produce forever. A three-tier membership with a dead £25 tier is worse than a one-tier membership that is honoured — it advertises the artist's unreliability at the top of their own page. My instinct is that the platform should push artists toward **fewer tiers than they want**, which is the opposite of what a revenue-maximising design would do and possibly the opposite of what artists will accept. No evidence. Q-02. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Billing is CX-M24; the membership product is here | See DT-01. A plugin licence completes; a membership never does. |
| D-02 | The health metric is artist output, not fan engagement | See DT-02. The inversion that makes this feature different from every subscription tool. |
| D-03 | Cancellation is as easy as joining | The alternative is chargebacks and complaints, which cost more than the retained month — and a fan trapped by a dark pattern tells other fans. |
| D-04 | Existing entitlements survive billing outages | Never revoke a two-year member's access because a processor is down. |
| D-05 | Limited tiers are hard caps | A capped tier is a scarcity promise, same as a limited pressing. |
