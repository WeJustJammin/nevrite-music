## Overview

Fans paying artists **ongoing** rather than per-object: membership tiers, the vault of things members get,
tips, crowdfunding and pre-order campaigns, and the virtual events that are increasingly what a membership
buys. Patreon proved the model — recurring fan money now materially exceeds streaming for most independents —
and PledgeMusic proved the failure mode, collapsing in 2019 owing artists millions because fan money was spent
before the records were made.

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: a tier defines an entitlement; the vault checks
that entitlement; a virtual event is gated by it; a crowdfunding backer receives a tier-shaped reward; and
tips are the ungated version of the same impulse. They share one entitlement model and one pot of fan money
with obligations attached. Specified separately they would each invent their own entitlement check and their
own fund policy — and the fund policy is the one that ends companies.
