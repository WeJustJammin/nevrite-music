# Feature: Virtual Fan Events (Livestreams & Listening Parties)

> **Number**: 20.05.06
> **Parent**: [Memberships, Patronage & Campaigns](./20.05-memberships-patronage-campaigns-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

A scheduled, access-gated event for the artist's own audience: a livestream from a rehearsal room, a listening
party for a record on release day, a members-only Q&A. It gives a membership a **heartbeat** — a vault is
asynchronous and easy to ignore, an event is an appointment — and it is the one tier benefit that does not
require the artist to keep manufacturing exclusive content. They just have to turn up and play.

> **Merged** — the sweep listed "Ticketed Live Streams & Virtual Shows" (14) and "Ticketed Listening Parties"
> (15) as two candidates. They are one mechanic (a scheduled, access-gated, streamed event for your own
> audience) differing only in content: one you perform, one you play. Index D-04.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Schedules an event, sets access (members free / tier N+ / ticketed / open), streams, sees who is in the room — and sees them as **fan records**, not viewer counts. Knowing that the person watching from Osaka has been a member for three years is something no streaming platform can tell them. |
| Producer | None | No visibility. A producer streaming their own thing is the Musician lens. |
| Operator | Config | Index Q-04 — currently narrow. **A grassroots venue streaming its own shows to a supporter scheme is a real, underserved product**, and the room already has the PA, the desk and the acts. Possibly an under-scope. |
| Fan | Read-only (attends) | Sees it announced, gets reminded, gets in — free as a member or by ticket. Watches. Possibly tips (`-cx.md` CX-05). Does not get ejected if their membership lapses mid-show. |

## Behavior

The domain-owned part is narrow and worth stating precisely, because most of this feature belongs elsewhere:

| Concern | Owner |
|---|---|
| The ticket, the sale, the box office | **19 Ticketing** |
| Audio/video transport, the room, presence | **CX-M11 Real-Time Rooms, Presence & Audio Transport** |
| Production, crew, scheduling of a real show | **18 Show Production & Touring** |
| **Entitlement gating + fan-list audience + the membership relationship** | **here** |

What makes it D2F rather than "a show with a ticket" is that the audience is *your fan list* and access is
*your membership tier*. Strip those and it is a domain 19 event that happens to be online.

Two access paths, one door (`-cx.md` CX-03 Q3): a member's entitlement and a ticket-holder's ticket must
grant **identical** access, or they will diverge and someone will be wrongly locked out of a gig they paid for.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — chat/interaction model, recording and vault archival,
whether the platform streams at all or embeds elsewhere.

### Happy Path

1. Musician schedules a listening party for release day: members free, others £5
2. Entitled members notified; non-members see it and can join a tier or buy a ticket — **the event is a membership conversion surface, which is its second job**
3. Release day: fans arrive, the record plays, the artist talks over it
4. A fan joins the £10 tier **during** the stream to get in; access is immediate (`-cx.md` CX-03 Q2) — waiting would waste the only urgency this domain ever manufactures
5. Recording lands in the vault ([20.05.02](./20.05.02-exclusive-content-vault.md)) for members who missed it
6. Tips arrive during and after (`-cx.md` CX-05)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Membership lapses mid-stream | **Do not eject someone from a gig they are watching.** Grace to the end of the event. The humane answer is also the obvious one. | Nothing. |
| Fan joins a tier mid-stream | Immediate access. Making them wait for a billing cycle wastes the conversion. | They're in. |
| **Stream fails mid-show** | The one failure with no recovery. A ticketed audience paid for something that did not happen; a member's heartbeat became a disappointment. Live is live. | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — refunds for ticket-holders (19), and what a member is owed for a free thing that failed. |
| Artist's upload is 2 Mbps from a rehearsal basement | The normal case, not the exception. Rehearsal rooms have concrete walls and terrible wifi, and that is where the content is. | CX-M11's problem, but the failure is ours in the fan's eyes. |
| Fan records and reposts the stream | Same as [20.05.02](./20.05.02-exclusive-content-vault.md) DT-01 — it is marketing, not theft. Do not build DRM for a livestream. | Nothing. |
| Timezone: "8pm" for a global fanbase | Somebody is watching at 4am. Unlike a broadcast ([20.03.03](../20.03-broadcast-fan-messaging/20.03.03-send-scheduling-throttling.md)), an event **cannot be timezone-shifted** — it happens once. The recording in the vault is the only answer, and it is not the same thing. | Local time shown; recording promised. |
| Chat becomes a harassment surface | A live room of strangers with the artist present. Fan anti-persona. Real-time moderation is a different discipline from post-hoc moderation and the platform will not have it on day one. | `[PENDING]` — 24. |
| Nobody comes | The quiet failure. 12 people in a room the artist told 4,000 fans about. Brutal, and worth designing away from — small numbers should not be broadcast to the artist mid-show. | `[PENDING]` |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Livestreams and listening parties are separate features | The sweep listed them as two candidates | ❌ REJECTED — one mechanic, two contents. Both are a scheduled, access-gated, streamed event for your own audience; one you perform and one you play. Splitting them would produce two nodes sharing an access model, a scheduling model, a transport and a fan-notification path, differing only in what comes out of the speakers. Merged per index D-04. |
| DT-02 | This should be its own sub-domain — it has scheduling, gating, streaming, chat, tipping, recording | The capability count looks like a sub-domain | ❌ REJECTED — the capability count is an illusion, because almost none of them are domain-owned. Transport is CX-M11, the ticket is 19, production is 18, tipping is [20.05.03](./20.05.03-tipping-micro-patronage.md), the recording lands in [20.05.02](./20.05.02-exclusive-content-vault.md), notifications are CX-M07. A sub-domain here would be a folder with an index, a CX file, and a hollow middle. **What is genuinely ours is entitlement-gating a fan-list audience** — one capability. A feature. Worth recording because "it feels big" is exactly the wrong reason to create a sub-domain, and the count would have justified it. |
| DT-03 | The value is the revenue from ticketed streams | The candidate names both lead with "Ticketed" | ❌ REJECTED — ticketed-stream revenue was a 2020 lockdown phenomenon and it has substantially reverted; fans who can go to a gig go to a gig. The durable value is **the membership heartbeat**: an event is an appointment, and appointments are what convert a recurring payment from a subscription into a relationship. It is also the *cheapest* tier benefit to produce — unlike the vault it requires no exclusive content, only presence, which directly addresses the churn cause in [20.05.01](./20.05.01-membership-tiers-benefits.md) DT-02 (artists stop posting). **Reframing this as a membership feature rather than a revenue feature changes what to build**: optimise for low-effort recurrence, not for box office. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
