## Overview

The fan's own product. Every other sub-domain in domain 20 treats the fan as an object — a record, a segment,
a recipient, a buyer. This one treats them as a **user** (D-11), which is what they are: following artists,
being told when those artists do something they can act on, owning what they bought, and never again finding
out after the show that their favourite band played their city.

**This is the sub-domain that must not read as a professional tool.** `meta/personas.md` is unambiguous: the
Fan is "the only persona with no professional stake — they will not tolerate professional-tool complexity,
will not read a rider, and will never file a split sheet. **Their surface must be a different product wearing
the same brand.**"

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: following, alerting, owning and bootstrapping are
one loop. A follow is worthless without an alert; an alert has nothing to fire on without a follow; the
library is what a fan gets for their money and the strongest reason to return; and the bootstrap is what
populates the follow graph on day one — without it, a fan follows nobody and the alert product is dead on
arrival.
