# Feature: Channel Routing & Bulk Delivery (Email / SMS / Push)

> **Number**: 20.03.02
> **Parent**: [Broadcast & Fan Messaging](./20.03-broadcast-fan-messaging-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The dispatch layer: resolve each fan's permitted and preferred channel, render the right version, hand it to
the right transport, and collect what came back. It is where consent stops being a record and starts being an
enforcement point — a fan consented to email but not SMS gets email, whatever the artist composed
(`-cx.md` CX-01 Q3).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | Chooses which channels a campaign *may* use — a proposal, not a guarantee. Configures sender identity (from-name, reply-to, SMS sender ID where regulated). Sees per-channel delivery outcomes. Cannot force a channel onto a fan who did not consent to it. |
| Producer | None | No visibility. |
| Operator | Config | Same. A venue's SMS is arguably more valuable than an artist's — "doors in 1 hour, the support act moved" is genuinely urgent, which is exactly the content SMS is for and email is not. |
| Fan | Read-only | Does not configure routing directly; expresses channel preference via [20.01.03](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.03-fan-preference-centre.md), which routing obeys absolutely. Sees the result: one message, on the channel they chose. |

## Behavior

Per fan, per campaign, routing resolves: which channels hold a valid marketing consent for this artist
([20.01.02](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.02-consent-legal-basis.md)) → which of those the fan
prefers → one channel wins. **A fan consented on all three must not receive the same announcement three
times** — the deduplication rule is the difference between a channel strategy and a fan telling everyone your
platform spams.

The three channels are not variations of one thing:

| Channel | Cost | Consent bar | Latency expectation | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Email** | ~free | consent/legitimate interest | hours | spam folder — silent |
| **SMS** | real per-message | high (TCPA — statutory damages per message) | seconds | 3am buzz — loud, expensive, unforgettable |
| **Push** | free | OS-level, revocable outside our control | seconds | ignored, or app deleted |

Push is only available where the fan has an app — and `meta/constraints.md` declares **web as the only
surface**, with mobile an open question. Web push exists but is weak on iOS. This means push is, today, a
channel this domain mostly cannot use. D-13 already flags that fan gig alerts are "push-notification and
phone-shaped workflows" and that this strengthens the mobile-surface question. **This feature is a direct
piece of evidence for that decision.**

### Happy Path

1. Campaign dispatched to a 3,340-fan contactable audience
2. Per fan: consent resolved → preference resolved → channel chosen (2,890 email, 410 SMS, 40 push)
3. Each renders its own version ([20.03.01](./20.03.01-campaign-composer.md))
4. Handed to transports at the rate reputation permits ([20.03.03](./20.03.03-send-scheduling-throttling.md))
5. Telemetry returns: delivered, bounced, complained, opened, clicked → feeds [20.03.04](./20.03.04-deliverability-sender-reputation.md) (`-cx.md` CX-03)
6. Fan gets one message, once, on the channel they picked

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Fan consented on all three channels | One message on the **fan-preferred** channel (D-06). The fan's preference-centre choice ([20.01.03](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.03-fan-preference-centre.md)) is the priority key; the artist's channel set only narrows the candidate pool, it never re-ranks it. Never three copies. | One message, on the channel they picked. |
| Fan's only consented channel is SMS; the SMS leg fails | Not silently downgraded to email — they did not consent to email. Failure is a failure. | Artist sees it in the delivery report; fan sees nothing, correctly. |
| SMS to an invalid/landline number | Hard failure; suppress the number. | Delivery report. |
| Push token dead (app deleted) | Suppress; do not fall back to email without email consent. | Delivery report. |
| Transport provider outage mid-send | Campaign pauses, does not fail. Resumes without re-sending to those already delivered — idempotency per (campaign, fan) is mandatory. | "Paused — SMS provider is down. 1,204 of 3,340 sent." |
| Duplicate dispatch after a retry | Must be impossible. A fan receiving a tour announcement twice is a complaint; a fan receiving it four times is an unsubscribe. | Nothing. |
| Fan revokes consent mid-send | Remaining sends suppressed immediately (`20.01.03` CX-03). | Nothing. |
| Fan consented but expressed no channel preference | No preference recorded → deterministic default order. Email-default is the safe Level-1 assumption (cheapest, lowest consent risk); cost-first email vs engagement-first SMS is a product lever. `[PENDING — Step 5 Must-depth or /write-be-spec]`. | One message, on the platform-default channel. |
| Fan's SMS consent has lapsed (TCPA staleness) but the record still lists it | Routing gates on consent *freshness*, not just presence ([20.01.02](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.02-consent-legal-basis.md)). Stale consent is no consent; the channel drops from the candidate pool. | Falls to next consented channel, or suppressed if none. Report shows "consent stale". |
| Artist configures a reply-to they never read | Broadcast is one-way; inbound replies are D-04's concern and route to CX-M06, not here. A never-read reply-to is a composer/identity UX issue, not a routing one — flagged out. `[PENDING — /write-fe-spec, composer]`. | Reply lands in CX-M06 inbox (or a documented no-reply address). |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Dispatch begins | Progress: resolved / dispatched / delivered, live |
| Empty | Audience resolves to zero contactable | "Nobody on this list can be contacted" + why (no consent, all suppressed) — never a silent no-op |
| Populated | Send in flight or complete | Per-channel breakdown with outcomes |
| Error | Transport unavailable | Paused with cause + resume path. Never fails a whole campaign for one channel. |
| Partial | Some channels delivered, one paused | Explicit per-channel state — the artist must know their SMS did not go |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This duplicates CX-M07 (Notifications & Alerts) and should be dissolved into it | Node Classification Gate — both send messages to users | ❌ REJECTED — they share transport and nothing else. CX-M07 is the platform speaking transactionally to its own users ("your order shipped"): consent is implied by the relationship, volume is per-event, a failure is a support ticket. This is an artist speaking to 3,000 consenting strangers at once: consent is explicit and per-channel, volume is bursty, cost is real, and a failure is a regulatory or reputational event shared across all tenants. **They should share the transport layer and nothing above it.** Recorded as index D-01. |
| DT-02 | Push is a core channel for fan alerts | D-13 explicitly calls gig alerts "push-notification and phone-shaped workflows" | ❌ REJECTED **as currently scoped** — and this is the finding that matters. `meta/constraints.md` declares one responsive web surface; mobile is an open question. Web push on iOS requires the fan to add the site to their home screen, which approximately nobody does. So the channel D-13 says the fan product needs is the channel the declared surface cannot deliver. **[20.06.02 Gig Alerts](../20.06-fan-experience-discovery/20.06.02-gig-alerts-near-me.md) — the Bandsintown-killer and arguably the whole fan-side value proposition — degrades to email without a mobile surface.** This is concrete evidence for the mobile decision, not a preference. Escalated to `/ideate-validate`. |
| DT-03 | SMS is a nice-to-have | It is expensive and email is free | ⏳ **Leaning reject, not confident.** Evidence for: SMS is where D2F engagement actually went (Community.com, Laylo built businesses on exactly this), open rates dwarf email, and it is the only channel that reliably reaches a fan *at* a show. Evidence against: real per-message cost, TCPA exposure with statutory damages **per message** (a mis-sent blast is not a slap on the wrist — it is existential), and it partially substitutes for the push channel we may not have. Genuinely a scope/risk call for the owner, not a technical one. Index Q-03. |
| DT-04 | The artist should be able to force a channel for an *urgent* message ("the venue moved") regardless of fan preference | Q-02 tension: fan-respectful vs message-urgency. An urgent operational change is exactly what SMS exists for. | ❌ REJECTED **for this feature** — the tension dissolves on the D-01/DT-01 boundary. An urgent operational change ("doors moved", "show cancelled") is a **transactional** message tied to a purchase/relationship, which belongs to CX-M07's transport, where consent is implied by the relationship and channel is chosen by urgency. This feature is **marketing broadcast** to consenting strangers, where the fan's channel choice is sovereign. So there is no legitimate "artist override" *here*: if it is urgent enough to override preference, it is not a marketing broadcast. Fan preference wins absolutely — see D-06. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | One message per fan per campaign — channel dedupe is mandatory | Three copies of one announcement is the fastest way to convert a fan into an unsubscribe. |
| D-02 | Consent is the enforcement point, not the composer's channel set | The artist proposes channels; per-fan consent disposes. |
| D-03 | No silent channel fallback | Falling back to email for a fan who only consented to SMS is unlawful and treats consent as a routing hint. |
| D-04 | Idempotent per (campaign, fan) | Retries are inevitable; duplicate sends must not be. |
| D-05 | Share CX-M07's transport, nothing above it | See DT-01. |
| D-06 | Fan-set channel preference is the priority key; the artist's channel set only narrows the candidate pool, never re-ranks it | See DT-04. For marketing broadcast the fan's choice is sovereign; there is no artist "urgency override" here because urgent operational messages are transactional (CX-M07), not broadcast. Resolves Q-02. |
