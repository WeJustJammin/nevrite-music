# Feature: Deliverability, Sender Reputation & Suppression

> **Number**: 20.03.04
> **Parent**: [Broadcast & Fan Messaging](./20.03-broadcast-fan-messaging-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Whether the mail arrives. Reputation state per sender, bounce and complaint processing, suppression, domain
authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), and the circuit breakers that stop a bad send before it damages everyone
else's. **Sender reputation on this platform is a shared, multi-tenant asset** (index D-02): one artist
blasting a bought list generates complaints that push every other artist's mail into spam. That single fact
makes deliverability a platform-integrity mechanism rather than a per-artist setting.

> **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list. The sweep enumerated the channels
> ("Mailing List & Newsletter", "SMS, Push & Broadcast Channels") and assumed delivery. Delivery is the
> assumption that fails.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Reports | Sees their sender health: delivery rate, bounce rate, complaint rate, list health, and whether they are throttled and why. Sees suppression counts (never the mechanism). **Cannot tune anything** — not thresholds, not throttles, not suppression. The reputation is not theirs to spend. |
| Producer | None | No visibility. |
| Operator | Reports | Same. A venue with a decade-old box-office list ([20.01.04](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.04-fan-list-import-hygiene.md)) is the single highest-risk sender profile on the platform: enormous, stale, imported, and confidently believed to be consented. |
| Fan | None | Never sees it. Their "report spam" click is the most consequential input the system takes — it suppresses permanently and moves the sender's reputation. A complaint **is** a consent revocation. |

## Behavior

Reputation is computed per sender from delivery telemetry (`-cx.md` CX-03): hard bounces, soft bounces,
complaints, unsubscribes, blocks, and engagement. It governs throttle rate
([20.03.03](./20.03.03-send-scheduling-throttling.md), `-cx.md` CX-02) and, past a threshold, halts sends
mid-flight.

Suppression is **permanent and terminal**: hard bounce, complaint, or unsubscribe → the address is never
contactable by that sender again, and it survives re-import
([20.01](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience-cx.md) CX-06). No role — artist,
delegate, admin, support — can reverse it. Only the fan can.

The **circuit breaker** is the feature that matters most and is easiest to under-build: complaint rate crossing
a threshold mid-send halts the remainder. Not the next campaign — this one, now.

### Happy Path

1. Artist sends to a warm, engaged, platform-witnessed list
2. Telemetry returns: 98.2% delivered, 0.1% bounce, 0.02% complaint
3. Reputation holds; no throttle; next send goes at full rate
4. Artist sees a green sender-health panel and never thinks about any of this — **which is the goal**. Deliverability is a feature whose success is measured by the artist never learning it exists

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Artist blasts a freshly-imported unwitnessed list | Warm-up throttle from import provenance; complaint rate watched hard; halt on breach. | "Stopped — too many people marked this as spam. Here's what that means for your next send." |
| One artist's complaints degrade **everyone's** placement | The contagion. Prevented mechanically or not at all — the platform cannot rely on artists behaving. | Other artists see nothing (correctly) unless it is severe. |
| Sending domain lands on a blocklist | Platform-wide incident: every artist's mail degraded by one artist's behaviour. **This is the disaster scenario for the entire sub-domain.** | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — needs an incident runbook, not a UI. |
| Artist wants their own domain | Better isolation, better deliverability, and requires DNS records most musicians will not manage. Index Q-01. | `[PENDING]` |
| Fan marks as spam despite having consented | Suppressed permanently, reputation moves. Fans do this constantly instead of unsubscribing — which is why the unsubscribe must be easier than the spam button ([20.01.03](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.03-fan-preference-centre.md) D-01). | Aggregate only. |
| Role address (`info@`) in the list | Flagged at import. High complaint risk, zero fan value. | Flagged. |
| Spam-trap address in an old imported list | Severe reputation damage from a single send; traps exist to catch exactly the "decade-old list" case. | Halt + explanation. |
| Artist demands a suppressed fan be re-added ("she's my friend, she asked me") | No. Not by admin, not by support. The friend can re-subscribe herself in ten seconds. | "Only she can do that — send her the link." |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Health panel opened | Skeleton |
| Empty | Never sent | "No sending history yet" + what determines whether their mail arrives, stated once, plainly |
| Populated | ≥1 campaign | Health panel: delivery / bounce / complaint / list health, with the throttle state |
| Error | Telemetry pipeline lagging | Last-known with timestamp. **Reputation must fail *conservative*, not open** — unknown reputation throttles rather than opening the taps. |
| Partial | Some channels have history | Per-channel health (email warm, SMS new) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Deliverability is an implementation detail of sending — infrastructure, not product | Why the sweep omitted it; it looks like plumbing | ❌ REJECTED — it is the **defining multi-tenant hazard of the domain** and it has genuine product surface: what the artist is told, what they are prevented from doing, what happens when they are stopped mid-send. More importantly it inverts the incentive: because reputation is shared, the platform has a *commercial* interest in strict consent, not merely a legal one — which is a far more durable enforcement motive than compliance. That reframing is the reason this node exists. |
| DT-02 | Per-artist sending domains solve the contagion | The obvious architectural fix | ⏳ **Partially confirmed, genuinely unresolved.** Isolation is real: one artist's blocklisting stops being everyone's. But it demands DNS competence from musicians — the persona whose "current workaround" is *"a Linktree and a credits list in a Notes app"*. Most will never add a DKIM record, so the shared domain becomes the default path and the contagion persists for everyone who did not opt out. Hybrid (shared by default, own-domain for those who can) means the platform still carries the contagion **and** builds two paths. This is index Q-01 and I do not think it has a clean answer — it is a genuine trade-off. |
| DT-03 | The worst case is an artist getting throttled | Artist-centric read | ❌ REJECTED — the worst case is the **shared sending domain getting blocklisted**, which degrades every artist on the platform because of one artist's list. At that point the domain's core promise ("your owned audience, reachable") is false for everyone simultaneously, and the fix is measured in weeks of remediation with the mailbox providers. That asymmetry — one bad actor, platform-wide multi-week failure — is what justifies mechanical, non-overridable enforcement over advisory warnings. It needs an incident runbook at `/create-prd`, not just a threshold. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Reputation is a platform asset; artists get Reports, never Config | Index D-02. Shared consequence ⇒ no individual control. |
| D-02 | Suppression is terminal and irreversible by any role | Only the fan can undo their own opt-out. No admin path, because an admin path will be used. |
| D-03 | Circuit breaker halts mid-flight | See `-cx.md` CX-02. A breaker that waits for the next campaign has already let the damage out. |
| D-04 | Reputation fails conservative | Unknown reputation throttles. The alternative is opening the taps during an outage. |
| D-05 | The commercial motive is the enforcement motive | See DT-01. Framing consent enforcement as protecting *artists' deliverability* rather than as compliance makes it durable — it survives the first commercial argument to relax it. |
