# Feature: Storefront & Product Catalog

> **Number**: 20.04.01
> **Parent**: [Direct-to-Fan Storefront](./20.04-direct-to-fan-storefront-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-22

## Overview

The artist's shop and the products in it: what is for sale, at what price, in what quantity, to whom. It is
the commercial spine of the sub-domain — merch, records, downloads, bundles and members-only items all live in
one catalogue — and it is the point at which every product must bind to a payout plan
(`-cx.md` CX-02) before it can be sold. It owns the catalogue and the commercial rules; it does **not** own the
public HTML (CX-M23, index D-03) or the cart/checkout/tax (CX-M01/M02, index D-04).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Creates products, sets prices and stock, orders the shop, marks items members-only or presale-gated ([20.02.03](../20.02-segmentation-superfan-intelligence/20.02.03-perk-entitlements-presale-eligibility.md)). At listing time sees who else gets paid, pulled from the rights record rather than typed. Sees sales, and sees who bought — because a buyer is a fan record ([20.01.01](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.01-unified-fan-record.md)), not an anonymous order. Can preview the shop as a fan (see "Preview as fan" below). |
| Producer | None | No visibility on the catalogue. Their stake appears only at [20.04.04](./20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md) as a payee — even when the same human who ran the session is also the listing Musician (see the multi-hyphenate edge cases). |
| Operator | Full (venue merch) | A room's own shop: tote bags, posters, the venue t-shirt. Narrower than an artist's — no recordings, digital downloads or NYP (parent Role Matrix; 20.04.03 is `❌ None` for Operator). Selling an artist's merch on their behalf is index Q-04, unresolved — treated as out of scope here until decided. |
| Fan | Read-only | Browses and buys. Sees members-only items they cannot access as **visible but locked** — a locked door they can open by joining is a conversion surface; a hidden one is nothing. Sees scarcity honestly ("3 of 100 left") because in D2F scarcity is part of the product. Never sees drafts, scheduled-but-unreleased items, or sales/buyer data. |

## Behavior

The catalogue holds heterogeneous products under one roof: a POD t-shirt that never runs out, a pressing of
300 that is defined by running out, a download that is infinite, a bundle that is half instant and half a
supply chain (`-cx.md` CX-01). It must express all of them without flattening them into a `product` row with
forty nullable columns — the same failure D-14 rejected at domain scale (see DT-01).

### Product type taxonomy

A product declares exactly one **kind**, and the kind determines which fields and which downstream feature own
its detail. The catalogue stores the shared shell (title, description, images, price rule, publish state,
payout-plan binding) and delegates kind-specific structure:

| Kind | Fulfilment | Inventory model | Detail owned by | Payout plan source |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|--------------------|
| `digital-download` | Instant entitlement | Infinite | [20.04.03](./20.04.03-digital-sales-name-your-price-bundles.md) | Rights record (domain 09) |
| `merch-pod` | Print-on-demand, ships | Never sells out | [20.04.02](./20.04.02-merch-variants-print-on-demand.md) | Authored merch split (CX-04) |
| `merch-stocked` | Held stock, ships | Finite integer stock | [20.04.02](./20.04.02-merch-variants-print-on-demand.md) | Authored merch split (CX-04) |
| `physical-record` (vinyl/CD) | Held/pressed stock, ships | Finite; pre-order window allowed | [20.04.02](./20.04.02-merch-variants-print-on-demand.md) + Q-03 | Rights record (domain 09) |
| `bundle` | Mixed (instant + shipped) | Constrained by scarcest component | [20.04.03](./20.04.03-digital-sales-name-your-price-bundles.md) CX-01 | Per-component (CX-01 / CX-04) |
| `members-item` | Any of the above, gated | As underlying kind | [20.02.03](../20.02-segmentation-superfan-intelligence/20.02.03-perk-entitlements-presale-eligibility.md) | As underlying kind |

> The last column is the **source of the split percentages**, and it varies by kind. The **basis those
> percentages apply to** does not vary at all: it is one platform-wide constant (paid-net) for every kind, on
> every product, in every shop — see D-10 below and [20.04.04](./20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md) D-10/D-11.
> The column was renamed from "Payout basis" so the two cannot be confused into one field.

`members-item` is a **gate flag** on any other kind, not a seventh kind — a members-only download is a
`digital-download` with `visibility = members` and an entitlement requirement (20.02.03). Operators may only
create `merch-pod`, `merch-stocked`, and their bundles thereof; the taxonomy rejects a recording kind from a
venue shop at validation (422, `code: catalog/kind-not-permitted-for-role`).

### Pricing model

Every product carries exactly one **price rule**:

| Rule | Fields | Constraints |
|------|--------|-------------|
| `fixed` | `amount`, `currency` | `amount ≥ 0`; `0` renders as "Free" |
| `name-your-price` | `floor`, optional `suggested` | `floor ≥ 0`; `suggested ≥ floor`; a buyer-entered amount below `floor` is rejected (422, `code: catalog/below-floor`); digital-only per 20.04.03 |
| `member-price` | `public_amount`, `member_amount` | `member_amount ≤ public_amount`; requires the `members-item` gate or a member segment (20.02.03) |
| `presale` | underlying rule + `release_at`, optional `presale_amount` | `presale_amount ≤` post-release amount; charged now, fulfilled at/after `release_at` |

Prices are stored in **minor units** (integer, e.g. pence) against an ISO-4217 currency; display conversion is
CX-M20's job, never this feature's. Currency is set per shop at creation and is immutable per product once the
product has ≥1 sale (changing the settlement currency of a sold product is a payout-integrity hazard —
`code: catalog/currency-locked-after-sale`).

### Shop layout & ordering

- The shop is a **grid of product cards**. Each card shows: primary image, title, price (or "from {floor}" for
  NYP / "from {min variant}" for varianted merch), a scarcity badge when applicable, a members-only badge when
  gated, and format badges (e.g. "Vinyl", "Download").
- The Musician **orders products manually** (explicit `sort_index`, drag to reorder). Absent a manual order,
  the default sort is: pinned first, then `published_at` descending. Sold-out stocked items sink below
  in-stock items unless pinned.
- Products can be grouped into optional **collections** (e.g. "New", "Vinyl", "Members"). A product may appear
  in at most 8 collections; a collection is a saved filter/label, not a second copy of the product.
- **Preview as fan**: the Musician can render their own shop through the Fan lens (drafts hidden, members-items
  shown locked, scarcity shown). Preview never records a fan observation and never decrements stock.

### Publish lifecycle

`draft → scheduled(publish_at) → live → {sold_out | unlisted | archived}`. A product is **not sellable** — and
never enters `live` — until (a) it binds a resolvable payout plan (CX-02, see below) and (b) it passes kind
validation. `sold_out` applies only to finite-inventory kinds at `available = 0`. `unlisted` hides it from the
grid while keeping its direct URL valid (for a link-only drop). `archived` is soft-delete: removed from the
shop, retained for order history and payout statements, never hard-deleted while any order references it.

### Payout binding (CX-02) — the differentiator

Every product binds to a payout plan **at listing time**, before it can go `live`:

- For a **recording** (`digital-download`, `physical-record`, recording bundles): the plan is the rights record
  from domain 09 — already captured, already agreed, at the session. The catalogue **references** it by id and
  never copies it (D-02); a copied split drifts, and a drifted split is the exact reconstruction failure the
  platform exists to prevent.
- For **merch** (`merch-pod`, `merch-stocked`): there is no rights record. The split must be authored here
  (CX-04), defaulting to 100% to the listing entity; the recording split is **never** inherited onto merch.
- **The basis is not a catalogue field** (D-10). A product binds a payout *plan* — the percentages and who they
  belong to. The amount those percentages apply to is a platform-wide constant (paid-net), stated in the
  platform's terms and printed at split capture, and it is deliberately absent from the product shell, the
  price rule, and every kind's detail. There is no per-product, per-kind or per-listing basis setting to build,
  and adding one would reopen a ratified decision (DQ-19.1) rather than extend the catalogue.
- **No resolvable plan ⇒ not sellable.** The specified behavior when a recording has no rights record is the
  loud-asymmetric-acknowledgement path (see the edge-case row and DT-04): the product may still go live, but
  only after the lister explicitly confirms "no split on file — you are taking 100%", with a one-tap path to
  invite the collaborators. Whether the platform should instead **hard-block** such sales is the sub-domain's
  sharpest policy question and is a genuine user decision — Q-01, owned by the user, collected as an owner
  decision at `/create-prd`. The behavior above is what ships absent that decision; it does not pre-empt it.

### Happy Path

1. Musician finishes a record; the rights record already exists from the session (domain 09)
2. Opens the shop, creates a product: kind `digital-download`, price rule `name-your-price`, floor £5
3. System resolves the payout plan from the split: artist 70%, producer 20%, session drummer 10% — shown **as a
   fact to confirm, not a form to complete**
4. Musician confirms; adds a `bundle` — a `physical-record` vinyl pre-order bundled with an instant download
   (`-cx.md` CX-01); sets a pressing of 300 (`merch-stocked`-style finite inventory)
5. Publishes (`draft → live`). CX-M23 renders it publicly. [20.03.01](../20.03-broadcast-fan-messaging/20.03.01-campaign-composer.md)
   can announce it to a segment
6. First sale: the drummer's 10% is routed to the drummer without anyone doing anything. **The split they
   signed at the session paid them, silently, three years later.** That is the product

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Recording product has no rights record (track predates the platform, or was imported) | The common case for years. Specified behavior: allow listing via the loud-acknowledgement path — the lister must confirm "no split on file: you are taking 100%" (recorded as an acknowledgement event) and is offered a one-tap "invite collaborators" action. Whether to hard-block instead is Q-01 (owner decision, `/create-prd`). See [20.04.04](./20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md) DT-02. | Blocking modal: "No split on file. You'll receive 100% of every sale. Add the people who worked on this?" with **Add collaborators** / **Take 100%** buttons. |
| Artist partly owns a track (co-owned) and tries to list it unilaterally | Domain 09 governs. If 09 says a co-owner may not sell unilaterally, the listing is refused (403, `code: catalog/co-owner-consent-required`) and the co-owners are offered a consent request. The store is where that abstraction meets a Buy button. | "This track is co-owned by {N} people. They need to approve selling it." + **Request approval**. Product stays `draft`. |
| Limited pressing oversells (race at the last unit) | Must not happen. Adding a finite-inventory item to a cart places a soft stock hold for the cart window (15 min); the available count a fan can buy is stock minus items currently held in other carts minus items already sold. When that count reaches 0, the next add is refused rather than accepted and later cancelled. | "Sold out" — and it is true, not a race artifact. In-cart holds show "Reserved for 15:00". |
| Two co-managers of a band shop edit the same product concurrently | Only one of two concurrent edits to the same product may win. The later save is rejected (409, `code: catalog/stale-write`) rather than silently overwriting the earlier one; the losing editor is told their view is out of date before anything is discarded. | "This product changed since you opened it. Reload to see the latest, then reapply your edit." |
| POD provider drops a product line mid-sale | Open orders cannot be fulfilled. Product auto-moves to `unlisted`; affected open orders flagged for refund via CX-M14/CX-M01. Detail: [20.04.02](./20.04.02-merch-variants-print-on-demand.md). | Buyer: refund + notice. Artist: "{product} was discontinued by the print provider; {N} orders refunded." |
| Members-only item viewed by a non-member | Visible, locked, joinable — never hidden (D-05). Entitlement check is 20.02.03. | "Members only — join to unlock." |
| Price changed while items are in carts | Cart price holds for a bounded 30-min window from add-to-cart; changing price under a fan mid-purchase is a trust loss out of all proportion to the money. New price applies to new carts only. | Held price + expiry ("Price held until 14:32"). |
| Split changes (dispute resolves in domain 09) while a product is live | Sales **before** the change pay the old plan; **after**, the new. Retroactivity is a domain 09/10 question and must not be improvised here. The catalogue references the plan version at sale time. | Payout statement shows which plan version applied to each sale. |
| NYP amount submitted below the floor (or negative) | Rejected server-side (422, `code: catalog/below-floor`). The floor is authoritative; the client-entered amount is never trusted. | "Minimum is £{floor}." Field re-focuses. |
| Malicious content in title/description (script/HTML injection) | Stored as-is but never trusted; length-capped (title ≤ 140 chars, description ≤ 5,000 chars, 422 over cap) and rendered escaped by CX-M23. Control characters stripped on save. | Over-cap: inline validation error. Rendered output is inert text. |
| Client tampers the price on checkout (submits a lower amount) | The catalogue exposes the canonical price; checkout (CX-M01) recomputes from it and rejects a mismatch. The store never accepts a client-supplied price for a `fixed`/`member`/`presale` product. | Handled at checkout boundary; catalogue simply refuses to be the source of a spoofed price. |
| Catalogue read fails / network error loading the shop | Explicit error state with retry (see States). A shop that silently shows nothing looks like an artist with nothing to sell. | "Couldn't load the shop." + **Retry**. |
| Publish partially completes (payout binds, product fails to save, or vice versa) | Publishing is all-or-nothing: a product reaching `live` without a bound plan is not a state the system can be in. If any part of publish fails, the product stays `draft` and the fan-visible catalogue is unchanged — the fan never sees a half-published product. | "Couldn't publish — nothing was changed. Try again." |
| Multi-hyphenate: the listing Musician is **also** a Producer-payee on the same recording | No double-count and no role confusion. They manage the catalogue via the Musician (Full) lens; their payee stake surfaces only in [20.04.04](./20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md). The payout plan lists them once, at their agreed percentage. | In the shop: Musician view. In statements: a single payee line at their split. |
| Multi-hyphenate: one human owns both an artist shop (Musician) **and** a venue shop (Operator) | Two distinct catalogues under one identity, never merged. The Operator shop rejects recording/digital/NYP kinds; the artist shop allows them. Switching shops is an explicit context switch, not a role toggle inside one grid. | A shop switcher; each shop shows only its permitted kinds. |
| Fan record is a guest/anonymous checkout | A buyer is a fan record (DT-03), so guest checkout is a data-quality decision, not merely a conversion one — Q-02 (`/create-prd`). Until decided, a purchase creates at least a minimal fan record keyed to the payment identity. | Fan sees normal checkout; artist still sees a (possibly sparse) fan record, not an anonymous order. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Shop opened, catalogue request in flight | Product-grid skeleton (card placeholders), no layout shift on load |
| Empty | No products exist yet | For the artist: a seeded suggestion grid — what they could sell, drawn from what the platform already knows they have (a released record, a design, a tour) + **Create product** CTA. For the fan: not an empty shop — **no shop at all** (CX-M23 returns 404/hidden, not a bare page). |
| Populated | ≥1 published product | Grid with price/from-price, scarcity badge, members-only badge, format badges; collections if defined |
| Error | Catalogue request failed | Explicit error card + **Retry**. Never a silent empty grid — that reads as "artist with nothing to sell." |
| Partial | Draft/scheduled products exist alongside live ones, or a live product is missing a resolvable payout plan | Artist sees drafts and a "not sellable — bind a payout plan" banner on the affected product; fans see only the `live`, sellable subset |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The catalogue should reuse domain 13's listing model | Both sell things; obvious reuse instinct | ❌ REJECTED — settled at domain level by D-14 and it holds at feature level. A gear listing is qty=1, non-fungible, condition-graded, in a make/model catalogue. A merch product is qty=300, fungible, variant-dimensioned, with no catalogue at all. The union is a listing entity with forty nullable columns. The genuinely shared parts — cart, payments, shipping, tax — are **already cross-cuts** (CX-M01/M02/M14), which is precisely why the merge buys nothing. |
| DT-02 | The storefront's differentiator is a better shop | The obvious framing; Bandcamp/Shopify/Big Cartel all compete here | ❌ REJECTED — we will never out-shop Shopify and should not try. The differentiator is **the split binding** (`-cx.md` CX-02): this is the only storefront on earth that knows who played on the record it is selling, because it was in the room when they played. Every competing store pays 100% to the account holder and leaves the artist to Venmo their drummer, which mostly means the drummer is never paid. Framing this as "a better shop" loses; framing it as "the shop that pays your collaborators automatically" is the thesis (D-18) at its most concrete. |
| DT-03 | Fans buying merch are anonymous orders | Standard e-commerce assumption | ❌ REJECTED — **a buyer is a fan record.** That is the join no other store can make: the shop knows this person also came to three shows and has been on the list since 2019. It means the store is simultaneously the domain's revenue engine **and** its highest-quality fan-acquisition surface. It also means a guest checkout is a data-quality decision, not just a conversion one — see [20.01.01](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.01-unified-fan-record.md) and Q-02. |
| DT-04 | With no rights record, the store should hard-block the sale to keep the thesis pure | Consistency with D-18 — provenance must have consequences | ❌ REJECTED as the shipping default (though genuinely unresolved as policy — Q-01). Hard-block is thesis-pure and empties the launch catalogue: every track recorded before WeJammin existed becomes unsellable, so nobody opens a shop, so the store never captures a fan. The specified behavior instead makes the **absence loud and asymmetric** — "no split on file: you are taking 100%; add the people who worked on this?" with a one-tap invite. This is the personas lesson applied verbatim: *"the design must make the lazy path the correct path."* The binary enforce-vs-offer decision remains the user's (Q-01). |
| DT-05 | One flattened `product` row with a nullable column per kind is simpler than a kind-delegated model | Speed-of-build instinct | ❌ REJECTED — a shared shell + per-kind delegation (see taxonomy) keeps each kind's detail in the feature that owns it (20.04.02/03) and keeps the catalogue row honest. The flattened row is D-14's forty-nullable-column failure at feature scale; it also makes kind-scoped validation (Operator can't list recordings; NYP is digital-only) into a scatter of `if column is null` checks instead of a typed kind boundary. |
| DT-06 | Scarcity should be hidden until nearly sold out to avoid discouraging early buyers | Conversion-optimisation instinct borrowed from generic retail | ❌ REJECTED for D2F — in direct-to-fan, **scarcity is part of the product** (a limited pressing is *defined* by running out), and hiding it defrauds the fans who queued for exactly that scarcity. Show honest counts ("3 of 100 left"); surface a low-stock emphasis at ≤10% remaining or ≤10 units, whichever is larger. POD kinds show no count because they never run out. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Binds to **[20.04.04 D2F Revenue Split & Payout](./20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md)** — `-cx.md` CX-02. No payout plan, no sale. The **plan** binds here; the **basis** those percentages apply to does not live here at all (D-10 — it is 20.04.04 D-10/D-11's platform constant).
- **Reads 09 Rights & Ownership** ([../../09-rights-ownership/](../../09-rights-ownership/)) — the split, by reference, never copied (D-02). **The thesis dependency.** Also owns co-ownership consent (partly-owned edge case) and split-version-at-sale-time.
- **Reads 02 Credits & Attribution** ([../../02-credits-attribution/](../../02-credits-attribution/)) — who played on it; shown on the product page, which is where a credit becomes publicly *useful* rather than merely recorded.
- Writes fan observations to **[20.01.01 Unified Fan Record](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.01-unified-fan-record.md)** — see DT-03; a purchase is an observation, and guest checkout (Q-02) affects its quality.
- Feeds **[20.02.02 Superfan Score](../20.02-segmentation-superfan-intelligence/20.02.02-superfan-score.md)** — spend signal.
- Gated by **[20.02.03 Perk Entitlements](../20.02-segmentation-superfan-intelligence/20.02.03-perk-entitlements-presale-eligibility.md)** — members-only and presale items (the `members-item` gate + presale price rule resolve here).
- Delegates kind detail to **[20.04.02 Merch, Variants & POD](./20.04.02-merch-variants-print-on-demand.md)** (variants, stock/POD, records) and **[20.04.03 Digital Sales, NYP & Bundles](./20.04.03-digital-sales-name-your-price-bundles.md)** (downloads, name-your-price, bundle composition — CX-01).
- Rendered by **CX-M23 Public SEO Surfaces & Embeds** — index D-03. The shop page, its SEO, its embeds, and content-escaping of stored titles/descriptions.
- Consumes **CX-M01 Payments, Escrow & Payouts** (canonical-price recompute at checkout; the store never trusts a client price), **CX-M02 Tax & Fiscal Compliance**, **CX-M14 Shipping, Fulfilment & Logistics** — index D-04.
- Consumes **CX-M10 Media Handling & Audio Playback** — audio previews on product pages.
- Consumes **CX-M20 Localization, Currency & Timezone** — price is stored in minor units + ISO currency; all display conversion is CX-M20's.
- Touches **12 Release & Distribution** ([../../12-release-distribution/](../../12-release-distribution/)) — a release and a product are the same object seen from two domains; the D2F sale and the DSP release are the same record.
- Touches **01 Identity, Profiles & Organizations** ([../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/](../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/)) — *permission, currently unstated*. Publishing a product for an artist entity is one party acting for another and needs both of `01.03.02` D-05's conjuncts: the `list` activity, plus `merch` in the edge's `domains` for merch kinds. On a membership edge the domain conjunct is universal. Which domain a digital download or an NYP bundle sells into is not settled — it is the open taxonomy question recorded against DQ-R2-01, not a call this feature may make.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Products bind a payout plan at listing time | `-cx.md` CX-02. Determined before the first sale, not argued after — the platform's own principle applied to its shop. |
| D-02 | The split is referenced from domain 09, never copied; the plan **version at sale time** governs each sale | A copied split drifts, and a drifted split is the exact reconstruction failure this platform exists to prevent. Versioning resolves the "split changed while live" race without retroactivity. |
| D-03 | A buyer is a fan record, not an order | See DT-03. The store is the domain's best fan-acquisition surface, and only because it is not anonymous. |
| D-04 | Heterogeneous product **kinds** with per-kind delegation, not a flattened `product` row | See DT-01/DT-05. Same reasoning as D-14, one level down. The catalogue owns the shared shell; 20.04.02/03/20.02.03 own kind detail. |
| D-05 | Locked members-only items are visible | A locked door converts; a hidden one does nothing. |
| D-06 | No rights record ⇒ ship the loud-acknowledgement path (list with recorded "taking 100%" + one-tap invite), not a hard block | DT-04. Makes the lazy path the correct path (personas). The enforce-vs-offer policy stays a user decision (Q-01). |
| D-07 | Finite-inventory items reserve stock with a 15-min soft hold; cart price holds 30 min | Prevents overselling a scarcity that is the product; protects the fan from a price change mid-purchase. Both are bounded and stated to the fan. |
| D-08 | Prices stored in integer minor units + ISO-4217 currency; product currency immutable after first sale | Avoids float drift and protects payout integrity; display conversion is CX-M20's, never this feature's. |
| D-09 | Concurrent edits to one product: the later save is refused (409 stale-write), never silently merged or overwritten | Band/co-manager shops have multiple editors; silent last-write-wins would clobber a collaborator's change. |
| D-10 | **A product binds a payout plan, never a basis.** The basis is a platform-wide constant (paid-net) owned by [20.04.04](./20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md) D-10/D-11; no product kind, price rule or listing flow in this catalogue carries a basis field | Owner ratification of DQ-19.1 + DQ-19.3, 2026-07-22. The catalogue is where an implementer would most naturally add one — it already holds the price rule and the plan binding — so the absence is stated rather than implied. It also removes a listing-time gate 20.04.03 D-10 used to impose: a listing can no longer be blocked for an "unresolved basis", because there is nothing per-listing to resolve. D-02's plan-version-at-sale-time is untouched; the basis carries its own second version dimension (a platform **terms version** bound to the split agreement's date, 20.04.04 D-11), which the payout engine resolves and the catalogue never stores. |
