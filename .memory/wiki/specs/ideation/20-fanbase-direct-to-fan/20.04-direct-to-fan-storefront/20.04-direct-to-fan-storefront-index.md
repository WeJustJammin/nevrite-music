## Overview

The artist's own shop: merch, records, downloads, bundles — sold directly to fans, with the money and the
credits landing in the same system. This is where the domain's economics live (Bandcamp has paid artists on
the order of $1.5B; for most independents direct fan revenue materially exceeds streaming), and it is where
the platform's thesis cashes out most concretely: **because WeJammin holds both the split from the session
and the money from the sale, a D2F sale can pay the session drummer their agreed percentage automatically**
([20.04.04](./20.04.04-d2f-revenue-split-payout.md)). No storefront competitor can do that, because none of
them were in the room.

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: the catalogue, physical merch, digital music and
the revenue split are one system. A bundle sells a record and a t-shirt together, so digital and physical
share a cart and a fulfilment decision; and every sale — whatever its type — must resolve against a rights
record to be paid out. Specifying any one alone produces a catalogue that cannot express what artists sell.

**Why the store is here and not in domain 13 (Gear Marketplace)**: settled in the domain rationale and
ratified by D-14 — a t-shirt has no serial number, no condition grade, and no place in a make/model catalogue.
Forcing it into gear yields a `listing` entity with forty nullable columns. What the two genuinely share is
logistics, and logistics is already the CX-M14 Shipping cross-cut.

| D-01 | The D2F store lives in domain 20, not domain 13 | Domain rationale + D-14. A t-shirt has no serial, no condition grade, no make/model. The shared part is logistics (CX-M14). Merging produces a 40-nullable-column `listing`. | D-14, ratified 2026-07-16 |

The platform's differentiating claim is that provenance captured at the session has *consequences*. A storefront that pays 100% to the uploader regardless of the split on file makes the split decorative. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | The public storefront surface is rendered by CX-M23, not built here | CX-M23 (Public SEO Surfaces & Embeds) already serves 8 domains and owns public page rendering, SEO and embeds. This sub-domain owns the catalogue, the commerce and the money — not the HTML. | Node Classification Gate |
| D-04 | Cart, checkout, payments, tax and shipping are cross-cuts, not children | CX-M01 (Payments/Escrow/Payouts), CX-M02 (Tax), CX-M14 (Shipping). Reimplementing them here is exactly the duplication the domain rationale already pruned for. | Node Classification Gate |
