# Feature: Perk Entitlements & Presale Eligibility

> **Number**: 20.02.03
> **Parent**: [Segmentation & Superfan Intelligence](./20.02-segmentation-superfan-intelligence-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The bridge between knowing who your best fans are and doing something about it: granting a segment a
consequential promise — 48 hours of presale access, a members-only pressing, a discount, a vault drop, a
guest-list slot. **Eligibility is domain-owned; the presale mechanism is domain 19** (D-03). Ticketing knows
how to run a sale window and take money; it does not know who deserves to be in it, and cannot, because that
answer requires the fan graph.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Grants a perk to a saved audience: picks the segment, the promise, the window. "Top 200 fans get 48 hours before general on-sale." Sees who was granted, who redeemed, and what it cost. Cannot grant an entitlement over inventory they do not control — see CX-02 Q3. |
| Producer | None | No visibility. |
| Operator | Full | Grants room-level perks: early access for repeat attendees, a members' bar, first refusal on a residency. Also **receives** artist-granted entitlements as constraints on their box office — the artist's presale is the venue's allocation problem. |
| Fan | Read-only (own entitlements) | Sees what they are entitled to and until when. Never sees why they got it, never sees who else did. The invite arrives; the criterion does not. |

## Behavior

An entitlement is a **snapshot with a promise**, not a live query (`-cx.md` CX-04). At grant time it copies the
segment's membership and becomes immutable. This is deliberate denormalisation against D-01 (segments are live)
because the two objects have genuinely different requirements: an audience must be current, a promise must be
kept. A fan who earned a presale must not lose it overnight because someone else bought a t-shirt and pushed
them out of the top 5%.

The subtle consequence: **consent governs contact, not entitlement**. A fan who unsubscribes from email after
being granted a presale still holds the presale — they simply cannot be emailed about it. That is my reading and
it needs an owner ruling (parent Q-01); the opposite reading is defensible.

Handoff to 19 is deliberately narrow: an eligibility set and a window, not a query. 19 enforces at the box
office and never learns anything about the fan graph.

### Happy Path

1. Musician announces a tour; opens the "Manchester — active" audience ([20.02.01](./20.02.01-segment-builder.md))
2. Grants: "48-hour presale, opens Thursday 9am local"
3. System snapshots 1,203 eligible fans, issues the eligibility set to domain 19, and schedules the notification (CX-M07, timezone-aware via [20.03.03](../20.03-broadcast-fan-messaging/20.03.03-send-scheduling-throttling.md))
4. Thursday 9am, fan gets a message: *your early access is open*
5. Fan buys through 19's normal flow; 19 checks eligibility, sells, and reports the redemption back
6. Redemption feeds the [score](./20.02.02-superfan-score.md) — the ratchet (`-cx.md` CX-03), which is a known hazard, not an accident

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Fan qualifies via two overlapping segments | Idempotent per fan per sale: one entitlement, best window. Otherwise one fan gets two codes and — because allocation is finite — another gets none. | One invite. |
| Presale allocation smaller than the eligible set | **The most common real failure.** 1,203 eligible, 300 tickets. Granting an entitlement that cannot be honoured converts a reward into a disappointment, aimed precisely at the fans who care most. Must be caught at grant time, not at on-sale. | "You're promising 1,203 fans access to 300 tickets. Narrow the audience, or say plainly that it's first-come." |
| Show cancelled after grant | Entitlement revoked and fan **told**. A dead code the fan discovers themselves is worse than the cancellation. | Cancellation notice + revocation, together. |
| Fan revokes marketing consent post-grant | Keeps the entitlement, cannot be told about it. See parent Q-01 — my reading, not a settled ruling. | Nothing, which is the uncomfortable part. |
| Fan forwards their presale code | Governed by the window's code policy in 19, not here — a code carries an **optional** identity binding and the policy is chosen per window ([19.02.03](../../19-ticketing-box-office/19.02-on-sale-announce-presale/19.02.03-access-code-issuance-redemption.md) D-02). Bearer is friendly and scalpable; bound is safe and breaks buying a ticket for your mate. Platform default posture: 19.02.03 Q-02. | If bound: a clear explanation at redemption, not a generic rejection (19.02.03 edge cases). |
| Entitlement granted, then segment definition edited | Grant is unaffected — it snapshotted. This is CX-04 working as designed. | No change. |
| Scalper in the top band (score DT-04) | Gets the presale. The perk system faithfully rewards the anti-persona. Mitigated by attendance-dominant scoring, not by anything here. | Nothing — the fix lives in scoring. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Grant flow opened | Segment count resolving |
| Empty | No perks granted | Empty state with the pattern that works: reward attendance, not spend |
| Populated | ≥1 active entitlement | Granted / redeemed / expired counts per perk |
| Error | 19 rejects the eligibility set | Grant does not silently succeed. The artist must know their presale did not arm. |
| Partial | Granted, notification pending (timezone-scheduled) | "Scheduled — opens 9am local, staggered across 4 timezones" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a duplicate of domain 19's presale feature and should be dissolved into Ticketing | Node Classification Gate; the domain rationale already routed "presale mechanism → Ticketing" | ❌ REJECTED — the split is at the right joint. 19 owns the sale window, the allocation, the money and the enforcement. It cannot own *who deserves access* without importing the entire fan graph, the score and segmentation — i.e. without becoming domain 20. The interface is narrow and clean: **20 hands 19 an eligibility set and a window.** This is the clearest instance in the domain of a cross-domain boundary that pays for itself. |
| DT-02 | The hard problem is granting, so the design should focus there | Naive read — the grant is the visible act | ❌ REJECTED — the hard problem is **the promise exceeding the inventory**. 1,203 eligible fans and 300 tickets means 903 fans get an invitation to a disappointment, and they are by construction the fans who care most. A presale that fails feels worse than no presale, because the fan was told they were special first. The check must be at grant time, and it must be blunt rather than a warning nobody reads. Nothing in the candidate list anticipated this. |
| DT-03 | Perks are mostly a ticketing concern; merch/vault perks are a footnote | Presale dominates the candidate naming ("Superfan Perks & **Presale Eligibility** Segments") | ⏳ **Unresolved — I lean toward rejecting it, without confidence.** Ticketing perks are inventory-constrained (DT-02) and therefore hazardous. **Digital** perks — a vault drop ([20.05.02](../20.05-memberships-patronage-campaigns/20.05.02-exclusive-content-vault.md)), a name-your-price unlock, an early stream — are *unconstrained*: every eligible fan can be rewarded, nobody is disappointed, and the ratchet matters less because nothing is scarce. That suggests digital perks are the better default and presale is the dangerous special case, which inverts the candidate's framing. I do not have evidence for what artists actually want most. Flagged for Step 5. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Entitlements snapshot at grant; segments stay live | `-cx.md` CX-04. Two objects, two lifecycles — rather than one object failing at both. |
| D-02 | 20 owns eligibility; 19 owns the mechanism | See DT-01. Narrow interface: eligibility set + window. |
| D-03 | Over-promise is blocked at grant time | See DT-02. Disappointing your best fans is the worst outcome this feature can produce, and it is entirely preventable. |
| D-04 | Consent governs contact, not entitlement | An earned reward should not evaporate because a fan muted email. Owner ruling needed (parent Q-01). |
| D-05 | Idempotent per fan per sale | Overlapping segments must not double-grant; allocation is finite and double-granting takes from another fan. |
