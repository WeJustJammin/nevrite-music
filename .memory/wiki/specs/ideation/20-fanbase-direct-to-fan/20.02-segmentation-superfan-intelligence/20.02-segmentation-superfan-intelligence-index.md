## Overview

Turning a list of fans into answerable questions: who are my 200 best fans, who lives within 30 miles of
Manchester, who bought the last record but has not bought this one, who gets the presale. This sub-domain is
where the domain's competitive claim is cashed — segmentation here can key on **ticket scans, merch orders and
attendance**, because those events happened on the same platform, while a standalone email tool can only key
on opens and clicks.

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: the segment builder, the superfan score and the
perk/eligibility entitlements are one circuit. The score is an input to segments; segments define who is
entitled to a perk; perk redemption is itself a strong fan signal that feeds back into the score. Cutting any
one out leaves the other two lame.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Segments are **live queries**, not frozen lists | A fan who unsubscribes must leave every segment instantly, including one saved six months ago. A frozen list is a consent bug waiting to happen — the artist sends to a snapshot taken before the revocation. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | The superfan score is **never shown to the fan** | Ranking your audience is legitimate; telling a person their loyalty rank gamifies a relationship that is not a game, and tells someone they are a bad fan. Fans see outcomes, not scores. | Deep Think — see [20.02.02](./20.02.02-superfan-score.md) DT-03 |
| D-03 | Presale **eligibility** is domain-owned; the presale **mechanism** is domain 19 | The domain rationale already routed the presale mechanism to Ticketing. What is unowned there is the question "who deserves it", which requires the fan graph. 19 asks 20 for an eligibility set; 19 enforces it at the box office. | Domain 20 index rationale + Node Classification Gate |
| D-04 | Segmentation reads the fan record ([20.01.01](../20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience/20.01.01-unified-fan-record.md)) but resolves contactability at **send** time, not at segment time | A segment is a question about people ("who came to the last three shows"), not a mailing list. Conflating the two means an artist cannot count their audience without it being a marketing act. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
