# Feature: Segment Builder & Saved Audiences

> **Number**: 20.02.01
> **Parent**: [Segmentation & Superfan Intelligence](./20.02-segmentation-superfan-intelligence-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The query surface over the fan graph: build, name and save an audience from any fact the platform observed —
purchases, ticket scans, attendance, membership tier, geography, follow date, superfan band. Segments are
**live queries** (D-01), not frozen lists, so a fan who revokes consent leaves every audience the instant they
do, including audiences saved a year ago.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Builds audiences from observed facts: "bought the last vinyl, hasn't bought this one", "within 30 miles of Manchester", "attended 3+ shows", "top 5% score", "joined since the tour announcement". Saves and names them. Sees a live count and — critically — the count's **contactable subset**, which is always smaller (D-04). |
| Producer | None | No visibility. No fan list to segment. |
| Operator | Full (own venue's list) | Same builder over the venue's own graph: "attended 3+ shows at this room", "bought a ticket to a metal show in the last year", "lapsed since 2024". Genre/lineup-based segmentation on past attendance is arguably a venue's sharpest tool and has no incumbent product. |
| Fan | None | Invisible and should remain so. Fans do not benefit from knowing they are in "lapsed — win back", and the honest description of most segments is unflattering. |

## Behavior

The builder's differentiator is the **dimensions available**, not the UI. A standalone email tool can filter on
opens, clicks, and whatever fields were in the CSV. This builder can filter on *a ticket having been scanned at
the door* — which is not self-reported, not inferable, and not obtainable by any competitor, because the scan
happened on this platform (domain 19).

Segments resolve at use time. Counting is not marketing: an artist can ask "how many fans do I have in Glasgow"
without that question resolving contactability. Contactability resolves only at send (D-04), and that is where
the number shrinks.

#### Available dimensions

Every dimension is an **observed fact**, never a self-reported field. Grouped into six families; each condition
is `dimension · operator · value`, and conditions combine with `AND` / `OR` and one level of nesting (a group).

| Family | Example dimensions | Source domain |
|--------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Purchase** | bought item X / never bought X / bought in category (vinyl · merch · digital) / lifetime spend / days since last purchase | 20.01, commerce |
| **Ticket & attendance** | bought ticket to show X / **scanned at door** (attended) vs no-show / count of shows attended / attended at venue Y / attended a show in genre Z | **19 Ticketing** |
| **Membership** | tier / joined-since date / active vs lapsed (no activity in N days) | 20.04 |
| **Geography** | distance-from-point < N miles / city / region — derived from stated location OR shipping/billing address, never invented | CX-M13 |
| **Engagement** | follow date / superfan score band or percentile / opened-or-clicked a prior broadcast | 20.02.02, 20.03 |
| **Consent** *(computes the contactable subset, not a filter the artist adds)* | has email consent / has SMS consent | 20.01, D-04 |

The **scan** dimension is the uncopyable one (DT-01): purchase data can be imported from Shopify or Bandcamp, but
"was physically at the door" exists only where this platform ran the box office. The exact operator set and value
types per dimension are `[PENDING — /write-be-spec]`; the families and the scan-is-the-wedge shape are settled here.

#### Builder form

The builder is a **visual condition-tree** (D-05): rows of `dimension · operator · value`, grouped by AND/OR, with
the live count and contactable count recomputing on every edit. Natural-language entry ("fans near Manchester who
bought the last vinyl") is a **Could-tier accelerator** that compiles *into* the visual tree — the artist always
sees and can correct the resolved conditions before saving. Rationale: a free-text query that silently mis-parses
would send a campaign (real money, real consent exposure) to the wrong audience with no visible error; the tree is
auditable and precise, which is the property that matters for something that drives spend. This resolves Q-03.

### Happy Path

1. Musician is routing a tour and opens the builder
2. Adds condition: `distance from venue < 40 miles` (CX-M13)
3. Adds condition: `attended ≥ 1 show OR bought ≥ 1 item` — filtering out passive follows
4. Live count: 1,847 fans; **contactable: 1,203** (the gap is consent, and it is shown, not hidden)
5. Saves as "Manchester — active"; the audience is now reusable by [broadcast](../20.03-broadcast-fan-messaging/20.03-broadcast-fan-messaging-index.md) and by [entitlements](./20.02.03-perk-entitlements-presale-eligibility.md), and it stays live — fans who move to Manchester next month are in it automatically

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Segment resolves to zero | Common and demoralising for a new artist. Must explain *which condition* emptied it. | "0 fans — no one matches 'attended 3+ shows'. Your most-attended fan has been to 1." |
| Segment count differs from send count | Expected, by design (D-04). Never a surprise. | Both numbers, always, with the gap attributed to consent. |
| Fan revokes consent after segment is saved | Leaves the audience immediately — the query is live. | Count drops. No action needed. |
| Artist builds a segment that is effectively one person | Not blocked, but it is a targeting surface pointed at an individual. `[PENDING]` on whether a k-anonymity floor applies to *sends* (it should not apply to counts or to a fan record view). | `[PENDING]` |
| Score dimension unavailable (recompute failed) | Resolve on last-good score, never on null. A null silently empties an audience and the artist sends to nobody without knowing. | Stale-score indicator with the timestamp. |
| Geo segment for a fan with no location | Excluded. Location is often absent — most fans never state one. | Count shows "N fans have no location" so the gap is visible rather than mysterious. |
| Contradictory conditions (`attended ≥ 3 shows` AND `never bought a ticket`) | Resolves to zero — but the zero is a logic mistake, not an empty fanbase. Distinguish from a real empty result. | "0 fans — these two conditions can't both be true. A fan who attended can't have never bought a ticket." |
| Delegate (manager/label staff) builds or exports a segment | Access is scoped to what the ownership model grants (20.01.05); an unauthorized delegate cannot build over a graph they don't hold. Export specifically is the sharp edge — see Q-02. | `[PENDING — /create-prd-security]` for the export-vs-build split; build over own-graph is permitted. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Condition added | Count recomputes with a spinner; conditions stay editable |
| Empty | No fans, or no segments saved | Distinguish the two: "you have no fans yet" vs "you have no saved audiences" — completely different problems |
| Populated | Conditions resolve | Live count + contactable count + a sample of matching fans |
| Error | Query timeout on a large graph | Explicit failure. Never a partial count presented as whole — an artist making a tour-routing decision on a silently truncated number is worse off than one who saw an error. |
| Partial | Some dimensions degraded (stale score) | Count shown, degraded dimensions named |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The builder's advantage is the dimensions, not the interface | Domain rationale: "segmentation here is sharper than any standalone email tool because the platform holds ticket, merch and attendance data" | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it sharpens the claim. The uncopyable dimension is specifically the **scan**, not the purchase. Purchase data can be imported from Shopify or Bandcamp; a *door scan* exists only where the box office runs. This means the segmentation advantage is **conditional on domain 19 shipping** — if ticketing is deferred, this feature is a nicer Mailchimp, not a different product. That dependency is the single most important MoSCoW input in domain 20. |
| DT-02 | Segments should be frozen lists so campaigns are reproducible | Reasonable engineering instinct — reproducibility, auditability | ❌ REJECTED — a frozen list is a consent bug. The artist sends to a snapshot taken before someone revoked, and the platform has no defence. Reproducibility is satisfied by recording what a *campaign* actually sent to (an immutable send log), not by freezing the audience definition. Live segment, immutable send record. |
| DT-03 | A segment pointed at one identifiable fan is a targeting hazard nobody listed | Adversarial read of the builder against the Fan persona | ⏳ **Partially confirmed, unresolved.** The hazard is real (an artist or, worse, a delegate with list access, building "female fans, 18-25, within 5 miles" is a stalking tool with a marketing UI). But a k-anonymity floor would break legitimate uses — an artist looking at one fan's record is normal and must stay so. My read: the floor belongs on **send** and on **export**, not on counting or record viewing. Escalated to `/create-prd-security` — I am not confident enough to call it. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Segments are live queries; send records are immutable | See DT-02. Reproducibility belongs on the send, not the audience. |
| D-02 | Counting is not marketing — contactability resolves at send | An artist must be able to ask how many fans they have in a city without it being a consent event. Showing both numbers makes the consent cost visible, which is the honest presentation. |
| D-03 | Zero-result segments must name the emptying condition | The most common failure for a new artist, and a bare "0" teaches nothing. |
| D-04 | Counting resolves the audience; contactability resolves only at send. Both numbers are shown side by side, the gap attributed to consent. | An artist must be able to size a city without it being a consent event; showing the gap is the honest presentation. Referenced throughout. |
| D-05 | Builder is a visual condition-tree; natural language is a Could-tier accelerator that compiles into the tree and is always shown before save. | A mis-parsed free-text query would misdirect real spend and consent with no visible error; the tree is auditable and precise. Resolves Q-03. |
