# Feature: Fan Preference Centre & Unsubscribe

> **Number**: 20.01.03
> **Parent**: [Fan Graph & Owned Audience](./20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The fan's control surface over what every artist they follow may send them, and through which channel. It is
the only fan-side write path onto the consent ledger ([20.01.02](./20.01.02-consent-legal-basis.md)), and it
must work without a login — a fan forced to authenticate in order to unsubscribe will press "report spam"
instead, which converts one fan's irritation into a deliverability tax on every artist on the platform.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Fan | Full | Sees every artist and venue holding a marketing basis for them, on one page, with per-channel and per-purpose toggles. Can turn down frequency instead of leaving ("monthly digest" / "only shows near me" / "only new releases"). One-click global opt-out is always present and always honoured. Reachable from any message via a signed link with no login. |
| Musician | Read-only | Sees aggregate preference distribution for their own list — how many chose "shows only", how many muted SMS. Never sees a per-fan toggle history as an editable thing, and has no write path. Sees churn as a number, not as a per-fan feed of rejection. |
| Producer | None | No visibility — no fan list, no fans to have preferences about. |
| Operator | Read-only | Identical to Musician, for the venue's own list. |

## Behavior

The Preference Centre inverts the usual pattern: it is organised **by the fan**, not by the artist. A fan who
follows nine artists sees one page listing all nine, not nine separate unsubscribe pages found only by
digging through nine old emails. That is a materially better product than the incumbent norm and it is only
possible because the platform is consolidated — a per-artist ESP structurally cannot offer it.

Every toggle writes a consent event. Nothing is updated in place.

The **frequency down-shift** is the important behaviour: most unsubscribes are not "I dislike this artist",
they are "this is too much". Offering "less" alongside "never" preserves a fan relationship that a binary
unsubscribe destroys permanently. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` on the exact frequency tiers.

### Happy Path

1. Fan receives an email from an artist and taps "manage preferences" in the footer
2. Signed link opens the Preference Centre with no login challenge; the artist in question is anchored at the top
3. Fan sees all nine artists/venues they hear from, each with channel toggles and a frequency setting
4. Fan sets this artist to "shows near me only" and mutes SMS globally
5. System writes the consent events; confirms inline; the artist's next general campaign resolves this fan out, while a show announcement in their city resolves them in

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Signed link is old / expired | Must still work for unsubscribe. An expired token may gate the *full* centre behind login, but the one-click opt-out for the artist that sent it must never fail. | Opt-out confirmation; optional "log in to manage everything". |
| Forwarded email — someone else clicks unsubscribe | Unsubscribes the original recipient. This is the accepted industry trade-off; the alternative (login required) causes far more harm. | Confirmation. `[PENDING]` on whether the true owner is notified and offered a one-click undo. |
| Fan unsubscribes mid-send | In-flight campaign must suppress remaining sends to them, not merely apply next time. | Nothing — the correct outcome is silence. |
| Fan opts out of marketing, then buys a ticket | Still receives the ticket, the QR, the doors time. Different purpose. | Their ticket, as expected. |
| Fan has no WeJammin account (imported address) | Centre works via the signed link and shows only entities that hold a basis for that address. | Same page, narrower. |
| List-Unsubscribe header click (Gmail/Apple native) | Honoured as a full opt-out for that sender with no page load at all. | Native client confirmation. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Centre opened | Skeleton list of senders |
| Empty | Fan has no marketing relationships | "Nobody is emailing you" + how it happens (following, buying, signing up at a show) |
| Populated | ≥1 sender | Grouped-by-sender toggles + global controls |
| Error | Ledger write fails | **The opt-out must not appear to succeed.** Explicit failure + retry + a fallback opt-out path. A false "you're unsubscribed" is the worst outcome available here. |
| Partial | Signed token identifies one sender only | That sender's controls, fully functional; rest behind an optional login |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A cross-artist preference centre is the single fan-facing advantage consolidation buys that no incumbent can copy | Thesis (D-18): consolidation is the platform | ✅ CONFIRMED — Mailchimp/Klaviyo/Bandzoogle each sit behind one artist, so a "manage all the artists who email me" page is structurally impossible for them and trivial here. It is also the rare consolidation benefit that accrues to the **Fan**, who otherwise receives mostly obligations from this domain. Worth more than its size suggests. |
| DT-02 | Frequency down-shift meaningfully reduces permanent unsubscribes | Standard CRM practice; asymmetric cost of losing a fan forever vs annoying them monthly | ⏳ **Neither confirmed nor rejected honestly** — the practice is well-established generally, but I have no music-specific evidence, and music fans may behave differently (parasocial attachment cuts both ways). Recorded as a hypothesis to test, not a fact. Cheap to build, so the option value is high regardless. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | No login required to unsubscribe | A login wall converts unsubscribes into spam complaints, and complaints are a shared cost across all artists on the sending domain ([20.03.04](../20.03-broadcast-fan-messaging/20.03.04-deliverability-sender-reputation.md)). |
| D-02 | Organised by fan (all senders on one page), not by artist | See DT-01. This is a consolidation dividend and it is the fan's payoff for the domain existing. |
| D-03 | Offer "less" alongside "never" | A permanent unsubscribe is unrecoverable; a frequency reduction is not. Marked as a hypothesis (DT-02), not a proven win. |
| D-04 | Artists have no write path and see churn only in aggregate | Per-fan unsubscribe feeds train artists to take it personally and to chase fans off-platform. |
