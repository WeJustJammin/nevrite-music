## Overview

The identified fan record and the legal right to contact it. This sub-domain owns the substrate every
other child of domain 20 stands on: one durable record per fan per artist, assembled from the fan's
platform account (D-11 — fans are first-class users), their ticket scans, their store purchases, their
follows, and any list the artist imported from elsewhere. It also owns the question of what the artist
is permitted to *do* with that record — consent, legal basis, preference state — and the question
almost nobody asks until it hurts: **who owns the list when the band splits.**

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: the fan record, the consent state that gates
its use, the import path that creates records with weaker consent provenance, and the ownership/transfer
rules that decide who may act on it are not four independent things. Consent is a property of the record
whose strength depends on how the record was created; ownership decides whose consent is even being
relied upon. Change one and the other three move.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The fan record is **per artist-entity**, not global | A person is a fan *of someone*. A global "fan" row with an artist array cannot express per-artist consent, per-artist preference, or per-artist ownership on band split — all three of which this sub-domain must express. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3, domain 20 |
| D-02 | Consent capture stays domain-owned; consent *storage/DSAR* consumes CX-M18 | CX-M18 (Privacy, Consent & Data Portability) is the ledger and the subject-rights machinery. What lives here is the marketing-specific application: which artist may contact this fan, on which channel, under which legal basis. Nothing else in the platform has a marketing-consent surface — Community is peer-to-peer, Promotion is strangers. Not a duplicate. | Node Classification Gate |
| D-03 | Operator (venue) is a first-class holder of a fan list | Venues run mailing lists and promote their own programme; the mechanism is identical to an artist's. Excluding them would force domain 16 to reimplement this sub-domain. Scope consequence flagged as Q-02. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
