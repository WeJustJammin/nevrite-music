# Feature: Consent, Legal Basis & Proof-of-Consent

> **Number**: 20.01.02
> **Parent**: [Fan Graph & Owned Audience](./20.01-fan-graph-owned-audience-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The record of *why* an artist is permitted to contact a fan, captured at the moment the fan grants it and
held as evidence rather than as a boolean. Contactability is resolved per (fan, owning entity, channel,
purpose) — a fan who bought a ticket can be told the doors time but not sold a t-shirt; a fan who signed up
at the merch table can be sold the t-shirt but has not consented to SMS. This is the platform's own thesis
turned on consent: **capture the fact at the moment it is true, because reconstructing it later is worthless.**

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | Configures which purposes they want to rely on and sees, per segment, how many fans are actually contactable on each channel — usually a much smaller number than the list size, which is the point. Sees the evidence behind any single fan's consent (date, wording shown, source). **Cannot create, edit, or restore a consent state.** |
| Producer | None | No visibility — no fan list. |
| Operator | Config | Identical, scoped to the venue entity. Additionally relevant because a venue's lawful basis for ticket holders is usually transactional-only, and venues habitually market to it anyway. |
| Fan | Full | Grants and revokes, per artist, per channel, per purpose, via [20.01.03](./20.01.03-fan-preference-centre.md). Sees every basis every artist relies on for them and the evidence behind it. Their write always wins over the artist's. |

## Behavior

Consent is **append-only**. A grant is an event; a revocation is an event; the current state is derived. No
row is updated in place, because the evidentiary value of consent is entirely in the record of the moment —
a boolean `subscribed = true` proves nothing to a regulator or a complaint handler.

Each consent event captures: timestamp, owning entity, channel (email / SMS / push), purpose (marketing /
transactional / service), the **exact wording the fan was shown**, the source surface, and provenance strength
(platform-witnessed vs artist-asserted-at-import — see [20.01.04](./20.01.04-fan-list-import-hygiene.md)).

Purposes are not interchangeable. Revoking marketing must never suppress transactional — a fan who unsubscribes
from a newsletter and consequently stops receiving their own ticket QR is a support catastrophe wearing a
compliance costume.

### Happy Path

1. Fan enters their email into an artist's store checkout, with an unticked "also email me about new releases and shows" box
2. Fan ticks it and completes the purchase
3. System writes **two** consent events: transactional (basis: contract — they bought a thing) and marketing (basis: consent — they ticked), each stamped with the wording shown and the surface
4. Artist's next campaign to "everyone who bought the vinyl" resolves contactability per fan and includes this one on the marketing channel
5. Fan clicks unsubscribe in that campaign → revocation event → still receives their shipping notification, because that is a different purpose

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Artist tries to re-subscribe a fan who opted out | Blocked. There is no owner-side write path to consent at all — this is enforced by absence, not by a check. | "Only the fan can restore this. You can ask them in person; you cannot do it here." |
| Fan is in a jurisdiction requiring opt-in (EU/UK) but signed up under a US-style pre-tick | The pre-tick is not offered. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` on whether the platform enforces the strictest regime globally or geo-varies the capture UI. See Q-01. | `[PENDING]` |
| Consent evidence requested during a complaint | Artist exports the full event chain for one fan: wording, timestamp, source, IP. | A single-fan evidence pack. |
| Fan grants SMS consent but the artist has no SMS channel configured | Consent is recorded and dormant. It does not expire on non-use. | Artist sees "N fans consented to SMS — no SMS sender configured". |
| Fan record merged with conflicting consent states | Intersection of permissions, union of identities (`-cx.md` CX-02). Never the union of permissions. | Artist sees the narrower state and the reason. |
| Consent granted 6 years ago, never used | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — some regimes treat stale consent as expired. Does the platform age consent out, warn, or ignore? | `[PENDING]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Contactability panel opened | Skeleton; counts resolve after the segment |
| Empty | No consent events for this fan | "Not contactable" with the reason — never a blank |
| Populated | ≥1 event | Current state per channel/purpose + the evidence chain |
| Error | Consent ledger unreachable | **Fail closed** — contactability resolves to "not contactable" and sends block. Guessing here is unlawful. |
| Partial | Contactable on some channels only | Per-channel state, each with its own basis |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This duplicates CX-M18 (Privacy, Consent & Data Portability) and should be dissolved into it | Node Classification Gate — "is it a mechanism serving many domains?" | ❌ REJECTED — CX-M18 is the ledger, the subject-rights machinery and portability, and this feature consumes it. What is domain-owned is *marketing* consent: which artist may sell to which fan on which channel. Nothing else on the platform has that surface — Community (03) is peer-to-peer professional, Promotion (21) addresses strangers, Notifications (CX-M07) are the platform's own transactional messages. The mechanism is shared; the product is not. Recorded as D-02 on the parent index. |
| DT-02 | Consent should be stored as current state (a boolean per channel) with history as a nice-to-have | Simpler; what every basic ESP does | ❌ REJECTED — the entire value of consent is evidentiary, and evidence is the event, not the state. A boolean cannot answer "what exactly did she agree to, on what date, in what words" — which is the only question ever asked about consent. This is the *identical* failure the platform exists to fix for splits: a reconstructed record is worthless at the moment it matters. Append-only, derived state. |
| DT-03 | The hardest problem here is unlawful-marketing risk from artists, not from the platform | Fan persona anti-behaviour + the import path | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is a **multi-tenant** risk, not a per-artist one. On a shared sending domain, one artist blasting a bought list generates complaints that degrade deliverability for every other artist (see [20.03.04](../20.03-broadcast-fan-messaging/20.03.04-deliverability-sender-reputation.md)). The platform therefore has a commercial interest in strict consent, not merely a legal one — which is a much more durable reason to enforce it. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Consent is append-only events; state is derived | See DT-02. The evidence *is* the product. |
| D-02 | Contactability is per (fan, entity, channel, purpose) — not per list | A single boolean cannot express "tell me about the show, don't sell me a hoodie", which is a distinction fans genuinely hold. |
| D-03 | There is no owner-side write path to consent | Enforced by absence rather than permission check. An artist cannot re-subscribe a fan under any circumstance, including support escalation. |
| D-04 | Fail closed on ledger unavailability | The alternative is sending unlawfully during an outage. Blocking a campaign for ten minutes is recoverable; a regulatory complaint is not. |
