## Overview

The structured truth of a **recording space**: its rooms and their acoustics, everything a signal
passes through, how the studio sells its people, and what happens to your session data after you
leave. Where the venue spec (16.02) answers "will my show fit?", the studio spec answers "can I make
the record I want here, with whom, and will the files still exist in five years?"

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: rooms, inventory, staffing and archive are distinct
capabilities that interact as one purchasing decision. The room and the desk are chosen together;
the staffing model decides whether the booking is a room or a room-and-a-person; the archive policy
determines whether the session's provenance survives. Four capabilities, one decision.

**The Producer's sub-domain.** `personas.md` names the Producer the provenance capture point — the
one in the room when the facts become true. This is the part of domain 16 that describes the room.
