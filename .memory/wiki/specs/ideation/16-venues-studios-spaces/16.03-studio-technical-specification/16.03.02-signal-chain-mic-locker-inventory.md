# Feature: Signal Chain, Mic Locker & Instrument Inventory

> **Number**: 16.03.02
> **Parent**: [Studio Technical Specification](./16.03-studio-technical-specification-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17
> **Merged from**: sweep candidate 03 (kept whole — signal chain, mic locker and instruments are one decision)

## Overview

Everything in the studio that a signal passes through or is made by: the desk, preamps, converters,
outboard, the mic locker down to individual pairs, the DAW and its version, monitoring, and the
instruments — the piano and its last tuning, the drum kit, the amps, the Rhodes that half the
studio's bookings actually exist for.

The domain index calls this out explicitly: **"the mic locker IS the booking decision"**. A Producer
does not book a studio and then discover what is in it; they book a studio *because* of a pair of
C12s and a 1073. This is the single most persuasive listing content a studio has, and it is
currently a bulleted list on a website that was last updated when the Neve was still working.

**What this feature is, after the mandatory 15 cross-check (D-07, Q-01 — now resolved):** it is
**not an inventory**. Domain 15 already owns the gear taxonomy, the register, its condition, and
its publication path, and 15.07 D-01 is ratified in the other direction — *"the room listing
(domain 16) reads the register; it does not duplicate it."* This feature is **the studio's read
view over that projection, joined to the room, plus the three facts domain 15 does not model**:

| What 16.03.02 owns | Why 15 cannot own it |
|---|---|
| **Room binding** — which assets are bound to a room, pooled across the studio, or mobile | 15.07.01 registers assets against the **org entity**. The **room** is 16's spec- and calendar-carrying unit (domain D-06). Nothing in 15 knows a studio has three rooms. |
| **Simultaneous capacity** — channel counts, converter I/O, cue mixes | 15 models objects one at a time. "Can I track a 12-piece live?" is a property of the chain as a set, in a room, and no gear record answers it. |
| **The software half** — DAW, version, HDX/native, notable plugins | 15 is physical objects keyed `(maker, model, era, serial)`. A Pro Tools version is not that object and never will be. |

Everything else here — what an item *is*, whether it *works*, whether it is *published* — is read
from 15 and rendered. This feature stores none of it.

## Behavior

### The view, not the store

A Producer opens a studio room and sees its gear grouped by function: console/desk (make, model,
channel count, recall capability), preamps, converters and clocking, outboard, mic locker (with
pairing), DAW and version, monitoring, and instruments. Every item's **identity** comes from the
gear record (15.01.01), its **condition** from 15.07.02, and its **presence on the page at all**
from the Operator's publication choice (15.07.03). Nothing on this page is a field an Operator
typed into domain 16, and that is deliberate: 15.07.03 DT-01 established that a second store rots
by construction, and "sync" is the same rot running slower with silent failures.

The consequence is the whole point: **an item sold, broken, or disposed of leaves this view without
anyone remembering to do anything.** The Operator's named accidental anti-behaviour in
`personas.md` — *"publishes a stale gear list, so a band arrives to a room that can't run their
show"* — is structurally unavailable, because there is no second list to go stale.

### Per-item status is composed from three owners, not one

The `[SURFACE]` pass asserted a single status field (available / in repair / by request / hire
extra). The 15 cross-check shows that was three unrelated facts wearing one name. They have
different owners, different lifecycles, and different write rules, and fusing them would have
forced 16 to own two things it has no right to:

| Facet | Values | Owner | Changes when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Functional condition** | functional · degraded (+note) · faulty · out for service | **15.07.02** | Someone in the room finds a fault, or a service event (15.03) closes it. Hourly-to-yearly. |
| **Commercial inclusion** | included · hire extra (+price) · by request | **16.06.07** (rates are Operator-only — that domain's one absolute write rule, D-03) | The Operator changes their rate card. Rarely. |
| **Room binding** | room-bound · studio-pooled · mobile | **16.03.02 — this feature** | Gear moves. Constantly, and nobody logs it. |

The view renders all three together because the Producer's question is one question. It stores
only the third.

### Room binding, and why it is not a detail

15.07.01 registers assets against the **org entity** — the studio owns the console. Domain 16 D-06
makes the **room** the spec- and calendar-carrying unit. Between those two ratified decisions is a
fact neither domain holds: *which room is the Neve in.* For a single-room studio this is invisible.
For a three-room studio it is the entire booking decision, and it is the difference between a
correct search result and a wrong one.

Binding is three-valued because studios are:

- **Room-bound** — the desk, the patchbay, the monitoring, the mains, the grand piano. It is bolted,
  wired, or 400kg. Booking the room gets you this and nothing else does.
- **Studio-pooled** — the mic locker, the outboard in a rolling rack, the DI boxes. It serves every
  room and belongs to none. **The mic locker is precisely the thing that is not room-bound** — which
  is uncomfortable, because the mic locker is the thing people filter on.
- **Mobile** — the Rhodes, the amps, the kit. Wheeled to whichever room needs them, subject to
  someone being willing to wheel it.

Two consequences fall straight out, and both are load-bearing:

1. **A gear filter that returns rooms is answering the wrong question for pooled gear.** "Rooms with
   a Neve 88R" is a room query. "Rooms with a C12" is not — the C12 belongs to the studio and can go
   anywhere. The filter must resolve at the **studio** for pooled items and at the **room** for bound
   items, and present the result honestly ("Studio A — C12 available from the studio's locker").
2. **A studio can sell one microphone twice.** Room calendars are independent by design (D-06). Two
   rooms booked Tuesday 10:00–18:00, both chosen for the studio's single C12, is a double-sale of a
   pooled asset that no calendar detects, because no calendar owns it. See DT-07 and D-09.

### Simultaneous capacity — the fact a gear list cannot express

A list answers *do you have a 1073*. A session asks *can I track my band*. Those come apart
immediately and expensively: eight mics into four preamp channels tracks half a drum kit. A
Producer plans against **counts, not presence** — mic count by type, preamp channels, converter I/O
in and out, simultaneous cue mixes (16.03.01 already carries headphone/cue provision), and desk
channel count and recall.

This is also what 16.07 needs to do its job in the studio direction. 16.07 D-02 outputs
conflict / unknown / match — and against a presence-only inventory, "12-piece live tracking" can
only ever return `unknown`, which 16.07 D-06 says is what costs the Operator the enquiry. Capacity
counts are the fields that turn the studio half of the conformance check from a formality into a
result.

Capacity is **derived where it can be and declared where it cannot**: a count of published preamp
channels is arithmetic over the register; whether all sixteen can be used at once through this
particular desk in this particular room is a fact only the Operator holds. The derived number is
shown with the Operator's declared ceiling where the two disagree, lower value winning, because a
capacity claim that fails at 10am on a tracking day is the most expensive kind of wrong this
feature can be.

### The software half

15 models physical objects. A Pro Tools version is not one, and neither is Melodyne. But a Producer
bringing a session in asks about it before they ask about the room, because a session that will not
open is not a session: DAW and version, HDX vs native (a channel-count and I/O fact as much as a
software one), sample rate ceiling, notable plugins, and whether the studio's licences travel
(iLok) or are machine-bound. Plugins are goods in domain 14; what is *installed and usable in this
room* is not a purchase record and 14 does not carry it. So this half is 16.03.02's own declared
data — the only part of this feature that is a form the Operator fills in — pending the 14
cross-check (Q-04).

Note this does **not** re-open CX R-02, which rejected the archive↔DAW-version link as speculative.
That rejection was about session *recall rot* five years later, and stands. This is a **booking-time
compatibility fact** a Producer checks before they arrive, which is a different question asked by a
different person at a different time.

### Instruments, and the piano

The piano is the one instrument whose condition is genuinely time-varying, and the `[SURFACE]` pass
had it half-right: last-tuning date is necessary and not sufficient. **A tuning date is a fact about
the past; a Producer needs a fact about Thursday.** Two fields carry what the date cannot:

- **Pitch standard** — A=440 or A=442. A piano tuned to 442 is not usable for overdubs against
  tracks cut at 440, and the studio will not think to mention it because it is normal to them.
- **Holds pitch / does not** — an old piano that has dropped below concert pitch cannot be brought
  back in one tuning without breaking strings, so it is tuned *to itself*. It is in tune. It is
  also unusable for the session. That distinction is invisible in every studio listing ever
  published and it is the single most common piano surprise in the industry.

The tuning event itself is not a field here — it is a service event in 15.03, the same as a retube
or a recap, and it surfaces on this view the way every other condition fact does. What is 16's is
the pitch standard and the hold, because they are properties of the instrument *in this room's
humidity*, which is a room fact.

## Happy Path

1. Producer is planning a session that needs a specific vocal chain and a live 5-piece tracking date.
2. They filter: mic `C12 OR U47`, preamp `Neve OR API`, and **preamp channels ≥ 8**.
3. Results resolve the filter at the right level — bound gear at the room, pooled gear at the studio
   — and show, per result, which matched items are room-bound and which come from the studio's
   locker.
4. Producer opens Studio A: desk (Neve 88R, 48ch, total recall), matched pair of C414s
   *(studio locker — available)*, 1073 ×2 *(room-bound — available)*, LA-2A *(faulty — channel
   dead, reported 4 days ago by the house engineer)*, Steinway B *(tuned 2026-06-02, A=440,
   holds pitch)*, Pro Tools 2024.6 HDX.
5. Capacity panel reads: 16 preamp channels published, Operator ceiling 16, 4 cue mixes. The live
   tracking date is a `match`, not an `unknown`.
6. Producer books the compound (live room + control room + assistant) via 16.06.05. **The filter
   that produced the booking is captured onto the reservation as its gear dependency** — the C12 and
   the 1073s, because those are what they searched for (D-08).
7. Six days before the session the house engineer logs the second 1073 faulty (15.07.02, a
   ten-second write). The register changes; this view changes with it, and — because the
   reservation declared the dependency — **the Producer is notified that a booking of theirs
   depends on it**, six days out, when it is a phone call and not a crisis.
8. Producer re-plans against what is actually there, or moves the date. Nobody discovers it at 10am
   with the band in the live room.
9. Post-session, the harvest (16.05.06) asks three pre-filled questions about the least-certain
   published items. The Producer confirms the C414 pair is present and reports the Rhodes is not in
   the building. Presence is reconfirmed at zero cost; the Rhodes becomes contested.

## Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **A booked-for item goes faulty before the session** | The reservation's declared gear dependency (D-08) fires. 15.07.02 already requires notifying the booker on a fault; for studios the booker is a 16.06 reservation, not a 17 show | Notification naming the item, the affected reservation, and the date — not a silent page edit to a page they already read |
| **A booked-for item is sold or disposed of before the session** | Same dependency, different trigger. It vanishes from the register, therefore from this view, therefore from the booking's basis | Same notification; the item shows as removed rather than faulty, because "gone" and "broken" are different plans |
| **A held item is withdrawn — the house engineer leaves and takes their U87s** | Not a fault, so 15.07.02's notify path never fires; not a disposal, because it was never the studio's. The gap DT-14 names | Notification on dependency; the item was always visibly marked *held, not owned* on the view, so the Producer knew it could walk |
| **Two rooms booked for the same session slot, both filtered on the studio's single C12** | The pooled double-sale (DT-07). No room calendar detects it because no room owns the mic | Surfaced at the second booking as a conflict, **never a block** (mirrors 16.07 D-05) — the Operator may well have a plan; the platform does not get a vote |
| **Gear moved between rooms and nobody logged it** | The normal state. Binding is Operator-declared and decays like every other claim | Binding age is visible; a room-bound claim older than the register's last touch is shown with its age, per 16.05.05 |
| **Register unreachable during a room view** | 15.07.03 D-03 governs: last-known state **with its age**, never a blank | Gear section with a staleness banner. A blank reads as "this room has no gear", which is worse than an honestly-labelled stale list |
| **Register unreachable during a *search*** | The dangerous version, and the opposite rule. A degraded search silently returns fewer rooms — an answer that looks like an answer | **Gear filters fail loudly and refuse to return a partial result set.** A studio omitted from a search it should have won is invisible to both parties forever |
| **Presence match, capacity fail** — 8 mics, 4 preamp channels | Presence-only inventories return this as a match and the Producer finds out on the day | Capacity shown alongside presence; 16.07 returns `conflict`, not `match` |
| **Capacity undeclared** | Cannot be inferred — a rack of 16 preamp channels may not all reach this room | 16.07 returns `unknown` (D-02 there), and the Operator sees `unknown` cost them the enquiry (D-06 there) |
| **Derived count and Operator ceiling disagree** | Lower value wins, both shown | "16 channels published · 12 usable in this room (Operator)" |
| **Home / project studio — most of the supply** | 15.07 publishes **org** registers; a bedroom studio has no org entity, so its gear is a personal collection (15.04) and cannot publish at all. Most of the market is structurally excluded from gear search (DT-12) | Currently: room drops out of every gear filter. Proposed fix escalated as Q-02 — this is a structural exclusion, not a graceful degradation |
| **Operator's personal gear used in the business** | The line is fiction in small studios (15.01.01 DT-11; 15.07.01 Q-02). The register must not guess, and neither may this view | Item renders with whatever ownership 15 resolved. If `held`, it is marked held |
| **400-item register, 30 published** | Not this feature's problem, which is the payoff of the cross-check. Publication is already a deliberate opt-in subset (15.07.03 D-02) and the register's size is not disclosed | The published 30. The other 370 are not hinted at |
| **Commodity gear — 40 stands, 200 cables** | Quantity-tracked, not identity-tracked (15.07.01 DT-03) | Counts, no per-object identity |
| **Single vs matched pair** | Distinct capabilities. Two unmatched C414s do not substitute for a matched pair on overheads or Blumlein | Quantity **and** pairing, as separate facts (D-03) |
| **Two staff report conflicting conditions** | 15.07.02 Q-03's unresolved case arrives here as a display problem regardless of how they resolve it | Both reports, both attributed, both dated. Neither auto-wins |
| **Operator suppresses a fault to protect bookings** | 15.07.02 DT-02 established the platform cannot force honesty, only make it cheap | "No known faults" **never** renders as "verified working" (15.07.02 D-03). Absence of a report is absence of a report |
| **Visiting Producer reports gear the register says is present** | They hold no entity role, so this is not a condition write. It is a contested fact under 16.05.04 D-05 — last-agreed stands, contest visible (D-10) | Item + contested marker + who contested it and when |
| **House engineer reports the same thing** | They hold an entity role on the studio (15.07.01 D-02), so it is a direct condition write | Condition updates; no contest state, because there is no disagreement |
| **Serial leaks through a room photo** | 16.01.03's structured photo checklist asks for a control-room photo — which is a photo of the desk and the rack. **Domain 16 is the delivery mechanism for the exposure 15.07.03 D-02 unconditionally forbids in text.** 15.07.03 Q-03 owns the fix; this feature must not pretend the withholding holds | Escalated — see Cross-Cut Notes. The text withholding is real; the image hole is not ours to close but is ours to have found |
| **Gear list + address + a live availability calendar** | The composition 15.07.03 DT-02 feared, made materially worse by 16: a booking calendar publishes **when the room is empty**, which "opening hours" only implied (DT-10). Lockout tenancies (16.06.09) publish when the building is unattended | Studio gear is behind auth (Fan `None`), which reduces the exposure. It does not remove it — a signed-in account is not a trusted one. See Q-01 |
| **Operator lists gear they do not own to win bookings** | The Operator's stated intentional anti-behaviour ("misrepresents the room's specs"). 15's mint intents already defuse it: an `owned` mint carries an **Asserted** claim — the weakest tier, contestable | Claim strength and identity confidence are surfaced separately, never fused (15.01.01 DT-08). An unclaimed or held item says so |
| **Operator unpublishes an item mid-quote** | Race between a 15.07.03 publication change and an in-flight 16.06.07 quote. The quote is the commitment; the view is not | Quote stands as computed; the room view updates. A published price for an unpublished item is 16.06.07's to reconcile |
| **Bulk register import half-finished** | 15.07.01's `Partial` state. A half-imported register must never render as a complete one | Published subset only, with the import's incompleteness explicit — never a short list presented as the list |
| **Same item registered twice** | Duplicate gear records are 15.01.05's problem (contested identity); duplicate *places* are 16.05.07's. Neither is this feature's, and it must not silently dedupe by model name — two C414s **should** appear twice | Both render. Deduping by model would erase the pairing fact (D-03) |
| **Place is both a venue and a studio** (type is a set — D-06) | The Fan rules diverge on one record: 15.07.03 gives Fan read-only on a venue backline; 16.03 gives Fan `None` on studio gear. Both are correct | Resolved at the **room**, not the place: a Fan on the venue page sees the stage backline; the studio rooms and their locker are not on any Fan-visible surface |
| **DAW version changed after booking** | The Producer booked to bring a session that opened in 2024.6; the studio updated. A declared field, so it changes visibly | Same dependency mechanism as gear (D-08) — the software half is part of what was booked against |
| **Piano tuned recently but to A=442** | Date is fresh and the piano is unusable for the session. The failure a tuning date cannot express | Pitch standard shown beside the tuning date, always, not on request |
| **Piano is in tune with itself, below concert pitch** | Tuned "to itself" because it cannot be pulled up in one pass without breaking strings. In tune; unusable | `Holds pitch: no` — a first-class field, not a note (DT-11) |
| **Studio has no register at all** | The cold start, and studios have no seeding story (domain Q-07) — public data knows venues, not studios | Room drops out of every gear filter. For a studio this is close to invisibility, and it is honest |

## States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | Room view fetching the register projection | Skeleton in the gear section; the room's own facts (16.03.01) render immediately and do not wait on it — they have different owners and different failure modes |
| **Empty** | No org register, or nothing published | No gear section. Room is absent from every gear filter. Honest, and for a studio, near-fatal — the Operator sees which filters they lost (16.07 D-06) |
| **Partial** | Register published in part, or capacity undeclared | Published items render; undeclared capacity renders as **`unknown`**, never as zero and never as unlimited. A half-imported register is explicitly labelled as such |
| **Populated** | Register published, capacity declared, binding declared | Grouped inventory with per-item condition, inclusion, binding, pairing, and per-field ages. Searchable and conformance-checkable |
| **Error** | Register unreachable | **On a room view**: last-known state with its age (15.07.03 D-03) — never a blank. **On a search**: fail loudly, return no partial result set — a silently short answer is the worst outcome this feature has |
| **Stale** | Nothing touched the register or the binding for a long interval | Items carry "last confirmed present" — set by the harvest (16.05.06), not by the Operator. Absence of change is ambiguous between *accurate* and *abandoned*, and the view must not resolve that ambiguity in the Operator's favour |
| **Contested** | Post-session contradiction from a party with no entity role | Last-agreed value stands, contest visible (16.05.04 D-05), attributed and dated |
