# Feature: Studio Rooms & Acoustics

> **Number**: 16.03.01
> **Parent**: [Studio Technical Specification](./16.03-studio-technical-specification-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

The **fixed physical substrate of a recording space**: each room's dimensions and volume, its
ceiling height range, its surfaces and treatment mechanism, how many players it tracks, and — the
part nobody publishes — the facts that exist only **between two rooms**: isolation, sightlines and
tie-lines.

For the Producer this is not metadata. **The room choice is the creative decision.** A band
tracking drums needs height; a singer-songwriter needs a dead booth; a mix engineer needs a control
room they trust. But almost none of what makes a room *good* is expressible as a number, and this
feature's defining discipline is knowing exactly where that line falls (D-01).

**The deeper structural fact, found in this pass**: the questions a Producer actually asks are not
about a room, they are about a **room pair** — can Room A hear Room B, see Room B, and get a signal
to Room B (DT-09). Those three facts run over the same pairs as 16.01.02's `requires` graph, which
makes them the physical twin of the commercial one, and makes a contradiction between them
detectable for the first time.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| **Operator** | Full | Declares each room's fixed spec on a **claimed** record (16.01.02 D-11): dimensions, ceiling **min and max** (D-03), surfaces, treatment **mechanism** from a closed list (D-05), and its **tracking capacity** (D-08). Declares the three pair facts against each other room: isolation, sightline, tie-line (D-06). Declares the room's **loud-hours** constraint where noise egress imposes one (D-10) — the residential studio's curfew. Writes one **prose** blurb that is never filtered, never scored, never fed to conformance (D-05). May attach an acoustician's **measurement document** as evidence, which the platform stores, displays and dates but never parses into a field (D-04). Unlike a venue Operator they are **selling**, not submitting to a form (DT-04) — but only because capture stays group-wise and phone-native (16.02.02 D-02), which is what licenses the richer spec (D-11). |
| **Producer** | Read-only + suggest | **The primary consumer of this entire sub-domain.** Filters on ceiling min, tracking capacity, treatment mechanism, and the pair facts — because sightlines and tie-lines decide whether the session can be **tracked live at all**, which decides what kind of record can be made. Reads the room and the chain (16.03.02) as one query; a match on one is not a match (16.03 CX-01). Post-session they are the highest-authority corrector — **except when the room is their own** (DT-15): source class comes from the relationship to the record, never from the persona, or a studio-owning Producer launders an owner claim into first-hand rank. |
| **Musician** | Read-only + suggest | Reads it when booking a session with no Producer. Cares about the practical facts over the vocabulary: **does the band fit** (tracking capacity — the one field here they can contest from experience, D-08), is there a piano (16.03.02), can we be loud on a Sunday (loud-hours, D-10). Post-session they are a first-hand source on tracking capacity and loud-hours specifically, because both are facts you learn by being in the room and cannot learn any other way. |
| **Fan** | **None** | Not visible. Studio-type records are not surfaced to Fans at all (16.01.01 Role Lens; 16.03 index Role Matrix). The absence is deliberate rather than an oversight: there is no Fan act here — no ticket, no route, no door. The one Fan-adjacent studio fact (a room's discography) belongs to 02 Credits, attached to the **record**, not to this spec. |

## Behavior

### What this feature carries, and what it refuses to carry

| Class | Carried here? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Physical substrate — dimensions, volume, ceiling min/max, surfaces | ✅ Field | Observable, photographable, contestable, non-decaying (D-01) |
| Treatment **mechanism** — what is physically in the room | ✅ Closed enum, multi-select | A gobo can be photographed; "dead" cannot (D-05) |
| Pair facts — isolation, sightline, tie-line | ✅ Field, per **ordered/unordered pair** | Decisive, structurable, published nowhere (D-06) |
| Tracking capacity — how many players fit | ✅ Declared integer + confidence via harvest | Declared, never derived from area (DT-11) |
| Loud-hours — the noise-egress constraint | ✅ Field | The residential studio's curfew; without it the calendar sells a 2am drum session in a terrace (D-10) |
| Acoustic **character** — "warm", "punchy", "great room" | ❌ Never a field | A judgement. Structuring it produces a column every studio fills with "excellent" (D-01, DT-01) |
| Acoustic **measurement** — RT60, impulse response | ❌ Never a field. ✅ Evidence attachment | No persona carries an instrument, so no persona can contest it — and this registry trusts nothing it cannot contest (D-04, DT-06) |
| Current gobo/panel position | ❌ Banned | Its true decay rate is hours; booking lead is days-to-weeks (D-07, DT-10) |
| Intra-room sightline ("can the drummer see the singer") | ❌ Banned as a field | A session-layout question, not a room fact — decays with the gobos (DT-02) |

### The room-pair is the unit (D-06)

Three facts have no meaning about one room and complete meaning about two:

| Pair fact | Symmetry | Values | Why it decides a booking |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Isolation** | Symmetric (transmission loss is reciprocal) | `none` · `partial` · `full` · `not stated` | Whether two sources can be tracked at once without bleed |
| **Sightline** | Symmetric | `direct` · `glass` · `camera` · `none` · `not stated` | Whether a band can **track live**. A drummer who cannot see the singer plays to a click. |
| **Tie-line** | Symmetric | `present (n lines)` · `none` · `not stated` | **A room with no tie-line to a control room cannot be tracked into, however it sounds.** |

`none` and `not stated` are different values, inherited from 16.02.02 D-04 — and a third, **`not
applicable`**, is added here (D-09): a one-room studio has no pairs, so the question does not arise.
Conflating "no isolation" with "no second room" is the specific defect that makes a project studio
present as an incomplete commercial one.

**The pair set is exactly 16.01.02's dependency graph, read physically.** That produces the domain's
first free integrity check: a room that `requires` a control room it declares **no tie-line** to is a
self-contradiction — the Operator has declared both that the rooms are one booking and that no signal
can pass between them. The platform **asks**; it never resolves, never infers, and never writes
(16.03 CX R-03 — inference manufactures claims the Operator never made).

### Ceiling height is a range, not a number (D-03)

Real live rooms have pitched roofs, trusses, and a low bit under the mezzanine. A single
`ceiling_height` field is answered with the **apex** — which is DT-03's dishonesty (leading with the
flattering number) reproduced by the schema that was supposed to fix it. So: **min and max, both
required, min blocking.** A drummer under the 2.4 m section does not benefit from the 6 m apex.

Absence is enforced, not merely displayed: a room with no ceiling min **drops out of ceiling-filtered
search** (16.05.05 D-03), so the Operator learns by losing drum sessions. That is the mechanism
behind D-03's "its absence is visible" — visibility to the *buyer* is not the lever; cost to the
*seller* is.

### Fixed ∪ mobile — this feature owns the fixed half only (16.01.02 D-14)

The ratified room model splits a spec into fixed (room-owned) and mobile (place-owned, allocated
per booking, contended). That line cuts two questions the breadth pass filed here, and it cuts them
cleanly:

| Breadth pass filed here | Correct owner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "monitoring for control rooms (mains, nearfields, treatment)" | **Split** (DT-13) | **Soffited mains are built into the wall — fixed, this feature.** Nearfields go where the engineer goes — mobile, 16.03.02. The wall is a room fact; the box on the meterbridge is inventory. |
| "headphone/cue provision (how many mixes)" | **Split** (DT-18) | **Wired cue stations are fixed — this feature declares the count.** The headphone amp determines the number of *discrete mixes* and is mobile — 16.03.02. |

### The measurement path — evidence, never a field (D-04)

A measurement document (an acoustician's commissioning report, an ISO 3382 RT measurement, an
ITU-R BS.1116 / EBU Tech 3276 control-room report, an impulse response) may be attached with
**acoustician name + measurement date + standard cited**. It is stored, displayed, dated, and
decayed like any evidence. It is **never** parsed into a field, **never** filterable, **never**
scored, and **never** consumed by conformance. Three reasons, in order of force (DT-06):

1. **Nobody can contest it.** Every field in this domain earns trust by being correctable — by the
   Operator, the community (16.05.03), or a band who was there (16.05.06). A measurement is
   contestable only by another measurement, and no persona carries an instrument. **A field this
   registry cannot police is a field this registry must not keep.**
2. **A single RT60 is band-limited and misleading in rooms this size.** Below the Schroeder
   frequency the field is modal, not diffuse, and RT60 is undefined there — for a ~200 m³ live room
   at ~0.6 s that boundary sits near **110 Hz**; for a ~60 m³ control room at ~0.3 s, near **140 Hz**.
   The number therefore describes the mid-band and hides the low end, which is where small rooms
   actually fail. Publishing it is DT-01's error wearing a number's clothes.
3. **Supply is self-selecting.** A studio that paid an acoustician publishes the report as
   marketing; a studio with a bad number publishes nothing; a project studio has never had one. So
   presence signals **budget**, absence signals nothing, and a filter on it filters for spend.

A useful side effect: because the attachment has no machine consequence, **forging one buys nothing
but prose**. The evidence-not-field decision removes the attack surface along with the field.

### Treatment — structure the mechanism, dissolve the vocabulary question (D-05)

Q-02 asked enum vs free text for treatment. Both answers are wrong because the question enumerates
the wrong thing. Character (`dead`/`live`) is a judgement (D-01); mechanism is an **object you can
photograph**. Changing the subject dissolves the fight:

| Lane | Form | Filterable? | Scored? | Fed to conformance? | Contestable by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | Closed multi-select: `broadband absorption` · `bass traps` · `diffusion` · `variable — gobos` · `variable — hinged/rotating panels` · `variable — drapes` · `tuned resonators` · `none (untreated)` | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | Anyone who stood in the room; a photo (16.01.03 D-02) |
| **Surfaces** | Closed list per floor / primary wall / ceiling: `wood` · `concrete` · `stone/brick` · `plasterboard` · `carpet` · `mixed` | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | A photo |
| **Prose** | One free-text blurb, ≤ the Operator's own words, attributed to them | ❌ Never | ❌ Never | ❌ Never | Nobody — it is marketing and is labelled as marketing |

The prose lane is not a concession; it is a **pressure valve**. Without it the Operator smuggles
their prose into the enum and the enum rots — which is the failure mode D-01 predicted, arriving
through the back door. `none (untreated)` is a first-class value: an untreated room is a fact, and
most of the supply.

**A variable room declares the mechanism and the range, never the current state** (D-07). "Gobos +
hinged panels, absorptive↔reflective" is durable. "Currently dead" is false by lunchtime.

### Tracking capacity — declared, never derived (D-08)

"Does a 5-piece fit?" is the Musician's question and the Overview's promise. It is **not** derivable
from floor area: a 40 m² live room with a grand piano in it has 40 m² and fits four people. Deriving
occupancy would manufacture a claim the Operator never made (16.03 CX R-03).

So the Operator **declares** `tracking_capacity` — players comfortably tracked at once — and it is
the **only acoustic-adjacent field in this feature a band can correct from experience** (DT-11).
"We were six and it was cramped" is a first-hand fact from a completed reservation, which is exactly
16.05.06's machinery. It is the field that connects this feature to the domain's power supply.

**It is not 16.02.01 capacity.** No audience, no configuration, no statutory ceiling (16.01.06), no
ticket consequence. Same word, different fact; merging them would import a licensed-capacity ceiling
into a room that has no licence.

### Applicability — why a project studio is small, not incomplete (D-09)

Q-03 asked whether the spec degrades gracefully or makes small studios look inadequate. It degrades
gracefully **iff the field set is scoped by room type and inapplicable fields are absent rather than
blank** — because a blank is an accusation and an absent field is a different kind of room.

| Studio shape | What is asked | What is never asked |
|---|---|---|
| One-room project studio (most of the supply) | Dimensions, ceiling min/max, surfaces, mechanism, tracking capacity, loud-hours, prose | **Every pair fact** — N=1 ⇒ zero pairs ⇒ `not applicable`, not `not stated`. The room layer itself is invisible (16.01.02 D-03), so the word "room" never appears. |
| Control room / mix suite | Treatment mechanism, surfaces, soffited mains | Tracking capacity (meaningless), ceiling-for-drums |
| Booth | Dimensions, isolation to the live room, sightline to the live room | Tracking capacity beyond 1–2 |
| Full commercial compound | Everything, per pair | — |

Two consequences follow and are binding: the completeness meter **names missing fields, never a
percentage** (inherited from 16.01.02); and **completeness must never be a search-ranking input**
(D-09), because completeness is a function of room type and studio scale, so ranking on it ranks a
bedroom below a commercial room for being a bedroom rather than for being wrong.

### Happy Path

1. Producer needs to track a 5-piece live. Filters: live room, **ceiling min ≥ 4 m**, tracking capacity ≥ 5, sightline `direct|glass` to a control room, tie-line `present`.
2. System returns rooms matching on **fixed fields whose freshness is inside their profile** (16.05.05 D-03). Rooms with no ceiling min are absent from the result — not ranked last, absent (D-03).
3. Producer opens Live Room A: ceiling **3.8 m min / 6.1 m apex**, surfaces `wood floor / plasterboard walls`, mechanism `broadband absorption + diffusion + variable — gobos`, tracking capacity **8**, and the pair table: *Control 1 — sightline `glass`, tie-line `present (24)`, isolation `full`*; *Booth 1 — sightline `glass`, tie-line `present (8)`, isolation `full`*.
4. Every field shows its **age and source class** (16.05.03 D-01/D-02). Ceiling: `owner, 2 years` — and unlike the gear list next door, that age is not a problem, because a ceiling does not decay (16.05.05 D-01). Tracking capacity: `owner 2y · confirmed first-hand ×3`.
5. Photos tagged to the fields they evidence (16.01.03 D-02) render inline: the gobos, the glass, the floor. **This is where "is it a good room?" gets answered** — by evidence, not by a field (D-01).
6. Producer reads the room and the chain as one decision (16.03 CX-01) and requests the room. The platform reads `Live Room A requires Control 1` and holds both atomically as one compound (16.01.02 D-06; 16.06.05 D-02).
7. Post-session, the harvest fires from the completed reservation (16.05.06 D-02) with **3–5 pre-filled questions** targeted at this feature's least-certain fields (16.05.06 D-03) — tracking capacity and the sightline, never "how did it sound" (16.05.06 D-01: facts, not opinions).
8. The Producer confirms the sightline and corrects tracking capacity 8 → 6. The field takes a **first-hand** source class and its freshness resets. The Operator is shown the correction and may contest it (16.05.04 D-01: last-agreed stands, contest visible).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| "Is it a good-sounding room?" | Unanswerable in structured fields, by design. Routed to photos (16.01.03), the record's discography (02) and the prose lane — none of which this feature owns and all of which it points at | Physical facts + evidence, with no field pretending to be a verdict |
| Live room and control room are **one room** (a project studio) | The overwhelmingly common case, not an edge. Room layer invisible (16.01.02 D-03); every pair fact is `not applicable`, not `not stated` (D-09); the meter never mentions them | One room. No pair table rendered at all |
| A one-room studio's spec is compared to a commercial compound's | Completeness is **type-scoped** and **never ranks** (D-09). The bedroom is complete when *its* fields are filled | "Complete" on both. Neither meter mentions the other's fields |
| Treatment is variable (gobos, hinged panels, drapes) | Mechanism + **range**; a current-state field is banned — it would decay in hours against a booking lead of days-to-weeks (D-07) | "Variable — gobos and hinged panels · absorptive↔reflective" |
| Operator wants to say the room is "warm and punchy" | Goes in the **prose** lane: attributed, unfiltered, unscored, never fed to conformance. Refused entry to the mechanism enum (D-05) | Marketing copy, visibly labelled as the Operator's words |
| Operator uploads an acoustician's RT60 report | Accepted as **evidence** — acoustician, date, standard. Stored, displayed, decayed. Never parsed, never filtered, never scored (D-04) | "Measurement report — Northward, March 2024 (ISO 3382-1)" · viewable |
| Someone forges a measurement report | **No machine consequence, so no attack.** It cannot move a filter, a score or a match. It is prose with a PDF's confidence, and the evidence-not-field rule is why (DT-06) | Same as any attachment |
| Producer asks to filter by RT60 | The filter does not exist. Not hidden, not "coming soon" — the reason is stated once, in place | "We don't publish acoustic measurements as a spec — no two studios measure the same room the same way, and nobody in this system can check it. Here are the physical facts and the photos." |
| **Mix booked in the control room while the live room sits empty** | **Must not block.** `requires` is **directional and derived** (16.01.02 D-06/D-07): Live A requires Control 1; Control 1 requires nothing. The breadth pass's "the control room is usually exclusive to its live room" was the pairwise model 16.01.02 DT-05 rejected — it silently deletes the mix-suite revenue stream | Control 1 sells. Live A stays available |
| Two live rooms share one control room | Exclusion is **derived**, never declared: both `require` Control 1 ⇒ booking either excludes the other. This feature's pair table is where a Producer *sees why* | "Live Room B unavailable — Control 1 is in use with Live Room A" |
| Room declares `requires Control 1` **and** tie-line `none` to Control 1 | A self-contradiction: one booking, no signal path. Platform **asks**, never resolves — inference would manufacture a claim (16.03 CX R-03). Neither field is blocked or auto-corrected | "Live Room A needs Control 1 to run, but you've said there are no tie-lines between them. Which is right?" |
| Sightline claimed `direct`; the Producer who tracked there says the glass is behind the drummer | A contested **physical pair fact observed in use**. Proposed policy: **first-hand wins**, matching accessibility (16.05.04 D-03) — the Operator has both an incentive and a blind spot, and the cost of the error lands on a band who cannot track. **Proposed, not ratified — Q-01** | Last-agreed value + contested marker until resolved (16.05.04 D-01) |
| Two Producers post-session give different tracking capacities (6 vs 8) | Both are first-hand and both are right — they brought different bands. Last-agreed stands, contest visible (16.05.04 D-01); the field carries the count of confirmations, not a winner | "Owner says 8 · reported 6 (×1), 8 (×2)" |
| **Multi-hyphenate: a Producer who owns the studio "confirms" their own room's spec** | **Source class comes from the relationship to the record, not the persona** (DT-15). Their attestation is **owner**-class (16.05.03 D-02), not first-hand — otherwise a studio-owning Producer launders an owner claim into a higher rank using credentials the ranking never meant to price | Their input recorded as the owner's, not as a report |
| Multi-hyphenate: an Operator books their own room for their own band | A **block**, not a booking (16.01.02) ⇒ no reservation ⇒ no proof of presence ⇒ **no harvest**. So the rooms whose acoustics are best known to anyone produce the **least** contestable data. A second instance of domain Q-10 | Calendar block. No post-session prompt |
| Studio sells Live A and Live B to **two different clients at once** | Only sellable if the A↔B isolation supports it, and that is an Operator judgement, not a derivation. Modelled as `contends (facility: acoustic isolation)` (16.01.02 D-06) — both sell, the second is shown the rule and acknowledges, both are notified. **Bleed is a shared *medium*, not a shared facility, and 16.01.02 exemplified only facilities — Q-02** | "Live Room B is tracking 2–8pm. Loud tracking in Live Room A overlaps." |
| Isolation claimed `full`; it leaks at 80 Hz | Contestable by the Producer who tracked through it — the exact case first-hand correction exists for. **Not** contestable by a measurement, because measurements are evidence and evidence does not overwrite fields (D-04) | Contested marker; the leak described in the report, not scored |
| **Residential/home studio: a band books 11pm drums** | The room declares **loud-hours** (D-10); the calendar refuses the slot for loud sessions and offers it for quiet ones. Without this field the platform sells a session that ends with the neighbours and the police — and the Operator's switching trigger fires *against* us | "Loud tracking here runs 09:00–20:00 (residential). Overdubs and mixing, any hour." |
| A studio under a flight path / next to a railway | **Noise *ingress*** — the other half of external isolation (D-10). Declared, contestable by anyone who tracked there, and one of the few things a band learns instantly and an Operator has stopped hearing | "External noise: intermittent (rail, ~every 8 min)" · reported first-hand ×2 |
| Home/project studio at a residential address | Address precision reduced (16.01.01) — and this is **most of the market's supply**, not an edge case | Area-level until booked |
| Studio is refitted / re-treated | A **refit**, not lineage: same physical volume, same room, history follows (16.01.02 D-08). Freshness resets on the **affected fields only** — treatment and surfaces, not dimensions (16.05.05) | "Spec updated — still the same room" · treatment age resets, ceiling age does not |
| Studio **knocks through** (two rooms → one) | A **merge**: both rooms `Superseded`, one successor with `formed-from` lineage. **Every pair fact involving either room dies with them** — they describe a boundary that no longer exists. History is never re-attributed (16.01.02 D-08); copying an isolation claim onto a room with no wall would manufacture provenance | "This ends Live Room A and B as spaces. Their sessions and reports stay on their records." |
| Operator declares a subdivision as a "refit" to keep the reviews | Lineage declaration is a **contestable suggested edit**, not a unilateral write (16.01.02 D-08) — the Operator's incentive is skewed. This feature supplies the tell: **a subdivision creates pair facts where there were none.** A "refit" that adds a wall is visible in this feature's own data | Suggested edit, queued and contestable |
| Ceiling declared as one number (6 m) with a pitched roof | Min is **blocking**; the form cannot be completed with apex alone. Without this, D-03 hands the flattering number a field of its own (DT-03) | "What's the height at the lowest usable point?" — required |
| Ceiling min omitted entirely | The room **drops out of ceiling-filtered search** (16.05.05 D-03) — absent, not ranked last. The Operator loses drum enquiries and is told why. That is the enforcement of D-03 | "Rooms without a stated ceiling height don't appear in this search. Add yours." |
| Operator fills a long studio spec on a phone, loses signal mid-form | Fields commit **individually** — provenance is per-field (16.05.03 D-01), so capture is group-wise and phone-native (16.02.02 D-02). The in-flight field is lost; the form is not. **This is why D-11 can license a richer spec**: richness is safe only because the form was already chunked | Completed groups persist; one group shows retry |
| Malicious edit on an **unclaimed** studio record ("ceiling: 2.1 m") | Enters the suggested-edit queue (16.05.03); the room is permanently `Draft` and **never bookable** (16.01.02 D-11). Blast radius bounded to spec pollution and search dilution — never a fake sale | Pending suggestion, not inventory |
| Room spec exists but the record is unclaimed | Describable, permanently `Draft`. **A studio's spec with no claim is the cold-start norm** — and studio supply has no public-data seeding story at all (domain Q-07), so this state is thinner here than anywhere else in domain 16 | "Do you run this studio? Claim it to make it bookable" |
| Two Operator sessions edit the same pair fact concurrently | Last write wins on **independent** fields. On the **same pair edge**, the second write is rejected with the current value shown — a silently merged pair table feeds a graph 16.01.02 derives exclusion from, and a wrong derivation is a double-booking | "This changed while you were editing — here's what it says now" |
| A pair fact is asked for a pair that spans two **places** (a studio subleasing a venue's upstairs room) | Not asked. Pair facts are scoped **within one place record**; the room the studio operates (granted under 16.01.02 D-09, or room-claimed under D-15 — the scoping is the same either way) belongs to the venue's record and has no pairs with the studio's own rooms. Its own record's pairs are the record claimant's to declare | Pair table lists only same-record rooms |
| Producer filters on a pair fact and the pair table fails to load | **Fails closed**: pair-dependent results are withheld, not returned unfiltered. A room returned against an unloaded pair table is a room sold on a fact nobody checked | "Sightline and isolation filters are unavailable right now — showing nothing rather than something wrong" |
| Room has full spec, zero photos | Physical facts stand; the **shot list for the room's type** (16.01.03 D-01) names what is missing. DT-01's whole point is that the photos are load-bearing here in a way they are not for a venue's PA spec | "No photos of the live room yet" — the one gap the fields genuinely cannot cover |
| The Producer asks "can the drummer see the singer?" | **Not a field.** Intra-room sightline is a function of the gobo layout on the day — it decays in hours (DT-02, D-07). Answered by the photos, by the tracking capacity, and by the mechanism list | The inter-room pair table + photos. No field claims to answer it |

### States

**Field lifecycle** — per field, never per room (16.05.05 D-01)

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Not applicable** | The field does not arise for this room's type or shape — every pair fact at N=1 (D-09) | **Nothing.** The field is absent, not blank. A blank is an accusation |
| **Not stated** | Applicable, never declared | Named in the meter; the room **drops out of confident filtering** on that field (16.05.05 D-03) |
| **None** | Declared as absent — `none (untreated)`, isolation `none` | A fact, and a common one. Distinct from Not stated (16.02.02 D-04) |
| **Declared** | Operator filled it | Value + age + source class (`owner, 8 months`) |
| **Evidenced** | Declared + a photo tagged to that field (16.01.03 D-02), or a measurement attachment (D-04) | Value with its evidence inline |
| **Confirmed** | A first-hand source agreed post-session (16.05.06) | Value + `confirmed first-hand ×n` — the strongest state this feature can reach |
| **Contested** | A first-hand report contradicts the declared value | **Last-agreed value + contested marker** (16.05.04 D-01). The false-positive cost on an honest Operator is stated, not hidden (16.05.04 D-05) |
| **Stale** | Decayed past its field-class profile — treatment after a refit; loud-hours after a tenancy change. **Dimensions and ceiling never reach this state** | Value + age, visible; out of confident filtering, never hidden (16.05.05 D-02/D-03) |
| **Dead** | The room was `Superseded` (subdivide/merge) | Read-only on the superseded room. **Pair facts die with the boundary they describe** and are never copied to successors (16.01.02 D-08) |

**View states — Producer reading a room**

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Room spec fetched | Skeleton field groups; the **pair table renders last** — it needs sibling rooms |
| Empty | Claimed studio, default room, no spec declared | Not "no data". The room's **type-scoped** field list as a prompt: "Live room — add ceiling height and tracking capacity to appear in Producer searches" |
| Populated | Spec declared | Grouped fields with per-field age and source class; pair table; tagged photos inline; prose blurb visibly separated and labelled as the Operator's |
| Partial | Room fields load; the **pair table** does not | Room fields shown. **Pair-dependent filters and any `requires`-driven booking action disabled, not silently permitted** — an unknown graph must fail closed (16.01.02 Partial state) |

Loud-hours is the studio's curfew and is **structurally identical minus the statute**: its D-01 gives venue curfew licence-derived provenance that neither the Operator nor the community may waive; a residential studio's loud-hours has **no authority behind it** — it is a neighbour, a lease, or a marriage — so it is owner-declared, contestable, and decays. Same shape, different provenance class, and 16.05.03 D-02's ranking is what tells them apart. The **load-in** half of the same generalisation (a first-floor project studio, no lift, a Hammond) is currently nobody's.
- Touches **16.02.01 Capacity & Configuration Model** ([../16.02-venue-technical-specification/16.02.01-capacity-configuration-model.md](../16.02-venue-technical-specification/16.02.01-capacity-configuration-model.md)) — *rejected merge, recorded so it is not rediscovered*. `tracking_capacity` (D-08) is **not** 16.02.01 capacity: no audience, no configuration, no statutory ceiling (16.01.06), no ticket consequence, and it is contested by the band that used it rather than by a licensing authority. Same word, different fact; merging them would import a licensed-capacity ceiling into a room that has no licence.
- Touches **16.02.02 Stage, PA & Backline Spec** ([../16.02-venue-technical-specification/16.02.02-stage-pa-backline-spec.md](../16.02-venue-technical-specification/16.02.02-stage-pa-backline-spec.md)) — *shared pattern*. Its **D-04 (`none` ≠ `not stated`) is inherited and extended**: this feature needs a third value, `not applicable` (D-09), because a one-room studio has no pairs and neither existing value can say so without lying or accusing. Its **D-02 (group-wise, phone-native capture) is the precondition for D-11** — the studio spec may be richer only because the form was already chunked. Its Q-01 (enum vs free text) is **answered here for treatment specifically** (DT-07: enumerate the mechanism, not the character) — the move may not transfer to a PA spec, but the method does.
- Touches **16.03.03 Engineer Staffing Model** ([./16.03.03-engineer-staffing-model.md](./16.03.03-engineer-staffing-model.md)) — *shared entity* (16.03 CX-03, CX-05). Staffing is declared **per-room** (its D-03) — a mix suite is dry-hire-only while the live room never is — which is the same **type-scoped applicability** this feature's D-09 applies to fields. Two independent constraints on one compound: this feature's `requires` pairs and the engineer's calendar. 16.06.05 D-05 says compose around the person; the room is rarely the binding constraint.
- Touches **16.01.01 Place Record & Type Taxonomy** ([../16.01-place-records-rooms/16.01.01-place-record-type-taxonomy.md](../16.01-place-records-rooms/16.01.01-place-record-type-taxonomy.md)) — *permission + shared entity*: address precision is reduced for residential records, and **most studio supply is residential** — which makes D-10 (loud-hours) a majority-case field, not an edge case. Its Role Lens is the authority for this feature's Fan `None`.
- Consumed by **07 Music Projects & Collaboration** ([../../07-music-projects-collaboration/](../../07-music-projects-collaboration/)) — *shared entity*: a session in a room is a project event, and per 16.01.02 the **room** is the resolution at which "where the work happened" stops being an address and becomes provenance (D-18). This feature is what makes that room worth naming: "Live Room A" is a fact about a volume with a ceiling, a floor and a tie-line count. When that room is `Superseded`, the credit points at a volume that no longer exists — which is provenance working correctly, not a broken link.
- Consumed by **02 Credits & Provenance** ([../../02-credits-provenance/](../../02-credits-provenance/)) — *shared entity*: "recorded at" is a credit field, and a room's discography is the closest thing to an answer to the question D-01 refuses (DT-01 — "is it a good room?"). **The discography belongs to 02, attached to the record; this feature must not grow a `notable_sessions` field** — it would be an unverifiable claim in a registry that trusts only what it can contest (DT-06's test, applied to a different temptation).
- Touches **15 Gear Registry & Ownership** ([../../15-gear-registry-ownership/](../../15-gear-registry-ownership/)) — *boundary only, no claim*. The fixed∪mobile line (16.01.02 D-14) means **nothing this feature owns is gear** — a wall, a hole in a wall, and a soffit are not registrable assets. The mobile half's ownership fork (16.03.02 Q-01 / 16.01.02 Q-05 / 16.06.05 Q-02) does not touch this feature, and recording that explicitly is the point: **16.03.01 is the one feature in this sub-domain the 15 cross-check cannot move.**

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Structure the physical substrate; **refuse to structure the acoustic judgement** | "Warm" is not a field. Faking it produces a column every studio fills with "excellent" (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Sightlines are a first-class, machine-checkable field | They decide whether a band can track live — decisive, structurable, and published nowhere (DT-02). **Refined by D-06**: inter-room only; intra-room sightline is a session-layout question, not a room fact |
| D-03 | Ceiling height is first-class, declared as **min and max with min blocking**, and its absence **removes the room from ceiling-filtered search** | Dominant for drums; routinely omitted because floor area flatters. A single field is answered with the apex, reproducing the dishonesty it was meant to fix. Absence must cost the seller, not merely inform the buyer (DT-03; 16.05.05 D-03) |
| D-04 | Acoustic **measurements are evidence, never fields** — attributed, dated, displayed, never parsed, filtered or scored | No persona carries an instrument, so no persona can contest one, and this registry trusts nothing it cannot police. RT60 is also undefined below the Schroeder frequency (~110 Hz for a typical live room), so the number hides where small rooms fail. Answers Q-01 (DT-06) |
| D-05 | **Structure the treatment mechanism and the surfaces; never the character.** One clearly-labelled prose lane carries the character, unfiltered, unscored, never fed to conformance | Answers Q-02 by changing the noun: a gobo can be photographed, "dead" cannot. The prose lane is a pressure valve — without a legitimate home, the Operator's prose migrates into the enum and rots it (DT-07) |
| D-06 | **Isolation, sightline and tie-line are room-*pair* facts**, declared per pair, over exactly the pairs of 16.01.02's `requires` graph. A `requires` edge with no tie-line is surfaced as a **question**, never resolved or inferred | They are meaningless about one room. The pair set makes the physical graph the twin of the commercial one, which makes a self-contradiction detectable for free — and inference would manufacture claims (DT-09; 16.03 CX R-03) |
| D-07 | A variable room declares **mechanism + range, never current state**; a field whose true decay rate is shorter than the booking lead time is not a field | Gobo position decays in hours against a lead time of days-to-weeks. Decay cannot save it: an age on a value that is always wrong is worse than no value (DT-10; 16.05.05 D-01) |
| D-08 | **`tracking_capacity` is declared, never derived from area** — and it is this feature's only field a band can correct from experience | A 40 m² room with a grand piano fits four. Derivation manufactures a claim and would be indistinguishable from a declaration. It is the feature's only wire into the harvest (DT-11; 16.05.06) |
| D-09 | The field set is **scoped by room type**; inapplicable fields are **absent, not blank** (`not applicable` — a third value beyond `none`/`not stated`); the meter names gaps, never a percentage; **completeness never ranks** | Answers Q-03. A one-room project studio has zero pairs, so the pair questions do not arise. A blank is an accusation; ranking on completeness ranks a bedroom below a commercial room for being a bedroom, in a registry whose value is coverage (DT-14; 16.02.02 D-04; 16.01.02 D-03) |
| D-10 | **External isolation is a field, in both directions**: noise ingress (declared, first-hand-contestable) and **loud-hours** (the residential studio's curfew) | Most studio supply is residential. Without loud-hours the calendar sells a 2am drum session in a terrace and triggers the Operator's switching trigger against our own supply. Unlike a venue curfew it is **not** licence-derived, so it is owner-class and contestable (DT-12; 16.02.03 D-01) |
| D-11 | **The studio spec may be richer than the venue spec — and only because capture is already group-wise** | The studio's room is its product; the Operator is selling, not submitting (DT-04). But richness delivered as one long form would consume exactly that goodwill. 16.02.02 D-02 is the precondition, not a nicety |
| D-12 | **Source class is determined by the relationship to the record, not by the persona** | A studio-owning Producer confirming their own room enters at `owner`, not `first-hand`. Otherwise the multi-hyphenate launders an owner claim into a higher trust class using credentials the ranking never meant to price (DT-15; 16.05.03 D-02) |
| D-13 | **Fixed∪mobile cuts the monitoring and cue questions**: soffited mains and wired cue stations are this feature's; nearfields, headphone amps and mix counts are 16.03.02's | 16.01.02 D-14 already decides it — no new principle needed. A room-owned spec is false the moment the box is next door (DT-13, DT-18) |
| D-14 | **Tie-lines are this feature's, not 16.03.02's** — the room owns the path, the inventory owns what plugs into it | 16.01.02 D-14's fixed table already places them on the room. A tie-line is a hole in a wall: unmovable, non-decaying, and a property of a **pair** — which no inventory model can express. A live room with no tie-line is a rehearsal room (DT-08) |
