# Feature: Hospitality, Green Room & Backstage

> **Number**: 16.02.04
> **Parent**: [Venue Technical Specification](./16.02-venue-technical-specification-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

What the room provides to the people playing it: green room (or the absence of one — many rooms
have a corridor), backstage facilities, toilets and showers, food and drink provision, guest list
allowance, wifi, and a place to leave a laptop that isn't the stage.

This is the smallest-looking part of the venue spec and the one most likely to be dismissed as
trivia. It is also the half of the rider that gets mocked in the press and the half that
determines whether a touring band on their fifth night has anywhere to sit. Structurally, it is
the **standing baseline** that a show-specific rider (18) is negotiated against.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Operator | Config | Declares the standing hospitality baseline — what every act gets without asking. Sees rider requests that exceed the baseline surfaced at advance rather than sprung on the day. |
| Musician | Read-only + suggest | Reads it when deciding on a show and when writing a rider — knowing the baseline means the rider only asks for the delta. Post-gig, corrects it (the "green room" that is a stairwell). |
| Producer | Read-only | Low relevance for venues; matters when running a live recording with a crew who need somewhere to sit for six hours. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Backstage facts are not for the audience, and publishing guest-list allowances would create pressure the Operator does not want. |

## Behavior

Structured fields: green room (present / shared / none, capacity, lockable), backstage toilets and
showers, catering provision (none / buyout / hot meal / rider allowance), drinks allowance, guest
list allowance (number, and whether it comes off the settlement), wifi, secure storage, towels/
water baseline, smoking area, production office.

The important framing is that this is a **baseline, not a rider**. The rider belongs to the band
and lives in 18. This feature answers "what does this room provide as standard", which is what a
rider is written *against*. Conflating them puts a band's requirements into the venue's record —
wrong owner, wrong lifecycle.

### Happy Path

1. Operator declares the baseline: green room (shared, 8 people, lockable), 2 hot meals, £50 drinks, 4 guest list
2. Band reads the baseline before agreeing to the show — no email exchange
3. Band's rider (18) asks for 6 meals and 6 guest list — a delta of +4 meals, +2 guests
4. Conformance check (16.07) surfaces the delta at advance, not on the day
5. The delta is negotiated once, in the advance, with both baselines visible

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| There is no green room (very common in small rooms) | "None" is a first-class value and a useful one — a band with a 3-hour gap between soundcheck and doors needs to know | Explicit "No green room" |
| Green room is shared with the support act | Structured field — shared vs exclusive changes the answer materially | Shared, capacity 8 |
| Guest list allowance comes off the settlement | This is a **commercial** fact hiding in a hospitality field; belongs with the deal model (16.02.05) | `[PENDING — Step 5]` |
| Baseline varies by deal size (headline gets the room, support gets the corridor) | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — the model assumes one baseline per room; in practice it varies by billing | — |
| Rider asks for something the room physically cannot provide (a shower that doesn't exist) | Conformance check (16.07) flags it at advance | Conflict surfaced pre-show |
| Operator declares a generous baseline they don't honour | Post-gig harvest (16.05.06) is the only corrective; this field has no other check | Community correction |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Unstated | Nothing declared | Gaps; band must ask by email — the failure this feature removes |
| Declared | Operator filled the baseline | Structured baseline + last-verified age |
| Contested | Post-gig contradiction | Last-agreed value + contested marker |
| Stale | Decayed | Baseline with visible age |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Hospitality is trivia and belongs in a free-text notes field | It is the part of the rider that gets mocked; it looks like the least technical thing in the spec | ❌ REJECTED: it is a structured, negotiated, money-bearing part of every show. A buyout is cash, guest list often comes off settlement, and "is there a green room" determines whether a touring band spends three hours in a van. It is not technically complex, which is different from being unimportant — and structuring it is what lets 16.07 catch a rider/room mismatch before the day. |
| DT-02 | The venue's hospitality baseline and the band's rider are the same kind of thing | Both describe hospitality; both look like lists of requirements | ❌ REJECTED, and the distinction is load-bearing. The baseline is a **property of the room**, owned by the Operator, long-lived, decaying. The rider is a **property of the act**, owned by the band, per-tour, and belongs in 18. Merging them puts the band's requirements into the venue's record — wrong owner, wrong lifecycle, and it breaks the conformance check, which needs two independent sides to compare. |
| DT-03 | Guest list belongs here | It appears on every rider next to the catering | ⚠️ PARTIALLY REJECTED: guest list is a **commercial** term (it usually comes off the settlement and it interacts with 19 Ticketing's allocation) dressed as hospitality. Flagged to move to 16.02.05, and flagged as a 19 touchpoint — comped tickets are ticket stock. Left here for this pass with the ambiguity recorded rather than silently resolved. |
| DT-04 | One baseline per room | Consistent with the rest of the spec | ❌ REJECTED as universally true: the headline act gets the green room, the support gets the corridor. Billing-dependent hospitality is normal. Unmodelled — Q-02. Same shape as 16.02.03 DT-04 (day-varying facts) and 16.02.01 Q-02 (production-dependent capacity): **the venue spec is not actually a set of constants, and three features have now independently hit that wall.** |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The baseline is a **property of the room**; the rider is a property of the act (18) | Merging them breaks ownership, lifecycle, and the conformance check that needs two independent sides (DT-02) |
| D-02 | "None" is a first-class value | "No green room" is one of the most useful facts a small room can state |
| D-03 | Hospitality is structured, not free text | It is money-bearing and checkable; simplicity is not triviality (DT-01) |
| D-04 | Guest list flagged as commercial, not hospitality — resolution deferred rather than silently made | It comes off settlement and it is ticket stock (DT-03) |
