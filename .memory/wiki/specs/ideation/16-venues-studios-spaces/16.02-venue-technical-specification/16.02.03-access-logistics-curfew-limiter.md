# Feature: Access & Logistics (Load-In, Parking, Curfew, Sound Limiter)

> **Number**: 16.02.03
> **Parent**: [Venue Technical Specification](./16.02-venue-technical-specification-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The physical and temporal facts of getting a show into a room and out again: load-in route (door
width, steps, lift, distance from the vehicle), parking and unloading restrictions, get-in and
soundcheck times, curfew, and the **sound limiter** — the device that cuts the power when the room
gets too loud and that ends more shows than any other single piece of equipment in the UK.

These are the facts a tour manager needs and cannot get, and several of them are not the Operator's
opinion at all: curfew and decibel limits are usually **licence conditions** (16.01.06), legally
imposed and not negotiable at any price.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Operator | Config | Declares the route, restrictions and times. Cannot override licence-derived fields (curfew, dB limit) — they are read-only here and edited via the licence record (16.01.06). |
| Musician | Read-only + suggest | Reads load-in and parking before agreeing to a show — a 40-metre carry up two flights changes whether the fee is worth it. Post-gig, the highest-authority corrector of the load-in claim (they carried the gear). |
| Producer | Read-only | Relevant when recording live or bringing a mobile rig — where does the truck go, is there power, what time can we get in. |
| Fan | Read-only (partial) | Sees only the audience-facing slice: doors time, curfew as "show ends by", and public parking/transport. Never sees the load-in, the limiter, or the get-in schedule. |

## Behavior

Structured groups:

**Load-in**: route description, door dimensions, threshold/lip, steps count, lift (and its
dimensions — a lift that won't take a bass cab is not a lift), distance from the unloading point,
whether there is a dock or a street pull-up.

**Parking / vehicle**: unloading restrictions and times, permit requirements, van/truck size limit,
overnight options, the local enforcement reality (a loading bay with a 20-minute limit and an
enthusiastic warden is a different fact from a dock).

**Times**: get-in, soundcheck window, doors, curfew, hard-out/tear-down deadline.

**Sound limiter**: presence, threshold in dB, where it is measured, what it does when tripped (cuts
power / warns / trips the breaker), and whether it is licence-mandated or a landlord's device.

The limiter and curfew fields are **derived from the licence record where one exists**, carrying
statutory provenance (16.01.06 D-02). This matters practically: a promoter negotiating a midnight
finish needs to know instantly whether they are negotiating with a person or with a licensing
authority.

### Happy Path

1. Tour manager (a Musician) is offered a show and opens the room's access spec
2. Reads: street pull-up, 20-min loading bay, 3 steps, no lift, 30m carry, curfew 23:00 (licence)
3. Decides the fee does not cover the carry, or brings an extra pair of hands — either way, they knew
4. At advance (18), none of these questions are asked by email; they are already answered
5. Post-gig, the band confirms the carry distance was accurate (16.05.06), resetting the field's freshness

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Curfew is a licence condition, promoter wants to negotiate | Field is read-only with statutory provenance — the answer is no, and the reason is visible | "Curfew 23:00 — licence condition, not negotiable" |
| Limiter exists but the threshold is unknown (very common) | "Limiter present, threshold not stated" — presence and threshold are separate fields | Partial state, explicitly |
| Load-in claim contradicted post-gig ("step-free" vs 3 steps) | Contradiction flag (16.05.04); overlaps 16.01.04's performer accessibility route — the same physical fact | Contested marker |
| Times vary by day of week (curfew 23:00 midweek, 00:00 Fri/Sat) | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — the model assumes one value per field; day-varying times are the norm, not an exception | — |
| Loading restrictions vary by time of day (bay is a red route until 10:00) | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — same shape as the above; time-varying facts are unmodelled | — |
| Limiter is a landlord's device, not licence-mandated | Provenance differs (Operator claim vs statutory) but the practical effect is identical | Both shown with source |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Curfew is an Operator preference like any other spec field | It reads like an operating choice and sits among operating choices | ❌ REJECTED: curfew and dB limits are typically **licence conditions** — imposed by a licensing authority, legally binding, not the Operator's to waive. Modelling them as preferences invites a promoter to negotiate something that is not available at any price, and lets an Operator "agree" to a curfew extension they cannot lawfully grant. Provenance is the feature. |
| DT-02 | The sound limiter is a minor spec detail | It is one field among dozens | ❌ REJECTED: it is one of the highest-consequence facts about a UK room. A limiter set at 95dB will not run a rock show — the power cuts mid-set. Bands discover this at soundcheck, having already loaded in and travelled. It deserves its own structured group (presence / threshold / measurement point / trip behaviour), not a line in a notes field. |
| DT-03 | Load-in facts duplicate the performer accessibility profile (16.01.04) | Both describe steps, thresholds, door widths and distances | ⚠️ PARTIALLY CONFIRMED — a real overlap, deliberately kept. They are the **same physical fact serving two questions**: "can we get the gear in?" and "can I get in?". The answers diverge (a 30m carry is a nuisance to one band and a hard blocker to a wheelchair user). Resolution: shared underlying facts, two presentations. Flagged as Q-01 because a duplicated source of truth is a decay bug waiting to happen — two fields that can disagree about one staircase. |
| DT-04 | One value per field is sufficient | Simplest model | ❌ REJECTED: curfew varies by night, loading bays vary by hour, get-in varies by whether there is a matinee. Day/time-varying values are the norm in live music, and a single-value model forces the Operator to state the most restrictive case (losing them bookings) or the most permissive (causing failures). Unresolved — Q-02. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Curfew and dB limits are licence-derived with statutory provenance where a licence exists | Not the Operator's to waive; a promoter must know they are negotiating with an authority (DT-01) |
| D-02 | The sound limiter gets its own structured group | It ends shows; a notes-field mention is not proportionate to its consequence (DT-02) |
| D-03 | Load-in facts and the performer accessibility route share underlying facts, presented twice | Same staircase, two questions with different answers (DT-03) |
| D-04 | "Limiter present" and "threshold known" are separate fields | Presence is common knowledge; the threshold usually is not |
