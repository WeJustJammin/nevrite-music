# Feature: Stage, PA & Backline Spec

> **Number**: 16.02.02
> **Parent**: [Venue Technical Specification](./16.02-venue-technical-specification-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The structured technical truth of a performance room: stage dimensions and height, PA make/model
and coverage, FOH desk and position, monitor provision (wedges/IEM, how many mixes), mic and DI
stock, power (how many circuits, where, what type), and house backline if any.

This is the domain's flagship asset and the reason it exists. **Every advance in live music
re-asks questions the venue has answered a thousand times** — how big is the stage, how many
monitor mixes, is there a house drum kit — over email, per show, forever. Structured once, this
becomes reusable, comparable, and (uniquely) machine-checkable against a rider (16.07).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Operator | Full | Fills the spec once instead of answering it per email. Sees which unfilled fields cost them enquiries. Updates when the PA changes — the update is what the whole freshness machinery (16.05.05) exists to chase. |
| Musician | Read-only + suggest | Reads it to decide if the room can run their show, and to know what to bring. **Post-gig, they are the highest-value corrector** (16.05.06) — they just spent a night discovering the spec was wrong. |
| Producer | Read-only + suggest | Reads it when producing or recording a live show — what desk, can it split, is there a multitrack out. A live recording is a session, which makes this a provenance surface too. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. A Fan will never read a monitor spec; surfacing it is noise on a show page. |

## Behavior

The spec is **structured fields, not a PDF**. That distinction is the entire feature: the industry
already has tech specs — as PDFs on venue websites, unparseable, undated, and unqueryable. A
structured spec supports search ("rooms with 6 monitor mixes"), conflict detection against a rider
(16.07), field-level provenance and per-field decay (16.05).

Field groups: stage (width × depth × height, load capacity, riser availability), PA (make/model,
system type, coverage notes, subs, whether it is house-run or hireable), FOH (desk make/model,
position, number of channels, whether it is digital and what it can recall), monitors (wedge count,
mix count, IEM provision, who mixes), mic/DI stock, power, house backline (kit, cabs, keys —
usually the most contested field in the spec).

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`: the field list above is a first cut informed by
what an advance actually asks for. It has not been validated against a rider taxonomy, and the
degree of structure — enum vs free text per field — is the difference between a checkable spec and
a prettier PDF. That is the single most consequential unresolved question in this domain.

### Happy Path

1. Operator claims a venue; the spec is empty and the room is unsearchable on technical filters
2. Operator fills the stage group on their phone (5 fields), then the PA group from the amp rack label
3. Each field is timestamped and provenance-tagged; a photo (16.01.03) can evidence any of them
4. Musician filters for "stage ≥ 5m wide, 4+ monitor mixes"; the room now appears
5. At advance time (18), the band's rider is checked against this spec (16.07) instead of an email thread

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Venue has an existing tech spec PDF | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — ingesting it is the obvious onboarding path and the obvious trap: parsing a PDF into structured fields with unverifiable accuracy imports a claim while looking like a fact | — |
| PA is house-run only vs hireable vs bring-your-own | Structured field — this is a commercial fact wearing a technical costume, and it feeds the rate card (16.06.07) via extras | Provision model shown with the spec |
| House backline exists but is unreliable / half-broken | The field that decays fastest and the one bands complain about most. Post-gig harvest (16.05.06) is the only proposed corrective | Backline field with freshness age + community notes |
| Spec is filled once and never touched again | Decay (16.05.05) drops confidence; the spec presents as aged rather than current | "PA last verified 2 years ago" |
| Two rooms share a PA | Room exclusion (16.01.02 Q-01) — currently binary, but "co-bookable but degraded" is exactly this case | — |
| Field genuinely does not apply (no monitors at all) | "None" must be distinguishable from "unstated" — a room with no monitors is a fact, not a gap | Explicit "None" vs "Not stated" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty | Nothing filled | Room invisible to technical search; Operator sees lost-enquiry nudge |
| Partial | Some groups filled | Spec + explicit gaps ("monitors: not stated") |
| Complete | All groups filled | Full spec + completeness badge; eligible for conformance checking (16.07) |
| Stale | Decayed past threshold | Spec with per-field "last verified" ages; drops out of confident matching |
| Contested | Post-gig contradiction | Last-agreed value + contested marker (16.05.04) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A tech spec is a document | It is a document today, universally — every venue has a tech spec PDF | ❌ REJECTED, and this is the domain's central bet. A PDF cannot be searched, filtered, diffed against a rider, dated per-field, or corrected by the band who just played there. The entire unbuilt asset is the **structuring**, not the collection. If this feature ships as document upload, the domain has built nothing new. |
| DT-02 | Operators will fill a long structured form | They want bookings; the form is the price | ❌ REJECTED as stated: the Operator persona works "on a phone at a loading dock, not at a desk". A 60-field form is a wall. Mitigations that must be designed in, not bolted on: field **groups** completable independently, phone-native capture, photo-as-evidence, and — the strongest — 16.05.06's post-gig harvest, which extracts spec truth from the *band* rather than the Operator. The lazy path must be the correct path. |
| DT-03 | The band is a better source than the Operator for some fields | Operator anti-persona: "publishes a stale gear list, so a band arrives to a room that can't run their show" | ✅ CONFIRMED: for house backline and monitor reality especially, the band has just tested the claim empirically and has no incentive to flatter. This inverts the naive write model — the room's owner is not the best witness to the room. It is also what makes 16.05.06 a spec feature rather than a nicety. |
| DT-04 | Structure means enums everywhere | Enums are what make it checkable | ⚠️ UNRESOLVED — the sharpest open question here. Enums make conformance checking (16.07) possible but cannot express reality ("4 wedges but one is dead", "PA is fine for a 3-piece, not for a metal band"). Free text is honest and uncheckable. The likely answer is enum + structured caveat, mirroring 16.01.04 D-02 — but this needs Step 5, and getting it wrong makes 16.07 either impossible or useless. |
| DT-05 | This spec makes rider conflict detection possible | The domain index asserts exactly this | ✅ CONFIRMED — but it is **not free**, and the sweep did not list it as a child. Detection needs the spec (here) *and* the rider (18) *and* a matcher. Created the matcher as 16.07 rather than leaving it implied, because an asserted capability nobody owns does not get built. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The spec is **structured fields, not a document** | A PDF cannot be searched, diffed against a rider, or dated per field. The structuring IS the asset (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Spec capture must be phone-native and group-wise, never one long form | The Operator is at a loading dock, not a desk (DT-02) |
| D-03 | The band is a first-class source for spec truth, not just the Operator | The owner has an incentive to flatter; the band has just tested the claim (DT-03) |
| D-04 | "None" and "Not stated" are different values | A room with no monitors is a fact; an unfilled field is a gap. Conflating them poisons matching |
