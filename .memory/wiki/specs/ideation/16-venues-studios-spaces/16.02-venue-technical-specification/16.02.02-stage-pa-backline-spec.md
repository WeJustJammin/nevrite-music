# Feature: Stage, PA & Backline Spec

> **Number**: 16.02.02
> **Parent**: [Venue Technical Specification](./16.02-venue-technical-specification-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

The structured technical truth of a performance room: stage dimensions, height and obstructions; PA
make/model, provision model and who operates it; FOH desk, position, and the **usable input count**;
monitor provision (wedges/IEM, how many mixes, mixed by whom); mic and DI stock; power (phase,
amperage, connector, count, location); and house backline if any.

This is the domain's flagship asset and the reason it exists. **Every advance in live music
re-asks questions the venue has answered a thousand times** — how big is the stage, how many
monitor mixes, is there a house drum kit — over email, per show, forever. Structured once, this
becomes reusable, comparable, and (uniquely) machine-checkable against a rider ([16.07](../16.07-spec-conformance-check-rider-room.md)).

The problem it solves is not "the venue has no tech spec". Every venue has one. The problem is that
it is a PDF: unsearchable, undated, unqueryable, uncorrectable by the people who just proved it
wrong, and unable to answer the only question a band has — *can this room run my show?*

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Operator | **Full** on the 5 owned groups (stage, PA, FOH, monitors, power); **read-through** on the 2 gear groups (mic/DI stock, backline) | Fills the spec once instead of answering it per email, group by group, on a phone at a loading dock (D-02). Sees per-field which unstated fields are producing `Unknown` in other people's conformance checks and costing them enquiries ([16.07 D-06](../16.07-spec-conformance-check-rider-room.md)). Sees write-time contradiction flags on their own spec (D-08) — "you state 6 mixes; this desk has 4 auxes". Cannot edit the backline or mic groups here: those render from the org's asset register and are edited at source in [15.07.01](../../15-gear-registry-ownership/15.07-studio-backline-asset-register/15.07.01-org-owned-asset-register.md) (D-06). |
| Musician | Read-only + suggest | Reads it to decide if the room can run their show, and to know what to bring. Filters on it (stage ≥ 5 m usable, ≥ 4 mixes, ≥ 24 inputs). **Post-gig, they are the highest-value corrector** ([16.05.06](../16.05-curation-provenance-data-integrity/16.05.06-post-gig-session-data-harvesting.md)) — they just spent a night discovering the spec was wrong — but **only for the fields their booking actually exercised** (D-11). Cannot write directly; corrections are suggested edits ([16.05.03](../16.05-curation-provenance-data-integrity/16.05.03-suggested-edits-field-provenance.md)) or harvest answers. |
| Producer | Read-only + suggest | Reads it when producing or recording a live show — what desk, can it split, is there a multitrack out, is the FOH position behind glass. A live recording is a session, which makes this a provenance surface too. **A Producer who is house staff has `Config` on the backline group's condition via [15.07.02](../../15-gear-registry-ownership/15.07-studio-backline-asset-register/15.07.02-asset-condition-availability-state.md)** — they can change what this feature *displays* without holding write access to it, because the engineer is the one who finds channel 7 dead at 11pm. That path is a permission cross-cut, not a hole (D-06). |
| Fan | **None** | Not visible — not on the venue page, not on the show page, not at the URL. A Fan will never read a monitor spec; surfacing it is noise on a show page. The single exception the domain permits is a published backline *model name* on a public venue page ("Steinway D"), which is [15.07.03](../../15-gear-registry-ownership/15.07-studio-backline-asset-register/15.07.03-backline-list-publication.md)'s surface rendering there, not this feature's. |

## Behavior

The spec is **structured fields, not a PDF** (D-01). That distinction is the entire feature: the
industry already has tech specs, as PDFs on venue websites, unparseable, undated and unqueryable. A
structured spec supports search, conflict detection against a rider (16.07), field-level provenance
and per-field decay (16.05), and — the part nobody has claimed — **checking itself** (D-08).

### The Field Model — resolves Q-01, the domain's Q-04 and 16.07's Q-02

Every field is a **triple**: `value` + `caveats[]` + `note`. This is the enum-vs-free-text answer,
and it mirrors [16.01.04 D-02](../16.01-place-records-rooms/16.01.04-accessibility-profile.md)
("structured caveats, never a binary flag") as DT-04 predicted it would.

| Part | Type | Matchable by 16.07? | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| `value` | one of: `quantity` (number + unit) · `enum` (closed vocabulary) · `catalogue-ref` (an entry in 15's gear catalogue) · `boolean` | **Yes** — this is the only part a match is computed from | Exactly one type per field, fixed by the field. Two non-values are also legal: `none` (there is none — a fact) and `unstated` (a gap). They are different values and are never conflated (D-04). |
| `caveats[]` | 0..n, each `{kind: <closed vocabulary>, params: <typed>}` | **Yes** — a caveat qualifies, conditions or downgrades the value; it never replaces it | Caveat kinds are a closed list (below). A caveat with no matching kind is not a caveat — it is a note. |
| `note` | free text, ≤ 280 chars | **No — never** | Display-only. Never matched, never filtered on, never blocks, never carries a value. Its purpose is honesty about things no vocabulary can hold ("the room gets boxy above 100 dB"). A note is never promoted to a value by any automated path. |

**Caveat vocabulary** (closed, this feature's set):

| Kind | Params | Real example | Effect in 16.07 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `partial` | `usable: quantity` | 4 wedges, one dead → value `4`, `partial{usable: 3}` | Match computed against `usable`, result flagged `partial` |
| `conditional-on-configuration` | `config-ref` → [16.02.01](./16.02.01-capacity-configuration-model.md) | Demountable stage: 8 × 4 m standing, 5 × 4 m seated | Resolved against the show's configuration. **Configuration unknown → `Unknown`, never a guess** |
| `conditional-on-operator` | `operator: house-engineer \| visiting-engineer` | "6 mixes if you bring your engineer, 4 if we mix" | Resolved against the advance's declared engineer; unresolved → `Match, caveated` |
| `conditional-on-cost` | `rate-line-ref` → [16.06.07](../16.06-space-booking-reservations/16.06.07-rate-cards-inclusions-extras.md) | House kit available, £50 | `Match`, with the cost surfaced. Available ≠ included, and the band finds out at settlement otherwise |
| `conditional-on-notice` | `lead-time: quantity(days)` | "Extra wedges with 14 days' notice" | `Match` if `show date − today ≥ lead-time`; otherwise `Conflict` |
| `temporarily-unavailable` | `until: date`, `source: 15.07.02` | House amp out for service until 2026-08-02 | `Conflict` if the show date falls inside the window; `Match` after it. Set by the register, not typed here |

**Why free text alone was rejected**: it makes 16.07 impossible, and 16.07 is the reason to
structure the spec at all — without it, this feature is a prettier PDF (16.07 DT-04).
**Why enums alone were rejected**: they cannot express a real room, so the Operator's only options
are to lie or be filtered out — the exact trap [16.01.04 DT-02](../16.01-place-records-rooms/16.01.04-accessibility-profile.md)
identified for accessibility. The triple keeps the machine value checkable and gives reality
somewhere honest to live.

### Field Groups

Seven groups, each **independently completable** (D-02) — never one long form. Five are owned here;
two are read-throughs from domain 15 (D-06).

| # | Group | Owner | Fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Stage** | Here | `stage-type` enum `fixed \| demountable \| floor-level-no-stage` · `width` m · `depth` m · `height` m · `obstructions[]` (see below) · `load-capacity` kg/m² · `riser` enum `none \| fixed \| demountable-decks` + count. Stage *access* is not duplicated here — it read-throughs to the performer route in [16.01.04](../16.01-place-records-rooms/16.01.04-accessibility-profile.md) |
| G2 | **PA** | Here | `provision-model` enum `house-run-only \| house-PA-hireable \| dry-hire-no-PA \| bring-your-own-only \| none` · `system` catalogue-ref · `subs` boolean + count · `fills` boolean (front-fill/delay) · `who-operates` enum `house-engineer-required \| house-engineer-optional \| visiting-engineer-permitted` |
| G3 | **FOH** | Here | `desk` catalogue-ref · `desk-class` enum `digital \| analogue` · **`usable-input-count`** quantity (the stage-box reality, DT-06) · `available-channel-count` quantity · `recall` enum `full-show-file \| scene-only \| none` · `foh-position` enum `in-room-rear \| in-room-side \| balcony \| booth-behind-glass \| on-stage-no-foh-position` |
| G4 | **Monitors** | Here | `mix-count` quantity · `wedge-count` quantity · `iem` enum `none \| transmitters-only-bring-your-own-packs \| full-system` + pack count · `mix-source` enum `from-foh-desk \| separate-desk-with-engineer \| separate-desk-no-engineer \| musician-controlled-app` · `drum-fill` boolean · `side-fills` boolean |
| G5 | **Power** | Here | `supplies[]`, each `{phase: single \| three, amperage: quantity(A), connector: enum (**profile-scoped** — see Q-04), count, location: enum stage-left \| stage-right \| upstage \| foh \| external-generator-point}` · `stage-circuit-isolated-from-bar-kitchen` enum `yes \| no \| unknown` |
| G6 | **Mic & DI stock** | **Read-through** → 15.07 | Renders from the org's asset register. `di-count` and mic stock are register projections, not fields typed here (D-06) |
| G7 | **Backline** | **Read-through** → 15.07.03 | Renders from the register, filtered by condition (15.07.02). Models shown, serials withheld (15.07 D-03). Not typed here. **This is the field bands complain about most and the one that decays fastest — so it is the one that must not be a hand-maintained second list** (D-06) |

**`obstructions[]`** is a structured list, each `{kind: pillar \| low-ceiling \| fixed-dj-booth \|
fire-exit-keep-clear \| cable-trap \| other, position, footprint}`. It exists because
width × depth is a lie about a real stage (DT-10).

### Completeness is tiered, not binary (D-09)

| Tier | Threshold | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| `Unstated` | 0 core fields | Room is invisible to technical search; every 16.07 field returns `Unknown` |
| **`Checkable`** | 6 core fields: stage `width`, stage `depth`, FOH `usable-input-count`, monitor `mix-count`, PA `provision-model`, backline present y/n | The room enters conformance checking with real answers on the fields riders most often specify. **Design target: ≤ 90 seconds on a phone.** This is the tier that matters — the wall in DT-02 is beaten by making the first 6 fields the valuable ones, not by persuasion |
| `Advance-ready` | All 5 owned groups | The advance (18) runs off the spec instead of an email thread |
| `Complete` | Owned groups + both read-throughs resolved (register populated) | Full spec; the room is comparable on every axis the domain models |

### Self-checking — the spec checks itself before any rider exists (D-08)

Write-time consistency checks, **all non-blocking flags, never rejections** (a blocked Operator
lies; see 16.01.04 DT-02):

| Check | Flag |
|---|---|
| `available-channel-count` > the catalogue max for the named `desk` | "This desk has 16 channels; you've stated 32." Doubles as an **inflation detector** against the Operator's named anti-persona |
| `mix-count` > the catalogue aux/mix capacity for the named `desk` | "You state 6 mixes; this desk has 4 auxes" |
| `usable-input-count` > `available-channel-count` | Physically impossible; one of the two is wrong |
| `mix-count` ≥ 1 while `wedge-count` = 0 and `iem` = `none` | "6 mixes, 4 wedges, no IEM — where do the other 2 go?" |
| `provision-model` = `none` while `system` catalogue-ref is present | Contradiction |
| `provision-model` = `house-run-only` while `who-operates` = `visiting-engineer-permitted` | Contradiction |
| Σ `obstructions[].footprint` ≥ stated stage area | The obstructions consume the stage |
| Any `quantity` outside its plausibility band (width 1–30 m · depth 1–20 m · height 0–2 m · channels 1–256 · mixes 0–32 · wedges 0–24) | "That's unusual — confirm?" **Flag, never reject** — real rooms are genuinely odd |

At cold start, when no rider exists to check against, this is the **only** check available — and it
is the one that catches the inflated spec.

### Existing tech spec PDFs — behavior specified, scope call remains open (Q-06)

If ingestion ships, it ingests to **drafts, never to values** (D-10):

1. A parsed field enters a **draft queue**; it never occupies the field, is never matched by 16.07,
   never appears in search, and never resets a decay clock.
2. The Operator confirms **per group** (≤ 7 fields), with the source excerpt shown beside each
   parsed value. There is **no "confirm all"** — bulk confirmation is rubber-stamping, which is how
   a parse error becomes a fact.
3. Confirmation **is an assertion**: the field becomes `owner`-class (16.05.03 D-02) dated to the
   confirmation, not to the PDF. The Operator is not transcribing; they are stating it is true now.
4. If the document carries a date, it is shown during confirmation — *"this document is dated 2019.
   Still true?"* **Ingestion must not launder age**, and it must not launder authorship either.
5. A draft **never** overwrites a field held at a higher or first-hand provenance class. The draft is
   discarded for that field and the divergence is shown: *"your document says 4 mixes; a band
   verified 6 last month."*

This keeps 16.05.03's source ranking intact — no new class is added to it — because a draft is not a
source, it is a proposal.

### Happy Path

1. Operator claims a venue ([16.05.02](../16.05-curation-provenance-data-integrity/16.05.02-claim-ownership-verification.md)); the spec is `Unstated` and the room is unsearchable on every technical filter.
2. Operator fills the **Stage** group on their phone at the loading dock — 7 fields, including one obstruction (`pillar`, stage-left, 0.4 × 0.4 m). Each field commits on entry; nothing is batched behind a Save button.
3. Operator fills the **PA** group from the amp rack label: `house-PA-hireable`, `system` picked from the catalogue, 2 subs, `house-engineer-optional`.
4. Operator fills FOH and monitors. On `mix-count: 6`, the spec flags itself: *"this desk has 4 auxes — 6 mixes?"* Operator adds `conditional-on-operator{operator: visiting-engineer}` — the 6th mix exists only when a visiting engineer brings a monitor desk. **The contradiction was caught before any band ever read it.**
5. At 6 core fields the room crosses to `Checkable`; each field is timestamped and provenance-tagged `owner` (16.05.03), and a tagged photo (16.01.03) can evidence any of them.
6. The backline group shows the register's projection (15.07.03) — house kit, two cabs — with condition, without the Operator maintaining a second list.
7. Musician filters "stage ≥ 5 m wide, ≥ 4 mixes, ≥ 24 inputs"; the room now appears, with per-field freshness attached.
8. At advance time (18), the band's rider is checked against this spec (16.07): the input list (26 channels) meets `usable-input-count: 32`; the 6-mix requirement returns `Match, caveated — with your engineer`; power returns `Unknown`. One email is sent instead of eleven.
9. Post-gig, the reservation completes (16.06.03) and the harvest fires (16.05.06) — but only on the fields this booking exercised: they used the house PA and the house kit, so it asks about those and **not** about the 63 A supply they never touched (D-11).
10. Band confirms the PA, contradicts the kit ("kick pedal broken"). The PA field's clock resets at first-hand class; the kit opens a contest (16.05.04) **against the register**, where the condition state lives — not against a duplicate string in this feature.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Operator states 32 channels; catalogue says the named desk has 16 | Write-time inflation flag (D-08). Non-blocking — a desk can be expanded with a stage box, and the Operator may be right | "This desk has 16 channels. Expanded? Add a caveat or confirm 32" |
| 4 wedges, one dead | `value: 4` + `partial{usable: 3}`. 16.07 matches against 3 | "4 wedges (3 usable)" |
| "6 mixes with your engineer, 4 with ours" | `conditional-on-operator`. The commonest real monitor fact in live music, and unrepresentable in any existing spec | "4 mixes — 6 with a visiting engineer" |
| Demountable stage; dimensions differ per configuration | `conditional-on-configuration` keyed to 16.02.01. Resolves CX-01's silent inconsistency from this side | "8 × 4 m standing · 5 × 4 m seated" |
| A conformance check runs and the show's configuration is not yet decided | **`Unknown` — never the larger value, never an average.** Guessing here is exactly the false confidence 16.07 DT-05 fears | "Depends on configuration — not yet set" |
| Two venue staff edit `mix-count` concurrently | No silent overwrite. Second writer is shown the other value and its author; they choose. Both attempts are in the per-field revision history (16.05.03 D-04) | "Changed to 6 by Sam 40 seconds ago. Keep 4, or take 6?" |
| A harvest correction lands while the Operator is editing the same field | The correction does not overwrite and is not discarded — it opens a contest (16.05.04 D-01: last-agreed stands, contest visible) | Operator's value stands, marked contested; band's value shown beside it |
| Operator's phone has no signal at the loading dock | Field capture completes locally and syncs; the Operator is **never** asked to retype. A sync-time divergence takes the same non-silent merge as a concurrent edit | Field shows `pending sync`; no data loss, no modal |
| Room genuinely has no PA (dry hire) | `none`, not `unstated`. A rider requiring a PA returns **`Conflict`** — correct and useful. Under `unstated` it would return `Unknown` and the band would ask by email, which is the whole failure we exist to end | "PA: none — dry hire" |
| Rider requires 3-phase; power group unstated | `Unknown`. **The most dangerous unknown in the spec** — everyone assumes sockets exist and nobody asks about the supply they actually need (DT-08) | "Power: not stated — ask the venue" + Operator nudge naming the lost check |
| Band brings their own PA and assumes it bypasses the sound limiter | The spec must not license that inference. A limiter is on the *building's* noise, not on the venue's desk — bringing a PA changes nothing about the limiter's threshold, whatever its provenance — statutory where the record's regime profile declares an instrument that imposes it, the venue's own stated limit where it does not (16.02.03 D-01/D-13) (DT-09, CX-03) | Limiter (16.02.03) shown against the PA group regardless of `provision-model` |
| Room has a large PA and a 95 dB limiter | Both facts true; the combination means a rock show cannot happen there. Surfaced as a derived annotation (CX-03) | "Large system — 95 dB limiter. This room cannot run a loud show" |
| Operator inflates the spec to win bookings (named anti-persona) | Three independent detectors: the catalogue contradiction (D-08), the band's post-gig contradiction (16.05.06), and the conformance check that will fail publicly at advance. Structure makes inflation *expensive* rather than *impossible* | Contested marker; the Operator is notified, not punished |
| A rival Operator submits false edits to a room | Never a direct write. Suggested-edit path (16.05.03) at `community` class — the weakest rank above `seeded` (16.05.03 D-02); it cannot displace an owner value | Suggestion queued to the Operator |
| Band had a bad night and downgrades a field out of spite | The harvest only asks countables ("how many wedges?"), never judgements. Opinion is not this domain's data (16.05.06 D-01). A vindictive answer to "how many wedges were there?" is a falsifiable lie, not a review | Contest opens on a number, resolvable by a photo |
| **Operator is also the Musician playing their own room** | The multi-hyphenate breaks witness independence. The harvest **must not** treat the owner-as-band as a first-hand corroborator — it would launder an owner claim into a community verification (DT-13) | Harvest prompt suppressed for the fields on the room they own; shown as owner re-confirmation instead |
| **Producer is the venue's house engineer** and "corrects" the desk fields | Same laundering, via a different door: they are staff. Independence is computed from **entity roles** (15.07.01 D-02), not from the persona lens | Edit accepted at `owner` class, not `first-hand` — correctly, since it is the venue speaking |
| Band brought their own PA and never heard the house one | The harvest must not ask about it, and their **silence is not a confirmation** (D-11) | Question not asked; the field's clock does not reset |
| The house PA, the 63 A supply and the second monitor desk are never used by anyone | Decay (16.05.05) marks them stale forever because no booking witnesses them. **Decay + harvest systematically starves the fields nobody exercises** (DT-12) | Permanently aged fields on facts that may be permanently true — the domain-level hole, raised to 16.05.06 |
| Catalogue has no entry for a vintage/obscure desk | Free-text model accepted as an **unmatched catalogue-ref**. The field is `stated`, not `unstated`; catalogue-dependent consistency checks are **skipped, not failed** | "Desk: Soundcraft Series 4 (not in catalogue)" — no false contradiction flag |
| Operator has no asset register at all (most Operators, at launch) | The backline group offers a minimal-detail create path that **writes to 15.07.01**, not to a local list. One store, two doors (D-06) | "Add the house kit" → creates the register entry inline, 2 fields |
| 15.07.02 marks the house amp `faulty` at 11pm | This feature's backline group reflects it the same night, with no Operator action. A check dated inside the fault window returns `Conflict` | "House amp — out for service until 2 Aug" |
| Two rooms share one PA (16.01.02 Q-01) | The PA group is a shared-resource read on both rooms. Both correctly show the same system; **a same-night double-booking of both rooms is a PA conflict, not a calendar conflict** — and current room exclusion is binary, so it cannot express "co-bookable but degraded" | Both rooms show the system; the degradation is currently unexpressible → Q-08 |
| Whole PA is replaced in a refit | A group-level "we changed the PA" resets the **PA group's** clocks only. It must not touch the stage group — buildings do not move (16.05.05: physical dimensions never decay) | "PA group re-verified today" · stage untouched |
| Operator deletes a value (6 mixes → unstated) | Deletion is a revision, not an erasure (16.05.03 D-04). 16.07 flips `Match` → `Unknown`. A deletion immediately following a contest is a **silent revert** and must be visible | Field shows "not stated" + the history shows what was removed and when |
| Stage `load-capacity` unstated; band brings a 900 kg Hammond + Leslie or a full riser | `Unknown`. Quiet, rare, and the one that ends with equipment through a stage deck | "Load capacity: not stated — ask before you load in" |
| Operator answers power with "we have plenty of sockets" | Refused **as a value** — it is not a quantity. It is accepted as a `note`, which is never matched. The field asks the answerable question (amperage, phase, count), not the flattering one | Free text lands in the note; the field stays `unstated` and keeps nudging |
| Field value is genuinely implausible (stage width 300 m) | Flagged for confirmation, **never rejected**. Some rooms are festivals | "300 m is unusual — confirm?" |
| Rider is prose, so no automated match is possible (16.07 Q-01) | This spec's value **does not depend on the rider being structured**. Search, filters, the advance checklist and the self-check all work against a prose rider or none at all. 16.07 loses; 16.02.02 does not | Spec renders and filters normally |
| Fan reaches the URL directly | Not rendered. No fallback view, no teaser. The one thing a Fan may ever see is a backline *model name* on a public venue page, which is 15.07.03 rendering there | 404/not-found on the spec surface |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Spec requested; register read-throughs (G6/G7) resolving | Owned groups render first; the two gear groups skeleton independently — a slow register never blocks the stage spec |
| Empty (`Unstated`) | Nothing filled | Room invisible to technical search; every 16.07 field returns `Unknown`. Operator sees the lost-enquiry nudge naming the **6 core fields** and the 90-second target, not a 40-field form |
| Partial (`Checkable`) | 6 core fields filled | Spec + explicit per-field gaps ("monitors: not stated"), and the room is live in conformance checking. Gaps are named, never blanked |
| Partial (`Advance-ready`) | All 5 owned groups filled | Full owned spec; the advance runs off it. Read-through groups may still be empty and say so |
| Populated (`Complete`) | Owned groups + register resolved | Full spec + completeness badge; comparable on every modelled axis |
| Draft-pending | PDF ingested, not yet confirmed | Draft values shown **beside** the source excerpt, in a confirm queue, greyed and explicitly excluded from search and matching. Never in the field |
| Read-through unavailable | Register empty or unreachable | "House backline — not stated" + inline 2-field create path. **Never** a local text box (D-06) |
| Offline-pending | Field committed with no signal | Field shows `pending sync`; value visible and usable locally; no retype, no modal, no loss |
| Error | Save rejected (permission, validation, conflict) | Field-level error naming the cause and the fix. A group's other fields keep their values — one bad field never discards a group |
| Contradicted | Write-time self-check failed (D-08) | Non-blocking flag on the two fields in tension, naming both: "you state 6 mixes; this desk has 4 auxes" |
| Stale | Decayed past the class threshold (16.05.05) | Per-field "last verified" age; drops out of confident matching (16.05.05 D-03), stays visible (D-02) |
| Contested | Post-gig or community contradiction | Last-agreed value + contested marker (16.05.04 D-01), both values shown |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A tech spec is a document | It is a document today, universally — every venue has a tech spec PDF | ❌ REJECTED, and this is the domain's central bet. A PDF cannot be searched, filtered, diffed against a rider, dated per-field, or corrected by the band who just played there. The entire unbuilt asset is the **structuring**, not the collection. If this feature ships as document upload, the domain has built nothing new. |
| DT-02 | Operators will fill a long structured form | They want bookings; the form is the price | ❌ REJECTED as stated: the Operator persona works "on a phone at a loading dock, not at a desk". A 60-field form is a wall. Mitigations designed in, not bolted on: field **groups** completable independently, phone-native capture, photo-as-evidence, the `Checkable` tier at 6 fields (D-09), and — the strongest — 16.05.06's post-gig harvest, which extracts spec truth from the *band* rather than the Operator. The lazy path must be the correct path. |
| DT-03 | The band is a better source than the Operator for some fields | Operator anti-persona: "publishes a stale gear list, so a band arrives to a room that can't run their show" | ✅ CONFIRMED: for house backline and monitor reality especially, the band has just tested the claim empirically and has no incentive to flatter. This inverts the naive write model — the room's owner is not the best witness to the room. It is also what makes 16.05.06 a spec feature rather than a nicety. **Qualified by DT-11 and DT-13**: the band is a better witness only for what it touched, and only when it is genuinely independent of the room. |
| DT-04 | Structure means enums everywhere | Enums are what make it checkable | ✅ **RESOLVED at Step 5 → D-05.** Enums make 16.07 possible but cannot express reality ("4 wedges but one is dead", "6 mixes with your engineer"). Free text is honest and uncheckable. The answer is the **triple**: typed value + closed-vocabulary caveat + display-only note — and it does mirror [16.01.04 D-02](../16.01-place-records-rooms/16.01.04-accessibility-profile.md), as predicted. The note is the release valve that stops the enum forcing a lie; the caveat is what stops the note having to carry meaning the machine needs. |
