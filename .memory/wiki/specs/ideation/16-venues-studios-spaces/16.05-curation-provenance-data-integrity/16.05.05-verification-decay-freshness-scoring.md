# Feature: Verification Decay & Freshness Scoring

> **Number**: 16.05.05
> **Parent**: [Curation, Provenance & Data Integrity](./16.05-curation-provenance-data-integrity-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Facts about physical places rot. The PA gets replaced, the drum kit loses its snare, the ramp gets
sold, the lift breaks, the studio re-treats the live room, the venue changes its curfew. **A
directory that treats a 2019 claim as a 2026 fact is not a registry — it is a liability**, and it is
the failure mode every venue and studio directory currently exhibits.

Decay attaches a half-life to every field: how long since it was verified, how fast this kind of
fact goes stale, and therefore how much confidence to present. It is what stops the domain's
structuring bet from becoming a very well-organised set of lies.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Operator | 📊 Reports | Sees their record's freshness and which fields are decaying, framed as lost enquiries rather than as nagging. One-tap re-verification ("still true?") is the whole interaction — anything heavier does not happen. |
| Musician | 👁️ Read-only | Sees per-field ages and confidence. Decides how much to trust a spec before travelling to it. Refreshes fields implicitly by answering the post-gig prompt (16.05.06). |
| Producer | 👁️ Read-only | Same, and cares more — a session is planned around a gear list, and a stale gear list wastes a booked day. |
| Fan | 👁️ Read-only (minimal) | Sees nothing of decay mechanics. At most, a "may be out of date" note on a venue's public facts. Confidence scores are professional-tool complexity the Fan will not tolerate. |

## Behavior

Every field has a **verification timestamp** and a **decay profile**. The profiles are not uniform,
and the variance is the design:

| Field class | Decay rate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Physical dimensions (stage size, ceiling height) | Effectively never | Buildings do not move |
| Load-in route, parking | Slow | Changes with refits and local council decisions |
| PA, desk, monitors | Medium | Replaced every few years |
| Mic locker / backline / instruments | Fast | Sold, broken, in repair, borrowed |
| Rehearsal-room backline condition | Fastest in the domain | Tested nightly by whoever books the room |
| Rates, deal terms | Fast | Commercial terms age badly; a 3-year-old split is misinformation |
| Statutory declarations, insurance | Expiry-driven, not decay-driven | They have dates; use the date, not a curve |

Verification comes from four sources, and **not all resets are equal** — the reset carries the
source's strength, because a self-attestation by the party who profits from the record is worth less
than an independent one (Q-01, resolved):

| Source | Reset strength | Presentation after reset |
|---|---|---|
| Independent first-hand report (16.05.06) | **Full** — clock to zero, highest confidence | "Confirmed by a visiting band, 3 months ago" |
| Accepted community edit (16.05.03) | **Full** — an outside party asserted it against moderation | "Community-confirmed, 3 months ago" |
| Tagged photo (16.01.03) | **Full for what the photo shows** — a photo is evidence, not a claim | "Photo-verified, 3 months ago" |
| Operator self-confirmation ("still true?") | **Partial** — clock resets but confidence caps below independent, and the field still reads as owner-asserted | "Owner-confirmed, 3 months ago" |

The **age** is the same across sources; the **label of who confirmed it** is what carries trust, and
in confident filtering (D-03, 16.07) an owner-only-confirmed field is down-weighted relative to an
independently-confirmed one of the same age. The point is not to punish the Operator — a one-tap
confirmation is cheap and better than silence — but to stop a room that self-confirms daily from
out-ranking a room that a real band verified last month.

The output is **presentation, not suppression** — a stale field is shown with its age, not hidden. A
hidden field is a fact the user cannot even ask about; an aged field is a fact with a caveat.

### Happy Path

1. Venue's PA field was verified 26 months ago; the profile says medium decay
2. Field's confidence has dropped; the record shows "PA — last verified 2 years ago"
3. Operator sees a prompt: "Is your PA still a d&b Y-Series?" — one tap
4. Operator confirms; clock resets; confidence restored with no data entry
5. A band books; post-gig, they confirm the PA independently (16.05.06) — a stronger reset than the Operator's own

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Seeded record, never verified | Starts decayed and stays there; presented as never-verified (16.01.05 DT-03) | "Never verified" |
| Operator confirms everything without checking | Self-confirmation is a **partial reset** (D-06): the clock resets but confidence caps below independent and the field reads "Owner-confirmed", so a self-confirmed room does not out-rank an independently-verified one | "Owner-confirmed, N ago" (weaker than "Confirmed by a visiting band") |
| Operator taps "still true?" daily to farm freshness | Repeated self-confirmation cannot raise a field above the owner-asserted ceiling; a second owner tap inside the same decay window is a no-op on confidence (only the newest independent signal moves the ceiling) | No visible change; badge stays owner-asserted |
| Owner self-confirms a field a community edit just contradicted | The contradicting community edit (16.05.03) is the stronger signal; it wins the value and the owner "still true?" tap does not overwrite it. Owner may dispute through 16.05.03's flow, not by re-confirming | Field shows the community value + "disputed by owner" if raised |
| Field is stale but still true (the PA has not changed in a decade) | Decay produces a **false stale** — a correct field presented as doubtful. The cost of the model, paid honestly | Age shown; value unchanged |
| Room is busy; post-gig reports arrive constantly | The best case — the highest-traffic rooms stay fresh for free, exactly where accuracy matters most | Fresh, high confidence |
| Room is never booked on-platform | The structural hole: no transactions, no harvest. Non-transaction paths (Operator tap, community edit, photo) still work but none is reliable; a room with no traffic *and* no engaged owner stays "never verified" — the correct, honest floor, not a bug (D-08). Trades (16.01.07 DT-02) live here permanently | Persistently "never verified" / low confidence |
| Confidence surfaced to any persona | Never a percentage. Ages ("verified 3 months ago") and a source label are the only presentation; "73% confident" is precision the model does not have and a UX that helps nobody (D-07) | Age + source label, never a number |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Fresh | Recently verified | Value, no caveat |
| Ageing | Past half the decay window | Value + "verified N months ago" |
| Stale | Past the window | Value + prominent age + drops out of confident filtering |
| Never verified | Seeded, never touched | "Never verified" — the honest label for much of the registry |
| Expired | Date-driven fields (statutory declarations, insurance) | Expired, not stale — a different and harder state |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Decay is a per-record freshness score | Simplest: how old is this record? | ❌ REJECTED: a record's stage dimensions are permanent and its mic locker changes monthly. One score for both is meaningless — it would either flag a permanent fact as stale or present a rotten gear list as fresh. **Decay is per-field with per-class profiles**, which follows directly from per-field provenance (16.05.03 D-01). |
| DT-02 | Stale fields should be hidden | Do not show users bad data | ❌ REJECTED: a hidden field is a question the user cannot ask. "PA: d&b Y-Series, verified 2 years ago" is useful — the band can plan and ask to confirm. A blank is useless. **Present with age; never suppress.** The exception is *filtering*: a stale field should drop out of confident matching (16.07), because a match on a rotten fact is worse than no match. |
| DT-03 | The Operator confirming is sufficient verification | They own the room; they know | ⚠️ PARTIALLY REJECTED: it is cheap and it is better than nothing, but it is a **self-attestation by the party with an incentive to flatter** (`personas.md` Operator anti-persona). A one-tap "still true?" costs nothing to answer dishonestly or carelessly. An independent first-hand report (16.05.06) is a strictly stronger reset. Whether they should reset the clock by the *same amount* is Q-01 — and probably not. |
| DT-04 | Decay is a mechanism serving many domains | Gear condition (15), artist profiles (01), credits (02) all age | ✅ CONFIRMED — recorded as a **cross-cut**. Timestamp + half-life + confidence presentation is generic machinery. What stays domain-owned is the **decay profile table** — knowing that a ceiling height never decays and a rehearsal-room snare decays weekly is music-domain knowledge that does not transfer to a credits database. Mechanism cross-cut, profiles domain-owned. |
| DT-05 | Decay solves the stale-data problem | It is the feature for exactly that | ❌ REJECTED — **decay only *labels* the problem; the harvest solves it.** Decay makes staleness visible; it does not make anything fresh. The only mechanisms that actually refresh a field are the Operator's tap and the post-transaction report (16.05.06). This makes 16.05.05 and 16.05.06 a matched pair — decay without harvest is a registry that accurately documents its own decline. |
| DT-06 | Every verification source resets the clock by the same amount (Q-01) | Simplest: a reset is a reset | ❌ REJECTED — a reset must carry its **source's strength**. Owner self-confirmation is a self-attestation by the party with an incentive to flatter (`personas.md` Operator anti-persona: "publishes a stale gear list"). Treating it as equal to an independent band's post-gig report lets a room farm freshness by tapping "still true?" and out-rank a room a real band actually verified. **Resolution: owner self-confirmation is a partial reset with a confidence ceiling below independent sources; the age is shown identically but the source label and filter weight differ** (D-06, D-03). |
| DT-07 | Confidence should be shown as a percentage (Q-02) | Numbers feel precise and rankable | ❌ REJECTED — the model does not *have* percentage-grade precision; a decay curve over a guessed profile table (Q-03) cannot honestly produce "73% confident". A number invents rigor the data lacks and means nothing to a Fan and little to a Musician. **Resolution: present ages ("verified 3 months ago") plus a source label, never a percentage** (D-07). Ages are honest, human, and degrade gracefully into "never verified". |
| DT-08 | A never-booked room can still be kept fresh somehow (Q-04) | There must be a non-transaction freshness path | ⚠️ PARTIALLY REJECTED — non-transaction paths **exist** (Operator tap, accepted community edit, tagged photo) but **none is reliable**: the tap is self-attested and weak, edits need an engaged community that a dead room lacks, photos are sporadic. A room with no on-platform traffic *and* no engaged owner has no freshness path at all, and **that is correct** — the honest "never verified" floor, not a defect to engineer around (D-08). Trades and long-tail rooms (16.01.07 DT-02) live here permanently. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Decay is **per-field with per-class profiles**, never per-record | A ceiling height never decays; a rehearsal-room snare decays weekly. One score describes neither (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Stale fields are **presented with their age**, never hidden | A blank is a question the user cannot ask; an aged value is a fact with a caveat (DT-02) |
| D-03 | Stale fields drop out of **confident filtering** even though they stay visible | A match on a rotten fact is worse than no match (DT-02, 16.07) |
| D-04 | Statutory declarations and insurance are **expiry-driven**, not decay-driven | They carry real dates; a decay curve over a known expiry is a worse model than the date |
| D-05 | Decay labels; it does not fix. 16.05.06 is its required counterpart | Decay without harvest documents the decline accurately and changes nothing (DT-05) |
| D-06 | Verification resets carry **source strength**: owner self-confirmation is a **partial reset** (confidence ceiling + owner-asserted label + lower filter weight); independent report, community edit, and photo are **full resets** | A self-attestation by the incentivised party cannot equal an independent one; stops freshness-farming (DT-06, Q-01) |
| D-07 | Confidence is presented as **ages + source label, never a percentage**, to every persona | The model lacks percentage-grade precision; a number invents false rigor and helps no persona (DT-07, Q-02) |
| D-08 | Non-transaction freshness paths exist (tap, edit, photo) but are unreliable; a room with no traffic and no engaged owner stays **"never verified" permanently, and that is the correct floor** | Engineering around the never-booked room would fabricate freshness the platform cannot honestly claim (DT-08, Q-04) |
