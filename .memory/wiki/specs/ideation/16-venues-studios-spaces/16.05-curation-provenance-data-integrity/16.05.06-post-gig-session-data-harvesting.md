# Feature: Post-Gig / Post-Session Data Harvesting

> **Number**: 16.05.06
> **Parent**: [Curation, Provenance & Data Integrity](./16.05-curation-provenance-data-integrity-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The moment after a gig or a session when the platform asks the one party who just tested every claim
on the record: **was it as described?** The load-in, the backline, the monitor count, the accessible
route, the green room that turned out to be a corridor.

This is the domain's engine. Everything else in 16.05 is scaffolding around it: decay (16.05.05)
labels staleness but cannot fix it; provenance (16.05.03) ranks sources but needs a strong one to
rank; conflict resolution (16.05.04) needs an independent witness to corroborate against. **Harvest
is the only mechanism in the domain that produces fresh, first-hand, disinterested facts** — and it
is the same capture-at-source pattern the whole platform is built on (D-18), applied to rooms
instead of splits.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The primary contributor. After a gig, gets **one** prompt: a handful of yes/no confirmations on the claims that matter, pre-filled from the spec, answerable in seconds on a phone in a van. Their answers are the strongest facts the registry has. |
| Producer | Full | Same, after a session — and the higher-authority source on studio facts (gear, sightlines, isolation). `personas.md` makes them the trust broker: their attestation outweighs a self-claim. |
| Operator | 📊 Reports | Does not contribute; **receives**. Sees what came back about their room — corrections, contradictions, corroborations. Framed as free QA on their own listing, because that is what it is. |
| Fan | ❌ None | No harvest path. A Fan attending a gig did not test the load-in and has no first-hand knowledge of anything on the spec. Their post-gig relationship is with the artist (20), not the room's data. |

## Behavior

Triggered by a **completed reservation** (16.06.03) — which is the whole reason booking belongs in
this domain rather than in 17 or 05. The booking is what proves the person was in the room; without
it, a "first-hand report" is an anonymous claim.

The prompt is deliberately minimal and must stay that way: 3–5 questions, pre-filled from the
riskiest, fastest-decaying, or most-contested fields on that specific record. Not a survey. A band
at 1am loading out will answer three taps and nothing more.

Question selection is the interesting mechanic: ask about what the platform is **least sure of** —
fields that are decayed (16.05.05), contested (16.05.04), or never verified. Every answer is a
strong-class provenance event (16.05.03) that resets a clock and possibly corroborates a contest.

**One prompt, not many.** 16.02.06 DT-04 established that the PRO setlist ask and the spec
correction ask must share this moment. A band will tolerate one post-gig interruption. Two is one
too many, and losing the harvest to prompt fatigue costs the domain its only engine.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — response-rate reality. The entire domain's data
quality rests on an assumption nobody has tested: that bands will answer.

### Happy Path

1. Band plays a venue; the reservation completes (16.06.03)
2. Next morning, one prompt: "Quick check on The Bunker — 3 questions"
3. "Load-in listed as step-free. Was it?" → No → "How many steps?" → 3
4. "House kit listed. Was it usable?" → Yes. "PA listed as d&b. Right?" → Yes
5. Three fields get strong-class provenance; one contest opens (16.05.04); two clocks reset. Total time: 20 seconds

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Nobody answers | **The domain's central risk.** Every freshness claim rests on this response rate, and it is unmeasured | `[PENDING — Step 5]` |
| Band answers carelessly to dismiss the prompt | Yes-tapping produces false confirmation — worse than no answer, because it resets a clock on a false fact | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — is a confirmation weaker than a correction? |
| Band is angry about the gig and trashes the spec | A retaliation vector against the room's data. Distinct from a review (a cross-cut) — a spec fact is not an opinion | Corroboration threshold (16.05.04) limits it |
| Gig happened off-platform | No reservation, no trigger, no harvest. Most gigs at launch are off-platform — **the loop only works where the booking already lives here** | — |
| Prompt arrives during load-out | Timing is the whole design. Too early: they are working. Too late: they have forgotten and moved on | `[PENDING — Step 5]` |
| Same room, same band, every week (rehearsal) | Prompt fatigue is guaranteed. High-frequency rooms need a much lower prompt rate — the opposite of what "ask about uncertain fields" implies | `[PENDING — Step 5]` |
| Answers contradict the Operator | Exactly what it is for → 16.05.04 | Contest opened |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Pending | Reservation completed | Prompt queued |
| Prompted | Delivered | 3–5 questions, pre-filled |
| Answered | Contributor responded | Thanks + what changed because of them |
| Ignored | No response in window | Prompt expires quietly — never nagged; a nagged musician is a lost one |
| Harvested | Answers applied | Provenance events written, clocks reset, contests opened |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a review/feedback feature | It is a post-transaction prompt, which is what review systems are | ❌ REJECTED, and the distinction is sharp. A review is an **opinion** about an experience ("great room, lovely staff"). This harvests **facts** about a physical object ("3 steps, not step-free"). Facts are verifiable, corroborable, and correctable; opinions are none of those. Reviews are a platform cross-cut serving 05/13/14/16. This is not that, and merging them would turn falsifiable facts into vibes. |
| DT-02 | This is a nice-to-have data-quality enhancement | It is one of six curation features | ❌ REJECTED — **it is the domain's engine, and everything else in 16.05 is scaffolding around it.** Decay labels staleness and fixes nothing (16.05.05 DT-05). Provenance ranks sources but needs a strong source to exist. Conflict resolution needs an independent witness. Seeding produces a registry that immediately begins to rot. Harvest is the only mechanism that generates fresh first-hand disinterested facts. Cut it and the domain is a stale directory with good intentions. |
| DT-03 | It is the same capture-at-source pattern as the platform's thesis | D-18: provenance captured at the moment of creation | ✅ CONFIRMED — and this is domain 16's structural justification. The platform's argument is that facts must be captured while they are true, because reconstruction from memory is contested and lossy. A venue's load-in is exactly such a fact: known perfectly by the band on the night, and never by anyone afterwards. **The booking is what puts the platform in the room** — the same causal chain the thesis describes for splits, applied to spaces. This is why booking must live in this domain: without the reservation there is no trigger, no proof of presence, and no harvest. |
| DT-04 | Ask everything while we have their attention | It is the one moment they are engaged | ❌ REJECTED: a band at 1am has approximately three taps of patience. A survey is a 0% response rate. 3–5 questions targeted at the platform's **least certain** fields, pre-filled, phone-native. And per 16.02.06 DT-04, this moment is **shared** with the PRO setlist ask — one prompt total, not one per feature that wants a piece of it. |
| DT-05 | Bands will answer | The whole domain's data quality assumes it | ⚠️ **UNVALIDATED — the domain's largest single risk, and it must be stated plainly.** Every freshness guarantee in 16 rests on an untested behavioural assumption. Arguments for: the ask is tiny, the band has just experienced the thing, and there is reciprocity (they used the registry to pick the room). Arguments against: they are tired, they are unpaid, and the beneficiary is the next band. **If the response rate is near zero, decay (16.05.05) does not degrade gracefully — it just accurately documents a rotting registry.** This assumption should be tested before the domain's MoSCoW is finalised. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Harvests **facts**, not opinions — explicitly not a review feature | Facts are verifiable and correctable; merging with reviews turns falsifiable claims into vibes (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Triggered by a **completed reservation** — the booking is the proof of presence | Without it, a first-hand report is an anonymous claim. This is why booking lives in this domain (DT-03) |
| D-03 | 3–5 questions, targeted at the platform's least-certain fields, pre-filled, phone-native | A band at 1am has three taps of patience; a survey scores zero (DT-04) |
| D-04 | **One** post-gig prompt shared with the PRO setlist ask | Two prompts after a show is one too many, and prompt fatigue costs the domain its engine (16.02.06 DT-04) |
| D-05 | Ignored prompts expire quietly; no nagging | A nagged musician is a lost one, and the domain needs them next time |
