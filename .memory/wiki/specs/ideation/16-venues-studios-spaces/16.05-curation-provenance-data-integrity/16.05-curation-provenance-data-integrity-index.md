## Overview

The write model that makes domain 16 a **registry** rather than a set of listings. Records are born
unclaimed and seeded from public data; facts are contested between the owner and the people who were
in the room; every field carries where it came from and how old it is; and the whole thing is kept
honest by asking the band, after the gig, whether the room was as described.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: seeding produces records that need dedup; dedup needs
provenance to decide survivorship; claiming changes who writes and who suggests; suggestions produce
conflicts; conflicts need an independent witness; the witness comes from the harvest; the harvest
resets the decay clocks. Seven capabilities in one dependency cycle — the densest interaction graph
in the domain.

**This is what the domain index means** by "a machine with nothing in common with a single-writer
gear listing". It is the sub-domain that justifies domain 16's existence as a domain.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Established as a sub-domain | Seven capabilities in one dependency cycle: seed → dedup → claim → suggest → conflict → harvest → decay → seed. The densest interaction graph in domain 16 | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 classification |
| D-02 | **Duplicate Detection & Merge added** as a Deep Think child | Named in the domain index's rationale, absent from all 22 sweep candidates. Without it, seeding produces a registry worse than empty | 16.05.07 DT-01 |
| D-03 | Provenance is **per-field** with a ranked source class: statutory > owner > first-hand > community > seeded | One record mixes all five; record-level provenance must lie about most of them | 16.05.03 D-01, D-02 |
| D-04 | **Conflict default: last-agreed stands, contest visible** — not owner-wins | Owner-wins makes this a listings product and favours the party with an incentive to flatter. Policy is per field class; accessibility is the first-hand-wins exception | 16.05.04 D-01, D-03 |
| D-05 | Claim proves **control**, not ownership; proof must anchor to data predating the claim | The platform cannot adjudicate legal ownership. Anchoring to claimant-supplied data is the domain's central vulnerability | 16.05.02 D-01, D-02 |
| D-06 | Re-ingestion may add and fill; it may **never** overwrite a human edit | Source data is the weakest provenance class | 16.05.01 D-02 |
| D-07 | Decay **labels**; harvest **fixes**. They are a matched pair | Decay without harvest documents the registry's decline accurately and changes nothing | 16.05.05 DT-05 |
| D-08 | The harvest is triggered by a **completed reservation** — this is why booking belongs in domain 16 | The booking is the proof of presence; without it a "first-hand report" is an anonymous claim | 16.05.06 DT-03 |
| D-09 | Merge is non-destructive, reversible, and biased toward under-merging | A wrong merge corrupts two calendars and live bookings; a missed duplicate is merely untidy | 16.05.07 D-02, D-04 |
