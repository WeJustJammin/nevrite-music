## Overview

Selling the room's time. Availability and holds, synchronisation with the calendar the Operator
already uses, the reservation from request to completion, backfilling perishable slots, compound
bookings that reserve a room and a person and the gear together, the posture that decides whether a
room can be bought at all, what it costs, and the tenancies that are not bookings.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: nine capabilities in a tight interaction web. Holds feed
backfill; sync decides whether the calendar is truthful or harmful; compounds need atomic holds across
several calendars; posture decides whether the calendar is even visible; rate cards are computed over
compounds; completion triggers the domain's data engine.

**Why booking lives in domain 16 at all** (rather than in 17 or 05): the domain index's wrong-cut
test — "the mic locker IS the booking decision". And a second, mechanical reason discovered during
this pass: **the completed reservation is the proof of presence that makes the post-gig harvest
(16.05.06) first-hand.** Without booking here, the registry has no engine.
