# Feature: Booking Posture & Enquiry/RFQ Routing

> **Number**: 16.06.06
> **Parent**: [Space Booking & Reservations](./16.06-space-booking-reservations-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23
> **Merged from**: sweep candidate 16, plus "booking contact" from candidate 01 — a booking contact is a routing target, not a spec field

## Overview

How a room accepts business, and where the request goes. **Booking posture** is the discriminator:
instant-book (pick a slot, pay, done), enquiry-only (tell me what you want and I will answer), or
contact-only (here is a number). **Routing** is what happens to an enquiry — who receives it, in what
form, and whether it becomes a structured negotiation or an email.

The posture is not a setting; it is a statement about the business. **Most venues will never
instant-book** — a Saturday night is a curated bill, not a slot, and a venue that lets anyone buy one
has lost control of its programming. A platform that treats enquiry-only as a degraded mode has
misunderstood most of its supply.

Two facts discovered during deepening reshape the feature and are carried in D-06 and D-07:

1. **Posture is not a choice from three — it is the highest rung a room currently qualifies for.**
   It falls directly out of 16.05.02's claim control tiers (D-08 there): an unclaimed room has nobody
   to receive an enquiry, a Provisionally-claimed room may reply but may not commit, and instant-book
   requires a Full claim *plus* a rate card *plus* a payout destination that a claim never grants
   (16.05.02 D-06). The Operator may always choose a **lower** rung; they can never choose a higher
   one. This makes posture the claim funnel's front door, not a preferences screen.
2. **The seam with 17 is drawn by use type, and the test is "who invoices whom".** The requester
   declares what they want the room *for*, and that declaration routes the thread. Room hire is 16's
   end to end; a bill slot is 17's from the moment it is declared. This resolves the sub-domain's Q-06
   and matches 17.07's own boundary ("Studio enquiries are domain 16's").

## Behavior

### Posture

Posture answers one question — **how can this room's time be acquired for this purpose?** — and it is
declared per **room × use type**, never per room alone.

**The three rungs**, in ascending order of what the platform is prepared to let happen:

| Rung | What it means | What the room must have |
|---|---|---|
| **Contact-only** | Here is a number. The platform carries no thread and adds nothing. | Nothing. This is the **default** for every unclaimed record. |
| **Enquiry-only** | Tell me what you want and I will answer. A structured thread exists. | A claim — Provisional or Full (16.05.02 D-08). Provisional may **reply** but not **commit** (no hold, no quote). |
| **Instant-book** | Pick a slot, pay, done. | Full claim + published rate card (16.06.07) + configured payout destination (01/23) — which **a claim never grants** (16.05.02 D-06). |

The Operator selects any rung **at or below** the room's qualifying rung. Downgrading is a first-class
commercial act and is the point of D-01/D-02: a Full-tier venue with a rate card and a payout profile
choosing enquiry-only for its Saturdays is exercising the feature as designed, not failing to
configure it. Upgrading past the qualifying rung is refused with the specific missing prerequisite
named ("Instant-book needs a payout destination — set one up in Finance") rather than a generic error.

Posture changes are **never retroactive**. An in-flight enquiry survives a posture change, because it
is a live conversation with a human; only new requests see the new rung.

### Use type — the routing discriminator

The requester declares what they want the room for. The declaration selects the enquiry's payload
**and** decides which domain owns the resulting thread (D-06).

| Use type | Money direction | Thread owner |
|---|---|---|
| Rehearsal | Requester pays the Operator | **16** — through to 16.06.03 |
| Recording session | Requester pays the Operator | **16** |
| Dry hire — private event | Requester pays the Operator | **16** |
| Dry hire — promoter's night | Requester pays the Operator for the room | **16** (the promoter's separate deals with the acts are 17's — two threads, one night) |
| **Bill slot** — the act plays a programmed show | The Operator/promoter pays the act, or splits the door | **17** — 16.06.06 routes and stops |

**The test is "who invoices whom".** If the requester will receive an invoice for the room, it is 16's.
If the requester expects to be *paid* — guarantee, door split, percentage — it is 17's, and this
feature's only job is to hand them to 17.07 with the room's identity attached.

### Enquiry payload

An enquiry is a **structured object carrying the requester's identity, credits and history** (D-03) —
the only thing the platform has that email does not, and where domain 16 touches the provenance thesis.

**Common core — every room-hire enquiry:**

| Field | Type | Required | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use type | enum (above) | Yes | Requester — routes the thread (D-06) |
| Requester | entity ref — person or band/org (01) | Yes | Auto, from the account |
| Date or window | exact date · date range · recurring window ("any Tuesday in March") | Yes | Requester |
| Start + duration | time + hours, in the **room's** local zone, always | Yes for rehearsal/recording; optional for dry hire | Requester |
| Headcount | integer | Yes | Requester — gated against declared occupancy (16.01.06) |
| Budget | currency range | No | Requester |
| Requirements | multi-select **from the room's own published spec vocabulary** (16.02/16.03/16.04) — never free text | No | Requester |
| Hirer compliance | declarations against the **hirer-requirements slot** (16.01.06 D-06) — liability cover held + level, electrical/fire-safety record, named responsible person. The instrument *names* come from the room's regime profile, never from this form: "PLI" and "PAT" are the retired UK profile's vocabulary and the **US names are a live `[PENDING]` on 16.01.06 D-06**, deferred to `/create-prd-security` | Conditional: required iff the room declares the requirement (16.01.06 D-04) | Requester |
| Note | text, 1,000 chars, escaped, links stripped for unverified accounts | No | Requester |
| Context panel | credits (02), platform booking history, prior bookings **at this room** | Auto | Platform — **D-03, the thesis touchpoint** |

**Per use type, added:**

| Use type | Additional fields |
|---|---|
| Rehearsal | Band (01 entity) · one-off or regular (regular → offers 16.06.09) · backline needed |
| Recording session | Tracking / overdubs / mixing (decides room + gear) · engineer required or bringing own (16.03.03) · project name (**optional** — sessions are confidential) · archive/recall expectation (16.03.04) |
| Dry hire | Event type · public or private · ticketed? · expected attendance · door/curfew needs · who is promoting · which venue staff are required (16.06.07 mandatory extras) |

**Deliberately absent, and this is the seam made checkable (D-15):** fee, guarantee, deal shape,
expected draw, split, genre fit, support slot. Those are **17.07's** qualifying fields. If one of them
appears on a 16 enquiry form, the seam has been crossed and the form is asking a hirer to negotiate a
deal that does not exist.

**Attachments**: none. An enquiry accepts a **link to a structured artefact the platform already
holds** (the act's rider or tech spec) — never an uploaded PDF. An unstructured PDF is exactly the
input format this feature exists to replace, and 16.07 already owns spec conformance.

### Qualification — the gates

An Operator taking 40 enquiries a week cannot read 40 enquiries a week (17.07 DT-02, the same insight
arriving from 17's side). But a small venue will **never configure a rules engine**, so:

**Hard gates — derived from facts the room already declares. Zero configuration. Auto-decline with a
real reason, never silence** (17.07 D-03's precedent):

| Gate | Source | Reason returned |
|---|---|---|
| Date unavailable | 16.06.01 | "14 March isn't available. Nearest open: 12, 19, 21 March." |
| Below minimum block | 16.06.03 | "This room's minimum booking is 4 hours." |
| Declared headcount > declared occupancy | 16.01.06 D-03 | "This room is licensed for 12 people." |
| Use type not accepted | This feature | "This room doesn't take dry hire." |
| Room is under a lockout tenancy | 16.06.09 | "This room isn't available for booking." (never discloses the tenant) |

**Soft gates — Operator-configurable, and every one is optional:** budget floor, lead-time minimum
("no enquiries inside 14 days"), use types offered.

**Never a gate — hirer compliance** (D-12). See DT-09.

### Routing

The target is a **role**, not a person (D-08): `bookings · this room · this use type`. A person is
**bound** to the role; when the binding is empty, dead, or the bound person's account is
deactivated, enquiries fall back to the Operator org (01) and the Operator is told the binding broke.
Routing to a person is the single most common way a venue silently stops answering — bookers leave,
and the inbox they left behind keeps accepting mail.

### Lapse

Unanswered enquiries are the industry's default outcome. **An enquiry lapses; it never dies silently**
(D-09). The window is derived from lead time, not fixed — a tour routed 8 months out cannot expire in
14 days, and a rehearsal enquiry for Thursday is dead by Wednesday:

```
lapse_at = min( now + clamp(lead_time × 0.25, 24h, 21d), requested_slot_start )
no date declared → 14 days flat
```

The `min` against the slot start is load-bearing: without it, an enquiry for tomorrow would lapse
after the date it asked about.

| Lead time | Derived window |
|---|---|
| ≥ 84 days | 21 days (cap) |
| 60 days | 15 days |
| 30 days | 7.5 days |
| 14 days | 3.5 days |
| 4 days | 24 hours (floor) |
| 18 hours | 18 hours (slot start wins) |

One reminder to the role holder at 50% of the window. One — not a drip. At lapse both parties are
told, and **lapse is not a decline**: it is "no answer", said honestly, which is already more than
email does.

### Hirer requirements

Surfaced at enquiry, not at the door (16.01.06 D-04). The **liability-cover requirement** is shown
**before** the requester commits, they declare against it, and the declaration travels with the
enquiry. The platform relays; it does not adjudicate (D-12, DT-09).

**Vocabulary note (DQ-14.1/DQ-14.2).** This feature reads the **slot**, never the instrument. What the
room requires is whatever its **regime profile** names for the hirer-requirements slot (16.01.06
D-05/D-06); the UK naming this section was originally written in ("PLI", "PAT") retires with that
profile, and the US instrument names are a live `[PENDING]` deferred to `/create-prd-security`
(16.01.06 Q-04). Nothing in the enquiry form's shape depends on which names land — the GBP figures
below are narrative illustration, not a default.

### Conversion

The thread becomes: enquiry → reply → hold (16.06.01) → booking (16.06.03), or it dies with a reason,
tracked either way. **An enquiry reserves nothing** (D-13) — and the requester is told so explicitly,
because the industry's mental model says otherwise.

### Happy Path

1. Musician routing a tour opens a venue's page. The room shows **enquiry-only** for dry hire and
   **instant-book** for weekday rehearsal — two postures, one room, per D-02.
2. They select use type **dry hire — promoter's night**, 14 March, 180 expected. The form's hard gates
   run against the room's own records before it will submit: 14 March is open (16.06.01), 180 is under
   the licensed 350 (16.01.06), the room takes dry hire.
3. The room declares a **liability-cover requirement** on its hirer-requirements slot (16.01.06 D-04)
   — £2m here is narrative illustration and the instrument name is the profile’s, not this form’s. The
   form surfaces it and asks them to
   declare; they hold £5m and declare it. It is not a gate (D-12) — the Operator decides.
4. They submit. The enquiry carries: use type, date, headcount, requirements picked from the room's own
   spec vocabulary, the liability-cover declaration, and the auto-attached context panel — their verified credits
   (02) and platform history. **The booker will not have to google them.**
5. Lead time is 240 days → `clamp(60d, 24h, 21d)` = 21 days. The requester sees "we'll chase on 27 July;
   this lapses on 7 August" and "this doesn't reserve the date" (D-13).
6. Routing resolves the role `bookings · Live Room · dry hire` → bound to Sam, a freelance booker who
   also books two other venues (D-08).
7. Sam sees it ranked, with the context panel and the liability-cover declaration attached, alongside 3 other
   enquiries — not the 11 that arrived, because 7 were auto-declined with reasons (D-11).
8. Sam replies by **granting a hold** — one action, which is the reply (DT-10). 14-day TTL starts
   (16.06.01 D-02). Indicative terms come from the venue's published standing deal (16.02.05).
9. The lapse clock stops. Thread converts: hold → confirm → booking (16.06.03). The promoter's separate
   deals with the acts on the bill are 17's, and are a different thread on the same night (D-06).

| Qualified | Passed gates, routed | Ranked in the role holder's inbox with the context panel attached |

### Surface States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Posture + gates resolving (needs claim tier, calendar, rate card, payout status) | Room page renders; the **booking panel** skeletons. Never render a guessed posture — a wrong "Book now" is worse than a slow one |
| Empty | Booker has no enquiries | The room's active hard gates, listed — "you auto-decline: unavailable dates, under 4h, over 12 people" |
