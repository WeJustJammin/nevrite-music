# Feature: Rate Cards, Inclusions & Extras

> **Number**: 16.06.07
> **Parent**: [Space Booking & Reservations](./16.06-space-booking-reservations-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23
> **Split from**: sweep candidate 17 — session archive policy moved to 16.03.04 (a studio property, not a booking mechanic)

## Overview

What the room costs, what that price includes, and what it does not. Hourly/daily/lockout rates, the
day rate that is really ten hours, what is bundled (an engineer, a kit, a PA) versus what is a hire
extra, and the extras themselves — backline hire, an assistant, overtime, a recall fee.

The gap between the advertised rate and the invoice is where trust in this domain is won or lost. A
studio quoting "£300/day" that arrives at £520 with an assistant, a Rhodes and an overtime hour has
not lied — every item was real — but the client did not know, and now the relationship has a problem
that will outlive the session.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Operator | Full | Sets rates per room, defines inclusions and extras with prices. This is **the** commercial field the community may never touch (16.05.03 D-03) — the platform's one absolute write rule. |
| Musician | 👁️ Read-only | Reads the rate and — the intervention — sees the **full price with extras before committing**, not on an invoice afterwards. Rehearsal-room booking is almost entirely a price decision. |
| Producer | 👁️ Read-only | Reads it while quoting a client. A Producer who under-quotes because the assistant was an unlisted extra eats the difference or has an argument. They need the true total to do their own job. |
| Fan | ❌ None | No visibility. Never sees a room's rates. |

## Behavior

Structured, per-room: rate units (hourly / half-day / day / lockout / per-show), the actual hours a
"day" means, minimum block (16.06.03), inclusions (what the rate covers), extras (item, price, unit),
and how overtime is priced.

The design rule that makes it worth building: **the quote is computed, not advertised.** A client
selecting a room, an assistant and a Rhodes for 10 hours should see the real total before paying —
including the extras their compound implies (16.06.05) and the mandatory ones they did not choose
(16.03.03 D-04). The industry's default is a headline rate and a surprise; the platform's job is to
remove the surprise, not to make the headline lower.

**Rates decay fast** (16.05.05) — a two-year-old rate card is misinformation, and unlike a stage
dimension it changes with inflation, demand and the Operator's mood.

**The extras taxonomy** — five kinds, each an `(item, price, unit)` row the Operator defines, each
with a `mandatory | optional | compound-implied` flag that decides whether it surfaces in a quote
automatically:

| Kind | Examples | Unit | Surfacing |
|------|----------|------|-----------|
| Staffing | assistant, second engineer, runner | per-hour or per-session | mandatory (16.03.03) or optional |
| Gear / backline hire | Rhodes, valve amp, extra mic pair | per-item per-session | optional, or compound-implied (16.03.02 inventory status) |
| Time | overtime, minimum-block top-up, lockout | per-hour | conditional — triggered by the booking's duration |
| Consumables / media | tape, drive, session archive delivery | per-unit | optional |
| Fees | recall fee, cancellation/deposit charge | flat | policy-driven (recall fee lives here; the policy is 16.03.04) |

Whether the quote is public per posture — an enquiry-only venue may publish nothing (16.02.05 D-02) —
and whether the computed total is **binding** are the two open questions (Q-01, Q-03). The binding
question is an owner decision and is left `[PENDING — Q-01, owner]` deliberately: it decides the
feature's worth and cannot be resolved by fabrication.

### Happy Path

1. Producer selects Live Room A for 10 hours; headline rate £300/day
2. Adds the mandatory assistant (16.03.03) and a Rhodes hire (16.03.02) — both surface automatically
3. System computes: £300 + £120 assistant + £40 Rhodes + £0 overtime = **£460, before payment**
4. Producer quotes their client £460 with confidence and no exposure
5. Session runs 2 hours over → overtime at the pre-agreed rate → no argument at 2am

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| "Day rate" means 10 hours, not 24 | Structured — the hours a day means is a field, because everyone assumes and nobody agrees | "Day = 10 hours" |
| Mandatory extra the client did not choose | Surfaced automatically in the computed quote, not on the invoice (16.03.03 D-04) | Included in the total |
| Room is enquiry-only and publishes no rate | Legitimate (16.02.05 D-02). "On enquiry" is a valid state, not a gap | "Rates on enquiry" |
| Rate card is 2 years old | Decays fast (16.05.05); a stale rate is misinformation, not merely aged | Rate + last-verified age |
| Operator charges something not on the card | The invoice/quote gap. `[PENDING — Q-01, owner]` — is the quote binding? If it is not, the feature's whole value evaporates | Depends on Q-01 |
| Extra's price is missing/unresolved at quote time | Quote cannot be totalled honestly — the platform refuses to show a partial number as if complete | "Couldn't total this booking" + retry (Error state) |
| Community submits a suggested rate edit | Rejected at the write boundary — rates are Operator-only (16.05.03 D-03, DT-03); only facts are community-correctable, not terms | Suggestion blocked with the terms-vs-facts reason |
| Lockout priced monthly | Not a rate card in the same sense — it is a tenancy (16.06.09) | Handled by 16.06.09 |
| Dry hire vs full-service pricing | Different staffing models are different products at different rates, not one rate plus an extra | Per-model rates |
| Venue's hire fee vs a door split | For a dry-hire venue the rate card *is* the deal (16.02.05); for a door-split venue there is no rate card at all | Depends on deal shape |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Room card opened, rate card fetching | Skeleton rows where rate + extras will render |
| Empty / Not published | Enquiry/contact posture, no rates set | "Rates on enquiry" — a valid state, not a gap (16.02.05 D-02) |
| Populated / Published | Operator set rates | Rate + inclusions + extras list |
| Quoted | Client composed a booking | **Computed total before payment**, itemised (base + each extra) |
| Stale | Rate last-verified age past decay threshold (16.05.05) | Rate with a visible age badge and a re-verify prompt to the Operator |
| Error | Quote computation failed (e.g. an extra's price unresolved) | "Couldn't total this booking — the venue was contacted" with a retry, never a wrong number shown as right |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A rate card is a price list | It lists prices | ❌ REJECTED: **the value is the computed quote, not the list.** Every studio already publishes a headline rate; the client's problem is that the headline is not the price. A list plus a surprise is the status quo. A total that includes the mandatory assistant, the hire item their compound implies, and the real meaning of "day rate" — before payment — is the feature. The list is table stakes; the computation is the product. |
| DT-02 | Extras are optional add-ons | The word "extra" says so | ❌ REJECTED: many are **mandatory** (a required assistant, 16.03.03 D-04) or **implied** by the compound (booking the live room means the control room). An optional-add-on model lets the client build a quote that is not the price they will pay, which is the exact failure the feature exists to remove. Extras are compound members with prices (16.06.05 D-01). |
| DT-03 | Rates should be community-correctable like other fields | Everything else in this domain is | ❌ **REJECTED — this is the domain's one absolute write rule.** 16.05.03 D-03: the community corrects **facts**; only the Operator sets **terms**. A community-suggested rate is not a correction of reality, it is a fabrication of a commercial term, and it would bind an Operator to a number they never agreed. The line is bright and worth restating here because a rate looks superficially like any other stale field. |
| DT-04 | Session archive policy belongs with rates | The sweep bundled them in candidate 17 | ❌ REJECTED — split to 16.03.04. The recall **fee** is an extra (it lives here); the **policy** is a durable studio property that decays, is read before booking, and is provenance-bearing. Bundling them filed a provenance concern under invoicing. |
| DT-05 | The computed quote should be binding | If we show a total, we should honour it | ⚠️ UNRESOLVED, and it decides the feature's worth. A non-binding quote is a nicer-looking estimate — the client is still exposed and the trust problem survives. A binding one makes the platform a party to the commercial terms, which is a liability position and one many Operators will refuse. This is the same tension as 16.02.05 DT-04 (indicative terms) with more money attached. Q-01 — an owner decision. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The product is the **computed quote**, not the published list | Everyone publishes a headline; the client's problem is that the headline is not the price (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Extras include **mandatory** and **compound-implied** items, surfaced automatically | An optional-only model lets a client build a quote that is not what they will pay (DT-02) |
| D-03 | Rates are **Operator-only** — the domain's one absolute write rule | The community corrects facts; a community-suggested rate fabricates a term (DT-03) |
| D-04 | "A day" carries its actual hours as a field | Everyone assumes and nobody agrees |
| D-05 | Session archive policy split to 16.03.04; only the recall fee stays | Bundling filed a provenance concern under invoicing (DT-04) |
| D-06 | **Boundary with the room spec's provision posture, stated once: this feature owns the price and the rate-card term; the room spec owns whether a thing is provided at all.** A `hire-extra` posture with no rate line here is an **incomplete rate card**, not a contradiction; an `included` posture is **never inferred** from the absence of a rate line | Owner decision (DQ-15.A3; 16.04 D-08). Without the sentence the two enums overlap and drift, which is 15.07.03 DT-01's duplication failure one level up — at *term* level rather than item level. Extending this feature to carry the provision fact was rejected: "the band must bring a kit" is not a commercial term, and a room with no rate card would then have no provision posture at all, while D-03 makes this the one feature the community may never touch |
