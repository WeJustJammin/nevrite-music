# Feature: Rehearsal & Practice Space Specification

> **Number**: 16.04
> **Parent**: [Venues, Studios & Spaces](./venues-studios-spaces-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

The spec sheet for a rehearsal room: what backline is in it and whether that is included or hired,
the access model (staffed hours vs 24-hour fob), room size and what it comfortably holds, storage,
and whether it is sold **by the hour** or as a **lockout** — a monthly tenancy on a room that
becomes the band's own.

A rehearsal room is the least glamorous and most frequently used space in music: a working band is
in one weekly, versus a venue a few times a year and a studio maybe once. It is deliberately a
**feature, not a sub-domain** — unlike a venue or a studio it has no interacting capability clusters.
There is one short spec sheet, and pretending otherwise would inflate the structure to match its
neighbours rather than its reality (D-01). On the D-18 thesis it is the domain's **frequency** play:
the lowest-value transaction in the domain but potentially the highest-value *habit*, which is what
consolidation needs to win the user.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Operator | Full on the room's own fields; **read-through** on backline | Declares the **provision posture** per backline category (D-08), access model, room size, storage, and whether the room is hourly, lockout, or both. Does **not** type a backline list here — the items live in the org's asset register (15.07.01) and render from its publication (15.07.03), filtered by condition (15.07.02) — with an inline minimal-detail create path that writes to the register, exactly as 16.02.02 D-06 does for venues. Rehearsal Operators are the most price-and-availability-driven supply in the domain — the spec is short because the decision the buyer makes is short. |
| Musician | Read-only + suggest | **The primary consumer.** Picks a room on four facts: is there a kit, is there a bass rig, can we get in tonight/at 10pm, and what does it cost. Post-session, the **corrector** — the backline claim is tested every single time someone uses the room, and the Musician is the one who reports it wrong (16.05.06). Their correction now lands **on the register's condition state** (15.07.02), not on a list this feature holds, which is the whole point of D-06. |
| Producer | Read-only | Peripheral. Occasionally books a rehearsal room for pre-production, in which case they care about backline *quality* and whether they can leave gear set up overnight (storage + access). |
| Fan | None | Not visible and never surfaced. A rehearsal room is a supply-side working space with no fan-facing dimension — nothing to attend, buy, or follow. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

The feature is a single structured spec sheet the Operator fills and Musicians filter against — plus
one **read-through** it does not own.

- **Backline items — read-through, not a field** (D-06). The item list renders from the org's asset
  register publication (15.07.03), filtered by condition (15.07.02). **This feature types no item
  list.** It is the same read-through both sibling room types already confirmed locally — 16.02.02
  D-06 for a venue's stage backline, 16.03.02 D-04/D-05 for a studio's locker — and 15.07 D-01 names
  rehearsal inside its own scope line. A duplicated list rots by construction: two stores, two update
  paths, one maintained by a busy person at a loading dock (15.07.03 DT-01). The venue path's inline
  minimal-detail create — *one store, two doors* (16.02.02 D-06) — applies here unchanged, because
  the alternative is an Operator who will not open an asset register to list a room.
- **Backline granularity — quantity-tracked, with identity retained per item where the Operator
  wants it** (D-07). A rehearsal room's stock is 40 mic stands, 200 cables and four interchangeable
  practice amps; per-object identity on that is the highest onboarding cost in the domain loaded onto
  its lowest-value room type, and ceremony is what kills the rehearsal path.
- **Provision posture — typed here, per category** (D-08). A single enum per backline category:
  `included` · `hire-extra` · `bring-your-own-only` · `none`, with **`unstated` as a distinct
  non-value** (16.02.02 D-04). This is the fact a rehearsal buyer decides on and the one thing the
  register cannot say — the register holds items, not terms.
  > **Boundary, stated once so the two enums cannot overlap: 16.06.07 owns the price and the
  > rate-card term; 16.04 owns the provision posture; 15 owns the items.** A `hire-extra` posture
  > with no rate line is an incomplete rate card, not a contradiction — and an `included` posture is
  > never inferred from the absence of a rate line.
- **Access model** — `staffed hours` (with a schedule) / `24h fob` / `by arrangement`, plus an
  **access lead time** (how long before first use access can actually be granted — see Edge Cases).
- **Room size + comfortable band size** — square footage and the honest "this holds a 4-piece
  comfortably, a 6-piece tightly" number.
- **Storage** — can gear be left between sessions, and in what form (open room / locker / cage).
  A distinct, commonly-sold thing, separate from lockout.
- **Sale model** — `hourly` / `lockout` / `both`.

**Lockout is the fact that makes this feature more than a stub.** A lockout is not a booking — it is
a monthly **tenancy** on a dedicated room the band treats as their own, leaves gear in, and pays for
whether or not they use it. Modelling it as 30 recurring bookings gets the billing, the calendar and
the cancellation model all wrong (D-02). It is handled in **16.06.09**; this feature only carries the
flag that a room *is* lockout-capable and its monthly price.

## Happy Path

1. Band needs a room tonight at 8pm with a kit and a bass rig, within 5 miles.
2. Filters rehearsal rooms: drums provision `included`, available tonight, distance ≤ 5 mi.
3. Results show matching rooms with the **register's published items** rendered (15.07.03) at their
   graded condition (15.07.02), the room's **provision posture per category** beside them, plus price
   (16.06.07) and access model.
4. Band picks a room and books it hourly (16.06.03); the reservation captures its gear dependency
   from the filter that produced it (16.03.02 D-08) — see the Edge Cases for what that means on a
   quantity line.
5. Post-session, the band is prompted: "was the kit as described?" (16.05.06). The answer writes to
   the **register's condition state** (15.07.02), not to a list here. This is the fastest-decaying
   claim in the domain — tested nightly — which is exactly why it must not live in two places.

## Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Backline is listed but broken (the universal rehearsal-room condition) | **Graded condition on the register** (15.07.02 D-01), not a flag here. Post-session harvest (16.05.06) is the corrective and it writes to the register — no self-declared spec survives nightly contact | "Kit — degraded: no snare (3 reports)" |
| **The register is empty, or the org publishes nothing** | The read-through renders **`unstated`**, never `none` — the two are different values (16.02.02 D-04, and 16.02.02's *Read-through unavailable* state). This is why `bring-your-own-only` must be **stated**: an empty register cannot assert it | "Backline — not stated" + Operator's inline create path |
| **The Operator is a sole trader with no org entity** | **Blocked population.** 15.07 publishes **org** registers; a sole-trader rehearsal Operator has no org entity, so their gear is a personal collection (15.04) with no publication path — they cannot publish backline at all, and the room drops out of every backline filter. This is the same structural exclusion 16.03.02 DT-12 found for bedroom studios, arriving in the room type where it costs the most supply. Not resolvable from this side — **Q-04** | Room has no backline section and is invisible to backline-filtered search |
| **"4 amps, 1 faulty" — which one?** | Under D-07's quantity mode the condition attaches to a **count**, not to an object, so the register cannot say which. This **degrades 15.07.02 D-01's per-asset graded condition** into a second shape (condition-on-a-count) that domain 15 must carry — **Q-05** | "4 practice amps — 1 reported faulty" |
| **A reservation's captured gear dependency points at a quantity line** | 16.03.02 D-08 captures the filter that produced the booking so the booker is notified when *that item* goes faulty. On a count there is **no referent** — "the amp you booked for" does not identify an object. The mechanism must be respecified for quantity lines before it can fire here — **Q-06** | Currently: no dependency notification on quantity-tracked stock |
| Operator wants identity tracking on one good item (a maintained Gretsch among four practice amps) | Supported — D-07 is a **mode per item**, not a mode per room. Identity-tracked and quantity-tracked stock coexist in one room's read-through | The Gretsch renders as an object; the amps render as a count |
| Room is lockout-only, buyer tried to book by the hour | Not hourly-bookable at all; a different product with different economics — booking flow refuses and redirects to the tenancy enquiry (16.06.09) | "Lockout only — £X/month. Enquire about tenancy." |
| Access is 24h fob but the fob takes 3 days to issue | **Access lead time** is a first-class field on the spec (resolves Q-02). A "tonight" search hides rooms whose lead time exceeds the gap to the requested slot | "Earliest access: {date} — 3-day fob issue" |
| Musician suggests an edit (kit was actually a 5-piece, not 4) | Read-only + suggest, and the write path forks on the entity role exactly as 16.03.02 D-10 sets out: staff → a direct register write; a hirer with no role → a contested fact (16.05.04 D-05). Either way it does not overwrite silently, and either way it lands on the register, not here | "Suggested change pending operator review" |
| PA present but two of four mics dead | Same shape as the amps row — a count with a condition on part of it. No per-item identity unless the Operator asked for it (D-07) | "PA — 2 of 4 mics reported working" |
| Room is inside a venue or studio (rehearsal room in the basement) | Place type is a **set** (16.01.01) — one record, `type=[venue, rehearsal]`, with separate rooms. No duplicate listing | Handled by the type set |
| Band wants to leave gear between sessions without a lockout | Storage field — a distinct, commonly-sold thing (locker/cage), independent of the tenancy product | "Storage: lockers available (£Y/mo)" |
| Two bands filter for and try to book the last "tonight" slot at once | Standard booking-layer concurrency (16.06.03) — first confirmed wins, the other sees the slot gone. This feature only supplies the spec, not the calendar lock | "That slot was just taken" |
| Backline claim so stale its age is unknown | Field carries a visible last-verified age; unverifiable fields render as claims, not facts | "Kit — last confirmed 8 months ago" |

> **Condition vocabulary — partly answered, no longer fabricated here.** D-06 inherits 15.07.02
> D-01's **graded condition with a note** rather than inventing a rehearsal-specific grade, which is
> most of what DT-03's unresolved corner was asking for. What the inheritance does *not* answer is
> the distance between "a maintained Gretsch" and "a shell pack with no snare" *within* the same
> grade — the note carries it, and the note is not comparable. Narrowed and still open as Q-01.

## States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Filter submitted, results resolving | Skeleton list of room cards |
| Empty | No room matches the filter (no backline-included room available tonight nearby) | Empty state: "No rooms match — widen distance or drop the backline filter" + relaxed-filter CTA |
| Populated | Rooms matched | Room cards with backline + inclusion + access + price |
| Error | Search/booking request failed | "Couldn't load rooms — retry" with retry action |
| Unstated | Operator declared nothing, **or the org has no published register**, **or the Operator has no org entity at all** (Q-04) | Invisible to backline-filtered search — for a rehearsal room, near-total invisibility. Renders as `unstated`, never as `none` |
| Read-through unavailable | The register is unreachable | Last-known published state **with its age** (15.07.03 D-03), never a blank — a blank reads as "this room has no gear", which is worse than an honestly-labelled stale list |
| Declared | Operator filled the room's own fields; register published | Register-backed items at their graded condition + provision posture per category + access + storage + sale model |
| Contested | Post-session contradiction logged | Contested markers with report counts, **on the register's condition state** (15.07.02), not on a list here |
| Stale | Fields decayed past a freshness window | Fields render with visible ages, downgraded from facts to claims |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Rehearsal spaces deserve a sub-domain like venues and studios | Symmetry — the sweep listed rehearsal alongside them | ❌ REJECTED. Applying the classification gate honestly: a rehearsal room has **one** spec sheet with no interacting capability clusters. A venue's curfew constrains its running order which constrains its deal; a studio's staffing model changes what a booking is. Nothing in a rehearsal room's spec constrains anything else in it. Symmetry is aesthetic, and inflating structure to match is exactly the domain-count inflation D-17 warns about. Feature. |
| DT-02 | Lockout is a booking with a long duration | It is a room reserved for a period of time | ❌ REJECTED — the feature's most important finding. A lockout is a **tenancy**: billed monthly regardless of use, no availability semantics (the room is never released), gear left in situ, rolling notice rather than a fixed end. 30 bookings gets billing, calendar and cancellation all wrong. Different product, same door → 16.06.09. |
| DT-03 | Backline condition can be captured in the spec | If we can structure a mic locker we can structure a drum kit | ⚠️ UNRESOLVED and honest: "drum kit" spans a maintained Gretsch and a shell pack with no snare, and the industry has no vocabulary for the difference. It is the fastest-decaying claim in the entire domain — tested nightly. Post-session harvest (16.05.06) is the only plausible mechanism, which makes rehearsal rooms the **best test case** for whether the harvest loop works at all: highest frequency, lowest stakes, fastest feedback. **⚠️ Partly resolved (owner ratification, DQ-15.A1 → D-06):** the vocabulary is no longer this feature's to invent — condition is inherited from 15.07.02 D-01's graded-with-note model along with the items themselves. The residue is narrower and stays open as Q-01, and the "captured in the spec" framing is now wrong in a second way: it is captured in the **register**, and this feature reads it. |
| DT-04 | Rehearsal rooms are a low-value corner of the domain | Cheap, unglamorous, small transactions | ❌ REJECTED on the consolidation half of the thesis (D-18). They are the **highest-frequency** place transaction in music — a working band uses one weekly. Frequency builds the daily habit, and D-18 says consolidation is what wins the user. The lowest-value transaction may be the highest-value habit. |
| DT-05 | Access lead time belongs in booking posture (16.06.06), not this spec | It looks like a booking constraint (Q-02) | ❌ REJECTED for this level. A fob that takes 3 days to issue is a **property of the room's access model**, not of a given booking's timing — it is true of every booking, so it lives with the access model here. Booking posture (16.06.06) *consumes* it to hide impossible slots. Keeping it on the spec avoids re-deriving it per booking. |

|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Classified as a **feature**, not a sub-domain | One spec sheet, no interacting capability clusters. Symmetry with venues/studios is aesthetic (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Lockout is a **tenancy**, not a long booking — handled in 16.06.09 | Monthly billing, no availability semantics, gear in situ, rolling notice. 30 bookings gets all three wrong (DT-02) |
| D-03 | ~~Per-item backline inclusion model (included / hire extra / BYO)~~ — **SUPERSEDED by D-06 + D-08** (owner ratification, DQ-15). The *items* are no longer typed here at all (D-06), and the commercial fact is restated as a **per-category provision posture** with `unstated` as a distinct non-value (D-08) | "Backline available" is still three different commercial facts — the correction is where they live, not that they exist. Per-item inclusion could not survive a read-through it does not own, and per-item on quantity-tracked commodity stock (D-07) has no object to attach to |
| D-04 | Rehearsal is the domain's **frequency** play, not its value play | Weekly use beats annual use for the daily habit D-18 requires (DT-04) |
| D-05 | **Access lead time** is a spec field, consumed by booking posture | It is a property of the access model, true of every booking, not of one booking's timing (DT-05, resolves Q-02) |
| D-06 | **Backline is a read-through from 15, not a list here.** Items render from 15.07.03 filtered by 15.07.02 condition; this feature types no item list and holds no second store. The inline minimal-detail create path writes to 15.07.01 | Owner decision (DQ-15.A1). Confirms 15.07 D-01 for rehearsal rooms — a **per-feature confirmation** in the sibling convention (16.02.02 D-06, 16.03.02 D-04/D-05), and 15.07 D-01 names rehearsal in its own scope line. A local list contradicts two ratified 15.07 decisions rather than filling a gap, needs a condition store, a fault-reporting surface and a permission model no source defines, and reproduces the exact failure 15.07.03 DT-01 exists to prevent. It also breaks on a `[venue, rehearsal]` record, which would otherwise hold register-backed backline for one room type and typed backline for the other. **This does NOT close domain-15 Q-07 or 15.07 Q-01** — both remain open and Owner=User |
| D-07 | **Quantity-tracked mode for commodity rehearsal stock, with identity-tracking retained per item where the Operator wants it** | Owner decision (DQ-15.A2), which is 15.07 Q-03. The only option consistent with what two other specs already publish: 15.07.03's commodity edge case and 16.03.02's commodity row both state counts, not identity, and both cite 15.07.01 DT-03 as settled — and DT-03 proposes exactly this for exactly this room type (40 mic stands, 200 cables, four interchangeable practice amps). Identity-only loads the highest onboarding cost onto the lowest-value room type, against the finding that **ceremony is what kills the rehearsal path** (16.06.01 D-19/DT-15). **This ratifies work inside domain 15**: 15.07.01 DT-03 calls it a genuine model addition, not a UI concession |
| D-08 | **The provision posture is typed here, per backline category**: `included` · `hire-extra` · `bring-your-own-only` · `none`, with **`unstated` a distinct non-value**. 15 owns the items; **16.06.07 owns the price and the rate-card term; 16.04 owns the provision posture** | Owner decision (DQ-15.A3). The only option under which the two facts a rehearsal buyer actually decides on — *is a kit included*, *must we bring one* — can be **stated** rather than inferred. Inference fails against source in both directions: an empty register renders `unstated`, not `none`, so `bring-your-own-only` is unassertable; and `included`-as-absence-of-a-rate-line is indistinguishable from an Operator who has not built a rate card. Extending the rate card was rejected because it puts a non-commercial fact inside the feature whose one absolute rule is that only the Operator sets terms (16.06.07 D-03), and a room with no rate card would then have no provision posture at all. Mirrors the venue sibling structurally: 16.02.02 G2 types a **provision-model enum** locally while its read-throughs sit beside it |
