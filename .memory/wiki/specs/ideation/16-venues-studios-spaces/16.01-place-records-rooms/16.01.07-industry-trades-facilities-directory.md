# Feature: Industry Trades & Facilities Directory

> **Number**: 16.01.07
> **Parent**: [Place Records & Rooms](./16.01-place-records-rooms-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

Place records for the music industry's supporting trades and facilities — luthiers and repair
shops, backline and PA rental houses, production/lighting companies, pressing plants, duplication
houses, instrument retailers with service departments. These are physical places with addresses,
turnaround times and specialisms, and they are the part of the industry with the worst discovery
of all: finding a luthier who can do a neck reset is currently a forum post.

They fit this domain because they are **place-shaped, not service-shaped**: you take the thing to
them, or they bring the thing to you. But they are the domain's weakest members — most have no
bookable calendar and no technical spec, which is 80% of what this domain does.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Operator | Full (when claimed) | A trade is an Operator too — a rental house or a luthier claims their record, states specialisms, turnaround, and whether they take enquiries. Most will never take a calendar booking; their posture is enquiry-only (16.06.06). |
| Musician | Read-only + suggest | The primary consumer. Finds a repair shop that does what they need, near them, and has been vouched for. Suggests records and edits — the community knows the good luthier, no public dataset does. |
| Producer | Read-only + suggest | Finds rental houses and production companies when a session or show needs gear the room doesn't have. Also a heavy supplier of records — Producers know the trades. |
| Fan | None | Not surfaced. A Fan has no reason to find a pressing plant, and exposing trades to a consumer audience only adds moderation load with no user value. |

## Behavior

A trade record is a place record (16.01.01) with `type=trade` and a **trade profile** instead of a
technical spec: trade category, specialisms (fretwork, valve amps, vintage tape, lacquer/vinyl,
brass repair), turnaround, whether they do postal/mail-in work, whether they travel, and callout
radius for the mobile ones.

Most trades are **not bookable**. Their booking posture is enquiry-only or contact-only, which
means this feature exercises almost none of 16.06. That is the honest read: for trades, this
domain is a directory, and only a directory.

The boundary with 05 Services is **resolved**: D-02's **address test** decides it — premises = trade
record here, no premises = 05 Services — and DT-04 applies it to exactly the mobile-tech case ("no
address means no place record… A mobile tech is a service provider (05)").

`[PENDING — /write-be-spec]` — the trade profile fields above are a reasonable first cut and have
still not been validated against any real trade taxonomy. The validated field set (categories,
specialism vocabulary, turnaround) is a schema shape, not an ideation fact, and it is downstream of
the keep-or-cut call on this feature (16.01 index Q-02).

### Happy Path

1. Musician's guitar needs a neck reset; searches trades near them, filtered to fretwork
2. Results show luthiers with specialisms, turnaround and provenance ("added by 3 users")
3. Musician opens a record: address, specialisms, photos, community notes
4. Posture is enquiry-only, so Musician sends an enquiry describing the job (16.06.06)
5. Luthier replies off the calendar entirely — no reservation, no deposit

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Trade is one person with no premises (mobile amp tech) | Not a place — belongs in 05 Services. The address test is the discriminator: no address, no record here | Redirected to a service listing |
| Trade is a retailer with a service department | Two facts, one address; `[PENDING — Q-03 → /create-prd-architecture]` — retail is 13 Gear Marketplace's territory, the service bench is this feature's, and the record model does not yet handle the split |  — |
| Pressing plant | Fits the address test, but overlaps 12 Release & Distribution's manufacturing concern | Recorded as a trade; the 12 boundary is an open question |
| Trade has no calendar, no spec, no rate card | Correct and expected — most trades are directory-only | Record with profile + enquiry button only |
| Nobody maintains trade records | Real risk: no booking flow means no post-transaction harvest (16.05.06), so trade records decay with nothing to refresh them | Stale records with low confidence |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Unclaimed | Community-added or seeded | Record + "Do you run this? Claim it" |
| Claimed | Trade verified ownership | Verified profile, Operator-controlled |
| Enquiry-only | Default posture | Contact/enquiry, no calendar |
| Stale | Decayed, no refresh mechanism | Low-confidence marker, "last verified" age |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |

| DT-01 | Trades belong in this domain | Sweep candidate 06 placed them here | ⚠️ WEAKLY CONFIRMED, with an honest caveat. They pass the address test (place-shaped, you take the thing there) and they have no better home — 05 Services is human-output-shaped, 13 is goods-shaped. But they exercise almost none of what makes this a domain: no technical spec, no availability, no compound booking. They sit here because they are place records, not because they belong to the machinery. |
| DT-02 | Trade records will stay fresh like venue records do | The curation loop (16.05) applies to every place record | ❌ REJECTED — and this is the feature's structural weakness. Venue and studio freshness is driven by the **post-transaction harvest** (16.05.06): a gig happened, so ask the band. Trades have no on-platform transaction, so there is no harvest trigger and nothing resets the decay clock. Trade records will rot faster than anything else in the registry, and no mechanism currently proposed fixes that. |
| DT-03 | This feature earns its place on the thesis | D-18: provenance is the wedge, consolidation is the platform | ❌ REJECTED: it contributes nothing to provenance (a luthier does not generate a credit or a split) and little to daily consolidation (a neck reset is a once-every-three-years event). It is coverage-driven — the honest MoSCoW read is `could`, and it is the first thing to cut. |
| DT-04 | A mobile tech with no premises is a trade | They do the same work as the luthier with a shop | ❌ REJECTED: no address means no place record. The address is the whole reason this domain owns trades. A mobile tech is a service provider (05) — the discriminator is clean and should be enforced, or this domain slowly absorbs all of 05. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Trades are place records with a trade profile, not a technical spec | They have no stage, no signal chain, no acoustics — the spec sheets do not apply |
| D-02 | The **address test** is the boundary with 05 Services | Premises = trade; no premises = service provider. Without this, 16 absorbs 05 (DT-04) |
| D-03 | Most trades are enquiry-only; no calendar is expected | Directory-only is the correct and honest model for this member |
| D-04 | Recorded as the domain's weakest child; proposed `could` | No provenance value, no daily hook, and no freshness mechanism (DT-02, DT-03) |
