## Overview

The entity layer of the registry: what a place record *is*, what a room *is*, and the facts that
are true of every place type regardless of whether it has a stage or a mic locker. Everything else
in domain 16 hangs off this — the venue spec (16.02), the studio spec (16.03), the curation
machinery (16.05) and the booking engine (16.06) all operate on the record and room defined here.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: the record, the room, the media, the accessibility
profile, the lifecycle status and the statutory records are distinct capabilities that interact
directly with each other — the room composes into the record, photos evidence the accessibility
claim, statutory conditions constrain the spec, and status cascades from the place to every room's
calendar. Six capabilities, mutually dependent.
