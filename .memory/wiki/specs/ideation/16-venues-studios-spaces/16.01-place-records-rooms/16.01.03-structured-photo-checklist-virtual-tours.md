# Feature: Structured Photo Checklist & Virtual Tours

> **Number**: 16.01.03
> **Parent**: [Place Records & Rooms](./16.01-place-records-rooms-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Photography of a place, captured against a **required shot list** rather than as a free-for-all
gallery. Every venue and studio listing on the internet shows the same three flattering wide
shots and none of the four pictures a band actually needs: the load-in door, the stage from the
back of the room, the patch bay, and the parking. A structured checklist turns photos from
marketing into **spec evidence** — and photos are the cheapest way to verify a claim that text
cannot ("the load-in is step-free").

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Operator | Full | Uploads against a per-room-type shot list, sees which required shots are missing and how that suppresses their listing completeness. Can add free-form marketing shots beyond the checklist. Can commission/embed a virtual tour. |
| Musician | Read-only + contribute | Views the checklist gallery when deciding to book. **Can contribute photos after a booking** (16.05.06) — a band's load-in photo is worth more than the Operator's, because it is not selling anything. |
| Producer | Read-only + contribute | Reads the patch bay / desk / mic locker shots as spec evidence — for a Producer these photos ARE the spec check. Contributes post-session. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees venue marketing shots and the accessibility-relevant shots (entrance, step-free route) on the public show page. Does not see load-in, patch bay, or back-of-house. |

## Behavior

Each room type has a **required shot list** derived from what the corresponding spec claims:
- Venue room: stage from FOH, room from stage, load-in door (with a scale reference), FOH position, green room, merch position, accessible entrance
- Studio room: live room wide, control room / desk, patch bay, mic locker, isolation booths, piano/backline
- Rehearsal room: room wide, backline in place, access door, parking

Photos are **tagged to the spec field they evidence**, which is what makes them do work: the
load-in photo attaches to the load-in field (16.02.03) and its freshness (16.05.05) is the load-in
field's freshness. A structured photo is a verification artefact, not decoration.

Virtual tours are an **embed**, not a build. `[PENDING — Step 5]`: whether tours are supported at
all at launch is a MoSCoW question; the hosting decision is routed to `/create-prd`.

### Happy Path

1. Operator opens their room; sees a shot list with 7 required shots, 2 captured
2. Operator photographs the load-in door on their phone at the dock, uploads in place
3. System prompts for the field tag (pre-selected from the checklist slot)
4. Listing completeness increases; the load-in field's freshness clock resets
5. Missing-shot prompts persist until the list is complete

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Operator uploads a flattering wide shot into every slot | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — no automated shot-type validation is assumed. Community flagging (16.05.03) is the only proposed control | — |
| Photo contradicts the text spec (stairs visible on a "step-free" load-in) | Community can flag the field; enters conflict resolution (16.05.04) | Field shows contested marker |
| Photos are years old; the room has been refitted | Photo freshness decays with the field it evidences (16.05.05) | "Photos last verified 3 years ago" |
| Photo contains people / performers | `[PENDING]` — likeness and privacy handling is a cross-cut, not a domain rule | — |
| Band photo shows something the Operator does not want public (a mess, a broken monitor) | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — Operator take-down rights vs community evidence is unresolved and is the same tension as 16.05.04 | — |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty | No photos | Placeholder + "This room has no photos — listings with the full shot list get more enquiries" |
| Partial | Some required shots present | Gallery + explicit "missing: load-in, green room" |
| Complete | Full shot list captured | Full gallery, completeness badge |
| Stale | Shot list complete but decayed | Gallery with "last verified" ages per shot |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Photos are marketing and belong to the Operator | Every existing venue/studio directory treats them this way | ❌ REJECTED: the Operator's anti-persona behaviour is "misrepresents the room's specs to win bookings". Photos supplied solely by the party with an incentive to flatter are not evidence. The checklist plus **post-booking contribution by the band** is what converts photos into verification — the contributor with no incentive is the credible one. |
| DT-02 | A required shot list is a chore Operators will refuse | Operators are on a phone at a loading dock; extra admin is a defection trigger | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it changes the design. Capture must be phone-native, one shot at a time, in place, with the field tag pre-selected. If it requires a desk session with a folder of photos it will not happen. Also the incentive must be visible: completeness → enquiries. |
| DT-03 | Virtual tours are a differentiator worth building | Matterport-style tours are impressive and studios love them | ❌ REJECTED for this pass: a tour answers "is it nice?" — a question the market already answers. It does not answer "will my show fit?", which is the unbuilt asset. Tours are an embed at most; the hosting/streaming decision is architecture, routed out. Structured photos beat tours on the thesis. |
| DT-04 | Photos should be tagged to spec fields, not to rooms | Tagging is extra effort per upload | ✅ CONFIRMED: an untagged gallery cannot evidence anything. Tagging is what lets a photo carry the field's freshness and lets a contradiction (stairs in a "step-free" shot) be detectable at all. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Photos are captured against a per-room-type **required shot list** | Free galleries produce marketing; checklists produce spec evidence (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Photos are tagged to the spec field they evidence | Untagged photos cannot carry freshness or surface contradictions (DT-04) |
| D-03 | Bands/Producers can contribute photos post-booking | The contributor with no sales incentive is the credible one (DT-01) |
| D-04 | Virtual tours are an embed, not a build | Tours answer "is it nice?"; the domain's asset is "will my show fit?" (DT-03) |
