# Feature: Backline List Publication

> **Number**: 15.07.03
> **Parent**: [Studio & Backline Asset Register](./15.07-studio-backline-asset-register-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

Publishing a filtered slice of a room's asset register to its public listing on domain 16 — what
gear the room has, at the detail a visiting band needs and no more. It exists to fix a failure
personas.md names outright: the Operator "publishes a stale gear list, so a band arrives to a room
that can't run their show." The fix is structural rather than motivational: **the listing reads the
register instead of duplicating it**, so there is no second list to go stale.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | The reader this exists for. Reads a room's backline before booking and before advancing, and needs it to be *true* — which is the one thing a hand-maintained list never is. Sees models and condition; never serials. |
| Producer | Config | Configures publication on the entity's behalf. Also a reader when booking a room for a session, where the stakes are precise: does that specific outboard exist and work. |
| Operator | Full | Chooses which assets are public and at what detail. Then stops maintaining anything — the list derives itself, which is the entire point. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees a published backline on a public venue page (domain 16) if they scroll that far. "Steinway D" means something to some fans. They see model names only, like everyone outside the building. |

## Behavior

Publication is a **projection** of the register (15.07.01) filtered by condition (15.07.02). The
Operator selects what's public once; thereafter the list derives. An asset sold, disposed of, or
broken changes the public list without anyone remembering to do anything — which is the only
mechanism that survives contact with a busy Operator.

**Model published, serial withheld.** A public list of a room's vintage mic collection, attached to
an address and opening hours, is a shopping list with directions. The visiting band needs to know
it's a U47; nobody outside the building needs its serial, and publishing it would create an
exposure the Operator never asked for. This is not the Operator's choice to make — the platform
creates the exposure, so the platform withholds the serial.

**Items and condition, never terms (D-04).** The projection states what the room has and what state
it is in. It does not state whether that gear is included in the room fee, hireable as an extra, or
something the band must bring: the provision posture is typed per category on the room spec
([16.04](../../16-venues-studios-spaces/16.04-rehearsal-practice-space-specification.md) D-08), and
the price and rate-card term belong to
[16.06.07](../../16-venues-studios-spaces/16.06-space-booking-reservations/16.06.07-rate-cards-inclusions-extras.md).
The corollary matters more than the rule: an empty or unpublished register renders as **unstated**,
never as "this room provides no backline". Reading `none` out of an absent read-through is the
overstatement domain 15 D-06 forbids, and it is why the posture is typed rather than inferred.

**Every room type reads through separately.** Venue (16.02.02 D-06), studio (16.03.02 D-04/D-05) and
rehearsal (16.04 D-06) each confirmed the read-through for itself, and each writes new assets into
the register through an inline minimal-detail create path rather than holding a list. Rehearsal
backline in particular renders from this feature filtered by condition (15.07.02), including
quantity-tracked lines as counts — 16.04 types no item list at all.

### Happy Path

1. Operator finishes their asset register (15.07.01) and ticks the assets worth publishing — the console, the pianos, the backline, the notable outboard.
2. Public listing on domain 16 renders from the register: models, counts, condition.
3. Engineer logs a fault (15.07.02); the public list updates itself the same night. Nobody remembered to do anything, because nobody had to.
4. Visiting Musician reads the list while advancing and sees the amp is out for service — six weeks before the show, when it's a phone call rather than a crisis.
5. They bring their own, or use the compatibility oracle (15.06.02) against what's actually there.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Asset breaks | Public list reflects it automatically | Condition surfaced without an Operator action — the mechanism, not a policy |
| Asset sold or disposed | Vanishes from the listing | No stale entry survives, because there's no separate entry to survive |
| Operator wants to hide a fault | The central tension | See 15.07.02 Q-01 — unresolved, and the default matters more than the control |
| Room has gear on loan (15.08) | Publishing it advertises someone else's property as the room's | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — and it may vanish when the owner takes it back |
| Serial visible in a published photo | The withholding is bypassed by the image | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — same hole as 15.04 Q-02 |
| Commodity gear (40 stands, 200 cables) | Quantity, not identity | Publishes as counts with count-level condition — "4 practice amps, 1 faulty" — per 15.07.01 D-03 and 15.07.02 D-04 (ratified from DT-03) |
| Room has no register, or nothing published | There is nothing to read through | Renders **unstated**, not "no backline" — the provision posture is 16.04's typed field (16.04 D-08), never inferred from an absent projection |
| Operator has no org entity | This feature publishes org registers only | No publication path exists at all — a structural exclusion of real supply, not a cold start. See sub-domain Q-04 and 16.03.02 DT-12 |
| Band advanced against a list that changed after | Both parties' documents were true when exchanged | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — mirrors 15.06.03 DT-03 exactly, pointing the other way |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Listing rendering | Skeleton on the venue page's gear section |
| Empty | Nothing published | Venue page shows no gear section — honest; better than an empty promise. Reads as *unstated*, never as "this room provides no backline" (D-04) |
| Populated | Assets published | Model list with condition, no serials; quantity-tracked lines render as counts with count-level condition |
| Error | Register unreachable | Show the last known state **with its age**, never a blank that reads as "no gear" |
| Partial | Some assets published, some private | Only the published slice; the register's size is not disclosed |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The venue listing should carry its own gear list, and the register can sync to it | The obvious architecture: domain 16 owns the listing, so it owns the listing's content | ❌ REJECTED — **this reproduces the exact failure the feature exists to fix.** A duplicated list rots by construction: two stores, two update paths, and one of them is maintained by a busy person at a loading dock with no reason to think about it. personas.md names the Operator's accidental anti-behaviour as publishing a stale gear list, and "sync" is just a slower version of the same thing — sync failures are silent and nobody notices a list that's quietly six months old. The only structural fix is that **there is nothing separate to go stale**: the listing is a projection of the register. This is the single strongest argument for the sub-domain's existence, and if it were rejected, domain 16 could just carry free text and 15.07 would be optional (see Q-01 on the sub-domain index). Ratified as D-01. |
| DT-02 | Publishing the register is straightforwardly good — more information helps everyone | The transparency instinct | ❌ REJECTED — a room's full register is a **theft target**. Published in full it says: this address holds £200k of vintage microphones, here are the models, here are the serials, and here are our opening hours and our quiet Tuesdays. Venue theft is endemic (personas.md) and frequently insider-adjacent — the people who rob venues are often people who have been in them. This is the same composition hazard as 15.04 DT-01 (value + location + absence), but *worse*, because a venue's address is necessarily public and its schedule is its product. Publication must be a deliberate subset, serials withheld unconditionally, and the register's total size not disclosed. |
| DT-03 | The Operator should decide whether to publish serials | Their gear, their room, their call — consistent with treating them as the owner | ❌ REJECTED — the exposure is created by **the platform**, in a place the Operator cannot see the consequences of, and the benefit to them is zero. No visiting band has ever needed a serial to advance a show; the only parties served by a public serial are a thief cloning it onto a fake (15.01.01 DT-04) and a thief shopping. Offering the choice means some Operators tick it — probably the ones with the best gear, who are proudest of it. Serials are withheld unconditionally. This is one of the few places in the domain where the platform overrides the owner's choice, and it is justified because the platform, not the owner, is the party creating the hazard. Ratified as D-03 on the sub-domain index. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The listing reads the register; it never duplicates it | Per DT-01. Any design with two stores rots, and "sync" is a slow rot with silent failures. |
| D-02 | Serials are withheld unconditionally, not by Operator choice | Per DT-03. The platform creates the exposure and no visiting band needs a serial. |
| D-03 | On a register error, show last-known state **with its age** — never a blank | A blank gear section reads as "this room has no gear", which is worse than a stale list honestly labelled as stale. |
| D-04 | The projection carries **items and condition only** — never the provision posture and never the price | Owner decision 2026-07-22 (DQ-15.A3), stated here as the explicit boundary it requires: **16.06.07 owns the price and the rate-card term; 16.04 owns the provision posture** (`included` · `hire-extra` · `bring-your-own-only` · `none`, with `unstated` a distinct non-value); this feature owns the items and their state. Without the sentence written down the two enums overlap. The rule that makes it necessary: an empty register renders `unstated`, so `bring-your-own-only` is not assertable by inference, and "included" is indistinguishable from an Operator who has never built a rate card. See sub-domain D-07. |
| D-05 | Rehearsal rooms publish through this feature like every other room type | Owner decision 2026-07-22 (DQ-15.A1). 16.04 D-06 renders backline from here filtered by 15.07.02 condition and types no item list — a per-feature confirmation of D-01 in the sibling convention, following 16.02.02 D-06 and 16.03.02 D-04/D-05. It resolves nothing about the 15.07-vs-16 boundary (sub-domain Q-01, domain Q-07), and it inherits DT-01's reason: a local list would be a second store with a second update path, rotting by construction. |
