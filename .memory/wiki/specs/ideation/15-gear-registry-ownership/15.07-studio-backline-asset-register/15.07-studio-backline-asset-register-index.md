## Overview

The organisation's side of the registry: gear owned by a studio, venue or rehearsal space rather
than by a person, with the condition and availability state that determines whether it can actually
be used, and the publication path that puts it on the room's listing. It exists because an
Operator's inventory is a *business asset with a service history and a value*, not a bullet point
in a room description — and because the domain's rationale names "studio asset registers" as one of
the uses that justify a registry existing with no transaction attached.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: registering an org's assets, tracking their condition
and availability, and publishing a subset to a public listing are three capabilities that feed each
other continuously — condition gates what's publishable, publication is a projection of the
register, and the register is worthless to the business if the published view drifts from it.

>
> This is the one sub-domain in domain 15 where the four personas genuinely diverge:
> - **Operator** owns it — it's their business inventory.
> - **Producer** gets ⚙️ Config because the engineer who works in a room is routinely the person who knows the Neve's channel 7 is dead, without owning the room. Staff access is a role on the **entity**, not on the asset (see D-02).
> - **Musician** reads a published backline list as a visiting player deciding whether the room can run their show.
> - **Fan** sees a published backline list only where it appears on a public venue page (domain 16) — a fan browsing gigs may see "Steinway D" and care; they see nothing else here.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The room listing (domain 16) **reads** the register; it does not duplicate it | personas.md names the Operator's accidental anti-behaviour precisely: "publishes a stale gear list, so a band arrives to a room that can't run their show." A duplicated list rots by construction — there is no version of "remember to update both" that works. Single source of truth is the only structural fix. | Deep Think DT-01 on 15.07.03 |
| D-02 | Asset access is a role on the **entity**, not a permission on the asset | Studio staff need to log a fault on the console without owning it. Per-asset permissions for a room with 200 assets and 6 staff is unadministerable and would be abandoned. | Deep Think DT-02 on 15.07.01 |
| D-03 | Publication shows model, withholds serial | A public list of a room's vintage mic collection with an address attached is a shopping list with directions. The visiting band needs to know it's a U47; nobody outside the building needs its serial. | Deep Think DT-03 on 15.07.03 |
| D-04 | This sub-domain owns "the asset exists and works"; domains 16/17 own "it's free on Tuesday" | Availability-as-condition (is it functional?) and availability-as-booking (is it reserved?) are different questions with different owners. Conflating them would drag the booking calendar into the registry. | Deep Think DT-03 on 15.07.02 |
