## Overview

The organisation's side of the registry: gear owned by a studio, venue or rehearsal space rather
than by a person, with the condition and availability state that determines whether it can actually
be used, and the publication path that puts it on the room's listing. It exists because an
Operator's inventory is a *business asset with a service history and a value*, not a bullet point
in a room description — and because the domain's rationale names "studio asset registers" as one of
the uses that justify a registry existing with no transaction attached.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: registering an org's assets, tracking their condition
and availability, and publishing a subset to a public listing are three capabilities that feed each
other continuously — condition gates what's publishable, publication is a projection of the
register, and the register is worthless to the business if the published view drifts from it.

> **Legend**: ✅ Full access · ⚙️ Configuration only · 👁️ Read-only · 📊 Reports only · ❌ No access
>
> This is the one sub-domain in domain 15 where the four personas genuinely diverge:
> - **Operator** owns it — it's their business inventory.
> - **Producer** gets ⚙️ Config because the engineer who works in a room is routinely the person who knows the Neve's channel 7 is dead, without owning the room. Staff access is a role on the **entity**, not on the asset (see D-02).
> - **Musician** reads a published backline list as a visiting player deciding whether the room can run their show.
> - **Fan** sees a published backline list only where it appears on a public venue page (domain 16) — a fan browsing gigs may see "Steinway D" and care; they see nothing else here.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The room listing (domain 16) **reads** the register; it does not duplicate it | personas.md names the Operator's accidental anti-behaviour precisely: "publishes a stale gear list, so a band arrives to a room that can't run their show." A duplicated list rots by construction — there is no version of "remember to update both" that works. Single source of truth is the only structural fix. | Deep Think DT-01 on 15.07.03 |
| D-02 | Asset access is a role on the **entity**, not a permission on the asset | Studio staff need to log a fault on the console without owning it. Per-asset permissions for a room with 200 assets and 6 staff is unadministerable and would be abandoned. | Deep Think DT-02 on 15.07.01 |
| D-03 | Publication shows model, withholds serial | A public list of a room's vintage mic collection with an address attached is a shopping list with directions. The visiting band needs to know it's a U47; nobody outside the building needs its serial. | Deep Think DT-03 on 15.07.03 |
| D-04 | This sub-domain owns "the asset exists and works"; domains 16/17 own "it's free on Tuesday" | Availability-as-condition (is it functional?) and availability-as-booking (is it reserved?) are different questions with different owners. Conflating them would drag the booking calendar into the registry. | Deep Think DT-03 on 15.07.02 |
| D-05 | The read-through (D-01) is confirmed **per room type**, not once for domain 16 | 16.04 rehearsal rooms renders backline from 15.07.03 filtered by 15.07.02 condition and types no item list of its own (16.04 D-06) — the third sibling to confirm D-01 locally, after 16.02.02 D-06 (venue stage/PA/backline) and 16.03.02 D-04/D-05 (studio signal chain and mic locker). A local list in 16.04 would have contradicted D-01 and D-04 rather than filled a gap, and would have needed a condition store, a fault-reporting surface and a permission model no source defines. This is a per-feature confirmation in the sibling convention — it does **not** resolve Q-01 or domain Q-07, both of which remain open and Owner=User. | Owner decision 2026-07-22 (DQ-15.A1) |
| D-06 | The register carries a **quantity-tracked** mode for commodity stock alongside identity-tracked assets, with identity-tracking retained per item where the Operator wants it | **Resolves Q-03.** 15.07.01 DT-03 proposed exactly this for exactly this room type — 40 mic stands, 200 cables, four interchangeable practice amps gain nothing from per-object identity while the registration cost is real. Two specs already publish counts rather than identity and both cite DT-03 as settled: 15.07.03's commodity edge case and 16.03.02's commodity row. Identity-only would load the heaviest onboarding on the lowest-value room type. This is a genuine model addition inside domain 15 (domain D-09), not a UI concession, and it degrades condition to a fact about a count — see 15.07.02 D-04. | 15.07.01 DT-03; owner decision 2026-07-22 (DQ-15.A2) |
| D-07 | This sub-domain owns the **items and their condition**; it owns neither the provision posture nor the price | The room spec types the per-category provision posture — `included` · `hire-extra` · `bring-your-own-only` · `none`, with `unstated` a distinct non-value (16.04 D-08) — and 16.06.07 owns the price and the rate-card term. 15.07 publishes what exists and what state it is in; it never states whether a kit is included in the room fee or must be brought, and an empty or unpublished register renders as *unstated*, never as "this room provides no backline". Without this line stated explicitly the two enums overlap. | Owner decision 2026-07-22 (DQ-15.A3) |

## Open Questions

| # | Question | Owner | Deferred To |
|---|----------|-------|-------------|
| Q-01 | **Boundary with domain 16**: a venue/studio listing could simply carry a free-text gear list, and for many Operators that would be enough. What this sub-domain adds is serial identity, service history, valuation and custody — real, but is it wanted by an Operator who just wants to list a room? If not, 16 owns a simple list and this sub-domain serves only the minority who want asset management. **Still open** — 16.04 confirmed the read-through for rehearsal rooms locally (D-05), which is a per-feature confirmation, not a resolution of the boundary. | User | `/ideate-validate` |
| Q-02 | Is a **dealer's stock register** the same thing as a studio's asset register? Consignment stock (15.08) is org-held, not org-owned, and dealers have no persona (personas.md Q-01). If dealers are in scope, this sub-domain grows a second, quite different consumer. | User | `/ideate-validate` |
| Q-03 | ~~Does a rehearsal space's backline need per-item identity at all, or is "4 amps, 2 kits" sufficient?~~ **RESOLVED** — quantity-tracked mode for commodity stock, with identity-tracking retained per item where the Operator wants it. See D-06. | User | ✅ owner decision (DQ-15.A2) |
| Q-04 | **Supply with no org entity cannot publish at all.** This sub-domain registers and publishes **org** assets — the owner is an entity and access is a role on it (D-02) — so an Operator who never creates an org (the sole-trader rehearsal room, the bedroom studio) has no register to read through and, since the read-through is the only publication path (D-01, D-05), no way onto a room listing at all. 16.03.02 DT-12 found the same exclusion from the other side. This is a structural exclusion of a real population, not an onboarding inconvenience. Does the entity model admit a single-person org, does this sub-domain admit a person-owned register, or is that supply out of scope? | User | `/create-prd`, with domain 01 |
