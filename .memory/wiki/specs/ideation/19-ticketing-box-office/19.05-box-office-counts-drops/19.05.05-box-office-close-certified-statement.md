# Feature: Box Office Close & Certified Statement

> **Number**: 19.05.05
> **Parent**: [Box Office Counts, Drops & Day-of-Show](./19.05-box-office-counts-drops-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The moment the night's numbers stop being observations and become an attested fact that money is computed from. The box office closes: the window stops selling, the scanners reconcile, every counter freezes, and the parties in the room certify the result **while they are still in the room**.

**This is the domain's thesis feature.** The parent domain's rationale says the domain exists because *"even if WeJammin never sells a ticket, it must ingest box office data or settlement is fiction"* — and this is where the fiction stops. The platform's thesis (D-18) is that provenance means capturing the fact at the moment it is true, because the alternative is reconstruction from memory once the money is contested. A box office statement is that argument in its purest form: tonight it costs one signature from two people standing ten feet apart; in six weeks it costs an argument, and it may be unobtainable at any price.

**Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep candidate list.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only + counter-attest | Sees the certified statement — the actual document, not a summary of it — because it is what their money is computed from, and **counter-attests it** (in the room, or async within a deadline per D-07). This is a confirm/decline write, not editing authority — they cannot alter a counter. Whether their counter (or a lapse of it) *gates* settlement is the residual policy question (Q-01). Today the artist typically sees a settlement sheet after the fact and takes it on trust; here they attest to the same object the Operator did. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. No box-office surface. The Producer is the platform's trust broker *in a studio*, not at a box office. |
| Operator | Full | Runs the close: stops the window, confirms device reconciliation, reviews the count, certifies. Note the conflict of interest — they certify the number that determines what they pay out (Q-01). |
| Fan | None | Never sees this. It is the parties' commercial record. |

## Behavior

The close is a **sequence with a gate at each step**, not a button:

1. **Quiesce** — the window stops selling (19.05.04); no new writes to paid.
2. **Reconcile** — every scanning device must have pushed its log (19.04.02 D-04). An unreconciled device means scanned is short by an unknown number, and a statement certified on an unknown number is worthless.
3. **Resolve** — outstanding items surface: door-time over-allocation adds (19.03.03), admitted-but-refunded tickets, double-scan conflicts, cash float discrepancy.
4. **Freeze** — every counter becomes immutable.
5. **Certify** — the statement is attested, timestamped and attributed to the human who certified it.
6. **Hand off** — the statement becomes the input to settlement (domain 17).

The statement carries paid, comp, scanned, killed, gross, fees by recipient, walk-up split by payment method, over-allocation adds attributed to whoever authorised them, and the reconciliation exceptions — each as a distinct line, none aggregated away.

### Happy Path

1. Show ends. Operator initiates close.
2. Window is confirmed shut; walk-up can no longer write.
3. System checks every device: all reconciled. (If not, close blocks — see edge cases.)
4. System surfaces the night's exceptions: 2 door-time comps authorised by Dave at 19:42 against the artist's allocation; 1 double-scan at the side door; float short by £5.
5. Operator reviews and resolves or annotates each.
6. Counters freeze. Statement renders — every line, no aggregation.
7. Operator certifies. The Musician, still in the building, sees the same document and (per Q-01) counter-attests.
8. Statement hands off to settlement (17). The number is now a fact with two names on it.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| A scanning device is unreconciled | Scanned is short by an unknown amount | **Close blocks.** Certifying a partial count is certifying a wrong number, which is worse than certifying late (19.04.02 D-04). Blocked state names the exact device and its last-seen time; certify is disabled, not merely warned |
| A device is lost/dead and will never reconcile | The count cannot be completed | Close proceeds only via an explicit **written-off exception** naming the device, its last reconciled scan count, and the unknown gap. The statement carries that line verbatim — an honest gap beats a confident fabrication. Write-off requires a typed reason string; it cannot be an empty acknowledgement |
| Door-time comp added after freeze (19.03.03) | Mutates a certified number | Rejected. If the change is legitimate, the certified statement is **superseded, not edited** — a new version is certified, the prior version retained immutably, and every party who saw v1 is notified that v2 exists and why. See `19.03-guest-list-comps-cx.md#CX-03` |
| The Operator certifies a number the Musician disputes | The whole reason for certification | Statement enters **Disputed** — a first-class certified state, not a block. The disagreement is recorded *tonight* with the evidence live (DT-02), and hands off to settlement (17) and Trust & Safety (24) as a dispute, not reconstructed later |
| Externally-ticketed show (19.07) | Paid comes from an ingested count that may not have arrived yet, or may be wrong | The statement states source and freshness per line (19.05.01 D-02) and certifies the attestation, e.g. "260 as reported by DICE at 21:04; door scanned 251; both parties accepted 260 for settlement." See DT-03 |
| External count has not arrived at close time | A required line is unpopulated | Close enters **Partial** and cannot certify a `[FINAL]` statement; the Operator may certify a **provisional** statement flagged `awaiting-external-source`, which settlement (17) must not treat as final. The missing source is named on the line, not hidden |
| Statement certified, then a refund is processed (19.06.01) | The certified number is now historically true but currently false | Correct — the statement records the close, not a live figure. Post-close refunds are a settlement adjustment (17), never a statement edit (D-06) |
| Nobody closes the box office | The count drifts; settlement runs on an uncertified number; the platform has changed nothing | The close must be the path of least resistance, not an extra chore (DT-01, D-01). If a show ends and no close is initiated within 30 minutes of the scheduled show-end time (default), the Operator is prompted; if still no close by the settlement run, the uncertified count is flagged to settlement as **uncertified**, never silently promoted to fact |
| **Musician has already left the building (they usually have)** | Synchronous in-room counter-attestation is impossible | The Operator certifies as the single attesting party; the statement enters **Awaiting counter-attestation** and is delivered to the Musician (or the booked entity's authorised delegate — band, manager, agency per domain 01) with the full artifact, a diff against any drop they last saw, and a deadline (default 72h, or the settlement run, whichever is sooner). On the deadline the statement records **counter-attestation lapsed** — silence is captured *as silence on the record*, never as agreement. Whether a lapse still gates settlement is the residual policy question (Q-01); the async mechanism itself is decided (D-07, DT-04) |
| Certifier is also the counterparty — Musician is the Operator (self-promoted show, artist-run room) | The two attesting roles collapse into one human (multi-hyphenate) | Permitted, but the statement records that certifier and counterparty are the **same identity** and stamps it `self-certified` — a single-signature fact, not a two-name one. Counter-attestation is auto-satisfied only when the booked entity and the operating entity are provably the same (domain 01); if the artist is one member of a band that is the booked entity, the *other* members remain valid counter-attesters and are notified (D-08) |
| A non-Operator attempts to initiate or certify the close | Certification authority is scoped | Rejected — the attempt is refused and the actor told they are **not authorised to certify**. Only an identity with Operator-Full on this event may certify; Musician Read-only may *view and counter-attest*, never certify; Producer and Fan have no surface here |
| Two Operator-role users hit **Certify** on the same statement concurrently | Double-certification race | First attest wins; the second is told the statement is **already certified** and shown the winning certifier's name and timestamp. Certification is idempotent on statement version — the second user lands on the certified artifact, not an error |
| Network/write failure mid-certification | The attest commit does not complete | Certification is atomic: either the statement is certified with attribution and timestamp, or it remains **Frozen** and un-certified. A half-written attestation is never persisted; the Operator sees "certification did not complete — retry" with counters still frozen, so no data is lost and no partial fact is created |
| Operator freezes, then self-authorises comps to depress payable gross before certifying (malicious) | An attested number manufactured against the counterparty | Every comp and over-allocation add carries an author and a timestamp (19.03.03) and is a **distinct line** (D-05), never netted into gross. Comps authored after the manifest freeze appear on the statement as post-freeze adds by that author — visible to the Musician on the same artifact they counter-attest, which is precisely what makes the manipulation detectable rather than buried |
| Zero-sale / fully-papered close (no paid tickets, or an entirely comped night) | There is still a night to certify | Close runs normally and certifies a valid statement whose paid line is `0`; comp, scanned, killed and float lines still render. An **empty** close is a certified fact ("this show sold nothing / was fully comped"), not a skipped one — the absence is itself the record settlement needs |
| Cash float short | Common | Recorded on the statement as its own line with the shortfall amount and float-count time, never netted into gross (D-05) |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Open | Show live | Close not available; counters mutable |
| Closing (Loading) | Operator initiated; system running reconciliation checks | Per-step progress with each gate named; the sequence, not a spinner |
| Blocked (Error) | A device unreconciled, or a required external source absent | Exactly which device or source, its last-seen time, and the one action that clears it — walk it into signal, or write it off with a reason |
| Exceptions pending (Partial) | Reconciliation surfaced conflicts (comps, double-scans, float) | Each exception listed with its cost and a resolve/annotate action; certify disabled until each is addressed |
| Empty | Reconciliation complete, paid count is `0` | A valid zero/comped statement ready to certify — the absence is the fact, not a dead end |
| Frozen (Populated) | All gates cleared; counters immutable | The full statement renders, every line distinct, nothing aggregated away |
| Certified | Attested by the Operator | Immutable artifact with certifier name, role, and timestamp |
| Awaiting counter-attestation | Musician not present at certify time | Statement delivered to the Musician/delegate with a deadline; Operator sees "sent, awaiting counter-attestation, due {timestamp}" |
| Counter-attested | Musician (or valid band delegate) confirmed | Two names on the fact |
| Counter-attestation lapsed | Deadline passed with no response | Recorded on the statement as lapsed — silence as silence, not consent; settlement gating per Q-01 |
| Self-certified | Certifier and counterparty are the same identity (multi-hyphenate) | Single-signature statement, stamped `self-certified` |
| Disputed | A party rejected the statement | **Captured as part of the record** — the dispute is a fact about tonight (DT-02); routes to settlement (17) and Trust & Safety (24) |
| Certification failed (Error) | Write failed mid-attest | "Certification did not complete — retry"; counters still Frozen, no partial fact persisted |
| Superseded | A legitimate post-freeze change forced re-certification | Prior version retained immutably; current version flagged, all v1 viewers notified |
| Handed off | Settlement consumed it | Statement is the settlement's cited input |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The close must be **the lazy path** — the easiest way to finish the night, not an extra step at the end of it | Producer persona's worst-accidental — *"closes a project without capturing splits ('we'll do it later') — the exact failure the platform exists to prevent, committed by the persona best placed to prevent it. **The design must make the lazy path the correct path**"*; Operator's *"settlement is arithmetic the system already did"* | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is the single most important sentence in this file. The persona doc states the platform's central design law and this feature is where it applies most literally. At 23:40 the Operator wants to go home. If certifying is a form they fill in after the last person leaves, it will not happen — and a domain that only works when a tired person does optional admin is a domain that does not work. The close must be *how you end the night*: the thing that turns the lights off, prints the float count, and tells you you are done. It should be pulling the Operator toward it, not waiting for them. If we get this wrong, everything else in this sub-domain is decoration on a number nobody certified. |
| DT-02 | A disputed statement is a failure of the close | Instinct — certification means agreement, so disagreement means the flow broke | ❌ REJECTED, and the inversion is the point. A dispute captured tonight, with both parties present, the scanners' logs live, the float on the table and the door staff still in the building, is a **success** — it is the platform doing exactly what `problem-statement.md` says nothing currently does: being present when the fact becomes contested, while the evidence still exists and the relationship is still functional. The failure is not disagreement; the failure is a statement certified by one party at midnight, unread by the other, that becomes an argument six weeks later when nobody remembers whether the side door was scanning. So the flow must have a first-class *disputed* state that records the disagreement as part of the record, rather than blocking the close until someone gives in. Forcing agreement at 23:40 would just produce coerced signatures, which is a worse artifact than an honest disagreement. |
| DT-03 | The certified statement is only meaningful when WeJammin sold the tickets | Reasonable-sounding scope limit — how can we certify a number we did not produce? | ❌ REJECTED — and this rejection is what makes the parent domain's claim true. The parent's rationale is that the domain exists *even if WeJammin never sells a ticket*, and this feature is why. On an externally-ticketed show the statement certifies something different but not less valuable: **what was reported, by whom, when, and what the parties agreed it meant** — "DICE reported 260 at 21:04; the door counted 251 scanned; both parties accepted 260 for settlement." That is a certified fact about an ingested number, and it is exactly the artifact that does not exist today. The provenance is of the *attestation*, not of the sale. Scoping this feature to WeJammin-sold inventory would concede the domain's whole argument and leave settlement a fiction on every show the platform did not ticket — which, early on, is all of them. |
| DT-04 | Counter-attestation must be **synchronous and in the room** — both parties looking at the same object at 23:40 — or it is worthless | The thesis's purest form: the fact attested by both parties *at the moment it is true*, while the evidence is live (D-18, problem-statement.md) | ❌ REJECTED as a hard requirement, though it remains the ideal. The Musician has almost always left by close — the Operator's persona note and the artist's own workflow both put the artist gone before the float is counted. If counter-attestation is *only* valid synchronously, then in the overwhelming common case it never happens, and the feature degrades to single-party certification — the exact failure DT-01 warns against, dressed as rigour. The resolution is a **provenance-preserving async fallback**: the Operator certifies as the single party immediately (the count is frozen and attributed *now*, not deferred), and the statement carries an explicit *awaiting-counter-attestation* state delivered to the Musician with a deadline. Critically, a lapsed deadline is recorded **as a lapse, not as agreement** — the record distinguishes "both parties attested," "one attested and the other declined," and "one attested and the other never responded" as three different facts. This keeps the mechanism honest without making it dependent on a tired person and an already-departed artist standing in the same room. What remains genuinely open is the *policy* on a lapse — does an un-countered statement still gate settlement? — which is a product decision (Q-01), not a mechanism gap. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The close is the path of least resistance to ending the night, not an end-of-night chore | The persona doc's own design law: make the lazy path the correct path. Optional admin by a tired person does not happen (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Close blocks while any device is unreconciled; a permanently-lost device requires an explicit written-off exception on the statement | Certifying an unknown is worse than certifying late; an honest gap beats a confident fabrication |
| D-03 | Disputed is a first-class certified state, recorded as part of the record | A dispute captured tonight with the evidence live is a success, not a failure. Forcing agreement at 23:40 produces coerced signatures (DT-02) |
| D-04 | The statement certifies externally-ticketed shows too — attesting what was reported, by whom, when, and what the parties accepted | Otherwise settlement stays a fiction on every show WeJammin did not ticket, which concedes the domain's rationale (DT-03) |
| D-05 | Every line is distinct — no aggregation. Over-allocation adds, float discrepancies and exceptions are never netted into gross | The netted-away line is always the one a disputing party needs |
| D-06 | Post-close refunds are settlement adjustments, never statement edits | The statement records the close; it is not a live figure |
| D-07 | When the Musician is absent at certify time, counter-attestation is **async with a deadline** (default 72h or the settlement run, whichever is sooner). The statement records three distinct outcomes — counter-attested, declined, and lapsed — never collapsing silence into consent | The artist has almost always left; a synchronous-only requirement means the feature never fires (DT-04). The mechanism is decided; only whether a lapse gates settlement remains open (Q-01) |
| D-08 | When certifier and counterparty are the same identity, the statement is `self-certified` (single-signature). When the artist is one member of the booked *entity* (band per domain 01), the other members remain valid counter-attesters and are notified | A multi-hyphenate running their own show cannot counter-sign themselves into a two-name fact; but a band is an entity, so its other members are real counterparties (personas.md structural fact; D-19) |
| D-09 | Certification is atomic and idempotent per statement version — concurrent certifies resolve first-write-wins, the loser being shown the already-certified artifact; a failed attest leaves the statement Frozen and un-certified, never half-attested | A partial or double attestation is a corrupt fact; the whole value of the artifact is that it is unambiguously attributed |
