## Overview

The numbers, who sees them, and the moment they become final. This sub-domain owns the live state of the room (sold, comped, gate-scanned, window-admitted, the derived admissions total, held, remaining — 19.05.01 D-09), the **drop** — the industry's daily sales report to the parties — the pacing read that says whether an on-sale is in trouble while there is still time to act, the day-of-show walk-up sale, and the **box office close**, where the night's numbers stop being observations and become the certified input to money.

**This is the sub-domain that justifies the parent domain's existence.** The parent's rationale states it plainly: *"even if WeJammin never sells a ticket, it must ingest box office data or settlement is fiction and draw intelligence is guesswork."* Everything here is the "or settlement is fiction" half.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: five capabilities interacting around one contended count. The live count is mutable state; the drop is an immutable snapshot of it that drives decisions; pacing is a derived read that triggers action on the manifest; walk-up mutates the count *during* the scanning window, when device replicas are already stale; and the close freezes it against writes that are still arriving. The interactions are races, not reads.
