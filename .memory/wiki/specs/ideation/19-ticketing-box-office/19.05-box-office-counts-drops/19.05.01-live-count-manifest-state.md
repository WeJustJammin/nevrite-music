# Feature: Live Count & Manifest State

> **Number**: 19.05.01
> **Parent**: [Box Office Counts, Drops & Day-of-Show](./19.05-box-office-counts-drops-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The current state of the room, as one number set that everything else reads: sold, comped, scanned, held, killed, remaining. This is the canonical count — the state that drops snapshot (19.05.02), pacing derives from (19.05.03), walk-up mutates (19.05.04), and the close freezes (19.05.05).

It exists as its own feature because the alternative — every consumer computing its own count from the manifest — guarantees that the artist's number, the venue's number and the fire officer's number disagree, and that nobody can say which is right.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Reports | Sees the count for their own show, scoped to what their deal grants — typically sold and remaining, sometimes gross. Sees it in the drop (19.05.02) rather than by watching a dashboard, because they are not sitting at a desk. On the night, sees scanned vs sold — the number that tells them how full the room actually is. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. No box-office surface. |
| Operator | Full | Sees every counter, live, including held and killed inventory and the held-inventory alert (19.01.02 DT-02). This is their operational instrument on the night. |
| Fan | None | Never sees the count. Deliberately: exposing "12 left!" is a scarcity-marketing pattern hostile to the Fan persona, and it is also frequently a lie once holds are considered. See D-04. |

## Behavior

The count is maintained as distinct counters — never one number with filters (19.03.02 D-02):

| Counter | Meaning | Consumed by |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid** | Sold, money received | Settlement (17). The door-split number |
| **Comp** | Issued free, inventory spent | Artist's guest-list cost (19.03.02) |
| **Scanned** | Physically walked in, deduplicated across gates | Fire officer; the artist's real room-fullness |
| **Held** | Reserved, unsold, not yet released | The held-inventory alert — the money nobody decided to lose |
| **Killed** | Removed permanently for physical reasons | Capacity reconciliation |
| **Remaining** | Sellable right now | The storefront |

Every counter carries a provenance: which writer moved it, when. Paid moves from sales and walk-up; comp from issuance; scanned from device reconciliation.

### Happy Path

1. Manifest locks (19.01); counters initialise from it — remaining = capacity − killed − held, everything else zero.
2. On-sale runs; paid and remaining move with each sale.
3. Guest list submissions issue comps; comp rises, remaining falls, paid unchanged.
4. Holds auto-release at their deadlines (19.01.02 D-01); held falls, remaining rises.
5. Show night: scanners push scans; scanned rises, deduplicated across gates (19.04.03).
6. Walk-up sells at the window; paid rises during the scanning window (`#CX-03`).
7. Doors close; devices reconcile (19.04.02); scanned reaches its final value.
8. Close (19.05.05) freezes every counter and certifies them.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Scanned exceeds paid + comp | Someone got in on a ticket that does not exist — a double-scan (19.04.03) or a counterfeit | Surfaced live to the Operator, not discovered at close. This is the count's most important alarm |
| Scanned well below paid + comp | Normal — no-shows, especially comps | Not an alarm; it is the number the artist actually wants |
| Held inventory high while remaining approaches zero | The forgotten-hold failure (19.01.02 DT-02) | Prominent alert while release still converts to revenue |
| Counters read during a write | An internally inconsistent snapshot destroys trust in the whole report | Snapshots must be consistent; see `#CX-01` |
| Device unreconciled at close | Scanned is short by an unknown amount | Close blocked (19.04.02 D-04) |
| Capacity reduced after sale | Paid may exceed the new capacity — an oversold room | See 19.01.02 Q-02; a real incident with no clean answer |
| External-ticketed show | Paid does not originate here at all; it is ingested (19.07) | Counters must carry a source; a count assembled from two rails must say so. See DT-02 |
| Count queried with no network by the Operator on the floor | The instrument is unusable exactly when it matters most | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — the door's offline story (19.04.02) does not currently extend to the count view |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Initialised | Manifest locked, not on sale | All counters at their starting values; remaining = sellable |
| Live | On sale | Counters moving; Operator sees the full set, Musician sees their scoped subset |
| Constrained | Remaining low, held high | Held-inventory alert with a release CTA |
| Show night | Doors open | Scanned becomes the live number; sold is now history |
| Reconciling | Doors closed, devices pushing | Scanned still settling; count marked provisional |
| Anomalous | Scanned > paid + comp | Alarm with the offending scans identified |
| Frozen | Close certified (19.05.05) | Counters immutable; the statement is the artifact |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Scanned exceeding paid + comp must alarm live, during the show** | Reasoning about which count anomaly is actionable while the show is happening | ✅ CONFIRMED — it is the only count anomaly with a same-night response. Every other discrepancy (no-shows, stale replicas, unreconciled devices) is a tomorrow-morning report. But scanned > paid + comp means people are in the room on tickets that do not exist — which is a capacity and licensing exposure before it is a revenue one, and the Operator can act on it *tonight* by tightening the door. Note the tension with 19.04.02 DT-01 ("reconciliation produces a report, not a repair"): that is true of individual conflicts, which cannot be undone. The *aggregate* is different — you cannot eject the person who double-scanned, but you can stop the next twenty. The aggregate alarm is actionable precisely because it is not about any individual scan. |
| DT-02 | The count is one number set, regardless of where the tickets were sold | Simplicity instinct — a room has one capacity, so it has one count | ❌ REJECTED — every counter needs a **source**. The parent domain exists partly because WeJammin must ingest foreign box-office data (19.07), which means a real show's paid count may be 140 from WeJammin plus 260 from DICE, arriving on a delay, possibly disagreeing with itself. A sourceless count silently blends an authoritative number with an ingested one and makes the resulting disagreement unattributable — which is precisely the reconciliation problem 19.07.03 exists to solve, made invisible. The count must always be able to say where each number came from and how fresh it is. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The count is one canonical state with distinct counters, not a per-consumer computation | Every consumer computing its own count guarantees the artist's and venue's numbers disagree with nobody able to say which is right |
| D-02 | Every counter carries a source and a freshness | A count blended from WeJammin and an ingested platform must be able to say which is which (DT-02) |
| D-03 | Scanned > paid + comp alarms live | The only count anomaly with a same-night response (DT-01) |
| D-04 | The count is never exposed to Fans | "12 left!" is scarcity marketing hostile to the Fan persona, and it is usually untrue once holds are counted |
