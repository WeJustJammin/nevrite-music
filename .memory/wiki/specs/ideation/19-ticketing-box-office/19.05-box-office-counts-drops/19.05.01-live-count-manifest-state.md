# Feature: Live Count & Manifest State

> **Number**: 19.05.01
> **Parent**: [Box Office Counts, Drops & Day-of-Show](./19.05-box-office-counts-drops-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The current state of the room, as one number set that everything else reads: paid, comped, scanned, held, killed, remaining. This is the canonical count — the state that drops snapshot (19.05.02), pacing derives from (19.05.03), walk-up mutates (19.05.04), and the close freezes (19.05.05).

It exists as its own feature because the alternative — every consumer computing its own count from the manifest — guarantees that the artist's number, the venue's number and the fire officer's number disagree, and that nobody can say which is right. One canonical count, with provenance on every counter, is the only way the number that decides who gets paid (17) is the same number the fire officer trusts and the same number the artist reads in their drop.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Reports | Sees the count for their own show, scoped to what their deal grants (D-05) — always paid and remaining; gross/net only when the deal is a percentage-of-gross or percentage-of-net split where the artist must verify the base. Sees it through the drop (19.05.02) rather than a live dashboard, because they are not at a desk. On the night, sees scanned vs paid — the number that tells them how full the room actually is. Never sees held or killed inventory (venue's operational internals). |
| Producer | None | Not visible. No box-office surface. A Producer's provenance work ends at delivery of the recording; the room's count is the Operator's and the performing Musician's concern, not the session owner's. |
| Operator | Full | Sees every counter, live, with provenance and freshness per counter, including held and killed inventory and the held-inventory alert (19.01.02 DT-02). This is their operational instrument on the night. Owns the count; configures which counters a given deal exposes to the Musician (D-05). |
| Fan | None | Never sees the count. Deliberately: exposing "12 left!" is a scarcity-marketing pattern hostile to the Fan persona, and it is frequently a lie once holds are considered. See D-04. The storefront reads `remaining > 0` as a boolean (available / sold out), never the number. |

## Behavior

The count is maintained as distinct counters — never one number with filters (19.03.02 D-02):

| Counter | Meaning | Consumed by |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid** | Sold, money received | Settlement (17). The door-split number |
| **Comp** | Issued free, inventory spent | Artist's guest-list cost (19.03.02) |
| **Scanned** | Physically walked in, deduplicated across gates | Fire officer; the artist's real room-fullness |
| **Held** | Reserved, unsold, not yet released | The held-inventory alert — the money nobody decided to lose |
| **Killed** | Removed permanently for physical reasons | Capacity reconciliation |
| **Remaining** | Sellable right now (= capacity − killed − held − paid − comp) | The storefront (as a boolean to Fans) |

Every counter carries **provenance** (which writer moved it, when) and **freshness** (as-of timestamp of the last write it reflects). Paid moves from online sales and walk-up (19.05.04); comp from issuance (19.03.02); scanned from device reconciliation (19.04.03); held/killed from the manifest (19.01.02). A counter assembled from more than one rail — WeJammin plus an ingested platform (19.07) — carries a **source breakdown**, never a silently blended total (DT-02, D-02).

### Happy Path

1. Manifest locks (19.01); counters initialise from it — `remaining = capacity − killed − held`, paid/comp/scanned all zero, each stamped with the lock time as its freshness.
2. On-sale runs; each sale atomically increments paid and decrements remaining in one transaction, stamping provenance (channel, order id, timestamp).
3. Guest-list submissions issue comps (19.03.02); comp rises, remaining falls, paid unchanged.
4. Holds auto-release at their deadlines (19.01.02 D-01); held falls, remaining rises; the release is stamped as a system-actor write.
5. Show night: scanners push scans; scanned rises, deduplicated across gates before landing here (19.04.03).
6. Walk-up sells at the window (19.05.04); paid rises during the scanning window (`#CX-03`), from a device whose scanner replica may be stale.
7. Doors close; devices reconcile (19.04.02); scanned reaches its final value and its freshness catches up to real time.
8. Close (19.05.05) freezes every counter, records the certifying actor, and promotes the count from observation to attested fact — the number settlement (17) computes from.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Scanned exceeds paid + comp** | Someone got in on a ticket that does not exist — a double-scan (19.04.03) or a counterfeit | Surfaced live to the Operator (D-03), not discovered at close. The offending scans are identified. This is the count's most important alarm. Suppressed offline unless the local replica's paid+comp freshness is under the staleness threshold (DT-04) |
| **Scanned well below paid + comp** | Normal — no-shows, especially comps | Not an alarm; it is the number the artist actually wants. No warning surfaced |
| **Held inventory high while remaining approaches zero** | The forgotten-hold failure (19.01.02 DT-02) | Prominent alert to the Operator while release still converts to revenue; a release CTA. Never shown to the Musician or Fan |
| **Counters read during a write** | An internally inconsistent snapshot destroys trust in the whole report | Reads are served from a consistent snapshot — a report may lag the write by a bounded interval but is never internally contradictory (paid=142 with a gross implying 141). See `#CX-01` |
| **Two writers move paid at the same instant** (online sale + walk-up sale) | Concurrent increments to the same counter | Each write is atomic and provenance-stamped; the count is the ordered sum, never a lost update. Last-writer-wins is forbidden on counters — increments compose |
| **Empty — manifest locked, zero activity** | No sale, no comp, no scan yet | Initialised state: all activity counters at zero, `remaining = sellable`. Not an error and not blank — an explicit "on sale opens {time}" or "on sale now, 0 sold" |
| **Musician requests a counter their deal does not grant** | e.g. a flat-guarantee artist asking to see gross | Request denied at the read boundary, not filtered client-side — the ungranted counter never leaves the server. Musician sees only their granted subset (D-05); the absence is silent, not an error |
| **Network failure — Operator queries the count offline on the floor** | The instrument must stay usable exactly when it matters most | Count served from the scanner replica already downloaded for the door (19.04.02). Scanned is live-local (this device's scans + peer scans received before signal dropped); paid/comp/held are frozen at replica sync time and each shown with its as-of age. State = **Partial**. Calm freshness indicator, not an alarm (mirrors 19.04.01 DT-03). See DT-03 |
| **Malicious input — forged/replayed scan event** | An actor injects scans to inflate scanned or trigger a false alarm | Scans are only accepted from authenticated scanner sessions (19.04.01); a forged event is rejected before it reaches the counter. A genuine counterfeit *ticket* still shows as the scanned>paid+comp anomaly (D-03) — the count reports it, the door acts |
| **Malicious input — Musician tampering to inflate their read of gross** | Artist tries to read or alter a number the deal hides | Counters are read-only to the Musician and scoped server-side (D-05); there is no write path and no ungranted field to tamper with |
| **Partial data — blended count, one source stale or missing** | External-ticketed show: paid = 140 WeJammin + 260 DICE, DICE feed delayed or down (19.07.02) | Count shows the source breakdown with the stale/absent source flagged by its freshness; the total is marked **blended** and never silently averaged. Reconciliation of a disagreement between sources is 19.07.03's job; the count's duty is to make the disagreement visible, not resolve it (DT-02, D-02, Q-03) |
| **Device unreconciled at close** | Scanned is short by an unknown amount | Close blocked (19.04.02 D-04); scanned stays marked provisional until every device reconciles |
| **Capacity reduced after sale** | Paid may exceed the new capacity — an oversold room | Remaining goes negative and is surfaced as an oversold alert to the Operator, not clamped to zero (clamping would hide the incident). Resolution has no clean answer — see 19.01.02 Q-02 |
| **Multi-hyphenate — the Operator is also the performing Musician** | One human owns the box office and is the artist on the deal | Sees the Full Operator count (ownership wins over the deal-scoped Musician lens; there is nothing to hide from yourself). But the counter-attestation the close relies on (19.05.05, `#CX-05`) collapses — the party who certifies is the party who is paid. Flagged to the close as a single-party certification, not silently accepted. See Q-02 |
| **Refund after a sale** | Paid must fall back; the seat returns to sellable | Paid decrements and remaining increments, both provenance-stamped as a refund (distinct from a never-sold seat, for audit). If the ticket was already scanned, the refund does not reduce scanned — the person was in the room; the discrepancy is a real fact the close must carry |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Initialised | Manifest locked, not on sale | All activity counters at their starting values; `remaining = sellable`; "on sale opens {time}" |
| Loading | Count view opened, first fetch in flight | Skeleton counters with last-known values dimmed; resolves in one round-trip |
| Live | On sale, online | Counters moving in real time; Operator sees the full set with provenance, Musician sees their scoped subset via the drop |
| Constrained | Remaining low, held high | Held-inventory alert with a release CTA (Operator only) |
| Show night | Doors open | Scanned becomes the live number; paid is now history for room-fullness purposes |
| Partial | Offline on the floor, or a blended source stale | Scanned live-local, other counters frozen with as-of ages; calm freshness indicator; the alarm may be suppressed (DT-04) |
| Reconciling | Doors closed, devices pushing | Scanned still settling; count marked provisional |
| Anomalous | Scanned > paid + comp (with fresh-enough base) | Alarm to the Operator with the offending scans identified |
| Oversold | Paid + comp > current capacity | Oversold alert; remaining shown negative, not clamped |
| Error | Count fetch fails and no local replica exists (e.g. an admin desk with no scanner) | Explicit failure with retry and last-known-good timestamp — never a silent zero, which would read as "sold nothing" |
| Frozen | Close certified (19.05.05) | Counters immutable; the certified statement is the artifact |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Scanned exceeding paid + comp must alarm live, during the show** | Reasoning about which count anomaly is actionable while the show is happening | ✅ CONFIRMED — it is the only count anomaly with a same-night response. Every other discrepancy (no-shows, stale replicas, unreconciled devices) is a tomorrow-morning report. Scanned > paid + comp means people are in the room on tickets that do not exist — a capacity and licensing exposure before it is a revenue one, and the Operator can act on it *tonight* by tightening the door. Note the tension with 19.04.02 DT-01 ("reconciliation produces a report, not a repair"): that is true of individual conflicts, which cannot be undone. The *aggregate* is different — you cannot eject the person who double-scanned, but you can stop the next twenty. The aggregate alarm is actionable precisely because it is not about any individual scan. |
| DT-02 | The count is one number set, regardless of where the tickets were sold | Simplicity instinct — a room has one capacity, so it has one count | ❌ REJECTED — every counter needs a **source**. The parent domain exists partly because WeJammin must ingest foreign box-office data (19.07), which means a real show's paid count may be 140 from WeJammin plus 260 from DICE, arriving on a delay, possibly disagreeing with itself. A sourceless count silently blends an authoritative number with an ingested one and makes the resulting disagreement unattributable — precisely the reconciliation problem 19.07.03 exists to solve, made invisible. The count must always be able to say where each number came from and how fresh it is. |
| DT-03 | **The offline count view should refuse rather than show stale numbers** | Instinct — a wrong number is worse than no number, so fail loud when the base cannot be trusted | ❌ REJECTED — this repeats the mistake 19.04.01 DT-03 already caught at the door: offline is the *normal* state in these venues, not a fault. Refusing the count offline makes the Operator's instrument dark exactly on the night, at the loading dock, with no signal — when they most need to know how full the room is. The scanned counter is *authoritative-local* offline (this device's own scans are ground truth), and paid/comp/held were fresh at sync. The right posture is the door's: show the numbers, stamp each with its as-of age, alarm on *staleness* not on *offline*, and let the Operator judge. Refusing is the loud-offline anti-pattern wearing a safety costume. |
| DT-04 | The live scanned>paid+comp alarm can fire safely on a stale offline base | Follows from wanting the alarm always-on | ❌ REJECTED as stated — the alarm compares scanned (live-local, growing) against paid+comp (frozen at sync). If a batch of walk-up or online sales landed after this device synced, paid is understated here and the alarm cries counterfeit at legitimate ticket-holders. Firing it unconditionally offline manufactures false accusations at the door. Resolution: the alarm fires offline only while the paid+comp freshness is within a staleness threshold; beyond it, the anomaly is shown as *possible, unverified — base is N minutes stale*, not as a confirmed alarm. Once the device reconnects and paid catches up, a genuine anomaly re-asserts as a hard alarm. The alarm's confidence is a function of the base's freshness, not a constant. |
| DT-05 | Remaining should be a single sellable number the Operator can trust at a glance | Instinct — one number is easier to act on | ❌ REJECTED as a *single* number — remaining collapses two very different quantities: seats truly sellable, and seats sellable-if-a-hold-is-released. An Operator glancing at "remaining = 8" sells eight and is then surprised by a 40-seat hold they forgot (the 19.01.02 DT-02 failure). Remaining must decompose on demand into `free` vs `recoverable-from-holds`, so "nearly sold out" and "sold out unless you release the artist hold" are never the same screen. The scarcity is real for the Fan-facing boolean; for the Operator it must always be legible as its parts. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The count is one canonical state with distinct counters, not a per-consumer computation | Every consumer computing its own count guarantees the artist's and venue's numbers disagree with nobody able to say which is right |
| D-02 | Every counter carries a source and a freshness; blended counts show a per-source breakdown, never a silent average | A count blended from WeJammin and an ingested platform must be able to say which is which (DT-02) |
| D-03 | Scanned > paid + comp alarms live, with the offending scans identified | The only count anomaly with a same-night response (DT-01) |
| D-04 | The count is never exposed to Fans; the storefront reads `remaining` only as a boolean | "12 left!" is scarcity marketing hostile to the Fan persona, and it is usually untrue once holds are counted |
| D-05 | The Musician's counter subset is deal-scoped and enforced server-side; the default grant is paid + remaining, widening to gross/net only when the deal's split is a percentage of that base | Filtering client-side would leak the venue's business; the artist must be able to verify the base a percentage deal is computed on, but a flat-guarantee artist has no claim to the venue's gross |
| D-06 | Reads are served from a consistent snapshot; counter increments compose atomically and never lose an update | A report that is internally contradictory (paid implying a different gross) destroys trust in every number on the page (`#CX-01`); concurrent writers to paid must sum, not overwrite |
| D-07 | The offline count is served from the door replica: scanned live-local, other counters frozen-with-freshness, alarm gated on base staleness | The Operator's instrument must work at the loading dock with no signal; offline is normal, not a fault (DT-03, DT-04) |
| D-08 | Remaining decomposes on demand into free vs recoverable-from-holds for the Operator | A single remaining number hides forgotten holds — the 19.01.02 DT-02 failure (DT-05) |
