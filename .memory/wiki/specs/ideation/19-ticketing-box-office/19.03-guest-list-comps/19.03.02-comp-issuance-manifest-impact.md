# Feature: Comp Issuance & Manifest Impact

> **Number**: 19.03.02
> **Parent**: [Guest List & Comps](./19.03-guest-list-comps-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

Turns a submitted name into a real, scannable ticket, and records what that costs the room. A comp decrements sellable inventory and increments the comp count — but never the paid count, which is the number the door split is computed from. That asymmetry is the entire reason comps belong in Ticketing and not in a CRM.

This feature owns the accounting truth of a giveaway: 20 comps on a 300-cap room is 20 seats that could not be sold and, on a 70/30 door split at a £15 face, a specific £210 the artist chose not to take home (20 × £15 × 0.70). Today that number is computed by someone with a calculator after the show, if at all. Here it is a running figure the artist watches accrue while the decision to spend it is still open.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees the comp count on their show and — the part that matters — **what it cost them**: for each comp, face value × their split percentage, summed, shown live before the submission deadline (19.03.01) while they can still change their mind. Sees delivery status per guest (issued / delivered / delivery-failed / scanned / no-show). Cannot issue comps directly, cannot void them, cannot see other parties' comps or the room's remaining sellable inventory. They submit names (19.03.01); this feature does the issuance, delivery and accounting. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. No box-office surface. A Producer who is also gigging that night sees this only through their Musician lens on a show where they are on the deal — never as a Producer. |
| Operator | Full | Issues, voids and reissues comps; sees comp count against paid count and the admissions counters in the live view (19.05.01 D-09 — gate `scanned`, window `admitted`, and the derived `admissions_total`); owns the manifest impact and the price level each comp binds to. Can issue a comp directly without a guest-list submission (press, industry, a favour) — a **venue comp** attributed to the venue's count, not any party's allocation. Can waive a facility fee at issuance. |
| Fan | Read-only | A comped guest receives a ticket in their fan wallet (19.12) indistinguishable from a bought one and sees only that. They see no price, no "COMP" label, and no indication anyone spent anything on them. If the price level carries a facility fee that is not waived, the guest sees only that nominal fee at checkout — never the face value and never that it is a comp. |

## Behavior

Issuance draws one unit from the manifest (19.01.02) at the price level the comp is bound to, marks that unit `comp`, and delivers a ticket through the ordinary ticket delivery path (19.12). The unit leaves sellable inventory the moment the comp is issued — not at the door, not at scan.

Every comp **binds to a price level** even though the guest pays nothing toward it (D-05). The price level determines two things: which inventory block the unit is drawn from (a £15 GA comp and a £45 balcony comp remove different units and, on reserved seating, different seats), and the face value used to compute the artist's cost. Face value here is an accounting figure only — the guest is charged £0 toward settlement.

The count model keeps these as separate numbers (D-02) because they are routinely and expensively conflated. The counter set itself is authored at `19.05.01 D-09`; what matters here is that a comp never touches paid:
- **Paid** — sold, money received. The settlement number the door split reads.
- **Comp** — issued, no money toward face. Reduces sellable inventory. What the artist spent.
- **Scanned** — the **gate-observed** admissions counter: a barcode presented at a door and accepted. A comp lands here when the guest arrives, and it is not the same as either number above, because comps no-show at a far higher rate than paid tickets.
- **Admitted** / **`admissions_total`** — window admissions (walk-up, admitted at birth and never scanned) and the derived all-bodies total that composes them with `scanned`. Comps never write `admitted`; they always arrive through the gate. Named here only so this feature's readers use the same vocabulary as 19.05.01 (19.05.01 D-09).

Issuance against the manifest is **atomic**: the unit is reserved, the comp record is created with a real barcode, the allocation spend (19.03.01) is decremented, and delivery is dispatched, all as one transaction. If any step that must succeed fails (no inventory), the whole transaction rolls back and no allocation is spent.

### Facility fees

Some rooms levy a per-ticket facility/restoration fee even on a free ticket — a "free" ticket that costs the guest £3 (Q-02, `#R-02`). The behavior is specified so the accounting stays honest whether or not v1 exposes the toggle:

- A comp's **face value is always £0 to settlement** — it never enters the paid count or the door-split base, fee or no fee.
- If the bound price level carries a facility fee and the Operator does not waive it, the guest pays **only that fee** (a £0-face + fee transaction) at delivery (19.12). The fee is **venue revenue on a separate fee line**, never the paid count.
- Press/industry/goodwill comps are issued fee-waived; the Operator sets the waive flag at issuance.
- Whether v1 ships the facility-fee-on-comp toggle at all is a product decision (Q-02) — the accounting model above holds regardless.

### Happy Path

1. Musician submits a guest name against their allocation (19.03.01), bound to a price level.
2. System draws one unit from the manifest (19.01.02) at that price level, atomically.
3. System issues a comp ticket with a real, single-use barcode and delivers it to the guest via the ordinary path (19.12).
4. Live count (19.05.01) updates in the same transaction: sellable −1, comp +1, paid unchanged, scanned unchanged.
5. Musician's Read-only view updates with the running cost of their guest list: Σ(face value × their split %), shown against the still-open deadline.
6. Guest arrives and scans exactly like a paying customer (19.04.01); scanned +1.
7. At close (19.05.05), the certified statement carries paid, comp, the gate-observed `scanned`, the window `admitted` and the derived `admissions_total` as distinct lines (19.05.01 D-09), and any facility-fee collection as a further fee line.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Comp issued with no inventory left | Issuance fails atomically; allocation not spent. The allocation was a right to name guests, not a right to conjure seats | Blocks at submission (19.03.01) with `409 {error: "no sellable inventory at this price level", code: "COMP_NO_INVENTORY"}`, surfaced early rather than at the door. See `19.03-guest-list-comps-cx.md#CX-01` Q3 |
| Comp issuance races a paid sale for the last unit | Manifest is the single source of truth; the two transactions serialise on it. The **paid sale wins by default** (it is money); the losing comp issuance fails atomically and rolls back the allocation spend | Comp side gets `409 COMP_NO_INVENTORY`; the Operator/party is told the allocation could not be filled. This is the operative resolution feeding `#CX-01` Q5 |
| Comp guest no-shows | Very common — comp no-show rates run materially above paid. The seat is gone regardless; comp count unchanged, scanned not incremented | Scanned count reflects reality; the gap between comp and scanned is visible in post-show reporting and is genuinely useful to a Musician sizing next time's list |
| Comp counted in the paid count | Structurally impossible — separate counters, not one with a type flag (DT-02). Settlement reads `paid`, which never includes comps | Settlement arithmetic cannot silently favour the artist because comp units are never addable to `paid` |
| Comp voided pre-deadline | Unit returns to sellable inventory; comp count −1, sellable +1; barcode invalidated; guest notified their ticket was withdrawn | Operator voids; guest's wallet ticket flips to `voided` and the door lookup no longer admits it |
| Comp voided **after** the submission deadline | Barcode invalidated and guest notified, but the unit does **not** return to sale — the deadline (19.03.01 D-02) already closed the return window; a seat freed at 20:00 cannot be sold | Operator sees the void succeed but with a "not returned to sale — past deadline" note; comp count −1, sellable unchanged |
| Void attempted on an already-scanned comp | Rejected — the guest is already inside; voiding cannot un-admit them | `409 {code: "COMP_ALREADY_SCANNED"}`; Operator is told to handle it as an in-venue matter, not a ticket void |
| Double-void / concurrent void of the same comp | Optimistic version lock on the comp record; first void wins | Second actor gets `409 {code: "COMP_STALE_VERSION"}` and a refreshed state showing it is already voided — idempotent from the user's perspective |
| Guest arrives, comp was never delivered (bad email/no channel) | Name is on the list and the comp record + barcode exist, but nothing reached the guest | Door lookup by name against issued comps (19.04.01) admits them; the record exists even when delivery failed — the whole benefit of D-02/DT-01 |
| Reissue after a delivery failure | Operator corrects the contact channel and reissues; the **same barcode** is re-delivered (the ticket object persists). If the barcode may have leaked, reissue mints a **new** barcode and invalidates the old in the same transaction | Guest receives the ticket at the corrected channel; comp count unchanged (reissue is not a new comp) |
| Comp on a **reserved-seating** show | Issuance must select a specific seat from the manifest. To protect adjacency for remaining sellable inventory — the same concern as hold auto-release in 19.01.02 — comps are drawn from a **comp-designated block or from edge/rear seats first**, never scattered through prime contiguous rows. Voiding returns that specific seat. If no seat in the comp block remains, issuance falls back to the next non-adjacency-breaking seat, and only then to `COMP_NO_INVENTORY` | Party sees the seat assigned on the delivered ticket; Operator sees comps clustered, not scattered, preserving sellable adjacency |
| Room charges a facility fee on a comp | Face value stays £0 to settlement; if the price level carries a fee and it is not waived, the guest pays only that nominal fee (e.g. £3) at delivery, booked as venue revenue on a separate fee line — never the paid count or door-split base. Press/industry comps are issued fee-waived | Guest sees only the small fee at checkout, never the face value or the word "comp"; Operator sees fee collections on their own line at close. Product scope of the toggle: Q-02, `#R-02` |
| Comp issued at the door after box office close | Mutates a count settlement is already reading; rejected or forces re-certification with all parties notified | See `#CX-03` and 19.05.05 — silently mutating a certified count is the failure the certified statement exists to prevent |
| Operator issues a **direct venue comp** (no allocation) | Draws from the manifest and increments the comp count like any comp, but attributes to the **venue's** count, not any party's allocation, so no artist cost accrues | Operator names the beneficiary and reason (press/industry/favour); it appears under the venue comp line, distinct from party allocations |
| Multi-hyphenate: comped guest is also a Musician with a pro account | The comp lands in their **fan wallet** (19.12) as an admission. It does **not** post to their artist profile as a credit or provenance — admission is not authorship | Guest sees an ordinary ticket in their wallet; nothing appears on their artist/credits surface (02) |
| Multi-hyphenate: the Operator issuing comps is also the Musician on the deal | The two capabilities stay separate — issuing as Operator draws venue inventory; a comp against **their own allocation** still decrements that allocation and accrues cost to their artist side. The system attributes by the allocation the comp is drawn against, not by the human's identity | The person sees both surfaces; a self-issued party comp still shows as spend against their own guest-list budget, not a free venue comp |
| Malicious/garbage input in guest name or contact | Name and contact are treated as untrusted: escaped on render (no HTML/script execution in wallet, door lookup, or delivery email per `security-first`), and the delivery channel is format-validated before dispatch | Invalid email is caught pre-dispatch and surfaces as a delivery-channel error on the party's view, not as a silent failed send |
| Operator bulk-issues comps to drain sellable inventory (fat-finger or abuse) | Each issuance still atomically decrements sellable inventory; a per-show sanity guard flags a comp burst that exceeds the show's total allocation + a configurable venue-comp ceiling | Operator sees a confirmation gate before comps push the room below a configurable sellable-inventory floor; the action is logged and attributable |
| Forwarded/duplicated barcode at the door | The barcode is single-use (19.04.01); the first scan admits, the second is rejected | Second presenter is refused at scan with a "already scanned" result; not this feature's job beyond issuing a single-use barcode |

### States

#### Ticket lifecycle states

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Pending | Name submitted, issuance transaction in flight | Party sees the entry as `issuing` |
| Issued | Comp ticket created with barcode, inventory drawn, delivery dispatched | Guest has a ticket; party sees the name confirmed with a delivery status |
| Delivered | Delivery channel confirmed receipt | Party sees `delivered`; guest has the ticket in their wallet |
| Delivery failed | Bad email / no contact channel / bounce | Party is prompted to fix the contact detail and reissue — not the guest's problem to solve at the door |
| Scanned | Guest admitted at the door (19.04.01) | Scanned count +1; party can see who actually came |
| No-show | Show ended, comp never scanned | Visible in post-show reporting; useful to a Musician sizing next time's list |
| Voided | Operator voided | Barcode invalidated; guest notified; inventory returned only if pre-deadline |

#### View/render states (Musician cost view, Operator live view)

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Cost/count view requested; live count subscribing (19.05.01) | Skeleton on the count and cost figures; last-known value dimmed until fresh |
| Empty | No comps issued yet for the show | Comp count `0`, running cost `£0.00`, with the party's remaining allocation as context |
| Populated | Comps issued; live count subscribed | Paid / comp / `scanned` / `admitted` / derived `admissions_total` counters (19.05.01 D-09) and, for the Musician, the running cost against their split, updating live |
| Error | Live count subscription drops or issuance fails | Counters show last-known value with a `reconnecting` indicator; issuance errors surface the specific code (`COMP_NO_INVENTORY`, `COMP_ALREADY_SCANNED`, `COMP_STALE_VERSION`) with the offending action, never a generic failure |
| Partial | Some comps issued but a delivery batch is still in flight or some deliveries failed | Count is authoritative; delivery column shows `n issued, m delivered, k failed` so the gap is explicit, never hidden |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A comp must be a **real ticket with a real barcode**, not a name on a door list | Problem statement's root cause — *"no system is present at the moment of creation, so the record is always a reconstruction"*; Producer persona's *"capture as a byproduct, zero extra admin"* | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is the sub-domain's load-bearing decision. A name on a list cannot be scanned, so it never enters the scanned count; it cannot be reconciled at settlement, so the comp number is reconstructed by memory or by counting biro marks on paper; and it cannot be revoked. Issuing a real ticket makes the comp a first-class object that flows through scanning, counting and settlement with no special-casing anywhere downstream. The cost is that comps consume inventory at issuance rather than at the door — which is not a cost, it is the accurate model. |
| DT-02 | Paid, comp and scanned can be one counter with a type flag, derived on read | Modelling economy — they are all "units of admission" | ❌ REJECTED — they have different *arrival times* and different *consumers*, and collapsing them is how settlement goes wrong. A comp is consumed at issuance (days before), a paid ticket at purchase, a scan at 20:30 on the night. Settlement reads paid; the fire officer reads the all-bodies admissions total; the artist's guest-list cost reads comp. A single counter with a flag makes every consumer filter correctly every time — and the one place someone forgets is the place the artist is underpaid. Separate counters make the wrong answer unrepresentable rather than merely unlikely. **This test was later applied to admissions themselves and split them further**: a gate scan and a walk-up window admission also have different arrival times and different consumers, so `scanned` is gate-observed only, `admitted` is window-only, and the all-bodies total `admissions_total` (paid-only projection `admissions_paid`) is derived from both — authored at 19.05.01 D-09 (owner decision DQ-17.1 / DQ-17.2, 2026-07-22). The counter set is therefore no longer three; the argument for splitting is unchanged and is what produced the extra split. |
| DT-03 | A comp must **bind to a price level**, not be priced at a flat zero | Role Lens — the Musician sees cost = face value × split, which requires a face value; and reserved-seating comps must occupy a *specific* inventory block | ✅ CONFIRMED — a comp priced at a flat zero has no face value to multiply by the split, so the artist's cost view (D-03) cannot exist, and it has no inventory block to draw from, so on a tiered or reserved show it cannot know *which* seat to remove. Binding to a price level costs the guest nothing (face is £0 to settlement) but makes both the cost figure and the correct inventory decrement computable. This is the mechanism the whole feature's accounting rests on. Closes Q-01. |
| DT-04 | Reserved-seating comps can be drawn from anywhere in the manifest, like GA comps | GA comps are fungible, so the seat choice looks like it shouldn't matter | ❌ REJECTED — scattering comps through prime contiguous rows damages adjacency for remaining sellable inventory exactly as premature hold release does (19.01.02). Two friends buying together cannot sit together if a comp sits between them. Comps must be drawn from a comp-designated block or from edge/rear seats first, preserving sellable adjacency, and voiding must return the *specific* seat. The seat is not a detail; it is the difference between selling the rest of the row and not. |
| DT-05 | A facility fee on a comp can be folded into the paid count because the guest paid *something* | The guest handed over money, so it feels like a sale | ❌ REJECTED — the facility fee is venue revenue on a separate line, not face value, and folding it into `paid` would inflate the door-split base by tickets that were given away. The face value stays £0 to settlement regardless of the fee; the fee lands on its own line. Otherwise a 20-comp night with a £3 fee would add £60 of "paid" the artist has a claim to on money that was never a sale. |
| DT-06 | Void should always return the unit to sale | Voiding frees a seat, and a free seat should be sellable | ❌ REJECTED — only true before the submission deadline. A void at 20:00 frees a seat that cannot realistically be sold, and returning it to sale would misrepresent live sellable inventory to the box office at the worst moment. Post-deadline voids invalidate the barcode and notify the guest but leave sellable inventory unchanged, mirroring the deadline's own return-window logic (19.03.01 D-02). |
| DT-07 | Admission by comp should post to a Musician-guest's provenance/credits like other platform facts | The platform captures facts at the moment they are true (D-18), and attendance is a fact | ❌ REJECTED — the thesis is about **authorship** provenance (who played, who wrote, who owns), not attendance. A comped Musician attending a show is a Fan-lens admission, not a credit; posting it to their artist profile would pollute the credit graph with non-authorship facts and hand the anti-persona a way to inflate reputation. The comp lands in the fan wallet and stays there. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A comp is a real ticket with a real barcode, drawn from the manifest at issuance | A name on a list cannot be scanned, counted or reconciled — the reconstruction failure in miniature (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Paid, comp and admissions are separate counters, never one counter with a type flag | Different arrival times, different consumers; conflation is how the artist gets underpaid (DT-02). **Refined, not reversed**: the same test later split admissions in two — `scanned` (gate-observed) and `admitted` (window) — with the all-bodies total `admissions_total` derived from both. The counter set is authored at 19.05.01 D-09 and is no longer three (owner decision DQ-17.1 / DQ-17.2, 2026-07-22) |
| D-03 | The Musician sees the running cost of their guest list (Σ face × their split) against their split, before the deadline | The information is only useful while the decision is still open |
| D-04 | Comped guests are shown nothing indicating a comp | Indistinguishable from a bought ticket, by design (mirrors 19.03.01 D-04) |
| D-05 | Every comp binds to a price level; face value is £0 to settlement but is used for cost accounting and to select the correct inventory block | Without a bound price level there is no face value to cost and no block to draw from — the accounting cannot exist (DT-03, closes Q-01) |
| D-06 | Reserved-seating comps draw from a comp-designated block or edge/rear seats first, never scattered through prime contiguous rows; voiding returns the specific seat | Scattered comps damage sellable adjacency exactly as premature hold release does (DT-04) |
| D-07 | A comp's face value never enters the paid count or door-split base; an optional facility fee, if not waived, is venue revenue on a separate fee line | Folding a facility fee into `paid` inflates the split base with money that was never a sale (DT-05) |
| D-08 | Voids return inventory to sale only before the submission deadline; post-deadline voids invalidate the barcode and notify the guest but leave sellable inventory unchanged | A seat freed at doors cannot be sold; returning it misrepresents live inventory (DT-06, mirrors 19.03.01 D-02) |
| D-09 | Issuance is atomic against the manifest; on losing a race for the last unit the paid sale wins and the comp fails, rolling back the allocation spend | The manifest is the single source of truth; a comp must never leave an allocation spent against inventory it did not get (operative resolution feeding `#CX-01` Q5) |
| D-10 | A comped guest's admission does not post to their provenance/credits, even when they hold a Musician account | Admission is not authorship; posting it pollutes the credit graph and enables reputation inflation (DT-07) |
