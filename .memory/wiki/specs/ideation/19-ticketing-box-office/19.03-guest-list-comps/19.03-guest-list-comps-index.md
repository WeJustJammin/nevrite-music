## Overview

Admission granted without payment: the artist's guests, the venue's guests, the support act's guests, the industry, the press, the person the promoter owes a favour to. Comps look trivial and are not — every comp is a seat that cannot be sold, and on a door-split deal every comp directly reduces the money the artist takes home.

**Why it lives in Ticketing** (parent domain rationale): guest list is an admission concern with a settlement consequence. Comps deplete the manifest but not the paid count, and the paid count is what the door split is computed on. It is also the single most reliable source of conflict at 7pm on a show night.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities interacting through a shared budget and a shared manifest. Allocation sets each party's comp budget; submission spends it against real named humans; door-time changes blow past both, at the worst possible moment, and must still reconcile to a settlement number.
