## Overview

Getting box-office numbers into WeJammin for shows that WeJammin did not ticket. Most shows on the platform — especially early on, and possibly forever — will be sold by DICE, Eventbrite, Ticketmaster, See Tickets, Skiddle, or a venue's own WordPress plugin. WeJammin does not own that rail and mostly will not.

**This sub-domain is the parent domain's load-bearing argument.** The domain rationale states it exactly: *"even if WeJammin never sells a ticket, it must ingest box office data or settlement is fiction and draw intelligence is guesswork — so the domain exists either way and build-versus-integrate is a scoping decision inside it, not grounds for omission."* Everything upstream of here (config, on-sale, door) is optional in a way that this is not: without ingested counts, domain 17 cannot settle, 19.05 has nothing to certify, and 22 has nothing to analyse.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities interacting through a foreign count of unknown quality. Connectors decide what can be got and how; ingestion maps a foreign model onto ours and is where fidelity is lost; reconciliation is where our number and theirs disagree and someone must decide which is true; and manual entry is the honest fallback for the majority of venues that have no API at all — which turns an integration problem into an attestation problem.
