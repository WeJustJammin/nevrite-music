# Feature: Count Discrepancy Detection & Reconciliation

> **Number**: 19.07.03
> **Parent**: [External Ticketing Integration & Count Reconciliation](./19.07-external-ticketing-integration-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

Their number says 260. Our door scanned 251. Which one does the artist get paid on?

This feature detects the disagreement, presents it with enough context to be resolvable, and records what the parties decided. It cannot compute the right answer — there frequently is no right answer available — so its job is to make the disagreement **visible before the money moves**, rather than after, which is the entire difference between this platform and a spreadsheet.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees that a discrepancy exists on their show, its size, and what was decided. **This is the feature that most changes their life** and it is the one they have least power over: today the artist is simply told a number. Seeing "the platform reported 260, the door counted 251, the venue accepted 260" is more than any artist gets today, even read-only. Escalation is via dispute (24), not via this feature. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. No box-office surface. |
| Operator | Full | Sees discrepancies, investigates, decides which number stands, and records the decision with a reason. Note the conflict of interest: they decide the number that determines what they pay (Q-01). |
| Fan | None | Never sees this. |

## Behavior

Discrepancy detection compares the sources available for one show:

- **Ingested paid** (19.07.02) — what the foreign platform reported.
- **Scanned** (19.04) — the **gate-observed** count, and only that: what WeJammin's own door physically observed, *if WeJammin ran the door*. Never `admissions_total` (D-07).
- **Manual entry** (19.07.04) — what a human said.

A discrepancy is any material gap between sources. Materiality is a threshold, not a boolean — scanned will always be below paid because of no-shows, so a raw diff is meaningless without an expected no-show band.

Resolution is a **decision, recorded**: which number stands, who decided, why, and whether the counterparty accepts it. Unresolved discrepancies above the threshold block certification (19.05.05).

### Happy Path

1. Show ends. Ingested paid = 260; scanned = 251; expected no-show band for this room is 3–8%.
2. 251/260 is a 3.5% shortfall — inside the band. No discrepancy; nothing fires.
3. On a different show: ingested paid = 260, scanned = 198. A 24% shortfall, far outside the band.
4. Discrepancy raised **before** the close, with context: the gap, the band, the mapping assumptions in play (`#CX-02`), and the candidate causes.
5. Operator investigates — a scanner was unreconciled (19.04.02), or the mapping folded comps into paid (19.07.02 Q-01).
6. Operator records the decision: which number stands and why.
7. The Musician sees the discrepancy and the decision on the certified statement (19.05.05), not a single clean number that hides it.
8. If they disagree, the statement's disputed state captures it (19.05.05 DT-02).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| WeJammin did not run the door | **There is no second source.** Reconciliation has nothing to reconcile against | The most important limitation of this feature. See DT-01 |
| Scanned below paid | Always true — no-shows are universal | Meaningless without an expected band. A naive diff would fire on every show ever |
| Scanned above ingested paid | People got in on tickets the platform does not know about | Real signal: double-scans (19.04.03), counterfeits, or a badly stale ingest |
| The cause is our own mapping | Indistinguishable from their number being wrong | Reconciliation must interrogate the mapping's assumptions, which is why they are first-class data (19.07.02 D-02) |
| Operator decides in their own favour | Structural conflict of interest — they adjudicate what they pay | Recorded and visible to the counterparty. Attribution, not prevention — the domain's consistent posture |
| Unresolved at close | Certifying over an unexplained gap | Close blocked or the statement carries the discrepancy explicitly (19.05.05 D-05) |
| Discrepancy discovered after certification | Late-arriving restatement from the platform | Settlement adjustment, not a statement edit (19.05.05 D-06) |
| Both sources are the same platform | No independence at all | A "reconciliation" between a number and itself is theatre. See DT-02 |
| No-show band unknown for a new room | The threshold cannot be set | Fall back to a conservative default and say so; an honest gap beats a fabricated band |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Single source | Only one count exists (no WeJammin door) | **Explicitly stated as unreconciled** — not silently presented as agreed. See DT-01 |
| Agreed | Sources within the band | Quiet; nothing to see |
| Discrepancy | Gap outside the band | Raised with context and candidate causes, before close |
| Investigating | Operator working it | Open, with the sources and mapping assumptions available |
| Resolved | Decision recorded | Which number stands, who decided, why |
| Accepted | Counterparty agreed | Two names on the resolution |
| Disputed | Counterparty disagreed | Recorded as part of the record (19.05.05 DT-02) |
| Blocking | Unresolved and material at close | Close gated |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Reconciliation requires WeJammin to run the door** — without a gate-observed `scanned` count there is no independent source, and the feature degrades to displaying a number and hoping | Following the data: the only count WeJammin independently observes is the **gate-observed** `scanned` (19.04) — never the derived `admissions_total`, which contains WeJammin's own window sales (D-07, 19.05.01 D-09). Ingested and manual both originate with the counterparty | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is the most consequential finding in this sub-domain — it inverts a MoSCoW assumption. The parent domain's rationale argues the domain exists *even if WeJammin never sells a ticket*, because it must ingest counts or settlement is fiction. True. But ingesting a number the venue supplied and displaying it back to the artist is not settlement, it is **transcription**: the artist is still trusting the venue's word, now with better typography. What makes it a *fact* rather than a claim is a second, independently-observed source — and the only one available is WeJammin's own door. This means 19.04 (Door Scanning) is not an optional convenience for externally-ticketed shows; it is the thing that makes 19.07 worth having. If the incumbent owns the barcode and WeJammin cannot scan (parent Q-03), this feature reduces to 19.07.04's attestation model, which is honest but much weaker. **The door and the reconciliation stand or fall together** — an argument for scanning even on shows we did not sell. |
| DT-02 | Reconciling an ingested count against a manually-entered count from the same venue provides a useful check | It looks like two sources | ❌ REJECTED — it is one source with two spellings. Both numbers originate with the same party, who has the same interest and, usually, is reading one from the other's dashboard while typing. A disagreement between them detects a typo, not a fact; an agreement between them proves nothing at all and, worse, *looks* like corroboration on a statement. Presenting that as reconciliation would manufacture false confidence in exactly the situation where confidence is least warranted — which is worse than presenting a single unreconciled number honestly. Independence is the property that matters, not count. Hence the "single source" state (above) must say **unreconciled** out loud rather than quietly rendering an agreed-looking number. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A single-source count is explicitly labelled **unreconciled**, never rendered as agreed | Silent single-source display manufactures false confidence exactly where it is least warranted (DT-02) |
| D-02 | Materiality is a threshold against an expected no-show band, not a raw diff | Scanned is always below paid; a naive diff fires on every show ever held |
| D-03 | Two counts from the same party are not two sources | Independence is the property that matters, not count (DT-02) |
| D-04 | Resolution is a recorded decision — which number, who decided, why, and whether the counterparty accepts | The Operator adjudicates what they pay; attribution is the only available control |
| D-05 | Discrepancies are raised before the close, not discovered after | Before the money moves is the entire difference from a spreadsheet |
| D-06 | The discrepancy and its resolution appear on the certified statement | A single clean number that hides a 24% gap is a lie of omission |
| D-07 | WeJammin's side of a reconciliation is the **gate-observed `scanned`** counter, never the derived `admissions_total` | `admissions_total` includes `admitted` — WeJammin's *own* walk-up window sales (19.05.01 D-09) — so reconciling it against an ingested count would compare a number against a component of itself and manufacture the false corroboration D-03 exists to forbid. "Independently observed" in DT-01 means the gate and nothing else (owner decision DQ-17.2, 2026-07-22) |
