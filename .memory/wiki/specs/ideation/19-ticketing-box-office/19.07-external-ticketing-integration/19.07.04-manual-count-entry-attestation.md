# Feature: Manual Count Entry & Attestation

> **Number**: 19.07.04
> **Parent**: [External Ticketing Integration & Count Reconciliation](./19.07-external-ticketing-integration-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

Someone types the number in. This is not the sad fallback for when the integration fails — for the majority of venues WeJammin is actually targeting, it is **the primary path**, because their ticketing is a WordPress plugin, a Facebook event, a friend with a card reader, or a cash box.

The moment you accept that a human types the number, the problem stops being an integration problem and becomes an **attestation problem**: not *how do we fetch this*, but *who says this is true, and does the counterparty accept it?* That is a question WeJammin is unusually well-placed to answer, because it is the same question the entire platform exists to answer about credits and splits (D-18).

**Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep candidate list (parent D-02).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only (+ counter-attest) | Sees the entered figures, **who attested them, at what timestamp, and whether the entry was made at the close or reconstructed later** — and can accept or dispute the count. Read-only on the *entry* (they never edit the numbers), but they hold a first-class **counter-attestation** action: one tap to Accept, one to Dispute (routes to 24). Today the equivalent is a text message saying "we did about 200" and no record it was ever said. Whether counter-attestation is required, optional, or async-with-a-deadline is Q-01; the mechanism below specifies all three branches so the code is buildable under any answer. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. No box-office surface. A Producer running a show is acting as an Operator and is scoped under that lens, not this one. |
| Operator | Full | Enters paid/comp/gross per price level, sees the free sanity checks live, and attests. The attestation is personal — a named human said this, at a specific time, and that record outlives their memory of it. Can revise (which creates a *new* attestation, never an edit) and can declare the count **unknown** rather than fabricate a figure. |
| Fan | None | Never sees this. Box-office internals are not a fan surface. |

## Behavior

Manual entry writes directly into WeJammin's canonical count (19.05.01) — paid, comp, gross, per price level — recorded as **manually originated** (a human typed it, not a connector), carrying a named **attesting human** (the authenticated Operator identity) and an entry timestamp. Because the figures are typed *into our model already*, there is no foreign schema to map (sub-domain CX `R-02`), which is exactly what makes manual entry lossless where a thin connector is lossy (DT-02).

The attestation is the feature. A number without a name on it is a rumour; the same number carrying "entered and attested by Sarah Okafor, 23:41, 14 March, at the close" is a fact about what Sarah said — a different and far more durable object. It does not make the number *correct* — nothing can — but it makes it **attributable**, which is what ends arguments.

Counter-attestation by the Musician (Q-01) is what promotes it from "the venue's claim, recorded" to "an agreed fact" — the thesis applied exactly.

**Free integrity checks run live as the Operator types** (D-06). They are cheap arithmetic, not guarantees, and each states its own reasoning so the Operator can dismiss a false positive:

| Check | Rule | On failure |
|-------|------|-----------|
| Capacity ceiling | `paid + comp ≤ licensed capacity` (19.06) | **Hard block** — cannot attest until corrected or capacity is confirmed overridden with a reason; you cannot legally admit more than capacity |
| Face-value sanity | `gross ÷ paid` within ±10% of the weighted face value of entered tiers | **Soft warning** — surfaced with the arithmetic (e.g. "gross £2,140 ÷ 214 paid = £10.00/head vs £12.50 face; −20%") |
| Door floor | `paid ≥ scanned` when WeJammin ran the door (19.04), where `scanned` is the **gate-observed** count only — never `admissions_total`, which includes WeJammin's own window sales (19.05.01 D-09, 19.07.03 D-07) | **Soft warning** — paid below the gate count means more people were scanned in than were sold (double-scans, counterfeits, or a wrong number); genuine signal, see 19.07.03 |
| Non-negativity & type | every figure is an integer ≥ 0; gross is a non-negative money value | **Hard block** — rejected at the field with an inline message; never persisted |

### Happy Path

1. Show has no connector (19.07.01) — the platform is unsupported, or there is no platform at all.
2. At close (19.05.05), the Operator is prompted to enter the count as **part of ending the night**, not as a separate chore afterwards (19.05.05 DT-01, this feature D-03).
3. Operator enters paid, comp and gross per price level, reading from whatever their actual system is: a dashboard, a cash box, a clicker, a notebook.
4. Free checks run live (table above): capacity ceiling and non-negativity as hard gates; face-value and door-floor as soft warnings shown with their arithmetic.
5. Operator attests. Their name and the entry timestamp bind to the number permanently; the count transitions to **Attested** and is immutable.
6. The Musician — ideally still in the building — is shown the number and its attestation, and taps **Accept** (→ Accepted) or **Dispute** (→ Disputed, routes to 24). If they have already left, counter-attestation becomes asynchronous (see edge cases).
7. The attested (and, where obtained, counter-attested) count feeds the certified statement (19.05.05) and settlement (17), carrying its `source`, its attestation chain, and its at-close-vs-reconstructed flag.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Number is simply wrong — an honest fat-finger at midnight | The most likely failure, unfixable by any amount of process. Free checks catch implausible values; a *plausible* wrong number passes | Face-value / capacity / door-floor checks fire on the catchable cases with their arithmetic. A plausible wrong number is attested as stated — attribution, not correction (DT-01) |
| Number is deliberately understated on a door split | The Operator anti-persona's commercial temptation | Attestation does not prevent it; it makes it a named, dated, personally-attributed claim — a meaningfully different thing to make falsely. A pattern of self-favouring attestations is a trust signal (24). Same posture as 19.07.03 |
| **Musician has already left the building** | In-room counter-attestation is impossible, so it becomes **asynchronous**: the count is Attested, and enters `Awaiting acceptance`. The Musician is notified in-app and by email (their configured channel) with the figures, the attester, the timestamp, and Accept/Dispute actions. A response window opens (default **72 hours**, or until settlement runs — whichever is sooner). On response → Accepted or Disputed. On lapse → `Accepted (lapsed)`, a state **textually distinct** from an active acceptance: the statement records "artist did not respond within the window", never "artist accepted". Whether a lapse may unblock settlement, or must hold it, is the live half of Q-01 | Musician: a notification with one-tap Accept/Dispute and a visible countdown. Operator: the count marked `Awaiting acceptance` with the deadline. Statement: the exact acceptance mode (in-room / async / lapsed / disputed), never collapsed to a checkmark |
| Entered days later from memory | **The exact failure the platform exists to prevent** (problem-statement root cause), reproduced inside the platform. Entry made outside the close window is permanently flagged **Reconstructed** (D-04) — never silently equal to a same-night attestation | The entry carries a visible "reconstructed — entered {N} days after the show, not at the close" badge on the count and on the statement; downstream consumers weigh it accordingly (DT-03) |
| **Venue has genuinely no idea** — cash box, no clicker, no list | `unknown` is a **first-class supported state** (D-07), not a zero and not a blank that reads as zero. The Operator attests "the count is unknown" — attribution still applies: a named human states they cannot supply a figure. Settlement (17) cannot compute a door-based split from `unknown` and must fall back to the pre-agreed guarantee/flat fee, or block and escalate; whether `unknown` may proceed to settlement at all is Q-02 | Operator: an explicit "I don't have this count" action, distinct from entering 0. Musician & statement: "paid: unknown (attested by {name}, {time})" — an honest gap, never a fabricated number |
| Paid < scanned (gate count) | More people were scanned in at the door than were sold | Soft warning at entry; genuine signal (double-scans 19.04.03, counterfeits, or a wrong number). Compared against the gate-observed `scanned` only — a walk-up admitted at the window never scans and is counted in `admitted` (19.05.01 D-09), so it can neither trip nor mask this check. Does not block attestation |
| Operator revises after attesting | The number moves after a name was put to it | Revision is a **new attestation** superseding the old, never an edit (D-05). Both versions persist; the original is not erased. Any prior Musician acceptance is invalidated and re-requested against the new figures |
| No scanned count either (WeJammin ran no door) | Nothing independent to check against | Count is single-source and **explicitly labelled unreconciled** (19.07.03 D-01), never rendered as agreed |
| **Two Operators enter counts for the same show concurrently** | Two humans race to enter the count for the same show. Silently keeping only the most recent submission would discard one human's attested figures | Entries are attestations, not overwrites: the second submission is recorded as a *competing attestation*, both are retained with their attesters, and the divergence is surfaced as a discrepancy (19.07.03) for the venue to resolve — never silently dropped |
| **A non-Operator attempts entry** (e.g. a Musician, or an Operator without box-office rights on this show) | Write is refused | The attempt is rejected as unauthorized and nothing is recorded; the entry surface is not rendered for read-only roles at all, so the refusal is a backstop rather than the primary gate. The Role Matrix (parent index) is the authority |
| **Network / app failure between typing and attesting** | Submission must not half-persist a figure that looks attested but is not | Un-attested figures are preserved and re-offered to the Operator on return ("you have an unsaved count for {show}"); nothing enters the canonical count until attestation succeeds and is acknowledged. A failed attestation returns the Operator to `Entering` with figures intact, never to a phantom `Attested` state |
| **Partial entry** — some price levels filled, others blank | Blanks are ambiguous: is a blank tier zero, or unknown? | Each tier must be explicitly 0 or explicitly `unknown` before attestation; a truly empty tier blocks attestation with "tier {name}: enter a figure or mark unknown". No tier is silently coerced to 0 |
| **Malicious / absurd input** — negatives, 10⁹, non-numeric, injection strings | Rejected before persistence | Non-negativity/type check hard-blocks at the field; values exceeding capacity hard-block; text is never rendered back unescaped (security-first). No user string reaches settlement math |
| **The attester is also the performing Musician** (multi-hyphenate: a musician running their own self-promoted show) | Operator and counterparty collapse into one human, so counter-attestation is self-attestation and proves nothing | System detects that the attesting identity and the settlement counterparty are the same identity and labels the count **self-attested / single-party — unreconciled** (mirrors 19.07.03 D-03: two spellings of one source are not two sources). Settlement still runs, but the statement never shows a second name it does not have |
| Restatement arrives after certification (e.g. venue later finds the real number) | The statement is immutable (19.05.05) | Handled as a settlement adjustment with a fresh attestation, not a statement edit — same posture as 19.07.03 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Required | Show closing (19.05.05), no connector | Entry presented **inside** the close flow, not as a task afterwards |
| Empty | Close reached, nothing entered yet | Zeroed tier fields with a clear CTA; not a fabricated default — empty ≠ zero |
| Entering (Loading) | Operator typing; each keystroke re-runs the free checks | Per-tier fields in WeJammin's model; checks evaluate live with their arithmetic shown |
| Warning | A soft check failed (face-value, door-floor) | Stated plainly with the numbers, **before** attestation; dismissible with an acknowledgement, does not block |
| Blocked | A hard check failed (over capacity, negative/non-numeric, a blank un-marked tier) | The specific field flagged with the rule violated; the Attest action is disabled until resolved |
| Error | Attestation submission failed (network/server) | "Couldn't save your count — nothing was recorded. Your figures are kept; try again." Returns to Entering with data intact; never a phantom Attested |
| Attested | Operator confirmed | Number + attester name + timestamp, immutable |
| Awaiting acceptance | Attested but Musician not present in-room | `Async` badge with a live countdown to the response deadline (default 72h) |
| Accepted | Musician counter-attested (in-room or async) | Two names on the fact, with the acceptance mode recorded |
| Accepted (lapsed) | Response window expired with no Musician action | Distinct from an active acceptance: "artist did not respond within the window" — never shown as an active accept |
| Disputed | Musician disagreed | Recorded as part of the record (19.05.05 DT-02); escalation routes to 24 |
| Revised | A new attestation supersedes an earlier one | Both versions in the record; the original is not erased; any prior acceptance is invalidated and re-requested |
| Reconstructed | Entered outside the close window, from memory | **Marked as such, permanently** — a memory is not an observation (DT-03) |
| Unknown | Operator attests the count cannot be supplied | "Paid: unknown (attested)" — an honest gap; distinct from 0; settlement handling per Q-02 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Manual entry is an **attestation** problem, not a data-entry problem — the design target is attribution, not accuracy | Problem statement's root cause — *"no system is present at the moment of creation, so the record is always a reconstruction"*; `personas.md` on the Producer as trust broker — *"their attestation is worth more than a self-claim"* | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it reframes the feature from a compromise into a product. Nothing can make a typed number *correct*; validation can catch a fat finger and nothing more. But WeJammin does not need the number to be provably correct — it needs it to be **attributable and accepted**, because that is what settlement runs on and what ends disputes. "Sarah said 214 at 23:41 and the artist accepted it in the room" is a stronger commercial artifact than an API-fetched 214 nobody looked at, because the disagreement was resolved while both parties were present. The platform's whole thesis is that a fact captured at the moment, with the parties there, beats a reconstruction — and that thesis does not require an API. It requires being in the room, which manual entry is. |
| DT-02 | A manually-entered count is inherently lower-quality than an API-ingested one | Obvious-sounding hierarchy: machines beat humans | ❌ REJECTED, and the inversion is genuinely useful. A **thin** connector (19.07.01's aggregate tier) yields one number with no tier detail, no comp separation, and a mapping that may silently fold comps into paid (19.07.02 Q-01) — nobody has looked at it, nobody has attested it, and its errors are systematic and invisible. A human reading their own dashboard and typing per-tier numbers into *our* model has no mapping layer at all (`R-02` in the sub-domain CX), produces richer data, and has personally attested it. The API number is more *precise*; the attested number is more *accountable*. For settlement, accountability is worth more — which means manual entry should sit alongside connectors as a legitimate choice (D-02), not beneath them as a fallback, and a venue with a thin API might reasonably be *advised* to type it in instead (Q-03). |
| DT-03 | A count entered three days later is the same fact as one entered at the close | It is the same number about the same night | ❌ REJECTED, and this is the platform's own thesis pointed at itself. `problem-statement.md` says the industry's failure is that *"the facts are reconstructed years later, from memory"* — a count typed in on Tuesday from memory of Saturday is that exact failure, committed inside the system built to prevent it, and if the system records it identically to a same-night attestation then it has laundered a reconstruction into a fact. That is worse than not capturing it, because it looks authoritative. Late entries are marked **Reconstructed**, visibly and permanently (D-04), so downstream consumers — and the artist — can weigh them accordingly. This also creates the right pressure: if entering at the close produces a stronger record than entering it Tuesday, the close is where it gets entered — the lazy-path-is-the-correct-path design law (19.05.05 DT-01). |
| DT-04 | Counter-attestation is only meaningful when the two parties are genuinely distinct | Adversarial reading of the multi-hyphenate persona fact (`personas.md`: one human holds several roles simultaneously) | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it forces a real guard. A musician who runs their own self-promoted show is the Operator *and* the settlement counterparty at once — so "attest, then counter-attest" is one person signing their own claim twice, which manufactures the appearance of agreement where there is none. This is structurally identical to 19.07.03 DT-02 (an ingested and a manual count from the same party are one source with two spellings). The count must therefore compare the attesting identity against the settlement counterparty identity and, when they match, label the result **self-attested / single-party — unreconciled** rather than showing a phantom second name. The multi-hyphenate is not an edge case bolted on; it is the reason the identity check is load-bearing rather than cosmetic. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The design target is **attribution, not accuracy** | Nothing makes a typed number provably correct; attribution is what settlement runs on and what ends disputes (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Manual entry sits **alongside** connectors as a legitimate path, not beneath them | A thin API is more precise and less accountable than an attested human entry; for settlement, accountability wins (DT-02) |
| D-03 | Entry belongs **inside the close flow**, at the moment | A count typed on Tuesday from memory of Saturday is the industry's failure reproduced inside the fix (DT-03) |
| D-04 | Late entries are permanently marked **Reconstructed** | Recording a memory identically to an observation launders a reconstruction into a fact (DT-03) |
| D-05 | Revision is a **new attestation**; the original stands in the record | An attestation someone put their name to is not editable; a prior acceptance is invalidated and re-requested |
| D-06 | Free checks run **live at entry**: capacity ceiling and non-negativity as hard blocks; face-value (±10%) and door-floor as soft warnings shown with their arithmetic | They catch fat fingers, the most common error; they cannot catch a plausible wrong number and must not pretend to |
| D-07 | **`unknown` is a first-class, attestable state** — distinct from 0 and from blank | An honest "we don't know" beats a fabricated number; whether `unknown` may proceed to settlement is Q-02 |
| D-08 | When the Musician is not present, counter-attestation runs **asynchronously** with a bounded response window (default 72h / until settlement), and a lapse is recorded as **Accepted (lapsed)** — never as an active acceptance | Keeps the counterparty in the loop without stalling the close; the exact window and whether a lapse blocks settlement is the open half of Q-01 |
| D-09 | The count compares **attester identity vs settlement-counterparty identity**; a match is labelled **self-attested / single-party — unreconciled** | The multi-hyphenate can be both parties; a second signature from the same human is not corroboration (DT-04; mirrors 19.07.03 D-03) |
| D-10 | Concurrent entries are **competing attestations**, both retained and surfaced as a discrepancy — a later entry never silently supersedes an earlier one | An attested figure a human put their name to must not be silently overwritten by another's |
