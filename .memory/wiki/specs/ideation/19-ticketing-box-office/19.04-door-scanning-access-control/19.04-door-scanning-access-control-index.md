## Overview

The physical moment: a human presents a ticket at a door and is admitted or refused. This sub-domain owns validation, the offline capability that makes it work in real rooms, coordination across multiple doors and scanners, and the ID/age check that a ticket scan cannot perform.

**The constraint that shapes everything here**: music venues have terrible connectivity. Basements, load-in doors, concrete, and 300 phones fighting for one cell tower. A door that requires a network is a door that fails on the night, in front of a queue, and no amount of correctness elsewhere survives it. Offline-capable is not a feature of this sub-domain — it is a precondition of it.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities interacting through a manifest that is being replicated to devices, mutated locally, and reconciled under time pressure. Validation defines accept/refuse; offline sync makes validation possible without a network and creates the double-scan problem; multi-scanner coordination is where that problem becomes real; and the age check is a second, independent admission decision that can refuse a perfectly valid ticket.
