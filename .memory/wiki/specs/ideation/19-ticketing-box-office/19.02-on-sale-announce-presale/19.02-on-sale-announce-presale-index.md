## Overview

The controlled opening of a show to buyers: when it is announced, when it goes on sale, who gets in early, and what happens when demand exceeds supply in the first sixty seconds. This sub-domain owns the **mechanism** of gated access — allocation windows, code issuance, redemption limits, queue fairness — and nothing about who deserves that access.

**The boundary that matters** (from the parent domain's rationale, and a verifier's correct flag): this sub-domain owns *how* a presale works; **domain 20 Fanbase & Direct-to-Fan** owns *who* gets a code. Fan decides who, Ticketing decides how. Superfan scoring, segment definition and audience selection are 20's; allocation, codes, windows and redemption are here.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: five capabilities that interact tightly under time pressure. The on-sale schedule defines when windows open; presale windows carve allocation out of the manifest; codes gate the windows; the queue arbitrates when a window's demand exceeds its allocation; and the waitlist captures the demand the allocation could not serve. An on-sale is the moment all five are live simultaneously and any one failing is publicly visible.
