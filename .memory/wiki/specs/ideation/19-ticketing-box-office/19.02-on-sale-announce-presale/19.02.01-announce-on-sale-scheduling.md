# Feature: Announce & On-Sale Scheduling

> **Number**: 19.02.01
> **Parent**: [On-Sale, Announce & Presale Access](./19.02-on-sale-announce-presale-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Sets the two moments that define a show's public life: **announce** (the show becomes known to exist) and **on-sale** (tickets become buyable by the general public). Between them sits the presale ladder (19.02.02). This feature owns the clock — including the embargo before announce, when the show exists in the system, the deal is signed, the manifest is built, and nobody outside the parties may know.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | Proposes and agrees announce/on-sale timing — it is routinely a booking term (routing conflicts, tour-wide coordination, an embargo tied to a release date in domain 12). Sees the schedule for their show and is notified of changes. Cannot move it unilaterally. Self-promoting Musicians hold Full access as their own Operator. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. No box-office surface. |
| Operator | Full | Sets announce and on-sale datetimes, manages the embargo, and executes or reschedules the on-sale. The only role that can move a public on-sale after codes are distributed. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees the announce (the show appears) and the on-sale time, and can set a reminder for it. Sees nothing before announce — an embargoed show is invisible, not "coming soon". |

## Behavior

A show carries an **embargo** state until its announce datetime, at which point it becomes publicly visible with an on-sale time attached. At the on-sale datetime, public inventory becomes buyable. Presale windows (19.02.02) run between the two.

All datetimes are anchored to the **venue's local timezone**, because that is the timezone every human involved is reasoning in — "on sale Friday 10am" means 10am at the room, regardless of where the artist, promoter or fan is.

### Happy Path

1. Operator opens the show's on-sale setup. Manifest (19.01) must be locked first — the system blocks scheduling an on-sale for a show with no sellable inventory.
2. Operator sets the announce datetime and the public on-sale datetime, in the venue's local timezone.
3. Operator optionally builds the presale ladder (19.02.02) in the gap between them.
4. System validates ordering: announce ≤ every presale start ≤ every presale end ≤ public on-sale.
5. Operator confirms. Show sits in embargo; the schedule is visible to the parties (Operator, Musician) and nobody else.
6. At announce, the show becomes public with its on-sale time; fans can set reminders.
7. At on-sale, public inventory opens. The system does not require an Operator to be awake or present.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| On-sale scheduled with no locked manifest | Blocked — an on-sale with undefined inventory is a money incident | Blocking error routing to 19.01 |
| Announce leaks before the embargo lifts | Not a system failure but a real one; the show's existence is discoverable from any party's leak | `[PENDING]` — is there an "announce early" recovery flow? |
| Public on-sale moved earlier, orphaning a presale window | Windows now end after public sale, leaking presale allocation into public sale | Blocking error naming each affected window; cascade must be explicit, not silent (see `#CX-01`) |
| Schedule changed after codes distributed | Fans hold codes stating a time that is no longer true | Warning naming the code-holder count; change triggers notification fan-out to every holder |
| On-sale time falls in a DST transition at the venue | 10am local is unambiguous; the underlying instant is not, in the 1–2am window | System stores the instant; `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` for on-sales scheduled inside a DST gap |
| Tour-wide on-sale — 30 dates, one moment | Currently 30 separate schedules to keep in sync by hand | See parent Q-01; `[PENDING]` |
| Artist and venue disagree on the on-sale time | Not a system failure — a deal dispute | Schedule is a term of domain 17; divergence surfaces there |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Embargoed | Show created, before announce | Parties see the show and its schedule; Fans see nothing at all |
| Announced | Announce datetime passes | Fan sees the show with an on-sale countdown and a reminder CTA |
| Presale live | A presale window opens | Fan sees a presale entry point if they hold access; otherwise the public countdown continues |
| On sale | Public on-sale datetime passes | Public inventory buyable |
| Sold out | Public inventory exhausted | Waitlist CTA (19.02.05) replaces the buy CTA |
| Off sale | Sales closed (door time, or manual) | Show marked off-sale; walk-up may still be available (19.05.04) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Schedule datetimes must anchor to **venue local time**, not to the actor's timezone or to UTC in the UI | Operator persona works "on a phone at a loading dock"; artists tour across timezones; the phrase "on sale Friday 10am" is universally understood to mean 10am at the room | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it has teeth beyond display. A touring Musician in another timezone configuring their own presale, and a Fan in a third, must all see the *same* wall-clock moment described the way the industry describes it. Rendering an on-sale in the viewer's local timezone is the technically-correct choice that produces the wrong conversation. Store the instant, speak the venue's clock. |
| DT-02 | Announce and on-sale are the same event with a delay — most small shows announce and go on sale simultaneously | Observation that DIY/club shows usually do exactly this | ❌ REJECTED — they are the same *moment* in the common case but not the same *event*, and collapsing them removes the embargo, which is the state that matters most. A show exists in the system from the moment the deal is signed (domain 17), often months before announce, with a manifest, holds and a guarantee attached. That period needs a first-class state where the show is real to the parties and non-existent to everyone else. Simultaneous announce+on-sale is a schedule where the two datetimes are equal — a config, not a model. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | All schedule datetimes are expressed in the venue's local timezone | It is the timezone the industry speaks in and the only one all parties share (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Embargo is a first-class state, distinct from "not yet announced" | The show is real to the parties for months before it is real to the public (DT-02) |
| D-03 | An on-sale cannot be scheduled against an unlocked manifest | Selling undefined inventory is a money incident, not a validation nicety |
| D-04 | Schedule changes cascade explicitly and block; they never silently truncate a window | A window someone holds a code for is a promise already made |
