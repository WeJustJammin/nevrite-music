## Overview

Everything that must be true before a ticket can be sold: what tickets exist, what they cost, how many there are, where the buyer stands or sits, and who is holding inventory back from sale. This sub-domain owns the **manifest** — the authoritative statement of the room's sellable inventory — and every price, fee and allocation attached to it. It is the upstream dependency of every other sub-domain in 19: on-sales draw from it, comps deplete it, scanning validates against it, and settlement reconciles to it.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: five distinct capabilities that interact through the manifest. Price scaling assigns prices to inventory blocks; allocations reserve blocks away from public sale; fee structure changes the number the buyer sees for the same block; the seating model determines whether a block is a section or a set of specific seats; accessible inventory is a legally-distinct block that cannot be allocated or released like the others. Change any one and the others must re-derive.
