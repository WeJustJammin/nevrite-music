# Feature: Ticket Types & Price Scaling

> **Number**: 19.01.01
> **Parent**: [Ticket Configuration, Scaling & Allocations](./19.01-ticket-config-scaling-allocations-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Defines what a buyer can actually purchase for a given show and what each option costs. "Scaling the house" is the industry term for dividing a room into priced blocks — Front Standing, Balcony, Restricted View — and assigning a face value to each. This feature also owns *timed* tiers (early-bird, advance, day-of) where the face value advances on a schedule or on a sold-quantity trigger rather than by location. It owns the **face value only**; the all-in price the Fan is legally shown is derived downstream in 19.01.03, and the inventory each level is attached to is owned by 19.01.02. Everything here is a per-show statement, never a room attribute (DT-01).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | On their own show, sees the scaling and its gross potential, and can **propose** changes — a concession tier for students, a hard ceiling on their headline price. Cannot unilaterally set or delete a price level: the face value is a **term of the booking deal in domain 17**, so a change is a proposal that routes to 17.04's amendment flow, not a direct edit. The one exception is self-promotion — where the Musician promotes their own show there is no counterparty deal, so they act as their own Operator with Full access and set face values directly (see the multi-hyphenate edge case). Never sees another party's hold prices or internal block names. |
| Producer | None | Not visible, and not a gap. The Producer's surface is the **session** (`meta/personas.md`), which has no box office — no manifest, no price levels, no on-sale. A Producer running a ticketed listening party reaches admission through **19.11 RSVP**, whose capacity control is an invite list, not a priced manifest. Exposing scaling to the Producer lens would imply a box office the session does not have. |
| Operator | Full | The only role that can **create or delete** a price level. Sets face values and currency, defines timed tiers and their triggers, re-prices before on-sale freely and after on-sale under the constraints below (CX-02), and configures concession ticket types and their eligibility mode. Sees every level, its inventory, its gross potential and full edit history. Cannot raise a level's inventory above the room's licensed capacity (validated against 19.01.02) or set a negative face value. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees the price levels as **purchase options**, each with a single all-in number (19.01.03) and, where a timed tier is running, the price it is currently honouring and when it advances. Never sees the block structure, internal level names, held-back levels, or that a concession tier exists that they are not eligible for. A sold-out level is greyed with the next available tier surfaced. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

A show's manifest (19.01.02) is divided into one or more **price levels**. Each price level has: a name, a face value (a **non-negative integer in the currency's minor units** — pence, cents), a currency (a single ISO-4217 code, defaulting to the venue's settlement currency — D-04), an inventory reference (a count for GA, a seat set for reserved — 19.01.04), and a display order. The face value is the *only* money datum this feature owns; fees and tax are layered in 19.01.03 and the sum is what the Fan sees first (CX-02).

A **ticket type** is what the Fan selects. In the simple case it maps 1:1 to a price level. **Concession types** (student, member, under-18) attach to the *same* inventory block at a different face value plus an eligibility condition — they are a ticket-type variant on a shared block, not an independent price level (D-03, DT-05), so they never double-count capacity.

A **timed tier** is a scheduled or quantity-triggered advance of the face value on the same inventory — "first 100 at £20, then £25". A tier carries a from-value, a to-value, and a trigger that is either a **datetime** or a **sold-quantity threshold** (both are supported — D-07, DT-06). The advance must be **atomic with respect to in-flight carts**: a cart holds the price it was quoted for the cart's lifetime (the cart-hold TTL owned by 19.02.04), and a tier advance never re-prices a cart already open (D-08). Quantity thresholds count `carted` units — reserved at cart creation, not only at checkout — so the cheap tier cannot be kept alive by holding carts open (D-05, DT-03).

### Happy Path

1. Operator opens the show's ticketing setup and selects the room's saved scaling template (or starts from the room's default capacity, read from domain 16 via 19.01.02).
2. Operator names each price level and sets its face value (in minor units) and inventory count. Currency defaults to the venue's settlement currency; the Operator may change it, but all levels in one show must share one currency (D-04).
3. System shows the resulting **gross potential** — Σ (level inventory × face value) — which is the number the booking deal in domain 17 is negotiated against.
4. Operator optionally adds a timed tier to a level: from-value, to-value, and a trigger (a datetime, or a sold-quantity threshold).
5. Operator optionally adds a concession ticket type to a level: its face value, an eligibility condition, and an enforcement mode (honour-system-with-door-check, or verified — D-03).
6. Operator saves. System validates all-or-nothing — the save succeeds whole or not at all: every level has a non-negative face value; Σ inventory across all levels does not exceed the room's licensed capacity (19.01.02); currency is a single valid ISO-4217 code; every timed tier's to-value ≥ its from-value; every verified concession names a resolvable verifier.
7. Scaling is saved all-or-nothing and locked to the show, and becomes available to the on-sale (19.02). No on-sale can be scheduled until scaling is locked.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Price-level inventory sums to more than licensed capacity | Save rejected at validation — overselling a licensed cap is a safety and licensing breach, not a business choice (D-03 parent, 19.01.02) | Blocking error naming the licensed cap, the attempted total, and the overage, with the source licence reference |
| Timed tier advances while a Fan has tickets in cart at the old price | Cart price is **held for the cart's lifetime** (D-08). The unit was decremented from the active tier at cart creation, so a quantity trigger already counts it (D-05). The advance applies only to carts opened after it fires | Cart shows the price it is honouring and, if a tier is imminent, a countdown; the Fan is charged the quoted price, never a different one |
| Cart at the cheap tier expires unpurchased | The reserved `carted` unit returns to `remaining`. It re-enters at the tier **currently active at expiry** — if the tier has since advanced, the freed unit is now dear; the abandoned cart does not preserve the cheap price for the next buyer | No Fan-facing artefact; the live count (19.05.01) reflects the returned unit at the current tier |
| Operator re-prices a level **up** after tickets sold | Permitted. Two cohorts now hold the same inventory at different prices; new buyers pay more, prior buyers are unaffected. Change is synchronous-blocking so the storefront and settlement recompute from one value (CX-02) | Confirmation naming the affected-buyer count and that prior buyers keep their price |
| Operator re-prices a level **down** after tickets sold | Never silent (D-06, DT-07). If a price-guarantee policy is in force, prior buyers are owed the delta and the change routes a refund batch to 19.06; if no policy, only new buyers benefit. Operator must pick which before commit | Warning naming the affected-buyer count, the per-buyer delta, and the two guarantee options; commit blocked until one is chosen |
| Two Operator staff edit the same scaling concurrently during a live on-sale | First commit wins; the second edit is refused as **stale** and **never applies twice** — a double edit could oversell or mis-price, so the second edit must be re-based on the winner's state before it can land | Second actor sees who committed, when, and the current state re-rendered — a named-actor stale rejection, not a generic "try again" |
| Face value set to zero | Allowed, but a £0 ticket that traverses the payment rail is a defect surface with no benefit (D-02). Routes admission to 19.11 (free/RSVP) instead | Prompt offering to convert the level to an RSVP event; the paid rail is not created |
| Negative face value entered | Rejected at field validation — a negative face value is nonsensical and would invert settlement | Inline error on the offending level; save blocked |
| Absurd face value (e.g. > 1,000,000 minor units / £10,000) | Not blocked outright — premium/charity lots are real — but warned as a likely fat-finger, and the entered value is echoed in words to force a second look | Warning restating the value in words with a confirm; a missing decimal (£2000 for £20.00) is the failure this catches |
| Concession ticket type with no eligibility check configured | If enforcement mode is honour-system, it is sold as a lower-priced type with a **door-check flag** printed on the ticket (default — Q-01 governs whether hard verification is ever built). If mode is "verified" but no verifier is named, save is blocked | Honour-system: warning at save that this is a lower price with no gate. Verified-with-no-verifier: blocking error naming the missing verifier |
| Concession face value exceeds the base level's face value | Rejected — a "concession" dearer than the general price is a configuration error, not a discount | Inline error comparing the two values; save blocked |
| Currency differs from the venue's settlement currency | Allowed per level-set, but **all levels in one show must share one currency** (D-04). Cross-border touring (an EU run) is modelled as one show per market, each single-currency; a manifest mixing currencies is a settlement defect | Blocking error if two levels carry different currencies; a per-market touring run is created as separate shows. The deeper touring-settlement architecture is Q-03 |
| Timed tier to-value below its from-value | Rejected — a tier that advances *downward* is a scheduled price cut masquerading as an advance and would silently trigger the price-guarantee path (D-06) | Inline error on the tier; save blocked |
| Malicious input in a level or type name (script/markup) | Stored escaped; the name is display text only and never interpolated into a query or rendered as HTML (`security-first`) | The literal characters render as typed; no execution |
| Save fails mid-commit (network drop, save aborted partway) | The save is all-or-nothing — a partial scaling never persists. The Operator's unsaved state is preserved, and a failed save leaves the prior locked state untouched, so a retry does not collide with itself | "Not saved — retry" with the edited state intact; no half-written level exists on reload |
| Room template loaded but licensed capacity not yet confirmed from domain 16 | Partial state — gross potential shown as **provisional**, and save is blocked until the capacity source resolves, because validating Σ inventory against an unknown ceiling is meaningless | Incompleteness banner naming the missing datum (licensed capacity) and that it blocks save |
| Price level deleted while it holds inventory, holds, or sales | Deletion blocked while any hold, cart, or sale references the level — an orphaned unit has no price to sell or release at (19.01.02 CX-01). A re-home flow reassigns bound inventory to another level first | Blocking error listing the references, with a reassign-then-delete flow |
| Multi-hyphenate: same human is the show's Musician **and** its Operator (self-promotion) | Role collapse suspends nothing (D-16 pattern). In the Operator lens they set face values directly; in the Musician lens the "propose to the booking deal" path is inert because there is no counterparty deal — so no domain-17 ceiling binds. Their own stated ceiling, if any, is advisory to themselves | The self-promoting Musician sees the editable grid (Operator lens); the propose-to-deal control is absent, replaced by direct edit, with a note that no external booking deal governs this show's face value |
| Scaling edited after an on-sale has begun (any locked change) | The Locked state permits only the constrained post-on-sale operations above; structural changes (add/delete level, change currency) are refused while the show is on sale | Refused with the reason "on sale — [operation] would invalidate live carts and settlement"; the permitted operations are offered instead |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Setup opened; capacity/configuration being read from 19.01.02 / domain 16 | Skeleton of the level grid; the capacity ceiling and its licence source resolve last and are never rendered until known |
| Empty | New show, no scaling configured | Empty state offering the room's saved templates and a "start from capacity" CTA |
| Draft | Scaling entered, show not on sale | Editable grid with live gross-potential total; blocking flags on any level missing a valid face value, currency, or inventory |
| Populated (Locked) | On-sale has begun | Grid read-only except the permitted post-on-sale operations (constrained up/down re-price), each of which warns and is audited with actor, time and reason |
| Advancing | A timed tier's datetime or quantity trigger fires | Operator sees the tier transition in the live count view (19.05.01); in-flight carts are unaffected (D-08) |
| Exhausted | A price level's inventory is fully sold | Level shown as sold out; Fan sees it greyed with the next available tier surfaced |
| Partial | Template loaded but licensed capacity unconfirmed, or a required datum missing | Populated view with an explicit incompleteness banner naming the missing datum and what it blocks; save disabled |
| Error | A validation or save failure | Inline error on the offending level (or a stale-state rejection during concurrent edit); save blocked, edited state preserved |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Scaling must be **per-show, not per-room** — the same room is scaled differently for a seated folk show and a standing rock show | Operator persona ("inventory is time and space… physically constrained"); standard seats-in/seats-out venue practice | ✅ CONFIRMED — modelling scaling as a room attribute breaks the most common real configuration. The room supplies a *template*; the show owns the manifest. |
| DT-02 | Dynamic/demand-based pricing is expected because every incumbent does it | Ticketmaster Platinum, DICE surge experiments | ❌ REJECTED for breadth — a product/values decision, not a discovered capability, and directly hostile to the Fan's success criterion ("money reaches the artist rather than a reseller") and the anti-scalping posture of 19.09. Raised as parent Q-02, not modelled as a node. |
| DT-03 | A quantity-triggered tier ("first 100 at £20") must count **carted** units toward the threshold, reserved at cart creation — not only paid units at checkout | Refresher/scalper behaviour: if only paid units count, holding carts open keeps the cheap tier alive indefinitely and lets a bot farm the low price | ✅ CONFIRMED — counting at cart creation makes the advance deterministic and closes the cart-holding gambit. An expired cart returns its unit at the *current* tier, so abandonment does not preserve the cheap price. |
| DT-04 | A price level needs an explicit **currency** field even though most shows are single-market | EU touring runs are routine (D-15 latency work aside, the artist plays five countries in a week); a face value with no currency is ambiguous the moment settlement crosses a border | ✅ CONFIRMED — currency is a first-class attribute of the level, defaulting to the venue's settlement currency, single per show. The *touring-settlement* architecture (one show multi-currency vs per-market shows) is deferred as Q-03. |
| DT-05 | A concession tier (student/member) should be modelled as its **own price level** | Superficial symmetry with general levels — each has a name and a face value | ❌ REJECTED — a concession shares the *same inventory block* as its base level at a different face value and eligibility condition. Its own level would double-count capacity against the licensed ceiling. It is a ticket-type variant on a shared block (D-03). |
| DT-06 | Timed tiers advance by **wall-clock only** (a date/time schedule) | The obvious early-bird mental model | ❌ REJECTED — the indie/club norm is the *quantity* trigger ("first 100"), and date and quantity triggers coexist on real on-sales. Both must be first-class (D-07); quantity is arguably the more common of the two in the target market. |
| DT-07 | A post-on-sale price **decrease** can be applied silently, like any other edit | Symmetry with the freely-permitted increase | ❌ REJECTED — a decrease after buyers exist creates a price-guarantee liability and a fairness incident. It must surface the affected-buyer count and force a guarantee choice (refund the delta via 19.06, or benefit new buyers only) before commit (D-06). |

> Every Deep Think hypothesis is logged — confirmed and rejected — as the audit trail of exploration beyond the obvious.

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **19.01.02 Capacity Manifest, Holds & Allocations** ([path](./19.01.02-capacity-manifest-allocations-holds.md)) — a price level is meaningless without an inventory block to attach it to; releasing a hold resolves its price against the tier state at the instant of release; a level cannot be deleted while inventory references it. See `#CX-01`.
- Touches **19.01.03 Fee Structure & All-In Pricing** ([path](./19.01.03-fee-structure-all-in-pricing.md)) — face value is the input to the legally-mandated all-in display; changing it recomputes the Fan-facing total and the settlement split, computed once so displayed total == charged total. See `#CX-02`.
- Touches **19.02 On-Sale & Presale** ([path](../19.02-on-sale-announce-presale/)) — scaling must be locked before an on-sale can be scheduled; timed-tier triggers interleave with the on-sale/presale schedule.
- Touches **19.02.04 Cart / Reservation Hold** — a cart holds its quoted price for the cart-hold TTL; a tier advance never re-prices an in-flight cart (D-08), and quantity thresholds count `carted` units reserved at cart creation (D-05).
- Touches **19.06 Refunds & Adjustments** — a post-on-sale price *decrease* under a price-guarantee policy routes a delta-refund batch here (D-06).
- Touches **19.05.01 Live Count** — a tier advance and a level exhaustion are surfaced in the live count view; the raw count is never exposed to Fans (19.05.01 D-04).
- Touches **17 Live Booking & Settlement** ([path](../../17-live-booking-settlement/)) — gross potential is what a guarantee/door-split deal is negotiated against; the face value is a *term of that deal*, which is why the Musician holds Config (propose via 17.04) rather than Full, except when self-promoting.
- Touches **19.11 RSVP / Free Admission** — a zero-value level routes admission here instead of the paid rail (D-02).

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Scaling is owned by the show, not the room | Rooms are reconfigured between shows; a room-level manifest cannot express seats-in/seats-out (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Zero-value tickets route to 19.11 rather than through the paid rail | A £0 ticket that traverses payments is a defect surface with no benefit |
| D-03 | Concession types are ticket-type variants on a **shared inventory block**, not independent price levels | Modelling a concession as its own level double-counts capacity against the licensed ceiling (DT-05) |
| D-04 | A show's scaling is **single-currency**; each price level carries an explicit ISO-4217 code defaulting to the venue's settlement currency; touring runs are modelled as one show per market | A manifest mixing currencies is a settlement defect; a level with no currency is ambiguous at any border (DT-04). Deeper touring-settlement architecture is Q-03 |
| D-05 | Quantity-triggered tier thresholds count `carted` units, reserved at **cart creation** | Counting only paid units at checkout lets carts be held open to keep the cheap tier alive (DT-03) |
| D-06 | A post-on-sale price **decrease** is never silent — it forces an affected-buyer count and a price-guarantee choice before commit | A decrease after buyers exist is a guarantee liability and a fairness incident (DT-07) |
| D-07 | Timed tiers support **both** datetime and sold-quantity triggers | The quantity trigger ("first 100") is the club/indie norm and coexists with date-based early-birds (DT-06) |
| D-08 | A cart holds its quoted price for the cart's lifetime; a tier advance never re-prices an in-flight cart | Charging a Fan a price different from the one quoted is a trust and (in the UK/EU) a consumer-law failure — resolves prior Q-02 |
