# Feature: Ticket Types & Price Scaling

> **Number**: 19.01.01
> **Parent**: [Ticket Configuration, Scaling & Allocations](./19.01-ticket-config-scaling-allocations-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Defines what a buyer can actually purchase for a given show and what each option costs. "Scaling the house" is the industry term for dividing a room into priced blocks — Front Standing, Balcony, Restricted View — and assigning a face value to each. This feature also owns *timed* tiers (early-bird, advance, day-of) where the price changes on a schedule or on a sold-quantity trigger rather than by location.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | Sees the scaling for their own show and can propose changes (a cheaper tier for students, a hard ceiling on their headline price). Cannot unilaterally re-price — the face value is a term of the booking deal in domain 17. Where the Musician self-promotes their own show, they hold Full access and act as their own Operator. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. The session-owner lens has no box-office surface. A Producer running a ticketed listening party reaches admission through 19.11 RSVP, which has no price scaling. |
| Operator | Full | Creates price levels, sets face values, defines timed tiers and their triggers, re-prices before on-sale, and re-prices after on-sale under constraint (CX-02). The only role that can add or delete a price level. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees the tiers as purchase options with an all-in price each (19.01.03). Never sees the block structure, the internal names, or that a tier exists but is entirely held back. |

## Behavior

A show's manifest is divided into one or more **price levels**. Each price level has a name, a face value, a currency, and a set of inventory (a count for GA, seat identities for reserved — see 19.01.04). A **ticket type** is what the Fan selects; in the simple case it maps 1:1 to a price level, but concession types (student, member, under-18) can attach to the same inventory at a different face value with an eligibility condition.

Timed tiers are a scheduled or quantity-triggered advance of the face value on the same inventory — e.g. first 100 at £20, then £25. The advance must be atomic with respect to in-flight carts.

### Happy Path

1. Operator opens the show's ticketing setup and selects the room's saved scaling template (or starts from the room's default capacity).
2. Operator names each price level and sets its face value and inventory count.
3. System shows the resulting gross potential — capacity × face value per level, summed — which is the number the booking deal in domain 17 is negotiated against.
4. Operator optionally adds a timed tier: face value, trigger (date/time or quantity sold), and the value it advances to.
5. Operator saves. System validates that allocated inventory across all price levels does not exceed the room's licensed capacity, and that every level has a non-negative face value.
6. Scaling is locked to the show and becomes available to the on-sale (19.02).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Price levels sum to more than licensed capacity | Save is rejected — overselling a licensed capacity is a safety and licensing breach, not a business choice | Blocking error naming the licensed cap, the attempted total, and the overage |
| Timed tier advances while a Fan has tickets in cart at the old price | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — candidate rule: cart price is held for the cart's lifetime, and the held quantity is decremented from the old tier at cart creation, not at checkout | Cart shows a countdown and the price it is honouring |
| Operator re-prices a level after tickets have sold at the old price | Two cohorts now hold the same inventory at different prices; permitted for increases, but a decrease likely triggers a price-guarantee obligation | Warning naming the affected buyer count before confirm |
| Face value set to zero | Allowed, but routes admission through 19.11 (free/RSVP) rather than the paid rail — a £0 ticket with a payment flow is a defect | Prompt offering to convert to an RSVP event |
| Concession tier with no eligibility check | Sold as a general tier; a student price with no student verification is just a lower price | Warning at save; verification is a `[PENDING]` decision |
| Currency differs from the venue's settlement currency | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — cross-border shows are real (EU touring) | — |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty | New show, no scaling configured | Empty state offering the room's saved templates and a "start from capacity" CTA |
| Draft | Scaling entered, show not on sale | Editable grid with live gross-potential total |
| Locked | On-sale has begun | Grid is read-only except for permitted post-on-sale operations, each of which warns |
| Advancing | Timed tier trigger fires | Operator sees the tier transition in the live count view (19.05.01) |
| Exhausted | A price level's inventory is fully sold | Level shown as sold out; Fan sees it greyed with the next available tier surfaced |
| Error | Validation failed | Inline error on the offending level, save blocked |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Scaling must be **per-show, not per-room** — the same room is scaled differently for a seated folk show and a standing rock show | Operator persona's "inventory is time and space… physically constrained"; standard venue practice of seats in/seats out | ✅ CONFIRMED — modelling scaling as a room attribute would break the most common real configuration. Room supplies a *template*; the show owns the manifest. |
| DT-02 | Dynamic/demand-based pricing is expected by a domain expert because every incumbent does it | Ticketmaster Platinum, DICE surge experiments | ❌ REJECTED for breadth — this is a product/values decision, not a discovered capability. It is also directly hostile to the Fan persona's success criterion ("money reaches the artist rather than a reseller") and to the anti-scalping posture of 19.09. Raised as Q-02 on the parent index rather than modelled as a node. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Scaling is owned by the show, not the room | Rooms are reconfigured between shows; a room-level manifest cannot express seats-in/seats-out (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Zero-value tickets route to 19.11 rather than through the paid rail | A £0 ticket that traverses payments is a defect surface with no benefit |
