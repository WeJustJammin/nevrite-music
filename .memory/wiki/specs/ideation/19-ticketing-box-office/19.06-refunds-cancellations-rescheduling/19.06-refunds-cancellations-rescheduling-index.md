## Overview

What happens when the show does not happen, or does not happen as sold, or the buyer changes their mind. Three flows that look like one and are not: an individual buyer wanting their money back, an event cancelling and owing everyone their money back at once, and a date moving — which is neither a cancellation nor nothing, and which carries statutory refund rights that a cancellation does not need to think about.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities with genuinely different money shapes, different actors, and different legal characters, interacting through a shared refund rail and a shared manifest. A reschedule *triggers* an opt-out refund window; an indefinite postponement *becomes* a cancellation by operation of law in some jurisdictions; and a cancellation on an externally-ticketed show (19.07) means WeJammin owes refunds on money it never held.
