## Overview

Everything that happens to a release after it is live: correcting a typo without breaking the
record, pulling it from a store, and being forced to pull it by someone else. Distribution
products treat post-release as an afterthought, which is backwards — a release is live for
decades and in the builder for a fortnight, so almost all of its lifetime is spent here.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: voluntary takedown, update/redelivery and involuntary
takedown share machinery (all are ERN variants from one generator, 12.02.01 DT-03) but differ
completely in trigger, permission and human consequence. An involuntary takedown is initiated by
someone other than the artist, may be legally compelled, and blocks the redelivery the artist
would otherwise reach for. They interact: an unresolved involuntary takedown gates the other two.

**Renamed from the sweep candidate.** The candidate was "Takedowns & Redelivery", which reads as
two operations. The sub-domain is really the *post-release life of a record*, and naming it that
way is what surfaced involuntary takedown — which the candidate list did not contain and which
is the case with actual legal weight.

> **Legend**: ✅ Full access · ⚙️ Configuration only · 👁️ Read-only · 📊 Reports only · ❌ No access
>
> **12.05.03 gives the Musician only `Read-only` — the sole feature in domain 12 where the artist
> cannot act on their own release.** That is the definition of "involuntary" and it is the point,
> not an oversight. They see what happened, who initiated it, and the route to contest it (24) —
> but they cannot lift it, because if they could it would not be a takedown.
>
> **Producer read-only on takedown** for the same reason as 12.03.02: a record they made
> disappearing from stores is news they are entitled to, without a say in the decision. Their
> `Config` on 12.05.02 exists because an asset-driven redelivery (a corrected master) is theirs.
>
> **Fan is `None` throughout**, though they are the party who most visibly experiences a takedown
> — the record vanishes from their library. Domain 12 does not tell them; that is 20's to decide,
> and it may be nobody's. See Q-03.
