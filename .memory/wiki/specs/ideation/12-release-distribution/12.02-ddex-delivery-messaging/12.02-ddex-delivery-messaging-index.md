## Overview

The machinery that turns a ready release into messages a DSP will accept: generating the DDEX ERN
(Electronic Release Notification), bending it into each partner's particular profile and version,
handing the message and its assets to that partner's transport, and interpreting whatever comes
back. This is the layer that actually talks to Spotify, Apple, Amazon, Beatport and ~150 others,
and it is invisible to the user by design — the artist should never learn what an ERN is.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: message generation, per-partner profile conformance,
transport choreography and acknowledgement parsing are four distinct capabilities in constant
interaction. The profile determines what the generator may emit; the transport differs per partner
and per profile; acknowledgement semantics differ per partner and feed both the status tracker
(12.03) and the validation rule packs (12.01.02).

**Narrowed from the sweep candidate.** The candidate was "DDEX Messaging (ERN/DSR/RIN/MWL/MEAD)",
which bundled five standards that belong to four different domains. This sub-domain owns only the
**outbound release-delivery** standards (ERN, MEAD, and takedown/update messages). DSR (sales
reporting) is inbound and belongs to 10 Royalties; RIN (recording information) belongs to 02
Credits; MWL (musical work licensing) belongs to 09/11. The shared machinery beneath all of them —
schema validation, version negotiation, partner conformance, transport — is recorded as the
**DDEX Message Rails & Partner Conformance** cross-cut. See domain CX and D-02 below.

> **Legend**: ✅ Full access · ⚙️ Configuration only · 👁️ Read-only · 📊 Reports only · ❌ No access
>
> **This sub-domain is deliberately near-invisible.** Three of four children give the Musician
> read-only or no access, and 12.02.02 is platform-operated with no user surface at all. That is
> the design goal, not an oversight: the value of a distributor is that the artist never learns
> what an ERN is. The Musician's read-only access exists only so that when something breaks they
> can see *that* it broke and what was sent — the absence of that view is why artists experience
> distribution as a black box.
>
> **`[PENDING]` — Admin/Operator-of-the-platform lens.** 12.02.02 has a real operator surface
> (rule authoring, partner onboarding, message inspection) but its actor is a platform admin,
> which ideation-index.md Q-02 flags as not yet a persona. Do not read the `None` row as "no UI
> needed" — read it as "no *persona* UI needed".
