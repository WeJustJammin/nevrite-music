## Overview

Registering a recording's fingerprint with the UGC platforms — YouTube Content ID, Meta Rights
Manager, TikTok — so that when someone else's video uses the track, the artist gets paid instead of
the video getting muted. This is distribution to a category of platform that does not sell music
at all: the "store" is other people's content, and the "sale" is a claim against it.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: fingerprint registration, claim/whitelist management
and claim disputes are three capabilities with continuous interaction. Registration creates claims;
whitelisting suppresses claims the artist does not want made (against their own videos, their
label's, their fans'); disputes arise when a claim hits content that should not have been claimed
or when two parties claim the same audio. None is meaningful alone — registration without
whitelisting claims the artist's own channel.

**This sub-domain is where provenance is worth the most money and does the most damage.** Content
ID revenue splits are notoriously wrong because nobody knows the real ownership. WeJammin does
(D-10). But the same fingerprint that collects revenue also **automatically claims against
strangers**, including collaborators and fans, at machine scale and with no human in the loop.
It is the only place in domain 12 where the platform acts *on the artist's behalf, against third
parties, continuously, forever*.
