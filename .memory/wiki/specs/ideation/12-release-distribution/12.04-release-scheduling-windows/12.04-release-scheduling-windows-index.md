## Overview

Time. When the release comes out, how far ahead it must be delivered for each store to honour that
date, which editorial and pre-save windows open and close on the way, and what has to be finished
by when for any of it to work. A release date is not a date field — it is the anchor from which a
dozen other deadlines are computed backwards, several of them owned by third parties who will not
move.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: the release date, DSP lead times, the editorial pitch
window, the pre-save window and the rollout plan are five capabilities that constrain each other
continuously. Move the date and every other window moves. Miss the pitch window and the date is
still fine but the release is materially worse off. The lead time is not advice — it is the
partner's rule, and violating it means the date silently does not happen.

**Merged from two sweep candidates.** The sweep listed "Release Scheduling, Pre-Save & Editorial
Pitch Windows" (04) and "Release Rollout Deadlines" (08) separately. They are one thing: the
rollout plan's deadlines are *derived* from the release date and the pitch window, so separating
them would put the computation in one node and its inputs in another. See D-02.
