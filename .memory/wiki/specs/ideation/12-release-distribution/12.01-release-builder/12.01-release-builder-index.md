## Overview

Everything that turns a finished set of audio files into a deliverable release object: track
composition and sequencing, the metadata that DSPs contractually require, the audio and artwork
specs each partner enforces, the label copy that names who owns and administers the recording,
and — the reason this sub-domain matters strategically — the **Delivery Readiness Gate** that
refuses to hand a release to the DDEX layer until its splits balance and its rights are clear.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: composition, validation, asset conformance, label
copy and the readiness gate are five distinct capabilities that interact continuously. The gate
reads the composition's contributor roll-up and the asset conformance result; adding a track
re-opens validation; label copy determines which identifier prefix the release draws from; a
rejection from the DSP layer (12.03) re-enters here as a validation failure. None of them is
meaningful alone.

**The thesis mechanic lives here.** Per the problem statement, provenance fails because nothing
is present when the facts are true and nobody will open an app to file a split sheet. Distribution
is the one moment in a music career where the artist *wants something from the platform* — and is
therefore the one moment they will fix their metadata. The Delivery Readiness Gate (12.01.03)
converts that want into leverage. This is the single most load-bearing feature in domain 12.

>
> **Operator is `None` throughout by design.** A venue/studio operator has no stake in a release
> object. The one plausible exception — a studio that co-owns a master under a spec-deal — is
> handled as that operator holding a **Musician-lens rights position** in domain 09, not as an
> Operator-lens permission here. Recorded so downstream spec writers do not invent an Operator UI.
>
> **Fan is `None` throughout.** The Fan-facing consequence of a release (it exists, it is
> pre-savable, it is followable) is surfaced by domains 20/21 and by 12.04.03 — never by the
> builder. Per personas.md, the Fan "will never file a split sheet"; the builder is a
> professional tool and must not leak into the Fan surface.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Classified as **sub-domain**, not feature | 5 interacting capabilities: gate reads composition + asset results; rejection re-enters as validation failure; label copy drives identifier prefix | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | The Delivery Readiness Gate is a **child of the builder**, not of the DDEX layer | The gate must fire while the user is still authoring and can fix things. A gate at the DDEX boundary fires after the user has mentally shipped, which is the failure mode described in personas.md ("we'll do it later") | Agent, Deep Think |
| D-03 | Label Copy folded in here rather than made a domain-level feature | (P)/(C) lines and label-of-record are metadata authored on the release object; separating them from composition produces two screens for one mental act | Agent, Deep Think |

## Open Questions

| # | Question | Owner | Deferred To |
|---|----------|-------|-------------|
| Q-01 | Is the readiness gate **hard** (delivery is impossible until splits balance) or **soft** (deliverable with a recorded override + audit entry)? Hard maximises provenance capture and is the thesis in its purest form; soft prevents the platform from holding a time-critical release hostage over a co-writer who has gone silent. This is a product decision with a direct line to D-18. | User | MoSCoW / `/ideate-validate` |
| Q-02 | Does WeJammin support **compilations and multi-artist releases** where contributors are not WeJammin users at all? The gate assumes counter-attestation is possible; a 1998 compilation reissue has nobody left to attest. | User | `/ideate-validate` |
| Q-03 | **Physical formats** (vinyl, cassette, CD) — pressing, catalog numbers, barcode for retail, distribution to record stores. Genuinely part of "release" for independent artists, and a completely different machine from DDEX. In scope, out of scope, or a later phase? No directive exists either way — flagged rather than invented. | User | `/ideate-validate` |
