# Feature: Release Composition & Track Sequencing

> **Number**: 12.01.01
> **Parent**: [Release Builder & Delivery Readiness](./12.01-release-builder-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The release object itself: what type of release this is (single / EP / album / compilation), which
recordings it contains, in what order, on which volume, which project each recording came from,
and who is billed on it. This is where a finished WeJammin project (07) becomes a commercial
product with a shape a DSP will accept, and where the contributor roll-up that the readiness gate
(12.01.03) evaluates is assembled.

It is a thin feature by design and that thinness is the point. Composition owns **membership,
order and release-level billing** — nothing else. Every fact about a *recording* (its title, its
display artist, its credits, its splits, its ISRC) is owned upstream by 02/07/09/12.07 and merely
**read** here. A builder that lets an artist author recording facts at release time is a
reconstruction engine wearing a release UI, which is precisely the failure
[problem-statement.md](../../meta/problem-statement.md) names as the root cause.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Creates the release, picks its type, adds recordings they hold a rights position on (from projects in 07, from their existing catalog, or by uploading a standalone master), drags to sequence, groups into volumes, marks the focus track, and sets the **release-level** primary artist. Sees the per-track contributor roll-up assembled live from 02/09, the origin of every recording (`from project` vs `imported master`), and the running count of what still blocks delivery. Cannot edit any recording-level fact from here — clicking a track title opens the recording in 02/07. |
| Producer | Config | On a release they do not own: composition is read-only (no reorder, no retitle, no type change, no add/remove) but they can **correct the recording-level facts they are authoritative for, at the recording's source, from a deep link in this view** — which take/master is canonical, instrument and role credits for the session they ran, and whether a track is an alternate mix or a distinct recording. Sees, on every track, whether their own attestation is what the gate is waiting on. On their own release they act with the Musician lens — but see D-09: their Producer attestation on a release they own is still marked self-sourced and never counts as counter-attestation. |
| Operator | None | Not visible. A venue/studio operator has no relationship to a release object. A live-album master recorded at their room reaches this feature as a **recording contributed by a Musician**, not as an Operator action. A studio holding points under a spec-deal appears here as a rights holder in the 09 ledger — under the Musician lens — never as an Operator permission. Downstream spec writers must not invent an Operator UI for this feature. |
| Fan | None | Not visible, and must never be. The Fan sees the *result* of composition (tracklist, order, features) via the pre-save page (12.04.03) after scheduling and via the DSP after delivery. Per [personas.md](../../meta/personas.md) the Fan surface is "a different product wearing the same brand" — if builder vocabulary (roll-up, rights position, volume grouping) leaks into 12.04.03, the feature is broken. |

## Behavior

### What composition owns, and what it only reads

| Fact | Owner | Composition's relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Release type, title, release-level primary artist | **This feature** | Authored here |
| Track membership (which recordings are on it) | **This feature** | Authored here |
| Track order, volume/disc grouping | **This feature** | Authored here |
| Focus track designation | **This feature** | Authored here (consumed by 12.04.02) |
| Recording origin (`from-project` / `imported-master`) | **This feature** | Recorded at add time (D-06) |
| Recording title, version/subtitle, display artist | Recording (02 / 07) | Read + deep-link only |
| Credits per recording | 02 | Read only — never authored here |
| Splits, ownership, co-owner set | 09 | Read only — the add check reads it (D-02) |
| ISRC / UPC | 12.07 | Read only — assigned at delivery |
| Per-store rules and thresholds | 12.01.02 rule pack | Read only — surfaced here, owned there (D-04) |
| Master-use / mechanical licences | 11 | Read only — surfaced at add time (D-05) |
| (P)/(C) lines, label of record | 12.01.05 | Not touched |

### The recordings-not-files model

A release is a container of **recordings**, not of files. A recording is the canonical entity that
carries the ISRC (12.07 D-01), the credits (02) and the splits (09) — the same recording can appear
on a single, then an album, then a compilation, without duplicating or forking its provenance. This
distinction is the whole reason the release layer can be thin and the provenance layer can be
authoritative, and it is why there are **no release-scoped overrides of recording facts** (D-03).

### Release type is a validated choice, not a dropdown

Type drives DSP classification, editorial eligibility and chart eligibility, and the DSPs disagree
on where the boundaries fall. Composition **surfaces the constraint at the moment of the type
choice** rather than at delivery (CX-05) — but it does **not own the thresholds**. Those live in
the versioned rule pack (12.01.02 D-02, D-04 here), because partner rules change without notice and
a threshold hardcoded in the builder is guaranteed stale. Composition owns the *moment*; validation
owns the *rule*.

Findings are scoped to the stores currently selected in 12.03.01. If no stores have been selected
yet, the constraint is evaluated against the **union of all available stores** and labelled as such
("across all stores — narrows once you pick stores"), because an unqualified "valid" is a lie
(12.01.02 D-01).

### Add-eligibility: two paths, one of which the SURFACE spec was missing

| Path | Requirement | Typical case |
|---|---|---|
| **Rights position** | The adding party holds a position in the 09 ownership ledger that includes distribution control of the master (see Q-05) | The artist releasing their own record |
| **Licensed inclusion** | The adding party holds a **master-use licence** (11) from the recording's owner, scoped to term and territory | A compilation curator, a label reissue, a DJ mix |

The licensed-inclusion path exists because **a compilation curator holds no rights position on
almost any track by construction** (DT-04). Without it, the `compilation` release type is unusable
— a hole the breadth pass did not surface.

A **credit is never sufficient**. A session drummer is credited on a master they do not own and
cannot distribute (D-02, DT-02). Gate on rights or licence; display credits.

### The contributor roll-up

For each track, composition assembles a read-only roll-up from 02 (who is credited), 09 (who owns
what, who has confirmed, who is a co-owner, whether the recording is frozen or disputed) and 12.07
(ISRC state). The roll-up is what the readiness gate evaluates (CX-01) — composition never
evaluates it, never blocks on it, and never renders it as a verdict. It renders **facts and their
absence**, per 09 D-06: silence reads as silence. "No credits recorded" is never rendered as "no
credits exist".

The roll-up is a projection, not state. It is recomputed on every membership change and keyed to a
recording version so a late-arriving confirmation is never applied to a stale row (CX-01 q5).

### Origin is recorded, always

Every recording carries an **origin** fact set at add time and never editable afterward:

| Origin | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `from-project` | Sourced from a WeJammin project (07) with a session record — the platform was in the room |
| `imported-master` | Uploaded as a standalone master; credits and splits are asserted by the uploader, not witnessed |

This is recorded regardless of whether it is ever *displayed* (D-06, resolving Q-02). It is a fact,
cheap to capture at source and impossible to reconstruct later — which is the thesis applied to the
feature itself. Whether it surfaces as a visible confidence tier across the product is a product
decision escalated to domain Q-09, not a licence to fail to record it.

### Sequencing

Order is a release-level fact, persisted with an optimistic version token so two band members
dragging simultaneously produce a detectable conflict rather than a silent overwrite. Volume/disc
grouping is supported for **digital** multi-volume releases (deluxe editions, bonus discs) — this is
a DDEX/DSP-carried field and is not a physical-format concern (DT-15). Physical side grouping is
parent Q-03 / domain Q-07 and is not assumed here.

Composition does **not** suggest an order (DT-14). Sequencing is the last artistic act in making a
record and it carries no provenance fact.

### Happy Path

1. Musician creates a release — from a completed project (07), which pre-populates the tracklist
   with that project's recordings in project order, or from scratch (empty).
2. Musician picks the type. The builder shows, inline at the choice, what each type means for the
   stores selected in 12.03.01: track-count and duration thresholds from the rule pack (12.01.02),
   plus the editorial and chart consequence. No thresholds are hardcoded here (D-04).
3. Builder offers four add sources: **projects** (recordings where the Musician holds a rights
   position in the 09 ledger), **catalog** (recordings already on a prior WeJammin release),
   **licensed inclusion** (compilation type only — recordings covered by a master-use licence in 11),
   and **upload a master** (standalone, off-platform).
4. Musician adds a recording. The track row appears immediately (P95 < 150 ms) with a skeleton
   roll-up; adding a track is never blocked on the roll-up resolving.
5. Builder records the origin (D-06), runs the eligibility check against 09, and fills the roll-up
   (P95 < 500 ms per track row): contributors from 02, split status from 09, ISRC state from 12.07.
6. If the add carries a cost the Musician cannot see, the builder states it **at the add**, not at
   delivery — per the domain's "show the price at the decision" pattern: previously released
   (ISRC reused, stream history does not carry — DT-06), cover (mechanical licence needed per
   territory — DT-05), licensed inclusion (term expiry creates a future takedown obligation —
   DT-12), imported master (no session record).
7. Musician sequences the tracklist by drag. Order persists optimistically (P95 < 300 ms) against a
   version token.
8. Musician groups tracks into volumes if the release is multi-volume, and marks exactly one focus
   track. The builder states what the focus track binds: the editorial pitch in 12.04.02.
9. Musician sets the release-level primary artist. On a compilation with 2+ distinct track-level
   primary artists, the builder pre-fills **"Various Artists"** and explains why, leaving it
   overridable.
10. The live readiness summary updates on every change, separating **"you can fix this"**
    (validation, 12.01.02) from **"you are waiting on a person"** (the gate, 12.01.03) — never one
    undifferentiated problems list (CX-02).
11. Validation clean + gate green → release advances to scheduling (12.04.01). At the delivery
    handoff, membership is **re-validated against 09** (D-07, DT-11) — add-time eligibility was a
    snapshot and rights are mutable.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Recording already released on an earlier WeJammin release | Allowed. The recording is reused, ISRC preserved and not reissued (12.07 D-01); the release draws a new UPC. Stream/save history is **per product entry** and does not carry — the builder must not imply it does (DT-06). | Badge: "Previously released on *{release}* · ISRC reused. This album's play count starts at zero — the single keeps its own." |
| Recording has no rights position and no licence for this party | Add rejected. Credit ≠ ownership (D-02). | "You don't hold a rights position on this recording. Ask {owner} to add it to their release, request a master-use licence, or resolve ownership in Rights." |
| Compilation curator holds no rights position on a track (DT-04) | Rejected on the rights path; offered the **licensed-inclusion** path (D-05). | "You don't own this recording — that's normal for a compilation. Request a master-use licence from {owner} to include it." |
| Licensed inclusion whose licence **term expires after the release date** | Allowed. The expiry is recorded as a **scheduled takedown obligation** on that track (D-12), routed to 12.05.01. | "Licensed to {date}. This track must come off stores on {date} — we'll schedule it and remind you 60 days before." |
| Licensed inclusion whose **territory** is narrower than the release's target territories | Allowed; the track's territory scope narrows the release's (12.03.01). | "Licensed for {territories} only. Track 4 won't ship to {excluded} — the rest of the release still will." |
| Licence for an included track is **revoked** before delivery | Track becomes ineligible; release falls back from Ready to Draft. If already delivered → 12.05.03. | Track row goes amber: "The licence for this track was withdrawn on {date}. Remove it or resolve with {owner} to deliver." |
| Recording is a **cover** — underlying work has writers outside the release's rights holders (DT-05) | Allowed. A mechanical-licence requirement is surfaced at add and emitted to the gate; the obligation is owned by 11. | "This is a recording of a work by {writers}. You'll need a mechanical licence in {territories} before this ships. Start in Licensing." |
| Track count / duration violates the chosen type's rules | Surfaced **at the type choice**, not at delivery (CX-05). Thresholds from the rule pack (D-04). | "For the stores you've picked, 4 tracks over 30 minutes classifies as an EP, not a single — and singles get the editorial slot you're pitching for. Change type or drop a track." |
| Two tracks are the same recording (duplicate add) | Rejected. | "This recording is already track 3." |
| Alternate mix added as a separate track | Allowed but flagged — it is a **distinct recording** needing its own ISRC (12.07). See Q-04: distinctness is currently user-asserted. | "Alternate mixes are separate recordings and get their own ISRC at delivery. If the audio is identical to track 2, stores will treat this as duplicate content and may strike the release." |
| Identical audio submitted twice under two "recordings" (DT-13) | Not detectable pre-delivery today (Q-04). Composition warns; it does not block — the platform does not adjudicate (09 D-04). | Same warning as above; no block. |
| Compilation with contributors who are not WeJammin users | **Add is never blocked.** The roll-up marks each non-user contributor `unregistered — invitable`, and emits them to the gate, which owns the attest-by-link path (12.01.03 D-03). Composition's job is to make the attestation burden visible at add time, not to prevent the add. | Roll-up row: "3 of 5 contributors aren't on WeJammin. They can confirm by link — no account needed. Adding this track adds 3 people to your chase-list." |
| Contributor is deceased / unreachable (catalog reissue) | Composition still adds; the roll-up records the absence as absence (09 D-06). Whether it is *passable* is the gate's hard/soft question (12.01.03 Q-01) and parent Q-02. | "No confirmation on file for {name}." — never "unverified", never "invalid". |
| Recording is **part-owned**; co-owners have not been told about this release | Add allowed (holder has a position). Co-owners are notified when the release leaves Draft **or** after 60 continuous minutes of membership, whichever is first (D-08). Their position (consent / objection / silence) is recorded as a fact and emitted to the gate. Whether an objection **blocks** is Q-01. | At add: "You own 40% of this master. {Names} own the rest — we'll let them know this release exists before it goes anywhere." |
| Track removed inside the 60-minute debounce | Pending co-owner notification is **withdrawn**, never sent (D-08, CX-01 q4). | Nothing — the point is that nobody was disturbed. |
| Track removed after contributors were already nudged | Nudges for that recording are withdrawn; the roll-up items disappear from the gate. Confirmations already given persist on the **recording**, not the release (CX-01 q5). | "Removed. We've withdrawn the confirmation request we sent Jamie." |
| Contributor confirms at the exact moment the owner removes the track | Confirmation is idempotent and attaches to the recording. It survives, and is still valid when the recording appears on a later release. | Contributor sees a normal confirmation. Owner sees nothing — the track is gone. |
| Rights position **transferred or reverted** after add, before delivery (09.03) | Membership re-validation at the delivery handoff catches it (D-07, DT-11). Release falls back from Ready to Invalidated. | "Track 2's ownership changed on {date} — you no longer hold a position that lets you distribute it. Remove it or resolve in Rights." |
| Recording enters a **rights freeze / escrow** (09.04.03) or its ledger is `disputed` (09.01.06) after add | Track becomes ineligible; the release cannot advance. Composition does not adjudicate — it reports. | "Track 5 is frozen while a rights conflict is resolved. It can't ship until that's settled." |
| Two collaborators reorder the tracklist concurrently | Version-token conflict. The later write is rejected, not merged — a merged sequence is an order neither person chose. | "Sam reordered this while you were dragging. Here's their version — reapply your change or keep theirs." (side-by-side, no silent overwrite) |
| Two collaborators add a track concurrently | Both adds land; the duplicate check catches a same-recording collision (deterministic on recording ID). Position is append-order. | Normal — plus the duplicate message if they collided. |
| Sequence save fails (network) | Local order is retained and retried: 3 attempts, exponential backoff (1s / 4s / 16s), then the drag is reverted to last-known-persisted. The tracklist is never left showing an order that isn't saved. | Toast: "Couldn't save the order — retrying." Then, on failure: "We couldn't save that reorder. Your last saved order is back." |
| Roll-up fetch fails for **some** tracks | Partial state. Those rows show a per-row error with retry. **Editing is not blocked; the gate must read the failure as unknown, never as ready** (CX-01 q2). | Per-row: "Couldn't load credits for this track. Retry." Summary: "Readiness is incomplete — 2 tracks couldn't be checked." |
| Roll-up fetch fails for **all** tracks (02/09 unreachable) | Composition remains fully editable. Delivery is not reachable. Auto-retry. | Non-blocking banner: "We can't check credits and splits right now. Your work is saved; we'll re-check automatically." |
| Musician tries to advance a release with **zero tracks** | Blocked at composition, not at validation. | "A release needs at least one track." |
| Type changed after tracks were added (album → single with 10 tracks) | Allowed; the type constraint is re-surfaced immediately with the specific overage. | "As a single, this can't have 10 tracks for {stores}. You'd need to drop 7 — or keep it as an album." |
| Type changed **after gate-green** | Re-opens validation, **not** the gate — provenance facts do not depend on release type (CX-05 q5). | "Type changed — we're re-checking store rules. Your contributor confirmations still stand." |
| Membership changed after gate-green | Re-opens **both** validation and the gate — a new recording brings new unmet provenance conditions (CX-01). | "Adding this track re-opens readiness — it brings 2 people who haven't confirmed yet." |
| Source project (07) is deleted after its recordings were added | Release is unaffected. The recording is canonical and lives in 02/09, not in the project (DT-01). Only the project's *workspace* is gone. | Nothing. (If the project's master file was the asset, see next row.) |
| Master file replaced or deleted upstream (07 / Media cross-cut) | Re-opens asset conformance and the gate; the gate keys on asset **version**, not a boolean (CX-03 q5). | "The master for track 3 changed. We're re-checking it — this may re-open readiness." |
| Release is declared a **continuous mix / gapless** and is then reordered (DT-07) | Reorder is allowed but warned — the segues are rendered into the masters and reordering makes them audible artifacts. | "This is a continuous mix — the crossfades are baked into the audio. Reordering will make the joins sound wrong unless you re-render." |
| Focus track changed after the editorial pitch was submitted (12.04.02) | Allowed; the pitch is bound to a track and is invalidated. Cost stated at the change. | "Your Spotify pitch is for *{old track}*. Changing the focus track withdraws it, and the pitch window closes {date}." |
| No focus track set | Allowed — it is not required for delivery, only for pitching. Not a blocking condition. | Advisory: "No focus track. You can still deliver, but you can't pitch for editorial." |
| Two focus tracks | Impossible by construction — marking a second moves the designation. | The old focus badge moves to the new track. |
| Compilation reaches 2+ distinct track-level primary artists | Release-level artist pre-fills as **"Various Artists"**, overridable. | "Two or more artists on this — stores expect 'Various Artists' as the release artist. Override if you mean something else." |
| Musician overrides "Various Artists" to their own name on a multi-artist compilation | Allowed; becomes a validation warning (12.01.02) and a wrong-artist-page risk (12.03.04). Not blocked — the platform does not adjudicate billing. | Warning: "Billing a multi-artist compilation to one artist usually lands on the wrong artist page and loses editorial eligibility. Sure?" |
| Release shape matches a known stream-farming heuristic (e.g. 60 tracks, most under ~35 s) (DT-08) | **Warned, never blocked** (D-10). The signal is routed to 24 (Trust & Safety), which does adjudicate; composition does not. | "This shape — many very short tracks — is what stores' fraud checks look for. It's likely to be rejected and can put your whole catalog at risk." |
| Release exceeds the platform ceiling (proposed 200 tracks / 20 volumes) | Rejected at composition. Per-store maxima are a rule-pack finding (12.01.02), not a composition rule. | "200 tracks is our ceiling. Most stores cap far lower — check the store rules panel." |
| Same recording added to two in-flight releases by the **same** owner (single + album, same week) | Allowed — that is the standard pre-release single pattern. ISRC is shared; UPCs differ. | "Also on *{single}*, releasing {date}. Same recording, same ISRC — that's how a pre-release single works." |
| Same recording added to two in-flight releases by **two different** rights holders | Allowed at composition — both may legitimately hold positions. Any exclusivity conflict is 12.03.01's, not this feature's. | "{Other holder} also has this recording on a release scheduled {date}." (informational; no block) |
| Standalone master uploaded with **no credits at all** | Allowed. Origin = `imported-master`. The roll-up shows an empty credit set as **empty**, not as clean (09 D-06). The gate owns whether it can ship. | "No credits on this recording yet. Add them now while you remember — this is the record that proves who played on it." |
| Standalone master fingerprint-matches an existing WeJammin recording owned by someone else | See Q-04 — not detectable pre-delivery today. If/when it is: composition surfaces the match and routes to 24, never auto-rejects (09 D-04). | `[Q-04]` — behavior specified, capability unconfirmed. |
| Producer attempts to reorder, retitle, or change type | Rejected by the Config lens. Their deep links to recording facts remain available. | "Only the release owner can change the tracklist. You can still fix the credits and version on any recording here." |
| Producer marks a track "alternate mix"; Musician marked it a distinct recording | The Producer's assertion is recorded as a **Producer attestation on the recording** and surfaces as a contradiction the gate must resolve — the platform does not pick a winner (09 D-04). | Both see: "You and {name} disagree about whether this is an alternate mix or a separate recording. This needs settling before delivery." |
| **Multi-hyphenate**: the release owner is also the Producer who ran the session | Musician lens applies (superset). But their Producer attestation is marked **self-sourced** and is never counted as counter-attestation (D-09, DT-09). | Roll-up: "Your own attestation — not independent. Jamie still needs to confirm their credit." |
| **Multi-hyphenate**: a band entity owns the release; a member composes it | The release is owned by the org entity (01), not the person. Membership changes are attributable to the individual in the audit ledger. | Byline on the release: the band. In history: "Sam added track 4." |
| Release is delivered to 1+ stores, then edited here | Composition is locked. Membership and order changes route to 12.05.02, which owns the cost model — composition does not assert what an edit costs. | "This is live on 3 stores. Changes go through Update & Redelivery, which will tell you what each one costs." |
| User attempts to add a recording by ID they have no relationship with | Rejected identically to the no-rights-position case — the response must not reveal whether the recording exists. | "You don't hold a rights position on this recording." (no existence disclosure) |
| Empty volume left after removing its only track | Volume is collapsed automatically; no empty volumes are ever delivered. | Volume disappears; remaining volumes renumber. Toast: "Volume 2 was empty — removed." |
| Track numbers gap after a removal | Positions are dense and recomputed; there is no user-visible track-number field to desync. | Tracks renumber 1..N. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | Opening a release; pulling recordings and the roll-up | Skeleton tracklist rows. Track rows render before their roll-up (step 4) — the tracklist is never held hostage by 02/09. |
| **Empty** | New release, no recordings | "Add recordings from your projects, or upload masters." Four CTAs: Projects · Catalog · Licensed inclusion (compilation only) · Upload. |
| **Draft** (Populated) | 1+ recordings; validation or gate unmet | Tracklist + live readiness summary split into "your fixes" (12.01.02) and "waiting on people" (12.01.03) — never one list (CX-02). |
| **Partial** | Roll-up resolved for some tracks, failed or pending for others | Populated tracklist; affected rows carry a per-row error + retry. Summary reads "Readiness incomplete — 2 tracks couldn't be checked", **never** "ready". |
| **Ready** | Validation clean and gate green | Tracklist with green readiness state + "Schedule delivery" CTA (→ 12.04.01). |
| **Invalidated** | Membership re-validation at delivery handoff found a rights change, freeze, licence revocation, or a rule-pack update (CX-02 q5) | Falls back from Ready to blocked, with the **specific** cause named and the affected track highlighted. Never fails silently at delivery, and never silently re-greens. |
| **Locked** | Delivered to 1+ stores | Read-only tracklist. Edits route to 12.05.02 (update & redelivery). |
| **Error** | 02/09 unreachable; roll-up fetch failed wholesale | Non-blocking banner; **editing is never blocked**, delivery is. Auto-retry. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A release should contain **recordings**, not audio files — so the same recording can appear on single→album→compilation carrying one ISRC and one provenance chain | Industry standard (ISRC is per recording, UPC per release); D-10 requires WeJammin to own the chain of title end-to-end | ✅ CONFIRMED — a file-centric model forks provenance on every reissue, which is precisely the reconstruction failure in problem-statement.md. Ratified downstream by 12.07 D-01. |
| DT-02 | The builder should let a Musician add **any** recording they have credits on | Naive read of "credits = access" | ❌ REJECTED — a **credit** is not a **rights position**. A session drummer is credited on a master they do not own and cannot distribute. Conflating them would let a contributor release someone else's record. Gate on rights (09), display credits (02). This distinction is invisible in every competitor and is a real source of disputes. |
| DT-03 | Release type is cosmetic metadata | Assumption | ❌ REJECTED — type drives DSP classification, editorial eligibility and chart eligibility, and DSPs disagree on the boundaries. It must be a validated, constraint-surfacing choice, not a dropdown. |
| DT-04 | Add-eligibility gating on rights position (D-02) is sufficient for every release type | The rule as written after DT-02 | ❌ REJECTED — **it makes the `compilation` type unusable.** A compilation curator holds no rights position on almost any track by construction; a label reissuing a 1998 comp, a DJ delivering a mix, and a charity compilation are all *licensed* inclusions, not owned ones. The correct model is a **second eligibility path: master-use licence from 11** (D-05). This is a real hole the breadth pass shipped: it offered `compilation` as a type and a rule that forbids composing one. |
| DT-05 | A **cover version** is just another recording as far as composition is concerned | Recording-centric model | ❌ REJECTED — a recording of someone else's work needs a **mechanical licence** per territory before it can be sold, and the platform *knows* whether the underlying work's writers sit outside the release's rights holders because 09 models the work/recording duality. Not surfacing this at add time means the artist discovers it at rejection or, worse, ships an unlicensed cover and takes the liability. The obligation belongs to 11; the **moment of surfacing** belongs here (domain pattern: show the price at the decision). |
| DT-06 | Adding a previously-released single to an album carries the single's accumulated stream history | The artist's near-universal expectation | ❌ REJECTED as a factual claim, ✅ CONFIRMED as a **required disclosure**. The recording keeps its ISRC, but a release is a separate product entry and its counters start at zero. Per-DSP linking behavior varies and belongs in the rule pack (12.01.02 D-02), not hardcoded here. The failure nobody designs for is the artist who sequences their biggest single as track 1 expecting its numbers to seed the album, and reads the zero as a delivery failure. |
| DT-07 | Track order is free to change at any time before delivery | The drag affordance implies it | ❌ REJECTED for **continuous-mix / gapless releases** — DJ mixes, live albums and segued concept albums have their crossfades rendered *into the masters*. Reordering produces audible joins that no DSP flag fixes; the gapless flag removes silence, it does not re-render audio. Continuous mix must be a release-level declaration, and reordering a declared one must warn. A builder whose drag handle silently destroys a record's most deliberate quality is a real, shippable defect. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **07 Music Projects & Collaboration** ([path](../../07-music-projects-collaboration/)) — *trigger dependency + shared entity*. Recordings and their master files originate in projects; creating a release from a project pre-populates the tracklist. **Deleting a project must not orphan a release** — the recording is canonical and lives in 02/09 (DT-01). Origin `from-project` is set from this seam (D-06).
- Touches **02 Credits & Attribution** ([path](../../02-credits-attribution/)) — *shared entity, read-only*. The contributor roll-up is **read, never authored, here**. Authoring credits at release time is the reconstruction anti-pattern the product exists to kill. Composition renders absence as absence (09 D-06).
- Touches **09 Rights & Ownership** ([path](../../09-rights-ownership/)) — *permission + trigger dependency*, the hardest dependency this feature has. Add-eligibility reads the ownership ledger (09.01.02), not credits (DT-02) and not the points record (09.02.02 — see Q-05). The **co-owner set** drives D-08's notification. A ledger entering `disputed` (09.01.06) or a rights freeze (09.04.03) invalidates membership. Term/territory/reversion (09.03) makes eligibility **mutable**, which forces re-validation at delivery (D-07, DT-11). The work/recording duality is what lets composition detect a cover (DT-05).
- Touches **11 Music Licensing** ([path](../../11-music-licensing/)) — *hard dependency, newly surfaced*. The `compilation` release type is **unusable without 11**: licensed inclusion requires a master-use licence (DT-04, D-05), and covers require mechanicals (DT-05). Licence term/territory flows back as delivery scope and as a scheduled takedown obligation (DT-12). This edge did not exist in the breadth pass and materially changes 12.01's dependency graph — **Step 6 should treat it as a domain-level CX candidate, not a feature footnote.**
- Touches **12.01.02 Metadata Validation** ([path](./12.01.02-metadata-validation-conformance.md)) — *trigger dependency*. Composition surfaces type constraints at the moment of choice; the **thresholds are rule-pack data owned there** (D-04, CX-05). Membership, order and type changes all re-trigger validation.
- Touches **12.01.03 Delivery Readiness Gate** ([path](./12.01.03-delivery-readiness-gate.md)) — *trigger dependency* (CX-01). Composition assembles the roll-up the gate evaluates and **emits three new condition types** the gate's SURFACE list did not have: co-owner release position (D-08), master-use licence present for a licensed inclusion (D-05), mechanical licence present for a cover (DT-05). It also emits attestation **source** (D-09), which the gate needs to avoid counting self-attestation as counter-attestation.
- Touches **12.01.04 Asset Conformance** ([path](./12.01.04-audio-artwork-asset-conformance.md)) — *no direct edge* (CX R-03 rejects it, correctly), but a replaced master re-opens conformance and the gate via the **recording**, and the alternate-mix distinctness question (Q-04) would land here or in 12.06 if the capability exists.
- Touches **12.01.05 Label Copy** ([path](./12.01.05-label-copy-distributor-of-record.md)) — *shared entity* (CX-04). The label/band entity named there determines which identifier prefix the release draws from (12.07) and which org entity (01) owns the release.
- Touches **12.03.01 Store & Territory Selection** ([path](../12.03-dsp-store-territory-management/)) — *state dependency*. Type constraints are scoped to selected stores; a licensed inclusion's **territory narrows the release's delivery scope**, and an exclusivity conflict on a shared recording is theirs, not ours.
- Touches **12.03.04 Artist Profile Linking & Disambiguation** ([path](../12.03-dsp-store-territory-management/)) — *trigger dependency*. The release-level and track-level artist names set here are the input to profile linking. The "Various Artists" override case is the most common route to the wrong-artist-page disaster that 12.03.04 exists to prevent.
- Touches **12.04.02 Editorial Pitch** ([path](../12.04-release-scheduling-windows/)) — *state dependency*. The focus track binds the pitch; changing it after submission withdraws the pitch, and the pitch window is time-boxed.
- Touches **12.04.03 Pre-Save** ([path](../12.04-release-scheduling-windows/)) — *notification fan-out*. The tracklist becomes **Fan-visible before delivery**. Composition changes after a pre-save page is live change what fans were shown — a trust surface, and the only place this professional feature reaches a Fan.
- Touches **12.05.01 Takedown** ([path](../12.05-catalog-lifecycle-after-release/)) — *scheduled trigger*. A term-limited licensed inclusion creates a takedown obligation dated at licence expiry (DT-12, D-12). Nothing else in the pipeline creates a scheduled takedown from a composition-time act.
- Touches **12.05.02 Metadata Update & Redelivery** ([path](../12.05-catalog-lifecycle-after-release/)) — *state handoff*. Composition locks at first delivery; membership and order edits route there. **Composition must not assert what an edit costs** — 12.05.02 owns the cost model.
- Touches **12.06 Content ID & UGC Claiming** ([path](../12.06-content-id-ugc-claiming/)) — *capability dependency*. Fingerprinting is the only thing that could turn the alternate-mix assertion into a verified fact (DT-13, Q-04).
- Touches **12.07 Identifier Assignment** ([path](../12.07-identifier-assignment-at-delivery.md)) — *shared entity*. One recording, one ISRC, forever (12.07 D-01) is the invariant that makes this feature's whole model coherent. Reuse on a later release is normal; a distinct recording gets a new one.
- Touches **24 Trust, Safety & Disputes** ([path](../../24-trust-safety-disputes/)) — *signal routing*. Fraud-heuristic shapes (DT-08) and Producer/Musician contradictions about a recording's nature are routed, never adjudicated (09 D-04).
- Touches **01 Identity & Organizations** ([path](../../01-identity-organizations/)) — *shared entity*. A release is owned by an **org entity** (band/label), not a person; individual acts are attributed in the audit trail. The multi-hyphenate case (D-09) is a persona-model consequence, not a permissions bug.
