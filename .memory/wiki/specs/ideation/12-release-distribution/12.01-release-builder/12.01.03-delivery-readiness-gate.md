# Feature: Delivery Readiness Gate

> **Number**: 12.01.03
> **Parent**: [Release Builder & Delivery Readiness](./12.01-release-builder-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The checkpoint that refuses to deliver a release whose provenance is incomplete: splits that do
not sum to 100%, contributors who have not confirmed their credit, recordings with unresolved
ownership, or uncleared samples. It is not a validation rule — 12.01.02 checks whether the DSP
will *accept* the release; this gate checks whether the release is *true*.

**This is the load-bearing feature of the entire domain, and arguably of the product.** Per
problem-statement.md, the root cause is that no system is present at the moment of creation, and
per personas.md the Producer's worst accidental behaviour is closing a project without capturing
splits. Distribution is the only moment in a music career when the artist wants something badly
enough to do the admin. The gate converts that want into the leverage that makes provenance
capturable at all. D-10 raised this domain to `core` on exactly this reasoning.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Sees the gate as a checklist of *people and facts*, not fields: "Splits on track 3 sum to 90% — 10% unassigned", "Jamie hasn't confirmed their bass credit", "Sample on track 5 has no clearance on file". Can chase each item with one action (nudge, assign, resolve) without leaving the gate. Cannot deliver while blocking items remain (subject to Q-01 in the parent index). |
| Producer | Full | Same view, and the **most consequential actor here**: they are the trust broker whose attestation the gate is trying to collect. On a release built from a session they ran, the gate surfaces exactly the facts they know and nobody else does. If they resolve the gate at build time, provenance is captured; if they defer, the platform's core promise fails at the one moment it was collectable. Design implication: the lazy path must be the correct path. |
| Operator | None | Not visible. No Operator holds provenance facts about a recording. (A studio owed a spec-deal points position appears here as a **rights holder** via domain 09 — under the Musician lens, not an Operator permission.) |
| Fan | None | Not visible, and must never be. This is the professional surface at its most professional. |

## Behavior

The gate evaluates a release against provenance conditions sourced from domains 02 (credits),
09 (rights) and 07 (project record). Representative blocking conditions — `[PENDING — Step 5]` for
the exhaustive set and their exact severities:

| Condition | Source domain | Why it blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Splits on a recording do not sum to 100% | 09 | Royalties (10) cannot be paid on an unbalanced split; the unassigned share becomes a black-box dispute later |
| A named contributor has not confirmed their credit | 02 | An unconfirmed credit is a self-claim — the exact thing that makes Jaxsta/Muso.AI reconstructions contested |
| Recording has unresolved ownership (competing claims) | 09 | Delivering contested ownership manufactures the dispute the platform exists to prevent |
| Sample/interpolation present with no clearance on file | 09 / 11 | Exposes every downstream collaborator (a named Musician anti-persona accidental failure) |
| No rights position for the delivering party | 09 | Delivering a master you do not control |

**The gate's real job is social, not technical.** Every blocking item is a person who needs to do
something, so the gate is designed as a chase-list with one-tap nudges, visible response state,
and an escalation path — not as a form with red asterisks. A gate that only says "invalid" pushes
the artist to fabricate a split to get past it, which is worse than no gate.

The counter-attestation asymmetry from personas.md applies: a Producer confirming "Jamie played
bass" is worth more than Jamie claiming it. Where the Producer has attested, the gate should
treat the fact as stronger and say so.

### Happy Path

1. Musician finishes composition and clears blocking validation (12.01.02).
2. Gate evaluates and shows a readiness checklist grouped by *person*, not by field.
3. For each unmet item, Musician takes the offered action — assign the unassigned 10%, nudge Jamie
   to confirm, attach the clearance doc.
4. Contributors confirm (in-app / via notification); items clear live as they respond.
5. Gate reaches green; delivery unlocks; the readiness state is written to the provenance ledger
   as a timestamped fact ("splits balanced and attested at delivery, {date}") — this is the record
   that survives.
6. Release advances to scheduling (12.04.01).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Contributor never responds; release date is imminent | The pressure case Q-01 exists to resolve. Hard gate = release slips. Soft gate = deliver with recorded override. | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — depends on Q-01 |
| Contributor **disputes** their split rather than ignoring it | Gate blocks; routes to dispute handling (24) | "Jamie disagrees with the split on track 3. This needs resolving before delivery." |
| Contributor is not a WeJammin user | Must be invitable to attest without full onboarding — otherwise the gate is unpassable for anyone with an off-platform collaborator | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — likely a lightweight attest-by-link flow |
| Contributor is deceased / unreachable (catalog reissue) | Hard gate is unpassable by construction | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — see parent Q-02 |
| Musician assigns the unassigned 10% to themselves to clear the gate | **Passes the gate and is a real abuse path** — the gate cannot distinguish a correct assignment from a land-grab | Counter-measure: any split change after contributors have viewed it re-opens their confirmation. See DT-04. |
| Splits sum to 100% but were never contested because nobody looked | Passes | Acceptable — attestation, not adjudication, is the standard |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Evaluating against 02/09 | Skeleton checklist |
| Blocked | 1+ unmet blocking conditions | Chase-list by person, each with an action; delivery CTA disabled with a specific reason |
| Awaiting others | All Musician-side actions done; contributors pending | "Waiting on Jamie (nudged 2 days ago)" + escalate action |
| Ready | All conditions met | Green state, delivery unlocked, "attested at {timestamp}" |
| Overridden | Soft-gate override used (if Q-01 resolves soft) | Amber state, override reason recorded and visible to all contributors |
| Error | 02/09 unreachable | Delivery blocked, editing not blocked; auto-retry |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Distribution is the correct enforcement point for split capture because it is the only moment the artist wants something from the platform | problem-statement.md root cause ("absence at the point of truth"); D-10; the domain's own top-level rationale | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is the *second-best* moment, not the best. The best is the session (07/12.01 sources from it). The gate is the **backstop for provenance the session failed to capture**, not the primary capture point. If the gate is doing heavy lifting, capture upstream is failing. That reframing matters: the gate's success metric is *how little it has to block*, not how much it catches. |
| DT-02 | A hard gate maximises provenance capture and should therefore be the design | Naive reading of the thesis | ⚠️ CONTESTED — escalated to parent Q-01 as a product decision. A hard gate on a release date the artist has already announced is leverage the platform may not survive using. It converts "the platform that keeps my record straight" into "the platform that held my album hostage" — which turns D-18's *earned* lock-in into the *hostile* lock-in that problem-statement.md Q-02 explicitly warns against. The gate must be strong enough to work and soft enough to stay loved. Owner's call. |
| DT-03 | Contributors who are not WeJammin users can be ignored | Convenience | ❌ REJECTED — the Musician persona's collaborators are mostly *not yet* on the platform (that is what growth means). A gate unpassable without full onboarding of every collaborator makes early releases impossible and turns the flagship feature into the reason nobody can ship. A lightweight attest-by-link path is mandatory, and it doubles as the product's best viral loop: the gate *forces* a warm invite from a trusted collaborator, which personas.md names as the Musician's switching trigger. |
| DT-04 | The gate is gameable by the release owner assigning unclaimed shares to themselves | Musician anti-persona ("inflates credits"), Producer anti-persona ("assigns themselves a larger split and pushes it through while contributors aren't paying attention") | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real hole. A balanced split is not a *correct* split. Mitigation: attestation must be **per-share, not per-release** — a contributor confirms their own number, and any post-view change to any share re-opens every confirmation. Without this, the gate certifies arithmetic and calls it provenance, which is worse than nothing because it launders a land-grab as verified. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The gate is a **chase-list of people**, not a form of fields | Every blocking item is a human who must act; a form invites fabrication to get past it |
| D-02 | Attestation is **per-share**, and any post-view change re-opens all confirmations | Otherwise the gate certifies arithmetic and launders a land-grab as provenance (DT-04) |
| D-03 | Non-user contributors must be able to attest via a lightweight link | Otherwise the flagship feature blocks every early release (DT-03) |
| D-04 | Gate success is measured by how little it blocks | It is a backstop for upstream capture failure, not the primary capture point (DT-01) |
