# Feature: Delivery Readiness Gate

> **Number**: 12.01.03
> **Parent**: [Release Builder & Delivery Readiness](./12.01-release-builder-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The checkpoint that refuses to deliver a release whose provenance is incomplete: master splits that
do not sum to 100%, split rows nobody consented to, recordings with competing ownership claims, or
uncleared samples. It is not a validation rule — 12.01.02 checks whether the DSP will *accept* the
release; this gate checks whether the release is *true*.

**This is the load-bearing feature of the entire domain, and arguably of the product.** Per
problem-statement.md, the root cause is that no system is present at the moment of creation, and
per personas.md the Producer's worst accidental behaviour is closing a project without capturing
splits. Distribution is the only moment in a music career when the artist wants something badly
enough to do the admin. The gate converts that want into the leverage that makes provenance
capturable at all. D-10 raised this domain to `core` on exactly this reasoning.

**The gate owns nothing.** Every fact it evaluates is owned by 02 (credits), 09 (rights) or 07
(the project record); every remedy it offers is an action in one of those domains. It is a pure
projection with a button — per [12.01 CX-01](./12.01-release-builder-cx.md) synthesis Q1. This
matters more than it sounds: the deepening below repeatedly finds that a mechanism the gate
appears to need already exists upstream, and the correct move is to cite it rather than build a
second one that will drift.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Owns the chase list. Sees blocking items grouped by *person*, not by field: "Track 3 master splits total 90% — 10% unassigned", "Priya hasn't signed her 15% on track 5 (asked 9 days ago, 1 nudge left)", "Sample on track 7 has no clearance on file". Each item carries exactly one action — open a split amendment (09.02.04), send the one nudge they have left, attach the clearance — or, where no action exists, says so plainly rather than showing a dead button (DT-05). Sees split percentages only where 09 already authorises it; the gate never widens 09's visibility. Cannot deliver while blocking items remain (subject to Q-01). |
| Producer | Full | Same surface, and the **most consequential actor here** — the trust broker whose facts the gate is trying to collect. On a release built from a session they ran, they see the roster because they were in the room, and they can clear the items they are authoritative for: attaching a sample declaration, supplying the clearance doc, correcting which recording a contribution attaches to. They **cannot** consent on another party's split row (09.02.04 D-09 — consent is per row, by the party named) and **cannot** attest a credit they are themselves credited on (02.04.01 D-11). Full access to the gate, near-zero authority over its outcome: the domain's recurring high-power/low-authority shape. If they resolve the gate at build time, provenance is captured; if they defer, the platform's core promise fails at the one moment it was collectable. Design implication: the lazy path must be the correct path. |
| Operator | None | Not visible. No Operator holds provenance facts about a *recording*, and 02.04.01 D-05 already forecloses the tempting exception — an Operator attests room facts only, never contributions, because "a room cannot know who played what". A studio owed a spec-deal points position appears here as a **rights holder** via 09, under the Musician lens, not as an Operator permission. |
| Fan | None | Not visible, and must never be. This is the professional surface at its most professional. Per personas.md the Fan "will never file a split sheet"; the only Fan-visible consequence of this gate is a release that does or does not appear on its announced date (12.04.03 pre-save), and the gate must never explain itself to them. |

## Behavior

### Preconditions vs. conditions

The gate has two layers, and conflating them is the failure mode
[CX-02](./12.01-release-builder-cx.md) exists to prevent.

**Preconditions** are machine checks. The gate is **not presented** until they pass — because
chasing a co-writer for a signature on a release that will be rejected for a title-casing rule
spends the one favour the artist gets to ask (CX-02 synthesis Q2). Order the human cost, not the
machine cost.

| # | Precondition | Source | If unmet |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-1 | Zero **blocking** validation findings (warnings and advisories do not gate) | [12.01.02](./12.01.02-metadata-validation-conformance.md) | Gate not presented; user is still in validation |
| P-2 | Every master analysed — pass or advisory | [12.01.04](./12.01.04-audio-artwork-asset-conformance.md), CX-03 | Gate presented in `Checking`, **never** `Blocked`. An unanalysed master is not a failed master. |
| P-3 | Release contains ≥1 recording | [12.01.01](./12.01.01-release-composition-sequencing.md) | Gate not presented |

**Conditions** are the gate's own: social checks over facts other humans own. These are enumerated
exhaustively below.

### The condition taxonomy

Five families. The family determines the semantics, the remedy, and — critically — whether the
item is **chaseable at all**.

#### Family A — Consent (09.02). Blocking · attributable · chaseable.

The party signs their own row. Silence is a visible status, never an answer (09.02.04 D-07).

| # | Condition | Severity | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-1 | Master splits on a recording ≠ 100% | **Blocking** | Royalties (10) cannot be paid on an unbalanced split; the unassigned share becomes a black-box dispute later |
| A-2 | A master split row is unconsented by the party it names | **Blocking** | An unconsented row is one person's assertion about another person's money |
| A-3 | A party's master share **decreased** since their last consent | **Blocking** | 09.02.04 D-09 — friction belongs exactly where the harm is; a decrease requires explicit re-confirmation of the decreased figure |
| A-4 | A master split row reads 0% | **Blocking — data error** | 09.02.04 D-14 forecloses 0% rows entirely (removal-by-agreement or a WFH designation instead). A 0% row reaching the gate means the ledger is wrong, not the release. Routes to 09, not to the artist. |
| A-5 | **Publishing** splits ≠ 100%, or unconsented | **Warning** | Does **not** block delivery — see DT-07. Blocks CMO registration in 10, which is where publishing accuracy becomes load-bearing. |

#### Family B — Conflict (09.04, 02.05, 12.01.05). Blocking where delivery *is* the harm. Not chaseable — routed.

Nobody can be nudged out of a conflict. These route to a case, not to a chase list.

| # | Condition | Severity | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| B-1 | A **competing ownership claim** on the master (09.04.01) — someone asserts the delivering party does not own what they are delivering | **Blocking** | Delivering a master under contested ownership publishes an ownership claim to every DSP on earth. If it is wrong, it is infringement, not an accounting error. This is the one place where delivery itself is the harm. |
| B-2 | A **split percentage dispute** open on a row that is consented and balances (Priya signed 15%, now says it should have been 20%) | **Not blocking** | The share escrows (09.04.03); the record ships. See DT-15 — blocking here makes an open dispute a veto over an uninvolved co-owner's release date, which is the exact weaponisation 09.04.03 DT-01 forecloses. |
| B-3 | A **credit dispute** (02.05) open on a named contributor | **Not blocking the release** — blocks the credit | The contested credit is withheld from the delivered metadata (ERN 12.02.01, RIN 02.08) until resolved; everything else ships. Per the domain's "never claim more than you know" pattern: publishing a claim under active dispute is claiming more than we know. Per 24 D-03, the sanction acts on the *publication*, never on the ownership record. |
| B-4 | (P) line diverges from the 09 ownership record **and a co-owner has objected** | **Blocking** | Resolves [CX-06](./12.01-release-builder-cx.md). An objected divergence is a conflict — Family B — and belongs to 09/24. |
| B-5 | (P) line diverges from 09, **unobjected** | **Warning** | 12.01.05 D-01 already collects a recorded justification and notifies co-owners. Escalating to a block when nobody has objected makes the platform the adjudicator of an uncontested claim — foreclosed by 02.04.02 D-15 and 24 D-10 (the platform does not adjudicate truth). The notified co-owner decides; the gate does not decide for them. |

#### Family C — Third-party rights. Blocking under **both** answers to Q-01. No override exists.

Everything in Families A, B, D concerns people who are already parties. Family C concerns people
who are not — and who never agreed to bear the risk of this release. The release owner has no
standing to override a right that is not theirs to waive.

| # | Condition | Severity | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-1 | A **declared** sample or interpolation with no clearance on file (09.01.06) | **Blocking — no override** | Exposes every downstream collaborator (a named Musician anti-persona accidental failure: "uploads a stem containing an uncleared sample and exposes every collaborator downstream"). The rights holder is not a party to this release and cannot consent to bearing its risk. |
| C-2 | An **undeclared** sample: fingerprint match found (12.06) | **Warning** | Detection is probabilistic. Prompts a declaration or a dismissal-with-reason; the dismissal is recorded. Blocking on a probabilistic match would make a false positive undeliverable. |
| C-3 | A featured artist whose 09 deal record names a label, with no "appears courtesy of" consent attached | **Warning** | DT-08 — real, standard, and universally the reason an indie release gets pulled *after* the fact. Detectable only when the featured artist is on WeJammin with their deal recorded, so it cannot be a block. |
| C-4 | A **declared** AI contribution (02.10) or voice/likeness use with no consent record (09.05) | **Blocking — no override** | Structurally identical to C-1: a third-party right, undeclarable by the person who would benefit from ignoring it. |

#### Family D — Standing & lifecycle (09.03).

| # | Condition | Severity | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-1 | The delivering party's rights position was **revoked or reverted** between the recording being added and delivery (09.03.02) | **Blocking — for the affected territory only** | 12.01.01 D-02 gates add-eligibility on rights position, so "no rights position at all" is caught upstream. The residual case at the gate is the interesting one: the position existed at add-time and does not now. Territory-scoped because reversion is territory-scoped. |
| D-2 | A co-owner has not consented **to this release** (distinct from consenting to their split) | **See Q-04** | 12.01.01 Q-01 lands here. Consent-to-release is a distinct permission from ownership and from split consent, and the industry handles it via label agreements WeJammin may not have. |
| D-3 | A share on the release is **frozen / in escrow** (09.04.03) | **Not blocking** | 09.04.03 D-01: freeze is scoped to a share, never a work. A work-scoped effect would turn one party's dispute into a weapon against uninvolved co-owners. The money is held; the record ships. |

#### Family E — Evidence integrity.

| # | Condition | Severity | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-1 | 02 or 09 unreachable — a condition cannot be evaluated | **Blocking · shown as `Unknown`, never `Ready`** | CX-01 synthesis Q2. Fail closed. A gate that fails open delivers unattested provenance, which is the exact failure the domain exists to prevent. Mirrors 02.04.02 D-11 — on derivation failure the label is *absent*, not defaulted. |
| E-2 | A pinned evidence version moved between gate-green and dispatch | **Re-evaluate · hold dispatch if now unmet** | DT-09. A gate is a check, not a lock. |

### What is deliberately NOT a condition

Recorded explicitly because the `[SURFACE]` pass listed the first of these as blocking, and a
reader will look for it.

| Not a condition | Why not |
|---|---|
| **"A named contributor has not confirmed their credit"** | This act does not exist in the model. 02.04.01 D-11: the attester may never be the credited party. A contributor cannot confirm their own credit; a *witness* attests it. Blocking on attestation is also structurally unchaseable — see DT-06. Removed. |
| A credit sitting at a low provenance rung (02.04.02) | Rung is derived from current evidence and moves both ways (02.04.02 D-01). A low rung is a quality signal, not a falsehood. Blocking on it would make the gate a reputation check. |
| An **open split amendment** on a recording | 09.02.04 D-03: the current version keeps governing while an amendment is pending. Money must not stop because someone opened a proposal — and neither must releases, or opening a frivolous proposal becomes a veto over someone else's release date. See DT-14. |
| A contributor's **refusal** to attest | Invisible by design (02.04.01 D-06/D-07) and correctly so. See DT-06. |
| Loudness, artistic choices, mix decisions | 12.01.04 D-01 — block what a partner rejects; advise what a person might regret. Not the gate's business either. |

### The gate's real job is social, not technical

Every Family A item is a person who needs to do something, so the gate is designed as a chase-list
with attributed nudges, visible response state, and an honest end-state — not as a form with red
asterisks. A gate that only says "invalid" pushes the artist to fabricate a split to get past it,
which is worse than no gate.

The counter-attestation asymmetry from personas.md applies but is **not** the gate's mechanism:
a Producer confirming "Priya played bass" is worth more than Priya claiming it, and that asymmetry
is priced by 02.04.02's rung model. The gate reads the rung and displays it; it does not gate on it.

### Chase mechanics — the gate does not own the ask

Every nudge the gate offers is an act in 02.04.01 or 09.02.04 and inherits their caps. The gate is
a button on someone else's channel and must render that channel's true state.

| Cap | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Split proposal resends | 3 notified resends per rolling 7 days, per proposal | 09.02.04 D-10 |
| Attestation asks | 1 request + ≤2 nudges per (credit × attester), at ≥7d and ≥21d, then **closed forever** | 02.04.01 D-09 |
| Per-recipient budget | ≤5 outstanding, ≤10 per rolling 30 days, **all requesters combined**; excess **queues** | 02.04.01 D-09 |
| Dormancy | 5 consecutive unanswered → channel dormant | 02.04.01 D-09 |
| Suppression | No ask at all between parties with an open dispute (02.05) or an active block (24) | 02.04.01 D-19 |
| Attribution | Requester-initiated only. **The platform never nudges automatically.** | 02.04.01 D-08 |

Consequences the gate must render honestly rather than paper over:

1. A nudge tapped against a full per-recipient budget is **queued, not sent**. The gate says so.
2. The chase action **runs out**. See DT-05 and the `Exhausted` state.
3. Between blocked parties the item has no action *at all* — not a spent one, an absent one.

### Chase-list visibility

The chase list is the **release owner's** view. A contributor sees only their own outstanding item
— never the roster of who else is holding things up. Publishing "Priya is the holdout" to the other
four contributors is a pressure mechanism the platform builds and cannot control, and it converts a
private silence into a public one. This is the same instinct as 02.04.01 D-06 (refusals are never
attributable), applied to silence.

An **override**, by contrast, is published to every contributor (see `Overridden`) — because it is
the owner's own act, not a third party's silence.

### Evidence pinning and re-evaluation

The gate is a projection over already-authored facts, so re-evaluation is a read: it runs
**continuously** from first presentation until dispatch, not on an interval. No blocking spinner —
the concrete latency budget belongs to `/create-prd-compile`, which `meta/constraints.md` has not
yet set; inventing one here would be a fabricated number.

When the gate reaches `Ready` it **pins the version of every fact it was green against**:

| Pinned | Version source |
|---|---|
| Each master split sheet | 09.02.04 D-01 — amendment supersedes, never mutates, so every sheet has a version |
| Each master asset | CX-03 synthesis Q5 — the gate keys on asset version, never a boolean |
| Validation rule pack | 12.01.02 D-02 — rule packs are versioned data |
| Each clearance record | 09.01.06 |

At dispatch, every pin is re-checked (E-2). This is what makes the ledger entry defensible: "splits
balanced and consented at delivery, {timestamp}" is only true if it was still true at delivery.

## Happy Path

1. Musician clears all blocking validation findings (12.01.02) and every master finishes analysis
   (12.01.04). Only now is the gate presented — P-1..P-3 met.
2. Gate evaluates Families A–E against 02/09 and presents a readiness checklist grouped by
   *person*, not by field. Track 3's splits total 90%; Priya's 15% on track 5 is unsigned; the rest
   is green.
3. Musician taps the 10% item → opens a split amendment in 09.02.04, assigning the unassigned 10%
   to the co-writer. The proposal enumerates its downstream damage before sending (09.02.04 D-11).
   The gate item moves from `Blocked` to `Awaiting`.
4. Musician taps Priya's item → sends nudge 1 of 2, attributed to them, not to WeJammin
   (02.04.01 D-08). Gate shows "asked 9 days ago · 1 nudge left".
5. Co-writer consents to the 10%; Priya signs her 15%. Both consents attach to the **recording**,
   not to the release (CX-01 Q5), and are idempotent — so they also clear the same recording's items
   on every other release it appears on.
6. Items clear live as they respond. Gate reaches `Ready`; delivery unlocks; every evidence version
   is pinned.
7. Release advances to scheduling (12.04.01). The gate stays live — it is a projection, and the
   facts underneath it can still move.
8. At dispatch, all pins are re-checked. Unchanged → the release goes out, and readiness is written
   to the provenance ledger as a timestamped fact: "master splits balanced and consented by all
   named parties at delivery, {timestamp}, against split sheet v3". **This is the record that
   survives** — the artifact the user takes with them (problem-statement Q-02).

## Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Contributor never responds; release date is imminent | The pressure case Q-01 exists to resolve — and DT-05 shows it is not a strictness dial but a deadlock. **Hard**: after 1 request + 2 nudges the channel closes forever (02.04.01 D-09) and the release becomes permanently undeliverable with no user action available. **Soft-with-teeth (recommended)**: delivery proceeds with a recorded, published override; the record still says unconsented. | **Hard**: "Priya hasn't signed. You've used every nudge. Your options: remove track 5, or wait." **Soft**: "Deliver anyway? Every contributor will see that you did, permanently, and this release will carry an *unverified splits* state until Priya signs." |
| Contributor **disputes** their split percentage (B-2) | Not blocking. Share escrows (09.04.03 D-01); delivery proceeds under the current governing sheet | "Priya disputes her 15% on track 5. Her share will be held until it's settled — the release isn't affected." |
| Someone claims they own the master outright (B-1) | **Blocking.** Routed to 09.04.02 as a case. Not chaseable, no nudge affordance | "Someone else claims ownership of track 5. This has to be settled before delivery — it's not something we can nudge anyone about." |
| Contributor is not a WeJammin user | The link delivers the **ask**, never the **answer** — 02.04.01 D-18: an attestation is recorded only through an authenticated act, and a link alone never records one. The link lands on a minimal authenticated identity creation, then the consent act. Costs the recipient one extra tap; the alternative is forgeable consent, which is not evidence. This is also the product's best growth loop — a warm invite from a trusted collaborator, which personas.md names as the Musician's switching trigger (DT-03). | Recipient: "Dara added you to *{release}* — you're down for 15% of track 5. Confirm it's yours." → sign-in → sign. Owner: "Priya isn't on WeJammin yet. She'll get a link; she'll need to sign in to sign." |
| Contributor is deceased / unreachable (catalog reissue) | Consent is **unobtainable**, not merely absent. 09.02.04 D-07 forecloses deemed consent; there is no waiver (DT-11). The gate distinguishes *unconsented* from *unconsentable*: an imported historical split sheet is evaluated for **balance and conflict** (A-1, B-1, C-1), never for **fresh consent** (A-2), because attestation of a 1998 session is impossible and demanding it makes catalog undeliverable. The record ships saying "imported, unattested" — which is true. See DT-16 and parent Q-02. | "This split came from your imported catalog. We can't ask a 1998 session to sign, and we won't pretend it did — the record will show it as imported." |
| Musician assigns the unassigned 10% to themselves | **Passes A-1 and is a real abuse path** — the gate cannot distinguish a correct assignment from a land-grab (DT-04). Mitigation is **already upstream and must not be rebuilt here**: consent is per-row (09.02.04 D-09), a decreased share requires explicit re-confirmation (D-09), and a material amendment invalidates every attestation on the row (09.02.04 D-10 / **02.04.01 D-10** — the flat identity test over (party, role, work); 02.04.02 D-13's entailment carve-out is superseded). The gate reflects these; it does not implement them. | The other parties see the delta per party (09.02.04 D-05), not just the new numbers. |
| Splits sum to 100% but nobody looked | Passes | Acceptable — consent, not adjudication, is the standard. The ledger records who signed and when, not whether it was fair. |
| Owner taps nudge; recipient's per-recipient budget is full | **Queued, not sent** (02.04.01 D-09). The gate must not report a send that did not happen — "never claim more than you know" | "Priya has a lot of these right now. Yours is queued and will go out when there's room." |
| Every nudge is spent; contributor still silent | New `Exhausted` state. The item has no action. This is a designed end-state, not an error (DT-05) | "Nothing left to try on this one. Remove track 5, or wait for Priya." (+ override, if Q-01 resolves soft) |
| Owner and contributor have blocked each other (24), or have an open dispute (02.05) | Ask is **suppressed** at source (02.04.01 D-19). The item has no action from the moment it appears | "You and Priya can't message each other. This one can't be resolved here." |
| Gate goes green; owner then edits the split | Cannot happen silently — a split edit **is** an amendment requiring consent (09.02.04). The gate re-opens because the ledger moved, not because the gate watched the UI | Gate returns to `Blocked`, naming the amendment |
| Same recording is on two releases; release B's owner opens an amendment on it | Release A's gate **stays green** — 09.02.04 D-03: the current version keeps governing. A's owner is notified via the amendment's downstream-damage enumeration (09.02.04 D-11). A ships under the split that was true at delivery. See DT-14 | A's owner: "Someone proposed a change to track 5's splits. Your release isn't blocked — it'll go out under the current sheet." |
| Contributor consents at the exact moment the owner removes the track | Consent is idempotent and attaches to the **recording**, not the release (CX-01 Q5). Never lost; still valid when the recording resurfaces on a later release | Contributor sees a normal confirmation. Owner sees the track gone. |
| Track removed while a nudge is outstanding | The pending ask is **withdrawn**. Leaving "please sign your split on track 5" alive for a track that no longer exists is a real trust leak (CX-01 Q4) | Contributor: the ask disappears. |
| Split sheet is amended between gate-green and dispatch | E-2 fires. Pin moved → re-evaluate. If the new sheet is unconsented, dispatch is **held** and the owner is told exactly which fact moved (DT-09) | "Held. Track 5's splits changed after this release was cleared — the new sheet isn't signed yet." |
| Rights revert in one territory between green and dispatch (D-1) | Territory-scoped block. The release delivers everywhere else | "Your rights to track 5 reverted in Germany on {date}. We'll deliver everywhere else and hold Germany." |
| Master is replaced after gate-green | Asset version pin moves (CX-03 Q5). Analysis re-runs; the gate returns to `Checking`, not `Blocked` — an unanalysed master is not a failed one | "New master on track 5. Re-checking — nothing's wrong yet." |
| Rule pack updates between green and dispatch | P-1 is a **precondition**, not a condition — a rule-pack regression un-presents the gate rather than failing it. The release falls back to validation and says why; it does not fail silently at delivery. Resolves CX-02 Q5 from the gate's side | "Apple changed a rule after this was cleared. One thing to fix before it goes out." |
| 09 reachable, 02 unreachable (partial) | Per-condition `Unknown`. Family A still evaluates; Family B credit conditions do not. Delivery blocked (E-1); editing **not** blocked | Per-item "can't check right now" + auto-retry. Never a blanket red. |
| Self-produced solo release | Zero conditions. The gate is **green by construction** and does nothing — see DT-12. This is the majority release shape and the gate must not tax it | `Empty` state: "Nothing to resolve — you hold every share and every credit on this release." |
| Multi-hyphenate is both release owner and a credited contributor | Their own split row still requires their consent (one tap), but their attestation of their own credit is void by construction (02.04.01 D-11, 02.04.02 D-08 — independence is per human, not per hat). The gate must not offer an action that would be void | The self-attest affordance is **absent**, not disabled — matching 02.04.01 D-14's rule that below the floor the affordance is absent |
| Split rows total 100% but one row reads 0% (A-4) | Ledger error, not a release error. 0% rows cannot exist (09.02.04 D-14). Routes to 09; the artist is not asked to fix it | "Something's wrong with track 5's split sheet on our side. We're on it — you don't need to do anything." |
| Fingerprint match on a track the artist swears is original (C-2) | Warning only. Dismissal-with-reason is recorded and is itself evidence | "This sounds like it might contain *{track}*. If it doesn't, tell us why and we'll note it." |
| Malicious contributor opens a bogus ownership claim to block a rival's date | B-1 blocks — so this is a real veto. **The cost of the veto is raised, not removed**: to block a release you must claim the *master* (B-1), not haggle over a *percentage* (B-2, which escrows and ships). Claiming a master you do not own is a 24 fraud matter with sanctions attached. See DT-15 | Owner: routed to the case, with the claimant named. |
| Contributor consents, then retracts (02.04.03) before dispatch | Retraction preserves history, never deletes (02.04.03 D-01). E-2 fires at dispatch; the row is unconsented again; delivery holds | "Priya withdrew her signature on track 5. Held." |
| Owner overrides, then the contributor signs later (Q-01 soft) | The override does not vanish. "Overrode, then it was resolved" ≠ "was never overridden" — the same principle as 02.04.03 D-01. The release's `unverified splits` state lifts; the ledger keeps both facts | "Priya signed. This release is no longer marked unverified — the override stays on the record." |

## States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Not presented | P-1 or P-3 unmet | The gate does not exist yet. User is in validation or composition. Deliberate — the gate must not be a preview of future homework. |
| Checking | P-2 pending — assets still analysing | "Checking your masters." Per-track progress. **Not** `Blocked` — CX-03: an unanalysed master is not a failed master, and collapsing them trains users to distrust the gate and click through it. |
| Loading | Evaluating conditions against 02/09 | Skeleton checklist. Never a blocking spinner over the builder. |
| Empty | Zero conditions — solo self-produced release (DT-12) | "Nothing to resolve — you hold every share and every credit on this release." Delivery unlocked. No checklist, no ceremony. |
| Blocked | ≥1 unmet blocking condition **with an available action** | Chase-list grouped by person. Each item: the fact, the person, the one action, the cadence state ("asked 9 days ago · 1 nudge left"). Delivery CTA disabled with a specific reason — never a generic "not ready". |
| Awaiting others | All owner-side actions taken; parties pending | "Waiting on Priya — asked 9 days ago, 1 nudge left." Per-item, not a global spinner. |
| Queued | Nudge tapped against a full per-recipient budget (02.04.01 D-09) | "Queued — Priya has a lot of these right now." Distinct from `Awaiting`, because we have not actually asked yet and must not imply we have. |
| Exhausted | Every available action spent; item still unmet (DT-05) | "Nothing left to try on this one." Remaining moves listed explicitly: remove the track, wait, or (if Q-01 resolves soft) override. **The button is gone, not greyed** — a dead button is a lie about what is possible. |
| Unresolvable | Ask suppressed at source — block or open dispute (02.04.01 D-19), or a Family B conflict | "This can't be resolved here." Routes to the case (09.04.02 / 02.05 / 24). No nudge affordance ever existed. |
| Unknown | 02/09 unreachable — a condition cannot be evaluated (E-1) | Per-item "can't check right now" + auto-retry. Delivery blocked; **editing not blocked**. Never renders as `Ready`; never renders as a blanket red. Mirrors 02.04.02 D-11 — absent, not defaulted. |
| Partial | Some conditions evaluated, some `Unknown` | Mixed checklist with per-item unknown rows. Honest about what was and wasn't checked — the same shape as 12.01.02's "Checked {N} of {M} stores". |
| Ready | All blocking conditions met | Green. Delivery unlocked. Evidence versions pinned. "Splits balanced and signed by all named parties — {timestamp}." |
| Held at dispatch | A pin moved between green and dispatch (E-2, DT-09) | "Held. Track 5's splits changed after this release was cleared." Names the exact fact that moved. Not a silent failure, not a silent send. |
| Overridden | Soft-gate override used (only if Q-01 resolves soft) | Amber. Override reason recorded, permanent in the ledger, **published to every contributor**, and the release carries an `unverified splits` state until resolved. |
| Error | Gate evaluation itself failed (not the facts — the projection) | Non-destructive banner + auto-retry. Editing unaffected. |
