# Feature: Weather Monitoring & Contingency

> **Number**: 18.17
> **Parent**: [Show Production & Touring](./show-production-touring-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

For outdoor and temporary-structure shows, weather is not colour commentary — it is the thing that
stops the show, and wind load on a temporary roof is a life-safety limit with a number attached.
This feature attaches contingency thresholds and an agreed decision chain to an event, and watches
the forecast against them.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees the forecast on their day sheet and any contingency state ("show on / weather hold / delayed"). Never makes the call — but is the person waiting in a van to find out. |
| Producer | Full | Sets thresholds (wind speed, lightning distance, rain), names the decision-maker, drafts the contingency plan, and receives escalating alerts as thresholds approach. |
| Operator | Full (own site) | For an outdoor site, the Operator usually owns the structural limits and often *is* the decision-maker — their structure, their licence, their liability. Supplies the thresholds the structure was certified to. |
| Fan | Read-only (derived) | Sees only the public consequence — delay, evacuation instruction, cancellation — pushed via 19/21 to ticket-holders. Never sees thresholds or the internal decision chain. |

## Behavior

Thresholds are attached per event and derived where possible from the structure's certificate
(18.16). Forecast data is monitored ahead of the show; breach or projected breach raises an alert
to the named decision-maker, who records a decision. The system never makes the call — it makes
the call *unavoidable* and records who made it.

### Happy Path

1. Producer marks the event outdoor; contingency thresholds are requested.
2. Thresholds and a named decision-maker are set; a contingency plan is attached.
3. Forecast is monitored from T-72h; a projected wind breach raises an amber alert at T-6h.
4. Decision-maker records "delay doors 45 min" with a reason; the schedule (18.07.03) updates.
5. Ticket-holders are notified through 19; crew and band via the day sheet re-notify.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Forecast provider unavailable | Alerting degrades openly; never silently stops watching | "Weather data unavailable since 14:02 — monitor manually" |
| Threshold breached with no decision recorded | Escalates up the named chain; unresolved breach is a loud, persistent state | "WIND THRESHOLD BREACHED — no decision recorded (23 min)" |
| Indoor show marked outdoor by mistake | Thresholds requested for a room with no weather exposure | "This venue is indoor — skip contingency?" |
| Local microclimate differs from the forecast | Forecast is advisory; on-site anemometer reading can be entered manually and overrides | "On-site reading 52 km/h overrides forecast 38 km/h" |
| Decision-maker unreachable at the moment it matters | Fallback chain named at setup, not improvised at T-0 | "Escalated to [fallback] after 10 min" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Contingency opened | Threshold form skeleton |
| Empty | Outdoor event, no thresholds | "No thresholds set — this show has no weather plan" |
| Populated | Thresholds + monitoring active | Forecast vs threshold gauges and time-to-show |
| Error | Provider failure | "Monitoring stopped — manual watch required" |
| Partial | Thresholds set, no decision-maker | "No one is named to make the call" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a weather widget — nice-to-have, low value | First-pass instinct | ❌ REJECTED as framing — a weather *widget* is worthless, but a **threshold + named decision-maker + recorded decision** is a life-safety and liability artifact. Temporary stage collapses have killed people, and the failure was always "nobody owned the call". The value is the decision chain, not the forecast. |
| DT-02 | The system should auto-cancel on breach | Automation instinct | ❌ REJECTED — the platform must not make a life-safety call from a third-party forecast. It escalates, records, and makes the absence of a decision impossible to ignore. Autonomy here is both wrong and uninsurable. |
| DT-03 | Weather monitoring is an integration, therefore not-product | Node Classification Gate | ✅ CONFIRMED in part — the forecast provider integration is routed to `/create-prd-stack`. The thresholds, decision chain and recorded decision are product and stay here. |
| DT-04 | Weather also matters off the stage | Domain expert lens | ✅ CONFIRMED — winter routing over a mountain pass is a drive-time and driver-safety fact (18.12.03), not a stage one. The forecast is relevant to routing feasibility as well as to the show itself. |
| DT-05 | Relevance is narrow — most shows are indoor | Scope lens | ✅ CONFIRMED — this is why the MoSCoW proposal is `wont` for now despite the argument in DT-01. It is genuinely important *and* genuinely not the wedge; festivals and outdoor promoters are a later audience. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The system never makes the weather call — it escalates and records who did | A life-safety decision from a third-party forecast is both wrong and uninsurable |
| D-02 | Value is the decision chain and the record, not the forecast display | Stage collapses trace to "nobody owned the call", not to a missing forecast |
