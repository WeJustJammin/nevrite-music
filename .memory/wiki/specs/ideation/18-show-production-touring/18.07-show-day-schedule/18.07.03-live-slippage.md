# Feature: Live Schedule Slippage & Real-Time Updates

> **Number**: 18.07.03
> **Parent**: [Show Day Schedule & Timing](./18.07-show-day-schedule-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The show day as it actually runs: the truck is 90 minutes late, soundcheck compresses, doors hold,
the support overruns, and the curfew margin evaporates. Every schedule in live music is obsolete by
mid-afternoon, and the industry's answer is shouting and WhatsApp. This is the feature that makes
the platform useful *on the day* rather than only before it.

> **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list. See DT-01.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Gets told their soundcheck moved, on the device in their pocket, without asking anyone. This is the whole of what they need and roughly none of what they get today. |
| Producer | Full | Slips items, sees the downstream cascade before committing, and watches the curfew margin as it shrinks. The person who is currently doing this arithmetic in their head at 17:30. |
| Operator | **Full** | Slips their own items — load-in, doors — because the delay is frequently theirs (the room wasn't clear, the previous event overran). They see the curfew consequence immediately, which is their problem. |
| Fan | Read-only (public times only) | Only if the act publishes it: doors holding or stage times moving, pushed to ticket-holders via 19/21. Fans standing in a queue in the rain are the audience for this, and the platform is the only party that knows. |

## Behavior

An item is slipped by moving it or marking it late; downstream items shift by default, and the
conflict check (18.07.02) re-runs immediately with the new arithmetic. The cascade is previewed
before it's committed, and the change propagates to whoever is affected — day sheets, call times,
and (if published) fan-facing set times.

### Happy Path

1. Truck arrives 90 minutes late; Producer slips load-in.
2. Cascade preview: soundchecks compress, doors hold 30 min, curfew margin drops to 4 min.
3. Producer commits with an adjustment — support's set trimmed by 5 min.
4. Affected people are notified: crew calls, band, venue, support act.
5. Doors hold is published to ticket-holders through 19.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Slip breaks curfew | The check re-runs live; the consequence is immediate and loud | "Curfew now breached by 11 min — what gives?" |
| No signal in the venue | The feature is useless exactly when needed most — the domain's hardest constraint | Surface settled (PWA, v1 — `meta/constraints.md` § Project Surfaces, D-28/D-70); the offline behaviour the user sees is `[PENDING — /create-prd-architecture]` — see Q-01 |
| Nobody updates the system | The schedule is fiction and the platform is worse than useless (it's confidently wrong) | Staleness indicator: "last updated 14:02" |
| Two people slip conflicting items | Concurrent mutation on a jointly-owned timeline at high tempo | Per-item ownership limits it; genuine conflict needs a rule |
| Published set times move | A public promise changes; ticket-holders were told 21:00 | "This is public — 340 ticket-holders will be notified" |
| Slip cascades into crew hours / driver hours | A late load-out can break a driver's legal hours for tomorrow | "Load-out 00:30 → driver rest breaks tomorrow's 09:00 departure" |
| Everything is late but nobody wants to say so | Social, not technical; the system can only make the cost visible | Margin display, not blame |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The sweep's schedule model is incomplete without slippage | Deep Think — domain expert lens | ✅ CONFIRMED, node created — the candidate list has "Run of Show, Set Times, Changeover & Load-in/Soundcheck Scheduling", which describes building a plan. **But the plan's defining property is that it is wrong by 16:00.** Every incumbent produces a static schedule; the actual coordination happens in WhatsApp, and the schedule becomes a historical document. A platform that only builds the plan cedes the entire show day. This is the difference between a document tool and being in the room. |
| DT-02 | Slippage is just editing the schedule | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — the difference is **cascade and propagation under time pressure**. At 11:00 a schedule change is a conversation; at 17:30 it's a chain of consequences across crew calls, curfew, driver hours and 340 ticket-holders, and nobody has time to trace it. The value is the cascade preview, not the edit. |
| DT-03 | Everyone will keep it updated | Optimism | ❌ REJECTED — this is the feature's central risk and it is unsolved. If the system isn't updated it is *worse than nothing*, because a confidently-wrong schedule stops people asking. Staleness must be visible, and the update burden must approach zero — one tap on "doors happened", not a form. Whether that's achievable is the honest open question. |
| DT-04 | Offline is an architecture problem | Boundary lens | ⚠️ CONFIRMED AS THE HARDEST CONSTRAINT — this feature is needed *precisely* where signal is worst (a venue basement, a load-in dock, a room full of 500 phones). It is not a nice-to-have NFR here; it decides whether the feature exists. Combined with 18.06.02 D-03 and 18.10 Q-01, this is the domain's strongest case for a mobile/PWA surface — which `meta/constraints.md` currently leaves open. |
| DT-05 | Slippage is confined to today | Scope lens | ❌ REJECTED — a late load-out breaks a driver's rest, which breaks tomorrow's departure, which threatens the next date's load-in. The cascade **crosses days**, into 18.12.03 (driver hours) and 18.11.02 (routing feasibility). Tonight's overrun is tomorrow's cancelled show, and nobody connects those two facts today. |
