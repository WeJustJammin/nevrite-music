# Feature: Curfew, Sound Limit & Conflict Checking

> **Number**: 18.07.02
> **Parent**: [Show Day Schedule & Timing](./18.07-show-day-schedule-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Validates the day's timeline against the room's operating constraints: hard curfew, sound limits,
permitted hours, load-out noise windows. It is pure arithmetic that nobody does until it's too late.

> **The constraints have two provenance classes, and the check does not care which** (D-06, per
> DQ-14.4 / global D-72). **Where the room's regime profile declares an instrument that imposes
> operating hours** (`16.01.06` D-05), the constraint is statutory: a breach is a fine, a review of
> the instrument, and a genuine route to the venue closing. **Where the profile declares none — as
> the US launch profile does** — the same numbers are the **Operator's own claims labelled as
> claims** (`16.02.03` D-01/D-13), theirs to set and to lift, and a breach is a commercial matter
> between the parties, not an offence. The arithmetic, the margin bands and the notification chain
> are identical in both states; only the **stakes sentence** the platform shows changes. This file
> never names the instrument.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees the consequence in plain terms: "you have 45 minutes, not 52 — cut two songs." Doesn't see the constraint record behind it. **The consequence clause is provenance-dependent** (D-06): "…or the venue gets fined" only where the profile declares a statutory instrument; where it declares none the honest line is "…or you overrun the venue's stated hard stop." |
| Producer | Full | Sees every conflict with its cause and its margin, and decides what gives — a shorter set, a tighter changeover, earlier doors. |
| Operator | **Config (supplies constraints)** | The room's declared operating conditions *are* the constraints. They declare curfew, sound limits and permitted hours in 16; they don't run the check. They see conflicts raised against their room and are the party who bears a breach. **Whether those values are theirs to write is profile-dependent** (D-06): statutory-derived and read-only where an instrument imposes them (`16.01.06` D-02/D-05), their own editable claims where none does (`16.02.03` D-01/D-13). |
| Fan | None | Invisible. A fan feels it as a short set or a hard stop mid-encore. |

## Behavior

Runs continuously against the timeline (18.07.01), comparing the end of the last set plus load-out
against the venue's curfew, and the sound-limit conditions against the show's nature. Constraints
come from the venue's data in 16. The check reports margin, not just pass/fail — because "you clear
curfew by 3 minutes" is a different fact from "you clear it by 40".

### Happy Path

1. Timeline is built; the checker runs automatically.
2. Sets + changeovers + load-out are compared against curfew and permitted hours.
3. Margin is reported: "curfew 23:00, last set ends 22:47 — 13 min margin".
4. A setlist change eats the margin; the check re-runs and flags it before it's a problem.
5. The margin is on the frozen pack and the day sheet, so it's visible to whoever is running late.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Set durations are estimates | The margin is an estimate too — it must say so | "13 min margin (±8 min — 7 songs unmeasured)" |
| Venue has no curfew data | `unknown`, never "no curfew" — the difference is a fine | "No curfew data for this room — confirm with the venue" |
| Sound limit is a limiter that cuts power | Different failure entirely: the PA dies mid-song, not a fine | "Room has a 96 dB limiter — hard cut, not a warning" |
| Curfew is for the *audience*, load-out has a separate window | Two constraints, often confused | "Curfew 23:00 (music off) · Load-out noise stops 23:45" |
| Margin is technically fine but unrealistically tight | 3 minutes is not a margin in live music | Margin banded — "TIGHT: 3 min" not "✅ clear" |
| Encore not counted | Conditional items must be counted at worst case | "With encore: overruns by 4 min" |
| Show runs late anyway | This is 18.07.03's job — the check becomes live | Handed to slippage |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a validation rule on the schedule form, not a feature | Node Classification Gate | ❌ REJECTED — it consumes data from **three other domains** (venue licence conditions from 16, set durations from 18.06, bill structure from 18.02), reports a graded margin rather than valid/invalid, and its failure has legal consequences no other schedule error has. A form validator doesn't reach across domains or cost someone a licence. |
| DT-02 | Curfew is one number | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — there is music-off curfew, venue-empty curfew, load-out noise curfew, and permitted-hours licensing, and they're all different times. Conflating them produces a check that passes while the act is still breaching something. The load-out window is the one most often forgotten and the one neighbours complain about. |
| DT-03 | Pass/fail is the useful output | Design instinct | ❌ REJECTED — **margin is the product**. "Clears by 3 minutes" and "clears by 40 minutes" are the same boolean and completely different nights. In live music a 3-minute margin *is* a breach, because something always runs long. The check must band the margin, not just compare it. |
| DT-04 | The estimate problem is acceptable | Optimism | ❌ REJECTED — an estimated margin computed from unmeasured set durations is false precision on a number with legal consequences. The uncertainty must propagate visibly (18.06 D-04). This is the single strongest argument for measuring setlist durations at rehearsal, and it's why 18.19 DT-02 matters more than it looks. |
| DT-05 | The act bears the curfew risk | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — **the venue pays.** The fine, the licence review and the closure risk are the Operator's, while the incentive to play longer is the act's. That asymmetry is the reason curfew is contested at all, and it's why the constraint must come from the venue's data rather than the act's assumption. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Margin, banded — not pass/fail | A 3-minute margin and a 40-minute margin are the same boolean and different nights |
| D-02 | Curfew is several distinct constraints, not one number | Music-off, venue-empty, load-out noise and permitted hours are different times |
| D-03 | Estimate uncertainty propagates into the margin | False precision on a number that costs a licence is worse than an honest range |
| D-04 | Constraints are venue-sourced, because the venue bears the penalty | The act's incentive is to play longer; the Operator pays the fine |
| D-05 | Default margin bands: `BREACH` (< 0 min), `TIGHT` (0–10 min), `CLEAR` (> 10 min), each widened by the estimate uncertainty (`±X`) before banding | Resolves Q-01 at a safe default. 10 min is the floor at which a single over-run song does not automatically breach; genre/venue overrides deferred to `/write-be-spec`. Bands are Operator-overridable per room, not a global constant |
| D-06 | **The check is provenance-blind; only the stakes copy is provenance-dependent.** The constraint is statutory **only where the room's regime profile declares an instrument that imposes it** (`16.01.06` D-05); where it declares none — the US launch profile — the same values are Operator claims labelled as claims, theirs to set and to lift (`16.02.03` D-01/D-13, `16.06.03` D-25). Arithmetic, bands and notifications are identical in both states. This file names the **constraint**, never the instrument | DQ-14.1/14.4, global D-72. The prior text asserted "the constraints a venue's **licence** imposes" and "in the UK a genuine route to a venue closing" as the universal model, which contradicts the launch profile and imports a retired UK vocabulary. D-04 (venue-sourced, because the venue bears the penalty) is untouched and is in fact stronger in the claim state: with no authority behind the number, the venue is the *only* source for it |
