# Feature: Rider Templates, Versioning & Per-Date Overrides

> **Number**: 18.04.04
> **Parent**: [Riders](./18.04-riders-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The machinery that lets one rider serve forty dates: templates to start from, versions so a rider
that changed in March doesn't rewrite what a February show was advanced against, and per-date
overrides for the dates that genuinely differ (acoustic set, festival slot, in-store). Without it,
riders get copy-pasted per tour and immediately drift into forty divergent truths.

> **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list. See DT-01.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees which rider version applies tonight and what's different about this date ("acoustic set — no drum riser"). For a DIY act, they use templates as the on-ramp to having a rider at all. |
| Producer | Full | Creates and maintains templates, cuts versions, sets per-date overrides, and decides when a recurring redline should become a rider amendment. |
| Operator | None | Sees only the resolved rider for their date. Version history and other dates' overrides are none of their business and would leak the act's tour shape. |
| Fan | None | Never visible. |

## Behavior

Resolution is layered: **template → act rider → tour rider → date override → accepted redlines**.
Any date resolves to exactly one rider. Versions are immutable once a date has been advanced against
them — a frozen advance (18.03.05) references a version, not a moving document. The recurring-redline
signal (18.04.01 DT-03) surfaces here as a proposed amendment.

### Happy Path

1. Producer starts a new act's rider from a template ("4-piece rock, small clubs").
2. The rider is edited into the act's real needs and attached to the tour.
3. One date is an acoustic in-store; Producer sets a date override removing the backline.
4. Mid-tour, a gear change requires a rider update; a new version is cut.
5. Already-advanced dates keep v1; upcoming dates resolve to v2 and their diffs re-run.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Rider changes after dates were advanced | Advanced dates keep their version; upcoming dates re-diff | "v2 affects 6 upcoming dates. 3 advanced dates stay on v1." |
| Change is safety-critical (power spec was wrong) | Advanced dates must be re-opened, not silently left on v1 | "This change affects advanced dates — re-open them?" |
| Same redline on 7 of 9 dates | Prompts an amendment — the rider is asking for something the act doesn't need | "You've traded this away 7 times — remove it from the rider?" |
| Override vs redline confusion | Override = our variation for this date; redline = negotiated exception with the venue | "This is your change, not a venue negotiation — override or redline?" |
| Template drift (act edits diverge from template) | Templates are a starting point, never a live link | "Templates don't sync — this is your rider now" |
| Act plays two set types on one tour | Two tour riders, or one with overrides — both allowed | "Full band (7 dates) · Acoustic (2 dates)" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Version view opened | History skeleton |
| Empty | Single version, no overrides | "One rider, all dates" |
| Populated | Versions/overrides exist | Version timeline + per-date resolution map |

| Partial | Override set, not advanced | "Override applies to [date] — not yet advanced" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The sweep's rider model is incomplete without a version/override layer | Deep Think — domain expert lens | ✅ CONFIRMED, node created — the candidate list has "Technical & Hospitality Riders" as a flat thing. But a rider's defining property is that **one serves forty dates over years**, and the moment a date needs something different, a flat model forces a copy. Copies are how riders drift into forty truths, which is the state of the industry today. The version layer is what makes 18.04 D-02 (act-level riders, per-date overrides) implementable rather than aspirational. |
| DT-02 | An advanced date can follow the live rider | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — a venue advanced against what the rider said *then*. Silently moving it is a broken agreement and, if the frozen pack has evidentiary weight (18.03.05 Q-02), a corrupted record. Versions must be immutable once advanced against. The safety-critical exception is an explicit re-open, not a silent update. |
| DT-03 | Overrides and redlines are the same mechanism | Modelling instinct | ❌ REJECTED — and the distinction is load-bearing. An **override** is unilateral: *we* need something different tonight (acoustic set). A **redline** is bilateral: the *venue* can't meet something and we agreed an alternative. Collapsing them makes the act's own choices look like venue failures, which poisons the recurring-redline signal that DT-04 depends on. |
| DT-04 | Templates are a convenience feature | Scope lens | ⚠️ PARTIALLY REJECTED — they're the **on-ramp for acts that have no rider at all**, which is most of the target market. A working musician playing 200-cap rooms doesn't have a rider; they have a text message. A template is how they get a diffable rider without knowing what one is — and the diff is worthless until they have one. Templates are an adoption mechanism, not a nicety. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Resolution order: template → act → tour → date override → redlines | Any date must resolve to exactly one rider, deterministically |
| D-02 | Versions are immutable once a date has been advanced against them | The venue agreed to what it said then; silently moving it breaks the agreement and the record |
| D-03 | Overrides (unilateral) and redlines (bilateral) are different objects | Collapsing them makes our own choices look like venue failures and poisons the amendment signal |
| D-04 | Templates never live-sync to derived riders | A template is a starting point; a live link would rewrite acts' riders from under them |
