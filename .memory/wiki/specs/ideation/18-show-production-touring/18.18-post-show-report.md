# Feature: Post-Show Report & Notes

> **Number**: 18.18
> **Parent**: [Show Production & Touring](./show-production-touring-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The nightly record of what actually happened: attendance, what ran late, what gear failed, what
the venue promised and didn't have, and whether you'd come back. It closes the event lifecycle and
— crucially — it is the mechanism by which the platform learns the *real* truth about a venue,
correcting domain 16's self-reported capability data with evidence from people who were there.

It exists because the record that matters most is the one nobody has time to write: after load-out
at 01:00, no touring party types a report from scratch. So the report is a *confirmation surface*,
not a blank form — the system pre-fills everything it already knows (planned-vs-actual schedule,
the advance diff, unresolved shortfalls) and asks the human only for the judgement it cannot infer.
Filing is one confirming action, not an admin chore, which is the only design under which reports
actually get filed.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (own show) | Files a short report — how the room sounded, how they were treated, would they return. For DIY acts this is the whole report. Sees their own history of a venue before booking it again. |
| Producer | Full | Files the substantive report: schedule variance vs plan, technical failures, capability discrepancies (promised console vs actual), crew performance, incidents. Their report is the highest-trust input to venue truth. |
| Operator | Read-only (aggregate) + Config (right of reply) | Cannot see individual attributed reports about their room by default; sees aggregate signals and any *capability discrepancy* raised against them, with a right of reply and a path to correct their listing. |
| Fan | None | Never sees production reports. Fan-facing venue sentiment is a separate, differently-sourced thing in domains 16/20 — conflating the two would turn a professional log into a public review. |

## Behavior

The report is pre-filled from what the system already knows — planned vs actual times from the
schedule, the advance diff, unresolved shortfalls — so the human only adds judgement. Structured
discrepancy items ("rider said Midas M32, room had a Behringer X32") route to domain 16 as
correction proposals rather than free-text complaints.

### Happy Path

1. Show hits `performed`; the report opens pre-filled with planned-vs-actual variances.
2. Producer confirms or corrects the auto-detected items and adds notes.
3. Producer flags one capability discrepancy against the venue listing.
4. Report is filed; the event moves toward settled; the discrepancy becomes a proposed correction in 16.
5. Next time anyone advances that venue, the correction is visible in the diff.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Report never filed | Event still settles; venue truth loses the signal; a reminder decays after N days | "3 shows without reports — your venue history is thin" |
| Venue disputes a discrepancy | Correction is held; both sides' evidence attached; unresolved items age out or escalate to 24 | Operator: "An artist reports the console differs — confirm or dispute" |
| A single report is malicious or wrong | One report never rewrites capability data; corroboration threshold required before a listing changes | Operator sees nothing until threshold met |
| An incident (injury, safety) is reported | Routes to 24 Trust & Safety, not the venue-truth loop | "This looks like a safety incident — it will be handled separately" |
| Report contains a payment dispute | Routes to 17 settlement, not here | "Settlement issues are handled on the deal — open it there?" |
| User with no role on this show tries to file | Blocked — only the show's assigned production party (Producer/TM) and performing Musicians can report; a Fan or unrelated user cannot | "You weren't on this show — only its production team and performers can file a report" |
| Two production members file for the same show | One report per show; contributors edit a *shared* report rather than creating duplicates; last-write-wins per field with an edit-attribution trail | "Alex updated the schedule variance 4 min ago" |
| Report edited after filing | Edits allowed within an editable window (e.g. 7 days), then locked; every change is versioned so a correction routed to 16 can't be silently rewritten after corroboration | "Filed — editable until {date}. 2 revisions." |
| Operator never answers a right-of-reply | The reply prompt ages out after N days; unanswered discrepancies proceed on the corroboration path without the Operator's input, never blocked by their silence | Operator: "Reply window closes in 3 days" |
| Discrepancy names gear the listing never claimed | Not a correction to an existing spec — logged as a *new capability signal* for 16 to consider adding, held to the same corroboration threshold | "This isn't listed yet — we'll suggest it to the venue once corroborated" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Report opened | Skeleton with variance placeholders |
| Empty | Show performed, nothing entered | Pre-filled variances only + "add your notes" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a low-value admin chore and should be Could/Won't | First-pass instinct | ❌ REJECTED — it is the **feedback loop that makes the advance diff trustworthy**. Domain 16's capability data is self-reported by Operators who have an incentive to overstate. Without corroborated correction from people in the room, the diff — the domain's strongest wedge — degrades to comparing a rider against marketing copy. This is high leverage, not admin. |
| DT-02 | Venue reports are reviews and should work like reviews | Marketplace lens | ❌ REJECTED — a review is subjective and public; a capability discrepancy is a factual claim about a listed spec, checkable and correctable. Merging them makes venues fight facts as if they were opinions and makes the correction loop adversarial. Keep them separate. |
| DT-03 | One report should be enough to flag a venue's data as wrong | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — single-source correction is trivially weaponisable by a disgruntled act. Corroboration threshold required; the Operator's right of reply is a fairness floor. |
| DT-04 | Most of the report can be written by the system | Domain expert lens | ✅ CONFIRMED — planned-vs-actual times, unresolved advance items and shortfalls are already known. Asking a TM to type at 01:00 after load-out is why reports don't exist today. Pre-fill everything; ask only for judgement. |
| DT-05 | The report is where a show's performance provenance should be captured | Thesis lens (D-18) | ❌ REJECTED here, ✅ relocated — who actually performed belongs to the performed setlist capture (18.06.04) at the moment it's true, not reconstructed in a report hours later. Reconstruction is precisely the failure the thesis names. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Capability discrepancies are factual corrections routed to 16, not reviews | Facts are checkable and correctable; reviews are opinions and become adversarial |
| D-02 | A single report never mutates venue capability data — corroboration threshold required | Single-source correction is weaponisable |
| D-03 | The report is pre-filled from system-known variance; humans add only judgement | Reports don't get filed today because they're typed from scratch after load-out |
| D-04 | One report per show, co-edited by the production party — not one report per person | A show is a single event; multiple attributed reports would fragment the venue-truth signal and invite duplicate-count gaming of the corroboration threshold |
| D-05 | Filed reports are editable within a bounded window, then locked; all edits versioned | Reports routed to 16 as corrections must not be silently rewritten after corroboration, but same-week factual fixes are legitimate. Exact window `[PENDING — /write-be-spec]` |
