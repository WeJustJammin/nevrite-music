# Feature: Tour Expense & Receipt Capture

> **Number**: 18.13.03
> **Parent**: [Tour Finance](./18.13-tour-finance-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Turning a shoebox of thermal-paper receipts into a reconciled float. It is the least glamorous
feature in the domain and the one without which the other two are fiction: a float can't reconcile
and a budget can't have actuals if nobody knows what the £140 went on.

> **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list. See DT-01.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config (own spend) | Photographs a receipt when they spend tour money — fuel, strings, a parking charge. Sees what they've submitted and what's been reimbursed. They rarely spend, but when they do it's cash and it's forgotten. |
| Producer | Full | The person with the shoebox. Captures the float's spend, categorises it, and reconciles at the end of the week or the tour. |
| Operator | **None** | Not their business. |
| Fan | None | Never visible. |

## Behavior

A receipt is a photo, an amount, a category, a date, and — crucially — a source: float cash, a card,
or someone's own money to be reimbursed. Capture must work offline at a petrol station and take
seconds. Receipts reconcile the float (18.13.01) and become cost actuals in the budget (18.13.02).

### Happy Path

1. TM buys fuel with float cash and photographs the receipt at the pump.
2. Amount and category are captured; the source is "float".
3. The float balance drops; the budget's fuel line accrues an actual.
4. At the end of the week the float reconciles: opening − payouts − receipts = the envelope.
5. A £40 gap is recorded as a variance with a reason, not silently absorbed.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| No receipt (cash to a stagehand) | An expense with no document; must be recordable, not blocked | "No receipt — who was paid, and for what?" |
| Receipt is illegible thermal paper | Photograph it *now*; it will be blank in three weeks — a real, physical fact | "Captured 14 Mar" |
| Offline at a petrol station | Must queue and sync — the failure mode is that it never gets captured | "Saved — will sync" |
| Receipt syncs after its float period was reconciled and cash handed out | Within the grace window it posts to the closed period and the variance and reimbursements recompute; after it, it posts to the current open period carrying its true spend date, so the cost relocates but is never lost (D-05). A sync never reopens a closed period on its own — reopening is the Producer's explicit, attributed act. Window length is Q-03 | Inside the window: "Added to last week — {name} is owed £40." After it: "Posted to this week, dated {spend date} — last week is closed." |
| Foreign currency | Amount, currency, and the rate at the time — not at reconciliation | "€38 @ 1.14 = £33.33" |
| Someone spends their own money | A reimbursement, not a float expense — different flow, different person owed | "Reimburse [name] £24" |
| Personal spend on the tour card | The most common reconciliation problem, and an awkward conversation | "Flagged as personal — deduct from PD?" |
| Nobody captures anything | The default; the float is then unaccounted and the budget has no actuals | "£140 unaccounted since [date]" |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Capture at the moment of spend, offline, in one photograph | Thermal receipts are literally blank in three weeks — this is why tour accounting is reconstructed |
| D-02 | Expenses without receipts are recordable with an explanation | A stagehand paid in cash at 01:00 leaves no document; rigidity returns everyone to the shoebox |
| D-03 | Every expense carries a source (float / card / own money) | Reimbursement and float reconciliation are different flows with different people owed |
| D-04 | The float is the music-specific part; capture/OCR is generic | No off-the-shelf expenses tool models a touring cash float |
| D-05 | **A receipt that syncs after its period closed posts to that closed period for a bounded grace window, and to the current open period after it — carrying its true spend date either way. Reopening a reconciled period is possible but only as a deliberate, attributed act by the Producer, never something a sync performs.** | Owner-ratified 2026-07-30. Late arrivals are **guaranteed by the design**, not tolerated by it: D-01 requires offline capture at the moment of spend because thermal receipts are blank in three weeks (DT-02), and both reconciliation points close hard ([18.13.03](#) weekly, [18.13.01](./18.13.01-per-diems-float.md):37 at tour end). Each treatment produces a different float balance and a different reimbursement owed to the person who spent the money, so silence here is a money bug. The grace window keeps the honest case honest — most late receipts surface within days — while everything after it posts forward, dated truthfully, so **the cost is never lost and the closed week stays closed**. Reopening stays available because the books must be able to be made true, but a period whose closure a background sync can undo is not a closure, and reimbursements were paid against it. Rejected: absorbing the receipt into the closed period's recorded variance, because it silently moves a number the Producer already acted on — [18.13-tour-finance-cx](./18.13-tour-finance-cx.md) CX-01 synthesis Q1, *"a system that silently balances is lying"*. The **budget** answers this differently and correctly ([18.13.02](./18.13.02-tour-budget-actual.md), CX-02 synthesis Q5 — provisional throughout); the float cannot borrow that answer, because reconciliation **is** the act of declaring the number final. Window length is **Q-03** |
