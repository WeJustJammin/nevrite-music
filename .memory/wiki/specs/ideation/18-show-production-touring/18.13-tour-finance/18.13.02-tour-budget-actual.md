# Feature: Tour Budget vs Actual

> **Number**: 18.13.02
> **Parent**: [Tour Finance](./18.13-tour-finance-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Whether the tour makes money, and where it goes if it doesn't. A tour is a business with its own
P&L — fees and merch in, travel, crew, backline, per diems and hotels out — and the question "can we
afford to play Glasgow?" is answered by this object or by a guess. Most touring musicians find out
whether a tour worked when it's over.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | **Reports (band-scoped)** | Sees whether the tour is making money and roughly where it goes — because they're often a profit partner. Does **not** see line-level crew salaries: a bassist knowing the FOH engineer's day rate is a real problem. Provisional; see Q-01. |
| Producer | Full | Builds the budget, tracks actuals, and answers "are we losing money on this run?" while there's still time to act. |
| Operator | **None** | The act's P&L is emphatically not the venue's business — this is the same firewall as 18.01 D-01 (crew must not see the fee), pointed at the other party. |
| Fan | None | Never visible. |

## Behavior

Actuals accrue from things the platform already knows: settlement from 17, merch counts from 18.15,
per diems and float spend from 18.13.01/03, rentals from 18.09.02, travel from 18.12.01. The budget
is the plan; the variance is the product. Per-date P&L is the useful grain, because "this run loses
money" is less actionable than "these three dates lose money".

### Happy Path

1. Producer builds a budget: expected fees, merch, and cost lines per date.
2. The tour runs; actuals accrue automatically from settlement, merch and float spend.
3. Date 4's variance flags: backline rental £180 against a £250 fee.
4. Producer sees the pattern (18.09.02 D-03) and carries the rig instead of renting it.
5. At tour end the P&L is arithmetic that was already done, not a reconstruction.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Costs live off-platform | The budget is only as complete as the data; gaps must be visible | "Actuals cover 60% of budgeted lines" |
| Settlement is delayed | Fees arrive weeks later; the mid-tour P&L is provisional | "3 dates unsettled — income provisional" |
| Cash spend with no receipt | The float's unaccounted variance is a real cost with no category | "£140 unaccounted — uncategorised" |
| Band members are partners | They have a claim to the P&L, but it contains crew salaries (Q-01) | Band-scoped totals, not line-level personnel |
| Tour loses money but builds the audience | The number isn't the whole decision — and the platform must not imply it is | Presented as a fact, not a verdict |
| Multi-currency run | FX on income and costs, at different times and rates | "Mixed GBP/EUR — rates as of settlement" |
| Cross-collateralised with a label advance | Tour support and recoupment change what "profit" means entirely | Recoupment position consumed from 10.03.03, never recomputed here — see Q-02 |
