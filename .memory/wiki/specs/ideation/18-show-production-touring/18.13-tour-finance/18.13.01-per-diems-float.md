# Feature: Per Diems & Tour Float

> **Number**: 18.13.01
> **Parent**: [Tour Finance](./18.13-tour-finance-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The daily cash each person gets to eat, and the envelope the TM carries to pay for everything else.
Per diems are the most visible money on a tour — everyone knows their number and notices when it's
wrong — and the float is the least tracked, because it's physical cash that leaves an envelope and
is reconciled from memory.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only (own PD) | Sees their own per diem — rate, days, what's been paid, what's owed. **Never sees anyone else's**, because on a tour where the crew are paid differently to the band, that's a live grievance. |
| Producer | Full | Sets rates, pays out, carries the float, and reconciles it. The TM's least favourite hour of the week, done with a shoebox of receipts. |
| Operator | **None** | Not their business. A buyout they pay (18.04.02) reaches the float as income — they see the buyout, not the float. |
| Fan | None | Never visible. |

## Behavior

Per diem = rate × days × person, derived from the roster's date ranges (18.08.01) — so it computes
rather than being counted. The float is a cash balance: in (band money, buyouts, merch cash) and out
(per diems, local crew, sundries), reconciled against receipts (18.13.03). Payouts route through the
platform's money-movement rail where they're electronic, and are recorded where they're cash.

### Happy Path

1. Producer sets a per diem rate (£25/day) for the tour.
2. Amounts derive from the roster: 9 people × their date ranges.
3. The float is opened with an amount; merch cash and a venue buyout land in it.
4. Per diems are paid — electronically where possible, cash where not — and each person sees theirs.
5. At tour end the float reconciles: opening + income − payouts − receipts = what's in the envelope.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Float doesn't reconcile | The normal outcome; needs a variance with a reason, not silent absorption | "£140 unaccounted — 3 days, no receipts" |
| Different rates for band and crew | Universal, and the reason per diems are scoped to self | Each person sees only their own |
| Cash in a foreign currency | An ATM withdrawal at a bad rate is a real cost nobody records | "Withdrew €200 @ 1.14 — £5.20 in fees" |
| Buyout replaces catering | Money in lieu of food; the person may get it directly or via the float | "£15 buyout — paid as per diem or into the float?" |
| Someone joins mid-tour | Per diem derives from their date range, not the tour's | "[Name]: dates 6–11, 6 days" |
| Per diem is taxable income | It often is, above statutory limits, and nobody tells the musician | Routed — see Q-02 |
| TM pays a stagehand £80 cash | Float out, no receipt, a person who's gone | "Cash payment — who received it?" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Per diems are a small payments feature | Scope lens | ⚠️ PARTIALLY REJECTED — small in mechanics, but it's the money a musician **actually touches daily**, and it's the one number on a tour everybody checks. Getting it wrong is a morale event, not a rounding error. It's also the domain's most concrete link into the payouts cross-cut and into 23. |
| DT-02 | Cash is a legacy edge case | Modernisation instinct | ❌ REJECTED — **touring runs on cash** (D-04): merch takings, stagehands, per diems in territories where nobody's card works, a bar tab. A model that assumes electronic money describes a tour that doesn't exist, and a float is *definitionally* cash — that's what a float is. The platform's job is to make cash *accounted for*, not to pretend it away. |
| DT-03 | Per diem amounts are typed | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — it's rate × days × person, and all three are known (the roster holds date ranges). Typing it is how people get shorted for the two days they joined late. Derive, like every other count in this domain. |
| DT-04 | The float is the TM's problem | Boundary lens | ❌ REJECTED — the float is where **merch cash, buyouts and local crew payments all converge**, which makes it the meeting point of three other sub-domains' money. It's also the least-tracked money on the platform, and "£140 unaccounted" is a fact that should exist rather than a feeling the TM has. |
