## Overview

The money that moves on the road: per diems paid to people daily, a cash float the TM carries, the
receipts that account for it, and the budget the whole run is measured against. It is distinct from
the platform's other money domains because it is **operational cash in a physical world** — a TM
with an envelope, paying a stagehand at 01:00 in a currency they got from an ATM.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three capabilities in a reconciliation loop. Per diems and the float
are cash out; receipts account for what the float was spent on; and budget-vs-actual is where both
land and where the tour is judged. The float cannot be reconciled without the receipts, and the
budget is meaningless without the actuals the other two produce.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Tour finance is operational cash, not accounting | Domain 23 (Career, Finance & Business) owns the musician's financial life; 17 owns settlement. This sub-domain owns the **envelope of cash a TM carries and what happens to it**. The distinction is real: nothing else in the platform deals in physical currency handed to a person at 01:00. | Agent classification, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | The tour is the budget unit | A tour has a P&L of its own — it can lose money while the act has a good year, and that's the number the decision "do we tour?" depends on. Rolling it straight into 23 loses the unit of decision. | Agent, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | Per diems are a payout, using the platform's payouts rail | Paying nine people a daily amount is money movement, which is a cross-cut, not a thing this sub-domain builds. Same discipline as local crew hire (18.08 D-03) and backline sourcing (18.09 D-02). | Agent, 2026-07-16 |
| D-04 | Cash is a first-class citizen, not a legacy edge case | Touring runs on cash: merch takings, stagehand payments, per diems, a bar tab. A finance model that assumes electronic money describes a tour that doesn't exist. | Agent Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
