# Feature: Event Record & Lifecycle States

> **Number**: 18.01
> **Parent**: [Show Production & Touring](./show-production-touring-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The single event record every other object in this domain hangs off — one show, one date, one
venue, one bill. It carries the lifecycle state machine that gates which production objects may be
created, edited, frozen or read. Domain 17 owns the commercial deal; this record is the
**operational twin** the deal creates on confirmation, and it exists so that the people who run the
show (crew, venue production, support acts) can do their work without ever seeing the money.

It solves a problem nobody names because it has no artifact today: **a show has no identity.** It
exists as a date in a calendar, a thread in WhatsApp, a PDF in an inbox and a line on a settlement
sheet, none of which know about each other. Give the show one identity that survives a date change,
a cancellation and a venue's rebranding, and the advance, the day sheet, the ticket, the setlist and
the settlement all finally refer to the same thing.

**Why it is a Must** (MoSCoW ledger): *"Nothing else in the domain exists without it. Every other
node attaches to this record."* All 195 Musts are ratified in scope (D-20).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| **Musician** | Full | Creates a one-off show, adds a date to a tour, or accepts a support slot; sees the date's state on their calendar and the state's consequence ("rider locked — changes now go to the venue as change requests"). On a DIY act they *are* the Producer for this record and drive `confirmed → performed`. **Fee visibility follows their party role on the 17 deal, not their production role here** (D-06) — the drummer who signed the deal sees it; the drummer depping for one night does not. Cannot move the record to `settled` — that is 17's transition (D-04). |
| **Producer** | Full | The TM/PM lens; the record's primary actor. Owns `confirmed → advancing → advanced → day-of → performed`. Freezes the advance (which is what moves the record to `advanced`, D-05), marks the show performed, opens the post-show report (18.18), files a cancellation claim from the artist side. Sees unresolved advance items on the frozen record — `advanced` never means "the venue agreed to everything" (D-05). |
| **Operator** | Config | Sees **only records at their own venue**, and only from `confirmed` onward — never the artist's other dates, never the route (CX-10). Sets venue-side facts on the record (doors, curfew, load-in/load-out windows, house production contact), responds to advance items, and can file a cancellation claim from the venue side. **Cannot** edit the bill, the act's objects, or drive any lifecycle transition except their own side of the show-day handshake. |
| **Fan** | None | The event record is professional infrastructure and is never exposed. Fans see a *derived public listing* (date, doors, set time, on-sale, bill) owned by 19/21, projected from this record — never the record. A record in `advancing` with 6 unresolved items looks, to a fan, exactly like one that is fully advanced: **the operational state is not a public fact** (D-07). Cancellation and postponement are the only states with a mandatory public projection. |

## Behavior

The record is created by a confirmed booking in 17, or manually for shows booked off-platform, or
as a rehearsal (18.19 D-01 — a rehearsal is an event subtype). Its **entry state is always
`confirmed`**: the record does not exist during 17's hold ladder (DT-05). State is the domain's
central gate — riders edit freely in `confirmed`, enter change-control at `advanced` (18.03.05),
and become read-only history at `performed`. Cancellation and postponement are first-class states,
not deletions: a cancelled show still has costs, deposits and a settlement tail.

### The State Machine

| State | Owned by | Entered when | What it gates |
|-------|----------|--------------|---------------|
| `confirmed` | 18 | 17 marks the deal confirmed; or manual creation; or a rehearsal is scheduled | All production objects editable. Advance not yet open. |
| `advancing` | 18 (Producer) | Producer opens the advance (18.03) | Advance items live. Rider/plot/manifest still freely editable. |
| `advanced` | 18 (Producer) | Producer **freezes** the advance (18.03.05 D-01) | Production pack frozen. Edits become change-control events, never blocked (18.03.05 D-02). |
| `day-of` | 18 (system) | 00:00 **venue-local** on the show date (D-08) | Auto-freeze fires if never frozen (18.03.05 edge case). Live slippage (18.07.03) opens. |
| `performed` | 18 (Producer or system) | Producer marks performed; **or** system infers it (D-09) | Production pack read-only. Post-show report (18.18) opens. Settlement handed to 17. |
| `settled` | **17** | 17 completes settlement | 18 mirrors and displays it; 18 never writes it (D-04). |
| `archived` | 18 | 18's own tail complete (report filed, load-out check closed, gear reconciled) **and** 17 reports `settled` | Record is history. Referenced by credits (02), never edited. |
| `cancelled` | 18 | A party files a cancellation claim (D-10) | Production objects preserved read-only. Costs/deposits handed to 17. Terminal. |
| `postponed` | 18 | A party files a postponement with or without a new date (D-11) | With a new date → returns to `confirmed`, advance re-opens with a diff. Without → limbo; tickets held by 19. |

**Skips are legal and subtype-determined** (D-12). A rehearsal has no Operator counterparty and no
bilateral advance: `confirmed → day-of → performed`. A festival slot advances against the
**promoter's** production office rather than a venue (D-03). The machine never requires a state that
has no counterparty to perform it.

**Every time on this record is venue-local** (D-08). The show date is the date of the *performance*,
not of load-in or load-out.

### Happy Path

1. Domain 17 marks a deal confirmed → an event record is auto-created at `confirmed`, linked to the
   deal by reference. **Deal terms are not copied onto the record** — only date, venue, act and
   role-on-bill cross the boundary (D-02).
2. Producer opens the record. Venue (16), date, bill (18.02) and act are pre-filled from the deal;
   the venue's local timezone is resolved from 16 and pinned to the record (D-08).
3. Operator receives the record on their venue calendar and sets venue-side facts: doors, curfew,
   load-in and load-out windows, house production contact.
4. Producer moves the record to `advancing`, which opens the advance workflow (18.03) and runs the
   first venue-capability diff (18.03.02).
5. Advance items resolve. Producer **freezes** the advance (18.03.05) → the record moves to
   `advanced` and the production pack freezes at a version. Unresolved items are carried onto the
   frozen pack, visibly, rather than hidden (D-05).
6. At 00:00 venue-local on the show date the record moves to `day-of`. If it was never frozen, it
   auto-freezes with open items recorded as unresolved (18.03.05).
7. After the show the Producer marks it `performed` → the production pack goes read-only, the
   post-show report opens (18.18), and settlement is handed to 17.
8. 17 completes settlement → the record mirrors `settled`. When 18's own tail is also closed
   (report filed, load-out check closed), the record moves to `archived` and becomes the historical
   fact that performance credits (02, via 18.06.04) reference.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Show booked off-platform | Record created manually at `confirmed`; venue may be an unclaimed stub in 16; record is flagged **self-asserted** (D-13) and carries no evidentiary weight until a counterparty attests | "This venue isn't on WeJammin — capability diff unavailable" + invite CTA; badge: "Unverified — no counterparty has confirmed this show" |
| Date moves (postponement, new date known) | Record keeps identity and history; new date written; state → `postponed` → `confirmed`; advance re-opens with a diff against the **old date's** venue facts; frozen pack version is retained as history, not discarded | Banner: "Date changed 14 Mar → 22 May. 6 advance items need re-confirmation. Previous frozen pack kept as v3." |
| Postponed, new date **TBA** | State stays `postponed` indefinitely; 19 holds tickets rather than voiding them (DT-04); at 30 days the Producer is prompted to set a date or convert to `cancelled` | Day 30: "Postponed 30 days with no new date. Ticket holders are still holding. Set a date or cancel?" |
| Show cancelled after freeze | State → `cancelled`; production objects preserved read-only; incurred costs and deposits handed to 17; the **cancelling party** is recorded, not just the reason (D-10) | Party + reason picker; "Costs already incurred: 3 items (£840) — handed to settlement"; venue notified with the claim attributed |
| Both parties file conflicting cancellation claims | Both claims recorded with actor and timestamp; the record becomes `cancelled` once and a second claim does not re-cancel it; **18 does not adjudicate who owes what** — both claims are handed to 17 (D-10) | "Two cancellation claims filed. The financial consequence is determined in settlement." Neither party sees a verdict from 18. |
| Venue delisted, closed or merged in 16 between confirm and show | Record retains a **tombstone reference**; capability data goes stale; diff degrades to `unverified` rather than disappearing | "Venue data unverified — advance manually. This room's listing was removed on 3 Apr." |
| Venue renamed/rebranded mid-tour | Record's venue reference resolves to the current entity; the **frozen pack keeps the name as of freeze** so the printed advance sheet matches what the driver is looking for | Frozen sheet shows the historical name with a footnote: "Now trading as [new name]" |
| Two records for the same show, created by the same act/org | Soft duplicate warning on (act, venue, ±1 calendar day); merge prompt showing both records' attached objects and which would survive | "You already have a show at this venue on 14 Mar. Same show? Merge / keep both" |
| Two records for the same venue+date created by **different acts** | **No warning shown.** Duplicate detection is scoped to the creator's own org (D-14) — surfacing it would reveal that another act plays that room that night, which is competitively sensitive (CX-10) | Nothing. The collision is the venue's calendar to know (16/17), not the artist's. |
| Matinee + evening show, same venue, same date | Legitimate — **not** a duplicate. Uniqueness is (act, venue, performance start), not (act, venue, date) | Two records, both valid; the ±1-day soft warning is dismissible with "different performance" |
| Multi-night run / residency at one venue | N records (N performances, N ticket events, N settlements, N setlists) linked by a **run** reference; **the advance is shared** — night 1's advance is the run's advance, nights 2..N reference it and do not re-diff (DT-06); only per-night deltas (setlist, guest list, counts) are separately owned | "Nights 2 and 3 inherit the advance from night 1. Per-night: setlist, guest list, counts." |
| Support act added after the advance is frozen | The record's state does **not** regress (D-15). The addition is a change-control event (18.03.05) against the frozen pack; the venue is notified with acknowledgement tracking | Producer: "Adding an act to a frozen advance — the venue will be notified and must acknowledge." Operator: "Bill changed after freeze — acknowledge" |
| Support act's TM wants to run their own production | The record is show-scoped and one state (D-15); the support act's act-scoped objects are theirs, shared objects (input list, changeover, schedule) are read-only, and their **travel/crew/day sheet hang off their own tour** (18.11/18.12), which references this date | Support TM sees: their rider/plot editable; bill and schedule read-only; headliner's rider, fee and guest list absent entirely (18.02) |
| Nobody presses "performed" | Record would sit in `day-of` forever — post-show report never opens, 17's settlement never triggers, CX-06 and CX-08 both die silently. System auto-transitions to `performed` at venue-local curfew → else scheduled set-end + 60 min → else 23:59 on the show date, and marks it **`system-inferred`, not attested** (D-09) | "Marked performed automatically — nobody confirmed this. [Confirm it happened]" Badge persists until a human or a counterparty attests. |
| Show is cancelled *on the day*, after `day-of` | `day-of → cancelled` is legal; the auto-`performed` transition is suppressed | "Cancelled on the day — costs incurred today are still handed to settlement" |
| Artist plays a venue they also operate (multi-hyphenate) | Legal and common. Self-dealing on the advance is **detected and marked, never blocked** — the bilateral handshake becomes self-confirmation, which has no evidentiary value (D-16) | "You're on both sides of this advance. Confirmations are recorded as self-asserted." |
| One human is both band member and TM on a DIY act | No conflict — the personas are lenses (personas.md), not accounts. Fee visibility resolves from their **17 deal party role**, not from the fact they're driving the record (D-06) | They see the fee because they signed the deal, not because they opened the record |
| Concurrent edit — Producer changes the date while 17 pushes a deal amendment | Once the record reaches `advancing`, core facts (date, venue, act, role-on-bill) have **one authoritative editor at a time**; a conflicting later change is not silently applied — it is rejected and re-presented as a proposed change to accept or discard | "The date changed in the booking while you were editing. Yours: 22 May. Booking: 23 May. Keep / accept" |
| Two people mark `performed` offline, simultaneously | Same terminal state either way, so there is no conflict — the first attested transition stands and the second is recorded as corroboration rather than overwriting it | Nothing. Both see `performed`. |
| Offline: `performed` queued offline while another user files `cancelled` online | Resolved by actor + timestamp; `cancelled` filed *before* the show start time wins; a `performed` attested *after* it prompts a human | "Conflicting: cancelled at 16:20, marked performed at 23:40. Which happened?" |
| Show day, no signal (venue basement — index Q-06) | The record **stays readable with no signal** from its last-synced copy; a state transition made offline is held and re-applied on reconnect carrying the time it was actually made, not the time it synced | Cached record with "Last synced 18:42 · 3 changes queued" |
| Band splits; the Band entity (01) is dissolved | The record **survives** — the venue, promoter and other acts all legitimately hold the fact that the show happened, and it is the evidence behind performance credits (02). Reference resolves to a tombstone | "This act no longer exists on WeJammin" — the show, date and venue remain |
| Fabricated record claiming a performance at a famous venue | **The core abuse case** (personas.md anti-persona: "inflates credits to claim work they didn't do"). A self-created off-platform record is a self-assertion and is marked as such forever unless a counterparty attests (D-13). It can never, on its own, evidence a performance credit in 02 | Creator: badge "Unverified". Anyone else: "Self-reported — no counterparty confirmed this show." |
| Malicious/garbage venue-side facts (curfew 03:00, doors yesterday) | Venue-side facts are Operator-writable but validated against the record's own timeline: doors ≤ set start, curfew > set end, load-in < load-out | "Doors (21:00) are after the first set (20:30) — check these times" |
| Linked deal in 17 is voided/unwound after the record exists | Record does not vanish — it moves to `cancelled` with the cancelling party recorded as "booking voided" | "The booking behind this show was voided. The show is cancelled; 2 incurred costs go to settlement." |
| DST transition on the show date | The show date has a 23- or 25-hour night. All times resolve against the venue's local zone **including the transition** (D-08); a 01:30 load-out on a fall-back night is genuinely ambiguous and is stored with the offset | "Clocks change tonight — load-out 01:30 (BST→GMT). Drive time and rest clock account for the extra hour." |
| Tour crosses a DST boundary mid-run (US and EU change on different dates) | Each record's times are independent and venue-local; nothing is computed in a single "tour timezone" (D-08) | Nothing surprising — which is the point |
| Record's venue is a field/temporary structure with no entry in 16 | Record created against a **place**, not a listing; diff unavailable; advance is manual | "No venue listing — advance manually. Capability diff unavailable." |
| Record opened by someone with no relationship to it | Not found. The record is not addressable outside the act's org and the venue's org (D-17) | 404 — never "you don't have access", which would confirm the show exists |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | Record opened | Skeleton of the production-pack tiles, with the header (date, venue, act, state) rendered first from cache — the four facts that orient someone at a loading dock |
| **Empty** | `confirmed`, nothing advanced, no objects attached | "Nothing advanced yet" + primary "Start advance" CTA; venue-side facts shown if the Operator has already set them |
| **Populated** | Objects attached | Production-pack dashboard: per-object completion, the lifecycle state and what it gates, unresolved advance items, and the next transition available to *this* role |
| **Error** | Linked deal or venue unresolvable | "Can't load the linked booking — retry / unlink". Unlinking is permitted and downgrades the record to self-asserted (D-13) rather than deleting it |
| **Partial** | Some production objects missing at `day-of`; or venue data `unverified`; or record is `system-inferred` performed | Amber banner listing exactly what never got done, and what that means: "Auto-frozen with 4 items unresolved · venue capability unverified · nobody confirmed this show happened" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The event record is the same object as domain 17's booking/deal and should not exist twice | Sweep boundary between 17 and 18 | ❌ **REJECTED** — different lifecycles, actors and lifespans. The deal is done when signed; the operational record's life *starts* there and runs weeks later with different people (crew, venue production) who must never see deal terms. One object would force fee visibility onto crew. |
| DT-02 | Cancellation is a terminal delete | Naive modelling | ❌ **REJECTED** — a cancelled show has a financial tail (deposits, non-refundable travel, incurred costs) and a reputational one. Cancellation is a state with its own workflow, not an absence. |
| DT-03 | A festival slot, a support slot and a headline show are the same record type | Domain expert lens | ✅ **CONFIRMED** — same record, different `role_on_bill` and a different set of objects the artist owns vs consumes. A festival slot advances against the *promoter's* production, not a venue's. **Resolves Q-01**: two orthogonal dimensions, not two record types — `role_on_bill` carries the bill position, and the **production counterparty** (venue office vs promoter's production office vs none, for a rehearsal) carries the advance direction. A festival slot is `role_on_bill: support` + `counterparty: promoter`. No new type. |
| DT-04 | Postponement can be modelled as cancel + new record | Naive modelling | ❌ **REJECTED** — it destroys the advance history and the ticket link (19 must roll tickets over, which requires record identity to survive the date change). |
| DT-05 | The lifecycle starts at `enquiry → hold` | The [SURFACE] pass carried `enquiry → hold → confirmed …` as 18's machine | ❌ **REJECTED — boundary correction.** `enquiry` and `hold` are **17's hold ladder** (17.01 "Availability, Holds & Confirmation"; index D-01 calls the ladder "the most industry-specific mechanic in the corpus"). The record's entry state is `confirmed`. Nobody advances a hold, and a held date has no production. Held dates appear on a route (18.11) as a **17-owned pencil projection**, not as event records — consistent with CX-10 ("a hold dropping in 17 can silently make the rest of the route infeasible"), where the hold is read from 17, not mirrored into 18. Modelling holds here would put 17's ladder in two places and give the Operator a production object for a date they haven't sold. |
| DT-06 | A multi-night run at one venue is N independent records | Domain expert lens — residencies, 3-night runs | ✅ **CONFIRMED, with a correction the sweep missed.** N performances = N records (N ticket events, N settlements, N setlists, N performed-setlist captures). **But the production is amortised**: one load-in, one load-out, one advance. Treating nights 2..N as independent means advancing the same room three times and a manifest that appears to load in and out nightly. Nights 2..N reference night 1's advance via a `run` link; only per-night deltas are separately owned. This is the one place where the domain's per-date advance model (18.03 D-01) is wrong by default. |
| DT-07 | The show date is a date | Domain expert lens | ❌ **REJECTED — and this is the failure nobody designs for.** A show on "Friday 14th" loads in 08:00 Friday and loads out 02:30 Saturday, spanning two calendar days in the venue's local zone, on a tour crossing zones. Every incumbent models a naive date and every incumbent is wrong at 01:00. The record must pin the **venue's local timezone** as the authority for every time on it, and "the show date" must mean the date of the *performance*. Consequences that fall out immediately: "tomorrow's day sheet" is ambiguous at 01:00 (18.10); curfew is a wall-clock local time, statutory or Operator-claimed according to the room's regime profile (18.07.02 D-06; `16.02.03` D-01/D-13); the driver's rest clock crosses midnight (CX-06); and a show on the night the clocks change has a 23- or 25-hour night. US and EU DST dates differ, so an Atlantic-crossing tour in late October hits both — which is why nothing may ever be computed in a single "tour timezone". |
| DT-08 | Somebody will always mark the show performed | Optimistic modelling | ❌ **REJECTED — the domain's weakest link, and this record is where it bites.** The CX notes already flag that CX-06 (the cascade) and CX-08 (the venue-truth loop) both depend on someone recording actuals — a behaviour "the domain hopes for and cannot guarantee". If nobody presses `performed`, the record sits in `day-of` forever, 18.18 never opens, 17's settlement never triggers, and both flagship loops die *silently*. Therefore: **infer the transition, but never launder the inference into evidence.** A `system-inferred` performed is an operational convenience; only human attestation, a performed-setlist capture (18.06.04) or a completed settlement (17) is proof the show happened. Conflating them would let the platform manufacture the very provenance it exists to capture — the thesis (D-18) inverted. |
| DT-09 | Duplicate detection on (artist, date, venue) is safe | The [SURFACE] pass proposed it as a plain uniqueness check | ❌ **REJECTED — it leaks.** Warning an artist that a record already exists for (venue, date) tells them **another act is playing that room that night**. CX-10 establishes the route as competitively sensitive and gives the Operator *no* visibility into a tour for exactly this reason; a duplicate warning would breach the same firewall in the opposite direction. Detection must be scoped to the creator's own org. Cross-act collisions at a venue are the venue's calendar to resolve (16/17). The key is also wrong: (act, venue, date) forbids a legitimate matinee + evening double. |
| DT-10 | Cancellation's load-bearing field is the reason | The [SURFACE] pass proposed a reason picker | ❌ **REJECTED — the load-bearing field is the *party*.** Cancelled-by-artist, cancelled-by-venue, cancelled-by-promoter and force-majeure have different deposit-forfeiture directions in 17.05 and different consequences for payment-reliability reputation (17.12). Neither party may unilaterally set the other's liability, so cancellation is a **claim by an attributed actor**, not a fact. 18 records who claimed what and hands both claims to 17; **18 never adjudicates**. A reason picker without an actor produces a record that says a show was cancelled "due to illness" and cannot say whose. |
| DT-11 | `advanced` means both parties agree the show is advanced | The open question inherited from the [SURFACE] pass (Q-02) | ✅ **CONFIRMED that it does NOT — resolved from existing sibling decisions, not escalated.** 18.03.05 D-01 makes the freeze the completion event; D-02 says change control "never blocks"; D-03 says the freeze is overridable with a stated reason; 18.03 D-04 says completion is a state, not a percentage. Together these settle it: the **Producer freezes unilaterally** and the record moves to `advanced`. `advanced` therefore means *"the artist's plan is frozen"*, never *"the venue agreed to everything"*. The venue's disagreement is carried as unresolved items **on** the frozen pack — corroborated by 18.03-cx: the pack is "honest about what was never settled". A venue veto would be a blocking gate, which 18.03.05 D-02 explicitly rejects because blocking gates get bypassed by not using the system. **Q-02 closed.** |
| DT-12 | Every record traverses every state | Naive modelling | ❌ **REJECTED** — a rehearsal (18.19 D-01, an event subtype) has no venue counterparty and no bilateral advance: `confirmed → day-of → performed`. The machine must permit subtype-determined skips. A machine that demands an `advanced` state from an event with nobody to advance *with* produces a permanently-amber record, and permanently-amber records train people to ignore amber. |
| DT-13 | Lifecycle state should be per-act on a multi-act bill | Considered: a support added 3 days out is not "advanced" just because the headliner is | ❌ **REJECTED** — it would make one show simultaneously advanced and not-advanced, and the venue's production office needs exactly one answer to "is this show advanced?". 18.02's happy path already runs **one advance for the bill with per-act sections the venue confirms independently** — so per-act completeness is tracked by the advance's sections (18.03.01), which is the right place for it. A late support act is a change-control event against the frozen pack (18.03.05), not a state regression. |
| DT-14 | An event record is evidence that a performance happened | The provenance lens — 18.06.04 is the domain's provenance node | ❌ **REJECTED as stated, and this is the record's sharpest thesis constraint.** A self-created off-platform record is a **self-assertion** — the exact thing personas.md names as the Musician's worst intentional behaviour ("inflates credits to claim work they didn't do"). Per the domain's *Approval, Countersign & Attestation* cross-cut, self-assertion is categorically weaker evidence than counter-confirmation. So the record carries a provenance grade, permanently: a record with a counterparty attestation (venue confirmed the advance, or 17 settled it, or the Operator confirmed the date) can evidence a performance credit in 02; a record nobody but the creator has ever touched cannot, at any age. **The record must never be able to launder a claim into a credit by getting old.** |
| DT-15 | Fans should see the lifecycle | Considered: on-sale pages showing "advancing" | ❌ **REJECTED** — the operational state is not a public fact. A record in `advancing` with 6 unresolved items and a fully-advanced one are the same show to a ticket buyer, and exposing the difference would broadcast an act's production problems to its audience. Fan visibility is the derived listing (19/21) only. The two exceptions are `cancelled` and `postponed`, which have a mandatory public projection because a fan holds a ticket. |
| DT-16 | Deleting the act should delete its shows | Naive modelling / a naive reading of erasure duties | ❌ **REJECTED** — the venue, the promoter and every other act on the bill legitimately hold the fact that the show happened, and it is the evidence behind performance credits (02). A dissolved Band entity resolves to a tombstone; the show survives. The genuine tension is a *person's* erasure request against a record a venue also holds — see Q-03. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **17 Live Booking & Settlement** ([../17-live-booking-settlement/](../17-live-booking-settlement/)) — **trigger dependency + permission firewall.** 17.01's confirmation creates this record; 17.05 consumes its cancellation claims and incurred costs; 17.09's settlement drives the `settled` state 18 mirrors but never writes. **Deal terms must not be readable from the production side** (D-01/D-02) — only date, venue, act and role-on-bill cross. The inverse of CX-10's firewall. `enquiry`/`hold` belong to **17.01, not here** (DT-05).
- Touches **16 Venues, Studios & Spaces** ([../16-venues-studios-spaces/](../16-venues-studios-spaces/)) — **shared entity.** The venue reference supplies capability truth for the diff *and* the local timezone that every time on this record resolves against (DT-07). 16 must support **tombstone resolution**: a delisted, closed or merged venue cannot orphan live records (D-18 in effect via index Q-09's pattern — a data requirement originating here and landing elsewhere).
- Touches **19 Ticketing & Box Office** ([../19-ticketing-box-office/](../19-ticketing-box-office/)) — **trigger dependency + state race.** On-sale, doors and capacity derive from this record. Postponement must **roll** tickets, not void them (DT-04) — which requires record identity to survive the date change. **19 owns the refund window on an indefinite postponement**, and 18's 30-day prompt must not contradict it.
- Touches **02 Credits & Attribution** ([../02-credits-attribution/](../02-credits-attribution/)) — **shared entity + the thesis.** An archived record is the fact a performance credit references. Its **provenance grade** (attested vs self-asserted, DT-14) must travel with it — 02 must be able to distinguish a countersigned show from a typed-in claim, or the credit graph is gameable by anyone who can type a venue's name.
- Touches **01 Identity, Profiles & Organizations** ([../01-identity-profiles-organizations/](../01-identity-profiles-organizations/)) — **shared entity + permission.** The **Band entity**, not a person, is the artist party (personas.md). 01 must support **entity tombstones** so a dissolved band doesn't orphan its history (DT-16). Fee visibility resolves through the person's 17 deal-party role, not their production role here (D-06).

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Event record is distinct from the booking/deal record (17) | Different actors, lifespans and confidentiality — crew must never see the fee (DT-01) |
| D-02 | Only date, venue, act and role-on-bill cross the 17→18 boundary; the deal is **referenced**, never copied | Copying deal fields onto the operational record would recreate the fee-visibility leak D-01 exists to prevent |
| D-03 | The **production counterparty** is a dimension of the record (venue office / promoter's production office / none), orthogonal to `role_on_bill` | Resolves Q-01 without a second record type — a festival slot is `support` + `promoter` (DT-03) |
| D-04 | `settled` is **17's** transition; 18 mirrors and displays it, never writes it | Settlement is computed from deal terms 18 cannot see (17 D-01) |
| D-05 | The freeze is the `advancing → advanced` transition, and it is **unilateral by the Producer**. `advanced` = "the artist's plan is frozen", not "the venue agreed" | Follows directly from 18.03.05 D-01/D-02/D-03 and 18.03 D-04. **Resolves Q-02** (DT-11) |
| D-06 | Fee visibility follows the person's **17 deal-party role**, never their production role on this record | The multi-hyphenate breaks role-based fee gating: the DIY drummer *is* the TM and *is* the signing party. Gating on the production role would hide a musician's own fee from them (DT — Role Lens) |
| D-07 | The lifecycle state is not a public fact; only `cancelled` and `postponed` project to fans | Exposing `advancing` broadcasts an act's production problems to its audience (DT-15) |
| D-08 | Every time on the record resolves against the **venue's local timezone**, pinned at creation; the show date is the date of the *performance* | A show spans two calendar days across a tour crossing zones and DST boundaries (DT-07) |
| D-09 | `performed` is auto-inferred (curfew → set-end + 60 min → 23:59 venue-local) but marked **`system-inferred`, never attested** | Without inference, 18.18 and 17's settlement never trigger; with laundered inference, the platform manufactures its own provenance (DT-08) |
| D-10 | Cancellation records the **cancelling party** as an attributed claim; 18 never adjudicates liability | Deposit direction and reliability reputation depend on who cancelled, and neither party may set the other's liability (DT-10) |
| D-11 | Cancellation and postponement are states, not deletions; postponement without a new date is a legal, indefinite state | Both have financial and ticketing tails requiring record identity to survive (DT-02/DT-04) |
| D-12 | State skips are legal and determined by event subtype | A rehearsal has nobody to advance with; a permanently-amber record trains people to ignore amber (DT-12) |
| D-13 | The record carries a permanent **provenance grade**: attested (counterparty confirmed) vs self-asserted | A record must never launder a claim into a credit by getting old (DT-14) — the thesis (D-18) applied to this record |
| D-14 | Duplicate detection is **scoped to the creator's own org**; the key is (act, venue, performance start) | A cross-act warning leaks that another act plays that room that night, breaching CX-10's firewall in reverse; the date-only key forbids a legitimate matinee + evening (DT-09) |
| D-15 | Lifecycle state is **show-scoped** (one state per record), not per-act | The venue's production office needs one answer to "is this show advanced?"; per-act completeness belongs to the advance's per-act sections (DT-13) |
| D-16 | Self-dealing (artist plays their own venue) is **detected and marked, never blocked** | It is legitimate and common; but the bilateral handshake becomes self-confirmation, which has no evidentiary value under the Attestation cross-cut |
| D-17 | A record is not addressable outside the act's org and the venue's org — unauthorized access returns not-found, never "access denied" | "Access denied" confirms the show exists, which is the same leak as D-14 |
