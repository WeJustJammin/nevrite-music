## Overview

The multi-show container: a set of dates in an order, with the geography and time between them, and
the compiled book that describes the whole thing. The tour is what makes this domain more than a
per-show tool — it is the scope at which riders, crew, budgets and gear are held, and the scope at
which the platform can see patterns no tour manager can (this rig is rented on 9 of 11 dates; this
drive is not legal).

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three capabilities that constrain each other. The container holds and
orders dates; feasibility checking validates that the order is *physically and legally possible*
given drive times, curfews, load-ins and contract terms; and the tour book compiles the result into
the artifact everyone carries. Reorder the dates and the feasibility changes and the book is wrong.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The tour is the scope at which riders, crew, budget and gear are held | Not merely a folder of dates. Every other sub-domain attaches to it: one rider serves 40 dates (18.04 D-02), one roster covers a date range (18.08.01), one budget spans the run (18.13.02). The container is what makes those scopes meaningful. | Agent, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | Feasibility is checked, not optimised | The platform tells you a route is impossible; it does not compute the optimal one. Real routing is dominated by avails, offers, radius clauses and relationships — constraints a solver cannot see. The check is achievable and useful; the solve is a fantasy that would be confidently wrong. | Agent Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | Routing operates on holds, not just confirmed dates | Routing happens while dates are still holds (domain 17). A route with a hold in it is speculative, and the whole point of routing is deciding *which* holds to confirm. A confirmed-only model would be useless at the moment it's needed. | Agent, from 17 boundary analysis |
| D-04 | The tour book is a render, like the day sheet | Same mechanism, wider scope (18.10 D-01). A hand-typed tour book is a snapshot that's wrong by date 3. | Agent, from 18.10 DT-05 |
