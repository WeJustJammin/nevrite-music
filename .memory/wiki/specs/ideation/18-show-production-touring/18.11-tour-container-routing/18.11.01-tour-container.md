# Feature: Tour Container & Date Sequencing

> **Number**: 18.11.01
> **Parent**: [Tour Container & Routing](./18.11-tour-container-routing-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The object that turns a pile of dates into a tour: a named run with a date range, an act, a set of
shows (confirmed and held), and the days between them. It is the scope at which everything else in
this domain is held — one rider, one roster, one budget, one manifest — which is what makes it more
than a folder.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Creates and sees their tour — this is *their* run of dates and, for a DIY act, they built it. Sees the whole shape: where they're going, how long they're away, what's confirmed and what isn't. |
| Producer | Full | Manages the container and everything hung off it. Adds dates, sequences them, and sees the run as one object rather than eleven emails. |
| Operator | **None** | A venue is a node on someone's tour, not a participant in it. They see their own date (18.01) and nothing about the route — an act's tour shape is competitively sensitive and none of their business. |
| Fan | Read-only (announced dates) | Sees announced dates through 20/21 — "playing near you" — never holds, cancellations in progress, or the operational container. |

## Behavior

A tour holds dates in sequence, including **holds** from domain 17 that aren't confirmed yet, plus
non-show days (travel, days off, promo). It is the attachment point for the rider version, crew
roster, budget and manifest. Adding or removing a date re-runs feasibility (18.11.02) and
invalidates the tour book (18.11.03).

### Happy Path

1. Producer creates a tour: name, act, date range.
2. Confirmed dates and holds from 17 are added; days between are typed (travel, off, promo).
3. The rider, roster, budget and manifest attach at tour level.
4. Feasibility runs across the sequence; the route is legal and drivable.
5. Holds convert to confirmed; the tour firms up date by date.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| A hold never confirms | A gap in the route; the rest may become unroutable | "Bristol dropped — 400 mi Manchester→Exeter" |
| Dates added by an agent, run by a TM | Two people, two purposes, one object (Q-01) | — |
| Act plays two set types on the run | Rider overrides per date (18.04.04), not two tours | "Full band (7) · Acoustic (2)" |
| One-off show, no tour | Every date needs a home; a single show shouldn't require a fake tour | Show exists standalone; tour-scoped objects fall back to act-level |
| Tour crosses a border mid-run | Triggers 18.14; changes per diems, driver rules, carnets | "Crossing to EU on date 5 — 3 requirements" |
| Overlapping tours (a member on two runs) | A person cannot be in two places; a real conflict | "[Name] is on [other tour] 14–16 Mar" |
| Festival dates dropped into a tour | Same container, different advance shape — the date is a member but its advance runs through the promoter/festival, not the act's own rider flow (18.04) | "Festival date — advance via [festival], rider sent, backline shared" |
| Operator tries to view the tour route | Denied — an Operator sees only their own date (18.01), never the surrounding sequence | Their date shows; no route, no neighbouring dates, no "next stop" |
| Two editors sequence the same tour at once (agent + TM, Q-01) | Last-write on the date list would silently drop a change; edits are per-date so concurrent edits to *different* dates merge, same-date edits conflict-flag | "[Name] moved this date while you were editing — review" |
| Delete a date that other objects reference (crew call, budget line) | Removing a date cascades: its non-show days re-type, roster ranges shrink, budget lines detach; not a hard delete of dependents | "Removing Leeds also drops 1 travel day and 2 crew calls — confirm" |
| Reorder confirmed dates into an infeasible route | Allowed — the container doesn't block it; feasibility (18.11.02) flags the result rather than refusing the edit | "Route now 400 mi over driver-hour limit on date 5" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A tour is a folder of shows | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — it is the **scope at which almost everything in this domain is held**. One rider serves the run (18.04 D-02); the roster is date-ranged against it (18.08.01); the budget spans it (18.13.02); the manifest travels with it (18.09.01). Remove the container and every one of those scopes collapses to per-date, which is the copy-paste drift the domain exists to end. |
| DT-02 | A tour is only confirmed dates | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — **routing happens on holds** (D-03). An agent holds six dates and decides which four to confirm based on whether they route. A container that only accepts confirmed dates is useless precisely when it's needed — before the decisions are made. This is also where the 17/18 boundary is most porous (Q-01). |
| DT-03 | Non-show days are gaps | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — a travel day is a scheduled day with driver hours, a hotel, and per diems attached; a day off still costs money and has a location. Modelling them as absence loses the tour's actual cost and makes driver-hour checking (18.12.03) impossible. |
| DT-04 | Every show belongs to a tour | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — most working musicians play one-off shows, not tours, and forcing them to create a fake tour to play a Tuesday is the kind of ceremony that loses users. The container must be optional, with tour-scoped objects falling back to act level. **This matters for the beachhead**: the Musician persona plays scattered dates, not runs. |
| DT-05 | A tour holds exactly one act (resolves Q-02) | Package/co-headline reality — support acts and co-headliners share a run | ✅ CONFIRMED (one *primary* act) — the container is scoped to **one headline act**: it owns the run, the budget (18.13.02) and the manifest (18.09.01). Support/co-headline acts attach as **billed participants on the dates**, not co-owners of the container — each keeps its own rider, roster and settlement. Rejected the alternative (a multi-act container) because it forces one budget and one manifest to span independent businesses, collapsing the very scopes D-01 exists to keep clean. A co-headline where neither owns the run is the genuine edge — modelled as two linked tours sharing a date list, deferred: `[PENDING — /write-be-spec]` (Q-03). |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The tour is the scope for riders, crew, budget and gear | Remove it and every scope collapses to per-date copy-paste drift |
| D-02 | Holds are first-class members of a tour | Routing decisions are made on holds, before confirmation |
| D-03 | Non-show days are scheduled days with costs and constraints | A travel day has driver hours, a hotel and per diems |
| D-04 | The container is optional — a one-off show needs no tour | Most working musicians play scattered dates; ceremony loses them |
| D-05 | One primary act owns a tour; support/co-headline attach as billed participants per date | One budget and one manifest cannot span independent businesses; multi-act ownership collapses D-01's scopes (DT-05) |
