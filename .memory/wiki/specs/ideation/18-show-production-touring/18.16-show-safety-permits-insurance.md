# Feature: Show Safety, Permits & Insurance Certificates

> **Number**: 18.16
> **Parent**: [Show Production & Touring](./show-production-touring-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Holds the documents a show legally cannot happen without — public liability insurance, electrical
test certificates, pyro and special-effects permits, noise permits, temporary structure sign-off —
and knows which ones this date demands, whether they're valid on the day, and who has seen them.
Today these are PDF attachments in an email chain, and their expiry is nobody's job.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only + Config (own certs) | Uploads their own certificates (e.g. public liability for a self-employed performer); sees whether tonight's date is document-clear. Rarely engages unless something is missing. |
| Producer | Full | Owns the pack: uploads certs, maps them to each date's requirements, chases what's missing, and is the person who gets stopped at the door without them. Sets expiry alerts. |
| Operator | Config + Read-only | Declares what this venue/jurisdiction requires (their licence conditions are the source of the requirement) and inspects the artist's certificates. Uploads venue-side documents the artist's insurer may demand. |
| Fan | None | Invisible. The fan's interest is served indirectly — the show is safe and legal — but no fan surface exists. Venue capacity and accessibility facts they *do* see come from 16/19. |

## Behavior

Requirements are derived from the venue and jurisdiction (16) plus the show's own risk features
(pyro, temporary structures, outdoor). Each requirement resolves to a document with an issuer,
validity window and a viewer log. The check runs as part of the advance and blocks `advanced`
state if a hard requirement is unmet.

### Happy Path

1. Event enters advancing; required documents are derived from venue + jurisdiction + show features.
2. Producer uploads or attaches existing certificates from the tour's document pack.
3. System validates each against the show date (valid on the day, not merely today).
4. Operator marks each as inspected/accepted; the advance's compliance section clears.
5. Expiry alerts fire ahead of any date where a document lapses mid-tour.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Certificate expires mid-tour | Every affected downstream date flags at once, not one at a time. The requirement is named by its **slot**; the instrument that fills it comes from the date's jurisdiction profile (16.01.06 D-05/D-06), never from this file | "{Profile-declared liability cover} expires 14 Mar — affects 9 dates from 15 Mar" |
| Jurisdiction requires a document we don't know about | Requirement set is incomplete; Operator can add a custom requirement | Operator: "add a requirement this venue needs" |
| Document uploaded but unreadable/wrong | Human inspection is the gate — the system never claims a PDF is valid insurance | "Uploaded — awaiting venue acceptance" (never "compliant ✅") |
| Show goes ahead without a required document | Non-blocking record of the override with who authorised it | "Proceeding without noise permit — authorised by [name]" |
| Pyro added late | New requirement appears after freeze; triggers change control (18.03.05) | "Adding pyro adds 2 permit requirements — re-open advance?" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Compliance section opened | Requirement list skeleton |
| Empty | No requirements derived | "No documents required for this date" (with a caveat that this is derived, not guaranteed) |
| Populated | Requirements + documents present | Checklist with validity dates and inspection status |
| Error | Requirement derivation failed (venue data missing) | "Can't derive requirements — venue jurisdiction unknown" |
| Partial | Some documents missing/expiring | Amber list of gaps with days-to-show |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The system should assert compliance ("✅ compliant") | Product instinct | ❌ REJECTED — asserting legal compliance from a parsed PDF creates liability the platform must not accept and a false confidence that gets a show shut down. The system tracks *presence, validity window and acceptance*; a human accepts. Language must never imply legal sign-off. |
| DT-02 | Expiry is a per-document property, not a per-date one | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — validity must be evaluated against the *show date*, not today. A cert valid at upload and expired at show time is the exact failure this exists to catch, and it fails silently in every email-based workflow. |
| DT-03 | Requirements are the artist's problem | Boundary lens | ❌ REJECTED — the requirement originates from the venue's statutory conditions and local law, which the Operator knows and the artist does not. Derivation must be venue-sourced; only the *fulfilment* is the artist's. **⚠️ Refined (owner ratification, DQ-14.1 → 16.01.06 D-05/D-06):** "the venue's licence conditions" was a UK-shaped generalisation. **Whether a statutory instrument imposes a condition at all is profile-declared** — the US launch profile declares none for the operating conditions (16.02.03 D-01/D-13), so in it the venue-sourced requirement is the Operator's own claim rather than a derived statutory fact. The rejection stands either way: the requirement is still venue-sourced and still not the artist's to originate. |
| DT-04 | This is a document store, so it is architecture, not product | Node Classification Gate | ❌ REJECTED — the storage is infrastructure, but "which documents does *this date in this jurisdiction* demand, and are they valid on the day" is domain knowledge and a product capability. The store is routed to `/create-prd`; the requirement engine stays. |
| DT-05 | Insurance certificates are also demanded of the venue by the artist's side | Domain expert lens | ✅ CONFIRMED — it is two-sided. Larger acts demand the venue's liability cover and structural safety records — **slots**, named per the date's jurisdiction profile (16.01.06 D-06), never hardcoded. The requirement engine must be symmetrical, which the candidate list did not anticipate. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The platform never asserts legal compliance — it tracks presence, validity and human acceptance | Asserting compliance from a parsed PDF creates liability and false confidence |
| D-02 | Validity is evaluated against the show date, not the upload date | A cert that lapses mid-tour is the silent failure this exists to catch |
| D-03 | Requirements are venue/jurisdiction-derived; fulfilment is artist-side | The artist cannot know a room's licence conditions |
