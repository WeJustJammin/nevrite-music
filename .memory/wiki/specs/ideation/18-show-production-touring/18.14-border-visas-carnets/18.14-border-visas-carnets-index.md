## Overview

Everything a border demands before a tour is allowed through it: the right to work (visas and
permits), the right to bring the gear back out (carnets), and the tax the destination country takes
off the top before you've been paid. Post-Brexit this became the single largest new cost and barrier
in European touring, and it is the reason a great many UK acts stopped crossing.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three capabilities with one trigger and one shape. A border crossing
fires all three; each resolves to a document or a filing with a validity window and a deadline; and
each is derived from data the platform already holds — the roster (who), the manifest (what), and
the fee (how much). Miss one and the date cannot happen, which makes them a coherent gate rather
than three unrelated forms.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The platform never gives immigration, customs or tax advice | Three regulated advice domains in one sub-domain. Getting any of them wrong doesn't produce a bug — it produces a musician refused entry, gear impounded, or an incorrect tax filing. The platform tracks **requirements, documents, deadlines and validity**; a human (usually a specialist) advises. This is the same posture as 18.16 D-01 on compliance certificates, applied to much higher stakes. | Agent Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | All three derive from data already held | A carnet is the gear manifest (18.09.01) in a customs format. A visa application needs the roster (18.08.01) and the dates (18.11.01). Withholding needs the fee (17) and the territory. **Nothing here requires new data** — only a new projection of existing data, which is the consolidation dividend (D-18) at its most concrete. | Agent, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | Withholding tax is included despite an uncertain home | It is triggered by a border crossing, computed on a show fee, and invisible to every act until the money arrives short. It may belong in 23 or 17; it is placed here because the *trigger* is here. Flagged for ratification (Q-01). | Agent Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-04 | The whole sub-domain is proposed `wont` | Not because it doesn't matter — it is the biggest real barrier in European touring — but because it is deep, jurisdictional, perishable specialist knowledge serving the minority of acts who cross borders. It is a later-phase domain, and the honest place for it is Won't-Have-Now rather than a Should nobody builds. | Agent MoSCoW reasoning |

| Q-01 | Does withholding tax belong in 18 (border-triggered), 17 (deducted at settlement) or 23 (the act's tax position)? All three have a claim. (Third boundary question of this shape in the domain — see 18.13 Q-01, 18.15 Q-01.) | User | `/ideate-validate` |
