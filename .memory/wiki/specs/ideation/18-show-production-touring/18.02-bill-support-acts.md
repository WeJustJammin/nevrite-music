# Feature: Bill & Support Act Management

> **Number**: 18.02
> **Parent**: [Show Production & Touring](./show-production-touring-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Manages who plays a show, in what order, for how long — and critically, which production objects
each act owns privately versus shares with the bill. A three-act bill is not three shows; it is
one venue, one shared input list, one changeover budget, and three sets of riders that must not
leak into each other.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (own act) / Read-only (bill) | Adds support acts to their own headline show; as a support act, sees their own slot, set length, load-in and shared backline — never the headliner's rider, fee, or guest list. |
| Producer | Full | Sets billing order and set lengths, decides what is shared (house kit, input list, changeover plan) vs private, and runs one advance covering the whole bill against the venue. |
| Operator | Config | Sees the full bill because they must schedule it, but sees each act's *technical* needs only — never inter-act commercial terms. Can propose a local support act to the headliner. |
| Fan | Read-only (derived) | Sees the public bill — who's playing, billing order, stage times — via the domain 19/21 listing. Never sees set lengths as production data, changeover, or any act's rider. |

## Behavior

Each act on the bill is a **participant record** carrying a slot (order index, set length in
minutes, changeover-after in minutes) and a scope pointer to its own production objects (rider,
stage plot layer, guest list, advance section). One event has exactly one bill; the bill is not a
new production object but a **scoping-and-ordering layer** over objects that already exist in their
own sub-domains (D-01).

The default confidentiality posture is **private per act**, enforced at the data layer, not the UI
(DT-02): an act's rider, fee, hospitality and guest list are readable only by that act, the
Producer/headliner running the bill, and the Operator (technical needs only, never commercial
terms). Sharing is **opt-in and object-scoped** — the Producer explicitly marks a house kit, input
list, or a stage-plot layer as shared, and only those objects cross the act boundary. Nothing is
shared by default.

Support acts are frequently added late and by the venue rather than the artist, so the bill accepts
edits from two origins — artist-side (Producer/headliner) and venue-side (Operator) — but
venue-originated additions are **proposals** that only take effect on headliner approval (DT-03). A
participant may be an on-platform Band entity (domain 01) or an off-platform name-only entry that
carries a manually-entered technical need but no rider, no guest list, and no advance section
(D-03).

### Happy Path

1. Producer opens the event's bill and sets the headline act (defaults to the artist on the deal).
2. Producer adds a support act — either an on-platform Band entity or an off-platform name.
3. Producer assigns slot order, set length, and changeover duration; the run of show (18.07.01) recomputes.
4. The support act is invited; on acceptance they get their own private rider/plot slot and see the shared objects.
5. Advance (18.03) runs once for the bill, with per-act sections the venue confirms independently.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Venue adds a local support without asking | Change is proposed, not applied; headliner approval required (DT-03) | Headliner: "The venue proposed adding [act] at 19:30 — approve/decline" |
| Support act tries to read the headliner's fee, rider, or guest list | Blocked at the data layer, not just hidden in the UI (DT-02) — the field is never sent to that act's client | Support: the object simply does not appear; no "restricted" placeholder that confirms it exists |
| Support act tries to reorder the bill or edit another act's slot | Rejected — slot/order edits are Producer/headliner-only; a support act has Full only on its **own** act | "Only the headliner can change billing order" |
| Two editors reorder the bill at the same time | Last-write on the ordered list is rejected against a stale version; the second editor re-bases on the current order | "The bill changed while you were editing — review the new order before saving" |
| Support act's rider needs gear the headliner's plot occupies | Conflict flagged on the shared stage plot (18.05) | "Support needs a second kit — no space in the current plot" |
| Set lengths + changeovers exceed the curfew window | Curfew check (18.07.02) fails at the **bill** level, not per act | "Bill overruns curfew by 12 min — trim a set or a changeover" |
| Same act added twice (e.g. venue proposal duplicates an artist add) | De-duplicated on Band entity id; off-platform names matched by fuzzy name + warned, not auto-merged | "[act] is already on this bill — merge or keep both?" |
| Support act drops out day-of | Slot vacated; run of show (18.07.01) recomputes; doors/set times may shift | "Removing [act] frees 35 min — apply to changeover or move doors?" |
| Off-platform support act | Name-only participant; manual tech need only; no rider, no guest list, no advance section (D-03) | "This act isn't on WeJammin — their tech needs must be added manually" |
| Off-platform act later joins WeJammin | Name-only entry can be claimed/linked to the new Band entity; prior bill history back-fills | "[act] is now on WeJammin — link this slot to their profile?" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Bill management is a sub-domain (each act has riders, advance, merch, guest list) | Node Classification Gate | ❌ REJECTED — those objects already exist and belong to their own sub-domains; the bill only adds *scoping and ordering* to them. Making it a sub-domain would duplicate every production object per act. One capability: who plays, when, and what they can see. |
| DT-02 | The dangerous failure here is confidentiality, not scheduling | Domain expert lens | ✅ CONFIRMED — a support act seeing the headliner's fee, guest list, or hospitality rider is a relationship-ending leak. Default-private per act with explicit sharing is the only safe posture, and it must be enforced at the data layer, not the UI. |
| DT-03 | Support acts are always chosen by the headliner | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — locals are routinely added by the venue/promoter, sometimes as a condition of the deal. The bill must accept venue-originated proposals under headliner approval. |
| DT-04 | Merch cut and buy-on/pay-to-play terms belong here | Domain expert lens | ⏳ DEFERRED — these are commercial terms between acts (17's territory) but they surface on the bill. Boundary flagged as Q-02. |
| DT-05 | An off-platform support act can still receive an advance section and payment through the platform (resolves Q-01) | Step 5 Should-depth | ❌ REJECTED — the advance (18.03) and settlement (17) run against **platform participants** with a payout identity; a name-only entry has no account, no verified identity, and no payout rail, so it cannot receive an advance section or on-platform money. It is a scheduling/technical placeholder only. Its commercial terms are handled off-platform until it claims a WeJammin account, at which point the slot links and the advance section becomes available (D-03). |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Bill is a feature, not a sub-domain | It scopes and orders existing production objects rather than owning new interacting capabilities |
| D-02 | Default confidentiality is private-per-act with explicit sharing, enforced at the data layer | Cross-act leakage of fees/riders/guest lists is the domain's highest-consequence failure; UI-only hiding is insufficient |
| D-03 | Off-platform support acts are name-only scheduling/technical placeholders with no advance section and no on-platform payout (resolves Q-01) | Advance and settlement require a verified platform identity and payout rail; a claimed link later unlocks these |
