# Feature: Backline Sourcing & Rental Requests

> **Number**: 18.09.02
> **Parent**: [Backline & Gear Manifest](./18.09-backline-gear-manifest-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Turning a capability shortfall into gear on the stage. The diff (18.03.02) says the room has no bass
rig; this is what happens next — hire it from the venue, from a local rental company, or bring it.
It is the only place in the domain where a *problem the platform detected* becomes a *transaction the
platform can complete*.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees what they'll actually be playing tonight — "hired Ampeg SVT, not your rig". Cares deeply and is usually told last. |
| Producer | Full | Decides per shortfall: bring, rent, substitute or redline. Books the rental, and pays for it out of the tour budget. |
| Operator | **Full (often supplies)** | Many venues rent backline themselves — it's a revenue line and they're the closest supplier. They quote against the act's shortfall directly. |
| Fan | None | Never visible. |

## Behavior

A shortfall from the diff carries context the act didn't have to type — what's missing, for which
date, in which city, to which spec. Resolution routes to the venue (who often has it), the gear
marketplace (13), or a local rental supplier. A confirmed rental becomes a date-scoped manifest
entry (18.09.01) and a tour cost (18.13.02).

### Happy Path

1. The diff reports a shortfall: no bass rig at this venue.
2. Producer chooses "rent"; the request is pre-filled from the rider's spec and the date.
3. The venue quotes £X; a local supplier quotes £Y.
4. Producer confirms the venue's; it appears on the date's manifest and in the budget.
5. The advance item closes; the rider's shortfall is resolved without a redline.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| No local supply | The common case outside major cities — cold start with geography | "No suppliers in [city] — ask the venue, or carry it" |
| Rental costs more than the show earns | The economics of a shortfall are the real decision | "Bass rig £180 · Show fee £250" |
| Rented gear differs from the rider spec | A rental is itself a substitution needing the strictness rule (18.04.01 D-02) | "Available: Ampeg SVT (rider says Aguilar). Acceptable?" |
| Rental doesn't arrive | Discovered at soundcheck with no recovery | "Rental unconfirmed 4 hours out" |
| Same shortfall on 9 dates | Carrying it is cheaper than 9 rentals — an aggregate the platform can see and the TM can't | "Renting on 9 dates: £1,620. Consider carrying it." |
| Damage to rented gear | A claim between the act and the supplier | Routes to 13/24 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Sourcing opened | Shortfall list skeleton |
| Empty | No shortfalls | "Nothing to source — the room has everything" |
| Populated | Shortfalls exist | Per-shortfall options with quotes, costs and dates |
| Error | Request failed | "Couldn't send — retry" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is the diff's payoff and the domain's clearest commercial link | Domain rationale + D-18 | ✅ CONFIRMED — it is the one place where **the platform detects a problem and can complete the transaction that solves it**. Nothing else in the domain closes that loop: the diff finds a gap, and instead of generating an email it generates a booking. This is consolidation and provenance meeting in one flow, and it's the strongest argument that 18 and 13/16 belong on the same platform. |
| DT-02 | Sourcing needs its own rental marketplace | Naive scoping | ❌ REJECTED — same rejection as local crew hire (18.08.03 DT-01). Gear rental is domain 13's shape (and often 16's, since venues rent their own). A parallel rail here forks the marketplace and splits the supplier's reputation and payouts in two. This feature *generates demand*; it doesn't fulfil it. |
| DT-03 | The venue is a supplier of last resort | Boundary lens | ❌ REJECTED — the venue is usually the **first** supplier: they're in the building, they often own a house rig, and it's a revenue line they want. Modelling them as a supplier gives the Operator a direct commercial reason to keep their capability data accurate — which is the exact incentive the diff (18.03.02 DT-04) needs and doesn't otherwise have. That's a rare alignment and worth designing around. |
| DT-04 | Each shortfall is decided in isolation | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — the **aggregate is the insight**. Renting a bass rig on 9 of 11 dates costs more than carrying one, and no TM sees that because they solve each date as it arrives. The platform sees the whole route at once. That's a decision-support capability that only exists because the tour is one object (18.11.01). |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Uses the gear rental rail (13/16); does not build a marketplace | Forking would split supplier reputation and payouts |
| D-02 | The venue is modelled as a first-choice supplier | It's revenue for them, and it gives the Operator a commercial reason to keep capability data accurate — the incentive the diff otherwise lacks |
| D-03 | Shortfalls are analysed in aggregate across the tour, not per date | Renting on 9 of 11 dates costs more than carrying it, and no TM can see that |
