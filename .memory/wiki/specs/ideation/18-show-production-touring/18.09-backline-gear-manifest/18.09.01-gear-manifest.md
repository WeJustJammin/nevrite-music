# Feature: Backline & Gear Manifest

> **Number**: 18.09.01
> **Parent**: [Backline & Gear Manifest](./18.09-backline-gear-manifest-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The list of every physical item the tour moves: instruments, amps, drums, cases, cables, spares —
with owner, serial, value and weight. It is the same list a carnet needs, an insurer needs, a
freight forwarder needs and a load-out check needs, which is why it should be built once from data
the platform already holds rather than typed four times into four documents.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config (own gear) | Adds their own items from their registry (domain 15) — their bass, their pedalboard, their spare strings. **Their gear is theirs**, on the tour and after it. |
| Producer | Full | Owns the tour manifest: what's travelling, what the venue supplies, what's rented per date. Uses it for freight, carnets, insurance and the load-out check. |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees what's arriving in their building — how much, how heavy, how many hands it needs — and what they're expected to supply. That's a real planning input for load-in. |
| Fan | None | Never visible. Gear lists are theft targets: a public list of what's in the van and where it'll be is an inventory for someone else. |

## Behavior

Items **reference** the gear registry (15) rather than duplicating it, so serial, value, photo and
proof of ownership come from the record that already exists. The manifest resolves per date —
travelling items plus rentals for that date, minus what the venue supplies. It rolls up to weight
and value totals that freight, insurance and customs all need.

### Happy Path

1. Producer creates the tour manifest; band members add their own registered gear.
2. The technical rider (18.04.01) declares what the venue supplies; the manifest covers the rest.
3. Weight and value totals roll up for freight and insurance.
4. A date's diff shows the venue can't supply a bass rig — sourcing (18.09.02) adds a rental.
5. At load-out, the date's resolved manifest is the checklist (18.09.03).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Gear isn't in the registry | Ad-hoc entry allowed, but it lacks the serial a carnet needs | "Not registered — no serial. Carnets need one." |
| Item is shared band property | Ownership is per-person by default (D-03); band-owned needs an explicit owner | "Who owns the PA? It affects insurance and customs." |
| Item is rented for one date | Date-scoped entry (D-05) belonging to nobody on the tour; no registry serial, so it carries the vendor's own | "Hired bass rig — 14 Mar only" |
| Gear is sold mid-tour | Registry (15) and marketplace (13) both change; the manifest must follow | "Sold — removed from remaining dates" |
| Weight matters (fly dates, freight) | Totals are a hard constraint, not information | "Fly date: 240 kg — 60 kg over allowance" |
| Consumables (strings, sticks, batteries) | Not registry items; quantity-tracked, not serial-tracked | "Consumables — quantity only" |
| Same item on two tours | A person's gear can only be in one place; conflicts are real | "[Name]'s bass is on [other tour] on 14 Mar" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Manifest opened | Item list skeleton |
| Empty | No manifest | "No gear listed" + import-from-registry CTA |
| Populated | Items exist | Grouped by owner with weight/value totals and per-date resolution |

| Partial | Some items unregistered | "18 of 24 items have serials" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The manifest is a list the TM types | Incumbent behaviour | ❌ REJECTED — domain 15 already holds this gear with serials, photos and proof of ownership. Typing it again creates a **second truth about the same physical object**, and the registry's version is the one with the serial the carnet needs and the insurer wants. The manifest references; it does not copy. This is the same derive-don't-transcribe rule as the rest of the domain, reaching across a domain boundary. |
| DT-02 | One manifest per tour | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — the manifest is **per date**: rentals appear for one night, the venue supplies different things in each room, and a fly date carries a fraction of what the bus does. A tour-level list can't express any of that, and every downstream consumer (freight, customs, load-out) wants the per-date resolution. |
| DT-03 | Gear belongs to the band | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — **the bassist's rig is the bassist's.** This matters in three places where "whose is it?" is the whole question: insurance (whose claim?), customs (whose serial on the carnet?), and load-out (whose amp is missing?). Band-owned-by-default gets all three wrong and is contradicted by `personas.md`'s multi-hyphenate, whose gear moves between three bands. |
| DT-04 | Weight is a detail | Scope lens | ❌ REJECTED — on a fly date, weight *is* the constraint, and excess baggage on instruments is a notorious and expensive surprise. The registry may not hold weights (Q-01), which makes this a data requirement flowing back to 15. |
| DT-05 | The manifest serves one purpose | Purpose lens | ✅ CONFIRMED as the key insight — **one list, five consumers**: freight, insurance, carnet (18.14.02), load-out check (18.09.03) and the venue's load-in planning. Today it is typed separately for each, in different formats, and they disagree. The consolidation argument (D-18) in miniature: the value isn't the list, it's that there's only one of it. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Items reference the gear registry (15); never re-typed | Two truths about one physical object, and only the registry's has the serial |
| D-02 | The manifest resolves per date, not per tour | Rentals, fly dates and venue-supplied gear all vary nightly |
| D-03 | Ownership is per-person | Insurance, customs and load-out are all "whose is it?" questions |
| D-04 | One list, five consumers | Freight, insurance, carnet, load-out and venue load-in are typed separately today and disagree |
| D-05 | Rentals are **date-scoped manifest entries**, not registry references | A hired item belongs to nobody on the tour and has no registry record — modelling it as a registry reference would force a fake ownership row; a date-scoped ghost carries the vendor's serial (18.09.02) and vanishes when the date passes |
