## Overview

What gets played, in what order, for how long — and everything that has to fire in sync with it:
backing tracks, click, timecode, cues and charts. The setlist looks like the most trivial object in
live music (a sheet of paper taped to the floor) and is actually the spine of the show: it drives
set duration, the run of show, the curfew margin, every performer's cues, and every show file's
playback order.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: four capabilities that generate and constrain each other. The
authored setlist produces the stage-ready output; show files are ordered *by* the setlist and
re-order with it; and the performed setlist — what actually happened — is a different object from
the plan, captured at the moment it's true. Change the running order and the other three are wrong.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The planned setlist and the performed setlist are different objects | This is the sub-domain's most important decision and it comes straight from the thesis (D-18). The plan is intent; the performance is fact. Overwriting the plan with what happened destroys both — you lose what you meant to do and you get a "record" that was edited after the fact. Provenance requires the fact be captured separately, at the time. | Agent Deep Think from D-18, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | WeJammin does not build a playback engine | Backing tracks and click run in Ableton/MainStage/Playback on a dedicated machine on stage. Competing with that is a different product, and a web platform has no business being in the audio path when a show is running. What WeJammin owns is **custody, versioning and ordering** — knowing which file is current, which is a real and unsolved problem. | Agent Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | The setlist is private to the act | Venues do not get the setlist. It drives the *duration* they need, which reaches them through the run of show (18.07.01) — not the songs. | Agent, Role Matrix analysis |
| D-04 | Setlist durations should be measured, not estimated | Every downstream number (set end, curfew margin, changeover) inherits the setlist's timings. Rehearsal (18.19) and performed capture (18.06.04) are where real durations come from. | Agent, from 18.19 DT-02 |
