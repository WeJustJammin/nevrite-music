## Overview

Who is working, when they're called, what they're allowed near, and who is hired locally to make up
the difference. A touring party is not a fixed team — it is a core roster plus per-date local crew,
plus deps, plus the venue's own staff, and each of those people needs a call time, an access level
and, sometimes, paying.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three capabilities that generate each other. A local hire becomes a
roster entry with a call time; a roster entry needs a credential; a credential's access level is
derived from the role the roster assigned. Hire → roster → call → credential is one chain, and the
head count it produces silently drives catering, rooming and per diems in three other sub-domains.

> **Persona note — the sharpest in the domain.** Crew are not a persona. Per `meta/personas.md`
> (D-19) there are four: Musician, Producer, Operator, Fan. A tour manager, a monitor engineer and a
> backline tech map onto **Producer** (the "runs the room, coordinates contributors, de-facto
> project manager" lens); a musician who also techs maps onto **Musician**. But a **local stagehand**
> hired for four hours maps cleanly onto **none of them** — they are labour, not a career persona.
> This sub-domain is where the four-persona model is under the most strain. Escalated as Q-01, and
> it corroborates `personas.md` Q-01 from a different direction than the dealer question does.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Call times derive from the run of show, never typed | A call time is "load-in minus 30" — a function of the schedule (18.07.01). Typed call times are stale the moment the schedule slips, which is the moment they matter. This is the same derive-don't-transcribe discipline as the input list and the day sheet. | Agent, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | The roster's head count is a shared input, not local data | Party size drives hospitality quantities (18.04.02 D-01), rooming (18.12.02) and per diems (18.13.01). Adding a crew member silently changes a rider obligation two sub-domains away — this must be visible, not silent. | Agent, from 18.04-riders-cx CX-02 |
| D-03 | Local crew hire is a *service booking*, not an employment record | Hiring a stagehand for a night is the same shape as booking any service provider (domain 05): a scoped brief, a rate, a person, a payment. Building a parallel hiring rail here would fork the marketplace. | Agent classification, 2026-07-16 |
| D-04 | Credentials and tickets are the same mechanism, different issuance | A laminate and a ticket are both "this person may enter this area at this time", scanned at a door. Recorded as a cross-cut with domain 19 rather than built twice. | Agent cross-cut detection, 2026-07-16 |
