# Feature: Tour Merch Inventory & Per-Show Counts

> **Number**: 18.15
> **Parent**: [Show Production & Touring](./show-production-touring-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The count-in / count-out ledger that tracks physical merch across a tour: what left the lock-up,
what sold tonight, what the venue takes, what's left in the box. Merch is often the most
profitable line on a small tour and the least instrumented — it is reconciled with a pen on the
back of a setlist and settled in cash at 01:00.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Sees stock on hand by size/design, tonight's sales, and what they're owed after the venue cut. For a DIY act they do the counting themselves at the table. |
| Producer | Full | The TM lens: opening count, closing count, comps and damages, venue percentage, cash reconciliation into the tour float (18.13.01). |
| Operator | Config (own cut) | Sees the count agreed for settlement and their contractual percentage — the numbers they settle on. Cannot see the tour's aggregate stock or other dates' sales. |
| Fan | None | Fans buy merch; they never see inventory. The fan-facing purchase surface belongs to domain 20 (D2C) — this feature only knows the counts behind it. |

## Behavior

A tour-level stock pool decrements per show. The count is a two-sided artifact where a venue takes
a percentage: both parties must agree the closing number because money depends on it. Sales made
through the platform's own D2C rail (domain 20) at the show should decrement the same pool as cash
sales at the table — otherwise there are two truths.

### Happy Path

1. Producer loads the tour's opening stock by SKU (design × size).
2. At the venue, opening count is recorded (or carried from last night's closing count).
3. Show runs; card/platform sales auto-decrement, cash sales are counted at the end.
4. Closing count is entered; sold = opening − closing − comps − damages; venue cut computes.
5. Both sides confirm the count; the number hands to 17 for settlement and the float updates.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Counts don't reconcile (stock missing) | Variance shown, not silently absorbed; requires a reason code | "8 units unaccounted — theft / miscount / comp?" |
| Venue disputes the closing count | Count locked pending agreement; escalates to 17/24 | "Count contested — settlement held" |
| No signal at the merch table | Counts entered offline, synced later; card sales queue | "Offline — 3 counts pending sync" |
| Restock mid-tour | Stock added to the pool with a source (order from domain 20/13) | "+120 units added at [date]" |
| Merch seller is a hired local, not crew | Access scoped to counts for that date only | Seller sees only tonight's SKUs and counts |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Merch opened | SKU grid skeleton |
| Empty | No stock loaded | "No merch on this tour" + load stock CTA |
| Populated | Stock + counts exist | SKU grid with on-hand, tonight's sales, cut |
| Error | Sync failed | "Counts saved locally — couldn't sync" |
| Partial | Opening counted, show not closed | "Open count — close out after the show" |

| DT-02 | Cash and platform sales can be tracked separately | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — two ledgers for one box of shirts guarantees drift. Both rails must decrement one stock pool or the closing count is meaningless. |

| DT-04 | Merch stock crossing a border is a customs problem | Domain expert lens | ✅ CONFIRMED — merch is goods and is routinely seized or taxed at borders; carnets typically cover gear, not stock for sale. This is a real link to 18.14.02 that the candidate list missed. |
