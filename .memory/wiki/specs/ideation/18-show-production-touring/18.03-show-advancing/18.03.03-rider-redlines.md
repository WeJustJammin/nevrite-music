# Feature: Rider Redlines & Negotiated Exceptions

> **Number**: 18.03.03
> **Parent**: [Show Advancing](./18.03-show-advancing-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The record of what was agreed to be different — per date. A rider is a starting position, not a
contract of fact: the venue can't do the third wedge mix, the act accepts a substitute console,
catering becomes a buyout. Today these exceptions live in a reply to an email and are forgotten by
show day, which is why bands arrive expecting things that were traded away three weeks ago.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees what was traded away on this date — "no wedges tonight, IEMs only; catering is a £15 buyout". This is the persona most harmed when exceptions are invisible, because they're the one who finds out at soundcheck. |
| Producer | Full | Proposes and accepts exceptions, sets which rider items are negotiable vs hard, and owns the record of what this date actually promises. |
| Operator | Full (own side) | Raises the exception ("we can't do that, here's what we can do"), proposes the substitute, and accepts the artist's counter. Their alternative is the honest half of the negotiation. |
| Fan | None | Internal negotiation. Fan-visible consequences (e.g. an accessibility provision changing) surface through the venue's public info in 16/19, never as a redline. |

## Behavior

A redline is scoped to **one date**, never to the rider itself — this is the central rule. It has a
requested item, a proposed alternative, a state (`proposed / accepted / rejected / withdrawn`), and
both parties' identities. Accepted redlines override the rider *for this date only* and become part
of the frozen advance pack, so the day sheet and the diff both reflect reality rather than the
opening position.

### Happy Path

1. Diff (18.03.02) reports a shortfall: the room has an X32, the rider says M32.
2. Operator proposes an exception: "X32 available, engineer familiar with it".
3. Producer reviews and accepts; the redline is recorded against this date.
4. The diff re-runs — the shortfall becomes an accepted exception, not an open item.
5. The advance sheet and day sheet show the X32; nobody arrives expecting an M32.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Exception accepted, then the venue can't honour it either | Redline re-opens as a fresh shortfall with its history intact | "Second failure on this item — history: 2 exceptions" |
| Redline touches a contractual term (fee, merch cut) | Not a redline — routes to 17, which owns deal terms | "This changes the deal — handle it on the contract" |
| Redline touches an access/accessibility provision | Cannot be silently accepted; requires the affected person's confirmation | "This affects an access requirement — [name] must confirm" |
| Same exception on every date of a tour | Signals the rider itself is wrong; prompts a rider amendment (18.04.04) | "You've redlined this on 7 dates — update the rider?" |
| Redline proposed after freeze | Becomes a change-control event (18.03.05), not a quiet edit | "Advance is frozen — this needs re-approval" |
| Verbal agreement never entered | The gap this feature exists to close; a phone-note redline can be recorded by either party | "Agreed by phone — [name], 14 Mar" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Redlines opened | Exception list skeleton |
| Empty | No exceptions | "Nothing negotiated — the rider stands as written" |
| Populated | Exceptions exist | Per-date list: requested → agreed, with who accepted and when |
| Error | Save failed | "Couldn't record — retry (this is not agreed until it saves)" |
| Partial | Proposed, awaiting acceptance | "Proposed by [venue] — awaiting your call" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A redline should edit the rider | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — and this is the feature's whole reason to exist. One rider serves 40 dates; editing it because Manchester has no wedges corrupts the other 39. Redlines are **per-date overlays**, and the rider is immutable from the advance's perspective. The recurring-redline prompt (edge case 4) is how a genuine rider change gets proposed *deliberately*. |
| DT-02 | Redlines are a legal/contractual object | Naming lens | ❌ REJECTED — the word comes from contract markup, but a rider exception is operational, not contractual. Fee, merch cut and buy-on changes are 17's. Conflating them would put crew-visible production data in the same object as deal terms — the exact confidentiality break 18.01 D-01 exists to prevent. |
| DT-03 | Any exception can be accepted by the Producer alone | Authority lens | ❌ REJECTED for access provisions — an access rider item (18.04.03) exists because a specific person needs it; a TM trading it away without that person's confirmation is both a dignity failure and, plausibly, a legal one. Access redlines need the affected person in the loop. |
| DT-04 | The value is the negotiation UI | Product instinct | ❌ REJECTED — the value is that **the agreed position is the one that reaches show day**. The negotiation happens on the phone regardless. What's missing today is the durable record that propagates into the diff, the advance sheet and the day sheet without anyone retyping it. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Redlines are per-date overlays; they never mutate the rider | One rider serves 40 dates — editing it for one corrupts the rest |
| D-02 | Commercial terms are not redlines — they route to 17 | Keeps deal terms out of crew-visible production data |
| D-03 | Access provisions cannot be redlined without the affected person's confirmation | A TM trading away someone's access need is a dignity and compliance failure |
