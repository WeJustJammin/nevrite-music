# Feature: Advance Freeze & Late-Change Control

> **Number**: 18.03.05
> **Parent**: [Show Advancing](./18.03-show-advancing-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The moment the advance stops being a conversation and becomes the plan — and the controlled path
for the changes that inevitably come after it. Without a freeze, "advanced" is a feeling; with one,
there is a single dated pack that everyone worked from, and every later change is an event with an
author, a reason, and a notification.

> **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list. See DT-01.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees that the show is advanced and what changed since. Gets told when a late change affects them ("soundcheck moved 90 minutes"), which is the only part they need. |
| Producer | Full | Declares the freeze, and is the approver for every post-freeze change. Sees the change log and who was notified. This is their accountability record when something goes wrong on the night. |
| Operator | Config (can request) | Requests a post-freeze change ("the local support needs 20 minutes"), cannot apply one unilaterally to the artist's pack. Sees the frozen pack as the authoritative version for their room that night. |
| Fan | None | Internal. A late change with public consequence (doors moving) reaches fans through 19/21's own notification path, not this one. |

## Behavior

Freeze requires zero hard-outstanding items (18.03.01) and produces an immutable, versioned pack:
rider as-agreed, accepted redlines, schedule, contacts, backline, crew. After freeze, every change
is a change-control event — proposed, approved, applied, notified — with a blast-radius preview
showing who is affected and which artifacts (day sheet, advance sheet, run of show) go stale.

### Happy Path

1. Producer sees zero hard-outstanding; freezes the advance at T-5d.
2. The pack is versioned and distributed; everyone works from v1.
3. At T-1d the venue requests a load-in change; it arrives as a proposal, not an edit.
4. Producer sees the blast radius — 2 crew calls, the day sheet, the run of show — and approves.
5. v2 is issued; only the affected roles are re-notified with a changed-lines summary.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Show day arrives, advance never frozen | Auto-freeze at `day-of` with open items recorded as unresolved | "Auto-frozen with 4 items unresolved — recorded" |
| Change needed with no time to approve (T-2h) | Emergency path: apply-and-notify, with the author recorded | "Applied immediately by [name] — 9 people notified" |
| Hard item still open but the TM wants to freeze | Freeze allowed with an explicit override and a reason | "Freezing with 2 hard items open — state a reason" |
| Change proposed by someone who left the tour | Proposal rejected; authorship must be a current participant | "You're no longer on this date" |
| Cascade: a late setlist change alters the run of show and curfew margin | Blast radius spans sub-domains, not just this pack | "This change breaks the curfew margin by 6 min" |
| Nobody reads the v2 notification | Unacknowledged critical changes escalate to the individual | "3 people haven't seen the 17:30 soundcheck change" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Freeze view opened | Pack summary skeleton |
| Empty | Advance not begun | "Nothing to freeze" |
| Populated | Frozen | Versioned pack, change log, per-change notification status |
| Error | Freeze failed | "Couldn't freeze — retry" |
| Partial | Frozen with overrides / unresolved items | "Frozen v1 — 2 items unresolved. See reasons." |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The sweep's advancing model is incomplete without a freeze | Deep Think — domain expert lens | ✅ CONFIRMED, node created — the candidate list had a checklist, a diff, and a sheet, all of which describe advancing as an *open-ended conversation*. But every real advance has a moment where it becomes the plan, and everything bad in live production happens **after** it: the change nobody was told about. Without a freeze there is no "the plan", so there is nothing for a change to be a change *to*, and the sheets have no meaningful version. This is the object the other four hang off. |
| DT-02 | Change control means blocking changes | Naming instinct | ❌ REJECTED — changes are normal and constant in live production; blocking them makes the system something people work around, which is worse than the email chain. The goal is that a change is **authored, notified, and blast-radius-visible** — never that it's prevented. |
| DT-03 | Notification is enough | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — a notification nobody opened is the same as no notification, and at 17:00 the difference is a crew member at the wrong door. Acknowledgement tracking on critical changes, with escalation, is the difference between a record and an outcome. |
| DT-04 | Freeze is a hard gate requiring zero open items | Process instinct | ❌ REJECTED — real shows freeze with things unresolved, constantly. A gate that can't be overridden gets bypassed by not using the system. Override with a stated reason keeps the record honest *and* keeps people inside the tool — the same "make the lazy path the correct path" principle the Producer persona's anti-pattern demands. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The freeze exists — advance completion is a state, not a percentage | Without "the plan", a change isn't a change to anything and versions are meaningless |
| D-02 | Change control authors and notifies changes; it never blocks them | A blocking gate gets bypassed by not using the system |
| D-03 | Freeze can be overridden with a stated reason | Real shows freeze with open items; an unoverridable gate drives people out of the tool |
| D-04 | Critical changes track acknowledgement, not just delivery | An unopened notification at 17:00 is a crew member at the wrong door |
