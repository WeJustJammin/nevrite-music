## Overview

Advancing is the two-sided reconciliation between what an act needs and what a room can actually
provide, run in the weeks before a show. Today it is a 40-question email chain per date, repeated
nightly, and it is the most universally hated workflow in live music. Because WeJammin holds both
the structured rider (18.04) and the venue's technical truth (domain 16), advancing collapses from
correspondence into a computed diff — the domain's strongest wedge, and one only possible where
both datasets live in one place.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: four capabilities that interact tightly — a checklist that tracks
completion across two parties, a diff engine that generates most of the checklist's items,
redlines that resolve the diff's conflicts, and a rendered sheet that projects all three. None
stands alone: the diff feeds the checklist, the checklist gates the sheet, redlines mutate what
the diff considers settled, and the freeze governs all of them.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Advancing and Riders are separate nodes | The sweep bundled "Technical & Hospitality Riders" and "Rider Compliance, Redlines & Venue Capability Diff" as peers. Riders are *authored content* with a lifecycle of their own (templates, versions, reuse across a tour); advancing is a *per-date reconciliation process* that consumes them. One rider serves 40 advances. | Agent classification, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | The diff is the product, not the checklist | Every incumbent ships a checklist. The checklist is a commodity; the diff — rider vs the venue's structured technical truth — is what only a platform holding both datasets can do. Design priority follows. | Agent, from domain rationale + D-18 thesis |
| D-03 | Advancing is two-sided and symmetrical | Not "the artist fills a form the venue reads". Both parties hold facts the other needs, and both must confirm. A one-sided design reproduces the email chain with better fonts. | Agent, Operator persona analysis |
| D-04 | Advance completion is a state with a freeze, not a percentage | "80% advanced" is meaningless at 17:00 on show day. The advance either is or isn't settled, and changes after that point are change-control events. | Agent, Deep Think DT-01 on 18.03.05 |
