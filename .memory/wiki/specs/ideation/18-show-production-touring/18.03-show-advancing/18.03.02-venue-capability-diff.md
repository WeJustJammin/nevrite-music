# Feature: Venue Capability Diff & Requirement Reconciliation

> **Number**: 18.03.02
> **Parent**: [Show Advancing](./18.03-show-advancing-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The engine that computes, per date, **what the venue must supply and whether it can** — by taking the
act's structured requirements (rider 18.04, input list 18.05.02, plot 18.05.01, monitors 18.05.03),
subtracting what the act is already bringing (manifest 18.09.01), and comparing the remainder against
the room's structured technical truth (16.02). Every comparison resolves to `match`, `shortfall`, or
`unknown` — and `unknown` is the honest majority result, not a failure of the engine.

This is the single feature that justifies the domain's claim to be a wedge: no incumbent can compute
it, because none of them holds both sides. It is also the domain's most dangerous feature, because a
confidently wrong diff is worse than no diff — it removes the tour manager's instinct to check, and
the band leaves the wedges at home.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Never sees a diff view. Sees the plain-language consequence where they already are — on the day sheet (18.10) and their own rider section: "no bass rig in this room — you're bringing yours", "wedges tonight, no IEMs". Two exceptions where they are more than a reader: (a) an **access requirement they own** (18.04.03) produces a diff row **only they** see unless they have permitted disclosure to the Operator — the diff never discloses an access need on its owner's behalf; (b) on a DIY act with no Producer, the Musician *is* the advance owner and inherits the Producer lens below (18.03 Q-02). |
| Producer | Full | The primary consumer and the only role that reads the diff *as a diff*. Sets nothing here — strictness lives on the rider (18.04.01), the manifest lives in 18.09.01 — but decides per shortfall: **bring, rent (18.09.02), substitute, redline (18.03.03), or accept**. Sees basis, confidence and severity on every row, the collapsed match band's stale/caveated count, constraint notices, and bill-contention warnings. Re-reads it as either side's data changes, and it is the diff-at-freeze that goes into the pack. |
| Operator | Config (supplies one input) | **Does not run the diff and never sees it as a diff.** Their listing in 16.02 is one of the three inputs, so diff quality is a direct function of their listing quality — that is the incentive loop (16.07 D-06), not a nag. What reaches them is the checklist's projection (18.03.01): questions about unstated fields on their room, and shortfalls raised against it, which they answer, correct in their listing, or meet with a redline. They never see the act's manifest, strictness flags, other dates, or the rider sections that produced no row against them. |
| Fan | None | No visibility and no derived artifact. This is professional infrastructure computed weeks before a ticket holder is affected by it. The only fan-visible trace is second-order and travels a different path entirely: if a shortfall changes doors/set times, that reaches fans through 19/21's public schedule projection, never from here. |

## Behavior

### The three inputs (not two)

The naive model is *rider vs room*. That model is wrong and would kill the feature by alert fatigue.
The real computation is:

```
requirement set  −  what the act brings  =  what the venue must supply
                                              ↓  compared against
                                         the room's structured spec (16.02)
```

| Input | Source | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Requirement set | 18.04.01 rider · 18.05.01 plot · 18.05.02 input list · 18.05.03 monitors · 18.04.02 hospitality · 18.04.03 access | Act (Producer owns; Musicians contribute sections) | Each item carries a **strictness flag** (18.04.01 D-02) and a category |
| What the act brings | 18.09.01 backline & gear manifest; touring crew (18.08) | Act | Netted **before** comparison, never after |
| Room capability | 16.02 venue technical specification (and 16.03 for studio dates) | Operator | Read-only here; the diff never writes to 16 |

**Bringing gear does not delete a requirement — it transforms it.** An act carrying its own bass rig
still needs somewhere to put it (stage area, 18.05.01) and a way to run it (power, 16.02.02 D-14).
The netted requirement becomes `stage footprint + power draw` and is compared against the room's
space and power. If the manifest item does not carry footprint/draw (a 15 Gear Registry attribute),
the transformed requirement resolves to `unknown` — never to "solved".

### Outcome vocabulary — three outcomes, five qualifiers

The primary axis is three-way and matches 16.07 D-02 exactly (16 calls a shortfall a *conflict*; the
words differ, the semantics must not — see Q-05):

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `match` | The room states a value that satisfies the requirement |
| `shortfall` | The room cannot meet the requirement — **including when the room explicitly states `none`** |
| `unknown` | Nothing can be concluded. **Never rendered as a match** (D-01) |

Every row additionally carries five qualifiers. These are what make the three outcomes honest:

| Qualifier | Values | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| `basis` | `act-history` · `identity` · `attribute-satisfied` · `declared-none` · `not-stated` · `undiffable` · `pool-exhausted` | *How* the outcome was reached. `declared-none` → `shortfall`; `not-stated` → `unknown` (16.02.02 D-04 — "none" and "not stated" are different values, and conflating them poisons matching) |
| `caveats[]` | The closed vocabulary from 16.02.02 D-07: `partial`, `conditional-on-configuration`, `conditional-on-operator`, `conditional-on-cost`, `conditional-on-notice`, `temporarily-unavailable` | "6 mixes with your engineer, 4 with ours" is the commonest real monitor fact in live music. A caveated match is not a match (DT-07) |
| `confidence` | Field verification age vs its decay profile (16.05.05 D-01): `fresh` · `ageing` · `stale` | A match on a rotten fact is worse than no match (16.05.05 D-03) |
| `severity` | `show-stopping` · `degrading` · `comfort` | Derived from the requirement's strictness and category. Towels and 3-phase power are not peers (DT-17) |
| `judgement-required` | boolean | Set when the comparison is **not computable** — near-match under identity strictness, `"or similar"` prose, or holistic capability (PA adequacy). A `judgement-required` row is never auto-classified `match` |

### Matching semantics — what is computable and what is not

- **Countable / enumerable facts are matched on typed attributes**: channels, mixes, sends, amperage,
  phase, connector, quantity, metres, kilograms. `usable-input-count` (stage box) and
  `available-channel-count` (desk) are **separate targets** (16.02.02 D-12, 18.05.02 D-03) — a 32-ch
  desk with 24 stage lines fails a 28-source act, and only one of those numbers reveals it.
- **Named-model requirements resolve against the act's declared strictness**, not against a similarity
  judgement. `"any 32-ch digital desk"` → attribute predicate → `match`, basis `attribute-satisfied`.
  `"M32 or nothing"` → identity predicate → `shortfall` + `judgement-required` + candidate substitute
  (DT-10).
- **Holistic capability is never computed.** "Is the PA big enough for this room" is a function of
  system × room × deployment × operator, and inferring it from a model name manufactures the
  confidently-wrong match that 16.07 DT-05 names as the worst failure mode (16.02.02 D-13). It
  resolves to `unknown` + `judgement-required`, with the catalogue reference shown as a fact.
- **First-hand history outranks the listing.** Where the act has played this room and the platform
  holds evidence (18.18 post-show report, 18.09.03 load-out log), that evidence sets `basis:
  act-history` and ranks above an unverified listing field (DT-11; corroborates 16.02.02 D-03 — the
  owner has an incentive to flatter, the band has just tested the claim).

### Constraint notices — the inverted pass

A requirement-driven diff is structurally blind to every venue constraint the rider never thought to
state (DT-08). So a **second, inverted pass runs unconditionally**: every constraint field on the
room (16.02.03 curfew, dB limiter, load-in window, lift hours, pyro/smoke prohibition, power cut-off,
shared dock) is surfaced as a **constraint notice** whether or not any requirement addresses it.

Constraint notices are not diff rows and carry no outcome — they are facts requiring judgement, and
they are the reason the feature catches the show that ends at 96dB rather than at curfew.

### Re-runs

The diff is a **subscription, not a moment** (this answers 16.07 Q-03 from 18's side — see DT-13).

| Trigger | Type |
|---|---|
| Advance opens (event enters `advancing`, 18.01) | Synchronous, first run |
| Rider, plot, input list, monitor or manifest change | Change-triggered |
| Venue spec change in 16.02 | Change-triggered — **and produces a change notice, never a silent re-run** (CX-01 synthesis #3) |
| Redline accepted / withdrawn (18.03.03) | Change-triggered — the overlay is consulted, the rider is not mutated (CX-03) |
| Bill changes — act added, removed, or reordered (18.02) | Change-triggered — re-allocates the contended pool (DT-09) |
| **Scheduled re-check** while `advancing` | **Time-triggered, and non-optional**: a 16.02 field can cross its decay threshold with nobody editing anything, so a change-triggered re-run will never fire for it. Proposed cadence T-14d and T-2d (Q-06 — needs calibration against 16.05.05's profile table, itself flagged as a guess in 16.05.05 Q-03) |
| Freeze declared (18.03.05) | **Terminal** — the diff is snapshotted into the pack and stops re-running against live data. Post-freeze changes route through change control (CX-04/05), never through a silent re-diff |

Row identity is stable across re-runs — **never positional** (CX-01 synthesis #5). A re-run must not
discard an in-flight answer or resurrect a resolved shortfall.

### What the diff does not do

- **It never blocks.** A shortfall is information; the act decides whether four mixes is workable
  (16.07 D-05). The diff does not gate booking, advancing, or the freeze — the freeze reads the
  checklist's outstanding count, never the diff (CX R-03).
- **It never notifies.** It is silent by construction. The checklist (18.03.01) owns item generation,
  ownership, nudging and urgency; the only notification the diff itself justifies is a **delta notice**
  when a re-run changes a previously-settled outcome (DT-16).
- **It never writes to 16.** A redline cannot change a room's listed capability; a genuine correction
  is a 16 edit routed via 18.18 (CX-03 synthesis #3).
- **It never scores the room.** A compatibility number is the pass/fail lie under a different name
  (DT-15). Ranking rooms is 16's search product.

### Happy Path

1. Event enters `advancing` (18.01); the diff runs synchronously against the act's rider, plot, input
   list and monitor requirements for this date.
2. It **nets the manifest first**: the act is carrying its own guitar amps and IEM rack, so those 6
   requirements are transformed to `stage footprint + power` rather than compared against house
   backline. 41 requirements → 35 the venue must supply.
3. It compares the 35 against the room's 16.02 spec and returns: **21 matches, 4 shortfalls, 10
   unknowns**, plus **3 constraint notices** from the inverted pass.
4. Producer opens the result. The match band is collapsed to one line and states its own weakness:
   *"21 matched — 4 on data older than 12 months, 2 conditional."*
5. Producer expands the 4 shortfalls: no bass rig (`declared-none`, show-stopping); M32 requested,
   X32 present (`shortfall` + `judgement-required`, strictness = identity); 4 wedges vs 6 mixes
   required (`attribute-satisfied` fails, show-stopping); no towels (`declared-none`, comfort).
6. Producer resolves each: **rent** the bass rig (→ 18.09.02), **redline** the console (→ 18.03.03,
   Operator proposes "X32, our engineer knows it"), **redline** the monitors down to 4 mixes with the
   affected performers notified, **waive** the towels.
7. The 10 unknowns become questions on the checklist owned by the Operator (CX-01) — "do you have
   3-phase power?", "is the house drum kit still there?".
8. Producer reads the 3 constraint notices — *"96dB limiter, A-weighted, cuts stage power"*, *"curfew
   22:30"*, *"lift stops at 18:00"* — and adds the limiter to the FOH brief. **No requirement produced
   these rows; nothing else in the industry would have surfaced them.**
9. Operator answers the questions in their own room's listing; the answers re-verify those fields in
   16 (16.05.05) — the advance has just harvested fresh venue truth as a byproduct (DT-12).
10. The diff re-runs; the redlines are consulted as a per-date overlay; unknowns fall to 0, shortfalls
    to 0 open. The checklist's hard-outstanding count reaches zero and the advance can freeze
    (18.03.05).
11. At freeze the diff is snapshotted into the pack. It stops re-running. What the band arrives to is
    what the pack said.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Venue not on WeJammin / unclaimed stub in 16 | Diff cannot run. Degrades to a manual checklist, **loudly** — never silently to an empty result that reads like "all clear" | "No venue data — this is a manual advance. Invite the venue?" |
| Venue listing is below `Advance-ready` completeness (16.02.02 D-09) | Diff runs; nearly every row is `unknown`/`not-stated`. The result leads with the coverage failure, not with the matches | "This room has stated 6 of 34 fields we need. 3 of 35 compared." |
| Venue data is stale — listed console sold last year | **The worst outcome: confidently wrong.** Mitigated three ways — a `stale` field demotes `match` → `unknown` on a `show-stopping` requirement (16.05.05 D-03), age is on the row, and 18.18 corrects the record afterwards | "Console: last verified 14 months ago — we've asked the venue to confirm" |
| Venue field crosses its decay threshold mid-advance with no edit | Change-triggered re-runs never fire for this. The **scheduled re-check** catches it and demotes the row | "3 rows changed since your last look — 2 went stale" |
| Equivalence, not identity (Midas M32 vs Behringer X32) | Resolved by the act's strictness flag, not by a similarity model. `"any 32-ch digital desk"` → `match`. `"M32 or nothing"` → `shortfall` + `judgement-required` + candidate substitute | "Console: X32 present, M32 requested — you marked this hard. Accept as an exception?" |
| Rider says "or similar" / "TBC" | Unstructurable intent. `unknown`, basis `undiffable`, `judgement-required` — **never auto-matched** | "'Or similar' can't be diffed — set a strictness rule on the rider, or call it" |
| Requirement has no counterpart field in the venue schema | `unknown`, basis `not-stated`, never `match` | "No data on 3-phase power at this venue" |
| Room explicitly states it has **none** of something | `shortfall`, basis `declared-none` — categorically different from `unstated` (16.02.02 D-04). This is an honest venue being precise, and rendering it as `unknown` would waste their precision | "No house drum kit — stated by the venue" |
| Venue overstates capability to win the booking | Diff passes; failure surfaces on the night. The 18.18 → 16 truth loop corrects the record; repeat divergence is a trust signal, not just a data fix (Q-07) | Handled by the 18.18 → 16 loop; the act sees the corrected field on the next date |
| Act inflates the rider to extract free rentals from venues | The system cannot detect it from one date. The **aggregate redline pattern can** (18.04.01 DT-03): redlined on 7 of 9 dates = an item the act does not need | "You've redlined this on 7 of 9 dates — update the rider?" (via 18.04.04) |
| Act brings its own console/rig | Netted out **before** comparison. The requirement transforms to space + power, not to "solved". Without netting, the diff reports a shortfall the band fixed months ago and the TM learns to ignore it | "You're carrying this — the room needs 1× 32A single-phase and 2.4 m of stage-left wing" |
| Manifest item lacks power draw / footprint (not in 15) | Transformed requirement resolves `unknown`, never `match` | "You're bringing this — we don't know what it needs. Confirm power and footprint." |
| Shared bill: 2 acts, 4 wedges, both diffs say match | **Pool contention** (DT-09). Capability is allocated across the bill, not per act. The second act's requirement is netted against the pool minus prior allocations → `shortfall`, basis `pool-exhausted` | "4 wedges on this stage; the headliner has all 4. You have 0." |
| Festival / promoter-run site with no 16 listing | The supplying party is the promoter, not an Operator with a room record. Diff runs against a **per-event capability declaration** rather than a listing; every row's owner is the promoter (partially resolves 18.03 Q-03 — the remainder is Q-04 here) | "Festival advance — capability stated by the promoter, not a listed room. Nothing here is verified." |
| Support act shares the headliner's backline | Netted from the **headliner's** manifest, not the room. Requires cross-act consent (18.02); without it the shortfall stands | "Headliner has agreed to share the kit — 3 requirements netted" |
| Access requirement (18.04.03) with no disclosure permission | **The diff must not disclose an access need on its owner's behalf.** The row is computed and shown to the person only; it is `undiffable-by-consent` to the Operator and generates no venue-side question until permitted (18.04.03 D-03) | (Person) "This needs the venue to know. Share it with them?" · (Operator) sees nothing |
| Access requirement, disclosure permitted | Diffed against 16.01.04 — which models **audience** accessibility, not performer-side (18.04.03 D-04, domain Q-09). Almost always `unknown`, and that is the honest answer | "Step-free stage access: not stated. 16 doesn't model this yet." |
| Operator edits their spec mid-advance | Re-run fires **with a change notice** — a venue silently changing another party's checklist is the failure CX-01 synthesis #3 names | "[Venue] updated their monitor spec — 2 rows changed, 1 shortfall resolved" |
| Operator edits the spec to make a shortfall disappear | Same notice, plus the edit is a 16.05.03 field-provenance event. Self-attestation by the party with an incentive to flatter — the row's confidence reflects the source, not the timestamp (16.05.05 Q-01) | "Venue changed this field 2 hours after the shortfall was raised — self-confirmed" |
| Multi-hyphenate holds both sides — the drummer who runs the venue | Both inputs are writable by one identity, so the diff is trivially satisfiable by editing the listing, which corrupts 16 for every other act. The identity collision is **recorded on the diff and visible** (DT-14) | "Both sides of this advance are authored by the same account" |
| Multi-hyphenate: the act's FOH engineer is also the house engineer | Collapses a `conditional-on-operator` caveat — "6 mixes with your engineer" is satisfied by the same human twice. Recorded as satisfied-by-identity, not silently assumed | "6 mixes — conditional on your engineer. [Name] is both. Confirmed?" |
| Diff engine fails / venue spec unresolvable | The checklist **degrades to manual and is never blocked** by the diff (CX-01 synthesis #2). A failed diff must never present as a clean diff | "Couldn't read venue data — manual advance. Retry." |
| Partial input: rider is 60% structured (imported PDF) | Two different gaps, counted separately and never merged: *undiffable requirements* ("we can't ask") vs *unstated venue fields* ("they didn't answer") | "35 compared · 10 unknown (venue hasn't said) · 6 uncomparable (your rider isn't structured)" |
| Imported venue tech-pack PDF (16.02.02 D-10) | Ingestion produces **drafts, never values**. A draft never feeds the diff until confirmed per-group by the Operator — ingestion must launder neither age nor authorship | "12 fields read from the venue's tech pack — unconfirmed, not used in matching" |
| Race: re-run fires while an item is being answered | Row identity is stable and non-positional; the in-flight answer survives and reconciles against the new outcome (CX-01 synthesis #5) | "[Operator] is answering this — your re-run kept their answer" |
| Race: re-run completes during the freeze | The freeze snapshots the diff. A result that lands mid-freeze becomes a **change-control proposal against v1**, not a silent pack mutation (CX-04 synthesis #5) | "New shortfall arrived during freeze — approve as a change to v1?" |
| Redline accepted, then the venue's data changes to satisfy the original requirement (they bought the M32) | The redline surfaces as **unnecessary** rather than silently persisting (CX-03 synthesis #5) | "You traded this away — the room now has the M32. Restore the rider position?" |
| Redline accepted, diff re-runs, shortfall re-reported | **Alert-fatigue death.** Accepted redlines are a per-date overlay the diff must consult; a resolved shortfall renders as an agreed exception, never as a red flag (CX-03) | "Console: agreed exception — X32 (accepted 14 Mar by [name])" |
| Same venue, next tour, same redline | The overlay is **per-date**. Next year starts from the rider, not from last year's compromise (CX-03) | "You accepted an X32 here in March. The rider still asks for an M32." |
| Venue-side backline is a read-through from 15.07 (16.02.02 D-06) and the item is out on hire / broken / listed for sale on 13 | Registry availability is part of the capability fact. "Has a drum kit" ≠ "has a drum kit that will be here on the 14th" | "House kit: registered, but on hire until 16 Mar" |
| Requirement is commercial, not technical ("venue supplies 4 crew at their cost") | Not the diff's. Deal terms route to 17 (18.03.03 D-02); the diff reports the *capability* (4 crew exist), never the *cost* | "Crew: 4 available — cost is a deal term, not a capability" |
| Caveated match — `conditional-on-cost` | **A caveated match is not a match** (DT-07). It leaves the collapsed band and generates a checklist item, because a cost is a decision somebody has to make | "Bass rig: available at £40 — that's a match with a price. Accept or bring yours?" |
| Caveated match — `conditional-on-notice` | Same; the notice period is diffed against **time-to-show**, so it can silently expire | "3-phase needs 14 days' notice. Show is in 9 days." |
| Malicious/abusive free-text in a rider note shown to the Operator | Not a diff concern — the note is display-only, never matched (16.02.02 D-05). Abuse routes to 24 | Standard report control on the note |
| Studio date rather than a show | Same engine, room side reads 16.03 instead of 16.02. The requirement set is a session's, not a rider's | Same three-band result |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | Diff running — first run on advance open, or a re-run | "Comparing 35 requirements against [venue]" with the netting step named, because netting is the step users do not expect: "6 netted — you're bringing those" |
| **Empty** | No structured rider exists yet for this act | "Build a rider first — there's nothing to compare." CTA to 18.04.01 (start from template / import PDF). **Distinct** from the venue-side empty case, which reads "No venue data — this is a manual advance. Invite the venue?" — conflating them tells the wrong party to do the wrong work |
| **Populated** | Diff complete, coverage adequate | Three bands. Shortfalls first, ordered by `severity`; unknowns second, grouped by owning side; matches **collapsed to a single line that states its own weakness** — "21 matched — 4 on data older than 12 months, 2 conditional". Constraint notices sit above all three, because nothing generated them and nobody asked for them. Data age on every row |
| **Error** | Diff failed, or the venue spec is unresolvable | "Couldn't read venue data — manual advance." Retry. **The checklist is never blocked by this** (CX-01 synthesis #2) and a failed diff never renders as a clean one — an empty result and a failed result must never look alike |
| **Partial** | Diff ran with gaps on either side | Coverage bar with the two gap classes kept separate: "35 compared · 10 unknown (venue hasn't stated) · 6 uncomparable (your rider isn't structured)". Below `Advance-ready` on the venue side (16.02.02 D-09), the coverage failure leads and the matches are demoted: "This room hasn't stated enough to check" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is the domain's wedge and everything else supports it | Domain rationale + D-18 thesis | ✅ CONFIRMED — and the corollary matters more: the diff is **worthless without domain 16's supply**. This domain's value is entirely gated on venue-side data density. That is a sequencing fact the MoSCoW and the beachhead question (problem-statement Q-03) must absorb: 18 cannot be a beachhead ahead of 16. Recorded as domain D-10. |
| DT-02 | String/spec matching is sufficient | Naive implementation | ❌ REJECTED — the interesting cases are all *equivalence*: an X32 is a fine substitute for an M32 for most acts and unacceptable for some; a 32-channel desk meets a 28-input list. Without an equivalence model the diff is either uselessly strict or dangerously loose. See DT-10, which narrows what that model actually has to be. |
| DT-03 | Unknown can be treated as fine-until-proven-otherwise | Design instinct | ❌ REJECTED — a silent `unknown→match` is how a band arrives to a room with no 3-phase power. `unknown` must be visually and structurally distinct from `match`, and the requirement's strictness (not the diff) decides whether it blocks. The whole point is to eliminate the assumption. Agrees with 16.07 DT-02 independently. |
| DT-04 | Venue data quality is the venue's problem | Boundary lens | ❌ REJECTED — a confidently wrong diff is worse than no diff, because it removes the TM's instinct to check. Data age must be visible, and the post-show correction loop (18.18) is a load-bearing dependency, not a nice-to-have. |
| DT-05 | The requirement↔capability diff is specific to venues | Cross-cut lens | ⚠️ PARTIALLY REJECTED — the *mechanism* (structured requirement set vs structured capability set → match/shortfall/unknown with strictness and equivalence) recurs in casting (04: role needs vs musician skills), services (05: brief vs provider capability), and gear (13/15). Recorded as a cross-cut candidate at medium confidence; the *venue* diff and its field-level semantics stay domain-owned. Reconciled with 16.07 D-04 in DT-13. |
| DT-06 | The diff has two inputs — rider vs room | Naive modelling; the way the industry describes advancing | ❌ **REJECTED — there are three.** The act's manifest (18.09.01) must be netted out *before* comparison, or the diff reports shortfalls the band solved months ago: "no bass rig" against a band carrying two. That is the alert-fatigue death — the TM stops reading a list that is wrong about things they already know. Corroborated independently by 18.04.01's cross-cut note ("what the act brings vs what the rider asks the venue to supply is one decision with two records") and by 18.09.02, whose entire premise is that a shortfall is what the act *isn't* bringing. **Second-order finding**: netting does not delete a requirement, it *transforms* it — your own bass rig still needs stage area and a socket. A diff that deletes the row loses the power requirement, which is precisely the field 16.02.02 D-14 calls "the field that quietly ends shows". |
| DT-07 | A `match` is a binary fact | Naive modelling | ❌ **REJECTED — and this is the most under-modelled row type in the feature.** 16.02.02 D-07 defines six caveats, and D-07's own justification is that *"6 mixes with your engineer, 4 with ours" is the commonest real monitor fact in live music and no existing spec can say it*. A caveated match is a match with a condition, a cost, or a notice period attached — and every one of those is a decision a human must make. Collapsing caveated matches into the collapsed match band reproduces exactly the surprise the feature exists to prevent, and does it while displaying a green tick. **Caveated matches must leave the collapsed band and generate checklist items.** `conditional-on-notice` is the sharpest: a notice period is a silent deadline, so it must be diffed against time-to-show, not just displayed. |
| DT-08 | The diff is requirement-driven, therefore complete | Adversarial lens — "what can this engine structurally never see?" | ❌ **REJECTED. This is the failure mode nobody designs for.** A requirement-driven diff can only report on facts the rider states — so the venue's show-ending constraints produce **zero rows**. No rider says "we will be loud", so a 96dB A-weighted limiter that cuts stage power generates nothing; no rider says "we will finish when we finish", so a 22:30 curfew generates nothing; no rider states truck length, so a street-level load-in through a fire door generates nothing. These are among the most common ways a UK show actually fails, and a perfect diff is blind to all of them. Requires an **inverted second pass**: every constraint field on the room (16.02.03) is surfaced as a **constraint notice** unconditionally, regardless of whether a requirement addresses it. Not computed as a verdict — surfaced as a fact requiring judgement, because the act is the only party who knows whether their show fits inside it. |
| DT-09 | Capability is per-act | Cross-domain lens (18.02 Bill & Support Acts) | ❌ **REJECTED — capability is a pool contended across the night.** Four wedges exist. The headliner's diff says `match`. The support's diff says `match`. There are still four wedges and two acts, and both have a green tick on the same physical objects.
