# Feature: Advance Checklist & Two-Sided Completion Tracking

> **Number**: 18.03.01
> **Parent**: [Show Advancing](./18.03-show-advancing-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The per-date list of everything that must be settled before the show, with each item owned by a
named side and tracked to explicit completion. It replaces the email chain's defining pathology:
nobody knows what is still outstanding, because "outstanding" only exists in the head of whoever
last read the thread.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | For a DIY act, the Musician *is* the advance owner — they see every artist-side item and answer the venue's questions. In a managed act they see only items assigned to them (usually hospitality and their own tech needs) and their own outstanding count. |
| Producer | Full | The primary actor. Opens the advance, owns artist-side items, chases venue-side items, sees both sides' completion state, and decides when the advance is settled. |
| Operator | Full (own side) | Sees the venue-side items assigned to them and the artist's requirements they must answer. Cannot mark artist-side items complete. Sees one advance per date, never the act's other dates. |
| Fan | None | Entirely internal. No fan surface, no derived fan-facing artifact — the outputs (doors, set time) reach fans only through the schedule's public projection in 19/21. |

## Behavior

The checklist is **generated, not authored** — most items come from the capability diff (18.03.02),
the compliance requirement engine (18.16), and the rider's structured sections. Each item has an
owning side, a status (`open / answered / confirmed / waived`), and a thread. Completion requires
confirmation from the *other* side: an item the artist marks done is `answered`, not `confirmed`.

### Happy Path

1. Event enters `advancing`; the checklist self-populates from the diff, rider and requirements.
2. Producer reviews, adds any free-text items the generator couldn't know, and sends the advance.
3. Operator opens their side, answers items, and raises questions on the ones they can't meet.
4. Each answered item is confirmed by the other side; the outstanding count falls.
5. At zero hard-outstanding, the advance can be settled and frozen (18.03.05).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Venue never opens the advance | Escalating nudges; falls back to an emailable advance sheet (18.03.04) | "No response in 6 days — send the sheet by email instead?" |
| Item is disputed, not merely open | Item enters a redline (18.03.03) rather than sitting `open` forever | "Venue can't meet this — negotiate an exception" |
| Advance done off-platform (phone call) | Items can be marked confirmed with a source note; the record still exists | "Confirmed by phone — [name], 14 Mar" |
| Both sides edit the same item simultaneously | Last-write-wins is unacceptable on a contested item; item locks to the editor with the other side notified | "[Operator] is answering this now" |
| Show is 3 days out with 9 items open | Time-to-show drives item severity, not a flat list | Items sorted by risk × time-to-show, with a hard-blocker band |
| Support act items | Scoped per act (18.02); the venue sees all, the acts see their own | Support act sees only their section |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Advance opened | Item list skeleton with side badges |
| Empty | Advancing started, nothing generated (venue not on platform) | "No venue data — build the advance manually" + manual CTA |
| Populated | Items generated | Two-sided list with per-side outstanding counts and time-to-show |
| Error | Diff or requirement generation failed | "Couldn't generate items — add them manually / retry" |
| Partial | One side engaged, other silent | "Your side: 0 open. Venue: 7 unopened." |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The checklist should be authored by the TM | Incumbent behaviour | ❌ REJECTED — an authored checklist is just a to-do list, and every incumbent has one. The value is that WeJammin *knows* what needs advancing because it holds the rider and the venue's spec. Generated-first, manually-extended. |
| DT-02 | An item marked done by one side is done | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — this is the email chain's exact failure: "I told them" ≠ "they know". Two-sided confirmation is the mechanism, and it's the same counter-attestation pattern the Producer persona makes valuable in the credits domain. Self-assertion is weaker evidence than counter-confirmation. |
| DT-03 | The venue will refuse to engage — the wedge dies on Operator adoption | Adversarial lens | ⚠️ CONFIRMED AS RISK — this is the feature's central threat and the reason the emailable fallback (18.03.04) exists. The Operator's own switching trigger (per `personas.md`: a show that failed on a preventable technical mismatch) is the counter-argument, but adoption is not assumed. Escalated to Q-01. |
| DT-04 | Flat lists are fine | Design instinct | ❌ REJECTED — 40 items three days out is not the same as 40 items three weeks out. Severity is risk × time-to-show; a curfew conflict at T-2d and a towel count at T-2d are not peers. |
| DT-05 | Off-platform advancing must be representable | Domain expert lens | ✅ CONFIRMED — advancing happens on the phone constantly, and a system that can't record "confirmed by phone" pushes users back to the thread. Recording *that a fact was settled and by whom* is worth more than forcing the channel. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Items are generated from the diff/rider/requirements first, manually extended second | An authored checklist is a commodity; a generated one requires holding both datasets |
| D-02 | Completion requires counter-confirmation from the other side | "I told them" ≠ "they know" — the email chain's core failure |
| D-03 | Off-platform confirmations are first-class, with a source note | Forcing the channel pushes users back to the thread |
