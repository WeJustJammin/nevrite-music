## Overview

Getting a dozen people and a van of gear between cities and into beds: travel legs, hotels, who
shares with whom, which vehicle, and who is legally allowed to drive it. It is the least glamorous
part of touring and the part that most often makes a tour financially or physically impossible.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three capabilities with a shared dependency and real interactions. All
three consume the touring party from the roster (18.08.01); the rooming list is a function of who's
travelling; and driver hours constrain the routing feasibility that determines whether the travel
legs are even legal. Book a leg, house the people, drive it legally — one chain.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | WeJammin does not book travel — it records it | Booking flights and hotels means being a travel agent: inventory, GDS integrations, cancellation liability, and competing with Booking.com. What the platform owns is that the booking **exists, is linked to a date, and appears on the day sheet**. This is a record, not a marketplace. | Agent Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | The touring party comes from the roster; it is never re-entered | Same head count that drives catering (18.04.02 D-01) drives beds and seats. Re-entering it creates the "catering for 5, party of 9" failure in a second place. Note the local-labour exclusion from 18.08-cx CX-02 applies here too — stagehands don't need beds. | Agent, from 18.08.01 D-04 |
| D-03 | Driver hours are a compliance capability, not a convenience | A tachograph breach is a criminal matter for the driver and the operator, and a crash caused by fatigue is the worst outcome in this entire domain. It also *binds routing feasibility* (18.11.02 D-02), which makes it the most consequential item here. | Agent Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-04 | Rooming is separated from accommodation | Booking four rooms is logistics; deciding who shares with whom is interpersonal, sometimes sensitive, and touches the same person-owned data as the access rider. Different data, different privacy. | Agent classification, 2026-07-16 |
