# Feature: Travel & Accommodation

> **Number**: 18.12.01
> **Parent**: [Travel, Accommodation & Ground](./18.12-travel-logistics-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The record of how the tour physically moves and where it sleeps: flights, ferries, trains, hotel
nights, check-in and check-out, confirmation references. WeJammin does not book any of it — it
records that the booking exists, ties it to a date, and puts it on the day sheet so nobody has to
find a confirmation email at 06:00.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only (own) | Sees their own travel and hotel on the day sheet — lobby call, flight, which hotel. Doesn't see what it cost or other people's arrangements. |
| Producer | Full | Records what they booked elsewhere, links it to dates, and is the person currently holding 40 confirmation emails and a spreadsheet. |
| Operator | **None** | Not their business, and a hotel is not a WeJammin Operator — they're a hotel. This sub-domain has no supply side on the platform. |
| Fan | None | Never visible. Knowing which hotel a band is in is a safety concern, not a feature. |

## Behavior

A travel leg or hotel night is a record with a date, a reference, a cost and a set of people from
the roster. It renders onto the day sheet (18.10) and the tour book (18.11.03), which is where the
value actually lands. Missing bookings render as loud gaps rather than silence.

### Happy Path

1. Producer books flights and hotels wherever they normally do.
2. They record each booking against a tour date — reference, times, cost, who's on it.
3. Party size comes from the roster; the system flags if fewer beds than people.
4. It renders on each day sheet: lobby call, flight, hotel, address.
5. A route change flags every downstream booking that no longer fits.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Nothing booked yet | Loud gap, never a blank (18.10 D-03) | "Hotel: NOT BOOKED — 3 days out" |
| Party grew after booking | Beds vs people mismatch, detected because both are known | "9 people, 4 rooms booked for 6" |
| Route changes; hotel is now in the wrong city | Bookings are date-linked, so a moved date orphans them | "Hotel booked in Leeds — date moved to Bristol" |
| Someone books their own travel | Common for band members meeting a tour; a record with no cost to the tour | "[Name]: own travel — arriving 14:00" |
| Costs sit in a booking email, not here | Double entry (Q-03) or an incomplete budget — pick one | — |
| Split accommodation (band hotel, crew hostel) | Normal and slightly awkward; per-group bookings | "Band: [hotel] · Crew: [hostel]" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | WeJammin should book travel — it's an obvious revenue line | Product ambition | ❌ REJECTED — booking flights and hotels means GDS integration, inventory, cancellation liability, and competing with Booking.com and Expedia on their turf with none of their supply. It is a **different company**, and there is no music-specific edge in it: a band books a Travelodge exactly like everyone else. Same class of rejection as the playback engine (18.06.03 D-01) and route optimisation (18.11.02 D-01) — three places where the ambitious version is someone else's product. |
| DT-02 | So this feature has no value | Follow-on from DT-01 | ⚠️ GENUINELY UNCERTAIN — this is the honest answer, and it's flagged as Q-01 rather than argued away. The *value* is that the day sheet knows the lobby call and the hotel address without anyone digging through email. The *cost* is asking a TM to type what they already have in a confirmation email. Whether that trade is worth it is unresolved, and if it isn't, this node should be cut rather than defended. |
| DT-03 | Party size can be typed here | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — it's in the roster (18.08.01 D-04), and typing it again reproduces the "catering for 5, party of 9" failure in a second place. Note the local-labour exclusion (18.08-cx CX-02) applies: stagehands hired for a night don't need beds, so `count(roster)` is the wrong number here too. |
| DT-04 | Travel is a single record type | Naive modelling | ❌ REJECTED — a flight (fixed, expensive, penalising to change), a hotel night (per-person, per-night), and a ferry with a vehicle (constrained by the van's height) have different shapes and different failure modes. Flattening them loses the constraints that matter. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Record-only. WeJammin does not book travel | GDS, inventory and cancellation liability are a different company with no music-specific edge |
| D-02 | Party size derives from the roster, excluding local labour | Typing it twice reproduces the catering failure |
| D-03 | Flights, hotel nights and vehicle ferries are distinct record types | Different constraints, different failure modes |
