## Overview

Getting a work, a recording and a person **known to the bodies that collect** — PROs/CMOs for performance, mechanical societies for reproduction, neighbouring-rights societies for the performer's share, and sub-publishers for territories the platform cannot reach directly. Money does not arrive because you own something; it arrives because you are registered, in the right territory, with the right identifiers, in a format the society accepted.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: registration is not one act. A party must be affiliated and carry society-issued identifiers (`10.01.01`); a payload must be generated from the splits graph and pass validation (`10.01.02`); it must be transported in the society's format (`10.01.03`); and the acknowledgement or rejection must be reconciled back onto the work (`10.01.04`). These interact continuously — a missing IPI fails validation, a failed validation never reaches transport, and a rejection invalidates the registration state that everything downstream in domain 10 assumes is true.

**The gate that hangs over all of it**: nobody can register works with a PRO unless they are an accredited publisher or administrator, or they act through one. See parent `Q-03`. Every child in this sub-domain is downstream of that answer.
