# Feature: Society Affiliation & Party Identifiers

> **Number**: 10.01.01
> **Parent**: [Society Registration & Delivery](./10.01-society-registration-delivery-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The record of **which collecting bodies a party is known to, and under what number**: PRO/CMO membership (PRS, ASCAP, BMI, GEMA, SACEM…), the IPI/CAE that identifies a writer or publisher across every society on earth, the SoundExchange/PPL performer account, the MLC member ID, and the publisher entities a person controls. Without this a registration payload cannot be built, because a society does not accept "Rob on drums" — it accepts an IPI.

This is the least glamorous feature in the domain and the one that silently blocks everything else. A split captured perfectly at the moment of creation (domain 09) still earns nothing if the writer's IPI is absent or wrong, and the failure is invisible: the registration is simply rejected or, worse, accepted against the wrong party.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Records their PRO membership and IPI/CAE, their SoundExchange or PPL performer account, and any publisher entity they control (most self-released writers are their own publisher and do not know it). The multi-hyphenate problem lands hard here: the same human is a writer at PRS, a performer at PPL, and a publisher entity with a *different* IPI — three numbers, three roles, one person (`personas.md`). |
| Producer | Full | Same as Musician, plus the publisher/administrator side: a producer with points is often paid through an entity, and a producer who runs a label has a label IPI. Their affiliation set is usually the messiest on the platform. |
| Operator | None | A venue or studio does not affiliate to a PRO as a rights-holder. They hold a *licence to play music* (the other side of the same institution), which is a cost of doing business and lives in domain 16, not here. The near-miss is worth naming: "the venue has a PRS number" is true and irrelevant to this feature. |
| Fan | None | No surface. Not simplified, not read-only — absent. |

## Behavior

The feature holds, per party, a set of **affiliations**: (body, territory, role the party holds at that body, identifier, status, effective dates). The role dimension is the part that naive models get wrong — a person is not "a member of PRS"; they are a *writer member* of PRS, possibly also a *publisher member* under a separate entity with a separate IPI, and those two are different parties in every registration payload they appear in together.

Identifiers are **externally issued and externally authoritative**. WeJammin cannot mint an IPI, cannot validate one against the CISAC database without a relationship (parent `Q-03`), and cannot know that a user typed their own number correctly. The honest posture: the platform records what the user asserts, marks it unverified, and lets the first registration acknowledgement (`10.01.04`) be the thing that proves it — because a society accepting a payload naming that IPI is the only confirmation available.

Affiliation is also **exclusive and territorial** in ways that produce real conflicts: you assign performing rights to one PRO, and assigning them to a second for the same territory is not a configuration error but a contractual breach. Members also *resign and switch*, and works registered under the old affiliation do not automatically follow.

### Happy Path

1. Musician adds an affiliation — picks the body, states their role at it (writer / publisher / performer), enters their identifier
2. System records it as **asserted**, and immediately shows what it unblocks: "N of your works can now be registration-validated"
3. The affiliation is used to build the next registration payload (`10.01.02`)
4. The society acknowledges the payload naming that identifier (`10.01.04`)
5. The affiliation flips to **confirmed** — proven by use, not by lookup

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| User does not know their IPI | The overwhelmingly common case. Most working writers have one and have never seen it | Guidance to find it (it is on their PRO's member portal); the platform must not pretend it can look it up |
| User has no PRO membership at all | They are earning nothing from performance and do not know it. This is a **leakage finding**, not an error state | Surfaced as a gap, routed to `10.05.03` |
| Typo'd IPI | Registration is rejected, or — worse — accepted against a real stranger's IPI. Silent misdirection of money | Checksum/format validation catches some; only `10.01.04` catches the rest |
| Two affiliations for the same right + territory | Contractual conflict, not a UI conflict. The platform must refuse to build a payload rather than pick one | Blocked with the conflict named |
| Writer resigns from PRO A, joins PRO B | Works stay registered at A until re-registered. The gap is a classic leakage window | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — re-registration cascade is real and unscoped |
| Same human, writer IPI and publisher IPI | Correct and normal, not a duplicate. Any dedupe that merges them destroys every payload they co-appear in | Both held, distinctly, on one identity |
| Party is a Band entity (`personas.md`, domain 01) | Bands do not hold IPIs; their *members* do. A band can hold a publisher entity | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — see Q-02 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Absent | No affiliation recorded | Empty state naming what it costs: works cannot be registered |
| Asserted | User entered it | Recorded, marked unverified; downstream work unblocked optimistically |
| Confirmed | A society acknowledgement referenced it (`10.01.04`) | Confirmed, with the acknowledgement as the evidence |
| Conflicted | Two affiliations claim one right + territory | Blocked; payload generation refuses |
| Lapsed | Membership resigned/expired | Historical registrations flagged; re-registration prompted |
| Invalid | Rejected by a society as unknown | Named as the rejection reason, not as a generic error |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a profile field and belongs in domain 01 with the rest of identity | The obvious read — it is a number attached to a person | ❌ **REJECTED**, with a caveat. An IPI is not an attribute of a person; it is an attribute of **a party in a role at a body in a territory** — one human routinely holds three, and merging them corrupts every payload. Domain 01 models a human and their organizations; it has no concept of "writer member of PRS in the UK, effective 2019". More decisively: the identifier is only meaningful as an input to registration and only ever confirmed by a registration acknowledgement, both of which live here. **Caveat**: the *display* of these on a profile is domain 01's, and Q-01 keeps the ownership question open for `/create-prd-architecture`. |
| DT-02 | The platform can validate an IPI by looking it up | What any engineer would assume; CISAC maintains the authoritative database | ❌ **REJECTED** — the IPI database is not public. Access requires society/CISAC membership, which is the same accreditation gate as parent `Q-03`. This forces the honest design above: **assert, then let the first acknowledgement confirm**. Worth stating plainly because a spec written on the assumption of lookup would produce a validation step that cannot be built. |
| DT-03 | A missing affiliation is an empty field | Standard data modelling | ✅ **CONFIRMED as the wrong framing, and inverted.** A writer with no PRO affiliation is not missing data — they are **losing money right now** and have been for years. The absence is the single highest-value finding the platform can make about a user, and it belongs in `10.05.03` leakage detection as a positive signal rather than sitting quietly as a blank input. This inverts the feature's job: it is a diagnostic surface wearing a settings form's clothes. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | An affiliation is `(body, territory, role, identifier, status, dates)` — never `(person, society)` | DT-01. One human holds several; the role dimension is what registration payloads consume. |
| D-02 | Identifiers are **asserted, then confirmed by acknowledgement** — never looked up | DT-02. The CISAC database is not reachable without the accreditation this platform may not have. |
| D-03 | Conflicting affiliations **block payload generation** rather than resolving by precedence | A double assignment of one right in one territory is a contractual breach, and picking a winner would commit the user to it silently. |
| D-04 | Absence of affiliation is routed to leakage detection as a **finding**, not held as an empty field | DT-03. |
