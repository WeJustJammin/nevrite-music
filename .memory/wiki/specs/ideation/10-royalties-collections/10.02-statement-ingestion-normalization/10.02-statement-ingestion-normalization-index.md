> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Taking the **statements that arrive** — from PROs, mechanical societies, distributors, labels, DSPs and sub-publishers — and turning a pile of mutually incompatible files into normalized royalty lines attached to works, recordings and people. Every source has its own format, its own period convention, its own currency, its own identifiers and its own idea of what a "line" is; none of them agree, and none of them will change for us.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: ingestion is not parsing. Sources must be connected and their arrivals tracked (`10.02.01`); each format needs its own adapter (`10.02.02`); every line must be matched to something in the catalogue, which is entity resolution against an inconsistent external vocabulary (`10.02.03`); money must be normalized across currencies and periods (`10.02.04`); and the lines that match nothing need somewhere to live where they are visible rather than dropped (`10.02.05`). These interact tightly and destructively — a bad parse produces a plausible bad match, a bad match produces a confident wrong payment, and the exception queue is the only thing standing between the two.

**Why it matters disproportionately**: this sub-domain is the **ungated half of domain 10**. Ingesting a statement the user already receives requires no accreditation, no society relationship and no publisher standing (parent `Q-03`). A user uploads their DistroKid and PRS statements, and the platform tells them — for the first time — what they earned, from what, split how. That is deliverable at launch. Almost nothing else in this domain is.
