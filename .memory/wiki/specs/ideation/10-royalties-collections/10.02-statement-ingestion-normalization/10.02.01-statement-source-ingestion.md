# Feature: Statement Source Connection & Ingestion

> **Number**: 10.02.01
> **Parent**: [Statement Ingestion & Normalization](./10.02-statement-ingestion-normalization-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Getting statements **into the platform at all**: registering which sources a payee entity has income from, receiving each statement (uploaded, forwarded, or fetched where a source permits it), retaining the original file as evidence, and tracking arrival against the cadence that source is supposed to keep. **A statement that never arrives is as much a finding as one that does** — and it is the only finding in this feature that requires no adapter, no catalogue and no counterparty.

This is the front door of the domain's ungated half (parent `D-05`, domain `D-03`). It requires no society relationship, no publisher standing and no accreditation — only a user with a PDF their distributor already emailed them. It is also the one place in domain 10 where the platform can be wrong for free: registering a source that turns out not to exist costs a false expectation, not a wrong payment (`DT-04`).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| **Musician** | **Full** (own payee entities) | Declares their income sources — PRS as a writer, their band's DistroKid, the label that owes them, a sync agent — and feeds statements in by upload or forward. **The value arrives before any parsing does**: `personas.md` describes their money as living in "a spreadsheet" across six platforms, and a list of sources with next-expected dates and arrival status is already more than they have. They configure the reporting relationship (`D-06`), not the parse. They never map a column (`10.02.02` D-03) and never see an adapter. |
| **Producer** | **Full** (own + entities they administer) | Identical capability, different scale and scope. A Producer running a label or administering a band's catalogue ingests statements **about other people's earnings** — which is where `Q-03` stops being theoretical. They are also the party most likely to hold the *paying* side of a statement (`10.03` Role Matrix: Producer configures terms because they are the payer), and the party whose doctored upload would be laundered into the platform's own arithmetic (`DT-14`). Divergence from Musician is **volume and permission scope, not behaviour** (parent Role Matrix finding). |
| **Operator** | **None** | A venue's PRS/PPL relationship is an **outbound licence fee** — the document they receive is an invoice, not a royalty statement, and it is domain 16/23's cost surface. `10.06` gives Operator ⚙️ Config because their licence *generated* live performance money; nothing about that produces an income statement addressed to them. An Operator who also plays uses this feature **as a Musician**, on their own payee entity — the multi-hyphenate, not a second Operator surface (`personas.md`). |
| **Fan** | **None** | No royalty income, no source, no surface. Consistent with the domain-wide finding: Fan is `None` across all ten of domain 10's children — absent, not simplified (`royalties-collections-index.md` Role Matrix). |

## Behavior

The model is a **source registry**: a set of declared expectations about money that should arrive, held against a payee entity, existing independently of any statement having arrived. That independence is the point (`D-02`). Declaring *"this band is with PRS and PRS distributes quarterly"* is what turns a missing distribution from an unobservable non-event into a dated, denominated finding — and missing distributions are `10.05.03`'s highest-confidence leakage input.

Parent `D-03` governs everything below: **an ingested statement is evidence, not truth.** The platform records that a source asserted something, on a date, in a file it retains. It does not adopt the assertion.

### What a source actually is — the triple, not the company

The tempting model is *"the user is with PRS"*. It is wrong on the first real user (`DT-05`). A source is a **(counterparty, payee entity, reporting relationship)** triple:

- One human is a PRS member **as a writer** and, if they self-publish, has a **publisher** relationship with the same society. Two statements, two payment rails, two different arithmetics, one counterparty.
- `personas.md`'s multi-hyphenate is in two bands and solo. That is potentially three DistroKid accounts, three payees, three registry entries — **same company, three sources**.
- A label pays them as an artist *and* they run a label that pays others. Same counterparty class, opposite side of the money.

Registering "DistroKid" once and hanging every statement off it collapses distinct payees into one expectation and one dedupe scope — which is how the band's money silently disappears (`DT-07`, `D-07`).

### What a source record holds

Resolving `10.02.05` Q-04 — the registry owes two fields it did not previously state, plus what the surface pass already had:

| Field | Why it exists | Who supplies it |
|---|---|---|
| Counterparty | Selects the adapter family (CX-05) | User, from a known list or free text (`D-11`) |
| **Payee entity** | The party this source pays. Scopes dedupe, permission and attribution | User; an entity from domain 01 |
| **Remittee** | *Who the money was actually paid to* — the user, an entity they administer, or a third party. **Required by `10.02.05` D-15**; without it the platform's headline claims a third party's money as the user's | Declared at registration; **confirmed per statement** from the parse (`10.02.02` D-19) |
| Reporting relationship | Writer / publisher / performer / artist / licensor — the same counterparty pays differently per role | User |
| Class — **calendared or triggered** | Decides whether the overdue clock runs at all (`D-08`) | Platform, from the counterparty |
| Expected cadence + next-expected | The promise the absence violates | 10.09 for calendared; observed for triggered (`D-09`) |
| **Correction window** | **Required by `10.02.05` D-17** — anchors every ageing alarm in the sub-domain. Undeclared is a **finding**, and the platform's fallback is labelled the platform's (`D-16`) | Source, where published; otherwise a labelled default |
| Expected currency | A **flag** against the file, never an override (CX `R-04`) | User |
| Channel + custody | Upload / forward / fetch — evidentiary weight, not convenience (`D-12`) | Platform, per statement |
| Arrival history | Observed cadence, and the only cadence a triggered source has | Platform |
| Status | Active / suspended / closed / unaffiliated | Both |

### The remittance relationship — who the money was paid to

`10.02.05` DT-06 established that the platform probably never touches this money: PRS pays the writer's bank, DistroKid pays the account holder. The statement is a document *about* a payment that completed elsewhere. So the registry's remittee field is not bookkeeping — it is **the field that decides whether a sentence about money is true**. Four remittance shapes, four different sentences downstream:

1. **The user** — *"you were paid £4,200."* Their money, already in their bank.
2. **An entity they administer** — *"the band was paid £4,200; your share is computed from the split."* Not personally theirs.
3. **A third party** — a label statement paying the label, naming forty artists. *"Someone else was paid; here is what it says about you."* The platform must never denominate this as the user's.
4. **Unknown** — the honest state, and it must be a state (`D-06`), because a default here is a financial claim made by accident.

Declared at registration, **confirmed against the parse per statement** (`10.02.02` D-19 emits the payee party). Where declaration and file disagree, the file wins and the registry entry is flagged — the same rule as currency (CX `R-04`), for the same reason.

### Calendared sources and triggered sources — the clock only runs on one

A society distributes on a **published calendar**: PRS's four annual distributions happen on dates PRS announces, to everyone, whether or not any given member earns anything. A distributor pays on a **trigger**: a balance crosses a withdrawal threshold, or the user requests a payout. These are not two cadences — they are two different kinds of fact, and treating them alike manufactures false alarms at scale (`DT-08`).

| | Calendared | Triggered |
|---|---|---|
| Example | PRO/CMO distributions, mechanical society cycles | Distributor payouts, label accounting on request |
| Expectation source | **10.09's published calendar** — never re-stored here (`D-09`) | Observed arrival history only |
| Silence means | **Overdue** — a finding | **Possibly correct** — a balance below the source's threshold is not a missing statement (`10.02.02` D-07 parses at-source holds; `10.04.03` is the same shape inside the platform) |
| Detector | The clock (`D-02`, domain `D-04`) | Balance-not-paid-out, routed to `10.05.03` — **not** an overdue alarm |

Running the overdue clock on a triggered source produces a monthly alarm on money that was never owed this month. Three of those and the user mutes the channel, which destroys the calendared alarms too — `10.02.05` DT-03's failure delivered by a sibling.

### Three channels, and the thing that separates them is custody

`10.02.02` DT-10/D-20: the parse is identical across channels; the **evidentiary weight is not**. That is the real axis, and it re-orders the channels from how the surface pass ranked them:

| Channel | Witness | What it costs | What it is actually for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Upload** | **None but the user's word** | Nothing; works for every source that exists; depends on no counterparty's cooperation | **The durable channel** (`D-01`) — not a stopgap (`DT-01`) |
| **Forward** | **DKIM on the carrying email** — the file is attributable to the source by signature | An email address and a routing rule | **Custody at zero platform cost** — and an arrival fact even when it carries no file (`D-13`) |
| **Fetch** | The platform's own retrieval | Usually **the user's PRO/distributor credentials** — a standing breach liability (`security-first.md`), broken by MFA, broken silently by a portal redesign | A per-source luxury, never an architectural assumption (`Q-01`) |

`personas.md` names statement-inflation as the Musician's worst intentional behaviour, and the upload channel has nothing to check it against. Custody is what stops a self-uploaded file being laundered into an assertion the platform makes (`10.02.02` Q-05) — and the attack that matters is not inflation (`DT-14`).

### The notification without the file — an arrival is a different fact from a statement

The design consequence nobody plans for: **societies do not email you their statement. They email you that it exists.** *"Your PRS statement is ready — log in to view it."* A forwarded notification therefore carries a DKIM signature and **no statement**, which appears to make forwarding worthless for exactly the sources where custody matters most.

It does the opposite, and this is `D-13`. The notification proves a **distribution occurred, on a date, addressed to this member** — signed by the source. That fact satisfies the cadence clock completely and satisfies the parse not at all. Two facts, separable, and the platform needs the first far more urgently than the second:

- *"PRS distributed on 15 October and you were in it"* — closes the overdue expectation, sets the next, and is **certain**.
- *"Here is what PRS paid you"* — needs the file, an adapter, and `10.02.02`'s gates.

So a source has two arrival states, not one: **notified** and **received** (`D-13`). A source can sit `notified` indefinitely — the money arrived, the platform knows the date, and the file is still on a portal the user has not opened. That is a nag, not a leak, and it must read as one.

### Overdue — three causes, one of which is not the user's

The feature's headline output, and the surface pass under-specified it into uselessness. Silence has three causes with three owners (`DT-09`):

| Cause | Meaning | Owner | Is it leakage? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The source did not distribute** | The society's cycle slipped. Affects every member — a fact about PRS, not about this user | Nobody | **No.** Noise. |
| **The source distributed and paid nothing** | A real, recorded **zero**. *"PRS paid you £0.00"* is a completely different fact from *"PRS didn't distribute"*, and both render as £0 on a careless dashboard (`10.02.02` edge cases) | The catalogue / registration state | **Yes** — `10.05.03`'s highest-confidence input |
| **The source distributed, paid the user, and the file never reached the platform** | The user did not upload it. The modal case | The user | **No.** A nag. |

The platform can separate the first from the other two, and this is the **only legitimate cross-user read in the sub-domain**. If 400 payees registered against the same (counterparty, cycle) and 380 received, PRS is not late — the absence is this user's. If none received, the cycle slipped and no user should be alarmed. The signal leaks nothing: *"PRS distributed on time this cycle"* is a fact about PRS, not about any member — which is precisely why it survives where `10.02.03` D-08 and `10.02.05` DT-10 forbid cross-user hints. Those forbid reading **another user's catalogue**; this reads an **aggregate about a counterparty**. Threshold and quorum are calibration, not principle (`Q-06`).

### Duplicate vs restatement — the decision that cannot be made at intake

Parent `D-04` says the two are distinguished **before anything is calculated**. The surface pass implied that means at arrival, by content hash. It does not, and the correction is load-bearing (`DT-10`):

1. **Byte-identical to a prior ingest of the same (counterparty, payee)** → **certain duplicate**. Rejected at intake, the prior named, no parse required. Cheap and safe.
2. **Not byte-identical** → **undecidable at intake.** Portals regenerate PDFs per download with a timestamp in the file; the same statement downloaded twice is two different files. Hash dedupe reports "new" and the user's income doubles. The decision needs the parsed content — same (counterparty, payee, cycle) and the same line set and the same stated total is a duplicate; the same cycle with a **different** stated total or line set is a **restatement**, and both versions are retained forever (parent `D-04`).
3. **Amount-equality is not available.** `10.02.04`'s pass-through chains report the same money twice, in two currencies, with different amounts and different periods, via a society and its sub-publisher — neither currency, amount, nor period matches, and neither statement is a duplicate of the other. The check must be told this rather than discover it in production (`10.02.04` Cross-Cut Notes).

So dedupe is **two-stage**: certain at intake for byte-identical files; otherwise decided after the parse and **before calculation**. Parent `D-04` is upheld — the boundary was always "before anything is calculated", never "before anything is read".

The stakes justify the care. A duplicate produces no error: it produces a **doubled royalty, calculated confidently, paid, then clawed back** through `10.03.04`'s overpayment path — the nastiest flow in the domain. A restatement misfiled as a duplicate silently discards a correction to money that already moved.

### Coverage — the registry publishes what it does not have

`10.03.02` D-08 requires this feature to declare its own incompleteness: an escalation counter drawing on 3 of 5 registered sources **under-counts, escalates late, underpays — and reconciles perfectly against itself**, so nothing detects it. The registry therefore publishes coverage on every derived figure: *"counted from 3 of 5 registered sources"*.

And the honest second half, which the sibling could not state from where it stands: **5 is not "all". It is what the user declared.** The registry's own completeness is unknowable — the platform knows the sources named, never the sources that exist. Coverage is a lower bound on a lower bound, and it must never be rendered as a percentage of a true total the platform does not have (`D-17`, domain `D-15`).

### The registry proposes itself — discovery

*"What sources do I have?"* is the honest starting state for `personas.md`'s Musician, and a settings form is the wrong shape for a question the user cannot answer (`DT-15`). The platform holds better evidence than the user's memory:

- **Statements name counterparties.** A PRS statement names sub-publishers; a label statement names the distributor underneath it. Every ingested file is a source-discovery instrument for sources not yet registered.
- **The credits graph implies sources.** A credit on a release that is on streaming services implies a distributor exists and someone is being paid — `D-18`'s thesis doing operational work again (`10.02.05` D-12's shape, pointed at the registry).
- **`10.01.01`'s affiliations imply sources.** A PRS membership implies PRS statements.
- **`10.05.01`'s black-box search** finding a society holding money for you is, definitionally, a source you are not registered with.

Every one of these is a **candidate the user confirms**, never a registered fact (domain `D-15`). A source the platform invents produces an expectation the platform invented, and the alarm that follows is a lie with a date on it.

### A source with no affiliation is a finding

Users are wrong about their own income (`DT-13`). *"I'm with PRS"* may mean they are a member — or that their **publisher** is, and they are not, and PRS will never send them anything because it should not. That registry entry produces a permanent false overdue: an alarm that can never be resolved, firing quarterly, forever.

So the registry is reconcilable against `10.01.01`'s affiliations, and the mismatch is a **finding, not an error**: *"you registered PRS as a source; we have no PRS affiliation on record for this entity. Either the affiliation is missing, or your publisher collects and this source should sit against them."* Both readings are useful; the platform does not pick (domain `D-15`). `10.01.07`'s chains are the same shape from the other end — money arrives through a sub-publisher and the user names the wrong end of the chain.

### Multi-party statements — the document is one user's, the money is several people's

Resolving the surface pass's deferral on `Q-03`. The behaviour is specifiable; only the permission is not.

- **Intake accepts it.** A label statement naming forty artists is a normal statement, and the modal one for a Producer administering a label.
- **The whole file is parsed** (`10.02.02` D-13) — filtering at parse makes Σ(lines) ≠ stated total by construction and destroys the domain's only hard oracle, on exactly the statements that need it most. Privacy is enforced downstream on visibility, not upstream on comprehension. `Q-03` has one fewer possible answer than it looks like it has.
- **The remittee decides the sentence.** Lines whose party is neither the user nor an entity they administer are `foreign` in `10.02.05` — evidence about someone else's money, never denominated as the user's (`10.02.05` D-15).
- **The uploader sees what they uploaded.** They already hold the paper; re-showing them their own document discloses nothing new. **The new harm is the platform's re-presentation** (`10.02.05` Q-10) — aggregating a non-user's earnings across the several documents a Producer administers builds a financial profile of a person who has no account, no relationship, and no consent.
- **A named third party is never notified.** Telling a drummer *"a label statement says you earned £40"* is a disclosure of someone else's document, triggered by someone else's upload. If that money is theirs, the route is a claim they initiate — not a notification the platform pushes (`Q-05`).
- **The platform does not aggregate across uploaders.** Two labels' statements naming the same session player must not sum into a profile of them.

What remains open is not the behaviour but the permission: may the platform **store and display** third parties' earnings at all (`Q-03`), and for how long against a six-year financial retention obligation (`Q-02`).

### Retention — the file is the evidence

Originals are retained unchanged, forever, including files the platform cannot read (`D-03`). A statement is evidence: `10.08`'s dispute argues about the original, not the platform's parse of it; an adapter correction (`10.02.02` Q-03) re-parses the file years later; a restatement is only provable against its predecessor. **The parse is an interpretation and interpretations are versioned; the file is the fact.**

This collides with three things at once and the collision has no clean answer (`DT-12`, `Q-02`): a financial-record retention duty measured in years, a GDPR erasure right held by a **non-user named on a label statement** who never agreed to anything, and an evidence requirement that outlives both.

### What the registry will not do

- **It does not hold a calendar.** 10.09 owns published distribution dates; the registry reads them (`D-09`). Two copies of one expectation is the second-source-of-truth failure the platform exists to prevent, and they would disagree within a year.
- **It does not vote on the file's contents.** Expected currency flags a disagreement and never overrides it (CX `R-04`). Same for remittee.
- **It does not gate intake on parseability** (`DT-11`). A source the platform cannot parse still produces overdue findings, and those are worth having on day one.
- **It does not ask the user to fix a parse.** `10.02.02` D-03: adapters are the platform's job, per source, once.
- **It does not decide attribution.** A source narrows right type and territory, but that reaches matching through the **parse**, never by matching reaching back here (CX `R-01`).

### Happy Path

1. Musician adds a source: counterparty **PRS**, payee entity **their band**, relationship **writer**. The platform classes it **calendared** and reads the next distribution date from `10.09` — it does not ask the user for a date they do not know (`D-09`)
2. The platform reconciles the entry against `10.01.01`: a PRS affiliation exists for that entity. No finding. Expectation set: next distribution **15 October**, correction window from the source where published (`D-16`)
3. **15 October** — a DKIM-signed PRS notification is forwarded to the platform address. It carries **no file**. The platform records **`notified`**: PRS distributed, on this date, and this member was in it (`D-13`). The overdue expectation is closed and the next is set — **with no adapter involved**
4. The user downloads the PDF from the PRS portal and uploads it. Custody: **upload — the user's word** (`D-12`)
5. Intake: not byte-identical to any prior ingest for this (counterparty, payee). **Not decidable as a duplicate here** — it proceeds (`D-10`)
6. The original is retained unchanged (`D-03`) and handed to `10.02.02` with its declared source identity, which selects the adapter (CX-05)
7. The parse returns the statement's payee party — **the band**, matching the declared remittee. No flag. The declared currency (GBP) matches the file. The cycle and line set are new: **not a duplicate, not a restatement** (`D-10`). The statement reaches `10.02.02`'s reconciliation gate
8. Arrival is reconciled against the expectation; the source moves to **received**; **next-expected** is set from 10.09's next date
9. Six weeks later the same band's **DistroKid** month passes with no statement. DistroKid is **triggered**, not calendared: the balance is below the payout threshold and no alarm fires, because nothing was owed (`D-08`)
10. The following January **PRS's cycle passes with nothing**. Across the platform, 380 of 400 registered PRS payees received that cycle — **PRS was not late** (`D-14`). The absence is this band's, it is money-denominated, dated, and routed to `10.05.03` as the domain's highest-confidence leakage input (`D-02`, `DT-02`)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|

| Source distributed but paid **£0.00** | A real, recorded zero — **not** silence | *"PRS distributed and paid you nothing"* ≠ *"PRS didn't distribute"*. Different findings, both £0 on a careless dashboard (`10.02.02`). Only this one is leakage |

| Forwarded email is **spoofed** (no valid DKIM) | An unsigned assertion wearing a signed channel's clothes | Custody downgrades to **upload-equivalent** (`D-12`). Forwarding's whole value is the signature; without it, it is an upload with extra steps |
| **Uploaded statement is doctored — inflated** | `personas.md`'s named worst intentional Musician behaviour | Displaying a user their own numbers is safe (parent `D-03`). Publishing a verified income figure into 23's financial identity or `10.08` evidence is a different act — `10.02.02` Q-05 |
| **Uploaded statement is doctored — deflated, by an administrator** | A Producer administering an entity uploads a statement showing less income than arrived. Every payee's split computes smaller, **with the platform's arithmetic behind it** | **The attack that matters** (`DT-14`), and it is the mirror of `personas.md`'s Producer worst-intentional. Custody is the only defence: a fetched or DKIM-witnessed file cannot be edited; an upload can |
| Upload is a **zip bomb, XXE, or formula-injection** payload | An uploaded statement is an untrusted file | → 24 Trust & Safety (`10.02.02`). Intake is a hostile boundary, not a form field |
| **Network failure mid-upload** — truncated file | A truncated CSV **parses**. If the stated total is at the end, the total is gone → `Unoracled` (`10.02.02` D-09) and money is silently lost | Intake verifies **file completeness at receipt**, not by inference from the parse. An incomplete transfer is never an ingest (`D-15`) |
| Truncated file whose total row survives | Fails monetary reconciliation → blocked (`10.02.02` D-02) | The gate catches it. The dangerous truncation is the one that loses the oracle, not the one that fails it |
| **File is a scanned PDF** of a printed statement | Real, and common with older labels and societies — **exactly the back catalogue where `D-16`'s recovery value lives** | **Intake is not blocked** (`D-04`). Retained (`D-03`), `No adapter` (`10.02.02`), platform says so. The cadence clock runs regardless — the registry's value does not depend on the parse (`DT-11`) |
| Source in a **currency the user does not think in** | Normal for foreign income | → `10.02.04`. The registry's expected currency may **flag** a disagreement and never overrides the file (CX `R-04`) |
| Registry says GBP; the file is EUR | The source changed remittance currency, or aggregates foreign income | The **file wins; the registry has no vote** (CX `R-04`). A stale expectation overriding an observed fact is `10.02.04` D-04's error class |
| **Source's correction window is unknown** | `10.02.05` D-17's clock has no anchor and every deadline is the platform's invention | A **finding on the registry entry** (`D-16`), and the fallback is labelled *the platform's deadline, not the source's*. Whether windows are knowable at all: `10.02.05` Q-05 |
| **Counterparty is renamed, merged or acquired** | A distributor is bought; a society restructures. Adapter selection is keyed on source identity (CX-05) | Source identity **survives the rename**; the format change is `10.02.02` D-04's versioning problem. A new entry would orphan the arrival history and reset the only cadence a triggered source has |
| **Source ceases to exist owing money** | A label folds. The entry is overdue forever | **`closed`** — the clock stops; the debt does not. Routed to `10.05.03` / `10.08`. An eternal alarm is a muted alarm |
| User deletes a source with statements ingested | Deleting the expectation deletes the only record that money should have arrived | Sources **close, never delete** (`D-21`). Parent `D-04`'s append-only discipline applied to the registry itself |
| **Producer's admin relationship over an entity ends** | They leave the label. Their access to statements they ingested, about other people's money, does not obviously end with it | Access is scoped to the **payee entity**, so it ends with the relationship — but the **evidence remains** with the entity (`D-03`). Who may read it afterwards is `Q-05` |
| Two administrators upload the same statement **simultaneously** | Both authorised, both acting in good faith | First commit wins; the second sees *"Jamie uploaded this 12 seconds ago"* (`10.02.05`'s shape). The byte-identical check makes the loser a no-op, not an error |
| One statement bundles **three usage vintages** | A PRS distribution routinely carries 2025 UK radio, 2023 live, and 2022 international via a sub-publisher | **There is no statement-level period** (`10.02.02` DT-07) — only a distribution cycle with a name. The registry tracks **cycles**, never periods; period is per-line and `10.02.04`'s (`D-22`) |
| Registry cadence and 10.09's calendar **disagree** | Two copies of one expectation | Cannot happen: the registry **reads** 10.09 and stores no calendar (`D-09`). The disagreement is designed out rather than reconciled |
| **Coverage is 3 of 5 registered sources** | A derived counter under-counts, escalates late, underpays — **and reconciles perfectly against itself** | Coverage declared on every derived figure (`10.03.02` D-08). *"Counted from 3 of 5 registered sources"* — never a bare number |
| Coverage rendered as *"60% of your income"* | **5 is not "all" — it is what the user declared** | Forbidden (`D-17`). A percentage of a total the platform does not have is an invented number (domain `D-15`). Lower bound on a lower bound, stated as such |
| Day-1 user registers ten years of back catalogue sources | Cycles closed years ago; correction windows shut long before signup | Honest and uncomfortable: *"past this source's correction window — we can show you what you earned; nothing here can be corrected"* (`10.02.05`). **Never-attemptable is not attempted-and-failed** |
| **First upload, empty catalogue** | Every line unattributed | **Not this feature's failure and not an exception** — it is onboarding (`10.02.05` DT-13). The registry's own job succeeded the moment the source was declared |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | File received; completeness verified; handed to `10.02.02` | Progress against **the statement**, not the line count. The registry's work is done at receipt; the wait belongs to the parse |
| **Empty** | No sources declared | **The activation moment, not a blank.** Seeded candidates from affiliations, credits and `10.05.01` (`D-18`): *"you're a PRS member — is PRS paying you, or your publisher?"* Never a settings form |
| **Populated** | Sources declared | Per source: payee entity, relationship, remittee, class, next-expected, last-received, coverage. **A list of sources with arrival status is already more than the spreadsheet** (`personas.md`) |
| **Error** | Intake failed — incomplete transfer, hostile file, unreadable container | The file was **not ingested**, and it says so (`D-15`). An incomplete transfer is never a statement. Distinct from `Unreadable`, which is an ingested file the platform cannot parse |
| **Partial** | Some sources registered, others implied and unconfirmed | Coverage stated with its two bounds (`D-17`): counted from 3 of 5 **registered**, and registered is not all |
| Declared | Source registered, nothing received | Expectation set. For calendared: next-expected from 10.09. For triggered: **no clock**, and it says so (`D-08`) |
| **Unaffiliated** | No matching `10.01.01` affiliation | A **finding**, not an error (`D-19`). Both readings shown; the platform does not pick |
| **Notified** | DKIM-signed arrival notice, **no file** | *"PRS distributed on 15 Oct and you were in it."* Clock closed, next set, **no adapter involved** (`D-13`). A nag to fetch the file — not a leak |
| Received | File in, retained, unparsed | Received, original retained unchanged, custody recorded (`D-12`) |
| Parsed | `10.02.02` succeeded and reconciled | Handed onward. Arrival reconciled; next-expected set |
| **Overdue** | Calendared cycle passed, nothing arrived, **and the cross-user signal says the source distributed** | **A finding** (`D-02`, `D-14`) — money-denominated, dated, routed to `10.05.03` |
| **Cycle slipped** | Cycle passed and the source distributed to nobody | **Not a finding.** Suppressed. A fact about the source, not about the user (`D-14`) |
| **Undeterminable** | Cycle passed; too few registrations to tell distribution from absence | *"We can't tell whether this source distributed"* — never a confident verdict (`Q-06`, domain `D-15`) |
| Duplicate | Byte-identical to a prior ingest of the same (counterparty, payee) | Rejected at intake, prior named (`D-10`) |
| Restatement | Same cycle, different content, **decided post-parse** | Both retained (parent `D-04`); prior's lines `superseded`; `10.03.04` engaged |
| Unreadable | Ingested; no adapter, or a scanned image | **Retained** (`D-03`). The file is evidence even when the platform cannot read it; the clock still runs (`DT-11`) |
| **Suspended** | Fetch credentials broke, MFA challenged, or the portal changed | **Loud** (`D-05`). Degraded to upload **explicitly** — a silently stale connection is the failure the registry exists to prevent |
| **Closed** | The source ended, or the user closed it | Clock stops; **the entry and its statements remain** (`D-21`). Any debt routes to `10.05.03` / `10.08` |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The platform should connect to sources via API — manual upload is a stopgap that a serious product would outgrow | Standard product instinct; every fintech aggregator works this way | ❌ **REJECTED as the primary channel.** Almost no PRO or distributor offers a statement API. Where a "connection" exists it is usually credential-based scraping of a member portal, which means the platform holds a user's PRO login — a standing breach liability (`security-first.md`) that MFA breaks anyway. **Upload is not the stopgap; it is the durable channel**, because it is the only one that works for every source and depends on nobody's cooperation. The design consequence matters: build for the file, treat fetch as a per-source luxury, and never let the architecture assume a connection exists. |
| DT-02 | The feature's job is to receive statements | The name of the feature | ❌ **REJECTED as half the job — and the less valuable half.** The **source registry is the feature**, and its highest-value output is produced when nothing arrives. A declared source with a known cadence turns silence into an overdue event; without the registry a missing quarterly distribution is literally unobservable, because there was never a promise of a specific amount on a specific date. This is `10.01.04` DT-02's pattern in a second location: the domain's characteristic failure is an absence that produces no event, and the fix is always to declare an expectation first so the absence has something to violate. |
| DT-03 | Duplicate detection is a hygiene concern | Standard import handling | ✅ **CONFIRMED as necessary, but re-scoped as a money-safety control.** A duplicated statement does not produce a visible error — it produces a **doubled royalty, calculated confidently, paid out, and then clawed back** via `10.03.04`'s overpayment path, which is the nastiest flow in the domain. Meanwhile a *restatement* looks almost identical (same source, same period, different numbers) and must NOT be deduplicated. The two cases are one keystroke apart and have opposite handling. This is not hygiene; it is the boundary between correct money and a clawback. |
| DT-04 | The registry should be strict about what it accepts — a source it cannot verify is a source it should refuse | The consistent reading of `10.02.03` D-01 ("money never moves on a probabilistic match") applied to this end of the pipeline | ❌ **REJECTED — the error budget inverts here, and the inversion is structural.** This feature sits at the opposite end of the same pipeline from `10.02.03` and faces the opposite asymmetry. At attribution, a false positive **moves money to the wrong person** — so the platform fears false positives and refuses to guess. At intake, a false positive costs **a false expectation**: an alarm nobody can resolve. A false *negative* — a source never registered — is money that can never be noticed missing, **forever, with no event and no trace**. That is the domain's defining failure (`D-04`), and it is unrecoverable rather than merely wrong. So: **permissive on entry, strict on inference.** Accept any source the user names, including ones the platform has never heard of and cannot parse; then let nothing unverified produce a confident claim. `10.05.01` D-02 has the same inversion for the same reason — where the platform controls no money, fear the false negative. |
| DT-05 | A source is a company — the user is "with PRS" and "with DistroKid" | The obvious model, the shape of the words, and how every account-linking UI in existence works | ❌ **REJECTED, and it breaks on the first real user rather than at scale.** A source is a **(counterparty, payee entity, reporting relationship)** triple. One human is a PRS member as a **writer** and, self-publishing, holds a **publisher** relationship with the same society — two statements, two rails, two arithmetics, one counterparty. `personas.md`'s multi-hyphenate in two bands plus solo has three DistroKid payees. A Producer is paid **by** labels and pays **through** their own. Collapsing to the company merges distinct expectations into one clock, and — the expensive part — merges distinct **dedupe scopes**: the band's statement and the user's own for the same cycle become duplicates of each other, and one is rejected. Nothing errors; the band's money simply never appears. **This is the single cheapest place in the domain to make the multi-hyphenate structurally unrepresentable**, and `personas.md` warns in its first paragraph that a "user has a role" model is wrong for this product. |
| DT-06 | The registry describes the user's income | The natural reading of "source registry", and the surface pass's implicit frame | ❌ **REJECTED — it describes documents about money that was mostly paid to someone else's bank account, and `10.02.05` DT-06 already found the cost of getting this wrong.** The platform probably never touches this money (domain `Q-07`): PRS pays the writer's bank, DistroKid pays the account holder, a label pays the label. So *"£4,200 of your money"* is a **sometimes-false financial claim** made by a hardcoded sentence. The fix is a registry field the surface pass did not have — the **remittee** — and four derived sentences rather than one (`D-06`). This resolves `10.02.05` Q-04's first half from the side that owns the data, and it is worth noting *how* it was found: a sibling reasoning about its headline discovered a missing field in **this** feature's model. The sub-domain's features are not independently specifiable, which is CX-05's point arriving as evidence. |
| DT-07 | The registry hangs off the user | Every account model, and the obvious owner of "my sources" | ❌ **REJECTED — it hangs off the payee entity, and the difference is money.** `personas.md`: bands, labels and studios are **first-class entities** that get paid independently of their members. The band's DistroKid pays the band. So: whose registry entry is it when two members both register it? Whose statement is it when the drummer uploads what the bassist already uploaded? Scope the registry to the **uploading user** and the second upload is a duplicate — **rejected, silently, correctly by its own logic, and the band's money is gone**. Scope it to the **payee entity** and the second uploader simply sees the first's entry. The same choice fixes permission (a Producer's access ends with the administration relationship, not with the file they happened to touch) and dedupe (`D-07`) in one move. The failure is invisible: nothing errors, no line is unmatched, no exception queues. The money is never ingested, so `10.02.05` never sees it either. |
| DT-08 | Every source has a cadence; declare it and the clock does the rest | `D-02` taken at face value, and the surface pass's whole model | ❌ **REJECTED for half of all sources, and the half it breaks is the one users check daily.** A society **distributes on a published calendar** — PRS's cycles happen on announced dates whether or not any member earns. A distributor **pays on a trigger** — a balance crosses a threshold, or the user requests a payout. For a triggered source, **silence is frequently correct**: nothing was owed this month because the balance sat below the threshold, a fact the source itself prints on the statement (`10.02.02` D-07 parses at-source holds; `10.04.03` is the identical shape inside the platform). Run the overdue clock on it and the platform alarms monthly about money that was never owed. Three false alarms and the user mutes the channel — **which kills the calendared alarms too**, and those are the ones that were worth having. `10.02.05` DT-03's failure, delivered by a sibling, against the one output this feature exists to produce. Class the source; run the clock only where an expectation is genuinely knowable (`D-08`). |
| DT-09 | An overdue source means money is missing | The whole premise of `DT-02`, and true often enough to be dangerous | ❌ **REJECTED — silence has three causes, three owners, and only one is leakage.** (a) The source did not distribute: its cycle slipped, which affects every member and is a fact about PRS, not about this user. (b) The source distributed and paid **nothing**: a real, recorded zero — the finding, and `10.05.03`'s highest-confidence input. (c) The source distributed, paid the user, and **the file never reached the platform**: the modal case, and a nag. Flattening them makes the alarm untrustworthy, and an untrustworthy alarm is muted, and a muted alarm makes `DT-02`'s entire argument for the registry worthless. Note what this exposes: **the platform cannot distinguish (b) from (c) alone** — both look like silence from inside one user's account. It needs either the notification (`D-13`) or the file. That is a limit on the feature's own headline claim, and it should be stated rather than discovered. |
| DT-10 | Duplicate detection is content hashing at intake | The surface pass's own "(source, period, content)", and correct-looking | ❌ **REJECTED — the modal duplicate is not byte-identical, and hashing reports it as new.** Portals regenerate PDFs per download with a generation timestamp inside the file; the same statement downloaded twice yields two hashes, and *"same period, different content"* is the platform's definition of a **restatement** (parent `D-04`) — so the naive check does not merely miss the duplicate, it **actively misclassifies it as a correction**, opening a `10.03.04` overpayment against money that was never doubled. `10.02.04` closes the obvious fallback: amount-equality cannot work either, because a pass-through chain reports the same money twice in two currencies with different amounts and periods, and **neither statement is a duplicate**. So the decision requires the parsed content — cycle, line set, stated total — and dedupe is **two-stage**: certain at intake for byte-identical files, decided post-parse otherwise, always **before calculation**. Parent `D-04` is upheld and sharpened: the boundary was always "before anything is calculated", never "before anything is read". |
| DT-11 | Registering a source the platform cannot parse is pointless | The reasonable read of a pipeline whose next step is an adapter | ❌ **REJECTED, and this is what makes the feature shippable ahead of its own sub-domain.** The registry's highest-value output — **overdue** — requires no adapter, no catalogue, no identifiers and no counterparty cooperation. *"Your label has paid you annually for six years and this year has not"* is a complete, dated, actionable finding produced from a declared expectation and a silence. `10.02.02` Q-01 says adapter coverage is a long tail and a labour problem; if intake gated on parseability, the registry's value would be **bounded by the slowest, most expensive part of the sub-domain**, and the eleventh source — somebody's entire livelihood — would produce nothing at all. Decoupled, a source the platform will never parse still earns its registry entry. This also resolves the surface pass's scanned-PDF deferral outright: intake is not blocked, the file is retained as evidence (`D-03`), the platform says it cannot read it, and the clock runs anyway. |
| DT-12 | Retention is a storage and cost question | It looks like one: keep the files, pay for the bytes | ❌ **REJECTED — it is a three-way conflict of laws with no clean answer, and the third party is the part nobody designs for.** A statement is (1) a **financial record** with a multi-year retention duty, (2) **evidence** `10.08` may need after that duty expires and `10.02.02` Q-03 may re-parse years later, and (3) **personal data about people who are not users** — a label statement names forty artists, none of whom agreed to anything, one of whom may demand erasure. The platform is holding a document about a person with no account, no relationship, and no way to know it exists. Every resolution is uncomfortable: erase and the platform destroys evidence the uploader is legally required to keep; retain and it refuses a statutory right on behalf of a party it never contracted with. `D-03` decides only the **evidential** question (the file is never altered); it cannot decide this one, and pretending otherwise would smuggle a legal position in as a storage default. |
| DT-13 | A source the user declares is a source that exists | The registry is user-asserted, and users know their own income | ❌ **REJECTED — users are systematically wrong about their own income in one specific direction.** *"I'm with PRS"* frequently means their **publisher** is with PRS and they are not — so PRS will never send them a statement, because it should not. That entry produces a **permanent false overdue**: quarterly, forever, unresolvable by any action the user can take, and `DT-08`'s muting failure arrives by a second route. `10.01.07`'s sub-publishing chains are the same error from the other end — money arrives through a chain and the user names the wrong link. The registry must therefore be reconcilable against `10.01.01`'s affiliations, and the mismatch is a **finding, not a validation error**: two readings (the affiliation is missing / the publisher collects), both useful, and the platform does not pick (domain `D-15`). Note the shape — `DT-04` says accept everything; this says **infer nothing from acceptance**. The two are the same rule stated from opposite ends. |
| DT-14 | The upload channel's risk is a user inflating their own statement | `personas.md` names statement-inflation as the Musician's worst intentional behaviour, so it is the obvious threat | ❌ **REJECTED as the threat that matters — the dangerous edit is downward, and it is the Producer's.** Inflation harms the inflater's own claim: it corrupts what 23 asserts about them and what `10.08` will accept, which is `10.02.02` Q-05's question and is bounded by parent `D-03` (evidence, never truth) for display. **Deflation is an attack on a third party.** A Producer administering a label uploads a statement showing less income than actually arrived; `10.02.02` parses it, it reconciles perfectly against its own doctored total (`10.02.02` D-08: the oracle proves completeness, not truthfulness), `10.03.01` computes every payee's share from it, and the drummer is underpaid **with the platform's own arithmetic and explainability behind it** (domain `D-08`). The platform does not merely fail to catch it — **it launders it**, and the derivation WeJammin sells as its differentiator becomes the instrument. This is `personas.md`'s Producer worst-intentional ("assigns themselves a larger split and pushes it through") achieved without touching a split at all. Custody is the only structural defence: a fetched file has the platform's retrieval as witness and a DKIM-forwarded one is signed — **neither can be edited before ingest; an upload can** (`D-12`). It also gives `10.02.02` Q-05 a sharper form: the question is not only what the platform may assert *about* the uploader, but what it may compute *for other people* from a file only the uploader has seen. |
| DT-15 | The registry is a settings form the user fills in at onboarding | It is a list of things the user knows about themselves | ❌ **REJECTED — the modal user cannot answer the question, and the platform has better evidence than their memory.** *"What sources do I have?"* is precisely the state `personas.md` describes (six platforms, a spreadsheet, nothing connected); a blank form asks the user to already have the artifact the product exists to build. But four discovery signals sit in the building: **ingested statements name counterparties** (a PRS statement names sub-publishers; a label statement names the distributor beneath it), **`10.01.01`'s affiliations imply sources**, **`10.05.01`'s black-box hits are by definition sources you are not registered with**, and **the credits graph implies a distributor** for any release that is out. The last is `D-18`'s thesis doing operational work — the platform was in the room, so it knows the work exists, so it knows money exists — and it is the same move `10.02.05` D-12 makes to resolve its queue. The constraint is absolute: **candidates the user confirms, never registered facts** (domain `D-15`). A source the platform invents produces an expectation the platform invented, and the overdue alarm that follows is a fabrication with a date on it. |
| DT-16 | An arrival is a statement — the file is the event | The word "ingestion", and the only shape the surface pass modelled | ❌ **REJECTED, and the correction hands the feature its cheapest certainty.** **Societies do not email statements; they email that a statement exists** — *"your PRS statement is ready, log in"*. A forwarded notification therefore carries a DKIM signature and **no file**, which looks like it destroys forwarding's value for exactly the sources where custody matters most. The opposite: the notification proves a **distribution occurred, on a date, addressed to this member**, signed by the source — which **completely satisfies the cadence clock and not at all the parse**. That is the split the feature needed, because `DT-02` says the clock is where the value is and `DT-11` says the parse is the slow, expensive part. So `notified` is a first-class state (`D-13`): the highest-certainty, lowest-cost, zero-adapter arrival signal available, obtained from an email the user forwards in two seconds. It also correctly reframes the resulting gap: a source sitting `notified` is **a nag, not a leak** — the money arrived and nobody is withholding it, which is a completely different sentence from overdue, and `DT-09` says conflating those sentences is how the alarm dies. |
| DT-17 | An overdue alarm is a fact about this user's account | Every alarm in every product, and how `D-02` reads at first | ❌ **REJECTED — the platform holds a signal no individual user can have, and it is the only cross-user read in this sub-domain that survives.** From inside one account, *"PRS was late"* and *"PRS skipped me"* are indistinguishable, and `DT-09` says alarming on the wrong one poisons the channel. Across accounts they are trivially separable: if 380 of 400 registered PRS payees received the cycle, **PRS was not late** and the absence is this user's. If none received, the cycle slipped and **nobody should be alarmed**. The signal is legitimate precisely where `10.02.03` D-08 and `10.02.05` DT-10 draw their line: those forbid reading **another user's catalogue** into this user's decision — an assertion about someone's private data. This reads an **aggregate about a counterparty** — *"PRS distributed on time"* is a fact about PRS, discloses nothing about any member, and is the sort of thing PRS itself announces. The platform's multi-tenancy is, for once, an epistemic advantage rather than a liability; it is what lets the registry tell noise from a finding. Below quorum it degrades to *"we can't tell"* (`Q-06`), never to a guess. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **10.02.02 Parsing** ([10.02.02-statement-parsing-format-adapters.md](./10.02.02-statement-parsing-format-adapters.md)) — the direct consumer (CX-05). This feature supplies the file, the **source identity that selects the adapter**, and the **custody** of the input (`10.02.02` D-20, `D-12` here). Two dependencies run the other way and are **new commitments this pass makes**: dedupe cannot complete without the parse (`D-10`), so `10.02.02` runs on files whose duplicate status is undecided; and `10.02.02` D-19's parsed payee party is what **confirms or contradicts** the registry's declared remittee (`D-06`). A registered currency expectation may *flag* a disagreement with the file and never overrides it (CX `R-04`). `10.02.02` D-13 (the whole file is parsed) is what makes multi-party intake specifiable at all.
- Touches **10.02.05 Exception Queue** ([10.02.05-unmatched-line-exception-queue.md](./10.02.05-unmatched-line-exception-queue.md)) — **`Q-04` there is resolved here** (`D-06`, `D-16`): the registry now holds the **remittee** (four derived sentences, not one hardcoded claim — `10.02.05` D-15) and the source's **correction window** (the anchor for `10.02.05` D-17's ladder; undeclared is a finding, and the fallback is labelled the platform's). Also **shared principle, not shared entity** (CX-04): both features manufacture events from absences (domain `D-04`). And `D-10`'s duplicate-vs-restatement decision must land **before the queue counts anything**, or a duplicate upload doubles the Open total overnight.
- Touches **10.02.04 FX & Period** ([10.02.04-currency-fx-period-normalization.md](./10.02.04-currency-fx-period-normalization.md)) — **a detection constraint imposed on this feature** (`10.02.04` Cross-Cut Notes): pass-through chains report one collection twice, in two currencies, with different amounts and different periods, so **amount-equality cannot detect a duplicate**, and neither statement is one. `D-10` is told this rather than discovering it. CX `R-04` stands otherwise: the file's currency wins; the registry has no vote. `10.02.02` DT-07's multi-vintage distributions are why the registry tracks **cycles, never periods** (`D-22`) — period is per-line and `10.02.04`'s.
- Touches **10.02.03 Line Matching** ([10.02.03-line-matching-catalogue-resolution.md](./10.02.03-line-matching-catalogue-resolution.md)) — **a deliberately rejected direct edge** (CX `R-01`), restated because `D-18`'s discovery flows will tempt it: source knowledge reaches matching **through the parse**, never by matching reaching back here. The live relationship is the **inverted error budget** (`DT-04`): matching fears false positives because a wrong match moves money; intake fears false negatives because an unregistered source is money nobody can ever notice missing. Same pipeline, opposite ends, opposite bias — and both are correct.
- Touches **10.09 Distribution Calendar & Money-in-Flight** ([../10.09-distribution-calendar-money-in-flight.md](../10.09-distribution-calendar-money-in-flight.md)) — **hard dependency, and a boundary sharpened this pass**: 10.09 owns the published cycle dates; the registry **reads them and stores no calendar** (`D-09`). The surface pass's *"the two expectations must not disagree"* (`10.09` Cross-Cut Notes) is designed out rather than reconciled — two copies of one expectation is the second-source-of-truth failure the platform exists to prevent. **New**: 10.09's calendar only exists for **calendared** sources; triggered sources have no calendar to read and no clock to run (`D-08`), which is a distinction 10.09's own model does not currently make.
- Touches **10.05.03 Leakage Detection** ([../10.05-recovery-leakage/10.05.03-royalty-leakage-detection.md](../10.05-recovery-leakage/10.05.03-royalty-leakage-detection.md)) — **the consumer of this feature's headline output**, and `DT-09` narrows what it may consume: of three causes of silence, only *"the source distributed and paid nothing"* is leakage. A slipped cycle is noise (`D-14`) and an un-uploaded file is a nag. Feeding all three through would poison the domain's highest-confidence detector with the domain's noisiest signal. **At-source held balances** on triggered sources route here too (`D-08`), not to the overdue clock.
- Touches **10.03.02 Deal Terms → Royalty Rate Application** ([../10.03-calculation-recoupment/10.03.02-deal-terms-rate-application.md](../10.03-calculation-recoupment/10.03.02-deal-terms-rate-application.md)) — **trigger dependency discovered there, honoured here** (`10.03.02` D-08, DT-06): the registry's coverage **bounds the escalation counter**, and a user with 3 of 5 sources registered escalates late and is **underpaid silently, reconciling perfectly against itself**. `D-17` supplies the coverage declaration and adds the half the sibling could not see: **the denominator is what the user declared, not what exists**, so coverage is never a percentage of a true total.
- Touches **10.03.04 Restatement, Adjustment & Overpayment** ([../10.03-calculation-recoupment/10.03.04-restatement-adjustment-overpayment.md](../10.03-calculation-recoupment/10.03.04-restatement-adjustment-overpayment.md)) — **the cost centre for `D-10`'s errors**. A duplicate mistaken for a restatement opens an overpayment that never existed; a restatement mistaken for a duplicate silently discards a correction to money that already moved. Restatement vs duplicate is decided here and paid for there.
- Touches **10.01.01 Affiliation** ([../10.01-society-registration-delivery/10.01.01-society-affiliation-party-identifiers.md](../10.01-society-registration-delivery/10.01.01-society-affiliation-party-identifiers.md)) and **10.01.07 Sub-Publishing** ([../10.01-society-registration-delivery/10.01.07-sub-publishing-territory-administration.md](../10.01-society-registration-delivery/10.01.07-sub-publishing-territory-administration.md)) — **new edge** (`DT-13`, `D-19`): a registered source with no corresponding affiliation is a finding, because *"I'm with PRS"* often means the user's publisher is and they are not. Note the parent CX's boundary holds: `10.01.04` owns registration **belief**, this feature owns arrival **fact**, and neither may infer the other (`royalties-collections-cx.md` CX-01 Q1) — the reconciliation surfaces a contradiction, it never resolves one.
- Touches **10.08 Statement Disputes & Audit Rights** ([../10.08-statement-disputes-audit-rights.md](../10.08-statement-disputes-audit-rights.md)) — **the reason `D-03` is absolute**: a dispute argues about the original file, not the platform's parse of it. `personas.md` names the Musician's switching trigger as losing money to a fact they could not prove; the retained original is that proof. `DT-14`'s deflated upload is the case where the evidence and the uploader are the same party.
- Touches **09 Rights & Ownership** ([../../09-rights-ownership/](../../09-rights-ownership/)) — **the payee entity is 09/01's, not this feature's** (`D-07`). The registry hangs off it, scopes dedupe and permission by it, and invents none of it. An administration relationship ending is 09/01's event with a consequence here (`Q-05`).
- Touches **01 Identity, Profiles & Organizations** ([../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/](../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/)) — bands, labels and studios are **first-class entities** (`personas.md`), which is the whole basis of `DT-07`. A registry scoped to the human rather than the entity makes the multi-hyphenate's band income structurally unrepresentable.
- Touches **02 Credits & Attribution** ([../../02-credits-attribution/](../../02-credits-attribution/)) — **`D-18`'s discovery flow reads the credits graph to propose sources** (`DT-15`): a credit on a released work implies a distributor and implies money. `D-18`'s thesis doing operational work — the platform was in the room, so it knows the work exists. Candidates the user confirms, never facts (domain `D-15`).
- Touches **10.05.01 Black Box Search** ([../10.05-recovery-leakage/10.05.01-black-box-unclaimed-search.md](../10.05-recovery-leakage/10.05.01-black-box-unclaimed-search.md)) — **a discovery input** (`DT-15`): a society holding unclaimed money for you is, by definition, a source you are not registered with. And a warning about this feature's own failure shape — a registry whose expectations nobody checks is the platform accumulating its own silence (domain `D-09`).
- Touches **23 Career, Finance & Business** ([../../23-career-finance-business/](../../23-career-finance-business/)) — **the boundary a domain expert would ask about first, and nobody names**: the **statement is not the payment**. A statement saying £4,200 and a bank credit of £3,890 is routine and unexplained — netting across statements, at-source deductions, the bank's own FX. The registry records what the statement **states** as remitted (a parsed fact, `10.02.02` D-07); reconciling that against money that actually landed is **23's**, and today **nobody does it, which is where money quietly goes** (`Q-07`). 23's income aggregation also consumes this registry and inherits its coverage bound (`D-17`) and its custody question (`10.02.02` Q-05).
- Touches **24 Trust, Safety & Disputes** ([../../24-trust-safety-disputes/](../../24-trust-safety-disputes/)) — intake is a **hostile boundary**: an uploaded statement is an untrusted file (zip bombs, XXE, formula injection) and, per `DT-14`, a forgeable assertion whose forgery underpays a third party.
- Touches **16 Venues, Studios & Spaces** ([../../16-venues-studios-spaces/](../../16-venues-studios-spaces/)) — **an explicit non-edge** (Role Lens): an Operator's PRS/PPL document is an **invoice**, a cost, and 16/23's. It is not a royalty statement and does not belong in this registry. `10.06` is the only place in domain 10 where an Operator has standing, and it is about the licence that generated the money, not a statement addressed to them.
- Rides **Media Handling** (`CX-M10`) — retention of large original files, unchanged, indefinitely, including ones the platform cannot read.
- Rides **Privacy, Consent & Data Portability** (`CX-M18`) — `DT-12`'s three-way collision: financial retention, `10.08` evidence, and erasure requested by a **non-user named on someone else's label statement**.
- Rides **Notifications & Alerts** (`CX-M07`) — overdue delivery is platform machinery; **money-denominated framing, the notified/overdue/slipped distinction (`DT-09`, `DT-16`), and the refusal to alarm triggered sources (`DT-08`)** are domain-owned. This feature's alarms are the ones most likely to be muted, and a muted alarm makes `DT-02`'s whole argument worthless.
- Rides **Canonical Data, Taxonomy & Entity Resolution** (`CX-M17`) — counterparty identity must survive renames, mergers and acquisitions (`D-23`), because adapter selection is keyed on it (CX-05) and a new entry orphans the arrival history.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Upload is the primary channel**; fetch is a per-source luxury | `DT-01`. No API exists for most sources, and credential-holding is a liability the platform should not seek. The architecture never assumes a connection exists. |
| D-02 | The **source registry exists independently of any statement** | `DT-02`. Only a declared expectation makes a missing distribution observable. Domain `D-04`'s third independent arrival. |
| D-03 | Originals are **retained unchanged**, even when unparseable | A statement is evidence (parent `D-03`). `10.08` argues about the file; `10.02.02` Q-03 may re-parse it years later; the platform's parse is an interpretation, not a replacement. |
| D-04 | Duplicate ≠ restatement, and the two are distinguished **before anything is calculated** | `DT-03`. One is a clawback, the other a correction; they look identical on arrival. *Sharpened by `D-10`*: "before calculated", never "before read". |
| D-05 | A broken fetch connection **fails loudly** | `DT-01`. A silently stale connection reproduces exactly the failure the registry exists to prevent — an expectation nobody is checking. A fetch returning a known or cached file closes no expectation. |
| D-06 | The registry holds the **remittee**, and the money sentence is **derived** from it — four shapes: the user, an administered entity, a third party, **unknown** | `DT-06`; resolves `10.02.05` Q-04a and supplies `10.02.05` D-15. The platform probably holds none of this money (domain `Q-07`), so one hardcoded sentence is a financial claim that is sometimes false. Declared at registration, confirmed per statement against `10.02.02` D-19; the file wins on disagreement. `unknown` is a state, not a default. |
| D-07 | The unit is the **(counterparty, payee entity, reporting relationship) triple**, and the registry hangs off the **payee entity** — never the uploading user | `DT-05`, `DT-07`. Fixes dedupe scope (the band's statement is not the drummer's duplicate), permission scope (access ends with the administration relationship), and expectation scope (writer and publisher are two promises) in one move. Scoped to the user, the band's money is silently never ingested. |
| D-08 | Sources are **calendared or triggered**, and the overdue clock runs **only on calendared** | `DT-08`. Silence from a triggered source is frequently correct — a balance below the source's payout threshold. Alarming on it mutes the channel, which kills the calendared alarms that were the point. Triggered silence routes to `10.05.03` as a balance-not-paid-out signal, never as overdue. |
| D-09 | The registry **reads 10.09's calendar and stores none of its own** | Two copies of one expectation is the second-source-of-truth failure the platform exists to prevent, and they disagree within a year. Triggered sources have no calendar to read; their only cadence is observed. |
| D-10 | Dedupe is **two-stage**: byte-identical to a prior ingest of the same (counterparty, payee) is a certain duplicate at intake; anything else is **decided on parsed content, before calculation** | `DT-10`. Portals regenerate PDFs per download, so hash dedupe reports a duplicate as *new* — and *"same period, different content"* is the platform's definition of a restatement, so the naive check misclassifies rather than merely missing. Amount-equality is unavailable (`10.02.04`). Parent `D-04` upheld. |
| D-11 | Sources may be registered from a **known list or as free text** | `DT-04`, `DT-11`. A source the platform has never heard of is somebody's entire livelihood, and refusing it makes its absence unobservable forever. |
| D-12 | Every statement carries the **custody of its channel** — upload (the user's word) / forward (DKIM-witnessed) / fetch (the platform's retrieval) | `10.02.02` D-20, `DT-14`. The parse is identical; the evidentiary weight is not. Custody is the only structural defence against a doctored upload, because a witnessed file cannot be edited before ingest. A forward without valid DKIM downgrades to upload-equivalent. |
| D-13 | **`notified` is a first-class arrival state**: a signed notification with **no file** closes the cadence expectation and feeds no parse | `DT-16`. Societies email that a statement exists, not the statement. The notification proves a distribution occurred, on a date, to this member — the highest-certainty, zero-adapter signal available, and it is exactly what the clock (`D-02`) needs. A source sitting `notified` is **a nag, not a leak**. |
| D-14 | Overdue is decided with a **cross-user distribution signal** about the counterparty | `DT-09`, `DT-17`. From one account, *"PRS was late"* and *"PRS skipped me"* are indistinguishable; across accounts they are trivially separable. Legitimate where `10.02.03` D-08 and `10.02.05` DT-10 forbid cross-user reads, because it is an aggregate **about a counterparty**, not an assertion about anyone's catalogue. Below quorum: *"we can't tell"* (`Q-06`) — never a guess. |
| D-15 | An **incomplete transfer is never an ingest** — file completeness is verified at receipt, not inferred from the parse | A truncated CSV parses. If the stated total sat at the end, the file is `Unoracled` (`10.02.02` D-09) and money is silently lost past the domain's only hard oracle. |
| D-16 | The registry holds the source's **correction window**; an undeclared window is a **finding**, and the platform's fallback is **labelled the platform's deadline, not the source's** | Resolves `10.02.05` Q-04b and anchors `10.02.05` D-17's ladder. Domain `D-04` (a clock must exist) and domain `D-15` (never invent a number and present it as fact) are both satisfied only by an explicit, labelled default. Whether windows are knowable at all: `10.02.05` Q-05. |
| D-17 | **Coverage travels with every derived figure**, and it carries **two bounds**: counted from N of M **registered** sources, and registered is **not all** | `10.03.02` D-08. A counter drawing on 3 of 5 sources under-counts, escalates late and underpays while reconciling perfectly against itself. The second bound is this feature's to state: the denominator is what the user declared, so coverage is never a percentage of a true total the platform does not have (domain `D-15`). |
| D-18 | The registry is **seeded from evidence and confirmed by the user** — never a blank settings form, never an auto-registration | `DT-15`. Four signals the platform already holds (ingested statements naming counterparties, `10.01.01` affiliations, the credits graph, `10.05.01` hits) against a question the user genuinely cannot answer. Candidates only: an invented source produces an invented expectation and an alarm that is a fabrication with a date on it. |
| D-19 | A registered source with **no corresponding affiliation is a finding**, not a validation error | `DT-13`. *"I'm with PRS"* often means the publisher is and the user is not — a permanent, unresolvable false overdue. Two readings shown, both useful; the platform does not pick (domain `D-15`). Parent CX-01 Q1 holds: belief and arrival never infer each other. |
| D-20 | On a multi-party statement: **the whole file is parsed; the uploader sees the document they already hold; no third party is notified; nothing is aggregated across uploaders** | `10.02.02` D-13 (filtering at parse destroys the oracle). The uploader already has the paper, so re-showing it discloses nothing new; **the new harm is the platform's re-presentation** — building a financial profile of a non-user across the documents a Producer administers. `Q-03` decides storage and display; this decides everything else. |
| D-21 | Sources **close; they are never deleted** | Deleting the expectation deletes the only record that money should have arrived — `D-02` in reverse. Parent `D-04`'s append-only discipline applied to the registry itself. A closed source stops the clock and keeps the debt (`10.05.03`, `10.08`). |
| D-22 | The registry tracks **distribution cycles, never periods** | `10.02.02` DT-07: one PRS distribution bundles multiple usage vintages, so there is no statement-level period — only a cycle with a name. Period is per-line and `10.02.04`'s. A statement-level period here would attribute old usage to today's owners (domain `D-02`), silently. |
| D-23 | **Counterparty identity survives renames, mergers and acquisitions** | Adapter selection is keyed on source identity (CX-05), and a new entry orphans the arrival history — which is the *only* cadence a triggered source has (`D-08`). The format change that follows an acquisition is `10.02.02` D-04's versioning problem, not an identity change. |
