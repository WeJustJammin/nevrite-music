# Feature: Unmatched Line & Exception Queue

> **Number**: 10.02.05
> **Parent**: [Statement Ingestion & Normalization](./10.02-statement-ingestion-normalization-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16
> **Origin**: Deep Think addition — not a sweep candidate

## Overview

Where **money the platform received but could not attribute** goes, and where it stays **visible** until someone resolves it. Lines that matched nothing (`10.02.03`), lines with no usable period (`10.02.04`), lines the parser reconciled but could not identify — all of it is real money that arrived, belonging to somebody, that the platform cannot yet name.

The sweep did not propose this. It exists because the alternative is a `NULL` in a matching table, and a `NULL` is how a platform silently becomes the thing it was built to fight. Domain 10's headline promise is that it can find money the industry lost in exactly this way (`10.05.01`). A queue that hides its own unattributed money would be the industry's black box, rebuilt, by the company selling the cure.

| Fan | None | No surface. |

## Behavior

The queue holds every line the pipeline could not honestly attribute, **with its money intact and denominated**. Three properties are non-negotiable:

- **It is money, not errors.** The queue's primary unit is a currency amount. An exception queue presented as a list of failed rows will never be looked at; the same queue headed "£340 unattributed" will. This is the same content and a completely different product.
- **Ageing is the alarm.** A line sitting unresolved for four quarters is not a backlog item, it is money going stale — and it is the exact condition that becomes society black-box money elsewhere in the industry. The domain's recurring pattern applies again (`10.01.04` D-02, `10.02.01` D-02): **an absence produces no event, so the clock must produce one.**
- **Nothing is dropped, ever.** A line the platform cannot attribute after ten years is still a line. Deleting it makes the money disappear from the only record that knew it existed.

Resolution routes differ by cause and must not be flattened into "fix it": an unmatched line may need a catalogue correction (`10.02.03`), an identifier from domain 09, a work that was never registered (`10.01`), or an admission that it is genuinely somebody else's money and should be returned or reported.

### Happy Path

1. A line fails attribution anywhere in the pipeline; it lands here with its amount, source, period and reason
2. It is grouped with siblings — the same mangled title across 400 lines is one problem and one decision, not 400
3. The user (or the platform, via a later catalogue improvement) resolves the group
4. Resolved lines re-enter calculation (`10.03.01`) with their money intact
5. The queue's total shrinks — and the total, not the row count, is the metric

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Queue is thousands of lines worth £3 | Volume with no value. The queue becomes noise and stops being read | Grouped and value-ranked; **never** presented as a flat list |
| A single line worth £4,000 | Buried among the £3 lines under any row-count-based UI | Value ranking is what makes the queue usable at all |
| Line is genuinely not yours | A label statement's other artists (parent `Q-03`) | Resolvable as "not mine" — a first-class outcome, not a failure to match |
| Line ages past a source's claim window | Some sources will not correct beyond a period. **The money is now permanently lost** | The clock must alarm before this, or the queue is theatre |
| Catalogue improves later | Old unmatched lines become matchable retroactively | Re-matching must sweep the queue — resolution is not a one-shot at ingest |
| Resolved wrongly | Money moves to the wrong party | Reversible (`10.02.03` D-05) → `10.03.04` |
| Queue never gets looked at | The realistic default for any exception queue ever built | **This is the design risk**, not an edge case. See DT-03 |
| Money is unattributable AND the payee is unknown | Compounds with `10.04.03` — unattributed *and* unpayable | The platform's own black box, twice over |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Queued | Attribution failed | Amount, source, period, reason |
| Grouped | Siblings clustered | One decision covering many lines |
| Resolved | Attributed | Re-enters calculation |
| Not mine | Disclaimed | First-class outcome; the money is somebody else's fact |
| Ageing | Unresolved past a threshold | **Escalating alarm**, sized in money |
| Stale | Past the source's claim window | Honest, permanent loss — recorded, not hidden |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Unmatched lines are an implementation detail of matching — a `NULL` and a retry | The obvious engineering treatment, and why the sweep produced no candidate for it | ❌ **REJECTED, and the omission is exactly the failure the domain sells against.** An unattributed line is **money that arrived and belongs to somebody**. A `NULL` in a matching table is, functionally and precisely, a black box: money held by an institution that cannot name its owner. `10.05.01` is built on the premise that the industry does this and that finding the money is worth real activation value — so a WeJammin that quietly accumulates its own unattributed pile is selling a cure for a disease it has. The queue must exist as a **product surface with a currency total**, not as an ops backlog. |
| DT-02 | Resolution happens at ingest — you match what you can and the rest is lost | One-shot pipeline thinking | ❌ **REJECTED.** The catalogue improves continuously: a work gets registered (`10.01`), an ISRC arrives (domain 09), a credit is claimed (`02.03`), a user confirms a title variant. Every one of those makes previously-unmatchable lines matchable **retroactively**. The queue must be swept whenever the catalogue changes, not just when a statement lands. This inverts the pipeline's shape — it is not fire-and-forget; the queue is a standing corpus re-evaluated against a growing graph. It is also, quietly, the mechanism by which the platform's value compounds: the same statement is worth more money to the user next year than it was this year. |
| DT-03 | Building the queue solves the problem | The natural conclusion once the queue exists | ❌ **REJECTED — every exception queue ever built goes unread, and there is no reason to think this one is special.** The mitigation is not process, it is **denomination**: a queue headed "412 unresolved rows" is an ops chore and will rot; a queue headed "**£340 of your money we can't attribute**" is a personal financial fact and gets clicked. Same data, different product. The metric follows the same logic — track the **money** in the queue and its age, never the row count, because optimising row count rewards clearing the £3 lines and leaves the £4,000 one sitting. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The queue is **denominated in money**, not rows | DT-03. It is the difference between a product surface and an ops backlog nobody reads. |
| D-02 | **Nothing is ever dropped** | DT-01. Deleting an unattributed line deletes the only record that the money exists. |
| D-03 | The queue is **re-swept whenever the catalogue changes**, not only at ingest | DT-02. A growing graph retroactively resolves old lines — the platform's value compounds over the same statements. |
| D-04 | Lines are **grouped and value-ranked** | 400 lines of one mangled title is one decision. A flat list buries the £4,000 line under the £3 ones. |
| D-05 | Ageing is an **escalating, money-sized alarm** | Source claim windows close. The domain's recurring pattern: an absence produces no event, so the clock must. |
| D-06 | "Not mine" is a **first-class resolution** | Some money genuinely belongs to someone else, and forcing every line to resolve to the user is how misattribution happens. |
