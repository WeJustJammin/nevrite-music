# Feature: Payee Royalty Statements

> **Number**: 10.04.02
> **Parent**: [Disbursement & Payee Statements](./10.04-disbursement-payee-statements-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The **statement a payee actually reads**: what you earned, from what, through which split, less what, leaving what — with every number traceable back to a usage line on a source's statement and a split someone confirmed in a room.

Royalty statements are the most notorious documents in the music industry. They are unintelligible by construction: composed by the party who owes the money, using terms they interpreted, over splits they reconstructed, netted through deductions they did not itemise. **This feature is where WeJammin's whole thesis becomes a document.** If a payee cannot follow their own number, the platform built a nicer-looking version of the thing it exists to replace.

## Behavior

A statement covers a period, per payee, and must state four things that industry statements systematically confuse:

1. **Earned** — before recoupment, before holds. The fact of the work having made money (`10.03.01` DT-02).
2. **Deducted** — itemised, never compounded: society commission, sub-publisher retention at source, admin fee, platform fee, withholding tax.
3. **Applied** — against advances (`10.03.03`), against overpayment offsets (`10.03.04`), into escrow (`10.04.04`).
4. **Paid** — what actually moved, in what currency, at what realised rate.

`£0.00` is a legitimate value for line 4 and an illegitimate value for the statement. The industry's characteristic move is to show only line 4 — technically true, actively concealing — and it is why an artist believes their record made nothing. **The statement must say "you earned £4,200; £4,200 went against your advance; £11,800 remains" even when it moved nothing**, because that sentence is the entire difference between informing and concealing.

The traceability chain is the deliverable (`10.03` D-03): amount → derivation → line → source statement → usage period → the split as of that period → the session where the split was agreed. Every link exists in the platform already; the statement is where the chain is finally shown to the person it is about. **No competitor can produce the last two links at any price**, because they were not in the room.

Honesty about the chain's limits is mandatory: at-source netting (`10.01.07` D-03) breaks it at the sub-publisher, and the statement must say so rather than reconstructing a plausible gross.

### Happy Path

1. A payout run completes (`10.04.01`) — or a period closes with nothing payable
2. A statement is generated for **every** payee, including those paid nothing
3. It states earned / deducted / applied / paid, itemised, in the payee's reporting currency with source amounts available (`10.02.04` D-02)
4. Any figure drills down to its derivation, its source line, and the split as of the usage period
5. Where the chain is broken (at-source netting), the statement says where and why

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Paid £0.00, earned £4,200 | The industry shows £0.00. **The whole point is not to** | Earned / applied / remaining, all four lines |
| Restatement changes a prior statement | The previous statement was wrong and the payee has it | New version + explicit delta + **cause** (`10.03.04` D-02). Never a silent reissue |
| At-source netting upstream | Gross unknowable; the chain dead-ends at the sub-publisher | Disclosed: "net at source; gross not available". Never reconstructed |
| Statement includes another party's data | A payer-issued statement must show the payee's share, not the whole session's economics | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`; parent `Q-03` family |
| Thousands of sub-penny lines | Literal truth is unreadable | Aggregated with drill-down. Summarising is fine; **hiding is not** |
| Payee disputes a number | The statement is the argument's subject | → `10.08`, carrying the derivation as opening evidence |
| Escrowed amount (`10.04.04`) | Earned, not paid, held, contested | Named as held, with the reason — not omitted |
| Payee is a shell (`02.03`) | A statement about money for a person with no account | → `10.04.03`. The statement exists; nobody can read it |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The statement is a report — a rendering of numbers that already exist elsewhere | The obvious classification; it is literally a document generated from data | ❌ **REJECTED — the statement is where the entire domain's value is delivered or lost, and calling it a report is how it gets built last and badly.** Everything upstream — as-of splits (parent D-02), immutable derivations (`10.03.01` D-01), itemised deductions (`10.03.01` D-05), earned-vs-paid separation (`10.03.01` DT-02) — is **only ever experienced here**. A payee never sees the calculation engine; they see one document. If it renders as `£0.00` with no working, every one of those upstream decisions was wasted effort and the platform shipped a prettier version of the statement it exists to replace. `10.03` D-03 says the derivation is the deliverable; **this is the only place it is delivered.** |
| DT-02 | Traceability means linking to the source statement line | The natural stopping point — you show which line paid, and that is more than anyone else does | ❌ **REJECTED as stopping two links short of the differentiator.** Curve, Exactuals and Reprtoir can link an amount to a source line; that is a solved problem and not worth building a company on. WeJammin can go further: line → **split as of the usage period** → **the session where that split was agreed, by those people, on that date** (`02.02.03`). Those last two links are the ones no competitor can produce at any price, because they were not in the room — which is precisely `problem-statement.md`'s root cause ("absence at the point of truth") stated as a product feature. **The statement is where `D-18`'s wedge is finally visible to the person it protects.** Stopping at the source line throws that away for a UI convenience. |
| DT-03 | Showing everything is honest, so the statement should show every line | Transparency maximalism, and a defensible instinct in a domain built on not hiding | ❌ **REJECTED — 40,000 sub-penny lines is technically complete and functionally a wall.** An unreadable statement conceals as effectively as a blank one; the industry's statements are, in fact, frequently *too detailed* rather than too sparse, and that is not an accident. The resolution is **aggregate by default, drill on demand, never omit**: summarising is a service, hiding is a lie, and the difference is whether the detail is reachable. This is the same distinction `10.02.05` D-01 draws for the exception queue — a money-denominated summary that can be opened, versus a row dump nobody reads. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **A payout without a traceable statement is forbidden**; a statement is issued even when nothing was paid | Parent D-04, `10.04.01` D-03. `£0.00` alone is the industry's concealment. |
| D-02 | The statement states **earned / deducted / applied / paid** — four lines, never one | `10.03.01` DT-02. Collapsing them is how an artist concludes their record failed. |
| D-03 | Traceability runs **to the session**, not to the source line | DT-02. The last two links are the differentiator; nothing else in the market can produce them. |
| D-04 | **Aggregate by default, drill on demand, never omit** | DT-03. Summarising is a service; hiding is a lie; the test is whether the detail is reachable. |
| D-05 | Broken chains are **disclosed at the break** | `10.01.07` D-03. At-source netting makes gross unknowable; inventing one is the reconstruction failure. |
| D-06 | A restated statement is a **new version with an explicit delta and its cause** | `10.03.04` D-02. A silent reissue is worse than the original error. |
