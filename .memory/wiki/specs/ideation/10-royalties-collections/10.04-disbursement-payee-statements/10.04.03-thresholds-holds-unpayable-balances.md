# Feature: Thresholds, Holds & Unpayable Balances

> **Number**: 10.04.03
> **Parent**: [Disbursement & Payee Statements](./10.04-disbursement-payee-statements-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16
> **Origin**: Deep Think addition — not a sweep candidate

## Overview

**Money that is correctly calculated, genuinely owed, and cannot be paid.** The payee is below the minimum payout threshold. The payee has no payout method. The payee is an unclaimed shell from a session roll call (`02.03`) — a real person with a real credit who has never heard of WeJammin. The payee is dead. The payee failed KYC. The amount is smaller than the fee to send it.

The sweep did not propose this, and the omission matters more here than anywhere else in the domain. **This is where WeJammin builds its own black box.** `10.05.01` is the platform's headline activation event — *"we found you £800 the industry lost"* — and the industry lost it in exactly this way: money held by an institution that knows the amount, knows the work, and cannot reach the person. A platform that accumulates the same pile behind a `balance` column is selling a cure for a disease it has.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees their own held money and precisely why it is held — under threshold, no method, KYC incomplete. All of those are fixable by them, which is the point: a held balance with a stated cause is a task, and a held balance with no cause is a theft. |
| Producer | Config | Threshold policy on entities they administer, and — more consequentially — visibility of money owed to people who are **not on the platform**, generated by sessions they ran. They are the only channel to those people (`credits-attribution-cx.md` CX-04), now with a cash amount attached. |
| Operator | None | No surface. |
| Fan | None | No surface. |

## Behavior

Every unpayable balance carries a **cause and a remedy**, and they are not interchangeable:

| Cause | Whose problem | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Below threshold | Nobody's | Accrue; pay when it clears |
| No payout method | The payee's | Add one — the balance is already theirs |
| Unclaimed shell | Structural | Somebody must tell a human. The platform cannot |
| KYC incomplete/failed | The payee's, or nobody's | Complete it; some cases are unresolvable |
| Deceased | Nobody's | Estate/probate (domain 09) |
| Sanctioned | Legal | Cannot pay, possibly ever |

Three principles govern all of them:

- **The balance is the payee's money, held.** Not the platform's revenue, not float, not a rounding sink. It appears on their statement (`10.04.02` D-01) whether or not they can read it.
- **It ages, and ageing alarms.** The domain's recurring pattern for the third distinct time (`10.01.03` D-03, `10.02.01` D-02, `10.02.05` D-05): an absence produces no event, so the clock must produce one. A balance sitting for three years is the black box forming in real time.
- **The cause is stated, always.** "Held" with no reason is indistinguishable from theft, and it is what the industry does.

### Happy Path

1. A payout run selects a payee whose money cannot move (`10.04.01`)
2. The balance is held with its cause, and the payee's statement says so
3. The cause is surfaced as an actionable task where the payee can act — add a method, complete KYC, wait for the threshold
4. The payee acts; the balance joins the next run
5. Where the payee cannot act (shell, deceased), the balance ages and escalates — visibly

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Shell payee with real earnings | Money for a person who does not know the platform exists. **The platform's own black box** | Only a Producer can tell them. See DT-02 |
| Balance ages past any plausible claim | It becomes indistinguishable from what `10.05.01` sells against | Escalating alarm; and an unresolved policy question (Q-01) |
| Threshold set high | Slower payouts, cheaper fees, and a growing float the platform holds | Policy, and a conflict of interest. See DT-03 |
| Payee never adds a method | Balance grows forever. The most common long-tail case | Visible, aged, theirs |
| Deceased payee | Money accrues to an account nobody can access | Domain 09's estate machinery, money-side. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| KYC permanently fails | The money can never be paid to them, legally | Honest dead end; a rare and genuinely hard case |
| Held balance + restatement | The held amount was wrong | Re-derived; held balances are not immune to `10.03.04` |
| Platform shuts down | Whose money is it then? | The question every held balance implies and nobody asks. `[PENDING — Step 5]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Payable | Method + threshold + KYC all good | Joins the next run |
| Accruing | Below threshold | The amount, the threshold, and the gap. Not an error |
| Blocked (actionable) | No method / KYC incomplete | **A task with a cause**, on their statement |
| Unreachable | Shell / deceased | Held; the platform cannot fix this alone |
| Ageing | Held past a threshold | Escalating alarm, sized in money |
| Frozen | Sanctions / legal | Cannot pay; stated plainly |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Unpayable balances are a payment-rails concern — the payout provider handles minimums and failed transfers | Node Classification Gate; `CX-M01` covers payouts, holds and reserves explicitly | ❌ **REJECTED, and the rejection is the parent domain's D-09.** The rails handle a *failed transfer*. They have no concept of **money owed to a person who does not exist on the platform** — a shell payee from a session roll call (`02.03`), holding a real credit and a real neighbouring-rights claim (`10.01.06`), who has never heard of WeJammin. That balance is not a payment failure; it is a **black box**, and it is definitionally the thing `10.05.01` sells recovery from: money held by an institution that knows the amount, knows the work, and cannot reach the person. The platform building the cure must not quietly accumulate the disease behind a `balance` column with no clock and no surface. This is domain-owned because it is the domain's own integrity at stake. |
| DT-02 | The shell-payee problem is `02.03`'s (claiming and cold-start), already logged and already known | It is literally the same break — `credits-attribution-cx.md` CX-04, a shell with no notification channel | ❌ **REJECTED — money changes the problem, and it changes it in both directions.** CX-04's break is that a shell has no channel and the only invitation is a Producer saying "go claim your page". That is a *social* act about *reputation*. Here the same act is about **cash**: "there is £340 waiting for you" is a categorically stronger message than "your credits are listed", and it might make the growth loop work where reputation alone does not. But it also creates two problems `02.03` never had: **holding a stranger's money is a legal position, not a product state** (parent `Q-02`), and *telling* someone about money they never asked you to hold uses a channel they never opted into. `02.03` can afford to wait for its shells. This cannot — the balance ages, and the platform's own criticism ages with it. |
| DT-03 | The payout threshold is a cost-efficiency setting | Standard; every payout system has one, and transfer fees are real | ❌ **REJECTED as neutral — a threshold is a policy with a conflict of interest inside it.** Every pound below the line is a pound the platform **holds** rather than pays. Set it high and payouts get cheap, the float grows, and the platform quietly becomes a place where small earners' money accumulates indefinitely — which is a fair description of the societies this domain criticises. Set it low and fees eat amounts smaller than themselves. There is no neutral value, the platform's interest is not aligned with the payee's, and the honest handling is to make the threshold **visible, justified, and ideally the payee's to set** — the last of which nobody in the industry does. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A held balance is **the payee's money**, and it appears on their statement | It is not float, not revenue, not a rounding sink. |
| D-02 | Every held balance carries a **cause and a remedy** | "Held" with no reason is indistinguishable from theft, and it is the industry's behaviour. |
| D-03 | Held balances **age and alarm** | Third independent occurrence of the domain's pattern (`10.01.03` D-03, `10.02.01` D-02, `10.02.05` D-05): an absence produces no event, so the clock must. |
| D-04 | The **threshold is visible and justified**; ideally the payee's to set | DT-03. There is no neutral value, and the platform's interest opposes the payee's. |
| D-05 | Shell-payee balances are **domain-owned**, not delegated to the rails | DT-01. This is where the platform either avoids or becomes what it criticises. |
