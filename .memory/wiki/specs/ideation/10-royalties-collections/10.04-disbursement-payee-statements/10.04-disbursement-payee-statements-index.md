## Overview

**Getting the money to the people and telling them why**: the periodic payout run that moves thousands of small amounts to many parties, the statement that explains each one, the balances that cannot be paid at all, and the money held back because someone is arguing about it.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: a payout produces a statement; a disputed line is withheld into escrow and the statement must explain the withholding; an unpayable balance is a payout that could not happen and a statement line that must still exist. They interact at every step, and the interaction is where the domain's integrity is decided — a payout with no statement is a bank transfer, and a statement with no traceable derivation is what the industry already sends.

**The question hanging over the whole sub-domain**: does WeJammin move money at all? The parent's `Q-07` is unresolved, and it splits this sub-domain cleanly. If the platform only ever computes, `10.04.02` (statements) is the entire deliverable and it is still valuable — a band leader who received a lump sum can be told exactly what each member is owed. If it disburses, it holds other people's money, which is regulated activity (parent `Q-04`) and a different company.

> **Finding**: **Nobody can release their own escrowed money** — not the payee, not the payer, not the administrator. That is deliberate (`10.04.04` D-02) and unusual for this platform, which elsewhere prefers the parties to settle things themselves (`02.05` D-03's escalation ladder). Money is the one case where "let the parties sort it out" means "whoever holds it wins".
>
> **Finding**: The Musician/Producer divergence is again **payee vs payer** scope, not capability — consistent with `10.03`'s Role Matrix. Downstream spec writers should build one surface with a scope, not two products.
