## Overview

**Finding money that is missing** — in two directions that are the same problem at different times. **Black box recovery** (`10.05.01`, `10.05.02`) is money already collected by a society, sitting unattributed, belonging to someone who cannot find it. **Leakage detection** (`10.05.03`) is money that should be arriving and isn't — an unregistered work, an uncovered territory, an unaffiliated performer, a distribution that never came.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not two features**: they interact causally. Leakage detection's findings are the *reason* black-box money exists — an unregistered work is why its royalties are unattributed at the MLC. And a black-box hit is retrospective evidence of a past leak, which tells the detector where its blind spots are. Fix the leak and you stop making black box; find the black box and you learn where you leaked. Search (`10.05.01`) and claim (`10.05.02`) are likewise distinct: one is a public-data problem the platform can solve alone, the other requires standing with an institution it may not have.

**The domain's activation event lives here**, and so does its most uncomfortable tension: `10.01.04` DT-02 notes that if registration works, WeJammin is the reason its own users have less black-box money to find. The recovery pitch and the prevention competence point at different roadmaps.
