# Feature: Claim Submission & Evidence Pack

> **Number**: 10.05.02
> **Parent**: [Recovery & Leakage](./10.05-recovery-leakage-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16
> **Origin**: Split from sweep candidate 07 — the claim half, separated from search

## Overview

Turning *"this unclaimed work looks like mine"* into **a claim a society will accept**: assembling the evidence, formatting it the way that body wants it, submitting it, and tracking the response through a process that can take months.

Separated from `10.05.01` because the two have **opposite gating**. Searching public unclaimed data needs nobody's permission. Filing a claim on someone's behalf needs a mandate from them and standing with the institution — the parent domain's `Q-03` in its most expensive form. Bundling them would have hidden that the valuable half is free and the gated half is separable.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Claims their own found money. What they gain is not the claim — they could file it themselves — but the **evidence pack**: a session, a date, a roll call, an attested credit. Their `personas.md` pain is "re-proving who they are and what they've played on"; this is that act, automated, aimed at an institution. |
| Producer | Full | Own claims, plus claims for entities they administer. Also the **corroborating witness** for other people's claims — they ran the room, and their attestation is worth more than a self-claim (`personas.md`: the trust broker). |
| Operator | None | No surface. A studio can confirm a session happened (`02.05` Role Lens) but has no claim of its own. |
| Fan | None | No surface. |

## Behavior

A claim is (claimant, work/recording, asserted role and share, **evidence**, body, submission, response). The platform's contribution is concentrated entirely in the evidence:

- A **captured session** (`02.02`) — who was present, when, what they played, confirmed by the people there on the day.
- **Attestations** (`02.04`) — the trust broker's confirmation, not a self-claim.
- **A split agreed at creation** (domain 09) — dated, signed, contemporaneous.

That is the platform's whole pitch pointed at an institution: `problem-statement.md` says the industry reconstructs from memory at the moment the answer becomes valuable. **A claim is that exact moment.** Everyone else arrives at the MLC with an assertion; a WeJammin user arrives with a contemporaneous record. Whether any society *cares* is the open question that decides the feature's value (Q-01).

`10.05.01` DT-03 hands this feature a hard requirement: **a claim must cost something to make.** Search deliberately over-produces candidates (`10.05.01` D-02, inverted error budget); if claiming is a frictionless button, the platform becomes an industrial false-claim generator pointed at bodies it needs relationships with. The evidence requirement *is* the friction, and it is load-bearing rather than bureaucratic.

### Happy Path

1. User confirms a candidate from `10.05.01` as plausibly theirs
2. System assembles the evidence it holds — session, roll, attestations, split, identifiers
3. The claim is formatted for that body's process and submitted (or handed to the user to submit — see Q-02)
4. Response returns, over weeks or months
5. Accepted: the money is released by the body, the work becomes registered (`10.01.04`), and the leak that caused it is closed (`10.05.03`)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| No evidence beyond "I remember" | The overwhelming majority of back-catalogue claims. **The platform has nothing the user doesn't** | Honest: this is a self-assertion. `problem-statement.md`'s failure, in the flesh |
| Society rejects | With a terse code, months later | Same translation problem as `10.01.04` D-04 |
| Society ignores it | The worst outcome and a common one | Expected-by + alarm — the domain's pattern again (`10.01.03` D-03) |
| Competing claim on the same work | Someone else says it's theirs. The body holds the money | → domain 09 dispute; the black box gets deeper, not shallower |
| Claim was wrong | The user claimed in good faith and was mistaken | Withdrawable; a pattern of it is a 24 signal |
| Body won't accept third-party claims | Parent `Q-03` in its concrete form | Hand off to the user; the evidence pack still has value |
| Evidence exists but predates WeJammin | The back catalogue — where recovery value is concentrated | The platform has no session for it. Structural, not fixable |
| Claim accepted, money paid elsewhere | Body pays a publisher/administrator, not the person | `[OPEN — Q-03 → /create-prd-architecture]` Whether the platform can observe the payee at all is unresolved |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The evidence pack is WeJammin's advantage — nobody else can produce a contemporaneous session record | The platform's thesis (`D-18`) applied to claiming, and it is a genuinely good argument | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED as an argument, REJECTED as a reliable mechanism — and the gap between those is the feature's whole risk.** The logic is impeccable: everyone else arrives at the MLC with an assertion, a WeJammin user arrives with a dated session confirmed by the people in the room. But **it assumes the society consumes evidence, and societies consume *forms***. A claim process is a defined intake with defined fields; there is frequently no field for "here is a contemporaneous attestation from four people". Our evidence may have no slot, no reader, and no standing — exactly like `10.01.06` D-03's finding that attestation tiers have no standing with PPL, reached independently. **The advantage may be real and unusable**, and the difference is an institutional fact the platform cannot discover by reasoning. Q-01 is the highest-value cheap question in this domain. |
| DT-02 | The recovery pitch — *"we found you £800"* — is the domain's headline, so this feature is a Must | Parent domain Overview; every instinct about activation | ❌ **REJECTED as a MoSCoW conclusion, and the reason is a stack of gates nobody added up.** Recovery-with-claiming requires, in order: (1) a credits graph the new user does not have (`10.05.01` DT-02), which needs import, which is gated on source licensing (`credits-attribution-index.md` Q-10); (2) bulk access to unclaimed corpora, gated on their terms (parent `Q-01`); (3) a mandate to act for the user; (4) standing with each body (parent `Q-03`); (5) societies that read evidence (DT-01/Q-01). **Five gates, four of them outside the platform's control.** Meanwhile `10.05.01` search alone is gated on one, and delivers most of the emotional payload — *"there are 43 works here that look like yours"* is nearly as powerful as claiming them and requires nobody's permission. **The activation event and the claim are separable, and separating them is what makes the activation shippable.** That is why candidate 07 was split. |
| DT-03 | Claiming is a form-filling convenience | The obvious framing — the user could file it themselves, we just make it easier | ❌ **REJECTED — it is an act with legal weight, made at volume, by people who do not perceive it as one.** A false claim is a false statement to an institution, and `10.05.01` DT-03 shows how the incentive is built: 400 low-confidence candidates plus a frictionless button equals 400 claims, filed cheerfully, in good faith, by someone who reasons "I'll claim it and they'll tell me if I'm wrong". At scale that makes WeJammin the largest source of bad claims a society has ever seen — pointed at the exact institutions it needs relationships with (parent `Q-03`), destroying the standing the rest of domain 10 depends on. **The evidence requirement is not UX friction; it is the thing that stops the product weaponising its own users against its own counterparties.** |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Split from `10.05.01`** — search is ungated, claiming is not | Parent D-03, DT-02. Bundling would hide that the activation event is shippable without the gated half. |
| D-02 | **A claim must cost something to make** | DT-03, `10.05.01` DT-03. The evidence requirement is the friction, and it protects the platform's counterparty relationships from its own users. |
| D-03 | The platform is **honest about evidence strength**, including when it has none | A back-catalogue claim with no session behind it is a self-assertion. Dressing it up would be the reconstruction failure with better UI. |
| D-04 | Submissions carry an **expected-by date**; silence alarms | `10.01.03` D-03's pattern, reused. Fourth occurrence in this domain. |
| D-05 | Where the platform cannot file, it **hands off the evidence pack** | The pack has value even when the platform has no standing. This is the ungated fallback. |
