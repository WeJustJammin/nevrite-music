# Feature: Royalty Leakage Detection

> **Number**: 10.05.03
> **Parent**: [Recovery & Leakage](./10.05-recovery-leakage-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Detecting **money that should be arriving and isn't**. A work registered at PRS and nowhere else, so every territory outside the UK pays nothing. A performer with fifteen credits and no PPL membership. A distribution that was due in October and never came. A work whose splits sum to 97%. A mechanical never registered because the writer thought their PRO covered it.

Leakage is invisible by construction — **it is an absence, and absences produce no events**. Nobody notices money that never arrived, because there was never a promise of a specific amount on a specific date. This feature manufactures the promise so the absence becomes visible.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Reports | Their gaps, **sized in money where it can honestly be sized**: "you have 12 works registered in the UK only", "you are not registered with the MLC", "PRS didn't distribute in October". Not configurable — a leakage finding is a diagnosis derived from state owned elsewhere, and anything editable here would be a second source of truth for a gap. |
| Producer | Reports | Same, across their catalogue and administered entities. The highest-yield persona: more works, more territories, more sessions, more gaps. |
| Operator | None | No surface. |
| Fan | None | No surface. |

## Behavior

Every detector follows one shape: **an expectation the platform can derive, compared against reality.** The sources of expectation already exist elsewhere in the domain, which is why this feature is cheap to build and disproportionately valuable:

| Detector | Expectation source | Reality source |
|---|---|---|
| Work registered in ≤1 territory | `10.01.04` per-territory state | Registration state itself |
| Performer eligible, unaffiliated | `10.01.06` credit-derived eligibility | `10.01.01` affiliations |
| Distribution overdue | `10.09` calendar + `10.02.01` cadence | Statement arrivals |
| Registration overdue / rejected | `10.01.04` expected-by | Acknowledgements |
| Splits sum < 100% | Domain 09 ledger | The ledger |
| No mechanical registration | `10.01.05` eligibility | Registration state |
| Foreign territory uncovered | `10.01.07` map | The map's gaps |
| Registered but unmatched (MLC) | `10.01.05` DT-02 | The MLC's public pool |

The honesty constraint is severe and must not be relaxed: **the platform mostly cannot say how much money is leaking.** It can say "you are not registered in Germany"; it cannot say "that costs you £400/year" without knowing German streaming volume it does not have. Inventing the number would be more compelling and would be a lie — and it is exactly the lie a growth-motivated product would tell.

### Happy Path

1. The platform derives expectations from state it already holds — registrations, affiliations, cadences, calendars
2. Reality is compared: what registered, what arrived, what matched
3. Gaps surface as findings, ranked by confidence and by money **where money is knowable**
4. Each finding names its remedy and its owner — register the work, join PPL, chase the society
5. The remedy closes the leak, and (per parent D-02) stops producing future black-box money

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Gap is real, size unknown | The normal case | "Not registered in Germany" — never a fabricated amount |
| Gap has no cheap remedy | Sub-publishing (`10.01.07` DT-01) is a commercial act, not a click | Honest and unactionable. Still worth knowing |
| False positive | "You're not registered" when they are, via a route the platform can't see | Erodes trust fast; conservatism matters more than coverage |
| User has no data | New user, nothing to detect against | The cold-start wall again (`10.05.01` DT-02) |
| Every finding at once | A career's accumulated gaps, delivered on day one | **Overwhelming and demoralising.** See DT-03 |
| The platform's own failure | An unregistered work because *our* payload was blocked (`10.01.02`) | Must be attributed honestly, not reported as the user's gap |
| Gap is deliberate | The user chose not to register somewhere | Dismissible; a dismissed finding must stay dismissed |
| Leak found years late | The money is past the claim window | → `10.05.01`, if it is even still recoverable |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| No data | Nothing to detect against | Honest emptiness |
| Clean | Expectations met | The best state and a rare one |
| Findings | Gaps detected | Ranked, with remedies and owners |
| Unsizable | Gap real, money unknown | Named without a number |
| Dismissed | User's deliberate choice | **Stays dismissed** — repeated nagging is what makes users mute a product |
| Remedied | Gap closed | Verified against reality, not against the user's word |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is analytics — it belongs in domain 22, or rides `CX-M30` (Analytics Instrumentation & Per-Domain Reporting) | Node Classification Gate. It is a set of reports over data that already exists, which is the textbook shape of an analytics feature | ❌ **REJECTED.** Every detector needs an **expectation** — what *should* have happened — and every expectation is domain-10 knowledge that exists nowhere else: per-territory registration state (`10.01.04` D-01), credit-derived neighbouring-rights eligibility (`10.01.06` D-01), society distribution calendars (`10.09`), source cadences (`10.02.01` D-02). Domain 22 has the reporting surface and none of the semantics; a detector there would be reduced to charting numbers with no notion of what is missing. **The reporting rides `CX-M30`; the expectation is domain-owned.** Same split as `10.02.03` DT-02 (machinery shared, discriminator domain-owned) and `10.04.01` DT-01 (rails shared, semantics domain-owned) — the third independent instance of the pattern in this drill. |
| DT-02 | Leakage detection is a nice-to-have next to recovery's "we found you £800" | Recovery has the money and the drama; this has a list of gaps | ❌ **REJECTED — it is the cheaper, ungated, compounding half, and the naive roadmap has them backwards.** Every input already exists (the table above is entirely internal state); it needs **no** society relationship, **no** accreditation, **no** external corpus, and **no** legal gate — where `10.05.01` needs at least one and `10.05.02` needs five (`10.05.02` DT-02). And per parent D-02 the causality runs one way: **a closed leak prevents the black box that recovery would later have to find.** `10.01.04` DT-02 already names the tension — if prevention works, WeJammin destroys its own recovery pitch's supply. The resolution is honest and unglamorous: **recover the back catalogue once; prevent from today forever.** Only one of those is a business. |
| DT-03 | More findings is better — surface every gap so the user can act | Completeness instinct, and it feels like honesty | ❌ **REJECTED — a career's accumulated leakage delivered on day one is not information, it is an indictment.** "You have lost money in 14 ways for 11 years" is true, unactionable, and reads as an accusation delivered by a product the user opened five minutes ago. Half the findings have no cheap remedy (`10.01.07` DT-01: sub-publishing is a commercial act, not a button); several are unsizable (D-02); several will be things they chose. The user closes the tab. **The correct shape is one finding at a time, ranked, remediable, sized where honest** — which is `single-question.md`'s discipline applied to diagnostics rather than to questions, and it is the difference between a product that helps and one that shames. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **The expectation is domain-owned; the reporting rides `CX-M30`** | DT-01. Third instance of the shared-machinery/domain-semantics split in this drill. |
| D-02 | **Money is stated only where it is honestly knowable** | "Not registered in Germany" is true and useful. "That costs you £400/year" is a fabrication, and it is the lie a growth-motivated product would tell. |
| D-03 | **One finding at a time, ranked, remediable** | DT-03. `single-question.md`'s discipline applied to diagnostics. A career of gaps at once is an indictment, not information. |
| D-04 | **Dismissed findings stay dismissed** | Some gaps are choices. Re-nagging is how a product gets muted. |
| D-05 | The platform's **own failures are attributed to itself** | An unregistered work caused by our blocked payload is our gap, not the user's. Same principle as `10.03.04` D-04. |
| D-06 | Conservative beats comprehensive | A false "you're not registered" destroys trust faster than a missed finding builds it. |
