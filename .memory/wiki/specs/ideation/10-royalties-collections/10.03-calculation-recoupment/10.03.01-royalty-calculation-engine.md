# Feature: Royalty Calculation Engine

> **Number**: 10.03.01
> **Parent**: [Royalty Calculation & Recoupment](./10.03-calculation-recoupment-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The arithmetic that turns **a matched royalty line into per-person amounts**: take £4.11 of PRS performance income for a work, resolve the split that was true during the usage period, apply the deal terms sitting on top of it (`10.03.02`), and produce an amount per party — with the whole derivation preserved.

The number is the easy part. **The derivation is the product.** `problem-statement.md` argues the industry's defining failure is that nobody can explain where the money went; every royalty statement in music is unauditable because the split was reconstructed from memory and the terms were interpreted by the party who owed the money. WeJammin has the split from the room (`D-18`). If the engine cannot show a payee exactly how £4.11 became their £0.62, it has thrown away the only thing it has that Curve and Exactuals do not.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees the working: this usage, this line, this split as of this period, these deductions, your amount. They cannot alter any of it — every input belongs upstream. The value is that for the first time the answer to "why is this £0.62?" is a chain of facts rather than a shrug. `personas.md` describes their money as living in a spreadsheet; this is what replaces the spreadsheet's guesswork. |
| Producer | Read-only | Same. Their points enter as a deal term (`10.03.02`), not as a special case in the engine — and the distinction matters, because a producer who could adjust the engine could adjust everyone's money. |
| Operator | None | No surface. |
| Fan | None | No surface. |

## Behavior

Per matched line, the engine resolves in a fixed order:

1. **Which right, which territory, which usage period** — from `10.02.02`/`10.02.04`. These select everything downstream.
2. **The split as of the usage period** — read from domain 09's ledger at that point in time, never as it stands today (parent D-02). This is the single most consequential resolution in the sub-domain and the one most likely to be got wrong silently.
3. **Deal terms in effect for that period** — `10.03.02`. Points, admin fees, escalations, net-vs-gross.
4. **The deduction stack, itemised** — society commission, sub-publisher retention at source (`10.01.07` D-03), platform fee, admin fee. Compounding, and the reason writers cannot state their own effective rate.
5. **Per-party amounts**, each carrying its full derivation.

Recoupment is deliberately **not** in this list. `10.03.03` decides whether an amount is *payable*; the engine decides what it *is*. Merging them makes an earning indistinguishable from a payment, which is precisely the confusion that lets an unrecouped artist believe they earned nothing.

The engine is **immutable and versioned** (parent D-04). A recalculation produces a new version alongside the old; the old is never overwritten, because a clawback is defined as the difference between two versions and cannot exist if one of them is gone.

### Happy Path

1. A matched, confirmed line arrives from `10.02.03` with its usage period and source amount
2. Engine reads the split as of that usage period from domain 09
3. Deal terms in effect for that period are applied (`10.03.02`); deductions are itemised, not netted
4. Per-party amounts are produced, each with a complete derivation chain
5. Amounts flow to `10.03.03` for recoupment, then to `10.04` for statement and payout

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Split changed after the usage period | The engine must use the **old** split. Current-state resolution pays today's owners for yesterday's usage | Parent D-02; requires 09's as-of read (parent `Q-01`) |
| Split is disputed (domain 09 / `02.05`) | The party is contested; paying either side asserts a verdict the platform does not have | Calculated but **held** → `10.04.04` escrow |
| Splits sum to 97% in the period | The ledger is broken for that period. 3% belongs to nobody | Must not be silently redistributed. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| Deal terms are unrecorded | The engine has no parameters. Very common — most independent deals are a PDF nobody typed in | Calculates the raw split and **says the terms are unknown**, never assumes zero |
| Amount rounds to sub-cent | £0.0004 per party per line, across millions of lines. Real money in aggregate | Rounding policy must be explicit and consistent; see Q-02 |
| At-source netting (`10.01.07`) | Gross is unknowable; the derivation chain **breaks** at the sub-publisher | Disclosed as net-at-source. The explainability promise is bounded, and must say where |
| Negative line (`10.02.02`) | Returns and adjustments produce negative earnings | Round-trips honestly → `10.03.04` |
| Party is an unclaimed shell (`02.03`) | Money calculated correctly for a person with no account | Correct, and unpayable → `10.04.03` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Pending | Line matched, not yet calculated | Queued |
| Calculated | Amounts derived | Per-party amounts with full working |
| Held | A party's split is disputed | Calculated, not payable → `10.04.04` |
| Unparameterised | No deal terms recorded | Raw split applied; terms flagged unknown, not assumed |
| Superseded | Recalculated | New version; old retained (parent D-04) |
| Broken | Split does not resolve for the period | Blocked with the ledger gap named — never a best guess |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The engine's job is to produce correct amounts | The feature's name, and what any royalty system is for | ❌ **REJECTED as the primary framing — the deliverable is the derivation, and the number is a byproduct.** Curve, Exactuals, Reprtoir and every label's internal system already produce correct-ish amounts; none of them can explain one. The reason is structural: their split was reconstructed from memory and their terms were interpreted by the party who owed the money, so the chain ends in a judgement rather than a fact. WeJammin's chain ends in **a split six people confirmed in a room on a date** (`D-18`, `02.02.03`). That is the only differentiator this feature has, and it is destroyed by any optimisation that stores the total and discards the working. **Store the derivation; the number is recomputable from it. Store the number and the derivation is gone forever.** |
| DT-02 | Recoupment belongs in the engine — an unrecouped artist earns nothing, so the amount is zero | Plain reading, and how many label systems genuinely behave | ❌ **REJECTED, and the conflation is a moral failure as much as a modelling one.** An unrecouped artist **earns** their share and **receives** nothing; those are different facts, and collapsing them is exactly how an artist comes to believe their record made no money. The engine computes the earning. `10.03.03` computes payability. Keeping them apart is what lets a payee statement say the true and useful thing — *"you earned £4,200; £4,200 went against your advance; you have £11,800 to go"* — instead of the industry-standard, technically-true, actively-misleading **£0.00**. This is not a schema preference; it is the difference between a statement that informs and one that conceals. |
| DT-03 | Rounding is an implementation detail | Sub-cent amounts are obviously immaterial | ❌ **REJECTED.** A million lines × six parties × a sub-cent remainder is real money with no owner, and every rounding scheme decides who gets it — round-half-up favours whoever is listed first, round-down accumulates a residue the platform silently keeps. The residue is small, it is *someone's*, and a platform that built `10.02.05` on the principle that unattributed money must be visible cannot then quietly pocket rounding dust. It must be **explicit, consistent, disclosed, and reconciled** — the sum of the parts must equal the whole, and where it cannot, the remainder needs a named destination rather than a default. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **The derivation is stored; the number is derived from it** | DT-01. Storing the total discards the platform's only advantage in this sub-domain. |
| D-02 | **Recoupment is not in the engine** | DT-02. Earning and payability are different facts; merging them is how artists are told they made nothing. |
| D-03 | The split is read **as of the usage period** | Parent D-02. Current-state resolution silently pays the wrong party. |
| D-04 | **Rounding is explicit, disclosed and reconciled** — the parts must sum to the whole | DT-03. Rounding dust is somebody's money; a platform built on `10.02.05`'s principle cannot pocket it. |
| D-05 | Deductions are **itemised, never netted** | A compounded effective rate is why no writer can explain their own royalty. Consistent with `10.01.07` D-04. |
| D-06 | Unknown deal terms are **flagged**, never assumed to be zero or standard | Assuming a term invents a fact — the reconstruction failure `problem-statement.md` names. |
