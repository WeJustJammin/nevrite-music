## Overview

Turning **normalized royalty lines into per-person amounts**: applying the split that was true when the usage happened, applying whatever deal terms sit on top of it (producer points, admin fees, escalations, net-vs-gross receipts), netting the result against unrecouped advances, and dealing with what happens when any of those inputs change after money has already moved.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: the arithmetic (`10.03.01`) is meaningless without the terms that parameterise it (`10.03.02`); recoupment (`10.03.03`) consumes the calculation's output and simultaneously determines whether any of it is payable; and restatement (`10.03.04`) mutates all three retroactively. They interact continuously and destructively — a split change six months after a payout is a recalculation, a recoupment-position change, and a clawback, in one event.

**The domain's honest claim lives here**: `problem-statement.md` argues the industry's defect is that nobody can explain where the money went. Every other platform's royalty statement is unauditable because the split was reconstructed and the deal terms were interpreted by the person who owed the money. WeJammin has the split from the room (domain 09, captured per `D-18`). If the calculation is not fully explainable line-by-line, the platform has thrown away its only advantage in this sub-domain.

> **Finding**: **Nobody has Full access to anything in this sub-domain, including the platform's own users.** That is deliberate and worth carrying downstream: every input here is owned by another domain (splits by 09, deals by 09/23, lines by 10.02), and this sub-domain only computes. A UI that lets anyone type a number into a royalty calculation has created a second source of truth for the platform's most protected fact — the same error `10.01.02` D-01 forbids one sub-domain over.
>
> **Finding**: The Musician/Producer split here is **payee vs payer**, not seniority. A Producer configures terms and advances because they are on the paying side of a deal; when they are the payee, their access is identical to a Musician's. Do not model this as a permission tier.

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Sweep candidates 11 (Calculation Engine) and 12 (Advances & Recoupment) grouped as a sub-domain; **2 Deep Think additions** | Recoupment is not downstream of calculation — it is a term *inside* it. Neither candidate covered deal terms (`10.03.02`) or restatement (`10.03.04`), and without those the "engine" is arithmetic with no parameters and no history. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | The calculation **reads domain 09's ledger as of the usage period** — never current state | The parent's D-02, applied. A Q1 2024 usage pays the people who owned it in Q1 2024. Current-state resolution does not error; it produces a plausible wrong number and pays the wrong party. | `10.02.04` DT-02; `10.01.07` DT-02 |
| D-03 | **Explainability is the deliverable, not the number** | Every competitor can produce a number. `problem-statement.md`'s thesis is that nobody can explain it. If a payee cannot trace their amount back to a usage line and a split captured in a room, the platform built a worse Curve. | Deep Think, Step 3 |
| D-04 | Calculations are **immutable and versioned**; a recalculation supersedes, never overwrites | `10.03.04` requires the old calculation to still exist — a clawback is the difference between two versions. Overwriting makes restatement unrepresentable and money movements unexplainable. | `10.03.04` D-01 |
| D-05 | Nothing in this sub-domain is **user-editable** | Every input has an upstream owner. An editable calculation is a second source of truth for the split, created at the moment of maximum financial motivation. Same reasoning as `10.01.02` D-01. | Node Classification Gate, Step 3 |
