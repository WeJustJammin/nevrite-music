# Feature: Restatement, Adjustment & Overpayment

> **Number**: 10.03.04
> **Parent**: [Royalty Calculation & Recoupment](./10.03-calculation-recoupment-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17
> **Origin**: Deep Think addition — not a sweep candidate

## Overview

What happens when **something that was already true stops being true**: a source restates a period, a split changes retroactively after a dispute resolves (domain 09), a deal term was recorded wrong, a match is reversed (`10.02.03` D-05), an adapter bug is fixed (`10.02.02` Q-03). Every one of those changes a number the platform already calculated — and, frequently, already paid.

The sweep proposed none of this. It is the feature that decides whether the domain is honest, because **retroactive change is not an edge case in royalties; it is the normal weather.** DSPs restate. Societies adjust. Disputes resolve years late. A royalty system that assumes its inputs are final is not a royalty system.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Told what changed, why, by how much, and what it means for money they may have already spent. This is the platform at its most exposed: "we paid you £900 and the correct figure was £600". `personas.md` describes a persona whose switching trigger is losing money to a fact they couldn't prove — a clawback is that experience delivered by the platform that promised to prevent it, and how it is communicated is the entire product. |
| Producer | Config | Can *cause* a restatement by correcting a term (`10.03.02`) or a split. The asymmetry is uncomfortable: the party who records the terms can trigger a clawback against the party who cannot. |
| Operator | None | No surface — the Operator sells time and space (venues/studios, domains 16-19); recording royalties never flow to them, so a restatement of a royalty line has nothing to touch in their world. Their own settlement corrections live in domain 19, not here. |
| Fan | None | No surface — a Fan is never a royalty payee, so no calculation of theirs is ever restated. The only Fan-adjacent money movement (a refund) is a domain 19/20 concern, not a royalty restatement. |

## Behavior

A restatement is **a new calculation version, not an edit** (parent D-04). The old version is retained permanently; the delta between versions is the adjustment; and the adjustment's sign decides everything about what happens next:

- **Positive delta** (owed more) — easy. It joins the next payout. Nobody complains.
- **Negative delta** (owed less) — the hard case, and it splits again:
  - **Not yet paid** → the amount simply reduces. Invisible, painless.
  - **Already paid** → **overpayment.** The money is gone, it was spent, and the platform wants it back.

The overpayment path is the nastiest flow in domain 10 and has no comfortable answer. Options: offset against future earnings (standard practice, means the payee earns nothing for months with no notice and no agreement), request return (usually futile), or absorb it (a real cost, and an incentive to be careless). The choice is a product and legal decision, not an implementation one (Q-01).

The causal chain must be preserved and shown. A restatement caused by a source's restatement is the source's fault; one caused by an adapter bug is the platform's; one caused by a split change is domain 09's; one caused by a Producer correcting a term in their own favour is `10.03.02` DT-02's problem arriving downstream. **They feel identical to the payee and they are morally different**, and the platform's credibility depends on saying which is which rather than sending one generic apology.

### Happy Path

_There is no happy path. The best case is a small, well-explained, attributed correction._

1. An input changes — source restatement, split correction, term fix, match reversal
2. Affected calculations are identified — **including transitively**: recoupment positions, statements, payouts
3. A new calculation version is produced; the old is retained; the delta is computed
4. Impact is classified: unpaid reduction (painless) or overpayment (not)
5. The payee is told what changed, **why, and whose change it was**, before any offset touches their money

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Overpayment already spent | It is gone. The platform wants it back from someone who does not have it | Offset / request / absorb — an unresolved product decision (Q-01) |
| Restatement un-recoups an artist | They were recouped, got paid, now they are not recouped and the payment was wrong | `10.03.03` DT-01's exact scenario; compounds the clawback |
| Cascade across periods | One split change restates 12 quarters, each with its own payout and recoupment state | Recalculation is transitive and unbounded. See Q-03 |
| Restatement of a restatement | Sources do this. Version 3 of Q1 2024 | Versions, not edits (parent D-04) |
| Platform's own bug caused it | An adapter fix (`10.02.02` Q-03) changes historical numbers | **The platform must say so.** Attributing its own bug to "a correction" is a lie by omission |
| Payee has left the platform | The adjustment still computes and is retained against their identity (identities persist; accounts closing does not delete the ledger). Owed-more accrues as an unclaimed balance held for them. Overpaid-and-gone collapses to the same three bad options as Q-01 but with the offset lever removed — there are no future earnings to net against — so it degrades to request-or-absorb | An owed-more departed payee sees a reclaimable balance on return; an overpaid departed payee triggers a request/absorb decision. Exact retention window and unclaimed-balance escheatment rules `[PENDING — Step 5 Must-depth or /write-be-spec]` |
| Delta is £0.02 | The correction costs more than the money | Materiality threshold needed; see Q-02 |
| Restatement during an open dispute (`10.08`) | The number under argument changes mid-argument | The dispute must absorb the restatement, not race it |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Stable | No change | The current calculation version |
| Restating | Input changed, recalculating | In progress; downstream payouts held |
| Adjusted (positive) | Owed more | Joins the next payout |
| Adjusted (negative, unpaid) | Owed less, not yet paid | Reduced; explained |
| **Overpaid** | Owed less, already paid | The hard state. Offset / request / absorbed — with the cause named |
| Cascading | Transitive restatement across periods | Scoped and shown, never a silent bulk rewrite |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Restatement is an edge case — most royalty lines are calculated once and stay correct | The sweep's implicit position: 18 candidates, none of them this | ❌ **REJECTED — restatement is the normal weather, and its omission from the candidate list is the single largest gap the sweep left in this domain.** DSPs restate routinely. Societies adjust distributions. Disputes resolve years after the money moved (`02.05`, domain 09). The platform's own adapters get fixed (`10.02.02` Q-03) and its own matches get reversed (`10.02.03` D-05). A royalty system built on the assumption that inputs are final is not a royalty system — it is a calculator that will be wrong within one quarter and unable to represent its own wrongness. Every design decision in this sub-domain (immutable versions, derived recoupment, as-of splits) exists **because** of this feature; a sweep that omitted it would have produced a schema that cannot express the correction it will need in month three. |
| DT-02 | An overpayment is recovered by offsetting future earnings — that is what the industry does | Standard practice everywhere, and the path of least resistance | ❌ **REJECTED as a default, though it may survive as a choice.** Silent offset means a musician's next three months of income vanish with no notice, no agreement and no explanation — from the platform whose entire pitch is that the music industry does exactly this to them. `personas.md` names the Musician's switching trigger as *losing money to a fact they couldn't prove*; a silent offset **is** that experience, administered by the product sold as the cure. Whatever the mechanism turns out to be (Q-01), it cannot be silent, and consent is not a nice-to-have here — it is the difference between this platform and the one it is replacing. |
| DT-03 | A restatement is a restatement — the payee needs to know their number changed | The information-completeness view: give them the delta and the new figure | ❌ **REJECTED — cause is not metadata, it is the whole message.** Four restatements identical in size and sign are morally unrelated: *Spotify restated Q1* (nobody's fault, unavoidable), *we shipped a parser bug* (the platform's fault, apologise), *your co-writer's split dispute resolved against you* (a third party's fact, and 09's story to tell), *the producer who pays you edited their own points* (`10.03.02` DT-02 — a conflict of interest arriving as arithmetic). The payee experiences one identical negative number in all four cases. **Sending one generic "your royalties were adjusted" for all four is the industry's behaviour and the reason nobody trusts a royalty statement.** Preserving and displaying causation is not a UX refinement; it is the only thing that makes a clawback survivable. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A restatement is a **new version**; nothing is ever overwritten | Parent D-04. A delta between two versions is undefined if one is gone. |
| D-02 | **Causation is preserved and displayed** | DT-03. Four identical numbers with four different causes; one generic message is the industry's behaviour and the reason its statements are not trusted. |
| D-03 | Overpayment recovery is **never silent** | DT-02. A silent offset is the exact harm the platform sells against. |
| D-04 | The platform **names its own faults** as its own | An adapter bug reported as "a correction" is a lie by omission, and it is the easiest lie in the domain to tell. |
| D-05 | Restatement is **transitive** — calculations, recoupment, statements and payouts all recompute | A split change 12 quarters back is 12 restatements, not one. |
