# Feature: Advances & Recoupment Position

> **Number**: 10.03.03
> **Parent**: [Royalty Calculation & Recoupment](./10.03-calculation-recoupment-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Tracking **money that was fronted and the earnings eating it back**: an advance, a producer fee paid up front, recording costs, tour support — and the running position against them. "Am I recouped?" is the single most-asked and least-answered question in a music career, and the reason it is unanswered is that the party who knows is the party who benefits from you not knowing.

The domain's honest observation: an artist who cannot see their recoupment position cannot tell whether their record is a success, cannot value their own catalogue, and cannot negotiate anything. Making the number visible and continuous is a small feature with a disproportionate effect on the power asymmetry it sits inside.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Their position: what was fronted, what has been earned against it, what remains, and — the part nobody ever gets — **when it is projected to clear** at the current rate. `personas.md` names their success criterion as "income visible in one place"; this is the number that decides whether any of that income is theirs yet. |
| Producer | Config | Records advances they fronted (a producer fee against points is an advance, whether or not anyone called it that) and reads positions on deals they administer. The payer side. |
| Operator | None | No surface. |
| Fan | None | No surface. |

## Behavior

An advance is a **recoupable balance with a scope**: what was fronted, against which income, at what rate, cross-collateralised with what else. Every one of those dimensions is where the money actually gets decided:

- **Scope** — recoupable from this record's royalties only, or from everything the artist ever earns? Cross-collateralisation is the mechanism by which a successful second album pays off a failed first one, and it is invisible to almost everyone it happens to.
- **Rate** — some costs recoup at 100% of earnings, some at 50%. Determined by the deal terms (`10.03.02`), not by this feature.
- **Which income** — an advance against master royalties does not usually recoup from publishing. Getting this wrong moves real money between pockets.

The position is **derived, never stamped** — the same principle `credits-attribution-index.md` D-09 reached for provenance tiers, arriving here from a different direction. Statements get restated (`10.03.04`), splits change, terms get corrected; a stored "recouped: yes" would survive the correction that unmade it, and would keep paying someone who is no longer recouped.

The critical separation, inherited from `10.03.01` DT-02: **this feature decides payability, not earnings.** The artist earned £4,200; £4,200 went against the advance; they receive £0. All three are true, and the statement must say all three (`10.04.02`).

### Happy Path

1. An advance is recorded with its scope, its rate and its cross-collateralisation
2. Earnings are calculated (`10.03.01`) — independently, without reference to recoupment
3. Earnings within scope are applied against the balance at the recoupment rate
4. Position updates: fronted, earned-against, remaining, and a projected clear date at the current run rate
5. Once cleared, earnings become payable and flow to `10.04`

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Cross-collateralised across projects | Album 2 pays off album 1's deficit. Almost nobody understands this is happening to them | Must be **visible and explicit** — it is the mechanism, not a footnote |
| Restatement reduces past earnings | The artist was recouped; now they are not; money was already paid | → `10.03.04` clawback. The nastiest flow in the domain |
| Advance to a band, earnings to individuals | Whose earnings recoup a band's advance, and in what proportion? Domain 01's Band entity | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`; see Q-02 |
| Never recouped | The overwhelmingly common outcome | Honest, with a projected clear date that may be "never at this rate" |
| Advance not recorded | The position is a fiction — the artist looks recouped and isn't | Flagged: the balance is unknown, not zero |
| Producer fee against points | An advance nobody called an advance. Routinely unrecorded | Modelled as one; the naming is the only thing that differs |
| Recoupment rate ≠ 100% | 50% recoupment on some costs means earnings both pay down and pay out | A term (`10.03.02`), not a special case here |
| Position disputed | Usually a term dispute in disguise (`10.03.02`) | → `10.08`; affected amounts held |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Position requested; derived-on-read computation running (D-01) — sum earnings-in-scope, apply rate, project clear date | Skeleton on the four figures (fronted / earned-against / remaining / clear date); last-known position shown greyed until the fresh compute resolves, never a blank |
| Empty (No advance) | Nothing fronted against this scope | "No advance on record — everything earned here is payable." Not an error state; the honest majority-of-independent-artists case |
| Populated — Unrecouped | Balance outstanding | Fronted / earned-against / remaining / projected clear date |
| Populated — Recouped | Balance cleared | Earnings payable from the clearing point forward |
| Populated — Cross-collateralised | Scope spans projects | The linkage shown explicitly (D-03), not buried — which project's deficit is being paid down by which project's earnings |
| Unknown | Advance suspected but not recorded (D-04) | **Flagged as unknown, never rendered as zero** — a distinct visual treatment from "No advance", because the difference is the difference between recouped and clawed-back |
| Reversed | Restatement (`10.03.04`) pushed a cleared position back under | Clawback exposure surfaced; affected payability re-derives downward |
| Error | Computation cannot complete — a dependency is unavailable (earnings from `10.03.01`, terms from `10.03.02`) or internally inconsistent | "Position can't be shown right now" with retry; **never a fabricated or stale-as-current number** — a wrong recoupment figure is worse than an absent one, since someone acts on it |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The recoupment position is a stored balance that decrements as earnings arrive | The obvious model — it is literally a running total | ❌ **REJECTED — it is derived, exactly like `02.04.02`'s provenance tier, and for exactly the same reason.** Restatements reduce past earnings (`10.03.04`), splits change retroactively (parent D-02), terms get corrected, matches get reversed (`10.02.03` D-05). A stored balance would survive every one of those corrections and keep asserting "recouped" after the earnings that recouped it were unmade — and would keep paying someone accordingly. `credits-attribution-index.md` D-09 reached "derived, never stamped" from provenance; this reaches it from money. **Two unrelated domains converging on the same principle from opposite directions is a signal it is a platform-level truth**, and it carries the same cost 02 flagged: derived-on-read is a live constraint on the locked Supabase decision. |
| DT-02 | Recoupment is a label concept and most WeJammin users have never had an advance, so this is a low-value feature | The independent-artist framing; `personas.md`'s Musician is not signed to anyone | ❌ **REJECTED — the concept is universal and only the vocabulary is label-specific.** A producer's fee paid up front against points **is** an advance. Recording costs fronted by a bandmate **are** an advance. Tour support, a manager's float, a studio's deferred rate: all advances, all recoupable, none of them ever called that, and — decisively — **none of them tracked by anybody**. Two musicians who agree "you cover the studio, take it back off the first earnings" have created a cross-collateralised recoupable balance with no record and no arithmetic, and they will fall out about it in eighteen months. That is the same failure as an uncaptured split (`problem-statement.md`) in a different currency, and it is squarely in the platform's thesis rather than adjacent to it. |
| DT-03 | Showing the recoupment position is a reporting feature | It is a number on a screen | ✅ **CONFIRMED as necessary, and re-scoped — it is a power-asymmetry feature wearing a dashboard's clothes.** The reason "am I recouped?" is the most-asked and least-answered question in music is not technical difficulty; it is that **the only party who can compute it is the party that benefits from the artist not knowing**. The number is trivial arithmetic over data the label already has. Its scarcity is structural, not computational. A platform that computes it continuously, from statements the artist supplied themselves (`10.02.01` — **no label cooperation required**), changes who holds the information — and does it without needing anyone's permission or accreditation. That makes this one of the highest-leverage-per-line-of-code features on the map, and it should not be MoSCoW'd as a reporting nicety. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The position is **derived, never stored** | DT-01. Same principle as `credits-attribution-index.md` D-09, reached independently from money rather than provenance. |
| D-02 | An advance is **any recoupable fronted amount**, whatever it was called | DT-02. A producer fee, a covered studio bill and a tour float are all advances; none are ever named as such. |
| D-03 | **Cross-collateralisation is shown explicitly** | It is the mechanism by which a success pays for a failure, and it is invisible to nearly everyone it happens to. |
| D-04 | An unrecorded advance makes the position **unknown, not zero** | An artist who looks recouped and isn't will be clawed back. |
| D-05 | This feature computes **payability**; `10.03.01` computes **earnings**. They never merge | `10.03.01` DT-02. The separation is what lets a statement inform rather than conceal. |
