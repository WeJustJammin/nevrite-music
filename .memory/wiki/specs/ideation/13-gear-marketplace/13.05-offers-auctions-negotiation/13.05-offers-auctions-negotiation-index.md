## Overview

The mechanisms by which a price is agreed rather than stated: offers and counter-offers, timed auctions, and the reverse case where a buyer posts what they want and sellers come to them. Used gear does not sell at list price — haggling is the cultural norm of the category, not a feature request — and each of these three is a different answer to "how do buyer and seller find a number?"

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: offers, auctions and ISO are three price-formation mechanisms that interact and conflict — an ISO post is answered with an offer, an auction and an offer on the same unit contend for the same claim, and all three race the same inventory (13.03.03).
