# Feature: Offers & Counter-Offers

> **Number**: 13.05.01
> **Parent**: [Offers, Auctions & Negotiation](./13.05-offers-auctions-negotiation-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

A buyer proposes a price below asking; the seller accepts, declines or counters. This is how used gear actually trades — the asking price is an opening position, and a marketplace that only supports Buy Now is fighting the category's culture. Acceptance is a binding claim on the unit, which is what separates this from chat.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (both sides) | Offers on gear they want; fields offers on gear they're selling. The persona's whole relationship with the category — nobody pays sticker for a used pedal. Wants offers in one place, not scattered across DMs. |
| Producer | Full (both sides) | Same, with longer, more considered negotiations on expensive outboard where a 10% move is real money and the buyer may be a stranger in another country. |
| Operator | Full (both sides) | Fields offers at volume across fleet stock; needs bulk rules (auto-accept above X, auto-decline below Y) or it becomes a full-time job. |
| Fan | Read-only (buyer side only) | May make an offer — it's a buying act. No seller side (13.03.01 Role Lens). Least likely to know that offering is normal and expected; the affordance must invite it, not hide it behind "contact seller". |

## Behavior

An offer is a price proposal with a lifetime. The seller may accept (claiming the unit — 13.03.03), decline, or counter (which transfers the decision back). Offers are **structured**, not messages: they carry an amount, an expiry and a state, so acceptance can be atomic. Live offers restrict the seller's price edits (13.03.02 D-03).

### Happy Path

1. Buyer makes an offer below asking
2. Seller is notified; reviews with the price guide (13.04) alongside
3. Seller counters
4. Buyer accepts the counter
5. Unit is claimed atomically; buyer proceeds to checkout (13.06.01)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Multiple offers on one unit | Seller may only accept one — the second acceptance must fail atomically (13.03.03 Edge Cases) | Seller: "You already accepted an offer on this unit" |
| Buyer offers, then buys via Buy Now | Offer becomes moot; must be withdrawn cleanly | Offer auto-withdrawn on purchase |
| Buyer ghosts after acceptance | Depends on whether acceptance binds (sub-domain Q-01) — the reservation expires either way | Unit returns to `active`; ghosting feeds buyer reputation (CX-M08) |
| Seller accepts, then can't ship | Seller cancellation; costs them under CX-M08 | Cancellation reason captured |
| Offer on a bundle (13.03.05) | Bundle atomicity means an offer is on the whole bundle, never a constituent | "Splitting is a conversation, not an offer" (13.03.05 D-03) |
| Lowball spam | High-volume derisory offers waste seller attention | Seller-set minimum offer threshold; auto-decline below it |
| Offer pending when seller wants to reprice | Blocked (13.03.02 D-03) | "Withdraw the pending offer first" |
| Offer accepted on a unit that gets a stolen-registry hit (13.03.07) | Claim exists; listing held | Escalates to domain 24 before money moves |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Offer submitting | Inline spinner |
| Empty | No offers on a listing | Seller: "No offers yet". Buyer: "Make an offer" affordance |
| Populated | Offers exist | Seller: offer list with amounts, expiries, buyer standing |
| Error | Accept fails (raced) | "This unit was claimed" — never an ambiguous failure |
| Partial | Countered, awaiting buyer | "Countered — awaiting response (expires in N)" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Offers can be handled through messaging (CX-M06) — it's a conversation about price | Haggling is social, and the platform already has a contextual inbox serving this domain | ❌ REJECTED: an offer must be **atomically acceptable**, because acceptance claims a qty=1 unit (sub-domain D-01). A message saying "ok, £900 then" cannot be arbitrated against a simultaneous Buy Now — there is no state to lock. Messaging *around* an offer is right and expected (CX-M06); the offer itself must be a structured object with a state machine. This distinction is what makes 13.03.03's single-arbitration-point invariant implementable at all. |
| DT-02 | Offers are a buyer-side feature — a way to pay less | The obvious framing | ⚠️ REFRAMED: offers are the domain's **price-discovery instrument in thin markets** — and they run exactly where the price guide is mute (13.04.03 DT-02: thin is permanent for most of this catalog). On a model with two comps in six years, the guide cannot say what it's worth; a live offer *can*, because a real buyer with real money has stated a number. **Declined offers are therefore evidence too** — a pattern of offers clustering at £900 against a £1,400 asking price is market information the comp set will never contain. Whether that becomes an input to 13.04 is a real question (Q-03), and it partially answers 13.04.03 Q-03's "is the guide worth building if it's mute on the rare?" — the mechanism that speaks where the guide can't already exists in this file. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | An offer is a structured object with a state machine, never a message | Per DT-01 — atomicity requires state. |
| D-02 | Acceptance claims the unit atomically through the single arbitration point | Per sub-domain D-01 and 13.03.03 CX-01 synthesis Q5. |
| D-03 | Sellers may set a minimum offer threshold with auto-decline | Lowball spam is an attention tax the Operator persona cannot absorb at fleet volume. |
| D-04 | The offer affordance is prominent, not hidden | Offering is the category norm; the Fan persona won't know that unless the UI says so. |
