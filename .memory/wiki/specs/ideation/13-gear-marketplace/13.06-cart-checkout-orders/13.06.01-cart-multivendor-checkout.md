# Feature: Cart & Multi-Vendor Checkout

> **Number**: 13.06.01
> **Parent**: [Cart, Checkout & Orders](./13.06-cart-checkout-orders-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Buying from several independent sellers in one transaction. The gear-specific difficulty is that a cart containing a pedal and a Hammond organ has two fulfilment paths with nothing in common — one goes in a padded envelope, the other needs a freight quote, a lift gate and possibly a refusal — and one of them may be legally unshippable to the buyer's country. This is not standard multi-vendor checkout with different sellers; it is checkout where line items have irreconcilable physics.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (as buyer) | Buys a pedal and a set of strings from two sellers; expects one payment. Hits the freight cliff when they add something heavy without expecting it. |
| Producer | Full (as buyer) | Buys outboard, frequently internationally, where landed cost (13.07.02) can be 30% on top and *must* be visible before commitment, not after. |
| Operator | Full (as buyer) | Buys at volume for a venue/studio; most likely to hit oversize freight, and the persona checking out from a phone at a loading dock. |
| Fan | Full (as buyer) | **The one place in this domain where Fan has full primary access.** Buys strings or a first instrument. Zero tolerance for professional complexity — will abandon at the first thing they don't understand, and a freight quote that arrives after they've entered card details is exactly that thing. |

## Behavior

The cart holds intent, **not claims** (13.03.03 D-02) — a unit in a cart is still buyable by anyone. Checkout splits by vendor: each vendor's lines get their own fulfilment quote, eligibility check and order. **Checkout may legitimately partially fail**, and that has to be a designed outcome rather than an error, because a cart's fulfilability is only known per-vendor, per-destination, per-item.

### Happy Path

1. Buyer adds items from two sellers
2. Buyer enters a destination; system quotes per vendor and checks eligibility per item
3. Cart shows per-vendor totals including freight and landed cost
4. Buyer pays once; system creates one order per vendor
5. Claims are taken atomically at this point (13.03.03) — not before

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **One item can't ship to the destination (CITES — 13.07.03)** | Cannot fail the whole cart; cannot silently drop it | "The rosewood-fingerboard Martin can't ship to your country — remove it and continue?" Explicit, itemised, and *before* payment |
| Cart contains a 7ft grand piano | Freight, not parcel — quote may need a human or may be impossible | Freight items are quoted differently and may say "quote required" rather than a number |
| A unit is claimed by someone else while it sits in the cart | Expected — a cart is not a claim | "Someone bought the Jazzmaster while it was in your cart" + routes onward (13.03.03 D-01) |
| Payment succeeds, one vendor's claim fails | Partial capture, or full reversal? Genuinely hard | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see DT-02 |
| Landed cost unknown at checkout | Buyer commits blind to a possible 30% surprise | Must be quoted, not estimated after the fact (13.07.02) |
| Stolen-registry hit lands between cart and capture | Re-screen at checkout (13.03.07 D-02) | Sale held before money moves |
| Buyer is in a 230V country buying a 100V amp | Warning, not a block (13.01.05 D-01) | Second warning point, acknowledged at checkout |
| Mixed cart with digital goods (domain 14) | Undefined | Sub-domain Q-01 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Quote calculation | Per-vendor quote spinner — the slowest step; freight APIs are not fast |
| Empty | Nothing in cart | Empty cart with routes back to search |
| Populated | Items + destination known | Per-vendor breakdown with freight and landed cost |
| Error | Payment fails | Cart preserved intact; claims released |
| Partial | Some lines ineligible | Itemised eligibility report — never a generic "checkout unavailable" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Multi-vendor checkout is a solved pattern — split the payment, create N orders | Every marketplace does this; CX-M01 already owns the money split | ❌ REJECTED: the *payment* split is solved and is CX-M01's (sub-domain D-01). The **fulfilability** split is not, and it is unique to this domain. In digital goods (14) every line delivers instantly and identically; in services (05) there is no shipping at all. Here, each line has its own freight class, its own export legality, its own voltage question, and its own possibility of being **undeliverable at any price**. A cart is therefore not "N orders with one payment" — it is N *independent feasibility questions* that must all be answered before a single payment is authorised. This is the clearest vindication of D-14 in the whole domain. |
| DT-02 | Payment should be authorised first, then claims taken | Standard checkout ordering — money first, fulfilment after | ❌ REJECTED: it inverts the risk. Authorising payment before claiming a qty=1 unit means a buyer can be charged for an amp that was sold 200ms earlier — and refunding is not a fix, because the buyer wanted *that* amp (13.03.03 D-01). Claims must be taken **before or atomically with** authorisation. But this creates the genuinely hard case in Edge Cases: multi-vendor means N claims and one payment, and a claim can fail after another succeeded. **Either the payment is per-vendor (N authorisations, partial success permitted) or the cart is all-or-nothing.** The former is honest and complex; the latter is simple and punishes the buyer for one vendor's race. Unresolved, and it interacts directly with CX-M01's escrow model. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A cart holds intent, never a claim | Per 13.03.03 D-02 — cart-holds on qty=1 gear let one user freeze what others want. |
| D-02 | Eligibility is resolved per item, per destination, **before** payment | Per DT-01 — a cart is N feasibility questions, not one. |
| D-03 | Claims are taken before or atomically with authorisation, never after | Per DT-02. |
| D-04 | Ineligible lines are itemised and explained, never silently dropped or generically failed | The Fan persona abandons at the first unexplained thing. |
