## Overview

Where a claim becomes a transaction: the multi-vendor cart, the checkout that has to quote wildly different freight per vendor and may legitimately be unable to fulfil part of itself, the order lifecycle through to delivery — and the settlement hook that writes ownership into the registry. It is the domain's money path and, per D-18, the moment the gear domain contributes to the provenance record rather than merely consuming it.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: cart, order lifecycle, staged payment, service add-ons and ownership transfer are distinct capabilities with heavy interaction — settlement triggers transfer, transfer must reverse on refund, and add-ons attach to order lines that ship separately.
