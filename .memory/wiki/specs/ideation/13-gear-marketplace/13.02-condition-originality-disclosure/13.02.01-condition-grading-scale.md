# Feature: Condition Grading Scale

> **Number**: 13.02.01
> **Parent**: [Condition, Originality & Disclosure](./13.02-condition-originality-disclosure-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

A standardised, enumerated scale describing the physical and functional state of a unit — Mint / Excellent / Very Good / Good / Fair / Poor / Non-Functioning, or equivalent. It is the single most price-relevant attribute after the model itself, and it must be an enum rather than prose, because the price guide (13.04) needs to compare like with like and buyers need to filter.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Picks a grade when listing (with per-grade definitions visible at the point of choice, not buried in a help page); filters by grade when buying. The persona most likely to over-grade their own gear — not dishonestly, but because they're fond of it. |
| Producer | Full | Grades studio gear where "functional" matters more than cosmetic — a scuffed rack unit that works perfectly is a different proposition from a mint one that drifts. Uses the functional dimension harder than the cosmetic one. |
| Operator | Full | Grades ex-rental and ex-backline stock, which is systematically road-worn. Needs the scale to say "heavily used but professionally maintained" without that reading as "broken". |
| Fan | Read-only | Reads grades as a buyer. Cannot list, so never grades. Most reliant on per-grade definitions being plain-language — the persona has no calibration for what "Very Good" means in gear. |

## Behavior

Grade is a required enum on every listing. Each grade carries a **published definition** shown inline at selection. Grade is constrained by disclosed flaws (13.02.02) — a unit with a disclosed crack cannot be graded Mint. Grade is **independent of originality** (13.02.03) per D-01: a refinished instrument can be Mint.

### Happy Path

1. Seller reaches the condition step of listing creation
2. System shows the grade enum with the definition of each grade inline
3. Seller selects a grade
4. System cross-checks against disclosed flaws and challenges impossible combinations
5. Grade publishes on the listing and becomes a buyer-facing facet

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Grade contradicts disclosed flaws (Mint + "headstock repair") | Challenge at listing time; do not silently accept | "A repaired headstock isn't Mint — did you mean Excellent?" |
| Cosmetically mint, functionally broken | The scale must carry both dimensions or it lies | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — whether this is one enum or two (cosmetic + functional) is unresolved. See DT-02. |
| Systematic over-grading by a seller | Buyer disputes accumulate against them; feeds seller performance (CX-M08) | Pattern surfaces in seller scorecard, not on the individual listing |
| Vintage gear where "wear" is desirable (relic'd, played-in) | Grade is not desirability. A road-worn 1966 amp is Good and worth more than a Mint reissue | Grade stated; the market prices it (13.04) |
| Non-functioning / for-parts | Must be a first-class grade, not an afterthought — a large real segment | "Non-functioning / for parts" as an explicit grade |
| Seller declines to grade | Not permitted — required field | Cannot publish |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Listing form render | Grade enum with definitions |
| Empty | Not yet selected | Required-field prompt |
| Populated | Grade selected | Grade badge on listing with definition on hover/tap |
| Error | Cross-check service down | Allow the grade; flag for async review — never stall the listing |
| Partial | Grade set, flaws not yet disclosed | Grade provisional until 13.02.02 completes; cross-check runs on completion |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Grading should be free-text so sellers can describe nuance | Sellers genuinely want to explain; gear condition is nuanced | ❌ REJECTED: prose is unfilterable, uncomparable and unenforceable. The price guide cannot build a comp set from "some honest wear, plays great". Prose belongs *alongside* the enum as a description, never *instead of* it. Sub-domain D-02. |
| DT-02 | One scale can carry cosmetic and functional condition together | Reverb's scale does exactly this and buyers read it fluently | ⚠️ CONFIRMED WITH TENSION: a single scale is what the market already understands (Q-01 — portability of comps argues hard for the de-facto standard). But it genuinely fails on the "mint-looking, broken" and "ugly, flawless" cases, which are common in studio outboard — precisely the Producer persona's segment. A second functional dimension would fix it and break comp portability. **This is a real trade-off requiring the owner, not an agent call.** |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Constrained by **13.02.02 Mandatory Flaw Disclosure** ([path](./13.02.02-mandatory-flaw-disclosure.md)) — disclosed flaws bound the achievable grade.
- Independent of **13.02.03 Mod & Originality Disclosure** ([path](./13.02.03-modification-originality-disclosure.md)) — orthogonal axis per sub-domain D-01.
- Feeds **13.04.01 Price Guide & Comps** ([path](../13.04-price-discovery-market-data/13.04.01-price-guide-comps.md)) — grade is a comp-set dimension; a guide that ignores grade is noise.
- Consumes **CX-M16 Search & Discovery** — grade is a primary buyer facet.
- Feeds **CX-M08 Reviews, Ratings & Portable Reputation** — systematic over-grading is a seller-performance signal.
- Escalates to **Domain 24 Trust, Safety & Disputes** ([path](../../24-trust-safety-disputes/)) — "not as described" is the single most common gear dispute.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Grade is a required enum with inline published definitions | Per DT-01 — definitions must be at the point of choice or sellers guess. |
| D-02 | Impossible grade/flaw combinations are challenged at listing time, not silently accepted | Cheapest possible dispute prevention: one prompt now vs. an adjudication later. |
| D-03 | Grade cross-check failure never blocks publish | Consistent with the domain-wide rule that the listing flow must not stall. |
