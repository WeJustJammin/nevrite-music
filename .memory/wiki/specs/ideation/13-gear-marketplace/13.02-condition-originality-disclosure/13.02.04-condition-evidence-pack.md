# Feature: Condition Evidence Pack & Dispute Baseline

> **Number**: 13.02.04
> **Parent**: [Condition, Originality & Disclosure](./13.02-condition-originality-disclosure-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE] — **Deep Think addition** (not in the sweep's candidate list)
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The timestamped record of what this unit demonstrably looked like at listing and at dispatch, sealed so it can serve as the factual baseline when a buyer says it arrived damaged. Disclosure (13.02.02/03) records what the seller *claimed*; the evidence pack records what was *shown*, when. Without it, every damage dispute is one memory against another — which is the exact failure the platform's whole thesis exists to prevent, applied to chattel instead of splits.

**Why this exists as a node**: the sweep listed disclosure and it listed damage claims (#13), but nothing connecting them. Damage claims without a pre-dispatch baseline are unresolvable — the claims process presupposes an artifact the candidate list never created.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (both sides) | As seller: photos taken at listing are sealed into the pack; prompted to add dispatch photos when printing a label. As buyer: prompted to photograph on arrival before unpacking further, which is what protects *them*. |
| Producer | Full (both sides) | Same. Disproportionately ships high-value outboard where a damage claim (13.07.04) turns on whether the transformer was already loose. |
| Operator | Full (both sides) | Ships/receives fleet gear constantly; the pack is the only practical way to run condition accountability at volume. Dispatch capture must be phone-fast at a loading dock — the persona's stated constraint. |
| Fan | Read-only (own orders) | Sees the seller's evidence pack for gear they bought, and is prompted to capture arrival evidence. Cannot see other people's packs. No seller side. |

## Behavior

The pack accumulates automatically from artifacts the flow already produces — listing photos, required flaw photos (13.02.02 D-03), dispatch photos, arrival photos — each timestamped and immutable once sealed. **It is a byproduct, not a chore**: the seller is never asked to "build an evidence pack", they are asked for photos at moments they were already taking photos. Sealed at dispatch; arrival evidence appends but never overwrites.

### Happy Path

1. Seller lists; listing photos and required flaw photos are captured into the pack
2. Seller prints a shipping label; system prompts for 2–3 dispatch photos including packing
3. Pack seals at dispatch with a timestamp; both parties can view it
4. Buyer receives; is prompted to photograph on arrival before full unpacking
5. No dispute: pack is inert. Dispute: the pack is the baseline both sides argue against

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Seller skips dispatch photos | Pack seals with listing evidence only — weaker but valid. **Not blocking** | "Without dispatch photos, a damage claim is harder to defend" — consequence stated, choice left |
| Buyer opens a damage claim with no arrival photos | Claim proceeds; evidentiary weight is lower | Prompt at claim time: "Do you have photos from when it arrived?" |
| Photos are of a different unit entirely (fraud) | Pack cannot detect this alone; serial visible in dispatch photos is the counterweight | Escalates to domain 24 |
| Damage occurred in transit, genuinely nobody's fault | Exactly what the pack exists to establish — routes to insurance (13.07.04) not to blame | Claim routed to carrier/insurance path |
| Listing photos are stock/catalog images not the actual unit | Unit-specific media is required for used gear; stock images defeat the entire mechanism | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — enforcement approach unresolved |
| Local pickup (13.11) — no dispatch, no transit | Pack is largely moot; condition is verified in person. Different failure mode entirely | Pack reduced to listing evidence |
| Pack contents requested after account deletion | Retention vs. erasure conflict — CX-M18 | `[PENDING]` — see Q-02 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | An evidence pack is a separate artifact the seller assembles when needed | The naive framing — "upload your evidence" at dispute time | ❌ REJECTED: evidence assembled *at dispute time* is exactly the reconstruction failure the problem statement names as the root cause ("no system is present at the moment of creation, so the record is always a reconstruction"). By then the unit is in another country and the photos were never taken. The pack must accumulate **as a byproduct of the flow**, at the moment the fact is true. This is the platform's founding thesis (D-18) applied to physical goods — and the strongest argument that this node belongs in the domain at all. |
| DT-02 | Blocking dispatch until photos are captured would make the pack reliable | Reliability instinct — a mandatory pack is a complete pack | ❌ REJECTED: it inverts the incentive and copies the failure the Producer persona is defined by ("closes a project without capturing splits"). The fix there was **make the lazy path the correct path**, not add a gate. Here: prompt at label-print (already in the flow, already holding a phone), state the consequence, never block. A blocked dispatch is an angry seller; a defended seller who skipped photos once and lost a claim is a seller who photographs forever. |
