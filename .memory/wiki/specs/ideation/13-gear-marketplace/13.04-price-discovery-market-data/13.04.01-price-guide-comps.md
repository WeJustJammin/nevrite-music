# Feature: Gear Price Guide & Comps

> **Number**: 13.04.01
> **Parent**: [Price Discovery & Market Data](./13.04-price-discovery-market-data-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

For a given catalog model at a given condition and originality, what have units actually sold for — the transaction history, the range, the trend. Used gear has no manufacturer price; value is entirely comparative, and the only honest source is completed sales. This is the domain's most-consulted read surface and the reason the catalog (13.01) has to be right.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Two moments: "what's mine worth?" before selling, and "am I being ripped off?" before buying. The persona's stated pain — a career of fragments — includes never knowing what their own gear is worth without trawling completed listings by hand. |
| Producer | Read-only | Values studio inventory, often for insurance or for a partner buyout rather than a sale. Hits thin comp sets constantly — rare outboard sells a handful of times a decade. |
| Operator | Read-only | Prices ex-fleet stock and values an asset register. The only persona likely to consult the guide at volume, which is a distinct surface from a single model page. |
| Fan | Read-only | Checks whether a first guitar is fairly priced. Zero calibration — most likely to read a midpoint as a fact and least likely to notice a two-comp sample. **The persona the confidence model (13.04.03) is written for.** |

## Behavior

Comps are completed sales (13.03.02's immutable sold listings), bucketed by model × condition × originality (D-01). The guide reports a distribution — range, median, recent trend — not a single number. Every figure is presented with its evidence: how many comps, over what period. **A price without its sample size is not a price**, it's an assertion.

Concretely, for a chosen bucket the guide surfaces four things: (1) a **range** — the low and high of qualifying sold prices, outliers shown not hidden (D-01); (2) the **median**, chosen over the mean because a small sample with one celebrity sale or one panic dump has a mean that describes no real transaction; (3) a **trend** — whether the bucket is rising, flat, or falling, computed over a rolling window whose length is set by data density (a Stratocaster resolves in weeks, a rare compressor may need years) rather than a fixed calendar period; and (4) the **evidence header** — comp count and date span — carried on the figure itself, never in a footnote. The specific minimum comp count below which the bucket may not quote at all, and the exact trend-window bands, are owned by 13.04.03 (`[PENDING — see 13.04.03; do not fabricate a threshold here]`). This file owns *what is shown and when it may be shown*; the aggregation and materialisation pipeline is architecture (routed to `/create-prd-architecture`).

### Happy Path

1. User lands on a model page, or on the guide from search
2. System selects comps: this model, matching condition band, matching originality state
3. Guide renders the distribution — range, median, trend, comp count, period
4. User expands to see the actual sold listings behind the number
5. Seller uses it to price (13.04.02); buyer uses it to judge an asking price

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Fewer than N comps for a model/condition bucket | The bucket cannot support a number — handled by 13.04.03, not by widening silently | Confidence-gated; see that file |
| Bucketing too narrowly leaves every bucket empty | Widening is the obvious fix and it silently lies — a "1962 Jazzmaster" bucket widened to "all Jazzmasters" spans 20× in price | Widening must be **disclosed** when it happens, never silent |
| An outlier sale (a celebrity-owned unit, a mispriced panic sale) | Skews a small sample badly | Distribution not mean; outliers visible, not smoothed away |
| Provenance-bearing unit (13.03.06) | "Played on 11 records" has no comp set — provenance is unpriceable by comparison | Guide must decline to price it rather than quote the base model. See DT-02. |
| Model merged/unmerged by moderation (13.01.02) | The comp set changes underneath a live number | Recompute; the blast radius 13.01.01 DT-02 warned about, realised here |
| Comps exist but are all stale | A bucket has enough sales to quote, but the most recent is years old — the number is real yet no longer describes today's market | Recency surfaced as prominently as count; a stale bucket is flagged, not quoted as current. Band ownership: 13.04.03 |
| Currency and region differences | The same model sells for very different money in the EU vs. US (import duty, availability) | Region-scoped comps; CX-M20 |
| Seller games the guide with wash sales | Selling to yourself to manufacture comps | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — real, and the guide's credibility depends on it. See Q-02. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Model/guide page | Distribution skeleton |
| Empty | No comps at all | "No sales recorded yet" — honest, not a guess |
| Populated | Sufficient comps | Range, median, trend, comp count, period, expandable evidence |
| Error | Guide unavailable | Section omitted; listings still function |
| Partial | Thin comps | Confidence-gated rendering — 13.04.03 owns this |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The price guide can launch with the marketplace | It's listed as a core capability in the domain's justification ("price guide from comps"), so it reads as foundational | ❌ REJECTED: a price guide **is** its comp set. At launch there are zero completed sales, therefore zero comps, therefore no guide — for months at least, and for years on any model that isn't a Stratocaster. The guide is a *consequence* of marketplace liquidity, not a feature that can be shipped alongside it. This forces a real strategic choice (sub-domain Q-01): ingest external market data to bootstrap, or accept the guide is a year-two feature. **This directly undercuts treating the price guide as part of the domain's launch justification.** |
| DT-02 | More data always improves the guide, so ingest and bucket everything | Standard data instinct | ❌ REJECTED: this domain's own structure defeats it. A provenanced instrument (13.03.06) is *sui generis* — one of one — and averaging it into the base model's comps corrupts the base model's number *and* fails to price the unit. Likewise a heavily modified unit (13.02.03) has no peers. The guide must **decline to price** the unpriceable rather than quote a number that describes a different object. The instinct to always produce an answer is precisely what makes price guides untrustworthy on the units where money is actually at stake. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Report a distribution with its sample size, never a bare number | A price without its evidence is an assertion wearing a statistic's clothes. |
| D-02 | Bucket widening is disclosed, never silent | Per Edge Cases — a silently widened bucket is the most plausible way this feature lies. |
| D-03 | Decline to price the unpriceable (provenanced, heavily modified, one-of-one) | Per DT-02. |
