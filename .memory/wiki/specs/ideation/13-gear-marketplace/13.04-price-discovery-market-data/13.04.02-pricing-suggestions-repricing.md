# Feature: Pricing Suggestions & Repricing

> **Number**: 13.04.02
> **Parent**: [Price Discovery & Market Data](./13.04-price-discovery-market-data-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Turning the price guide into an action: suggesting a price while a seller is listing, telling them when a live listing has drifted out of the market, and — for sellers who want it — adjusting automatically. It exists because "why isn't this selling?" is the seller's most common question and the guide already holds the answer.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (own listings) | Gets a suggested range at listing time and a nudge when a listing has sat unsold below the market's attention. Wants a hint, not an algorithm — the persona sells a few items and has an opinion about each. |
| Producer | Full (own) | Suggestions are frequently useless here — rare outboard has no comps (13.04.03). The honest answer is often "we can't suggest a price", which this persona will respect and a bad suggestion would not survive. |
| Operator | Full (own) | The only persona plausibly wanting **automatic** repricing across a fleet. Also the persona whose auto-repricing could race a competitor's auto-repricing into a spiral. See DT-02. |
| Fan | None | No listing surface, so no repricing. Fans are affected only as buyers, by prices that move — which is why 13.03.02 D-03 blocks price edits under a live offer. |

## Behavior

Two modes. **Suggestion** is passive: a range with its evidence and confidence, shown at listing time and on a stale listing. **Auto-repricing** is active: opt-in, bounded by a seller-set floor, adjusting a live listing against the market. Both consume 13.04.01 and are gated by 13.04.03 — no confidence, no suggestion.

### Happy Path

1. Seller reaches the price step of listing creation
2. System shows a suggested range with comp count and confidence
3. Seller prices inside, above or below it — freely
4. Weeks later the listing hasn't sold; system nudges with current market position
5. Seller adjusts, or ignores it

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| No comps / thin market | **No suggestion.** Silence beats a fabricated number | "Not enough sales data to suggest a price" — 13.04.03 governs |
| Unit has provenance (13.03.06) or heavy mods (13.02.03) | Base-model comps don't describe it; suggesting them undervalues it, possibly badly | Suggestion withheld with the reason stated — the seller must know *why* |
| Auto-reprice would cross the seller's floor | Never cross it | Reprice halts; seller notified |
| Auto-reprice under a live offer | Blocked by 13.03.02 D-03 — an offer is against a stated price | Listing skipped with reason |
| Two sellers auto-repricing against each other | Race to the floor with no human in the loop | See DT-02 — a real market-structure risk, not a bug |
| Suggestion becomes an anchor everyone follows | The guide stops measuring the market and starts *setting* it | See DT-01. Genuinely unresolved. |
| Seller prices far above the suggestion deliberately | Entirely legitimate — they know something the comps don't | No friction, no warning, no penalty |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Price step reached | Inline suggestion skeleton |
| Empty | No comps | "Not enough data to suggest a price" |
| Populated | Suggestion available | Range + comp count + confidence + evidence link |
| Error | Guide unavailable | Field stays manual; never blocks |
| Partial | Low confidence | Wider range, prominent caveat, or withheld entirely per 13.04.03 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A price suggestion is a neutral readout of the market | It's derived from real sold data, so it looks like measurement | ❌ REJECTED: a suggestion shown at the moment of pricing is an **anchor**, and anchors are causal. If enough sellers accept it, the next comp set is made of prices the guide suggested — the guide begins measuring its own output. It stops being evidence and becomes a self-fulfilling index, while *looking* more authoritative as its sample grows. This is a market-structure consequence nobody in the candidate list considered, and it is why suggestion must be a **range with visible evidence** rather than a number — a range with 6 comps behind it invites judgement; "£1,240" invites compliance. |
| DT-02 | Auto-repricing is a convenience feature for high-volume sellers | It reads as a dealer productivity tool, which is how every marketplace ships it | ⚠️ CONFIRMED WITH CONSTRAINT: it is that, *and* it is an automated-agent market. Two Operators auto-repricing against each other on comparable stock will race toward their floors with nobody deciding anything — the well-documented retail-algorithm failure mode. In a qty=1 non-fungible market the damage is bounded (units aren't interchangeable, so the race has no perfect substitute to converge on), which is a genuine structural mitigation. But on the fungible half of this domain (13.03.05 counted stock — strings, valves, new pedals) the goods **are** interchangeable and the mitigation evaporates. Auto-repricing on counted stock is where this bites, and that is exactly the segment a dealer would enable it on first. Bounds — floor, rate limit, adjustment ceiling — are load-bearing. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Suggestions are ranges with visible evidence, never a single number | Per DT-01 — a range invites judgement; a number invites compliance and closes the feedback loop. |
| D-02 | No confidence, no suggestion — silence over fabrication | The Producer persona's segment is mostly thin-market; a bad suggestion there destroys trust in the whole surface. |
| D-03 | Auto-repricing is opt-in, floor-bounded and rate-limited | Per DT-02. |
| D-04 | Pricing away from the suggestion carries no friction or penalty | Sellers routinely know things the comps don't (provenance, a matched pair, a genuinely rare finish). |
