## Overview

What a piece of gear is actually worth, derived from what units like it actually sold for — plus the pricing guidance sellers get from it, and the honesty about when there isn't enough evidence to say. Used gear has no list price; a 1962 Jazzmaster is worth what the last few sold for, adjusted for condition and originality. The price guide is the domain's strongest public surface and its most dangerous one: quoted with false confidence on a thin comp set, it becomes a number people trade real money against.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: the guide, the repricing guidance derived from it, and the confidence model that governs when either may speak are three capabilities that interact continuously — repricing consumes the guide, and the confidence model gates both.
