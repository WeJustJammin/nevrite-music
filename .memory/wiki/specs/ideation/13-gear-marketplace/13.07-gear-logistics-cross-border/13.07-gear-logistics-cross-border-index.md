## Overview

The physics the domain index calls brutal: moving objects that are heavy, fragile, irreplaceable, sometimes made of endangered species, and frequently worth more than the vehicle carrying them. Generic carrier rates, labels and tracking are **not** here — they were extracted to CX-M14 when the domain map was ratified. What remains is what CX-M14 cannot know: that a Hammond organ is a freight shipment with a lift gate, that a rosewood fingerboard needs a CITES permit, that landed cost on a $4k preamp can add 30%, and that a damage claim on a $40k instrument is not a parcel claim.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: freight physics, customs and landed cost, restricted materials, and high-value damage claims are four distinct capabilities that interact — CITES determines whether freight is even legal, landed cost determines whether the buyer proceeds, and a damage claim's value determines the insurance regime that should have been chosen at dispatch.
