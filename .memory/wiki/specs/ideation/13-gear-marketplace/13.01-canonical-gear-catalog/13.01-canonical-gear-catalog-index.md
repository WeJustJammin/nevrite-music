## Overview

The shared, deduplicated record of every gear **model** that exists — make, model, year range, variant, finish, and the per-category attribute schema that makes faceted search possible. Individual listings and individual instruments both point at a catalog model; without that join, the price guide has no comp set, alerts have nothing to match, and facets degrade to text search. This is the spine the rest of the domain hangs from.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: model records, contribution/moderation, serial decoding, listing↔model matching and fitment relationships are five distinct capabilities that interact continuously — a contribution creates a model that matching then resolves against, and a bad merge silently corrupts every comp downstream.
