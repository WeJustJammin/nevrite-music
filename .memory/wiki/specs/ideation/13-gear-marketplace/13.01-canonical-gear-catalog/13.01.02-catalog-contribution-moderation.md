# Feature: Catalog Contribution & Moderation

> **Number**: 13.01.02
> **Parent**: [Canonical Gear Catalog](./13.01-canonical-gear-catalog-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The path by which a model that isn't in the catalog gets into it, the path by which a model that
is in the catalog gets **changed**, and the review that stops the catalog rotting. Gear's long
tail is boutique, vintage and one-off — no licensed dataset covers it, so the catalog must be
grown by the people listing the gear. That makes contribution a listing-flow concern, not an
admin chore, and makes moderation the thing standing between a useful catalog and a poisoned one.

The catalog is the spine (13.01 index): a wrong model record does not fail loudly — it produces a
price guide that quotes confident wrong numbers, facets that hide real listings, and a registry
(domain 15) whose unit identity points at the wrong type. **Every failure in this feature is
silent.** That single property drives almost every decision below: no time-boxed undo, no
FIFO queue, no self-approval, no irreversible merge.

Under D-18 (provenance is the wedge), this feature carries a second job the breadth pass missed:
the contribution record **is** provenance. Reverb's catalog cannot tell you who asserted that a
'62 Jazzmaster has a 7.25" fingerboard radius, or on what evidence. This one can, because every
attribute value on a model record carries its assertion chain (DT-08).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| **Musician** | Full (contribute + earn category-scoped review rights) | Hits "model not found" while listing a boutique pedal or an inherited one-off; fills a 4-field inline model form (make, model name, year range, category) plus the category's required attributes; keeps listing without waiting. As a buyer, never sees the mechanism — only the wrong comp it produced. Earns review rights only in categories where their contributions **survived** 90 days unmerged and uncorrected (D-05). **Recused** from reviewing any model bound to their own listing, active or sold within 90 days (D-07) — the multi-hyphenate case: the same human is contributor, moderator and seller in one category on one afternoon. |
| **Producer** | Full (contribute + earn review rights) | Same path; disproportionately the source of studio/outboard models (converters, preamps, rare mics, 500-series modules) that no consumer dataset carries. Hits the **year-is-identity** case hardest — a 1970s U87 and a 2010s U87Ai are different products sharing a name, and contributing one as a "variant" of the other is the highest-frequency bad contribution in this category. Same recusal rule. |
| **Operator** | Full (contribute at volume) | Contributes backline and PA models when building rental/venue inventory (13.10, domain 16). Often the only source for road-worn or discontinued production gear. **The only persona who hits bulk contribution** — a 300-item fleet intake cannot answer 40 inline "is this new?" prompts, so their path is the deferred-review queue (D-09), not the inline form. |
| **Fan** | None on contribution and moderation; **report-only** | Cannot create, edit, merge or review a model record. Rationale: contribution requires gear expertise the persona explicitly lacks, and the catalog's blast radius (13.01.01 DT-02) makes open contribution an attack surface. **But Fan is not mute**: Fan can flag a model record as wrong via the platform-wide report affordance (domain 24 / CX-M27). A report is not a contribution — it creates a moderation signal, never a record. This exists because CX-05 identifies Fan as *the persona who bears the harm of a wrong voltage attribute with no access to the mechanism that caused it*; leaving them with no channel at all makes the harm unreportable by the only person positioned to notice it. Fan otherwise sees the catalog read-only, including a provisional badge. |

## Behavior

Contribution is inline in the listing flow (13.01.04 routes here on match failure), never a
separate destination. **The listing flow never stalls** (domain D-03): a submitted model publishes
as `provisional` immediately, the listing binds to it and goes live, and moderation is
asynchronous. Nothing a moderator does can un-publish a listing.

### The four write operations

The breadth pass framed this feature as "contribution" — a create-only flow. It is four operations
with **inverted blast radius** (DT-03), and they cannot share a policy:

| Operation | Blast radius | Policy |
|---|---|---|
| **Create** | 1 listing (the contributor's own) | Inline, provisional-publish, no review gate. |
| **Edit** | Every listing + every registry entry bound to the model — unbounded | Tiered (below). An edit to a canonical model is the real attack surface, not a create. |
| **Merge** | Both models' full bound sets, plus every comp derived from either | Two moderators above threshold; always reversible; never automated (D-11). |
| **Split / unmerge** | The survivor's full bound set, re-divided | Same as merge, plus a re-pointing decision per bound listing. |

### Model lifecycle

```
                      ┌──────────────► canonical ──────────► disputed ──┐
                      │                    │                     │       │
  (contribution) → provisional             │                     └───────┘
                      │                    ▼                  (resolves back to
                      ├──────────────► merged ◄───┐            canonical, or to
                      │                    │      │            merged/edited)
                      └──────────────► rejected   └── unmerge ─┘
                                                       (indefinite, D-06)
```

| Transition | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `provisional` → `canonical` | Moderator acceptance, **or** corroboration (D-04) — never elapsed time alone. |
| `provisional` → `merged` | Dedupe confirmed at review; the provisional's bound listings re-point to the survivor. |
| `provisional` → `rejected` | One of the five rejection classes below. The bound listing survives; it does not. |
| `canonical` → `merged` | Two-moderator merge (D-08). |
| `canonical` → `disputed` | A manufacturer/builder contests their own record (DT-04), or an upheld Fan/seller report contradicts a safety-bearing attribute. Record stays live and readable; the contested attribute renders as contested. |
| `merged` → `canonical` | Unmerge. Available **indefinitely** (D-06). |

### Edit tiering

| Fields touched | Model state | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Identity-bearing (make, model name, year range, variant designation, category) | `canonical`, ≥1 bound listing or registry entry | **Review-before-apply.** The edit is a proposal; the live record does not change until accepted. |
| Safety-bearing (voltage, mains market) in a mains-powered category | any | **Review-before-apply**, top queue priority (D-03). |
| Additive gap-fill: blank → value, non-safety attribute | any | **Applies immediately**, tagged contributor-asserted with its assertion chain (D-10). Reversible by any moderator with standing. |
| Correction: value → different value, non-safety, non-identity | `provisional` | Applies immediately. |
| Correction: value → different value, non-safety, non-identity | `canonical` | Applies immediately if the contributor holds standing in the category; otherwise queued as a proposal. |

### The merge test (D-12)

Two records merge **iff one was created because the other could not be found** — i.e. they name
the same manufactured product under the same brand designation. Merge is a test of **identity**,
never of **equivalence**:

- ✅ "Klon Centaur" + "Klon Centuar" (typo) → merge.
- ✅ "Fender Jazzmaster 1962" + "Fender Jazzmaster '62" → merge.
- ❌ Harley Benton X + Sire Y, same OEM chassis, same components → **never merge.** They are distinct
  products with distinct comp sets and a large, real price delta. Related by a `same-chassis-as`
  link, not collapsed.
- ❌ Squier Classic Vibe + Fender American Vintage → never merge. Brand is identity here.
- ❌ Klon Centaur + Wampler Tumnus → never merge. A clone is its own legitimate product.
- ❌ 1962 original + 1999 reissue → never merge (13.01.04 DT-02 names this as the canonical trap).

"They're basically the same thing" is the most tempting bad merge in this domain and it flattens
exactly the distinctions the market prices. A moderator who cannot articulate *which record was
created because the other wasn't found* does not have a merge.

### Corroboration — how a provisional graduates without a human (D-04)

A `provisional` model auto-promotes to `canonical` when **all** of:

1. ≥3 listings bound to it, from ≥3 **distinct** seller accounts;
2. each of those accounts is ≥30 days old with ≥1 completed sale (sock-puppet floor);
3. zero unresolved dedupe candidates outstanding against it;
4. no safety-bearing attribute is blank or `unknown`;
5. it has not been reported (domain 24) in the preceding 14 days.

Rationale: independent sellers converging on the same model record is evidence no single
contributor can cheaply fake. **Usage is the moderation signal.** Auto-promotion is also the
cheapest operation to reverse — a canonical model that shouldn't exist is still merge-able;
a bad merge is not equivalently recoverable. Auto-promotion never merges, edits, or splits.

### Queue ordering

The moderation queue is ordered by **blast radius**, descending — `(bound listings + bound registry
entries)`, with safety-bearing items pinned above everything. Never FIFO. A provisional with 40
bound listings is 40× the exposure of one with 1, and FIFO reviews them in the order they were
unlucky enough to be created.

### Happy Path

1. Seller reaches the model field in listing creation and types "Fairfield Circuitry Barbershop v2"; 13.01.04 returns zero candidates.
2. System offers **"Add this model"** inline, with make (`Fairfield Circuitry`) and category (`Effects › Overdrive`) prefilled from the failed search — the seller re-types nothing.
3. Seller supplies model name, year range (`2015–`), and the category's required attributes. Fields validate on blur with inline errors; the form submits on Enter.
4. The category schema marks `mains_voltage` safety-bearing (this is a 9V pedal → not applicable → field absent). For a mains-powered category the field would be **required**, with an explicit **"I don't know"** option — never silently blank (D-03).
5. System runs dedupe (CX-M17) against **canonical and provisional** records, debounced 400 ms after the last keystroke on the model-name field, and shows "did you mean" candidates ranked with distinguishing attributes.
6. Seller confirms it is genuinely new. Optional: cites a source (maker's site, catalog scan, serial chart). Citation is optional here and required for identity/safety attributes on mains-powered or high-counterfeit categories (D-10).
7. Model is created as `provisional`. The record stores who asserted each attribute, when, and on what cited evidence (CX-M19).
8. A post-write dedupe sweep re-runs the new provisional against every record created in the preceding 15 minutes — this catches the concurrent-contribution race that a pre-submit check structurally cannot (DT-06).
9. Listing binds to the provisional model and **publishes immediately**. It is text-searchable and facet-searchable; its model page shows a `Provisional` badge; its price-guide panel shows "not enough comps yet" (13.04.03), not a fabricated number.
10. Two other sellers list the same pedal over the following weeks. On the third distinct qualifying seller with no outstanding dedupe candidate, the model auto-promotes to `canonical` (D-04). Badge clears. No human was required.
11. Contributor is notified of the promotion (CX-M07). The contribution is credited to their category standing **only after surviving 90 days unmerged and uncorrected** (D-05).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Contribution duplicates an existing model under a different name | Dedupe surfaces it pre-submit against canonical **and** provisional records. If it slips through, moderation merges and re-points the listing; the listing never breaks | "This looks like **[existing model]** — use that instead?" with the distinguishing attributes shown, plus "No, mine is different" |
| **Bad merge of two genuinely distinct models** | The worst failure in the sub-domain and the reason for D-06/D-08/D-12: it poisons every comp derived from either model, silently. Merge is always reversible with a full audit trail (CX-M19); unmerge has **no expiry** | Moderator sees merge history and the originating rationale; on unmerge, every affected listing re-points and every affected seller is notified of the comp change (D-13) |
| Seller inflates spec to make their unit look better (claims a rarer variant) | Model-level spec is not per-unit. Unit claims belong to 13.02 disclosure and are the seller's liability. Contribution cannot be used to manufacture a rarer type for one unit — a new variant designation is identity-bearing, so it is review-before-apply | Model page shows canonical spec with assertion provenance; listing claims are separately attributed to the seller |
| **Contribution queue outgrows moderation capacity** | **RESOLVED.** Three mechanisms, none of which is "wait": (a) provisional-publish means the backlog costs nobody a listing; (b) corroboration (D-04) drains the long tail without a human; (c) the queue is blast-radius-ordered with safety items pinned, so capacity is spent where exposure is. Time-based auto-canonicalization is explicitly **rejected** (DT-07) — it lets any bad actor win by waiting | Contributor sees "In review — your listing is live" with no ETA promised. Moderator sees a queue ordered by exposure with a pinned safety band. Backlog depth is an admin metric (CX-M27), never a user-facing one |
| Seller abandons the form mid-contribution | Draft listing retains the partial model input; **no orphan model record is created**. Nothing enters the queue | Draft resumable, model input preserved |
| **Two sellers contribute the same missing model concurrently** | Each pre-submit dedupe ran before the other record existed, so both pass — a pre-submit check cannot catch this by construction. The post-write 15-minute sweep (Happy Path 8) flags the pair; moderation merges; the loser's listing re-points to the survivor. **Neither seller is blamed, neither listing is interrupted** | Both listings stay live throughout. The losing contributor sees "merged into [survivor]"; the contribution still counts toward standing if it was correct (a race is not a mistake) |
| **Contribution is a unit, not a type** — "1962 Jazzmaster with Mastery bridge and Novak pickups" | The single most common bad contribution. 13.01.01's rule is absolute: a model is a type, never a unit. **Fix-forward, not reject**: moderator re-points the listing to the base model and routes the deltas to 13.02.03 modification disclosure | "This describes *your* instrument, not a model. We've linked it to **1962 Fender Jazzmaster** and moved the mods to your listing's disclosure — check it's right" |
| Contribution is a bundle ("pedalboard, 5 pedals") | Same class as above. Not a model. Routed to 13.03's bundle handling; base models linked where identifiable | "Bundles aren't catalog models — list this as a bundle instead" |
| **Contribution rejected as fabricated / not a real product** | Model is deleted. **The listing is not.** It falls back to unmatched-published (13.01.04 D-01) — text-searchable, no comps, no facets. This resolves the open rejection-behaviour item in CX-01 §2: a rejection costs the listing its catalog benefits, never its existence | "We couldn't verify this model, so your listing isn't linked to the catalog. It's still live. [Link it to an existing model] to get price data and filtered search" |
| Contribution is right but in the wrong category, or under a misspelled make | **Fix-forward.** Moderator corrects and accepts. Rejecting a correct contribution over a fixable field trains contributors to stop contributing | Contributor sees "Accepted — we moved it to Effects › Fuzz", not a rejection |
| **Safety-bearing attribute left blank** (mains category, no voltage) | Cannot happen silently: the field is **required** with an explicit "I don't know" (D-03). `unknown` ≠ absent — `unknown` renders as a loud warning downstream (13.01.05 D-02, fail loud on voltage), and blocks corroboration (D-04.4), pinning the model to the human queue at top priority | Contributor: "Mains voltage is required — pick one, or 'I don't know' if you're not sure." Buyer: "Mains voltage unconfirmed for this model — check the unit before you buy" |
| **Safety-bearing attribute contributed *wrong*** (230V asserted on a 100V Japan-market amp) | The genuine residual risk — required fields defeat blanks, not errors. Four mitigations, none of which blocks the listing: (1) safety-bearing values are review-before-apply on canonical models; (2) while `provisional`, the warning renders as contributor-asserted and unverified, not as fact; (3) the assertion chain names who claimed it (CX-M19), so it is attributable rather than anonymous; (4) any Fan or seller can report it (Role Lens), and an upheld report moves the record to `disputed`. **This resolves the CX-05 conflict without contradicting D-01** — the cost is one form field, not a stalled listing. Liability remains a user decision (13.01.05 Q-01) | Buyer sees "Japan-market (100V) — *contributor-supplied, unverified*" until a moderator confirms. Severity is never carried by colour alone (CX-M21) |
| **Moderator holds an active listing bound to the model under review** | Hard recusal (D-07). Includes sold-within-90-days and the *merge target*, not only the source — merging *into* the model your unit is bound to is the manipulation | Moderator sees the item greyed with "You have a listing bound to this — recused". It re-queues to another moderator |
| **The only moderator with standing in a thin category is the conflicted one** | Real and common — perhaps two people on the platform know vintage Japanese synth modules, and one of them is selling one. Recusal must not become deadlock: the item escalates to admin (CX-M27) **with the conflict disclosed**, and the corroboration path (D-04) still runs independently of any human | Contributor sees "In review" unchanged. Admin sees the item with the conflict stated, not hidden |
| **Moderator merges to manipulate comps** — the Discogs failure mode | The people who care enough to moderate a category are the people who trade in it (DT-05). Merge/split/unmerge on any model with ≥10 bound listings **or ≥1 registry entry** requires two moderators with standing, neither recused. A reversed merge suspends the initiating moderator's standing in that category pending review | Merge history is public on the model page — who, when, why, and who seconded |
| **Brand-distinct, spec-identical models proposed for merge** (Harley Benton / Sire / OEM twins) | Refused by the merge test (D-12). Linked `same-chassis-as`, never merged. Merging them merges comp sets whose price delta is the entire point | Moderator sees "These are different products — link as same-chassis instead?" |
| **A manufacturer or builder disputes their own model record** | Their contribution is welcome and weighted; their claim is **not authoritative** (DT-04). Record moves to `disputed`, the contested attribute renders as contested with both assertions visible, and a two-moderator decision resolves it. A boutique builder's own spec sheet is strong evidence; a large manufacturer's denial that an undocumented transition-era variant exists is not | Model page shows "Spec contested — manufacturer states X, catalog records Y", both attributed |
| **A merge lands while a bound listing is mid-checkout** | This resolves the open item in CX-03 §5. The merge applies to the record and the listing re-points immediately, but **anything rendered to a buyer inside an active checkout is snapshotted at checkout start and does not mutate mid-flow**. A comp set is displayed information, never a term of sale; terms are 13.06's and are immutable once in checkout regardless | Buyer sees nothing change. Post-completion, the listing shows the new binding |
| **Merge/unmerge fan-out into domain 15 registry entries** | The unbounded fan-out CX-03 names. Registry entries re-point to the survivor, **but ownership-chain events are immutable** — each event records the model ID as of the event, and the merge is appended as a catalog annotation (CX-M19). The chain gains history; it never rewrites it. A provenance platform that silently rewrites its own chain has destroyed the thing it exists to sell | Registry owner sees "This model record was merged into [survivor] on [date]" as an annotation on the timeline, not a mutated past |
| **Unmerge after the survivor's record was edited post-merge** | Which record gets the edits? Rule: edits made post-merge attach to the **survivor**; unmerge restores the source record to its pre-merge state and leaves post-merge edits on the survivor, flagging any that plausibly described the source for moderator triage. Never guess — surface it | Moderator sees "3 edits made since the merge — do they belong to [source] or [survivor]?", one at a time |
| **Bulk contribution at Operator volume** (300-item fleet intake) | Inline prompting does not scale and is 13.01.04 Q-03's live problem. Bulk contributions **defer, never auto-create**: unmatched rows produce listings published unmatched (13.01.04 D-01), plus a single reviewable batch of proposed models grouped by dedupe cluster. The Operator resolves the batch when convenient; nothing was created without a human seeing it. **Bulk is the one path where a contribution is not created inline** — because 300 unreviewed provisionals from one account is exactly what corroboration (D-04) is designed to distrust | Operator sees "247 listed. 53 models we don't recognise — review as a batch" with clusters, not 53 modals |
| **Sock-puppet corroboration** — 3 fake accounts, 3 fake listings, forced canonicalization | Blocked by D-04.2 (each account ≥30 days old with ≥1 completed sale). Not unbreakable, but the cost of forging it exceeds the prize: the prize is a *canonical* record, which is still merge-able and still carries the forger's assertion chain. The attack that would actually pay — a merge — is unreachable this way (merges are never automated, D-11) | Nothing user-facing. Detected clusters route to domain 24 |
| **Contributor deletes their account / exercises erasure** | A provenance ledger and a right-to-erasure collide, and this is the platform's first encounter with a tension it will hit everywhere. Resolution: the **assertion survives** — "the 1962 year range was asserted on 2026-03-04, citing [source], and confirmed by two moderators" is a fact about the catalog, not personal data — but the contributor identity is replaced with a stable opaque contributor reference. Standing and credit are destroyed. The catalog does not lose its evidential chain because a contributor left | Model page shows "asserted by a former contributor" with the date, citation and confirmations intact |
| **Contribution of a counterfeit as a legitimate model** | The catalog never carries "fake X" as a model — a counterfeit is a fraudulent *unit*, not a type, so it fails the type/unit rule and routes to domain 24. Contributions in known-counterfeited categories (SM58, U87, Klon — the most-faked products in the category) are elevated to a higher review tier rather than corroboration-promoted | Contributor sees "In review". Report routing is invisible to them |
| **Contribution cites a source that is itself wrong** | Secondary gear references contradict each other routinely; this is normal, not exceptional. The citation is recorded as *what was cited*, never as truth. Contradicting citations move the attribute to `disputed` with both visible — the catalog states the disagreement instead of silently picking a winner | "Year range contested: [source A] says 1962–64, [source B] says 1962–65" |
| **Category has no attribute schema yet** (cold catalog, novel category) | Contribution can create a **model**; it can never create a category or an attribute schema — that is 13.01.01's content and an admin-tier operation. Model lands `provisional` with `category: unclassified`: no facets derivable, listing publishes text-searchable, and a schema request is routed to admin (CX-M27) | "Listed. We don't have filters for this kind of gear yet — your listing is searchable by text" |
| **Network failure mid-submit** | The listing draft and every entered model field survive the failure; retrying the same submission resolves to a single provisional model — a repeated or double submit never produces two provisionals for the same contribution | Error with retry; nothing re-typed |
| **Dedupe check does not return** (>3 s) | Never a blocker (domain D-03). The seller may submit anyway; the post-write sweep (Happy Path 8) does the dedupe afterward and merges if needed | "Still checking — you can add it anyway, we'll flag duplicates if we find any" |
| **Free-text abuse in model name/notes** (spam, slurs, injected instructions) | Contribution free-text is untrusted input rendered on a public catalog page and read by moderators. Screened before it is publicly renderable; anything held routes to domain 24. A held contribution still does not block the listing — it publishes unmatched | Contributor sees "In review". Nothing renders publicly until cleared |
| **Standing holder goes rogue after earning it** | Standing is category-scoped and revocable: 2 upheld conflict-of-interest findings, or 1 merge reversed by the two-moderator path, suspends standing in that category pending admin review. Nothing they did stands unreviewed — every merge they made carries their name in the audit trail (CX-M19) and is unmerge-able indefinitely (D-06) | Model pages show the merge history unchanged; corrections re-notify affected sellers |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading — dedupe** (contributor) | 400 ms after last keystroke on model name | Inline spinner beside the field; the rest of the form stays editable — dedupe never freezes the form |
| **Loading — submit** (contributor) | Contribution submitted | Button in progress state; listing draft already saved, so nothing is at risk |
| **Empty — form** (contributor) | Contribution opened from a failed match | Make + category **prefilled from the failed search**; model name focused; nothing re-typed |
| **Empty — queue** (moderator) | No contributions pending in the moderator's standing categories | "Queue clear" — scoped to *their* categories, so an empty state never implies the platform's queue is empty |
| **Populated — provisional** (all) | Contribution accepted into the catalog as `provisional` | `Provisional` badge on the model page + "added by [contributor], in review". Listing live and facet-searchable. Price guide panel: "not enough comps yet" (13.04.03), never a fabricated number |
| **Populated — canonical** (all) | Moderator acceptance or corroboration (D-04) | Badge clears; assertion provenance remains visible per attribute |
| **Populated — queue item** (moderator) | Item at the top of the blast-radius queue | Proposal beside the dedupe candidates, exposure count (`bound listings + registry entries`), citation if given, contributor's surviving-contribution count in this category. Safety-band items pinned above with a distinct label |
| **Populated — disputed** (all) | Manufacturer contest, upheld report, or contradicting citations | Record stays live and readable; only the contested attribute renders as contested, with both assertions attributed. The rest of the record is unaffected — a dispute is not a takedown |
| **Error — submit fails** (contributor) | Network/service failure | Error with retry; listing draft and all model fields preserved; a retried submit yields one provisional model for the contribution, never a duplicate |
| **Error — dedupe unavailable** (contributor) | Dedupe service down or >3 s | **Fails open**, never closed. "You can add it anyway — we'll check for duplicates afterwards." Post-write sweep covers it |
| **Partial — spec incomplete** (all) | Model accepted with optional attributes blank | Blank rows render "spec incomplete — contribute" on the model page. Facets that depend on a blank attribute simply omit the model rather than sorting it as zero |
| **Partial — safety attribute unknown** (all) | Required safety field answered "I don't know" | Model page and every bound listing show "Mains voltage unconfirmed — check the unit". Model is pinned to the human queue and **cannot** corroboration-promote (D-04.4). This is the one Partial state that is loud rather than quiet — an unshown voltage warning is a destroyed amp (13.01.05 D-02) |
| **Partial — merge in flight** (moderator) | One moderator has proposed a merge above the two-moderator threshold | "Awaiting a second moderator" — record unchanged and fully live meanwhile. A proposed merge changes nothing until seconded |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Contribution can be an admin-only back-office function | Simplest-first instinct | ❌ **REJECTED**: gear's long tail is unbounded and mostly boutique/vintage. Admin-only contribution means every long-tail listing blocks on a human, which kills the listing flow — the one thing that must never stall. Contribution must be inline and provisional-publish. |
| DT-02 | Open contribution needs a trust gradient, not a binary permission | Wikipedia/Discogs precedent + 13.01.01 DT-02 (blast radius) | ✅ **CONFIRMED**: catalog corruption is silent and downstream. Earned category-scoped moderator standing (contributions accepted in a category → review rights in that category) matches how gear expertise actually distributes — a drum tech should not be merging vintage tube amps. |
| DT-03 | **Contribution and editing are the same feature with the same policy** | The breadth pass's framing — this file was titled and scoped as a create-flow, while 13.01.01's Role Lens says "cannot edit a model directly — edits go through 13.01.02", making edits this feature's job and this feature never mentioned them | ❌ **REJECTED — and the blast radius is inverted from the intuition.** A *create* has blast radius 1: a brand-new model has exactly one bound listing, the contributor's own. An *edit to a canonical model* has blast radius N: change one field on a model with 400 bound listings and you have moved 400 price-guide entries. The whole provisional-publish design is tuned for the create case and is actively wrong for the edit case. **The attack surface was never contribution — it is the edit nobody specified.** Drives the edit tiering table and D-14. |
| DT-04 | **The manufacturer's claim about their own model is authoritative** | The obvious instinct — who knows a Fender better than Fender? Every catalog with a brand relationship assumes it | ❌ **REJECTED**, and this is the finding a gear domain expert would arrive at first. Manufacturers routinely deny that undocumented variants existed — factory seconds, parts-bin transition builds, Norlin-era inconsistency, the '59 burst's fabled variance, Fender's transition years. **The vintage market's most valuable facts are precisely the ones the manufacturer never documented and often disowns.** A manufacturer-authoritative override would delete the catalog's most valuable knowledge on the say-so of the party with the least interest in admitting it. Manufacturers get a weighted contribution channel and a `disputed` state; they never get an override. |
| DT-05 | **Earned category-scoped standing is a clean trust model** | DT-02's own conclusion, taken at face value | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED WITH CONSTRAINT — DT-02 built its own attack surface.** Standing is earned by contributing in a category; the people who contribute enough in a category to earn standing are, definitionally, the people who *trade* in that category. DT-02 hands review rights over vintage guitars to vintage-guitar dealers. Three live attacks: merge a variant into a base model so your unit reads as the rarer thing; split a model to mint a rare designation for your stock; sit on a competitor's contribution. This is the Discogs failure mode, arrived at honestly. Fixes: hard recusal (D-07), two-moderator merges above threshold (D-08), public merge history, revocable standing. **The trust gradient is still right — it just cannot be trusted alone.** |
| DT-06 | The pre-submit dedupe check prevents duplicate models | It is what the dedupe check is for | ❌ **REJECTED for the concurrent case — a pre-submit check cannot catch this by construction.** Two sellers contributing the same missing model minutes apart each run dedupe *before the other record exists*; both correctly return zero and both correctly pass. No amount of pre-submit rigour fixes a check that ran against a world where the duplicate had not been written yet. Requires a **post-write sweep** against a recent-creation window (15 min), which is a different mechanism, not a better check. CX-01 §5 flagged the symptom; the structural reason is that the check is on the wrong side of the write. |
| DT-07 | **Provisional models should auto-canonicalize after N days** | The obvious backlog answer, and the one Q-01 proposed | ❌ **REJECTED**: time-based promotion means **any bad actor wins by waiting.** It converts the moderation queue from a filter into a delay, and it promotes precisely the records nobody could be bothered to verify — the exact population most likely to be wrong. It also inverts the incentive: the more suspect a contribution, the longer it sits, the more certainly it graduates. **Superseded by corroboration (D-04): promotion is earned by usage, not survived by clock.** Three independent qualifying sellers binding listings to the same record is evidence a single contributor cannot cheaply fake — and unlike a human review it costs nothing and scales with the long tail it must serve. |
| DT-08 | **The contribution record is provenance, not data hygiene** | D-18 (provenance is the wedge) applied to this feature + the domain index's central finding, that every swept candidate already exists at Reverb | ✅ **CONFIRMED — this is the feature's D-18 contribution and it was entirely absent.** Reverb's catalog is opaque: nobody can tell you who asserted that a '62 Jazzmaster has a 7.25" radius, when, or on what evidence, so a disputed spec is unresolvable and the catalog is a black box you either trust or don't. If **every attribute value carries its assertion chain** — who claimed it, when, citing what, confirmed by whom — the catalog stops being a database of claims and becomes a citable record, which is the same move the platform makes on splits. It costs one extra structure on a write that is already happening. CX-M19 already carries merges (per the breadth pass); extending it from merges to **every attribute assertion** is the whole delta. |
| DT-09 | Contributions should require a cited source | Discogs requires source images; serious gear reference (Gruhn's, the Blue Book, maker serial charts) is citation-shaped by nature | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED WITH CONSTRAINT**: mandatory citation on every field kills the inline flow this feature exists to protect (DT-01) — a seller adding a boutique pedal will not go find a catalog scan. Citation is **required only for identity-bearing and safety-bearing attributes in mains-powered and high-counterfeit categories**, optional everywhere else, and always recorded as *what was cited* rather than as truth. Uncited attributes are usable but render as contributor-asserted and carry less weight in a dispute. |
| DT-10 | Contribution should be gamified — points, leaderboards, contributor ranks | Wikipedia/Discogs precedent, and the obvious way to solve a cold catalog (13.01 Q-01) | ❌ **REJECTED**: leaderboards optimise for **volume**, and this feature's failure mode is confidently-wrong volume. A contributor racing a leaderboard produces exactly the plausible-looking, unverified, semi-duplicated records that corroboration and dedupe then have to clean up — the incentive points at the harm. Standing is weighted by **survival** instead: contributions still standing unmerged and uncorrected after 90 days. Survival is unfakeable by effort and directly measures the only thing that matters. |
| DT-11 | Automated/model-assisted moderation can drain the queue | The obvious scaling answer for an unbounded long tail | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED WITH CONSTRAINT — automation may propose, never dispose.** Automation is genuinely good at *surfacing and ranking* dedupe candidates (that is CX-M17's job already) and useless-to-dangerous at *deciding* merges, because the failure profile of a confident automated merge is identical to the worst failure in this sub-domain: silent, plausible, and downstream. Same shape as 13.01.04 D-02 (auto-bind only at high confidence, never on reissues). Merges are never automated (D-11); corroboration (D-04) is not an exception — it promotes, which is cheap to reverse, and never merges, which is not. |
| DT-12 | **Spec-identical products should merge** | Data-hygiene instinct: two records, same specs, same chassis, same factory → one product | ❌ **REJECTED — this is the most tempting bad merge in the domain and it destroys the domain's most valuable distinctions.** The same OEM chassis ships as a Harley Benton, a Sire and three other badges at wildly different prices; Squier and Fender share designs and not markets; a Klon and a Tumnus are electrically close and commercially unrelated. Merging on equivalence merges comp sets whose **price delta is the entire product of 13.04**. The merge test is **identity, not equivalence**: two records merge iff one was created because the other could not be found. Equivalence gets a `same-chassis-as` link. |
| DT-13 | A merge undo window of 30/90 days is sufficient | Every system with a destructive operation has a time-boxed undo; it is the standard pattern | ❌ **REJECTED — a time-boxed undo is exactly useless for a failure whose defining property is being undetectable.** CX-03 states it: nobody sees a bad merge; they see a price guide that is subtly wrong. A merge discovered at day 200 — when a seller finally asks why their variant quotes the base model's comps — is precisely the merge worth reversing, and a 90-day window has already made it permanent. **Unmerge never expires** (D-06). The undo window is a pattern borrowed from failures that announce themselves. |
| DT-14 | **The registry fan-out can re-point ownership chains like it re-points listings** | They are both "bound entities"; CX-03 lists them in one breath as an unbounded fan-out | ❌ **REJECTED — they are categorically different and the difference is the whole thesis.** A listing binding is a *pointer* and re-pointing it is harmless. A domain-15 ownership-chain event is *evidence*, written at settlement by 13.06.05 (D-05: settlement writes the ownership chain) — the gear analogue of capturing a split in the session. Re-pointing an event's model ID silently rewrites what a chain says happened. **A provenance platform that rewrites its own chain to tidy a catalog merge has destroyed the thing it exists to sell.** Registry entries re-point; chain *events* are immutable and record the model ID as of the event, with the merge appended as a catalog annotation (CX-M19). The chain gains history; it never loses it. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Consumes **CX-M17 Canonical Data, Taxonomy & Entity Resolution** — the generic dedupe / merge / alias / unmerge machinery. The gear-specific merge *test* (D-12, identity-not-equivalence) is domain content and lives here; CX-M17 supplies the mechanism, not the criterion. **Additional requirement raised by DT-06**: dedupe must run post-write against a recent-creation window, not only pre-submit — a pre-submit-only substrate cannot serve this feature.
- Consumes **CX-M19 Audit Log & Provenance Ledger** — **scope extended by DT-08**: the breadth pass consumed CX-M19 for merge reversibility only. This feature needs per-attribute assertion provenance (who asserted, when, citing what, confirmed by whom) on *every* model attribute, plus immutable catalog annotations on domain-15 chain events (DT-14). That is a materially larger ask than "log the merges" and Step 6 must size it.
- Consumes **CX-M27 Admin, Back-Office & Support** — the moderation queue surface (blast-radius ordering + pinned safety band), the conflicted-thin-category escalation (D-07), attribute-schema requests for unclassified categories, and backlog depth as an admin metric.
- Consumes **CX-M07 Notifications & Alerts** — two distinct fan-outs: (1) decision notices to the contributor; (2) **comp-shift notices to every affected seller when a merge or unmerge re-points their listing** (D-13). Fan-out (2) resolves 13.01.04 Q-02 and is unbounded by construction — a merge on a popular model notifies every bound seller at once.
- Consumes **CX-M09 Roles, Permissions & Delegated Authority** — category-scoped moderator standing is a delegated-authority grant with a **recusal predicate** (D-07: recuse if the actor has any listing bound to the source *or* the merge target, active or sold within 90 days). A permission model that cannot express "permitted, except against entities you have a commercial interest in" cannot serve this feature.
- Consumes **CX-M08 Reviews, Ratings & Portable Reputation** — candidate substrate for standing (Q-02). **Note for Step 6**: standing here is weighted by *survival*, not count (DT-10), and is revocable per-category — if CX-M08 is a count-and-average reputation model, it is the wrong substrate and standing is separate.
- Consumes **CX-M18 Privacy, Consent & Data Portability** — erasure vs. an immutable assertion ledger. Resolved here as: assertion survives de-identified, contributor identity pseudonymised, standing destroyed. **This is the platform's first instance of a tension it will hit in every provenance domain (02, 09, 15)** — Step 6 should check whether the same resolution holds there.
- Consumes **CX-M16 Search & Discovery** — provisional models must appear in facet search. If they don't, provisional-publish is a lie: the listing is live but invisible to the filtered searches that are the entire carrot for matching (13.01.04 DT-01).
- Consumes **CX-M21 Accessibility** — the unverified/unknown safety warning is a safety warning; severity cannot be carried by colour alone.
- Consumes **CX-M29 Bulk Import, Sync & Migration Tooling** — bulk contribution defers to a reviewable batch, never auto-creates (D-09).
- Writes **13.01.01 Model Records, Taxonomy & Attribute Schemas** ([path](./13.01.01-model-records-taxonomy-attributes.md)) — the highest blast-radius write path in the domain (CX-03). **Bounded**: this feature creates and edits *models*; it can never create a *category* or an *attribute schema*. **New requirement raised here**: the attribute schema must mark attributes as safety-bearing and identity-bearing, because both edit tiering and corroboration gating (D-04.4) key off those flags and neither is derivable from the attribute's type.
- Triggered by **13.01.04 Listing↔Model Matching** ([path](./13.01.04-listing-model-matching.md)) — match failure is the sole entry point. Returns a provisional model for binding. Resolves that file's Q-02 (yes — affected sellers are notified on a comp shift, D-13) and contributes to its Q-03 (bulk defers to a batch, D-09).
- Safety seam with **13.01.05 Fitment, Compatibility & Region/Voltage Warnings** ([path](./13.01.05-fitment-compatibility-voltage.md)) — **CX-05's flagged conflict is resolved here** (D-03) without contradicting D-01: safety-bearing attributes are required-with-explicit-unknown, `unknown` renders loud, provisional values render as unverified, and a blank blocks corroboration. Cost is one form field; the listing never stalls. The **liability** half remains a user decision (13.01.05 Q-01).
- Candidate re-open of **CX file R-01** (Serial Decoding ✗ Contribution): R-01 correctly rejected decoding as a *seeder* of contributions — a serial says nothing about model spec. But decoding is a **validator at review time**: a contributed year range of 1962–64 against a serial that decodes to 1972–74 is a free, high-signal moderation flag. Different direction, different claim. Flagged for Step 6, not asserted.
- Feeds **13.04 Price Discovery & Market Data** ([path](../13.04-price-discovery-market-data/)) — every merge, unmerge and identity edit moves a comp set. 13.04.03's thin-market confidence governs what a provisional model may say about price (Happy Path 9).
- Feeds **Domain 15 Gear Registry & Ownership** ([path](../../15-gear-registry-ownership/)) — merges re-point registry entries; ownership-chain events are immutable (DT-14). Registry binding also raises the two-moderator threshold (D-08) regardless of listing count: one registry entry is enough.
- Routes to **13.02.03 Modification & Originality Disclosure** ([path](../13.02-condition-originality-disclosure/13.02.03-modification-originality-disclosure.md)) — "contribution is a unit, not a type" is the highest-frequency rejection, and its fix-forward lands the modification deltas here.
- Routes to **Domain 24 Trust & Safety** — free-text abuse screening, counterfeit assertions, sock-puppet clusters, and the Fan report channel that is Fan's only access to this feature.
- Touches **13.06 Cart, Checkout & Orders** ([path](../13.06-cart-checkout-orders/)) — a merge landing mid-checkout must not mutate what a buyer is looking at; comps snapshot at checkout start.
- Touches **13.13 Authorized Dealer Program & MAP** ([path](../13.13-authorized-dealer-map-pricing.md)) — DT-04's manufacturer-contributor arrives at the same persona gap 13.13's empty Role Lens reports and domain Q-03 raises. **Note the distinction for Step 6**: a *dealer* persona and a *manufacturer* persona are not the same gap, and DT-04's answer (welcome, weighted, never authoritative) means the manufacturer gap may need no persona at all — only a claim channel.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Contribution is inline + provisional-publish, never blocking** | Per DT-01 — a blocked listing is a lost listing. Reaffirmed against the CX-05 safety conflict: D-03 resolves it at the cost of one form field, not a stall. |
| D-02 | **Moderator standing is earned and category-scoped** | Per DT-02 — gear expertise is narrow and deep, not general. Constrained by D-05/D-07/D-08 after DT-05 found this decision builds its own attack surface. |
| D-03 | **Fan has no contribution or moderation access — but has a report channel** | Persona explicitly lacks the domain expertise; catalog blast radius makes open contribution an abuse surface. Reporting is not contribution: it creates a signal, never a record. Fan is the persona harmed by a wrong voltage attribute (CX-05); leaving them no channel makes that harm unreportable by the one person positioned to notice it. **Also resolves CX-05**: safety-bearing attributes are required-with-explicit-"I don't know"; `unknown` ≠ absent and renders loud; provisional values render contributor-asserted; a blank or unknown blocks corroboration and pins the model to the human queue at top priority. |
| D-04 | **Provisional graduates by corroboration, never by elapsed time** | Per DT-07. ≥3 bound listings from ≥3 distinct accounts (≥30 days old, ≥1 completed sale), zero outstanding dedupe candidates, no blank/unknown safety attribute, no report in 14 days. Usage is the moderation signal; independent convergence is evidence one contributor cannot cheaply fake. Auto-promotion is the cheapest operation to reverse — it never merges. **Resolves Q-01.** |
| D-05 | **Standing is earned by contribution survival, not contribution count** | Per DT-10. 10 contributions in a category surviving 90 days unmerged and uncorrected → review rights in that category. Volume metrics reward the exact behaviour that harms this feature. |
| D-06 | **Merge is always reversible and unmerge never expires** | Per DT-13. A time-boxed undo is useless against a failure whose defining property is that nobody notices it. The merge discovered at day 200 is the one worth reversing. |
| D-07 | **Hard recusal on commercial interest** | Per DT-05. A moderator may not act on a model bound to any of their listings — active or sold within 90 days — nor on a merge whose *target* is so bound. Recusal must not deadlock: a thin category with one conflicted moderator escalates to admin with the conflict disclosed, and corroboration runs regardless. |
| D-08 | **Two moderators for high-blast-radius merges** | Per DT-05. Merge/split/unmerge on a model with ≥10 bound listings **or ≥1 registry entry** requires two moderators with standing, neither recused. One registry entry is enough because domain 15 is evidence, not display (DT-14). |
| D-09 | **Bulk contribution defers to a reviewable batch; it never auto-creates** | Per the Operator's 300-item intake. Unmatched rows publish unmatched (13.01.04 D-01) and produce one dedupe-clustered batch. 300 unreviewed provisionals from a single account is exactly the population D-04 exists to distrust. |
| D-10 | **Every attribute carries its assertion chain; citation required only where stakes demand it** | Per DT-08 + DT-09. Who asserted, when, citing what, confirmed by whom — on every value. Citation mandatory for identity-bearing and safety-bearing attributes in mains-powered and high-counterfeit categories; optional elsewhere; always recorded as *what was cited*, never as truth. This is the feature's D-18 contribution. |
| D-11 | **Automation may propose, never dispose** | Per DT-11. Automated assistance may surface and rank dedupe candidates; it may never decide a merge. A confident automated merge has the exact failure profile of the worst failure here — silent, plausible, downstream. Same shape as 13.01.04 D-02. |
| D-12 | **The merge test is identity, not equivalence** | Per DT-12. Two records merge iff one was created because the other could not be found. Spec-identical, chassis-identical and "basically the same" are never merge grounds — they get a `same-chassis-as` link. A moderator who cannot say which record was created in ignorance of the other does not have a merge. |
| D-13 | **Sellers are notified when a merge or unmerge moves their comps** | Resolves 13.01.04 Q-02. A comp set changing underneath a live listing is a material change to the seller's pricing information; silence would make the platform's own catalog maintenance an invisible repricing event. |
| D-14 | **Edits are tiered by blast radius, not treated as contributions** | Per DT-03. Identity- and safety-bearing edits to canonical models are review-before-apply; additive gap-fill applies immediately with provenance. The create path's never-block policy is correct for creates and wrong for edits. |
| D-15 | **A manufacturer's claim is weighted, never authoritative** | Per DT-04. Contests move the attribute to `disputed` with both assertions visible; a two-moderator decision resolves it. The vintage market's most valuable facts are the ones manufacturers never documented and often disown. |
