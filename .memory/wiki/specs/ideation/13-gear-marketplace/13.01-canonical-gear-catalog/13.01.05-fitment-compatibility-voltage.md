# Feature: Fitment, Compatibility & Region/Voltage Warnings

> **Number**: 13.01.05
> **Parent**: [Canonical Gear Catalog](./13.01-canonical-gear-catalog-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Catalog-level relationships that answer "will this actually work for me?" — does this pickup fit that route, does this case fit that guitar, and will this 100V Japanese amp survive being plugged into 230V. It reads model attributes plus the buyer's context and warns before money moves. The voltage half prevents destroyed equipment and a guaranteed dispute; the fitment half prevents a return.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees "this is a 100V Japan-market unit — you're in the UK (230V), it needs a step-down transformer" on the listing and again at checkout. Sees whether a part fits the instrument they own (via their registry entries in domain 15). |
| Producer | Read-only | Same warnings, weighted to rack gear and outboard where region variants are rife and mains voltage is not user-switchable on older units. |
| Operator | Read-only | Reads fitment when buying spares/consumables for a backline fleet, and voltage warnings when sourcing gear internationally for a venue or studio. |
| Fan | Read-only | The persona most likely to be harmed by an unwarned voltage mismatch — least likely to know it's a question. Warning must be plain-language, not spec-shorthand. |

## Behavior

Fitment and compatibility are **relationships between catalog models** (part↔instrument, case↔instrument, PSU↔pedal), not attributes of a listing. Region/voltage warnings are derived: model's market/voltage attribute × buyer's shipping destination → a warning severity. Warnings surface at listing view and again at checkout; they inform, they do not block.

### Happy Path

1. Buyer in the UK views a listing for a Japan-market amp
2. System compares the model's voltage/market attribute against the buyer's destination
3. Listing shows a plain-language warning with what would be required to use it safely
4. Warning repeats at checkout as an acknowledgement, not a blocker
5. Buyer proceeds knowingly, or filters it out

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Model has a user-switchable voltage selector | No warning, or an informational note only | "Voltage-switchable — safe for your region" |
| Unit was modified for another region (a Japan amp rewired for 230V) | **Catalog cannot know this** — it is a unit fact, not a model fact. Belongs to 13.02.03 mod disclosure | Listing-level mod disclosure overrides the model-derived warning; conflict surfaced |
| Buyer's destination unknown (not logged in, no address) | Warning cannot be derived; show the model's market attribute neutrally | "Japan-market (100V)" stated as spec, no personalised warning |
| Fitment data absent for a part | No claim made — silence, not a guess | Fitment section absent |
| Fitment relationship is wrong (bad data) | Buyer buys a part that doesn't fit → return under 13.08.01 | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — whether bad fitment data is platform liability or seller liability is unresolved and matters |
| Listing unmatched to a model (13.01.04) | No model attributes → no warnings derivable | Nothing shown — a real cost of unmatched listings |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Listing/checkout render | Inline with listing load; never a separate blocking fetch |
| Empty | No fitment/region data for this model | Section omitted entirely — no "unknown" noise |
| Populated | Warning derived | Plain-language warning with severity and remedy |
| Error | Derivation fails | Fail silent on fitment; **fail loud on voltage** — an unshown voltage warning is a destroyed amp |
| Partial | Model market known, buyer destination unknown | Neutral spec statement without personalisation |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Fitment and voltage are the same feature | They arrived as one sweep candidate (#24) and both answer "will this work for me?" | ⚠️ CONFIRMED WITH TENSION: kept as one node because both are *derivations from catalog model relationships* and share a surface. But they differ in stakes — a wrong fitment costs a return; a wrong voltage costs a destroyed amp and possibly a fire. Hence the split failure policy in States: fail silent on fitment, fail loud on voltage. Flagged for Step 5 — if the stakes asymmetry drives divergent behaviour, split them. |
| DT-02 | The model-derived voltage warning is authoritative | It comes from canonical catalog data, so it looks trustworthy | ❌ REJECTED: a *unit* may have been rewired, and the catalog only knows *models* (13.01.01: "a model record is a type, never a unit"). Unit-level mod disclosure (13.02.03) must be able to override a model-derived warning. This is the general trap of this sub-domain — model facts masquerading as unit facts. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Warnings inform, never block | The buyer may legitimately want the 100V unit (they own a transformer, or they're a collector). Blocking is paternalistic and wrong. |
| D-02 | Fail silent on fitment, fail loud on voltage | Per DT-01 — asymmetric stakes justify asymmetric failure behaviour. |
| D-03 | Unit-level disclosure overrides model-derived warnings | Per DT-02. |
