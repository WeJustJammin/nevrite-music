# Feature: Listing↔Model Matching & Entity Resolution

> **Number**: 13.01.04
> **Parent**: [Canonical Gear Catalog](./13.01-canonical-gear-catalog-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP] — **Deep Think addition** (not in the sweep's candidate list)
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The join that binds a seller's free-text intent ("62 Jazzmaster, refin") to a canonical model record. It is the single point where the catalog either earns its keep or becomes decorative: an unmatched listing has no comps, no facets, no alerts, no price guide entry — and, per [13.01.05](./13.01.05-fitment-compatibility-voltage.md), no safety warnings. This is the mechanism, not the data — it decides what a listing *is*.

**Why this exists as a node**: the sweep listed the catalog (13.01.01) and contribution (13.01.02) but never the resolution step between them. Without it, the two are unconnected: one holds models, the other adds models, and nothing attaches a listing to either.

**What makes it hard is not string similarity.** Gear listings are not natural language — they are a compressed trade argot in which a two-character token is routinely the highest-information signal in the string ("R9" = Gibson Custom Shop '59 Les Paul reissue; "MIM" = Made in Mexico, a 3–5× price axis; "AB763" = a Fender amp circuit, more precise than the model name; "pre-CBS" = Fender before January 1965, a price-defining term containing no year). A generic matcher scores these as noise and scores the marketing prose around them as signal. It gets the reissue-vs-original case backwards at an order of magnitude of value.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (as seller) | Types a title while listing; sees ≤5 ranked candidates with the attributes that *distinguish* them (year range, country, reissue status) rather than the attributes they share; picks one, or escalates to "add this model" ([13.01.02](./13.01.02-catalog-contribution-moderation.md)). Hits the reissue trap most (guitars are the deepest reissue category) and the partscaster case uniquely — this is the persona who owns a guitar assembled from four makers' parts. As a buyer, never sees this surface; they feel its absence as a listing missing from a filtered search. |
| Producer | Full (as seller) | Same flow, failing more often: studio outboard is the thinnest part of any gear catalog, so zero-candidate → contribution is this persona's *normal* path, not an exception. Also the persona for whom **revision is identity** — a U47 tube and a U47 FET share a name and are different microphones; an 1176 Rev A and a Rev F share a name and are different compressors. Binding to the name alone is binding to the wrong product. |
| Operator | Full (as seller) | Matching at volume via [13.03.04](../13.03-listings-inventory/13.03.04-bulk-listing-channel-sync.md), where per-row prompting cannot exist. Their real input is not free text but **their own SKU** — they already ran this identification in their own back office. Sees a grouped review queue (K decisions, not N rows) and, after the first import, near-total auto-bind from SKU memoisation (D-08). |
| Fan | None | No listing surface, so no matching surface — the persona never makes a bind. They are, however, the persona most *harmed* by a wrong one: per CX-04, a mis-bound model yields a wrong voltage attribute and therefore a wrong or absent safety warning, and Fan is the persona least equipped to notice its absence (13.01.05 Role Lens). Fan's only recourse — reporting a listing that misrepresents its model — is domain 24's reporting surface, **not** a matching surface. The explicit absence is the point: matching's worst blast radius lands entirely on the persona with zero access to it. |

## Behavior

Matching resolves `(title, category, optional serial, optional seller SKU) → ranked candidate models + confidence`. It runs at three moments, uses six ranked signals, and gates auto-bind on **structural eligibility** before it ever looks at a score.

**The governing asymmetry (D-02)**: a wrong bind is worse than no bind. No bind costs the seller comps and facets — a visible, recoverable loss. A wrong bind produces a *confidently wrong* comp set, a price guide entry that lies, and a safety warning derived from the wrong model. Every threshold in this feature is therefore set for **precision, not coverage**.

**Every failure degrades to `unmatched`, which is a legal, publishable state** (D-01). This is a load-bearing property: matching needs no rollback path, because there is no failure that leaves the listing in an illegal state. A bind that half-writes is a listing that is unmatched.

### The Signals — ranked

| # | Signal | Weight | Why it ranks here |
|---|--------|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Seller's prior confirmed bind for the same SKU** | Decisive | A human already made this claim once. A prior human decision is stronger evidence than any text score (D-08). |
| 2 | **Decoded serial** ([13.01.03](./13.01.03-serial-decoding-dating.md)) | Decisive when unambiguous | Scheme-backed. The only signal that separates a 1962 original from a 1999 reissue when both titles read "62 Jazzmaster". |
| 3 | **Title text** × catalog + gear lexicon | Primary | The seller's claim, stated deliberately, in ≤80 chars. |
| 4 | **Category** (explicit selection) | Gate | Wrong category → wrong attribute schema → wrong facets → underivable warnings. Category precedes model, always. |
| 5 | **Description text** | Tie-break only | **Never primary.** Descriptions mention other models constantly ("sounds like a Klon", "beats a JCM800", "compare to a '59"). A matcher reading prose binds to the comparison. Title is the claim; description is prose. |
| 6 | **Listing price** | Negative only | Suppresses auto-bind; never drives or rejects one (DT-05). |

### Auto-Bind Eligibility — structural, not a tuned knob

A model is **auto-bind-eligible iff it has no ambiguity sibling** in the catalog. Four sibling classes, each of which makes model identity textually unrecoverable:

| Sibling class | Example | Why it blocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Reissue / relic / tribute** | 1962 Jazzmaster vs. 1999 AVRI vs. 2015 Custom Shop '60 Relic | Near-identical text, order-of-magnitude price spread. The relic's title deliberately foregrounds a year it was not built in. |
| **Market / voltage variant** | Fender Twin Reverb 100V (Japan) vs. 120V (US) | Same model name. **This is the safety case** — see DT-06. |
| **Revision** | UA 1176 Rev A / D / F / LN; U47 tube vs. FET; TS808/TS9/TS10; Big Muff Triangle/Ram's Head/Civil War; MXR Phase 90 script vs. block | For this class the revision *is* the product. Producer's category, overwhelmingly. |
| **Country of manufacture** | MIA / MIM / MIJ / CIJ / MIK Stratocaster | 3–5× price axis, frequently absent from the title entirely. |

**Why structural beats numeric**: Q-01 asked for a per-category threshold table. Per-category thresholds are twelve unfalsifiable magic numbers that nobody can ever audit. Sibling-derivation is computed *from the catalog itself* (13.01.01's model graph), degrades correctly, and is explainable to a seller in one sentence. A newly contributed model with no siblings is auto-bind-eligible on day one; the moment its reissue is contributed, **both become ineligible and every prior auto-bind against them becomes a review candidate** (D-11). The catalog's growth tightens the gate automatically.

### Auto-Bind Gates — all must hold

1. Top candidate is **auto-bind-eligible** (no ambiguity siblings)
2. Confidence **≥ 0.95** — *calibration seed, see Q-01*
3. **Margin**: top − runner-up **≥ 0.25** — *calibration seed*. Magnitude alone is insufficient: the reissue trap produces **two** high-confidence candidates, and a 0.96/0.94 pair must never auto-bind despite a 0.96 top.
4. **Price sanity**: if the model has ≥5 comps and the asking price falls outside [0.25×, 4×] the comp band → suppress. Under 5 comps the gate does not apply (that is [13.04.03](../13.04-price-discovery-market-data/13.04.03-valuation-confidence-thin-market.md)'s territory, and a model with no comps has nothing to poison).
5. **Single maker token** in the title. Two or more ("Warmoth" + "Fender" + "Lollar") → partscaster suspicion → suppress.
6. **No identity-modification vocabulary**: "partscaster", "parts guitar", "Frankenstrat", "parts-built", "loaded with", "swapped", "aftermarket", "conversion". Per 13.02 D-02 (condition and originality are orthogonal axes), **modification that changes what the object *is* blocks matching; modification that changes its condition or originality does not.** "Refin" is a 13.02 disclosure fact — a refinished '62 Jazzmaster is still a '62 Jazzmaster and binds normally. "Partscaster" is not a Stratocaster and must not bind to one.
7. **Serial does not contradict** the candidate (13.01.03 decode within the candidate's year range, subject to the era rule below).

Any gate fails → **no auto-bind, present the picker**. No gate ever rejects the listing (D-01).

**Precision target: ≥0.99** on auto-binds, measured against a hold-out of human-confirmed binds. Coverage is explicitly not a target. This number is derived from D-02, not chosen: if a wrong bind is worse than no bind, the acceptable wrong-bind rate is the rate at which auto-bind stops being a net gain.

### Year Tolerance — asymmetric

Default tolerance between a seller's asserted year and a model's year range: **±1 year**, silently. Sellers routinely list the year they think, build year and sale year differ by one constantly, and the error is worth approximately nothing.

**Across a catalog-flagged era boundary, tolerance is zero.** A one-year error is worth ~$0 in 2019 and ~$8,000 across January 1965. Era boundaries are catalog knowledge (13.01.01) — Fender pre-CBS (Jan 1965), Gibson Norlin (1969), Fender CBS→FMIC (1985), Marshall JMP→JCM800 (1981), Zildjian Old K→New K (1977). A ±1 crossing of a flagged boundary suppresses auto-bind and prompts.

### The Three Moments

| Moment | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Debounced 300 ms after last keystroke. Candidates rendered at P95 ≤ 400 ms. At 2,000 ms, stop spinning and show manual model search. |
| **Publish** | Binds are re-resolved at publish, never trusted from draft state. A draft held open for a week may be bound to a provisional that moderation has since merged, split or rejected (DT-08). |
| **Post-publish re-match** | Unmatched listings are re-matched when (a) a new model is contributed in their category, (b) nightly while the listing is active, (c) on seller edit. This is not optional: without it, the catalog's growth never heals the listings that were unmatched *because* the catalog was thin, and the unmatched pool only ever grows (DT-04). |

### Happy Path

1. Seller types "1962 Fender Jazzmaster sunburst" into the listing title, category `Electric Guitars` already selected
2. System debounces 300 ms, returns ≤5 ranked candidates at P95 ≤ 400 ms
3. Top candidate is `Fender Jazzmaster (1958–1965), USA` at 0.96; runner-up is `Fender American Vintage '62 Jazzmaster Reissue (1999–2012)` at 0.93
4. **Auto-bind is suppressed** — margin 0.03 < 0.25, and both models carry a reissue sibling (gates 1 and 3 fail). This is the designed outcome, not a miss.
5. Picker renders both, surfacing only the attributes that *distinguish* them: year range, country, reissue status, comp band
6. Seller enters the serial; [13.01.03](./13.01.03-serial-decoding-dating.md) decodes `L-series, 1963–1964`; the reissue candidate drops out of range and the picker re-ranks to a single scheme-backed candidate
7. Seller confirms the model, then selects variant/finish (`Sunburst`)
8. Listing binds. Facets, comps, price guide entry and voltage/market warnings light up
9. Buyer's saved search for "1962 Jazzmaster" matches this listing — fan-out fires once for this (listing, model) pair, ever (D-09)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Zero candidates** (boutique / one-off) | Route to 13.01.02 contribution; publish against the returned provisional | "Can't find it? Add this model" |
| **Cold catalog — zero candidates is the norm** | Not an error state. Matching renders as a contribution funnel, not a failed picker (sub-domain Q-01) | "Be the first to add this model" — never "no results found" |
| **Weak candidates only** (all < 0.5) | Show them anyway, ranked, *above* the contribute CTA | Weak candidates + "None of these? Add this model" — hiding weak candidates manufactures duplicate contributions that 13.01.02's dedupe then has to catch |
| **Multiple plausible candidates** (reissue vs. original) | Never auto-bind — gates 1 and 3 both fail | Candidate picker surfacing only distinguishing attributes (year, country, reissue status, comp band) |
| **Relic'd Custom Shop** — "Fender Custom Shop 1960 Strat Relic", built 2015 | Reissue sibling → ineligible. The year in the title refers to a *different guitar*; serial decode is the only escape | Picker with "1960 original" vs "Custom Shop '60 Relic (2015)" and build year from decode |
| **Partscaster** — "Warmoth body, Fender neck, Lollar pickups" | Gates 5 and 6 fail. **High-confidence wrong candidates — the inverse of the boutique case.** Not a Stratocaster; must not bind to one | "This looks parts-built — parts-built instruments aren't catalog models. List it unmatched, or add it as its own model." Routes to 13.01.02 |
| **Parts/accessory bound to the whole** — "Fender Stratocaster pickguard", £40 | Category gate + price gate (£40 vs. a $2k comp band → outside [0.25×, 4×]) → suppressed | Picker scoped to `Guitar Parts`, not `Electric Guitars`. A $40 comp in a Strat's comp set is a permanent lie the price guide tells |
| **Bundle / lot** — "Strat + case + 3 pedals" | Detected via ≥2 distinct high-confidence model tokens across distinct categories, or bundle vocabulary ("bundle", "lot", "+ case and"). Never auto-binds. Seller picks a primary model; listing carries a `bundle` flag | "Multiple items detected — pick the main one." **Comp eligibility is 13.04's decision consuming the flag** — a $22k bundle comp'd as a $20k Strat poisons the band upward |
| **Left-handed variant** | Handedness is a variant axis; lefty models have distinct and thinner markets. Bind to the RH model gives a wrong comp | Variant picker includes handedness; not an eligibility blocker (titles state "lefty" reliably) |
| **Seller binds up deliberately** — a reissue listed against the original's model to ride its comps | **Match is a claim, and claims are attributed and retained** (D-04, feeds CX-M19). Serial decode + mandatory disclosure (13.02) are the counterweights; mismatch is a domain-24 concern | Comps shown are the *bound* model's; the bind is attributed to the seller in listing history |
| **Seller binds obscure deliberately** — binds to a rare variant with n=2 comps so 13.04.03 declines to speak and cannot contradict the asking price | **The subtler direction, and nobody designs for it** (DT-07). Matching emits a `thin-variant-bind` signal to 13.04 when the seller picks a variant with <5 comps while a sibling with ≥20 comps ranked within margin. Matching does not block | Nothing at match time. 13.04 decides whether to render the parent model's band alongside the thin variant's |
| **Keyword stuffing** — "Fender Strat Gibson Les Paul Martin D-28 rare vintage" on a $20 cable | Matching is **not** the anti-stuffing mechanism — the seller's *pick* binds, not the text, so stuffing cannot poison a bind. It games *text search*, which is CX-M16's problem (DT-09) | Nothing from matching. Category + price gates make every candidate ineligible anyway |
| **Counterfeit bound to the genuine model** — a "Chibson" Les Paul bound to the real Gibson model | Matching cannot detect counterfeits and must not pretend to. It also must not be the mechanism that launders one: the bind is an attributed claim, retained as evidence | Bind proceeds; 13.02 disclosure, 13.01.03 decode labelling and domain 24 are the counterweights |
| **Serial contradicts the chosen model** | Bind follows the **seller's** choice (CX-02 synthesis Q5). Contradiction is recorded to CX-M19 and surfaced to the seller. Whether the *buyer* sees it is 13.02's call — see Q-04 | "You picked the 1962 original; the serial decodes to 1972–74. Which is right?" — surfaced, never auto-resolved |
| **Registry contradicts the bind** — serial is registered in domain 15 to model X; seller binds the listing to Y | Contradiction surfaced and recorded. Bind still follows the seller (same principle as CX-02) | "Your registry says this serial is a [X]. You're listing it as a [Y]." |
| **Multi-hyphenate, both roles at once** — the Producer who registered the unit is the Musician now selling it | **There is no adversarial party to catch the error.** Both records are the same human's assertion, so a registry↔bind agreement proves consistency, not truth. The contradiction check above silently loses its power in exactly this case | No difference in UI. Recorded distinctly (13.01.03 D-02) so a dispute can tell self-agreement from corroboration |
| **Listing publishes unmatched** | Allowed (D-01). Text-searchable only; no comps, no facets, no alert matches, **and no voltage/market warnings** (CX-04) | "Not linked to a catalog model — link it to appear in filtered searches, price data and safety info" |
| **Bound provisional is merged into an existing model** (duplicate) | Same object, more comps. Listing re-points silently; comps improve. No notification — nobody wants "your comps changed 3%" | Listing history entry only |
| **Bound model is merged and identity changes** — the '62-original model absorbs reissues, or country/year range shifts | **Notify — but the trigger is actionability, not change** (D-06). Notify iff an identity axis (year range, country, reissue status) changed, **or** the asking price crossed the comp band boundary in either direction | "Your listing's model changed. You're now priced above market for it." — never "your comps moved 3%" |
| **Bound model is unmerged / split** | Seller must re-choose. **Auto-assignment is forbidden**: an unmerge means the platform previously conflated two things, and guessing again is how you re-conflate them. Listing **stays published**, drops to unmatched-pending-choice | "We split this model in two. Which is yours?" — listing stays live throughout |
| **Bound provisional is rejected by moderation** | Three rejection subtypes, three behaviours — CX-01 synthesis Q2 resolved (D-07): **duplicate** → re-point to survivor, silent. **Nonsense/invalid** → unbind to unmatched, notify, invite re-contribution. **Abuse** → unbind and escalate the *listing* to domain 24 | Duplicate: nothing. Nonsense: "The model you added wasn't accepted — your listing is live but unlinked." Abuse: 24's flow |
| **Merge lands mid-checkout** | CX-03 synthesis Q5 resolved (D-10): the buyer bought the **listing**, not the model. Comps are advisory and never part of the agreement. The bind is snapshotted onto the order at order creation; the re-point does not reach in-flight orders | Buyer sees nothing. The order carries the bind it was placed against |
| **Rebind-as-bump** — seller rebinds repeatedly to re-trigger saved-search alerts | **A spam vector nobody designs for** (DT-03). Fan-out is deduped: once per (listing, model) pair, **ever** — rebinding back to a previously-notified model does not re-fire. Hard ceiling of 1 rebind-triggered fan-out per listing per 30 days regardless | Nothing. Alert waves simply don't fire |
| **Re-match proposes a bind on a live unmatched listing** | Auto-binds only under the identical gates; always notifies; rate-limited to one proposal per listing per 7 days | "We found a catalog model for your listing — [X]. Link it?" |
| **Re-match on a listing whose seller is absent** | Proposal goes nowhere; listing stays unmatched indefinitely. Acceptable — unmatched is legal | Nothing |
| **Bulk import: 400 rows, 120 unmatched** | **No relaxation of the gates** (D-05). Publish unmatched (D-01) + a review queue **grouped by proposed candidate and normalised title** — 120 rows are typically ~12 distinct models × ~10 units, so one decision resolves a whole group | "280 linked · 120 need a model — **12 decisions**" |
| **Bulk, second import, same SKUs** | SKU memoisation (D-08) auto-binds regardless of eligibility — the seller already made the claim by hand once | "398 linked from your previous decisions · 2 new" |
| **Seller recycles a SKU for a different product** (routine in small shops) | The memoised bind is confidently wrong *at volume* — the failure mode of D-08. Memoisation invalidates when the incoming title diverges from the establishing title beyond a token-similarity floor (Jaccard < 0.6, *calibration seed*) | Row falls back to normal matching and lands in the grouped queue |
| **Concurrent bind** — two tabs / two devices | Bind carries a version. Conflicting write returns conflict; no silent last-write-wins on a claim | "This listing's model was changed elsewhere — reload to see the current one" |
| **Two sellers contribute the same missing model concurrently** | Two provisionals; both listings bind; CX-M17 dedupe catches it at moderation; both re-point to the survivor via the duplicate-merge path (silent) | Nothing — this is the silent case by design |
| **Moderator sees an obviously wrong bind** | **Moderators may unbind; they may not rebind** (D-12). Unbind is subtractive — it removes a claim. Rebind is additive — it makes a claim on the seller's behalf, and claims belong to the seller. Requires category-scoped standing (13.01.02 D-02) | Seller: "A moderator unlinked your listing's model — please re-link it." Listing stays published |
| **Match service is down** | Manual model search fallback; unmatched publish permitted. Never blocks (D-01) | "Model matching is unavailable — search manually or link it later" |
| **Bind write fails after publish succeeds** | Degrades to `unmatched`, a legal state. **No compensating transaction exists or is needed** (D-03) | Listing is live and unlinked; re-match will propose it |
| **Title pasted from another marketplace** — 800 chars of marketing copy | Match on the **title field only**; description is tie-break at most (signal 5). A matcher reading 800 chars of prose binds to whatever the prose compares the item to | Candidates from the title; long paste truncated at the title field's limit |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | 300 ms after last keystroke in the title field | Inline debounced candidate spinner; never blocks typing |
| Loading (degraded) | No candidates returned by 2,000 ms | Spinner stops; manual model search replaces it. Never spins indefinitely |
| Empty (no input) | No title text yet | Model search field with a category hint |
| Empty (cold catalog) | Category genuinely has no models | "Be the first to add this model" contribution funnel — **not** an error, not "no results" |
| Populated | Candidates returned | ≤5 ranked candidates, each showing confidence and only the attributes that *distinguish* it from its siblings |
| Populated (auto-bound) | All 7 gates passed | Bound model shown with a visible, one-click "Change model" — auto-bind is always reversible (D-02) |
| Error | Match service unavailable | Manual model search fallback; unmatched publish permitted; listing draft preserved |
| Error (conflict) | Concurrent bind from another session | "This listing's model was changed elsewhere — reload" |
| Partial (variant unselected) | Model bound, variant/finish not chosen | "Which finish/variant?" — comps render at the model's full band width, honestly wide, and narrow once resolved |
| Partial (contradicted) | Serial decode or registry contradicts the bind | Bind stands; contradiction rendered inline to the seller with both claims side by side |
| Partial (unmatched, published) | Listing live with no bind | "Not linked to a catalog model" banner with the concrete cost stated: no filtered search, no price data, no safety info |
| Partial (pending re-choice) | Bound model was unmerged | "We split this model — which is yours?" Listing stays live and searchable throughout |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Matching should block publish until a model is bound — an unmatched listing is a bad listing | Data-purity instinct; a clean catalog argues for it | ❌ REJECTED: the listing flow is the one flow that must never stall (same reasoning as 13.01.02 DT-01, ratified as domain D-03). A seller with a genuinely uncatalogued boutique pedal who is told "you cannot list this" leaves. Unmatched publish is degraded but permitted; the *incentive* to match is that matched listings get comps, facets and alert reach — carrot, not gate. |
| DT-02 | High-confidence auto-bind is safe | Efficiency instinct — fewer prompts, faster listing | ⚠️ CONFIRMED WITH CONSTRAINT: auto-bind is safe **only if reversible and only above a high threshold**, because a wrong bind is worse than no bind — it produces a *confidently wrong* comp set and a price guide entry that lies. The reissue-vs-original case is the canonical trap: high textual similarity, order-of-magnitude price difference. Reissues must be excluded from auto-bind. **Extended by DT-06 (three further sibling classes) and DT-10 (margin, not magnitude).** |
| DT-03 | Rebinding is a spam vector | Reasoning about the alert fan-out on the *other* side of a bind — nothing in the sweep looked at matching from the buyer's notification surface | ✅ CONFIRMED: a rebind makes a listing newly eligible for saved searches on the new model, which fires an alert wave. A seller who rebinds between two plausible models five times triggers five waves to the same buyers — **"bump" behaviour, free, with no listing edit and no fee**. Every marketplace with saved searches eventually grows this exploit. Dedupe per (listing, model) pair forever, plus a hard 30-day ceiling (D-09). The obvious "notify on rebind" design is the exploit. |
| DT-04 | Matching is a listing-creation-time event | The framing inherited from the Happy Path and from the name of the feature itself | ❌ REJECTED: this makes the catalog's growth worthless to every listing that predates it. A pedal published unmatched in month 1 *because the model didn't exist* stays unmatched forever, while the model gets contributed in month 3 by someone else. **The unmatched pool would only ever grow, and the thinnest categories — Producer's outboard — would be permanently the worst served.** Matching is a continuous background process with a listing-creation-time entry point (D-13). |
| DT-05 | Listing price should be a match signal | It is genuinely informative — a £40 "Stratocaster" is a pickguard, not a guitar | ⚠️ CONFIRMED, SUPPRESSION-ONLY: price may **suppress** an auto-bind (route to a human); it must never **drive** a bind or reject a listing. Reason: comps derive from bound listings, so if price gated binding, the comps gating future binds were themselves gated by comps — a circular loop that entrenches whatever the guide already believes and quietly excludes genuine outliers (the underpriced estate-sale find). **Suppression-only breaks the loop**, because a suppressed bind routes to a human whose confirmation then legitimately enters the comp set. The human is the loop-breaker. |
| DT-06 | The reissue is the only auto-bind trap | DT-02 named it and stopped there | ❌ REJECTED — there are four, and **the one DT-02 missed is a safety failure, not a data failure**. Per CX-04, voltage/market warnings are *derived* from the bound model's attributes. A 100V Japan-market Twin Reverb auto-bound to the 120V US model (identical name, identical title) yields **no warning for a US buyer** — the model says 120V, the market is 120V, everything agrees, and the actual unit runs hot until it dies. The warning that should have fired is not suppressed; it is **underivable**, and nobody knows it should have existed. Market/voltage variants, revision variants (1176 Rev A vs F; U47 tube vs FET) and country-of-manufacture variants (MIA/MIM/MIJ) all join reissues in the exclusion set. **Matching failure is a safety failure by way of 13.01.05, and this is the load-bearing reason auto-bind is structurally gated rather than score-gated.** |
| DT-07 | Sellers game matching by binding **up** — claiming the more valuable model | DT-02's assumption, and the intuitive fraud | ⚠️ CONFIRMED BUT INCOMPLETE — **the opposite direction is subtler and nobody designs for it.** Binding up is loud: it contradicts the serial, contradicts disclosure, and invites a domain-24 report. **Binding *obscure* is silent and works better.** 13.04.03 correctly declines to speak on thin markets — so a seller who binds to a rare variant with n=2 comps has *successfully silenced the price guide* and can ask anything without contradiction. The platform's own honesty mechanism becomes the evasion. Matching cannot block it (the rare variant may be genuine) but must emit a `thin-variant-bind` signal so 13.04 can render the parent band alongside. **13.04.03's integrity depends on a signal only matching can produce.** |
| DT-08 | A draft's bind can be trusted at publish | Drafts hold state; re-resolving looks wasteful | ❌ REJECTED: drafts are held open for days. In that window the bound provisional may be merged, unmerged or rejected by moderation (13.01.02's async queue). Publishing a week-old bind to a model that no longer exists produces a dangling listing — or worse, a listing bound to a merged-away record whose comps are now someone else's. **Binds resolve at publish, not at draft.** The cost is one lookup; the alternative is silent corruption proportional to draft age. |
| DT-09 | Matching should defend against keyword stuffing | Every marketplace has this problem, so this feature must own some of it | ❌ REJECTED — inverted responsibility. In this design **the seller's pick binds, not the text**; the text only ranks candidates *shown to the seller*. Stuffing a title with model names produces a longer candidate list for the stuffer to scroll, and nothing else. Stuffing games **text search** (CX-M16) and **alerts**, not matching. A rejection worth recording because the instinct to bolt anti-abuse onto every surface would have added a mechanism that defends against nothing. |
| DT-10 | Confidence magnitude is a sufficient auto-bind gate | The standard formulation, and what Q-01 implicitly assumed by asking for "the threshold" | ❌ REJECTED: **the trap produces two high-confidence candidates, not one.** "62 Jazzmaster" scores 0.96 against the original and 0.94 against the reissue — a threshold of 0.95 auto-binds the original and is wrong roughly half the time, at 10× value. Magnitude measures *how well the top candidate fits*; it says nothing about *whether anything else fits equally well*. **Margin is the gate that matters**; magnitude is necessary and nowhere near sufficient. |
| DT-11 | A dealer's input is free text | The Happy Path's framing — everyone types a title | ❌ REJECTED, and this is the answer to Q-03. **An Operator does not identify gear; they already did.** A shop with 400 units has an internal catalog with its own SKUs and ran this identification in its back office months ago. Their export's highest-information column is the **SKU**, which no text matcher looks at. Memoising `(seller, SKU) → model` from the first human-confirmed bind means import #2 auto-binds at ~100% **regardless of eligibility rules**, because a prior human claim by this same seller outranks any text score (D-08). This does not bend the quality bar for bulk — it raises it, using evidence a per-row prompt would have thrown away. Failure mode: shops recycle SKUs, so memoisation must invalidate on title divergence. |
| DT-12 | Image matching from listing photos is the missing signal | A domain expert's obvious expectation, and the only signal that could catch a partscaster or a relic without a serial | ⏳ NOT REJECTED — ARCHITECTURALLY OUT OF SCOPE. It is genuinely the only non-serial signal that sees the *object* rather than the seller's description of it. But it is a model/ML decision belonging to `/create-prd`, and **behaviourally it changes nothing here**: an image signal slots in as one more ranking input beneath the same seven gates, and its output would be subject to the same sibling-exclusion (a photo cannot distinguish a good relic from an original either — that is the entire point of a relic). Recorded so `/create-prd` knows the slot exists and the gates already accommodate it. |
| DT-13 | This feature's real scope is "listing → model" | The node's name, inherited from the gap it was created to fill | ⚠️ CONFIRMED WITH DOUBT — see Q-03. The *capability* is `free text + serial + category → canonical model`, and at least four other consumers need exactly that: domain 15 at registration, 13.09.03 at trade-in intake, 13.10 for rate cards, and domain 16 for venue backline specs. Each will otherwise build its own resolver against the same catalog, and they will disagree — which reproduces the reconstruction failure D-18 exists to end, inside the catalog. Flagged for Step 6 rather than decided here: promoting it is a structural change above this node's authority. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Consumes **CX-M17 Canonical Data, Taxonomy & Entity Resolution** — the generic resolution substrate (dedupe, merge, alias, unmerge). The gear-specific ranking signals, the gear lexicon and the sibling-exclusion rule live here. *Shared entity: the model record.*
- Consumes **[13.01.03 Serial Decoding](./13.01.03-serial-decoding-dating.md)** — a decoded serial is the strongest available match signal and the only reliable escape from the reissue trap (CX-02). *Trigger dependency: serial entered → decode → re-rank.*
- Consumes **[13.01.01 Model Records](./13.01.01-model-records-taxonomy-attributes.md)** — sibling-derivation (D-11) reads the model graph, and the era-boundary flags and gear lexicon are catalog knowledge with no declared home (Q-02). *Shared entity + a genuine gap.*
- Routes to **[13.01.02 Catalog Contribution](./13.01.02-catalog-contribution-moderation.md)** on match failure, and consumes its moderation outcomes: merge, unmerge and all three rejection subtypes re-point or unbind live listings (D-07). *Trigger dependency + state race — moderation writes asynchronously underneath live binds.*
- Gates **[13.04 Price Discovery](../13.04-price-discovery-market-data/)** — no bind, no comps. Emits the `bundle` and `thin-variant-bind` signals it needs; **[13.04.03](../13.04-price-discovery-market-data/13.04.03-valuation-confidence-thin-market.md)'s thin-market integrity depends on a signal only this feature can produce** (DT-07). *Trigger dependency.*
- **Safety-couples to [13.01.05 Fitment & Voltage](./13.01.05-fitment-compatibility-voltage.md)** via CX-04 — warnings derive from the bound model's attributes, so a mis-bound market/voltage variant makes a safety warning **underivable, not merely missing** (DT-06). The persona harmed (Fan) has zero access to the mechanism. *Trigger dependency with a safety consequence — the highest-severity cross-cut on this node.*
- Consumed by **[13.03.04 Bulk Listing & Channel Sync](../13.03-listings-inventory/13.03.04-bulk-listing-channel-sync.md)** — matching at volume. Resolves that file's matching half via grouped queues (D-05) + SKU memoisation (D-08); its Q-01 (disclosure/media at bulk) is untouched by this. *Trigger dependency.*
- Contradiction-checks against **Domain 15 Gear Registry** — a serial registered to model X while its listing binds to Y is surfaced and recorded. **Silently powerless when the registrant and the seller are the same multi-hyphenate**, which is the common case. *Shared entity: the serial-keyed unit.*
- Feeds **CX-M19 Audit Log & Provenance Ledger** — every bind is an attributed claim retained as dispute evidence (D-04), stored distinctly from decoded facts (13.01.03 D-02). *Shared entity.*
- Fans out to **CX-M16 Search & Discovery / saved-search alerts** — a rebind is an alert-eligibility event, deduped per (listing, model) pair forever with a 30-day ceiling to close the rebind-as-bump vector (D-09). *Notification fan-out.*
- Snapshotted by **[13.06 Cart, Checkout & Orders](../13.06-cart-checkout-orders/)** — the bind is captured onto the order at creation so a merge cannot re-point a comp set under an in-flight transaction (D-10). *State race.*
- Escalates to **Domain 24 Trust, Safety & Disputes** — abuse-rejected models unbind and escalate the *listing*; deliberate mis-binds are a 24 concern, not a matching one. Fan's only recourse against a wrong bind lives here. *Permission + trigger dependency.*

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Unmatched listings may publish; no gate ever rejects a listing | Per DT-01 and domain D-03 — never stall the listing flow. |
| D-02 | Auto-bind only at high confidence **and** high margin, always reversible, ambiguity-siblings excluded | Per DT-02, DT-06, DT-10 — a wrong bind is worse than no bind. Every threshold is set for precision (≥0.99), not coverage. |
| D-03 | Every matching failure degrades to `unmatched`, a legal publishable state | No failure leaves an illegal state, so matching needs no rollback or compensating transaction. Falls out of D-01; worth naming because it removes a whole class of machinery. |
| D-04 | A bind is an attributed claim, retained as dispute evidence | Matching cannot detect counterfeits or lies and must not pretend to — but it must not launder them either. Feeds CX-M19. |
| D-05 | Bulk does **not** relax the gates | Per DT-11 — relaxing for bulk gives Operators wrong comps at scale, which is worse than none. The decision *count* is reduced by grouping (N rows → K decisions), never the quality bar. |
| D-06 | Merge notification triggers on **actionability**, not on change | Resolves Q-02. Notify iff an identity axis changed or the asking price crossed the comp band boundary. "Your comps moved 3%" is noise nobody acts on; "you're now priced above market" is not. |
| D-07 | Moderation rejection has three subtypes with three behaviours | Resolves CX-01 synthesis Q2. Duplicate → silent re-point. Nonsense → unbind + notify. Abuse → unbind + escalate to 24. One word ("rejected") was hiding three outcomes. |
| D-08 | `(seller, SKU) → model` is memoised from the first human-confirmed bind and outranks every text signal | Per DT-11 — resolves Q-03. A prior human claim beats any score. Invalidated on title divergence (Jaccard < 0.6) because shops recycle SKUs. |
| D-09 | Alert fan-out on rebind is deduped per (listing, model) pair forever, ceiling 1 per listing per 30 days | Per DT-03 — the obvious "notify on rebind" design is a free bump exploit. |
| D-10 | The bind is snapshotted onto the order at order creation | Resolves CX-03 synthesis Q5. The buyer bought the listing, not the model; comps are advisory and never part of the agreement. |
| D-11 | Auto-bind eligibility is **derived from the catalog's sibling graph**, not configured per-category | Resolves Q-01's structural half. Twelve tuned thresholds are unauditable; sibling-derivation is explainable, self-tightening as the catalog grows, and makes prior auto-binds review candidates when a new sibling appears. |
| D-12 | Moderators may **unbind**; they may not **rebind** | Unbind is subtractive (removes a claim); rebind is additive (makes a claim on the seller's behalf). Claims belong to the seller. Requires category-scoped standing per 13.01.02 D-02. |
| D-13 | Matching is continuous, with a listing-creation entry point — not a creation-time event | Per DT-04 — otherwise catalog growth never heals old listings and the unmatched pool only grows, hurting the thinnest categories most. |
| D-14 | Match on the **title**; description is a tie-break signal at most | Descriptions name other models constantly ("sounds like a Klon"). A matcher reading prose binds to the comparison. Title is the claim; description is prose. |
