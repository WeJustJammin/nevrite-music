## Overview

The two alternatives to selling it yourself: hand it to a dealer as part-payment for something else (trade-in), or hand it to someone who sells it on your behalf and takes a cut (consignment). Both exist because most people would rather accept less money than deal with photographing, listing, disclosing, packing and shipping an instrument — and both hinge on a stranger's assessment of what the thing is actually worth.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: trade-in, consignment and intake grading are three capabilities that interact — they are alternatives offered at the same decision point ("£800 now, or consign it and likely get £1,100 minus 15%"), and both depend on the same physical inspection that may contradict the owner's own account of the gear.
