## Overview

Undoing a sale, routing a repair, and the three-way mess of warranty on gear that may be new, used, or B-stock. Returns here are statutory in most of the platform's likely markets, expensive in ways retail returns aren't (who pays return freight on a 60kg amp from another country?), and complicated by the fact that the returning object is the *same* object — its condition on return is itself a claim.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: returns, repair routing and warranty are three distinct capabilities that interact — a return may become a warranty claim, a warranty claim needs the original order and the serial, and a repair may be the alternative to either.
