# Feature: Listing Lifecycle & Relisting

> **Number**: 13.03.02
> **Parent**: [Listings & Inventory](./13.03-listings-inventory-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The state machine a listing moves through — draft → active → reserved → sold → ended — plus editing rules, expiry, and relisting when a sale falls through. It matters more for gear than for fungible retail because a qty=1 listing has no "back in stock": when it sells it is gone forever, and when a sale collapses the unit must return to market carrying its history, not as a fresh listing pretending nothing happened.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (own listings) | Manages a handful of listings; edits price when nothing bites; relists after a buyer ghosts. Wants "why isn't this selling?" answered — which is where the price guide (13.04) enters. |
| Producer | Full (own) | Similar volume; longer sale cycles on niche outboard, so expiry and relisting matter more — a rare unit may sit for months and must not silently die. |
| Operator | Full (own) | Manages fleet-scale lifecycle where individual listings blur into inventory state; drives lifecycle via bulk operations (13.03.04), not one-by-one. |
| Fan | None | No listing surface (per 13.03.01 Role Lens). Sees lifecycle only as a listing that vanished — hence the ended-listing handling in Edge Cases. |

## Behavior

The lifecycle is authoritative over availability; 13.03.03 enforces it under concurrency. **Edits are constrained by state**: freely editable while active with no offers; price-relevant edits are restricted once an offer is live (13.05.01) or the unit is reserved. A sold listing is immutable and becomes a comp (13.04.01).

### Happy Path

1. Seller publishes → `active`
2. Buyer purchases → `reserved` (payment authorising) → `sold`
3. Listing becomes immutable and enters the comp set
4. Order lifecycle (13.06.02) takes over

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Buyer doesn't pay / abandons | Reservation expires; listing returns to `active` | "Back on sale" — and the reservation history persists internally |
| Sale falls through post-payment (seller can't ship, damaged in storage) | Relist — but the listing carries a cancellation against the seller | Relist CTA; cancellation counted toward seller performance (CX-M08) |
| Seller edits price while an offer is pending | Not permitted — an offer is against a stated price | "Withdraw the pending offer first" |
| Seller edits the *unit's* condition after purchase | Materially changes what was bought; should void, not silently update | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see 13.02 CX-02 synthesis Q5, unresolved and shared |
| Listing expires unsold | Ends; relist offered with the original media/disclosure intact | "Relist in one tap" |
| Sold listing hit by a search engine or an old link | Must not 404 — sold listings are the price guide's evidence and SEO's substance | Sold-state page with price, date, and comps for the same model |
| Seller deletes a listing to erase a bad history | Delete must not destroy comp data or dispute evidence | `[PENDING]` — see Q-02 |
| Unit sold off-platform mid-listing | Seller ends it manually; no penalty, but repeated patterns are a disintermediation signal | End-listing reason picker |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Listing management view | Listing table skeleton |
| Empty | Seller has no listings | "List your first piece of gear" |
| Populated | Listings exist | State-grouped listing table |
| Error | State transition fails | Error with retry; state never left ambiguous — the source of oversell |
| Partial | Reserved pending payment | "Reserved — awaiting payment (expires in N)" for the seller; unavailable for other buyers |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A sold listing can be archived or deleted — its job is done | Standard e-commerce instinct; sold items are clutter | ❌ REJECTED: the sold listing **is the comp**. Deleting it destroys the price guide's only evidence base (13.04.01) and erases the strongest SEO surface the domain has (CX-M23 — "what is my 1972 Telecaster worth" is a search with enormous volume). Sold listings must persist as immutable public records. This also means a seller cannot delete their way out of a bad price history — which is correct, and needs saying (Q-02). |
| DT-02 | Relisting after a failed sale is just creating a new listing | It looks identical from the form's perspective | ❌ REJECTED: a relist carries history a fresh listing doesn't — a prior reservation that collapsed, possibly a buyer dispute, possibly a seller cancellation. Treating relist as "new" launders that history and lets a seller cycle a problem unit indefinitely with a clean face. **The relist must inherit the unit's identity** (serial → domain 15 where registered), not just its form fields. Note this contradicts nothing but requires the unit, not the listing, to be the identity anchor — an architectural point. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Sold listings are immutable and permanent | Per DT-01 — they are the comp set and the SEO surface. |
| D-02 | Relists inherit unit history, not just form fields | Per DT-02 — prevents laundering a problem unit's history. |
| D-03 | Price edits are blocked while an offer is live | An offer is made against a stated price; moving it mid-offer is bad faith. |
