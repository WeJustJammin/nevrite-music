# Feature: Inventory & Oversell Prevention

> **Number**: 13.03.03
> **Parent**: [Listings & Inventory](./13.03-listings-inventory-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Making sure the one 1962 Jazzmaster is sold exactly once. Used gear is qty=1 and non-fungible, so overselling is not a backorder inconvenience — it is a promise the seller physically cannot keep, and the loser of the race wanted *that unit*, not one like it. This feature owns the product answer to that race: who wins, what the loser sees, and how the seller is told.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (own listings) | As seller: sees the unit reserved the instant it's claimed. As buyer: the persona most likely to *lose* a race on a hunted-for vintage piece, and the one who cares most — they've had a saved search running for months. |
| Producer | Full (own) | Same. Loses races on rare outboard where the next one may be years away. |
| Operator | Full (own) | The persona that oversells hardest — fleet stock listed across multiple channels (13.03.04), sold in a shop, listed here, and now double-promised. Cross-channel is their oversell surface, not concurrent buyers. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees availability state as a buyer. No inventory controls. Most confused by losing a race — needs "someone bought it 4 seconds before you" in plain language, not a stock error. |

## Behavior

Listing state (13.03.02) is authoritative; this feature defends it under concurrency. A purchase or accepted offer claims the unit atomically, moving it to `reserved`. Multiple simultaneous claims resolve to exactly one winner. **The losing buyer's experience is a product surface, not an error path.**

### Happy Path

1. Two buyers hit Buy Now on the same unit within milliseconds
2. Exactly one claim succeeds; unit → `reserved`
3. Winner proceeds to checkout (13.06.01)
4. Loser is told immediately and clearly, and offered somewhere to go: similar listings, a saved search, an ISO post (13.05.03)
5. Seller sees one sale, never two

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Two buyers race on one unit | One wins atomically | Loser: "Someone bought this 4 seconds before you" + similar units + "alert me if it relists" — never a 500, never a silent cart removal |
| Buyer wins, then abandons payment | Reservation expires, unit returns to `active`; the loser should be told | "The Jazzmaster you missed is back" — a genuinely delightful moment, and free |
| Two accepted offers on one unit (13.05.01) | An accepted offer is a claim; the second acceptance must fail | Seller: "You already accepted an offer on this unit" |
| Sold on another channel, still listed here (Operator's real case) | Sync (13.03.04) is best-effort; a window always exists. Seller must cancel, which costs them | Seller cancellation → CX-M08 performance hit. See DT-02. |
| Unit in a cart, not yet purchased | **A cart is not a claim.** Carts hold intent; only purchase claims | "In someone's cart" is not shown — it would be pressure UX and it isn't true |
| Bundle contains a unit also listed individually (13.03.05) | One physical object, two listings → both must reserve together | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — shared-unit reservation across listings unresolved |
| Seller has genuine multi-qty stock (new pedals, strings) | qty>1 is a different regime; oversell is a stock count, not a race | Both regimes must exist — see DT-01 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Claim attempted | Momentary "securing this item" — must be fast enough to be invisible |
| Empty | n/a | — |
| Populated | Available | "Available" + Buy Now |
| Error | Claim lost | Explicit loss message with routes onward (not an error dialog) |
| Partial | Reserved, payment pending | Buyer: checkout timer. Others: "Reserved — alert me if it returns" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This domain is entirely qty=1, so inventory is a single boolean | D-14's own framing ("qty=1 non-fungible stock") and the domain index's "brutal physics" | ❌ REJECTED — and this is a real gap in D-14's reasoning. Candidate #23 (parts, consumables, bundles, B-stock) is **genuinely fungible with real stock counts**: strings, valves, patch cables, a dealer's 12 identical new pedals. The domain therefore carries **two inventory regimes**, not one, and the qty=1 story — while correct about what makes the domain distinct — is not complete. Flagged to the domain index; it slightly weakens the D-14 boundary argument without overturning it (13.03.05 D-01 keeps them clearly separated). |
| DT-02 | Cross-channel sync makes cross-channel oversell solvable | Sync is listed as a candidate (#19) and reads like the fix | ❌ REJECTED: sync is best-effort over systems WeJammin does not control, with propagation delays measured in minutes. A unit sold in a physical shop at 14:02 cannot be un-sellable here at 14:02:30. **Cross-channel oversell is unpreventable in principle**; the honest design is to make cancellation cheap, fast and non-punitive *for this cause specifically*, rather than pretending sync is a guarantee. This directly contradicts the naive reading of candidate #19 and matters for Operator adoption — a seller punished for physics stops listing. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The losing buyer is a designed experience, not an error | The persona wanted *that* unit after months of searching. A 500 here is a lost user. |
| D-02 | A cart is not a claim; only purchase claims | Cart-holds on qty=1 gear would let one user freeze a unit others want. Also rules out "3 people are looking at this" pressure UX. |
| D-03 | Two inventory regimes coexist: qty=1 non-fungible and counted-stock fungible | Per DT-01. |
| D-04 | Cross-channel oversell is treated as unpreventable; cancellation for that cause is cheap and non-punitive | Per DT-02 — punishing sellers for physics loses the Operator persona. |
