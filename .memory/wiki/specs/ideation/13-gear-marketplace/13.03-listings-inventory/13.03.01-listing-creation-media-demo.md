# Feature: Listing Creation, Media & Unit Demo

> **Number**: 13.03.01
> **Parent**: [Listings & Inventory](./13.03-listings-inventory-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The flow that turns a physical object in someone's room into a listing — model binding, condition, disclosure, price, media, and the gear-specific one: an **audio/video demo of this actual unit**. Used gear is non-fungible, so a demo of *this* amp is evidence, not marketing; it is the closest a remote buyer gets to playing it. This is the domain's highest-traffic write path and the one that must never stall.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Lists a pedal from their phone in a few minutes. The multi-hyphenate "part-time gear flipper" in the persona definition lives in this flow. Records a quick demo clip of the unit because they own it and can play it. |
| Producer | Full | Lists outboard from the studio. Uniquely able to record a *credible* demo — they have the room, the signal chain and the ears. A Producer's demo of a compressor is worth more than any spec sheet. |
| Operator | Full | Lists surplus/ex-rental stock, often in batches, often from a loading dock on a phone. Needs the flow to survive one-handed, bad-signal use — the persona's stated constraint. |
| Fan | None | No listing surface. Rationale: selling gear is a professional act requiring payout onboarding (CX-M15/CX-M01), tax identity (CX-M02) and disclosure liability the persona has no context for. A Fan who starts selling gear is behaving as a Musician — the personas are behavioural lenses, not account types (`meta/personas.md`). |

## Behavior

Creation is a resumable draft that composes the other nodes: model binding (13.01.04), condition and disclosure (13.02), price (optionally guided by 13.04), media, and fulfilment options. Media is **unit-specific by default** — stock photos defeat the evidence pack (13.02.04). Audio/video demo is optional but prompted, because it is the highest-converting artifact in used-gear commerce and the one only the owner can produce.

### Happy Path

1. Seller starts a listing; system asks what it is (13.01.04 matching)
2. Seller binds a model (or contributes one — 13.01.02)
3. Seller sets condition + completes disclosure (13.02)
4. Seller adds photos; system prompts for a demo clip
5. Seller sets price; system may show the price guide (13.04) alongside
6. Seller sets fulfilment (ship / local pickup / both) and publishes

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Draft abandoned mid-flow | Resumable indefinitely; nothing is lost | Draft list with resume CTA |
| Model can't be matched | Contribute inline, or publish unmatched (13.01.04 D-01) | "Add this model" / "list without linking" |
| Connectivity drops mid-upload at a loading dock | Draft persists; media retries | Queued-upload indicator; publish available once required media lands |
| Seller has no payout method configured | Cannot complete a sale even if listed | Blocked at publish with route to CX-M15 onboarding — the one non-negotiable prerequisite |
| Demo clip contains copyrighted music (playing a riff to demo a guitar) | Genuine and unresolved — a demo of a guitar *is* someone playing a song | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see DT-02. Escalates to CX-M10 + domain 24. |
| Seller lists a unit they've registered in domain 15 | Registry pre-fills model, serial, mods, photos — the consolidation payoff | "Listing your 1972 Telecaster? We already know its specs" |
| Same unit listed twice by the same seller | Serial collision detectable where a serial exists | Duplicate warning |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Form/media loading | Progressive form; never a full-page block |
| Empty | New draft | "What are you selling?" single field |
| Populated | Draft complete | Preview of the published listing |
| Error | Publish fails | Draft preserved; retry; explicit reason |
| Partial | Required media/disclosure incomplete | Publish CTA disabled with a named blocker — never a generic "form invalid" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A unit-specific audio/video demo is marketing polish — nice, not structural | It reads as a content feature, and the sweep listed it parenthetically | ❌ REJECTED: for non-fungible used gear the demo is **evidence of function**, not promotion. It is the only way a remote buyer can assess "does this amp actually sound right", and it is uniquely producible by the seller (who owns the thing) and uniquely credible from a Producer (who owns the room). It also feeds the evidence pack — a demo is a timestamped record that the unit worked at listing time, which pre-empts an entire dispute class ("it arrived dead"). This is a **should**, not a **could**. |
| DT-02 | Demo audio is unproblematic content | It's a few seconds of someone playing gear | ❌ REJECTED: a guitar demo is a person playing a copyrighted riff, and this platform *owns a rights stack* (D-10, domains 09–12). WeJammin hosting unlicensed recordings of copyrighted compositions — while positioning itself as the industry's rights infrastructure — is a credibility problem before it is a legal one. Nobody in the candidate list noticed this. Needs a real answer, not a takedown process. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Media is unit-specific by default; stock imagery is not the norm | Stock photos defeat the evidence pack and make "not as described" unadjudicable. |
| D-02 | Demo clips are prompted, never required | Per DT-01 they are high-value, but requiring them would stall the flow (sub-domain D-01) for sellers who can't play the instrument they inherited. |
| D-03 | Payout onboarding is the only hard publish prerequisite | Everything else degrades; a seller who can't be paid cannot transact at all. |
