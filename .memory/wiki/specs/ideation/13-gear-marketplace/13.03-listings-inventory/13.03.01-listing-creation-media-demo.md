# Feature: Listing Creation, Media & Unit Demo

> **Number**: 13.03.01
> **Parent**: [Listings & Inventory](./13.03-listings-inventory-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The flow that turns a physical object in someone's room into a listing — model binding, condition, disclosure, price, media, and the gear-specific one: an **audio/video demo of this actual unit**. Used gear is non-fungible, so a demo of *this* amp is evidence, not marketing; it is the closest a remote buyer gets to playing it. This is the domain's highest-traffic write path and the one that must never stall (domain D-03).

It is also, per DT-08, the **first moment the platform learns that a unit exists** — model, serial, condition, photos, a recording of it working, and a named holder, all volunteered in one sitting by someone with every incentive to be accurate. Everything domain 15 wants to know arrives here. 13.06.05 writes *who owns it*; this flow writes *that it is*. If this flow does not emit the unit, the registry only ever learns about gear that sold, and the consolidation loop CX-07 describes has no way to start.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (as seller) | Lists a pedal from their phone in one sitting. The multi-hyphenate "part-time gear flipper" in `meta/personas.md` lives in this flow — they list 2–5 units a year, remember none of the conventions between times, and will abandon a form that asks them to learn something. Records a demo because they own the thing and can play it. The persona most likely to hit the **partscaster** case (13.01.04 gate 6) and the **estate-sale** case (DT-05) — inherited gear they cannot play and cannot describe. Also the persona whose listing photos most often carry home-GPS EXIF on a $40k instrument (DT-12). |
| Producer | Full (as seller) | Lists outboard from the studio. **Uniquely able to record a credible demo** — they have the room, the chain and the ears, and their gear's defects are *audible rather than photographable* (13.02.02's `audible` evidence class). A Producer demoing an 1176 is simultaneously demoing it and disclosing its noise floor, whether they intend to or not (DT-04). Their catalog binds fail most (outboard is the thinnest catalog region, 13.01.04 Role Lens), so contribution-inline is their normal path, not an exception. Per DT-07 their credibility is **not** rendered on the clip. |
| Operator | Full (as seller) | Lists surplus and ex-rental stock, often in batches (13.03.04), often one-handed at a loading dock on a bad connection — the persona's stated constraint. The persona for whom D-06 exists: **the obligations do not bend for bulk, the capture moment moves to label print**, when they are physically holding the unit and already have the phone out. Their fleet units frequently already carry platform-held condition records (rental returns, repairs), so pre-fill does the most work here. The only persona routinely listing a unit they do not currently hold — out on rental, in repair custody, or on consignment (edge cases below). |
| Fan | None | No listing surface exists for this persona, and a deep link to it does **not** render a 403 — it routes to CX-M15 seller activation (D-09). Rationale: selling gear requires payout onboarding (CX-M01/CX-M15), tax identity, and disclosure liability the Fan persona has no context for; but per `meta/personas.md` the boundary is porous ("may be a hobbyist musician too"), and a Fan who starts selling gear **is behaving as a Musician** — the personas are behavioural lenses, not account types. Denying them is modelling personas as account types, which D-19 explicitly rejects. Activating them is the correct behaviour. The Fan is, however, the persona **most harmed** by this flow's failures: they are least able to read a `Stock imagery` marker as a warning, least able to hear that a demo clip contains the hum nobody declared, and (per 13.02.02) least able to interpret a flaw string. Bears on Q-03. |

## Behavior

Creation is a **resumable draft** that composes the other nodes rather than owning them: model binding (13.01.04), condition grade (13.02.01), flaw and originality disclosure (13.02.02/03), price (optionally guided by 13.04), media, and fulfilment. This file owns **media and the demo**, the **draft/publish transaction**, and the **unit-emission event** (D-07). Everything else is composed.

**The governing rule is domain D-03: the flow never stalls.** Every degradation publishes. There are exactly two publish blockers in this flow and both are inherited, not invented here:

| Blocker | Source | Why it is worth a stall |
|---|---|---|
| Material flaw evidence missing | 13.02.02 D-03 | The single deliberate exception in the listing flow. A material flaw with no capture is the "not as described" dispute, pre-loaded. |
| No payout method configured | D-03 here | A seller who cannot be paid cannot transact. Everything else degrades to a worse listing; this degrades to a fictional one. |

Everything else — unmatched model, no demo, stock imagery, no serial, no disclosure — **publishes with a marker and a stated consequence**. This mirrors 13.02.02's `Condition itemised` / `Condition not itemised` pattern: the incentive to do it properly is that a properly-made listing gets comps, facets, evidence-pack protection and buyer trust. Carrot, not gate.

### Media — the three classes

Media is **unit-specific by default** (D-01). The three classes are not interchangeable and the pipeline must not treat them as one:

| Class | What it is | Processing | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Listing photos** | This unit, as it is | EXIF GPS + camera serial stripped at ingest (D-11); orientation normalised; perceptual hash computed for stock/duplicate detection (D-02) | Public commerce media on a valuable object at a residential address. See DT-12. |
| **Flaw evidence** | The flaw itself, framed (13.02.02 D-03) | Stripped and hashed as above. **No enhancement, no denoise, no normalisation** (D-05) | Enhancing a photo of a hairline check erases the hairline check. |
| **Demo / flaw audio** | This unit, functioning | Transcode for playback only. **CX-M10's loudness normalisation, noise suppression and gate MUST be bypassed** (D-05) | The exact processes that "improve" audio are the processes that erase a hum, a rattle, a scratchy pot and a noisy transformer — which is to say, they erase the disclosure. See DT-06. |

**Caps** (calibration seeds — the pattern follows 13.01.04 Q-01: behaviour is specified, constants need data):

| Limit | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Photos per listing | 20 (min 1 for used gear to earn `Unit photographed`) | Seed. Reverb's practical norm is 8–12. |
| Photo file size | ≤ 25 MB, ≤ 8000 px longest edge | Seed. Rejects image bombs; accommodates a modern phone. |
| Accepted photo types | JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP — **validated by content sniff, not extension** | A polyglot file renamed `.jpg` is the standard media-upload attack. |
| Demo clip length | ≤ 60 s | **Not** a seed. Nobody needs 5 minutes to hear whether an amp works, and every second past ~60 s increases composition-rights exposure (DT-02) for zero evidentiary gain. |
| Demo clip size | ≤ 100 MB | Seed. 60 s of 1080p video. |
| Accepted audio/video | AAC/MP3/WAV/M4A; H.264/HEVC | Whatever a phone produces without the seller converting anything. |
| Demo clips per listing | 3 | Seed. One per functional claim (clean, driven, effects loop) is the realistic ceiling. |

### The Demo — what it is and is not

The demo is **evidence of function, not a mix** (DT-01, DT-06). The UI frames it as such — *"we're checking it works, not making a record"* — because the seller's instinct is to apologise for their phone mic and then not record anything. A phone in front of a cranked Twin sounds bad; that is fine, and the listing must say so, because a seller who believes a bad demo hurts them will record no demo.

**Medium follows the failure mode** (DT-11):

| Gear class | Default medium | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Amps, pedals, outboard, synths, electronics | **Audio** | The claim is "it makes the right noise and no wrong noise". Video adds nothing and adds a face. |
| Mechanical: tape transports, Leslie rotors, piano/organ actions, drum pedals, tremolo arms, faders | **Video** | The claim is **motion**. A Leslie that spins up slow is a failure you cannot hear in a still frame or describe in a checkbox. |
| Acoustic instruments | **Audio** | Same as electronics. |
| Sealed / new-in-box | **None** | Opening it to demo it destroys the thing being sold. This is the one place where absence of a demo is not a signal at all. |

Demos are **prompted, never required** (D-02) and carry a visible **capture date** forever (D-04).

### The Serial Photo — one prompt, four payoffs

The single highest-leverage prompt in this flow (DT-12). One photo of the serial plate:

1. Feeds **13.01.03** serial decoding → the only reliable escape from 13.01.04's reissue trap
2. Feeds **13.03.07** stolen-serial screening → the gate cannot fire without a serial
3. Feeds **13.02.04**'s fraud counterweight (*"serial visible in dispatch photos is the counterweight"*)
4. Feeds **domain 15**'s unit record (D-07) → and therefore CX-07's pre-fill loop, and 13.03.06's provenance panel

It is prompted, not required — some gear genuinely has no serial (most pedals, most pre-1970 cymbals, most parts), and requiring it would stall the flow for the categories least able to comply.

### Stock Imagery — resolving Q-02

**Prohibition is not the mechanism.** The platform cannot prove a photo depicts a given unit, and a rule it cannot enforce is a rule that punishes only the honest. What *is* detectable is the case that actually matters:

| Detection | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Photo perceptually matches a **catalog/manufacturer image** (13.01.01 model media) | Listing publishes. Earns `Stock imagery` marker instead of `Unit photographed`. Excluded from flaw-free buyer facets. **Contributes nothing to the evidence pack** (13.02.04) — the pack renders `No unit evidence` for this listing. |
| Photo perceptually matches **another live listing's** photo | Same markers, **plus** escalation to domain 24. This is photo theft, which is endemic on used-gear marketplaces and is the leading indicator of a listing for a unit the seller does not have. The original seller is **not** notified — a tip-off is a retaliation and doxxing vector, and 24 owns the response. |
| Stock-matched image submitted as **material flaw evidence** | **Rejected.** A stock image cannot show this unit's crack. This is not a new gate — it is 13.02.02 D-03's existing gate applying correctly. |
| Condition grade is `New` / `Sealed` (13.02.01) | **Stock imagery is legitimate and unmarked.** A sealed box has nothing to photograph but the box, and it is precisely where the evidence pack matters least — there is no prior use to dispute. |

So: stock photos are **permitted, marked, and structurally worthless** on used gear, and **normal** on sealed stock. The seller's incentive is the marker and the facet exclusion, not a rule.

### Happy Path

1. Seller opens a new listing. Single field: **"What are you selling?"** No account setup, no category tree, no wizard chrome.
2. Seller types `1972 Fender Telecaster`. System debounces 300 ms and returns ≤5 ranked candidates at P95 ≤ 400 ms (13.01.04). **Draft is created and persisted on the first keystroke** — before any candidate returns, before any field is confirmed.
3. **Registry short-circuit**: the seller's domain-15 records are matched against the typed text first. A registered unit hits → the flow collapses to a confirmation: *"Listing your 1972 Telecaster? We already know its specs."* Model, serial, mods, prior condition record and existing photos pre-fill; the seller re-affirms rather than re-enters (13.02.02 D-07 — a pre-filled answer is not an answer until touched). This is the consolidation thesis paying out in the most ordinary place in the product (CX-07).
4. Seller binds a model, or contributes one inline (13.01.02), or proceeds unmatched (13.01.04 D-01). None of the three blocks.
5. Seller is prompted for the **serial photo** with the reason stated in one line: *"This is how we date it, check it isn't reported stolen, and prove it's yours."* 13.01.03 decodes; the decode may re-rank and narrow the bind (13.01.04 Happy Path step 6).
6. Seller sets condition grade (13.02.01) and completes the category flaw checklist (13.02.02) — 6–12 items, pre-filled from any platform-held record for this unit, each re-affirmed.
7. Seller adds photos. Each uploads immediately, EXIF-stripped, hashed. The publish CTA is live throughout — photos are not a gate except for material flaw evidence.
8. System prompts for a demo, medium chosen by gear class: *"Play it for 30 seconds. Phone mic is fine — we're checking it works, not making a record."* Seller records in-flow; the clip is stamped with its capture date and lands unprocessed.
9. Seller sets price. If ≥5 comps exist, the 13.04 band renders alongside; if not, 13.04.03 declines to speak and the field renders alone rather than with a fabricated estimate.
10. Seller sets fulfilment: ship / local pickup (13.11) / both. **First-ever publish only**: payout onboarding (CX-M15) is required here — the one hard prerequisite (D-03).
11. Seller publishes. System **re-resolves the model bind** (13.01.04 DT-08 — never trusts draft state), screens the serial (13.03.07), transitions the listing to `active` (13.03.02), and **emits the unit to domain 15** (D-07) — existence, serial, model, condition, media — default private, per domain Q-04's pending ratification.
12. Listing is live. Listing photos, flaw evidence and the demo seal into the evidence pack (13.02.04 D-01) as its first half. If the unit is registered and consent holds at both ends, the provenance panel (13.03.06) lights up on the same page — the registry link having now paid twice (CX-07).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Draft abandoned mid-flow** | Resumable indefinitely; nothing expires, nothing is garbage-collected. A draft is a unit that exists — the seller's memory of the flow is the only perishable thing here | Draft list with a resume CTA and the unit's photo |
| **Draft held open 6 weeks; bound model was merged/split meanwhile** | Binds re-resolve at publish, never from draft state (13.01.04 DT-08). A merged-away provisional re-points silently; an unmerged model drops to pending-choice | Silent for a merge. For a split: "We split this model in two — which is yours?" |
| **Draft held open; price guide moved 20%** | Nothing. Price is the seller's claim, not the platform's. The band renders at its current value at publish; the seller is not chased about it | Current band, no interruption |
| **Connectivity drops mid-upload at a loading dock** | Draft persists locally; every media item queues. Retry 5× with backoff (2 s, 8 s, 30 s, 2 min, 10 min — *seeds*), then holds in the queue **indefinitely and visibly**. Nothing is ever silently dropped. **Publish stays available** unless a material flaw photo is among the unlanded items | Per-item queued indicator: "3 photos waiting for signal — you can publish now" / "Publish is waiting on 1 flaw photo" |
| **Seller has no payout method configured** | Hard block at publish — the one non-negotiable prerequisite (D-03). Draft fully preserved; onboarding is inline, not a redirect that loses the draft | "You're one step from live — we need to know where to pay you." Routes to CX-M15 onboarding, returns to the draft |
| **Seller is in a country where payouts are unsupported** | Same hard block, different resolution — and there isn't one. Do not let them build a listing that can never publish: this is surfaced at draft start, not at publish | "We can't pay out in [country] yet" — stated at step 1, with a watchlist signup, never after 20 minutes of work |
| **Demo clip contains a copyrighted *composition* (seller plays a riff)** | **Not detectable and the platform must not pretend it is** — a live performance is not the recording; acoustic fingerprinting matches masters, not compositions. Clip publishes. Seller affirms *"this is me, playing this unit"* at upload — an attributed claim retained to CX-M19 (same pattern as 13.01.04 D-04). Notice-and-takedown via domain 24 is the recourse. **The platform does not assert a licence it does not hold.** Whether WeJammin acquires a blanket publishing licence — which it is uniquely positioned to do, holding domain 11 — is Q-01 | One-tap affirmation at upload. No warning, no interstitial, no legal wall in a commerce flow |
| **Demo clip contains a commercial *sound recording*** — a backing track, or a record played through the amp under test | **Detectable, and blocked.** Acoustic fingerprint at transcode (CX-M10) matches masters. Clip rejected; **listing publishes without it** (domain D-03 — the clip fails, the flow does not) | "We found a commercial recording in this clip. Play the gear itself and we'll take it." — the reason stated exactly, never "upload failed" |
| **Fingerprint service is down** | **Fail open**, consistent with 13.03.07's publish-time posture. The clip publishes; screening runs async and can retract the clip later without touching the listing | Nothing. A retraction later reads: "We removed a demo clip — it contained a commercial recording." |
| **Demo clip is 12 minutes of noodling** | Rejected at 60 s with an in-flow trim, not a re-record. Making a seller start over is how you get no demo | Trim handle pre-set to the loudest 30 s: "Pick your 60 seconds" |
| **Seller can't play the instrument they're selling** — estate sale, inherited gear, a drummer selling a synth | **A first-class path, not a failure state** (DT-05). The demo prompt carries an explicit *"I can't play this"* option that dismisses it permanently for this listing with zero friction and **routes to consignment (13.09)** — which exists precisely for this person, and who is holding the most valuable and least-documented gear on the platform | "Can't play it? A dealer can list it for you — and get more for it." Not a shrug, a route |
| **Seller lists a unit they've registered in domain 15** | Registry pre-fills model, serial, mods, prior condition records and photos (CX-07). The consolidation payoff, in the most everyday place it occurs | "Listing your 1972 Telecaster? We already know its specs." |
| **Same unit listed twice by the same seller** | Serial collision detected where a serial exists. **Warn, never block** — a seller may legitimately hold two of the same model, and only the serial distinguishes them; where there is no serial, the platform cannot tell | "You have another live listing with this serial — is this the same one?" with a link to it |
| **Same serial listed by two *different* sellers, both live** | At least one is wrong, and 13.03.07 does not catch this — it screens against the *registry*, not against other listings. **Both publish** (13.03.07 D-01: not an accusation; the modal case is a unit that changed hands and a stale listing). Both escalate to domain 24. **Neither seller is told the other exists** — that is a tip-off and retaliation vector | Nothing to either seller. 24 owns the response |
| **Photo carries home GPS in EXIF** | **Stripped at ingest, always, silently** (D-11). Non-negotiable: a public listing for a $40k instrument with the owner's home coordinates attached is a burglary advertisement — the same theft-targeting risk 13.03.06 D-03 gates its provenance panel on, arriving here through a channel nobody looks at | Nothing. The seller must never have to know this was a risk |
| **Photo contains a face, a child, an address label, or a neighbour's window** | **Not auto-detected and not blocked.** A face-detector that pulls down a listing photo because the seller's kid walked past is a flow-staller with a false-positive rate no seller will tolerate. Removal is reactive via domain 24 / CX-M18 | Nothing at upload |
| **Malicious media** — polyglot file, image bomb, 4 GB "photo", zip-in-JPEG | Rejected at ingest: content-sniffed type validation (never extension), decompression-bounded decode, size and dimension caps above. Failure is per-item — one bad file never fails the upload batch | "We couldn't read that file" per item; the other 11 photos land |
| **Malicious text** — script or markup in title/description | Escaped on render, never stored as markup, never trusted from the client. Not this feature's mechanism (`security-first`); recorded so no spec writer assumes the listing form is trusted input | Nothing. Their markup appears as the text they typed |
| **Price of 0 or absurd** | Price must be > 0. **No upper cap** — a $250k Blackguard Tele is a real listing and a platform-invented ceiling would exclude exactly the segment where fees matter most. A price outside the comp band [0.25×, 4×] suppresses auto-bind (13.01.04 gate 4) and **never blocks** | Price > 0 validated inline. Out-of-band price: "That's well under market for this model — is that intentional?" — asked once, dismissible, never enforced |
| **Concurrent edit — two devices on the same draft** | The draft carries a version. A conflicting write returns a conflict; **no silent last-write-wins on a seller's claim**. Same principle as 13.01.04's concurrent-bind case | "You edited this listing on another device — reload to see the current version" |
| **Publish fails after media landed** | Draft and all media preserved. Publish is **idempotent on a client-generated draft key** — a retry after an ambiguous failure must never produce two listings for one unit, which for qty=1 stock is an oversell the seller created by tapping twice | Retry with the explicit reason. Media does not re-upload |
| **Publish succeeds; the unit emission to domain 15 fails** (D-07) | Listing is live; the unit event queues and retries. **Degrades, never blocks** — same principle as 13.01.04 D-03: the failure leaves no illegal state, so no compensating transaction exists or is needed | Nothing. The registry catches up |
| **Publish succeeds; the evidence pack seal fails** | Listing is live; the pack seals async. If it never seals, the pack renders `Listing evidence only` (13.02.04's existing Partial state) rather than being absent | Nothing at publish |
| **Media uploads but transcode fails** (CX-M10) | Photos render; the demo clip does not play. **Partial state, listing stays live** — a broken clip must never take a listing down | "This demo clip couldn't be processed — re-record it?" on the seller's view. Buyers see no clip, not a broken player |
| **Seller swaps the demo after publish** — replaces it with a different unit's clip | Media is **versioned, never overwritten**. The evidence pack seals what was shown (13.02.04 D-03); an order **pins the media version the buyer bought against** (13.02.02's pinning pattern). A later swap cannot mutate what a buyer saw | Seller: "Replacing this clip — buyers who already ordered still see the original." |
| **Demo audibly contains a flaw the seller didn't declare** — the amp hums through the whole clip | **The platform does not detect this and must not try** (DT-04). It doesn't need to: the clip is in the evidence pack, timestamped, and a buyer can cite it at second `0:14` in a 13.08.01 "not as described" claim. The seller has disclosed the hum whether they meant to or not — which is the mechanism working, not failing | Nothing at listing. Everything at dispute |
| **Demo audio is quiet, noisy, clipped, phone-mic'd** | **Left exactly as recorded** (D-05). CX-M10's normalisation/denoise/gate are bypassed for this class. A gate that removes a hum has removed the disclosure | "Phone mic is fine — we're checking it works, not making a record." |
| **Seller lists a unit currently out on rental (13.10), in repair custody (13.08.02), or on consignment (13.09.02)** | They own it; they are not holding it. They cannot photograph it, cannot demo it, cannot ship it today. Listing **publishes**; if the unit's registry record carries an open custody event, the fulfilment step requires an explicit dispatch date **after** custody ends and blocks same-day dispatch claims. The deeper question — who bears the loss if it's destroyed while out — is domain Q-09, unresolved platform-wide | "This unit is out on rental until 12 Aug — buyers will see 'ships after 12 Aug'." |
| **Consignee lists a unit they don't own** (13.09.02) | A CX-M09 delegation: they act *as* the seller without owning. The listing's media obligations are unchanged — the consignee is holding the unit, which is the only thing this flow needs | Normal flow; listing attributes the consignor per 13.09.02 |
| **Category has no flaw checklist authored yet** (new category) | 13.02.02 presupposes an authored checklist. Publish with `Condition not itemised` — never invent a generic checklist at runtime, which is exactly the meaningless "Light/Moderate/Severe" scale 13.02.02 DT-11 rejected | "We don't have a condition checklist for this category yet — describe it in your own words." |
| **Bulk import row** (13.03.04) | Per D-06: obligations do **not** relax; the **capture moment moves to label print**. The row publishes with `Unit not photographed` + `Condition not itemised`, no evidence pack, and no demo. At label print — seller holding the unit, phone out, already prompted for dispatch photos (13.02.04 D-02) — media and disclosure complete. The domain-level question of whether the bar bends at all remains the owner's (domain Q-13) | Import: "400 live · 400 need photos — we'll ask when you ship." Label print: "Two photos and this listing's protected." |
| **Fan hits the listing surface via deep link** | **Not a 403.** Routes to CX-M15 seller activation (D-09). A Fan who wants to sell a guitar is behaving as a Musician; refusing them is modelling personas as account types, which D-19 rejects | "Want to sell gear? Here's what we need." — the activation flow, not a wall |
| **A minor attempts to list** | KYC at payout onboarding (CX-M01/CX-M15) fails them, so the payout gate **age-gates by accident**. Recorded because accidental gates break silently and nobody owns them: if payouts ever relax, this gate vanishes and nobody will notice. CX-M31 does not currently serve this domain — domain Q-12 | Onboarding rejection, from a component that does not know it is doing age assurance |
| **Multi-hyphenate, both roles at once** — the Producer who recorded the session that gives this unit its provenance is the Musician now selling it | **There is no adversarial party.** The provenance panel (13.03.06) requires consent at both the session and owner ends (D-03 there) — but when both ends are the same human, consent is self-granted and the panel's implication of independent corroboration is false. Same silent failure as 13.01.04's registry-contradiction case | No UI difference. **Recorded distinctly** to CX-M19 so a dispute can tell self-agreement from corroboration |
| **Seller with rich provenance and a stolen-registry hit** (CX-06's Step 5 question) | **The hold wins the page, unambiguously.** 13.03.07's hold removes the listing from `active` (CX-04) — there is no page for a provenance panel to win. Provenance rendering next to an allegedly-stolen unit is the platform advertising the resale value of stolen property, which is the highest-stakes case in the domain | Seller: 13.03.07's hold flow. Buyers: nothing — the listing is not live |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Empty (new draft)** | Seller opens the flow | One field: "What are you selling?" No category tree, no wizard, no step counter. The draft persists on the first keystroke |
| **Empty (no drafts, no listings)** | First-ever seller | "Sell your first piece of gear" with the payout prerequisite stated **up front** (D-03) — never discovered at minute 20 |
| **Empty (registry-backed)** | Seller has registered units and no drafts | Their registered units as one-tap listing starts: "List your 1972 Telecaster" — the pre-fill loop's front door (CX-07) |
| **Loading (matching)** | 300 ms after last keystroke in the title | Inline candidate spinner. **Never blocks typing.** Stops at 2,000 ms and falls back to manual model search (13.01.04) |
| **Loading (media)** | Photo/clip selected | Per-item thumbnail with progress. **The form stays fully usable and the publish CTA stays live** — media never blocks the flow except for material flaw evidence |
| **Loading (transcode)** | Demo clip uploaded, processing | Clip appears with a processing chip. Publish is not held on it |
| **Populated (draft complete)** | All composed steps answered | A preview of the **published listing as a buyer will see it** — not a form summary. The seller's last check is against the artifact, not the input |
| **Populated (live)** | Publish succeeded | Live listing + its markers: `Unit photographed` / `Condition itemised` / `Demo included` / `Serial verified` — or their absences, each stating its concrete cost |
| **Partial (queued media)** | Connectivity lost; items queued | "3 photos waiting for signal — you can publish now." Per-item state, indefinite retention, nothing dropped |
| **Partial (publish blocked)** | A material flaw photo is missing (13.02.02 D-03) | **The named blocker, never a generic "form invalid"**: "Add a photo of the headstock repair to publish." One blocker, named, with the fix |
| **Partial (publish blocked, payout)** | No payout method, first publish | "You're one step from live — we need to know where to pay you." Inline onboarding, draft preserved |
| **Partial (unmatched)** | Published with no model bind | "Not linked to a catalog model — no filtered search, no price data, no safety info" (13.01.04's language, unchanged) |
| **Partial (stock imagery)** | Photos matched catalog/other-listing hashes | `Stock imagery` marker + "Buyers can't see the actual unit, and this listing has no dispute protection" — the consequence stated, the choice left |
| **Partial (no demo)** | Published, demo declined | No marker, no shaming, no absence indicator. **A missing demo is not a signal** — the estate-sale seller (DT-05) and every sealed unit would be punished for nothing |
| **Partial (custody)** | Unit is out on rental / in repair | "Ships after 12 Aug" on the listing; same-day dispatch unavailable |
| **Error (publish failed)** | Publish transaction failed | Draft and media fully preserved; explicit reason; idempotent retry. Never "something went wrong" |
| **Error (conflict)** | Concurrent edit from another session | "You edited this listing on another device — reload to see the current version" |
| **Error (clip rejected)** | Fingerprint matched a commercial recording | "We found a commercial recording in this clip. Play the gear itself and we'll take it." Listing publishes regardless |
| **Error (transcode failed)** | Clip processing failed | Seller: "This demo clip couldn't be processed — re-record it?" Buyer: no clip, not a broken player. Listing stays live |

## Deep Think Annotations
