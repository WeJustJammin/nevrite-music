# Feature: Parts, Consumables, Bundles & B-Stock

> **Number**: 13.03.05
> **Parent**: [Listings & Inventory](./13.03-listings-inventory-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The stock that doesn't fit the one-unit story — strings, valves, patch cables and other counted consumables; multi-item bundles ("pedalboard, all five pedals, sold together"); and B-stock/open-box units with factory-acknowledged cosmetic defects and reduced warranty. Each breaks a different assumption the rest of the sub-domain makes, which is why they need naming rather than silently distorting the qty=1 model.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Sells a bundled pedalboard rather than eight listings; buys strings and valves as counted stock. Bundling is how the persona actually clears gear — as a rig, not as parts. |
| Producer | Full | Sells racks and matched sets (a matched pair of mics is a bundle where splitting destroys the value — the pair *is* the product). Buys consumables at volume. |
| Operator | Full | Sells B-stock and ex-demo; stocks consumables for a venue/studio; the persona most likely to hold genuine counted inventory. |
| Fan | Read-only (as buyer) | Buys strings and accessories — plausibly the persona's most common gear purchase. No selling. Needs B-stock's meaning explained plainly ("cosmetic defect, full function, shorter warranty"), which the persona will not infer. |

## Behavior

Three distinct shapes sharing a listing surface:
- **Counted stock** (consumables, parts): qty>1, fungible, restockable — the second inventory regime named in 13.03.03 DT-01/D-03.
- **Bundles**: one listing, several physical objects, sold together; the constituent units may also exist individually in the registry (domain 15) and possibly as their own listings.
- **B-stock/open-box**: qty=1-ish, factory-graded defect, distinct warranty terms — condition grading (13.02.01) applies but the *reason* differs, and warranty (13.08.03) diverges from both new and used.

### Happy Path

1. Seller chooses the listing shape (single unit / bundle / counted stock)
2. Form adapts: counted stock asks quantity and skips per-unit disclosure; bundles ask for constituent items
3. Bundle constituents may bind to catalog models individually
4. Listing publishes under the right inventory regime
5. Buyer sees a shape-appropriate listing — stock count, or a bundle contents list

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Bundle constituent also listed individually | One object, two listings — both must reserve together | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — shared with 13.03.03 Q-01 |
| Buyer wants one pedal out of a bundle | Splitting is a negotiation (13.05.01), not a system operation | "Ask the seller" — no automatic split |
| Counted stock runs out | Restockable, unlike a unit — listing persists at qty=0 | "Out of stock" ≠ "sold" — a distinction the qty=1 lifecycle doesn't have |
| B-stock warranty differs from new and used | Warranty (13.08.03) must carry three regimes, not two | B-stock warranty terms stated on the listing |
| Bundle condition grading | Does the grade apply to the worst item, the average, or per-item? | `[PENDING]` — per-item is honest but heavy; see Q-01 |
| Counted stock disclosure | A pack of strings has no flaw checklist | Disclosure suppressed for counted stock — correctly |
| Bundle sold; constituents' registry ownership (domain 15) | One order → several ownership transfers | Feeds 13.06.05 — a real fan-out |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | These three (parts, bundles, B-stock) are one feature because they're all "not a single used unit" | The sweep bundled them as candidate #23, and they share a negative definition | ⚠️ CONFIRMED WITH TENSION — kept as one node **for this breadth pass** because each is individually small and they share the listing surface. But they are united only by what they are *not*, which is a weak basis. Counted stock breaks the inventory regime; bundles break the object↔listing 1:1 assumption; B-stock breaks the warranty regime. Three different assumptions, three different blast radii. **Flagged for promotion review at Step 5** — if MoSCoW keeps all three, this is probably a sub-domain. |
| DT-02 | Bundles are a presentation concern — several items shown as one | The naive reading; it looks like a UI grouping | ❌ REJECTED: a bundle is an **atomicity claim**. Sold together means reserved together (13.03.03 Q-01), shipped together or not at all (13.07 — a bundle can contain one item that can't ship to the buyer's country under CITES), transferred together in the registry (13.06.05 → domain 15), and returned together or partially (13.08.01). Every one of those is a real question the "presentation" framing hides. Bundles are the most structurally invasive of the three. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Establishes the second regime for **13.03.03 Inventory & Oversell Prevention** ([path](./13.03.03-inventory-oversell-prevention.md)) — counted stock, per that file's D-03.
- Complicates **13.06.05 Ownership Transfer on Settlement** ([path](../13.06-cart-checkout-orders/13.06.05-ownership-transfer-on-settlement.md)) — a bundle is a one-to-many transfer.
- Complicates **13.07.03 CITES & Export Compliance** ([path](../13.07-gear-logistics-cross-border/13.07.03-cites-export-compliance.md)) — a bundle is only as shippable as its most restricted item.
- Diverges from **13.08.03 Warranty** ([path](../13.08-returns-rma-warranty/13.08.03-warranty-registration-transfer-claims.md)) — B-stock is a third warranty regime.
- Suppresses **13.02.02 Flaw Disclosure** ([path](../13.02-condition-originality-disclosure/13.02.02-mandatory-flaw-disclosure.md)) for counted stock — no per-unit checklist on a pack of strings.
- Touches **Domain 14 Digital Goods** ([path](../../14-digital-goods-marketplace/)) — hardware bundled with a software licence (an interface shipping with a DAW) is a bundle spanning both domains. D-14 kept them separate; this is where that boundary is tested.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Counted stock and qty=1 units are distinct regimes on a shared surface | Per 13.03.03 D-03 — one form, two behaviours, never a merged model. |
| D-02 | Bundles are atomic, not presentational | Per DT-02 — reserve, ship, transfer and return implications all follow. |
| D-03 | Bundle splitting is a negotiation, not a system operation | Auto-split would break the atomicity claim and the seller's pricing intent. |
