# Feature: Bulk Listing & Multi-Channel Inventory Sync

> **Number**: 13.03.04
> **Parent**: [Listings & Inventory](./13.03-listings-inventory-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Listing at volume, and keeping this marketplace's view of a seller's stock consistent with the other places that stock is also for sale. It is primarily an **adoption lever, not a convenience**: a dealer with 400 units already listed on Reverb and eBay will not hand-key them here, and will not risk selling the same amp twice. If this doesn't exist, the supply side of the marketplace is limited to people selling one pedal.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | Rarely bulk-lists — a handful of units at most. May sync a small existing Reverb presence when migrating. Sees this as an import-once tool. |
| Producer | Config | Similar; occasionally clears a studio of outboard in one pass, which is a batch of 20–40 with heterogeneous, hard-to-template disclosure. |
| Operator | Full | **The persona this feature exists for.** Runs fleet/shop stock across channels; needs continuous sync, not a one-off import, and needs it to work from a back office at volume. Their oversell surface is cross-channel (13.03.03 DT-02). |
| Fan | None | No listing surface at all, let alone bulk. |

## Behavior

Two distinct capabilities under one node: **bulk listing** (many units in, via CSV/API — the generic half, consuming CX-M29) and **inventory sync** (continuous, bidirectional-ish reconciliation of availability across channels — the gear-specific half, domain-owned per sub-domain D-03). Sync is **best-effort by nature** (13.03.03 DT-02) and must be honest about that rather than promising consistency it cannot deliver.

### Happy Path

1. Operator connects an existing channel or uploads an inventory export
2. System runs matching at volume (13.01.04) and reports what bound cleanly vs. what didn't
3. Operator reviews the unmatched/ambiguous set in one screen rather than 400 prompts
4. Listings publish; sync begins
5. A unit sells anywhere; the other channels are updated as fast as their APIs allow

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| 400 imported rows, 120 unmatched to catalog models | Cannot prompt per-row. Publish unmatched (13.01.04 D-01) + a review queue | "280 linked, 120 need a model — review now or later" |
| Per-item disclosure (13.02.02) at bulk volume | Unresolved and potentially fatal to adoption — a mandatory per-unit flaw checklist × 400 is unusable | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — shared with 13.02.02 Q-01. See DT-02. |
| Unit sells on another channel | Sync propagates when it can; a window always exists | Cancellation for this cause must be cheap (13.03.03 D-04) |
| Sold here, sync to other channel fails | The seller is now oversold *elsewhere*, caused by us | Sync failure surfaced loudly to the seller — silent failure is worse than no sync |
| Channel API rate-limits or breaks | Degrade to periodic reconciliation; never silently stop | Sync health indicator with last-successful timestamp |
| Two channels disagree about availability | No global authority exists. This marketplace's own state wins *here* | Conflict surfaced to seller, not auto-resolved |
| Bulk price update across 400 listings | Must respect the offer-pending edit restriction (13.03.02 D-03) per listing | "12 listings skipped — offers pending" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Import running | Progress with per-row outcomes, resumable |
| Empty | No channels connected | "Bring your existing inventory" onboarding |
| Populated | Sync active | Sync health, last reconcile time, conflict count |
| Error | Import/sync failed | Per-row error report; partial success preserved — never all-or-nothing |
| Partial | Some rows matched, some queued | Split view: live vs. needs-attention |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is all generic bulk-import tooling and belongs entirely in CX-M29 | CX-M29 (Bulk Import, Sync & Migration Tooling) already exists and serves gear-marketplace, so the whole candidate looks like a cross-cut | ⚠️ PARTIALLY CONFIRMED — split accordingly (sub-domain D-03). **Bulk import** genuinely is CX-M29: CSV parsing, column mapping, error reporting, resumability are identical for gear, digital goods, venues and credits. **Cross-channel availability sync is not**: it exists only because a qty=1 physical unit can be sold in a shop while listed online — digital goods have infinite stock, services aren't inventory, venues use CX-M05 reservation. The gear-specific half stays; the generic half is consumed. |
| DT-02 | Bulk listing is a solved shape — it's a CSV upload | It looks like standard marketplace onboarding | ❌ REJECTED: this domain has made per-unit obligations mandatory that no CSV carries. A gear listing requires a bound catalog model (13.01.04), a condition grade (13.02.01), a completed category flaw checklist (13.02.02), and unit-specific media (13.03.01 D-01). **A CSV row has none of these.** Either bulk listings are second-class (unmatched, undisclosed, stock-photo'd — degrading the exact quality bar this domain is built on), or bulk is far harder than "upload a CSV". **This is an unresolved conflict between D-01-quality listings and Operator adoption, and it is the most consequential open question in the sub-domain.** |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Bulk import consumes CX-M29; cross-channel availability sync stays domain-owned | Per DT-01. |
| D-02 | Sync is honest about being best-effort; sync failures surface loudly | A silent sync failure causes an oversell the seller didn't know was coming. |
| D-03 | Imports are partial-success, never all-or-nothing | 400 rows failing because of row 212 is unusable at Operator scale. |
