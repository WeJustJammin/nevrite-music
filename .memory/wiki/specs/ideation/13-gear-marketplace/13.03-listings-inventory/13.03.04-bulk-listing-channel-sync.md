# Feature: Bulk Listing & Multi-Channel Inventory Sync

> **Number**: 13.03.04
> **Parent**: [Listings & Inventory](./13.03-listings-inventory-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-19

## Overview

Listing at volume, and keeping this marketplace's view of a seller's stock consistent with the
other places that stock is also for sale. It is primarily an **adoption lever, not a convenience**:
a dealer with 400 units already listed on Reverb and eBay will not hand-key them here, and will not
risk selling the same amp twice across channels. Without it, the supply side of the marketplace is
capped at people selling one pedal — and the Operator persona (the reason this feature exists)
never arrives.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | Rarely bulk-lists — a handful of units at most. May run a one-time import to migrate a small existing Reverb presence. Treats this as an import-once tool, not an ongoing sync; the sync-health surface is mostly irrelevant to them. |
| Producer | Config | Similar, but occasionally clears a studio of outboard in one pass — a batch of 20–40 with heterogeneous, hard-to-template disclosure (each unit's condition/mods differ). Imports honestly incomplete rows, then completes each unit's disclosure and media at label print when holding it; templated flaw answers are prohibited (13.02.02 D-08). |
| Operator | Full | **The persona this feature exists for.** Runs fleet/shop stock across channels; needs continuous bidirectional-ish sync, not a one-off import, and needs it to work from a back office at volume. Their oversell surface is cross-channel (13.03.03 DT-02). Owns channel connections, reviews the match queue, sets bulk price/availability rules. |
| Fan | None | No listing surface at all, let alone bulk. A Fan is a buyer only; nothing in this feature is visible to them. Reason: bulk listing and inventory sync are seller-back-office capabilities with no buyer-facing counterpart. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

Two distinct capabilities under one node:

- **Bulk listing** — many units in, via CSV upload or channel API pull. The **generic half**,
  consuming CX-M29: file parsing, column mapping, error reporting, resumability. Identical in
  shape to bulk-importing digital goods, venue slots, or credits.
- **Inventory sync** — continuous, bidirectional-ish reconciliation of *availability* across
  channels. The **gear-specific half**, domain-owned per sub-domain D-03. It exists only because
  a qty=1 physical unit can be sold in a walk-in shop while still listed online, so "is this unit
  still available?" has no single authority.

Sync is **best-effort by nature** (13.03.03 DT-02): every external channel has its own API latency,
rate limits, and downtime, so a propagation window always exists. The feature's core design
principle is **honesty over false consistency** — it must surface the window and its failures
loudly rather than imply real-time truth it cannot deliver (D-02).

### Happy Path

1. Operator connects an existing channel (OAuth to Reverb/eBay via CX-M22) or uploads an inventory export (CSV)
2. **Interim matching slice:** system runs listing↔model matching at volume (13.01.04), retaining that feature's auto-bind gates and recording which rows bound versus which require review
3. Operator reviews unmatched/ambiguous rows in a queue grouped by proposed candidate and normalised title — K model decisions, not 400 sequential prompts; prior human-confirmed `(seller, SKU) → model` bindings auto-bind repeat SKUs unless the title diverges
4. **Interim grading slice:** imported rows use the Operator's seller-set per-upload grade, visibly flagged `bulk_defaulted`; resolved and unresolved model rows both publish, with unresolved rows marked as unlinked
5. **Interim disclosure/media slice:** imported rows carry `Condition not itemised` and `Unit not photographed`; no flaw answer is templated. At label print, when the seller holds the unit, disclosure and media complete and earn the corresponding trust/evidence protection
6. The interim slices above are source-locked mechanics, not a final quality-bar decision: parent **domain Q-13 is User-owned** and may ratify, revise, or replace the cross-slice bulk policy.
7. A unit sells anywhere; the other channels are updated as fast as their APIs allow, and a sync-health surface shows last-successful reconcile time per channel

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| 400 imported rows, 120 unmatched to catalog models | Preserve matching gates. Publish unmatched rows (13.01.04 D-01) and queue them by proposed candidate and normalised title; one group decision resolves all rows in it. Prior human-confirmed SKU bindings auto-bind repeat stock unless title divergence invalidates them (13.01.04 D-05/D-08). | "280 linked · 120 need a model — 12 decisions" |
| Bulk grade for 400 imported rows | Grade remains required. Apply the Operator's seller-set per-upload default to each row, store `bulk_defaulted: true`, disclose it on each listing, and reduce its comp weight (13.02.01 D-11). | "Graded in bulk" |
| Per-item disclosure and media at bulk volume | Import publishes every row with `Condition not itemised` and `Unit not photographed`; templated flaw answers are prohibited. The seller completes disclosure and media at label print, while physically holding the unit. The listing remains live but unprotected until then (13.02.02 D-08; 13.03.01 D-06). | "400 live · 400 need photos — we'll ask when you ship." At label print: "Two photos and this listing's protected." |
| Unit sells on another channel, sync propagates late | Sync updates this marketplace when the channel reports it; a window always exists. Cancellation for this cause must be cheap (13.03.03 D-04) | Buyer here may hit a just-sold unit; refund/cancel is one tap, framed as a cross-channel timing artifact, not seller fault |
| Sold **here**, sync-out to another channel fails | The seller is now oversold *elsewhere*, caused by us — the worst failure mode. Never fail silently | Sync failure surfaced loudly per affected channel + unit, with a manual "mark sold on X" fallback |
| Channel API rate-limits or goes down | Degrade to periodic reconciliation with backoff; never silently stop syncing | Sync-health indicator per channel: green / degraded / stalled + last-successful timestamp |
| Two channels disagree about a unit's availability | No global authority exists (13.03.03 DT-02). This marketplace's own state is authoritative *here*; the conflict is not auto-resolved | Conflict flagged to seller in the review queue for a human decision |
| Bulk price update across 400 listings, some with offers pending | Must respect the offer-pending edit restriction (13.03.02 D-03) per listing — skip locked rows, apply the rest | "388 updated · 12 skipped — offers pending" |
| Non-owner team member triggers a bulk action on org-owned stock | Permission-gated to the Operator's org role; a member without listing-write cannot import, sync, or bulk-edit | "You don't have permission to manage this shop's inventory" |
| Partial CSV — row 212 malformed among 400 | Import is partial-success, never all-or-nothing (D-03). Valid rows publish; the bad row is reported | Per-row error report; 399 published, row 212 flagged with the parse error |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Import or channel pull running | Progress with per-row outcomes, resumable across a page reload |
| Empty | No channels connected and nothing imported | "Bring your existing inventory" onboarding with connect/upload CTAs |

| Error | Import or sync failed | Per-row / per-channel error report; partial success preserved — never all-or-nothing |
| Partial | Some rows matched-and-live, some queued | Split view: live listings vs. needs-attention queue |

The gear-specific half stays; the generic half is consumed. |
| DT-02 | Bulk listing is a solved shape — it's just a CSV upload | It looks like standard marketplace onboarding | ❌ REJECTED: a CSV cannot carry a bound catalog model, grade, completed category checklist, or unit-specific media. | DT-03 | One-way sync (we only read external availability) would be simpler and safe enough | Two-way write to competitor channels is fragile and commercially fraught (Q-02, Q-03) | ⚠️ PARTIALLY CONFIRMED — read-only sync eliminates the "sold here, sync-out failed" oversell-elsewhere failure and needs no write scopes on foreign APIs, so it is the safer default. The read/write split is a product+legal decision, not a behavior one — deferred to `/create-prd` (Q-03). Behavior here is written to be honest under *either* choice: the sync-health surface and loud-failure principle hold regardless of direction. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Consumes **CX-M29 Bulk Import, Sync & Migration Tooling** — the generic import half per DT-01 (CSV parse, column map, resumable jobs, per-row error report).
- Consumes **CX-M22 Integrations, Public API & Webhooks** — channel OAuth connections and the sync transport (webhooks in, API writes out).
- Consumes **CX-M27 Admin, Back-Office & Support** — the seller's back-office surface where the review queue and sync-health dashboard live.
- Drives **13.01.04 Listing↔Model Matching** ([path](../13.01-canonical-gear-catalog/13.01.04-listing-model-matching.md)) at volume: its interim mechanics group review queues from N rows to K model decisions and memoise prior human-confirmed `(seller, SKU) → model` binds (D-05/D-08).
- Constrained by **13.02.01 Condition Grading** and **13.02.02 Mandatory Flaw Disclosure** ([paths](../13.02-condition-originality-disclosure/)) — their interim mechanics permit a disclosed `bulk_defaulted` grade and publish `Condition not itemised` without templated flaw claims, then re-prompt disclosure at label print (13.02.01 D-11; 13.02.02 D-08). These slices do not resolve the parent quality-bar choice in domain Q-13.
- Feeds **13.03.03 Inventory & Oversell Prevention** ([path](./13.03.03-inventory-oversell-prevention.md)) — sync is the cross-channel oversell mitigation, and per 13.03.03 DT-02, an inherently incomplete one.
- Depends on **Domain 01 Identity & Organizations** — org roles/permissions gate who in a shop may import, sync, or bulk-edit (see the non-owner edge case).
- Touches **Payments / Cancellation cross-cut** — a late-sync oversell must resolve to a cheap cancellation/refund path (13.03.03 D-04).

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Bulk import consumes CX-M29; cross-channel availability sync stays domain-owned | Per DT-01 — only the qty=1 physical-unit sync is gear-specific. |
| D-02 | Sync is honest about being best-effort; sync failures surface loudly per channel and unit | A silent sync failure causes an oversell the seller didn't know was coming — worse than no sync. |
| D-03 | Imports are partial-success, never all-or-nothing | 400 rows failing because of row 212 is unusable at Operator scale. |
| D-04 | Bulk actions are permission-gated to org listing-write role | Shop stock is org-owned; a member without listing rights must not import, sync, or bulk-edit. |
| D-05 | **Interim bulk mechanics are source-locked; the quality-bar decision is not.** | Matching grouped queues/SKU memoisation, `bulk_defaulted` grading, `Condition not itemised`/`Unit not photographed` markers, and label-print disclosure/media completion are each supplied by their owning sources (13.01.04 D-05/D-08; 13.02.01 D-11; 13.02.02 D-08; 13.03.01 D-06). They are interim behavior only and do **not** mandate no relaxation. The parent quality-bar choice remains **domain Q-13, User-owned**. |
