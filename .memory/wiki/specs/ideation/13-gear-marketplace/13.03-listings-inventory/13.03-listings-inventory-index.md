## Overview

Everything about putting a specific unit up for sale and keeping the fact that it exists accurate — creation, media, lifecycle, the qty=1 oversell problem, bulk and multi-channel operation, and the non-instrument stock (parts, bundles, B-stock) that doesn't fit the one-unit model. It also carries the two listing enrichments that only exist because WeJammin is more than a marketplace: what this instrument played on, and whether it's stolen.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: creation, lifecycle, inventory integrity, bulk sync, and the provenance/screening enrichments are distinct capabilities with heavy mutual interaction — a bulk sync writes inventory, inventory state drives lifecycle transitions, and lifecycle transitions gate screening.
