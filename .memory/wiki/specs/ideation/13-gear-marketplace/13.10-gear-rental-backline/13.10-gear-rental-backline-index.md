## Overview

Renting gear rather than buying it: a backline for a show, an amp for a session, a fleet of drum kits for a festival. The object goes out, comes back, and goes out again — which means the inventory is time-boxed rather than sold, the money is a deposit plus a rate, and the interesting question is not "did it sell" but "what condition did it come back in".

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: rental listings and rate cards, time-based availability, and deposits/damage assessment on return are three capabilities that interact — the rate card prices the calendar, and the return condition determines what happens to the deposit.

> **⚠️ Promotion candidate.** Rental's order model is fundamentally different from sale's: reserved over a date range, returned, re-let, with the *same unit* generating revenue indefinitely. That is the same irreconcilability D-14 used to keep 13, 14 and 05 apart — and it may mean rental doesn't belong inside a sales marketplace at all. It also overlaps 16 (Venues/Studios sell time and space) and 18 (Show Production needs backline). See Open Questions.
