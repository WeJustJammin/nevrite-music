# Feature: Local Pickup & Meetup Safety

> **Number**: 13.11
> **Parent**: [Gear Marketplace (Physical Goods)](./gear-marketplace-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Buying gear from someone in your city and collecting it in person. It is the right answer for a lot of this category — a piano shouldn't ship, a £60 pedal shouldn't cost £40 to post, and a buyer paying £4,000 for a vintage guitar wants to play it first. It also puts two strangers in a room with a valuable object and cash, which is a safety problem and a revenue problem at once.

**Split from sweep candidate #20** ("Local Pickup, Geo Commerce & Meetup Safety"): map/geo discovery is CX-M13, which already serves gear-marketplace. What remains — pickup as a fulfilment mode and the safety of the exchange — is domain content.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (both sides) | Sells a pedal to someone across town; buys an amp they want to hear first. The persona's most common transaction shape for cheap gear, and the one where shipping costs more than the item. |
| Producer | Full (both sides) | Collects heavy outboard rather than trusting it to a courier — for a rack unit, the safest transit is their own car. |
| Operator | Full (both sides) | Trade counter collection; also the persona most likely to have premises where a meetup is inherently safe. |
| Fan | Full (as buyer) | Buys a first guitar locally. **Most vulnerable in a meetup**: no experience, carrying cash, meeting a stranger, and unable to assess the gear or the situation. The safety guidance exists primarily for this persona. |

## Behavior

Pickup is a **fulfilment mode** chosen at listing (13.03.01) and honoured at checkout. It bypasses CX-M14 entirely — no label, no tracking, no carrier. Safety guidance surfaces at arrangement time, and the payment question (does money move through the platform, or does cash change hands?) determines almost everything else about this feature.

### Happy Path

1. Seller lists with local pickup enabled
2. Local buyer purchases or agrees a price
3. Both arrange a time and place; safety guidance surfaces
4. Buyer inspects in person and takes it
5. Both confirm; the transaction closes

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Cash changes hands off-platform** | No fee, no escrow, no protection, no ownership transfer (13.06.05) — the platform introduced them and got nothing | The revenue hole and the provenance hole, at once. See DT-01. |
| Buyer inspects and walks away | Entirely legitimate — inspecting is the point | No penalty; the listing returns to `active` |
| **Escrow with no tracking number** | Escrow releases on delivery proof; there is no proof | See DT-01 — the mechanism that makes remote sales safe simply doesn't fit |
| Meetup goes wrong (robbery, assault) | Two strangers, a valuable object, likely cash | Guidance: public places, daylight, police station safe-exchange zones. Real harm, real duty of care |
| Buyer's home address exposed | Pickup requires a place; a home is the default | Never surface home addresses; suggest neutral locations |
| Seller is a Fan meeting a stranger | The persona doc's Fan anti-persona includes being a minor | Safeguarding — CX-M31 does **not** currently list gear-marketplace. See Q-03. |
| Item too big for the buyer's car | A Leslie needs two people and a van | Stated in the listing |
| Stolen gear sold at a meetup | No screening at the point of exchange unless it went through the platform | The public serial lookup (13.03.07 D-04) is the answer, and it works for anyone |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Resolving nearby pickup listings / opening arrangement | Skeleton on the map+list, spinner on the arrange-pickup panel |
| Empty | No pickup-enabled listings within CX-M13 radius | "Nothing near you" with a widen-radius CTA — via CX-M13 |
| Populated | Pickup available on a listing | "Collect from [area]" — an area, never an address (D-01) |
| Error | Geo lookup fails, or one party cancels the arrangement | "Couldn't load listings near you — retry" / "Pickup cancelled — the listing is active again" |
| Partial | Arranged, not yet collected | "Pickup arranged for Saturday" plus the surfaced safety guidance (D-02) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Local pickup is a shipping option — the same transaction, collected instead of posted | The obvious framing, and how the sweep listed it (#20) | ❌ REJECTED: it dissolves **three** load-bearing mechanisms simultaneously. (1) **Escrow** (CX-M01) releases against delivery proof; a meetup produces none. (2) **The evidence pack** (13.02.04) is built for a dispatch/arrival baseline that doesn't exist — though it matters less, since the buyer inspected in person. (3) **Ownership transfer** (13.06.05) fires on settlement; a cash meetup never settles, so the domain's entire provenance contribution is **silently skipped on what may be the most common transaction shape in the category**. That last point is the serious one: the incumbent's local product takes no fee precisely because it can't hold the money, and copying that copies a hole straight through the platform's thesis. **Local pickup is not a shipping option; it is a different transaction with three of the domain's mechanisms missing.** |
| DT-02 | Meetup safety is a content problem — publish guidance and link a safe-exchange map | It's what every classifieds platform does, and it discharges the obligation cheaply | ⚠️ REJECTED as sufficient. Guidance is necessary and it is not the mechanism. The platform **created this meeting** — it matched two strangers and told one to bring cash to the other. That is a duty of care, and it lands hardest on the **Fan**, who per the persona doc has "no professional stake", may be a minor, and is the least equipped to assess either the gear or the situation. Real mechanisms exist and none are in the candidate list: identity verification before an address is shared, never surfacing home addresses (only areas), suggesting neutral or police-designated exchange locations, and an in-app confirmation that both parties arrived and left. Notably CX-M31 (Safeguarding & Minor Protection) serves education, community and ticketing — **but not gear-marketplace**, despite this feature putting a possible minor in a room with a stranger and cash. That looks like a genuine gap in the ratified cross-cut list. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Consumes **CX-M13 Geo, Location & Map Discovery** — the geo half of sweep candidate #20; already serves this domain.
- **Bypasses CX-M14 Shipping, Fulfilment & Logistics** entirely — no carrier, no label, no tracking.
- Breaks **13.06.05 Ownership Transfer** ([path](./13.06-cart-checkout-orders/13.06.05-ownership-transfer-on-settlement.md)) — per DT-01, and that file's Q-03 asks this from the other side.
- Strains **CX-M01 Payments, Escrow & Payouts** — escrow without delivery proof.
- Reduces **13.02.04 Condition Evidence Pack** ([path](./13.02-condition-originality-disclosure/13.02.04-condition-evidence-pack.md)) to listing evidence only.
- Often the right answer for **13.07.01 Freight** ([path](./13.07-gear-logistics-cross-border/13.07.01-freight-oversize-packing.md)) — a piano shouldn't ship; that file routes here.
- Should consume **CX-M31 Safeguarding & Minor Protection** — which does **not** currently list gear-marketplace. See Q-03.
- Escalates to **Domain 24 Trust, Safety & Disputes** ([path](../24-trust-safety-disputes/)) — meetup incidents, scam patterns.
- Consumes **CX-M18 Privacy, Consent & Data Portability** — address exposure.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Home addresses are never surfaced; listings show an area | The buyer needs to know it's collectable, not where the seller sleeps. |
| D-02 | Safety guidance surfaces at arrangement time, not in a help page | Per DT-02 — guidance nobody reads is guidance nobody has. |
| D-03 | Local pickup is a distinct transaction shape, not a shipping option | Per DT-01 — three mechanisms don't apply, and pretending otherwise hides the holes. |
