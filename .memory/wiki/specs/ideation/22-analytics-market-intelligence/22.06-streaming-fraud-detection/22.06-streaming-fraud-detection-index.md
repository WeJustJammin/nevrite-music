## Overview

Spotting artificial streaming and fake engagement in the ingested data — and telling the artist
**before their distributor does**. The stakes are not academic: DSPs now levy per-track fines on
distributors for artificial streams, distributors pass them on, and the end state is a takedown of
the artist's catalog. Most artists affected did not buy the fraud themselves; they bought a
"playlist promotion" package and had no way to know what they were buying.

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: anomaly detection with an evidence dossier,
and promo-vendor risk scoring, are distinct capabilities that interact bidirectionally — a dossier
names the vendor whose campaign coincided with the anomaly, and accumulated dossiers are what
constitute a vendor's risk score, which then annotates the next artist's campaign before they buy.
The loop is the design.

**Why this is domain 22's and not domain 24's**: domain 24 (Trust, Safety & Disputes) owns *platform*
trust — scams, moderation, disputes between WeJammin users. This fraud happens on **external DSPs**,
is detected from **externally ingested data**, and lands on the artist's **distributor relationship**,
not on WeJammin's marketplace. The exception is real and routed: when the promo vendor is selling on
WeJammin (domain 21), enforcement is 24's, and 22 supplies the evidence. Boundary recorded as D-02.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The product is the **warning**, not the detection | Detection is a means. The artist's actionable moment is *before* a distributor strike, when they can still cancel a campaign, disavow to the distributor, or preserve evidence. A detector that reports after a takedown has produced a post-mortem, not a product. | Agent, Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | This is 22's, not 24's — **except** when the vendor sells on WeJammin | External fraud on external platforms detected from external data is analytics. A WeJammin-hosted vendor selling artificial streams is a platform-integrity matter and enforcement belongs to 24; 22 supplies evidence. | Node Classification Gate + boundary reasoning, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | WeJammin **detects and warns**; it never accuses, and never reports to a DSP | We cannot verify fraud, only observe anomalies. An accusation we cannot prove is a defamation risk and a false-positive machine aimed at our own users. The artist decides what to do with their own evidence. | Agent, Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
