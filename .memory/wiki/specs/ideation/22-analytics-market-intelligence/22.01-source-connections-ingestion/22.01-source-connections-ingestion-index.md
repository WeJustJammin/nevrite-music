## Overview

Everything that gets external numbers *into* WeJammin: the user-facing act of connecting a
Spotify for Artists / Apple Music for Artists / TikTok / Instagram account, the lifecycle of that
connection as tokens expire and platforms revoke, and the honest surfacing of what is missing when
a source goes dark. This sub-domain owns nothing analytical — it owns **supply**, and the truth
about supply.

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: account connection & auth lifecycle,
per-source connector behavior (a DSP and a social network return incomparable shapes on
incomparable cadences), and ingestion health/gap surfacing are distinct capabilities that
interact directly and non-trivially: a token expiring silently produces a gap, and a gap that is
not surfaced turns every downstream chart, alert and insight into a confident lie. The interaction
*is* the design problem.

**Scope boundary — product vs architecture**: the *connection* is product (the user connects,
re-auths, sees health). The scheduler, queue, rate-limit budget and time-series store are
architecture and are routed to `/create-prd-architecture`, not modelled here.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | DSP and social ingestion are **separate features, not one generic "connector"** | They differ in what they return (streams/listeners/saves vs followers/views/watch-time), cadence (daily batch vs near-real-time), auth model (artist-claim-gated vs open OAuth), and honesty (DSPs report to the rights-holder; socials report to the account owner). One generic connector feature would spec none of that. | Agent classification, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | Ingestion **gaps are a first-class product surface**, not an ops concern | Every incumbent hides gaps behind an interpolated line. Given 22.08 (Credit-Linked Performance) is proposed `must`, a silent gap corrupts the one thing this domain exists to prove. Honesty about missing data is the differentiator, not a caveat. | Agent, Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | Ingestion scheduler / rate-limit budget / metrics store are **not modelled as product** | Architecture wearing a product costume. Routed to `/create-prd-architecture`. | Node Classification Gate |
