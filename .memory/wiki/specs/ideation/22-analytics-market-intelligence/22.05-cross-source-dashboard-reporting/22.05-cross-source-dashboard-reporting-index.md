## Overview

The destination. Everything 22.01–22.04 ingests, matches and derives arrives here as the one view
that answers the question the domain index names as the concrete pain: *"an artist logs into six
dashboards and can never answer 'is my career growing?'"* Plus the three things people actually do
with an answer — get told without looking (alerts and digests), show someone else (sharing and
export), and ask "compared to what?" (benchmarking).

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: the dashboard, alerts/digests, sharing/export
and benchmarking are distinct capabilities that interact around **one shared object — the report
definition**. A digest is a scheduled render of a report; a share link is a permissioned render of the
same report; a benchmark is that report with a comparison cohort attached. They are four verbs on one
noun, which is precisely the sub-domain test.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Alerts and digests are **one feature**, not two | Both answer "tell me without me looking"; both are rules over the same series; both share a delivery path and — critically — a **noise budget**. Split across two features, each independently reasonable, they collectively train the user to mute analytics entirely. The budget can only be enforced in one place. | Agent classification, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | The system **describes co-occurrence and never asserts causation** | Nothing in the ingested data supports a causal claim. "Your TikTok spike caused your streaming spike" is unprovable; "these two happened four days apart" is observable. Given D-18, asserting the former would fabricate exactly the class of fact the platform exists to end. | Agent, Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | Data honesty (gaps, estimates, imported provenance) **travels to every render** — dashboard, digest, share, export | Otherwise sharing becomes a laundering mechanism: the artist sees the hole, the label sees a clean chart. See 22.01.03 D-03. | Agent, 2026-07-16 |
| D-04 | Export exists to make the lock-in **earned, not hostile** | Directly answers problem-statement Q-02. If the user can leave with their data, staying is a judgement about value rather than a hostage situation — and the thing they cannot take (the counter-attested credit graph) is the honest moat. | Agent, thesis-derived, 2026-07-16 |
