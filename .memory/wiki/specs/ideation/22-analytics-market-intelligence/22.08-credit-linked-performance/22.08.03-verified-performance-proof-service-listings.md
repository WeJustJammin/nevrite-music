# Feature: Verified Performance Proof for Service Listings

> **Number**: 22.08.03
> **Parent**: [Credit-Linked Performance](./22.08-credit-linked-performance-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-18
> **Origin**: **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list.

## Overview

Publishing a credit-backed performance claim onto a service listing: *"my credited catalog has 40M
verified streams — counter-attested, not claimed."* This is the moment provenance converts into
income, which is the whole economic argument of D-18. Every competitor's equivalent is a self-written
bio; this one cannot be faked, because the credits behind it were attested by the people who were in
the room.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Publishes a role-scoped proof onto their listing (domain 05); chooses scope; sees it update or degrade. For the session player this replaces "I've played with some great people" — an unverifiable sentence every musician writes — with something a client can trust without knowing them. |
| Producer | Full | Same, and higher-stakes: their listing **is** their business. A producer's proof is the single most commercially consequential number this domain produces. |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees a proof when evaluating whom to book or hire for their room. Consumes trust they had no other way to get. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees proofs on public listings. **The Fan's only touchpoint in this entire sub-domain** — and it is genuinely useful to them: a proof is a legible signal of whether an artist is what they claim, without requiring any industry literacy. |

## Behavior

A proof is a **public assertion derived from a private rollup**, published where it does commercial
work. Its integrity rules are the feature:

- **Counter-attested credits only** (index D-02). Self-claims are excluded — the entire value is
  unfakeability, and a laundered self-claim destroys the badge for everyone using it honestly.
- **Role-scoped** (CX-03). A drummer's listing asserts drumming credits, not a lifetime total across
  every role.
- **Asserts less than the rollup knows** (CX-02 Q3). A total, never the catalog — the catalog reveals
  relationships that are frequently confidential (ghost production, NDA'd session work).
- **Degrades honestly** (index D-03). A shrinking rollup shrinks the proof; a revoked credit
  invalidates it. WeJammin must never publicly assert a claim it knows to be false.

### Happy Path

1. User opens their service listing (domain 05) and adds a performance proof.
2. Chooses role scope; system shows what will be asserted and what evidence backs it.
3. Publishes; the listing shows a proof with a verification affordance a client can inspect.
4. Client sees a claim they can trust without knowing the person — which is the entire point.
5. Proof re-derives as the catalog grows.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Credit revoked after publishing | Proof invalidates — non-negotiable | Owner told before a client asks. See `#CX-02` Q2 |
| Coverage is 30% | The proof understates their real career | Honest and unflattering. **The most likely reason this feature disappoints its own persona** — see DT-03 |
| Source gaps, rollup shrinks | Proof degrades | Never a silent downward restatement on a public commercial claim |
| Imported (self-claimed) numbers | Excluded from proof | 22.01.04 D-02, Q-02. May leave a session player with almost nothing to assert |
| Ghost production / NDA'd work | Credit exists privately; cannot be published | Total only, never the catalog — the confidentiality is the reason for CX-02 Q3's rule |
| Estimated contribution (22.03.01) | Never in proof | 22.03.01 D-03. Inference cannot back a verified claim |
| Streams later found fraudulent | Excluded upstream (22.04.01 D-04); proof shrinks | Restatement disclosed |
| Big numbers from one lucky record | Technically true, commercially misleading | `[PENDING — owner decision, /create-prd]` — does proof show distribution, not just total? A drummer with one 40M hit and 39 dead records is not a 40M drummer. See Q-02 |
| User wants to assert something we can't verify | Refused | The refusal **is** the product. A badge that asserts anything asserts nothing |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| None (empty) | No proof published | CTA on the listing |
| Publishing (loading) | Owner confirmed publish; rollup is being derived and scoped | Skeleton badge + "Preparing your proof…"; the listing is not yet showing an assertion, so nothing false is ever visible mid-derive |
| Published (populated) | Proof live | Badge + inspectable evidence |
| Degraded | Coverage/data shrank | Lower claim, disclosed to the owner |
| Invalidated | Credit revoked | Removed; owner notified with the reason |
| Unverifiable | No counter-attested credits | Feature unavailable — honestly. The cold-start case (DT-03) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is where provenance **becomes money**, and it is the thesis's economic proof | Reasoning from D-18 | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is the answer to the question D-18 leaves open. The thesis says provenance is "the reason they cannot leave" — but a musician does not stay for a database; personas.md is explicit that "nobody wakes up wanting a credits database" (the Jaxsta/Muso.AI failure). They stay because the record **wins them work**: personas.md's Musician success criterion is literally "no re-proving themselves to each new client" and "time-to-book a session drops". This feature is that criterion implemented. It converts an archive into an asset, and it is what makes the switching cost **earned rather than hostile** (problem-statement Q-02) — you can export your data (22.05.03 D-04), but you cannot export the counter-attestations, because they are other people's testimony about you. That is the honest moat, stated precisely. |
| DT-02 | What breaks that nobody mentioned? **A badge that can be gamed is worth less than no badge.** | Reasoning about the failure mode of every verification scheme ever built | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it drives every rule in this feature. The moment a self-claimed credit or an imported CSV can mint a verified proof, the badge means "this person filled in a form" — and it is then **worse than nothing**, because it launders unverified claims with our brand attached and clients learn to discount it. personas.md names this attack directly as the Musician's worst intentional behavior: "games the verified-credit graph to manufacture reputation". The badge's value is *entirely* in what it refuses. That inverts the usual product instinct — the feature's quality is measured by how many users it turns away, and there will be sustained pressure to loosen it every time a real user with real work can't qualify. **The pressure will be justified in each individual case and fatal in aggregate.** |
| DT-03 | Therefore this feature will disappoint the persona it exists for — at first | Reasoning from DT-02 + 22.08.01 DT-02's coverage finding | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is the uncomfortable finding this sub-domain must not hide. Stack the constraints: counter-attested credits only (index D-02), coverage is low and permanently patchy (22.08.01 DT-02), imported numbers excluded (22.01.04 D-02), estimates excluded (22.03.01 D-03). For a session drummer joining in year one with a decade of pre-WeJammin work, **the honest proof is nearly empty** — their real career is invisible to it, because none of it was captured here. The temptation to accept self-claims to fill the gap is exactly the temptation that destroys the badge (DT-02). The honest resolution is that this feature is **forward-looking by construction**: it proves the work you do *from now on*, and its value compounds with tenure. That is a hard message and it is the true one — and it means 22.08.03's value curve is the platform's retention curve, which is arguably the strongest possible reason to build it early even though it looks empty at launch. It also reframes the cold-start (22.08.01 DT-03): the badge is not a launch feature, it is a **promise** whose credibility depends on never having been loosened. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Counter-attested only** — no self-claims, no imports, no estimates | DT-02. The badge's entire value is what it refuses; one exception makes it meaningless. |
| D-02 | Proof **re-derives and degrades**; a revoked credit invalidates it | Publishing a claim we know to be false is the exact failure D-18 exists to prevent, committed by us on our own surface. |
| D-03 | Proof asserts a **total, never the catalog** | Some credits are confidential (ghost production, NDA'd work). The claim must not out the relationship. |
| D-04 | Proof is **role-scoped** | A lifetime cross-role total is true and misleading; misleading-but-true is how a badge dies slowly. |
| D-05 | The feature is **forward-looking by construction** and says so | DT-03. Better an honest empty badge that compounds than a full one that means nothing. |
