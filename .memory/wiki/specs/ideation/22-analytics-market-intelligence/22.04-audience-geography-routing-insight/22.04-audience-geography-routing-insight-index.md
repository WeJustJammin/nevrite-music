## Overview

Where the listeners are, and what to do about it. Audience geography at city granularity, the
routing and market-opportunity insight it enables, and the causal loop back the other way — did
playing a city actually grow it? This is where analytics stops describing and starts producing an
action with money attached.

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: geography, routing insight and show-impact
attribution form a closed loop, not a list. Geography feeds routing; routing produces a show; the
show's impact is measured back against geography; that measurement corrects the next routing call.
Each is a distinct capability and each consumes the previous one's output.

**Why this sub-domain is the domain's strategic core**: the domain index names it — *"audience
geography versus tour history is where it converts into an action no incumbent can produce, because
no incumbent also holds the routing data."* Chartmetric can show a listener map. It cannot show what
happened after you played there, because it does not hold your booking history. WeJammin holds
domains 17 (Live Booking) and 18 (Show Production), so it can close the loop. That is a
consolidation-derived advantage exactly as D-18 predicts: being where the work happens is what makes
the insight possible.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The **loop**, not the map, is the product | A listener map is a commodity — Spotify for Artists gives it away free. The loop (geography → routing → show → measured impact → corrected geography) is not available anywhere, because it requires holding booking data and streaming data together. This is the domain's only true structural advantage over Chartmetric et al. | Agent, Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | The Operator sees **aggregate market data only**, never an artist's private numbers | An artist's city-level listener count is commercially sensitive — it is their negotiating position on a guarantee. Exposing it to the counterparty who sets that guarantee inverts the power relationship the platform claims to serve. | Agent, Deep Think, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | Routing output is an **input to a human decision**, never a recommendation engine | Routing is constrained by facts we do not hold: van logistics, visas, agent relationships, a drummer's day job. A confident "play Leeds on the 14th" from a system blind to all of it is worse than useless. | Agent, 2026-07-16 |
