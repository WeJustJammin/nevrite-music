# Feature: Scouting Discovery Search

> **Number**: 22.07.03
> **Parent**: [A&R Scouting Signals & Watchlists](./22.07-ar-scouting-watchlists-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Finding artists you don't already know, by criteria rather than by name: genre, momentum, geography,
career stage — and, distinctively, by verified credit. This is the feature that turns a watchlist from
an address book into a scouting tool, and it is also the feature whose output is a **list of named
people ranked for evaluation**, which is a thing to build carefully or not at all.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Searches for collaborators: "session drummers within reach who've played on records like mine". The thesis-aligned use (parent D-03) and, notably, a **search domain 05's services marketplace also serves** — see DT-02. |
| Producer | Full | Searches for artists to work with. Their org-context version is the A&R case the parent's persona mapping absorbs. |
| Operator | Full | Searches for artists to book, filtered by catchment draw — "who has an audience within 50km at my capacity". This is 22.04.02's Operator view arriving through a different door, and the overlap is real (Q-02). |
| Fan | None | Never rendered. Consumer artist discovery exists in domains 20/21 and is a different product: it recommends music to love, not people to evaluate. |

## Behavior

Search is **consent-scoped at query time**, not at render (CX-01 Q3): a non-consenting artist is not
a filtered-out result, they are not a result. Filtering after the fact leaks existence — result
counts, pagination gaps and "no results" are all inference channels.

Criteria available:
- Artist metrics (momentum, audience scale, geography) — the commodity axis every incumbent has.
- Career stage and catalog shape.
- **Verified credits** — the differentiated axis: "played drums on records in this genre, credits
  counter-attested". Nobody else can offer this, for the reasons in 22.07.02 DT-02.

Results are **people**, ranked. That framing is deliberate and uncomfortable, and it is why D-01 caps
what a result may contain.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Artist has consent off | Not a result at all | No trace — not a redacted row, not a count discrepancy |
| Search returns 4,000 artists | Bulk evaluation surface | Caps and rate limits; a search tool that can enumerate the user base is a scraper |
| Criteria proxy for protected characteristics | Genre + geography can proxy for ethnicity | `[PENDING — Step 5]`. Real, and unaddressed by anyone in this category. See Q-03 |
| Non-user artists in results | Consent has no subject to ask | `[PENDING]` — 22.07.01 Q-02, same ethical floor |
| Result's momentum is fraudulent | Ranked as a rising star on fake data | Requires 22.06 gating — 22.07.02 DT-03, arriving here too |
| Search by credit surfaces a session player | The differentiated case, and the good one | Their invisible work becomes findable — which is how they get hired |
| Scout contacts everyone in the results | Cold outreach at scale | Contact is domains 03/04's; a scale limit belongs there, but the *list* originates here |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty | No query yet | Criteria builder |
| Loading | Query running | Skeleton |
| Populated | Results (consent-scoped) | Ranked list with visible-by-consent metrics |
| No results | Nothing matched | "No matches" — identical whether the cause is criteria or consent |
| Capped | Too broad | "Narrow your criteria" — never an enumeration |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Search over artist metrics is the feature | The obvious framing; what incumbents ship | ❌ REJECTED as WeJammin's version: metric search is a commodity computed from purchasable data, and Chartmetric does it better with a decade's index behind it. The version worth building is **credit-native search** — "find me a drummer whose counter-attested credits are in this genre and trending". That is uncopyable (22.07.02 DT-02) and it serves the primary persona *as the subject*: it makes the session player findable, and findability is how they get the next job (D-18: accumulated proof wins the next job). Building the metric version would be spending our scarce build on their moat rather than ours. |
| DT-02 | What breaks that nobody mentioned? **This is domain 05's search wearing a different hat.** | Cross-domain reasoning against the services marketplace | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is a real structural duplication the sweep did not catch. Domain 05 (Services Marketplace) must already have "find me a session drummer in this genre" — that is its core query, and it is *consented by construction* because the drummer **listed themselves for hire**. This feature proposes nearly the same search over nearly the same people, differing only in that the subject didn't opt in. Stated that way, the honest conclusion is uncomfortable for this sub-domain: **the consented version already exists in 05, and this is the unconsented version of it.** The genuine delta is searching people who have *not* offered themselves — which is exactly the part that needs 22.07.04 and exactly the part that may not deserve to exist. Escalates parent Q-02 from a values question to a **duplication** question: if 05 does this consented, what is left here? Recorded as Q-01 and routed to `/ideate-validate`. |
| DT-03 | Consent-scoping at render is sufficient | The natural implementation shortcut | ❌ REJECTED: filtering after query leaks existence through result counts, pagination gaps and timing. An artist who opted out of scouting must be **absent from the index**, not hidden in the response — otherwise a determined scout infers the opt-out itself, and "who opted out of being scouted" is a signal nobody consented to publish, arguably more revealing than the data they withheld. The scoping must be in the query. This is the difference between a privacy feature and a privacy gesture. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Consent-scoped **in the query**, never at render | DT-03. Post-filtering leaks the opt-out itself through counts and timing. |
| D-02 | **Credit-native search is the version worth building**; metric search is not | DT-01. The metric version competes on the incumbents' moat; the credit version is ours and serves the primary persona as its subject. |
| D-03 | Results are **capped**; enumeration is not a supported operation | A search that can walk the user base is a scraper with a UI. |
| D-04 | "No results" renders **identically** regardless of cause | Otherwise the absence is itself a disclosure. |
