## Overview

Pointing the domain's machinery **outward**: watching artists you don't own, scoring momentum, and
searching for people worth working with. This is the same computation as 22.05.04's benchmarking
cohort, aimed in the opposite direction — inward it is self-knowledge, outward it is surveillance of
our own primary persona. That inversion is the sub-domain's defining tension and the reason consent
is modelled here as a gate rather than a setting.

**Why this is a sub-domain (2+ interacting capabilities)**: watchlists, momentum signals, discovery
search and visibility consent are distinct and interact in a loop — search populates watchlists,
signals fire alerts on watchlists, and consent gates all three at the subject's end. Consent is not a
sibling capability but a constraint on the other three, which is precisely the kind of interaction
that makes a folder rather than four files.

**Merged candidates**: sweep candidates 07 ("A&R Scouting Signals & Watchlists") and 08 ("Artist
Discovery Signals & Scouting") are near-duplicates describing one capability from two angles. Merged
here; the merge is recorded in the return value.

## The Persona Problem — read this before drilling further

The sweep's provisional personas for this domain included **A&R**, **label** and **booking agent**.
None of these exist in the ratified set (D-19): `Musician`, `Producer`, `Operator`, `Fan`. This
sub-domain is the only place in domain 22 where that gap has teeth, because scouting's natural user
is a label A&R.

The mapping applied here, per D-19's entity model (labels are **entities** in domain 01, experienced
*through* personas — a persona is not always a person):

| Sweep persona | Mapped to | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| A&R / label | **Producer** (acting in an org context) | Producers scout constantly — it is how they find artists to work with. A label-affiliated A&R is an org membership under domain 01's entity model, not a fifth persona. |
| Booking agent / promoter | **Operator** | Venues and promoters scout who to book. This is the Operator's genuine scouting motive and it is not the same as a label's. |
| Manager | **Musician** or **Producer** in an org context | Same entity-model reasoning. |

This is a **mapping, not an invention** — no persona outside the four is used. But it is load-bearing
and it may be wrong; it directly parallels `personas.md` Q-01 (is a professional dealer a distinct
persona?) and is escalated as Q-01 below.
