# Feature: Momentum & Breakout Signals

> **Number**: 22.07.02
> **Parent**: [A&R Scouting Signals & Watchlists](./22.07-ar-scouting-watchlists-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Detecting that an artist is accelerating — growth rates crossing thresholds, engagement deepening,
placements compounding — and telling whoever is watching them. This is the classic A&R signal that
Chartmetric and Soundcharts sell, and the place where WeJammin could compute something none of them
can, precisely because our users told us the truth about their work.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | "That collaborator you played with is breaking out" — a prompt to reach out while the relationship is still warm. The peer-facing use (parent D-03). |
| Producer | Full | "This artist is accelerating" — a prompt to pitch for the next record. Their genuine A&R-shaped motive without needing an A&R persona. |
| Operator | Full | Distinctively geographic: "this artist is growing **in your catchment**" — a prompt to book them before they price themselves out. This is the Operator's real signal and shares 22.04's geography machinery, not 22.05.04's cohort machinery. |
| Fan | None | Never rendered. A fan learning an artist is "breaking out" is a discovery feature (domains 20/21) with different framing and no professional evaluation attached. |

## Behavior

Momentum is computed from acceleration rather than level: rate-of-change in listeners, followers,
saves, placements and — the differentiated part — **credited work** (see DT-02). It fires against
watchlists (CX-02) rather than across the whole index, because unscoped momentum is a firehose.

Signals are **descriptive observations, not predictions** — the same rule as 22.03.03's curator
intelligence, for the same reason. "This artist's saves grew 3× over 6 weeks" is observable. "This
artist will break" is a forecast that would be wrong most of the time and consequential when acted on.

Consent is evaluated at **fire time**, not watch time (CX-02 Q3). An artist who revokes stops
generating signals immediately, including to people who were watching them beforehand.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Growth is artificial (bot streams) | The signal is fraud's signature, not a breakout | Must be gated by 22.06 — the same failure as 22.03.01 DT-03, one step worse: here we'd tell a *third party* someone is breaking out on fake numbers. See DT-03 |
| Small artist, small absolute numbers | 3× of nothing is nothing | Floors required; percentage growth on tiny bases is noise |
| Artist revokes consent mid-window | Signal suppressed at fire time | Nothing fires. Not "signal with stale data" |
| Signal fires on a subject who never wanted scouting | The core problem 22.07.04 gates | Nothing fires if consent is off |
| Seasonal or release-driven spike | Momentum misreads a release as acceleration | Release context required, else every release day is a breakout |
| Every watched artist "breaking out" at once | Threshold miscalibration | Noise budget shared with 22.05.02 (D-01), enforced at composition |
| Signal about a Musician fires to a label org | The sharpest form of parent Q-02 | `[PENDING]` — depends on whether org watchlists exist (22.07.01 Q-01) |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Quiet | No signals | Nothing. The normal state |
| Fired | Threshold crossed, consent OK, fraud-gated | Signal with the observation and its window |
| Suppressed | Below floors, or fraud-flagged | Nothing |
| Gated | Subject revoked consent | Nothing — indistinguishable from quiet, deliberately. See D-03 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Momentum signals are a commodity — every incumbent has them | Competitive reasoning | ✅ CONFIRMED for the **artist-metric** version: growth-rate thresholds on DSP data are table stakes, computed from data anyone can buy, and Chartmetric does it better because they have a decade of index. Building it is competing on someone else's moat — the same conclusion 22.03.03 DT-01 reached. It is a `could` at best. |
| DT-02 | The **credit-graph momentum signal** is uncopyable | Reasoning from D-18 + the credit graph | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is the strongest and most uncomfortable finding in this sub-domain. "This drummer's *verified credits* are accelerating — three sessions this month, all on records that are growing" is computable **only** on WeJammin, because only WeJammin holds counter-attested credits captured at source (personas.md: the Producer's attestation is worth more than a self-claim). It surfaces the session player — the multi-hyphenate who is *invisible to every existing A&R tool* because they have no artist profile, no monthly listeners, and no scoreboard presence. That is a real and unusual good: this persona has never been findable, and being findable is how they get work (D-18: the accumulated proof is what wins the next job). **And it is the sharpest possible version of the surveillance problem** — it works precisely *because* our users honestly told us what they played on, for a completely different reason. The provenance data they contributed to protect themselves becomes the instrument by which they are evaluated. Both things are true simultaneously; that is parent Q-03 and it is a genuine values question, not a design detail. |
| DT-03 | What breaks at the intersection with 22.06 (Fraud)? | Cross-domain failure reasoning | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is worse here than in 22.03.01. There, unfiltered fraud means we congratulate a user on their own impending takedown — bad, but the harm lands on the person who can act on it. **Here we tell a third party that someone is breaking out on numbers that are fake.** A producer or venue acts on that signal — pitches, books, allocates money — and the artist is then exposed as fraudulent (whether or not they knew). We would have manufactured a reputational catastrophe for a user out of data we failed to filter, and delivered it to exactly the people whose opinion of them matters. Fraud gating is therefore a **hard precondition** for this feature, not a refinement — and since 22.06 is proposed `could`/`wont`, that is a real sequencing constraint on 22.07.02, mirroring 22.06.01 Q-03. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Descriptive observation, never prediction** | Same reasoning as 22.03.03 D-01: a forecast would be wrong most of the time and acted on anyway. |
| D-02 | Consent evaluated at **fire time**, not watch time | Otherwise revocation only stops new watchers, not existing ones — which is not revocation. |
| D-03 | A gated signal is **indistinguishable from quiet** | If "no signal because they opted out" were distinguishable from "no signal because nothing happened", the opt-out itself becomes a disclosure. |
| D-04 | Absolute floors, not just percentage growth | 3× of nothing is nothing, and it would dominate every signal list. |
