## Overview

The domain's read surface: what's happening, to the people you care about. The feed is not a content
product — it is a **projection of 23 other domains' events onto the social graph**. A credit confirmed
(02), a release shipped (12), a gig announced (17), a plugin published (14), a studio's Tuesday freed
up (16) — each is a feed-eligible event, and the feed's job is to decide which of them a given person
should see. Native posts exist but are the minority case; a music feed whose content is mostly people
posting about themselves has already failed.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three interacting capabilities. Event sourcing (what is
eligible to enter, and from where) constrains ranking (what surfaces), and ranking is meaningless
without user-facing relevance controls (what the reader can force or suppress). Change the event source
set and the ranking inputs change; change the controls and the fan-out cost model changes.

**Boundary — presence is NOT owned here.** The candidate child was "Activity Feed, Ranking & Presence",
but D-15 already extracted `Real-Time Rooms, Presence & Audio Transport` as a ratified cross-cut serving
Education, Services, Projects and Community. This sub-domain **consumes** presence; it does not own it.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The feed is a **projection of other domains' events**, not a content silo | Every one of the 24 domains emits state changes a follower would want. If the feed only carries native posts it becomes a social-media clone competing with Instagram, which WeJammin will lose. The consolidation half of the thesis (D-18) is what gives this feed content nobody else can assemble. | Node Classification Gate + D-18 |
| D-02 | Presence is a **consumed cross-cut**, not a child of this sub-domain | D-15 already extracted `Real-Time Rooms, Presence & Audio Transport` as a ratified cross-cut. The candidate name bundled it; the ratified decision unbundles it. | D-15, ideation-index.md |
| D-03 | The Fan feed and the professional feed are **the same feature with different event-source weighting**, not two products | Per D-12, fans and professionals share one surface. Per personas.md the Fan/hobbyist boundary "is porous" — a hard split forces a declaration the multi-hyphenate persona rejects. But the weighting difference is severe enough to flag as Q-01. | D-11, D-12, personas.md |
| D-04 | Feed event eligibility is decided **per emitting domain**, not centrally | Only domain 09 knows whether an ownership change is public. A central allowlist here would encode 23 domains' privacy rules in the wrong place and rot immediately. | Deep Think, DT-02 on 03.02.01 |
