# Feature: Feed Composition & Event Sources

> **Number**: 03.02.01
> **Parent**: [Activity Feed & Ranking](./03.02-activity-feed-ranking-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Defines what is allowed to enter the feed, from where, and to whom it fans out. This is the feature that
makes WeJammin's feed structurally impossible for a point solution to copy: because the platform is where
the work happens (D-18), the feed can carry a confirmed credit, a shipped release, an announced tour date,
a freed studio Tuesday and a listed cymbal in one stream. Reverb can only show you listings; Bandsintown
can only show you gigs. The event-source set **is** the consolidation thesis made visible.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Receives the widest event-source mix, because their multi-hyphenate identity makes nearly every class relevant — credits confirmed by collaborators, gigs from bands they follow, gear from people they know, releases from their scene. They are also a high-volume *emitter*: their work in 8+ domains generates the events other people's feeds consume. |
| Producer | Full | Receives project- and session-shaped events. Per personas.md they work in a DAW and will not tend a feed — so their value here is almost entirely as an **emitter**: the credit they confirm is the highest-trust event class on the platform, and it lands in every contributor's followers' feeds. |
| Operator | Full | Receives a narrow band — acts they book, local scene activity, enquiries. Emits room-shaped events (availability, upcoming shows, gear changes). Per personas.md much of their work is on a phone at a loading dock, so their feed is a glance surface, not a browse surface. |
| Fan | Full | Receives gigs and releases from followed artists, and effectively nothing else. Per personas.md they will not read a rider or evaluate a session credit — professional event classes are noise to them. They emit almost nothing (reactions, at most). Their volume (D-13a) makes their fan-out the platform's dominant cost. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

Each of the other 23 domains emits state changes. Per D-04, **the emitting domain decides eligibility** —
whether a given event is public, scoped, or never leaves. Composition then routes eligible events across
the social graph: follows (03.01.01) are the primary fan-out set, connections (03.01.02) and scene
membership (03.06.01) are secondary sets with different weights.

An event carries its own semantics, not a generic "activity" blob: a confirmed credit has an evidence
basis, a gig has a date and a geography that determine whether it is actionable, a listing has a price
and a lifespan. Flattening these into a uniform item is what makes ranking (03.02.02) impossible to do
well — see DT-01.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — the authoritative event-source catalogue (which of the
23 domains emit what, at what visibility) cannot be written until those domains are drilled. It is
enumerated at Step 5 or later, and is the single largest unknown in this sub-domain.

### Happy Path

1. A domain records a state change — e.g. a Producer confirms a credit naming a contributor (domain 02)
2. The emitting domain evaluates feed eligibility against its own visibility rules (D-04)
3. Composition resolves the fan-out set from the social graph — the contributor's followers, their scene
4. The event is delivered, carrying its type-specific payload and its evidence basis
5. Readers see a card that reflects what actually happened, with the actions that event affords

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Event retracted after fan-out — credit disputed (02/24), gig cancelled (17), listing withdrawn (13) | Delivered items must be retroactively removed or amended; an append-only feed cannot do this | Item disappears or is marked cancelled. A cancelled gig must show as cancelled rather than vanish — silent removal means fans turn up |
| Emitting domain leaks a private event (a private project's activity, domain 07) | A privacy breach sourced in the wrong domain but surfaced here — this feature gets blamed for a bug it cannot prevent | Nothing; the damage is that it was seen |
| High-fan-out entity (band with 40k followers) emits | Fan-out cost spikes; per D-13a this is the platform's dominant load | N/A — surfaces as latency |
| Follower's scene is empty / they follow nobody active | Feed is empty at exactly the moment first impressions are formed | Empty state — the cold-start problem DT-03 on 03.01.01 confirmed as this domain's real difficulty |
| Same underlying fact emits from two domains (a release shipped in 12 also confirms credits in 02) | Duplicate cards for one event | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — needs a dedupe/collapse rule |
| Event references an entity the reader cannot see (a credit on a private/unreleased project) | Card must degrade, not leak | `[PENDING]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Feed opening | Skeleton cards; per personas.md the Operator's phone-at-loading-dock context makes perceived speed a real requirement |
| Empty | No followed entities, or none active | Empty state with follow/scene CTAs — see cold-start note above |
| Populated | Events delivered | Typed cards with type-specific affordances |
| Error | Fetch failed | Error with retry |
| Partial | Some event sources unavailable (a domain's service degraded) | Feed renders with what resolved rather than failing whole — a degraded feed is far better than no feed |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Feed events can be a uniform `activity` record with a type field and a payload blob | Standard feed modelling | ❌ REJECTED: the event classes have irreconcilable decay and actionability semantics. A gig dies at its door time and is worthless 200 miles away; a confirmed credit is a permanent fact with no decay; a listing dies on sale. A uniform record forces ranking to special-case every type anyway, having already thrown away the fields it needs to do so. This mirrors D-14's reasoning about the merged-marketplace `listing` entity with 40 nullable columns. |
| DT-02 | A central allowlist here should decide what is feed-eligible | Simplicity; one place to reason about feed privacy | ❌ REJECTED → became D-04. Only domain 09 knows whether an ownership change is publishable; only 07 knows if a project is private. A central list would encode 23 domains' privacy rules in the wrong place and drift the moment any of them changes. Eligibility is the emitter's decision; composition only routes. |
| DT-03 | What a domain expert would expect that nobody mentioned: **the feed is where credit confirmation requests should land** | The thesis (D-18) — "the design must make the lazy path the correct path" (personas.md, Producer anti-persona) | ✅ CONFIRMED as the highest-value idea in this sub-domain. The Producer's worst accidental behaviour is closing a project without capturing splits. If the pending-confirmation prompt is a card in the feed they already open — not an email, not a separate admin surface — the lazy path becomes the correct path. This makes the feed a **write surface**, which raises Q-04 on the parent (it then inherits other domains' permission models). It also reframes Q-02: the feed may be justified by provenance capture rather than by engagement. |
| DT-04 | Failure mode at the intersection with Trust & Safety: the feed is the platform's fastest abuse amplifier | Adversarial reasoning; D-13b (second moderation population) | ✅ CONFIRMED: every other surface here is 1:1 or small-group. The feed is the only one where a single act reaches 40,000 people, and per D-13a the fan population makes that reach real. Any moderation model that assumes review-before-reach fails on fan-out latency; any that assumes review-after-reach accepts that the harm has already landed. Routes to domain 24 — flagged now because it constrains 24's design, not this feature's. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Events are typed with type-specific payloads, not a uniform activity blob | DT-01 — decay and actionability semantics differ irreconcilably by class. |
| D-02 | The emitting domain owns eligibility (parent D-04) | DT-02 — 23 domains' privacy rules cannot live here. |
| D-03 | A cancelled or retracted event must be visibly amended, not silently removed | A vanished gig card means a fan turns up to a cancelled show. Silence is the worse failure. |
