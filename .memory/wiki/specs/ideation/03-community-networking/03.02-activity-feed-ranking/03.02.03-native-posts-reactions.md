# Feature: Native Posts & Reactions

> **Number**: 03.02.03
> **Parent**: [Activity Feed & Ranking](./03.02-activity-feed-ranking-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Freeform authored content — a post, and the reactions to it. This is the minority case in a feed whose
value comes from work events (D-01 on the parent), and it is deliberately scoped small. A music platform
whose feed is mostly people posting about themselves is competing with Instagram on Instagram's terms and
will lose. Posts exist to carry the things no domain event can express: a question, a shout-out, "the
session yesterday was unbelievable", "RIP" — the connective tissue between the evidenced facts.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Posts and reacts. Per personas.md they are "not a star" making a living from a portfolio of small income streams — so a post here is usually working communication (looking for a dep, gear moved, thanks for the session), not broadcast. The temptation to post for reach is real, and their anti-persona behaviour ("manufacture reputation") is exactly what an engagement-ranked post surface would reward. |
| Producer | Full | Posts rarely — per personas.md their work happens in a DAW and any flow requiring them to leave it will not be used. This applies to posting as much as to split capture. Their absence from the post surface is a *fact to design around*, not a gap to nudge them out of. |
| Operator | Full | Posts operationally — "Tuesday free", "backline changed", "load-in is now round the back". Per DT-03 these are arguably structured room events (domain 16) wearing a post's clothes, and the better product may be to give them the structured affordance instead. |
| Fan | Read-only | Reads and reacts; **cannot author** a post into the professional feed (D-01 below). Per personas.md they are first-class users (D-11) but have no professional stake, and per D-13a they outnumber professionals by orders of magnitude — an open fan-authoring surface would swamp the feed by volume alone and import a consumer-social moderation load (D-13b) for no gain in the thesis. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

A post is authored text (plus `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` on media — audio attachment
in a music platform is not a small question, see Q-02) with a visibility scope: public, connections, or a
specific scene (03.06.01). Reactions are lightweight acknowledgement; per `03.02-activity-feed-ranking-cx.md#CX-02`
they are the highest-volume lowest-value notification class on the platform and will need aggregation.

Posts compete for feed slots against evidenced events but are structurally outranked by them (D-02 on
03.02.02). This is the mechanism that stops the loudest poster outranking the most-hired musician.

### Happy Path

1. User authors a post and sets its visibility scope
2. System fans it out to the scoped audience via the graph (03.02.01)
3. Readers see it ranked below evidenced events of comparable recency
4. Readers react or reply; the author is notified, aggregated at volume

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Post edited after fan-out | Delivered copies diverge from the source | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — an edit after reactions changes what people endorsed |
| Post deleted after reactions/replies exist | Replies orphan | `[PENDING]` |
| Post contains uncleared audio (a stem, a sample) | Per personas.md the Musician's worst accidental behaviour is "uploads a stem containing an uncleared sample and exposes every collaborator downstream" — a post is a much easier place to do this than a project | Routes to domain 24 / DMCA. See Q-02 — this is the main argument against audio attachments |
| Post is the vector for spam ("check out my beat") at scale | The flood that makes music platforms unusable for the Producer persona | Rate limits; routes to 24 |
| Fan reacts at D-13a volume to a 40k-follower band's post | Notification storm | Aggregation, not per-reaction notification |
| Post announces something that should have been a domain event (a release, a gig) | Unstructured data that the platform could have captured properly — a small instance of the reconstruction failure the whole product exists to prevent | See DT-02 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Composer opening | Composer skeleton |
| Empty | No posts on a profile/scene | Absent section — not a prompt to post; manufacturing posting pressure is how the feed degrades |
| Populated | Posts exist | Post cards with reactions and replies |
| Error | Write failed | Draft preserved and retried — losing an author's text is unforgivable |
| Partial | Fan-out incomplete | Author sees the post; audience is still resolving |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Fans should be able to post into the feed, since D-11 makes them first-class users | D-11's plain reading | ❌ REJECTED: first-class **user** does not mean identical affordances — personas.md is explicit that the Fan's "surface must be a different product wearing the same brand". D-13a (orders-of-magnitude volume) plus D-13b (a second moderation population with different failure modes) means fan authoring would swamp the professional feed and import a consumer-social moderation burden with no thesis payoff. Fans read and react. This is a genuine tension with D-11 and is flagged as Q-01 for the owner rather than settled by an agent. |
| DT-02 | An expert would expect that **most posts are unstructured versions of things the platform should capture properly** | The domain thesis (D-18): the product exists because facts get recorded as memory instead of data | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is the most useful insight here: "just finished tracking with @X @Y" is a credit trying to happen. "Free next Tuesday" is an availability signal (03.03.03). "Anyone need a dep for Friday?" is an opportunity (04). The right response is not to ban posts but to **detect and offer the structured upgrade** — post a shout-out naming collaborators, get offered a credit confirmation. This turns the post surface into a provenance funnel rather than a competitor to it, and pairs with DT-03 on 03.02.01 (the feed as capture point). |
| DT-03 | The Operator's posts are actually room events | Role Lens analysis above | ✅ CONFIRMED: "Tuesday free" is domain 16 availability, "backline changed" is a room spec change (which per personas.md, if stale, means "a band arrives to a room that can't run their show"). Letting an Operator post this as freeform text means the data is invisible to booking and matchmaking. Same pattern as DT-02 — offer the structured affordance. Recorded as a CX to 16. |
