# Feature: Ranking, Relevance & Feed Controls

> **Number**: 03.02.02
> **Parent**: [Activity Feed & Ranking](./03.02-activity-feed-ranking-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Decides which eligible events actually surface, and gives the reader authority over that decision. The
central design rule is that ranking must weight **evidenced** events above **asserted** ones — a confirmed
credit outranks a post claiming the same thing — because that is the domain thesis (D-18) expressed as a
ranking function. An engagement-optimised feed would systematically bury the Producer persona, who works
in a DAW and posts nothing, while promoting whoever posts most; that inverts the platform's value.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | Sets what they want to see across their several simultaneous roles — and per personas.md that set is genuinely wide, so per-source-type controls matter more for them than for anyone. They are the persona most likely to actually open these settings, because they are the persona most spammed by their own breadth. |
| Producer | Config | Per personas.md they will not leave the DAW to tune a feed. Their controls must **default correctly** because they will never be opened — meaning the default weighting is their real interface, and any design that relies on configuration has already lost them. |
| Operator | Config | Wants a geographic and roster filter — their room, their acts, their city — and nothing else. Their context (phone, loading dock) means the default view must be the useful one in a single glance. |
| Fan | Config | Per personas.md they "will not tolerate professional-tool complexity" — the control surface exposed to them must be minimal (location radius, followed artists) and everything else defaulted away. Their volume (D-13a) means their defaults set the platform's compute bill. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

Ranking scores each candidate event for each reader. The inputs that are defensible from what we know:
graph proximity (follow / connection / verified collaboration distance, per 03.04.01), geographic
relevance (a gig 200 miles away is not actionable; per D-15 the ~25–30 ms desync ceiling means even
collaboration has a physical radius), event class weight (evidenced above asserted), and recency
weighted by that class's decay profile (a gig decays to its door time; a credit does not decay).
Scene membership is a candidacy boundary only: it can make scene content eligible for that member's
own feed, but it must never add a rank feature, boost, or tie-breaker. “In your scene” is a
legibility label over geographic relevance, not a membership-weighted score.

Controls are the reader's override: suppress a source type, force chronological, mute a topic or an
entity without unfollowing (the social escape valve — you cannot unfollow your bandmate's side project
without it being noticed). Per `03.02-activity-feed-ranking-cx.md#CX-01`, a suppression should stop
fan-out rather than filter at render.

**Architecture boundary:** `/create-prd-architecture` selects the executable formula and materialization
technique after the source catalogue is fixed. Every formula must use only the listed evidence inputs
and preserve the candidacy/rank boundary above.

### Happy Path

1. Reader opens the feed
2. System scores eligible events using graph position, geography, event class, recency, and controls; scene membership only supplied candidacy
3. Feed renders highest-relevance first, with the reason legible ("because you follow X", "in your scene"); the latter explains geographic candidacy, never a membership boost
4. Reader mutes a source type or entity; the change takes effect on the next composition and persists

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Cold start — no engagement history to rank on | Ranking has no signal; personalisation is a lie at this point | Falls back to geo + follows + chronological. Per parent Q-03 this may be the correct permanent design, not just a fallback |
| Reader in a thin scene (20 users scattered, per the domain overview) | Ranking cannot manufacture content that does not exist | Seeded scene content (03.06.04) rather than an empty feed |
| Ranking suppresses something the reader needed — a gig by a followed artist in their city | The single worst failure in this feature: it breaks the Fan's stated success criterion ("never misses a show by an artist they follow", personas.md) | Nothing — they find out afterwards. See DT-02: this class must bypass ranking entirely |
| Reader mutes an entity they are connected to | Must be silent — a visible mute is a social incident | No signal to the muted party, ever |
| Engagement-gaming (posting for reach) | Native posts are the only reader-controllable-volume source; see `03.02-activity-feed-ranking-cx.md#CX-02` | Degraded feed quality |
| Control change with fan-out-on-write | Precomputed rows can still exist after the reader changes a control | The next read applies the new control before rendering; asynchronous regeneration removes obsolete rows, so stale materialization never becomes stale exposure |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Feed opening | Skeleton |
| Empty | Nothing scores above threshold, or nothing exists | Empty state with a widening CTA (expand radius, join a scene) — never an apology |
| Populated | Ranked events | Ranked feed with legible reason-for-inclusion per card |
| Error | Scoring unavailable | Degrade to chronological rather than fail — a worse feed beats no feed |
| Partial | Some sources unavailable | Render what resolved |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Ranking should optimise engagement, like every consumer feed | Convention; it is what ranking *means* in practice elsewhere | ❌ REJECTED: engagement optimisation systematically promotes the persona who posts and buries the persona who works. Per personas.md the Producer is in a DAW and posts nothing — yet they are the trust broker whose presence makes the platform valuable, and the capture point for the entire provenance thesis. A feed that buries them optimises against D-18. Ranking optimises for **actionability** (can the reader do something about this?), not attention. |
| DT-02 | An expert would expect that some events must **bypass ranking entirely** | personas.md Fan success criterion; the Bandsintown/Songkick failure mode the Fan persona is defined by | ✅ CONFIRMED: "artist you follow is playing your city" is not a feed item to be scored — it is an alert that must be delivered. If ranking can suppress it, the product fails at the one thing the Fan came for, and the failure is invisible (they never know what they missed). This means the ranked feed and the alert path are **different mechanisms**, and the alert path is the one carrying the Fan's actual value. It strengthens the parent's Q-02: perhaps the alert surface is the Must and the feed is the Could. |
| DT-03 | Geographic weighting is a nice-to-have refinement | Naive assumption | ❌ REJECTED: geography is close to the *primary* axis in this domain, not a refinement. The domain overview makes local scenes the cold-start strategy ("200 users in one city feels alive"); personas.md makes the Fan's need explicitly local ("played their city"); D-15 gives collaboration itself a physical radius via the ~25–30 ms desync ceiling. A feed ranked without geography would be wrong for every persona simultaneously. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Ranking optimises actionability, not engagement | DT-01 — engagement optimisation inverts the platform's value by burying the Producer. |
| D-02 | Evidenced events structurally outrank asserted ones | The thesis (D-18) as a ranking rule; the anti-gaming defence per `03.02-activity-feed-ranking-cx.md#CX-02`. |
| D-03 | Geography is a primary ranking axis, not a filter | DT-03 — the cold-start strategy, the Fan's need, and the collaboration radius are all geographic. |
| D-04 | Alert-class events bypass ranking entirely | DT-02 — the Fan's success criterion cannot be subject to a relevance score. |
| D-05 | Mutes are always silent to the muted party | A visible mute between connected professionals is a social incident the product should never cause. |
| D-06 | Scene membership controls candidacy only; it is never a ranking feature, boost, or tie-breaker | D-08 in 03.06.01 and the evidence-over-participation rule require geographic and graph evidence to determine rank independently of membership. |
