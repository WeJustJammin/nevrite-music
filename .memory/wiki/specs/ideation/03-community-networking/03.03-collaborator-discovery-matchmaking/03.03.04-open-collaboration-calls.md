# Feature: Open Collaboration Calls

> **Number**: 03.03.04
> **Parent**: [Collaborator Discovery & Matchmaking](./03.03-collaborator-discovery-matchmaking-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The demand side: "I have a track that needs a vocalist — indie-folk, remote fine, split-based." A call is
a peer, informal, split-or-unpaid request with no hiring process — no brief, no shortlist, no deadline
management. That last clause is the entire boundary with domain 04 (Opportunities & Casting), and it is
thin enough that this feature is flagged as the most likely duplicate in domain 03 (parent Q-01).

The call is also where the platform's provenance thesis (D-18) meets its highest-risk moment: **a
split-based collaboration is a split that has not been agreed yet.**

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Posts calls for their own tracks and answers others'. Per personas.md they occupy both sides in the same day, so the surface cannot ask which side they are on. Their anti-persona risk lands here: a call is where "we'll sort the split out later" gets said, and per personas.md that is the failure the platform exists to prevent. |
| Producer | Full | Posts calls to fill contributor seats on projects they run. Per personas.md they are the trust broker and the capture point — which makes them the right party to be pushed for split terms *at call time* (DT-02). The moment money or a selection process enters, D-02 on the parent says this belongs to domain 04 — a boundary they will not perceive and will cross constantly. |
| Operator | None | No access (parent Role Matrix). An Operator does not seek creative collaborators; they seek acts to book (domain 17) and services for the room (domain 05). |
| Fan | None | No access (parent D-05). Per personas.md the Fan "will never file a split sheet" — and a split-based call is precisely a split sheet waiting to happen. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

A call declares a need in the same vocabulary the signals use (`03.03-collaborator-discovery-matchmaking-cx.md#CX-02`)
— role, genre, remote/in-the-room (a physics question per D-15), and terms (split-based, unpaid, credit-only).
It is matched against open-to signals and is browsable.

Whether it is also **pushed** to matching people is unresolved (parent Q-04): push clears the market faster
and is a broadcast spam tool wearing a matchmaking costume.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — response handling (how a responder is evaluated, whether
there is a shortlist) is deliberately unspecified, because specifying it would turn this into domain 04.

### Happy Path

1. User posts a call — role, genre, terms, remote/in-the-room
2. System matches against open-to signals; the call becomes discoverable
3. Matching people see it (pushed or pulled — Q-04) and respond through the reachability path (03.04.04)
4. The poster picks someone; per DT-02 the split conversation is forced **here**, before the work starts
5. Accepting a responder should create the project/session record the credit will later hang off (domain 07/02)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Operator or Fan reaches the call surface (direct link, deep link) | Neither persona has access (Role Lens; parent Role Matrix, D-05). An Operator seeks acts and rooms, not creative collaborators; a Fan will never respond to a split-based call | Not-authorized state — the call is not addressed to them, no post/respond affordance shown |
| Poster tries to publish a call with terms left blank ("we'll sort it later") | Blocked at post time — terms (split-based / unpaid / credit-only) are a required field, never a nullable one. This is D-01 enforced in the UI, not a warning | Cannot submit; the terms field is flagged as required with the reason ("collaborators need to know the deal before they respond") |
| Call filled while responses are in flight | Late responders get silence and learn responding is pointless — the mechanism that kills the supply side of a two-sided market | Call closes and late responders are told. Silence is the expensive failure |
| Call is a disguised job ("vocalist needed, paid") | This is domain 04, but the poster does not know that | Boundary leak — parent Q-01. Likely the surfaces must route rather than police |
| "Split to be agreed later" | Per personas.md this is the Producer's worst accidental behaviour and the exact failure the platform exists to prevent | DT-02 — terms are mandatory at call time |
| Call attracts 200 responses (a well-known poster) | The poster cannot triage; responders get silence | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — and if the answer is a shortlist, this has become domain 04 |
| Call is bait to harvest stems ("send me your best vocal take") | A real scam in music collaboration — free work extracted through a fake call | Routes to domain 24. Flagged in Q-03 |
| Responder delivers, poster ghosts, work is used anyway | Uncredited use of a contribution — the platform's core wrong, committed inside its own feature | DT-02 mitigates by capturing terms up front; unresolved beyond that |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a distinct feature from domain 04's opportunities | The sweep's placement of candidate 04 in this domain | ⏳ DEFERRED — genuinely unresolved and it is this domain's biggest structural risk. The stated distinction (D-02 on the parent: a hiring process) is real but thin, and **users do not classify their own needs**. A Producer needing a vocalist thinks "I need a vocalist", not "is this a casting or a collaboration?". Two surfaces answering one question means both look half-empty, which in a thin scene is fatal. Retained as a node for this breadth pass per the coverage rule, escalated as parent Q-01. An agent must not resolve this — it is a product decision. |
| DT-02 | What a domain expert would expect that nobody mentioned: **the call is the earliest possible split-capture point** | The thesis (D-18) applied to this feature; personas.md's Producer anti-persona ("closes a project without capturing splits — the design must make the lazy path the correct path") | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it converts this feature from a noticeboard into a thesis-carrying surface. The split conversation is easiest **before** the work exists, when nobody knows whose contribution will matter and everyone is friendly — exactly the moment personas.md identifies ("credit + split captured while everyone is present and friendly"). Once the vocal take is delivered and it is brilliant, the negotiation is adversarial. So: terms are **mandatory** on a call, and accepting a responder should instantiate the project/split record rather than leaving it to be reconstructed. This is the single strongest argument for keeping this feature in domain 03 rather than folding it into 04. |
| DT-03 | Failure mode at the intersection with Rights (09) and Trust (24): a call is an attack surface for free work | Adversarial reasoning about the anti-persona | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real and known scam: post a call, collect 40 speculative vocal takes, use one, credit nobody. The responders have delivered copyrightable work with no agreement. This is not hypothetical — it is a standard complaint about open collaboration boards. It reinforces DT-02 (terms up front) and adds a second requirement: a **speculative submission must not be a copyright transfer**, and the call must say what happens to unused submissions. Routes to domain 09 and 24. |
