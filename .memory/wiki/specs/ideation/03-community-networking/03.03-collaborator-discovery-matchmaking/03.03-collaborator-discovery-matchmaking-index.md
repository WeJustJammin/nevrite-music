## Overview

The two-sided market for *unpaid or split-based creative collaboration* — finding the person you should
make something with. The supply side declares appetite (open-to signals); the demand side declares need
(open collaboration calls); a matching layer connects them, scored against the verified collaboration
graph, geography, and the physical latency ceiling D-15 established.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: search, scoring, supply signals and demand calls are four
capabilities that only work as a system. An open call is matched against open-to signals *by* the scoring
layer; the scoring layer is worthless without supply signals to score; search without scoring is a
directory, which is the thing that has failed repeatedly. Change the signal vocabulary and both the call
form and the scorer change with it.

**Merged from three candidates**: `Collaborator Discovery & Matchmaking` (03), `Open Collaboration Calls`
(04), and `Open-To Status & Availability Signals` (08) were classified as one system rather than three —
they are the matcher, the demand side, and the supply side of a single market.

**Boundary hazard — this sub-domain abuts domain 04 (Opportunities & Casting).** See Q-01; it is the
most likely duplicate in this domain and an agent should not resolve it unilaterally.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Candidates 03, 04 and 08 merged into one sub-domain | They are the matcher, the demand side and the supply side of one market. Split across three nodes, each is incoherent: open-to signals with nothing reading them are a profile field; calls with no matcher are a noticeboard; a matcher with no signals has nothing to score. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | This sub-domain is scoped to **unpaid/split-based creative collaboration**; paid engagements with a hiring process belong to domain 04 | The distinction is a hiring process, not money: a call here is "I need a vocalist for this track, split-based" — peer, informal, no shortlist. Domain 04 is briefs, applications, shortlists, deadlines. The boundary is thin and is flagged as Q-01 rather than asserted as settled. | Node Classification Gate + domain 04's charter |
| D-03 | Matching is scored against the **verified collaboration graph**, not self-declared skills alone | The domain's structural insight (overview) is that a graph derived from verified collaboration is real by construction. A matcher that reads only self-declared tags is a directory — and directories for music collaboration have failed repeatedly because everyone claims everything. | Domain overview; D-18 |
| D-04 | Geographic and latency constraints are **first-class match inputs**, not filters | Per D-15 the ~25–30 ms desync ceiling caps playable radius at a few hundred miles — this makes latency-aware collaborator matching the retained justification for domain 08's existence. A matcher ignoring physics proposes collaborations that cannot happen. | D-15 |
| D-05 | Fans have **no access** to any child here | Per personas.md the Fan has no professional stake and will "never file a split sheet". A fan in the collaborator market is either a hobbyist musician (in which case they are acting as Musician) or a harassment vector. | personas.md; D-11 boundary |
