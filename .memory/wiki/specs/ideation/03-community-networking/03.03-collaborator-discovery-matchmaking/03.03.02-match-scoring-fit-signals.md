# Feature: Match Scoring & Fit Signals

> **Number**: 03.03.02
> **Parent**: [Collaborator Discovery & Matchmaking](./03.03-collaborator-discovery-matchmaking-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The scoring layer that turns a candidate set into an ordered answer. Its inputs are what make this domain
non-copyable: evidenced capability (the credit graph, domain 02), declared appetite (03.03.03), graph
proximity (03.04.01), and physical feasibility (geography bounded by D-15's ~25–30 ms desync ceiling).
No point solution holds more than one of these. The scoring model itself is not the moat — the inputs are,
and they only exist because the platform is where the work happens (D-18).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Experiences scoring as result order and as the stated reason for each match ("played on 3 records in your genre; 2 hops via your bassist; 40 miles"). Per DT-02 they should see the **reasons**, not a number. They are also scored *as a candidate* — which means this feature quietly determines their access to work, and they will (correctly) suspect it. |
| Producer | Read-only | Same, but their stake is filling a seat correctly. Per personas.md they are the trust broker — a bad match wastes a session they are running, which costs money and a relationship, so their tolerance for noise is near zero. |
| Operator | None | Not scored and does not score. Their discovery need (acts to book) runs through domain 04/17, whose scoring is a different problem with different inputs (draw, fit for the room, date availability). |
| Fan | None | No access (parent D-05). |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

### Happy Path

1. A candidate set arrives from search (03.03.01) or a call (03.03.04)
2. Each candidate is scored across the inputs above
3. Results order by score, each carrying its human-readable reasons
4. The reader acts on a result; the outcome (contacted / worked with / ignored) is feedback — see Q-02

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Candidate has appetite but no evidence (newcomer) | Structurally buried — DT-02 on 03.03.01, the rich-get-richer defect | Unresolved; parent-level Q-01 on 03.03.01 |
| Candidate has evidence but stale appetite | Ghost — the false positive that costs the demand side its trust | Decay in 03.03.03 |
| Thin scene: every query returns the same 3 hubs | Scoring cannot manufacture supply; entrenches the scene's incumbents | The mechanism R-03 rejected collaborative filtering for — but proximity scoring reproduces it. See DT-03 |

| Someone reverse-engineers the weights and games their profile | The evidence inputs are expensive to fake (a credit needs a Producer's counter-attestation), which is the defence | See DT-03 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The scoring algorithm is the competitive moat | The instinctive assumption about any matching product | ❌ REJECTED: the algorithm is commodity — any competent team writes a weighted scorer. The moat is the **inputs**, and the inputs exist only because the platform is in the room where the work happens (D-18). Jaxsta could build a better scorer tomorrow and have nothing to score. This reframing matters for MoSCoW: effort belongs in capturing the inputs (domain 02), not in tuning weights. |
| DT-02 | Users should see a match score (a number, a percentage) | Convention across matching products | ❌ REJECTED: a number invites comparison and dispute ("why am I an 82?"), makes the system look objective when it is a set of guessed weights, and turns a professional's access to work into a legible target to optimise. Per personas.md the Musician is a working professional whose income depends on this ordering — showing them a number they cannot control invites exactly the wrong relationship with the product. Show the **reasons** (evidence, path, distance); they are honest, actionable, and hard to argue with because they are facts. |
| DT-03 | What breaks at the intersection with the cold-start reality: proximity scoring entrenches the scene's hubs | Adversarial reasoning; the domain overview's "200 users in one city" | ✅ CONFIRMED and it is the same defect R-03 rejected collaborative filtering for — meaning the rejection did not actually escape the problem. Proximity scoring in a thin scene returns the few well-connected people to everyone, which is the rich-get-richer machine again, arriving by a second route. In a dense scene it is correct behaviour; in a thin one it makes the market feel closed to precisely the newcomers the scene needs. The honest conclusion is that **scoring weights must be density-aware** — proximity should count for less where the graph is thin. Recorded as Q-03. |
| DT-04 | Endorsement counts (03.01.03) should be a scoring input | The obvious pairing — they are right there in the same domain | ❌ REJECTED: per DT-01 on 03.01.03, the endorsement gate raises the cost of fake endorsements but does nothing about sincere reciprocal inflation between real collaborators. Feeding an inflatable metric into the function that allocates work turns a vanity artefact into a market-distorting one, and creates a direct incentive to farm endorsements. The credit graph already says what endorsements pretend to say, with counter-attestation behind it. This reinforces Q-04 on 03.01.03 (is endorsement worth building at all). |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Scoring reads evidence, appetite, proximity and feasibility — never endorsements | DT-04 — an inflatable metric must not allocate work. |
| D-02 | Users see reasons, not scores | DT-02 — a number invites dispute and gaming; facts do neither. |
| D-03 | Missing inputs are stated, not silently dropped | A score computed on fewer axes while presented identically is a misrepresentation of the evidence claim. |
| D-04 | The moat is the inputs, not the model | DT-01 — directs MoSCoW effort toward domain 02, not toward weight-tuning here. |
