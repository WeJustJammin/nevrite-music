# Feature: Referral & Recommendation Requests

> **Number**: 03.04.03
> **Parent**: [Warm Intros & the Collaboration Graph](./03.04-warm-intros-collaboration-graph-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The push direction of the same graph: a broker puts someone forward rather than a requester asking to be
put forward. "I can't take this mix — talk to Sarah, she engineered three records with me." In real music
economics this is how most work is actually allocated: an overbooked professional passes the job on, and
the pass-on carries their name. The referral is the highest-trust signal on the platform because the
referrer is spending their own credibility on an outcome they will hear about.

Its structural relationship to intros is only the direction of initiation (`03.04-warm-intros-collaboration-graph-cx.md#CX-04`),
which raises a real question about whether it deserves a separate feature (Q-01).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Asks contacts to refer them for work, and refers others when they cannot take a job. Per personas.md their income is "a portfolio of small income streams" — the referral is how a portfolio person handles a clash without losing the relationship, and how they get the next stream. Being referred is worth more to them than any profile optimisation. |
| Producer | Full | The highest-value referrer, and the most frequent one — per personas.md they are the trust broker and are the person clients call first. When they are booked, the referral is what they say instead of no. This is arguably the platform's most economically important social act, and it currently happens entirely in WhatsApp. |
| Operator | Config | Refers engineers, backline suppliers and acts to other rooms. Config-level: their referrals are room-adjacent (a house engineer, a dep) rather than creative. Per personas.md they are frequently both buyer and seller, so their referrals cross both directions. |
| Fan | None | No access (parent Role Matrix). A Fan has no professional standing to refer anyone, and a referral from someone with no evidenced relationship carries no information. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

Two directions, and they are genuinely different acts:

- **Pull** — "would you refer me for X-type work?" A request to be kept in mind. Low-value, easy to ignore, and per DT-02 possibly a bad feature.
- **Push** — "I can't take this; talk to Sarah." A referral attached to a **specific real opportunity**, in the moment the referrer declines it. High-value, time-critical, and per DT-01 this is where the feature actually lives.

`[PENDING — /decompose-architecture]` — whether the referral binds to an opportunity object
(domain 04 / 05) or is freeform depends on the unresolved 03/04 boundary (Q-01 on 03.03.04).

### Happy Path (push — the case that matters)

1. A professional receives an approach they cannot take — a service enquiry (05), a booking (17), a call (03.03.04)
2. Declining offers "refer someone" as a first-class action, in the decline flow itself
3. They pick from people they have evidenced edges with (03.04.01), so the referral is checkable
4. The referred party and the original requester are connected, carrying the referrer's name and the basis
5. The referrer's involvement ends — per D-02 they carry no liability for the outcome

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Referral goes badly — the referred party is unreliable | The referrer's credibility is damaged by someone else's behaviour | D-02: a referral is not a warranty, and must not be displayed as one. If referring is risky, nobody refers |
| Referred party is unaware they were referred | They get an approach with no context | They are told at referral time — a referral is a statement about them and they should know it exists |
| Pull-referral requests pile up on a Producer | The same fatigue mechanism as intros (DT-03 on 03.04.02) | DT-02 — this is the argument for cutting the pull direction |
| Referral used to farm reciprocity ("refer me and I'll refer you") | Corrupts the signal exactly as reciprocal endorsement does (03.01.03 DT-01) | Unresolved — and it is the same defect, appearing a third time in this domain |
| Referrer refers someone they have no edge with | An unevidenced referral is a claim with nothing behind it | Restricted to evidenced edges (D-01) |
| The decline never happens on-platform — they just text Sarah | The realistic default; per personas.md the workaround is "a WhatsApp thread" | The feature only wins if the decline flow is already on-platform, which requires 05/17 adoption first |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | What a domain expert would expect that nobody mentioned: **the referral's moment is the decline** | Reasoning about how music work is actually allocated; personas.md's "portfolio of small income streams" | ✅ CONFIRMED and it reframes the feature entirely. The sweep named this "Referral **Requests**" — the pull direction — but the economically important referral is the push, and it happens at a precise moment: when a booked professional says no. That moment lives in domains 05 (service enquiry), 17 (booking) and 03.03.04 (call) — **not here**. So this feature's real form is a *capability offered inside other domains' decline flows*, which strongly suggests it is a **cross-cut mechanism**, not a standalone feature. It is retained as a node for this breadth pass and flagged as Q-02; the pattern matches the domain's cross-cut test almost exactly. |
| DT-02 | The pull direction ("please refer me") is worth building | The sweep's framing — it is what the candidate was named | ❌ REJECTED for this pass: "keep me in mind for mix work" is an open-to signal (03.03.03) addressed to one person, and it lands on the same over-asked Producer who is already fatigued by intro requests (DT-03 on 03.04.02). It creates a third inbound queue for the platform's most valuable persona while duplicating a signal the matcher already reads. If a musician wants to be referred, the honest mechanism is to be findable with evidence — which is 03.03, not this. |
| DT-03 | A referral is a warranty | The intuitive reading — the referrer is vouching, so surely they are on the hook | ❌ REJECTED → became D-02. If referring carries reputational liability for the referred party's conduct, rational people stop referring, and the platform loses the highest-trust signal it has in exchange for a fairness principle nobody asked for. A referral means "I have worked with this person and they are worth talking to" — a statement about the past, which the graph can evidence. It is not a guarantee about a future engagement the referrer will not be part of. This must be visible in the UI, or referrers will assume the liability exists and behave accordingly. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Referrals are restricted to evidenced edges | An unevidenced referral is a claim with nothing behind it — the same worthlessness the domain exists to escape. |
| D-02 | A referral is not a warranty, and must not be displayed as one | DT-03 — reputational liability makes rational people stop referring, destroying the platform's highest-trust signal. |
| D-03 | The referral's home is the **decline flow** of other domains, not a standalone surface here | DT-01 — the moment is when a booked professional says no, and that moment lives in 05/17/03.03.04. |
| D-04 | The pull direction is not built for now | DT-02 — it duplicates open-to signals and adds a third inbound queue on the most fatigued persona. |
