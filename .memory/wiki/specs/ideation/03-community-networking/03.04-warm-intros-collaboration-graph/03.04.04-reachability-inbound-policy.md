# Feature: Reachability & Inbound Contact Policy

> **Number**: 03.04.04
> **Parent**: [Warm Intros & the Collaboration Graph](./03.04-warm-intros-collaboration-graph-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

> **Deep Think addition** — this feature was **not** in the sweep's candidate list for domain 03. It is the
> collaboration graph's second job, and its absence was the largest gap in this domain's breadth. See DT-01.

## Overview

Who may reach you unasked, expressed as a graph query. "Anyone", "people within 2 hops", "people I have
worked with", "nobody without an intro". This is the graph read as a **permission** rather than a route
(`03.04-warm-intros-collaboration-graph-cx.md#CX-02`), and it governs the messaging cross-cut, connection
requests (03.01.02), and call responses (03.03.04).

It exists because every music-networking product dies the same death: the people worth reaching get flooded
by the people who want to reach them, and they leave. Per personas.md, the Producer is the platform's
provenance capture point — if the flood drives them out, D-18's entire thesis loses its mechanism.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | Sets their inbound wall. Per personas.md they are "not a star" making a living from small income streams — a musician who walls themselves off **loses work**, so their correct setting is permissive, and the product must not encourage them to copy a famous person's posture. Their real need is the opposite of the Producer's. |
| Producer | Config | The persona this feature exists for. Structurally flooded — everyone wants access to the person who runs sessions. Per personas.md they will not leave the DAW to tune settings, so their **default matters more than their control**, and per DT-04 the default cannot be a wall they never chose. |
| Operator | Config | Also flooded (everyone wants the room), but with a crucial asymmetry: per personas.md an unsold Tuesday is "gone forever", so blocking inbound is blocking revenue. Their wall must separate *enquiries* (wanted, and really domain 17's booking flow) from *pitches* (unwanted) — a distinction the other personas do not need. |
| Fan | Config | Sets who may reach **them**. Per personas.md the Fan's anti-persona risk runs outward (harassing artists) but fans also receive unwanted contact, and per D-13c a fan population brings statutory duties including minor safety. A minor's inbound default is a compliance matter, not a preference. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

Policy is a threshold over the graph: reachable if within N hops, or if an evidenced edge exists, or if
introduced (03.04.02), or unconditionally. Per `#CX-02` this makes graph traversal a **hot path on every
inbound contact attempt** — the largest architecture consequence of this domain, routed to `/create-prd-architecture`.

The wall is only tolerable because the intro is a sanctioned door (`#CX-03`). A wall with no door does not
stop contact — it moves contact to Instagram, where per personas.md the platform sees nothing and earns
nothing.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — the policy vocabulary and per-persona defaults are
unresolved and depend on graph density, which does not exist yet (DT-03).

### Happy Path

1. Someone attempts inbound contact — a DM, a connection request, a call response
2. System evaluates the target's policy against the graph path between the parties
3. Permitted → delivered, with the path shown as context ("2 hops via your bassist")
4. Not permitted → the sender is offered the intro route (03.04.02) instead of a dead end

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Cold start — the graph is empty, so nobody is within 2 hops of anyone** | A 2-hop wall makes the platform unusable on day one for everyone | DT-03: reachability **cannot ship before the graph is dense**. This is a hard sequencing constraint with MoSCoW force |
| Sender is walled out | A visible refusal tells a spammer exactly what to fake and confirms the target is reachable-in-principle | Silence to the sender, or an intro offer. Never a detailed reason — the wall must not be a probe for mapping someone's network |
| Target walls themselves off and loses work | Per personas.md the Musician's livelihood depends on being reachable — this feature can actively harm the core persona | Defaults must be persona-aware (DT-04); the product must not present walling-off as sophistication |
| Operator walls off and blocks enquiries | Blocking inbound is blocking revenue on perishable inventory | Enquiries and pitches must be separable — see Role Lens |
| Everyone routes around the wall via an intro request | The exemption becomes the spam channel (`#CX-03`) | Intro requests need their own caps (D-04 on 03.04.02) |
| A minor's inbound policy | Per D-13c, age assurance and minor safety are statutory, scaling with reach | Not a preference — a compliance-set default. Routes to 24 and `/create-prd-security` |
| Veteran with a pre-platform career hits everyone's 2-hop wall | The most experienced user on the platform is the least reachable, and the least able to reach | Compounds parent Q-04. The graph's cold-start defect becomes an access defect |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Contact attempt | Evaluating; must be fast — this is on the message send path |
| Permitted | Path satisfies policy | Contact delivered with path context |
| Blocked | Path fails policy | Sender sees the intro route, not a rejection |
| Error | Traversal failed | **Fail open or closed?** Failing closed silently drops legitimate contact; failing open lets the flood through on every degradation. Unresolved — Q-04 |
| Default | User never configured | The setting that actually governs the platform, per DT-04 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **This feature was missing from the candidate list, and its absence was the domain's largest gap** | Deep Think: what would a domain expert expect that nobody mentioned? | ✅ CONFIRMED — this is the addition this pass exists to find. The sweep enumerated 16 candidates covering how to *find* and *reach* people, and not one covered **who is allowed to reach you**. That is the failure mode that has killed every music-networking product: a producer with 500 unread "check out my beat" messages stops opening the app. Per personas.md the Producer is the provenance capture point — their exit removes D-18's mechanism entirely. The graph already computes the answer for intros; reading it as a permission is nearly free and is arguably the graph's *more* valuable job. Without it, this domain builds a discovery engine pointed at people who will leave because of it. |
| DT-02 | This is a spam setting / anti-abuse concern belonging to domain 24 | The natural classification — it looks like a mute list | ❌ REJECTED → became parent D-05. Domain 24 owns *blocks* (a bilateral, adversarial, after-the-fact severance of a specific person). This is a *product* mechanism: it is the graph deciding, in advance, who has standing — the same computation that powers intros and matching, with a threshold. Blocks are reactive and about individuals; reachability is proactive and about structure. Conflating them would put the graph's second-most-valuable read behind an abuse team's roadmap, and would mean the feature that makes the Producer willing to be present is classified as a moderation tool. |
| DT-03 | Reachability can ship alongside the rest of the domain | Naive sequencing | ❌ REJECTED: it inherits the graph's cold-start emptiness (`#CX-02`). At launch nobody is within 2 hops of anyone, so any graph-based wall blocks everything. Shipping this early makes the platform a ghost town by construction — and worse, it would look like the product working as designed. Reachability is a **density-gated feature**: permissive defaults until the graph is dense, then walls become meaningful. This is a sequencing constraint that MoSCoW must respect, not a tuning detail. |
| DT-04 | Defaults are a minor UX decision | Product-management framing | ❌ REJECTED: per personas.md the Producer will not leave the DAW to configure anything, so **the default is their entire experience of this feature**. And the correct default is persona-*inverted*: the Producer needs a high wall (flooded), the Musician needs a low one (needs work, per personas.md "not a star"), the Operator needs a split one (enquiries yes, pitches no), the Fan needs a compliance-set one (D-13c). One default cannot serve four personas whose needs point in opposite directions — but per personas.md's structural fact, **one human is routinely three of these personas in one day**. So the default cannot even be keyed to a persona. This is genuinely hard and I do not have an answer — recorded as Q-01 rather than fabricated. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Reachability is a product feature, not a spam setting (parent D-05) | DT-02 — it is the graph as permission, and it is what makes the Producer willing to be present at all. |
| D-02 | A walled-out sender is offered the intro route, never a bare rejection | `#CX-03` — a wall with no door moves the conversation to Instagram, costing the platform the provenance and the fee. |
