# Feature: Collaboration Graph Derivation & Path Finding

> **Number**: 03.04.01
> **Parent**: [Warm Intros & the Collaboration Graph](./03.04-warm-intros-collaboration-graph-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The derived graph whose edges are **verified collaborations**, not clicks. When a Producer confirms a credit
naming five contributors (domain 02), those six people acquire evidenced edges to each other — facts about
work that happened, counter-attested by the person who was in the room. Path finding then answers "how am I
connected to this person, and through whom", with the evidence attached. This feature is the domain's
structural core: matching (03.03.02), intros (03.04.02) and reachability (03.04.04) are all consumers of it.

Per D-02 on the parent, this makes **domain 02 a hard upstream dependency** — this feature cannot exist
before credits do.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees their path to someone ("2 hops — via your bassist, who played on 4 records with them"). Per personas.md their pain is re-proving themselves to each new client; a path is the cheapest proof there is. They never write the graph — their credits do that in domain 02, which is precisely the property that makes it trustworthy. |
| Producer | Read-only | Sees paths, and is structurally the **most central node** — per personas.md they run the sessions, so they are the shortest path to most people. Their centrality is earned by work, not by networking, which is the entire point. It also makes them the most-asked broker (DT-03 on 03.04.02). |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees paths to acts and their local density (03.06.02). Their edges come from bookings (domain 17), not credits — which per DT-03 means the graph has **two edge sources with different evidentiary weight**, a fact the sweep did not surface. |
| Fan | None | No access (parent Role Matrix). A browsable industry-wide graph of who works with whom exposed to a consumer population is a stalking aid and a competitive-intelligence gift (parent Q-02). The Fan has no professional use for a path. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

The graph is derived, not authored. Edge sources, in descending evidentiary weight:

| Edge source | Domain | Weight | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmed credit (counter-attested) | 02 | Strongest | A Producer attested it; per personas.md their attestation "is worth more than a self-claim" |
| Settled booking / show | 17 | Strong | Money moved and an event happened — hard to fake |
| Shared project membership | 07 | Medium | Evidenced association, but no work is proven complete |
| Manual connection | 03.01.02 | Weakest / excluded | The "accept?" click the domain overview calls worthless. Included at all? — parent Q-01 |

Path finding returns paths with evidence per hop. Per parent Q-03 the depth limit is unresolved, but a
4-hop path is a Bacon number: technically a route, socially worthless.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — edge weighting, decay (does a 2009 session still connect
you?), and depth limit are unresolved and depend on domain 02's shape.

### Happy Path

1. A Producer confirms a credit in domain 02, naming contributors who confirm their parts
2. Derivation creates evidenced edges among the participants
3. A user views someone's profile or a match result; path finding resolves the connection
4. The path renders with its evidence per hop — enabling an intro request (03.04.02)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Veteran with a pre-platform career** | 20 years of credits that predate WeJammin means 0 edges; the graph rates them below a newcomer with 3 sessions | Parent Q-04 — a serious cold-start defect with no clean answer. Importing claimed history reintroduces unverified claims, which is the thing the graph exists to escape |
| Credit disputed after derivation (domain 02/24) | An edge built on a contested fact | Edge suspends; paths citing it must resolve at request time, not from cache (`03.04-warm-intros-collaboration-graph-cx.md#CX-01`) |
| Path exists but is ancient (one session in 2009) | Technically evidenced, socially meaningless — the broker will not remember them | Decay `[PENDING]`. An undecayed graph makes stale paths look warm, which burns brokers |
| Two people, no path | The common case at cold start, and the case that makes a 2-hop reachability wall unusable early (`#CX-02`) | Honest "no path" — never a fabricated weak one |
| Sybil ring manufactures mutual credits | Fake sessions → fake edges → fake centrality | Counter-attestation raises the cost (you need a colluding Producer), but does not eliminate it. Routes to 24 — see DT-04 |
| Traversal at scale on every message send (`#CX-02`) | 2-hop traversal becomes a hot path, not a batch job | Routed as **not-product** to `/create-prd-architecture` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Path query | Skeleton; per the above this is genuinely expensive |
| Empty | No path | "No connection" — honest, and common early |
| Populated | Path found | Path with per-hop evidence |
| Error | Traversal failed/timed out | Degrade to "unknown", never to "no path" — those are different claims and conflating them is a lie about evidence |
| Partial | Path found but a hop's evidence is disputed | Path shown with the disputed hop flagged |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The verified graph is this domain's moat and is non-copyable | Domain overview; D-18 | ✅ CONFIRMED: no competitor can retroactively manufacture it. Jaxsta can ingest liner notes but cannot produce "who would introduce you to whom", because that requires being present when the work happened (problem-statement.md: "absence at the point of truth"). But the confirmation carries a hard consequence the sweep did not state: **the graph is worth nothing until domain 02 has volume**. Every feature in this sub-domain is downstream of credit adoption. That is a sequencing fact with direct MoSCoW force — shipping intros before credits ships an empty product. |
| DT-02 | The graph should include manual connections at a lower weight | Pragmatism — the verified graph is empty at launch | ⏳ DEFERRED (parent Q-01) — and the tension is genuine, not resolvable by an agent. Verified-only is pure and empty; mixed is useful on day one and quietly reintroduces the click-graph worthlessness the domain overview names as the repeated failure. My inclination is verified-only with manual edges shown *separately* and never blended into a single "connection strength" — but this is a product decision. |
| DT-03 | What a domain expert would expect that nobody mentioned: **the graph has multiple edge sources, not one** | Reasoning across domains 02, 17, 07 and the Operator persona | ✅ CONFIRMED: the sweep framed this as "the credit graph", but the Operator persona has no credits — per personas.md they "sell time and space rather than creative output". A venue that has hosted an act 12 times has an evidenced relationship as real as a session credit, sourced from domain 17. If the graph only reads domain 02, **the entire Operator persona is invisible in it** — no paths to venues, no intros to bookers, and domains 16/17 lose the domain's core mechanism. This is a significant gap: the graph must be multi-source with per-source evidentiary weight, and that changes its derivation contract. |
| DT-04 | Failure mode at the intersection with Trust & Safety: the graph is a sybil target | Adversarial reasoning; personas.md anti-persona ("games the verified-credit graph to manufacture reputation") | ✅ CONFIRMED and it is named in the personas doc as intentional anti-persona behaviour. Counter-attestation means a fake edge needs a colluding Producer — expensive but not impossible, and a ring of five colluding accounts can manufacture a dense, entirely fictional local scene. Because the graph feeds matching, ranking, reachability and intros, a successful sybil attack corrupts *every* consumer at once. This makes graph integrity a domain-24 concern of the highest order, and it argues for detection at derivation (anomalous edge patterns) rather than only at report time. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The graph is derived, never authored | Parent D-02 — derivation from evidence is the whole value; an authorable graph is a click-graph. |
| D-02 | Edges are multi-source with per-source evidentiary weight | DT-03 — a credit-only graph makes the Operator persona invisible and strands domains 16/17. |
| D-03 | Traversal failure degrades to "unknown", never to "no path" | They are different claims. Conflating them misrepresents evidence, which is the one thing this product cannot do. |
| D-04 | Path evidence resolves at request time, not from cache | A disputed credit must not keep vouching for a path (`#CX-01`). |
