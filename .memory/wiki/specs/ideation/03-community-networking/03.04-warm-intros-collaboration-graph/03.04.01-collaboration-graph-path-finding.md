# Feature: Collaboration Graph Derivation & Path Finding

> **Number**: 03.04.01
> **Parent**: [Warm Intros & the Collaboration Graph](./03.04-warm-intros-collaboration-graph-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The derived graph whose edges are **verified collaborations, not clicks**. When a Producer closes a session
in domain 02 and each named contributor confirms their own part, the people who were actually in that
session acquire evidenced edges — facts about work that happened, counter-attested by someone who was in
the room. Path finding then answers exactly one question: **"how am I connected to this person, and through
whom"** — with the evidence for every hop attached and stated in full.

This feature is the sub-domain's structural core: matching (03.03.02), intros (03.04.02) and reachability
(03.04.04) are all consumers of it. Per D-02 on the parent, that makes **domain 02 a hard upstream
dependency** — this feature cannot exist before credits do.

It is also the one feature in domain 03 that carries the platform's central claim directly. Per
`meta/problem-statement.md`, every competitor is a **reconstruction engine** — they ingest liner notes and
self-asserted claims and try to reconcile them. This graph is not a reconstruction: each edge traces to a
specific session, confirmed by a specific human, on a specific date. That property is the entire product,
and it is destroyed by exactly one thing — admitting a single edge that cannot show its evidence. Every
decision below descends from refusing to do that.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Asks "how do I reach this person" and gets a path with its evidence stated in full ("via Dana — you: 3 sessions, most recent Mar 2024; Dana and them: 4 sessions, most recent Nov 2025"). Per personas.md their pain is re-proving themselves to each new client; a cited path is the cheapest proof there is, because it substitutes a checkable fact for a pitch. They **never write the graph** — their credits do that in domain 02, which is precisely the property that makes it trustworthy. Their only write is unilateral edge suppression (D-13): they may render any edge of their own non-traversable, silently and without a reason. |
| Producer | Read-only | Sees paths, and is structurally the **centre of the graph** — not by degree but by topology (DT-06). Because the session owner is the only person guaranteed to have been present with every contributor, nearly every 2-hop path in the graph has a Producer as its middle node. Their centrality is earned by work, not by networking, which is the entire point — and it makes them the broker for almost every intro on the platform (03.04.02 DT-03). Same read-only write model, same suppression control. |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees paths to acts and to the humans who book them. Their edges come from **public, settled performances** (domain 17), attached to their own human node — not to the venue entity (D-09). A promoter who has booked 400 acts is a genuine hub and a genuinely useful broker: in music the local promoter *is* the scene's connective tissue, and this is the single mechanism that makes that true on the platform. Per DT-03 the graph is multi-source precisely so this persona is not invisible in it. Their private studio bookings produce **no** edges (D-07). |
| Fan | None | No access — not to paths, not to a browse surface, not to their own 2-hop neighbourhood. Per parent Q-02 an industry-wide map of who works with whom, exposed to a consumer population, is a stalking aid and a competitive-intelligence gift; per personas.md harassing artists through open contact surfaces is named Fan anti-persona behaviour. The Fan has no professional use for a path — their route to an artist is the follow and domain 20, not a broker. They are not a node source either: a Fan's follows never create edges. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

The graph is **derived, never authored** (D-01). Nothing in domain 03 writes an edge. Three properties
define it, and everything else follows from them:

1. **An edge is a claim about work, made by a human who was there.**
2. **An edge that cannot show its evidence is not an edge** (D-07).
3. **A path is only ever answered from the asker's own node outward** (D-06).

### Edge sources and classes

Edges carry a **source class**. Classes are ordered and **lexicographically dominant over volume and
recency** (D-12): no quantity of Class C edges outranks a single Class A edge, ever. Class is a fact about
how the edge was evidenced, not a tuning knob.

| Edge source | Domain | Class | Citable | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Session attestation** — session owner ↔ each contributor, confirmed at the session | 02 | **A** | Yes, once the credit is public | The strongest fact the platform holds. Per personas.md the Producer's attestation "is worth more than a self-claim", and it was captured while everyone was present rather than reconstructed |
| **Session co-presence** — contributor ↔ contributor, same session | 02 | **A** | Yes | Both were in the room, and the session owner attested both. Requires session-level granularity from 02 — see the fallback below |
| **Retroactive attestation** — a pre-platform credit claimed and confirmed by a named participant | 02 | **B** | Yes | Same counter-attestation mechanism applied to memory instead of the moment. Weaker because it is not capture-at-source — which is the exact defect `problem-statement.md` names in every competitor. **Admitted — Q-01 resolved** (`02.04.02` rung 5 + D-07) |
| **Settled public performance** — act ↔ the human who booked or promoted it | 17 | **B** | Yes | Money moved and the event happened in public — it was advertised and ticketed (domain 19). Hard to fake, and citable without disclosing anything not already public |
| **Confirmed representation** — artist ↔ manager/agent, both parties confirm | 01 | **C** | Yes | Declared and counter-attested by two parties with real commercial stakes. No work is proven, but per DT-07 this is the edge that actually opens doors on the live and licensing side |
| **Released project membership** | 07 | **C** | Yes, once released | Evidenced association; no work proven complete |
| Private studio booking | 17 | — | **No → excluded** | Not citable ⇒ not traversable (D-07). The existence of the booking is the confidential fact |
| Unreleased project membership | 07 | — | **No → excluded until release** | Same. Routing a path through an unreleased project discloses that the project exists and who is on it |
| Release-level co-credit with no shared session | 02 | — | **Excluded** | The string-section fallacy — DT-05. Thirty players credited on one record who were never in a room together do not know each other |
| Manual connection ("accept?") | 03.01.02 | — | **Excluded** | D-10. This is the click-graph the domain overview names as LinkedIn-for-music's repeated failure |
| A successful intro | 03.04.02 | — | **Excluded** | CX `R-01` — an intro proves two people were introduced, not that they collaborated |

**Fallback if domain 02 lacks session granularity**: the graph degrades to **attestation edges only**
(session owner ↔ contributor, Class A). It never degrades to release-level co-credit — that would trade a
missing edge for a false one, and a false edge is unrecoverable. The result of the fallback is a strict
star: every contributor connected only to the Producer. That is a poorer graph but a true one.

### Derivation and freshness

- An edge becomes traversable **within 60 seconds** of the pair's mutual confirmation in domain 02.
- An edge requires attestation by a **second human** (D-14). Where the multi-hyphenate is both session owner
  and contributor, their own contribution is self-attested and produces **no** edge — per personas.md this
  is named Producer anti-persona behaviour ("assigns themselves a larger split and pushes it through").
- Suspension needs no propagation: because path evidence resolves at **request time** (D-04), a disputed
  credit stops vouching for a path the instant domain 02/24 marks it, not 60 seconds later.
- **Edges never decay and are never deleted** (D-11). The fact is permanent — deleting evidenced history is
  the reconstruction failure run in reverse. What changes is **disclosure**: the age of the most recent
  evidenced event is always shown, and the human judges whether the broker will remember.

### Traversal rules

| Rule | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Query root | **Always the asker's own node** (D-06) | Third-party pair queries ("how is A connected to B") are a browse surface with extra steps |
| Max depth | **2 hops** (D-05) | At 2 hops one human knows both ends and can vouch. At 3+ nobody does, and per DT-09 displaying it leaks two relationships between people the asker does not know, for no actionable gain |
| Intermediaries | **Human, claimed, active nodes only** (D-09) | A venue cannot vouch; an unclaimed profile has nobody home. Entities and unclaimed nodes are valid **endpoints**, never brokers |
| Edge admission | **Citable only** (D-07) | If we cannot show why, we cannot use it — using it discloses that it exists |
| Result size | **≤5 candidate paths** | More than five is a list, not a decision. If more exist, the count is stated |
| Ordering | **(source class → shared-event count → recency)**, never a blended score (D-12) | Per domain D-04, a single "connection strength" number overstates what is known and hides which fact produced it |
| Beyond limits | `"No path within intro range"` — **never** `"no connection"` | The first is accurate; the second is a claim about the world that the platform has not checked |
| Suppressed / blocked edges | Non-traversable; indistinguishable from absence | D-13; and per 03.04.04 D-03 a refusal must never be a probe |

### Rate limits (security-first)

User-initiated path queries are capped at **60 distinct targets per rolling hour** and **500 per rolling
day** per account. This is comfortably above genuine use (paging through a search result set) and far below
useful enumeration of a graph with hundreds of thousands of nodes. System-initiated evaluations
(reachability on the message-send path, per `#CX-02`) are internal, are not user-addressable, and are not
capped by this rule. **Exact figures are proposed, not locked** — ratified at `/create-prd-security`.

### Performance requirements (proposed budgets)

| Path | Proposed budget | Why |
|---|---|---|
| User-visible path query (profile, match result) | **P95 < 300 ms** | It renders inside a page the user is already waiting on |
| Reachability evaluation on the message-send path | **P95 < 50 ms** | Per `#CX-02` this runs on every inbound contact attempt; it must not be perceptible in a send |
| Derivation → edge traversable | **< 60 s** | A credit confirmed on the day should path on the day |

Budgets are **product requirements, not engineering commitments** — `/create-prd-compile` ratifies them and
`/create-prd-architecture` decides how they are met.

### Happy Path

1. In domain 02, a Producer closes a session and confirms who did what; each named contributor confirms
   their own part. The Producer's own contribution, if any, is confirmed by a second human.
2. Within 60 seconds of each pair's mutual confirmation, derivation writes a **Class A** edge for that
   pair — attestation edges to the session owner, co-presence edges among contributors who shared the
   session. No edge is written for anyone merely co-credited on the same release.
3. A Musician opens a target's profile, or a match result (03.03.02). An **ego-rooted** path query runs
   from the viewer's own node.
4. Traversal walks outward, **max 2 hops**, human/claimed/active intermediaries only, citable edges only,
   resolving each edge's live status rather than reading a cached path (D-04).
5. Up to 5 candidate paths render, ordered by (source class → shared-event count → recency). Each states
   its evidence in full and in plain language:
   *"Via **Dana Okafor** — you and Dana: 3 sessions together, most recent March 2024. Dana and **Ines
   Brahim**: 4 sessions together, most recent November 2025."*
   No score. No "strong connection" badge. The facts and their dates, and the human decides.
6. The viewer selects a path and opens an intro request (03.04.02). The path is **re-resolved at that
   moment**; if any hop's status changed since step 5, the request fails closed rather than proceeding on a
   path that no longer exists (`#CX-01` synthesis Q5).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Veteran with a 20-year pre-platform career** | Their credits predate WeJammin, so they arrive with 0 edges and the graph rates them below a newcomer with 3 sessions | The graph does not solve this and must not pretend to. **Routed to domain 02 as Q-01**: if 02 admits retroactive attestation, those credits produce **Class B** edges and path evidence must say so verbatim — *"confirmed from memory in 2026"* vs *"confirmed at the session"*. If 02 refuses them, the veteran has 0 edges and that is 02's decision, not the graph's. What the graph will **never** do is ingest a third-party database (Discogs, MusicBrainz, Jaxsta) — that is exactly the reconstruction engine `problem-statement.md` names as the root-cause failure |
| **Sixty people credited on one record** | Naive derivation creates 1,770 edges and asserts that a 30-piece string section knows the rapper. They have never met | No such edges. The unit is the **session**, not the release (DT-05, D-08). Everyone gets a Class A edge to the session owner who was present with them; peers get edges only where they shared a session |
| **Remote async contributor** — uploads a take, speaks to nobody | Post-2020 the room is distributed (`problem-statement.md` "Why Now"). They were in the session asynchronously | An attestation edge to the session owner only. No peer edges. This is correct and is why the graph is a star (DT-06) |
| **A path exists but is 15 years old** — one session in 2009 | Technically evidenced, socially dead; the broker will not remember the requester's name | The edge stands (D-11) and the age is stated: *"1 session together, most recent September 2009."* The system does **not** decide the broker forgot — that is a claim about a human mind that no evidence supports. Per domain D-04, disclose and let the requester judge. The requester will not pick it |
| **A path is 4 records deep but last touched in 2009** | The naive decay model would kill this edge. It is wrong to: in music, "we made four records together" does not expire — that person is in your life | Shown with its full volume and age. This is exactly why decay is disclosure and not weighting (DT-14) |
| **Credit disputed after derivation** (02/24) | An edge built on a contested fact | The edge suspends. Paths citing it re-resolve at request time (D-04, `#CX-01`), so it stops vouching immediately. In-flight intro requests fail closed |
| **Dispute resolved in the claimant's favour** | The fact is restored | Edge reinstates; paths cite it again. No user action, no notification — the graph never announces its own churn |
| **Dispute resolved against the claimant** | The fact was never true | Edge removed permanently. Any intro in flight that cited it fails closed with *"this path no longer resolves"* |
| **Two people, no path** | The common case at cold start | Honest `"No path within intro range"` — never a fabricated weak one, never a 3-hop consolation. Per `#CX-02` this is also what makes a 2-hop reachability wall unusable early, which is 03.04.04's D-04 problem, not a reason to loosen the graph |
| **Brand-new account, zero edges** | Every query returns the empty state | This is the cold start, stated plainly. Per domain D-05 and DT-01 it is a **sequencing fact** — the graph is worth nothing until domain 02 has volume. It is not a defect to paper over with click-edges |
| **A path runs through an unreleased project** (07) | The existence of the project is the confidential fact; citing the edge discloses it, and citing it vaguely discloses it anyway | Excluded until release (D-07). The path renders without it — which may mean no path at all. Correct: a secret we cannot state is not evidence we can offer |
| **A path runs through a private studio booking** (17) | Same class of leak — a venue's booking history is competitive intelligence | Excluded (D-07). The Operator's edges come from **public** performances, which were advertised and ticketed and disclose nothing new |
| **Venue with 400 shows becomes a mega-hub** | If the venue entity were traversable, every act in the city would be 2 hops from every other act via the room they all played. True, and useless — the room does not remember anyone | Entity nodes are **terminals, not intermediaries** (D-09, DT-11). Shared venue is a co-location, not a collaboration, and produces no act↔act edge. The *promoter* who booked both is a human node with real edges to both, and is a genuinely useful broker |
| **Legacy band with a new member** | A drummer joins in 2020; the band has credits from 2011. Naive membership propagation hands them the entire network of a record they were not on | Membership never propagates edges (D-15, DT-12). Membership is date-bounded, and a path through a band node is not possible anyway (D-09). Per personas.md, retroactive network inheritance is the credit-inflation anti-persona with a UI |
| **The multi-hyphenate on one session in two roles** — played bass, also owns the studio | One human, one node, regardless of how many hats they wore that day. Otherwise the graph would place a person 2 hops from themselves | One node. Multiple source-class edges to the same counterparty aggregate and display side by side; they are never summed (D-12). Per personas.md's structural fact, any model that fragments the multi-hyphenate is wrong before it starts |
| **The multi-hyphenate attests their own contribution** | Self-attestation would let one account manufacture a credit and an edge with no second party | No edge (D-14). A second human must confirm |
| **Same pair, two sources** — a credit *and* a public show | Real: you recorded with them and they booked your gig | Both shown, ordered by class: *"3 sessions together (most recent Mar 2024) · 1 show at The Lexington (Aug 2023)."* Never merged into one number |
| **You worked with someone you never want to see again** | The graph would cheerfully route your intro through your abuser, your ex-bandmate, or the person who sued you | **Unilateral edge suppression** (D-13): either party may render the edge non-traversable, silently, reasonlessly, at any time. The other party is never told — telling them is a defamation surface and a retaliation trigger. The credit in domain 02 is untouched: the work happened and the record survives. The edge simply stops being a route |
| **Suppression asymmetry** | If suppression were one-directional, the suppressor could still be asked to broker toward the person they suppressed | Suppression is **edge-scoped**, not direction-scoped: a suppressed edge is non-traversable for every path either party would be asked to act on. A path either party would not walk is not a path |
| **Suppression used to hide a network** | Someone suppresses every edge to look unconnected | Permitted, and cheap to permit. These are their relationships. Per domain D-04, `"no path within intro range"` was never a claim that no relationship exists, so nothing is misstated |
| **The target has blocked you** (24) | A block is authoritative and overrides everything here | Edges hard-removed from traversal. The result is byte-identical to any absent path — `"No path within intro range"`. Revealing the block would make the graph a block-detector |
| **Broker deactivates their account** | A broker who is not there cannot broker; and displaying an absent person's relationships to strangers serves nobody | The node becomes non-traversable within 60 s. Paths through them return the empty state. As a **target** they are still findable — with no path, which is the truth |
| **GDPR erasure** (`constraints.md` — DSAR unresolved) | Erasure must remove the human from the graph immediately | Node and edges non-traversable at once. Whether the underlying credit survives is domain 02/09 and the GDPR cross-cut's decision, not the graph's — but the graph must be able to drop a node without waiting for that answer |
| **Sybil ring manufactures mutual credits** | Fake sessions → fake edges → fake centrality. Because the graph feeds matching, ranking, reachability **and** intros, one successful ring corrupts every consumer simultaneously (DT-04) | Counter-attestation raises the cost — a ring needs a colluding session owner (D-14 means it needs at least two accounts to make one edge). But per DT-06 the star topology makes the ring **legible**: the signature is a single attester with high closed-triad density and near-zero external edges. Detection placement is Q-03 |
| **Path query used to enumerate the graph** | Unlimited pairwise queries are a browse surface, just slower (DT-13) | Ego-rooted only (D-06) — you can only ever learn your own 2-hop neighbourhood, which is your collaborators and their collaborators, reachable only via someone you already have evidenced work with. Plus the rate caps above |
| **Timing side-channel** | If "path found" is measurably slower than "no path", response time becomes an oracle for relationships the caller is not entitled to | Real but architectural. Routed to `/create-prd-security` alongside Q-03 — noted here because it is the one attack that survives every product-level control above |
| **Credit confirmed mid-query** | The result is stale by milliseconds | Acceptable — a missing brand-new edge is a false negative that costs nothing and self-corrects. The reverse (a suspended edge still vouching) is not acceptable, which is why D-04 resolves at request time |
| **Target is an unclaimed profile** (03.06.04) | A credit named someone with no account | They are a valid **endpoint** — you can see you are 1 hop away — but never an intermediary (D-09). No path routes through someone who cannot answer. Routes to the claim flow |
| **Query for a path to yourself** | Degenerate | *"That's you."* No traversal |
| **Traversal times out or the source is unavailable** | The system does not know the answer | `"Unknown"` — **never** `"no path"` (D-03). They are different claims, and conflating them is a lie about evidence in a product whose only asset is not lying about evidence. Consumers must fail closed on `unknown`: 03.04.02 must not broker on it, 03.04.04 must not decide reachability on it |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | Path query issued | Skeleton in the path slot; the rest of the profile renders. Budget P95 < 300 ms; beyond ~1 s the slot resolves to `Error`/unknown rather than spinning — a path is a garnish on a page, never a blocker |
| **Empty** | Zero paths within 2 hops (or the asker has zero edges) | *"No path within intro range."* Plus the honest alternative: a direct approach, if the target's reachability policy permits it (03.04.04). Never a 3-hop consolation, never "you might know…" |
| **Populated** | 1–5 paths found | Ordered candidate list, each with full per-hop evidence and dates. If >5 exist: *"and 4 more."* No score, no strength badge |
| **Error** | Traversal failed or timed out | *"Can't check right now."* This is `unknown`, and it is visibly not `no path` (D-03). Retry offered. Downstream consumers fail closed |
| **Partial** | A path resolved but one hop's evidence is disputed in 02/24 | The path renders with that hop flagged: *"this collaboration is currently disputed."* It is not silently dropped (which would misstate) and not silently used (which would overstate). Intro requests on a disputed path fail closed. **Note**: the *non-citable* variant of this state cannot occur — per D-07 a non-citable edge is never traversed, so no path can contain one |

| DT-07 | **What a music-industry expert would expect that nobody mentioned: the representation layer** | Reasoning about how a booking or a placement actually happens in this industry | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is the largest remaining gap in the graph's coverage. Approaching an artist around their representation is a professional error that gets you remembered badly. personas.md already names agencies and labels as first-class **entities** (domain 01), yet a graph whose only sources are credits, bookings and projects **cannot see representation at all** — because nobody played on anything. That is DT-03's blind spot recurring one level up: a collaboration-only graph makes the entire gatekeeper layer invisible, exactly as a credit-only graph made the Operator invisible. The resolution is that representation is not evidenced by work but it *is* **counter-attested**: the artist declares their manager and the manager confirms, both with real commercial stakes and real cost to lying. That is the same two-sided mechanism, applied to an affiliation instead of a session — admissible as **Class C**, weaker than evidenced collaboration (no work is proven) but categorically stronger than a click (two accountable parties confirmed a specific, checkable, commercially consequential relationship). Whether a representation path should be *preferred* over a collaboration path when both exist is a genuine product decision → **Q-02**. |
| DT-08 | **Citability is a precondition of traversability** | Following DT-03's multi-source confirmation into its privacy consequence | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is a defect the multi-source decision created that nobody caught. A multi-source graph mixes **public** edges (a credit on a released record is a published fact) with **confidential** ones (a studio's booking history is competitive intelligence; an unreleased project's personnel is the secret itself). The obvious mitigation — traverse the private edge but redact its evidence — **does not work**, and seeing why is the point: "you're 2 hops from that A&R via your engineer, relationship undisclosed" still discloses that your engineer knows that A&R, and for an unreleased project the *existence of the relationship* is the confidential fact. Redaction hides the label and leaks the edge. So the rule must be the strong one: **if we cannot show why, we cannot use it**. And it turns out this is not merely a privacy patch — it is the thesis restated. A path whose evidence cannot be shown is not evidence; it is an assertion, and asserting unverifiable relationships is precisely what `problem-statement.md` says every competitor does and this product exists to escape. The privacy rule and the product rule are the same rule. Consequences: 07 edges wait for release; 17 edges come only from public performances (a show was advertised and ticketed — citing it discloses nothing new) — and the Operator persona survives this intact, because a studio's sessions become citable as **credits** once the record ships. |

The obvious argument against depth is the Bacon number: at 4+ hops a dense industry graph is effectively complete, so the answer carries zero information, and an uninformative statement that *implies* a relationship is a domain-D-04 violation. That kills 4+ but not 3. What kills 3 is **disclosure**. At 2 hops the middle node is someone the asker already has evidenced work with, and the second edge is a published credit — the path is a join over facts the asker was already entitled to. At 3 hops the asker learns about **two relationships between people they do not know**, which is pure network intelligence with no actionable use: no single human on that chain knows both ends, so nobody can vouch, so it cannot become an intro (it becomes two intros, the first of which asks a broker to vouch for a stranger). It costs the graph's confidentiality and buys nothing. **Depth is 2. Full stop.** The false-negative worry is handled by wording rather than depth: `"no path within intro range"` is accurate and is not a claim that no connection exists. |
| DT-10 | The graph should carry a "would work with again" signal, so it stops offering brokers who had a terrible session with the requester | Adversarial: the graph's most embarrassing failure mode | ❌ REJECTED, but the underlying problem is real and is answered elsewhere. But adding a sentiment signal is the wrong fix three times over: it is an **endorsement**, which domain D-03 ("evidence beats participation") rejects and which 03.03.02 DT-04 already excluded as a scoring input; it makes the graph a defamation surface; and it is unverifiable — the one thing this graph does not do is carry claims nobody can check. The graph does not need to know why you suppressed an edge, and it must never ask. |

Every one of those paths is true and every one is worthless: the building does not remember you, and "we both played The Lexington" is a **co-location, not a collaboration**. The premise is right (a stale path burns a broker faster than anything else, per 03.04.02's edge cases) and the mechanism is wrong twice. First, the domain fact: **music relationships are episodic, not continuous**, and they do not expire on a curve. Four records together, last one in 2009, means that person is in your life — you would take their call tomorrow. One two-hour session as a hired sub in 2009 means they do not remember your name. Second, and decisive: a decay weight is the system making a claim about the contents of a human's memory. It has no evidence for that claim, and domain D-04 ("never overstate what is known") forbids it. Assistant engineers, techs, session subs and stand-ins are in nearly every session and know nearly everyone — and they are routinely **uncredited on the release**. Every competitor built on ingestion (Jaxsta, and any liner-note or label-submission pipeline) is structurally blind to them: you cannot ingest a name that was never printed. Because this graph derives from the **session** (D-08) and the session owner attests everyone who was actually there (D-14), those people get real Class A edges on day one. So capture-at-source does not merely produce *fresher* data than ingestion — it produces **a categorically different population**, and specifically the sub-population with the highest real-world connectivity per credit.
