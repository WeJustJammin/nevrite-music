## Overview

The domain's structural core. The collaboration graph is **derived from verified credits (domain 02), not
from "accept?" clicks** — which is the domain overview's central insight and the reason warm intros here
are meaningful rather than spam. Every edge in this graph is backed by a session that actually happened
and a Producer who counter-attested it. The graph then does two jobs: it finds the path between two people
who should meet (path finding → warm intro), and it decides who is allowed to reach you unasked
(reachability policy).

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities that only work together. The graph
derivation is worthless without a consumer; path finding is what makes an intro *warm*; intro brokering is
where a path becomes an action; reachability is the same graph read as a permission rather than a route.
Change the derivation rule and all three consumers change.

**Merged from two candidates**: `Warm Introductions & Referral Requests` (05) and `Collaboration Graph &
Verified Warm Intros` (06) were near-duplicates — one named the mechanism, the other the product built on
it. They are one system.

**Deep Think addition**: `Reachability & Inbound Contact Policy` (03.04.04) was **not** in the candidate
list. It is the graph's second and arguably more valuable job, and its absence was the largest gap in this
domain's sweep — see that file's DT-01.
