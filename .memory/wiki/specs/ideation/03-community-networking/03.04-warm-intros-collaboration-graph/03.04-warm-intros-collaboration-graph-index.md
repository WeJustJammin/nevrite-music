## Overview

The domain's structural core. The collaboration graph is **derived from verified credits (domain 02), not
from "accept?" clicks** — which is the domain overview's central insight and the reason warm intros here
are meaningful rather than spam. Every edge in this graph is backed by a session that actually happened
and a Producer who counter-attested it. The graph then does two jobs: it finds the path between two people
who should meet (path finding → warm intro), and it decides who is allowed to reach you unasked
(reachability policy).

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities that only work together. The graph
derivation is worthless without a consumer; path finding is what makes an intro *warm*; intro brokering is
where a path becomes an action; reachability is the same graph read as a permission rather than a route.
Change the derivation rule and all three consumers change.

**Merged from two candidates**: `Warm Introductions & Referral Requests` (05) and `Collaboration Graph &
Verified Warm Intros` (06) were near-duplicates — one named the mechanism, the other the product built on
it. They are one system.

**Deep Think addition**: `Reachability & Inbound Contact Policy` (03.04.04) was **not** in the candidate
list. It is the graph's second and arguably more valuable job, and its absence was the largest gap in this
domain's sweep — see that file's DT-01.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Candidates 05 and 06 merged | Near-duplicates: 06 named the mechanism (verified graph), 05 named the product (intros). Neither is coherent alone — an intro without a verified path is a cold ask, and a graph with no consumer is a table. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | The graph is **derived** from verified credits (domain 02), not authored here | The domain overview's core insight: "a graph derived from verified collaboration is real by construction". This makes domain 02 a **hard upstream dependency** — this sub-domain cannot exist before credits do, which has direct MoSCoW consequences. | Domain overview; D-18 |
| D-03 | The graph-as-read-service is a **cross-cut**; its product surfaces are owned here | Derivation and traversal serve 03 (matching, intros), 04 (casting), 05 (services ranking), 22 (analytics) and 24 (sybil detection). The mechanism spans; the intro/reachability products do not. Recorded in the domain CX and routed to the global CX. | Node Classification Gate, cross-cut test |
| D-04 | Intros are **double opt-in** — the broker consents before the target is ever named to the requester | An intro that exposes the target's willingness before they agree is a cold contact with extra steps, and it burns the broker's relationship rather than spending it. | Deep Think, DT-02 on 03.04.02 |
| D-05 | Reachability policy (03.04.04) is a **product feature**, not a spam setting | It is the graph read as a permission, and it is what makes the Producer and Operator personas willing to be present at all. Without it, the flood makes the platform unusable for exactly the personas whose presence gives it value. | Deep Think, DT-01 on 03.04.04 |
