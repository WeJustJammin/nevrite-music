# Feature: Forums & Craft Q&A

> **Number**: 03.07
> **Parent**: [Community & Networking](./community-networking-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Threaded discussion and question-answering about the craft — "how do I mic a kick drum in a low room",
"what's a fair split for a topline", "has anyone dealt with this PRS form". The differentiator is not the
forum (Gearspace has existed for 20 years and is fine); it is that an answer here can be **sourced to
evidence**: answered by someone with 200 verified tracking credits, not by an anonymous username with a
high post count. That is the domain thesis (D-18) applied to knowledge.

**Classified as a feature, not a sub-domain**: threads, replies, voting and tagging are one capability —
threaded discussion with Q&A affordances. See DT-03 for the promotion condition.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Asks and answers. Per personas.md they hold 3+ simultaneous roles, so they are asking as a drummer in one thread and answering as a mixer in another — and their credibility differs per topic. A single "reputation score" over a multi-hyphenate is meaningless; credibility must be per-craft, which the credit graph already supports and a post count never could. |
| Producer | Full | The persona whose answers are worth the most — per personas.md their attestation carries weight because they were in the room. Also the persona least likely to be here: they are in a DAW, and answering forum questions is unpaid time. The classic forum failure is that the people worth hearing from do not post (DT-02). |
| Operator | Full | Asks and answers on room-shaped topics — acoustics, licensing, insurance, dealing with promoters. Per personas.md they run the business "on the side", so operational Q&A is genuinely useful to them and is a topic band nobody else serves. |
| Fan | Read-only | Reads; does not post. Per personas.md they have no professional stake and no craft — and per D-13a their volume would swamp a professional Q&A by orders of magnitude. A craft forum where fans can post becomes a fan forum within a month, which is domain 20's product, not this one. Reading is genuinely valuable to the hobbyist end of the porous fan/musician boundary. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

Threads with replies, tagged by craft/topic, with Q&A affordances (accepted answers, voting). The novel
part is that authorship carries **evidenced credentials** from the credit graph (domain 02) — an answer
about drum tracking displays that its author has drum tracking credits, which is a fact, not a badge.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — voting mechanics, accepted-answer semantics, and whether
any reputation abstraction exists at all (DT-01) are unresolved.

### Happy Path

1. User asks a question tagged to a craft
2. People with evidenced standing in that craft see it (routed by the graph, not broadcast)
3. Answers arrive, each displaying its author's evidenced credentials in that craft
4. Asker marks an accepted answer; the thread persists as a searchable artefact

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Nobody answers | The default state of a low-traffic forum, and it is worse than no forum — an unanswered question is a visible sign of a dead product | DT-02. This is the feature's real risk, not moderation |
| The best answerer never posts (they are working) | Per personas.md the Producer is in a DAW; forum participation is unpaid | DT-02 — routing beats broadcasting, but does not create time |
| Evidenced credentials become a status game | People chase credits to look authoritative in forums | Credits require counter-attestation (domain 02), which makes this expensive — the same defence as elsewhere |
| Bad advice with strong credentials | Evidence of doing is not evidence of being right; a heavily-credited engineer can be wrong | The credential is context, not authority. Must not be displayed as a correctness signal |
| Fan-adjacent hobbyist wants to ask | The fan/musician boundary is porous (personas.md) — a hobbyist asking a genuine craft question is exactly who this serves | Read-only for Fans is a blunt line here; see Q-02 |
| Thread becomes rights/legal advice ("what's a fair split?") | Users taking legal positions from a forum thread on the platform that holds their actual splits | Real hazard — see Q-03 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This needs a reputation system (post counts, karma, badges) | Every forum has one; it is what "forum" implies | ❌ REJECTED: reputation-by-participation measures **who posts most**, which per personas.md is the inverse of who is worth hearing — the Producer with 400 sessions posts rarely, the enthusiast with 40 posts a week accrues authority. WeJammin already holds a better credential that cannot be farmed by posting: the credit graph. Displaying evidenced credentials per-craft is strictly more informative than a karma number and is immune to the participation-farming that makes forum reputation worthless. This is the same rejection as 03.03.02 DT-04 (endorsements as a scoring input) and 03.02.02 DT-01 (engagement ranking) — **the third time in this domain that a participation metric is rejected in favour of an evidence metric**, which suggests it is a domain-level principle. |
| DT-02 | The risk here is moderation | The obvious concern with any UGC surface | ❌ REJECTED as the *primary* risk: the primary risk is **emptiness**. Per personas.md the people worth hearing from are in DAWs, at loading docks, and on stages — not on forums. A craft Q&A with no answers is a visible advertisement that the platform is dead, and it fails at exactly the cold-start moment the domain is most fragile. Gearspace and r/audioengineering already have the answers and the density; competing with a 20-year-old forum from zero is a hard fight for a feature that is not thesis-critical. The mitigation is routing (put the question in front of the 5 people with evidenced standing, rather than broadcasting to a feed) — but that is a matching problem (03.03), not a forum. |
| DT-03 | This is a feature, not a sub-domain | Node Classification Gate | ✅ CONFIRMED for this pass: threads, replies, votes and tags are one capability with internal parts, not 2+ capabilities that *interact*. **Promotion condition**: if depth reveals that Q&A generates its own credential system interacting with the credit graph (per DT-01 it deliberately does not), or that contests/challenges merge in here, it should be promoted. Recorded per the template's promotion rule so the decision is auditable rather than assumed. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | No participation-based reputation system | DT-01 — it measures who posts, not who knows; the credit graph is a better, unfarmable credential. |
| D-02 | Credentials are displayed as context, never as a correctness signal | Evidence of doing is not evidence of being right, and a platform that implies otherwise is making a claim it cannot support. |
| D-03 | Fans read but do not post | Per D-13a their volume converts a craft forum into a fan forum, which is domain 20's product. |
