# Feature: Endorsements & Skill Vouching

> **Number**: 03.01.03
> **Parent**: [Connections, Follows & Endorsements](./03.01-connections-follows-endorsements-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

A third-party statement that a named person is good at a named thing. Endorsements are the most
compromised primitive in professional networking — LinkedIn's skill endorsements are worthless because
they cost nothing, are reciprocated socially, and require no basis in fact. This feature only earns its
place if it is gated by evidence: per D-03, you may only endorse a skill on someone you have a **verified
collaboration edge** with (a confirmed credit from domain 02). That constraint applies the domain thesis
(D-18) to vouching and is the difference between a signal and noise.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Endorses the craft skills of people they have verified credits with, and receives endorsements per skill. The multi-hyphenate structure matters here: a drummer who also mixes needs endorsements scoped to *drumming* and to *mixing* separately, from different collaborators, because the people who can speak to each are different sets. A single undifferentiated "endorsed 47 times" is useless to them. |
| Producer | Full | The highest-weight endorser — per personas.md their attestation "is worth more than a self-claim, which is precisely what makes counter-attestation valuable". A Producer endorsing the bassist on a session they ran is the strongest form this feature supports, because the credit graph already proves they were in the room. |
| Operator | Config | Can endorse reliability-type attributes (turned up, professional, easy to work with) for people who have worked in their room — evidenced by a booking record (domain 17), not a credit. Cannot endorse craft skills: a venue owner is not positioned to attest to someone's comping ability, and letting them do so is how the vocabulary degrades. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees endorsements on a profile as part of deciding who to follow or book for a private event, but cannot endorse and cannot be endorsed. Per personas.md the Fan has no professional stake; a Fan endorsement carries no information and would swamp the signal by volume alone (D-13a). |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

An endorsement attaches (endorser, endorsee, skill, basis) where **basis** is the evidencing artefact —
the shared credit, or for the Operator the shared booking. The basis is not decoration; it is the
authorization and it is displayed. "Endorsed for tracking drums by the engineer on 4 of their sessions"
is a claim a reader can weigh. "Endorsed 47 times" is not.

Endorsements are per-skill, and skills come from the same vocabulary the credit graph and matchmaking
(03.03) use — inventing a parallel skill taxonomy here would fragment the thing that makes both work.
`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — the vocabulary's owner is unresolved (Q-03).

### Happy Path

1. User views a collaborator's profile; system determines eligibility from the verified collaboration edge
2. User selects a skill to endorse from the skills that person's credits actually evidence
3. System records the endorsement with its basis and notifies the endorsee
4. The endorsement appears on the endorsee's profile grouped by skill, with the endorser's relationship shown

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| No verified collaboration edge exists | Endorsement is not possible | The endorse control is absent; per DT-02 the honest alternative offered is to confirm a credit together first — which converts a vanity action into a provenance action |
| Endorsing a skill the credit does not evidence (endorsing a drummer for mixing on a drum session) | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — either refuse, or allow with weaker basis | Unresolved: strict scoping is defensible but people genuinely know things about collaborators beyond one session's role |
| Relationship severed / credit disputed after endorsement | If the basis is invalidated, the endorsement built on it is unsupported | `[PENDING]` — parent Q-01; a disputed credit (domain 02/24) should arguably suspend endorsements derived from it |
| Reciprocal endorsement within seconds (you endorse me, I endorse you) | Detectable and is the classic inflation pattern | `[PENDING]` — the gate makes this expensive but not impossible between genuine collaborators |
| Endorsee does not want the endorsement (from someone they fell out with) | Endorsee must be able to hide it — a public statement on your profile you cannot remove is a harassment surface | Hide/remove control on the endorsee's side |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Profile opening | Skill groups render after eligibility and endorsement data resolve |
| Empty | No endorsements | Absent section rather than an empty prompt — an "endorse me" beg is the reciprocity spiral's first step |
| Populated | Endorsements exist | Grouped by skill with endorser identity and basis |
| Error | Write failed | Retry; idempotent per (endorser, endorsee, skill) |
| Partial | Endorsement whose basis is under dispute | `[PENDING]` — likely suppressed pending resolution |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Gating endorsement on a verified collaboration edge fixes the LinkedIn failure | Domain overview: "a graph derived from verified collaboration is real by construction" | ✅ CONFIRMED as necessary — an endorsement from someone with a proven shared session is evidence, not flattery. But **not sufficient**: bandmates with 40 shared credits will still reciprocally endorse each other into meaninglessness. The gate raises the cost of fake endorsement; it does nothing about sincere-but-worthless mutual back-scratching between real collaborators. Mitigation unresolved — recorded as Q-01. |
| DT-02 | An expert would ask what this adds over the credit graph itself | Q-04 in the parent index; the domain thesis | ⏳ DEFERRED — genuinely unresolved and it is the right question. Credits prove you *did* the work; endorsements assert you did it *well*. That is a real distinct axis — a credit list cannot distinguish the drummer everyone re-books from the one nobody calls twice. But it is also the axis most vulnerable to social pressure, and domains 05/13/16 already carry transactional reviews which measure the same thing with money attached. Recommend the owner treat this as a candidate cut (Q-04 on the parent). |
| DT-03 | Endorsements should be broadcast to followers | Convention; engagement mechanics | ❌ REJECTED for this pass: broadcasting is what converts a vouch into a social obligation to reciprocate, which is the documented mechanism of LinkedIn's collapse. If it is built at all it should be a profile credential, not a feed event. This directly contradicts `03.01-connections-follows-endorsements-cx.md#CX-03`, which is recorded at Medium confidence for exactly this reason. |
| DT-04 | What breaks at the intersection with Trust & Safety: endorsements are a defamation-adjacent surface | Adversarial reasoning about third-party statements | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real hazard nobody flagged: an endorsement is a *positive* statement so it seems safe, but its absence and its withdrawal are readable as negative statements, and per GDPR an endorsement is personal data about the endorsee authored by a third party — so the endorsee has rights over it that the *author* does not control. This is the same structural problem as the private rolodex notes (03.05.02) and should be solved once. Routes to domain 24 and `/create-prd-security`. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Endorsement requires a verified collaboration edge (D-03 on the parent) | DT-01. Necessary but not sufficient — see Q-01. |
| D-02 | Every endorsement displays its basis | An unbasis'd count is the LinkedIn artefact. The basis is what a reader weighs. |
| D-03 | The Operator endorses reliability attributes only, evidenced by bookings — never craft skills | An Operator has no standing on craft. Unscoped endorsement rights degrade the vocabulary for everyone. |
| D-04 | The endorsee can hide any endorsement on their profile | A third-party public statement you cannot remove is a harassment surface (DT-04). |
