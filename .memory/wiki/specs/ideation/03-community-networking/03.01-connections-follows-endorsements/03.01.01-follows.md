# Feature: Follows (Asymmetric)

> **Number**: 03.01.01
> **Parent**: [Connections, Follows & Endorsements](./03.01-connections-follows-endorsements-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The cheap, unilateral, no-consent edge: I want to know what you do next. A follow requires nothing from
the target and obliges them to nothing, which is what makes it the correct edge for the overwhelming
majority of the graph's volume — every Fan→artist relationship, and every "I track this producer's work"
relationship between professionals who have never met. It is the read set the activity feed fans out to,
and it is the graceful fallback when a professional connection is ineligible or declined (03.01-cx.md#CX-02).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Follows other musicians, producers, bands, studios and venues to track releases, credits and gig announcements. Being followed is a career metric they will read as reach — and per personas.md they are also the *entity* being followed via their band, so their own follower count and their band's are distinct numbers they will compare. |
| Producer | Full | Follows collaborators and studios; their follower list is largely other professionals rather than fans, so follower count is a poor vanity metric for them and a useful supply signal ("who is watching my output for hire signals"). |
| Operator | Full | Follows the acts they book and the local scene; their followers are the musicians and fans who want to know what is on at the room. A venue's follower list is effectively their local demand signal, and is the closest thing this domain has to their marketing channel. |
| Fan | Full | The **only** graph edge available to them (D-02). Follows artists, bands and venues; the follow is the promise that they will be told when that artist does something actionable near them — which is the Fan's entire success criterion per personas.md ("never misses a show by an artist they follow"). |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

A follow is a directed edge from a follower entity to a followed entity, created in one action, revocable
in one action, requiring no approval. It is the primary input to feed fan-out (03.02.01) and to the alert
routing that D-11 makes load-bearing for the Fan persona.

Per D-04, both endpoints are **entities**, not necessarily people: a band follows a venue, a studio follows
a label. The follower count displayed on a profile is therefore a count of entities, and a human who follows
an artist both personally and via their band has produced two edges — which is correct, not a bug.

### Happy Path

1. User views a profile (Musician, band, studio, venue) and selects Follow
2. System creates the edge immediately with no approval step and updates the button to a followed state
3. The followed entity's future feed-eligible events begin fanning out to the follower's feed (03.02.01)
4. The followed entity is notified — quietly, and subject to the volume suppression in DT-02

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Following an entity that has blocked you | Edge creation is refused; the block is a cross-cut owned by domain 24 and is authoritative over this feature | The follow control is absent, and the profile is not reachable at all — a refusal message would confirm the block exists |
| Followed entity is deleted or merged (duplicate band records reconciled in domain 01) | Edges must re-point to the surviving entity rather than dangle | Follow silently persists against the merged entity; no user-visible event |
| Fan follows an artist with no upcoming activity | Edge is created but produces nothing — the cold-start failure that makes the follow feel broken on day one | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — likely resolved by seeding from public gig data (see 03.06.04) |
| Bulk/automated following (a growth-hack bot) | Rate limiting applies; sustained abuse routes to domain 24 | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| Follower count used as a ranking input is inflated by purchased followers | Sybil resistance is a cross-cut to domain 24, not solvable inside this feature | N/A — surfaces as degraded ranking quality elsewhere |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Profile opening | Follow control renders in an indeterminate state until edge state is known — it must not flash "Follow" at someone who already follows |
| Empty | User follows nobody | Feed empty state with a CTA to follow — and per DT-03 this is the moment the product either bootstraps them or loses them |
| Populated | Edges exist | Following/followers lists, feed populated |
| Error | Edge write failed | Optimistic UI reverts with a retry — a follow is idempotent so retry is always safe |
| Partial | Followed entity exists but has produced no events | Profile shows followed state; feed contribution is zero |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Follows and connections can be one edge type with a `mutual` flag | Standard social-graph modelling; most platforms do this | ❌ REJECTED: the consent models differ (unilateral vs bilateral), the Fan is deliberately excluded from one and not the other (D-02), and the abuse profiles are unrelated. A shared table with a status enum forces every read to know which semantics apply, and makes D-02 a runtime check instead of a structural fact. |
| DT-02 | Follow notifications are trivial | Obvious-path assumption | ❌ REJECTED: D-13a states fans outnumber professionals by orders of magnitude. A working musician who signs 200 fans in a week gets 200 notifications of an event they cannot act on. Follow notifications for high-follower entities must aggregate or suppress — this is a product decision forced by the persona mix, not a scaling detail. |
| DT-03 | The follow graph's real problem is discovery, not mechanics | Domain overview: "200 users in one city feels alive, 20,000 scattered globally feels dead" | ✅ CONFIRMED: the mechanic is trivial and the cold start is the whole difficulty. A Fan who follows three artists with no local activity sees an empty product. This is why scene seeding (03.06.04) is a dependency of this feature feeling alive, not a nice-to-have — and why follow suggestions must be geo-weighted from day one. |
| DT-04 | An expert would expect follow to be splittable by content type ("notify me about gigs, not releases") | Bandsintown/Songkick precedent — the Fan's stated need is gigs specifically, not everything | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real expectation: personas.md defines the Fan's success as show attendance, and a Fan following for gig alerts does not want release-note noise. Whether the control lives on the follow edge or in feed/notification preferences (03.02.02) is unresolved — recorded as Q-02. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Follow requires no approval and is revocable unilaterally | Any consent step makes it a connection, which is a different edge with a different purpose (03.01.02). The value of the follow is precisely that it is free. |
| D-02 | Both endpoints are entities, not users | Per personas.md, bands/studios/venues act independently of their members. A user↔user edge cannot express "band follows venue". |
