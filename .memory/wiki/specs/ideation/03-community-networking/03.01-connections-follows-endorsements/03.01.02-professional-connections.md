# Feature: Professional Connections & Request Controls

> **Number**: 03.01.02
> **Parent**: [Connections, Follows & Endorsements](./03.01-connections-follows-endorsements-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The mutual, two-party-consent edge asserting a real working relationship. This is the feature that
LinkedIn-for-music has repeatedly failed at, and the domain overview names why: a graph of "accept?"
clicks is worthless. The design response is that a connection here is **not** the primary evidence of
a relationship — the verified collaboration edge derived from credits (domain 02) is. A connection is
the manual edge for relationships that exist but have no credit yet: the A&R you met at a conference,
the venue booker you have played for, the engineer you are about to work with.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Sends and accepts connections with collaborators, bookers and engineers. Per personas.md their roles are simultaneous — so a single connection may be professionally relevant in three contexts at once (they connect with a producer who is both a hiring client and a bandmate), and the edge must not force a single relationship label. |
| Producer | Full | Connects with the contributors they run sessions for. As the *trust broker* persona, their accepted connections are a higher-signal set than most — which makes their connection list a target for people who want proximity to it. |
| Operator | Full | Connects with acts, promoters and engineers who work the room. Their inbound is dominated by people who want to be booked, making request throttling (below) the feature they will actually feel. |
| Fan | None | The connect affordance is **not rendered** for the Fan persona (D-02) — not disabled, absent. They follow instead (03.01.01). Rationale: a Fan→Musician connection request is a contact vector with no professional content, and per personas.md the Fan "will not tolerate professional-tool complexity" — they do not want this control either. |

> **Access levels**: Full · Config · Read-only · Reports · None

## Behavior

A connection is a bilateral edge: one party requests, the other accepts, and either may sever. Unlike a
follow it carries an implied claim ("we have a working relationship") and unlocks capabilities —
endorsement eligibility (03.01.03, subject to D-03's stricter verified-collaboration gate) and inbound
reachability (03.04.04).

The **request controls** half is not a settings page bolted on; it is the feature's survival mechanism.
The Producer and Operator personas are inbound-flooded by construction — every musician wants access to
the person who books the room or runs the session. Ungoverned, this becomes the "check out my beat" flood
that makes the product unusable for exactly the personas whose presence makes it valuable.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — the specific throttle model (quota per period, graph-distance
tiering, or a request-costs-something model) is a product decision requiring the owner.

### Happy Path

1. User views a profile and selects Connect
2. System checks eligibility (requester is not a Fan per D-02; requester is within the target's inbound policy; no block exists)
3. User adds an optional context note — per DT-02 this is likely mandatory rather than optional
4. Target receives an actionable request showing the requester's verified credits and any shared collaboration path (03.04.01)
5. Target accepts or declines; on accept the mutual edge is created and both parties are notified

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Requester has exhausted their request quota | Request is refused before it reaches the target | A message explaining the quota and when it resets — the limit must be legible or it reads as a bug |
| Target's inbound policy excludes the requester (e.g. no shared path, per 03.04.04) | Request is not delivered | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — silent drop protects the target but wastes the requester's effort; a stated policy is honest but tells a spammer exactly what to fake |
| Request is ignored indefinitely | Pending requests accumulate on the target and become a guilt backlog | Requests expire — `[PENDING]` on the period. Expiry is preferable to an unbounded queue the Producer will simply stop opening |
| Both parties request each other simultaneously | Requests must resolve to one accepted edge, not two pendings that each need accepting | Auto-accept on the mutual case |
| Connection severed after endorsements exchanged | See Q-01 in the parent index — unresolved product decision | `[PENDING]` |
| A verified credit already exists between the two parties | The collaboration edge already exists; a connection adds nothing | Connect control should reflect that they are already connected by evidence — per DT-03 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Profile opening | Indeterminate control until edge and eligibility state resolve |
| Empty | No connections | Empty state with a CTA — likely seeded from existing verified credits (DT-03), so this state should be rare for anyone with any credit history |
| Populated | Edges exist | Connection list; pending inbound and outbound queues |
| Error | Write failed | Retry; request is idempotent per (requester, target) pair |
| Partial | Request sent, awaiting response | Pending state with the option to withdraw |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The connection is this domain's core graph edge | The candidate list's framing, and every social-network precedent | ❌ REJECTED: the domain overview states the opposite — a manually-clicked graph is worthless, and the graph that matters is derived from verified collaboration (domain 02). The connection is demoted to a **supplementary manual edge** for relationships with no credit yet. This reframing is the single most consequential call in this sub-domain: it means connection count must never be displayed as a status metric, or the product recreates the failure it exists to avoid. |
| DT-02 | The context note on a request should be optional, as it is everywhere else | Convention | ❌ REJECTED: an optional note yields no note, which yields a request the target cannot triage, which yields the Producer ignoring the queue entirely. The note is the only triage signal a stranger can offer. Mandatory context is the cheap, standard defence and it is what makes an accept decision possible in three seconds. |
| DT-03 | Connections should be **pre-seeded from verified credits** rather than requested | The domain thesis (D-18) applied to onboarding: the platform already knows who you have worked with | ✅ CONFIRMED: if a Producer confirms a credit naming five contributors (domain 02), those five relationships are evidenced facts. Requiring them to then click "connect" is asking for a worse copy of information the platform already holds. This inverts onboarding: the graph should arrive populated. Open question is whether these auto-edges are the same type as a requested connection or a distinct "collaborated with" edge — see Q-02. |
| DT-04 | An expert would expect connections to carry a relationship *type* ("hired me", "bandmate", "engineer on") | Music's multi-hyphenate structure (personas.md) | ⏳ DEFERRED to Step 5: typed edges are strictly more useful for matchmaking (03.03) and intro brokering (03.04), but per personas.md one pair routinely holds three simultaneous relationships — so it is a set, not a field, and possibly it is just the credit graph again wearing a label. Recorded as Q-03 rather than invented here. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Connections are supplementary to the verified collaboration graph, not the primary edge | DT-01. Prevents the worthless-graph failure the domain overview names. Consequence: connection count is not a displayed status metric. |
| D-02 | Fans have no connect affordance | Parent D-02, derived from personas.md. |
| D-03 | Context note on a request is mandatory | DT-02 — it is the only triage signal available for a stranger's request. |
