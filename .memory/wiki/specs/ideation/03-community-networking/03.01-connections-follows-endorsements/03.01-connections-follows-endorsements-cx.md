## Cross-Cut Details

### CX-01: Professional Connections ↔ Endorsements

**Relationship**: An endorsement is not a free-standing object — it hangs off a relationship. Per D-03,
you may only endorse a skill on someone you have a verified collaboration edge with (a confirmed credit
from domain 02) or, secondarily, a mutual professional connection. This means the connection graph is
read on every endorsement write as an authorization check, and it means the endorsement's displayed
weight carries the relationship's provenance ("endorsed by the engineer on 4 of their sessions").

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician**: both endorser and endorsed; the persona most affected because their multi-hyphenate identity means they collect endorsements across several distinct skills that different collaborators can each speak to.
- **Producer**: the highest-value endorser — per personas.md they are a *trust broker* whose attestation is worth more than a self-claim. An endorsement from the session owner is the strongest form this feature supports.
- **Operator**: can endorse reliability-type attributes of people who have worked in their room, but is not positioned to endorse craft skills. Config-level access.
- **Fan**: no access — cannot endorse, cannot be endorsed. Reading endorsements is part of deciding who to follow.

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: The connection edge is owned by 03.01.02; the endorsement holds a reference, not a copy. If both sides mutate concurrently (A endorses B at the same moment B severs), the endorsement write must re-check eligibility at commit, not at form-render. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` for the exact retraction rule (Q-01).
2. **Trigger chain**: Severing a connection triggers an evaluation of endorsements exchanged under it. Whether that evaluation retracts, hides, or does nothing is Q-01 — an unresolved product decision, not an implementation detail.
3. **Permission intersection**: Yes, and it is the whole point. No connection/collaboration edge → no endorse permission. This is the anti-inflation mechanism.
4. **Notification fan-out**: Endorsing notifies the endorsed party and (per CX-03, unconfirmed) their followers.
5. **State transition conflict**: A and B can race — see (1). The consistency impact is a public statement persisting past the relationship that justified it, which is exactly the Q-01 hazard.

### CX-02: Follows ↔ Professional Connections

**Relationship**: These are alternative edges over the same pair of nodes, and the choice between them
is the product's main defence against the "everyone connects with everyone" collapse. When a connection
is ineligible (Fan requester, per D-02) or declined, the follow is the graceful degradation — the
requester still gets the content they wanted without the target acquiring an obligation. In the other
direction, an existing follow is a weak positive signal that softens connection-request throttling: a
stranger with no edges gets a tighter quota than someone who has followed you for six months.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician / Producer / Operator**: experience both edges and must understand the difference without being taught it; the UI has to make "follow" the low-friction default so that "connect" retains meaning.
- **Fan**: experiences only the follow. Per D-02 the connect affordance is not rendered for them — not greyed out, absent.

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: Both edges can exist simultaneously over the same pair (a connection does not imply a follow, and unfollowing does not sever a connection). Neither owns the other; there is no merge.
2. **Trigger chain**: Accepting a connection MAY auto-create a mutual follow — a convenience most networks implement. If it does, unfollowing later must not silently sever the connection. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`.
3. **Permission intersection**: Yes — follow state is an input to connection-request rate limiting.
4. **Notification fan-out**: A follow notifies the target (quietly, and suppressible at Fan volume — see D-13a load concern). A connection request notifies with an action required.
5. **State transition conflict**: Blocking (a cross-cut to domain 24) must collapse both edges atomically. If a block severs the connection but leaves the follow, the blocked party keeps receiving content — a real harassment-adjacent failure.

### CX-03: Endorsements ↔ Follows

**Relationship**: `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — the open question is whether an
endorsement is a private credential attached to a profile or a broadcast event entering followers' feeds.
Broadcasting drives the reciprocity spiral that destroyed the LinkedIn endorsement's value; not
broadcasting means the feature generates no engagement and may not justify its build cost (Q-04).

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician**: the endorsed party — a broadcast is free promotion, which is precisely why it gets gamed.
- **Fan**: would see endorsements in-feed if broadcast; per personas.md they have no frame to evaluate a craft endorsement, so this is likely feed noise for them.

**Synthesis questions answered**: deferred — Medium confidence entry, per the template's rule that
synthesis questions may defer for non-High entries.
