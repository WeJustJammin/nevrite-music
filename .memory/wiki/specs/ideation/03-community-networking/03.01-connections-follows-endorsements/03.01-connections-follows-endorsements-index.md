## Overview

The edge-creation layer of the domain — the three distinct ways one node in the graph attaches to
another. **Follows** are asymmetric and cheap (a Fan follows a band; a musician tracks a producer's
output). **Connections** are mutual and professional (a claim of working relationship). **Endorsements**
are vouches that attach a skill claim to a specific person's credibility. These are three separate
edge types with three separate abuse profiles, and they interact: an endorsement's weight is a function
of the endorser's relationship to the endorsed, and the follow graph is what the feed reads.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three distinct capabilities that read and constrain each
other. Endorsement eligibility is gated by connection/collaboration state; connection request throttling
is derived from follow-graph position; the feed's fan-out target set is the union of follows and
connections with different weights. Each has its own lifecycle (a follow is unilateral and revocable
in one click; a connection needs two-party consent and has a severance protocol; an endorsement is a
public statement about someone else that persists after the relationship ends).

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Follow, connection and endorsement are **three edge types**, not one relationship with a status field | They have different consent models (unilateral / bilateral / third-party-assertive), different revocation semantics, and different abuse profiles. Collapsing them produces the LinkedIn defect the domain overview explicitly names — a graph of "accept?" clicks. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | Fans get follows but **not** professional connections | Per personas.md, the Fan has no professional stake and "will not tolerate professional-tool complexity". A Fan→Musician connection request is a harassment vector with no product value; the follow already expresses the intent. | personas.md (Fan unique constraint); D-11 |
| D-03 | Endorsement eligibility is **gated by a verified collaboration edge**, not open to anyone | Applies the domain thesis (D-18: provenance is the wedge) to the vouching surface. An endorsement from someone with a verified credit alongside you is evidence; an endorsement from a stranger is noise, and reciprocal endorsement inflation is what made LinkedIn's skill graph worthless. | Deep Think, DT-01 on 03.01.03; domain overview |
| D-04 | Graph edges are **entity-to-entity**, not person-to-person | Per personas.md, "bands, labels, studios, venues and agencies are first-class entities" that act independently of members. A band follows a venue; a studio connects to a label. Modelling edges as user↔user makes this impossible to retrofit. | personas.md structural fact 2; domain 01 |
