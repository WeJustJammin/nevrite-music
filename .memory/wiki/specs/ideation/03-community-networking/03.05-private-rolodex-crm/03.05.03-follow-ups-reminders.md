# Feature: Follow-Ups & Relationship Reminders

> **Number**: 03.05.03
> **Parent**: [Private Industry Rolodex & Contact CRM](./03.05-private-rolodex-crm-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

A private reminder pointed at a person and a time. "Chase the invoice Friday." "She said call back in
March." "Follow up on the festival slot after their tour." In a career assembled from a portfolio of small
income streams (personas.md), the follow-up that never happened is a real, recurring, unglamorous loss —
and per personas.md the current tool is a spreadsheet and a memory.

Reminders fire **only to their author** (`03.05-private-rolodex-crm-cx.md#CX-02`). The subject is never
involved, never notified, and never aware — the sub-domain's inviolable rule (parent D-01).

A reminder to chase is the mundane mechanism behind that pain, and it is the most concretely valuable thing in this sub-domain. |

Per personas.md deposits are "chased manually" and advancing means "chasing riders and tech specs by email" — their entire pre-show workflow is follow-ups. But per DT-02 that workflow belongs in domain 17/18 as structured state, not as private reminders they must remember to set. |
| Fan | None | No access (parent D-03). |

## Behavior

A reminder is (contact, time, reason). It fires to its author through the notifications cross-cut. Per
`#CX-02` reconciliation must carry reminders across when a shadow contact joins — otherwise the follow-up
stops firing at exactly the moment it became actionable.

Per `#CX-03` a reminder and a note may be one object ("said call back in March" is both); unresolved.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — recurrence, snoozing, and whether reminders can be
derived from platform state rather than set by hand (DT-02).

### Happy Path

1. User sets a reminder against a contact, with a reason
2. Reminder is stored privately
3. At the time, it fires **to the author only**, carrying the contact and the reason
4. Author acts, or snoozes, or dismisses

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Contact reconciles between setting and firing | Reminder must re-point atomically or it fires into nothing (`#CX-02`) | Fires correctly against the real account |
| Reminder duplicated by a copy-not-move reconciliation | Two reminders for one person; the user distrusts the feature after seeing this twice | Move, never copy |
| Reminder fires but the underlying thing already resolved (invoice was paid) | Nagging about a closed matter — the failure that trains users to ignore reminders | DT-02: platform-derived reminders would know; hand-set ones cannot |
| Any signal reaches the subject | Catastrophic — parent D-01 | Never happens; there is no path from this feature to the subject |
| User sets 200 reminders and ignores them all | The feature becomes a guilt list, then noise | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| Author deletes their account | Reminders die with them | Correct |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Reminder list opening | Skeleton |
| Empty | No reminders | Empty state |
| Pending | Reminder set, not yet due | Listed by date |
| Due | Time reached | Fires to the author |
| Snoozed | Author deferred | Re-scheduled |
| Done | Author dismissed | Cleared |
| Error | Write failed | Retry |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Reminders are the most concretely valuable feature in this sub-domain | Reasoning from personas.md's stated pains | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is worth noting *why* the ranking is inverted from expectation: the notes (03.05.02) are the feature that looks like the product, but they carry the whole compliance hazard (DT-02 there) and their value is diffuse. The reminders are unglamorous and map directly onto named, concrete losses in personas.md — the unpaid session, the chased deposit, the unconfirmed credit. If this sub-domain is cut to one feature, this is the one. |
| DT-02 | What a domain expert would expect that nobody mentioned: **most of these reminders should not be hand-set at all** | The thesis (D-18) applied — the platform is where the work happens, so it already knows | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it substantially deflates the feature. "Chase the invoice Friday" is a manual reminder about a fact the platform holds: domain 23 knows the invoice is unpaid and 30 days old. "Confirm the credit" is domain 02's own pending state. "Chase the deposit" is domain 17's booking state. Per personas.md, "the design must make the lazy path the correct path" — and a reminder you have to remember to set is the *opposite*: it works only for people who already have the discipline that would make them not need it. The correct product is **derived follow-ups from real platform state**, which mostly belong to the domains holding that state, not to a private reminders app. What remains genuinely here is the residue: follow-ups about things the platform does not know ("she said call back in March"). That residue is real but small. |
| DT-03 | This feature is coherent as part of the rolodex | The parent's framing | ⏳ DEFERRED: DT-02 splits it in two. The **derived** half belongs to 02/17/23 as their own state surfaced to their own users, and is arguably a cross-cut ("pending-action prompts derived from platform state") rather than anything owned here. The **hand-set** half belongs here and is small. If DT-03 on 03.02.01 is adopted — pending confirmations as feed cards — the derived half already has a home, and this feature shrinks further. Recorded as Q-02 rather than resolved. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Reminders fire to the author only, never the subject | Parent D-01 — inviolable. |
| D-02 | Reconciliation moves reminders, never copies them | `#CX-02` — duplicates destroy trust in the feature within two occurrences. |
| D-03 | Derived follow-ups belong to the domains holding the state, not here | DT-02 — a reminder you must remember to set inverts "make the lazy path the correct path". |
