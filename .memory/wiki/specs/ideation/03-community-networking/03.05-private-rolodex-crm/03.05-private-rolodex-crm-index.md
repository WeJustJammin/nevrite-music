## Overview

The private half of the network — the contacts, notes and follow-ups a working professional keeps about
the people in their industry, most of whom are **not on WeJammin**. Per personas.md the current workaround
is "a WhatsApp thread + a spreadsheet"; this is the spreadsheet. It is deliberately private: the public
graph (03.04.01) records what happened, and this records what you *think* about it — "great player, always
late", "pay him fast or he chases", "her partner books the Roundhouse".

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities that interact through one hard problem.
Contact records include off-platform shadow contacts; private annotations hang off those records; follow-ups
fire against them. The interaction that makes it a system is **identity reconciliation** — when a shadow
contact joins WeJammin, the notes and reminders you wrote about a person who did not exist on the platform
must attach to a real account, silently and without ever leaking to them.

**This is also the domain's largest privacy hazard.** Private notes about identifiable real people, authored
by third parties, are personal data under GDPR — and the *subject* has rights over them that the *author*
does not control. See 03.05.02 DT-02.

| D-01 | This is **private by construction** — nothing here is ever visible to the person it describes, or to anyone else | The feature has no value if it is not private (you cannot write "always late" in a surface he can read), and it becomes a weapon if privacy fails.
