# Feature: Band & Member Wanted

> **Number**: 04.06
> **Parent**: [Opportunities & Casting](./opportunities-casting-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

"Bassist wanted, originals, influences X and Y, we rehearse Tuesdays" — and its inverse, a musician
looking for a band to join. It is the one opportunity type where nobody is hiring anybody: both
sides choose, the compensation is usually nothing or negative, the audition is a rehearsal, and the
outcome is not an engagement but **membership of a Band entity** (domain 01). It survived as a
separate node rather than folding into the type taxonomy because those divergences are mechanical,
not cosmetic.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Both sides, and the only persona that really uses this. Posts on behalf of a band (which is an entity they are a member of, not a thing they own — see 01), or answers as someone looking to join. Their decision is not "can I do this job" but "do I want to spend two years of Tuesdays with these people", which no criteria model captures. |
| Producer | Full | Occasionally — a producer joining a project band, or a band seeking a member who also engineers (extremely common, and exactly the multi-hyphenate case `personas.md` centres on). Their credits are relevant evidence; their session rate is not. |
| Operator | None | A venue does not join a band. **Explicit absence**: an Operator's relationship to a band is booking it (17) or hosting its rehearsals (16), never membership. If an Operator is in a band, they are being a Musician in that moment — which is the persona model working as designed. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees member-wanted ads on the public board (04.02.01) if it is public — this is the most Fan-legible opportunity type and the least harmful to expose. **The porous boundary bites here**: `personas.md` says a Fan "may be a hobbyist musician too", and a hobbyist answering a member-wanted ad is the single most likely Fan→Musician conversion on the platform. They do not respond *as a Fan*; they become a Musician. |

## Behavior

Where it diverges from the standard spine, mechanically:

| Dimension | Standard opportunity | Member wanted |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------|
| Who decides | The poster | **Both sides** — mutual selection |
| Compensation | Declared, gated (04.01.03) | Usually none, sometimes negative (you chip in for the rehearsal room). **Legitimately unpaid** per CX-04 of 04.01 |
| The "audition" | A task, media, or nothing | A rehearsal — unpaid, in person, and legitimately so |
| Outcome | Engagement / booking / session | **Membership of a Band entity** (01) |
| Lifecycle | Deadline-bound | Open-ended; runs for months |
| Confidentiality | Rarely matters | **Often critical** — replacing a member who doesn't know yet (04.01.02 DT-02) |

### Happy Path

1. A band (entity, 01) posts a member-wanted ad. The compensation gate passes silently — unpaid is legitimate for this type (04.01 CX-04).
2. Musicians respond with evidence (04.03.02) and, more importantly, taste — influences, what they're looking for.
3. Band shortlists collectively (04.04.02) — this is the type where multi-reviewer is not an option but the entire point.
4. Rehearsal, not audition. Both sides assess.
5. Mutual yes → membership in 01. Not a booking, not an engagement.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Compensation gate fires on an unpaid ad | Would block a legitimate post. Type-scoping prevents it (04.01 CX-04) — this feature is the reason that cross-cut exists. | Nothing; the gate is correctly silent |
| Quiet replacement — the current member doesn't know | Confidentiality, and auto-escalation (04.01.02) would be catastrophic. **The most consequential edge case in the domain.** | See 04.01.02 Q-02 — unresolved |
| Applicant is a Fan / hobbyist | They become a Musician. The persona boundary is porous by design. | Conversion path; not a Fan feature |
| Mutual selection means the applicant rejects the band | The disposition model (04.05.01) assumes the poster decides. **Here the applicant can disposition the poster**, which the spine does not express. | See Q-01 |
| The rehearsal is unpaid work | Legitimately so — and the line between "come and jam" and unpaid labour is genuinely blurry for a band that gigs | See Q-02 |
| Band splits up mid-search | Post orphaned; the entity that posted no longer coheres | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| New member joins — what about splits on existing material? | **The thing nobody asks until it matters.** A new member playing old songs, writing new ones, and the band's existing splits are a fact they've walked into. | See DT-02 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Open | Ad posted | Live, open-ended, no deadline |
| Confidential | Quiet replacement | See 04.01.02 Q-02 |
| Trialling | Rehearsals happening | Both sides assessing — a state the spine has no name for |

| Closed | Filled or abandoned | Dispositions owed (04.05.01) |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Kept as a **separate node**, not a taxonomy entry | Three mechanical divergences from the spine — unpaid legitimacy, mutual selection, membership outcome (DT-01). |
| D-02 | Kept as a **feature, not a sub-domain**, at breadth | Its internal capabilities don't yet demonstrably interact independently of the spine. Flagged for promotion at Step 5 if the trial period and mutual selection develop structure. |
| D-03 | Outcome is **Band entity membership (01)**, never an engagement | `personas.md` models the Band as an entity experienced through personas. This feature must not invent a parallel membership model. |
| D-04 | Operator has **no access** — explicit absence | An Operator in a band is being a Musician. The persona model handles it; a role for Operators here would be a modelling error. |
