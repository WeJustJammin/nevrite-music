# Feature: Open Calls — Festival, Showcase & Competition Submissions

> **Number**: 04.07
> **Parent**: [Opportunities & Casting](./opportunities-casting-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The many-to-few, deadline-batched shape: a festival takes applications until March 1st, reviews
2,000 of them in a batch, and picks 40. It survived as a separate node because its mechanics diverge
from the spine in ways that are structural rather than typed — a submission window rather than a
rolling queue, batch review rather than triage, tiered outcomes rather than won/lost — and because
it is the exact shape that destroyed Sonicbids. The parent index names them as this domain's
cautionary tale, and this is the feature where their temptation lives.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The applicant, usually as or on behalf of a band (01 entity). Their exposure is the industry's oldest tax: applying to 30 festivals at £15 each, hearing back from 4. **This platform charges them nothing to apply** (04.01.03 D-02) — which is the whole reason this feature is worth having rather than ceding to Sonicbids' successors. |
| Producer | Read-only | Rarely applies to open calls; may appear as a credit on a submitting act's material. Their evidence (attestations) strengthens someone else's application (04.03.02). |
| Operator | Full | The poster — a festival, a venue running a showcase, a competition organiser. Their problem is 2,000 submissions and a fortnight, which makes the close-out obligation (04.05.01) simultaneously most needed and most expensive here. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees open calls on the public board (04.02.01) if public — a competition or showcase is public-facing by nature. **Explicitly excluded from voting**: see DT-02. |

## Behavior

Structural divergences from the spine:

| Dimension | Standard opportunity | Open call |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------|
| Submission | Rolling | **Window** — opens, closes, hard deadline |
| Review | Triage as they arrive (04.04.01) | **Batch** — nothing is reviewed until the window shuts |
| Volume | Tens | Thousands |
| Outcome | Won / not selected | **Tiered** — accepted, waitlisted, rejected |
| Fee temptation | Low | **Extreme** — the Sonicbids business model |
| Decision | Fit | Curation — a lineup is a portfolio, not a set of independent picks |

### Happy Path

1. Operator posts an open call with a window and a deadline. Compensation gate fires (04.01.03) — **no submission fee is expressible**.
2. Acts submit within the window, evidence-backed (04.03.02) with their pitchable asset packet.
3. Window closes. Batch review begins — blind if enabled (04.03.04), which is coherent here.
4. Curation: the lineup is assembled as a whole, not picked independently.
5. Tiered dispositions go out — accepted, waitlisted, rejected. All of them (04.05.01).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Operator wants to charge £15 per submission | **Refused — unrepresentable** (04.01.03 D-02). This is the feature where that decision earns its keep. | "WeJammin never charges musicians to apply." |
| 2,000 submissions, close-out obligation applies | Most needed, most expensive. Batch dispositioning is one action for thousands — which actually makes it *cheaper* here than in a rolling queue. | See Q-01 |
| Waitlist never resolves | The tier that rots. An act waitlisted in March finds out in July, or never. | See Q-02 — the waitlist is this feature's maybe-pile (04.04.01 DT-01) |
| Curation means a great act is rejected for genre balance | Correct and invisible. The rejection reason is "the lineup", not "you". | Honest reasons matter more here than anywhere |
| Fan voting on a competition | **Excluded** — see DT-02 | — |
| Deadline passes, act submitted at 23:59 | Window is hard; timezones make it soft in practice | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| Act is accepted → becomes a slot on a bill | Handoff to 17/18 (04.05.03) | The consolidation payoff |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Open | Window running | Deadline countdown; submission count hidden from applicants (it would deter) |
| Closed | Deadline passed | "Under review" — with an expected decision date, because silence for months is the failure |

| Decided | Lineup assembled | Tiered dispositions fire |
| Waitlisted | Tier assigned | See Q-02 — the rot state |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Kept as a **separate node**, not a taxonomy entry | Window/batch/tiered/curation are structural divergences from the rolling spine (DT-01, DT-03). |
| D-02 | **No submission fees** — inherited structurally from 04.01.03 D-02 | This is where that commitment costs money and therefore where it means something (DT-01). |
| D-03 | **No fan voting** | It extracts the artist's own audience as the price of consideration and rewards the act that needs it least (DT-02). Recorded now because the platform holds the fan graph and will be tempted later. |
| D-04 | Expected decision dates are **published** | Months of silence after a deadline is the ghosting this domain exists to end, wearing a festival's clothes. |
