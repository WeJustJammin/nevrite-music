# Feature: Alert Subscriptions & Delivery

> **Number**: 04.02.04
> **Parent**: [Discovery, Matching & Alerts](./04.02-discovery-matching-alerts-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-30

## Overview

The push half of discovery: a standing intent that fires when a matching opportunity publishes. The
parent domain rests its case on this feature — "alerts carry money and dep gigs are won in minutes"
— which makes it the domain's clearest claim on being a daily habit rather than a destination.
Its subscription object is the same one the board saves (CX-01); what this feature owns is the
decision of *whether, when, and how loudly* to interrupt someone.

It owns **policy, not pipe** (D-04): which tier, which suppression, which override, which copy. The
delivery mechanism — channels, tokens, retries, unsubscribe — is a platform cross-cut serving 04,
05, 12, 17, 19, 20, 21 and 24 (DT-03). The reason to be strict about that boundary is D-13: fan-scale
notification volume is a different load and moderation problem, and it must not set the policy for a
channel whose job is to wake a drummer at 11pm for a gig they can actually play.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The highest-value alert consumer on the platform. Holds several simultaneous intents because they hold several simultaneous roles (`personas.md`) — a bass alert and a mixing alert are both live and neither is a mode. Their tolerance for interruption is high *when the alert carries money* and near-zero otherwise, which is the whole design problem. They are also the only persona who reaches **Critical** tier (D-05), and the only one whose quiet hours are calendar-derived rather than clock-derived (D-08) — because they are on stage at 22:00 and asleep at 09:00. |
| Producer | Full | Brief and session-call alerts. Lower urgency, lower volume — a topline brief is not won in minutes, so **Digest** is the right default for them where it would be a failure for a dep. Never reaches Critical: Critical requires material confidence against a setlist (D-05), and a Producer's material is a rough, not a set. Their escalation lever is the clock, not confidence — a brief closing in 48h promotes to Timely (D-06). |
| Operator | Full | Gap-shaped alerts — "an act is seeking a support slot for a night I have open". Their calendar **inverts sign**: for a Musician a booked night suppresses, for an Operator an *empty* night amplifies (D-09). Their perishable inventory (`personas.md`: an unsold Tuesday is gone forever) means their latency budget is set by time-to-the-gap, not by post type — a digest arriving Monday about Tuesday is worthless. Also the persona most likely to be at a loading dock rather than a desk when the alert lands, which is a mobile-surface fact before it is a product one. |
| Fan | None | Fans get artist and show alerts — but from Fanbase (20) and Promotion (21), not from here. A Fan has no opportunity intent and no professional availability. Keeping this boundary sharp matters: D-13 warns fan-scale notification volume is a different load and moderation problem, and it must not enter this pipe. A Fan browsing the public board (04.02.01) can read postings; they cannot save one as an intent, because an intent is a claim to be considered for paid work. |

## Behavior

### The tier is computed, never declared

Urgency is a **computed property of the (post, recipient) pair**, not a field the poster sets. Every
poster believes their gig is urgent; letting them assert it is how the channel dies (D-02). Four
inputs compose:

| Input | Source | Contribution |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| Post type | 04.01.01 taxonomy | Sets the floor tier |
| Time-to-close | The post's deadline or urgency fuse | Promotes as the clock burns (D-06) |
| Material confidence | 04.02.03 | The **only** input that can reach Critical (D-05) |
| Context-raised | 04.01.01 D-02 — post raised from an existing show/session | **Gate** on Critical (D-07) |

### The tier ladder

Intrusion order: `Board-only` < `Digest` < `Standard` < `Timely` < `Critical`.

| Tier | Reached when | Delivery | Latency target | Quiet hours |
|------|-------------|----------|----------------|-------------|
| **Critical** | Post is context-raised (04.01.01 D-02) **AND** material confidence ≥ "played this exact set" or "played ≥60% of these songs in the last 24 months" **AND** time-to-close ≤ 24h | Device push → email fallback at +60s if no device channel acknowledges | P95 ≤ 60s publish → device handoff | **Overridden** (D-08 defines the window) |
| **Timely** | Dep call with unknown/low material confidence; support slot; **or** any post whose time-to-close ≤ 72h | Device push → email fallback at +5 min | P95 ≤ 60s, or queued to the quiet-hours boundary | Respected |
| **Standard** | Session call, crew job, function gig; any post with time-to-close ≤ 14d | Batched push or email per intent preference | Batch flushes every 60 min; P95 ≤ 65 min | Respected |
| **Digest** | Briefs, open calls, member-wanted; any post with time-to-close > 14d | Email + in-app | Daily 10:00 local (default) or weekly Mon 10:00 local; send time calendar-derived where available (D-08) | N/A |
| **Board-only** | User muted the intent, or preference floor set to board-only | Nothing pushed; post appears on the board and in the intent's count | On board load | N/A |

> **The 60% / 24-month thresholds are an initial calibration, not a law.** A dep who knows 15 of 25
> songs can chart the other 10 in an afternoon; below that they are learning a set, not depping.
> 24 months because repertoire decays. Both numbers must be validated against real MDs before the
> Critical tier ships — recorded as Q-05.

### The preference layer — bounded, one-directional-plus-one

Q-01 asked whether tiers are per-type or per-user. **Both** (D-10). The computed tier is a **ceiling**;
per-intent preference modulates *underneath* it:

| Preference on an intent | Effect |
|---|---|
| `Match computed` (default) | Deliver at the computed tier |
| `Quieter` | Clamp one rung down the ladder |
| `Digest only` | Clamp to Digest regardless of computed tier |
| `Board only` (mute) | Never push; the intent still matches and still counts |
| `Louder` | Raise **at most one rung, and never into Critical** |

**Why preference cannot reach Critical**: it is the receiver-side twin of D-02. Critical is *earned*
by material confidence against a real show, not selected. And the deeper reason is that the OS's mute
granularity is coarser than ours (DT-06): a user who opts every open call into push gets 40 pushes a
day, mutes WeJammin at the OS level, and thereby mutes their dep calls too. We cannot let a user's
preference on their quietest intent kill the channel the domain's value rests on.

### Volume caps

| Cap | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Alerts per (user, post) | **2 lifetime** (D-12) | The first is the match. The second is spent on whichever comes first: an eligibility-widening edit (04.01.02) or a deadline escalation (D-06, whose clock is unbound at Q-10). A third would be an edit-loop spam vector. |
| Timely per user | ≤ 5/hour, then batched into one "5 new matches" push | Above 5/hour the user is reading a feed, not receiving an alert |
| Standard per user | 1 batched push per 60 min | The batch *is* the cap |
| Digest per user | 1/day or 1/week per preference | — |
| Critical per user | **Uncapped** — but > 3 in 24h raises an anomaly signal to 24 | Critical is structurally rare by construction (context-raise + confidence + 24h fuse). A user receiving 4 in a day means someone is manufacturing show contexts to buy quiet-hours override. |
| Live intents per account | **20** (D-13) | A real multi-hyphenate holds 3–6 ("bass/London/paid", "mixing/remote", "teaching/local"). 20 is ~4× generous. Above that, an account is mirroring the board (DT-09). |

### Alert copy

- **The push body carries only facts that cannot change**: type, date, fee, distance, and the material
  reason. It **never** carries fill status, because a delivered push is unrecallable and a lock screen
  saying "Dep needed Friday" six hours after the gig filled is a lie the platform cannot retract (D-11).
- **Front-load the money and the date in the first 40 characters** — that is what survives truncation
  on a locked phone. Total body ≤ 150 characters as a design budget.
- **Material copy states only the recipient's own facts** (D-14): "You've played this set with The Hold
  — Mar 2025." Never "you know 18 of these 25 songs" — the count discloses the 7 they *haven't* played,
  which leaks the contents of a setlist they may not be entitled to see.
- **Never state or imply rank** (D-15). "You're our third choice" is what a 6-minute cascade delay says
  out loud; the copy must not say it in words as well.

### Happy Path

1. User saves an intent on the board (CX-01) — the same object, now standing. The save confirms
   explicitly that it is also an alert (04.02.01 D-03), and shows its computed default tier.
2. A post publishes and clears the compensation/spec-work gate (04.01.03). The gate is **pre-delivery**
   by construction: a push cannot be recalled (D-11), so moderation must happen before the pipe.
3. Targeting resolves (04.01.02) → the entitled pool. Targeting is evaluated **before** matching, so an
   alert can never leak the existence of an invite-only post (04.02.02 D-04).
4. Matching scores (04.02.02) → available + reachable survivors. Material confidence scores on the
   survivors only (04.02.03 D-01), if the post is a dep call and a setlist exists.
5. Suppression evaluates per recipient: booked (CX-03) → suppress and **write a trail record** (D-11);
   authored/published the post → suppress; already submitted (04.03) → suppress. Availability
   unreadable → **fail open**, deliver (04.02.02 D-03).
6. Tier computes from type × time-to-close × confidence × context-raise. The per-intent preference
   clamps it (D-10).
7. Dedup on **(user, post)** (D-03) — one human, one alert, even where three of their intents matched
   and one of them belongs to a Band entity they administer.
8. Quiet hours evaluate against the **calendar-derived window** (D-08), not a clock default. Critical
   overrides; every other tier queues to the boundary.
9. Delivery fires on the tier's channel ladder. The board is the standing pull fallback if it fails
   (CX-01 synthesis Q2).
10. The push lands as a **pointer, not a copy of the truth** (D-11). Tapping it resolves current state
    on the landing surface — including "filled 4 minutes ago".

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Musician is booked that night | Suppressed (CX-03) — the mechanism that protects the channel's signal ratio. A trail record is written (D-11). | Nothing pushed. Board shows "Hidden because you were booked (3)" — expandable, retained 90 days. |
| Operator has an empty Tuesday and a support-slot post lands | **No suppression — amplification.** Their calendar inverts sign (D-09): free is the boost, not the silence. | Alert delivered, ranked above posts on nights they're already sold |
| Availability unreadable | **Fail open** — deliver (04.02.02 D-03) | The alert arrives, no trail record |
| Availability read succeeds but is stale — the gig cancelled 10 minutes ago and hasn't synced | Wrongly suppressed. Trail record written. On the next availability sync, replay evaluates (D-11). | Board trail shows it; replay push arrives if the post is still open |
| A gig cancels; a suppressed post is still open and its date is still reachable | **Replay.** Conditions, all required: suppression record ≤ 30 days old **AND** post still open **AND** `post.starts_at ≥ now + computed travel time` (04.02.02 D-02). Tier capped at **Timely** — never Critical: the urgency clock already ran, and a 02:00 wake triggered by a third party's cancellation is an interruption the user cannot connect to any action of their own. | "Your Friday freed up — this dep call is still open." |
| A gig cancels but the post's date is now closer than the recipient's travel time | No replay. Alerting someone to a gig they cannot physically reach is the exact trust failure 04.02.02 D-02 exists to prevent. | Nothing. Still on the board. |
| A whole tour cancels — 40 suppressed alerts become replay-eligible at once | Batched: > 3 replays collapse into **one** notification naming the count. | "Your calendar freed up — 12 open opportunities you were hidden from." |
| One post matches two of a multi-hyphenate's intents | Dedup on **(user, post)**, not (intent, post) — one human, one alert (D-03) | One alert, naming both intents that matched |
| One post matches the recipient personally **and** via a Band entity they administer | Still one alert (D-03), with the entity context named. Dedup key is the human. | "Matches your 'bass/London' intent and The Hold's 'support slots' intent." |
| A Band entity holds the intent | Resolves to the entity's alert delegates (01) and fans out to those humans, then deduplicates per human (DT-04) | Each delegate gets one alert, marked as arriving on the band's behalf |
| A Band entity intent has **no** human delegates | Undeliverable. Not a silent drop — surfaced to the entity's admins on the board. | "The Hold's saved alerts have nobody to send to. Add a recipient." |
| Recipient authored the post | Never alerted. | Nothing |
| Recipient published the post **on behalf of** a Band entity | The publishing actor is never alerted. Other members of that entity holding matching intents **are** — they didn't post it, they're candidates. | Poster: nothing. Bandmates: normal alert. |
| Recipient already submitted to this post (04.03) | Suppressed. An alert about a post you already applied to is pure noise. | Nothing. Their submission status is on the board. |
| Post edited to widen targeting (invite-only → network) | The recipient is **newly eligible** — this is the one legal re-alert. Consumes alert 2 of 2 (D-12). | A normal alert for what is, to them, a new post |
| Post's deadline burns from > 14d into 72h | Tier escalates Digest → Timely (D-06). Consumes alert 2 of 2. **Which clock this reads — the post's date or its decide-by (`04.01.01` D-16) — is Q-10**, and it only diverges on briefs and open calls, which are exactly the Digest-floor types this row escalates. | "Closes in 2 days" — the second and last alert this post will ever send them |
| Deadline escalation already consumed alert 2, then the poster widens targeting | Nothing delivers. The post is on their board. Cap is a cap. | Nothing pushed |
| Poster edit-loops the post to re-alert the pool | Capped at 2 lifetime per (user, post) (D-12). ≥ 3 edit-republish cycles in 24h raises a signal to 24. | Recipients: nothing after the second. Poster: no feedback — telling them the cap exists teaches them to game around it. |
| Post fills before a Standard batch or Digest flushes | Dropped from the batch. A digest of dead links is the fastest way to make a digest unread. | Digest footer: "3 more closed before this digest." |
| Post fills **after** the push was delivered | The push is unrecallable (D-11). It is a pointer; the landing surface owns truth. | Lock screen still says "Dep needed Friday". Tap → "Filled 4 minutes ago." Better than a dead link. |
| Post is reported/removed after a Critical push landed | Cannot recall. Landing surface hard-blocks with the removal reason; all pending deliveries for that post are killed. | "This post was removed." |
| Abusive text in a post title reaching a lock screen | The push body is untrusted user content rendered **outside** the app, on a device the recipient may be showing to someone else. Sanitised and truncated; the 04.01.03 gate is pre-delivery. **Critical has no moderation window** — accepted risk, bounded by D-07: Critical is only reachable from a context-raised post, i.e. from someone who already has a show on the platform. | — |
| Device channel token is invalid/expired | Critical and Timely: email fallback fires (60s / 5 min). Standard and Digest: silent. Board always. | Critical/Timely: an email. Otherwise nothing — which is why the board must exist. |
| OS Focus / Do Not Disturb swallows a Critical | **We cannot override the OS.** Our quiet-hours override is a request, not a guarantee. | Poster is told "delivered to their device" — **never** "notified" (D-16). The distinction is the difference between a true statement and a false one. |
| Recipient is touring across time zones | Quiet hours evaluate against the **device-reported** zone at send; profile zone is the fallback. A musician in Berlin should not get an 08:00-London digest at 09:00 Berlin because their profile says London. | Alerts arrive on local time |
| Recipient is **on stage** 21:00–23:30 when a Critical fires for tomorrow | Calendar-derived quiet (D-08) does not suppress — it **defers to the end of the calendar event + 15 min**. They can't act from the stage anyway. Exception: if `time-to-close` < the deferral, deliver immediately — a fuse that burns during the set is worth the buzz in the pocket. | Alert lands at 23:45, not 21:10 |
| Recipient reads the alert on one of two devices | Deduped across devices; badge and in-app state clear everywhere | One read, everywhere read |
| Recipient mutes an intent | Board-only. The intent still matches and still counts — muting is a delivery preference, not a matching one. | Intent list shows "muted — 4 matches this week" |
| Recipient mutes WeJammin at the OS level because of open-call volume | Their Critical channel dies with it. This is precisely why preference cannot raise into Critical (DT-06) and why Digest is the floor for high-fan-out types. | Nothing. The most expensive silent failure in the feature. |
| Intent deleted between match and delivery | Dropped. The intent is the standing consent; without it there is nothing to deliver against. | Nothing |
| Account deleted / erasure request with queued alerts | Queue drained and dropped; suppression trail purged. | Nothing |
| Recipient creates 500 intents to mirror the board in real time | Cap of 20 live intents (D-13). The 21st is refused with the cap named. Bulk creation (> 5 intents in 60s) signals to 24. | "You can hold 20 saved alerts. Retire one to add another." |
| Recipient holds 20 intents and matches 30 posts/day | The caps are the answer, and they are why the caps exist. Timely batches above 5/h; Standard is 1 batch/60 min; Digest is 1/day. | At most a handful of interruptions, plus one digest |
| A user receives 4 Critical alerts in 24 hours | Anomaly signal to 24 (D-13 rationale). Alerts still deliver — throttling a real emergency to catch a fake one is the wrong trade. | Nothing different. The signal is internal. |
| Material confidence would name songs the recipient hasn't played | Copy states only the recipient's own facts (D-14). The count is permitted **only** if they are entitled to see the setlist. | "You've played this set with The Hold — Mar 2025." Never "18 of 25." |
| Recipient is rung 3 of a cascade (04.04.04) and notices the delay | Their rank is disclosed by **timing**, and this is unfixable — you cannot cascade serially and hide the order (04.04.04 DT-02). Copy never states or implies position (D-15). | A normal alert, 6 minutes later than Alice's. In a small dep scene, Alice and Bob will work it out. See Q-04. |
| Clock skew between poster device and platform on an urgency fuse | Close time is authoritative from the post record, never from a client clock. A fuse that a client can skew is a fuse a poster can extend. | — |
| No device-level channel exists (responsive web only) | **Critical is undeliverable.** It degrades to email, and email at 23:00 wakes nobody. The top tier — the one the whole domain's justification rests on — has no mechanism. See Q-03. | The dep call is missed. Silently. |
| Platform auto-creates an intent for a user from their profile (a tempting cold-start lever) | **Forbidden** (D-17). An alert against a self-declared intent is solicited; an alert against an intent we invented is unsolicited commercial contact to an individual. | Nothing — because it never happens |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Intent list opened | Skeleton list of intent rows |
| Empty — no subscriptions | New user, zero intents | "Save a search to get told when work appears" + a link to the board (04.02.01) |
| Populated — active | ≥ 1 live intent | Intent list, each row showing its computed default tier, its preference clamp, and its match count this week |
| Suppressed | Recipient booked (CX-03) | **Not silent** (D-11). Board shows "Hidden because you were booked (N)" — expandable to the list, visible only to them, retained 90 days |
| Replay-pending | Calendar frees; post still open and reachable | "Your Friday freed up — 3 open opportunities you were hidden from" |
| Batched | > 5 Timely/hour, or a Standard window open | One notification: "5 new matches" — opens the board filtered to them |
| Digest-pending | Digest-tier matches accrued | Nothing until 10:00 local (or the calendar-derived send time) |
| Delivered | Alert fired | Push/email/in-app with the **match reason stated** (04.02.01 D-02) — "you're free, 4 miles, £150, you've played this set" |
| Deferred | Calendar-derived quiet window active, tier < Critical (D-08) | Nothing until the window's boundary; board unaffected |
| Muted | User set an intent to board-only | Intent row: "muted — 4 matches this week". Still matching, still counted. |
| Partial — degraded matching | Availability or material source unreadable | Alert arrives (fail open, 04.02.02 D-03) with a thinner match reason — "£150, 4 miles" and no availability or material claim. **Never a fabricated reason.** |
| Error — undeliverable | No valid channel; or a Band entity intent with no delegates | Critical/Timely: email fallback. Otherwise the board carries it, and the intent row shows "delivery not configured" |
| Cap-refused | 21st live intent attempted | "You can hold 20 saved alerts. Retire one to add another." |
