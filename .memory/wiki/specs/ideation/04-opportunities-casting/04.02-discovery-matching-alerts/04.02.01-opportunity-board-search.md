# Feature: Opportunity Board & Search

> **Number**: 04.02.01
> **Parent**: [Discovery, Matching & Alerts](./04.02-discovery-matching-alerts-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The pull surface — browse, search, filter and save an intent across every published opportunity the
viewer is entitled to see. It is the thing the sweep called a "gig & job board", and it is the
product that Facebook groups and WhatsApp threads currently are: the parent domain names the dep
circuit, the function-band scene and the crew network as "the clearest displacement target in the
corpus". Its defining requirement is that the default view is not "everything" but "things I could
actually do".

Two forces decide whether it survives. **Cold start** is the launch risk — a board's entire value is
other people's supply (DT-01). **Rot** is the steady-state risk and the one that actually killed the
incumbents — every band/musician board in existence dies of ads that were filled and never closed
(DT-05). The board is a consumer of both cures: matching (04.02.02) makes the sparse pool feel
addressed, and the close-out obligation (04.05.01 — the domain's beachhead per domain D-04) is what
keeps the listings true. The board owns neither, and is where both are judged.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The primary consumer surface. Browses ranked by fit, filters by paid-only / instrument / date / radius / remote, saves intents that double as alert subscriptions (CX-01). The multi-hyphenate reality means they hold several intents at once — "bass, London, weekends" and "mixing, remote, any" are both live, and a UI making them pick one identity fails them per `personas.md`. They are also routinely a **poster on the same board** — the bandleader who posts Friday's dep call browses for Saturday's work an hour later — which is why self-match exclusion (D-04) is a Musician-shaped requirement, not a hygiene detail. |
| Producer | Full | Browses briefs, topline calls and session work. Less date-driven, more scope-driven — filters on deliverable type, delivery date and format rather than on a calendar, and **must not be radius-filtered out of remote work** (D-08). Their board is quieter and they check it deliberately rather than reflexively, which makes freshness marking (D-06) matter more for them than latency does: they will read a two-day-old brief, but not a dead one. |
| Operator | Full | Inverted use: browses acts seeking support slots and crew seeking work, to fill *their* gaps. Their board is a supply board viewed from the demand side, which is why the same object serves them at all. Their unit is the **perishable Tuesday** (`personas.md`) — their board query is date-shaped and short-horizon, and they are the persona most likely reading it on a phone at a loading dock, which is what makes the stale-offline state (D-09) a real state and not a courtesy. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees open-rung postings only (04.01.02 open rung) — member-wanted ads (04.06) and open calls (04.07) read as ambient scene activity. **Never sees**: contact detail, compensation (04.01.03 Role Lens), requirement checklists (04.01.04 Role Lens), match reasons, poster response reputation, tombstones, or any targeted / trusted-network / filtered-pool / invite-only post. Cannot save an intent — a Fan has no opportunity intent (CX-01), and an intent is an alert subscription this pipe must not carry (04.02.04 Role Lens). This lens exists because the board may be public for cold-start reasons (Q-01), and if it is, a consumer audience will land on it. |

## Behavior

The board renders one `Opportunity` object (04.01.01) across every type, filtered by entitlement,
ranked by fit, and marked for freshness. Three orderings compose, in this order:

| Layer | Rule | Owner |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Entitlement** | Targeting (04.01.02) resolves *before* anything else. A post the viewer is not on a rung for does not exist on their board — not greyed, not counted, not implied by a result count. | 04.01.02 (D-04 of 04.02.02) |
| **Fit** | Availability + reachability + criteria (04.02.02), plus material confidence where the type fires it (04.02.03). Preferred criteria rank, never exclude (04.01.04 D-02). | 04.02.02 |
| **Urgency decay** | Type-scoped (D-05). A 4-hour dep call and an 8-week festival deadline cannot share one recency curve. | This feature |

### Type-scoped board behaviour (D-05)

Types from the 04.01.01 taxonomy. "Primary sort" is the tiebreaker *within* fit, never a replacement
for it.

| Type | Primary sort | Leaves the board when | Tombstone retained |
|------|-------------|----------------------|--------------------|
| Dep / sub call | Time-to-gig ascending | Filled, or gig start time passes | 24 h |
| Session call | Delivery-date ascending | Filled, or stated delivery date passes | 24 h |
| Function / private hire | Event date ascending | Filled, or event date passes | 24 h |
| Audition / casting call | Deadline ascending | Deadline passes | 7 days |
| Brief / RFP | Deadline ascending | Deadline passes | 7 days |
| Crew / technical labour | Call date ascending | Filled, or call date passes | 24 h |
| Open call — festival / showcase / competition (04.07) | Deadline ascending; escalates presentation in the final 72 h ("Closes in 2 days") | Deadline passes | 14 days |
| Songwriting camp invitation | n/a — invite-only, never reaches the open rung | Withdrawn or accepted | None |
| Support slot | Show date ascending | Filled, or show date passes | 24 h |
| Member wanted (04.06) | Recency, 90-day half-life | Poster closes it, or 90 days elapse | 14 days |

**Freshness marking (D-06)**: any post with no poster activity for a type-scoped interval — 48 h for
dep/session/function/crew/support, 30 days for member-wanted, never for deadline-bearing types (the
deadline *is* the freshness signal) — renders a "Poster hasn't touched this since {date}" marker and
sinks below fresh posts of equal fit. At 2× the interval it auto-tombstones as "Assumed stale — no
response from the poster" and stops accepting submissions.

**Tombstones are content, not litter (D-07)**: a filled or closed post stays visible for the window
above, rendered as a struck-through row with the outcome and elapsed time ("Filled 4 minutes ago",
"Closed — 12 applicants answered"). They do three jobs at once: they stop a post vanishing under a
tapping thumb; they prove the market is alive when the open-post count is low; and they are the only
honest evidence a viewer has that posters here actually close their posts.

### Search

Search is structured-first. Matching runs over typed fields — type, instrument, doubling, date/date
range, location, radius, remote flag, compensation shape, compensation amount + currency, poster
entity — with the free-text title and description as a **lower-weighted** field. This is not a
relevance refinement; it is the defence against keyword stuffing (D-10). A poster who lists every
instrument in the description gains almost nothing, because instrument is a field and the field is
what ranks.

### Filters

| Filter | Behaviour |
|--------|-----------|
| Paid only | Filters on compensation *shape*, never a boolean. Excludes only `unpaid-legitimate` (04.01.03). Door split, door-split-after-costs, guarantee-vs-door, points and buyout all read as paid and are labelled by their own shape — a door split shown as "£150" is the misrepresentation the 04.01.03 gate exists to prevent. |
| Minimum fee | Currency-scoped (D-11). Applies only to posts denominated in the filter's currency. Other-currency posts are **not silently dropped**: they render below the fold under "3 posts in EUR — amounts not compared", with a one-tap indicative-conversion toggle that labels itself indicative. |
| Radius | Travel-time-with-gear, not straight line (04.02.02 D-02). **Never applies to remote-flagged posts** (D-08) — a Producer filtering "within 10 miles" must not lose every remote mix brief. Remote posts are a separate axis, shown labelled. |
| Date / date range | Rendered in the **opportunity's local timezone** with the viewer's offset shown when they differ (D-12). A gig is at a place; musicians tour. "Friday" means the venue's Friday. |
| Instrument / role | Multi-select. Defaults to the intent's, not to the profile's — the multi-hyphenate's board is per-intent. |
| Type | Multi-select over the 04.01.01 taxonomy. |
| Remote | Tri-state: any / remote only / in-person only. |

### Intents

An intent is the saved filter set + the sort. Saving it creates an alert subscription and says so
(D-03, CX-01). Intents are named by the user, default-named from their own contents ("Bass · London ·
Paid · Weekends"). A user may hold many; the board renders them as switchable views. Saving an intent
**field-identical** to an existing one is refused with a pointer to the original rather than creating
a duplicate — duplicates do not double-alert (04.02.04 D-03 dedups on `(user, post)`) but they do
fragment the board into views the user must maintain twice, which is the exact drift CX-01 exists to
prevent.

### Happy Path

1. Musician opens the board. Default view is their most-recently-used intent, or — for a first-time
   user with a profile — an intent seeded from their profile's primary instrument and base location.
2. System resolves entitlement first (04.01.02), then ranks by fit (04.02.02), then applies
   type-scoped decay (D-05). Availability and radius are applied before the user asks (CX-02).
3. Board renders. Each row carries: type badge, compensation shape badge, date in the opportunity's
   local timezone, distance-or-Remote, freshness marker if any, the poster's response-reputation
   signal (04.05.02, D-13), and **one match reason in a sentence** — "You're free · 6 miles ·
   You've played this set twice" (D-02).
4. User narrows by filter. "Paid only" is the filter that makes the board usable at all and is a
   direct consequence of the 04.01.03 compensation gate producing honest data to filter on. The
   result count updates on each filter change; no filter change silently drops a category (D-11).
5. Ranking is **stable for the session** (D-14). New or restacked posts do not reflow under the
   user's thumb; they accumulate behind an explicit "4 new" pill at the top of the list. The user
   taps it when they choose to.
6. User saves the intent. The confirmation states the dual nature explicitly: "Saved. We'll tell you
   the moment work like this appears." (D-03, CX-01.)
7. User opens a post. The board records the fit evaluation's timestamp; the post view re-evaluates
   eligibility on open (domain CX-02) and reconciles state.
8. Post view shows the requirement checklist pre-evaluated against their profile, with their own gaps
   shown honestly (04.01.04), the compensation in its declared shape, and — if the post is
   invite-only and they are on the list — a distinct "You were asked directly" treatment, because
   being named changes the applicant's calculus entirely (D-15).
9. User submits (04.03.01). The board row updates to "Applied" and stays in place; the post moves to
   their pipeline history (04.05.04).
10. The user's other intents are unaffected. Nothing about applying to a bass gig changes their
    mixing board.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Cold start — fewer than 5 open posts match the viewer's intent** | The board **changes shape rather than rendering a short list** (D-16). It states the true count without euphemism, shows the tombstone window (last 14 days of fills/closes for that intent) as proof the market is alive, and converts the failed pull into an explicit push promise. It never pads with near-misses presented as matches, and never fabricates activity. Where the first supply comes from is a go-to-market decision the owner owns — see Q-02 — but the board's behaviour is the same under every answer. | "Nothing open for bass in London this weekend. **3 dep calls were filled here in the last fortnight** — the last one 2 days ago. We'll tell you the moment the next one appears." + saved-intent CTA + "See 14 nearby, outside your radius" as a *named* widening, never auto-applied. |
| **Imported / third-party listings, if the owner's seeding answer (Q-02) involves them** | Provenance is labelled and the native apply path is **disabled** — a listing you cannot actually apply to, presented as native, is the fastest way to teach a musician the board is a lie. The board does not decide whether to import; it decides how an import must render. | "Listed on {source} — apply there. WeJammin can't track this one for you." No match reason, no checklist, no response-reputation signal, no tombstone. |
| **Matching unavailable** | Degrades to an unranked but **populated** board — never to an empty one (CX-02). Entitlement still resolves (a matching failure must never open a gate). | Chronological listing, no "why this matched", banner: "Ranking is unavailable — showing everything you're eligible for, newest first." |
| **Matching partially degraded — material scoring down, geo/availability up** | Partial ranking. Posts that would have carried material confidence rank on the base score and drop their material reason rather than showing a wrong one (04.02.02, CX-04). | Ranked board; the "You've played this set" reason is simply absent. No error — an absent reason is not a lie; a stale one is. |
| **Filters exclude everything** | Named loosening suggestion rather than a bare empty state — mirrors the poster-side pool counter (04.01.04 D-03). The suggestion names the **single** filter with the largest yield, not a generic "try fewer filters". | "0 results. Dropping 'within 10 miles' → 23." |
| **Filters exclude everything and no single filter recovers it** | Falls through to the cold-start shape (D-16) rather than looping loosening suggestions. | "Nothing matches, even loosened. Save this and we'll watch it for you." |
| **Post fills while the user is reading the board** | State change mid-view; for dep calls this is the *normal* case, not an edge one — minutes matter. Row converts to a tombstone **in place**. It does not vanish, does not reflow the list, and does not move. | Struck-through row: "Just filled." Kept visible rather than vanishing under the tap. |
| **Post fills between board render and the user's tap** | Post view opens and states the outcome honestly rather than 404-ing or bouncing them back to the list. The cascade race itself is 04.04.04's to resolve — the board's only duty is not to lie about when it last knew. | "Filled 40 seconds ago — you were 3rd to open it." Post remains readable; no submit control. |
| **Post expires (date passes) between fetch and render** | Auto-close is 04.01.01's transition; the board must never render a `closed` post as open. Renders as tombstone. | "Closed — the date passed." |
| **Post withdrawn by the poster while in the user's result set** | Tombstone, not deletion. Withdrawal is a disposition, not an escape from one (04.01.01 states) — a post that silently disappears is indistinguishable from one you imagined. | "Withdrawn by the poster." |
| **User's own post appears on their own board** | Excluded from candidate view (D-04). A saved intent that matches your own dep call ("bass, London, weekends" vs the bass dep you posted for your own band) is the multi-hyphenate defect in its purest form. Excluded from ranking, from counts, and from alerts. | Their own posts surface in a distinct "Your posts" affordance with applicant counts — the poster view (04.04.01), not the candidate view. |
| **A Band entity the user is a member of posts an opportunity** | Same exclusion, one level out (D-04). Band is an **entity**, not a persona (`personas.md`, D-19) — the entity posted, but a member of that band is not a candidate for their own band's dep call, and being alerted to it reads as the platform not knowing who they are. | The post appears under "Your bands' posts", never in their candidate board. |
| **A user leaves the band after the band posted** | Exclusion lifts at the next fetch — they are now a legitimate candidate. The board does not retroactively re-alert; the post is simply eligible from now on. | Post appears normally on the next refresh. |
| **A post escalates a rung (04.01.02) while the user has the board open** | Escalation can only ever *widen* entitlement, so a post can enter the user's view but never leave it by escalation. Newly-entitled posts accumulate behind the "new" pill (D-14) rather than injecting into the list. | "1 new" pill. On tap, the post appears with an "Opened up to your network" marker — the escalation is information, not noise. |
| **An invite-only post the user is named on** | Appears on their board — their board is *things targeted at them* — and is visually distinct (D-15). It is never on the open board and never on a Fan's. | "You were asked directly" treatment, with the poster's name. The ranked-cascade position (04.01.02 D-01) is **not** shown — telling someone they are 4th choice is a cruelty the platform has no reason to commit. |
| **Criteria tightened between board display and tap** (domain CX-02's named bug class) | The board stamps its fit evaluation with a timestamp. On open, the post view re-evaluates; if the user is now ineligible, the message states that it **changed**, not that they failed. The board lied a moment earlier — the honest response is to say so. | "The poster added a requirement after you saw this: reads notation. That's why you can't apply." — never a bare "You can't apply." |
| **Compensation edited downward while the post is on the board** | Whether the edit re-gates, notifies or voids submissions is 04.01.03's question (Q-02 there), not this feature's. The board's duty is narrower and absolute: it renders state at fetch, and on any change to a post the user has already seen in this session, the row carries an explicit change marker. Silent mutation of a number a user made a decision on is a bait-and-switch even if the poster meant nothing by it. | "Fee changed: £200 → £80" on the row, with the original struck through. |
| **A post the user already applied to** | Stays in place, marked, and is never re-ranked as a fresh opportunity. Re-showing someone a gig they already chased reads as the platform forgetting. | "Applied 3 days ago · Poster hasn't responded" (the response state comes from 04.05.01/04.05.02). |
| **Board scraped in bulk** | Not a product behaviour — routed to `/create-prd-security`. Job boards are scraped aggressively and the whole value of a two-sided market is the pool. Two product facts constrain the answer: contact detail is already gated at the open rung (04.01.02), and the structured fields the board ranks on are exactly the fields worth scraping. | — |
| **A post keyword-stuffs its description to rank on every query** | Structured fields carry the ranking weight; free text is a low-weight field (D-10). Stuffing buys little, so the incentive is weak before enforcement is needed. Residual abuse is 24's. | Nothing — the post simply does not rank where it doesn't belong. |
| **A poster re-posts an identical opportunity to resurface it** | Duplicate *detection* is 04.01.01's open question, not this feature's. The board's own defence is structural and needs no detection: ranking is fit-primary with type-scoped decay (D-05), and recency is only a tiebreaker within fit — so bumping a post gains the poster almost nothing (D-17). | Both posts render if detection doesn't catch it. Neither jumps the ranking. |
| **A post from a user the viewer has blocked** | Never renders, in either direction, and is excluded from result counts. Whether the *poster* is told their invite failed is 04.01.02's open question (leaking a block) — the viewer-side exclusion is unconditional and carries no such hazard. | Nothing. No tombstone, no gap, no count. |
| **A listing whose criteria encode a protected characteristic** ("under 25", "female vocalist") | Casting contains genuine occupational requirements that are indistinguishable in shape from discrimination — a role written for a female vocalist is legitimate; "crew, under 25" is not. The vocabulary is 04.01.04's and adjudication is 24's (DT-08). The board owns two things: a **report affordance one tap from every listing**, and a refusal to ever offer the viewer a filter over other people's protected characteristics. | Report control on every row and in the post view, pre-scoped to the listing. |
| **Network failure or bad signal mid-session** (the soundcheck / loading-dock case) | Last successful fetch remains readable, **stamped with its age** (D-09). The board never presents a stale dep call as open without saying when it last knew. It does not spin, and it does not blank a list the user could still use. | "Showing results from 14 minutes ago — tap to refresh." Rows carry no live state claims. |
| **Refresh fails while a stale board is displayed** | Stale board persists; the age stamp increments. Beyond 60 minutes the board stops asserting fit and drops to titles + dates, because a fit ranking computed an hour ago against a dep circuit is fiction. | "Couldn't refresh. This is over an hour old — dep calls this old are probably gone." |
| **New user with no profile** | Matching has nothing to rank on. The board does **not** render a bad ranked list and call it a match — it filters on what it honestly has (location if given, nothing else) and asks for the minimum that makes ranking real. It never blocks browsing to force profile completion. | Unranked, filtered board + persistent, dismissible: "Tell us your instrument and city and we'll rank these by what you can actually do." |
| **User with a profile but no availability data** | Fails open (04.02.02 D-03) — ranks and shows everything, suppressing nothing. A false positive costs a glance; a false negative costs a gig they never learn existed. | Ranked board; the "You're free" match reason is absent rather than assumed. |
| **A viewer's availability changes mid-session** (a gig cancels, a hold releases) | No live restack (D-14) — this resolves the CX-02 synthesis point that left it at "probably acceptable". It is not acceptable: the board is a list a working musician is reading with intent, and a list that reorders itself while being read is unreadable. | "4 new" pill. Nothing moves until they tap. |
| **Two of the user's own intents match the same post** | The post renders once per intent view (they are separate views), never twice within one view. Alert-side dedup is `(user, post)` (04.02.04 D-03) and is not this feature's. | One row per view. |
| **The board is open on two devices** | No cross-device sync of scroll or ranking. Each fetch is independently stamped (D-09/D-14). A stale tab is a stale board and says so. | The age stamp is the only reconciliation the board owes. |
| **A Fan taps a public dep call** | Sees the headline, not the phone number. Contact gating is 04.01.02's escalation rule; the compensation and checklist omissions are 04.01.03's and 04.01.04's Role Lens rules. | Public-safe rendering: title, act, city, date. No fee, no checklist, no contact, no apply control, no match reason. |
| **A Fan who is also a hobbyist musician wants to apply to a member-wanted ad** | The persona boundary is porous by design (`personas.md`) — this is the likeliest Fan→Musician conversion path (domain Role Matrix, 04.06). The board does not refuse; it routes to the profile step that makes them a candidate. | "Member-wanted ads go to musicians on WeJammin — add your instrument and you can answer this one." |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | Board opened, no cached fetch | Skeleton listing shaped like the intent's last result count — never a spinner. The shape is a promise the fetch is about to keep. |
| **Populated** | Posts matched, fit ranked | Ranked list; each row carries type, compensation shape, local-timezone date, distance-or-Remote, freshness marker, poster response signal, and one match reason (D-02). |
| **Unranked (degraded)** | Matching unavailable (CX-02) | Chronological, entitlement still enforced, no match reasons, explicit banner. Populated, never empty. |
| **Partial** | Some ranking inputs unavailable (material down, or no availability data) | Ranked on what is real; absent reasons are omitted, not guessed. No error surface — an omission is honest, a stale reason is not. |
| **Empty — cold start** | Fewer than 5 open posts match the intent (D-16) | Honest count + 14-day tombstone window as proof of life + saved-intent push promise + one *named* widening option. Never a bare "No results". |
| **Empty — over-filtered** | Filters exclude all, and one filter recovers it | "0 results. Dropping 'within 10 miles' → 23." The specific filter is named. |
| **Empty — unrecoverable** | Filters exclude all, no single filter recovers it | Falls through to the cold-start shape rather than looping suggestions. |
| **Empty — no entitlement** | Every matching post is targeted away from the viewer | Identical to cold start. The board must **never** hint that posts exist which the viewer cannot see — a count of hidden posts is a leak wearing a helpful face (04.01.02, 04.02.02 D-04). |
| **Stale (offline / failed refresh)** | Last fetch older than the refresh interval and refresh failed | Cached board + age stamp. Past 60 minutes, fit claims are dropped and only titles + dates remain (D-09). |
| **Error (fetch failed, no cache)** | First load fails | "Couldn't load the board." + retry. Never an empty state — an error rendered as emptiness teaches the user the market is dead when the market is fine. This distinction is the single most damaging error-state confusion available to this feature. |
| **No-profile** | New user, nothing to rank on | Unranked filtered board + persistent, dismissible ranking prompt. Browsing is never blocked. |
| **Saved-intent** | Intent saved | Explicit confirmation that it is now also an alert (CX-01) — the dual nature must be stated or users will save intents and wonder why nothing arrives. |
| **Intent-duplicate refused** | Save attempted with a field-identical existing intent | "You already have this one — it's called 'Bass · London · Weekends'." Points at the original; creates nothing. |
| **Poster view** | Viewer's own post, or their band's, in the result set | Distinct affordance — "Your posts" / "Your bands' posts" with applicant counts. Never in the candidate list (D-04). |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The default board view is **match-ranked, not chronological** | "Everything" is a listings page; "things I could do" is a product. Chronological is the degraded state, not the default. |
| D-02 | Match reasons are **shown, always**, as one sentence | An unexplained ranking that decides who gets offered paid work is a black box in the wrong place (DT-02). If the reason cannot be stated in a sentence a musician accepts, the ranking is doing something the product shouldn't. |
| D-03 | Saving an intent **creates an alert subscription**, and says so | Per CX-01. Silent duality means users save intents, receive nothing, and conclude alerts are broken. |
| D-04 | The viewer's own posts — **and their bands' posts** — are excluded from their candidate board, ranking and counts | The multi-hyphenate is poster and candidate on one board simultaneously (DT-04). Band is an entity, not a persona (D-19), so exclusion is entity-membership-aware. Alerting a bassist to their own band's bass vacancy proves the platform doesn't know who they are. |
| D-05 | Board decay, sort and tombstone windows are **type-scoped** | A 4-hour dep call and an 8-week festival deadline cannot share a recency curve; one ranking would always be wrong for someone. Mirrors 04.02.04 D-01 (type-scoped urgency) — the same reasoning, on the pull side. |
| D-06 | Listings carry a **freshness marker**, sink on staleness, and **auto-tombstone** at 2× the type's interval | Rot, not emptiness, is what killed every incumbent board (DT-03). The board cannot make posters close their posts — that is 04.05.01's — but it can refuse to present an abandoned post as live. |
| D-07 | Tombstones are **retained and rendered**, not deleted | They stop posts vanishing under a thumb, they prove the market is alive when the pool is thin, and they are the only evidence a viewer has that posters here actually close out. |
| D-08 | The radius filter **never applies to remote-flagged posts** | Geo is near-meaningless for the Producer (04.02.02 Role Lens). A radius silently eating every remote brief is a Musician-shaped default breaking a Producer's board. |
| D-09 | A stale board renders **with its age stamped**, never as a spinner or a blank; past 60 minutes it drops its fit claims | The Operator is at a loading dock and the Musician is at a soundcheck (`personas.md`). A fit ranking computed an hour ago against a dep circuit is fiction, and presenting it as current is the board lying about the only thing it must never lie about. |
| D-10 | Search ranks on **structured fields**; free text is a low-weighted field | Keyword stuffing is the board's cheapest abuse. Making the fields carry the weight removes the incentive before enforcement is needed. |
| D-11 | Minimum-fee filtering is **currency-scoped**, never silently converted | The fee is paid later at an unknown rate, so a post-time conversion is a made-up number attached to someone's income. Other-currency posts are shown flagged and excluded from comparison, never dropped. |
| D-12 | Dates render in the **opportunity's local timezone**, with the viewer's offset shown when they differ | A gig is at a place. Musicians tour. "Friday" means the venue's Friday, and getting this wrong misses gigs. |
| D-13 | Every listing renders the poster's **response reputation** (04.05.02) | It is what makes an applicant spend 20 minutes on a submission, and it is the incentive that makes the close-out obligation self-enforcing rather than merely obligatory (DT-03). |
| D-14 | Ranking is **stable for the session**; changes accumulate behind an explicit "N new" control | This resolves the CX-02 synthesis point left at "probably acceptable". It is not acceptable — a list that reorders itself while being read with intent is unreadable, and the dep-call-vanishing-under-the-tap case is the same defect at higher stakes. |
| D-15 | An invite-only post the viewer is named on renders **distinctly** — but never shows their **position** in the ranked cascade | Being asked directly changes the calculus entirely and must be visible (04.01.02 D-01). Being told you are 4th choice is a cruelty with no product purpose. |
| D-16 | Below 5 matching open posts, the board **changes shape**: true count, tombstone proof-of-life, push promise, one named widening | An empty board teaches the user the product is dead. Padding it with near-misses teaches them it lies, which is worse and permanent. Honesty plus a standing promise is the only offer a sparse board legitimately has. Where the supply comes from remains the owner's (Q-02); this behaviour holds under every answer. |
| D-17 | Ranking is **fit-primary**; recency is a tiebreaker within equal fit | Re-posting to resurface is the dominant strategy on every job board. Making bumping worthless is a structural defence that works whether or not duplicate detection (04.01.01) ever ships. |
| D-18 | The board renders **declared compensation verbatim** and never derives, aggregates or badges a market rate | The gate's honest data makes the board a price signal whether it means to or not (DT-06). A platform-computed rate estimate at the moment of a wage decision is the platform's thumb on a negotiation between two of its own users. Benchmarking is 23's, with owner consent, never an accident. |
| D-19 | The board is **finite and exhaustible** — no infinite scroll, no engagement ranking, no re-injection of seen posts | "Have I seen everything for Friday?" must be answerable (DT-10). A board you cannot finish cannot be trusted to have been read, and hiding the bottom is a lie the user detects on their second visit. |
| D-20 | Every listing carries a **report affordance**, and the board never offers a filter over other people's protected characteristics | The board is the publication surface for discriminatory criteria, so it must be the reporting surface (DT-08). Offering the filter would make the platform the instrument rather than the venue. Adjudication is 24's. |
