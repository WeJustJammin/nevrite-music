# Feature: Availability & Geo-Aware Matching

> **Number**: 04.02.02
> **Parent**: [Discovery, Matching & Alerts](./04.02-discovery-matching-alerts-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The base matching engine: given a published opportunity, who could actually do it — free on the
date, close enough to get there with their gear, and satisfying the post's criteria. It is
deliberately the unglamorous half of matching, and it is the half that works on day one. Its output
ranks the board (CX-02) and gates the alert pipe (CX-03), so its quality sets the ceiling on both.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Matched on their own availability and travel radius. Their radius is not a circle — it is travel time with a drum kit at 6pm on a Friday, and a straight-line filter will offer them gigs they physically cannot reach. As a poster they see the matched pool size while setting criteria (04.01.04). |
| Producer | Full | Geo barely applies — remote work has no radius. Their real constraint is **delivery capacity**: how many sessions they can turn around by a date. Matching them on location is close to meaningless; matching them on calendar load is the actual analogue. |
| Operator | Full | Inverted. They have a fixed location and match *others* against it; their "availability" is the room's, not theirs, and it is the perishable inventory from `personas.md` — an unsold Tuesday is gone forever. Their matching question is "who can fill this gap", not "what can I do". |
| Fan | None | Not matched against opportunities and never in a matched pool. A Fan has no professional availability the platform should reason about, and inferring one from their gig attendance would be surveillance wearing a feature's clothes. |

## Behavior

Three inputs compose into one score:

| Input | Source | Trust |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Availability | The availability cross-cut (D-02 of parent) — not owned here | Read-only, can be stale |
| Reachability | Travel time + gear burden + the post's location | Computed |
| Criteria satisfaction | 04.01.04 | Mixed (three tiers) |

### Happy Path

1. Post publishes (post-gate).
2. Engine resolves the entitled pool first — targeting (04.01.02) before matching, so an invite-only post never leaks via a match.
3. For each candidate: availability check → reachability check → criteria evaluation.
4. Score composes; "preferred" criteria rank rather than exclude (04.01.04 D-02).
5. Output feeds board ranking (CX-02) and alert gating (CX-03).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Availability data is stale or unreadable | **Fail open** — deliver/rank anyway (CX-03). A false positive costs a glance; a false negative costs a gig the user never learns existed. | Nothing — the alert simply arrives |
| Candidate is free but 300 miles away | Excluded by reachability, not availability | Poster: pool size reflects it; candidate: never sees the post |
| Candidate is free, close, but has a 4-hour gap between two gigs on the same day | The interesting case — technically available, practically not. Gear load-in/out and travel eat the gap. | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — gap-feasibility is real and unmodelled |
| Producer matched on geo | Near-meaningless. Their constraint is delivery capacity, not distance. | `[PENDING]` — see Q-02 |
| Opportunity is remote | Reachability is a no-op; the whole geo half of this feature disappears | Wider pool, no distance shown |
| Candidate's availability is private and they don't want a poster inferring it | Matching reads it; the poster must never see *why* someone ranked low (CX-02 synthesis Q3) | Poster sees rank, never the calendar |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Matching | Post published | Poster: "finding people…" |
| Matched | Pool resolved | Ranked pool with reasons (04.02.01 D-02) |
| Empty pool | Nobody available/reachable/eligible | Named cause — availability, distance, or criteria — because the three imply completely different fixes |
| Degraded | Availability source unreadable | Fail open; unranked or partially-ranked pool |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Availability is not this domain's property.** It is a calendar fact serving Services (05), Education (06), Venues (16), Live Booking (17), Show Production (18) and Career/Finance (23) — and the multi-hyphenate from `personas.md` is "booked" for reasons originating in all of them at once | Node Classification Gate cross-cut test; the persona's defining trait is simultaneous roles | ✅ CONFIRMED — routed to the global CX as a mechanism, not built here. This is also the double-booking prevention every one of those domains independently needs. Owning it here would make Venues depend on Opportunities to know if a room is free, which is absurd. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would reject a **radius** as the geo model. The real constraint is door-to-door travel time *with the gear the post requires*, and it is asymmetric — a drummer's 30 miles and a singer's 30 miles are different distances | Domain reasoning; a straight-line radius offers gigs that cannot be physically reached, which is the fastest way to make matching untrustworthy | ✅ CONFIRMED as the correct model. ⚠️ Partially deferred — the gear-burden input depends on Gear Registry (15) knowing what the musician actually hauls, which is a dependency the sweep did not connect. Without 15 this degrades to travel time with an assumed load. Logged as Q-01. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Availability is **consumed, not owned** — routed to the global CX | It serves eight domains and is the double-booking mechanism all of them need (DT-01). |
| D-02 | Geo is **travel time with gear burden**, not radius | A radius offers unreachable gigs and destroys trust in matching (DT-02). |
| D-03 | Availability failures **fail open** | A suppressed alert is a silent miss with a money cost and no error surface; a spurious alert costs a glance. |
| D-04 | Targeting is resolved **before** matching | Otherwise an alert or a rank leaks the existence of an invite-only post. |
