# Feature: Won-Opportunity Handoff

> **Number**: 04.05.03
> **Parent**: [Outcome, Response & Handoff](./04.05-outcome-response-handoff-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The moment an opportunity stops being one and becomes real work. The parent index states it
outright: "a won Opportunity CREATES a Services engagement" — and it generalises. A won dep call
puts a player on a stage that already has a lineup; a won brief creates an engagement with a fee;
a won session creates a contributor on a project. This is where the 24-domain consolidation bet
either pays off or is revealed as 24 products sharing a login.

It is not a screen. It is a **transfer of facts across a domain boundary**, executed once, in the
seconds after an acceptance, on behalf of people who will never think about it — and its entire
quality bar is that nobody notices it happened. If a winner has to re-enter one fact they already
gave the platform when they applied, the handoff failed even if every write succeeded.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full — both sides | **As winner**: their won dep call lands as a gig on the calendar, as a line on the show's call sheet (18), with the fee as a receivable (23) and — the part that matters most — as a pre-filled credit waiting to be confirmed the morning after the show (02). They never re-type a fact they gave at application. They see the show's call time, address, set length, and *their own* fee — not the band's settlement, not the other players' fees (DT-06). **As poster**: they see who is now on Saturday and, in a dep, **who came off** (DT-04). If two people were accidentally handed the same chair, they see both and they fix it themselves — the platform will not choose which promise to break. |
| Producer | Full — both sides | **As poster**: their won brief becomes an engagement (05) or a contributor on a project (07) with scope, deliverable and fee carried across intact. Their persona's whole pain is reconstructing facts later; a handoff that drops the fee or the contributor list recreates it on day one. **As winner**: same scoped landing — a session player joining a project sees the material, the session date and their fee, not the project's other splits. The handoff is also where a provisional split is one tap and free (DT-02 / Q-02), and where the intended cast is snapshotted while it is still known rather than remembered (DT-07). |
| Operator | Full | Their filled slot becomes a booking (17) against perishable inventory, and the deposit question arrives immediately. Their double-booking pain (`personas.md`) is fixed at acceptance, not here — the handoff **never re-checks availability and never vetoes** (DT-10). What they get here is the inverse of their current life: an act that won a support slot appears on the advance with contact details, tech needs and a stage time, without an email thread. When their winner cancels, the **slot** reopens (they sold time and space; the buyer left) — unlike a dep cancel, where the show stays and only the chair empties. |
| Fan | None | Never applies, never wins, never handed off to. A Fan may eventually *see* a downstream consequence — the show that a won open call created goes on sale (19) — but that is 19's flow reading 17's booking, not this handoff's surface. Explicitly: the handoff must not leak an unannounced lineup to the public. A dep landing on Saturday's call sheet is not an announcement. |

## Behavior

### 1. The handoff has three modes, and the mode is fixed at post time

The parent index's phrase "a won Opportunity **creates** a Services engagement" is true of only one
of the three. The others do not create anything.

| Mode | What it does | When |
|------|--------------|------|
| **Create** | A downstream entity that did not exist comes into being | The opportunity has no target reference — a function gig, a standalone brief |
| **Join** | An existing downstream entity gains a person | The opportunity carries a target reference — a dep call posted *from* Show #123, a support slot on a bill that already exists |
| **Propose** | A governance action is *proposed* to the entity that owns it, never executed | A member-wanted win, which would mutate a Band's membership (DT-11) |

**The mode is determined by whether the opportunity carries a target reference, and that reference
must exist at post time — never at win time.** Resolving the target at win time means asking the
poster "which show is this for?" after someone has already accepted, and asking is re-entry, which
D-01 forbids. This is an upstream requirement this feature imposes on 04.01.01 (DT-05).

### 2. Where a won opportunity goes, by type

| Won type | Becomes | Mode | In domain | Cast resolution |
|----------|---------|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Dep / sub call | A **substitution** on an existing show's lineup + a gig on the winner's calendar | Join | 17, 18 | Names who came **off** (DT-04) |
| Session call | A contributor on a project | Join (or Create if no project exists) | 07 | Winner named directly |
| Brief / RFP | An engagement with scope + fee | Create | 05 | Winner named directly |
| Function gig | A booking | Create | 17 | Winner named directly; if a Band won, unresolved (DT-09) |
| Crew job | An engagement **and** a call sheet entry — two targets | Create (05) + Join (18) | 05, 18 | Winner named directly |
| Support slot | A slot on an existing bill | Join | 17, 18 | If a Band won, unresolved (DT-09) |
| Member wanted | **Proposed** membership of a Band entity | Propose | 01 | Ratified by 01's governance, never by this handoff |
| Camp invitation | A contributor on the camp project | Join | 07 | Winner named directly |
| Open call (04.07) | A slot on a festival/showcase bill — **N winners, N handoffs** | Join | 17, 18 | Per act; each act's lineup unresolved (DT-09) |

### 3. What the handoff carries

Everything the platform already knows, in one direction, once:

| Fact | Source | Why it must travel |
|------|--------|--------------------|
| Date, time, location | The offer (04.04.03 D-01) | Re-asking a dep for the date of the gig they just accepted is the fragmentation the product exists to end |
| Fee shape and value | The offer, **not the post** | The offer's terms bind (04.04.03 D-01). Carrying the post's fee is the bait-and-switch one stage later |
| Scope / deliverable | The offer | 05's engagement is meaningless without it |
| Material refs (setlist, stems, charts) | The submission (04.03) | The dep already proved they know the material; the show already has the setlist |
| Contributor identity | The winner's profile (01) | The winner already exists; the handoff never creates a second identity for them |
| Replaced party, if a substitution | The opportunity's target reference | Without it the call sheet has six people for a five-piece (DT-04) |
| Back-reference, both directions | This handoff | "This credit came from this gig came from this dep call" is the provenance chain (D-02) |

**Facts travel; the opportunity does not become the authority.** After handoff, the downstream entity
is the live truth and the opportunity is the immutable record of what was agreed. They are allowed
to diverge, and the divergence is displayed, never reconciled by overwriting (DT-08).

### 4. What the handoff does NOT do

- **No veto.** It does not re-check availability, permissions, or eligibility. Acceptance already
  decided (04.04.03 D-03). A handoff that can refuse is a second gate that can strand someone who
  was told yes — the exact failure this feature is most afraid of (DT-10).
- **No undo.** There is no "reverse handoff" button. The handoff is not a decision; the acceptance
  was. Un-hiring someone is a cancellation, and cancellations run through the downstream domain's
  cancellation flow, with its policies, deposits and penalties (DT-10).
- **No governance.** It proposes membership; 01 ratifies it (DT-11).
- **No payment gate.** Whether the handoff crosses a fee boundary is a monetisation decision the
  owner has not made (Q-03). Until then it does not gate on money.

### Happy Path

The canonical case: a dep call for Saturday, posted from Show #123 (join mode).

1. Candidate accepts the offer (04.04.03). Availability updates atomically with the acceptance,
   not with the handoff. Disposition = `won` (CX-02).
2. Handoff fires **on the acceptance**, not on a schedule. The winner sees `Setting up your gig…`
   only if step 6 has not completed within **1 second**; the target is that they never see it.
3. Handoff resolves the mode from the opportunity's target reference: Show #123 exists → **join**.
4. Handoff assembles the carried facts (§3) from the offer and the submission. Nothing is asked of
   anyone.
5. Handoff writes into 18: the winner is added to Show #123's lineup and call sheet; **the replaced
   party is removed from Saturday** (DT-04). The winner's operational access to Show #123 is granted
   **scoped** — call time, address, set length, their own fee, the material — and not the settlement,
   the other players' fees, or the band's production workspace (DT-06).
6. Handoff writes the reciprocal facts: a gig on the winner's calendar; the fee as a receivable (23);
   a **pre-filled, unconfirmed credit** dated to the show, waiting for the morning after (02, DT-07);
   a back-reference in both directions (D-02); the pipeline entry links onward rather than
   dead-ending at "won" (CX-05).
7. **P95 within 5 seconds of acceptance**, the winner's view resolves to the gig itself — not a
   receipt, the actual thing, with the call time on it.
8. Four audiences are notified, not two (DT-12): **the winner** ("You're on. Saturday, 7pm load-in,
   The Venue"); **the losers**, whose dispositions fire from this same acceptance (CX-01, 04.05.01
   D-02); **the existing cast** of Show #123 — the MD, the other players, the venue, the engineer —
   who all have a new name on their sheet; and **the replaced party**, who is told he is off Saturday
   before he finds out from the call sheet.
9. The opportunity closes as `won`. It is now history and it is immutable.
10. Saturday happens. Nobody opens the app.
11. Sunday at **scheduled-end + 12 hours** — deliberately not at 1am load-out — the credit trigger
    fires. The winner and the MD are each asked one question about a pre-filled cast list: *did this
    happen, and were these the five people?* Confirmed → a counter-attested credit exists (02),
    captured at source, from a list nobody had to remember (DT-07). The instrument is 02's; the
    trigger and the date are this feature's.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Downstream creation fails — standard path** | **The acceptance is never rolled back.** A human said yes to another human; a failed write does not un-say it. State → `handoff-pending`. The write is retried with escalating backoff, **capped at 15 minutes** — a gig days out can tolerate the wait. If retries are exhausted → `handoff-failed`, both parties notified, poster gets a manual-completion affordance. (The retry cadence itself is a mechanism deferred to Q-06; what is fixed here is the **15-minute outer bound**, chosen because the standard dep circuit is not time-critical.) The opportunity still closes as `won`; the shortlist is **not** reopened; the losers' dispositions still fire. | Winner: "You're booked for Saturday, 7pm, The Venue — we're still adding it to the show's advance. Nothing you need to do." Then, on failure: "We couldn't add this to the advance. Your gig is still on. [Tell the MD]" — never a silent failure, never a suggestion the gig is in doubt |
| **Downstream creation fails — urgent path** | The dep circuit fills in minutes (domain index) and a failure that surfaces in 15 minutes is useless for a gig tonight. If the event date is **< 48h away** or the opportunity came through urgent-fill (04.04.04): retries are abandoned fast and the failure **escalates to a human within 60 seconds** — the product-timing bound that still leaves time to phone the venue. (The retry cadence is deferred to Q-06; the **60-second escalation** is the fixed behavioural contract.) | Poster, within a minute: "We couldn't add Sam to tonight's sheet. Sam has accepted and is expecting to play. Call the venue." The platform names the human action it cannot do |
| Handoff partially succeeds across two targets | A crew job writes an engagement (05) **and** a call sheet entry (18). Partial success is the norm, not an exception — each target retries independently on its own ladder. **A partial handoff is never presented as complete.** | "Engagement created. Still adding you to the call sheet." Explicit about which half landed |
| Target domain doesn't exist yet (create mode) | At launch, most won't (DT-03). Close as `won` and stop — no broken link, no half-entity, no promise the platform can't keep (D-04). But **stopping is not the same as dropping**: the handoff still emits the facts to the tools the parties actually use — a calendar entry they can import, the agreed terms in writing to both sides, and the pipeline entry (04.05.04) retains the record. Handing off to the user's own calendar is an honest degradation; going silent is not (D-08). | "You're booked: Saturday 1 Aug, The Venue, £200. [Add to calendar] — WeJammin doesn't manage show advances yet, so the rest is between you and Jo." |
| Target domain doesn't exist yet (join mode) | **Structurally impossible, and that is informative.** You cannot post a dep call *from* Show #123 if there is no domain in which Show #123 exists. Join-mode handoffs cannot exist before their target domain does. **At launch, every handoff is create-mode or nothing** — which makes DT-03's launch conditionality sharper than the sub-domain recorded it | N/A — the opportunity could never have been posted with a target |
| Target entity deleted between post and win | The dep call was posted from Show #123; the show got pulled while people were applying. Join has nowhere to land. Handoff **degrades to create-mode**: the winner still gets the gig record and the fee, because the promise was made. The poster is told the target is gone. Does not fail the winner. | Winner: normal confirmation. Poster: "Show #123 no longer exists. Sam has accepted the gig. If it's off, you need to cancel with Sam." |
| **Winner cancels after handoff — dep (substitution)** | **The show does not cancel because a dep pulled out.** The gig exists; the chair empties. The reversal of a handoff is not its inverse (DT-09). The original opportunity **stays closed as `won`** and records that the win was reversed — dispositions already delivered are facts and are never un-told. Instead the platform offers a **successor opportunity**: pre-filled with the original's date, venue, fee and material, back-referenced to it, and — because the date is now closer — routed straight into urgent-fill (04.04.04) if < 48h. | Poster, one tap: "Sam's out. Repost Saturday? Your shortlist from last time is still there — Priya and Marcus were runners-up." |
| Winner cancels after handoff — whole-booking | The Operator sold time and space; the buyer left. The **booking cancels and the slot reopens** as sellable inventory (17), subject to 17's cancellation and deposit policy — not this feature's. The perishability is exactly why the Operator's cancellation terms exist and why this handoff must not invent its own. | Operator: the slot returns to the calendar with 17's deposit outcome applied |
| Runner-up gets the successor | **Invited, never reinstated.** Priya was told "filled elsewhere" — a fact that was communicated and cannot be un-communicated (04.05.01 D-04's symmetry: dispositions are immutable once delivered). Un-dispositioning her would also corrupt the poster's response-reputation data (CX-01). She receives a **new offer**, framed honestly. | "The Saturday dep fell through — you were the runner-up. Still free?" Not: her old application silently going live again |
| Winner un-cancels ("childcare sorted") | Cancellation is final; there is no un-cancel. The chair may already have been refilled by the successor. If it hasn't, the poster can offer them the successor like anyone else. | "That gig's been reposted. [Apply]" — no privileged path back |
| Poster withdraws the offer in the same instant the candidate accepts | Acceptance is a fact the moment the candidate acts. A withdrawal arriving after it loses; the handoff fires. **The handoff has no veto** (DT-10) and will not adjudicate a race by breaking the promise that won it. The poster now holds a booking they must cancel through the downstream flow, with its consequences. | Poster: "Sam accepted before your withdrawal landed. Sam is booked. [Cancel the booking]" — the cost of the race lands on the party who created it |
| **Two winners handed the same chair** | The urgent-fill cascade (04.04.04) misfires, or two reviewers each make an offer. **Both handoffs execute.** Both humans were genuinely told yes; the platform will not silently pick which promise to break. The join records both and raises a conflict to the poster. | Poster: "You have two bassists for Saturday: Sam and Priya. Both accepted. You need to sort this out with one of them." Uncomfortable, and correctly so — the platform caused it and must not hide it |
| Multi-winner opportunity, one handoff fails | An open call selects 10 acts (04.07); a crew job hires 3. **N winners → N independent handoffs.** One failing does not fail the other nine. The opportunity closes when all N are dispositioned, regardless of handoff outcome. | Nine acts on the bill; one flagged `handoff-failed` with a named human action |
| **Band entity wins — the cast is unresolved** | 17 books the *Band*; 18's call sheet needs *humans* — names, phone numbers, who's driving, dietary requirements. The Band is not five people until someone says who's playing that night, and the lineup for a date is the membership (01) **minus whoever's depping**. The handoff lands the booking with an **explicitly unresolved cast**, never an assumed one — assuming the membership is the call sheet is how a venue expects a drummer who quit in March. | Band admin: "You're on the bill. Who's playing? 4 of 5 confirmed — [Post a dep call for drums]" |
| **The handoff loops back into this domain** | An unresolved cast generates a dep call (04.01). A won opportunity **creates a new opportunity**. The domain is recursive and the recursion is normal, not pathological — festival slot won → drummer can't make it → dep call → won → handoff joins the same show (DT-09). The back-reference chain must survive the loop or provenance breaks at depth 2 | Nothing special. It just works, and the chain reads: credit → show → dep call → festival slot |
| **Member-wanted win used as a governance bypass** | A band's rhythm guitarist posts "bassist wanted". If the handoff auto-grants Band membership, posting an ad became a way to give a stranger whatever membership carries — booking on behalf of the band, receiving its payments. **The handoff proposes; 01's governance ratifies** (DT-11). No exception, including for the poster who is themselves a member | Winner: "Jo's invited you to join The Band — waiting on the other members." Not: instant membership |
| Winner has never used the target domain | The whole point (D-03). No onboarding, no account creation, no "set up your profile to continue". The dep appears in the advance because they won a dep call, full stop. | The gig. Nothing else |
| **Onboarding-free is mistaken for permission-free** | D-03 says the winner lands without onboarding; it does **not** say they land as a member. A dep on Show #123 must not see the band's settlement, the other players' fees, or the venue's contract (DT-06). Scope: call time, address, set length, material, **their own fee**. Duration: handoff → scheduled-end **+ 7 days** (the settlement window), then reduced to permanent read-only on their own facts — their fee, their credit — which are theirs forever | Winner: a show page with their information on it. They never see a locked panel or a permission error, because the things they cannot see are not rendered |
| Fee unstated / TBC at post time | 04.01.03 permits "negotiable". D-01 says carry the facts — there is no fee fact to carry. Carry `fee: unstated` **explicitly**, never a blank and never `0`. An unknown fee that silently becomes zero is how a musician doesn't get paid; the downstream entity and 23's receivable both open with the fee flagged unresolved and visible to both parties | Both parties: "Fee: not agreed" — persistent, on the booking, until someone sets it |
| **Terms diverge after handoff** | The venue paid less. The date moved. The set got shorter. The opportunity says £200; the settlement says £150. **Both are true and neither is edited.** The opportunity is the immutable record of what was *agreed* (04.04.03 D-01 — the offer's terms are the evidence); the downstream entity is what is *happening*. The pipeline entry (04.05.04) shows the live state with the original agreement beside it (DT-08) | "Agreed £200 · Settled £150" — the divergence is the data 23 and 24 exist to work with. Overwriting it destroys the only evidence in a fee dispute |
| Poster tries to retro-edit the won opportunity to match downstream | **Refused.** A closed opportunity is history. Editing the agreed fee after the fact is the bait-and-switch of 04.04.03 D-01 executed after acceptance, where it is worth more and is harder to see | "This closed on 22 Jul and can't be changed. To change the fee, change it on the booking — both will show." |
| Winner's account suspended by 24 after handoff | The handoff is done and the booking is real. Suspension is 24's action on the *account*, not a cancellation of a commitment to a third party. The poster is told a human fact — "the person you booked has been suspended" — and decides. The platform does not silently vacate a gig, and does not leave the poster to discover it on Saturday | Poster: "Sam's account has been suspended. Your Saturday booking still stands as far as Sam knows." |
| Poster's account suspended after handoff | Same principle, inverted, and it bites harder: the winner has organised their week around this. The downstream entity survives; the winner is told; 24 decides whether the booking itself is voided. This handoff does not | Winner: notified. Their fee receivable (23) is flagged at risk, honestly |
| **Winner is the poster** (multi-hyphenate self-deal) | A Producer posts a session call for a bassist and, after a thin field, plays it themselves. Legitimate and common. The handoff executes: they are added as a contributor. But **the counter-attestation is worthless** — a credit self-attested by the person who also attested it is one signature wearing two hats. 02 must see the same identity on both sides and mark it self-attested, not confirmed. This feature's job is to pass the fact, honestly labelled, not to block the booking | Nothing at handoff time. Downstream in 02: "self-credited" rather than "confirmed by Jo" |
| Winner is already in the downstream entity | The band's own guitarist wins the support slot their band posted; a project contributor wins a session on the same project. Join is **idempotent** — the person is not added twice, their existing role is not overwritten, and their access is the union of what they had and what they won (never the intersection — winning must not take something away) | Nothing. The correct outcome of this is invisibility |
| **Handoff fires twice** (double-tap, retry after a timeout that actually succeeded) | Idempotent on the acceptance, not on the request. Two deps, two chairs; one dep, one chair, regardless of how many times the write is attempted | Nothing. A duplicate gig on a calendar is a support ticket and a lost trust point |
| Downstream entity cancelled entirely (show pulled) | CX-05 synthesis Q5's dangling pointer. The pipeline entry does **not** dead-end and does not vanish: it resolves to the last known truth — "Won · show cancelled 4 Aug" — because the applicant's question a month later is "what happened with that?" and "cancelled" is a complete answer (04.05.04 D-03's principle: history is not the poster's to erase) | "Won — The Venue, 1 Aug. Show cancelled." The win stays in their record; they did win it |
| Scheduled date moves downstream | The show moves to the 8th. The gig, the credit trigger and the receivable move with it — the downstream entity is the live truth (DT-08). The opportunity still says 1 Aug and is not edited. The winner is notified of a date change to a gig they accepted, because a moved date is a new availability question and the platform that moved it must ask | Winner: "The Venue gig has moved to 8 Aug. Still able to do it?" — not a silent calendar mutation |
| **Work happens, no credit captured** | The thesis's failure mode, committed by the domain best placed to prevent it. Mitigated by DT-07: the trigger, the date and the pre-filled cast list are all created here, at the only moment the cast is known rather than remembered. If the trigger is not created at handoff, nobody will open an app on Sunday to file a credit and the record becomes a reconstruction — which `problem-statement.md` names as the root cause | The Sunday prompt: "Did Saturday happen? [Yes] — and were these the five?" One question, pre-filled, from a list the platform already had |
| Deal taken off-platform after handoff | `personas.md` names this as the Musician's worst intentional behaviour, and the handoff is where the temptation crystallises — every fact needed to proceed elsewhere is now in both parties' hands. **Not solvable by withholding facts**: a handoff that hides the venue's address to protect a fee is not a handoff. Whether a fee boundary exists at all is the owner's call (Q-03) | See Q-03. The structural answer is that the *credit* (DT-07) only exists if the work stays visible here — the platform's leverage is what it gives, not what it withholds |
| Winner accepts on a phone at a loading dock and loses signal | The handoff is server-side; their connectivity does not affect whether they are booked. But they may never see the in-app confirmation, and a musician who is not sure whether they are booked will phone someone — which is the fragmentation returning. The confirmation must reach them **out-of-band** via the notification cross-cut, not only as a screen they didn't see | On reconnect: the gig, already there, with no "confirm" step waiting for them |

### States

**Handoff lifecycle**

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Not applicable | Any disposition other than `won` | Nothing — this feature has no surface for the 5 dispositions that end the story (CX-02) |
| Pending | Acceptance fired the handoff; targets not yet written | "Setting up your gig…" — only after 1s; target is that this state is never seen |
| Handed off | All targets written | The gig / engagement / booking itself, linked both ways (D-02) |
| Partially handed off | Multi-target handoff, some targets written | "Engagement created. Still adding you to the call sheet." — never presented as complete |
| Failed | Retry ladder exhausted (15 min standard / 60s urgent) | "Your gig is still on. We couldn't add it to the advance. [Tell the MD]" — plus a manual-completion affordance for the poster |
| No target | Target domain doesn't exist yet (D-04, D-08) | The agreed terms in writing + a calendar entry they can import. An honest dead-end, not a silence |
| Proposed | Member-wanted win awaiting 01's governance (DT-11) | "Waiting on the other members" |
| Reversed | Winner cancelled after handoff | The win stays in history; the chair (or the slot) is what changed. Poster is offered a one-tap successor opportunity (D-06) |
| Conflicted | Two winners, one chair | Both names, shown to the poster, with no platform-chosen resolution |

**UI states** (per template)

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Handoff in flight > 1s | "Setting up your gig…" — with the confirmed facts (date, venue, fee) already on screen, because those are known and waiting is not a reason to withhold them |
| Empty | A user who has never won anything | No handoff surface at all. Their pipeline (04.05.04) shows the board CTA; this feature does not render an empty state of its own |
| Populated | Handoff complete | The downstream thing itself — not a receipt for it. The call time is the success state |
| Error | Retry ladder exhausted | The gig, plus an honest, bounded failure: what didn't land, what still stands, and the named human action. Never "something went wrong" |
| Partial | Multi-target partial, or `fee: unstated`, or an unresolved Band cast | The landed half plus an explicit named gap: "Still adding you to the call sheet" · "Fee: not agreed" · "4 of 5 confirmed — [Post a dep call for drums]" |
