## Overview

What happens after the decision — for the winner, for everyone else, and for the platform. It holds
the domain's credibility bet (nobody gets ghosted, and posters who ghost are visible), its
consolidation payoff (a won opportunity *becomes* a booking, an engagement, a session — the parent
index's "clean handoff"), and the applicant's own record of where their career applications actually
went.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities that chain causally. Disposition
produces the data that response reputation is computed from; disposition = won is what fires the
handoff; and both feed the applicant's pipeline. Remove disposition and the other three have no
input — they are not co-located, they are downstream of each other.
