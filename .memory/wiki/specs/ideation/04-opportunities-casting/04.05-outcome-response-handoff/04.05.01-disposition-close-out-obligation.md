# Feature: Disposition & Close-Out Obligation

> **Number**: 04.05.01
> **Parent**: [Outcome, Response & Handoff](./04.05-outcome-response-handoff-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Every applicant gets an answer. A submission creates a debt owed by the party that posted, and that
debt is visible, dated, and cannot be tidied away — not by the applicant (D-04), not by deleting the
post (D-12), and not by the platform answering on the poster's behalf and calling it a response
(D-09).

This is the domain's credibility bet and its named beachhead candidate (domain D-04). The parent
index diagnoses Sonicbids and ReverbNation as the cautionary tale, and their collapse had two
causes: charging desperate artists to submit (killed structurally at 04.01.03 D-02) and never
answering them. **Every incumbent is still committing the second one daily.** Unlike this domain's
more novel features it has no credit-graph dependency, no cold-start problem, and costs nothing to
build — it works on day one with an empty platform.

The feature's real subject is **what the platform does when nobody decides**. Answering is the easy
half; 04.04.01's deepening already made a truthful "no" cost one tap. The hard half is that the
mechanism must not lie about absence, must not reward it, and must not pretend to have solved it.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Both sides, and the asymmetry is the point. As applicant they are ghosted constantly — "applied to 14, heard back from 3" is their lived experience (04.05.04). As poster they are the ghoster, usually not from malice but from a queue they never returned to (04.04.01 DT-01). The feature has to work on the same human in both directions on the same day. They are also the persona for whom **the debt is fragmented across acting identities** (04.01.01 D-07): a dep call owed personally, a member-wanted owed by their Band entity, a session call owed by a studio they co-own — three debts, one human, and per D-06 none of them is *theirs*. Their private "decisions you owe" view must aggregate what their public identities must not (DT-12). |
| Producer | Full | Owes dispositions on session calls and briefs. Their queues are smaller, so the obligation is cheap — and `personas.md` names them as the deferrer ("we'll do it later" is their worst accidental behaviour), which is this feature's failure mode wearing a persona. Their `won` is not merely an answer: it is the trigger for the handoff (CX-02) and therefore for the split-capture moment (04.04.03 DT-02, 04.05.03 D-05). A Producer who never dispositions never reaches the moment the thesis depends on. |
| Operator | Full | Owes dispositions on crew and support applications, and theirs is the only routinely **multi-slot** debt (04.01.01 D-08) — 2 riggers and an LD on one load-in is three independent obligations under one post. Theirs compounds: a venue with a bad response rate is one acts stop pitching to, which shrinks their supply of support acts (CX-01), and that lands in the venue reputation already accruing in 16/17. They are also **the persona who proves D-05**: their inventory is perishable, so a post they cannot delist while they owe answers is a Tuesday they cannot resell. A gate on closing costs them money directly, and they would route around it by posting off-platform — the exact harm domain D-03 fears about fees. |
| Fan | None | Never applies, never dispositions, and no obligation can exist under a Fan account in either direction. The absence is **structural, not a permission**: a Fan cannot post (domain Role Matrix), cannot pitch (04.03.05 D-04), and therefore has nothing to owe; and never submits, so has nothing owed to them. There is no Fan surface to hide. |

## Behavior

### The obligation

| Property | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **When it exists** | From the instant a submission lands. Not at triage, not at close. | D-01 |
| **Granularity** | Per submission, per **slot** — the slot is the fillable unit, the post is the container. Post state is derived. | D-07, 04.01.01 D-08 |
| **Who owes it** | The post's **acting identity** (the hirer), never the human who typed the post. | D-06, 04.01.01 D-07 |
| **Who is owed** | The submitting identity — which may itself be a Band entity (04.07). | D-01 |
| **What discharges it** | Exactly one disposition. Nothing else — not delisting, not deleting, not time. | D-05, D-11, D-12 |
| **What is never owed** | An answer to someone who withdrew. Nothing was owed the moment they left. | D-09 |

### Disposition values — deliberately few, and two columns that were one

The breadth pass carried six values and a single implicit notion of "answered". Deepening splits
that in two: **closing the applicant's entry and crediting the poster with a response are different
facts**, and conflating them is what makes auto-disposition into platform-assisted ghosting (DT-07).

| Value | Meaning | Set by | Closes the applicant's entry | Counts as the poster answering (04.05.02) | Fires handoff (CX-02) |
|-------|---------|--------|---|---|---|
| `won` | Offer accepted | Decider, via acceptance (04.04.03) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ **Yes** |
| `not selected` | Rejected, with a reason where one is derivable (D-10) | Decider | ✅ | ✅ | No |
| `filled elsewhere` | The slot went to someone else — including a cascade winner who never applied (04.04.01 DT-07) | **System**, triggered by a win | ✅ | ✅ — the win was the poster's act | No |
| `withdrawn by poster` | The slot or post was cancelled | Decider | ✅ | ✅ — a cancellation is an answer | No |
| `withdrawn by applicant` | The applicant pulled out | Applicant | ✅ | ➖ **Excluded from the denominator entirely** — you didn't answer, and you aren't charged for it | No |
| `expired` | The applicant never answered an offer within its fuse (04.04.03 D-02) | **System** | ✅ | ✅ — the offer was the poster's act | No |
| `no-decision` | The slot ended and **nobody decided** | **System only — never offerable to a poster** (D-08) | ✅ | ❌ **Counts against, never for** (D-09) | No |

`no-decision` is new at deepening and it exists because the breadth table had **no honest way to say
"the poster never answered"** (DT-08). `Expired` is its mirror image — the applicant's failure, not
the poster's — and telling an abandoned applicant `not selected` is a lie the platform tells on the
poster's behalf. Whether it fires *automatically* is Q-02, a values call. The value must exist
either way, because the state exists either way: 04.01.01 auto-closes a post at its decide-by, and
04.04.01's `Abandoned` state is reachable regardless of what Q-02 decides.

### Delist and discharge are two different acts

The breadth pass called both of them "close" and hung the gate on the compound. They are not the
same act and they must never share a control (D-05, DT-04):

| Act | What it does | Gated? |
|---|---|---|
| **Delist** | Stops new submissions landing on the slot. Instant, always available, no confirmation beyond the obvious. | **Never.** Blocking it manufactures the debt it punishes (DT-04). |
| **Discharge** | Answers the people already waiting. One disposition each. | This is the obligation. It is what Q-01's gate is *about*. |

Delisting never discharges anything, and the obligation survives it — as it survives deletion
(D-12), the post's decide-by (04.01.01), and the poster's absence.

### The reason model — resolved (D-10)

| Reason kind | When it applies | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| **Criterion-scoped** — "the post required PLI insurance; you don't have it" | Any bulk action (04.04.01 D-07 makes bulk *only* criterion-scoped) and any individual reject where a live-at-submit criterion is unmet | Machine-generated and **always carried**. Free, true, impersonal, un-resentable. |
| **Structural** — "the slot was filled" / "the post was cancelled" | `filled elsewhere`, `withdrawn by poster`, `no-decision` | Machine-generated and always carried. |
| **Free text** | A judgement call the machine cannot derive | **Never required.** Optional, and abuse-scanned (24). |

Required free text produces lies, not information — a poster who must write something permanent and
attributed writes "went with someone else" 400 times (04.04.01 DT-04). `not selected` with no
reason is therefore **permitted**, and it is honest: there was nothing true and cheap to say.

### Discharge rules

| Rule | Statement |
|------|-----------|
| **Exactly one** | One submission, one disposition, ever. Concurrent attempts are idempotent — the first commit is the truth (D-11). |
| **Irreversible** | A fired disposition is never un-fired. The applicant has been told, and un-telling is not available (04.04.01 D-02). |
| **Never on incomplete information** | Reject is unavailable when evidence, criteria or media failed to load (04.04.01 D-09). This feature depends on that bound: a `not selected` fired over a fact nobody saw is a lie with a timestamp. |
| **Never individually attributed, externally** | The answer is the acting identity's — "the band decided", never "Dave rejected you" (D-06, DT-11). Internal attribution is 04.04.02's call. |
| **Atomic with the applicant's view** | The pipeline entry (04.05.04) carries the outcome at the same instant. An applicant who refreshes must never see `submitted` after the decider has answered. |
| **Delivery is not discharge** | The obligation discharges on disposition, not on the applicant reading it. Delivery is the notification cross-cut's budget, and an unreachable applicant does not create an eternal debt. |

### Happy Path

1. A submission lands against a slot (04.03.01) or a target (04.03.05). **The obligation exists from
   that instant** — owed by the post's acting identity (D-06), owed to the submitting identity.
2. The poster's **open-obligation count increments in real time**. This is 04.05.02's only
   uncheatable signal (04.05.02 D-02): you either owe people right now or you don't.
3. The applicant's pipeline entry reads `submitted`; the poster's queue card reads untriaged. Same
   fact, two renderings (CX-03 synthesis Q1) — which is what makes the ghost visible to both parties.
4. Poster triages (04.04.01). **A reject fires its disposition immediately** (04.04.01 D-02) —
   `not selected`, carrying its criterion-scoped reason where one applies (D-10). The applicant is
   answered while the poster is still on the next card.
5. A **hold is not a disposition** (04.04.01 D-05). The obligation stands, and the applicant is told
   **a date, not a state**: "You'll hear by 14 Aug" (04.04.01 D-06). Never "you're a maybe".
6. An offer is issued and accepted (04.04.03). The winner's disposition is `won`, and **the handoff
   fires** (CX-02, 04.05.03).
7. **The winner's acceptance dispositions every remaining live candidate on that slot** as
   `filled elsewhere`, in one system act (D-02) — untriaged cards included. **Untriaged is not
   un-owed.** The losers' answers are triggered by the winner's success.
8. Each disposition lands atomically in its applicant's pipeline entry with its outcome and reason
   (CX-03). Entries that won link onward rather than dead-ending (CX-05, 04.05.04 D-04).
9. The slot's open-obligation count reaches **0**. The slot is **clear**. The post is clear when
   every slot is (D-07) — post state is derived, never set.
10. The poster **delists whenever they want**, before or after any of the above (D-05). Delisting
    stops new submissions; it never discharges anything.
11. Response signals update (CX-01): poster-authored answers count, withdrawals are excluded, and
    `no-decision` counts against (D-09).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Poster wants to stop the flow but still owes 12 people** | **The decomposition (D-05).** Delist is free and instant; the 12 remain owed. The breadth pass's gate hung on a compound act and would have blocked the harmless half. | "Delisted. Nobody new can apply. **You still owe 12 people an answer.**" |
| **Poster never returns at all** | **The case the gate cannot reach** (DT-03). A poster who never comes back cannot be blocked from an act they will never perform. The remaining levers are `no-decision` (Q-02) and the next-post gate (DT-05) — which only fires the day they return. | Applicants: see Q-02. Nothing here claims to have solved absence. |
| **Poster returns to post again while owing 40 answers** | **The only moment they are reachable** (DT-05) — the gate's real attachment point, not "close". Block or warn is Q-01. | "You owe 40 people an answer from your last 2 posts." Whether the Post button works is Q-01. |
| **Poster evades the gate by posting under a new Band entity** | **Structural and unclosable** (DT-06). The debt is the acting identity's (D-06); a fresh entity has none. Closing it by gating the *human* would block a band for a member's unrelated personal debt. | The evasion costs what it buys: a new entity posts with no history (01) and no signal (04.05.02 D-03 suppresses below threshold), which is itself visible. See Q-05. |
| **Poster bulk-rejects to clear the debt** | **The breadth pass's named failure mode — now foreclosed upstream** (04.04.01 D-07). There is no "reject remaining". Bulk action scopes to a criterion that was live at submit time, and **the criterion is what the applicant is told**. Bulk rejection stops being gaming and becomes the most honest rejection in the product. | Confirm names count and criterion: "Reject 312 candidates who don't meet 'released record'?" Applicant: "Not selected — this post required a released record." |
| **Poster adds a criterion *after* submissions land, to justify a bulk reject** | Blocked upstream: bulk action may only scope to criteria live at each candidate's submit time (04.04.01 D-07). A later criterion scopes to zero existing candidates. | "This criterion was added after these 312 applied — it can't be used to reject them." |
| **Sockpuppets mass-apply to push a poster over the gate threshold** | **Obligation-flooding — an unnamed attack** (DT-09). If debt blocks posting, applications are a denial-of-posting weapon. 04.04.01 D-10 collapses one identity to one card; it does not bound 40 identities, and criterion-scoped bulk reject does not help if the sockpuppets meet the criteria. | Not this feature's to mitigate — sybil is 01 + 24's. The gate's design must not assume the count is trustworthy. See Q-07. |
| **The winner's handoff fails, or the winner cancels afterwards** | **12 people were told `filled elsewhere` for a gig that evaporated** (DT-10). Dispositions are irreversible (D-11) and stand. The compensating act is a **new post** (04.01.01 D-03's close-and-repost), with the dispositioned offered one-tap re-apply. That is a new invitation, not a resurrection. | Losers: "This one reopened as a new post — re-apply in one tap?" Nobody is told their "no" was a mistake, because it wasn't one at the time. |
| **Poster deletes the post with people waiting** | **Deletion is not an escape hatch** (D-12). Deletion delists; entries survive (04.05.04 D-03); the debt stands and still counts against (04.05.02). Otherwise `delete` is the universal ghosting button and every other rule in this file is decoration. | Poster: "Deleting won't clear the 12 answers you owe." Applicants: entry persists, post shown as removed. |
| **Post expires at its decide-by with people waiting** | Auto-closes (04.01.01); **the obligation survives**. Held cards' resolve-by dates cannot outlive the decide-by by construction (04.04.01 D-05), so no hold is orphaned. | "This closed automatically. You still owe 12 people an answer." |
| **Applicant withdrew** | **No obligation was ever owed.** Removed from the count and **excluded from 04.05.02's denominator entirely** (D-09) — a withdrawal must neither credit nor penalise the poster. | Poster's count decrements. Applicant's entry closes as their own act. |
| **Disposition and withdrawal race** | Both are terminal; the record must be one, never both. **First commit wins**; the loser is told the truth rather than silently absorbed. | Poster: "They'd already withdrawn." Applicant: "You'd already been answered — here's the outcome." |
| **Two triagers reject the same card simultaneously** | Idempotent (D-11). One card, one outcome, **exactly one disposition** — 04.04.01's edge case and this feature's D-11 are the same rule seen from two sides. | Second triager: "Already rejected." |
| **One member of a band rejects unilaterally** | **Not a disposition.** In a multi-triager queue a reject is a vote (04.04.01 D-08). No half-answer is sent. | Triager: "Recorded. Waiting on 2 more." Applicant sees nothing. |
| **Two triagers disagree** | The candidate becomes contested and goes to the shortlist (04.04.01 D-08). **The queue declines to fire an irreversible disposition on a contested candidate** — this feature's irreversibility (D-11) is exactly why that rule exists upstream. | "Dave advanced, you rejected — moved to shortlist as contested." |
| **Bulk disposition of 312 fails halfway** | Idempotent per submission and resumable. **No applicant is ever notified twice**, and no submission is left silently un-dispositioned because a batch died. | Poster: "287 of 312 answered — finishing the rest." Never a silent partial. |
| **Multi-slot post: 3 slots filled, 1 still live** | Obligation is per slot (D-07). Candidates on filled slots get `filled elsewhere`; candidates on the live slot **still wait, correctly**. Post state is derived and reads partial. | "Drums and bass are done. **4 people are still waiting on keys.**" |
| **One human applied to two slots on the same post** (the doubling keys player who also sings) | **Two submissions, two obligations, answered independently.** Winning one slot does *not* disposition their candidacy on the other — doubling is ordinary in music, and auto-closing it would decide something the poster didn't. | Applicant: "Won — keys. Still waiting — vocals." Both true simultaneously. |
| **The multi-hyphenate owes under three identities at once** | The debt is per acting identity (D-06) and the *surface* is per post — so the debt fragments across exactly the persona least able to absorb fragmentation (04.04.01 DT-03). A private cross-identity "decisions you owe" view is the fix; publishing it is not (DT-12). | Private: all three, aggregated. Public: each identity's own, never joined. See Q-04, Q-06. |
| **A candidate holds triage rights on the queue they're in** | Self-triage is unrepresentable and overrides any 01 grant (04.04.01 D-11). They cannot disposition themselves. | "You've applied to this one — you can't review it." |
| **Urgent-fill cascade lands on a post with a live queue** | The cascade's first-yes ends the slot; every live card dispositions `filled elsewhere` at that moment (04.04.01 DT-07). D-02 applies to a winner **who never entered the queue**. Applicants lost to someone who never applied, and must be told truthfully and instantly (04.04.04 D-03). | Poster: "Filled by the cascade — your 12 remaining applicants have been told." Applicants: "Filled elsewhere." |
| **Terms change on a live post** | 04.01.01 D-09 re-gates and notifies every submitter with the delta and a one-tap withdraw. Withdrawals leave the denominator (D-09); submitters who stay are still owed; **submitters who ignore the re-gate are still owed** — silence is not consent and never reduces the debt. | "The date changed. Withdraw, or stay in." Debt unchanged for anyone who stays or says nothing. |
| **Applicant deletes their account while waiting** | The disposition still fires and still counts — it is a fact about the poster's behaviour (CX-01), not a message. Notification is dropped; the obligation discharges. | Poster: nothing unusual. No eternal debt to a nonexistent person. |
| **Applicant blocks the poster (24)** | Blocking must **never create an undischargeable debt**, or blocking becomes a way to permanently damage a poster's signal. Disposition fires, notification suppressed, obligation discharges. | Poster: unchanged. Applicant: outcome visible in their pipeline, not pushed at them. |
| **Poster is suspended by Trust & Safety mid-queue** | The debt outlives the suspension. **A takedown that leaves people waiting is the platform ghosting on the poster's behalf.** Suspension delists (D-05); the queue is dispositioned `withdrawn by poster` or `no-decision` per 24's reason — never left silent. | Applicants: "This post was removed." Never an eternal `submitted`. |
| **The posting org dissolves mid-queue** (the band splits) | The obligation **outlives the entity that incurred it** (D-06), and D-04 forbids applicants tidying it away. The debt is real and, for the moment, ownerless. Entity succession is 01's; this feature can detect the state and refuses to erase it. | See Q-05 — the same seam as 04.04.01 Q-05, seen from the debt's side. |
| **Free-text reason used to abuse 40 people** | An optional free-text reason is a channel to send "you're terrible" to forty musicians at once — a harassment vector the reason debate never named. Abuse scanning is 24's; that reasons are **never required** (D-10) removes the pressure that generates them. | Routed to 24 + `/create-prd-security`. |
| **A criterion-scoped reason at small N leaks who was rejected** | With 3 applicants, "rejected everyone without PLI" identifies them (CX-01 synthesis Q3). The aggregate leaks the individuals. | Suppression at small N is 04.05.02's (D-03 there); this feature must not assume its reasons are private. |
| **Poster answers everyone, hires nobody, reposts next week** | Debt clear, response rate perfect, zero value delivered. Not this feature's to catch — **04.05.02's post-to-hire rate is the one that sees it**, and 04.05.02 D-04's honesty (the metric measures responsiveness, not respect) is why this is not papered over. | The signals disagree, deliberately. |
| **`no-decision` fires and the poster's rate stays perfect** | **Would be the ultimate gaming** (DT-07) — abandonment cheaper than bulk-rejection, zero effort, platform as accomplice. Foreclosed by D-09: `no-decision` closes the applicant's entry and **counts against the poster, never for**. | Applicant: an honest answer. Poster: a worse signal than if they had rejected everyone. |
| **Auto `no-decision` is read as a rejection** | It is not one, and must never render as one (DT-08). It names the platform as actor and absence as cause. | "This closed without a decision. Nobody answered." Never "not selected". |
| **Post's decide-by is in a different timezone than the applicant** | Date semantics are type-scoped (04.01.01 D-10): presence types carry venue-local wall-clock, delivery types an absolute instant. Any `no-decision` delay (Q-02) computes in the post's own semantics — never the viewer's. | "You'll hear by 14 Aug" means the post's 14 Aug. |
| **Zero applicants; poster closes** | No obligation, no gate, no friction. **The mechanism must be invisible when there is no debt** — a poster who never ghosted anyone should never meet this feature. | Delist. Done. Nothing about obligations. |
| **Blind post (04.03.04)** | The applicant is anonymised to the poster, not to themselves. Disposition and reason deliver normally. | Unchanged for the applicant. |
| **400 pitches (A&R scale)** | **The integrity/collapse question, and 04.04.01 DT-09 sharpened it**: triage scales (400 taps ≈ 30 min), assessment doesn't. But a pitch has **no post criteria to scope a bulk action to** (04.04.01's note on 04.03.05), so the domain's main volume lever is unavailable exactly where volume is worst. | See sub-domain Q-02 / 04.03.05 Q-02 — unresolved, and the missing lever is the reason. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Obligation surface opened | Count skeleton. **The count is never rendered as 0 while loading** — a false "you're clear" is the one wrong answer this surface can give. |
| Empty | No submissions on any slot | Poster: nothing. No debt, no mechanism, no nag (see edge cases). Applicant: n/a. |
| Owed (Populated) | ≥1 undispositioned submission | Poster: the count, per slot, in people — "12 people are waiting on you", never "12 open tasks". Applicant: `submitted`, with age shown plainly (04.05.04 D-02). |
| Answered | Disposition set | Applicant: the outcome, and the reason if one exists (D-10). Poster: count decremented. |
| Partial | Some slots clear, some owing | "Drums and bass are done. 4 people are still waiting on keys." Derived, never set (D-07). |
| Delisted | Poster stopped the flow (D-05) | "Nobody new can apply. You still owe 12 people an answer." Delisting is not a state of the obligation — it is a state of the slot. |
| Debt | Poster attempts a **new post** while owing (DT-05) | "You owe 40 people an answer from your last 2 posts." Block or warn is **Q-01**. |
| Abandoned | Decide-by passed, cards untouched, poster gone | The case the gate cannot reach (DT-03). What fires here is **Q-02**. |
| No decision | `no-decision` set (Q-02 permitting) | "This closed without a decision. Nobody answered." Never a rejection (DT-08). |
| Error | The obligation surface fails to load | Retry. **No disposition action is offered against an unloaded surface** (04.04.01 D-09's principle) — a "no" fired at nothing is worse than a delay. |
| Clear | Every submission on every slot dispositioned | Post derives clear. Response signals update (CX-01). |
