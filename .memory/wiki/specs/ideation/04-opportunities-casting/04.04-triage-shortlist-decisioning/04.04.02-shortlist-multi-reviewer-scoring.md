# Feature: Shortlist & Multi-Reviewer Scoring

> **Number**: 04.04.02
> **Parent**: [Triage, Shortlist & Decisioning](./04.04-triage-shortlist-decisioning-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The deliberation stage: the small set that gets listened to properly, and the several people who
argue about it. Casting in music is rarely one person's call — a band picks a member together, a
venue and a promoter agree a support act, a producer defers to the artist and the label. This
feature exists because the sweep's triage candidate (04.04.01) assumed a single reviewer, and a
single-reviewer model cannot express the most common decision structure in the industry: the
**collective** call. Its job is to make disagreement legible and the decision auditable — never to
make the decision itself, which belongs to the band or org whose governance it inherits (D-03).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Band decisions are collective, and this is where that becomes real — every member reviews the shortlisted bassists, and the band as an **entity** (domain 01, per `personas.md`) is what decides, not the person who posted. The MD or bandleader may hold a casting vote, or may not, and that is a band-governance fact the platform inherits rather than invents. Reviews audition media, scores/comments, sees who else has and hasn't reviewed. |
| Producer | Full | Invites the artist and often the label into the shortlist review. Their persona is the session owner but not always the decider — a Producer shortlisting a topline writer usually needs the artist to agree, and their real role here is *convening* the decision: assembling the set, inviting reviewers, holding the shortlist until acceptance. |
| Operator | Full | Venue and promoter review support acts together — routinely two organisations with different interests (the promoter wants a draw, the venue wants a room that empties safely). A shared shortlist across org boundaries is the normal case, not an edge one; cross-org access is inherited from 01, not defined here. |
| Fan | None | Never reviews and never appears here. Casting is professional work with no fan-facing surface; a Fan has no submission to be shortlisted and no seat at the review. Explicit absence recorded so downstream spec writers gate this feature to professional/org contexts only. |

## Behavior

Shortlisting is expensive by design — this is the stage where audition media actually gets listened
to, evidence gets read properly, and people disagree. It is the small end of a funnel that 04.04.01
made coarse on purpose: triage was cheap and fast; the shortlist is slow and deep.

| Capability | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Small set, deep review | Triage was coarse (04.04.01 D-01); this is where the real assessment happens |
| Multiple reviewers | The decision structure is usually collective (DT-01) |
| Scoring / notes | So disagreement is legible rather than buried in a group chat |
| Per-reviewer progress | The stall is usually one person; the platform must show *who* hasn't looked |
| Reviewer invitation | Reviewers may not be able to see the queue — access starts at the shortlist (CX-01 synthesis Q3) |
| Disagreement surfaced, not averaged | A collapsed number hides the split that actually matters (DT-02) |
| Hold semantics | The shortlist is held until acceptance lands (CX-02) |

### Happy Path

1. Poster advances a small set of candidates from triage (CX-01) into the shortlist.
2. Poster invites reviewers — bandmates, the artist, the promoter. Invitation grants access that
   starts here, not at the queue (CX-01 Q3).
3. Each reviewer opens the set, listens to the audition media, reads evidence, and scores/comments.
   Blind un-blinds at this boundary if blind review was enabled upstream (CX-05).
4. The platform shows per-reviewer progress and the spread of scores — including who hasn't yet
   looked — without averaging them into a single ranking.
5. The group converges (in the app or off it). Whoever holds casting authority per 01's governance
   marks the decision; the offer path to #1 opens (04.04.03).
6. The shortlist is **held**, not discarded — #2 matters if #1 declines (CX-02).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

Primary failure modes for Level 1 — the ones that actually bite here. Long-tail governance detail is
marked and deferred where an honest answer needs 01's authority model or the BE spec.

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Reviewers disagree and don't converge | The normal case (DT-02). The platform surfaces the divergence and does **not** auto-resolve, average, or force a tiebreak — a band's argument is theirs. The decision simply cannot advance to an offer until a human with casting authority (inherited from 01) resolves it. | "No consensus yet" — per-candidate score spread and each reviewer's note shown side by side; the offer button is gated until someone with casting authority decides. No platform tiebreak exists (Q-01 resolved by D-02/D-03); whether 01 models a casting vote is `[PENDING — Q-03]`. |
| Scores averaged into a ranking | **Tempting and wrong.** 4,4,4,4 and 5,5,5,1 average identically and are completely different situations — the second has a bandmate who will resent this hire for two years. The platform never presents an averaged rank. | Distribution/spread, never a single mean. See Q-01. |
| Reviewer sees who else rejected a candidate | Attribution of verdicts is governed by a per-shortlist setting, not a hardcoded default (DT-03) — in a band "who rejected me" is socially explosive, but hiding it destroys accountability. Level 1 supports both attributed and anonymous-to-each-other scoring; the *default* is a governance choice, not the platform's to impose. | Each reviewer always sees their own verdict and the aggregate spread; visibility of *whose* verdict is which follows the shortlist's attribution setting. Default selection: `[PENDING — Q-04]` |
| A non-responding reviewer stalls the decision | Common and infuriating. The platform surfaces the stall by name and lets the poster nudge, but does **not** silently auto-advance past a missing reviewer — proceeding without them is a governance decision that belongs to 01's casting authority, not a timeout. | "3 of 4 reviewed — [name] hasn't looked." Actions: Nudge · Proceed (enabled only for a holder of casting authority). Escalation/auto-expiry timing: `[PENDING — /write-be-spec]` |
| Held candidate takes other work | The hold has no exclusivity teeth and shouldn't — a shortlisted player is free to accept elsewhere. But the poster's mental model assumes they're still there (CX-02 Q5), so the hold must expire *loudly* rather than rot silently. | If #2 signals unavailability, the poster is notified; if an offer is later sent to a candidate who has gone, it returns "no longer available" and the poster moves to the next. Hold has no lock. |
| Invited reviewer is outside the org (promoter reviewing at a venue's shortlist) | Cross-org access — normal for Operators, and a permission boundary this domain consumes rather than owns. | Routed to **01 (orgs)** + `/create-prd-security` for the access model. |
| Shortlisted applicant is never told | A shortlist is not an offer; telling raises hope the poster may not honour (CX-01 Q4). Default at Level 1: applicants are **not** auto-notified of shortlisting. The poster may optionally signal "still in consideration." | Applicant sees no state change unless the poster explicitly signals. Silence about the *rank* is correct; the applicant is answered with a date instead (04.04.01 D-06). Q-02 resolved. |
| Two reviewers score the same candidate at once | Concurrent scoring is expected — reviewers work independently and asynchronously. Each reviewer's score is their own record; there is no shared-cell contention because scores are per-reviewer, not a single mutable field. | Both scores land; the spread updates for everyone. Real-time refresh vs poll-on-open: `[PENDING — /write-fe-spec]` |
| All shortlisted candidates withdraw before a decision | The set empties out from under the reviewers. The decision cannot complete; the poster is pushed back toward the queue (04.04.01) to advance more, or the opportunity stalls. | "No candidates remain on this shortlist — advance more from your queue." |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Shortlist opening / media fetching | Skeleton of the candidate set; audition media shows a loading affordance until ready |
| Empty | Nothing advanced from the queue yet | "Advance candidates from your queue" with a link back to 04.04.01 |
| Assembled | Candidates advanced | The small set with full evidence + audition media, ready to review |
| Under review | Reviewers invited | Per-reviewer progress — including, explicitly, who hasn't looked yet |
| Diverged | Reviewers disagree | Disagreement surfaced as a spread, not averaged (D-02); offer path gated until casting authority resolves it (D-03) |
| Resolved | Casting authority converges/decides | Offer path opens (04.04.03) |
| Held | Offer out to #1 | Shortlist frozen pending acceptance (CX-02) |
| Released | Acceptance landed | Remaining candidates dispositioned (04.05.01) |
| Error | Media fails to load / invite fails / access denied | Inline error scoped to the failed item (media, invite, or reviewer access) with retry; the rest of the shortlist stays usable |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **The decider is usually not the poster, and often not a person.** A band decides as an entity; a venue and promoter decide jointly; a producer defers to the artist. The sweep's triage candidate assumes one reviewer with authority, which is the least common structure in music | Reasoning from `personas.md` — "bands, labels, studios, venues and agencies are first-class entities that book, sign, own and get paid independently of their members", and the Band is modelled as an entity in domain 01 | ✅ CONFIRMED — multi-reviewer is the default, not an enterprise feature. It also means **casting authority is inherited from 01's org/band governance**, not defined here. Who in a band can accept an offer on the band's behalf is a domain-01 fact this feature consumes. Logged as Q-03/parent Q-03. |
| DT-02 | **Averaging scores is the obvious design and it destroys the information that matters.** A candidate scoring 4,4,4,4 and one scoring 5,5,5,1 average identically, and they are completely different situations — the second one has a bandmate who will resent this hire for two years | Reasoning about what a score is for in a collective decision | ✅ CONFIRMED — surface disagreement rather than collapse it. The platform's job in a band decision is to make the argument legible, not to settle it. A product that hands a band a ranked list has quietly taken a decision that belongs to them, which is exactly what `decision-classification.md` says not to do — and here it would be doing it to *users*, not just in the spec. |
| DT-03 | **Anonymous-to-each-other scoring is the "safe" default and it is a trap either way.** Hiding who rejected whom protects the band socially; showing it preserves the accountability a band actually needs to live with the hire. Picking either as the platform default is taking a governance decision that isn't the platform's | Reasoning from parent Q-03 ("who rejected me" is socially explosive) against `decision-classification.md` (don't decide product/governance for the user) | ✅ CONFIRMED (as a design constraint) — attribution is a **per-shortlist governance setting**, not a hardcoded default. The platform supports both modes; which is default for a given band/org is inherited from 01 or chosen at shortlist creation, not imposed. The specific default-selection UX is Q-04. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Multi-reviewer is the default**, not an add-on | The collective decision is the common structure in music (DT-01), not the enterprise edge case. |
| D-02 | Disagreement is **surfaced, never averaged** | Averaging destroys the information that matters and quietly takes a decision that belongs to the band (DT-02). |
| D-03 | Casting authority is **inherited from 01**, not defined here — as `01.03.02` D-05's two flat axes: the action's verb in the edge's `activities` and the post's commercial domain in its `domains`, ANDed at the call site. On a band's membership edge the domains are universal; a representation edge (an MD or agent casting for an artist) must carry the post's domain, so a live-booking-only agent cannot decide a session call | Bands and orgs are first-class entities with their own governance (`personas.md`). This domain must not invent a second, conflicting authority model — and two flat lists are at most 12 plain-language statements per edge, never a permission grid (`01.03.03` D-02, DT-02). |
| D-04 | The shortlist is **held, not discarded**, until acceptance lands | #2 exists for the case where #1 declines (CX-02). |
| D-05 | Reviewer attribution is a **per-shortlist governance setting**, not a hardcoded default | Both anonymous-to-each-other and attributed scoring are legitimate; picking one for the user is a governance decision that isn't the platform's (DT-03). |
