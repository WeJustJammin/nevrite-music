# Feature: Offer & Acceptance

> **Number**: 04.04.03
> **Parent**: [Triage, Shortlist & Decisioning](./04.04-triage-shortlist-decisioning-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The moment a candidate becomes a hire: an offer with terms, an acceptance, and the handoff that
turns an opportunity into real work (04.05.03). It is the smallest feature in the domain and the
most consequential, because acceptance is where the platform stops describing intentions and starts
creating obligations — a booking, an engagement, a session, a fee someone is owed.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Both sides, often on the same day — offers a dep gig in the morning, accepts a session in the afternoon. Accepting is the point where their calendar (the availability cross-cut) must actually change, or they will be double-booked by the platform that was supposed to prevent it. |
| Producer | Full | Offers session and brief work. Their acceptance creates a Services engagement (05) per the parent domain's stated handoff, and — critically — it is the last quiet moment before work starts where a split conversation is still easy. |
| Operator | Full | Offers day rates, support slots and crew engagements. Their acceptance creates a booking (17) against perishable inventory, and the deposit question arrives with it. |
| Fan | None | Never offers, never accepts. |

## Behavior

The offer carries terms that were declared at post time (04.01.03) but may have moved — a
negotiated rate, a changed date, an added requirement. The offer, not the post, is what is accepted.

### Happy Path

1. Shortlist resolves (04.04.02) or the cascade fires (04.04.04).
2. Offer issued with terms — fee shape and value, date, scope, location.
3. Candidate sees the offer against what was originally posted, with any deltas shown.
4. Candidate accepts. Availability updates. The shortlist releases; remaining candidates are dispositioned (04.05.01).
5. Handoff creates the real thing — booking (17), engagement (05), session (07). See 04.05.03.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Terms drifted from the post | The offer's terms are what binds, and the delta must be **shown**, not silently applied — this is the bait-and-switch vector arriving one stage later than 04.01.03 anticipated | "Posted at £200, offered at £150." Visible, always. |
| Candidate declines | Falls to #2 (CX-02) — who may have gone cold | #2 gets an offer for a date they may have filled |
| Candidate never responds | The chain stalls. Decline must be cheap; silence must have a fuse. | Offer expiry |
| Two offers accepted for the same slot | The urgent-fill race (CX-04) — see 04.04.04 |
| Candidate accepts, then cancels | **Is acceptance a contract?** Unresolved and consequential — a dep who accepts and pulls out at 5pm has caused real harm and the platform recorded the promise. | See Q-01 |
| Acceptance doesn't update availability | The platform double-books the person it exists to unfragment | Availability update must be atomic with acceptance — see Q-02 |
| Band accepts — but who in the band? | Authority is 01's governance question (04.04.02 D-03) | See 04.04.02 Q-03 |
| Offer accepted, work happens, no credit captured | **The thesis's failure mode, at the exact moment it's preventable** | See DT-02 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Offered | Offer issued | Terms with deltas from the post shown |
| Expiring | Fuse running | Countdown — visible to both sides |
| Accepted | Candidate accepts | Handoff fires (04.05.03); availability updates |
| Declined | Candidate declines | Falls to #2 (CX-02) |
| Expired | Fuse burned | Same as declined, but the candidate learns they lost it by not answering |
| Withdrawn | Poster pulls the offer | A disposition; the candidate is owed the reason |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Acceptance is the first moment the platform creates an obligation rather than describing one** — and the domain has not decided whether it is a contract | Reasoning about what acceptance does downstream: a booking with a deposit (17), an engagement with a fee (05), a slot that is now unsellable (16) | ✅ CONFIRMED as a genuine and consequential gap. If acceptance is binding, cancellation needs penalties, disputes (24) and probably terms nobody has drafted. If it is not binding, it is a handshake with a database entry — which is still better than WhatsApp, but the Operator's perishable inventory (`personas.md`) makes a non-binding acceptance an expensive fiction. **Not an agent call** — logged as Q-01 and sub-domain Q-02. |
| DT-02 | **This is the split conversation's last easy moment, and the domain is about to walk past it.** `personas.md` names the Producer's worst accidental behaviour as closing a project without capturing splits — "we'll do it later" — and says outright that "the design must make the lazy path the correct path". Acceptance is the last point where everyone is present, friendly, and has not yet done any work to argue about | Reasoning from D-18 and the Producer persona's stated failure mode against this feature's position in the flow | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it may be the highest-leverage insight in the domain. The offer/acceptance moment is where a provisional split could be agreed at zero social cost, because nothing has happened yet and nobody is invested. Waiting until the session (07) or the release (12) means asking people to agree *after* they have opinions about their own contribution. ❌ REJECTED as buildable here — the split instrument belongs to 09 (Rights). What this domain owns is **the trigger and the timing**. Logged as Q-03 and raised in the domain CX. |
| DT-03 | A domain expert would expect **acceptance to be atomic with the calendar**, because the whole product promise is unfragmentation. Accepting a gig and not becoming busy is the platform reproducing the exact failure it was built to fix | Reasoning from the availability cross-cut (04.02.02 D-01) and the Operator's double-booking pain in `personas.md` | ✅ CONFIRMED — availability update must be atomic with acceptance, not eventually-consistent. A dep who accepts and remains "available" will be cascaded another gig for the same night by this platform's own matching (04.02.02). That is a self-inflicted double-booking and it is worse than the paper diary it replaced. Logged as Q-02. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The **offer's terms bind**, not the post's, and deltas are always shown | Terms drift between post and offer; hiding the drift is bait-and-switch one stage later than 04.01.03 guards. |
| D-02 | Offers **expire** | Silence stalls the whole chain and #2 goes cold while waiting on someone who was never going to answer. |
| D-03 | Availability updates **atomically** with acceptance | Otherwise the platform double-books the person it exists to unfragment (DT-03). |
| D-04 | The split-capture trigger belongs here; the split **instrument** belongs to 09 | This domain owns timing, not rights machinery (DT-02). |
