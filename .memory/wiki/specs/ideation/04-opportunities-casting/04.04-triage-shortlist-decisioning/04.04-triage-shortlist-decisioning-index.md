## Overview

How a pool of candidates becomes one hire. The review queue that makes 40 submissions tractable, the
shortlist that several people argue over, the offer that someone accepts — and the urgent variant
where none of that happens because it is 9pm and the drummer just dropped out. This sub-domain
absorbed the sweep's "A&R Review Queue, Triage & Decisioning" candidate, because a bandleader
triaging 40 dep applicants and an A&R triaging 400 demos need the same machine.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities that chain and contend. The queue
feeds the shortlist, the shortlist feeds the offer, and the urgent-fill path *bypasses all three* —
which makes it a genuine internal conflict rather than a variation. Multi-reviewer scoring and
first-accept-wins are opposite theories of how a decision gets made, and both are correct for
different opportunities.

| D-02 | **Urgent fill is a peer of the review queue, not a mode of it** | Deliberate review and first-accept-wins are opposite theories of decisioning.
