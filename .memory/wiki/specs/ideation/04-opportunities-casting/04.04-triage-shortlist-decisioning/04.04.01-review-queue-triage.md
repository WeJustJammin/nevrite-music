# Feature: Review Queue & Triage

> **Number**: 04.04.01
> **Parent**: [Triage, Shortlist & Decisioning](./04.04-triage-shortlist-decisioning-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The machine that makes a pile of submissions reviewable. Forty dep applicants, thirty crew
applications, four hundred demos — all the same problem: an unreviewable pile is not reviewed, and
an unreviewed pile is where ghosting comes from. This absorbed the sweep's "A&R Review Queue"
candidate, because triage is not an A&R feature; it is what every poster in this domain needs the
moment their post works.

The feature's real subject is **the cost of a decision**. Sub-domain D-03 makes 04.05.01's
close-out obligation depend on this file: an obligation to answer everyone is only honourable if
answering is a few taps. Every decision below is chosen to drive the per-candidate cost of a
*truthful* answer toward zero — because the alternative to a cheap "no" is not a considered "no",
it is silence.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | As poster — a bandleader triaging dep applicants, usually fast and on a phone between other things (soundcheck, a train, a break). Their triage is mostly recognition ("know them, know them, don't know them"), which is exactly what the evidence layer (04.03.02) can make legible for the third case. They are also the persona most likely to hold **several live queues at once** — a dep call for Saturday, a member-wanted ad, a session enquiry in 05 — which is where their queue is abandoned wholesale rather than card-by-card (DT-03). Their queue is single-triager by default; a Band entity (domain 01) can grant queue access to members, and that is the only way a second triager exists (D-08). |
| Producer | Full | Triages session and brief responses. Triages on evidence first, audition media second — and their queue is where the audition media actually gets listened to, which is a time cost nothing else in the domain matches (DT-09 quantifies it: the taps scale, the listening does not). They are also the persona `personas.md` names as the deferrer ("we'll do it later" is their worst accidental behaviour), so the hold blocker (D-05) is aimed squarely at them: they may defer, but not without naming what they are waiting for. |
| Operator | Full | Triages crew and support applications. Uniquely, much of their triage is genuinely binary — insurance or no insurance — which means their queue can be largely mechanical where others' cannot, and they are the persona for whom criterion-scoped bulk action (D-07) does the most work: "reject everyone without liability cover" is one action, one honest reason, and thirty answered applicants. (The criterion is a **slot**; the instrument that fills it is named by the jurisdiction profile, never by this domain — 04.01.04 D-07.) Their eligibility criteria are **declared, not verified** (04.01.04 D-04), so the queue must never present a self-declared cert with the tick of a verified fact. |
| Fan | None | Never reviews. A Fan has no post and no candidates. The exclusion is structural, not a permission: a Fan cannot post (domain Role Matrix), cannot pitch (04.03.05 D-04), and therefore no queue can exist under a Fan account to be permissioned. |

## Behavior

Triage is a three-way call kept deliberately coarse — in, out, or **held against a named blocker**:

| Action | Effect | Disposition fired? |
|--------|--------|--------------------|
| Advance | To shortlist (CX-01) | No — still live |
| Reject | Out | **Yes, immediately** (04.05.01) — except in a multi-triager queue, where a reject is a vote, not a disposition (D-08) |
| Hold | Deferred **against a named blocker with a resolve-by date** (D-05) | No — but the applicant is still told a date (D-06) |

### The hold blocker vocabulary — closed, and every entry has a resolution event

The breadth pass called the third option "maybe" and identified it as ghosting's actual mechanism.
Deepening decomposed it (DT-04): "maybe" was three different things wearing one label, and only one
of them was legitimate. What survives is a **hold**, and a hold is not offerable without a blocker.

| Blocker | Meaning | Owner of the blocker | Resolution event the platform can detect |
|---------|---------|----------------------|------------------------------------------|
| `awaiting-media` | Audition task set but not yet delivered (04.03.03) | Applicant | Media lands, **or** the audition deadline passes |
| `awaiting-reviewer` | Needs a bandmate / the artist / the promoter to look (04.04.02) | A named reviewer | That reviewer acts, **or** their review window closes |
| `awaiting-answer` | The poster asked the candidate a question (fee, travel, doubling) | Applicant | Applicant replies, **or** the reply window closes |
| `awaiting-eligibility` | A declared criterion is outstanding (04.01.04) | Applicant | The criterion is satisfied, **or** the post's decide-by passes |
| `awaiting-outcome` | Depends on whether an earlier candidate accepts | Another candidate | The offer resolves — accept or decline (04.04.03, CX-02) |

**`awaiting-outcome` is not a hold, it is a shortlist rank.** It is listed because posters will reach
for it, and selecting it **converts the card to a shortlist entry at rank N** (04.04.02) rather than
holding it in the queue. That is the second of the three "maybe" meanings being routed to the feature
built for it, per D-05.

**There is no blocker for "I don't want to decide yet."** That was the third meaning, and it is the
one that produces ghosting. It is made **unrepresentable**, not prohibited — the same move the domain
already made against applicant-side fees (domain D-03; 04.01.03 D-02). A poster who wants to defer
without a reason has two buttons available: advance, or reject.

### Rules that bound every action

| Rule | Statement | Source |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **Irreversibility rule** | The irreversible call (reject) is never available on incomplete information. Advance and hold are recoverable and stay available. | D-09 |
| **One candidate, one card** | Regardless of how many times an identity submitted. | D-10 |
| **Self-triage is unrepresentable** | A candidate cannot triage a queue they are a candidate in, and cannot see it, regardless of what 01 granted them. | D-11 |
| **Disagreement resolves upward** | In a multi-triager queue, a contested candidate goes to the shortlist. Never to a reject. | D-08 |
| **Bulk action is criterion-scoped** | Reject-many exists only when scoped to a criterion that was live at submit time. There is no "reject remaining". | D-07 |

### Happy Path

1. Submissions land (04.03.01) or pitches land (04.03.05) — **the same queue, not separate inboxes**
   (D-03). Each new arrival appends; the queue is live, not batched (except 04.07 open calls — see
   Edge Cases).
2. Every arriving candidate is **already eligible by construction** — a candidate failing a required
   criterion was blocked at submit time with the reason named (04.01.04). The queue therefore never
   contains a mechanically-disqualified card, and "filter the pile" is not a lever available here.
3. Poster opens the queue. The **card-by-card** view presents, per candidate, in this order:
   **(a)** identity, or the anonymised handle on a blind post (04.03.04); **(b)** criteria
   satisfaction as a checklist with gaps shown, not hidden (04.01.04); **(c)** **at most 3 evidence
   citations**, selected against *this post's* requirements (04.03.02 D-02), attested citations
   ordered before self-claimed ones and **visually distinct from them** (04.03.02 D-04, carried
   through here per DT-05); **(d)** audition media, if any, playable but **never required to play
   before a call can be made**.
4. Poster triages — advance / reject / hold. **One tap per call, no scroll required to reach the
   three actions on a 390px-wide viewport**, because the phone-between-other-things case is the
   modal one, not the desk case.
5. **Rejects fire dispositions immediately** (04.05.01, D-02). The applicant is answered while the
   poster is still on the next card.
6. **Advances go to the shortlist** (CX-01). **Holds carry a blocker and a resolve-by date**
   defaulting to — and never exceeding — the post's decide-by date (04.01.01).
7. **When a blocker resolves, the card returns to the queue as actionable** and is surfaced at the
   top of the default order — because a resolved blocker is the cheapest decision now available.
   It does not interrupt the card currently in hand.
8. **The applicant is told a date, not a state** (D-06): "You'll hear by 14 Aug." Never "you're a
   maybe" (which raises hope and leaks a rank), never silence (which is the void this domain exists
   to close).
9. Queue empties. The close-out obligation is satisfiable because triage was cheap (sub-domain
   D-03), and the post can close (04.05.01).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **The "maybe" pile never resolves** | **RESOLVED (Q-01) by D-05.** The pile existed because "maybe" was unconditional. A hold now requires a blocker from the closed vocabulary, every blocker has a detectable resolution event, and every hold has a resolve-by date bounded by the post's decide-by (04.01.01). There is no button for "I don't want to decide." | Held cards show the blocker, its owner, and the date: "Waiting on your audition file · resolve by 14 Aug." Poster's queue header shows held count with blockers grouped by owner. |
| **A hold's resolve-by date passes unresolved** | The card returns to the queue flagged **overdue** and is the first card the poster sees. It is **not auto-rejected here** — auto-disposition on expiry is 04.05.01 Q-02's lever and a values call this feature must not pre-empt. What this feature guarantees is that a silent pile becomes a **visible, dated, actionable debt**. | "3 held past their date. They're waiting on you." |
| **400 submissions (A&R scale)** | **RESOLVED (Q-02): triage scales; assessment doesn't — and what remains is Q-03, not a new question.** 400 taps at one tap each is ~30 minutes and tractable. 400 × 30s of audition media is 3.3 hours and is not (DT-09). The queue's available levers are mechanical, not evaluative: eligibility already pre-filtered the pool at submit (04.01.04), criterion-scoped bulk action (D-07) collapses declared-criteria decisions into one honest act, and the ≤3-citation pre-summary keeps the per-card glance constant as N grows. **The only remaining lever at 400 is ranking — which is Q-03, the domain's sharpest fairness hazard.** Q-02 was a restatement of Q-03 at volume. | Poster with 400: a criterion-scoped bulk bar ("312 don't have a released record") plus card-by-card for the rest. Never an auto-ranked list unless Q-03 says so. |
| **Poster bulk-rejects to clear the debt** | **The gate's named failure mode (04.05.01 DT-02 / CX-01 synthesis Q5), foreclosed by D-07.** There is no "reject remaining". A bulk reject must scope to a criterion, and **the criterion is what the applicant is told**. That converts bulk rejection from gaming into the most honest rejection in the product: true, impersonal, machine-generated, and un-resentable (DT-04). | Confirm names the count and the criterion: "Reject 312 candidates who don't meet 'released record'?" Applicant receives: "Not selected — this post required a released record." |
| **Poster adds a criterion *after* submissions land, to justify a bulk reject** | The bait-and-switch race (04.01.04 Q-02) arriving in the queue. **Bulk action may only scope to criteria that were live at the moment each candidate submitted.** A criterion added later scopes to zero existing candidates. | "This criterion was added after these 312 applied — it can't be used to reject them." |
| **Two triagers disagree — one advances, one rejects** | **RESOLVED. The non-destructive call wins the race** (D-08, D-09). Advance is recoverable; reject fires an irreversible disposition. The candidate becomes **contested** and moves to the shortlist, where disagreement is the designed content (04.04.02 D-02: surfaced, never averaged). **The queue never resolves an argument — it declines to fire an irreversible disposition on a contested candidate.** | Both triagers: "Dave advanced, you rejected — moved to shortlist as contested." |
| **Two triagers make the same call** | Idempotent. One card, one outcome, **exactly one disposition** (04.05.01 D-02 depends on this). The second call is a no-op, not a second event. | Second triager: "Already rejected." |
| **Unanimous reject in a multi-triager queue** | Disposition fires immediately — D-02 holds. Unanimity is what makes it the band's call rather than one member's. | Applicant: the answer, at the moment the last member calls it. |
| **A single member of a band rejects unilaterally** | **Not a disposition.** In a multi-triager queue a reject is a vote (D-08). The band is the decider (04.04.02 DT-01, D-03) and this feature must not invent a second authority model. Casting authority is inherited from 01. | Triager: "Recorded. Waiting on 2 more." Applicant sees nothing — no half-answer is sent. |
| **Same triager acts on two devices** | Last call wins for advance↔hold. **A fired reject cannot be unfired** — the applicant has already been told (D-02). Reversal is CX-01 synthesis Q5's territory and lives at 04.05.01. | "You already rejected this one on your phone." |
| **A candidate holds triage rights on the queue they're in** (multi-hyphenate: a musician applies to a session call at a studio org they co-own) | **Self-triage is unrepresentable** (D-11). The exclusion is automatic and **overrides any grant from 01** — governance grants access to a queue, never to your own candidacy. They do not see the queue at all: seeing the competition's evidence is a worse harm than seeing your own state. | "You've applied to this one — you can't review it." The queue is absent, not greyed out. |
| **Multi-hyphenate is a candidate in queue A and a triager in queue B** | **Explicitly not a conflict.** Stage is a property of the submission's relationship to a post, not of the person (CX-01 synthesis Q1) — "a person can be shortlisted for one thing and rejected for another on the same day". | Both surfaces work, independently. |
| **Poster never opens the queue at all** | The post worked and the applicants are in a void — worse than a post that failed. This feature contributes the **detection**: at **50% of the elapsed window between publish and decide-by, with ≥1 candidate and 0 triage actions**, the queue is `dormant` and the poster is told the cost in people, not in tasks. | "12 people are waiting on you. You haven't opened this." The gate itself is 04.05.01; absence is unreachable by a gate (04.05.01 DT-03) and this feature does not claim otherwise. |
| **Poster triages 39 of 40 and stops** | One person waiting is still a debt. `dormant` is a function of ≥1 untriaged card, not of a percentage of the pile. | "1 person is still waiting." |
| **Evidence layer (02) is unreachable** | **Reject is disabled for that card** (D-09). Rejecting on absent evidence is rejecting on a platform failure, and the applicant would be told "not selected" for a fact nobody saw. Advance and hold remain — both recoverable. | "Couldn't load their credits. You can advance or hold, but not reject on this." |
| **Audition media won't play** | Same principle (D-09). Reject disabled; a hold is offered pre-filled with `awaiting-media`, resolving when playback succeeds. | "Their file won't play. Held — we'll tell you when it's back." |
| **Criteria (04.01.04) unresolvable for a card** | Same principle. The checklist renders as unavailable rather than as all-gaps — an unloaded criterion must never present as a failed one. | "Couldn't check their eligibility." Reject disabled. |
| **A cited credit is disputed or revoked while the candidate sits in the queue** | The card's evidence changes under the poster (04.03.02 CX-02 synthesis Q5). Untriaged: the card is marked and reordered to the top, because the poster's basis for deciding changed. Already advanced: it is the shortlist's problem (04.04.02) and this feature does not reach back. Already rejected: nothing happens — the answer stands and is not revisited. | "One of their credits was withdrawn since they applied." |
| **Applicant edits their submission after being triaged** | **A rejected candidate cannot edit their way back into the queue.** The card records the version the triager acted on. Whether a submission is mutable at all is 04.03.01's call; the queue's rule is that a post-decision edit never resurrects a card. | Applicant: "This has been decided — edits won't reopen it." |
| **One identity submits 50 times** | **One candidate, one card** (D-10). Collapsing is structural — otherwise the queue is spammable by construction and the multi-submitter buys queue real-estate. Rate limiting at submit is 04.03.01 + 24's job; card collapse is this feature's independent guarantee. | Poster sees one card. Nothing indicates the 50 — it is not evidence about the person, it is an artefact. |
| **Blind post** | Anonymised set (04.03.04); triage on media and stripped evidence only. **This is not a cheaper queue with names removed — it removes the thing triage actually runs on** (DT-06). Recognition (DT-02) is unavailable, so every card becomes an assessment, and the queue's cost approaches the shortlist's. | Blind queues surface their own cost honestly at post time, not at triage time: "Blind review means you'll assess all 40, not recognise 30." |
| **Blind post at volume** | The two hazards compound: no recognition × 400 cards. Sub-domain D-03's guarantee (the obligation is honourable because triage is cheap) does not hold for a blind post at scale. **Recorded as a real limit rather than papered over** — see DT-06 and domain Q-08. | — |
| **Submission arrives after triage started** | Queue is live, not a batch. New cards append; **the ordering never changes underneath a card the poster is currently holding** — a re-sort mid-decision is how a poster rejects the wrong person. | New arrivals appear as a count at the header, applied on the next card fetch. |
| **Open call (04.07) — deliberately batched** | The queue is **locked until the deadline**: a count, no cards. Peeking early would reintroduce exactly the first-mover advantage that batching exists to remove. | "412 entries. Opens for review on 1 Sep." |
| **Urgent fill (04.04.04) fills the post while the queue is live** | **A collision the CX map models as a bypass but not as a race** (DT-07, CX-03). The cascade's first-yes ends the post; every live queue card is instantly stale and is dispositioned `filled elsewhere` (04.05.01) at that moment — the winner's acceptance triggering the losers' answers (04.05.01 D-02) applies to a winner who never entered the queue. | Poster: "Filled by the cascade — your 12 remaining applicants have been told." Applicants: "Filled elsewhere." |
| **Offer accepted from the shortlist (04.04.03) while triage continues** | Same mechanism, ordinary direction. Untriaged cards are dispositioned `filled elsewhere` in one action. Untriaged is not un-owed. | "Accepted. Your 9 untriaged applicants have been answered." |
| **Post's decide-by date passes mid-triage** | Post auto-closes (04.01.01); **the obligation survives** (04.05.01). Held cards' resolve-by dates cannot outlive it by construction, so no hold is orphaned by the close. | "This closed automatically. You still owe 12 people an answer." |
| **Poster wants to reject with a reason** | Feeds 04.05.01. A reason is the difference between a rejection and a non-answer — and it is the thing posters most want to avoid writing, for a reason the domain has not yet named: **in a small industry a permanent, attributed, free-text "no" is a liability, so required reasons produce lies, not information** (DT-04). This feature's contribution to 04.05.01 Q-03 is D-07: **the criterion is the only rejection reason that is simultaneously true, cheap, impersonal and socially survivable.** | Optional free text; a criterion-scoped reason offered pre-filled and true. |
| **All candidates withdraw** | The queue empties without the poster acting. **This is information about the post, not an absence of applicants** — usually the fee (04.01.03) or the date. No obligation is owed to a withdrawer (04.05.01). | "All 4 applicants withdrew." Routed toward the post, not the pile. |
| **Matching found nobody, so nothing ever lands** | Empty state carries the honest reason from 04.02.02 rather than an empty box that reads as platform failure. | "No applicants yet — 3 people match your criteria. 'Travel radius 5mi' is why." |
| **A triager's org role is revoked mid-triage** (removed from the band) | In-flight, uncommitted calls are void. **Dispositions already fired stand** — the applicant was told, and un-telling them is not available. Governance is 01's; the queue consumes the revocation, it does not adjudicate it. | "You no longer have access to this queue." |
| **The posting org dissolves while the queue is live** (the band splits) | The obligation (04.05.01) outlives the entity that incurred it, and 04.05.01 D-04 forbids applicants self-closing to tidy it away. Who inherits the debt is an 01 boundary this feature cannot resolve alone. | See Q-05. |
| **Invited shortlist reviewer tries to reach the queue** | Blocked. Their access starts at the shortlist, not before (CX-01 synthesis Q3) — a promoter invited to pick a support act has no business seeing who the venue rejected. | "You've been invited to the shortlist." The queue is not exposed. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Queue opened | Card skeleton with the **three action buttons live immediately** — the actions must not wait on evidence or media (D-09 disables reject until evidence resolves; it does not delay advance or hold) |
| Empty | Post published, no submissions | "No applicants yet" + the honest reason if matching found nobody (04.02.02) |
| Live | Submissions arriving | Count, unreviewed badge; new arrivals never re-sort the card in hand |
| Triaging (Populated) | Poster reviewing | Card-by-card: identity, criteria checklist with gaps, ≤3 evidence citations with attested/self-claimed visually distinct, media optional |
| Held | Card deferred against a blocker | The blocker, its owner, and the resolve-by date. **No longer the danger state** — Q-01 resolved by D-05 |
| Overdue | A hold's resolve-by date passed | Card returns to the top, flagged. Not auto-rejected (04.05.01 Q-02's call) |
| Contested | Two triagers disagreed | Moved to shortlist; "Dave advanced, you rejected" (D-08) |
| Locked (batched) | Open call before deadline (04.07) | Count only. "412 entries. Opens 1 Sep." |
| Partial | Evidence / criteria / media unavailable for a card | The unavailable element named as unavailable — never as absent, never as a failed check. Reject disabled (D-09) |
| Error | The queue itself fails to load | Retry. **No triage action is offered on an unloaded queue** — a call made against nothing is worse than a delay |
| Dormant | ≥1 candidate, 0 triage actions, ≥50% of the publish→decide-by window elapsed | "12 people are waiting on you" |
| Cleared | All triaged | Shortlist ready; dispositions fired for rejects; post closable (04.05.01) |
| Abandoned | Poster stopped returning; decide-by reached with cards untouched | See 04.05.01 — unreachable by a gate (04.05.01 DT-03) and not claimed as solved here |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Ghosting is not rudeness — it is a queue-design failure.** Posters don't decide to ignore applicants; they defer, the maybe pile grows, the decision gets made offline, and the queue is never returned to. The parent index lists "outcome & response tracking" as a capability as though the problem is tracking; it isn't, it's that the review never finished | Reasoning about *why* ghosting happens rather than treating it as a character flaw; the parent index names Sonicbids/ReverbNation credibility collapse, of which ghosting was a large part | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it relocates the fix. The anti-ghosting mechanism (04.05.01) cannot be a rule bolted on afterwards; it has to be **triage being cheap enough that finishing is easier than abandoning**. A close-out obligation on top of an expensive queue is a rule people route around by not posting. Recorded as sub-domain D-03. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would say the queue's real content is **recognition, not assessment**. A bandleader looking at 40 dep applicants isn't evaluating musicianship — they're sorting into "know them / know of them / don't know them", and only the third group gets actually assessed | Domain reasoning about how casting decisions are really made | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it explains why 04.03.02 matters more than it looks. The platform can turn "don't know them" into "you've both worked with these 3 people, and they played this set twice" — which is manufacturing recognition from facts. That is the credit graph doing the single most useful thing it can do in this domain, and it is only possible because of D-18's provenance half. |
| DT-03 | **The queue is not the only queue, and ghosting reappears one level up.** The domain models "the queue" as belonging to a post. A working multi-hyphenate's actual state is a dep call for Saturday, a member-wanted ad, a session enquiry in 05, and a lesson request in 06 — four queues. DT-01's mechanism (defer → pile → abandon) applies identically to *queues*: they abandon the whole surface, not card-by-card, and the per-queue forcing function (D-05) never fires because they never open the queue for it to fire in | Reasoning about `personas.md`'s central structural fact — "one human routinely occupies three of these personas in one day" — applied to the queue rather than to the profile. Nothing in the sub-domain, its CX, or 04.05 addresses cross-post decision load | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is a hole in the domain's model, not in this file's. The obligation is per-submission (04.05.01 D-01) but the *surface* is per-post, so the debt is fragmented across exactly the persona least able to absorb fragmentation. The fix is a **single cross-post obligation surface — "decisions you owe" — spanning 04, 05 and 06**, which is also D-18's consolidation half doing real work rather than decorative work. 04.05.01 Q-04 already asks whether the obligation mechanism should be shared with 05; this is the same seam observed from the queue side, and it argues the answer is yes. **Ownership is not this feature's to claim** — logged as Q-04 for Step 6 synthesis. |
| DT-04 | **Deniability is a social feature, not just laziness — and it decomposes "maybe" into three things.** DT-01 treats ghosting as a design failure, which is right but incomplete. Music is a small industry: the bassist rejected today is on your session next month and shares your agent. Ghosting is partly *social protection* — an un-sent "no" is deniable ("I never saw it"), and a queue that makes every "no" explicit, attributed and permanent is asking working musicians to give up a protection the industry's fabric currently affords them. A design that ignores this gets routed around exactly as domain D-03 fears about fees — by posting off-platform. Interrogating "maybe" under that lens splits it into three: **(1)** "I need more information" — legitimate and *self-resolving* if the missing thing is named; **(2)** "you're a real candidate but not my first choice" — not a maybe at all, a shortlist rank (CX-02's hold); **(3)** "I don't want to say no yet" — the pile, and pure conflict-avoidance | Domain reasoning about why posters actually defer, rather than assuming laziness; `personas.md` Producer's named worst-accidental ("we'll do it later"); domain D-03's precedent of foreclosing a harm structurally rather than by rule | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it produced the feature's central design (D-05, D-07). Meanings (1) and (2) get mechanisms — a blocker with a resolution event, and a shortlist rank. Meaning (3) is made **unrepresentable**, the same move used against applicant-side fees. **But the social force is real and must be answered, not overridden**: two consequences follow. First, **the disposition must not be attributable to an individual inside a group decision** — "the band decided", never "Dave rejected you" (bears on sub-domain Q-03 and 04.04.02 Q-04: externally never attributed; internally is 04.04.02's call). Second, and sharper: **a required free-text reason produces lies, not information** — a poster who must write something permanent and attributed writes "went with someone else" 400 times. That is a real argument for 04.05.01 Q-03, contributed from the only place the reason actually gets typed. The synthesis is D-07: **the criterion is the only rejection reason that is simultaneously true, cheap, impersonal, machine-generatable and socially survivable.** "The post required liability cover; you don't have it" is unarguable and un-resentable, and it is not a judgement of the person. |
| DT-05 | **The evidence pre-summary is where 04.03.02 D-04 gets silently violated.** D-04 there states that attested and unattested credits "must not present identically" — otherwise the graph is gameable and the citation is worth no more than the claim it replaced. But that rule was written for the submission surface. Triage compresses a candidate to a 3-second glance, and compression's whole job is to flatten. A pre-summary that renders "3 relevant credits" has obeyed D-02 (select against requirements) while destroying D-04 — **at exactly the moment D-04 exists to matter**, since the glance is where the inflated credit (`personas.md`: the Musician's worst intentional behaviour) actually pays off | Reasoning about what summarisation does to a trust distinction; nothing in 04.03.02 or this sub-domain's CX carries D-04 across the summarisation boundary | ✅ CONFIRMED as a live defect in the seam, caught before it was built. The pre-summary must carry the attestation distinction **structurally, not as a badge that compression will drop first** — attested citations ordered before self-claimed ones and visually distinct, with self-claimed labelled as such (the same "honest degradation beats a false tick" principle as 04.01.04 D-04).
