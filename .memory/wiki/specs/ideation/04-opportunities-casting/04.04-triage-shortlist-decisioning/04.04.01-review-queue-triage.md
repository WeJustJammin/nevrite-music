# Feature: Review Queue & Triage

> **Number**: 04.04.01
> **Parent**: [Triage, Shortlist & Decisioning](./04.04-triage-shortlist-decisioning-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The machine that makes a pile of submissions reviewable. Forty dep applicants, thirty crew
applications, four hundred demos — all the same problem: an unreviewable pile is not reviewed, and
an unreviewed pile is where ghosting comes from. This absorbed the sweep's "A&R Review Queue"
candidate, because triage is not an A&R feature; it is what every poster in this domain needs the
moment their post works.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | As poster — a bandleader triaging dep applicants, usually fast and on a phone between other things. Their triage is mostly recognition ("know them, know them, don't know them"), which is exactly what the evidence layer (04.03.02) can make legible for the third case. |
| Producer | Full | Triages session and brief responses. Triages on evidence first, audition media second — and their queue is where the audition media actually gets listened to, which is a time cost nothing else in the domain matches. |
| Operator | Full | Triages crew and support applications. Uniquely, much of their triage is genuinely binary — insurance or no insurance — which means their queue can be largely mechanical where others' cannot. |
| Fan | None | Never reviews. A Fan has no post and no candidates. |

## Behavior

Triage is a three-way call kept deliberately coarse — in, out, maybe:

| Action | Effect | Disposition fired? |
|--------|--------|--------------------|
| Advance | To shortlist (CX-01) | No — still live |
| Reject | Out | **Yes, immediately** (04.05.01) |
| Maybe | Held in the queue | No — and this is the pile that becomes ghosting |

### Happy Path

1. Submissions land (04.03.01) or pitches land (04.03.05) — same queue, not separate inboxes.
2. Poster opens the queue. Candidates presented with evidence summarised (04.03.02) and criteria satisfaction shown (04.01.04).
3. Poster triages fast — advance / reject / maybe.
4. Rejects fire dispositions immediately (04.05.01). Advances go to shortlist.
5. Queue empties. The close-out obligation is satisfiable because triage was cheap (sub-domain D-03).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **The "maybe" pile never resolves** | This is ghosting's actual mechanism — not malice, but deferral. Maybes accumulate and the poster mentally closes the project. | The maybe pile must have a forcing function — see Q-01. It is the single most important unresolved question in the feature. |
| 400 submissions (A&R scale) | Triage cost × 400 makes the close-out obligation crushing (04.03.05 Q-02) | See Q-02 |
| Poster never opens the queue at all | The post worked and the applicants are in a void. Worse than a post that failed. | See 04.05.01 — the obligation must reach a poster who never returns |
| Blind post | Anonymised set (04.03.04); triage on media and stripped evidence only | See 04.03.04 |
| Submission arrives after triage started | Queue is live, not a batch — except for open calls (04.07) which are deliberately batched | Ordering changes underneath the poster |
| Poster wants to reject with a reason | Feeds 04.05.01. A reason is the difference between a rejection and a non-answer, and it is also the thing posters most want to avoid writing. | See 04.05.01 |
| Two reviewers triage the same queue | Shortlist territory (04.04.02); at triage stage, conflicting calls need resolving | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty | Post published, no submissions | "No applicants yet" + the honest reason if matching found nobody (04.02.02) |
| Live | Submissions arriving | Count, unreviewed badge |
| Triaging | Poster reviewing | Card-by-card with evidence + criteria pre-summarised |
| Maybe pile | Deferred candidates | **The danger state.** See Q-01. |
| Cleared | All triaged | Shortlist ready; dispositions fired for rejects |
| Abandoned | Poster stopped returning | See 04.05.01 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Ghosting is not rudeness — it is a queue-design failure.** Posters don't decide to ignore applicants; they defer, the maybe pile grows, the decision gets made offline, and the queue is never returned to. The parent index lists "outcome & response tracking" as a capability as though the problem is tracking; it isn't, it's that the review never finished | Reasoning about *why* ghosting happens rather than treating it as a character flaw; the parent index names Sonicbids/ReverbNation credibility collapse, of which ghosting was a large part | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it relocates the fix. The anti-ghosting mechanism (04.05.01) cannot be a rule bolted on afterwards; it has to be **triage being cheap enough that finishing is easier than abandoning**. A close-out obligation on top of an expensive queue is a rule people route around by not posting. Recorded as sub-domain D-03. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would say the queue's real content is **recognition, not assessment**. A bandleader looking at 40 dep applicants isn't evaluating musicianship — they're sorting into "know them / know of them / don't know them", and only the third group gets actually assessed | Domain reasoning about how casting decisions are really made | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it explains why 04.03.02 matters more than it looks. The platform can turn "don't know them" into "you've both worked with these 3 people, and they played this set twice" — which is manufacturing recognition from facts. That is the credit graph doing the single most useful thing it can do in this domain, and it is only possible because of D-18's provenance half. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Triage is **coarse (in/out/maybe)**, not scored | Scoring at triage stage is expensive and premature; deliberation belongs to the shortlist (CX-01). Cheap triage is what makes the close-out obligation honourable (DT-01). |
| D-02 | Rejects fire dispositions **immediately** | Rejecting early is kinder than a long maybe — the applicant can take other work. This inverts the poster's instinct to defer. |
| D-03 | Pitches and submissions share **one queue** | A separate inbox is an email client (04.03.05 D-02). |
| D-04 | Evidence and criteria are **pre-summarised per candidate** | Triage is recognition (DT-02); the platform's job is to manufacture recognition from facts for the people the poster doesn't know. |
