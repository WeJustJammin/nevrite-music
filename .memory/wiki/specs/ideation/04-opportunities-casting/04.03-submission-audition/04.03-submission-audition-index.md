## Overview

The supply side of the spine: how a person becomes a candidate. A structured submission against a
post, the evidence that backs it, the audition task where one is required, the blind mode where
identity must be withheld, and the inverted case where a musician pitches with no post to pitch
against. This is where the domain touches the thesis most directly — an application backed by
verified credits is a citation rather than a claim, and no competitor holding no credit graph can
retroactively offer it.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: five capabilities with a live internal contradiction.
Evidence-backed application (04.03.02) attaches verified credits to prove who you are; blind review
(04.03.04) must strip exactly those credits to work at all. They are not independent features that
happen to sit together — one directly negates the other, and that tension is the sub-domain's
defining design problem.
