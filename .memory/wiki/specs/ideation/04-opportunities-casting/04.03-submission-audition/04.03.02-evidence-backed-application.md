# Feature: Evidence-Backed Application

> **Number**: 04.03.02
> **Parent**: [Submission & Audition](./04.03-submission-audition-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

An application whose claims are **citations, not assertions**. Instead of a CV saying "toured with X,
played on Y", the submission carries verified credits from the credit graph (02) with their
attestors attached — "bass on Y, attested by the producer who ran the session". This is a Deep Think
addition: no sweep candidate connected applications to the credit graph, and it is the single most
thesis-aligned feature in the domain. `personas.md` states the Musician's success criterion as
literally "credits are cited rather than claimed" and "no re-proving themselves to each new client";
this feature is that sentence implemented.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Their strongest asset. Sees their verified credits auto-selected against the post's requirements, chooses what to disclose (some credits are private), and applies without re-writing a CV they have re-written a hundred times. The multi-hyphenate gets the biggest gain: one identity carrying proof across bass work, mixing work and MD work, where today each context needs its own pitch. |
| Producer | Full | Both sides, asymmetrically. As applicant, cites their own credits. **As attestor, they are the reason anyone else's evidence is worth anything** — `personas.md` calls them a trust broker whose attestation outranks a self-claim. Every evidence-backed application in the platform is ultimately collateralised by Producers having confirmed things. |
| Operator | Read-only | Consumes evidence when hiring — "has this engineer actually mixed at this scale?" — but has few credits of their own to cite, since selling time and space generates no creative credit. **This is a real asymmetry**: the Operator persona benefits from the credit graph without contributing to it, which may mean their own reputation must come from elsewhere (17's settled shows, 16's completed bookings). |
| Fan | None | Never applies, never cited. A Fan's attendance history is not evidence of professional capability, and treating it as such would be the first step toward making fandom a career metric. |

## Behavior

Evidence types, in descending order of strength:

| Evidence | Source | Why it's strong |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| Verified credit with attestor | 02 | A named professional confirmed it at the time. Unfakeable retroactively — the thesis's core claim. |
| Material history | 18 + 02 | "Played this exact set" — see 04.02.03 |
| Prior collaboration with the poster's network | 02 | "Worked with 3 people you've worked with" — social proof that is a fact, not a follow |
| Completed engagement history | 05, 17 | Delivered, settled, no disputes |
| Self-claimed skills | 01 | Weak — this is what every competitor already has |

### Happy Path

1. Applicant opens a submission (04.03.01).
2. Evidence resolves against the post's requirements — the system selects which credits are *relevant to this ask*, not the whole CV.
3. Applicant reviews the selection, discloses or withholds private credits (CX-02 synthesis Q3).
4. Submits. Reviewer sees citations with attestors, not claims.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Cold start — nobody has verified credits** | Degrades to a self-claimed CV, i.e. exactly what every competitor offers. The differentiator does not exist on day one. | `[PENDING]` — see Q-01. The honest answer is that this feature's value accrues over time and is near-zero at launch. |
| Applicant has real credits from *before* the platform existed | The dominant case for years. A 20-year career has no verified credits here and the graph makes them look like a beginner. | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see Q-02. **This is a serious adoption hazard**, not a detail. |
| Credit is private | Per-submission disclosure — cite to one poster without publishing | Disclosure toggle per credit |
| Credit is disputed or revoked after submission | Evidence invalidated retroactively (CX-02 synthesis Q5) | `[PENDING]` — a 24 (disputes) boundary landing in casting |
| Applicant cites an inflated credit | `personas.md` names this as the Musician's worst intentional behaviour — "inflates credits to claim work they didn't do; games the verified-credit graph to manufacture reputation" | Attestation is the defence — an unattested credit must not present like an attested one |
| Blind post | Evidence stripped (CX-01) — the contradiction | See 04.03.04 |
| 02 unreachable | Submission completes by hand (04.03.01 D-03) | Manual entry |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| No evidence | Cold start / new user | Self-claimed fallback, honestly labelled as such |
| Resolving | Submission opened | "Finding your relevant credits…" |
| Selected | Evidence matched to requirements | Relevant citations with attestors, disclosure toggles |
| Stripped | Blind post | Evidence hidden — see CX-01 |
| Cited | Submitted | Reviewer sees citations |
| Invalidated | Credit revoked post-submission | `[PENDING]` |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Applications carry **citations with attestors**, not claims | `personas.md` states the Musician's success criterion as exactly this. It is also the only defence against the credit inflation their anti-persona section describes. |
| D-02 | Evidence is **selected against the post's requirements**, not dumped as a full CV | A reviewer with 40 full CVs is back to the triage failure that causes ghosting (04.03.01 DT-01). |
| D-03 | Credits are **referenced, never copied** into a submission | A copy fossilises a credit that may later be disputed or revoked. |
| D-04 | Unattested and attested credits **must not present identically** | Otherwise the graph is gameable and the citation is worth no more than the claim it replaced. |
