# Feature: Audition Tasks & Media Submission

> **Number**: 04.03.03
> **Parent**: [Submission & Audition](./04.03-submission-audition-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Where an opportunity requires a demonstration rather than a claim: record this part, mix this
excerpt, submit a take. It carries the audition half of sweep candidate 04 and it sits directly on
top of the spec-work gate (04.01.03) — because an audition task and unpaid spec work are the same
artefact viewed from opposite ends, and the only thing distinguishing them is scope, retention and
whether it is paid.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Records and submits a take against a defined task, sees exactly what is being asked and what happens to their recording if they lose. Their exposure is that a take is real labour — a 25-song audition set is a week's work, and doing it for free five times a year is the industry's quiet tax on working players. |
| Producer | Config | Sets the task as poster — and this is where the spec-work gate binds hardest (04.01.03 Role Lens). "Mix this track and we'll see" is an audition task and a free mix simultaneously; the difference is only whether the scope is capped and the work is returned or destroyed. As applicant, they submit mixes under the same rules. |
| Operator | Config | Sets tasks for house engineers and crew — often practical rather than musical ("patch this, show us a stage plot"). Their tasks are more likely to be assessable on-site than as media. |
| Fan | None | Never auditions. Also never *hears* audition media — a Fan encountering an unselected musician's failed take would be a small cruelty the platform has no reason to enable. |

## Behavior

The task definition is where the spec-work gate reaches into this feature:

| Task property | Why it exists |
|---------------|---------------|
| Deliverable scope | Caps the ask — "one verse", not "the album" |
| Round cap | Prevents iterative free work disguised as an audition process |
| Retention policy | What happens to the take if they lose — deleted, returned, or (the abuse) kept and used |
| Paid / unpaid | Type-scoped legitimacy (04.01.03 CX-04) |
| Material provided | Charts, stems, backing — an audition with no material is a test of guessing |

On the applicant side, a submission against a task is **media plus a structured envelope**: one or
more takes (audio and/or video), bound to the specific task and round, carried alongside the
structured application (04.03.01). A take is not a free-floating file — it is anchored to *this
task, this round, this applicant*, which is what lets the round cap, the retention policy and blind
mode (04.03.04) act on it. The poster names the accepted format(s) at task-definition time so the
applicant is not guessing at codecs or aspect ratios; anything outside that is rejected on upload,
not silently accepted and marked unassessable later.

### Happy Path

1. Poster defines the task while composing (04.01.01), and the spec-work sub-gate fires on it (04.01.03).
2. Applicant sees scope, rounds, retention and payment before spending any time.
3. Applicant records and submits media alongside their structured submission (04.03.01).
4. Reviewer assesses in the queue (04.04.01), blind if enabled (CX-03).
5. On loss, the retention policy executes — and it must actually execute, or the policy is decoration.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Task is unpaid and substantial (a full mix) | Spec-work gate refuses at post time (04.01.03) | Poster: "A test mix is work. Set a fee, or cap the scope." |
| Poster keeps a losing applicant's take and uses it | **The core abuse.** Retention policy is a promise; enforcement is not a UI problem. | `[PENDING]` — see Q-01. A watermark is the usual answer and it is a weak one. |
| Applicant has strong verified evidence for exactly this | Task should be waived or reduced (CX-05) — otherwise the platform holds the credit graph and asks them to re-prove anyway | "Sam's played this set twice — skip the audition?" |
| Blind mode enabled | Media stripped of filename, EXIF, DAW metadata, session name — every one a real leak vector (CX-03). **The audio itself remains unanonymisable.** | See 04.03.04 |
| Large media upload from a phone at a rehearsal | The realistic capture context. Upload is chunked and resumable; a dropped connection mid-upload does not lose the take or restart from zero. | Progress that survives a connection drop; "Resume upload" on reconnect; a warning before a large upload over cellular. Deep byte-level resume semantics: `[PENDING — /write-be-spec]` |
| Applicant submits a take containing an uncleared sample | `personas.md` names this as the Musician's worst accidental behaviour — it exposes everyone downstream. At submission the applicant attests they hold rights to all material in the take; the attestation travels with the media so a downstream flag (24) has an accountable owner. | "Confirm you have the rights to everything in this take" gate before submit. Detection/enforcement of a *false* attestation is a 09/24 boundary: `[PENDING — /create-prd-security]` |
| No material provided with the task | The audition tests guessing rather than playing | Warn the poster at task definition |
| Applicant submits after the opportunity has closed | Submission is refused; a take cannot enter a queue that no longer accepts entries | "This audition closed on {date}. Submissions are no longer accepted." |
| Uploaded file is the wrong format, corrupt, or over the size cap | Rejected on upload against the poster-declared accepted formats — never accepted-then-marked-unassessable | "This task accepts {formats}, up to {size}. This file is {reason}." with the file retained locally so they can re-pick, not re-record |
| Applicant tries to submit another take past the round cap | Refused — the round cap is the mechanism that stops an audition becoming iterative free work (Behavior table) | "You've used all {n} rounds for this audition." The cap is shown before they start, not sprung at the end |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Task defined | Poster sets it | Scope, rounds, retention, payment shown to applicants up front |
| Not started | Applicant eligible | "Record your take" + the provided material |
| Uploading | Media submitted | Progress; resumable, because this is a phone at a rehearsal |
| Submitted | Upload complete | In queue |
| Waived | Evidence threshold met (CX-05) | "No audition needed — your credits cover it" |
| Retained / destroyed | Outcome decided | Retention policy executes visibly |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **An audition task and unpaid spec work are the same artefact**, distinguished only by scope, retention and payment. "Record a verse so we can hear you" is an audition; "mix this track so we can hear you" is a free mix worth £400 — and the poster genuinely may not perceive the difference | Reasoning from sweep candidate 14 (spec-work guardrails) against candidate 04 (auditions), which the sweep listed as unrelated | ✅ CONFIRMED — which is why the task definition is gated by 04.01.03 rather than being free-form. The two candidates are not neighbours; one is the *constraint on* the other. This is the strongest argument for the guardrails being a publication gate rather than a policy page. |
| DT-02 | The **retention policy is where the real harm is**, and it is the part nobody builds. An applicant who loses has produced an asset; the poster has it; there is no mechanism that makes deleting it true. A watermark is the usual answer and it is weak — you cannot watermark a drum take usefully | Reasoning about failure modes; `personas.md` Producer anti-persona ("withholds stems as leverage") shows this persona already exercises asset leverage | ✅ CONFIRMED as the central unsolved problem of auditioning. ❌ REJECTED as solvable at breadth — enforcement is a rights (09) and disputes (24) problem, not a casting one. What *is* solvable here is **making the policy explicit before the applicant spends the time**, which at least converts a hidden risk into a priced one. Logged as Q-01. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Task definition is **gated by the spec-work rules** (04.01.03), not free-form | An audition task and unpaid spec work are the same artefact (DT-01). |
| D-02 | Scope, rounds, retention and payment are shown **before the applicant spends time** | Converts a hidden risk into a priced one. It does not solve DT-02, but a musician who knows the take may be kept can decline. |
| D-03 | Strong evidence should **waive** the audition (CX-05) | Otherwise the platform holds the credit graph and asks people to re-prove themselves anyway — the exact pain `personas.md` names. |
| D-04 | Uploads are **resumable** | The capture context is a phone at a rehearsal, not a desk. |
