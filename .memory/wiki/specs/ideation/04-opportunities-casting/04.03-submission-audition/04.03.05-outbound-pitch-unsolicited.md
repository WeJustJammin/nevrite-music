# Feature: Outbound Pitch (Unsolicited)

> **Number**: 04.03.05
> **Parent**: [Submission & Audition](./04.03-submission-audition-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The inverted case: a submission with no opportunity to submit to. "We'd love to open for you on the
London date", "here's our record, we think you'd like it" — the musician initiates against a target
who never posted anything. It carries sweep candidate 09 (Support Slot Pitching) and the outbound
half of candidate 10 (A&R), and it exists because modelling submissions as strictly post-anchored
would make the entire outbound half of the industry unrepresentable — which is most of how support
slots and A&R relationships actually begin.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Pitches for support slots, tour openings and label attention. Today this is an email to an address found on a website, into a black hole, with no way to know if it was read. Their gain here is not a better inbox — it is that a pitch lands in a *queue with a disposition obligation* (04.05.01) rather than a void. |
| Producer | Full | Pitches production services to artists they want to work with. Their pitch is credential-shaped rather than material-shaped — "here's what I've done, here's what I'd do with your record". |
| Operator | Read-only | The recipient, not the pitcher — they are pitched *at* by acts wanting slots. Their view is a controllable inbox with the ability to state what they will and won't accept, which is the difference between a channel and a nuisance. |
| Fan | None | Cannot pitch. This boundary is load-bearing: `personas.md` names harassment through open contact surfaces as the Fan's worst intentional behaviour, and an unsolicited-contact channel is exactly that surface. It must never be reachable from a Fan account. |

## Behavior

A pitch is a submission whose anchor is a **target** rather than a post (CX-04). Everything
downstream treats it identically — queue, triage, shortlist, disposition — because a pitch that can
be ignored consequence-free is the black hole the industry already has.

### Happy Path

1. Musician identifies a target — an act, a promoter, a venue, a label.
2. Target's pitch policy resolves: open / open-with-criteria / closed / referral-only.
3. Musician composes a pitch — evidence-backed like any submission (04.03.02), plus the pitchable asset packet (see Cross-Cut Notes).
4. Pitch lands in the target's queue (04.04.01) — the same queue as post submissions, not a separate inbox.
5. Target dispositions it (04.05.01), same obligation as any other submission.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Target hasn't opted in | **The crux.** Without opt-in this is cold contact aimed at the most-harassed people in the industry; with opt-in almost nobody opts in and the feature is dead. | See Q-01 — unresolved and a product-values decision |
| Target is closed to pitches | Blocked, and **said so before composing** — a musician spending an hour on a pitch to a closed door is the harm | "This act isn't taking pitches right now." |
| Target receives 400 pitches | The A&R reality. The disposition obligation (04.05.01) becomes crushing and the target either games it or leaves. | See Q-02 — the obligation may need to scale differently for unsolicited than for solicited |
| Target later posts a matching opportunity | Pitch should convert rather than duplicate — the musician already said they want this | `[PENDING — /create-prd-architecture]` — D-01 already makes the submission's anchor polymorphic (post OR target), so conversion is a re-anchoring of one object: whether the pitch re-anchors in place, forks into a post-anchored candidacy, or stays distinct is a data-model and state-transition decision, and it moves the disposition obligation (04.05.01) with it. Re-pointed from the completed `/ideate-discover` Step 5. |
| Pitch is spam or a bulk blast | The channel's dominant failure mode. Rate limits, criteria gates, referral requirements. | Routed to 24 + `/create-prd-security` |
| Pitching an act to open for them — but the act doesn't control the bill | Extremely common. The promoter or the venue picks support, not the headliner. The pitch went to the wrong target and the musician doesn't know it. | See Q-03 — a real domain fact the sweep missed |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A pitch is a **submission with a target anchor**, not a separate entity | Otherwise half the industry's hiring direction is unrepresentable (DT-01), and pitches would escape the disposition obligation that is the point. |
| D-02 | Pitches enter the **same queue** as post submissions | A separate inbox is an email client, and email is what this replaces. |
| D-03 | The target's pitch policy is shown **before composition** | An hour spent pitching a closed door is the avoidable harm. |
| D-04 | Never reachable from a Fan account | `personas.md` names open contact surfaces as the Fan harassment vector. Not a permission — an absence. |
