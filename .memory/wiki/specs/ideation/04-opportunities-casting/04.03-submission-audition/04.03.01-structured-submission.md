# Feature: Structured Submission

> **Number**: 04.03.01
> **Parent**: [Submission & Audition](./04.03-submission-audition-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The act of becoming a candidate: a structured, comparable response to an opportunity, replacing the
WhatsApp "yes mate I'm free" and the emailed PDF one-sheet that the parent domain identifies as the
displacement target. Structure exists for the *reviewer's* benefit — 40 free-text messages cannot be
triaged, and the triage failure is what causes the ghosting this domain is trying to end. Its
counterweight is that a dep call is answered in 90 seconds from a phone, so any structure heavy
enough to be useful to the reviewer must be light enough to not lose the applicant.

The resolution of that tension is the feature's whole design: the submission is **assembled from
facts the platform already holds** (D-01) rather than typed. Assembly is available only because of
the consolidation half of D-18 — the platform is already in the room holding the credits, the
calendar, the profile and the setlist — which makes this feature a direct expression of the thesis
in a domain that is nominally about a job board.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The primary applicant, and the persona whose submissions are the most time-pressured — a dep call answered in 90 seconds from a soundcheck. Sees the post's requirement checklist pre-evaluated against their profile (04.01.04), evidence pre-attached (CX-02), and their held calendar checked against the post's dates (D-06), so the submission is confirmation rather than composition. Declares which **role** they are applying in (D-05) — the multi-hyphenate's bass credits and mixing credits are not interchangeable evidence for a bass chair. Submits as themselves **or as a Band entity** they hold submit authority on (D-04). |
| Producer | Full | Both sides, asymmetrically. **As applicant**: applies to briefs with scope-shaped responses (approach, turnaround, rate) rather than availability-shaped ones — the free-text allowance (D-08) exists almost entirely for them, because "how would you approach this record?" has no structured form. **As poster**: they are the reviewer that structure serves, and the persona who most wants to ask an open question and least understands what 40 answers to it will cost them. They cannot both review and submit on the same post (D-10). |
| Operator | Full | Applies for house-engineer, resident and crew work; more often the receiving side. Their submissions are the only ones routinely gated on **document-backed criteria** (liability cover, electrical/rigging safety record, right to work — **slots**, whose instruments the jurisdiction profile names, 04.01.04 D-07) which 04.01.04 D-04 declares but does not verify — so an Operator's blocked-submission reason is the most common and the most consequential in the domain, and the fix is always "add the document to your profile", never "ask the poster". Their perishable-inventory constraint inverts here: they submit for a Tuesday they could not sell. |
| Fan | None | Cannot submit. A Fan has no professional standing to offer and no opportunity targets them; the submission surface must not be reachable from a Fan account at all, as an absence rather than a permission check (same reasoning as 04.03.05 D-04). If the board is public (domain Q-11) a Fan may *see* a member-wanted ad and want to respond; that is the porous-boundary case in `personas.md` ("may be a hobbyist musician too") and it resolves by them being a Musician in that moment — the conversion path 04.06's Role Matrix already names — not by a Fan submission path existing. |

## Behavior

A submission is the candidacy object. It is identified by the triple **(submitting entity, post,
role)** (D-05) — not by the human, because `personas.md`'s second structural fact is that a Band is
a first-class entity that books independently of its members, and not by the post alone, because
the multi-hyphenate can legitimately be two candidates for two chairs on one post.

The submission composes from three sources, in decreasing order of ideal effort:

| Source | Content | Applicant effort |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| Evidence (CX-02) | Verified credits with attestors, material history, prior collaborations — resolved against *this post's* requirements, not dumped as a CV (04.03.02 D-02) | Zero — assembled |
| Profile | Instrument and doubling, rates, location and travel radius, gear, delivery format, documents | Zero — inherited |
| Post-specific | The answer to *this* ask: per-date availability confirmation, criteria confirmations, compensation response where the shape invites one, and the poster's bounded questions | Minimal — the only real typing |

### The structured core vs. the free-text allowance

The **structured core** is always present and never substitutable (D-08): per-date availability,
criteria confirmations (04.01.04), and the compensation response where and only where the
compensation shape invites one (D-09). It is what makes triage tractable, and tractable triage is
what makes the close-out obligation honourable rather than punitive (domain CX-03).

The **free-text allowance** is bounded and, critically, **is not rendered in the triage view**
(D-08, DT-03). Free text is a shortlist-stage artefact collected at submission time. This is the
resolution of Q-01: the hazard was never free text existing — it was free text being the thing you
must read to sort 40 people. Structured fields triage; free text deliberates.

### Assembly is not auto-submission

D-01 removes the *typing*, not the *intent* (DT-11). Every submission requires the applicant to
affirmatively confirm availability and send. There is no one-click apply, no bulk apply, and no
saved application template.

### Happy Path

1. Applicant opens a post from the board (04.02.01), an alert (04.02.04), or a direct invite (04.01.02).
2. System resolves eligibility against the post's criteria (04.01.04) and renders the requirement checklist pre-evaluated — each criterion marked met / unmet / self-declared, with the tier shown (04.01.04 D-01: platform-verifiable, self-claimed and document-backed criteria must not present identically).
3. System resolves evidence against the post's requirements (CX-02) and pre-attaches the relevant citations with their attestors; the applicant reviews the selection and toggles disclosure on any private credit (04.03.02 CX-02 Q3).
4. If the applicant holds submit authority on one or more Band entities, and the post's type admits an act rather than a person, the system asks which entity is submitting (D-04). Default is the applicant themselves.
5. If the post admits more than one role, the applicant declares which role they are applying in (D-05). If it admits one, it is inherited silently.
6. System checks the post's date(s) against the applicant's held calendar (availability cross-cut) and against every member's calendar for a Band submission. Conflicts surface as a **named warning, never a block** (D-06) — visible to the applicant only, never to the poster (D-07).
7. Applicant confirms availability **per date** (D-03). A multi-date opportunity accepts partial availability as a valid submission, not as an ineligibility.
8. Applicant responds on compensation **only if the shape invites a response** (D-09) — a stated fixed fee offers accept/decline, never a counter field.
9. Applicant answers the poster's bounded questions, if any (D-08).
10. Applicant submits. The system confirms definitively that it sent — the "did it go?" ambiguity is the exact failure of the WhatsApp status quo, so the confirmation is a durable, re-checkable state change in the applicant's pipeline (04.05.04), not a momentary acknowledgement the applicant can miss or that vanishes on its own.
11. Submission lands in the poster's queue (04.04.01) — the same queue as pitches (04.04.01 D-03).
12. Applicant's pipeline updates (04.05.04) — and the poster now owes them a disposition (04.05.01 D-01).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Post fills before submission completes** | Normal for dep calls, not exceptional. Draft is retained against the post — the next dep call is often the same set and the same answers. | "Filled while you were writing." Draft kept and offered against the poster's next matching post. |
| **Post's deadline passes while a draft is open** | Hard cut at the poster's stated instant. The submission is timestamped at *submit*, never at draft-start — a draft opened before the deadline confers no grace. | "Closed at 23:00 BST on 14 Aug." Draft retained. |
| **Poster extends a deadline** | Extension re-opens drafts and notifies everyone holding one. The platform knows who started and didn't finish; telling them is the product working. Tells the poster nothing about who was drafting. | "The deadline moved to 21 Aug — your draft is live again." |
| **Deadline stated as "midnight"** | Deadlines are absolute instants displayed in the viewer's local timezone with the poster's timezone named. "Midnight" for whom is a real question on an international open call. | "Closes 00:00 21 Aug BST (that's 19:00 20 Aug your time)." |
| **Applicant fails a required criterion** | Blocked, with the specific criterion named and the profile action that fixes it (04.01.04). The criterion is a **slot**; the instrument in the string comes from the jurisdiction profile (04.01.04 D-07) | "Needs {profile-declared liability cover} — add yours to your profile." Never "you are not eligible." |
| **Criterion passes at match time, fails at submit time** | Domain CX-02 requires the two evaluations not diverge. The submit-time verdict binds; the match-time one was advisory. | The lapse is surfaced **at the moment it lapses** while the draft is open, not sprung at submit. "Your liability cover expired yesterday — this application is on hold." |
| **Criterion is "preferred" not required** | Ranks, never excludes (04.01.04 D-02). Submission proceeds. | Shown as an unmet preference, not a block. |
| **Post is edited after submission — material terms** | Fee, date, location, or required criteria changing after publication re-writes what the applicant agreed to. Handled by **04.01.01 D-09's single terms-change rule**: the applicant is **notified with a diff and shown [Stay] / [Withdraw]** — never suspended, never auto-withdrawn. **Silence = the applicant stays in the pool**; the submission remains visible in triage, marked as changed-since-applied, never hidden. Required-criteria changes are notified the same way. | "The fee changed from £200 to £120 since you applied. Stay in the running, or withdraw?" |
| **Post is edited after submission — immaterial** | Typo in the description, added detail. Submission carries forward untouched. | Nothing. Which fields are material is 04.01.01's list to own — routed there. |
| **Post's type is changed after publication** | Cannot happen — type is immutable after publication (04.01.01 D-03). Nothing to handle here; recorded so the boundary is explicit. | N/A |
| **Applicant resubmits (same entity, same post, same role)** | Supersedes. A correction is not a second candidate (D-02). | "Updated your application." |
| **Applicant applies to the same post in two roles** | **Two candidacies, not a supersede** — a bassist-and-engineer applying for the bass chair and the engineer chair is two people's worth of decision for one human (D-05, DT-05). | Both live in the pipeline independently, each with its own disposition. |
| **Applicant applies as themselves AND as their band** | Two candidacies. A solo artist and their band are two different acts pitching for one support slot, and both are legitimate (D-04, DT-04). | Both live independently. |
| **Resubmit after the poster has already triaged it** | Supersede is allowed — a genuine correction must not be blocked — but the poster's triage verdict is retained and the candidate is **flagged as updated with a diff of what changed** (D-11). Never a silent swap: the poster's "in" was a decision about v1. | Applicant: "Updated — the poster has already reviewed this, so they'll see what changed." Poster: "Updated since your review" + diff. |
| **Resubmit after an offer is live** | Blocked. You cannot change the terms of the thing that has been offered on. | "This has been offered on — talk to them directly." Hands off to 04.04.03. |
| **Withdraw before triage** | Removed from the queue; the poster's close-out obligation shrinks accordingly (04.05.01) | Confirmation. |
| **Withdraw after an offer is live** | **Not a withdrawal — a decline** (D-12, DT-07). Control changes and hands off to 04.04.03. | The decline path, not the withdraw path. |
| **Withdraw after acceptance** | Not this feature's to model. Breaking an accepted commitment lands on 04.04.03 Q-01 (is acceptance a contract?), which is unresolved. | Routed to 04.04.03. |
| **Applicant's calendar shows a conflict on the post's date** | Warn, never block (D-06). A musician may be depping it out, holding not confirmed, or planning to get out of it — the platform does not tell a musician what work they may take. The override is recorded on the submission, applicant-visible only. | "You're holding the Roundhouse that night." Submit remains enabled. |
| **Poster would benefit from seeing that conflict** | **Never disclosed** (D-07). It is private calendar data, it is frequently wrong (a hold is not a booking), and leaking it makes the calendar a liability the musician will stop maintaining — which destroys the availability cross-cut for everyone. | Poster sees nothing. |
| **Band submission where one member is double-booked** | Surfaced to the submitting member as a named conflict before submit (D-04). The band, not the platform, decides whether to apply anyway and dep that chair. | "Your drummer is booked on the 14th." |
| **Band submission — who may submit on the band's behalf?** | Authority is 01's governance question, the same shape as 04.04.02 Q-03. Until 01 answers, submit authority is an explicit per-entity grant and **fails closed** — no grant, no submission. | "You don't have submit authority for this band." Logged as Q-04. |
| **Multi-date opportunity, partial availability** | A valid submission in the `Partial` state (D-03), not an ineligibility. A drummer available for 2 of a 3-night run is half a solution, and the function-band world runs on exactly this. | "Available 2 of 3 nights" shown to the poster as a first-class candidacy. |
| **Poster asks everything in one open question** | The escape hatch always gets used. Bounded by D-08: type-scoped question ceiling (0/1/3), 500-char cap per answer, and — the real defence — free text never renders in the triage view (DT-03). The poster can be verbose; they cannot make triage expensive. | Poster hits the ceiling: "This type allows 1 question." |
| **Poster puts the real ask in the description and asks applicants to "cover it in Q1"** | Survives D-08 by construction and is not preventable at this layer. Its blast radius is capped at 500 characters and it never reaches triage. Description-field workarounds are 24's problem, the same conclusion 04.01.03 DT-03 reached about fee scams. | Routed to 24. |
| **Poster's fixed fee, applicant wants to counter** | No counter field exists (D-09). The applicant's responses are accept-and-apply or don't apply. | "The fee is £200, fixed." |
| **Applicants underbidding each other** | **The reverse-auction hazard** (DT-06). Rate responses are never visible between applicants; no live-lowest-bid surface is representable, by the same technique 04.01.03 D-02 used on applicant-side fees. | Each applicant sees only their own response. |
| **Poster with review access on a post wants to submit to it** | **Mutually exclusive** (D-10). Real case: a band posts for a singer and the guitarist wants the chair. Applying revokes their review access on that post for its lifetime; the choice is theirs and it is one-way. | "Applying means you can't review this post. Continue?" |
| **Poster submits to their own post** | Unrepresentable — rigging your own casting. Distinct from the case above: the sole owner of a post has no path to being a candidate on it. | Submit is absent. |
| **Evidence source (02) unreachable** | Submission must still complete by hand (CX-02 synthesis Q2, D-03). An outage that stops someone applying for work is a worse failure than an unbacked application. | "We can't reach your credits right now — you can add them by hand." Degraded, honestly labelled, never blocked. |
| **Credit revoked or disputed after submission** | Evidence is referenced, never copied (04.03.02 D-03), so the citation invalidates in place. The poster's queue reflects reality; the applicant is told. | Applicant: "A credit you cited is under dispute." A 24 boundary landing in casting. |
| **Submission to a blind post** | Evidence stripped (CX-01) — the sub-domain's central contradiction, unresolved at index Q-01. Blind mode is type-scoped (04.03.04 D-02) and incoherent for dep calls. | See 04.03.04. |
| **Network drops mid-submit** | A retry after a dropped submit produces **one candidacy, never two** — resubmitting the same draft can only ever result in a single submission. The 90-seconds-at-a-soundcheck reality means bad connectivity is the normal case, not the exception. | Either "sent" or "not sent" — never ambiguity. The pipeline (04.05.04) is the source of truth. |
| **Same draft edited on two devices** | There is **one draft per (entity, post, role)**, not one per device — a phone at soundcheck and a laptop later edit the same candidacy, and the applicant is told when their two views diverge rather than silently ending up with two drafts. A phone-then-laptop workflow is the normal case, not an edge case. | "This draft was updated on another device — showing the latest version." |
| **Post deleted while a draft is open** | Draft orphaned and retained. Nothing was submitted, so 04.05.04 D-03 (entries survive post deletion) does not apply — there is no entry. | "This post was removed." |
| **Post deleted after submission** | Entry survives; the applicant's history is not the poster's to erase (04.05.04 D-03). The disposition obligation is not dischargeable by deletion. | Entry remains in the pipeline with its age. |
| **Applicant is blocked by the poster (24)** | Never reaches submission — the post is not visible to them on the board at all (04.02.01's entitlement filter). A direct link resolves to "not available", **not** "you are blocked": telling someone they have been blocked is an invitation to retaliate off-platform. | "This opportunity isn't available." |
| **Invite-only post, applicant not invited** | Never reaches submission — targeting resolves before matching so an invite-only post never leaks via a match (domain CX-01). | Not visible. |
| **Urgent-fill post (04.04.04)** | **No submission exists.** The 9pm dep flow cascades a ranked list straight to offers, skipping discovery and submission entirely (domain CX-05). This feature must not assume every candidate has a submission — the person who plays the gig may never have applied. | Nothing here; see 04.04.04. |
| **Applicant blasts submissions at every open post** | Cheaper to spam than a pitch, because the structured core is assembled. A per-account ceiling exists set above any legitimate use — proposed 40 submissions / 24h, on the reasoning that a busy working musician applies to 5–15 in a day and 40 is indiscriminate by construction. The number is 24's to tune; the ceiling's existence is not optional (`security-first`). | "You've hit today's application limit." |
| **Free text containing links, markup or a phishing payload** | Free text is plain text. No rich media, no attachments (that is 04.03.03), URLs rendered as inert text and never auto-linked — the poster's queue is a high-trust surface being read fast, which is exactly what phishing wants. | Links appear as text. Routed to 24 + `/create-prd-security`. |
| **Free text containing PII or an off-platform contact** | The "call me on 07…" move that takes the deal off-platform — `personas.md` names it as the Musician's worst intentional behaviour. Not blocked at this layer; detection and policy are 24's. | Routed to 24. |
| **Applicant has no verified credits (cold start)** | D-01's assembly degrades toward typing and DT-02's resolution weakens — but does not vanish: profile and per-date availability still assemble, and the structured core is unaffected. What is lost is evidence, not structure. | Honest self-claimed fallback, labelled as such (04.03.02). See Q-03. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | Submission opened; eligibility and evidence resolving against the post | Requirement checklist skeleton with "Checking your credits…". The structured core is interactive immediately — evidence resolution must never gate availability confirmation, because the 90-second budget is spent before the credit graph answers. |
| **Empty** | No evidence resolves — cold start, or a genuinely unrelated post | Self-claimed fallback, honestly labelled. Not an error state and not presented as one; the submission is complete and valid without evidence. |
| **Eligible** | All required criteria met | Submit form, pre-filled and pre-evaluated |
| **Blocked** | A required criterion unmet | The named criterion + the profile action that fixes it. Never a bare refusal. |
| **Draft** | Started, not sent | Resumable across devices; survives the post filling, the post's deletion, and a missed deadline |
| **Partial** | Multi-date opportunity, applicant available for some dates | "Available 2 of 3 nights" — a first-class candidacy, not a warning (D-03) |
| **Submitted** | Sent | Position in the pipeline (04.05.04) and the disposition now owed |
| **Terms-Changed** | Post's material terms changed after submission | "The fee changed since you applied — stay in the running, or withdraw?" Stays visible in triage, flagged changed-since-applied. Silence keeps the applicant in the pool (04.01.01 D-09) — never suspended, never dropped. |
| **Superseded** | Resubmitted, same (entity, post, role) | "Updated." If already triaged, additionally: "they'll see what changed." |
| **Withdrawn** | Applicant pulls out before an offer exists | Removed from the poster's queue |
| **Dispositioned** | Poster responds | Outcome (04.05.01) |
| **Error** | Submit failed — network, or an eligibility lapse caught at submit | Definitive: "Not sent — retry" or the named lapse. Never ambiguous, never a silent failure; the draft is never lost. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Structure serves the reviewer, and the reviewer's failure is what causes ghosting.** 40 free-text messages cannot be triaged, so they aren't — and applicants never hear back. The parent domain's "outcome & response tracking" capability is downstream of this feature's structure existing | Reasoning about why ghosting happens rather than treating it as rudeness; the parent index lists outcome tracking as a distinct interacting capability | ✅ CONFIRMED — structure is not bureaucracy, it is the precondition for the disposition obligation in 04.05.01 being satisfiable at all. A poster cannot be required to disposition 40 applicants if dispositioning each one takes five minutes of reading. |
| DT-02 | The **structure/speed tension is the feature's real design problem**. A dep call is answered in 90 seconds from a phone at a soundcheck; a form is a form. Every field added to help the reviewer costs applicants at the margin, and the applicants lost are the busy working ones — the exact people the poster wants | Reasoning from `personas.md` Musician workflow and the parent index's "dep gigs are won in minutes" | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is why CX-02 (evidence-backed) matters more than it first appears. **The resolution is to assemble rather than ask**: a submission composed from facts the platform already holds is simultaneously maximally structured for the reviewer and near-zero effort for the applicant. That is the only way both sides win, and it is available only because of the consolidation half of D-18. |
| DT-03 | **The free-text escape hatch is safe if free text is deferred to the shortlist stage.** Q-01 assumed the choice was allow-or-ban, and both are bad: ban it and the poster asks in the description and takes the answers off-platform; allow it and triage dies. But 04.04.01 D-01 already ruled that triage is coarse (in/out/maybe) and *deliberation belongs to the shortlist* — so free text was never a triage input in the first place. Collect it at submission, render it at shortlist | Reasoning about why the escape hatch is dangerous, then noticing 04.04.01's existing decision already answers it | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it dissolves Q-01 rather than trading it off. The reviewer reads free text for the 5 who survive triage, never for the 40 who arrive. **The hazard was never free text existing; it was free text being the thing you must read to sort people.** Combined with a type-scoped ceiling and a character cap (D-08), a poster can be as verbose as they like and triage stays a 40-second job. |
| DT-04 | **A domain expert would immediately ask "who is submitting — the person or the act?"** `personas.md` states as a structural fact that bands are first-class entities that book, sign and get paid independently of their members, and 04.07's Role Matrix says the applicant is "usually via a Band entity". Yet nothing in this feature modelled the submitter as anything but a human | Reading `personas.md`'s second structural fact against the submission object; 04.07 Role Matrix | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real and material gap. (2) is the consolidation thesis doing work nobody asked for: a Facebook dep group structurally cannot know this. Resolved as D-04; the authority question is 01's and logged as Q-04. |
| DT-05 | **The multi-hyphenate breaks the supersede rule.** D-02 said a resubmission supersedes because "a correction is not a second candidate". But a drummer-who-mixes applying to a session call for both the drum chair and the engineer chair is *two candidacies for one human* — and the original rule silently deletes one of them. `personas.md` says a UX forcing "artist OR producer" fails this persona immediately; a supersede keyed on the human is that failure wearing a data model | Applying the multi-hyphenate lens — `personas.md`'s first structural fact — to D-02 as originally written | ✅ CONFIRMED as a defect in D-02 as stated. Fixed by keying identity on **(submitting entity, post, role)** (D-05). This also forces a real dependency out into the open: whether one post can carry more than one role/chair is 04.01.01's decision, not this feature's. Logged as Q-05. Two roles also means two dispositions owed, which is correct — the engineer chair rejecting you says nothing about the drum chair. |
| DT-06 | **The domain killed applicant-side fees and left applicant-side underbidding wide open.** 04.01.03 D-02 made money-flowing-from-the-applicant *unrepresentable* — the structural version of the Sonicbids lesson. But a free-text-or-numeric rate response on a competitive post achieves the same harm by the opposite route: 40 desperate players bidding each other down is a reverse auction, and the platform built the auction house | Reasoning about what a rate response *does* under competition, then testing it against D-03 (the anti-fee position is structural, not editorial) | ✅ CONFIRMED as a genuine hole in the domain's own stated position, found by taking that position seriously rather than treating it as settled. Resolved by the same technique that closed the fee hole: make the harm unrepresentable rather than prohibited. A counter-offer field exists **only** where the compensation shape is negotiable or quote-requested (04.01.03 D-03's per-shape schema already supplies the discriminator); rate responses are never visible between applicants; no live-lowest-bid surface exists (D-09). ❌ REJECTED the softer option of *allowing* counters everywhere with a "don't undersell yourself" nudge — a nudge against a market incentive is decoration, which is exactly what 04.01.03 D-01 rejected when it made compensation a gate rather than a field. |
| DT-07 | **Withdrawal and decline are different verbs and the state machine conflated them.** The `Withdrawn` state, as originally written, was reachable at any time. But withdrawing from a queue you are one of 40 in costs nobody anything, while "withdrawing" from a live offer is *declining an offer* and "withdrawing" after acceptance is breaking a commitment that someone has already built a show around | Walking the state machine against 04.04.03's existence rather than in isolation | ✅ CONFIRMED — the three are different events with different costs and different owners. Withdrawal is scoped to the pre-offer states only (D-12); once an offer is live the control changes verb and hands to 04.04.03; post-acceptance lands on 04.04.03 Q-01 (is acceptance a contract?), which is genuinely unresolved and not this feature's to invent. The Operator's perishable inventory is why this matters: a "withdrawal" the night before is a Tuesday that cannot be re-sold. |
| DT-08 | **A poster editing material terms after publication silently re-writes what applicants agreed to.** 04.01.01 D-03 saw this exactly and locked the *type* as immutable, reasoning that "every submission was made against that type's terms". The reasoning is correct and stops one field short: a fee cut from £200 to £120 after 20 people applied is the same violation, and it is the bait-and-switch the function-gig world runs on today | Extending 04.01.01 D-03's own stated reasoning past the field it was applied to | ✅ CONFIRMED — the principle is about consent, not about the type field. On a material change the applicant is notified with a diff and offered [Stay] / [Withdraw]; silence keeps them in the pool — never a suspend, never an auto-withdraw (D-13, aligning to 04.01.01 D-09's single terms-change rule). ❌ REJECTED making the post itself immutable after publication: over-correction. Posters legitimately fix typos, add detail, and answer questions in the description, and freezing the post would push every correction into a delete-and-repost that destroys the queue and the disposition obligation with it. The material/immaterial split is the right cut, and **the list of material fields is 04.01.01's to own** — routed there rather than duplicated here, because a second list would drift. |
| DT-09 | **You cannot both review and be reviewed on the same post — and the real case is not fraud, it's a band.** The obvious version (a poster applying to their own post to rig it) is trivially unrepresentable. The version that actually happens: a band posts a member-wanted for a singer, and the guitarist — who holds review access on the post, because it's their band's post — wants to try out for the singer chair. Legitimate, common, and it makes them a reviewer of their own candidacy and a reader of their rivals' | Applying the multi-hyphenate / two-roles-at-once lens the task named, to the poster/applicant boundary rather than within the applicant | ✅ CONFIRMED as real and unhandled. Resolved as mutual exclusion with an applicant-side, one-way choice (D-10): applying revokes review access on that post for its lifetime. | D-01 | Submissions are **assembled, not typed** | The only way to serve the reviewer's need for structure and the applicant's 90-second budget at once (DT-02). Depends on the consolidation half of D-18 holding the facts. |
| D-02 | Resubmission **supersedes** rather than duplicates — scoped by D-05's key | A correction is not a second candidate. Duplicates inflate the queue and the close-out obligation. As originally written this rule was keyed on the human and was wrong (DT-05). |

| D-04 | The submitter is an **entity** — a person or a Band — not necessarily a human | `personas.md`'s second structural fact: bands book, sign and get paid independently of their members (DT-04). Submit authority is an explicit per-entity grant and **fails closed** pending 01 (Q-04). |

| D-06 | Calendar conflicts **warn, never block** | A dep may be depping the conflict out, holding rather than confirmed, or about to drop it. The platform does not tell a musician what work they may take (DT-10). |
