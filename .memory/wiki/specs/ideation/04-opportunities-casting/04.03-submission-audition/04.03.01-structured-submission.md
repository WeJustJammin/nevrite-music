# Feature: Structured Submission

> **Number**: 04.03.01
> **Parent**: [Submission & Audition](./04.03-submission-audition-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The act of becoming a candidate: a structured, comparable response to an opportunity, replacing the
WhatsApp "yes mate I'm free" and the emailed PDF one-sheet that the parent domain identifies as the
displacement target. Structure exists for the *reviewer's* benefit — 40 free-text messages cannot be
triaged, and the triage failure is what causes the ghosting this domain is trying to end. Its
counterweight is that a dep call is answered in 90 seconds from a phone, so any structure heavy
enough to be useful to the reviewer must be light enough to not lose the applicant.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The primary applicant, and the persona whose submissions are the most time-pressured — a dep call answered in 90 seconds from a soundcheck. Sees the post's requirement checklist pre-evaluated against their profile (04.01.04) and their evidence pre-attached (CX-02), so the submission is mostly confirmation rather than composition. |
| Producer | Full | Both sides. Applies to briefs with scope-shaped responses (approach, turnaround, rate) rather than availability-shaped ones. As poster, they are the reviewer that structure serves. |
| Operator | Full | Applies for house-engineer / resident work; more often the receiving side. Their submissions carry document-backed criteria (insurance, certs) that others' do not. |
| Fan | None | Cannot submit — a Fan has no professional standing to offer and no opportunity targets them. If the board is public (04.02.01 Q-01) a Fan may *see* a member-wanted ad and want to respond; that is the porous-boundary case in `personas.md` ("may be a hobbyist musician too") and it resolves by them being a Musician in that moment, not by a Fan submission path. |

## Behavior

The submission composes from three sources, in decreasing order of ideal effort:

| Source | Content | Applicant effort |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| Evidence (CX-02) | Verified credits, material history, prior collaborations | Zero — assembled |
| Profile | Instrument, rates, location, gear | Zero — inherited |
| Post-specific | The answer to *this* ask: availability confirmation, rate response, the one question the poster set | Minimal — the only real typing |

### Happy Path

1. Applicant opens a post from the board (04.02.01) or an alert (04.02.04).
2. Requirement checklist shows pre-evaluated (04.01.04); evidence pre-attached (CX-02).
3. Applicant confirms availability, responds on rate if the shape invites it, answers the post's questions.
4. Submits. Lands in the poster's queue (04.04.01).
5. Applicant's pipeline updates (04.05.04) — and the poster now owes them a disposition (04.05.01).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Post fills before submission completes | Normal for dep calls, not exceptional | "Filled while you were writing." Draft kept — the next dep call is often the same set. |
| Applicant fails a required criterion | Blocked with the reason named (04.01.04) | "Needs PLI insurance — add yours to your profile." |
| Evidence source (02) unreachable | Submission must still complete by hand (CX-02 synthesis Q2) | Manual entry; an outage must never block someone applying for work |
| Applicant submits twice | Second replaces first — a resubmission is a correction, not a second candidate | "Updated your application." |
| Applicant withdraws | Removed from queue; the poster's close-out obligation (04.05.01) shrinks accordingly | Confirmation |
| Free-text answers to a structured post | The escape hatch always gets used. Some posters will ask everything in one open question and re-create the WhatsApp problem inside the product. | See Q-01 |
| Submission to a blind post | Evidence stripped (CX-01) — the contradiction | See 04.03.04 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Eligible | Criteria met | Submit form, pre-filled |
| Blocked | Required criterion unmet | Named reason + the profile action that fixes it |
| Draft | Started, not sent | Resumable; survives the post filling |
| Submitted | Sent | Position in the pipeline (04.05.04) |
| Superseded | Resubmitted | "Updated" |
| Withdrawn | Applicant pulls out | Removed from the poster's queue |
| Dispositioned | Poster responds | Outcome (04.05.01) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Structure serves the reviewer, and the reviewer's failure is what causes ghosting.** 40 free-text messages cannot be triaged, so they aren't — and applicants never hear back. The parent domain's "outcome & response tracking" capability is downstream of this feature's structure existing | Reasoning about why ghosting happens rather than treating it as rudeness; the parent index lists outcome tracking as a distinct interacting capability | ✅ CONFIRMED — structure is not bureaucracy, it is the precondition for the disposition obligation in 04.05.01 being satisfiable at all. A poster cannot be required to disposition 40 applicants if dispositioning each one takes five minutes of reading. |
| DT-02 | The **structure/speed tension is the feature's real design problem**. A dep call is answered in 90 seconds from a phone at a soundcheck; a form is a form. Every field added to help the reviewer costs applicants at the margin, and the applicants lost are the busy working ones — the exact people the poster wants | Reasoning from `personas.md` Musician workflow and the parent index's "dep gigs are won in minutes" | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is why CX-02 (evidence-backed) matters more than it first appears. **The resolution is to assemble rather than ask**: a submission composed from facts the platform already holds is simultaneously maximally structured for the reviewer and near-zero effort for the applicant. That is the only way both sides win, and it is available only because of the consolidation half of D-18. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Submissions are **assembled, not typed** | The only way to serve the reviewer's need for structure and the applicant's 90-second budget at once (DT-02). Depends on the consolidation half of D-18 holding the facts. |
| D-02 | Resubmission **supersedes** rather than duplicates | A correction is not a second candidate. Duplicates inflate the queue and the close-out obligation. |
| D-03 | Evidence-source outage must **not block submission** | An outage that stops someone applying for work is a worse failure than an unbacked application. |
