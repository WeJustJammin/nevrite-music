# Feature: Eligibility & Requirement Criteria

> **Number**: 04.01.04
> **Parent**: [Opportunity Posting & Targeting](./04.01-opportunity-posting-targeting-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

What a candidate must satisfy to be eligible to submit — instrument and doubling, reading ability,
transport and travel radius, gear, DAW and delivery format, and for crew the legal set: rigging
certification, public liability insurance, driving licence, right to work. This is the feature that
absorbed the sweep's Crew & Technical Labour candidate, because a crew job differs from a dep call
in *which criteria apply*, not in what the object is or how it flows.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | As poster: sets musical criteria — instrument, doubles, reads notation/charts/nothing, own transport, travel radius, backline provided or not. As applicant: sees criteria as a checklist against their own profile, with their gaps shown honestly rather than hidden ("you meet 4 of 5 — you don't read charts"). |
| Producer | Config | Sets skill/gear/format criteria — DAW, delivery format, sample rate, whether the player has a treated room. Their criteria are about *deliverability*, not presence: a remote session player who can't deliver at 48k/24-bit is ineligible regardless of talent. |
| Operator | Config | The only persona routinely setting **legal** criteria — PLI insurance, rigging/electrical certification, right to work, driving licence, age. These are liability facts, not preferences, and getting them wrong costs the Operator their insurance rather than a bad take. |
| Fan | None | Never sets criteria, never assessed against them. On the public board a Fan sees a post's headline but not its requirement checklist — the checklist is professional apparatus with no consumer meaning. |

## Behavior

Criteria fall into three tiers with sharply different trust properties, which is the whole
difficulty:

| Tier | Example | Who asserts it | Can the platform verify? |
|------|---------|----------------|--------------------------|
| **Platform-verifiable** | "Has played this material before", "has a verified credit on a comparable release", "owns a registered [gear item]" | The platform, from 02 / 15 | ✅ Yes — this is provenance doing work |
| **Self-claimed** | "Reads notation", "own transport", "doubles on keys" | The applicant | ❌ No — trust-on-assert |
| **Document-backed** | PLI insurance, rigging cert, right to work | A third party, evidenced by a document | ⚠️ Only if documents are collected and checked — which is a compliance decision nobody has made |

### Happy Path

1. Poster picks a type; the criteria vocabulary loads scoped to it (CX-02).
2. Poster marks each criterion required or preferred. Preferred is not decoration — it feeds ranking in 04.02.02 and scoring in 04.04.02 rather than exclusion.
3. Applicant sees the checklist pre-evaluated against their profile, gaps included.
4. Applicant submits; eligibility is evaluated at submit time as well as at match time.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Applicant fails a required criterion | Blocked from submitting, but **told which one** — a silent absence from the board teaches nothing | "You can't apply: this needs PLI insurance. Add yours to your profile." |
| Applicant fails a criterion but was explicitly invited (CX-03) | Invite **waives** eligibility (D-05) — an explicit invite is the poster naming a specific human, which outranks a criterion the system holds. The applicant may submit; the poster is shown the mismatch on the invite and on the submission so the choice is informed, not silent. | Applicant: no block, normal submit. Poster: "You invited them, but they don't read charts — invited anyway." |
| Poster tightens criteria on a live post with submissions | Submissions made under the old terms are **grandfathered** — they stay valid and are never silently dropped (D-06). The new criteria apply only to submissions made after the change. Affected already-submitted applicants who now fail are flagged to the poster (not excluded), and the tightening re-gates the post the same way 04.01.03's compensation edit does. | Existing applicant: stays in the pool, no notification of demotion. Poster: "3 existing applicants don't meet the new 'reads notation' rule — kept, flagged." New applicant: evaluated against tightened criteria. |
| Applicant self-claims a criterion falsely ("reads notation") | Not detectable at submit time. Surfaces at the gig, expensively. | — |
| Document-backed criterion required but no document store exists | Degrades to self-claim, which for insurance is a liability fiction — the Operator believes they checked something they didn't | `[PENDING]` — see Q-01 |
| Criteria so narrow nothing matches | Poster sees the pool collapse *while setting them*, not after publishing to silence | "0 people match. Loosening 'travel radius 5mi' → 47." |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Type chosen; vocabulary + pool count resolving | Skeleton criteria list; pool counter shows a spinner, not a stale number |
| Empty | Type chosen, no criteria set | Type-scoped vocabulary, nothing required |
| Configured (Populated) | Criteria set | Live pool-size counter — the feedback that prevents over-narrowing |
| Zero-match | Criteria exclude everyone | Warning with the specific criterion to loosen |
| Evaluated (applicant view) | Applicant opens post | Checklist with own gaps shown |
| Blocked (applicant view) | Required criterion unmet, not invited | Named reason + the profile action that fixes it |
| Invited-override (applicant view) | Required criterion unmet but applicant explicitly invited (D-05) | No block; submit allowed with the unmet criterion shown honestly ("you don't read charts — you were invited anyway") |
| Grandfathered (applicant view) | Applicant already submitted; poster later tightened criteria (D-06) | No change to their standing; no demotion notice — their submission stays valid under the terms they applied under |
| Error | Pool count or profile-evaluation lookup fails | Criteria still render (poster can configure); pool counter shows "count unavailable — retry" rather than a fabricated zero that would trigger a false Zero-match warning |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A domain expert would expect eligibility to be **verifiable where it can be**, and the platform is uniquely able to verify criteria nobody else can — "has played this material", "has a credit with this producer", "owns this instrument" are facts held in 02 and 15 | Reasoning from D-18 (provenance is the wedge) applied to eligibility; the candidate list only implied self-claimed criteria | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is the difference between this and a Facebook group. A Facebook group can ask "do you read?"; only a platform holding a verified credit graph can filter on "has actually done this". The three-tier model above exists because of this hypothesis. |
| DT-02 | The **legal criteria (insurance, certs, right to work) are a compliance trap wearing a product costume** — collecting insurance certificates means storing, verifying and expiring third-party documents, and an Operator who believes the platform checked something it didn't is worse off than one who knew to check themselves | Reasoning about Operator liability; `meta/constraints.md` lists no compliance decision covering document verification | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real trap. ❌ REJECTED as buildable at breadth — the *product* half (a criterion exists, it's declared, it's shown) is here; the *verification* half (document store, expiry, liability for a wrong check) is a compliance and architecture decision routed onward. **The dangerous middle is implying verification without doing it** — if documents aren't checked, the UI must say "self-declared", not show a tick. Logged as Q-01. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Criteria are **three-tiered by trust** (platform-verifiable / self-claimed / document-backed), not one flat list | They have different failure modes and must not present identically. Showing a self-claim with the same tick as a verified credit is a lie the UI tells. |
| D-02 | "Preferred" criteria **rank rather than exclude** | Over-narrowing is the dominant poster error; a hard filter on every nice-to-have collapses the pool to zero and the poster blames the platform. |
| D-03 | Pool size is shown **live while configuring**, before publishing | Discovering you excluded everyone after publishing to silence is a failure the poster attributes to the market, not to their filter. |
| D-04 | Document-backed criteria are **declared but not verified** at this stage, and must be labelled as self-declared | Implying a check that didn't happen transfers liability to the Operator invisibly (DT-02). Honest degradation beats a false tick. |
| D-05 | An **explicit invite waives eligibility**; the mismatch is shown to the poster, never silently suppressed | Resolves CX-03 / Q-03. On the dep circuit the poster's personal knowledge of a named player outranks any criterion the system holds; silently excluding an invited human is a failure with a real cost (the dep never learns they were asked). Informed override, not a hidden filter. |
| D-06 | Tightening criteria on a live post is **allowed but grandfathers existing submissions**; new criteria bind only new submissions | Resolves Q-02. People who applied in good faith under the old terms are never retroactively invalidated (the 04.01.03 bait-and-switch family). Newly-failing existing applicants are flagged to the poster, not dropped. Re-gates the post as the compensation edit does. |
