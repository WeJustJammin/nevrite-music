# Feature: Opportunity Post & Type Taxonomy

> **Number**: 04.01.01
> **Parent**: [Opportunity Posting & Targeting](./04.01-opportunity-posting-targeting-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-30

## Overview

The single `Opportunity` object that every demand-side need in the platform becomes, and the type
taxonomy that discriminates its behaviour. A dep call, a session call, a function sub, an audition,
a crew job, a brief, a festival open call, a camp invitation and a support-slot request are one
entity with one lifecycle and a type field — not nine products. This exists because the parent
domain's rationale is explicit that building the post→submit→triage→decide workflow once per type
is the waste that makes this domain worth consolidating in the first place.

The type field is not a label. It is the **key that selects four different rule sets**: which
eligibility vocabulary is offered (CX-02), whether "unpaid" is legitimate (CX-04), what a date
*means* — and therefore what the post's **decide-by** defaults to and when the post closes (D-10,
D-16) — and whether protected-characteristic criteria are lawful occupational requirements
or unlawful discrimination (D-15). Everything downstream of the composer is type-scoped, which is
why the type is chosen first and cannot be changed after publication (D-03).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Posts dep/sub calls ("need a drummer Friday, 25-song set"), member-wanted ads, function-band subs, and depping-out of their own booked work. Also the highest-volume *reader* of other people's posts — the parent index calls this the #1 reason they open the app. Their posts are urgent and low-ceremony; raised from a booked show (D-02) a dep call is **type → slot → fee → confirm, four interactions**, because the show already knows the date, venue and setlist — the fee it also knows is the one field that must never be inherited, and is declared blank every time (04.01.03 D-17). This is the persona where poster ≠ hirer bites hardest: a dep depping *out* is posting a slot on someone else's gig, and the bandleader is the hirer (D-07). |
| Producer | Full | Posts session calls (instrument + part + delivery date), topline/writer briefs, and mix/master engagement requests. Their posts carry material references (a rough, a stem set, a tempo/key) that Musician posts often do not — this is what makes 04.02.03 material-aware matching possible at all. **Their dates are deadlines, not wall-clocks** (D-10): "by Friday" is an instant, and a player in London and a player in LA must see the same instant, not the same numeral. They are also the persona the spec-work gate binds hardest, and therefore the persona with the strongest incentive to type-launder (DT-09). |
| Operator | Full | Posts support slots, house-engineer and crew calls, resident-artist openings, and "who's free to fill a cancelled Tuesday" — the perishable-inventory case from `personas.md`. Their posts are the only ones routinely carrying legal eligibility (insurance, certs, right-to-work) rather than musical eligibility, and the only ones routinely **multi-slot** (2 riggers + 1 LD for one load-in — D-08). Their dates are venue-local wall-clock, always: doors at 7 means doors at 7 (D-10). Their recurring calls (a weekly open mic, a resident night) seed the composer from a past post rather than from a show (DT-15). |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees posts distributed to the **open rung only** (04.01.02) on the public board (04.02.01) — never a targeted post, never contact detail, never the eligibility checklist. Cannot post: a Fan has no need to hire. **Not harmless, as this file previously claimed** — a member-wanted ad from a band with a following is a lineup-change announcement, and the Fan lens is the surface on which it becomes public (DT-08). **Exception risk**: the function/private-hire buyer *does* need to post and is not any of these four personas — see Q-01. |

## Behavior

The post is deliberately thin at its core with type-scoped extensions layered on. Four structural
facts, each of which the breadth pass got wrong or left unsaid:

**1. The post is a container; the slot is the unit that gets filled (D-08).**
The post carries context — type, acting identity, hirer, decider, date, **decide-by** (D-16),
location, compensation
policy. Each **slot** carries a role, a count, its own eligibility criteria and its own compensation
value. A function gig needing a dep drummer and a dep bassist is one post with two slots, not two
posts. The lifecycle `reviewing → decided → closed` is **per slot**; the post's overall status
simply follows its slots (`published` while any slot is open, `closed` when every slot is terminal).
A post can be
simultaneously decided on drums and reviewing on bass, and no single-state model can say that.
Soft ceiling: **25 slots per post** — past that it is a hiring round, and the shape that serves it
is the open call (04.07), which has batch review the spine deliberately lacks.

**2. Poster ≠ hirer ≠ decider (D-07).**
Music casting routinely separates the human who types the post, the entity that pays and contracts,
and the human who chooses. An MD casts for an artist. A studio manager posts a session the label
pays for. A dep depping out posts a slot on the bandleader's gig. Every post therefore declares an
**acting identity** (personal, or a Band/Studio/Venue/Agency entity from 01 where the human's edge to
that entity carries the posting activity — which of `01.03.03` D-01's closed seven that is remains
open at Q-08; the two-axis conjunction itself is `01.03.02` D-05) and a
**decider**. Where the human has 2+ affiliations the acting identity is an
**explicit, unskippable choice, never a silent default** — posting a session as yourself when you
meant the studio routes the reputation (04.05.02) and the payment counterparty to the wrong entity,
invisibly. This is `personas.md`'s multi-hyphenate fact and its "a persona is not always a person"
fact, made into two required fields. On a membership edge the domain conjunct is universal
(`01.03.02` D-05); on a representation edge — an MD posting for an artist, an agency for its roster —
the post's commercial domain must also be in the edge's `domains`, so a live-booking-only agent
cannot post a session call.

**3. Post-from-context is the default path; cold-typing is the fallback (D-02).**
The platform already holds the show, the setlist, the project and the fee. Retyping them is the
fragmentation the product exists to end. Context also makes duplicate detection *exact* rather than
heuristic (D-13), makes cancellation propagate honestly, and is the only source of a dep-out's
substitution policy (Q-04). Cold-typing is always available and always complete — this feature does
not require 17/18 to launch — but it is the degraded path and the composer says so.

**4. Every post has a `decide-by`, and nobody is asked for it (D-16).**
The decide-by is the date by which the poster will have decided — the date `04.04.01` D-06 renders
to an applicant as "You'll hear by 14 Aug", the ceiling on every hold's resolve-by (`04.04.01` D-05),
the denominator of the `dormant` trigger, and the date at which the post auto-closes. Five decisions
and two queue states depended on it before it existed. It is **always present**, so all of them are
bounded by construction; it is **derived** from the type's own date semantics (D-10) rather than
asked, so the four-interaction dep path stays four; and it is **overridable**, rendered as a derived
value with an edit affordance for the poster who wants to decide sooner than the gig. **One type
derives from a duration rather than a date**: `Member wanted` (04.06) has no event to point at and
never did, so its decide-by is **publication + 90 days** — ratified 2026-07-30 as a lifecycle value
in its own right, not borrowed from `04.02.01`'s 90-day board half-life, whose D-06 keeps that
board's windows presentation-only. The two types
where the decision date is honestly a different fact from the work date — brief/RFP and open call
(04.07) — get a real asked field instead, because the taxonomy exists to carry exactly that
distinction. It is a terms field (D-09): moving it is a change every waiting applicant is told about.

### The Taxonomy

Types identified from the sweep's candidates, all folded here rather than becoming nodes. Every type
carries five scoped properties, because those five are what the discriminator is *for*:

| Type | Origin | Date semantics (D-10) | Decide-by default (D-16) | Unpaid legit? (D-11) | Casting class (D-15) | What makes it distinct |
|------|--------|----------------------|--------------------------|----------------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Dep / sub call | 03 | Venue-local wall-clock | Derived — the event date | No | Performance | Urgency in hours; material-aware matching (04.02.03); first-accept fill (04.04.04) |
| Session call | 05 | Absolute deadline | Derived — the stated deadline | No | Performance | Carries material refs; outcome is a Services engagement or Project session |
| Function / private hire | 01 | Venue-local wall-clock | Derived — the event date | No | Performance | Buyer is not a platform persona — see Q-01 |
| Audition / casting call | 04 | Venue-local wall-clock | Derived — the event date | **Yes** (presence outcome) | Performance | Adds an audition task (04.03.03); may enable blind mode (04.03.04) |
| Brief / RFP | 05, 10 | Absolute deadline | **Asked** — a real field, blank and required, **never defaulted to the deadline** | No | Performance | Buyer-initiated, scoped deliverable; outcome is a Services engagement |
| Crew / technical labour | 07 | Venue-local wall-clock | Derived — the event date | No | **Labour** | Legal eligibility (certs, insurance, right-to-work), day rates, tour-length |
| Festival / showcase / competition open call | 08 | Absolute deadline | **Asked** — a real field, blank and required, **never defaulted to the deadline** (04.07 D-05) | No | Performance | Deadline-batched, many-to-few; the fee hazard (04.07) |
| Songwriting camp invitation | 11 | Venue-local wall-clock | Derived — the event date | No | Performance | Invite-only targeting; **cannot be cold-typed** (D-06) |
| Support slot | 09 | Venue-local wall-clock | Derived — the event date | **Yes** (door-split/presence) | Performance | Frequently has no post at all — see 04.03.05 outbound pitch |
| Member wanted | 06 | Venue-local wall-clock | **Derived from a duration — publication + 90 days** (D-16; the one type with no event date to derive from) | **Yes** (presence outcome) | Performance | Mutual selection, outcome is Band membership — see 04.06 |
| Open-mic / showcase slot | 08, 09 | Venue-local wall-clock | Derived — the event date | **Yes** (presence outcome) | Performance | Operator's honest-unpaid category; exists so door-split gigs don't have to lie |
| **General call** | — | Poster picks; both offered | Derived — follows whichever semantics the poster picked | **No** | **Labour** (strictest) | The escape hatch (D-04). Fully specified, deliberately strict: paid shapes only, core vocabulary only, no protected-characteristic criteria. |

Nine of the twelve rows derive to a date the post already carries, and a tenth — `Member wanted` —
derives from a **duration measured from publication**, because it carries no event date at all
(D-16). All ten pay **no extra interaction** for a decide-by and none of them can publish without
one (D-16). The two `Asked` rows are the
cases where the two dates are honestly different — a festival deciding in September for a June bill,
a brief deciding before delivery — and the taxonomy exists to carry exactly that distinction; they
are also the two least urgent shapes in the domain, so the field costs nothing that matters. On
those two the field is **blank and required, and is never pre-filled or defaulted to the deadline**
(04.07 D-05): a decide-by silently carrying the deadline's value would tell 2,000 acts they will
hear by 1 March, which is precisely the ghosting 04.07 D-04 exists to prevent, generated by the
platform rather than stated by the Operator. So on ten types the decide-by cannot be absent because
it is derived — nine from a date the post carries, one from a 90-day duration — and on two it cannot
be absent because publish is blocked until the poster states it — which is why **no type can publish
without a decide-by** either way. The per-type mapping above is
the senior-dev call; **tunable at `/create-prd`**, in the same way D-13's intervals are.

The taxonomy is **closed and platform-curated** (D-04). It is not extensible by posters, because an
unknown type has no compensation rule set and CX-04 makes that fail closed — a user-created type
would be unpostable by construction (DT-12). The long tail is served by the `General call` type plus
a **type-request signal**: a poster who cannot find their type names what they were trying to post,
in free text, and that text is a curation input — never a live type.

### Happy Path

**A. Post from context — the default (D-02).** A bandleader's drummer drops out at 9pm.

1. Poster opens the booked show (17) and taps "Need a dep". The type is inferred from the context
   (a booked show → dep/sub call) and shown, not asked.
2. System opens the composer **pre-filled from the show**: date (venue-local, D-10), the
   **decide-by derived from it** (D-16 — a dep call is a presence type, so it is the event date;
   shown with an edit affordance, never asked), venue,
   setlist (from 18), and the hirer — which is the **booking's** hirer, not the poster, because
   the poster is depping out (D-07). **Compensation is not among them.** It is blank and required,
   every time, because inheriting the booking's fee would disclose the fixer's margin to the dep
   (04.01.03 D-17, DT-15).
3. Poster picks the slot: role = drums, count = 1. Eligibility is pre-filled from the show's own
   requirements; the pool counter is live (04.01.04 D-03).
4. Poster declares the fee for the slot (04.01.03) — the one term the show holds and must not
   supply.
5. Poster taps publish. The compensation gate evaluates synchronously (CX-01) and passes on the fee
   just declared.
6. Post enters `published`; targeting resolves (04.01.02) and the ranked dep list becomes the
   urgent-fill cascade (CX-05). **Four interactions from "drummer dropped out" to "asking Sam"** —
   three of context and one of money, and the money one is the interaction the dep is entitled to.
   **The decide-by adds none of them** (D-16): it is derived at step 2 and only touched by a poster
   who wants to decide sooner than the gig.

**B. Cold-typed — the fallback.** A producer with no session in the system needs a bass player.

1. Poster taps "Post an opportunity" and picks a type from the closed list. **The taxonomy leads**,
   because it selects the date's meaning (D-10), the eligibility vocabulary (CX-02), the
   compensation rule set (CX-04) and the casting class (D-15). Nothing downstream can be asked
   until the type is known.
2. Poster picks the acting identity (D-07) — explicit, unskippable if they have 2+ affiliations.
   The decider defaults to the poster and is editable.
3. System loads the type-scoped composer: core fields plus that type's extensions. The date field's
   meaning came from the type (D-10), and the **decide-by follows it** — derived and editable on ten
   of the twelve types, a real asked field on brief/RFP and open calls, where the decision date is a
   separate fact (D-16). Where a past post
   of the same type exists, the composer offers to seed from it (DT-15). Fields autosave on blur.
4. Poster adds one or more slots (D-08), sets compensation per slot (04.01.03), sets requirements
   per slot (04.01.04), and chooses distribution (04.01.02).
5. Poster hits publish. The gate evaluates synchronously (CX-01). Duplicate detection runs
   (D-13) — tier 2, heuristic, warn-not-block.
6. On pass, the post enters `published` and is released to the board (04.02.01) and the alert
   fan-out (04.02.04). Publication is what fires the first alert; **bump and edit never re-fire it**
   (D-13). The pipe's own cap is **two alerts per (user, post), lifetime** — the second spent on an
   eligibility-widening edit or a deadline escalation, whichever comes first (04.02.04 D-12).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Type & taxonomy** | | |
| Poster picks the wrong type after filling the form (draft) | Type change re-scopes the vocabulary; fields the new type doesn't recognise are surfaced for explicit discard, never silently dropped (CX-02). If the date's semantics change (wall-clock ↔ deadline, D-10) the date is **cleared, not converted** — a converted date is a plausible wrong answer | "Changing to Crew job — 3 fields don't apply and will be removed: [list]. The date means something different for crew jobs, so you'll need to re-enter it. Keep a draft copy of this version?" |
| Poster tries to change type on a *published* post | Refused unconditionally, even at zero submissions (DT-13) — the alert fan-out already broadcast the old type. Sanctioned path: close and repost, pre-filled | "You can't change the type of a live opportunity — it's already gone out in alerts as a dep call. Close it and repost? Everything carries over except the type." |
| Poster's need fits no type | `General call` is offered (D-04) with its deliberately strict rules, alongside a free-text type-request that goes to curation, never to the taxonomy | "Nothing fitting? Post it as a General call — paid only — and tell us what you were trying to post." |
| Platform retires a type with live posts of it | Retired types stop being **offerable**; live and historical posts keep theirs and render normally (D-05). Types are never remapped | Poster: nothing. The post is unaffected. |
| A type ships without a compensation rule set | Fails closed (CX-04) — the type is not offerable at all | Not offered in the picker. Never a half-working type. |
| Compensation rules for a type tighten while posts are live | **Not retroactive** (D-12). Live posts were gated under the rules in force at publish. A rule added because a scam was found is a Trust & Safety takedown (24), not a silent re-gate | Poster: nothing, unless taken down by 24, which is 24's message |
| **Type-laundering (DT-09)** | | |
| Poster files a free test mix as "Member wanted" to reach an unpaid-legitimate type | Refused. Unpaid legitimacy requires a **presence outcome** (D-11) — member-wanted asks you to turn up to a rehearsal; it cannot ask you to send a mix. A slot requesting a deliverable forces the paid gate regardless of type | "Member wanted can't ask for a deliverable. If you need a mix, that's a session call — and it needs a fee." |
| Poster files a paid crew job as an "Audition" to reach GOR criteria (D-15) | Refused at the criteria step, not the type step — the audition vocabulary has no rigging cert, and the crew vocabulary has no protected characteristics. The two are disjoint, so laundering costs the poster the criteria they actually needed | "Auditions can't require a rigging certificate. Crew job?" |
| **Multi-slot & lifecycle (D-08)** | | |
| One slot filled, another still open | Post stays `published`; the filled slot shows `decided` and stops accepting submissions; the open slot is unaffected. Post-level status follows the slots, never set on its own | Board: "Bass — still looking. Drums — filled." Applicants to drums are dispositioned now (04.05.01), not at post close. |
| Two deciders fill the last slot simultaneously | Slot accepts exactly one decision; the loser's action is refused, not queued (04.04 owns the decision; this feature owns the slot's single-decision property) | Loser: "Alex just gave this to Sam — 8 seconds ago." |
| Poster reduces slot count from 2 to 1 with 5 submissions in | Terms change (D-09) — re-gates and notifies every submitter with the delta and a one-tap withdraw | Submitters: "This changed: 2 bass slots → 1. Still interested? [Stay] [Withdraw]" |
| Post needs 30 slots (a festival's whole crew) | Refused above 25; routed to the open call shape (04.07), which has the batch review a 30-slot spine post would need and doesn't have | "That's a hiring round, not an opportunity — open calls handle 30 slots properly." |
| **Terms changes (D-09)** | | |
| Compensation edited downward on a live post with applicants | Terms change (D-09): re-gate + notify + one-tap withdraw. **This answers 04.01.03 Q-02 at the object level** — the rule is not per-field, it is per-terms-set. Silence = applicant STAYS | Submitters: "The fee changed: £150 flat → door split. [Stay] [Withdraw]" (no action within the applicant's own timing = stays in the pool) |
| Criteria tightened on a live post with submissions | Terms change (D-09): same rule, same silence default. Already-submitted applicants are **never retro-invalidated** and **never silently grandfathered** — they are notified and choose; no response = they STAY. **This answers 04.01.04 Q-02** | Submitters: "This now requires: reads notation. [Stay] [Withdraw]" (no action = stays) |
| Date changed on a live post with applicants | Terms change (D-09): same rule, same silence default (no response = stays). People applied *because they were free that night*; a silent date change is the purest bait-and-switch in the domain and nobody had named it | Submitters: "Moved: Fri 24th → Sat 25th. [Stay] [Withdraw]" |
| Decide-by pushed back on a live post with applicants | Terms change (D-09): re-gate + notify + one-tap withdraw, same silence default (no response = stays). **This is the single most important reason decide-by is a terms field** — a poster who quietly moves "you'll hear by 14 Aug" to 30 Sep has ghosted twelve people while the UI keeps showing a date, and that is precisely the harm 04.05 exists to price. Shortening it notifies on the same rule; the notification is good news and costs nothing | Submitters: "The decision date moved: 14 Aug → 30 Sep. [Stay] [Withdraw]" |
| Date moved, and the derived decide-by moves with it | **One terms change, not two.** Where the decide-by is derived (D-16), a base-date move carries it and both appear in the *same* delta — a second notification for a field the applicant did not know was separate is noise | Submitters: "Moved: Fri 24th → Sat 25th, so you'll now hear by the 25th. [Stay] [Withdraw]" |
| Submitter never responds to a terms-change notification | **No auto-withdraw, no suspend, no reconfirm gate.** Silence is a stay: the submission remains live in the pool under the new terms. Withdraw is the only opt-out and it is explicit (one tap). Applies identically to compensation, criteria, date, and every other terms field (D-09) | Submitter: nothing further; their application stands. Poster: applicant still listed, badged "notified of change" |
| Description prose edited, material link added | Not a terms field. Freely editable, no notification, no re-gate | Nothing |
| **Duplicates & resurfacing (D-13)** | | |
| Post duplicates one the poster already has live — context-raised | **Tier 1, exact**: same source context (show/session/project) + same role slot = the same need, deterministically. The composer offers the existing post instead of creating a second | "You've already got a drums call open on this show. [Open it] [Bump it] [Post a second one anyway]" |
| Post duplicates one the poster already has live — cold-typed | **Tier 2, heuristic**: same acting identity + same type + same role + start within ±24h + same location → **warn, never block**. Two Tuesdays are two needs and a false block is worse than a duplicate | "This looks like your existing 'Bassist — Fri 24th, The Lexington'. [Bump that instead] [These are different — post it]" |
| Poster re-posts to resurface (board spam) | The behaviour is legitimate; the mechanism was not. **Bump** is the sanctioned action: 1 per rolling 24h per post, max 3 per post lifetime. Bump re-ranks the board and **does not re-fire alerts** (D-13) — otherwise bump is free notification spam | "Bumped. Next bump available in 24h — 2 left." |
| Urgent type wants to bump | Bump is not offered to dep/sub calls: their lifespan is shorter than the interval, and escalation (04.01.02) is their resurface mechanism | Escalation UI instead. Bump not shown. |
| **Concurrency & races** | | |
| Two admins of one Band draft the same dep call at once | Context-raised: the composer surfaces the sibling draft against the same context key before publish. Cold-typed: no key exists, so it surfaces at publish via tier-2 detection | "Alex started a drums call on this show 4 minutes ago. [See theirs] [Carry on anyway]" |
| Poster double-taps publish | **The second tap adds nothing** — it lands on the post the first tap already published; it never creates a second | The published post. No duplicate, no error. |
| Publish fails on the network (soundcheck, loading dock) | Draft is intact — fields autosave on blur, and a failed publish returns the composer with every value present and a retry. Retry is safe because a repeated publish lands on the same post rather than making a new one | "Couldn't publish — you're offline. Nothing's lost. [Retry]" |
| Source context is cancelled while the post is live (show cancelled in 17) | Post auto-transitions to `withdrawn`, and applicants are told **the real reason**. This is a better disposition than a generic withdrawal, and it is only possible because of consolidation | Applicants: "The show on the 24th was cancelled, so this dep call is closed." Not "the poster withdrew this." |
| **Off-platform fill (DT-07)** | | |
| Gig gets filled in the DMs and the post rots with 12 applicants | `Filled elsewhere` is a **first-class, one-tap terminal action** offered proactively on any post older than its type's typical fill window. One tap closes every open slot **and** fires the close-out (04.05.01) with an honest pre-written disposition to all applicants. This is the lazy path made correct | Poster prompt: "Did you fill this? [Yes, filled elsewhere] [Still looking]" → "Closed. All 12 applicants have been told it went elsewhere." |
| Poster uses `filled elsewhere` to escape the close-out obligation | It *is* the close-out — the disposition fires as part of the action, not instead of it. It is honest, so it must not score as ghosting in 04.05.02; it is also not free, because the applicants are still told | Applicants: "Filled — the poster found someone off-platform." |
| **Expiry & silence** | | |
| Post's **decide-by** passes while still `published` | **The decide-by is what closes the post** (D-16). Every open slot auto-transitions to `closed`, but the close-out obligation still fires (04.05.01) — an expired post does not excuse ghosting. This is the date `04.05.01`:68/:147 and `04.04.01`:136 have always cited; it now exists | "This closed automatically. You still have 12 applicants to respond to." |
| Post's **date** passes while still `published`, and the decide-by is later | **The date closes submission, the decide-by closes the post** (D-16). No new applications; the queue stays live and the poster still owes every waiting applicant an answer. On the nine date-derived types this branch never fires — the two dates are the same instant, both events land together, and the behaviour is exactly the one-date rule this row replaced. On `Member wanted` it is unreachable from the other side: the ad carries no event date to pass, only a decide-by at publication + 90 days, so submission and the post close together. It fires on a brief or an open call (04.07), where deciding after the deadline is the normal shape | Applicants: "Applications closed. Decisions by 30 Sep." Poster: queue open, countdown to the decide-by |
| Poster shortens the decide-by to before the post's date | Legitimate and supported — "I'll pick by Tuesday for a gig on Saturday". Submission and the post both close at the **earlier** of the two, because a poster who has stopped deciding must stop collecting applications. The gig itself is unaffected; it is not this object's event | Board: "Applications close Tue 20th." |
| Post expires with **zero** submissions | The platform's clearest failure signal, and the poster must not be left to conclude the market is empty (domain Q-06). The close-out names the **single narrowing factor with the largest pool delta**, computed from the same counter as 04.01.04 D-03 | "Nobody applied. Your travel radius was 5 miles — at 25 miles, 47 people matched." |
| **Permissions & identity** | | |
| Musician posts as a Band they're a member of but not an admin of | The acting-identity picker only offers entities where the human's edge carries the posting activity (Q-08 — the verb is unsettled; the conjunction is `01.03.02` D-05). Holding none means only personal identity is offered — never a silent fallback to personal when they picked the Band | "You can't post as The Wreckage — ask an admin for posting rights." |
| Poster names themselves as the decider and then applies to their own slot | Post-side: the post declares the decider (D-07), which is the precondition for detecting it. **A decider cannot be a candidate for a slot they decide** — they must hand the decision to another human on the hirer entity. `decider` is a field on the post, never a mandate activity, because `01.03.03` D-01's enum is closed. An MD taking a keys slot in the band they cast is completely normal; deciding it themselves is not. Rule lives in 04.04; the field that makes it detectable lives here | "You're the decider on this slot. To apply for it, hand the decision to someone else at The Wreckage." |

Confidentiality does not depend on the poster remembering to mark it — distinguishing "invite-only" from "doesn't exist" leaks the existence of a quiet vacancy (04.01.02 DT-02, DT-08) | "This opportunity isn't available to you." |

### States

#### Lifecycle — slot-level (the fillable unit, D-08)

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Open | Post published, slot unfilled | Board listing accepting submissions; live applicant count |
| Reviewing | First submission on this slot | Applicant count, triage entry point (04.04.01) |
| Submission closed | The post's date passed but the decide-by has not (D-16) | No new submissions; the queue is live and the decision is still owed. Only reachable where the two dates differ — a brief or an open call, or a poster who lengthened the decide-by |
| Decided | Offer accepted on this slot (04.04.03) | Winner shown; slot stops accepting; remaining applicants **to this slot** owed a disposition now, not at post close |
| Closed | All applicants on this slot dispositioned, or **the decide-by passed** (D-16), or filled elsewhere | Slot terminal; contributes to the post's overall status |

#### Lifecycle — post-level (container; **status follows the slots**, never set directly)

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Draft | Composer opened | Editable form, "not visible to anyone yet" |
| Gate-blocked | Publish attempted, compensation gate failed (CX-01) | Post stays in draft with the specific unmet condition named |
| Published | Gate passed; ≥1 slot open | Live on board, first alert fanned out — bump never re-fires it (D-13), cap 2 lifetime per (user, post) (04.02.04 D-12) — submission open |
| Reviewing | ≥1 slot reviewing, ≥1 open | Mixed: "Drums — filled. Bass — 6 applicants." |
| Decided | All slots decided, dispositions outstanding | Winners shown; the close-out is what remains |
| Submission closed | No slot is accepting, no slot is terminal — the post's date passed and the decide-by has not (D-16) | "Applications closed. Decisions by 30 Sep." The queue is live and every waiting applicant is still owed an answer (04.05.01). Reachable only where the two dates differ: a brief, an open call (04.07), or a lengthened decide-by |
| Closed | All slots terminal — in the ordinary case because **the decide-by passed** (D-16) | Archived to the poster's history (04.05.04), with its type preserved (D-05) |
| Withdrawn | Poster cancels, or source context cancelled, or poster suspended | Applicants notified — withdrawal is a disposition, not an escape from one. Reason is honest for a cancelled context, neutral for a suspension |
| Filled elsewhere | Poster taps the one-tap action (DT-07) | Every open slot closed + every applicant dispositioned in one action |

#### Composer & interaction states

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Composer opened from context (17/18) | Skeleton composer with the inherited fields resolving; the type is already shown because it was inferred, not fetched |
| Empty | Cold-type, poster has no affiliations and no past posts | Type picker only. No acting-identity step (nothing to choose between), no seed-from-past offer. Not an error — the ordinary first post |
| Populated | Type chosen, fields resolved | Type-scoped composer; inherited fields badged as inherited and editable; live pool counter (04.01.04 D-03) |
| Partial | Context exists but is incomplete — show booked but not advanced, so no setlist (18) | Inherited fields shown; **missing ones named and actionable**, never silently absent. The degradation is honest and it pulls the user into the domain that fixes it — "No setlist on this show yet — [add one] to match on who's played the material." Matching degrades to geo + availability (04.02.03) |
| Error | Publish failed (network, gate, validation) | Every typed value intact; the specific unmet condition named; retry safe because a repeated publish lands on the same post rather than making a new one |

| D-09 | The post has a **terms set** — type, acting identity, date, **decide-by (D-16)**, location, slot count, compensation, required criteria. **The decide-by is a terms field, not an operational one** — a poster who silently pushes "you'll hear by 14 Aug" out to 30 Sep has ghosted every applicant while the UI still shows them a date, which is the exact harm the 04.05 sub-domain exists to price; and D-09's whole point is that no field invents its own handling. This is the **single source of terms-change semantics** for the whole opportunity object; 04.01.03 (compensation) and 04.01.04 (criteria) defer to this rule and do not define their own. **Silence semantics are explicit and uniform across every change kind: no response = applicant STAYS in the pool.** The withdraw is opt-in and one-tap; there is **no auto-withdraw on silence, no silent grandfathering, and no suspend-until-reconfirm**. | D-16 | **Every post has a `decide-by`, and it is derived rather than asked.** It is never absent, so every mechanism that depends on it is bounded by construction. The default follows D-10's type-scoped date semantics, with **one named exception**: **presence types** (dep/sub call, function/private hire, audition/casting call, crew, camp, support slot, open-mic — every type whose date is venue-local wall-clock *and* which carries an event) derive it from the **event date**; **delivery types** (session call, brief — every type whose date is an absolute deadline) derive it from the **stated deadline**; and **`Member wanted` derives from a duration rather than a date — publication + 90 days** (04.06), because it is the one type with no event to point at. The composer renders it as a derived value with an **edit affordance**, so a poster who wants to decide sooner shortens it and nobody is forced into an interaction. **Two types get a real asked field** — brief/RFP and festival/showcase/competition open call (04.07 D-05) — because there the decision date is genuinely a separate fact from the work date. **The decide-by is what closes the post** (see the expiry rows and the slot lifecycle); the post's date closes *submission*, and on the nine date-derived types the two are the same instant.
