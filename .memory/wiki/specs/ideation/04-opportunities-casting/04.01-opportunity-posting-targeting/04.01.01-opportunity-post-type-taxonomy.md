# Feature: Opportunity Post & Type Taxonomy

> **Number**: 04.01.01
> **Parent**: [Opportunity Posting & Targeting](./04.01-opportunity-posting-targeting-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The single `Opportunity` object that every demand-side need in the platform becomes, and the type
taxonomy that discriminates its behaviour. A dep call, a session call, a function sub, an audition,
a crew job, a brief, a festival open call, a camp invitation and a support-slot request are one
entity with one lifecycle (`draft → published → reviewing → decided → closed`) and a type field —
not nine products. This exists because the parent domain's rationale is explicit that building the
post→submit→triage→decide workflow once per type is the waste that makes this domain worth
consolidating in the first place.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Posts dep/sub calls ("need a drummer Friday, 25-song set"), member-wanted ads, function-band subs, and depping-out of their own booked work. Also the highest-volume *reader* of other people's posts — the parent index calls this the #1 reason they open the app. Their posts are usually urgent and low-ceremony; the composer is optimised for a phone at a soundcheck, not a desk. |
| Producer | Full | Posts session calls (instrument + part + delivery date), topline/writer briefs, and mix/master engagement requests. Their posts carry material references (a rough, a stem set, a tempo/key) that Musician posts often do not — this is what makes 04.02.03 material-aware matching possible at all. |
| Operator | Full | Posts support slots, house-engineer and crew calls, resident-artist openings, and "who's free to fill a cancelled Tuesday" — the perishable-inventory case from `personas.md`. Their posts are the only ones routinely carrying legal eligibility (insurance, certs) rather than musical eligibility. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees public postings on the board only (04.02.01) — a member-wanted ad or an open call reads as ambient scene activity and is harmless. Sees nothing targeted, nothing invite-only, and no contact detail. Cannot post: a Fan has no need to hire. **Exception risk**: the function/private-hire buyer *does* need to post and is not any of these four personas — see Q-01. |

## Behavior

The post is deliberately thin at its core (who, what, when, where, type, compensation) with
type-scoped extensions layered on. Types identified from the sweep's candidates, all folded here
rather than becoming nodes:

| Type | Origin candidate | What makes it distinct (and why it's a field, not a product) |
|------|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Dep / sub call | 03 | Urgency in hours; material-aware matching (04.02.03); first-accept fill (04.04.04) |
| Session call | 05 | Carries material refs; outcome is a Services engagement or Project session |
| Function / private hire | 01 | Buyer is not a platform persona — see Q-01 |
| Audition / casting call | 04 | Adds an audition task (04.03.03); may enable blind mode (04.03.04) |
| Brief / RFP | 05, 10 | Buyer-initiated, scoped deliverable; outcome is a Services engagement |
| Crew / technical labour | 07 | Legal eligibility (certs, insurance, right-to-work), day rates, tour-length |
| Festival / showcase / competition open call | 08 | Deadline-batched, many-to-few; the fee hazard (04.07) |
| Songwriting camp invitation | 11 | Invite-only targeting; camp *organisation* is not this domain — see Cross-Cut Notes |
| Support slot | 09 | Frequently has no post at all — see 04.03.05 outbound pitch |
| Member wanted | 06 | Mutual selection, unpaid-legitimate, outcome is Band membership — see 04.06 |

### Happy Path

1. Poster taps "Post an opportunity" and picks a type — the taxonomy leads because it selects
   everything downstream (eligibility vocabulary per CX-02, compensation rules per CX-04).
2. System loads the type-scoped composer: core fields plus that type's extensions, pre-filled from
   the poster's context where it can (a dep call raised from a booked show inherits date, venue,
   and setlist without retyping).
3. Poster fills the need, sets compensation (04.01.03), sets requirements (04.01.04), and chooses
   distribution (04.01.02).
4. Poster hits publish. The compensation gate evaluates synchronously (CX-01).
5. On pass, the post enters `published` and is released to the board (04.02.01) and the alert
   fan-out (04.02.04).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Poster picks the wrong type after filling the form | Type change re-scopes the vocabulary; fields the new type doesn't recognise are surfaced for explicit discard, never silently dropped (CX-02) | "Changing to Crew job — 3 fields don't apply and will be removed: [list]. Keep as a draft copy?" |
| Poster tries to change type on a *published* post with submissions | Refused — type is immutable post-publication; every submission was made against the old type's terms | "You can't change the type of a live opportunity. Close this one and post again — applicants will be told it was withdrawn." |
| Post duplicates one the poster already has live | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — detection heuristic unknown. The hazard is real (posters re-post to resurface, which is board spam) but the rule is not yet decidable. | `[PENDING]` |
| A need doesn't fit any type in the taxonomy | Depends on Q-03 (fixed vs extensible taxonomy) — unresolved | `[PENDING]` |
| Post's date passes while still `published` | Auto-transitions to `closed`, but the close-out obligation still fires (04.05.01) — an expired post does not excuse ghosting | "This closed automatically. You still have 12 applicants to respond to." |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Draft | Composer opened | Editable form, "not visible to anyone yet" |
| Gate-blocked | Publish attempted, compensation gate failed | Post stays in draft with the specific unmet condition named (CX-01) |
| Published | Gate passed | Live on board, alerts fanned out, submission open |
| Reviewing | First submission received | Applicant count, triage entry point (04.04.01) |
| Decided | Offer accepted (04.04.03) | Winner shown; remaining applicants still owed a disposition |
| Closed | All applicants dispositioned, or withdrawn, or expired | Archived to the poster's history (04.05.04) |
| Withdrawn | Poster cancels | Applicants notified — withdrawal is a disposition, not an escape from one |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The 10 type-shaped candidates from the sweep (01, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11) are one object with a discriminator, not ten nodes | Parent domain index states the workflow "serves gigs, auditions, festival slots, briefs and camps, so building it five times is waste" | ✅ CONFIRMED — folded into this taxonomy. Two survived as separate nodes on genuine mechanical divergence, not type divergence: 04.06 (mutual selection, outcome is entity membership) and 04.07 (deadline-batched many-to-few with a fee hazard). |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect the post to be **raised from existing context**, not typed from scratch — the dep call exists *because* a booked show exists, and the show already knows the date, venue, setlist and fee | Reasoning from the Musician workflow in `personas.md` ("the dep circuit... gigs won in minutes") and D-18's consolidation half — the platform's advantage is that it already holds the surrounding facts | ✅ CONFIRMED — "post from context" is the consolidation payoff made concrete, and it is the precondition for material-aware matching (04.02.03). A cold-typed dep call has no setlist, so the domain's most novel feature degrades to a geo filter. Recorded as D-02 below. |
| DT-03 | The taxonomy is where **advance-fee fraud** enters — "pay £200 to secure your audition slot" is the oldest scam in casting and it arrives as a well-formed post | Reasoning about failure modes at the demand side; the Fan anti-persona in `personas.md` covers scalping but nothing covers *fraud against musicians* | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real hazard, ❌ REJECTED as this feature's job. Detection belongs to Trust & Safety (24) and its infrastructure to `/create-prd-security`; the *gate* that makes a money-flows-from-applicant post impossible to express is 04.01.03. Logged as a cross-cut, not built here. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | One `Opportunity` object, type as discriminator; 10 sweep candidates folded in as types | The parent domain's own stated rationale. Building per-type is the waste this domain exists to eliminate. |
| D-02 | **Post-from-context is the default composition path**, cold-typing is the fallback | The platform already holds the show, the setlist, the project and the fee. Retyping them is the fragmentation the product exists to end (D-18 consolidation half), and the context is exactly what 04.02.03 needs to match on. |
| D-03 | Type is **immutable after publication** | Every submission was made against that type's terms, eligibility vocabulary and compensation rules. Mutating it retroactively re-writes what applicants agreed to. |
