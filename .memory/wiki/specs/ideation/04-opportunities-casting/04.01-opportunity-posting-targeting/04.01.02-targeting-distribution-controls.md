# Feature: Targeting & Distribution Controls

> **Number**: 04.01.02
> **Parent**: [Opportunity Posting & Targeting](./04.01-opportunity-posting-targeting-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Who an opportunity is shown to. Real music hiring is overwhelmingly *not* an open call — it is a
poster working down a mental list of people they trust, and only falling back to the open board
when the list is exhausted. This feature makes that ladder explicit: invite-only → trusted network
→ filtered pool → open board, with the ability to escalate down the rungs as a post goes unfilled.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Hand-picks named deps in preference order — the single most-used path in the dep circuit, because a bandleader's ranked list of drummers *is* their casting process. Escalates to the open board only when the list runs dry, usually under time pressure. |
| Producer | Full | Targets prior collaborators (people they have verified credits with, per 02) before anyone else. Their trust signal is "I've worked with them", which the platform can actually evidence rather than guess. |
| Operator | Full | Targets pool-shaped rather than name-shaped — "verified local crew within 20 miles", "acts who've played here before", "engineers with PLI insurance". Their targeting is a filter, not a list, because they're filling recurring slots rather than one relationship. |
| Fan | None | Cannot target. Also cannot *see* targeted posts — a Fan's board view (04.02.01) contains only posts distributed openly. This is the boundary that keeps a dep call carrying a phone number away from a consumer audience. |

## Behavior

Four distribution rungs, ordered by narrowness:

| Rung | Who sees it | Typical use |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Invite-only | Named individuals | The dep ladder; camp rosters; a support slot offered to one act |
| Trusted network | Prior collaborators / verified-credit neighbours (02) | "Anyone I've actually worked with" |
| Filtered pool | Anyone matching criteria + availability | Operator crew calls; open-ish session calls |
| Open board | Everyone (Fan-visible) | Member-wanted, open calls, function gigs |

### Happy Path

1. Poster picks a rung. Invite-only is offered first for urgency-shaped types (dep calls), open board first for many-to-few types (open calls) — the type sets the default.
2. For invite-only, poster names people **in order** — the order is the feature, not decoration; it drives the cascade in 04.04.04.
3. Post publishes to that rung only.
4. If unfilled by a poster-set threshold (time elapsed, or list exhausted), the post **escalates** one rung out, and the poster is told rather than asked — under dep-call time pressure, a confirmation dialogue is a failure.
5. Escalation continues until filled or the open board is reached.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Invited person fails eligibility criteria (CX-03) | **Unresolved and important.** Recommendation carried to Step 5: honour the invite, show the poster the mismatch. Silently dropping a person the poster deliberately named is a silent failure with a real cost. | Poster: "Sam doesn't meet 'reads notation' — invite anyway?" |
| Escalation reaches the open board on a post carrying private contact detail | Escalation must strip or gate contact detail at the open rung | "This is now public — your number has been hidden. Applicants will message in-app." |
| Poster targets someone who has blocked them | Invite silently fails — but silently to *whom*? Telling the poster leaks the block. | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — a Trust & Safety (24) boundary. |
| Invite-only post to people who are all unavailable | Cascade exhausts immediately, escalates on rung, not on timer | "All 5 are booked that night — opening to your network now." |
| Poster escalates a post that was invite-only *because it was confidential* (a band quietly replacing a member) | Escalation leaks the fact of the vacancy | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — confidentiality may need to be a property separate from the rung. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty | No targets chosen | "Who should see this?" with the type's default rung pre-selected |
| Named list | Invite-only chosen | Ordered, drag-reorderable list with availability shown inline (04.02.02) |
| Escalating | Threshold hit | "No response from your list — opening to your network in 2 hours" with a cancel |
| Open | Escalated to board | Public listing, contact detail gated |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Targeting is really a **ranked cascade**, not a visibility setting — a bandleader doesn't "target 5 drummers", they ask their first choice and move down | Reasoning from how dep hiring actually works; the parent index's "dep gigs are won in minutes" implies serial urgency, not parallel broadcast | ✅ CONFIRMED — order is load-bearing and drives 04.04.04. This is also why targeting is *not* the same as eligibility (CX-03): a ranked list encodes human judgement the platform cannot compute. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect **confidentiality** to be orthogonal to reach. A band quietly seeking a replacement for a member who doesn't know yet is invite-only for secrecy, not for narrowness — and auto-escalation would be catastrophic | Reasoning about the member-wanted case (04.06) against this feature's escalation ladder; nobody in the candidate list raised it | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real and unhandled case. Escalation must be opt-out-able per post, and confidentiality is likely a separate flag rather than a rung. Logged as Q-02 — this is a genuine gap, not a resolved design. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Targeting is an **ordered cascade**, not a flat audience | Matches how dep hiring actually works and is the input 04.04.04 requires. A set has no first choice; a career does. |
| D-02 | "Trusted network" is defined by the **verified-credit graph (02)**, not the follow graph (03) | This is the thesis (D-18) applied to targeting: "people I have actually worked with" is a fact the platform holds and no competitor can retroactively manufacture. "People I follow" is copyable by anyone. |
| D-03 | Escalation **notifies rather than asks** | Under dep-call time pressure a confirmation dialogue is an unanswered notification, and the gig goes unfilled. Cancellable, not confirmable. |
