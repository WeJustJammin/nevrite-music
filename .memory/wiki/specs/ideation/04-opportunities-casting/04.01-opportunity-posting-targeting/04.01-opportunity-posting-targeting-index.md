## Overview

The demand side of the spine: how a need becomes a published, discoverable object. One
`Opportunity` entity carries every type the sweep found as a separate product — dep call, session
call, function gig, audition, crew job, brief/RFP, festival open call, camp invitation, support
slot — discriminated by type rather than duplicated per type. This sub-domain owns the object, its
type taxonomy, who is allowed to see it, what a candidate must satisfy to be eligible, and the
compensation gate that must be cleared before anything publishes at all.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities that interact directly. Targeting
constrains who can match against eligibility criteria; eligibility criteria are type-dependent (a
rigging cert matters for crew, a setlist matters for a dep); and the compensation gate *blocks
publication* of the object the other three describe. None of them is meaningful alone.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | **One `Opportunity` object with a type discriminator**, not one product per opportunity type | The parent domain's own rationale states the workflow "serves gigs, auditions, festival slots, briefs and camps, so building it five times is waste". The sweep listed 10 of 14 candidates as types; they are field-and-rule variations on one lifecycle, not separate mechanisms. | Parent domain index rationale; `/ideate-discover` Step 3 classification |
| D-02 | **Compensation disclosure is a publication gate, not a form field** | The parent index names Sonicbids/ReverbNation as the cautionary tale — "the mechanic is right; charging desperate artists per submission is what destroyed their credibility". A gate is a product-values commitment; a field is decoration. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 Deep Think |
| D-03 | Crew-specific and camp-specific candidates **merged** into taxonomy + eligibility rather than becoming nodes | Their entire distinctness is *which requirements apply* (rigging cert vs setlist) and *how targeting is set* (invite-only for camps). Both are parameters of features already here. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 classification |
