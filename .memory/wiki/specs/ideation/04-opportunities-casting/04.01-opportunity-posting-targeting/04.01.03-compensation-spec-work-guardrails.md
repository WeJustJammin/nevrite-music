# Feature: Compensation Disclosure & Spec-Work Guardrails

> **Number**: 04.01.03
> **Parent**: [Opportunity Posting & Targeting](./04.01-opportunity-posting-targeting-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The gate every opportunity must clear before it publishes: state what you are paying, in what
shape, and prove that no money flows *from* the applicant. This is the product's answer to the
failure the parent domain names outright — "Sonicbids and ReverbNation are the cautionary tale —
the mechanic is right; charging desperate artists per submission is what destroyed their
credibility." A field asking for compensation is decoration; a gate refusing to publish without it
is a position.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | As poster: declares fee shape (flat, day rate, door split, per-song, points) before publishing; blocked until they do. As applicant: sees the declaration as a first-class badge on every listing and can filter the board by it — "paid only" is the filter that makes the board usable. Their protection is that "exposure" is not an expressible value. |
| Producer | Config | The gate binds hardest here. "Send me a quick test mix and we'll see" is the canonical spec-work abuse in this persona's territory, and their own anti-persona entry in `personas.md` ("withholds stems as leverage") shows the leverage asymmetry is already recognised. Test/trial work must be declared as such, scoped, and either paid or capped. |
| Operator | Config | Declares guarantee vs door split vs door-split-after-costs — the three are wildly different and are routinely conflated to make a gig look better than it is. Door-split-only posts read honestly rather than as "paid". |
| Fan | None | Cannot post, so never encounters the gate. Does not see compensation on the public board either — a Fan browsing a member-wanted ad has no need for the rate, and exposing pro rates to a consumer audience is a pricing-transparency decision nobody has made. |

## Behavior

Two distinct protections that the sweep bundled as one candidate ("Spec Work & Unpaid Audition
Guardrails") but which defend against opposite attackers:

| Protection | Attacker | Mechanism |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **No applicant-side fees** | The *platform* (and posters charging for access) | Money flowing from applicant to poster/platform for the right to submit is not expressible in the data model. Not "discouraged" — unrepresentable. |
| **Spec-work limits** | The *poster* | Unpaid work products (test mix, spec topline, sample arrangement) must be declared, scoped, round-capped, and — where the type says so — paid. |

Compensation shapes, because a binary paid/unpaid flag is a lie in this industry:

- Flat fee · Day rate · Per-song · Hourly · Door split · Door split after costs · Guarantee vs
  door (whichever is greater) · Points/royalty share · Buyout · Unpaid-legitimate (type-scoped, CX-04)

### Happy Path

1. Poster reaches the compensation step; the shapes offered are scoped to the type (a member-wanted
   ad is not offered "day rate"; a crew job is not offered "points").
2. Poster picks a shape and enters values. Royalty/points shapes hand off to Rights (09) rather than
   being restated here.
3. If the post asks for any unpaid work product, the spec-work sub-gate fires: declare the
   deliverable, cap the rounds, and state what happens to the work if the applicant is not selected.
4. Publish. Gate evaluates synchronously (CX-01). Pass → published.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Poster tries to charge an application fee | Refused — unrepresentable, not merely blocked | "WeJammin never charges musicians to apply for work, and neither can you." |
| Poster picks "unpaid" on a type where unpaid is illegitimate (CX-04) | Refused | "A test mix is work. Set a fee, or mark it as a paid trial." |
| Poster picks "unpaid" on a member-wanted ad | Passes — joining a band is legitimately unpaid | Nothing; the gate is silent when it should be |
| Poster edits compensation *downward* on a live post with applicants | **The bait-and-switch race.** Re-gates, and applicants who submitted under the old terms must be notified — silent mutation rewrites what they agreed to | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — whether it re-gates, notifies, or voids submissions is undecided |
| Door split declared, but the actual door take is zero | Not this feature's problem at post time — settlement truth lives in 17 | — |
| Poster declares a fee and then doesn't pay after the work is done | Out of scope here; this gate governs *disclosure*, not enforcement. Non-payment is 24 (disputes) and 23 (finance). | — |
| A new type ships without a compensation rule set | **Fails closed** — the type cannot publish until its rules exist | Poster: generic "this opportunity type isn't available yet" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Undeclared | Composer opened | Shape picker, type-scoped |
| Declared | Shape + values entered | Preview of how it reads on the board — "£150 flat" vs "door split" look different and should |
| Spec-gate open | Unpaid work product requested | Deliverable / rounds / retention sub-form |
| Blocked | Publish attempted, gate failed | Post held in draft with the specific unmet condition named |
| Passed | Gate cleared | Post proceeds to publish |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The Sonicbids failure and the spec-work problem are **the same candidate but opposite attackers** — one defends against the platform's own monetisation temptation, one against the poster | Reasoning about sweep candidate 14, which bundled them; the parent index names only the Sonicbids half | ✅ CONFIRMED — kept as one feature (both are the publication gate) but as two named protections. The distinction matters because the first is a *business-model commitment the platform makes against itself* and the second is *enforcement against users*. Conflating them would let the first quietly erode. |
| DT-02 | A binary paid/unpaid flag is unusable in music. "Door split", "guarantee vs door whichever is greater", "points", and "buyout" are all normal and mean radically different things | Domain reasoning; Operator persona sells perishable time and routinely posts door-split gigs | ✅ CONFIRMED — compensation is a shape with a schema per shape, not a boolean plus a number. A binary flag would force honest door-split posts to be misfiled as "paid", which is the exact misrepresentation the gate exists to prevent. |
| DT-03 | The **advance-fee scam** ("£200 secures your audition") is what this gate actually kills, and it is a bigger real-world harm than spec work | Reasoning from 04.01.01 DT-03; nothing in the candidate list or `personas.md` anti-persona sections covers fraud *against* musicians — the anti-persona entries all cover musicians behaving badly | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is the strongest argument for "unrepresentable" over "prohibited". A prohibited thing gets expressed in the description field ("DM me about the slot fee") and needs detection; an unrepresentable one at least has no legitimate-looking surface. ❌ REJECTED as *fully solved here* — the description-field workaround is real, and detecting it is Trust & Safety (24) + `/create-prd-security`. This gate raises the cost; it does not close the hole. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Compensation is a **publication gate**, not a field | The parent index names the Sonicbids collapse as this domain's cautionary tale. A gate encodes the lesson in behaviour; a field encodes it in marketing copy. |
| D-02 | Applicant-side fees are **unrepresentable**, not prohibited | A prohibition needs enforcement and gets circumvented. Absence from the data model means there is no honest-looking surface for the scam. It does not eliminate the description-field workaround (DT-03) — that is 24's problem. |
| D-03 | Compensation is a **shape with a per-shape schema**, not a boolean + amount | Door split, guarantee-vs-door, points and buyout are all normal in music and mean different things. A binary flag forces honest posts to misrepresent themselves. |
| D-04 | Unpaid legitimacy is **type-scoped** (CX-04) | Joining a band, an open-mic slot and a rehearsal tryout are legitimately unpaid. A free test mix is not. Only the type tells them apart. |
