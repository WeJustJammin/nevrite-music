# Feature: Compensation Disclosure & Spec-Work Guardrails

> **Number**: 04.01.03
> **Parent**: [Opportunity Posting & Targeting](./04.01-opportunity-posting-targeting-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The gate every opportunity must clear before it publishes: state what you are paying, in what
shape, and prove that no money flows *from* the applicant as the price of being considered. This is
the product's answer to the failure the parent domain names outright — "Sonicbids and ReverbNation
are the cautionary tale — the mechanic is right; charging desperate artists per submission is what
destroyed their credibility." A field asking for compensation is decoration; a gate refusing to
publish without it is a position.

The gate governs **disclosure, not fairness**. It does not decide whether £80 for a four-hour
function gig is a good rate — the platform is not a rate regulator, and a platform that adjudicated
rates would push the dep circuit back to WhatsApp, which contradicts D-18's whole premise (being
where the work happens is the precondition for capturing anything). It decides one thing: that a
post cannot be **mistaken** for something it is not. "£200" that turns out to be for the whole
band, before a 200-mile drive, paid when the label pays, is not a lie the platform will carry.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | **As poster**: declares the six facets (shape, amount/basis, unit, currency, expenses, timing) before publishing; blocked until they do. The dep-out case is theirs specifically — depping out their own booked gig, where the fee they offer is legitimately less than the fee they were booked for, and the gate must never leak the difference (DT-15). **As applicant**: sees the declaration as a first-class badge on every listing and filters the board by it — "paid only" and "paid on the night" are the filters that make the board usable. Their protection is that "exposure" is not an expressible value, and that a `£800 / total for a 5-piece` post cannot render as `£800`. |
| Producer | Config | The gate binds hardest here. "Send me a quick test mix and we'll see" is the canonical spec-work abuse in this persona's territory, and their own anti-persona entry ("withholds stems as leverage") shows the leverage asymmetry is already recognised. Any unpaid work product must be declared, scoped in hours, capped at **one round**, and carry evaluation-only retention with no use rights (D-16). Their **points** declarations are the ones that need a base — a Producer saying "3 points" and a Musician hearing "3% of the money" are different numbers by roughly 5× (DT-13). |
| Operator | Config | Declares guarantee vs door split vs door-split-after-costs — three wildly different things routinely conflated to make a gig look better than it is. **"After costs" must enumerate the costs at post time** or it is not representable (DT-09): costs that are named after the show expand to fill the door, and that is the oldest trick in live music. Door-split-only posts read honestly rather than as "paid". Their perishable-inventory pressure (an unsold Tuesday) is the exact pressure that produces a downward edit at 4pm — which is why D-05 permits the edit while never reading an applicant's silence as consent to the new terms (04.01.01 D-09). |
| Fan | None | Cannot post, so never encounters the gate. **Also never sees the compensation badge**: the Fan-facing board projection omits it entirely, and per D-14 the logged-out/public projection *is* the Fan projection — so even if the board goes public and indexable (domain Q-11), a scrapeable index of every working musician's rate is never produced. Rates are commercially sensitive to the seller; price transparency as a *deliberate* product is 05.01.04's question, not a side effect of SEO. |

## Behavior

Two distinct protections that the sweep bundled as one candidate ("Spec Work & Unpaid Audition
Guardrails") but which defend against opposite attackers:

| Protection | Attacker | Mechanism |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **No consideration-gated applicant fees** | The *platform* (and posters charging for access) | Money flowing from applicant to poster/platform **as a condition of being considered** is not expressible in the data model. Not "discouraged" — unrepresentable. Note the precision: this is not "money never flows from the applicant" (D-12, DT-10). |
| **Spec-work limits** | The *poster* | Unpaid work products (test mix, spec topline, sample arrangement) must be declared, scoped in hours, capped at one round, and carry evaluation-only retention. Where the type says so, they must be paid. |

### The declaration is six facets, not a number

A compensation declaration is incomplete — and the post will not publish — until all six are
resolved. Each exists because its absence is a specific, common, real-world deception:

| # | Facet | Values | Why it is mandatory |
|---|-------|--------|--------------------|
| 1 | **Shape** | Flat fee · Day rate · Hourly · Per-song · Door split · Door split after costs · Guarantee vs door (whichever is greater) · Points/royalty share · Buyout · Paid trial · Unpaid-legitimate (type-scoped, CX-04) | A binary paid/unpaid flag forces honest door-split posts to be misfiled as "paid" (DT-02, D-03) |
| 2 | **Amount or basis** | A number in the declared currency; or the split basis (how many ways, who is in the pot, what comes off the top, enumerated); or the points base + recoupment terms | "Door split" without a basis is a promise with no arithmetic (DT-09). "5 points" without a base is a factor-of-5 ambiguity (DT-13). |
| 3 | **Unit** | Per person · Total for the act · Per song · Per day · Per hour · Per show | **The single most common misread in live music**: £800 for a 5-piece is £160 each (DT-05). For a one-person post the two collapse and the picker is skipped. |
| 4 | **Currency** | ISO currency code on every money amount, including the currency the door is taken in | Musicians travel; "150" is three different fees (DT-05) |
| 5 | **Expenses** | Included · Reimbursed against receipt (categories enumerated: travel, parking, tolls/congestion, accommodation, backline, freight) · Not covered | "£200 including travel" vs "£200 plus travel" is a ~£60 swing on a 200-mile drive, and deps lose money on gigs they already said yes to (DT-06) |
| 6 | **Timing** | On the night · On delivery · On acceptance (05.04.01) · N days after invoice (N declared) · On release · On recoupment | The most common complaint is not the rate, it is the wait. "£150 on recoupment" is not £150 (DT-07). It is also what makes the fee a dated receivable in 23. |

**Rejected as facets, deliberately:**

- **"Negotiable" / "competitive" / "DOE"** — unbounded openness is how every job board's compensation
  field dies (DT-11). A **range with a floor** is representable (`£150–£300`); the **floor is what
  renders on the badge and what the filter matches**, so widening the range to win the filter costs
  the poster the headline number.
- **A credit** — "no fee but you'll get a producer credit" is not a compensation shape (D-11, DT-14).
- **Exposure, "good for your profile", "great for the CV"** — no field accepts them.
- **£0 as a flat fee** — £0 is not a flat fee, it is unpaid, and unpaid is type-scoped (D-09).

### The spec-work sub-declaration

Fires whenever the post asks the applicant to produce anything the poster could use. Required:

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Deliverable | Named, with format (e.g. "rough mix, stereo bounce, MP3 320") |
| Declared effort | In hours. Rendered on the badge. Unpaid permitted only up to the platform ceiling (**proposed default: 2 hours** — a test mix is a session-length task; past that a Producer is doing the client's job for free). Ceiling value is Q-05. |
| Rounds | **Exactly 1** for unpaid. One round is a sample; two is a revision cycle, which is the work itself. More rounds ⇒ the shape must be `Paid trial`. No exception (D-16). |
| Retention | **Evaluation-only. No use rights. Returned or destroyed on non-selection.** "We keep it and may use it" is not expressible — collecting forty free test mixes, using the best, and hiring nobody is the actual scam, and this is the same unrepresentability logic as D-02. |
| If selected | Declared: does the trial work get paid retroactively, and does it fold into the engagement? |

`Paid trial` is the honest version of a test mix: a declared fee for the trial itself, **paid
regardless of selection**. The gate does not ban test mixes. It prices them.

### Happy Path

1. Poster reaches the compensation step. The shapes offered are **type-scoped** (CX-04): a
   member-wanted ad is not offered "day rate"; a crew job is not offered "points"; a dep call is
   not offered "buyout". Compensation is **never pre-filled from the source booking** even when the
   post was raised from context (04.01.01 D-02) — see DT-15.
2. Poster picks a shape. The facet form for that shape loads — a flat fee asks for amount, unit,
   currency; a door split asks for the basis; points ask for base and recoupment.
3. Poster enters values. Unit defaults to `per person` for single-player posts and is **required,
   never defaulted,** for band-level posts (04.06, 04.07) where the £800-for-a-5-piece misread lives.
4. Poster sets expenses treatment and payment timing. Both required; neither has a silent default.
5. If the post asks for any unpaid work product, the spec-work sub-gate fires: deliverable, declared
   effort in hours, one round, retention terms.
6. Poster sees the **badge preview** — exactly how this renders on the board. `£150 flat · per
   person · travel not covered · paid on the night` and `door split · 4 ways · after PA + sound
   engineer` look different, and they should.
7. Poster hits publish. **The poster is never shown a success state, and the post never appears on
   the board, until the gate has finished evaluating** (CX-01) — no "published!" then a silent
   retraction. A post that briefly surfaces before the gate finishes is an unvetted post on the
   board, which is the one outcome the feature exists to prevent.
8. Pass → the declaration is stamped as **version 1** and the post enters `published`, releasing it
   to the board (04.02.01) and the alert fan-out (04.02.04). The gate is upstream of every alert in
   the domain.
9. Points and buyout shapes carry their declared terms forward as **intent**, not as an instrument.
   The rights record is created in 09 at offer acceptance, where a counterparty finally exists
   (D-07, CX-08).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Poster tries to charge an application fee | Refused — unrepresentable, no field accepts it | "WeJammin never charges musicians to apply for work, and neither can you." |
| Poster picks "unpaid" on a type where unpaid is illegitimate (CX-04) | Refused | "A test mix is work. Set a fee, or mark it as a paid trial." |
| Poster picks "unpaid" on a member-wanted ad | Passes — joining a band is legitimately unpaid | Nothing; the gate is silent when it should be |
| **Member-wanted ad where members chip in £15/week for the rehearsal room** — money flows *from* the applicant | **Passes.** This is a shared cost, not a consideration-gated fee (D-12, DT-10): it falls due only *after* selection, is payable to a shared pot or a third party, and never to the poster personally. It is **mandatory to disclose** — £60/month of subs is material to whether you join. | Badge: "Unpaid · members share costs · ~£15/week rehearsal room" |
| Same, but the "shared cost" is payable to the poster personally | **Refused.** That is a joining fee wearing a rehearsal room's clothes. The recipient facet is what tells them apart. | "A cost you collect personally is a fee. WeJammin can't post that." |
| Poster declares a flat fee of £0 | Refused. £0 is not a flat fee — unpaid is a shape, not an amount, and it is type-scoped (D-09). This is the trivial bypass of the entire gate. | "£0 isn't a fee. If this is unpaid, say so — and unpaid isn't available for this type." |
| Poster declares a negative amount, a non-existent currency, or a 10-digit fee | Refused at the facet, with a concrete bound per shape: **money amounts** must be > 0 and ≤ 1,000,000 in the declared currency (7 digits — a seven-figure single gig fee is a fat-finger, not a dep call); **points/royalty share** must be > 0 and ≤ 100 (you cannot assign more than 100% of a base); **door-split ways** must be an integer 2–50 (a one-way split is a flat fee; past 50 the "pot" is not a band); **spec-work effort** must be > 0 and ≤ 8 hours (a full working day — past that it is a job, not a trial, regardless of the 2-hour unpaid ceiling in Q-05); **payment-timing N-days-after-invoice** must be an integer 0–180 (six months is the outer edge of a real receivable). Currency must be a live ISO 4217 code. | Inline error on the field, on blur, naming the bound it broke |
| Poster puts "DM me about the £200 slot fee" in the description | Gate passes (the declaration is clean) — **the gate does not read prose**. Detection is 24's. The gate raised the cost of the scam; it did not close the hole (DT-03). | Nothing here. The report path is 24.01. |
| Poster declares "door split" but leaves the basis empty | Blocked as **Partial** — the shape is picked, the arithmetic is missing | "Split how many ways, and what comes off the top first?" |
| Poster declares "door split after costs" without enumerating costs | Refused. Unenumerated "costs" expand to fill the door (DT-09). | "List what comes off the top. 'Costs' with no list isn't a split." |
| Poster declares "3 points" without naming the base | Refused (DT-13) | "Points of what — the master, the writer's share, or net receipts? And do they recoup?" |
| Poster declares a buyout without enumerating what it buys | Refused. An unscoped buyout is unrepresentable — same logic as D-02 (D-15, DT-08). | "A buyout has to say what it buys: master, publishing, neighbouring rights, name/likeness, AI training." |
| Buyout enumerates everything *except* AI/model-training use | **Passes, and the badge says so explicitly** — "AI training: not included". Silence is never assent here; the item is always rendered, present or absent (D-15). | Badge line: "Buyout · master + neighbouring rights · **AI training NOT included**" |
| Poster edits compensation **downward** on a live post with applicants | **The bait-and-switch race.** Re-gates. Declaration version increments. Every applicant who submitted under the old terms is notified and their submission is marked `terms-changed`, with **[Stay] / [Withdraw]** offered. **Silence keeps the applicant in the pool** (04.01.01 D-09) — silence is read as neither withdrawal nor as consent to the new terms. Staying is not agreeing: the reduced terms are re-shown and explicitly consented to at offer acceptance (04.04.03), so a musician who never saw the notice is never bound to £120 by silence and never loses their place over an unseen notice (D-05). | Applicant: "The fee on [post] changed: £200 → £120. You applied under the old terms. [Stay in the running] or [Withdraw]. If you do nothing, you stay in — and you'll see these terms again before any offer is final." |
| Poster edits compensation **upward** | Re-gates (must still be valid). Applicants are notified — good news, and it proves the mechanism runs both ways. **No reconfirm required**; nobody was harmed. | Applicant: "Good news — the fee on [post] went up: £200 → £250." |
| Poster edits compensation upward by £1 to re-trigger the alert fan-out | **Fails.** Compensation edits notify *applicants only* and **never re-fire the fan-out** (04.02.04) — an edit that bumps the post is a resurfacing exploit and is board spam by another name. | Nothing. The post does not move. |
| Poster makes 5 downward edits in 10 minutes | One notice, not five. Edits **batch on a 10-minute settle window**, and the notice states the net change from the last-notified terms. Kills the spam without creating a bypass. | Applicant: one notice, showing v1 → v6 net |
| Poster makes a 4th downward re-gate on one live post | **Locked** to further downward edits and flagged to 24.03 (fraud/risk ops). Repeated downward edits are the drip version of the bait-and-switch. Cap value is owner-tunable (D-05). | Poster: "You've reduced this three times. Close it and repost — applicants will be told." |
| Submission arrives during the 10-minute settle window | The submission binds to the declaration **version live at the moment it was created**, and is never silently re-bound to a later version. If the version later changed downward, the applicant stays in the pool on silence (04.01.01 D-09) but is never treated as having accepted the new terms until they explicitly consent at offer acceptance (04.04.03). | Applicant sees the terms they applied under, always |
| Two people edit compensation concurrently (band manager + band member) | A save **succeeds only if the terms the editor started from are still the current terms**; if the other person's save already landed, this save is refused and the form re-opens showing exactly what changed, so no edit is ever silently overwritten. Both edits pass through the gate; neither can put an unevaluated declaration live. | "Someone else changed this while you were editing: [diff]. Review and re-save." |
| A band member without post authority edits the band's compensation | Refused at the permission layer (01 governance), before the gate is reached. The gate never adjudicates *who* may declare, only *what* is declarable. | "Only [band]'s admins can change what this gig pays." |
| Poster deletes the whole compensation declaration on a live post | Refused — there is no path from `published` back to undeclared. The post can be withdrawn (which is a disposition, 04.05.01), not silently de-gated. | "You can't un-declare a live gig's pay. Withdraw it instead — applicants will be told." |
| Agency bulk-imports 50 crew calls via API/CSV, 6 rows lack compensation | The **6 rows fail; the 44 publish.** Batch import is not a bypass — there is no batch-level override, and failing the whole batch would train importers to pad rows with junk. | Import report: "44 published, 6 held: [rows] — no fee declared" |
| Platform admin asks to force-publish a vetted institutional poster past the gate | **Refused — no override exists** (D-10). An override converts "unrepresentable" into "prohibited, with exceptions", and the exception becomes a sales feature. Admins can **unpublish**, never force-publish. | Admin tooling has no such control (resolves 04.01 CX-01 synthesis Q3) |
| Post claims union/collective terms (MU, AFM) below the published minimum | Refused **when the platform holds that rate card** (05.01.03); otherwise the claim renders as **self-claimed and unverified**, never as verified. A false union claim is a real harm. The gate never imposes a floor on non-union work (DT-04). | "This is below the [MU] minimum for a [session]. Remove the union claim or raise the fee." |
| A new type ships without a compensation rule set | **Fails closed** — the type cannot publish until its rules exist (CX-04) | Poster: "This opportunity type isn't available yet." |
| Publish request times out; poster retries | **Retrying publish on the same draft can never create a second post or a second alert fan-out** — the retry re-evaluates and returns the same verdict, and if the first attempt actually succeeded the poster lands on the already-published post rather than a duplicate. | Same result, no duplicate post |
| Poster raises a dep call from their own booked show (04.01.01 D-02) | Date, venue and setlist inherit. **Compensation does not** (DT-15) — pre-filling it would silently disclose to the dep what the poster is being paid, and the fixer's margin is a legitimate third-party commercial term. | Compensation step is blank and required, every time |
| Door split declared; the actual door take is zero | Not this feature's problem at post time — settlement truth is 17's. The declared basis is what 17 settles against, which is precisely why the basis is mandatory. | — |
| Poster declares a fee and then doesn't pay after the work is done | Out of scope here; this gate governs *disclosure*, not enforcement. Non-payment is 24.04 (disputes) and 23.03 (receivables). | — |
| Poster uses an unpaid, non-selected applicant's test mix anyway | Out of scope here — the declaration made it evaluation-only, and enforcement of that is 09/24.05. The gate's job was to make the terms unambiguous enough to enforce. | — |
| Unpaid festival open call — pays nothing, sells tickets | **Publishes, badged `Unpaid`.** The platform does not adjudicate whether an unpaid festival slot is fair (DT-12); it refuses to let it be mistaken for paid. Whether unpaid commercial posts rank lower is 04.02's ranking question (domain Q-07), not this gate's. | Badge: "Unpaid" — same badge an open mic gets, which is the point |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | Compensation step opened; type-scoped shape list resolving (CX-04) | Skeleton shape picker. If the type's rule set has not resolved, publish stays disabled — never enabled-then-revoked. |
| **Empty / Undeclared** | Composer opened, nothing picked | Type-scoped shape picker, no defaults pre-selected. Copy names the gate up front: "Every gig on WeJammin says what it pays." |
| **Partial** | Shape picked, one or more of the six facets unresolved | Facet form with the unresolved ones named individually — never a generic "incomplete". Badge preview renders greyed with the gaps visible: `£150 flat · **unit?** · **expenses?** · paid on the night` |
| **Populated / Declared** | All six facets resolved | Live badge preview exactly as it will render on the board, plus the spec-work line if any |
| **Spec-gate open** | An unpaid work product is requested | Deliverable / effort-hours / rounds / retention sub-form, with the one-round rule stated before it is hit, not after |
| **Blocked** | Publish attempted, gate failed | Post held in `draft` with **the specific unmet condition named** — never "validation failed". The post is never lost. |
| **Passed** | Gate cleared | Declaration stamped v1; post proceeds to publish, board and fan-out |
| **Re-gating** | Compensation edited on a live post | Edit is evaluated before it commits; during the 10-minute settle window the poster sees "changes pending — applicants notified in N min", and can revert without a notice ever firing |
| **Terms-changed** | A downward re-gate committed | **Poster**: permanent "terms changed" marker on the post, with the original terms visible to every future reader — the badge's value is that it is true, and a post that was £200 yesterday is a fact the next applicant deserves. **Applicant**: [Stay] / [Withdraw] prompt — silence leaves them in the pool (04.01.01 D-09), with the reduced terms re-shown for explicit consent at offer acceptance. |
| **Locked** | 4th downward edit attempted | Downward edits refused; post is flagged to 24.03. Upward edits and withdrawal remain available. |
| **Error** | Gate evaluation itself fails (rule set unreachable, transient) | **Fails closed** — "We couldn't check this gig's pay terms. Nothing was published; your draft is saved. Retry." Never fails open; an unevaluated declaration reaching the board is the one outcome the feature exists to prevent. |

| D-01 | Compensation is a **publication gate**, not a field | The parent index names the Sonicbids collapse as this domain's cautionary tale. A gate encodes the lesson in behaviour; a field encodes it in marketing copy. |
| D-02 | Consideration-gated applicant fees are **unrepresentable**, not prohibited | A prohibition needs enforcement and gets circumvented. Absence from the data model means there is no honest-looking surface for the scam. It does not eliminate the description-field workaround (DT-03) — that is 24's problem. **Scope refined by D-12.** |
| D-03 | Compensation is a **shape with a per-shape schema**, not a boolean + amount | Door split, guarantee-vs-door, points and buyout are all normal in music and mean different things. A binary flag forces honest posts to misrepresent themselves. |
| D-04 | Unpaid legitimacy is **type-scoped** (CX-04) | Joining a band, an open-mic slot and a rehearsal tryout are legitimately unpaid. A free test mix is not. Only the type tells them apart. |
| D-05 | **Downward edits on a live post are permitted, versioned, and never silent** — re-gate, increment the version, notify every applicant, mark their submission `terms-changed`, and offer **[Stay] / [Withdraw]**. **Silence keeps the applicant in the pool**, aligned to the domain terms-set rule (04.01.01 D-09) — silence is read as neither withdrawal nor as consent to the new terms; explicit consent to the reduced fee is deferred to offer acceptance (04.04.03), where the current terms are re-shown. Batched on a 10-minute settle window; locked after 3 downward re-gates and flagged to 24.03. | *Resolves Q-02.* Rejected forbidding the edit outright (the 04.01.01 D-03 approach for type): budgets genuinely get cut, and a banned edit becomes a DM saying "actually it's £100" — invisible, off-platform, and worse. Being where the work happens (D-18) means the honest correction must be expressible. Rejected auto-withdrawing on silence: a musician who applied at £200 and never saw the notice should not lose their place over an unseen notice — the terms-set rule (04.01.01 D-09) makes silence keep them in, and the "never agreed to £120" concern is answered at offer acceptance, where the reduced terms are re-shown and explicitly consented to before anything binds. **Materiality is any change that reduces what the applicant receives or increases what they bear** — amount down, shape less certain (flat → door), per-person → total, expenses included → not covered, timing lengthened, recoupment added, buyout scope widened, spec-work rounds increased. **No de-minimis threshold** — a percentage floor invites five 4% edits. The batching window prevents the notification spam that a no-threshold rule would otherwise cause. The lock **count (3) is owner-tunable**; the existence of a lock is not. |
| D-06 | **Spec-work rules are one shared mechanism, owned by neither 04 nor 05** — evaluated by this publication gate and by 05's listing/quote gate | *Resolves Q-04.* If 04 owns it and 05 imports it, 05 forks it the first time a service case doesn't fit. If duplicated, posters arbitrage on day one — route the free-test-mix demand through whichever gate is softer. Same signature as the split-capture trigger (domain D-06): a mechanism derived independently in two places is a cross-cut. Flagged for Step 6 synthesis. |

| D-13 | **Unbounded "negotiable" is unrepresentable; a range with a floor is** — and the **floor** is what the badge shows and the filter matches | A poster who doesn't know the scope still knows their minimum. Pinning the filter to the floor removes the incentive to post `£100–£5000` (DT-11). |
| D-14 | **The public/logged-out board projection is the Fan projection** — compensation never renders publicly | Decides the Role Lens/board-visibility interaction without pre-empting domain Q-11 (public board?). A scrapeable index of every working musician's rate harms the seller and gifts the buyer; price transparency as a *deliberate* product is 05.01.04's question, not an SEO side effect. |

Recoupment on points follows the same always-rendered rule (DT-13). |
