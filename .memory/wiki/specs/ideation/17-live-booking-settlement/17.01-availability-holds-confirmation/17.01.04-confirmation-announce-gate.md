# Feature: Confirmation & Announce Readiness Gate

> **Number**: 17.01.04
> **Parent**: [Availability, Holds & Confirmation](./17.01-availability-holds-confirmation-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-30

## Overview

The terminal transition of a date: hold → **confirmed** → **announced**. Confirmation is the hard,
near-irreversible write that destroys every inferior hold, blocks the calendar on both sides, and
creates the booking record every downstream domain reads. Announce is a separate, later gate — a
show can be confirmed for months before it is announceable, and conflating the two is a live-music
modelling error with real commercial consequences.

This feature owns **two authorisation gates and nothing else**. It decides *whether* a date may
become a booking, and *whether* a booking may become public. It does not set the announce clock
(that is [19.02.01](../../19-ticketing-box-office/19.02-on-sale-announce-presale/19.02.01-announce-on-sale-scheduling.md)),
does not publish anything (that is domain 21), and does not sell a ticket (that is domain 19).
**Domain 17 decides when a show is allowed to exist publicly; it does not make it exist.**

**Deep Think addition** — this feature was not in the sweep's 18 candidates. The sweep modelled the
hold ladder but never modelled what the ladder resolves *into*, which would have left domains 19
(Ticketing) and 21 (Promotion) with no trigger.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **Must approve confirmation of their own date**, and the approval binds to the exact offer version they saw ([17.02.03](../17.02-offers-negotiation/17.02.03-offer-approval-chain.md)) — an agent confirming a version the band never approved is the failure this gate exists to prevent. Sees the confirmed date land on their merged calendar with its routing buffer, and is prompted to void the now-dead holds naming them on that date at other rooms (DT-08). **Owns the announce group** — the tour is theirs, so they decide which dates announce together, and only they may eject a date from the group. Holds one of the two announce keys; cannot announce unilaterally. |
| Producer | None | No role. The Producer persona owns the studio room, not the stage; studio booking is domain 16 and live crew is domain 18. A Producer who books a room is acting as an **Operator** in that moment, and a Producer who plays the show is acting as a **Musician** — neither lens is "Producer" here (`meta/personas.md`: personas are lenses on behaviour). |
| Operator | Full | Executes the confirmation once both approval chains are satisfied; sees exactly which inferior holds their confirm will destroy **before** committing, and is accountable for the notification that follows. Sets their room's **house default** for the waivable announce preconditions (contract, deposit) and holds the second announce key. Sees that a show sits in a tour announce group and how many members are still blocking — **never which rooms they are**, because another promoter's unannounced date is not their business. May request ejection; may not perform it. |
| Fan | None (until announce) | Nothing until announce fires, and then nothing *here* — the fan-facing artefact is created by domains 19 and 21. This gate is the trigger, not the surface. Two properties of this gate exist entirely for the Fan's protection and are invisible to them: **lineup honesty** (P-04 — the bill they buy is the bill they were sold) and **exactly-once announce** (DT-16 — a duplicate announce is a duplicate gig alert to every follower, and the alert *is* the Fan's entire relationship with the platform). |

## Behavior

### The two gates

| Gate | Question it answers | Irreversibility |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmation** | May this date become a booking? | Near-irreversible. Revert is a *compensating action* with permanent collateral damage — destroyed holds never return (CX-03). |
| **Announce** | May this booking become public? | **Absolutely irreversible.** There is no un-announce. Once a show is on a fan's calendar and screenshotted, the only exits are cancellation ([17.05.03](../17.05-deposits-balances-cancellation/17.05.03-cancellation-tiers-forfeit.md)) or postponement (domain Q-03). |

### Confirmation

Confirmation is a set of writes that **must be atomic**: destroy inferior holds on the room-date,
hard-write both calendars, create the booking record. A partial confirm leaves a date that is
neither held nor bookable — the worst possible state, and it must be unreachable rather than
recoverable.

**Confirmation preconditions** — all hard, evaluated at the moment of commit, not at the moment the
button rendered. **One exception exists in the whole set**: C-06 carries a break-glass override,
available only while a 17.01.03 challenge clock is live, exercisable only by the artist principal,
and priced by immediate notice to the injured party plus a permanent attributed breach record
(D-13, 17.01.03 D-15). C-01 through C-05 have no override of any kind, by any party, ever:

| # | Precondition | Why | On failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | An **accepted offer version** exists ([17.02](../17.02-offers-negotiation/)) | Resolves Q-03: *confirmed* means terms agreed; it does **not** mean contracted (DT-06) | Blocked; routes to the open offer thread |
| C-02 | Artist entity approval covering **that exact offer version hash** ([17.02.03](../17.02-offers-negotiation/17.02.03-offer-approval-chain.md)) | The band approved v2 and the agent accepted v4 is the canonical failure | Blocked, naming the approver and the version drift |
| C-03 | Operator-side sign-off satisfied for the fee (their own spend threshold, 17.02.03) | A talent buyer committing above their authority | Blocked, naming the internal approver |
| C-04 | This hold is at position 1, or every superior hold has collapsed | Position 3 confirming over a live 1st hold is the abuse the ladder prevents (CX-02, R-02) | Blocked, naming the position |
| C-05 | No existing `Confirmed` or `Announced` booking for the **same artist entity on the same venue-local date** | The canonical double-booking. Detection is artist-scoped, not room-scoped (D-04) — each room's calendar looks clean in isolation | **Hard block**, naming the conflicting date (not the counterparty's commercial terms) |
| C-06 | No radius-clause breach against an existing confirmed booking, **unless a waiver from the constraining promoter is on file** ([17.06](../17.06-radius-clause-exclusivity.md)) | The obligation is breached the moment the second date is confirmed, not when it announces (DT-10). Radius waivers are routinely negotiated — the block must have a legitimate door | **Blocked**, with the constraining contract and Operator named, and the **waiver-request instrument** ([17.06 D-04](../17.06-radius-clause-exclusivity.md)) offered inline — an in-app request on its own clock, escalating on 17.01.03's channel ladder against the constraining Operator. A lapsed or refused request grants nothing; the block stands. **Break-glass — the only override in C-01..C-06**, and all four conditions are conjunctive: (1) a [17.01.03](./17.01.03-challenge-release-expiry.md) challenge clock is live on this hold — never on an ordinary confirm; (2) the **artist principal** acts in person, never a delegate, and never Operator-side; (3) the constraining Operator is notified **at the moment of use**, not after the fact; (4) the confirm writes a named `radius-break-glass` breach event attributed to the confirming party into [17.12](../17.12-counterparty-relationship-payment-reliability.md) |

**Confirmation warnings** — surfaced, never blocking:

| # | Warning | Why it is not a block |
|---|---|---|
| W-01 | A **member** of the confirming artist entity is a member of another entity confirmed on the same date (DT-07) | Deps and subs are legitimate and routine. The band is not double-booked; the human is, and that is theirs to solve |
| W-02 | Travel infeasible against an adjacent confirmed date | The routing/buffer rule is owned by domain 18 and cannot be authored from inside 17 (CX-04) |
| W-03 | Holds naming this artist on this date exist at **other rooms** and are now dead (DT-08) | The artist may legitimately be playing two slots that day (in-store + evening). Prompt to void, default to void, never silent |

### Announce readiness

Announce is a separate gate with its own preconditions, which is why it is not folded into
confirmation. A show is routinely confirmed months before it is announceable. Announcing early —
before the bill is honest or the promoter has committed money — is a routine, expensive industry
mistake.

**The precondition set is two-tier** (resolves Q-01). A single fixed set is wrong in both
directions: too loose and the platform launders unsigned shows to the public; too rigid and the
Operator announces on Instagram instead, the gate never fires, and the platform loses the trigger
it exists to own (DT-12).

**Tier 1 — hard floor. Platform-fixed. Never waivable, by anyone, for any reason:**

| # | Precondition | Why it cannot be waived |
|---|---|---|
| P-01 | Booking state is `Confirmed` (not `Reverted`, not `Cancelled`) | Announcing an unconfirmed date announces a show that does not exist |
| P-02 | Both principals' announce consent recorded — **or** an announce term in the executed deal that pre-authorises one side to fire it | Neither side may make the other's show public unilaterally. This is D-02 applied to announce |
| P-03 | An announce datetime exists in [19.02.01](../../19-ticketing-box-office/19.02-on-sale-announce-presale/19.02.01-announce-on-sale-scheduling.md), **and** either an on-sale datetime exists or the show is explicitly marked free/RSVP admission (19.11) | "On sale Friday 10am" is *part of the announcement's content*. An announce with neither an on-sale time nor an explicit no-ticket marker is not an announce — it is a rumour |
| P-04 | **Lineup honesty**: every act *named* in the announce is confirmed, **or** the slot is explicitly marked TBA | The fan bought the bill they were sold. Dropping a named support after sale is a refund-rights problem, not an embarrassment (DT-05) |
| P-05 | Every member of the booking's **announce group** is itself announce-ready, or this booking has been ejected from the group (DT-04) | A tour announces as one moment. Half a tour announcing is not a tour announce |

**Tier 2 — waivable. Default ON. Operator sets the house default per room; a per-show waiver
requires the same two-key consent as announce itself, plus a recorded reason:**

| # | Precondition | Why waivable |
|---|---|---|
| P-06 | Contract executed ([17.04](../17.04-performance-contracts-deal-memos.md)) | Club and DIY shows announce on an agreed offer with paper following. Blocking this hard would push the announce off-platform for the majority of small shows — the exact population the product needs |
| P-07 | Deposit received and cleared ([17.05.01](../17.05-deposits-balances-cancellation/17.05.01-deposit-invoice-collection.md)) | Same reasoning, with a materially worse downside — see Q-02. Deposit terms vary; venue-hire deals invert who pays |

A waiver is **permanent, attributed, and visible on the booking forever**: who waived, when, which
precondition, and the stated reason. It is not a dismissible dialog. If a show later fails in a way
the waived precondition would have caught, the record of who waived it is already written — which is
this domain's thesis (capture the fact when it is true, do not reconstruct the argument afterwards)
applied to the gate itself.

**A precondition whose status cannot be read renders as `Unknown`, never as satisfied** (DT-14).
The gate fails closed. A green checklist that is green because a downstream domain timed out is
worse than no checklist.

### Announce groups (the tour announce)

An **announce group** is an artist-owned set of confirmed bookings that announce atomically. It
exists because the artist's announce decision is per-*tour* while this gate is per-*booking*, and
that grain mismatch is the whole substance of the artist/Operator timing conflict (DT-04).

- Membership is set by the **Musician**. The tour is theirs.
- A booking in a group is announce-blocked until every member is announce-ready (P-05).
- Only the Musician may **eject** a booking from its group. Ejection is the escape hatch that stops
  one slow room holding twenty-nine other rooms hostage — without it the group is a trap.
- An Operator sees *that* their show is in a group and *how many* members block it. Never which.
- An Operator may **request** ejection. The artist decides. The platform does not adjudicate.

### Announce execution

When both keys turn and every Tier-1 precondition holds, the gate opens and fires the trigger.

- **Exactly-once semantics are a product requirement, not an implementation detail** (DT-16). A
  duplicate announce is a duplicate gig alert to every follower of the artist. For the Fan persona
  the alert *is* the product; sending it twice is a trust incident at consumer scale (D-13).
- The trigger is **at-least-once delivered** to domains 19, 20 and 21, each of which dedupes on the
  announce record. Delivery: 3 retries with exponential backoff, then dead-letter with an alert to
  the Operator.
- **A failed downstream delivery never un-announces the show.** Announce commits locally the moment
  the keys turn; if 21's syndication to Bandsintown fails, the show is still announced and the
  syndication retries. The alternative — rolling back an announce because a third-party API was
  down — would make the one-way door two-way, which it is not.
- The durable-timer and delivery substrate is **architecture, not product** — routed to
  `/create-prd-architecture`, consistent with the sub-domain's D-03.

### Happy Path

1. A hold survives to a confirmable position — won challenge, or all superior holds collapsed
   ([17.01.03](./17.01.03-challenge-release-expiry.md), CX-03).
2. Agent proposes confirmation against a specific accepted offer version (C-01).
3. The artist entity's approval chain authorises **that version hash** (C-02,
   [17.02.03](../17.02-offers-negotiation/17.02.03-offer-approval-chain.md)); the Operator's internal
   sign-off clears the fee (C-03).
4. System evaluates C-04..C-06 and surfaces W-01..W-03. Operator sees the exact list of inferior
   holds this confirm will destroy, by room-date and position, **before** committing.
5. Operator executes. System **atomically** destroys inferior holds on the room-date, blocks both
   calendars with the routing buffer, and creates the booking record.
6. Every destroyed hold-holder is notified with the reason and a timestamp trail. Every other room
   holding a dead hold naming this artist on this date is prompted (W-03).
7. The booking sits **`Confirmed (unannounced)`** — 19's embargo state (19.02.01 D-02). Announce
   preconditions accumulate against a live checklist.
8. Contract executes (17.04); deposit clears (17.05.01); the bill is finalised or explicitly marked
   TBA (17.14 / P-04); 19.02.01 sets announce and on-sale datetimes in venue-local time (P-03).
9. The checklist goes green. Both principals turn their keys (P-02). If the booking is in an
   announce group, it waits for its slowest sibling (P-05).
10. Announce fires exactly once. Domains 19, 20 and 21 receive the trigger; the show becomes public;
    the Fan's follow becomes a gig alert.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Two Operators confirm the same artist-date from different rooms | The canonical double-booking. C-05 is artist-scoped, not room-scoped — each room's calendar looks clean in isolation. Evaluated at commit under a serialised check on the artist-date, so the race has one loser, not two winners | Second confirm hard-blocked, naming the conflicting **date and room**; never the other deal's terms |
| Both confirms commit in the same instant | Ordering is server-assigned and monotonic, ties broken by receipt timestamp, never by client clock (CX-01 §5). One wins; the loser is treated as a blocked confirm and its holds are **not** destroyed | Loser sees the block, position intact, nothing lost |
| Confirmation partially fails (holds destroyed, booking record not created) | The worst state — a date that is neither held nor bookable. **Unreachable by construction**; atomicity is a hard requirement, not a nice-to-have | Never seen. If the write cannot complete, nothing is destroyed and the confirm is refused with a retryable error |
| Deposit fails after confirm; booking reverts | Booking reverts to void. **Destroyed holds do not return** (CX-03) — the other parties have already re-routed. Compensating action only, never a rollback | Every party whose hold that confirmation destroyed gets a **first-refusal notification**, ranked by their destroyed position, with a 48h priority window (the corpus's standard challenge window) to re-place a hold at their prior position before the date returns to open avails. Wording is explicit that this is a **new offer, not a restoration** |
| Deposit clears, then reverses (chargeback / failed transfer) **after** announce | An announced show with no deposit. Cannot un-announce (DT-09). The money problem becomes a cancellation decision, not a gate decision | Booking flagged `Announced — deposit reversed`; routes to 17.05.03. The gate records the transition and gets out of the way |
| Announce fires before supports are locked | **Not a failure if the announce said TBA** (P-04, DT-05). It is a failure only when a *named* act is dropped after sale — which P-04 makes structurally impossible, because you cannot name an unconfirmed act | Announce with an unconfirmed named act is blocked, naming the act and offering "mark as TBA" |
| Artist wants to hold announce; Operator wants to announce now | A genuine commercial conflict with two legitimate claims. **The platform does not break the tie** (DT-15). Announce timing is a *deal term* (17.02/17.04); when the term is absent, two-key consent means deadlock is possible and honest | Both sides see the deadlock, the missing term named explicitly, and a route to agree it as a term. Escalation is contractual (17.05.03), not a platform override |
| Announce runway is too short to sell the show | Non-blocking warning derived from draw history ([17.11](../17.11-draw-history-market-intelligence/)) rather than a fixed constant. Interim default: warn when announce-to-show-date < 28 days — **a starting value to validate against real draw data, not a researched constant** | "Announced 12 days out. Comparable shows at this runway sold {n}% of capacity." Advisory only |
| A tour announce group contains one date that will never be ready | Without ejection this is a hostage situation: one slow promoter blocks the whole tour (DT-04) | Musician sees which member blocks and may eject it. Operator sees "2 of 12 dates not announce-ready" with no identities, and a "request ejection" action |
| Self-promoted show — the Musician **is** the Operator (multi-hyphenate) | Both principals resolve to one authorising identity. Two-key (P-02) collapses to one action and artist-approval (C-02) is self-approval. Both are **satisfied**, and both are recorded as `single-party (self-promoted)` — **not** as two independent consents (DT-13) | One confirm action, one announce action. The audit trail says one party agreed, because one party did. Faking two signatures would poison the provenance record this domain exists to produce |
| A band member is also the counterparty Operator | Conflict of interest in the artist-side approval chain. The gate **names** the conflict; it does not resolve it — the band's own governance rule (17.02.03) decides whether that member may vote on a deal they are selling | "{Member} is the counterparty on this offer. Your band's approval rule does/does not exclude them." Routes to 17.02.03 |
| Drummer plays in three bands; two bands confirm the same date | Not a double-booking of any *entity* — C-05 passes cleanly. The conflict is at the **person** level, which entity-scoped detection cannot see (DT-07). Deps are legitimate, so this warns, never blocks | W-01 to both entities **and the member**: "You are booked with {Band A} and {Band B} on {date}." Whether a dep is sent is theirs |
| Artist confirmed at room X; rooms Y and Z still hold them for that date | Y and Z are sitting on dead inventory — an unsold Tuesday is gone forever (`meta/personas.md`, Operator). Confirmation voids room-scoped inferior holds but **not** cross-room holds naming the artist; those are prompted, not auto-killed (W-03, DT-08) | Artist is prompted to void, defaulting to void, with a retain-for-reason path (matinee + evening is real). Rooms Y and Z are notified when voided |
| The other room's hold is for a different slot the same day (matinee vs evening) | Not a conflict at all. Depends on whether a hold attaches to a room-date or a show-slot — **unresolved** (sub-domain Q-01) and it bites hardest here | Retain path is offered with the slot named. Until Q-01 resolves, the artist decides rather than the system |
| Announce fires while the venue is in a DST transition | Datetimes are anchored to venue-local time (19.02.01 D-01); the gate authorises, it does not schedule. This gate is unaffected — DST-gap on-sales are an open edge in 19.02.01, tracked there, and correctly so | Nothing here. The gate has no clock of its own — a deliberate consequence of D-03 |
| Embargo leaks before announce | Not a system failure, but real pressure to announce early. The gate needs no special path — **the Tier-2 waiver is the emergency-announce mechanism** (answers 19.02.01 Q-03 from 17's side) | Both principals may waive contract/deposit with a recorded reason and fire. Tier-1 still holds — a leak does not license announcing a show with an unconfirmed named support |
| Announce trigger delivered; 21's syndication to Bandsintown fails | The show is announced. Syndication retries: 3 attempts, exponential backoff, dead-letter + Operator alert. **Announce is not rolled back** — a third-party outage cannot reopen a one-way door | Operator sees "announced; syndication to {target} failed, retrying". The show is public regardless |
| Duplicate announce attempt (double-click, retry, replayed trigger) | Idempotent. The second attempt returns the existing announce record and fires **no** second fan-out (DT-16) | "Already announced at {timestamp}." No fan sees a second alert |
| Announce precondition status unreadable (19 unreachable) | Renders `Unknown`, blocks announce. Fails closed (DT-14) | Checklist shows `Unknown — cannot reach ticketing` with retry. It never shows green |
| Confirmed show cancelled after announce | Escalates into cancellation terms (17.05.03) and fan refunds (19.06). This gate only records the transition | Handled by 17.05.03 |
| Attempt to revert an **announced** booking | Refused. Revert is reachable only from `Confirmed (unannounced)` (DT-09). Announced shows exit via cancellation or postponement | "This show is public. It cannot be reverted — it can be cancelled (17.05.03) or postponed." |
| Party confirms defensively under a 48h challenge, then the deal collapses | The hazard 17.01.03 DT-02 confirms is real. C-01 requires an accepted offer, so the defensive confirm at least has terms — but agreeing bad terms under a clock is still a commercial choice the product cannot prevent | The revert path runs (first-refusal to destroyed holders), and the confirm-then-revert is recorded as a derived reliability fact against the confirming party ([17.12](../17.12-counterparty-relationship-payment-reliability.md), DT-11) |
| Malicious confirm to destroy a rival's holds, then immediate revert | Grief vector. C-01/C-02 raise the cost (a real offer and a real entity approval are required), and the revert is permanently attributed via 17.12 | Repeat offenders carry a visible confirm-then-revert record. The platform does not block the first one — it makes the second one expensive |
| Offer version changes between the approval and the confirm click | C-02 binds to the version **hash**, so the approval no longer covers the offer. Blocked at commit, not at render | "Your band approved v2; this is v4. Re-approval required." Routes to 17.02.03 |
| Avail withdrawn between confirmable and confirm | The avail's publisher pulled the date. Holds must be notified and voided — compensating action, not rollback (CX-01 §2) | Confirm refused, naming the withdrawal. The ladder is void, not reordered |

### States

**Lifecycle state machine** — the booking's own states:

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Confirmable | Hold reached position 1 with no live challenge | "Confirm this date" available, with approval-chain status and the exact list of holds this confirm will destroy |
| Pending approval | Confirmation proposed, entity approval outstanding | Blocked, naming the approver, the offer version awaiting them, and what they are waiting on |
| Confirmed (unannounced) | Atomic confirm succeeded | Date locked on both calendars with routing buffer; live announce checklist showing every outstanding precondition and its tier. This is 19's `Embargoed` state seen from 17 |
| Announce-blocked (group) | All own preconditions met; a group sibling is not ready | Musician: which member blocks. Operator: how many block, not which. "Request ejection" available |
| Announce-ready | All Tier-1 met; Tier-2 met or waived | Announce action available to both principals; each key shown as turned or unturned, with who turned it and when |
| Announced | Both keys turned; trigger fired exactly once | Show handed to 19/20/21; the date is public. **Terminal for this gate** — no path back |
| Reverted | Downstream failure (e.g. deposit) **while unannounced** | Booking void; destroyed holds explicitly **not** restored; first-refusal notifications sent; the revert is attributed (17.12) |

**Surface states** — what the gate's UI shows:

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Checklist opened; precondition statuses being read across 17.02/17.04/17.05/17.14/19 | Skeleton checklist with each precondition row in a pending shimmer. Rows resolve independently — a slow row never blocks a fast one |
| Empty | Date has no holds and no offer | Not this feature's surface. Routes to 17.01.01 avails — there is nothing to confirm |
| Populated | Booking confirmed; all precondition statuses read | Full checklist: each precondition with tier (hard floor / waivable), status, the domain that owns it, and a route to the blocking artefact |
| Error | A precondition source is unreachable, or the confirm write is refused | Row shows `Unknown — cannot reach {domain}` with retry. Announce stays blocked. **Never renders green on a read failure** (DT-14) |
| Partial | Some precondition statuses read, others unresolved | Mixed checklist with unresolved rows explicitly `Unknown`. The announce action is disabled with "N of M preconditions could not be verified" — not with a false "not ready" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Confirmation and announce are one event | The sweep's implicit model — it listed neither, but "Event Listing Syndication" (candidate 18) implied announce follows confirm automatically | ❌ REJECTED: they are separated by weeks or months and by a real precondition set. Conflating them would auto-publish shows that have no signed contract and no support act — a mistake the industry makes manually and would not pay software to make faster. **This rejection is why the feature exists.** Independently corroborated by 19.02.01 DT-02, which rejected the same collapse from the ticketing side and identified the embargo state it destroys. |
| DT-02 | Confirming is reversible because a booking can be cancelled | Symmetry instinct | ❌ REJECTED: cancellation reverses the *booking*, not the *ladder*. The destroyed 2nd and 3rd holds are gone and have already re-routed. Confirmation is the point of no return for everyone who lost, and the product must be honest that "revert" is a compensating action with permanent collateral damage (CX-03). |
| DT-03 | The artist's approval of their own confirmation is a formality | Agent-authority instinct | ❌ REJECTED: a domain expert would identify agent-confirms-without-authority as one of the most common relationship-ending failures in booking. The artist is the body that has to be in the room. Modelling approval as optional would reproduce the exact failure — and it maps directly to the platform's thesis: capture the authority at the moment it is given, not reconstruct it in the argument afterwards. |
| DT-04 | The announce decision is per-booking | This gate's own grain — it is a feature of one booking | ❌ REJECTED, and this is the feature's largest structural gap. **The artist's announce decision is per-tour.** A 30-date tour announces at one moment; dribbling dates out as each room confirms destroys the press moment and is exactly what the Role Lens says the artist will not accept. A per-booking gate cannot express it. Resolved with the **announce group** (artist-owned, atomic, ejectable). The failure nobody designs for is the corollary: without ejection, one promoter who will never sign holds twenty-nine rooms hostage indefinitely — so the escape hatch is not a nicety, it is what makes the group safe to enter. |
| DT-05 | "Supports locked" is an announce precondition | The feature's own `[SURFACE]` draft asserted it | ❌ REJECTED as stated, and **refined into something better**. A domain expert would immediately object: "+ special guests TBA" is a completely standard announce and has been for decades. Blocking it would block the industry's normal behaviour. The real precondition is **lineup honesty** (P-04): every act you *name* must be confirmed; anything else must say TBA. A show announced as "Headliner + TBA" has promised nothing about supports, so changing them is not a refund-rights problem. A show that names a support and drops it has sold a bill it did not deliver. This is strictly better than "supports locked" — it permits the common case and blocks only the harmful one. |
| DT-06 | Confirmation requires an executed contract | Rigour instinct; the `[SURFACE]` draft's Q-03 asked it directly | ❌ REJECTED: **confirmed ≠ contracted**, and the industry has always known this. "We're confirmed for the 14th, deal memo to follow" is how the majority of shows are booked. Requiring paper before confirm would be technically correct and universally routed around — the parties would confirm by phone and the platform would never see it, which forfeits the capture the whole thesis depends on. Resolved: confirmation requires an **accepted offer version** (C-01), which is cheap and fast; contract execution is a *waivable announce* precondition (P-06). This also answers 17.01.03's DT-02 hazard as far as it can be answered: a defensive confirm under a 48h clock at least has terms attached. Resolves Q-03. |
| DT-07 | Artist-scoped double-book detection at the entity level is sufficient (D-04) | D-04 as originally written | ❌ REJECTED as sufficient — correct but incomplete. `meta/personas.md`'s **structural fact** is the multi-hyphenate: a drummer in three bands.
