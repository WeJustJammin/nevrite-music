# Feature: Confirmation & Announce Readiness Gate

> **Number**: 17.01.04
> **Parent**: [Availability, Holds & Confirmation](./17.01-availability-holds-confirmation-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The terminal transition of a date: hold → **confirmed** → **announced**. Confirmation is the hard,
near-irreversible write that destroys every inferior hold, blocks the calendar on both sides, and
creates the booking record every downstream domain reads. Announce is a separate, later gate — a
show can be confirmed for months before it is announceable, and conflating the two is a live-music
modelling error with real commercial consequences.

**Deep Think addition** — this feature was not in the sweep's 18 candidates. The sweep modelled the
hold ladder but never modelled what the ladder resolves *into*, which would have left domains 19
(Ticketing) and 21 (Promotion) with no trigger.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **Must approve confirmation of their own date.** An agent confirming without artist authority is a real and common failure, and the artist is the one who has to physically be there. Sees the confirmed date land on their merged calendar with its routing buffer. Has a say in announce timing — an artist announcing a tour wants all dates on sale together, not dribbled out as each room confirms. |
| Producer | None | No role. The Producer's live-adjacent equivalent (FOH engineer, backline tech) is crewing, which is domain 18. |
| Operator | Full | Executes the confirmation, notifies the destroyed holds, and controls announce timing against their own on-sale calendar — a room does not want two of its shows announcing the same morning and splitting attention. |
| Fan | None (until announce) | Nothing until announce fires, and then nothing *here* — the fan-facing artefact is created by domains 19 and 21. This gate is the trigger, not the surface. The Fan's only relationship to this feature is that its output is the first moment a show exists for them. |

## Behavior

**Confirmation** is three writes that must be atomic: destroy inferior holds, hard-write both
calendars, create the booking record. A partial confirm leaves a date that is neither held nor
bookable — the worst possible state.

**Announce readiness** is a separate gate with its own preconditions, which is why it is not folded
into confirmation. A show typically cannot announce until: the contract is executed (17.04), the
deposit is in (17.05.01), the bill is settled including supports (17.14), and an on-sale date is
agreed with Ticketing (domain 19). Announcing early — before supports are locked or a contract is
signed — is a routine, expensive industry mistake.

### Happy Path

1. A hold survives to a confirmable position (won challenge, or all superior holds collapsed).
2. Agent proposes confirmation; the artist entity's approval chain (17.02.03) authorises it.
3. Operator executes the confirmation. System atomically destroys inferior holds, blocks both
   calendars, and creates the booking record.
4. Every destroyed hold-holder is notified with the reason.
5. The booking sits **confirmed but unannounced**. Announce preconditions accumulate — contract
   signed, deposit received, supports locked, on-sale date agreed.
6. When preconditions are met, the announce gate opens; both principals must agree the announce
   moment; domains 19 and 21 receive the trigger.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Two Operators confirm the same artist-date from different rooms | The canonical double-booking. Detection must be **artist-scoped, not room-scoped** — each room's calendar looks clean in isolation | Hard block on the second confirm, naming the conflict |
| Confirmation partially fails (holds destroyed, booking record not created) | The worst state — a date that is neither held nor bookable | Must not be reachable; atomicity is a hard requirement, not a nice-to-have |
| Deposit later fails; booking reverts | Booking reverts, **destroyed holds do not return** (CX-03). Compensating action only | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| Announce fires before supports are locked | Bill changes after the public has bought tickets — a refund-rights and trust problem, not just an embarrassment | Announce gate should block; precondition set is `[PENDING — Step 5]` |
| Artist wants to hold announce; Operator wants to announce now | Genuine commercial conflict — the room needs the on-sale, the artist wants a coordinated tour announce | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — who wins is a product decision (Q-02) |
| Confirmed show cancelled after announce | Escalates into cancellation terms (17.05.03) and fan refunds (domain 19) | Handled by 17.05.03; this gate only records the transition |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Confirmable | Hold reached position 1 with no live challenge | "Confirm this date" available, with approval chain status |
| Pending approval | Confirmation proposed, entity approval outstanding | Blocked with the named approver and what they're waiting on |
| Confirmed (unannounced) | Atomic confirm succeeded | Date locked on both calendars; announce checklist showing outstanding preconditions |
| Announce-ready | All preconditions met | Announce action available to both principals |
| Announced | Both principals agreed the moment | Show handed to 19/21; the date is now public |
| Reverted | Downstream failure (e.g. deposit) | Booking void; destroyed holds explicitly **not** restored |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Confirmation and announce are one event | The sweep's implicit model — it listed neither, but "Event Listing Syndication" (candidate 18) implied announce follows confirm automatically | ❌ REJECTED: they are separated by weeks or months and by a real precondition set (contract, deposit, supports, on-sale date). Conflating them would auto-publish shows that have no signed contract and no support act — a mistake the industry makes manually and would not pay software to make faster. **This rejection is why the feature exists.** |
| DT-02 | Confirming is reversible because a booking can be cancelled | Symmetry instinct | ❌ REJECTED: cancellation reverses the *booking*, not the *ladder*. The destroyed 2nd and 3rd holds are gone and have already re-routed. Confirmation is the point of no return for everyone who lost, and the product must be honest that "revert" is a compensating action with permanent collateral damage (CX-03). |
| DT-03 | The artist's approval of their own confirmation is a formality | Agent-authority instinct | ❌ REJECTED: a domain expert would identify agent-confirms-without-authority as one of the most common relationship-ending failures in booking. The artist is the body that has to be in the room. Modelling approval as optional would reproduce the exact failure — and it maps directly to the platform's thesis: capture the authority at the moment it is given, not reconstruct it in the argument afterwards. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Announce is a separate gate from confirmation | They are separated by weeks and by a real precondition set (DT-01). Conflating them auto-publishes unsigned, unsupported shows. |
| D-02 | Artist approval is required for confirmation, not optional | Agent-confirms-without-authority is a top relationship-ending failure (DT-03), and capturing authority at the moment it is given is the platform thesis applied to booking. |
| D-03 | This gate owns the announce **trigger**, not the announce **artefact** | Syndication belongs to 21 and the on-sale to 19. Domain 17 decides *when a show is allowed to exist publicly*; it does not publish it. |
| D-04 | Double-booking detection is artist-scoped, not room-scoped | Each room's calendar looks clean in isolation; only the artist's merged view sees the conflict. |
