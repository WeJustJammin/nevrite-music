## Overview

The pre-deal state machine of a date: publishing what an artist or room is available for, placing
prioritised holds on it, resolving contention through the challenge mechanic, and converting a
surviving hold into a confirmed, announceable booking. This is the most industry-specific mechanic
in the entire corpus — the 1st/2nd/3rd hold ladder with a 24–48h challenge window is invisible to
outsiders and is the single strongest credibility signal available to this product.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities that interact continuously — an
avail is the resource a hold attaches to; a challenge forces the superior hold to confirm or drop;
an expiry silently returns the slot to avails; a confirmation destroys every inferior hold on the
date and unlocks announce. Change any one and the other three change behaviour.
