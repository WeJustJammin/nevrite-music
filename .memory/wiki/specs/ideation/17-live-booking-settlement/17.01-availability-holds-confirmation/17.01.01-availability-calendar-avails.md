# Feature: Availability Calendar & Avails Publishing

> **Number**: 17.01.01
> **Parent**: [Availability, Holds & Confirmation](./17.01-availability-holds-confirmation-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The publishable record of what is bookable and when — an Operator's open room-dates, an artist's
open touring windows. This is the resource every hold attaches to and the thing the Operator's
business actually is: perishable inventory where an unsold Tuesday is gone forever. It replaces the
"email + Google Calendar + a spreadsheet + phone calls" workaround documented in `meta/personas.md`.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Publishes **avails windows** rather than single dates — "we're routing the North of England, 12–19 March, want two mid-week dates". Sets fee expectations and radius sensitivity per window. Sees which of their dates are open, held, or confirmed, across every band and project they belong to simultaneously (the multi-hyphenate case: one human's calendar merges three bands' dates). |
| Producer | None | No stage-date inventory. A Producer's bookable resource is a studio room, which is domain 16's calendar, not this one. If a Producer books a venue they are acting as an **Operator** here. |
| Operator | Full | Publishes room-dates with the constraints that actually decide a booking: capacity, curfew, load-in window, whether the date is a hard blackout or a soft "make me an offer". Sets recurring blackouts (private hires, closed Mondays) and can mark a date as invite-only rather than publicly holdable. |
| Fan | None | Avails are never public. A held or available date is not a show and must never leak — publishing an unconfirmed date is a real commercial harm to both principals and creates fan expectations that routinely collapse. |

## Behavior

An avail is a **window with constraints**, not a boolean on a date. The Operator side publishes
room-dates; the Musician side publishes routing windows. The two are the same primitive viewed from
opposite ends of the deal, which is why they share a feature.

An avail's defining property: **publishing it does not commit anything**. It is an invitation to
hold (17.01.02), and it survives having holds placed on it — a date with three holds is still
displayed as holdable.

### Happy Path

1. Operator opens their room calendar and selects a date range with no existing confirmed bookings.
2. Operator marks the range available and sets per-date constraints — capacity, curfew, load-in,
   minimum guarantee they will entertain.
3. System publishes the avails to the discovery surface, scoped to the Operator's chosen visibility
   (open, or invite-only to specific agents).
4. An agent searching a routing window finds the date; the avail appears with its constraints
   attached so the agent can filter out rooms that cannot run their show.
5. Agent places a hold (17.01.02). The avail remains published and continues to attract holds.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Operator publishes a date that already has a confirmed booking | System refuses; confirmed dates are hard-blocked (CX-04) | "This date is confirmed for {show}. Cancel the booking to reopen it." |
| Operator withdraws an avail that has live holds | Withdrawal is permitted but every hold is voided and notified — a compensating action, not a silent delete | Confirmation dialog naming each affected holder before withdrawal proceeds |
| Musician's two bands publish overlapping avails for the same human | Detected as a personal-calendar conflict, not a band conflict — the bands are separate entities but the drummer is one body | Conflict warning on the individual's merged calendar; bands are not told about each other |
| Stale gear/capacity data on a published avail | The Operator anti-persona failure from `meta/personas.md` — band arrives to a room that cannot run their show | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — likely a staleness prompt tied to domain 16's spec record |
| Avail published for a room whose spec sheet (domain 16) is incomplete | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — unclear whether this should hard-block publishing | — |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty | No avails published | Empty calendar with "Open dates to start receiving holds" CTA |
| Populated | Avails published, no holds | Calendar with open dates marked and their constraints visible |
| Contended | 1+ holds on a published avail | Date shows hold count and ladder depth; still marked holdable |
| Blocked | Date confirmed | Date shows the confirmed show; not holdable |
| Stale | `[PENDING — Step 5]` | Constraint data older than a threshold — behaviour undecided |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Avails are per-date booleans | Naive booking-system modelling | ❌ REJECTED: an avail without capacity, curfew, and load-in constraints is useless — those constraints are what let an agent filter, and their absence is exactly why the current email workflow requires a phone call per date. A boolean avail rebuilds Google Calendar, which the Operator already has and already hates. |
| DT-02 | Artist-side avails and venue-side avails are different features | Two personas, two surfaces | ❌ REJECTED: they are one primitive from two ends. Both are "a window I will entertain offers on, with constraints". Splitting them duplicates the hold ladder, and worse, prevents matching an artist window against a room window — which is the actual product. |
| DT-03 | A published avail must be publicly visible to be useful | Marketplace-liquidity instinct | ❌ REJECTED: a domain expert would expect **invite-only avails** to be the norm at the top of the market. Agents guard routing plans; a venue publicly advertising an open Saturday signals weakness. Visibility scoping is not a nice-to-have — an always-public model would make the product unusable for exactly the professionals whose participation gives it liquidity. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Artist avails and venue avails are one feature, not two | They are the same primitive from opposite ends of a two-sided deal; splitting them would duplicate the hold ladder and break artist-window ↔ room-window matching. |
| D-02 | Avails carry constraints as first-class data, not free text | Constraints are what make an avail filterable, and filterability is the entire improvement over the email workflow. Free-text notes rebuild the phone call. |
