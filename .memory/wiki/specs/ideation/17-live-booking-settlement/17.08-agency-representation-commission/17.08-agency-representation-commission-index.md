## Overview

The economics of being represented — the commission terms attached to a representation relationship,
the accrual and deduction of that commission when a show settles, and the roster pipeline an agency
runs its business from. The agent is the party who makes most of the bookings in this domain and is
paid from the settlements it produces, so their money has to be modelled where the money is computed.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities that interact through the settlement
event — the terms determine the accrual, the accrual determines the deduction, and the pipeline is
the forward view of accruals not yet earned. The terms alone are a contract; the accrual alone is a
line item; together with the pipeline they are how an agency knows whether it is solvent.

> **Legend**: ✅ Full · ⚙️ Config · 👁️ Read-only · 📊 Reports · ❌ None
>
> **Persona mapping — the honest problem.** D-19 fixes four personas and the **booking agent is not
> cleanly any of them.** They are not a Musician (they make no music), not an Operator (they sell no
> time or space), not a Producer, not a Fan. This sub-domain maps them to **Musician** because the
> deal has exactly two sides and the agent is unambiguously on the artist's: they are paid from the
> artist's money, they are bound by the artist's authority rule, and the Operator has no visibility
> into them. `meta/personas.md` supports this reading (17 → "Operator (+ Musician as counterparty)")
> but the fit is imperfect and the whole Operator column being `None` is the symptom. **Escalated as
> domain index Q-01** — this is the sub-domain where the four-persona model is most strained.
>
> **Operator is `None` throughout, and that is correct, not an oversight.** What an act pays its agent
> is deliberately invisible to the promoter; a promoter who could see it would negotiate against it.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |

| D-01 | Classified as a sub-domain | Three capabilities interacting through the settlement event: terms → accrual → deduction, with the pipeline as the forward view of unearned accruals. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | The **representation relationship** lives in domain 01; its **economics** live here | An agency is an organization and representation is a relationship between an org and an artist — that is 01's model, and the same relationship governs non-live work. What is domain-owned here is the commission computed from a live settlement. Duplicating the relationship would give the platform two contradictory records of who represents whom. | Node Classification Gate — boundary test, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | Commission accrual is kept here rather than in 17.10 (Live Income Payout & Tax) | Considered and rejected: commission is not a payout split, it is a **deduction from one party's share** governed by a separate agreement with a separate counterparty. Folding it into the split would make the agent look like a band member. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
