## Cross-Cut Details

### CX-01: Representation & Commission Terms ↔ Commission Accrual & Deduction

**Relationship**: The commission terms compile to a formula the accrual evaluates against the
settlement — structurally the same pattern as the deal grammar → settlement formula edge that defines
the whole domain (17.03.01 → 17.09.01). The term that actually decides the money is not the
percentage but the **basis**: 20% of gross and 20% of net differ by thousands on the same show, and
the industry writes both.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician**: sees commission deducted from their own money and must be able to check the basis
  against what they agreed. This is the artist-side mirror of the settlement asymmetry the domain
  exists to fix — the agent knows the number and the artist historically did not.
- **Operator**: not affected and must not be — what the act pays its agent is not the promoter's
  business (index Role Matrix note).
- **Producer** / **Fan**: not affected.

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: The representation agreement is the single source of the terms; the
   accrual reads it. If the terms change mid-relationship, already-booked dates are governed by the
   terms in force when they were booked — not the current ones. **This is the sunset problem and it
   is 17.08.01's hardest edge.**
2. **Trigger chain**: Settlement finalises → accrual evaluates → deduction executes. If the
   settlement is later amended (17.09.06), the accrual must recompute — and if commission was already
   disbursed, that is a clawback from a third party.
3. **Permission intersection**: The agent's authority to negotiate (17.02.03) is separate from their
   entitlement to commission. An agent can be owed commission on a deal they had no authority to
   bind — which is exactly the argument that ends agency relationships.
4. **Notification fan-out**: Deduction notifies the artist and the agency. A recomputation must
   notify both again.
5. **State transition conflict**: A settlement amendment landing after commission disbursement.
   Real, and it makes the platform the party holding a clawback it cannot execute.

### CX-02: Commission Accrual & Deduction ↔ Roster Booking Pipeline

**Relationship**: The pipeline is the agency's forward view — the sum of commission on dates confirmed
but not yet played. Settlement is what converts a projection into a fact. An agency's solvency is a
function of this projection, which is why the pipeline is a real business tool and not a CRM board.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician**: the agency sees its own roster's pipeline; an individual artist sees their own dates,
  not the agency's book.
- **Operator** / **Producer** / **Fan**: not affected.

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: None — the pipeline is a derived view, not a record. It owns nothing.
2. **Trigger chain**: Confirmation (17.01.04) → pipeline entry → settlement → accrual → pipeline exit.
   A cancelled show removes the projection, which is how an agency discovers its forecast was wrong.
3. **Permission intersection**: An agency sees only its own roster's dates. Visibility across
   agencies would be a competitive disaster.
4. **Notification fan-out**: A cancellation moves the agency's forecast, not just the artist's
   calendar — arguably the agency should be told before the artist's date is even released.
5. **State transition conflict**: A provisional number inside a cross-collateralized run (17.03.03)
   means the pipeline is projecting from numbers that are themselves provisional. Compounding
   uncertainty; `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`.

### CX-03: Representation & Commission Terms ↔ Roster Booking Pipeline

**Relationship**: Terms define scope, and scope defines what enters the pipeline at all.
Representation is routinely limited by territory ("North America only") or by work type ("live
only") — a date outside scope is not the agency's business and must never appear in its forecast. Under
`01.03.02` D-05 the work-type limit is the edge's **`domains`** axis and the territory limit is the edge's
territory bound; both are edge-level, so the commission rate the pipeline projects is the same for every
domain a single edge admits. Whether "live only" is literally `live booking` from 01's five-domain set is
17.08.01 Q-04.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician**: an artist with three agents across three territories has three pipelines, each seeing
  only its own slice. The multi-hyphenate problem appearing on the representation axis.
- **Operator** / **Producer** / **Fan**: not affected.

**Synthesis questions answered**: Medium confidence — deferred to Step 5. The open issue: whether
scope is structured data (territory polygons, work-type enums) or free text in an agreement the
platform cannot read. If the latter, the pipeline cannot filter and this edge does not exist. DQ-R2-01 (A′)
settles the *shape* of the structured case — two flat axes, `activities` and `domains`, ANDed at the moment
of the act and never a cell grid (`01.03.02` D-05, `01.03.03` DT-02) — leaving only the free-text question
(17.08.01 Q-02) and the vocabulary question (17.08.01 Q-04).
