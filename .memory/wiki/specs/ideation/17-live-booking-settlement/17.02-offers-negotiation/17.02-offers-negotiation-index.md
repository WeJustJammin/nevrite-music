## Overview

The vehicle that carries a deal from "we like this date" to "we have agreed terms" — offer sheet
composition, the counteroffer thread with version history, the entity approval chain that
authorises acceptance, and the expiry that stops an offer being an indefinite free option. This
sub-domain owns the *negotiation*; the deal grammar it negotiates over is owned by 17.03.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities in continuous interaction — a counter
creates a new version, versions are what the approval chain approves, approval is what expiry races
against, and an expired offer collapses the thread. The naive "send offer → accept" flow is a single
feature; the real one is a versioned, multi-party, authority-gated, time-boxed negotiation.
