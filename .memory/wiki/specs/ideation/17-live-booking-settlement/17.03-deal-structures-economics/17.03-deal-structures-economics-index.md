## Overview

The grammar of live money — the typed vocabulary of deal types (flat guarantee, door deal, versus,
plus-bonus, walkout potential), the modelling that lets either side see what a structure pays before
agreeing to it, and the multi-show deals that make per-show settlement mathematically wrong. This
sub-domain owns the **content** of a deal; 17.02 owns the negotiation envelope that carries it.

**Why this sub-domain is the domain's keystone**: the deal structure **is** the settlement formula.
"$2,000 versus 85% of net after $6,000 expenses, whichever is greater" is not a description of a
deal — it is an evaluable expression awaiting a ticket count. This is the insight that justifies
keeping booking and settlement in one domain, and everything downstream in 17.09 depends on the
grammar defined here being typed rather than prose.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities in mutual interaction — the grammar
defines what can be modelled; modelling is what makes a structure agreeable; multi-show deals are a
grammar extension that breaks the modelling's per-show assumption and rewrites the settlement
boundary.
