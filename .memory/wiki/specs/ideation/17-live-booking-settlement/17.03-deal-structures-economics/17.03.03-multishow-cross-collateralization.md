# Feature: Multi-Show Deals & Cross-Collateralization

> **Number**: 17.03.03
> **Parent**: [Deal Structures & Economics](./17.03-deal-structures-economics-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

A run of dates negotiated and settled as **one deal**, where profitable shows subsidise unprofitable
ones. Cross-collateralization is the classic mechanic that makes per-show settlement mathematically
wrong: settle show 3 of 12 in isolation and the number is a fiction, because a sold-out Wednesday is
contractually paying for a dead Monday that has not happened yet.

**Deep Think addition** — absent from all 18 sweep candidates. Its absence would not have surfaced
in ideation at all; it would have surfaced in `/write-be-spec` as a schema-breaking discovery that
the settlement entity's grain is wrong.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | A tour deal binds the artist across every date — their exposure is the run, not the night. Sees run-level economics and **provisional** per-date numbers that will move as later dates report. The honest hazard: they may bank a provisional number that a bad Monday later takes away. |
| Producer | None | No role. |
| Operator | Full | A promoter buying a run of dates negotiates it as one deal. **A single-room Operator inside someone else's run may not know their date is cross-collateralized at all** — and may be contractually entitled to aggregate information they have no commercial right to see (CX-02). |
| Fan | None | Never visible; invisible in the ticket-buying experience. |

## Behavior

A run is an **entity above the shows**, carrying terms whose scope is the run rather than the date —
"guarantee against 85% of the aggregate net across 12 dates". These terms have no per-show meaning,
so the grammar (17.03.01) must carry a scope dimension rather than assuming every term resolves
within one show (CX-02).

The consequences are severe and they propagate:

- **Per-show breakeven becomes meaningless** (CX-03). The modeller must switch to run basis or lie.
- **Per-show settlement finality becomes impossible.** A show cannot be finally settled while the run
  is open — which directly contradicts 17.09's per-show settlement model. This is the sharpest
  unresolved structural conflict in the domain.
- **Any date's actuals retroactively move every other date's number**, across dates that may be
  months apart.

### Happy Path

1. Agent and promoter negotiate a run of 12 dates as a single offer (17.02.01) with run-scoped terms.
2. Deal is approved once, at run level, by the artist entity (17.02.03) — not twelve times.
3. Each date proceeds through its own holds, contract and show independently.
4. As each date's actuals land (17.09.02), the run recomputes; every other date's provisional number
   moves and the artist is told it moved.
5. When the final date settles, the run settles. Only then do per-date numbers become final and
   disbursement (17.10.02) can complete.

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A tour is a collection of single-show deals | The model implied by all 18 sweep candidates, none of which mention runs | ❌ REJECTED: a cross-collateralized run has terms that have **no per-show meaning at all** ("85% of the aggregate across 12 dates"). Treating a tour as 12 independent settlements produces 12 wrong numbers. This rejection is the reason the feature exists — and its absence from the sweep means the domain's settlement model would have been built on a false grain. |
| DT-02 | Per-show settlement can stay final; the run reconciles afterwards | Attempt to preserve 17.09's clean per-show model | ❌ REJECTED: **it is arithmetically impossible.** If show 3's number depends on show 11's attendance, show 3 cannot be final in March. The choice is real and consequential: either per-show settlements are provisional for the whole run (cashflow pain, correctness), or the run reconciles via a true-up after final per-show payments (cashflow relief, clawback risk). Both are used in the industry. **This is a product decision the owner must make** — it decides the settlement entity's grain and cannot be deferred to implementation (domain Q). |
| DT-03 | Cross-collateralization is a big-tour concern, irrelevant to this platform's users | Scope instinct — `meta/personas.md` describes working musicians, not stadium acts | ⏳ DEFERRED, leaning against dismissal. A four-date regional run negotiated as one deal with one promoter is squarely within the target persona's world, and it is cross-collateralized whether or not anyone uses the word. The mechanic is not stadium-only; it is *agency-tier and above*, which the platform reaches the moment an agent joins. Dismissing it would be convenient and probably wrong — but it is legitimately a MoSCoW question, not a modelling one. |
