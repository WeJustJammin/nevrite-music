## Overview

The money that moves **before** the show — the deposit that makes a booking real, the balance
schedule that runs up to showtime, and the terms that decide who keeps what when a show dies. This
is where a confirmed date stops being a promise and becomes a commitment, and it is the mechanism
behind the Operator's documented pain of chasing deposits manually.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities that interact through shared state —
cancellation forfeit is computed **against what was paid and when**, so the deposit record and the
balance schedule are the cancellation's inputs. Force majeure is a fourth: it overrides the
cancellation tiers entirely rather than being a tier within them, which is a structural difference,
not a special case.

> **Legend**: ✅ Full · ⚙️ Config · 👁️ Read-only · 📊 Reports · ❌ None
>
> **Direction note**: in the standard live deal the **Operator pays the artist**, so the deposit flows
> Operator → Musician. This inverts for venue-hire deals (the artist rents the room). The Role Matrix
> above describes the standard direction; the inversion is real and is 17.05.01 Q-02.
>
> **Producer is `None` throughout.** Studio deposits exist but belong to domain 16's room hire.
>
> **Fan is `None` throughout.** Fan-side refunds when a show cancels are domain 19's product, not
> this sub-domain's — a critical boundary (17.05.03 D-03).
