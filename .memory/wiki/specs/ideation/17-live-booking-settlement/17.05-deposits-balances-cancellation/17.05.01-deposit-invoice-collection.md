# Feature: Deposit Invoice & Collection

> **Number**: 17.05.01
> **Parent**: [Deposits, Balances & Cancellation](./17.05-deposits-balances-cancellation-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

The money that makes a booking real. A confirmed date with no deposit is a promise; a confirmed date
with 50% down is a commitment, and the difference is what stops both sides treating the date as
optional. It replaces the manual chasing that `meta/personas.md` records as the Operator's current
reality — "deposits chased manually".

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **Receives** the deposit in the standard live deal. It is their protection against a promoter who vanishes — by the time a show dies, the artist has already turned down other dates for it. Sees the deposit land, and its arrival is what makes them comfortable declining the competing offer. |
| Producer | None | No role. Studio deposits exist but belong to domain 16's room hire. |
| Operator | Full | **Pays** the deposit in the standard deal, on the terms written into the contract (17.04). Inverts on venue-hire deals where the artist rents the room — then the Operator is the one chasing (Q-02). |
| Fan | None | Never visible. The fan's money is ticket revenue (domain 19) and has no relationship to the deposit. |

## Behavior

The deposit is **instalment zero of the balance schedule** (CX-04), not a separate mechanism. Its
terms come from the contract; its collection runs on the platform's payments cross-cut.

Its two jobs:

1. **Gate the announce** (17.01.04) — a show that announces before the deposit lands is a show the
   promoter has not committed to, sold to fans who cannot be refunded from money that was never paid.
2. **Give cancellation terms substance** (CX-01) — forfeit against money held is arithmetic; forfeit
   against money not held is litigation.

The escrow question (index Q-01) decides which product this actually is: a platform holding the
deposit can *enforce* forfeit; a platform recording that a bank transfer happened can only *assert*
it.

### Happy Path

1. Contract executes (17.04) carrying deposit terms — amount and due date.
2. System issues the deposit invoice to the paying side against the schedule.
3. Operator pays through the payments cross-cut.
4. Receipt is recorded; the announce precondition (17.01.04) is satisfied; the artist is notified the
   money landed.
5. The deposit sits as instalment zero, feeding forfeit computation if the show later dies (CX-01).

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Deposit never paid; due date passes | Invoice moves to `Overdue`; escalating reminders fire (17.05.02); the announce stays gated so no tickets can go on sale; the date is blocked and the show is flagged at-risk to both parties | Paying side: overdue notice with amount and days late. Receiving side: "deposit not received — show at risk" with the option to hold the date or release it. Whether non-payment *auto-voids* the booking after a grace window is Q-01 — `[PENDING — Q-01 → /create-prd]`, an **[OWNER]** call the completed `/ideate-discover` Step 5 did not take |
| Underpayment — paying side remits less than the deposit amount | The invoice is **not** satisfied; the schedule records a partial receipt against instalment zero and the outstanding remainder stays due; the announce gate does **not** open on a partial (a half-paid deposit is not a paid deposit) | Paying side: "partial payment received — X remaining before the show can be announced". Receiving side: sees amount landed and the shortfall. Applied-vs-refunded handling of a stranded partial on later cancellation is `[PENDING — /write-be-spec]` — the ladder reads paid-to-date off the schedule and keeps no record of its own ([17.05.03 D-02](./17.05.03-cancellation-tiers-forfeit.md)), so allocating a sub-deposit balance is a ledger-contract rule, not a new deal term |
| Double payment — deposit paid twice (retry after a slow confirmation, or both parties pay) | The second receipt is detected as a duplicate against the same schedule instalment; the overage is not silently kept — it is surfaced as a credit/refund routed to the payments cross-cut | Both sides: "deposit already paid — duplicate detected"; the overpaid party sees a pending refund/credit. Exact refund path owned by the payments cross-cut |
| Deposit paid directly, outside the platform | Extremely common; the platform holds an **assertion**, not the money. Marking-paid is an attestation, not a settled receipt | Either side can mark the deposit paid off-platform; the record shows `Asserted` (not `Received`), is visibly unenforceable, and is disputable by the other side. Whether one-sided assertion or dual confirmation is required to satisfy the announce gate is DT-03 |
| Wrong party attempts to mark it paid | Only a principal to the deal (the paying or receiving side, or their authorised agent) may record payment or assert off-platform payment; a third party cannot | Non-principal: action unavailable / "you are not a party to this booking" |
| Venue-hire deal — direction inverts | The artist pays the Operator; every role in this file flips (D-02). The invoice is issued to the artist, forfeit accrues to the Operator | Direction is read from the deal, not assumed; both sides see the correct payer/payee. Modelled per-deal from the start vs venue-hire as a later case is Q-02 |
| Cancellation lands while a deposit payment is in flight | Rare, real, money-relevant — the payment may settle after the cancellation is recorded | The in-flight payment is reconciled against the cancellation outcome (forfeit/return per 17.05.03/04) rather than lost; exact settle-vs-reverse ordering is `[PENDING — /write-be-spec]` (CX-01 §5) — the same class as [17.05.02 Q-02](./17.05.02-balance-schedule-reminders.md): a deterministic precedence rule between two state machines writing one ledger row |
| Deposit received but the receiving entity has no payout method | Money held with nowhere to go | Held at the payments cross-cut; receiving side prompted to complete payout onboarding before funds can disburse — onboarding gap, not a deposit-logic failure |
| Deposit paid in a currency other than the settlement currency | **Not unmodelled — the deal already carries it.** FX basis is a first-class deal term, so the deposit converts on the basis the contract states; this feature defines no FX rule of its own | Denomination currency, settlement currency and rate basis ∈ {fixed, reference-fixed-on-date, settlement-date} are read from the deal, and the default is applied **visibly, never silently** — [17.03.01 D-05](../17.03-deal-structures-economics/17.03.01-deal-term-grammar-types.md) (resolving its DT-03). Residual — which FX reference source — is 17.03.01 Q-05 → `/create-prd-stack` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Not due | Contract executed, due date ahead | Amount and due date on the schedule |
| Invoiced | Due date reached or invoice issued | Payment request to the paying side |
| In flight | Payment initiated | Pending; announce still gated |
| Received | Payment settled | Announce precondition satisfied; artist notified |
| Overdue | Past due, unpaid | Escalating reminders (17.05.02); show at risk |
| Asserted | Marked paid off-platform | Recorded but unenforceable; disputable by either side. Paying-side self-assertion does not open the announce gate until the receiving side confirms (DT-03) |
| Partial | Received amount < deposit due | Shortfall shown; announce still gated; remainder outstanding on the schedule |
| Forfeited / Returned | Show cancelled | Allocated per 17.05.03 or voided per 17.05.04 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The deposit's purpose is cashflow for the artist | The obvious reading — money up front | ❌ REJECTED: its purpose is **commitment**, and cashflow is a side effect. The deposit exists so that a cancellation has a cost without a lawsuit (CX-01) and so that the artist can safely decline competing dates. A domain expert reads a deposit as a *risk-allocation instrument*, which is why its size tracks the counterparty's reliability rather than the artist's needs — and why 17.12's payment-reliability record should plausibly feed the deposit ask. |
| DT-02 | Whether the platform holds the deposit is an implementation detail for `/create-prd-stack` | Payments-are-architecture instinct; `meta/constraints.md` already defers the payments provider | ❌ REJECTED as *only* architecture. It is a **product decision with an architecture consequence**. Escrow makes this sub-domain compute enforceable outcomes and drags in KYC/AML (flagged `[PENDING]` in `meta/constraints.md`); non-escrow makes the platform a record-keeper of money it cannot touch, and every forfeit becomes an opinion. The same feature has two different values depending on the answer. Escalated as index Q-01 rather than deferred silently. |
| DT-03 | A one-sided "mark as paid" is enough to satisfy the announce gate on off-platform deposits | Symmetry with a real receipt — if the platform can't hold the money, trust the party who says it arrived | ❌ REJECTED for the announce gate specifically. The gate exists so fans are never sold tickets against money that was never paid (Behavior job 1); a single unverified assertion by the party who *benefits* from announcing is exactly the failure the gate guards. An `Asserted` deposit should therefore require the **receiving side's confirmation** (they are the one who'd know the money landed) before it opens the gate — a paying-side self-assertion records the claim but does not unlock announce. This is the counter-attestation pattern `meta/personas.md` already values in the Producer. Kept distinct from Q-01 (escrow-vs-direct): even under escrow this governs the off-platform-paid fallback. |
| DT-04 | The deposit amount is a fixed contract number and nothing in this feature should touch its size | It comes from 17.04; this feature only collects it | ✅ CONFIRMED for collection, with a boundary: this feature **collects** the amount the contract states and never sets or renegotiates it — sizing lives in 17.04, and whether the *ask* is informed by counterparty reliability is Q-03 (owned by 17.12, not here). Confirmed that keeping sizing out of collection is correct: mixing them would put deal-term logic in the payments path and let a collection retry silently re-price a deposit. |
