# Feature: Deposit Invoice & Collection

> **Number**: 17.05.01
> **Parent**: [Deposits, Balances & Cancellation](./17.05-deposits-balances-cancellation-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The money that makes a booking real. A confirmed date with no deposit is a promise; a confirmed date
with 50% down is a commitment, and the difference is what stops both sides treating the date as
optional. It replaces the manual chasing that `meta/personas.md` records as the Operator's current
reality — "deposits chased manually".

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **Receives** the deposit in the standard live deal. It is their protection against a promoter who vanishes — by the time a show dies, the artist has already turned down other dates for it. Sees the deposit land, and its arrival is what makes them comfortable declining the competing offer. |
| Producer | None | No role. Studio deposits exist but belong to domain 16's room hire. |
| Operator | Full | **Pays** the deposit in the standard deal, on the terms written into the contract (17.04). Inverts on venue-hire deals where the artist rents the room — then the Operator is the one chasing (Q-02). |
| Fan | None | Never visible. The fan's money is ticket revenue (domain 19) and has no relationship to the deposit. |

## Behavior

The deposit is **instalment zero of the balance schedule** (CX-04), not a separate mechanism. Its
terms come from the contract; its collection runs on the platform's payments cross-cut.

Its two jobs:

1. **Gate the announce** (17.01.04) — a show that announces before the deposit lands is a show the
   promoter has not committed to, sold to fans who cannot be refunded from money that was never paid.
2. **Give cancellation terms substance** (CX-01) — forfeit against money held is arithmetic; forfeit
   against money not held is litigation.

The escrow question (index Q-01) decides which product this actually is: a platform holding the
deposit can *enforce* forfeit; a platform recording that a bank transfer happened can only *assert*
it.

### Happy Path

1. Contract executes (17.04) carrying deposit terms — amount and due date.
2. System issues the deposit invoice to the paying side against the schedule.
3. Operator pays through the payments cross-cut.
4. Receipt is recorded; the announce precondition (17.01.04) is satisfied; the artist is notified the
   money landed.
5. The deposit sits as instalment zero, feeding forfeit computation if the show later dies (CX-01).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Deposit never paid; show approaches | The date is blocked, the artist has declined other work, and the promoter has risked nothing | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — auto-void on non-payment is the obvious answer and the obvious way to lose a show both parties wanted (Q-01) |
| Deposit paid directly, outside the platform | Extremely common; the platform holds an assertion, not the money | Both sides must be able to mark it paid; the assertion is disputable (index Q-01) |
| Venue-hire deal — direction inverts | The artist pays the Operator; every role in this file flips | Direction must be a deal property, not an assumption (Q-02) |
| Cancellation lands while a deposit payment is in flight | Rare, real, money-relevant | `[PENDING — Step 5]` (CX-01) |
| Deposit paid in a currency other than the settlement currency | FX exposure between deposit and showtime, unmodelled | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — see 17.03.01 DT-03 |
| Deposit received but the artist entity has no payout method | Money held with nowhere to go | Routed to the payments cross-cut; onboarding gap |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Not due | Contract executed, due date ahead | Amount and due date on the schedule |
| Invoiced | Due date reached or invoice issued | Payment request to the paying side |
| In flight | Payment initiated | Pending; announce still gated |
| Received | Payment settled | Announce precondition satisfied; artist notified |
| Overdue | Past due, unpaid | Escalating reminders (17.05.02); show at risk |
| Asserted | Marked paid off-platform | Recorded but unenforceable; disputable by either side |
| Forfeited / Returned | Show cancelled | Allocated per 17.05.03 or voided per 17.05.04 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The deposit's purpose is cashflow for the artist | The obvious reading — money up front | ❌ REJECTED: its purpose is **commitment**, and cashflow is a side effect. The deposit exists so that a cancellation has a cost without a lawsuit (CX-01) and so that the artist can safely decline competing dates. A domain expert reads a deposit as a *risk-allocation instrument*, which is why its size tracks the counterparty's reliability rather than the artist's needs — and why 17.12's payment-reliability record should plausibly feed the deposit ask. |
| DT-02 | Whether the platform holds the deposit is an implementation detail for `/create-prd-stack` | Payments-are-architecture instinct; `meta/constraints.md` already defers the payments provider | ❌ REJECTED as *only* architecture. It is a **product decision with an architecture consequence**. Escrow makes this sub-domain compute enforceable outcomes and drags in KYC/AML (flagged `[PENDING]` in `meta/constraints.md`); non-escrow makes the platform a record-keeper of money it cannot touch, and every forfeit becomes an opinion. The same feature has two different values depending on the answer. Escalated as index Q-01 rather than deferred silently. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The deposit is instalment zero of the balance schedule, not a separate mechanism | One schedule, one ledger; splitting them puts the same money in two places that can disagree (CX-04). |
| D-02 | Payment direction is a deal property, not an assumption | Venue-hire inverts it; hard-coding Operator→Musician would break every room-rental deal. |
| D-03 | Escalated escrow-vs-direct as a product question, not an architecture deferral | The same feature has two different values depending on the answer (DT-02). |
