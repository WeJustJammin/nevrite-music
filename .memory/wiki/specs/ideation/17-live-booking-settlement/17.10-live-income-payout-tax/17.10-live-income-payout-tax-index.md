## Overview

Getting the settled money to the people who earned it, minus what the state takes first. Defining how
live income divides among a band, its sidemen and its support acts; executing the disbursement; and
handling the foreign-artist withholding that makes an international date pay 30% less than the
contract says.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities that interact through the same pool —
the split defines who gets what, withholding takes its cut *before* the split on some regimes and
*after* on others (which is not a detail, it changes everyone's number), and disbursement executes
whatever survives both.

**The non-obvious reason this is domain-owned and not a reuse of domain 09**: **a band's live income
split is not its recording split.** The sideman drummer on a £15,000 tour gets a day rate and no
points; the same drummer on the record may have 5% of the master. Modelling live payout as "the
split sheet, applied to gig money" would pay the wrong people the wrong amounts, and would do so
confidently.

> **Legend**: ✅ Full · ⚙️ Config · 👁️ Read-only · 📊 Reports · ❌ None
>
> **17.10.03 inverts the sub-domain's role pattern.** Everywhere else here the Musician is the actor
> and the Operator is absent. On withholding, the **Operator is the withholding agent** — the party
> legally obliged to deduct and remit, and liable if they don't — while the artist can only read
> what was taken and try to reclaim it. That inversion is the feature's defining fact.
>
> **Producer is `None` throughout.** Live payout has no Producer role.
