# Feature: Offer Guidance & Guarantee Sizing

> **Number**: 17.11.03
> **Parent**: [Draw History & Market Intelligence](./17.11-draw-history-market-intelligence-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Answering "what should this offer be?" — for the Operator sizing a guarantee and, symmetrically, for
the artist deciding whether the one in front of them is fair. It runs on the artist's own draw record
where one exists (no consent question) and on comparables where it does not (every consent question).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Reports | **"Is this offer fair?"** — a question working musicians have never been able to answer without an agent's private knowledge. `meta/personas.md` describes someone who signs things they don't understand; guidance is the countermeasure that arrives *before* the signature. |
| Producer | None | No role. |
| Operator | Reports | "What should I offer?" — grounded in this act's history in this market, at this room size, in this slot. Decision support beside the decision, never the decision. |
| Fan | None | Never visible. |

## Behavior

Guidance evaluates two very different sources with very different provenance:

- **The act's own record** (17.11.01) — "you drew 340 here last year, mid-week, headlining." Honest,
  specific, and needs no permission from anyone (CX-02).
- **Comparables** (17.11.02) — "acts like you draw 250-400 in rooms like this." The only source for an
  act with no local history, and it is built from other people's data (CX-03).

The design constraint that matters: **guidance advises, it never decides.** The hold ladder already
established this (17.01.02 DT-02) — the 1st-hold decision encodes relationship, genre fit, room
strategy and favours owed, none of which are in the data. The same is true of a guarantee.

Guidance must show its **basis and its confidence**, or it launders a guess into an authority.

### Happy Path

1. Operator composes an offer (17.02.01) for an act with local history.
2. Guidance surfaces the act's own record — 340 last year, mid-week, headlining, at £12.
3. Operator sees a suggested guarantee range with that basis named and its confidence shown.
4. Operator decides — using the data, their relationship with the agent, and their instinct about the
   room.
5. The artist's side sees guidance too, symmetrically: is this offer in the range for what I draw here?

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| No own record and no corpus | Cold start — nothing to guide with | Refuses rather than guesses (17.11.02 D-03) |
| Own record exists but is one show | An anecdote presented as a basis | Confidence must degrade visibly, not silently |
| Both sides see guidance and it becomes the anchor | **The number stops being negotiated and starts being accepted** | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — see DT-03 |
| Guidance from comparables | Inherits every consent question in 17.11.02 | Gated by that feature's unresolved policy (index Q-01) |
| Act's history is stale — they broke big last year | Last year's 340 is this year's 1,400 | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — recency weighting is real and unmodelled |
| Guidance written back to the record | Would pollute settled facts with model output | Structurally forbidden (sub-domain R-01) |
| Guidance used to justify a lowball | "The data says 250" — data as a negotiating weapon | Foreseeable and unpreventable; part of DT-03 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Unavailable | No own record, no corpus | Nothing — the launch state |
| Own-basis | Act has local history | Range with the specific shows named as basis |
| Comparable-basis | No own history; corpus sufficient | Aggregate range, sample size, confidence, no sources |
| Low confidence | Thin basis | Range with visibly degraded confidence |
| Suppressed | Consent policy or threshold blocks | Explicitly unavailable, not silently narrowed |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Guidance is for the promoter — they set the guarantee | The obvious reading; the offer is theirs to make | ❌ REJECTED: **the artist's need is greater and is unmet.** A promoter already knows their market — it is their job and they do it every week. The working musician in `meta/personas.md` has *no* way to know whether £800 for a Thursday in Leeds is generous or an insult, and no agent to ask. Guidance is the first symmetric information either side of a live negotiation has ever had, and its distributional effect runs toward the artist. That is the reason to build it, and it is not the reason the sweep's candidate implies. |
| DT-02 | More data makes guidance better | The obvious trajectory | ⚠️ **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED — recency and trajectory beat volume.** An act's 2024 draw is worse evidence than their 2026 draw, and an act on the way up is systematically under-predicted by their own history — which is exactly the act everyone wants to book. A model weighting all history equally would be most wrong about the most interesting bookings. Volume without recency weighting produces confident answers about artists who no longer exist. |
| DT-03 | Guidance helps both sides converge on a fair number | The framing of the whole feature, and its most comfortable assumption | ❌ REJECTED as automatic, and this is the uncomfortable one. **A shared number stops being a suggestion and becomes an anchor.** Once both parties see "the data says £800", the negotiation is about £800 — and the party who benefits is whoever the number favours, not whoever is right. A promoter will cite it to lowball ("the data says 250"); an agent will cite it to hold firm. Worse, if the platform's guidance becomes the market's reference, **the platform is setting prices in a market it also operates in** — which is a real competition concern, not a UX one. The honest mitigations are showing confidence prominently and never presenting a point estimate. Neither fully answers it. Escalated (Q-01). |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Guidance advises; it never decides or ranks | Already settled for holds (17.01.02 DT-02); a guarantee encodes relationship and strategy the data cannot see. |
| D-02 | Basis and confidence are always shown | Without them guidance launders a guess into an authority. |
| D-03 | Never render a point estimate — ranges only | A point estimate is an anchor; a range is a conversation (DT-03). The weakest available mitigation, but a real one. |
| D-04 | Guidance never writes back to the record | Model output must not pollute a corpus of settled facts (sub-domain R-01). |
| D-05 | The feature is justified by the **artist's** unmet need, not the promoter's | The promoter already knows their market; the working musician has no way to know (DT-01). |
