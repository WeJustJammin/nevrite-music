## Overview

What settlement leaves behind. Every settled show emits a fact — this artist drew this many people in
this market, in this room, on this night — and those facts accumulate into the answer to the only
question that actually decides a booking: *how many will come?* The domain index states settlement is
"the source dataset for draw intelligence"; this sub-domain is what that dataset becomes.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three capabilities separated by a **confidentiality
gate** that is itself a product decision. The draw record is a fact about one artist derived from
their own settlements. Comparables aggregate across *other people's* settlements — commercially
sensitive data whose owners would be furious. Guidance applies either. The gate between record and
comparables is why these cannot be one node.

**Why this is the thesis, not analytics**: `meta/problem-statement.md` says the platform's value is
what compounds and cannot be taken away. A **verified** draw record — derived from a signed settlement
rather than claimed on a one-sheet — is exactly that: a fact no competitor can retroactively
manufacture, because they were not in the room when it became true.
