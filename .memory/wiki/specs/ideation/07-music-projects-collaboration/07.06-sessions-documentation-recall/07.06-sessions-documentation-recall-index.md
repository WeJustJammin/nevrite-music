## Overview

The session — the dated event where humans were in a room and work happened — plus everything needed to
reconstruct it later. This sub-domain contains **the most important feature in the domain and arguably in the
platform**: the session-close capture prompt (`07.06.02`), which is the mechanism by which
`meta/problem-statement.md`'s thesis stops being a claim and becomes a product.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: four capabilities that interact tightly — the session record knows who was in
the room, which is what makes the close prompt pre-fillable rather than a blank form; the snapshot captures the
machine state at the moment of close; the recall sheet is the human-readable half, partly derived from the
snapshot. Specified apart, the prompt has nothing to pre-fill with, and a prompt with an empty field gets
dismissed.

## Deep Think Additions to the Candidate List

Two of the four children here were **not in the sweep's candidate list**. This is the sub-domain where reading
`meta/personas.md` and `meta/problem-statement.md` changes the structure rather than merely decorating it:

| Added | Why the candidate list missed it |
|---|---|
| `07.06.01` **Session Record & Attendance** | The sweep had "Session Snapshot" and "Session Documentation" — both *artifacts of* a session — but no **session entity**. The Producer's "moment that matters" (`meta/personas.md`) happens *during a session*. A song is the output; a session is the event. Without it, a credit has no timestamp anchor, no witness list, and no context — and counter-attestation has nothing to be about. |
| `07.06.02` **Session Close & Capture Prompt** | The single behavior `meta/personas.md` explicitly demands and the sweep never named: "closes a project without capturing splits — **the design must make the lazy path the correct path**." That sentence describes a feature. It did not exist in the candidate list. |
