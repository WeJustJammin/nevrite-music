## Overview

The moment the session ends, the platform gets **one** short, pre-filled, confirmable moment with the people who
were in the room — and then the room empties and the moment is gone forever. **This feature is that moment: its
trigger, its budget, its ordering, and its aftermath.** It does not ask the questions; it decides which questions
get asked, of whom, in what order, and it guarantees that asking never costs the producer the ability to close.

Everything else in domain 07 exists, ultimately, to make this moment answerable: the roster supplies the names, the
session supplies the witnesses, the DAW bridge supplies the evidence, and the stage board supplies the occasion. If
this moment is squandered — on a blank form, on three competing notifications, on a gear question — WeJammin is a
nicer Dropbox.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Producer | Full | **The primary target of the entire feature.** They fire the close (a tap, or the bridge infers it), and they are the one whose attention this feature budgets. At close they get **at most one heavyweight ask** — the split sheet (`09.02.01`), and only when this session's work has no consented split (D-09, D-10). Their credit reconciliation is *durable* and deliberately does **not** compete for the moment (`02.02.03` D-05). Their attestation carries weight because they are the trust broker (`meta/personas.md`) — a producer saying Sam played drums is worth more than Sam saying it. Dismissing costs them nothing and the session still closes (D-02). |
| Musician | Full | Receives the **counter-attestation** half within 5 s of the close signal, ungated by anything the Producer does (`02.02.03` D-05) — because *they are the perishable input*: they are in the room, awake, phone in hand, and in ten minutes they will be in a car. One tap. For the multi-hyphenate this is how a career quietly accumulates proof without a form ever being filled in. As a ledger party they also receive the split for consent — the highest-stakes tap in the product. **A Musician who is also the session's Producer gets both surfaces but their self-directed card carries no broker weight** (`02.02.02` D-07) — see the multi-hyphenate edge case. |
| Operator | None | **Deliberately absent** — see parent R-01. The session happened in their room and they still have no role in capturing what was created in it. They do not see the moment, its content, whether it fired, or whether it was dismissed — the *fact of a dismissal* is itself commercially sensitive (it means "these people have no agreed split"). A studio with visibility into its clients' splits would be unbookable by professionals. **Build no Operator surface here.** |
| Fan | None | Not visible, and no Fan surface exists in this flow. A hobbyist musician who is in the session is present through the **Musician** lens (D-19 — personas are lenses, not account types), never the Fan one. |

## Behavior

This feature is **the close event plus an arbiter**. It does three things: it decides the session is over, it
commits that fact unconditionally, and it allocates the single scarce resource the close creates — *the attention
of people who are about to leave*.

### 1. The close commits first, unconditionally

The close is not contingent on any capture. The session transitions to `Closed` and the record commits **before**
any ask is dispatched, and regardless of whether any ask is ever answered (D-02). This ordering is the entire
feature: a blocked close teaches producers not to close sessions, and an unclosed session captures *nothing*, which
is strictly worse than a dismissed prompt. Domain D-04 states it as a domain-wide law: *non-blocking is absolute;
the reckoning happens at the exit.*

### 2. The moment has a fixed budget, and it is already spent

This is the finding that reframes the feature (DT-06). Three features independently and reasonably want this
moment: credits (`02.02.03`), splits (`09.02.01`), and the recall sheet (`07.06.04`). Each is defensible alone.
Together they are roughly **three times over budget**, and the producer who gets three notifications at 23:40
dismisses all three and learns to dismiss the fourth. Nobody owned the budget. This file does.

**The budget** (inherited from `09.02.01`'s design budget, because the split sheet is the heaviest single item and
it already spends the whole thing):

| Tier | Who | Fires | Cost ceiling | What |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Nobody | At close, silent | **0 interactions** | Session record commits; snapshot taken if the close is *meaningful* (`07.06.03` D-03) |
| 1 | **Contributors** | ≤ 5 s after the close signal, **ungated** | **≤ 2 interactions each** | The credit card (`02.02.03` half 1) — one card, one tap |
| 2 | **Producer** | Same instant | **≤ 8 interactions / ≤ 90 s** | **At most one** heavyweight ask — the split sheet (`09.02.01`), for **one** work (D-10) |
| 3 | Everything else | **Never at close** | — | Producer credit reconciliation (durable, `02.02.03` D-05); recall/gear sheet (`07.06.04` D-05); QC; readiness |

**Ordering is by perishability, never by value.** The split is worth more than the credit card; the credit card
fires first anyway, because the contributors leave in minutes and the producer is still packing up. `02.02.03` D-05
established this inversion inside domain 02; this file makes it the rule for the moment as a whole.

### 3. The ask is a confirmation, never a form — and if it cannot be, it does not fire

Pre-fill is not a nicety, it is the precondition (D-01). "Who played on this?" is admin and gets dismissed; "Sam
played drums, right?" is four seconds. It follows — and this is the rule the breadth pass missed — that **an ask
with nothing to pre-fill must not fire at all** (D-11). A blank card at 1am does not merely fail to capture; it
teaches the dismissal reflex that every future close depends on not having. Silence is a better outcome than a
blank form. Low-confidence guesses are questions, never pre-filled answers (`07.09.02` D-02, D-03).

### 4. The split is asked, but a number is never forced

Three answers, all one tap: **agree now** / **note it as open** / **not applicable**. "Open" is a first-class,
captured fact — not a failure state (D-03). Any design that treats "not agreed" as failure produces lies or
dismissals.

### Happy Path

1. Producer wraps at 23:40 — taps "end session", or the bridge detects the DAW closed (`07.09`); the wrap signal
   arrives
2. **The session closes.** Record commits, `Active → Closed`, before anything is asked. Nothing downstream can
   reverse this
3. **Within 5 s**, each contributor present in the session's delta gets one card on their phone: *"Ghost Town — Drums, tracks 1, 3, 4. Confirm?"* — ungated, not waiting on the Producer
4. **At the same instant**, the Producer gets exactly one card: *"Ghost Town — split not agreed. Agree now / Note as open / Not applicable"*
5. Sam taps confirm while still coiling a cable — **two taps, in the room**; the claim becomes a **verified credit** (`02.04.01`)
6. Producer taps "note it as open" — **four seconds** — and the open split is recorded as a fact, with the witness list attached
7. The Producer's credit reconciliation is waiting at 11am tomorrow, unhurried, because it is durable and nothing about it was perishable

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Producer dismisses the moment** | **Session closes anyway.** Completeness debt accrues (`07.08.03` D-04 — the debt ledger is what makes non-blocking honest). Re-offer per `02.02.03` D-11: **3 maximum**, scheduled by **local wall-clock** (next local morning, 09:00–11:00), then a permanent passive marker. **Circuit breaker: 3 consecutive skipped sessions → the ask stops firing for that Producer.** | Song shows an incomplete indicator; no nag, no block |
| **Split is genuinely undecided ("we'll sort it out")** | **The single most important case in the product**, and the realistic majority. The prompt accepts "not yet" and captures *the fact that it is open*, with the attendees on record. In three years the argument starts from "these four people were there on 14 Nov and never agreed" rather than from nothing. | "Split not agreed — note it as open?" — one tap; the openness is now a captured fact |
| **Session produced four songs** (a normal tracking day) | `09.02.01` D-06: parties carry forward, **percentages never** — so four works = four sheets = ~4× the budget. **Arbitration (D-10): the split ask covers exactly ONE work at close** — the one with the most contribution activity in this session. The other three go to the debt ledger and are asked at *their* next close. Four sheets at 23:40 is how you get zero sheets. | One card naming one song; the rest appear as debt on the board |
| **Pure writing session — guitar, notebook, zero DAW output** | **DT-11 — the case that breaks a naïve trigger.** If the ask keys off DAW output it fires never, and this is *the single most split-relevant session type in music*: publishing splits are born in the room where nothing was recorded. **D-12: the split ask keys off the work's open-split state plus a human-marked song link — never off DAW output.** | Split ask fires normally on a session with no takes |
| **Session closed with genuinely nothing** — no work, no song link, no contributions | **No ask fires at all.** Empty sessions are normal and not a defect (`07.06.01`). Prompting one is the nag that trains the dismissal reflex. | Session closes silently |
| **Nothing to pre-fill** — DAW parse failed, no track names, no roll | **The contributor half does not fire** (D-11) — a blank card is worse than no card. The Producer half fires with the roll only, or not at all. Parse failure never blocks ingest (`07.09.02` D-05). | No card, rather than an empty one |
| **Producer is also a performer** (the multi-hyphenate, the normal case) | Gets **both** surfaces: a contributor card for their own performance and the Producer's reconciliation. Their self-directed card is `author == subject` and carries **no broker weight** (`02.02.02` D-07); it is not a second, independent attestation. Their own row instead gets an **optional, unchecked** reciprocal line on each contributor's card (`02.02.03` D-08). | One card about themselves that visibly counts for less |
| **Two producers (co-production) both open the split sheet** | `09.02.04` D-04: concurrent proposals **queue**, never merge — exactly one may be `open`. First proposer holds the sheet; the second sees it live rather than forking it. Merging two proposed splits yields a split nobody proposed. | "Alex is proposing this split" — live, not a conflict dialog |
| **Producer closes and reopens repeatedly to re-fire the sheet at a contributor** | The attrition attack (`09.02.04` D-10 — the abuse model is attrition, not force). **D-15: the split ask is rate-limited to 1 per (work, contributor, 7 days) regardless of close count.** Closing is free; asking is not. | The ask does not re-fire; the Producer sees why |
| **Close fires during playback or an active take** | **Never interrupts** (`07.09.03` D-04) — interrupting a take gets the agent uninstalled the same day. The moment is held until transport has been stopped for 60 s, then fires. | Nothing during the take |
| **Five sessions close in quick succession** (batch bouncing at end of day) | Closes within a **10-minute window batch into one moment**, with per-session items inside it (`07.09.03` D-06). Five notifications is the dismissal failure in miniature. | One notification, N items |
| **Session closed by the bridge with no human present** (DAW quit, laptop shut) | Moment **deferred to the Producer's next appearance, never lost** (`07.09.03` D-05). But it is now honestly async: the contributors left three days ago, so their half routes to `02.04.01` as the attestation chase it actually is (`02.02.03` signal 5), not as a close prompt. | Prompt waiting next time they appear; contributors chased async |
| **Offline — no signal in the live room** | The close commits locally and queues. **Room mode** is the primary offline path (`02.02.03` D-12): taps are recorded as **witnessed, not attested**, and are never rendered as "confirmed by X". The distinction is load-bearing — a tap on the Producer's device is not the contributor's signature. | "Witnessed in room" — visibly a different, lesser thing |
| **Contributor left early** (packed up at 21:00; close at 23:40) | Their card still fires, but to a phone in a car. Converts at a materially worse rate, honestly. This is the cost of keying on close rather than on departure — accepted, because departure is unobservable. | Async card; no pretence it is the same thing |
| **Contributor never counter-attests** | Credit stays a **claim**, visibly unverified (`07.03.01` D-03), forever if necessary. **Never auto-promoted by time.** Silence is silence — never a refusal, and never shown to the Producer as one (`02.02.03` D-09). People are packing a kit, not making a statement. | "Unconfirmed" everywhere it appears |
| **Contributor disputes the claim** | Recorded as disputed; routes to domain 24. Neither party can overwrite the other. | **Producer inflates their own split at close** | `meta/personas.md`'s named worst-intentional behavior. Not preventable at write time — the structural check is that **every ledger party sees the whole ledger** (`09.02.01` D-09) and must consent, and any edit invalidates every consent including the proposer's (`09.02.01` D-07). | Contributors see the entire proposed ledger, not just their own line |
| **Someone played who is not on the roster or in attendance** | Producer can add them at close; unclaimed identity created (`07.03.01` D-02). Freehand names are **never silently bound** to an existing account (`02.02.01` D-15) — the wrong-Sam case mints a false claim and leaks a confidential title in one tap (`07.03.02` D-15). | "Add someone else?" — deliberately secondary, never a required field |
| **A named contributor has blocked the Producer** | They cannot be named at entry-creation time (`07.03.02` D-13) — a block revokes the ability to name, not merely the delivery. They never appear in the moment. The close still commits. | The Producer cannot add them |
| **Non-user with a global opt-out** (`07.03.02` D-14) | Credit is captured; delivery is suppressed; the credit is **permanently unverifiable**, and that cost is shown to the Producer honestly rather than hidden. | "This credit can never be confirmed" |
| **Session runs past midnight / ten hours, 1am, exhausted** | The **design case, not the edge case**. A session is a continuous working block bounded by a wrap signal or 6 h of inactivity — calendar days are irrelevant (`02.02.01` D-12). | One card, one tap |
| **The close is also a billing event** (paid session — 05 service order, 16 booking) | **DT-12 — refused.** The capture ask is **never** coupled to payment release (D-14). An attestation given to unlock a payment is not evidence, it is a receipt. | Two unrelated moments, never one screen |
| **Producer closes early by mistake** | Reopen within **30 minutes** un-closes the session and re-arms the moment. **Asks already dispatched are not recalled** — an attestation binds to the claim that was shown and read (`02.02.03` D-07); un-firing it would be rewriting what someone saw. After 30 minutes it is a new session (two sessions in a day is normal — `07.06.01`). | "Reopened" — dispatched cards stay valid |
| **Producer's device dies mid-close** | The close is inferred by the ladder's backstop (`02.02.03` signal 5: 72 h of no capture activity). Nothing is lost; it degrades to the async chase. | Prompt on next appearance |
| **A ledger party is a minor** (bedroom producers, teenage co-writers — common) | The close commits and the credit ask fires normally. The **split consent** question routes to `09.02.01` Q-05 (guardian co-consent), which is genuinely open and is not this file's to answer. This file does not gate the close on it. | Close unaffected; split consent per domain 09 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Armed** | Session `Active` | Nothing. The moment has not fired and must not announce itself |
| **Suppressed** | Wrap signal arrived during playback/record (`07.09.03` D-04) | Nothing — held until transport stops for 60 s |
| **Closing** (Loading) | Wrap signal accepted | Brief "closing session" — the record commits here, and this step **cannot fail into a blocked close** |
| **Empty** | Closed with no work, no song link, no contributions | Session closes silently. **No ask, no CTA, no nag** — the deliberate empty state |
| **Suppressed — no pre-fill** | Ask has nothing to pre-fill (D-11) | No card at all, rather than a blank one |
| **Populated / Prompting** | Close committed, pre-fill available | Contributors: one card each (≤ 2 taps). Producer: one card, one work (≤ 8 interactions / 90 s) |
| **Partial** | Pre-fill incomplete — roll present, parse failed, or low-confidence guesses withheld | The known rows, plus an explicit count of what could not be determined. **Never a guess rendered as a fact** (`07.09.02` D-02) |
| **Batched** | ≥ 2 closes within 10 min | One notification, N session items |
| **Deferred** | No human present at close, or offline | Queued for next appearance; contributor half re-routed to the async chase (`02.04.01`) |
| **Room mode** | Offline in the live room (`02.02.03` D-12) | Taps recorded as **witnessed, not attested** — visibly a lesser artifact |
| **Confirmed** | Producer/contributor acted | Credits written as claims; split recorded as agreed **or explicitly open** |
| **Dismissed** | Producer dismissed | Session is closed. Completeness debt accrues (`07.08.03`). ≤ 3 re-offers, then a passive marker |
| **Circuit-broken** | 3 consecutive skipped sessions (`02.02.03` D-11) | The ask stops firing. The platform accepts the answer it has been given |
| **Awaiting attestation** | Claims out, unconfirmed | "2 of 4 confirmed" on the song — a **pending invitation**, not a defect (D-05) |
| **Disputed** | A contributor disputed | Flagged; routes to 24; both positions retained |
| **Error** | Ask dispatch failed | **The close still stands.** The ask retries; entered data is never discarded (`09.02.01` D-05) — capture data is unrepeatable, and forcing re-entry means it does not happen |
