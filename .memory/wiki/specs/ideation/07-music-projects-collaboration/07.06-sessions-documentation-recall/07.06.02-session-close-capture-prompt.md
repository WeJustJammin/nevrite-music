# Feature: Session Close & Capture Prompt

> **Number**: 07.06.02
> **Parent**: [Sessions, Documentation & Recall](./07.06-sessions-documentation-recall-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

> **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list (sub-domain D-02). It is specified, almost
> verbatim, in `meta/personas.md`: *"closes a project without capturing splits ('we'll do it later') — the exact
> failure the platform exists to prevent, committed by the persona best placed to prevent it. **The design must
> make the lazy path the correct path.**"* That sentence is a feature description. The sweep produced 35
> candidates for this domain and none of them was this.

## Overview

The moment the session ends, the platform asks one short, pre-filled, confirmable question about who did what and
what the split is — and it takes four seconds. **This is the mechanism by which the platform's thesis stops being
a claim and becomes a product.** Every other feature in domain 07 exists, ultimately, to make this prompt
answerable: the roster supplies the names, the session supplies the witnesses, the DAW bridge supplies the
evidence, and the stage board supplies the moment. If this prompt does not get answered, WeJammin is a nicer
Dropbox.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Producer | Full | **The primary target of the entire feature.** At close they get a short, pre-filled attestation: "Sam played drums, Alex engineered, split still open — confirm?" Their attestation carries weight because they are the trust broker (`meta/personas.md`) — a producer saying Sam played drums is worth more than Sam saying it. |
| Musician | Full | Receives the **counter-attestation** half: "Producer says you played drums on Ghost Town — confirm?" One tap. This is the other half of the pair that turns a claim into a verified credit, and for the multi-hyphenate it is how their career quietly accumulates proof without them ever filling in a form. Also receives the split confirmation, which is the highest-stakes tap in the product. |
| Operator | None | **Deliberately absent** — see parent R-01. The session happened in their room and they still have no role in capturing what was created in it. A studio with visibility into its clients' splits would be unbookable by professionals. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

## Behavior

The prompt fires at session close (`07.06.01`) and at stage transitions (`07.01.03`). Four properties define it,
and each is the difference between a feature that works and one that does not:

**1. Pre-filled, never blank.** The system already knows who was in the room (attendance), what appeared (takes,
versions), and who holds what role (roster). The question is therefore a *confirmation*, not a form. "Who played
on this?" gets dismissed; "Sam played drums, right?" gets tapped. Parent CX-01.

**2. Non-blocking, absolutely.** Dismissing the prompt still closes the session (`07.01.03` D-02). A blocked close
teaches producers not to close sessions — and an unclosed session captures *nothing*, which is strictly worse than
a dismissed prompt. The lazy path must be the correct path, which means the lazy path must remain available.

**3. Short.** Four seconds, one screen, at 1am after ten hours. Every field added reduces the completion rate.
This is why the recall sheet's "what mic was on the kick?" cannot ride along (parent CX-04) and why the split
question is the *only* heavyweight item that earns a place.

**4. Attested, not asserted.** The producer's answer is a **claim**; the contributor's confirmation is a
**counter-attestation**; only the pair is a verified credit (`07.03.01` DT-03). Neither party can unilaterally
manufacture one, which is what makes the resulting graph worth anything.

### Happy Path

1. Producer wraps at 23:40 and hits "end session" (or the bridge detects the DAW closed)
2. One card: *"Ghost Town — Sam played drums, you engineered. Split not set. Confirm?"*
3. Producer taps confirm — **four seconds**
4. Credits written to domain 02 as claims; counter-attestation requests fan out to Sam and anyone else named
5. Sam taps confirm on his phone the next morning; the claim becomes a **verified credit** — captured at source, on the day, by the people who were there, exactly as `meta/problem-statement.md` says nobody currently does

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Producer dismisses the prompt | **Session closes anyway.** Completeness debt accrues (`07.08.03`); re-offered at the next natural moment (next session on the song, stage transition, handoff) | Song shows an incomplete indicator; no nag, no block |
| Split is genuinely undecided ("we'll sort it out") | **The single most important case in the product.** The honest answer is "not yet" and the prompt must accept it — pretending otherwise makes producers lie or dismiss. What it captures instead is the *fact that it is open*, with the attendees on record. That is a vastly better starting point than silence: in three years the argument starts from "these four people were there and never agreed" rather than from nothing. | "Split not agreed — note it as open?" — one tap, and the openness itself is now a captured fact |
| Contributor was in the room but did not play | Not pre-filled as a contributor (`07.06.01` D-03) — attendance is not contribution | Present in attendance, absent from the credit prompt |
| Someone played who is not on the roster or in attendance | Producer can add them at close; unclaimed identity created (`07.03.01` D-02) | "Add someone else?" — deliberately secondary, not a required field |
| Contributor never counter-attests | Credit stays a **claim**, visibly unverified (`07.03.01` D-03), forever if necessary. Never auto-promoted by time. | "Unconfirmed" everywhere it appears |
| Contributor **disputes** the claim | Recorded as disputed; routes to domain 24. Neither party can overwrite the other. | Both positions visible |
| Producer inflates their own split at close | `meta/personas.md`'s named worst-intentional behavior. Not preventable at write time — but every named party is notified and must counter-attest, which is the structural check. | Contributors see the proposed split and confirm or dispute |
| Session closed by the bridge with no human present (DAW quit, laptop shut) | Prompt deferred to the producer's next open, **not lost** | Prompt waiting next time they appear |
| Ten-hour session, 1am, exhausted | The design case, not the edge case — see property 3 | One card, one tap |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Not fired | Session active | Nothing |
| Prompting | Session close / stage transition | One card, pre-filled, dismissible |
| Confirmed | Producer confirmed | Credits written as claims; counter-attestation dispatched |
| Dismissed | Producer dismissed | Session closes; completeness debt accrues |
| Deferred | No human present at close | Prompt queued for next appearance |
| Awaiting attestation | Claims out, unconfirmed | "2 of 4 confirmed" on the song |
| Disputed | A contributor disputed | Flagged; routes to 24 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **The prompt must be pre-filled to the point of being a confirmation, not a question** — this is the entire difference between the thesis working and not working | `meta/personas.md`, Producer success criteria: "Credits and splits captured as a byproduct of running the session — **zero extra admin**." A blank form is admin by definition. Pre-filling requires knowing who was there (`07.06.01`), what they did (`07.09.02`, `07.04.03`), and who they are (`07.03.01`) — which is why those features exist. **They are not peers of this prompt; they are its inputs.** | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it reframes the whole domain's dependency graph. `07.06.01`, `07.03.01` and `07.09` are not independently valuable features that happen to be nearby; they are the machinery that makes this one four-second interaction possible. MoSCoW must treat them as a bundle, because shipping this prompt without them produces a blank form, and a blank form at 1am is dismissed forever. |
| DT-02 | Capture should be **blocking** at session close, because that is the one moment everyone is present and it will never come again | Genuinely tempting and superficially rigorous. `meta/problem-statement.md` says "the data is unrepeatable" — a split captured on the day costs one signature; reconstructed in three years it may cost a lawsuit or be unobtainable at any price. If it is that valuable, why not insist? | ❌ **REJECTED — and this rejection is the feature.** `meta/personas.md` names the failure precisely: the Producer's worst accidental behavior is closing without capturing, and "the design must make the lazy path the correct path." A blocking prompt does not make the lazy path correct — it **removes** the lazy path, and the producer routes around the platform entirely: they stop closing sessions, or they stop using WeJammin for the session, and the platform is out of the room. Then it captures nothing at all, forever, instead of capturing 70% now. **The correct move is to make the right path so cheap that laziness chooses it** — four seconds, pre-filled, one tap. Coercion loses the room; convenience wins it. This is the most important product decision in domain 07. |
| DT-03 | The split question belongs in this prompt at all | Worth challenging honestly: credits are low-stakes and social ("Sam played drums" — nobody objects). A split is money, and asking four exhausted people to agree percentages at 1am is asking for a bad decision or an argument at the worst possible time. Arguably the prompt should capture credits and leave splits for daylight. | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED WITH A CRUCIAL AMENDMENT.** The split *must* be asked — `meta/problem-statement.md` is unambiguous that the unrecorded split is "the single most litigated failure in music" and that the value decays to zero with time. But the prompt must **never force a number.** It offers three answers, all one tap: agree the split now / **note it as open** / not applicable. Capturing "these four people were in the room on 14 Nov and the split is explicitly open" is a *massive* improvement over silence — in three years the argument starts from an agreed witness list and an acknowledged gap rather than from nothing. **The open split is itself a captured fact, and it is the realistic majority case.** Any design that treats "not agreed" as a failure state will produce lies or dismissals. |
| DT-04 | Counter-attestation makes the credit graph trustworthy | The claim implicit in the attestation model (`07.03.01` DT-03) | ⚠️ **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED — with a cold-start problem the sweep never surfaced.** Counter-attestation requires the contributor to be *on the platform*, and early on most are not (`07.03.01` DT-01). So early credits are producer-claims awaiting confirmation that may take months or never arrive — which means **the platform's most valuable asset is unverified for its entire first phase**, precisely when it most needs to look credible. Two consequences: (a) the unverified state must be designed as a first-class, dignified state rather than a deficiency — "claimed by Producer, awaiting Sam" reads as a *pending invitation*, not a failure; (b) it turns the attestation request into the growth loop (`07.03.02` DT-01) — the thing that brings Sam to the platform *is* the credit waiting for him. The weakness and the growth engine are the same mechanism, which is the most elegant thing in this domain and also its biggest bet: if contributors do not come to claim, the graph never verifies. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Pre-filled confirmation, never a blank form** | Per DT-01 — the difference between the thesis working and not. Makes `07.06.01`/`07.03.01`/`07.09` its inputs, not its peers. |
| D-02 | **Non-blocking, absolutely.** Dismissal closes the session and accrues completeness debt | Per DT-02 — the single most important product decision in the domain. Coercion loses the room. |
| D-03 | The split is asked, but a **number is never forced** — "open" is a first-class, one-tap answer | Per DT-03 — the open split is itself a captured fact, and it is the realistic majority case. |
| D-04 | Credits are written as **claims**; only counter-attestation makes them verified | Per DT-04 and `07.03.01` D-03. Neither party can unilaterally manufacture a credit. |
| D-05 | The unverified/awaiting state is **dignified, not deficient** | Per DT-04's cold-start problem — early on, nearly everything is unverified, and it must not look broken. |
| D-06 | One screen, one tap, four seconds. Nothing else rides along | Per property 3 — every added field cuts completion. The recall sheet's gear questions are excluded for this reason (parent CX-04). |
| D-07 | The Operator has **no role** here | Parent R-01 — a studio with visibility into client splits is unbookable. |
