## Overview

Getting the work out of the project intact: the package built to whoever is receiving it and validated against
their spec, the automated technical checks, the readiness score that says whether a song is actually shippable,
the declaration of what came from somewhere else (samples, interpolations, AI), and the remix stem programs that
send audio deliberately outward.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: five capabilities in one gate — the recipient spec drives the package, the
package triggers the QC, the QC and the source declaration both feed the readiness score, and the score is what
tells a Musician whether they can ship. Pull any one out and the gate leaks.

**Why it is the domain's exit**: everything upstream manufactures facts; this sub-domain is where the facts are
checked and the work leaves. It is also where the domain's **completeness debt** — every capture prompt anyone
dismissed (`07.01.03` D-02, `07.06.02` D-02) — finally becomes visible and consequential, without ever having
blocked anyone along the way.
