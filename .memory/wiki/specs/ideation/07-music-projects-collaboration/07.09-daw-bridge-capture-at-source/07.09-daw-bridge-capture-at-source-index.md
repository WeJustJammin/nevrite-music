## Overview

The surface that reaches into where the work actually happens: the DAW. Bounces arrive without being uploaded;
session files are parsed so the platform knows what is in them; the capture prompt reaches the producer without
requiring them to open a browser. **This sub-domain is entirely absent from the sweep's 35 candidates**, and it
is the mechanism on which the domain's thesis depends.

## Why This Exists — the single most consequential Deep Think finding in domain 07

`meta/personas.md` states the Producer's unique constraint without ambiguity:

> **"Their work happens inside a DAW, not a browser. Any capture flow requiring them to leave the session and go
> fill in a web form will not be used."**

And `meta/problem-statement.md` states the root cause:

> **"No system is present at the moment of creation, so the record is always a reconstruction."**

Put together: the moment of creation is *inside a DAW*, and the platform's entire thesis is being present at it.
A web app is, by construction, **not present at the moment of creation**. The sweep produced 35 candidates for
this domain — song records, version control, review, approval, mastering — every one of which assumes the work
arrives in a browser somehow. **None of them asked how.**

This sub-domain is that question. It is also the domain's biggest open risk, because the honest answer may be
that `meta/constraints.md`'s single-web-surface constraint cannot deliver it (Q-01).

Is that enough, and should the thesis be restated honestly if so? | User | `/ideate-validate` |
