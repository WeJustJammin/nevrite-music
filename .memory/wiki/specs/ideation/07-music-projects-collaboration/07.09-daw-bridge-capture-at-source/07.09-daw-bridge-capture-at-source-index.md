## Overview

The surface that reaches into where the work actually happens: the DAW. Bounces arrive without being uploaded;
session files are parsed so the platform knows what is in them; the capture prompt reaches the producer without
requiring them to open a browser. **This sub-domain is entirely absent from the sweep's 35 candidates**, and it
is the mechanism on which the domain's thesis depends.

> **Scope note (2026-07-22).** None of this is v1. Per D-05 no client is authorised, and per domain D-09 the v1
> delivery of `07.06.02`'s capture prompt is **PWA web push + in-app** — not this sub-domain's local-agent
> notification (`07.09.03` D-01), which is deferred with the client surface itself.

## Why This Exists — the single most consequential Deep Think finding in domain 07

`meta/personas.md` states the Producer's unique constraint without ambiguity:

> **"Their work happens inside a DAW, not a browser. Any capture flow requiring them to leave the session and go
> fill in a web form will not be used."**

And `meta/problem-statement.md` states the root cause:

> **"No system is present at the moment of creation, so the record is always a reconstruction."**

Put together: the moment of creation is *inside a DAW*, and the platform's entire thesis is being present at it.
A web app is, by construction, **not present at the moment of creation**. The sweep produced 35 candidates for
this domain — song records, version control, review, approval, mastering — every one of which assumes the work
arrives in a browser somehow. **None of them asked how.**

This sub-domain is that question. It was also the domain's biggest open risk, because the honest answer may be
that `meta/constraints.md`'s single-web-surface constraint cannot deliver it (Q-01).

> **Answered 2026-07-22 (D-05).** It cannot, and **no non-web client is authorised**. The `meta/constraints.md`
> Desktop row is rewritten from "Not in scope. No directive." to *"not authorised; reopens only on the
> enumerated evidence"*, and the four reopen items are written out in full in D-05 below. Nothing in this
> sub-domain is rejected: `07.09.01/.02/.03` keep `Should`, unphased (D-06). What changes is what the platform
> is allowed to **claim** while the gate holds — for the whole v1 window WeJammin captures at the *first sharing
> moment* (`07.05.02`) plus the close prompt, and capture-at-source is the direction rather than the current
> claim (D-07). Read the rest of this file as ratified product intent behind a gate, not as v1 build content.

### D-05 — the four reopen-evidence items, in full

The gate reopens **only** on all four. They are surface-specific and are **not** borrowed from D-04's parser
gate; D-04 remains a **separate, additional** gate that applies *after* the surface question is reopened, never
instead of it.

| # | Evidence required | Source of the question |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | **Can producers on locked-down commercial studio machines install anything at all** — or are the highest-value users permanently unreachable by a client? | `07.09.01` Q-02 / DT-03(b) — "commercial studios routinely lock down their machines, so the producers with the most sessions… may be the least able to install it"; its edge-case row calls that case "common in commercial studios, and fatal for this feature" |
| (b) | **Does real-session evidence show track names carrying person signal often enough to be worth a client?** | Domain Q-08 / `07.09.02` Q-03 — the track-name premise is asserted from reasoning, not verified, and in electronic production there may be no people in the names at all |
| (c) | **A costed statement of the agent's builds, updates, signing, notarisation and support load** against `meta/constraints.md` Team = Solo and Budget = Lean | `07.09.01` DT-03(a) — "a local agent is a second surface, with its own builds, its own updates, its own signing and notarisation, and its own support burden, for a platform explicitly being built by one person" |
| (d) | **A read-scope model that is verifiable, not asserted**, for studios holding confidential client material | `07.09.01` D-06 / DT-03(c) — a background agent watching a folder and uploading audio is a legitimate security concern, and the honest answer to "what else does it read?" must be verifiable |

> **Nobody is currently assigned to gather these** (Q-04). They are owner-decision inputs, not tracked work.
