# Feature: In-Session Capture Surface

> **Number**: 07.09.03
> **Parent**: [DAW Bridge & Capture-at-Source](./07.09-daw-bridge-capture-at-source-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

> **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list (sub-domain D-01).

## Overview

Somewhere to *put* the capture prompt that does not require the producer to open a browser. `07.06.02` defines
the four-second interaction the platform's thesis depends on; `07.09.02` supplies its pre-fill. This feature is
the question nobody asked: **where does that prompt actually appear?** A prompt rendered in a browser tab the
producer will not open is a prompt that does not exist.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Never uses this. Benefits entirely: their credits get captured because someone else got asked at the right moment, on a surface they would actually look at. |
| Producer | Full | The only user, and the reason the feature exists. Sees the prompt where they are — not where the platform wishes they were. |
| Operator | None | Not their business (`07.06` R-01 — the Operator has no role in capture at all). |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

## Behavior

The prompt from `07.06.02` needs a surface that reaches a producer who is inside a DAW, at the end of a session,
probably tired, definitely not thinking about WeJammin. Candidate surfaces, honestly assessed:

| Surface | Reach | Cost | Honest read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Browser tab** | Zero at the moment that matters | Free | It is the flow `meta/personas.md` says will not be used |
| **Local agent notification** (native OS) | Good — the agent exists anyway (`07.09.01`) | Low | **Most likely answer.** It is *on* the machine, at the moment of close |
| **DAW plugin** (VST3/AU/AAX) | Best — literally inside the session | Very high | Native builds × 3 formats × 2 OSes, per-DAW quirks, forever |
| **Phone push** | Good — the producer is packing up, phone in hand | Medium | Needs a mobile surface `meta/constraints.md` does not have |
| **Email** | Poor | Free | Arrives among 200 others; answered never |

The prompt is **rare by design** — session close and stage transitions, not every bounce (parent CX-03). Its
power is entirely a function of its rarity: a prompt that fires twenty times a session is dismissed reflexively
within a week, and then the one that mattered is dismissed too.

### Which Moments Earn a Prompt (Q-01, designed at Level 1)

The whole feature lives or dies on this list. Getting it wrong in either direction is fatal: too many prompts and
the producer learns to dismiss reflexively; too few and the moment the record got made goes uncaptured. The
governing rule is DT-02 — **scarcity is the mechanism** — so the default posture is *silence*, and a moment must
clear a real bar to earn an interruption.

| Moment | Fire? | Why | Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Session close** (DAW quit, or project closed) | ✅ Primary | The canonical "record is now made" signal; the producer is transitioning out anyway | Fire once, ≥ 30 minutes of recorded session activity since last capture — trivial opens don't qualify |
| **Production stage transition** (`07.01.03` — an approved enum version's prompt-mapped semantic transition) | ✅ Primary | A semantic stage boundary means a body of contributor work just closed; the other genuinely prompt-worthy moment | Fire on the mapped transition event, once per boundary. Candidate labels never create this contract before P-01 validation and product-owner approval. |
| **First bounce of a new song** | ⚠️ Conditional | A first export often means "a thing now exists worth crediting" — but only if no capture exists yet for that song | Only when the song has zero captured contributors and the session has ≥ 2 confirmed contributors |
| **Every bounce / export** | ❌ Never | The DT-02 failure in its purest form — a bounce every 8 minutes trains dismissal | — |
| **Every save / autosave** | ❌ Never | Dozens per hour; carries no "record is made" meaning | — |
| **Mid-session, transport active** | ❌ Never (D-04) | Interrupting a take gets the agent uninstalled the same day | — |
| **Idle mid-session** (long pause, no close) | ❌ Never | A pause is not a close; the producer is still working | — |

Two hard modifiers sit on top of the table: (1) **never while transport is playing or recording** (D-04) — a
qualifying close that lands mid-playback waits until transport stops; (2) **de-duplication per song** — a song
already fully captured this session does not re-prompt on a later close (`07.06.02` owns the debt/re-offer logic).

**Provisional v1 implementation defaults:** a session-close prompt requires **30 minutes of recorded session
activity** since the last capture; a first-bounce prompt requires **2 confirmed contributors** and zero captured
contributors for that song. These are explicit implementation defaults, not immutable product policy. `/write-be-spec`
owns post-launch tuning against observed producer behaviour; it may revise them without changing the locked
prompt-moment taxonomy — which classes of moment are in, conditional, and out — because that taxonomy is the
product decision, not the tuning parameter.

### Happy Path

1. Producer finishes at 23:40 and quits Logic
2. The local agent sees the session close; the parse is fresh (`07.09.02`)
3. A native notification appears **on the machine they are already at**: "Ghost Town — Sam played drums, you engineered. Confirm?"
4. One click — four seconds, on a surface they were already looking at
5. Credits captured (`07.06.02`); counter-attestation dispatched; the producer never opened a browser

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Producer already walked away | Prompt deferred, **not lost** (`07.06.02`) | Waiting next time they appear |
| OS notifications disabled or suppressed | Very common on studio machines, where focus modes are standard | Falls back to the next surface; must not assume delivery |
| Prompt fires mid-session by mistake | **Actively harmful** — interrupting a take is unforgivable and would get the agent uninstalled | Never fire during active playback/record — see DT-02 |
| Producer dismisses reflexively | The realistic risk. Debt accrues (`07.08.03`); prompt re-offered at the next natural moment | Non-blocking, always |
| Session closes because the laptop lid shut | Not a real close. Deferred | Prompt waits |
| Multiple sessions closed at once | One prompt per song, batched — not five notifications | One card |
| Producer has no agent installed (locked-down studio, `07.09.01` DT-03) | **No surface at all.** Falls back to a browser prompt they will not see, i.e. no capture | The bridge's absence has this exact consequence |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Idle | Session active | Nothing — the correct state 99% of the time |
| Prompting | Meaningful close detected | One native notification, pre-filled |
| Confirmed | Producer clicked | Credits written; nothing more |
| Dismissed | Producer dismissed | Gone; debt accrues silently |
| Deferred | Nobody present | Queued for next appearance |
| Unavailable | Notifications suppressed / no agent | Falls back; capture likely lost |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **The prompt needs a surface, and "the web app" is not one** — this is the question the sweep never asked | `07.06.02` specifies the interaction beautifully and is silent on where it renders. `meta/personas.md` is explicit that a browser is not where the producer is at the moment of close. So the domain's most important feature had, until this sub-domain, **no delivery mechanism at all** — 35 candidates describing what to capture and nothing describing how the question reaches the person who can answer it. | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it exposes the domain's deepest structural risk: `meta/constraints.md` declares a single web surface, and the platform's core value-capture moment provably **cannot happen on it**. That is not a feature gap; it is a contradiction between the constraint and the thesis, and it is the reason sub-domain Q-01 escalates to the surface decision. |
| DT-02 | The prompt should fire whenever there is something to capture — bounces, saves, session close | The maximal-capture instinct, and it looks like it maximises the thing that matters | ❌ **REJECTED** — the prompt's power is *entirely* a function of its rarity. A producer bounces twenty times a session; twenty prompts is an interruption every eight minutes, and by day three the muscle memory is dismiss-without-reading. Then the one prompt that actually mattered — the split at the close of the session where the record got made — is dismissed too, reflexively, and the platform has trained its most important user to ignore its most important question. Worse, a prompt firing **during** a take is unforgivable: it interrupts a performance, and the agent gets uninstalled that day. **Prompt-worthy moments are rare and specific**: session close, stage transitions (`07.01.03`), never mid-playback. The scarcity is the mechanism, not a limitation of it — this is the same insight as `07.04.02` DT-02 (the friction is the feature), applied to attention rather than to decisions. |
| DT-03 | A DAW plugin is the ideal surface, since it is literally inside the session | The best-reach option in the table above, and the one that most fully honours the persona constraint | ⚠️ **PARTIALLY REJECTED on cost-benefit, not on merit.** A plugin *is* the best surface — it is in the session, it can see transport state, it can fire at a musically appropriate moment. It is also VST3 + AU + AAX × macOS + Windows, with per-DAW quirks, per-DAW validation, code signing, notarisation, and a permanent compatibility tail across every DAW update — for a platform explicitly being built without a platform team (`meta/problem-statement.md`). And its marginal gain over a **local agent notification** is small: the agent is already on the machine (`07.09.01`), already knows the session closed, and can fire a native notification at exactly the same moment. The agent gets ~90% of the value for ~10% of the cost, and it works with every DAW ever made rather than the three that got plugin builds. **Verdict: agent notification first; plugin only if the agent measurably fails to reach producers.** Same reasoning as `07.09.01` DT-01 — maximum coverage for minimum surface. |
| DT-04 | The **first bounce of a song** is a third prompt-worthy moment alongside session close and stage transition | It is a natural "a thing now exists" signal, and waiting for close risks missing a producer who bounces-and-walks without formally quitting the DAW | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED but only conditionally.** A first bounce genuinely can be the moment the record becomes real — but firing on *every* first-bounce would re-import the DT-02 failure through a side door, because "first bounce" is ambiguous when a producer bounces a rough idea they will never credit. The safe form is narrow: fire on first bounce **only** when the song has zero captured contributors *and* the session has ≥ 2 confirmed contributors — i.e. only when there is provably something to capture and nothing captured yet. In that narrow form it earns a place in the taxonomy as *conditional*; in any broader form it is rejected. It never overrides D-04 (transport-active suppression) or the per-song de-dup. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The **local agent notification** is the primary surface | Per DT-03 — ~90% of a plugin's value for ~10% of the cost, and it works with every DAW. |
| D-02 | A DAW plugin is **deferred**, not rejected — only if the agent measurably fails to reach producers | Per DT-03 — best surface, disproportionate cost, small marginal gain. |
| D-03 | The prompt is **rare by design**: session close and stage transitions only | Per DT-02 — scarcity is the mechanism, not a limitation. Twenty prompts a session trains dismissal. |
| D-04 | **Never fire during active playback or record** | Interrupting a take gets the agent uninstalled the same day. |
| D-05 | Undelivered prompts **defer**, never disappear | `07.06.02` — the capture is too valuable to drop because nobody was looking. |
| D-06 | Multiple closes **batch** into one prompt | Five notifications is the DT-02 failure in miniature. |
| D-07 | The **prompt-moment taxonomy is locked** (product): session close ✅, stage transition ✅, first-bounce ⚠️ conditional (DT-04), every-bounce / every-save / mid-session ❌ never | Per DT-02/DT-04 — which *classes* of moment prompt is a product decision; the numeric thresholds that separate them are `/write-be-spec` tuning, not product. |
