## Overview

Who is on this song, what they are allowed to touch, and what happens to unreleased audio once they can reach
it. The roster names contributors and their per-song roles; invitation brings outsiders in with a scope that
matches their actual involvement; the asset vault decides which of them can hear or download which asset, and
under what confidentiality terms.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: three capabilities in a tight causal chain — the roster role determines the
invitation scope, which determines the vault grant, which determines what a recipient can leak. Change a role
and access must recompute. They cannot be specified independently.

**Why the roster is not merely admin plumbing**: it is the provenance chain's *subject list*. A credit is a
statement about a person, and the roster is where that person becomes addressable. `07.06.02`'s capture prompt
is only pre-fillable because the roster already knows who was there. A weak roster makes every downstream
capture a blank form — and a blank form gets dismissed.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Roster membership is **per-song**, not per-project | A session player who played on track 4 is not on the album. Project-level membership would give them the whole record — including the unreleased tracks they have no business hearing, and the tracklist that is commercially confidential (`07.01.02`). Per-song is more work and is the only correct granularity. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | **Forensic watermarking is a cross-cut, not a child here** | Watermarking embeds a per-recipient identifier in audio — a mechanism serving 05 (service delivery), 07 (pre-release review), 11 (licensing), 12 (promo distribution) and 14 (digital goods). Its product expression here (leak tracing on a private link) is behavior of `07.05.02`, not a node. Recorded in `../music-projects-collaboration-cx.md` and returned for the global CX file. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | The generic permission engine is a cross-cut; what lives here is the **music-specific gating policy** | "Can user X read resource Y" is platform plumbing routed to `/create-prd`. "A session player hears the rough mix but never the stems, and a mastering engineer gets the stems but not the tracklist" is a product decision that only makes sense in this domain. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-04 | Vault default profiles are **validation-gated**: practitioner validation and an approved version precede v1 enforcement. | Owner ratification for A-04 / `r-53[0]`, 2026-07-20. The music-specific policy remains role-derived and per-song × sensitivity-class; generic authorization remains a cross-cut. | Owner decision, 2026-07-20 |
