## Overview

How feedback on a version gets given, gathered, resolved and finally signed off. Timestamped comments anchored to
the waveform; private links that put audio in front of people who are not on the roster; consolidation of the
five stakeholders who all said something different about the same 12 seconds; a formal sign-off trail; and the
revision-round counter that decides when "one more tweak" becomes billable.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: five capabilities in one loop — a comment lands on a version, gets triaged into
a revision round, produces a new version, and closes with an approval that pins the version it approved. Break
any link and the loop leaks: comments detached from versions are the reason feedback gets applied to the wrong
mix, and approvals detached from versions are why "you approved it" is unprovable.

**Why it matters here**: per the domain rationale, timestamped waveform feedback is "the highest-value
interaction in music collaboration and table stakes." It is also the domain's most credible **beachhead**
candidate (`meta/problem-statement.md` Q-03) — it is the one thing in the domain a user will adopt on day one
without being sold a thesis.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | **Split sweep candidate 19** ("Timestamped Waveform Review, Private Links & Listen Analytics") into `07.05.01` and `07.05.02` | The sweep bundled three things with different shapes. Timestamped review is an *authenticated collaboration* surface for people on the roster. Private links are a *distribution* surface for people who are not — a label A&R, a manager, a sync agent — and they carry an entirely different risk profile (leak vector, watermarking, no account). Listen analytics only exists because of the link. Bundling them would hide the domain's second-largest leak surface inside a comments feature. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | Comments anchor to a **version**, never to "the song" | A comment that outlives the version it was about is worse than no comment: "the vocal is too loud at 1:12" applied to v9 when it was written about v6 causes an actual wrong fix. Version-anchoring is what makes the whole loop honest. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | **Forensic watermarking is a cross-cut**, consumed by `07.05.02` | Sub-domain `07.03` D-02 — the mechanism serves 05, 07, 11, 12, 14. Its product expression here (per-recipient watermarking on a private link so a leak is traceable) is behavior of `07.05.02`, not a node. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
