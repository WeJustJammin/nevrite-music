# Feature: Multi-Stakeholder Feedback Consolidation & Triage

> **Number**: 07.05.03
> **Parent**: [Review, Feedback & Approval](./07.05-review-feedback-approval-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The producer's actual problem is not reading feedback — it is that five people commented on the same 12 seconds
and two of them want opposite things. This feature clusters comments by position and topic, **surfaces the
contradictions**, and lets the producer triage each one into accept / reject / defer. The accepted batch becomes
a revision round (parent CX-03). It is the least glamorous feature in the sub-domain and the one that saves the
most time.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees the consolidated picture — often the first time they realise their manager and their bandmate want opposite things about the chorus. Cannot triage: that is producer work, and a client triaging their own feedback defeats the purpose. Sees what was accepted and what was rejected, with reasons. |
| Producer | Full | Their power surface. Clusters, triages, replies, batches into rounds. This view is where a chaotic comment stream becomes a work list. |
| Operator | None | Not their business. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

## Behavior

Comments from every source — roster members (`07.05.01`) and link recipients (`07.05.02`, parent CX-01) — land
in one stream. Consolidation clusters them by playback position (comments within a few seconds of each other are
probably about the same thing) and, where possible, by topic.

**Contradiction detection is the point, and it must not resolve anything.** When the label says "vocal up" and
the artist says "vocal is too loud" about the same 12 seconds, the system's job is to make that visible and
*stop*. Adjudicating it is a human judgement involving money, power and relationships that no system has any
business making — and getting it wrong once would destroy trust in the whole feature.

Triage state (`accepted`, `rejected`, `deferred`) is owned by the producer. Rejection **must be communicated to
the author with a reason** (parent CX-02): silence makes them repeat the note next round, and it is how clients
come to believe they are being ignored.

### Happy Path

1. v6 gathers 14 comments from the band, the manager and the label over three days
2. Producer opens triage; comments are clustered into 6 topics by position
3. One cluster is flagged: the label wants the vocal up at 1:47, the singer wants it down. **The system says so and does nothing further.**
4. Producer takes it to a human conversation; the artist decides; producer marks the label's note rejected with a reason
5. Producer accepts 9 comments as revision round 2 (parent CX-03) and works from that list

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Two comments contradict at the same position | Flagged as a contradiction; **never auto-resolved** | "These conflict — the label wants the vocal up here, Sam wants it down" |
| A comment is vague ("make it punchier") | No clustering signal, no auto-interpretation. The system must not guess what "punchier" means. | Sits unclustered; producer can ask the author, in thread |
| Comments arrive after the round closed | Go to the next round; author told which round they landed in | "This will be in round 3" |
| Producer rejects a comment silently | **Not possible** — rejection requires a reason and notifies the author | Reason field required |
| 60 comments on one version | Normal for a label record with many stakeholders. Clustering is what makes it survivable. | Clustered view with an "unclustered" bin |
| Contradiction between a **paying client** and a band member | The system has no view on who outranks whom, correctly. It surfaces the roles and stops. | Both shown with their roles/relationship |
| Comments from a link recipient with weak identity | Clustered like any other, but visibly marked | "via link" preserved through consolidation |
| Cluster is wrong (two adjacent comments about different things) | Producer can split/merge clusters manually — the clustering is an assistant, not an authority | Manual override |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Triage opened | Skeleton |
| Empty | No comments | "No feedback yet" |
| Populated | Comments exist | Clusters by position, with counts, authors, contradiction flags |
| Contradiction | Conflicting comments in a cluster | Prominent flag; no suggestion, no resolution |
| Triaged | All comments actioned | Accepted list ready to become a round |
| Partial | Some comments untriaged | Progress indicator |
| Error | Fetch failed | Error with retry |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | **Contradiction detection is the whole feature**, and the sweep's framing ("consolidation & triage") undersells it | Reasoning from what actually costs a producer time. Reading 14 comments takes four minutes. Discovering *on the fourth revision* that the label and the artist have wanted opposite things for two weeks costs days of work and a relationship. Consolidation without contradiction detection is just a sorted list — a UI improvement. Contradiction detection is the thing that changes an outcome. | ✅ CONFIRMED — the defining capability. It also justifies the feature's separate existence rather than being a view inside `07.05.01`: a comment list can be a component; a contradiction surface is a product. |
| DT-02 | The system should **suggest a resolution** for contradictions — it knows the roles, so it could weight the label's note over the drummer's | The natural next step once contradictions are detected, and technically easy | ❌ **REJECTED — emphatically.** A system that adjudicates between a paying label and an artist about their own record has taken a side in a conflict involving money, power and authorship. It would be wrong often, and being wrong once — telling a producer to override an artist's own judgement on their own vocal — would end trust in the feature permanently. It is also exactly the kind of decision `.claude/rules/decision-classification.md` reserves for humans. **The system surfaces, humans decide.** This is the same principle as `07.02.02` DT-01 (attribution is evidence, not arithmetic) applied to feedback: the platform's job is to make the truth visible, not to render verdicts. |
| DT-03 | Clustering by playback position is sufficient | The obvious approach given timestamped comments | ⚠️ **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED** — position clustering is a good cheap signal and catches most of the value, because people genuinely do comment on the same moment. But it misses **structural** feedback: "the whole song drags" and "it's too long" and "cut a chorus" are the same note, live at no shared position, and are the most consequential feedback a record gets. Consequence: triage needs a **non-positional bin** for song-level feedback, treated as first-class rather than as unclustered debris. Notably the most important comment on a mix is often the one with no timestamp at all — which is an argument against a UI that treats the waveform as the only entry point. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | **Contradictions are surfaced, never resolved or suggested** | Per DT-02 — the system has no business adjudicating between an artist and their label, and being wrong once would be unrecoverable. |
| D-02 | Rejection **requires a reason** and notifies the author | Parent CX-02 — silence makes clients repeat notes and believe they are ignored. |
| D-03 | Clustering is an **assistant**, manually overridable | Per DT-03 — the clustering will be wrong sometimes and must never be authoritative. |
| D-04 | Song-level (non-positional) feedback is **first-class**, not unclustered debris | Per DT-03 — "it drags" is often the most consequential note on a record and has no timestamp. |
| D-05 | Triage is producer-only; the Musician sees results, with reasons | A client triaging their own feedback defeats the purpose; but they must see what happened to every note. |
