## Overview

The last mile of making a record: the brief that tells a mix engineer what the artist actually wants, the matrix
of alternate versions every real record needs (instrumental, clean, radio edit, TV mix), the mastering stage with
its loudness targets, and the format-specific and immersive deliverables that a release may or may not require.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: five capabilities in sequence, each constraining the next — a brief sets the
target the mix is judged against; the alternate matrix multiplies every mix decision by the number of variants;
mastering inherits every alternate and must treat them consistently; format masters and Atmos are parallel
deliverables derived from the same approved mix, not from each other. Change the alternate matrix and the
mastering workload changes with it.

**The honest boundary**: WeJammin does not mix and does not master. That work happens in a DAW and in specialist
tools, and the domain's established position (`07.04.03` D-02) is *be the record, not the tool*. What this
sub-domain owns is the **intent, the requirements and the record** around that work — which is where the failures
actually are.

| D-01 | **Mix Recall was re-homed out of this sub-domain** into `07.06.04` | Sweep candidate 24 bundled "Mix Brief, Reference Board & Mix Recall". Brief and recall are opposites: a brief is intent *going in*; a recall is machine and room state *coming out*, made of session documentation. | D-02 | **WeJammin does not build mixing or mastering tools** | Consistent with `07.04.03` D-02. Mixing is a craft with three decades of refined tooling; a browser mixer would be worse in every dimension and would consume the domain's entire build. The platform owns intent, requirements and the record — which is where the failures actually are. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | `07.07.04` and `07.07.05` are kept **separate**, not merged into "deliverable formats" | They look similar and are not. A DDP or vinyl pre-master is a *derivative of the approved stereo master* — same mix, different container and constraints. Atmos is **a different mix entirely**, made in a different session, with its own creative decisions and its own credits. Merging them would model Atmos as a format conversion, which is the single most common misunderstanding about immersive audio and would produce a wrong data model. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
