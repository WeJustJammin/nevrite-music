# Feature: Format-Specific Masters (DDP, vinyl pre-master, Apple Digital Masters)

> **Number**: 07.07.04
> **Parent**: [Mix & Master Workflow](./07.07-mix-master-workflow-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The container-and-constraint deliverables derived from an approved stereo master: a **DDP** image for a CD or
streaming-delivery pipeline, a **vinyl pre-master** with the treatment a cutting lathe requires, an **Apple
Digital Master** meeting Apple's delivery spec. Each is the same master in a different wrapper with different
rules — none of them is a new master (parent CX-03).

> ⚠️ **Weakest feature in the domain, honestly assessed.** Deep specialist deliverables, a tiny population, a
> large spec surface, near-zero provenance value, and entrenched dedicated tools. It is a strong `Won't` candidate
> (sub-domain Q-02) and the Deep Think below argues that case rather than defending the feature.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | None | An artist has no reason to touch a DDP. They care that the vinyl sounds right; they will never open the file. Their entire legitimate interest is "is the vinyl master done?", which is a stage-board fact (`07.01.03`), not this feature. |
| Producer | Full | In practice this is the **mastering engineer** specifically — not producers generally. Uploads the format deliverable, records what it is for, sees the constraint checks. A narrow slice of a narrow population. |
| Operator | None | Not their business. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

## Behavior

A format master is a derivative version (parent CX-03) with a declared purpose and a set of constraints the
platform can check:

- **Vinyl pre-master**: per-side runtime against the cut-quality ceiling (`07.01.02` DT-02), bass mono'd below a crossover, sibilance controlled
- **DDP**: track marks, gaps, ISRCs embedded, checksum
- **Apple Digital Master**: the delivery spec's sample rate/peak/format requirements

The platform does **not** create these (sub-domain D-02) — it receives them, records what they are for, checks
what it can, and keeps them in lineage so that a re-master marks them stale.

The one genuinely valuable behavior is **staleness detection**, and it has real money attached: a format master
sent to a pressing plant while the stereo master changed produces a pressed record that is wrong, and a pressed
record cannot be recalled (parent CX-03).

### Happy Path

1. Master approved (`07.05.04`)
2. Mastering engineer produces a vinyl pre-master in their own tools and uploads it, declaring purpose: vinyl, 2 sides
3. System records it as a derivative of the approved master; checks per-side runtimes against the cut-quality ceiling
4. Sides are 19 and 21 minutes — within tolerance; no flag
5. Delivered to the plant (domain 12); the version is **pinned forever** as what was actually pressed

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Vinyl side exceeds the cut-quality ceiling | The one check with real, non-recoverable consequences — a long side is cut quieter and thinner | "Side B is 26 minutes — cut quality will suffer above ~22" |
| Stereo master re-done after the format master shipped to a plant | **Real-money failure.** The pressing cannot be recalled. The pin (`07.04.02` D-03) records exactly what went | "The plant has a master derived from v7. The canonical master is now v9." |
| DDP uploaded that the platform cannot parse | Stored as bytes; no checks. Honest degradation (`07.04.05` D-05). | "Stored — contents not verified" |
| Format master with no parent (mastering engineer sent only the DDP) | Lineage root; staleness detection unavailable — which removes the feature's only real value | "No parent master — can't detect if this goes stale" |
| Apple's delivery spec changes | Third-party spec drift — same maintenance trap as `07.07.03` DT-03 | Spec must be maintained data with an "as of" date |
| Artist asks to see the DDP | They have no use for it (Role Lens: None) | Not surfaced |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| None | No format masters | Absent — the default for the overwhelming majority of releases |

| Stale | Parent master superseded | Flagged, with the delivered-to warning if it has shipped |
