## Overview

Every bounce, stem, take and master a song accumulates, held in an immutable timeline with lineage — what came
from what, who made it, when, and why. This sub-domain exists to kill `Mix_FINAL_v3_ACTUAL.wav`: an industry-wide
failure that is funny until the wrong master ships, and then costs real money and real relationships.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: six capabilities in continuous interaction — the timeline is what the canonical
resolver resolves *over*; comps are assembled *from* takes and become versions; stem exports are derived versions
that must stay in lineage with the mix they came from; integrity checks gate what may become canonical; and the
A/B player is the only way a human can actually judge two versions. Specify any one alone and it is wrong.

**Why this is not Git for audio**: version control here is not about merging. Audio does not merge — two people's
edits to a mix cannot be reconciled by a three-way diff, and any product that implies otherwise is lying.
What audio needs is *lineage*, *identity* and *an answer to "which one is the real one"*. That is a much smaller
and much more useful problem, and the industry has never solved it.
