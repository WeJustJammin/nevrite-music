## Cross-Cut Details

### CX-01: Song Record ↔ Production Stage Board

**Relationship**: The Song entity holds `current_stage`. The board reads it. Moving a card writes it.
The important direction is the reverse one: a stage transition **fires the capture prompt** appropriate
to that stage (entering `mix` asks "who tracked on this?"; entering `master approved` asks "is the split
final?"). The board is therefore not decoration — it is the trigger surface for provenance capture.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician**: sees their songs' stages; can move a song they own; is *prompted* on transitions where they are a listed contributor.
- **Producer**: moves stages for every song in projects they run; is the primary target of transition prompts (they are the capture point per `meta/personas.md`).
- **Operator**: no visibility — the stage of someone's song is not the studio's business.
- **Fan**: no visibility — unreleased work is private by default.

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: the Song owns `current_stage` — single writer of record. Two people dragging the same card concurrently is last-write-wins with a visible "X moved this to Mix while you were looking" toast. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` on whether a stage transition needs a lock.
2. **Trigger chain**: stage transition → capture prompt → (optionally) credit/split write into domain 02/09. If the prompt is dismissed the transition still commits — **the transition must never be blocked by the prompt**, or users learn to avoid the board. Instead the song accrues a completeness debt (see `07.08.03`).
3. **Permission intersection**: yes — only roster members with a producer-or-owner role can transition a stage (see `07.03.01`).
4. **Notification fan-out**: a transition notifies the roster. `master approved` notifies everyone with a split.
5. **State transition conflict**: backwards transitions are the open race (Q-03) — a song demoted from `master` to `mix` after sign-offs were collected leaves orphaned approvals in `07.05.04`.

### CX-02: Production Stage Board ↔ Release Container

**Relationship**: Release readiness is computed from the board, never typed in. The Release surfaces a
critical-path view: "3 of 11 songs are not at master. Earliest realistic date: the slowest one."

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician**: sees the release blocked-by list; this is often the first honest picture they get of their own timeline.
- **Producer**: sees which of their songs is the blocker and is the one who gets chased.
- **Operator**: none.
- **Fan**: none — the release date is not public until the Musician publishes it (domain 12/21).

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: the Release owns the tracklist; the Songs own their stages. No contested field — readiness is derived, so there is nothing to merge.
2. **Trigger chain**: the last Song hitting `master approved` flips the Release to `ready to deliver` and hands off to domain 12. If domain 12 rejects the package (spec failure), the Release must be able to fall back — `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`.
3. **Permission intersection**: yes — you can be on the roster of one Song and have no visibility of the Release it sits on (a session player should not see the unannounced album).
4. **Notification fan-out**: readiness flips notify the release owner, not the whole roster.
5. **State transition conflict**: a Song demoted after a Release was marked ready must un-ready the Release. This is the same race as CX-01's Q-03.

### CX-03: Song Record ↔ Release Container

**Relationship**: Membership is many-to-many with per-membership state. The same Song on an album and a
deluxe reissue may point at **different master versions** (album master vs remaster) at different sequence
positions. The membership edge — not the Song — holds the sequence number, the selected master, and any
release-specific edit (radio edit, clean version — see `07.07.02`).

**Role scoping**: `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — depends on resolving Q-02.

**Synthesis questions answered**: Medium confidence — deferred to Step 5 per the template's rule for Medium/Low entries.
