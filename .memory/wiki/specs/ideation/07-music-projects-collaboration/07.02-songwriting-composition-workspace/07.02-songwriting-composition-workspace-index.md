## Overview

Where the **work** (composition) is written, as distinct from where the **recording** is made. Holds the
idea inbox that catches a riff before it has a name, the lyric document that records who wrote which line,
and the chord/arrangement chart that defines the song's structure. This sub-domain is the publishing-side
mirror of the domain's thesis: `07.06` captures who *played* on the master, and this captures who *wrote*
the work — two different sets of people, two different splits, and the one the industry gets wrong most often.

**Why sub-domain, not feature**: three capabilities that feed each other continuously — an idea graduates
into a lyric or a chart; the arrangement's section map (`verse 1`, `chorus`) is the spine the lyric document
is organised by, so editing an arrangement re-anchors lyric attribution; and all three emit composition-side
attribution events into the same publishing split.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Composition attribution is **separate from** recording attribution and lives here | Publishing splits (who wrote it) and master splits (who played it) are different people at different percentages. A drummer on the record who wrote nothing gets a master credit and zero publishing. Conflating them is the most common amateur split-sheet error, and the platform's credibility with anyone who has ever been through a PRO registration depends on getting this right on day one. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3; consistent with `07.01.01` DT-01 |
| D-02 | The Producer persona is **Read-only** in the lyric workspace by default | Producers are frequently *not* writers, and a producer with edit access to the lyric document invites the exact anti-persona behavior `meta/personas.md` names: "assigns themselves a larger split than agreed and pushes it through while contributors aren't paying attention." Where the producer genuinely co-writes, the Musician grants writer access explicitly — which is itself an attributable act. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
