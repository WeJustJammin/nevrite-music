## Overview

Money that gets taken off the top before the musician sees it, and money they already took that has to be earned back. A manager's 20%, an agent's 10%, a label advance recouping against royalties, a catalogue stake sold to a fund — all of them are deductions and nettings applied to the income ledger (23.01) before anything reaches a bank account. The order in which they apply is not a detail: whether commission is calculated before or after recoupment changes the number materially, contracts differ on it, and getting it wrong is litigation.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies sub-domain status): underwriting an advance against verified income; representation deductions with their own sunset rules; the netting ledger that sequences everything; runway forecasting over what is left; and catalogue stake sales, which are the same mechanics with securities regulation attached.
