# Feature: Recoupment & Netting Ledger

> **Number**: 23.06.03
> **Parent**: [Advances, Commission & Recoupment](./23.06-advances-commission-recoupment-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The sequencer. When income arrives it may have to recoup an advance, pay a manager, pay an agent, and divert a sold stake — and the **order** in which those apply changes what the musician actually receives. Contracts specify different orders; nobody reads them; the numbers get computed by whoever holds the money and are almost never checked. This feature holds the balances, applies the contract's sequence, and shows the working.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Sees, probably for the first time, the full path from gross to net: what recouped, who took what, in what order, and why. The number they currently receive arrives with no explanation from a party who benefits from it being unexamined. |
| Producer | Full | Points recouping against a production budget, plus their own contributor obligations. They sit on both sides of a netting sequence more often than any other persona. |
| Operator | None | Not visible. No advances, no commission, no stakes — a venue's money arrives and is theirs. Explicit absence (parent Role Matrix). |
| Fan | None | Not visible — no income. |

## Behavior

Every income event passes through a **sequence** derived from contract terms (23.04.02): recoupment positions, commission rules, stake diversions. The sequence is a contract term, not a default — and if it is unspecified, the row **blocks rather than nets** (parent D-02). Balances are append-only; every netting shows its working.

### Happy Path

1. £10,000 royalty income lands; an £8,000 advance is unrecouped; a 20% commission applies.
2. The contract specifies recoup-then-commission.
3. The sequence applies: £8,000 recoups → position clears → 20% of the remaining £2,000 = £400 → net £1,600.
4. The musician sees each step with the clause each rule came from.
5. Had the contract said commission-first, the answer would have been £0 to them — and the platform would have computed *that*, because the contract is the authority, not the platform's preference.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| The sequence is unspecified in the contract | **Blocks.** Netting on a guess is silent money loss and, if wrong, a dispute the platform caused | Unresolved, flagged, with the specific term that is missing |
| Income reverses after it recouped (refund, chargeback) | The position must re-open; the arithmetic must run backwards correctly | Reversing entries, never edits (mirrors 23.01.01 D-01) |
| An advance covers only some income classes | A royalty advance usually does not recoup against session fees — but "usually" is not a specification | Scope per position, from the contract; ambiguity holds the row |
| Two positions compete for the same income | Two advances, or an advance and a stake sale | Priority is a contract term nobody thinks to specify until it matters |
| A commission sunsets mid-sequence (23.04.03) | The sequence changes underneath an in-flight calculation | Time-versioned rules; a netting must pin to the rules in force on the event's date |
| Cross-currency netting (23.01.03) | An advance in USD recouping against EUR income | Gross/deductions/net must convert at one rate/date or the row will not balance (23.01.03 DT-03) |
| The user disputes a netting | They have the working; the counterparty has their own | Evidence, not adjudication — the same posture as 23.04.04 D-01 |
| Position never clears | An advance that outlives the income that justified it | Honest visibility; 23.06.01's core risk made concrete |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Computing | Skeleton |
| Empty | No positions or rules | Feature invisible — most Musicians have neither an advance nor a manager |
| Populated | Positions active | Balances, sequence, per-event working |
| Error | Sequencing failed | Net withheld rather than guessed |
| Partial | Some rules unresolved | Rows held with the missing term named. A held row is honest; a netted-wrong row is a loss |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Order of operations is an implementation detail — pick a sensible default and move on | The engineering instinct, and the one that would do real damage | ❌ REJECTED, and this rejection is the sub-domain's spine (parent D-02). Recoup-then-commission and commission-then-recoup produce materially different money from identical facts. Real contracts specify both. A "sensible default" is the platform silently choosing who gets paid, on a matter the parties negotiated. Unspecified must **block**, not default — one of the few places in this product where refusing to compute is the correct behaviour. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect **"unrecouped" to be the normal state, and the musician not to understand what it means** | Any music lawyer or royalty accountant: most advances never recoup. An artist "earning" royalties against an unrecouped advance receives nothing for years, and frequently believes they are being cheated when they are being netted exactly as agreed. The single most common artist-label conflict is not fraud — it is an artist who does not understand that the money they can see is not money they will get. | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it reframes the feature as **explanation infrastructure, not arithmetic**. Showing the working — "£10k arrived; £8k recouped; here is the clause; you have £6k left to recoup" — defuses a conflict that eats the industry, and it is exactly the reconstruction-from-memory failure in `meta/problem-statement.md` applied to money. It also directly feeds runway (`#CX-03`): a musician who does not know what unrecouped means will forecast on gross and miss rent. |
| DT-03 | Netting is the platform's job, so the platform's computation is the answer | Natural assumption for a system holding both the money and the terms | ❌ REJECTED: the platform is **not a party** to these contracts, and its netting is an opinion about a private agreement it read via extraction. The label's accounting and ours will differ, and theirs is contractual. The honest posture is the same as 23.04.04: compute, show the working, cite the clause, and present disagreement as a **finding** rather than a correction. This is the third feature in the domain to land on "surface, do not adjudicate" (with 23.04.04 and 23.03.03's dispute handling) — a domain-wide invariant worth stating once at the parent level. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Sequence comes from the contract; unspecified **blocks** | Parent D-02, DT-01 — a default is the platform choosing who gets paid |
| D-02 | Append-only with reversing entries; rules pin to the event date | Mirrors 23.01.01 D-01; time-versioned rules are mandatory once sunsets exist |
| D-03 | Show the working, cite the clause, never adjudicate | See DT-02 and DT-03 — explanation is the product |
