# Feature: Cashflow Smoothing & Runway Forecast

> **Number**: 23.06.04
> **Parent**: [Advances, Commission & Recoupment](./23.06-advances-commission-recoupment-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

"Will there be money in March?" — the question that actually keeps working musicians awake, and the one no tool has ever been able to answer because no tool sees the whole picture. The platform knows what is booked (17), invoiced (23.03), recouping (23.06.03) and historically recurring (23.01). That makes a real runway forecast possible: not "you earned £30k last year" but "on what is confirmed, you are covered until March and thin after that."

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The persona's defining financial condition is lumpiness, not poverty — you can earn £30k and still miss rent in a bad quarter. This is the one screen that answers the actual anxiety, using confirmed bookings and invoices rather than a vibe. |
| Producer | Full | Their income is lumpier still — a production fee, then nothing for four months — and they owe contributors out of what arrives, so their runway is net of obligations they cannot defer without damaging relationships. |
| Operator | Read-only | The one child of this sub-domain an Operator can see. Their inventory is perishable — an unsold Tuesday is gone forever (`meta/personas.md`) — so forward booking visibility *is* cashflow for them. Read-only because the financing instruments around it (advances, commission, stakes) do not apply to a room. |
| Fan | None | Not visible — no income, no professional stake. |

## Behavior

Runway projects **net-to-me** (`#CX-03`), never gross: money that recoups never arrives. It composes confirmed future income (bookings, issued invoices, scheduled royalties) with historical recurrence, and it is explicit about which parts are confirmed and which are estimated.

### Happy Path

1. Musician opens runway in November.
2. It composes: 6 confirmed shows (17), 2 issued invoices (23.03), a recurring teaching block, an expected royalty period (10) — all net of an unrecouped advance and a 20% commission (23.06.03).
3. It shows: covered through February, a gap in March, thin through April.
4. The gap is visible **four months** before it arrives, when it can still be filled with work.
5. They take a March session they would otherwise have declined.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Forecasting on **gross** while an advance is unrecouped | Tells someone they are fine when they will receive nothing (`#CX-03`) | Net-to-me only. A gross runway is not a simplification, it is a lie |
| Off-platform income invisible to the platform | The forecast is systematically pessimistic; a musician with half their work off-platform sees a runway that says they are in trouble when they are not | The structural limit. Must be explicit about coverage or it will be disbelieved — and once disbelieved, never reopened |
| Confirmed income does not arrive (late payment, cancellation) | Confirmed ≠ received. The gap between them *is* the receivables problem (23.03.03) | Confirmed and received distinguished; a slow payer moves the runway |
| Expenses are not modelled | A runway of income with no costs is half a picture — a tour that grosses well can lose money | Must compose with 23.02.01 and 23.07; income-only runway would mislead |
| The forecast is wrong and someone relies on it | They declined work, or did not | Confidence must be visible; the honest output is a range, not a line |
| The runway says "you are fine" and they are not | Silent false comfort — the worst failure available here | Bias toward showing uncertainty rather than a reassuring point estimate |
| The runway shows a crisis | See DT-03 — this is where the feature stops being arithmetic and starts being about someone's life | Tone; and what the product does next is a values decision |
| The user's rent and personal costs are unknown | The platform sees business income, not household outgoings | A runway without outgoings measures the wrong thing. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — asking a musician to enter their rent is a very different product |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Forecasting | Skeleton |
| Empty | No confirmed future income | The most alarming empty state in the product. Must be honest but not cruel — "nothing confirmed ahead" is a fact, not a verdict |
| Populated | Forecast available | Range with confirmed vs estimated bands |
| Error | Forecast failed | Nothing rather than a wrong reassurance |
| Partial | Some sources unseen | Explicit coverage statement — "based on what we can see" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Musicians need to see their income; a runway is a nicer income chart | The dashboard framing | ❌ REJECTED: totals are backward-looking and answer a question nobody is asking. The question is "will there be money in March", and it is answerable **only** by a party that sees confirmed forward commitments across every income type — which is precisely what fragmentation prevents (`meta/problem-statement.md`) and what consolidation uniquely enables. This is arguably the clearest single expression of the consolidation half of the thesis in the entire domain: it is not a better version of an existing tool, it is a question no existing tool can answer at all. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect **the gap, not the total, to be the output** — and gaps are fillable if seen early | Any working musician or their manager: a four-month-out gap is a booking problem (take the session, chase the tour, add teaching); a two-week-out gap is a crisis with only bad options (an advance at a bad rate, a sold stake, borrowing). The value is entirely in **lead time**, and the platform is the only party that could ever provide it. | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it connects the whole domain: an early gap routes to **work** (04 Opportunities, 05 Services, 17 Booking) — a gap seen late routes to **debt** (23.06.01). The same feature either prevents the advance or precipitates it, depending purely on when it is surfaced. That makes lead time the design's primary quantity, and it means the runway is the natural place to answer 23.06.01's Q-02 ("does the platform ever say do not take this?") with "you do not need it; take this session instead." |
| DT-03 | A runway warning is a helpful notification | The obvious framing, and badly wrong in tone | ❌ REJECTED: "you will run out of money in March" is not a notification, it is news about someone's life, delivered by a piece of software, possibly on a phone at a loading dock. The persona is not an enterprise with a treasury function — it is a person whose livelihood is being forecast. This is the same duty-of-care territory that made 23.09 Wellbeing a `wont` proposal, and it arrives here through the front door instead. The feature must be opt-in, never gamified, never cheerful, and must not nag — and it must be paired with the route to action (DT-02) rather than delivering a verdict and walking away. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Net-to-me only; never gross | See `#CX-03` — a gross runway is a lie, not a simplification |
| D-02 | The gap and its lead time are the output, not the total | See DT-02 — early gaps route to work, late gaps route to debt |
| D-03 | Opt-in, never gamified, never nagging; paired with a route to action | See DT-03 — this is news about someone's life |
| D-04 | Ranges with visible confidence, never a reassuring point estimate | False comfort is the worst failure available here |
