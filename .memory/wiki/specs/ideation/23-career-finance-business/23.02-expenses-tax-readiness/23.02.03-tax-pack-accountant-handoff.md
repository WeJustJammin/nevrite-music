# Feature: Tax Pack Generation & Accountant Handoff

> **Number**: 23.02.03
> **Parent**: [Expenses & Tax Readiness](./23.02-expenses-tax-readiness-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The artefact that turns a year of platform activity into something an accountant can work from in an hour instead of a fortnight — income by source, expenses by category, foreign tax credits, asset additions, and an explicit list of everything the platform could not resolve. The domain rationale claims "the tax pack largely generates itself", and that is true precisely because the platform held the money (23.01) and a large share of the spend (23.02.01) as it happened. The pack does not compute a return; it assembles evidence.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Generates the pack for a tax year, reviews the blocker list, and hands it to an accountant via a scoped delegated grant. For a portfolio earner with ten payers this replaces the January reconstruction from bank statements and memory — the same reconstruction failure the platform exists to prevent, in its financial form. |
| Producer | Full | Same artefact. Their pack carries a heavy contractor-payments section (fees paid to contributors), which is both their biggest deduction and, in some jurisdictions, a reporting obligation. |
| Operator | Reports | Export into their existing books rather than a pack for filing — a venue is a company with a corporation-tax return this sub-domain does not attempt (D-02). Their honest need is a clean feed to Xero/QuickBooks, not an accountant handoff. |
| Fan | None | Not visible — no professional stake, no return. |

## Behavior

The pack is an **issued, immutable snapshot** for a period — the same posture as 23.01.04, and for the same reason: a document handed to a third party must not silently change afterwards. Generation is gated on completeness: unresolved gaps block a "final" pack rather than being quietly averaged away.

### Happy Path

1. Musician opens the tax pack for the closed year.
2. System assembles: income by source (verified/declared segregated), expenses by category, foreign tax withheld as credits (`#CX-03`), asset additions, unresolved items.
3. Three blockers show: 2 uncategorized receipts, 1 unadjudicated duplicate from an import.
4. Musician clears them; the pack becomes issuable.
5. They grant their accountant scoped, period-limited access. The accountant works from the pack, asks two questions instead of forty.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Uncategorized expenses / unresolved FX / unadjudicated import duplicates | **Blocks** a final pack | Named blockers with a path to fix each — not a warning banner over a confident total |
| Income arrives after handoff (a late royalty line, a settlement correction) | Snapshot is immutable; a superseding pack is issued | Both user **and** accountant notified — the accountant may already have filed |
| The year is already filed when a correction lands | Product cannot fix this; it can only surface it honestly | "This year has changed since you filed" — an amendment is the user's decision, not the platform's |
| Accountant needs raw data, not a pack | Export must include the underlying rows; no accountant trusts a summary they cannot drill into | Raw export alongside the pack |
| User's jurisdiction is unsupported (23.02.02) | No pack — raw export only | Honest refusal rather than a generic pack that looks filing-ready |
| Band entity income/expenses mixed into a personal pack | Entity separation must hold end-to-end | Separate packs per entity |
| Accountant access outlives the engagement | Grants must be revocable and time-bounded | Grant list with revoke |
| Platform's own 1099-K/DAC7 for the user disagrees with the ledger | Real and serious — the authority has the platform's figure, the user files ours | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`; see parent Q-02. A mismatch here is an audit trigger |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Assembling | Progress; assembly spans several sub-domains and is not instant |
| Empty | No activity in the period | Honest — nothing to pack |
| Populated | Pack assembled, no blockers | Preview + issue action |
| Error | Assembly failed | Retry; never a partial financial artefact |
| Partial | Blockers present | **Blocked from issuance**, blockers enumerated. A pack over unreconciled data is a wrong number wearing an official-looking hat |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | WeJammin should file the return — that is the real product | The ambitious framing, and D-03 says coverage is preferred over minimalism | ❌ REJECTED: filing is a **regulated activity** in most jurisdictions, carries direct liability for a wrong figure, and is an unbounded per-jurisdiction integration (each authority, each API, each annual change). The pack captures most of the value at a fraction of the exposure. Recorded as D-03 on the parent index. This is the rare place where the maximal-ambition directive (D-03 global) should lose to a hard external constraint — and the constraint is licensing, not effort, so the cost-blind rule does not apply. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect the pack's most valuable section to be the **exceptions list**, not the totals | Any accountant: totals are easy and untrustworthy; what costs them time is finding what is missing, miscoded or contradictory. A pack that says "these 6 things are unresolved and here is why" is worth more than one that presents a clean, confident, wrong total. Musicians' returns are exception-dense by nature — mixed-use assets, foreign withholding, lumpy income. | ✅ CONFIRMED: the blocker/exception list is a headline section, and blockers **gate** issuance rather than decorate it. Inverts the usual design instinct of hiding the mess. |
| DT-03 | Accountant handoff is a PDF email | Convenience assumption | ❌ REJECTED: it is a **scoped, revocable, period-limited delegated grant** — the clearest concrete use of the Representation & Delegated Access cross-cut anywhere in the domain, and the one that proves the cross-cut is real rather than theoretical. An accountant needs to drill into rows, ask questions against specific items, and re-categorize (`#CX-01`) — none of which a PDF supports. This also means the accountant is a **user of WeJammin who is not one of the four personas**, which is exactly why representation must be modelled as delegation rather than as a persona (`meta/personas.md` D-19 admits no fifth persona). |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Assembles **23.01** (income side, with verified/declared segregation and FX projections) and **23.02.01/23.02.02** (expense side) — see `23.02-expenses-tax-readiness-cx.md#CX-02`.
- Consumes **23.02.04 Cross-Border Withholding** as tax **credits**, not expenses — see `#CX-03`. The distinction is worth real money.
- Depends on the **Representation & Delegated Access** cross-cut — see DT-03. Without it there is no handoff, only an export.
- Touches **23.07 Budgeting & Project/Tour P&L** — same underlying rows, different cut (management vs statutory view).
- Touches **15 Gear Registry & Ownership** — asset additions section.
- Blocked by unresolved state in **23.01.02** (import duplicates) and **23.01.03** (FX) — this feature is where every upstream shortcut in the domain finally becomes visible.
