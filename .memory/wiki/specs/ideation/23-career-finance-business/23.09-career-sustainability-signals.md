# Feature: Career Sustainability Signals

> **Number**: 23.09
> **Parent**: [Career, Finance & Business Management](./career-finance-business-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The sweep's candidate 15, "Wellbeing & Career Sustainability", classified honestly rather than generously. The defensible core is narrow: the platform can see tour load (18), session load (07), and income volatility (23.01), so it *could* observe that someone has 21 shows in 23 days with no day off, or that their income has dropped 40% quarter on quarter. That is a real observation from data nobody else holds. Everything beyond it — mental-health resources, burnout scores, wellbeing content — is a different product with duties this platform is not equipped to carry.

**Proposed `wont`.** Recorded rather than dropped so the owner cuts it knowingly, and because the reasoning matters more than the feature: the analysis below is why it should not be built, not why it should.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Would see load and volatility signals derived from their own activity — nothing self-reported, nothing scored. The persona whose income is a portfolio of small streams is also the one most exposed to both overwork and sudden collapse, and they are the only persona for whom this is even arguable. |
| Producer | Full | Session load and income volatility. Their overwork is less visible than a touring musician's (nobody sees the 3am mix) and their income gaps are longer. |
| Operator | None | Not visible. A venue's sustainability is a business question — occupancy, margin, cashflow — already answered by 23.07 and 23.06.04. Framing a room's finances as "wellbeing" would be a category error. |
| Fan | None | Not visible — no professional stake, no load, no income. |

## Behavior

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — deliberately unspecified. Given the `wont` proposal and the special-category-data question (Q-01), specifying behaviour now would be inventing detail for a feature whose existence is doubtful and whose shape is dictated by a legal question that has not been asked yet. What is known: any version must be **derived from platform activity only**, never self-reported, or it becomes health data by construction.

### Happy Path

1. The platform observes 21 shows in 23 days across a confirmed tour (18).
2. It notes the load, factually, without a score and without a diagnosis.
3. The observation is available to the musician if they want it.
4. Nothing is inferred about their state of mind, and nothing is stored about their health.
5. That is the whole feature — and its thinness is the point.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| The signal implies a health inference | "You appear to be burning out" is a **health inference from behavioural data** — GDPR Art. 9 special-category territory whether or not anyone typed a symptom | The line: observe load, never infer state. Crossing it changes the platform's data classification wholesale |
| Self-reported wellbeing input is added | The moment a user types how they feel, WeJammin processes health data | Hard no. This is the boundary that keeps the feature legal |
| The signal is right and the user is in crisis | The platform has surfaced a serious problem and has no capability, duty framework or staff to respond | **Duty of care with no ability to discharge it.** The strongest argument for `wont` |
| The signal is wrong | 21 shows in 23 days is normal and fine for many working musicians — it is the job, not a symptom | A signal that pathologises ordinary professional life is insulting and useless |
| Income volatility flagged as a risk | Already covered, better, by 23.06.04 runway — with a route to action (work) rather than a diagnosis | Redundant. The useful half of this feature already exists elsewhere |
| Signposting to mental-health resources | A directory the platform cannot vet, maintain or take responsibility for | Out of scope; arguably 03 Community's, if anyone's |
| An insurer, funder or booker infers state from load data | Behavioural data becomes a health proxy about a named person | The reason this data must never leave the user's own view |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The platform's unique data (tour load, session load, income volatility) makes a genuine wellbeing product possible | The candidate's implicit promise, and it is seductive because the data really is there | ❌ REJECTED: the data supports **observation**, not the product. Load and volatility are facts; wellbeing is a state, and the step from one to the other is an inference about a person's health from their behaviour. That step is where the value supposedly is, and it is exactly the step the platform must not take. What remains once the inference is removed is a fact the musician already knows better than anyone — they were at the 21 shows. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect the **money half to be the wellbeing half** — and it already exists elsewhere | Any working musician, and any charity in this space (Help Musicians, MusiCares): the dominant stressor in a music career is financial precarity, not workload. The thing that keeps people awake is "will there be money in March" — which is **23.06.04 runway**, a feature with a route to action (find work) rather than a diagnosis. The load half is the job; the money half is the fear. | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it dissolves the feature: the useful, defensible, *actionable* version of "career sustainability" is already built as 23.06.04, and it is better precisely because it does not mention wellbeing. This is the strongest argument for `wont` — not that the need is unreal, but that the domain already serves it under a name that comes with an answer instead of a verdict. Note 23.06.04 DT-03 arrives at the same duty-of-care problem through the front door, which suggests the concern is real and belongs *there*, handled as tone. |
| DT-03 | Recording this as `wont` with reasons is worth more than building it | Meta-judgement on the candidate | ✅ CONFIRMED: the candidate is a well-intentioned idea that would create a special-category data classification, a duty of care the platform cannot discharge, and a redundancy with 23.06.04 — while producing something musicians would find patronising. It is worth having on the record so that when someone proposes "burnout tracking" in year two, the analysis already exists. `meta/personas.md` says the Musician's constraint is that their roles are simultaneous and their income cannot support six subscriptions; nothing in the persona research says they want a platform to have opinions about their mental state. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Would read **18 Show Production & Touring** (tour load), **07 Music Projects & Collaboration** (session load), **23.01 Income Aggregation** (volatility).
- **Superseded by 23.06.04 Cashflow Smoothing & Runway** — see DT-02. The actionable half already exists there, with a route to work instead of a diagnosis.
- The resource/signposting half, if it belongs anywhere, is **03 Community & Networking** — not a finance domain.
- Touches `security-first` and **24 Trust, Safety & Disputes** — health inference from behavioural data is a special-category classification with platform-wide consequences.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
