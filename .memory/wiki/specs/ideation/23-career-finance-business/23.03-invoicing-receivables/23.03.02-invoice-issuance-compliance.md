# Feature: Invoice Issuance & Compliance Fields

> **Number**: 23.03.02
> **Parent**: [Invoicing & Receivables](./23.03-invoicing-receivables-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-18

## Overview

Issue an invoice for off-platform work that is actually a valid invoice: sequentially numbered, carrying the tax identifiers the jurisdiction requires, with terms and a due date that make the debt chaseable. Musicians typically send a number in an email and then wonder why a label's accounts payable ignored it for 90 days — often because it was never a document their system could process. The unglamorous compliance fields are what make the invoice payable, and the terms set here are what make 23.03.03 possible at all.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Issues to labels, promoters, function clients, students. Their invoices are small and frequent; their compliance knowledge is near zero, which is exactly why their invoices get rejected or ignored by real accounts-payable systems. The platform knowing their tax status (23.02.02) means the fields fill themselves. |
| Producer | Full | Issues larger invoices to labels and artists, often milestone-based against a production agreement. More likely to be VAT-registered, more likely to be invoicing a company with a real AP process and a PO requirement they have never heard of. |
| Operator | Full | Issues for room hire and settlement shortfalls, and is also the archetypal **recipient** of invoices from acts. Their business is company-shaped and their invoicing is routine — they are the persona most likely to already have a tool, and the least likely to switch for this alone. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. A fan receives a **receipt** from a purchase (13/14/19/20), never an invoice. Different artefact, different domain, no overlap. |

## Behavior

An issued invoice is **immutable and sequentially numbered** — some jurisdictions mandate both, and a gap or a re-used number is a real problem, not a cosmetic one. Corrections are credit notes, never edits. Fields are pre-filled from the user's tax profile (23.02.02) and the accepted quote (`#CX-01`).

### Happy Path

1. Session is finished; Producer opens the accepted quote and converts it.
2. Invoice pre-fills: parties, scope from the quote, amount, tax treatment from the user's profile, next sequential number, terms (30 days), due date.
3. They add the label's PO number — the field exists because AP systems reject invoices without one.
4. Invoice issues as a link plus a PDF the recipient's AP system can actually process.
5. The due date starts the aging clock (`#CX-02`); if it passes, the ladder starts.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Invoice needs correcting after issue | Credit note + re-issue; never an edit | Correction path; sequence integrity preserved |
| Sequential numbering gaps (a deleted draft, a cancelled invoice) | Drafts must not consume numbers; cancellations leave a documented void | Numbering is assigned at issuance only |
| User is VAT/GST registered mid-year | Treatment changes prospectively; historic invoices do not move | Prospective only |
| Recipient's AP requires a PO the musician was never given | Extremely common cause of non-payment, and invisible to the musician — they think they are being ignored | PO field prompted; the ladder (23.03.03) should suggest it as a probable cause |
| Reverse charge / cross-border B2B VAT | Jurisdiction-specific and genuinely hard | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — depends on 23.02.02's jurisdiction rules |
| Invoicing as a **Band entity** | The band invoices; the band is paid; distribution is 23.07.03 | Entity context; the band's tax identity, not the member's |
| Invoice for work already paid through WeJammin | Should be impossible — parent D-02 | Blocked with an explanation |
| Recipient never opens it | Delivery, not payment, is the first failure. The musician assumes the debt is live | Delivery/open tracking so the ladder can distinguish "ignored" from "never arrived" |
| Withholding applies (a foreign payer) | The invoice must anticipate it or the payment will short-pay and look like a partial | Ties to 23.02.04; a short payment that is actually withholding must not be chased as a shortfall |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Issuing | Skeleton; number assigned only on success |
| Empty | No invoices | Explanation of the off-platform boundary |
| Populated | Invoices exist | List by status: draft, issued, viewed, part-paid, paid, overdue, disputed, written-off |
| Error | Issuance failed | Draft preserved, **no number consumed**. A number burned by a failed issue is a compliance gap |
| Partial | Part-paid | Balance tracked; the ladder chases the remainder, not the whole |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a PDF generator with a logo on it | The obvious framing, and what most invoicing tools actually are | ❌ REJECTED: an invoice is a **legally-shaped document** in most jurisdictions — sequential numbering, mandatory identifiers, retention, immutability. Treating it as a template means shipping invoices that AP systems reject and tax authorities do not accept. The platform's edge is that it already knows the user's tax profile (23.02.02), the counterparty, and the agreed scope (the quote) — so the compliant document assembles itself, which is the same "byproduct not admin" pattern as the credits thesis. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect **non-payment to be an AP-process failure, not a refusal to pay** | Anyone who has invoiced a label or a local authority: the invoice sat unpaid because it had no PO, went to the wrong address, or missed a payment run — not because anyone decided not to pay. Musicians read silence as hostility and chase emotionally (or, more often, not at all). The mechanical causes are knowable and mostly preventable at issuance. | ✅ CONFIRMED: PO capture, delivery tracking and correct-recipient routing belong at issuance, and the dunning ladder (23.03.03) should surface probable mechanical causes before escalating tone. This reframes half of the "chasing" problem as an **issuance** problem — the boring feature is where the money is. |
| DT-03 | Immutability and numbering are gold-plating for a musician invoicing £200 | Scope-cutting instinct — and it is defensible for the smallest cases | ⏳ PARTIALLY CONFIRMED: for a UK sole trader below the VAT threshold, invoice formalism genuinely is light-touch. But the same feature serves a VAT-registered Producer invoicing a label and an Operator invoicing companies, where it is not optional. Building the loose version first means rebuilding it, and **migrating historic invoices into a numbering scheme retroactively is not possible** — a sequence cannot be invented backwards. Recorded as parent Q-03; this is a decision that cannot be deferred cheaply, which is unusual and worth flagging. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Issued invoices are immutable; corrections are credit notes | Sequence integrity and evidential value both depend on it |
| D-02 | Numbers are assigned at issuance only, never to drafts | A burned number is a gap that must be explained to an authority |
| D-03 | PO capture and delivery tracking are first-class at issuance | See DT-02 — most non-payment is mechanical, and mechanical causes are preventable here |
