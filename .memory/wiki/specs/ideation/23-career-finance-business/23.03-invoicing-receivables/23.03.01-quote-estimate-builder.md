# Feature: Quote & Estimate Builder

> **Number**: 23.03.01
> **Parent**: [Invoicing & Receivables](./23.03-invoicing-receivables-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

A quote for work that will happen off-platform: a function, a label session, a mix, a teaching block, a room hire. Its value is not the document — anyone can write a number in an email — but that it **pins the scope before the work starts**, so the invoice afterwards has something to bill against and the argument about the extra day has an artefact instead of two memories.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Quotes a function band fee, a session day rate, a depping gig. Small, frequent, and currently sent as a WhatsApp message with a number in it. Their scope creep is constant and never re-papered — the extra hour, the extra song, the "can you also drive the PA". |
| Producer | Full | Quotes a mix or a production. Their scope variable is **revisions**, and "unlimited revisions" is a quote that cannot be honestly invoiced. Their quotes are larger, fewer, and more likely to involve a deposit and a milestone structure. |
| Operator | Full | Quotes room hire with a deposit and a cancellation policy attached — their quote is closer to a contract than the others', because their inventory is perishable (an unsold Tuesday is gone) and a no-show costs them the slot. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. A fan buys at a price; they never negotiate a scope. No quoting surface should ever appear in a Fan context. |

## Behavior

A quote is an **offer**: immutable once sent, versioned if it changes, with an expiry. It records scope in terms the invoice can later reference, and it must work when the recipient is **not a WeJammin user** — most off-platform counterparties are not here, which constrains delivery, acceptance and identity throughout.

### Happy Path

1. Musician is asked to play a wedding; the client is not on WeJammin.
2. They build a quote: fee, what is included (hours, sets, PA), what is not, deposit terms, expiry.
3. It goes out as a link the client can open without an account.
4. Client accepts; the acceptance is recorded with a timestamp — the fact exists rather than being remembered.
5. After the show the invoice (23.03.02) pre-fills from the accepted quote version, and any excess is explicit.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Client accepts verbally, never clicks | The common case. The artefact is unaccepted while the work proceeds | Manual "mark accepted" with a note — the platform must not pretend the click is the only reality |
| Scope grows during the work | The quote is immutable; a new version or a documented variation is needed | Variation path; the delta is attributable at invoice time (`#CX-01`) |
| Quote expires while the work is underway | Awkward and common — the artefact says no, the reality says yes | Expiry is soft: flagged, not enforced. Enforcing it would break the actual workflow |
| Client is not a WeJammin user | Must work anyway — link-based view and acceptance, no account required | No signup wall. A signup wall on a quote loses the user the gig |
| Recipient forwards the link to someone else | Acceptance identity is weak by construction | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — how much identity assurance an acceptance needs is unresolved |
| Work is actually **on-platform** (a service in 05) | Wrong tool — 05 owns that flow (parent D-03) | Redirect to the 05 booking flow rather than duplicating it |
| Client counter-offers | Negotiation is a real step, not an edge case | Versioning covers it; whether threaded negotiation belongs here is Q-02 |
| Quote for a **Band entity** | The band quotes, not the member | Entity context explicit |

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The quote's job is to look professional and win the work | The obvious framing — every quoting tool sells templates and branding | ❌ REJECTED: its job is to **pin scope so the invoice is defensible**. Musicians already win work on relationships and reputation, not on document design; what they lose is the argument afterwards about what was included. Reframing quote-as-evidence rather than quote-as-sales-collateral is the same move the platform makes with credits: capture the fact while everyone is still friendly (`meta/personas.md`, Producer's "moment that matters"). |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect the **deposit** to be the whole point for the Operator, and largely absent for the Musician | Any venue or studio operator: a deposit is how you survive no-shows on perishable inventory (`meta/personas.md` — an unsold Tuesday is gone forever). Musicians almost never take deposits, which is exactly why they eat cancellations for free — the function cancelled two days out costs them the date and they have no recourse. | ✅ CONFIRMED: deposit terms are a first-class part of a quote, and surfacing them to the Musician persona is a **behaviour change the platform can cause**, not just a field it can store. Requires the collection question (parent Q-02) to be answered — a deposit you cannot collect is a sentence in a PDF. |
| DT-03 | Non-user recipients are an edge case to handle later | Platform-centric assumption | ❌ REJECTED: they are the **majority case by definition**. This sub-domain exists for off-platform work (D-02), and off-platform work is mostly for people who are not on WeJammin. Every artefact here must be usable by a stranger with a link and no account. This also makes quotes a **growth surface** — the wedding client, the label A&R, the promoter all touch WeJammin without signing up. That is a distribution channel the domain map does not credit this domain with. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A sent quote is immutable; changes are versions | It is an offer. An editable offer is not evidence of anything |
| D-02 | Must work for non-users via link, with no signup wall | See DT-03 — the majority case, and a growth surface |
| D-03 | Expiry is soft — flagged, not enforced | See edge cases; enforcing it would break the real workflow |
