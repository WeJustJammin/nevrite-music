## Overview

Getting paid for the work that did **not** go through WeJammin's own checkout. On-platform work is paid by the payments/escrow cross-cut and needs no invoice; the rest of a musician's income — the label session billed directly, the function band's fee, the teaching block, the venue that pays 60 days after the show — arrives only if someone raises an invoice and then chases it. Chasing is the part musicians will not do, because the person who owes them money is usually someone they want to work with again.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies sub-domain status): quoting (a pre-work artefact that converts), invoice issuance (a legally-shaped document with compliance fields), and the dunning ladder plus receivables aging (an escalating, relationship-aware chase). Quotes become invoices; invoices become aged debt; the ladder is what closes the loop back to the ledger.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Classified as sub-domain, not feature | Quote, issuance and dunning are three capabilities with distinct artefacts, distinct legal properties and distinct failure modes, chained by conversion. A quote is a sales artefact; an invoice is a legally-constrained document; dunning is a relationship-managed process. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 — candidate 04 |
| D-02 | Scope is **off-platform work**; on-platform work is paid by the payments/escrow cross-cut and never invoiced | Invoicing work WeJammin already collected for would duplicate the checkout and confuse who is owed what. The boundary is: did the money move through WeJammin? If yes, no invoice exists. | Agent, boundary analysis vs domain 05 |
| D-03 | Quoting for **on-platform services** belongs to 05 Services Marketplace, not here | 05 owns service listings, rates and the booking flow. A quote here is for work that will never touch that flow. Overlap flagged as Q-01 because the line is thinner than it looks. | Agent, boundary analysis |

## Open Questions

| # | Question | Owner | Deferred To |
|---|----------|-------|-------------|
| Q-01 | Where exactly is the boundary with **05 Services Marketplace**? 05 has quotes, rates and bookings for on-platform work; this sub-domain has quotes and invoices for off-platform work. Two quote builders is a duplication smell — but merging them means 05 owns money for work it never brokered. D-02/D-03 state the *test* (did the money move through WeJammin?); what is open is whether the two quote instruments are one component or two, which is a component-boundary call. | User | `/create-prd-architecture` |
| Q-02 | **[OWNER]** Does WeJammin offer to **collect** off-platform invoices (payment link on the invoice), or only produce the document? Collecting converts off-platform income to platform-verified income (23.01.02's whole problem) and earns fees — but it makes the platform a payment intermediary for work it did not broker, with the fraud and chargeback exposure that follows. This is the highest-leverage open question in the sub-domain, and nothing downstream resolves it: it is a scope-and-liability posture only the owner can set (see also domain Q-03 and INV-04's regulated-role question, domain Q-04). | User | `/create-prd` |
| Q-03 | Is an issued invoice a legal document WeJammin has any duty around (sequential numbering, retention, immutability)? Some jurisdictions mandate all three. If so, "just a PDF generator" is the wrong mental model. | User | `/create-prd-security` |
