## Overview

The paper that governs a career: management agreements, agency deals, label and publishing contracts, production agreements, endorsement and sponsorship deals. Musicians sign these young, store them nowhere, and discover their terms years later when a term matters — an option that lapsed, a reversion window that opened and closed unnoticed, a commission that should have stopped at the end of a sunset clause and did not. The vault holds the documents, extracts the terms that have **dates attached**, and shouts before those dates pass.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies sub-domain status): document storage; extraction of key terms into a structured deal summary; date-driven alerting on options, reversions and obligations; and reconciliation of what the paper says against what the platform's own rights (09) and money (23.01) records say.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Classified as sub-domain, not feature | Storage, extraction, date-alerting and reconciliation are four capabilities that chain: extraction populates alerts; alerts and reconciliation both read the structured terms extraction produces. A vault without extraction is a folder; extraction without alerts is a summary nobody reads. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 — candidate 05 |
| D-02 | Scope is **business contracts**, not split sheets or licences | Split sheets and ownership records belong to 09 Rights & Ownership; sync/master licences belong to 11 Music Licensing. This vault holds the agreements that govern the *person's business* — management, agency, label, publishing, production, endorsement. The boundary matters because 09 and 11 already own their paper, and duplicating it would create two truths about the same rights. | Agent, boundary analysis vs 09/11 |
| D-03 | Generic document storage and e-signature are **cross-cuts**, not features of this vault | Riders (18), split sheets (09), licences (11), receipts (23.02.01) and contracts (23.04) all need storage, versioning, e-signature and retention. Building that here would be the fifth copy. What is domain-owned is the **deal semantics** — option windows, reversion dates, sunset clauses — not the filing cabinet. | Agent, Node Classification Gate cross-cut test |
