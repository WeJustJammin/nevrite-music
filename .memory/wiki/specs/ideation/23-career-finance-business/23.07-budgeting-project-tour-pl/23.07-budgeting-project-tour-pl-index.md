## Overview

Did that tour make money? Did that record? Nobody knows — the income arrived in six places over eighteen months and the costs were paid by four different people out of whatever they had. This sub-domain scopes money to a **thing** (a tour, a record, a project) rather than to a year, which is how musicians actually think about their work and how no accounting tool ever models it. It then answers the question that follows and that ends more bands than creative differences: who gets what out of what is left.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies sub-domain status): budgets set before the work; actuals accruing from platform activity during it; the P&L that closes it; and the distribution of whatever remains to the humans behind the entity that earned it.
