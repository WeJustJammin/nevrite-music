## Overview

Did that tour make money? Did that record? Nobody knows — the income arrived in six places over eighteen months and the costs were paid by four different people out of whatever they had. This sub-domain scopes money to a **thing** (a tour, a record, a project) rather than to a year, which is how musicians actually think about their work and how no accounting tool ever models it. It then answers the question that follows and that ends more bands than creative differences: who gets what out of what is left.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies sub-domain status): budgets set before the work; actuals accruing from platform activity during it; the P&L that closes it; and the distribution of whatever remains to the humans behind the entity that earned it.

| D-02 | **Band Entity Treasury & Member Distribution added — not in the candidate list** | `meta/personas.md` states bands are first-class entities that "book, sign, own and get paid independently of their members". A band that earns therefore has money, and that money has to reach humans — but the candidate sweep gave that no home anywhere in 23. The gap is structural: the sweep's provisional personas included "band treasurer", which D-19 then removed as a persona without relocating the capability. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | Partnership distribution is **not** a royalty split | Domain 09 owns splits in *works* (who wrote what). This is a partnership distributing *trading profit* (a tour's takings, net of costs, between five members) — different basis, different maths, different disputes. Conflating them would put touring income into the rights ledger. | Agent, boundary analysis vs 09 |
