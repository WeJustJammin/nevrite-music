## Overview

Running the business of a music career — total income across every lumpy source, expenses and tax readiness, invoicing, the deal and contract vault, goals and milestones, benchmarking, advances and insurance.

**Why this is a top-level domain**: idea.md names 'Career Planning: goal setting, milestone tracking' — which as described is a generic goal tracker nobody opens twice. Merged with finance because that is what makes it real: a goal auto-updating from platform activity survives; a manually maintained one dies in a fortnight. The financial half is the strongest under-served need in the corpus — a working musician's income arrives from ten unrelated payers on different schedules in different currencies, and no bank, accountant or fintech can see it; royalty-advance companies underwrite streaming royalties only, serving the top few percent. Because this platform would hold settlements, session payments, marketplace income, teaching income and royalty statements, it can compute the total picture nobody else can. Financial identity is the deepest possible earned lock-in, and the tax pack largely generates itself. Acknowledged: splitting gives one strong domain (Finance) and one thin one (Career); the merge is what keeps the thin half alive.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies domain status):

- unified income aggregation & financial identity
- expenses & tax readiness
- invoicing & payment chasing
- goals, milestones & cohort benchmarking
- deal/contract vault with option alerts
- advances, insurance & budgeting
