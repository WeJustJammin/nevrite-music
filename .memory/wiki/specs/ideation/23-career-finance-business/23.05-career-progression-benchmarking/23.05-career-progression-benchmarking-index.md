## Overview

The "Career" half of the domain — the half the parent index is openly sceptical about: "a generic goal tracker nobody opens twice." Everything here lives or dies on one rule: **nothing is manually maintained**. A goal that updates itself from platform activity survives; a milestone timeline that assembles itself from credits, shows and releases survives; a benchmark computed against a real peer cohort survives. Anything requiring a musician to come back and tick a box is dead in a fortnight, and this sub-domain should not ship it.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies sub-domain status): goals with roadmap templates that seed them; an auto-assembling milestone timeline that *evidences* goal progress without being asked; and cohort benchmarking that both suggests goals and tells the user whether they are underpriced.
