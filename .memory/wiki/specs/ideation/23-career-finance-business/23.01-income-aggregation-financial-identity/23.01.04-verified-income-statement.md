# Feature: Verified Income Statement & Proof of Earnings

> **Number**: 23.01.04
> **Parent**: [Income Aggregation & Financial Identity](./23.01-income-aggregation-financial-identity-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-18

## Overview

A self-employed musician cannot prove they earn a living. There is no payslip, no employer, no single statement — so the landlord says no, the mortgage adviser says no, and the visa officer asks for evidence that does not exist in any one place. Because WeJammin moved a large share of the money itself, it can issue something no bank or accountant can: a statement where each line is **platform-verified**, not self-asserted. This is the payoff of the whole sub-domain and the deepest earned lock-in in the product — it is value the user would *miss* if they left, not data held hostage (`meta/problem-statement.md` Q-02).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Generates a statement for a date range and a purpose (tenancy, mortgage, visa, advance). Sees exactly what the recipient will see, including how much of their income is verified versus declared — which is often an unwelcome truth and should be. |
| Producer | Full | Same artefact. Their income is lumpier but more invoice-backed, so their verified share tends to be higher — a statement that reflects that honestly is more useful to them than one that flatters everyone equally. |
| Operator | Reports | A venue or studio is usually a company with real books and an accountant; they do not need WeJammin to prove their income to a landlord. What they may want is the **counterparty** direction — evidencing what they paid an act. Read-only export, not a headline feature. |
| Fan | None | Not visible — no income, no professional stake. |

## Behavior

The statement is generated from ledger rows for a period, **segregating verified from declared** (D-03 on the parent index). It is an issued artefact with an identity and a timestamp — not a live view — because a document sent to a third party must not silently change afterwards.

The rendered content has four load-bearing parts: (1) the **verified total** and, separately, the **declared total** — never a single merged figure (D-02); (2) a **source breakdown** — how much came from sessions, gigs, services, releases, merch, so the recipient can see the shape of the income, not just its size; (3) a **stability view** — monthly distribution and payer diversity over the period (DT-02), which is the content that turns a portfolio income's apparent riskiness into evidenced resilience; (4) a **verification explanation** in plain language the recipient understands, plus the URL of the hosted check page that lets them confirm the artefact independently of the file they were handed.

Generation is an **issuance event**: the statement is frozen against the ledger as it stood at that instant, gets its own ID, and is preserved even after the underlying ledger moves. Later ledger changes do not rewrite it — they can only produce a *superseding* statement, and the check page reflects the supersession.

### Happy Path

1. Musician needs to prove income for a tenancy application.
2. Selects a 12-month period and the purpose.
3. System renders: verified total, declared total (separately), source breakdown, monthly distribution, and a verification explanation the recipient can actually understand.
4. Musician generates the statement; it gets an ID and an immutable snapshot.
5. Recipient verifies it against a WeJammin-hosted check page — this is what makes it evidence rather than a PDF anyone could edit in a text editor.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Most income is `declared`, not verified | Statement is honest about it; the verified figure may be embarrassingly small | No flattering — a statement that overstates provability is worse than useless when the recipient checks |
| Ledger changes after issuance (late event, reversal, refund) | The issued snapshot does not mutate; a superseding statement can be issued | "This statement is superseded" on the check page — the recipient sees the truth, not a silently rewritten document |
| A refund/chargeback lands after a statement was used to win a tenancy | Cannot be retracted from the recipient. Only the check page can reflect it | Supersession is visible; this is a real, unfixable limit |
| User forges a PDF outside the platform | The check page is the defence; the PDF is not | Recipients are directed to verify, not to trust the file |
| Income is a Band entity's, not the person's | Band income does not prove personal income; distribution (23.07.03) does | Personal statement shows distributions received, not band gross |
| Statement used for an **advance** underwriting decision (23.06.01) | Same artefact, internal consumer | Underwriter reads verified only |
| Insufficient history (new user) | Cannot be manufactured — the platform was not there. Import (23.01.02) fills the period but only as `declared` | Honest "N months of verified history" |
| An accountant/manager (delegate) generates the statement on the musician's behalf | Delegates may generate, but the artefact is always issued **as the account holder** and attributable to them — a delegate cannot promote trust tiers or alter figures (per CX-01: delegates import, never adjudicate) | Statement carries the holder's identity, not the delegate's; delegate action is audit-logged |
| A Band member tries to generate a *personal* statement from **Band** income | Rejected — band gross is not personal income; only distributions actually received (23.07.03) count as the member's. `[PENDING — Step 5 Must-depth / /write-be-spec]` for the exact entity-permission matrix on who may issue a Band-entity statement | "This income belongs to the Band, not you. Your statement shows distributions received." |
| Check-page URL is shared/leaked beyond the intended recipient | The page exposes someone's income, so access cannot be a permanent unguessable-URL leak (Q-04) | Access is time-boxed and/or gated; expiry and viewer policy `[PENDING — /create-prd-security]` |
| Recipient checks the page after the statement expired or was revoked by the user | Check page returns a definitive expired/revoked state — never a blank 404 that reads as "fake" | "This statement is no longer valid" — distinguishable from "never existed" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Generating | Progress; generation is not instant and should not pretend to be |
| Empty | No verified income in the period | Explanation of why (no platform income yet) and what would change it — not a dead end |
| Populated | Statement rendered | Preview identical to the recipient's view |
| Error | Generation failed | Retry. Never emit a partial financial artefact |
| Partial | Period contains unresolved FX or pending duplicate adjudication (23.01.02) | **Blocked** from issuance with the specific reason — an artefact built on unreconciled rows is a wrong number with a verification badge on it |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a PDF export of the income ledger | Obvious first framing | ❌ REJECTED: a PDF is not evidence — anyone can edit one. The value is entirely in **third-party verifiability**: a check page the recipient hits, keyed to a snapshot. Without that, this feature is a spreadsheet print and the lock-in evaporates. The artefact is the claim; the check page is the proof. |
| DT-02 | A domain expert would expect **income stability**, not just income total, to be the underwriting question | Any underwriter or letting agent: £30k from one payer who has now gone is worse than £30k from twenty payers over three years. A musician's portfolio income *looks* risky as a total but is often more diversified than a salary — and nobody has ever been able to show that. This inverts the musician's usual disadvantage into an advantage. | ✅ CONFIRMED: the statement must show distribution and payer diversity over time, not a single number. This is the feature's strongest and least obvious idea. |
| DT-03 | The visa case is the same as the tenancy case | Convenience assumption | ❌ REJECTED: a US O-1/P-2 or a Schengen work permit needs evidence of **professional standing and engagements** — contracts, itineraries, billing, credits — not just money. That evidence spans 02 (credits), 17 (bookings) and 18 (touring), not just 23.01. Recorded as Q-02; the visa pack is a distinct artefact and probably a different feature entirely. Honest scope cut rather than a false claim of coverage. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Consumes **23.01.01 Income Event Ledger** and is constrained by **23.01.02 Off-Platform Income Import** — see `23.01-income-aggregation-financial-identity-cx.md#CX-01`. This tension is the sub-domain's core: every imported row raises coverage but can only ever be `declared`, never `verified`.
- Blocked by **23.01.02** duplicate adjudication and **23.01.03 Multi-Currency FX** — see `#CX-04`. A statement issued over a period with an unreconciled duplicate or an unresolved FX conversion is a wrong number wearing a verification badge; issuance is blocked until the period reconciles (see States → Partial).
- Consumed by **23.06.01 Advance Underwriting & Offers** — the same artefact, internal recipient. The advance feature is only underwritable *because* this exists; the underwriter reads the verified total only.
- Feeds/parallels **23.02 Tax & Reporting** — the same ledger drives the tax pack. A double-counted or wrongly-FX'd row corrupts both artefacts identically, so reconciliation is a shared upstream dependency, not a per-artefact concern.
- Consumes **23.07.03 Band/Entity Distribution** — a person's statement counts distributions *received* from a Band, not the Band's gross. This is the source of the personal-vs-entity edge case.
- Deliberately excludes **23.01.05 Income-to-Work Linkage** — see rejected pair `#R-02`. Third parties have no business seeing the credits graph; magnitude/stability/provability is all the recipient needs.
- Touches **01 Identity, Profiles & Organizations** — the identity being evidenced, the delegate/accountant permission model (who may issue), and the Band-vs-person distinction.
- Touches **Payments/Payouts cross-cut** — verified rows exist only because a platform settlement occurred; this feature never moves money (parent D-02) and reads settlement events as the source of `verified` trust.
- Touches **24 Trust, Safety & Disputes** — issuing verification artefacts makes the platform a target for fraud; a forged or laundered statement is a reputational event for WeJammin, not just for the user, and the check page is the enforcement surface.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The statement is an **issued, immutable snapshot** with a hosted check page — not a live view or a PDF | See DT-01. Third-party verifiability is the entire value |
| D-02 | Verified and declared are never summed into one headline figure | Parent D-03; merging them makes the recipient discount the verified half too |
| D-03 | Stability and payer diversity are first-class content, not just the total | See DT-02 |
| D-04 | Issuance is **blocked**, not best-effort, when the period contains unreconciled duplicates or unresolved FX | A partial financial artefact with a verification badge is worse than none — it lends false authority to a wrong number. See States → Partial and CX-04 |
| D-05 | Delegates (accountant/manager) may generate but the artefact is always issued **as, and attributable to, the account holder** | Prevents a delegate laundering or misattributing income; consistent with CX-01 (delegates never adjudicate trust) |
