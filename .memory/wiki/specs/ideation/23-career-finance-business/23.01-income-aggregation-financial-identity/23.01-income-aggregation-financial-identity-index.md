## Overview

The ledger that answers "what did I actually earn?" for a human whose money arrives from ten unrelated payers, on different schedules, in different currencies, most of it too small for anyone else to bother tracking. Because WeJammin holds settlements, session fees, marketplace sales, teaching income and royalty statements, it can compute a total picture no bank, accountant or fintech can see — and then turn that picture into a **provable** one that a landlord, an underwriter or a visa officer will accept.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies sub-domain status): income events arriving from every earning domain; import of the off-platform half; currency normalization; the verified-vs-declared trust tiering that decides whether the resulting statement is worth anything; and the linkage back to the work that produced the money.
