# Feature: Income Event Ledger

> **Number**: 23.01.01
> **Parent**: [Income Aggregation & Financial Identity](./23.01-income-aggregation-financial-identity-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Every domain on WeJammin that causes money to reach a user emits a normalized income event into one ledger — a live settlement, a session fee, a gear sale, a plugin licence, a lesson, a royalty line, a sync fee, a merch order. This is the "income visible in one place" that appears verbatim in the Musician's Success Criteria in `meta/personas.md`, and it is nearly free: the platform is already the party moving the money, so the record is a byproduct rather than a data-entry chore.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The one screen that finally shows the portfolio: ten payers, different schedules, one running total. Filters by source domain, date, currency and trust tier. Their income is the long tail — many rows, small amounts, which is precisely why no bank or accountant has ever been able to see it. |
| Producer | Full | Same ledger, different shape: fewer, larger rows (session fees, points/backend from royalties) and a payer side — they also *pay* contributors, so their view must not present gross session income as if it were theirs to keep. |
| Operator | Full | Booking deposits, room hire, settlement shares. Their rows are high-volume and time-shaped (an unsold Tuesday earns nothing, and the ledger shows the hole). Often already has an accountant and a company — this is a feed, not their books of record. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Fans have no professional stake (`meta/personas.md`) and no income on the platform. A fan who is also a hobbyist musician sees this only once they earn — the ledger surfaces on first income event, not at signup. |

## Behavior

A ledger row is created by an **event emitted from the earning domain**, never by this domain reaching into another. The row records: amount, native currency, date, source domain, counterparty, trust tier (`verified` for platform-moved money), and an optional work reference (23.01.05). Rows are append-only; corrections are new rows, not edits — this is a financial record that must survive an audit and a dispute.

### Happy Path

1. A Musician plays a show; the Operator settles through domain 17.
2. The settlement completes and emits an income event: £280, GBP, source `17-live-booking-settlement`, counterparty = the venue entity, tier `verified`.
3. The row appears in the Musician's ledger without them doing anything — no form, no import, no receipt.
4. The running total, the source breakdown and the tax-year-to-date figure (23.02) all recompute.
5. Months later that £280 is still there, provable, attached to the show that produced it.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Payment is later refunded or charged back | A reversing row is appended referencing the original; the original is never deleted | Both rows visible; total corrected; a "reversed" marker on the original |
| Income arrives for a **Band entity**, not the person | Row belongs to the band's ledger; member distribution is 23.07.03's job, not this feature's | Band ledger is a separate context; personal total does not silently include band money |
| Gross vs net — platform fee, tax withheld at source, commission | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`. The row must carry gross, deductions and net, because tax cares about gross and cashflow cares about net. Getting this wrong misstates every downstream figure. | Both figures; deduction breakdown expandable |
| Source domain emits a duplicate event (retry, at-least-once delivery) | Idempotency key on the event; the second delivery is a no-op | Nothing — the user never sees the duplicate |
| Payout is split across several recipients (a session with 4 players) | Each recipient gets their own row for their own share; nobody sees the others' amounts | Own share only |
| Income event arrives for a period already exported to an accountant | Row lands, but the exported period is flagged as superseded | "This period has changed since you exported it" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Ledger opened | Skeleton rows with the running-total slot reserved |
| Empty | User has never earned on the platform | Not a generic empty state — an explanation of what will land here and which domains feed it, with a CTA to import history (23.01.02) |
| Populated | One or more rows | Grouped list, running total, source breakdown |
| Error | Ledger query failed | Error with retry. **Never** a partial total — a wrong number is worse than no number in a financial view |
| Partial | Some source domains unreachable / FX unresolved | Total shown with an explicit "excludes N rows pending" marker, never a silently short total |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The ledger should be authoritative for on-platform money — i.e. this domain moves it | Obvious first framing | ❌ REJECTED: payments/payouts/escrow is a platform cross-cut serving 05, 13, 14, 17, 19, 20. If 23.01 owned money movement it would become a shadow payments system and duplicate the ledger of record. 23.01 **subscribes**; it does not move. Recorded as D-02 on the parent index. |
| DT-02 | Income events are a simple per-domain read | Naive integration assumption | ❌ REJECTED: it is a **contract that ~10 domains must implement** — a normalized emission with idempotency, reversal semantics and gross/net decomposition. That is a cross-cut mechanism, not a feature detail. Returned as the `Income Event Emission Contract` cross-cut. |
| DT-03 | A domain expert would expect **gross vs net** to be a first-class field, not a display option | Any accountant or tax authority: liability is computed on gross, cashflow on net. A ledger that stores only what hit the bank cannot produce a correct tax pack — the platform fee and any withholding are deductible/creditable and must be recoverable per row. | ✅ CONFIRMED: gross, deductions and net are separate fields on every row. Flagged as the single most consequential schema decision in this sub-domain; feeds 23.02.03 directly. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Fed by **17 Live Booking & Settlement**, **05 Services Marketplace**, **13 Gear Marketplace**, **14 Digital Goods**, **19 Ticketing**, **10 Royalties & Collections**, **06 Education**, **11 Licensing**, **12 Release & Distribution**, **20 Fanbase** — every domain that causes money to arrive. This breadth is the domain's justification and its integration cost.
- Consumes the platform **Payments, Payouts & Escrow** cross-cut — reads its events, owns none of its money.
- Feeds **23.02 Expenses & Tax Readiness** (the income side of the tax pack), **23.06 Advances, Commission & Recoupment** (the base that commission and recoupment are computed against), **23.07 Budgeting & Project/Tour P&L** (the income line).
- Touches **01 Identity, Profiles & Organizations** — the Band entity earns independently of its members, so ledger ownership follows the entity model.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Append-only; corrections are reversing rows | A financial record that can be edited in place cannot survive a dispute or an audit — and disputes over money are exactly what this platform exists to prevent (problem-statement.md) |
| D-02 | The ledger is a subscriber, not a mover of money | See DT-01 |
| D-03 | Gross, deductions and net are separate fields | See DT-03 |
