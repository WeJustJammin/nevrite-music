# Feature: Income Event Ledger

> **Number**: 23.01.01
> **Parent**: [Income Aggregation & Financial Identity](./23.01-income-aggregation-financial-identity-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Every domain on WeJammin that causes money to reach a user emits a normalized income event into one ledger — a live settlement, a session fee, a gear sale, a plugin licence, a lesson, a royalty line, a sync fee, a merch order. This is the "income visible in one place" that appears verbatim in the Musician's Success Criteria in `meta/personas.md`, and it is nearly free: the platform is already the party moving the money, so the record is a byproduct rather than a data-entry chore. The ledger is **derived, not authoritative** (D-02) — it subscribes to income events emitted by the earning domains and by the platform Payments/Payouts/Escrow cross-cut, and owns none of the money movement itself.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The one screen that finally shows the portfolio: ten payers, different schedules, one running total. Filters by source domain, date range, native currency and trust tier; expands any row to its gross/deductions/net breakdown and its work linkage (23.01.05). Their income is the long tail — many rows, small amounts, which is precisely why no bank or accountant has ever been able to see it. They can annotate a row (private note) and initiate a dispute on a `verified` row, but can **never** edit an amount, delete a row, or promote a row's trust tier. |
| Producer | Full | Same ledger, different shape: fewer, larger rows (session fees, points/backend from royalties) and a **payer side** — they also *pay* contributors, so their view distinguishes gross session income they received from amounts that flow out to collaborators. The ledger presents only income (money in); payer-side outflows are a pointer to 23.07, so gross session income is never displayed as if it were theirs to keep. |
| Operator | Full | Booking deposits, room hire, settlement shares. Their rows are high-volume and time-shaped (an unsold Tuesday earns nothing, and the source breakdown shows the hole). They frequently already run an accountant and a company, so their expected mode is **feed-then-export** (23.01.02 / 23.02) rather than books of record. No income-to-work linkage row — their income attaches to a room and a date, not to a work (parent Role Matrix). |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Fans have no professional stake (`meta/personas.md`, D-11) and no income on the platform. A fan who is also a hobbyist musician sees this only once they earn — the ledger surfaces on **first income event**, not at signup (Q-01). Until then the finance surface is absent from their navigation, not shown empty. |

## Behavior

A ledger row is created by an **event emitted from the earning domain or from the platform payments cross-cut**, never by this domain reaching into another (D-02). The row records: the owning identity or entity, gross amount, itemized deductions, net amount, native currency, economic date, source domain, counterparty, trust tier (`verified` for platform-moved money, `declared` for imported per CX-04), an idempotency key, and an optional work reference (23.01.05). Rows are **append-only** (D-01); corrections are new reversing rows referencing the original, never in-place edits — this is a financial record that must survive an audit and a dispute, which is exactly the failure the platform exists to prevent (`problem-statement.md`).

Three money figures are first-class on every row (D-03): **gross** (what the counterparty was charged / the full earned amount), **deductions** (an itemized list — platform fee, commission taken by an agent/manager, tax withheld at source, processor fee), and **net** (what actually reached the user). Tax liability is computed on gross downstream (23.02); cashflow and proof-of-earnings are computed on net. A ledger that stored only the net figure that hit the bank could never produce a correct tax pack, because the deductions are variously deductible or creditable and must be recoverable per row.

### Happy Path

1. A Musician plays a show; the Operator settles through domain 17.
2. The settlement completes and the Payments cross-cut emits an income event: gross £300, deductions [platform fee £15, withholding £5], net £280, GBP, source `17-live-booking-settlement`, counterparty = the venue entity, tier `verified`, a stable dedup identity derived from the originating settlement so a retry can never double-count, work reference = the performance work-id if the credits graph holds one.
3. The row appears in the Musician's ledger without them doing anything — no form, no import, no receipt. The gross/net split is captured, not asked for.
4. The running total (net), the source breakdown, the gross-for-tax figure and the tax-year-to-date figure (23.02) all recompute; the FX reporting-currency projection (23.01.03) is looked up for the policy date and cached.
5. Months later that £280 net / £300 gross is still there, provable, attached to the show that produced it — with the platform fee still recoverable as a deductible expense.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Payment is later refunded or charged back | A reversing row is appended referencing the original by idempotency key; the original is never deleted (D-01). Gross, deductions and net are all reversed so downstream tax and cashflow figures self-correct | Both rows visible; running total corrected; a "reversed" marker on the original with a link to the reversing row |
| **Gross vs net decomposition** (platform fee, commission, tax withheld at source) | The row carries a gross figure, an itemized list of deductions (each naming its kind, amount and currency), and a net figure equal to gross minus the deductions. If an emitting domain supplies only a net figure and no deduction detail, the row is stored net-only — gross equals net, the deduction list is empty, and the row is flagged as having incomplete deductions so 23.02 knows the tax base is understated and can prompt the user | Both figures with an expandable deduction breakdown; rows lacking gross detail carry a "deductions not itemized — tax figure may be understated" badge |
| Income arrives for a **Band entity**, not the person | Row belongs to the band's ledger (owning identity = the entity, per domain 01); member distribution is 23.07.03's job, not this feature's | Band ledger is a separate context switch; the person's personal running total never silently includes band money |
| Source domain emits a **duplicate** event (retry, at-least-once delivery) | The idempotency key makes the second delivery a no-op — the existing row is returned, not a new one appended | Nothing — the user never sees the duplicate |
| Two platform emitters describe the **same economic fact** (e.g. a settlement event *and* a payout event for one payment) with **different** idempotency keys | Reconciliation on (owning identity, amount, economic-date window, counterparty): the platform-authoritative event (D-02) is kept, the other is linked as a corroborating reference, not a second income row. If confidence is below the auto-merge threshold, both are held and the user is asked to confirm — matching **proposes, never silently auto-merges**, because a wrong merge deletes real income (CX-04) | At most a "we linked two records of the same payment" note; if ambiguous, a confirm-merge prompt |
| Payout is **split** across several recipients (a session with 4 players) | Each recipient gets their own row for their own share only; recipients never see each other's amounts (row ownership is per-identity) | Own share only |
| The same human earns in **two roles from one event** (multi-hyphenate: performs *and* owns the room for the same show) | Two distinct income events with distinct sources (`17-live-booking-settlement` performer share, `16-room-hire` room income) and distinct counterparties → two separate rows on the one personal ledger. The ledger never collapses two economic facts into one row just because the owner is one person | Two rows, each labelled by its source role; source breakdown shows both streams |
| A **reversal or the FX rate arrives before the original event** (out-of-order / late delivery) | The reversing or projecting record is parked as pending, keyed to the missing original's idempotency key, and reconciled when the original lands; if the original never arrives within the reconciliation window, the orphan is surfaced for review rather than silently applied or dropped | The affected total shows an "N records pending reconciliation" marker; nothing self-corrects invisibly |
| Event arrives with a **missing or unresolvable counterparty** (source domain omitted it) | Row lands with `counterparty = unattributed`; the money is real and counted, only the payer identity is unknown. Never dropped | Row shown with "payer not identified" and a link to attach one if the user knows it |
| **Malicious / forged event** — a user attempts to submit a fabricated `verified` income event, or spoof another user's identity as owner | Rejected before any row is created: only the platform's own earning domains and the payments cross-cut may create rows, and the trust tier is set by the emitting domain, never by anything a user can supply. A `verified` tier can only originate from platform-moved money. Rows from a user-facing path can at most create `declared` rows on the caller's **own** ledger (that path is 23.01.02) | Rejected — the fabricated event never appears; the attempt is logged for Trust & Safety (domain 24). Users see nothing |
| **Trust-tier self-promotion** attempt (user or delegated accountant tries to flip a `declared` row to `verified`) | Refused; tier is immutable from any user-facing path and rises to `verified` only via an arriving platform event or an accepted evidence reconciliation (CX-01 / CX-04) | The tier control is not offered; any programmatic attempt is refused as a not-permitted action and the tier is left unchanged |
| **Delegated accountant** (representation cross-cut) views the ledger | Read + export + import-on-behalf, but **no** amount edits, **no** deletions, **no** tier changes, **no** dispute initiation on the user's behalf without explicit scope | The delegate sees a full read/export view with write controls hidden |
| Income event lands for a **period already exported** to an accountant | Row lands (never blocked); the exported period is flagged `superseded` so the stale artefact is known to be out of date (CX-02 freeze/restate) | "This period has changed since you exported it — re-export to reconcile" |
| A **credit the row is linked to is later revoked** (23.01.05 / domain 02) | The work linkage degrades to "unattributed income" — the money survives, the attribution does not (CX-03). No money is deleted when a credit is disputed | Row remains with its amount; the work link shows "credit revoked — earnings retained, attribution removed" |
| **Ledger query fails** on load | No partial total is ever rendered — a wrong number in a financial view is worse than no number. The view holds at an error state with retry | Error with retry; running total slot shows a dash, never a stale or partial figure |
| **FX rate source unavailable** for a row's reporting projection | The row stays visible with its native amount intact and an **unresolved** reporting projection; it is never dropped from the ledger (CX-02). The reporting-currency total shows "excludes N rows pending conversion" | Row visible in native currency; reporting total explicitly marked incomplete |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Ledger opened | Skeleton rows with the running-total slot reserved; no number shown until the query resolves |
| Empty | User has authenticated but has never earned on the platform | Not a generic empty state — an explanation of what will land here and which domains feed it, plus a CTA to import history (23.01.02). For a Fan-adjacent hobbyist this surface is not shown at all until the first event (Q-01) |
| Populated | One or more rows exist and the query resolved fully | Grouped list (by source or by period), net running total, gross-for-tax figure, source breakdown, trust-tier split (verified vs declared totals never merged, D-03 on parent) |
| Error | Ledger query failed, or the FX/aggregation service errored on the total | Error with retry. **Never** a partial or stale total — the number is withheld entirely rather than shown wrong |
| Partial | Some source rows resolved but FX unresolved, or reconciliation pending, or a source domain unreachable | Rows that resolved are shown; the reporting-currency total carries an explicit "excludes N rows pending (conversion / reconciliation)" marker, never a silently short total |

| DT-03 | A domain expert would expect **gross vs net** to be a first-class field, not a display option | Any accountant or tax authority: liability is computed on gross, cashflow on net. | DT-04 | Reconciling two records of one payment (settlement event + payout event) can be auto-merged on amount + date | The platform emits multiple events per payment lifecycle; naive dedup would count a payment twice | ❌ REJECTED as an auto-action: a wrong auto-merge **deletes real income**, and the ledger feeds the tax pack and the proof-of-earnings artefact — a document wrong in the user's favour (CX-04). ## Cross-Cut Notes

- **Fed by** every domain that causes money to arrive — **17 Live Booking & Settlement**, **05 Services Marketplace**, **13 Gear Marketplace**, **14 Digital Goods**, **19 Ticketing**, **10 Royalties & Collections**, **06 Education**, **11 Licensing**, **12 Release & Distribution**, **20 Fanbase**. Each must implement the same `Income Event Emission Contract` (normalized emission with idempotency key, reversal semantics, and gross/deductions/net decomposition). This breadth is the domain's justification and its integration cost — feeds the Step 6 cross-cut consolidation.
- **Consumes** the platform **Payments, Payouts & Escrow** cross-cut — reads its lifecycle events (settlement, payout, refund, chargeback), owns none of its money (D-02). The reconciliation of multiple lifecycle events for one payment (DT-04, CX-04) lives on this boundary.
- **Pairs with 23.01.03 Multi-Currency Normalization & FX** (CX-02): every row carries a native currency and amount that is immutable; FX supplies the derived reporting-currency projection. An unavailable rate leaves the projection unresolved but never drops the row.
- **Projected by 23.01.05 Income-to-Work Linkage** (CX-03): rows originating from creative work carry a `work_id`; the linkage is a read projection joining ledger rows to the credits graph (domain 02) and stores no money. A revoked credit degrades attribution but never deletes money.
- **Collides with 23.01.02 Off-Platform Income Import** (CX-04): imported rows land in this same ledger at tier `declared` and risk double-counting against events the platform already recorded — the highest-consequence failure in the sub-domain, resolved by propose-don't-auto-merge reconciliation.
- **Feeds 23.02 Expenses & Tax Readiness** (the income side of the tax pack — gross drives the liability), **23.06 Advances, Commission & Recoupment** (the base commission and recoupment are computed against), **23.07 Budgeting & Project/Tour P&L** (the income line).
- **Touches 01 Identity, Profiles & Organizations** — the Band entity earns independently of its members, so ledger ownership follows the entity model; row ownership is per-identity (personal vs entity) and drives the split-payout privacy boundary.
- **Touches 24 Trust & Safety** — rejected forged/spoofed event attempts are logged for moderation; the ledger is a financial record of record and its ingress is an attack surface.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Append-only; corrections are reversing rows | A financial record that can be edited in place cannot survive a dispute or an audit — and disputes over money are exactly what this platform exists to prevent (`problem-statement.md`) |
| D-02 | The ledger is a subscriber, not a mover of money | See DT-01 — prevents a shadow payments system duplicating the ledger of record |
| D-03 | Gross, itemized deductions and net are separate fields on every row | See DT-03 — tax is computed on gross, cashflow on net; a net-only emission is accepted but flagged `deductions_incomplete` |
| D-04 | Reconciliation proposes, never silently auto-merges | See DT-04 — a wrong auto-merge deletes real income that flows into the tax pack and proof-of-earnings; the platform-authoritative event wins the tie |
| D-05 | Trust tier is set by the emitter and is immutable from every user-facing path | Only platform-moved money is `verified`; no user or delegate may self-promote a tier — the credibility of 23.01.04's proof-of-earnings depends on it (D-03 on parent) |
| D-06 | Out-of-order records are parked and reconciled, never applied or dropped | See DT-05 — silently applying or discarding a late reversal mutates a financial total invisibly |
