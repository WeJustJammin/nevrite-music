# Feature: Union & Performer Session Reporting

> **Number**: 02.07
> **Parent**: [Credits & Attribution](./credits-attribution-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Emitting union session paperwork — the AFM B-form and its SAG-AFTRA equivalents — from data the platform already captured. When a union session happens, someone fills in a form listing every player, their instrument, the session type, the scale rate and the hours; that form drives pension and health contributions and eventually re-use payments. It is filled in by hand today, from the same memory that produces bad credits, and `02.02` captures nearly every field it needs as a byproduct.

**Classified as a feature, not a sub-domain**: generation → submission → status tracking is one pipeline, not interacting capabilities. It is `02.08` (export) pointed at a different consumer with a different form.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config | The union member whose pension contribution depends on this paperwork existing. Supplies their union membership details once, confirms their line, and sees whether the filing happened — which today they usually cannot, and chasing it is a known grind. |
| Producer | Full | Usually the signatory or the one who has to produce the form. This is pure admin relief for them: the roll call (`02.02.01`) and contribution log (`02.02.02`) already hold most of what the B-form asks for. |
| Operator | Read-only | A signatory studio's sessions have union implications, and the room may hold the signatory agreement. Their involvement is real but narrow. |
| Fan | None | No union, no session, no filing. Explicit absence. |

## Behavior

The capture-to-form fit is unusually good — roll call, intervals, instruments, roles and the session's date and location are exactly the B-form's fields — but the honest scope assessment is that this is a **narrow, deep, jurisdictionally-specific compliance surface**:

- It is **US-centric**. AFM and SAG-AFTRA are American; the UK's MU, Germany's GVL and every other territory have different instruments, forms and rules. The platform's addressable users are global, and this serves one region's union sessions.
- It is **a minority of sessions**. Most sessions WeJammin will capture are non-union.
- It is **externally-defined and moving**. Scale rates, session types and form structures are set by agreements the platform does not control and cannot version-lock, and being wrong here has financial consequences for the member.
- The **submission path is the hard part**, and it is not a data problem. Whether a union accepts a machine-generated filing is a relationship and integration question with an institution, not an engineering one.

That is why it is proposed `won't` for now (`credits-attribution-index.md` MoSCoW). The interesting strategic point is the inverse: for the subset it serves, this is a **lock-in that is earned rather than hostile** (`problem-statement.md` Q-02) — the platform is doing real work the user would otherwise do by hand, using data that already exists. Worth revisiting once capture density is real.

### Happy Path

1. A session is marked union, with the applicable agreement and signatory
2. Capture runs normally (`02.02`) — the same flow as any session, no extra ceremony
3. At close, the system assembles the form from the roll, intervals, instruments and roles it already has
4. Producer/signatory reviews the pre-filled form and completes the fields capture cannot know (scale category, session type)
5. Form is filed; each member sees their line and the filing's status

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Session was not marked union until afterwards | Common — the classification is often made later, by someone else. Form generates retroactively from captured data, which is precisely the point | Retroactive generation |
| Player is not a union member | Excluded from the filing; their credit is unaffected. Union status and credit are unrelated facts | Credit stands |
| Scale rates changed | Externally-defined and outside platform control. Wrong rates are a financial error for the member | `[PENDING — /create-prd]` — likely a maintained rate table with a staleness gate |
| Union has no machine submission path | The realistic case. Degrades to a correctly-filled document the human files, which still removes most of the work | Generated document |
| Non-US territory | Out of scope. MU, GVL and others have entirely different instruments | Not offered |
| Captured data is incomplete (nobody logged instruments) | Form cannot generate; falls back to manual with whatever is known pre-filled | Partial pre-fill |
| Filing is wrong and a member's pension contribution is short | The consequential failure. A generated form the human signed is still the human's filing, and the platform's role in the error is a real question | `[PENDING — /create-prd-security]` |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Feature, not sub-domain | Generate → submit → track is one pipeline. It is `02.08` with a different form. |
| D-02 | Union status and credit are independent facts | A non-member's credit is unaffected by their exclusion from a filing. Coupling them would make union membership a credit qualifier, which it is not. |
| D-03 | Scope is US (AFM/SAG-AFTRA) if built at all | Other territories are different instruments, not translations. |
