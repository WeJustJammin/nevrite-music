# Feature: Session Close Capture Prompt

> **Number**: 02.02.03
> **Parent**: [Session Capture](./02.02-session-capture-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

The single most important moment in the product. `personas.md` names it for both capture personas — the Producer's "the moment that matters: prompts/confirms who did what and what the split is, while everyone is present", and the Musician's "credit + split captured while everyone is present and friendly". This feature is that prompt: the thing that fires before the room empties and converts a session's working data (the roll, `02.02.01`; the log, `02.02.02`) into confirmed facts.

It exists to defeat one specific named failure: the Producer's worst accidental behavior — *closing a project without capturing splits, "we'll do it later"* — committed by the persona best placed to prevent it. `personas.md` gives the design instruction directly: **make the lazy path the correct path.**

The deepening pass found that the breadth design contradicted its own thesis. It gated the contributors' taps behind the Producer's review, which makes the perishable asset (people in the room, awake, with their phones out) wait on the durable one (a Producer's accuracy, repairable at any time). D-05 inverts that. Everything else here follows from one principle: **presence is the only input that cannot be recovered at any price.**

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config (confirms own) | Receives one card naming only themselves: their roll interval and every contribution attributed to them on this session's delta ("Drums — tracks 1, 3, 4; Rhodes — track 3"). One tap confirms the whole card; expanding it corrects any single line inline (wrong role, wrong track, "I wasn't on this"). **That tap is a confirmation, not an attestation** (D-17): `02.04.01` D-11 excludes the credited party from attesting their own credit, and `02.04.02` D-03 says self-assertion never raises the rung. It still earns its place — it carries the inline correction the async chase never collects (DT-06), it records the subject's agreement beside the Producer's assertion, and across the set the taps are the mutual presence corroboration `02.02.04` rests on (sub-domain CX-04). What separates WeJammin's graph from every self-asserted database is the second, **optional and unchecked** line — the reciprocal ask, "<Producer> ran this session — confirm?" (D-08) — because there the tapper is neither the asserter nor the credited party, which is the only shape `02.04.02` rung 5 accepts. Never sees another contributor's card. If they are not present or not a platform user, their row is asserted without them and chases asynchronously at a materially worse rate. |
| Producer | Full (owns the reconciliation) | Sees the whole picture — roll + log + gaps — ordered by what is missing: roll entries with no attributed work, attributed work with no named party, and the room's own credit row. Fixes and commits. **Their review is not a gate on anyone else's tap** (D-05). Can close the session, skip the prompt, and dismiss every re-offer without ever being blocked. It is the only row in the prompt naming a party who is never asked (DT-14). |
| Fan | None | Does not exist in this flow. Sees, eventually, only the committed and published credit (`02.01.02`), subject to embargo (`02.01.05`) — never the prompt, the gaps, the corrections or who declined to tap. |

## Behavior

The prompt reconciles three inputs — the roll (who was here), the log (what they did), and the gap between them — and asks each party to confirm only their own line. It holds no data of its own (sub-domain CX-02).

**Timing is the feature.** Fired too early, the session isn't done. Fired too late, the room is empty and it becomes email — which is the reconstruction workflow the platform exists to replace (`problem-statement.md`). The trigger is therefore tied to signals that mean "we're wrapping", never to a schedule.

### The wrap signal ladder

The prompt fires on the **first** of these to occur, and only once per session-delta (D-06):

| # | Signal | Available when | Reliability |
|---|--------|---------------|-------------|
| 1 | Producer taps "wrap" | Always — **and in v1 the only timely rung** | Certain, but depends on the persona doing the one thing they forget to do |
| 2 | DAW session closes | Plugin channel exists (sub-domain Q-02) — **UNAVAILABLE IN v1** (D-70) | High — the intended primary, once a bridge exists |
| 3 | Bounce / mixdown / export completes | Plugin channel exists — **UNAVAILABLE IN v1** (D-70) | High, but fires mid-session too (people bounce rough mixes) — only counts as wrap when followed by 20 min of no capture activity |
| 4 | Booked end time + 30 min with no capture activity | Session came from a booking (16/17) | Low alone (DT-03) — sessions overrun as routine; the activity check is what makes it usable |
| 5 | 72 h of no capture activity, no wrap signal | Always | The backstop for the session that never closes (a real and common state) |

Signal 5 is **not** a close prompt. The room emptied three days ago. It fires the Producer's reconciliation half only, and routes the contributor half to `02.04.01` as what it honestly is: an async attestation chase.

### The two halves — the deepening pass's central correction

The prompt is **two independently-firing halves against the same reconciliation state**. They are not sequential (D-05).

**Half 1 — the contributor asks. Perishable. Fires within 5 s (P95) of the wrap signal, ungated.**
Each present, platform-user contributor named in the session's delta gets one card, from the log exactly as it stands. Nothing waits for the Producer.

**Half 2 — the Producer's reconciliation. Durable. Fires at the same instant; completes whenever.**
The gaps — a roll entry with no work, a take with no name, the room row — are precisely the rows that have *no contributor to ask*, so decoupling costs nothing. The Producer can do this at wrap, at 11am tomorrow, or never.

The halves stay live against each other rather than snapshotting (sub-domain CX-02, Q5). If the Producer fixes a row while its contributor's card is open, the card updates in place.

**A tap attaches to a claim, not to a row** — confirmation and reciprocal attestation alike. If a contributor taps and the Producer then changes what that row says, the tap does not transfer — it stays attached to the claim that was actually shown and read, and the changed claim re-asks (D-07). A tap that silently migrates onto text its author never saw is a forged signature; where the tap was an attestation, that signature sits at the graph's highest tier.

### The delta rule

The prompt asks about **what is new since the last prompt on this session**, never the whole session (D-06, DT-08). A Mon–Wed session with an unchanged roll does not buzz the same drummer three nights running about the same drums; Tuesday's card carries Tuesday's rows or Tuesday does not fire at all. A prompt fired within the last 20 h for the same session with no new roll entries and no new attributions is suppressed entirely.

### Room mode

The design assumes "phone buzzes → they tap". At 4am mid-teardown, or in a basement studio with no signal — **the modal studio** — phones are in bags and the fan-out never lands. Room mode is the Producer's screen showing the roll, passed around, each person tapping their own line. It is the digital form of the session sign-off sheet contractors already physically pass around, and it is the only capture path that works with zero connectivity and zero contributor accounts.

**Room mode buys accuracy, not provenance** (DT-06). A tap on the Producer's device is indistinguishable from the Producer tapping, so its evidentiary weight is exactly the Producer's assertion — which is what it already was. What it does buy is the correction: the human in the room says "Rhodes, not piano," and that is a data-accuracy win the async chase would never have collected. Room-mode taps are recorded as **witnessed, not attested**, are never rendered to anyone as "confirmed by X", and convert to a real attestation only when the named party ratifies from their own account — one tap, async, or at claim time (`02.03.02`) if they were a shell.

### What the prompt must not become

A form. The moment this is a multi-step admin flow it is skipped, and every argument in `problem-statement.md` about the reconstruction failure applies to WeJammin itself. Contributor card: one screen, no scroll, one tap for the default action, and refusal costs exactly as much as confirmation (`02.04.01` constraint 3).

### Re-offer schedule

If the Producer never completes Half 2, credits persist at producer-asserted tier and the prompt re-offers **three times, scheduled by local wall-clock, never by elapsed offset** (D-11, DT-13): the next local morning (09:00–11:00), the following one, and once at 14 days. Then it stops permanently and degrades to a passive marker on the session. There is never a fourth.

**Circuit breaker**: a Producer who skips the prompt on 3 consecutive sessions drops out of push entirely for 30 days — the prompt still exists in-app, silently. At that point the nagging is the product's failure, not theirs, and DT-03's dismissal-training death spiral is already underway.

### Happy Path

1. Wrap signal fires — first of the ladder above (Producer taps "wrap" / DAW closes *(rung 2, not available in v1 — D-70)* / bounce + 20 min quiet *(rung 3, not available in v1 — D-70)* / booking end + 30 min quiet)
2. System computes the delta: roll entries, attributions and gaps new since the last prompt on this session
3. **Within 5 s, both halves fire at once.** Contributor cards dispatch to every present platform user named in the delta; the Producer's reconciliation view opens. Neither waits for the other
4. Each contributor sees one card — their interval, their attributed work, and the optional unchecked reciprocal line for the Producer's row. Most tap before they have packed up
5. Producer works the gaps in whatever order they like — roll entries with no work, work with no name, the room's row — or closes the app and does it tomorrow. Nothing blocks; nothing rolls back
6. Confirmed claims commit as session-captured credits (`02.01.01`, `02.04.02`) — the own-row tap is a confirmation, never an attestation, and lifts no rung by itself (D-17, `02.04.01` D-11); what it commits is the subject's agreement and any correction riding with it. The reciprocal line's taps (D-08) are the only attestations the prompt itself collects; every other row still commits, at producer-asserted tier
7. Unconfirmed rows hand off to `02.04.01` as async attestation requests; the handoff is the admission that the in-room moment did not fire
8. Committed credits publish per the session's embargo (`02.01.05`) — never immediately by default

### States

Two surfaces, and they can be in different states at the same time — that is D-05 working.

| Surface | State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|---------|-------|---------|---------------|
| Both | Armed | Session open, no wrap signal seen | Nothing. The prompt is invisible until it is relevant |
| Contributor card | Loading | Card opened; claim being fetched | Skeleton of the card's own shape (name, one row); never a spinner over an empty screen — the card must feel already-there |
| Contributor card | Populated | Claim loaded | Their interval, their rows, one tap; optional unchecked reciprocal line |
| Contributor card | Empty | Contributor is on the roll with zero attributed work in the delta | No card fires. Presence is not a credit (`02.02.01` D-01); asking someone to confirm nothing is noise |
| Contributor card | Confirmed | Tapped | "Confirmed" — terminal, and the last thing the product asks of them |
| Contributor card | Corrected | Expanded and edited | Correction sent to the Producer; the row is now unconfirmed pending their acceptance |
| Contributor card | Refused | "I wasn't on this" | Recorded; no escalation offered automatically (`02.04.01`) |
| Contributor card | Stale | The claim changed after they tapped | "This changed — confirm again?" one tap (D-07) |
| Contributor card | Error | Confirmation failed to send | Tap is held locally and retried automatically; if it still cannot send, it queues for sync and lands the moment connectivity returns. The user is never asked to tap twice for a network reason, and never sees the retry — the card reads "confirmed" the instant they tap (the retry policy itself is a `/create-prd` concern) |
| Producer view | Loading | Wrap fired; reconciliation computing | Skeleton ordered by gap type |
| Producer view | Populated | Reconciliation ready | Roll + log + gaps, ordered by what is missing; room row included |
| Producer view | Empty | Solo session, or empty roll + empty log | Does not fire at all (solo) / roll-first question (empty) |
| Producer view | Partial | Some contributors tapped, gaps remain | Per-row status; unconfirmed rows visibly heading for the async chase, which is the honest picture |
| Producer view | Error | Reconciliation failed to load | Retry; the log and roll are intact underneath — the prompt owns no data, so nothing is at risk (sub-domain CX-02) |
| Session | Complete | Every delta row confirmed | Confirmation; credits live subject to embargo (`02.01.05`) |
| Session | Skipped | Producer dismissed | Credits at producer-asserted tier; re-offers scheduled per D-11 |
| Session | Lapsed | 3 re-offers exhausted, or circuit breaker | Passive marker on the session; no further push, ever |
| Both | Pending sync | Offline (sub-domain D-03) | Room mode offered; local confirmations queued with a sync marker; capture never blocked |
| Both | Suppressed | Prompt fired < 20 h ago for this session, no new rows | Nothing fires (D-06) |

| DT-01 | The prompt should block session close until credits are captured — it is the most important moment, so enforce it | Enforcement instinct; the feature's own importance | ❌ **REJECTED, emphatically.** A tool that blocks a Producer at 4am gets uninstalled at 4:01am, and then the platform is not in the room at all — forfeiting every downstream fact, not just this session's. The honest fix is to *ask*, cheaply and skippably: one optional, unchecked line on each contributor's card. A split is a **single object that must sum to 100% and requires every writer** — one absent writer blocks the whole instrument, and often the song is not finished so the split is not yet *knowable*. Fusing their completion means the credit half — which is ready, cheap and perishable — waits on a negotiation that may take weeks. **The credit is never held hostage to the split.** Same moment, two acts, and now also: two independent completion criteria. |
| DT-10 | Whoever fires the prompt is a credited party on the session | Reasonable inference — they ran the room | ❌ **REJECTED.** The assistant, the tape op, the house engineer tracking for an absent producer — all routinely run the room with no credit on the record at all. A prompt that assumes its operator is a party will either invent a credit for them or hide rows from them. Reconciliation rights come from owning the session, not from being in its credit list; the two are orthogonal and must stay so. |
| DT-11 | Even a solo session should prompt — the Producer should confirm their own work | Consistency; every session produces credits | ❌ **REJECTED.** There is nobody to ask. A solo session's prompt is a person confirming to themselves what they just typed — it raises no tier (self-asserted stays self-asserted, `02.04.02`), collects no corroboration, and costs a dismissal. And it is almost certainly the platform's highest-volume session shape: the bedroom producer working alone. Nagging the modal case for zero information is how the prompt becomes background noise before it ever reaches the session that matters. → D-13 |
| DT-12 | A Band entity can confirm its own row at the prompt | Bands are first-class entities (`personas.md`, domain 01) and liner notes credit bands directly | ❌ **REJECTED — entities cannot tap.** An attestation is "evidence with a name on it" whose value comes from a human being accountable for having been in a position to know (`02.04.01`). "The band confirmed" names nobody and holds nobody accountable; it is an assertion wearing an attestation's clothes. The prompt therefore asks **only ever natural persons** — an entity's row is attested by an authorized human or not at all. This does not resolve domain Q-12 (whether the Band *holds* credits), but it constrains it: whatever the answer, the capture surface asks people. |
| DT-13 | Re-offers should be scheduled by elapsed time (T+18h, T+72h) | The obvious implementation; every notification system works this way | ❌ **REJECTED — elapsed-time scheduling is broken by the modal case.** T+18h from a 4am wrap lands at 10pm the next day, which is both useless and hostile, and the 4am wrap is the *normal* session, not an outlier. Re-offers are scheduled by **local wall-clock**: the next local morning, 09:00–11:00. This is a small detail that decides whether the whole re-offer mechanic reads as helpful or as a product that does not know what time it is — and the persona already told us they are exhausted. → D-11 |
| DT-14 | The Operator should be asked to confirm the room's credit row, since it is a claim about their business | Symmetry with every other named party; and a Producer *can* falsely claim they recorded at Abbey Road | ❌ **REJECTED for this feature, real as a problem.** The prompt is a private moment between the people who did the work; pulling the room into it makes a studio a participant in its clients' credit conversation, which is the intrusion the Role Lens already refuses. The room's row still gets captured — the Producer knows where they recorded — and it is corroborated for free by the booking (16/17) with no ask at all. Misappropriation of a room's name is real and is `02.05`'s (a refusable claim), not the prompt's. Worth naming explicitly: **the room row is the only row the prompt captures about a party it never asks.** |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The prompt fires at wrap, while people are physically present — provisional answer to sub-domain Q-03 | An in-room tap converts at a wholly different rate than an emailed request. Confirmation rate is the product's core metric, and physical presence is its largest single input. |
| D-02 | The prompt never blocks session close | DT-01. Blocking trades one session's data for the entire relationship. |
| D-03 | Credit confirmation and split confirmation share the prompt but are separate acts | DT-02, and much harder per DT-09: they have different quorum shapes and different completion criteria. The credit is never held hostage to the split. |
| D-04 | Contributors confirm one row with one tap — never a form | Any additional step is paid for in confirmation rate, which is the only number that matters here. A multi-hyphenate gets one card with several lines, not several cards (`personas.md`). |
| D-05 | **The contributor asks and the Producer's reconciliation are two independently-firing halves.** Both fire within 5 s of the wrap signal; neither gates the other | DT-04. Presence is perishable and unrecoverable at any price; a Producer's accuracy is durable and repairable via `02.01.06`. Gating the first on the second inverts the thesis. Costs nothing structurally — gap rows have no contributor to ask. **Resolves Q-03**: the 4am case is the design, not a variant of it. |
| D-06 | The contributor half asks the **delta** only; one prompt per session-delta; a fire within 20 h with no new rows is suppressed; duplicate fires are no-ops | DT-08. Re-asking confirmed claims nightly on a multi-day session trains the dismissal reflex that kills the prompt that matters. |
| D-07 | A tap — confirmation or reciprocal attestation — attaches to the **claim shown**, not to the row. A changed claim re-asks; it never inherits | A tap that migrates onto text its author never read is a forged signature; where the tap was an attestation, that signature sits at the graph's highest tier. |
| D-08 | The Producer's own row gets an **optional, unchecked** reciprocal line on each contributor's card | DT-05. Otherwise "produced by" is systematically the weakest credit in the room. Optional and unchecked because a pre-checked box manufactures consent, and one skippable line is not a form. |
| D-09 | A non-tap is silence, never a refusal, and is never shown to the Producer as one | People are packing a kit, not making a statement. Rendering silence as refusal turns the product's warmest moment into a social accusation. Only explicit refusals are recorded, and they never auto-escalate (`02.04.01`). |
| D-10 | Notification bodies name **no work and no party** unless the session is explicitly Public | DT-07. A push cannot be un-sent, so reacting to the embargo flag is too late; the default must be inverted. `02.01.05` enforces at query — a lock screen is not a query. |
| D-11 | Re-offers: 3 maximum, scheduled by **local wall-clock** (next local morning, 09:00–11:00), then permanent passive marker. Circuit breaker at 3 consecutive skipped sessions → no push for 30 days | DT-13. Elapsed-time offsets land at 10pm on the modal 4am wrap. Past the breaker, the nagging is our failure, not theirs. Cadence is owner-tunable; the wall-clock basis is not. |
| D-12 | Room mode exists and is the **primary** path offline; its taps are recorded as **witnessed, not attested**, and never rendered as "confirmed by X" | DT-06. It is the only capture path that survives zero connectivity and zero contributor accounts — i.e. the modal studio. It buys accuracy, not provenance. Converts to a real attestation only on ratification from the named party's own account. |
| D-13 | A solo session fires no prompt | DT-11. Nobody to ask, no tier movement, and it is probably the highest-volume session shape — nagging it is how the prompt becomes background noise before reaching the session that matters. |
| D-14 | Corrections are **structured**; free text is a private note to the Producer only, never broadcast to the room | An open free-text field fanned out to every contributor is an abuse surface built into the product's warmest moment (24). |
| D-15 | The prompt asks **only natural persons** | DT-12. An attestation's value is a human accountable for having been in a position to know; "the band confirmed" names nobody. Constrains, but does not resolve, domain Q-12. |
| D-16 | The prompt is scoped to the **session**, never to the work | A record's credits are assembled across months of sessions with disjoint rolls (the mix engineer was never at the tracking date). Asking "are this record's credits complete?" invites the honest answer "no", forever. |
| D-17 | **A contributor's own-row tap is a confirmation, never an attestation, and raises no rung on its own.** The only attestation this prompt collects is the reciprocal line (D-08) | Corrects a drift, not a design: `02.04.01` owns who may answer and its D-11 excludes the credited party from the addressable set — "their own row shows *waiting on others*, never a self-confirm control" — while `02.04.02` D-03/D-08 make self-assertion structurally incapable of moving the rung. The sub-domain's own synthesis already read it this way (CX-04: "a contributor confirming *their own* row is a self-assertion, not corroboration"), as do this file's own DT-11 and the multi-hyphenate edge case. The tap keeps three real jobs — the inline correction (DT-06), the subject's agreement recorded beside the Producer's assertion, and the set's mutual presence corroboration feeding `02.02.04`. What it does not do is put a Musician's credit at rung 5; that is Q-06. |
