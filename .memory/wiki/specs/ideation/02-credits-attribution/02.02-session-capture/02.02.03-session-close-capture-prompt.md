# Feature: Session Close Capture Prompt

> **Number**: 02.02.03
> **Parent**: [Session Capture](./02.02-session-capture-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The single most important moment in the product. `personas.md` names it for both capture personas — the Producer's "the moment that matters: prompts/confirms who did what and what the split is, while everyone is present", and the Musician's "credit + split captured while everyone is present and friendly". This feature is that prompt: the thing that fires before the room empties and converts a session's working data into confirmed facts.

It also exists to defeat one specific named failure: the Producer's worst accidental behavior — *closing a project without capturing splits, "we'll do it later"* — committed by the persona best placed to prevent it. `personas.md` gives the design instruction directly: **make the lazy path the correct path.**

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config (confirms own) | Gets asked, on their own phone, while still in the room: "here's what you're credited for — right?" One tap. This tap is the attestation (`02.04.01`) that separates WeJammin's graph from every self-asserted database. If they are not present, it becomes a notification they will probably ignore — which is why the prompt must fire while they are still there. |
| Producer | Full (triggers it) | Reviews the roll and the log, fixes what is wrong, fires the prompt. Their review is the assertion; everyone else's tap is the confirmation. They can close without capturing — and the design's job is to make that feel like leaving money on the table, not like skipping a form. |
| Operator | None | Not party to the credit conversation. Explicit absence: a studio prompting its clients about their credits would be an intrusion into the client's creative business. The room's own credit is asserted separately (`02.01.01`). |
| Fan | None | Does not exist in this flow. |

## Behavior

The prompt reconciles three inputs — the roll (who was here), the log (what they did), and what is missing — and asks each party to confirm only their own line. The Producer sees the whole picture; each contributor sees one row and one tap.

**Timing is the feature.** Fired too early, the session isn't done. Fired too late, the room is empty and it becomes email — which is the reconstruction workflow the platform exists to replace. The trigger must therefore be tied to a signal that means "we're wrapping" and not to a schedule: the DAW session closing, the booking's end time (domain 16/17), the Producer bouncing a mix, or the Producer saying so.

There is an unresolved tension the sub-domain records as Q-03: capturing *during* interrupts a Producer who is working; capturing *after* means the room is empty. The provisional answer (D-01) is that the prompt fires at wrap while people are still physically present, because a contributor's tap is worth vastly more in the room than the same tap chased by email a week later — the confirmation rate is the whole ballgame.

**What the prompt must not become**: a form. The moment this is a multi-step admin flow, it is skipped, and every argument in `problem-statement.md` about the reconstruction failure applies to WeJammin itself.

### Happy Path

1. Wrap signal fires (DAW session closes / booking ends / Producer bounces / Producer taps "wrap")
2. Producer sees the reconciled picture: roll + log + gaps, ordered by what is missing
3. Producer fixes and fires — one action
4. Each contributor's phone buzzes with one row and one tap; most confirm before they have packed up
5. Confirmed rows become session-captured + attested credits (`02.01.01`, `02.04.02`); unconfirmed rows persist at producer-asserted tier and chase asynchronously

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Producer skips the prompt entirely | The failure mode this feature exists for. Credits persist at producer-asserted tier from the log; the prompt re-offers later, decaying in urgency. It must never block session close — a blocking prompt gets the whole product uninstalled | Dismissible; resurfaces; never blocks |
| Contributor has left already | Becomes an async attestation request (`02.04.01`). Materially lower confirmation rate — this is exactly the loss the timing design exists to avoid | Push/email; credit sits unconfirmed meanwhile |
| Contributor is not a platform user | Their row is asserted, unclaimed; lands in the claim inbox (`02.03.02`) when they join | Producer sees "unclaimed" |
| Nothing was logged and the roll is empty | Prompt has nothing to ask. Degrades to "who was here?" — still better than silence | Minimal roll-first prompt |
| Contributor disputes their row at the prompt | Best possible outcome — disagreement surfacing in the room with everyone present, which is the cheapest moment it will ever be resolvable | Inline correction; Producer adjudicates live |
| Session ends at 4am | The prompt fires into exhaustion. Real, and probably the modal case | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — a lower-effort variant may be needed |
| No connectivity (studio basement) | Prompt runs locally; confirmations queue and sync (sub-domain D-03) | Normal flow with a sync marker |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Armed | Session open, wrap signal not yet seen | Nothing — the prompt is invisible until it is relevant |
| Prompted | Wrap signal fired | Producer's reconciliation view; contributors' one-tap rows |
| Partially confirmed | Some contributors tapped | Per-row status; unconfirmed rows chase async |
| Complete | All rows confirmed | Confirmation; credits live (subject to embargo, `02.01.05`) |
| Skipped | Producer dismissed | Credits at producer-asserted tier; re-offer scheduled |
| Pending sync | Offline | Local confirmations queued |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The prompt should block session close until credits are captured — it is the most important moment, so enforce it | Enforcement instinct; the feature's own importance | ❌ **REJECTED, emphatically.** A tool that blocks a Producer at 4am gets uninstalled at 4:01am, and then the platform is not in the room at all — which forfeits every downstream fact, not just this session's. The persona is a professional under time pressure with a client waiting. **The lazy path must be the correct path (`personas.md`) — that is a statement about gravity, not about gates.** Blocking is the opposite: it makes the correct path expensive and the lazy path (uninstall) permanent. |
| DT-02 | Contributors should confirm credit and split in the same tap, since `personas.md` describes both being captured in this moment | The personas literally pair them: "credit + split captured while everyone is present" | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED as a moment, REJECTED as a tap.** The human moment is shared and the platform should exploit that — this prompt is the natural host for domain 09's split confirmation. But one tap cannot mean both: a credit is "I played this" (uncontroversial, instant), a split is "I own this percentage forever" (consequential, and the Musician's worst accidental behavior is signing a split they don't understand). Fusing them means either trivializing the split or burdening the credit. **Same prompt, two acts, different weight.** The 02↔09 boundary runs straight through this UI and it is the highest-risk intersection on the map. |
| DT-03 | Wrap can be detected reliably from a schedule (the booking's end time) | Bookings exist in 16/17 and have end times | ❌ **REJECTED as a sole trigger** — sessions overrun as a matter of routine, and a prompt that fires at the booked end time while the band is still tracking is noise that trains the Producer to dismiss it. Once dismissal is a habit, the feature is dead. Schedule is one signal among several (DAW close, bounce, manual); the reliable one is probably the DAW, which routes straight back to sub-domain Q-02 — no plugin, no reliable wrap signal, no wedge. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The prompt fires at wrap, while people are physically present — provisional answer to sub-domain Q-03 | An in-room tap converts at a wholly different rate than an emailed request. Confirmation rate is the product's core metric, and physical presence is its largest single input. |
| D-02 | The prompt never blocks session close | DT-01. Blocking trades one session's data for the entire relationship. |
| D-03 | Credit confirmation and split confirmation share the prompt but are separate acts | DT-02. Same moment, different weight, different consequence. |
| D-04 | Contributors confirm one row with one tap — never a form | Any additional step is paid for in confirmation rate, which is the only number that matters here. |
