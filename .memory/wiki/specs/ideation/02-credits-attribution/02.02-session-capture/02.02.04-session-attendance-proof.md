# Feature: Session Attendance Proof

> **Number**: 02.02.04
> **Parent**: [Session Capture](./02.02-session-capture-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

The evidence layer under the roll call: signals that corroborate that a party actually was in the session, so that a credit's provenance tier rests on something more than assertion. Merged from sweep candidate #08, which listed it as a peer of the capture flow; it is not a peer — it has no meaning without a roll to corroborate (sub-domain D-02).

The honest framing: this is **corroboration, not proof**. Every available signal is spoofable by a motivated adversary, and pretending otherwise would be worse than not collecting them.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Config (consents to own) | Chooses whether to contribute corroborating signals (device presence in the room, co-sign of the roll). The upside is a stronger tier on their own credits; the downside is being tracked by a work tool. Must be able to decline without losing the credit — declining lowers the tier, it does not erase the fact. |
| Producer | Full | Sees which roll entries are corroborated and which rest on their word alone. Their own co-sign is itself the strongest routinely-available signal, because they are the trust broker (`personas.md`). |
| Operator | Read-only (room-side signal) | The room can corroborate that a session happened in it — a booking (16/17) that was checked in against is real, independent evidence a session took place. The room does not corroborate *who played what*, only that the session existed and, at most, who came through the door. |
| Fan | None | No visibility. Even in public discography, corroboration is compressed into the provenance rung (`02.04.02`) and rendered to a Fan as **one plain-language evidence sentence** — "Confirmed by 3 people who were there" — never the evidence trail, and never a "verified" badge: labels describe the evidence, not the conclusion (`02.04.02` D-02/D-04, DT-11). Exposing the trail publicly would be surveillance data on professionals. |

## Behavior

Candidate signals, in rough order of strength and inverse order of creepiness:

- **Co-sign** — other people on the roll confirm this person was there. Social, cheap, and the strongest one that does not require surveillance. It is also nearly free, since the close prompt (`02.02.03`) is already collecting taps from the same people.
- **Booking corroboration** — the room's own record that this session happened at this time (domains 16/17). Independent of the participants entirely.
- **Contribution artifacts** — a take, a stem, a file that exists and is attributable. Strong for remote/async contributors, who have no physical presence to corroborate at all.
- **Device/time presence** — the session app open, in the room, during the window. Weak evidence, high creep, spoofable by handing over a phone.
- **Geolocation** — pinpoint presence at the studio. Strongest-looking, most invasive, and trivially spoofed. Its apparent strength is its danger.

The design position at breadth: **prefer signals that are byproducts of the work over signals that surveil the worker.** A co-sign and a stem are things the session produces anyway; geofencing is a tracking system aimed at professionals for whom the tool is supposed to reduce admin, not add monitoring. A capture product that feels like surveillance loses the Producer, and losing the Producer forfeits the whole wedge.

### Happy Path

1. Roll call is populated; session runs
2. Byproduct signals accumulate without anyone doing anything — the close prompt's taps are co-signs, the booking corroborates, the takes exist
3. At close, each roll entry carries whatever corroboration it happens to have
4. Corroboration feeds provenance tier (`02.04.02`) — it does not create, gate or block credits
5. The tier surfaces publicly; the evidence trail does not

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Contributor declines all signals | Credit stands at whatever tier assertion alone supports. Declining must be free — a tool that punishes privacy gets called a tracker | No penalty beyond the tier |
| Remote contributor | No physical presence to corroborate; the artifact (their take) is the evidence. The normal post-2020 case (`problem-statement.md`), so artifact-based corroboration is primary, not a fallback |Corroborated by contribution |
| Everyone co-signs a fabricated session | The attack. Corroboration is only as honest as the corroborators — five colluding people produce a fully corroborated fake session. Topology is the only defence (`02.04.04`), not evidence strength — and that defence is **score-only**: ring demotion never moves the provenance rung (`02.04.02` D-19, `02.04.04` D-08), so it bites on traversal rank, search ordering and dispute weighting and never on the credit's public label. **Accepted cost, stated plainly**: a fully corroborated fiction keeps its top rung indefinitely | `[PENDING — Step 5]` |
| Signals conflict (device says absent, three people co-sign present) | The people win. Devices are proxies; humans were there | Conflict noted, not surfaced publicly |
| Corroboration arrives after the credit published | Tier can rise later. Tier is a current assessment of evidence, not a stamp applied at birth | Tier updates; the credit does not change |
| Studio has no signal | Local capture; corroboration syncs later (sub-domain D-03) | Pending marker |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Geolocation is the strongest attendance proof and should be the primary signal | Intuition — GPS at the studio at the time is hard evidence | ❌ **REJECTED on both counts.** It is trivially spoofed (the adversary who would fabricate a credit will fake a location), so it is not actually strong — it only *looks* strong, which is worse, because a high tier resting on a spoofable signal is a lie the product tells confidently. And it is maximally invasive: geofencing working professionals inside their workplace, in a tool sold as admin relief. **The strongest honest signal is other humans co-signing**, which the close prompt already collects for free. |
| DT-02 | Attendance proof should gate credit creation — no proof, no credit | Purity: the differentiator is verification, so verify or don't record | ❌ **REJECTED** — same error as `02.01.01` DT-02, arriving from a different direction. Gating on evidence means the true-but-unevidenced credit (which is most of them) cannot exist, recreating the empty-database failure the product is built to escape. Evidence sets the **tier**; it never gates the **fact**. This principle keeps recurring across the domain and should probably be a domain-level decision rather than repeated per feature. |
| DT-03 | Corroboration defeats fabricated credits | The feature's implicit purpose | ❌ **REJECTED** — it defeats the *lazy* liar, not the organised one. Five colluding people co-sign each other's fake session and every signal comes back green; the evidence layer reports full confidence in a fiction. Fabrication at scale is a **graph-topology** problem (`02.04.04`), not an evidence-strength problem. Believing otherwise is how a verification product ships a confidently-wrong graph. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Prefer byproduct signals over surveillance signals | DT-01. Co-signs and artifacts are produced by the work anyway; geofencing is monitoring bolted onto a tool sold as admin relief. |
| D-02 | Evidence sets the tier; it never gates the credit | DT-02. Recurs across the domain — proposed for promotion to a domain-level decision. |
| D-03 | Declining signals is free | A privacy-penalising work tool is a tracker, and professionals will treat it as one. |
| D-04 | Humans outrank devices on conflict | Devices are proxies for people; the proxy losing to the principal is the correct default. |
