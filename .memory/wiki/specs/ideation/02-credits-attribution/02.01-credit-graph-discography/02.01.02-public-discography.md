# Feature: Public Discography

> **Number**: 02.01.02
> **Parent**: [Credit Graph & Discography](./02.01-credit-graph-discography-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The public page that renders everything a party has been credited on, with the provenance tier visible on every line. This is the **destination that makes capture worth doing** — a Producer will not run a capture flow to feed a database, but they will to have a page that wins them the next client. It replaces the manually-maintained credits list in a Notes app and the PDF one-sheet (`personas.md`, Musician workaround).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (own page) | Owns their discography page: orders it, pins highlight credits, groups by role (a multi-hyphenate needs "as drummer" and "as mixer" as separate views — one flat list misrepresents them). Cannot add a credit here; the page is a projection of `02.01.01`. Can hide a credit from the page without deleting the record. |
| Producer | Full (own page) | Same page mechanics, but their discography is their sales collateral — the thing a client checks before booking. Their page also shows works where they were the *capturer*, which is a distinct reputational signal from being a contributor. |
| Operator | Full (the room's discography) | The studio/venue's page: "records made here". Directly commercial for an Operator — a studio's discography is why it gets booked. Populated by room credits, not by member credits. |
| Fan | Read-only | Browses any public discography; follows a party from it; walks from a record they love to the humans on it. This is the AllMusic/Discogs fan behavior and it is the graph's only organic distribution channel — every fan-facing credit page is a public entry point. Sees public-tier records only; embargoed and contested-suppressed records do not exist for them. |

## Behavior

A discography is a **query, not a document** — it is the current projection of the credit graph for one party. Nothing is authored here. The page's job is to make provenance legible without making it noisy: a reader must be able to tell at a glance which credits are session-captured-and-attested versus self-asserted-and-unconfirmed, because that distinction is the entire competitive claim against Jaxsta, Muso.AI and Discogs.

Grouping is by role first, chronology second. A flat reverse-chronological list is the wrong default for the multi-hyphenate — it renders a drummer who occasionally mixes as an incoherent smear.

### Happy Path

1. Fan or client lands on a party's discography (from search, a work page, a shared link, or an artist's own link-in-bio)
2. System renders the party's credits, grouped by role, provenance tier shown per line
3. Reader filters ("as producer", "2024–2026", "with <other party>") or walks to any work or co-contributor
4. Reader acts: follows (Fan), or opens a service enquiry / booking (client)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Party has zero credits | Empty state that offers the two on-ramps, not an apology | "Import your existing credits" (`02.03.01`) / "Start a session" (`02.02`) |
| Party has only unconfirmed self-asserted credits | Page renders, every line tier-marked. Honest but visibly weak — this is intentional pressure toward attestation | Tier markers throughout; prompt to request attestation (`02.04.01`) |
| Credit embargoed | Absent from the public page entirely — not greyed out, not "hidden credit" | Nothing. The owner sees it on their private view with an embargo marker |
| Party merged into another (`02.01.04`) | Old page 301s to the survivor; both credit sets union'd | Redirect; combined page |
| Massively prolific party (session player with 4,000 credits) | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — pagination/aggregation strategy undecided; a 4,000-row page is a real case, not a hypothetical | `[PENDING]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Page requested | Skeleton rows |
| Empty | No credits for this party | Empty state with import / start-session CTAs |
| Populated | Credits exist | Role-grouped list with tier markers |
| Sparse | Few credits, all low-tier | Populated list plus an attestation nudge to the owner (owner-only, never shown to visitors) |
| Error | Query failed | Retry; never a blank page — this URL is public and indexed |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The discography page is the product's primary organic acquisition surface, not just a profile tab | Every incumbent it replaces (Discogs, AllMusic, Jaxsta) is load-bearing for SEO; a credit page is the canonical answer to "who played X on Y" | ✅ **CONFIRMED** — and it inverts the cold-start problem: the page a Producer builds to win clients is simultaneously the page that pulls in the four uncredited players on it via the claim inbox (`02.03.02`). Capture → page → claim → more capture is the platform's only self-reinforcing loop. This makes the page a MUST, not a nice-to-have. |
| DT-02 | Fans do not care about credits — this page is professional-only | The Fan persona has "no professional stake" and "will not tolerate professional-tool complexity" | ❌ **REJECTED** — Discogs and AllMusic are fan products, not professional ones. "Who played that solo" is mainstream fan behavior. But the persona constraint still binds: the Fan's view must be a *reading* surface with no tier jargon, no claim affordances, no attestation prompts. Same data, different product — which is exactly what `personas.md` says the Fan surface must be. |
| DT-03 | Showing provenance tiers publicly will embarrass users into not using the product | Adversarial: nobody wants "unconfirmed" next to their name | ❌ **REJECTED** — inverted. The tier is the *incentive engine*: an unconfirmed line is a visible gap the owner can close in one tap (`02.04.01`), and the person who can close it is a collaborator we then acquire. Hiding tiers would remove both the differentiator and the growth loop. Risk is real but it is a copy/design problem, not a model problem. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Role-grouped, not flat-chronological, by default | The multi-hyphenate is the structural centre persona (`personas.md`). A flat list of a drummer/mixer/writer's credits reads as noise and misrepresents the career it is meant to prove. |
| D-02 | Provenance tier is shown publicly on every line | It is the competitive claim. A credits page that doesn't show how it knows is Jaxsta with better typography. |
| D-03 | The Fan view is the same data with a different product surface | Per `personas.md` Fan unique constraint — no tier jargon, no claim/attest affordances, reading only. |
