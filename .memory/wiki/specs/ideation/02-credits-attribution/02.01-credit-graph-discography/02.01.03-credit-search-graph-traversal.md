# Feature: Credit Search & Graph Traversal

> **Number**: 02.01.03
> **Parent**: [Credit Graph & Discography](./02.01-credit-graph-discography-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

The queries that only exist because the credits are a **graph** rather than a list: who played on this, what else have they played on, who have they played *with*, who is two hops from me and plays pedal steel. A discography answers questions about one party; traversal answers questions about the relationships between parties, which is the thing no liner note and no flat credits database can do.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | The warm-intro query: "who has played with someone I've played with, plays what I need, and is near me". This is how a dep gets found at 9pm for a 10am session. Also the self-serving query: "prove I've worked with X" when a client asks. |
| Producer | Full | Casting a session from the graph rather than from memory — "find me a bassist who has played on records with this drummer" — and vetting: does this player's claimed history actually connect to anyone I trust? |
| Operator | Full | "Which artists in my region have credits with people who've recorded here" — a booking-lead query. Also verification of an enquiry: is this band real, and who vouches for them? |
| Fan | Read-only (public tier only) | The rabbit-hole: from a record they love to the players on it to everything else those players touched. No connection-degree queries, no proximity queries, no contact affordances — a fan traversing to "people 2 hops from this artist" is a harassment vector (`personas.md`, Fan anti-persona: "harasses artists through open contact surfaces"). |

## Behavior

Three query shapes, one graph:

1. **Lookup** — resolve a work or a party, land on its ledger or discography.
2. **Co-contribution** — the edge that matters. Two parties are connected if they hold credits on the same work. Degree, frequency and recency of co-credit are the substance of "who do you actually work with".
3. **Constrained traversal** — co-contribution plus filters that make it actionable: role, instrument, era, genre, geography, provenance tier floor.

The tier floor is not a nicety. A traversal that treats a self-asserted credit as equal to a session-captured attested one is trivially poisoned — anyone can claim a credit with a famous producer and appear one hop from them. **Traversal must be tier-weighted or the graph is a reputation-laundering machine.** This is the same attack surface `02.04.04` polices from the other side.

### Happy Path

1. User enters a query — a name, a work, or a constrained need ("drummer, played with X, UK, available")
2. System resolves entities and traverses the graph, weighting edges by provenance tier
3. Results render with the *path* visible, not just the endpoint — "2 hops: you → <shared session> → them"
4. User walks the path to verify it, then acts: message, invite to project, open a service enquiry

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Query returns nobody | Empty state that relaxes a constraint rather than dead-ending | "No pedal steel within 2 hops — 47 within 3 hops" |
| Sparse graph (early platform) | Below the density floor (< 20 reachable attested-tier-or-higher nodes in the query's constraint scope, D-04) co-contribution is disabled and traversal degrades to lookup | Honest empty states; import CTA (`02.03.01`). This is the cold-start problem in its most visible form |
| Name collision (two players, same name) | Traversal is ambiguous until resolution (`02.04`) runs | Disambiguation prompt; both candidates shown with distinguishing credits |
| Result includes an embargoed credit | Never — embargo suppresses at query, not at render (`02.01.05` D-01) | The path simply doesn't exist for that user |
| Adversary constructs fake edges to appear near a famous party | Tier weighting demotes low-tier edges (D-01); ring detection (`02.04.04`) silently applies its existing per-attestation-edge demotion through the derived traversal weight | The edge remains subject to ordinary publication, viewer-visibility, embargo, provenance-floor, role, query-shape, fan-safety, and normal result-window gates. If returned, its path renders normally: no collusion-specific suppression, mark, hidden-result notice, annotation, detector explanation, or metadata. The demotion may lower ordinary rank or leave the edge outside a finite ordinary result window, but it never creates a separate visibility threshold or unweighted bypass (D-05). |
| Fan reaches a degree/proximity/contact query by URL manipulation or API poke | Blocked at the query layer, not the render layer (D-03) — the restricted query shape does not execute for a Fan-tier session regardless of how it was reached | Fan sees only the work↔party walk they are entitled to; the proximity result never materialises |
| Graph enumeration / scraping — actor crawls traversals to rebuild the credits DB off-platform | Per-session traversal rate-limited; deep multi-hop queries cost more budget than shallow lookups; abnormal breadth-first enumeration is throttled and flagged to `24 Trust & Safety` | Normal users never hit the ceiling; a scraper hits `429`-style backoff and a "slow down" state |
| A party on the path has gone private / deleted their account mid-traversal | Their node is suppressed from paths for viewers who lack access; a path that depended on them is treated as non-existent (same mechanic as embargo, `02.01.05`) | The path simply isn't offered; no "hidden node" placeholder that leaks the party's existence |
| User queries their own proximity to themselves / a party they already share a credit with | 0-hop / 1-hop resolves trivially to the shared work; no traversal needed | Direct co-credit shown ("you both played on <work>"), not a multi-hop path |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Idle | Search surface opened | Suggested queries seeded from the user's own graph |
| Loading | Query submitted | Skeleton results; multi-hop traversal is not instant |
| Populated | Results found | Ranked results with visible paths |
| Empty | No matches | Constraint-relaxation suggestions |
| Disabled | Constraint scope has < 20 reachable attested-tier-or-higher nodes (D-04) | Co-contribution not offered; Lookup only + import CTA — "Not enough credits yet in this region/role" |
| Degraded | Constraint scope has 20–99 reachable attested-tier-or-higher nodes (D-04) | Co-contribution runs with a "low confidence — sparse in this region/role" banner + import CTA |
| Error | Traversal failed/timed out | Retry with reduced hop depth |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Traversal must be tier-weighted, or the credit graph becomes a reputation-laundering machine | Musician anti-persona: "games the verified-credit graph to manufacture reputation" | ✅ **CONFIRMED** — and it is the most consequential finding in this feature. An unweighted graph lets any user manufacture proximity to any famous party by self-asserting one credit. The traversal is the *surface where the fraud pays off*, so it is where the weighting must bite. Any spec that treats search as a read-only convenience over the graph has missed this. |
| DT-02 | Co-contribution edges should decay with time | Domain-expert expectation: "who I work with" is a present-tense claim; a session in 2009 is history, not a relationship | ✅ **CONFIRMED** — recency must weight the edge. But the credit itself must *not* decay: the 2009 record is permanently true and permanently on the discography. The decay is a property of the **relationship inference**, not the fact. Distinguishing these is easy to get wrong and would silently corrupt the discography. |
| DT-03 | This feature is really just the search cross-cut with a music skin | Adversarial: every domain has search; platform search is surely a cross-cut | ❌ **REJECTED** — the *retrieval* is a cross-cut (indexing, ranking infra, query UX). The *graph traversal semantics* — co-credit edges, tier weighting, hop paths, recency decay — are domain-owned and exist nowhere else on the platform. Gear search does not have hops. The cross-cut is recorded; the semantics stay here. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Traversal is tier-weighted by default, with no unweighted mode | DT-01. An unweighted mode would be the exploit, and someone would ship a link to it. |
| D-02 | Paths are shown, not just endpoints | The claim "you are 2 hops from this person" is only trustworthy if the user can inspect the two hops. It is also the warm intro's substance — you need to know *who* to name. |
| D-03 | Fan traversal is restricted to work↔party walks; no degree/proximity/contact queries | `personas.md` Fan anti-persona is explicitly a harassment vector; a people-finding graph handed to fans is that vector armed. |
| D-04 | Density floor for co-contribution: **disabled** when the query's constraint scope (region × role/instrument, at the requested hop depth) has **< 20** reachable **attested-tier-or-higher** nodes; **degraded** (low-confidence banner) at **20–99**; **normal** at **≥ 100**. Self-asserted-tier edges do not count toward the floor. | Q-01. Shipping a broken-feeling search is worse than not shipping it. Counting only tier-weighted (attested+) nodes means a poisoned self-asserted graph cannot fake its way past the floor — consistent with D-01. |
| D-05 | Suspected-ring demotion changes ordinary traversal rank, not presentation — and **only** rank: the demotion moves the continuous internal score, never the provenance rung (`02.04.02` D-19, `02.04.04` D-08), so traversal is where the whole defence lands. | CQ-04 Option C, extended by DQ-06.1. An otherwise eligible edge consumes its silently demoted derived traversal weight and, if it survives all ordinary gates and the ordinary result window, renders with the same path affordances as any other returned edge. No collusion-specific threshold, hiding, label, tooltip, placeholder, rationale, notification, or detector metadata exists. `02.04.04` remains internal-only; topology alone cannot create a Domain 24 case or notice. |
