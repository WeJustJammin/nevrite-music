# Feature: Credit Record & Contribution Ledger

> **Number**: 02.01.01
> **Parent**: [Credit Graph & Discography](./02.01-credit-graph-discography-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The atomic unit of the whole platform's thesis: one assertion that **one party, in one role, contributed to one work**. Everything else in domain 02 either creates this record (capture, claiming), qualifies it (attestation, provenance tier), renders it (discography, search), or fixes it (amendment, dispute). The **ledger** is the set of these records for a given work — the answer to "who made this."

Its reason to exist is `problem-statement.md`'s root cause: *no system is present at the moment of creation, so the record is always a reconstruction*. This feature is the thing the platform is present in order to write. Every design call below resolves the same way — toward recording what was asserted, by whom, when, and on what evidence, and away from adjudicating whether it is so (domain D-10).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (own credits) | Sees every credit naming them across every work — captured, claimed, or imported — each with its provenance tier and its **assertion gap** (D-04: "tracked 1994, recorded here 2026"). Can propose a self-asserted credit on a work, accept an attestation request, set per-credit visibility (`02.01.05`), and propose an amendment (`02.01.06`). Cannot silently delete an attested credit (retraction is `02.04.03`, which leaves a trace). Cannot publish a credit naming *someone else* onto that person's page (D-10) — it queues for them. As a multi-hyphenate, routinely holds two records on one work (D-01) with two independently-derived tiers. |
| Producer | Full (sessions they ran) | Creates records for everyone in the room as a byproduct of running the session (`02.02`). Sees the full ledger for their works including credits they did not create. Their assertion carries a higher provenance tier than a self-claim — they are the trust broker (`personas.md`) — **except on their own credits, where they are the interested party and the record says so** (D-05). Sets the ledger's billing order (D-12). Marks a contribution as not-in-final-master at delivery (D-09) but cannot delete it. Withdraws a work; cannot destroy other parties' credits on it (D-08). |
| Operator | Read-only + own credit | Sees credits that name their room ("Recorded at X") and asserts the room's own credit on a session hosted there — the room is a **party**, not a location field (sub-domain D-03). Can contest a false room credit. Has no visibility into who played what on a session they merely hosted unless the Producer grants it. A room's credit and a room's staff's credits are separate records naming separate parties. |
| Fan | None | Never touches the record directly. Reads its published projection through `02.01.02` / `02.01.03`. Explicit absence: fans have no write path into the graph at any tier — self-assertion requires a professional identity link, or the graph becomes Discogs (crowd-edited and unattributable, `02.01.06` D-04). They also never see the ledger's internals: no assertion gap, no asserter identity, no origin, or owner-only comp-out trigger date/reason. When a not-in-final-master credit is otherwise public, `02.01.02` renders its outcome in plain language. |

## Behavior

A credit record binds:

| What it asserts | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Party** | A resolved identity — person, band, or organization (`02.01.04`, domain 01). Not always a person (`personas.md`; D-07). |
| **Role** | Chosen from the shared taxonomy (`02.06`), never free text. Optionally an instrument, on a separate axis (`02.06` D-02). |
| **Work** | A recording, a composition, or both — and which one is not optional (D-06). |
| **Contribution date** | When the work happened. |
| **Assertion date** | When this platform first recorded it. The **gap** between the two is the provenance signal (D-04). |
| **Asserter** | Who is making this claim — always recorded, including when the asserter is the party (D-05). |
| **Origin** | Which mechanism created it: session capture (`02.02`), self-assertion, external import (`02.03.01`). |
| **Provenance tier** | Derived, never stamped (`02.04.02` D-01, domain D-09). Read from this record's evidence; not a field this feature sets. |
| **Visibility** | Public / Embargoed / Private (`02.01.05`). Enforced at the record, evaluated at query. |

Detail (take-level, gear used, session reference, attendance evidence) hangs off the record rather than living in it. Evidence is **bound by reference, never inlined** (DT-09).

The record is deliberately **thin and non-negotiable about ownership**: a credit asserts contribution and asserts nothing about splits, royalties, or chain of title. A session drummer has a credit and zero ownership — collapsing these is the exact error the domain exists to avoid (domain index D-01). Splits live in domain 09.

### Uniqueness and accretion

At most one record exists for any given **(party, role, work)** — D-03. Two sessions for one track with the same drummer produce **one** credit with two evidence sets, not two credit lines. Evidence accretes to the record; the record does not multiply. This is what keeps a discography readable: one line per fact, however many times the fact was witnessed.

The corollary is D-01's other half: the same party in *two* roles produces two records, because they are two facts with independent evidence, independent attestation, and independent dispute surfaces.

### The publication gate (D-10)

Domain D-08 is absolute: **evidence sets the tier; it never gates the credit.** Existence and tier are settled — a true fact with weak evidence beats no fact.

Publication onto *someone else's* page is a different axis, and it has a fabrication vector D-08 does not cover. A bare self-assertion can name any party in the world; without a gate, "Questlove — drums — my demo" lands on Questlove's public discography as an unconfirmed line he never made and never saw. The graph would manufacture the exact anti-persona behavior `personas.md` warns about ("inflates credits", "games the verified-credit graph"), using its own openness as the weapon.

A record publishes to a party's public discography when **any one** of these holds:

- **(a)** That party asserted it themselves.
- **(b)** That party attested it (`02.04.01`).
- **(c)** Its origin has independent standing — session capture where they were present (`02.02`), or external import of an assertion already published in the world (`02.03.01`).

The gated case is narrow and exact: **a self-asserted credit naming a party other than the asserter, with no session capture and no external source.** It exists, it is tiered honestly, it is visible to the asserter and to the named party's claim inbox (`02.03.02`) — and it is invisible to the public until the named party acts. No credit is destroyed; one is merely not yet spoken in someone else's name.

### The not-in-final-master qualifier (D-09)

A player tracks parts that get comped out three months later. The session happened; the master does not contain them. Both facts are true, and capture-at-source only sees the first — so a system that captures at session close and never revisits will publish credits the liner notes do not have, generating credit inflation *by construction*.

The record therefore carries an outcome qualifier, set by the Producer at delivery, defaulting to **contributed**. Marking a contribution not-in-final-master is an assertion, not an erasure: the record survives, the tier is untouched, and `02.07` (union session reporting) still needs it — under AFM terms a player who tracked is owed whether or not the take survived. **Removing** the credit entirely is an amendment (`02.01.06`) requiring the credited party's agreement, never a Producer-side delete. Otherwise "comped out" becomes a one-click tool for erasing a person from a record they played on.

When the retained record otherwise passes its ordinary publication, visibility, embargo, and public-work-identity gates, `02.01.02` renders it publicly with the plain-language qualifier **"not in final master."** The qualifier does not publish, unembargo, or otherwise widen a record's visibility; it does not alter tier, ownership, union reporting, rights, registration, royalty, or payment treatment. The comp-out trigger date and reason remain owner-only. Domain 10 retains the separate question of whether the contribution registers for neighbouring rights.

### Withdrawal, not deletion (D-08)

The credit is an assertion about an event in the world. Deleting a project cannot un-happen the session. Works are **withdrawn** (removed from the withdrawer's surfaces, visibility falls back to participants-only per `02.01.05`), never deleted; credits on a withdrawn work survive at full tier, remain evidence, remain disputable, and remain royalty-relevant. A Producer withdrawing a project is exercising a control over their own page — not a control over four other people's careers.

### Bands (D-07)

Liner notes do both: "Performed by The X" *and* the member list. These are two facts at two granularities, and **neither is derived from the other**:

- Auto-fanning a band credit out to members gives a false credit to the drummer who missed that session.
- Auto-rolling member credits up to a band credit falsely bills the band for a session two members played on.

So a Band (an entity, domain 01, per D-19) holds credits directly, as a party, alongside its members' independent records. A band credit means what it meant on its contribution date: if the lineup changes in 2029, the 2019 band credit does not re-attribute (the same principle as `02.06` D-04 — history is not rewritten through a lookup table).

### Happy Path

1. Producer closes a session in `02.02`; the close prompt (`02.02.03`) emits one **draft** record per contributor per role
2. System resolves each party to an identity — existing user, unclaimed shell, or new (`02.01.04`) — and each role to the taxonomy (`02.06`), showing the match so a wrong one is caught
3. System stamps contribution date (the session's date) and assertion date (now); for a captured session the gap is ~0 — **this is the thesis, made legible on the record**
4. Producer confirms the roll; records leave draft. Records where the Producer is themselves the party are marked asserter == party (D-05) so the tier model cannot read a self-credit as a broker's assertion
5. Each named contributor is asked to confirm their own record (`02.04.01`); the Producer's assertion already stands behind it
6. On confirmation the record's evidence gains an attestation and its tier re-derives to session-captured + attested — the highest tier there is (`02.04.02`)
7. Record becomes visible per the session's embargo setting (`02.01.05`); if the session was not confidential it is public immediately
8. Record appears on the work's ledger, in the Producer's declared billing order (D-12), and on every named party's discography (`02.01.02`) subject to the publication gate (D-10)
9. At delivery the Producer marks any contribution that did not reach the final master (D-09); the affected party is notified

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Contributor is not a WeJammin user** | Record is created against an unclaimed identity shell; sits in the claim inbox (`02.03.02`) awaiting them. Origin is session capture, so the publication gate passes on limb (c) | Producer sees "credited — unclaimed"; the human sees nothing until they join and claim. Consent for shells is domain Q-11 |
| **Contributor never confirms** | Record persists at producer-asserted tier, not attested. It does **not** vanish and is **never** purged on a timer — a true fact with weak evidence beats no fact (domain D-08) | "Credited by \<Producer\> — unconfirmed", with the tier model's gap-closing affordance (`02.04.02` D-05) |
| **Same party credited in two roles on one work** | Two records, not one with a role array (D-01). A drummer who also engineered has two distinct facts with distinct evidence | Two rows on the work's ledger; two independently-derived tiers |
| **Same party, same role, two sessions for one work** | **One** record; the second session's evidence accretes to it (D-03) | One discography line; "witnessed across 2 sessions" in the evidence detail |
| **Party disputes a record naming them** | Record enters contested state (`02.05`); **stays on the work's ledger** with a contested marker rather than disappearing. It contributes **zero weight** to derived surfaces (search ranking `02.01.03`, tier-derived reputation) while contested. Separately, the named party may set it Private on their own page (`02.01.05`) — a control they already have, exercised independently of the contest | Contested badge on the work's ledger for all who can see the work. On the disputing party's own discography: gone, if they set it Private |
| **A contest is filed to censor a true credit** | This is why suppression-on-filing is refused. If contesting hid a credit from the work's ledger, contesting would be a one-click censorship tool and every inconvenient fact would be contested. The contest marks; it never suppresses (D-11) | Contested badge — visible, not absent |
| **Work is deleted / project abandoned** | Works are **withdrawn, never deleted** (D-08). Credits survive at full tier; visibility falls back to participants-only (`02.01.05` "Embargoed — indefinite"); the record stays evidence, stays disputable, stays royalty-relevant | Withdrawer sees it gone from their surfaces. Credited parties see it privately, marked "withdrawn — unreleased". Public sees nothing (`02.01.05` D-04: absent, not marked hidden) |
| **Producer credits themselves** | Origin is session capture but asserter == party. The record records this (D-05) so `02.04.02` derives a self-assertion tier for that line, not the broker tier it derives for the lines the Producer asserted *about others*. Without this the trust broker's own credits launder into medium tier by sitting in a captured session — the exploit `personas.md` names as their worst intentional behavior | Producer's own line reads "asserted by you", not "captured"; rises only when someone else in the room attests it |
| **Third party self-asserts a credit naming a famous stranger** | Publication gate (D-10). Record exists, tier is lowest, invisible to the public; lands in the named party's claim inbox. If they attest it, it publishes; if they contest it, `02.05`; if they never act, it never publishes and never dies | Asserter: "sent for confirmation — not published yet". Named party: an item in their inbox. Public: nothing |
| **Self-asserted credit on a work the asserter has a real claim to** | Publishes on limb (a) — their own page, their own assertion, lowest tier, honestly labelled. It also asserts something about *someone else's work*, so the work's owner is notified and may contest | Asserter's page shows it as "self-asserted". Work owner gets a notice with a contest affordance |
| **Bulk self-assertion — 500 credits on famous records** | Each is tiered honestly and each names a work whose owner is notified. **The volume itself is a signal this feature does not act on** — see Q-06; acting on it would collide with domain D-08 | Individually honest; collectively a T&S surface (domain 24) |
| **Band credit and member credits on one work** | Independent records, neither derived from the other (D-07). Both may exist; either may exist alone | Ledger shows "The X — performed by" and the individual member lines separately |
| **Band lineup changed since the credit** | The 2019 band credit does not re-attribute to the 2029 lineup. A band credit means the band on its contribution date | Band's discography unchanged; new members are not retroactively credited |
| **Who attests for a band?** | Unresolved — an entity has no fingers. See Q-04. Until resolved, band credits cannot rise above their asserter's tier | Band credit sits at asserter tier; no attestation affordance |
| **Contribution comped out of the final master** | Qualified, not deleted (D-09). Tier untouched. `02.07` still needs it for AFM reporting. When otherwise publicly eligible, `02.01.02` renders it once with the plain-language qualifier "not in final master"; the qualifier never widens visibility or exposes the trigger date/reason. | Player is notified. Owner sees the marker and trigger date; a public viewer sees the qualified line only when ordinary gates allow it. |
| **Producer tries to delete a comped-out player's credit** | Refused. Removal is an amendment (`02.01.06`) requiring the credited party's agreement — and changing the *party* is never an amendment at all (`02.01.06` D-03), it is a dispute | "That removes someone's credit — they have to agree" |
| **Two Producers on one session assert conflicting ledgers** | Both assertions are recorded. Contradictory records on one work (two parties each credited as sole producer) are **surfaced as a contradiction**, not silently unioned — the same rule merge applies (`02.01.04` edge cases). Routed to `02.01.06`, escalating to `02.05` on disagreement | "Two people asserted different producer credits on this work" |
| **Two amendments proposed on one record concurrently** | Serialized per record; the second proposer sees the first proposal rather than a stale record. Last-write-wins would silently drop a correction (sub-domain CX-03 Q1) | "Someone else has already proposed a change to this credit" |
| **Merge collapses two parties who each hold a credit on the same work in the same role** | Post-merge they are one (party, role, work) — the two records collapse to one and their evidence unions (D-03). If their roles differ, both survive (D-01) | One line where there were two; both evidence sets retained in history |
| **Merge collapses parties where one credit is embargoed and one public** | Visibility is a property of **each record** and is never inherited from the survivor (D-02 of the sub-domain; `02.01.05` D-02). The merged party's page shows the public credit and withholds the embargoed one. The race noted in sub-domain CX-01 Q5 is resolved *here* by the record refusing to inherit | No leak; the embargoed line stays participants-only |
| **Attestation arrives, then is retracted** | Tier re-derives downward (`02.04.03`, `02.04.02` DT-01). The record does not change; its evidence did | Tier label changes on a page others may have cited; credited party notified |
| **Role does not exist in the taxonomy** | Captured as a pending alias; the credit proceeds (`02.06` D-03). Blocking a fact to protect a vocabulary inverts their importance | "We'll add that" — never a dead end |
| **Party resolution fails for one contributor mid-capture** | That record holds unresolved (`02.01.04` "Unresolved"); the rest of the ledger commits. A partial ledger is a real ledger — the alternative is discarding four true credits to punish one ambiguous name | Producer sees "1 contributor needs identifying"; the other 4 records are live |
| **Network failure mid-capture; drafts partially emitted** | Drafts are not credits. An interrupted capture leaves the session's roll re-openable rather than a half-committed ledger; nothing publishes from a draft (`02.02`) | "Session roll incomplete — reopen to finish" |
| **Work has zero credits** | Legal and common — imported bare, or capture never ran. The ledger is empty, not broken | Empty state (see Ledger View States) |
| **Contribution date precedes the party's existence** | Data error, not a merge candidate — a band credited in 2018 that formed in 2020 is a bad import or a typo. Held for correction (`02.01.06`), same as conflicting identifiers (`02.01.04` edge cases) | "This date is before this band existed" flag |
| **Party is an organization that dissolves** | The record survives; the studio closed, the session still happened. Amendment rights on a dissolved org's credit inherit the same gap as a deceased contributor's — sub-domain Q-02 | Credit unchanged; no live amendment path |
| **Deceased contributor** | Record survives and keeps earning (session players' credits feed neighbouring rights after death). Who controls visibility and amendment is sub-domain Q-02; who adjudicates their merges is `02.01.04` Q-02 | Credit unchanged; controls inert |
| **Erasure request from a named party** | Collides with the joint-fact problem — see DT-03 and Q-01. No behavior is specified here because the answer is legal, not preferential | `[Open Question Q-01]` |
| **Fan attempts to add or edit a credit** | No path exists at any tier. Not a permission error — an absent affordance (`02.01.06` D-04) | Nothing; the surface does not offer it |

### Record Lifecycle States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Draft | Capture emitted it; session roll not closed | Editable row in the session's roll call; no discography presence, no tier, no publication |
| Unacknowledged | Self-asserted by a party other than the named one, no capture, no import (D-10) | Asserter: "sent for confirmation". Named party: claim-inbox item. Public: absent |
| Unconfirmed | Session closed (or import landed); named party has not responded | Visible with provenance tier "asserted by \<party\>" and the gap-closing affordance |
| Attested | Named party confirmed (`02.04.01`) | Evidence-describing label ("confirmed by 4 people present") — never the word "verified" (`02.04.02` D-02) |
| Embargoed | Work unreleased or session confidential (`02.01.05`) | Visible only to session participants; absent — not marked hidden — from public discography, search and export |
| Withdrawn | Parent work withdrawn (D-08) | Participants only, marked "withdrawn — unreleased". Record intact, tier intact, evidence intact |
| Contested | Dispute filed (`02.05`) | Contested marker on the work's ledger; zero weight in derived surfaces; downstream royalty/reputation effects held |
| Superseded | Amendment accepted (`02.01.06`) or merge collapsed it (`02.01.04`) | Replaced by successor; history retains the original and who changed it |

> Two of these are qualifiers layered on a state rather than states themselves: **not-in-final-master** (D-09) and **asserter == party** (D-05). Both persist across every transition above; neither is a lifecycle position.

### Ledger View States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Ledger requested | Skeleton rows at the ledger's known length where the count is known; otherwise a neutral skeleton. Never a spinner over a populated ledger — a tier re-derivation must not blank a page (`02.04.02` "Re-deriving: transient; should be invisible") |
| Empty | Work exists with zero visible records | For a Producer on their own work: the capture CTA — this is the platform's whole job, undone. For everyone else: "No credits recorded" — **indistinguishable from an entirely embargoed ledger** (`02.01.05` D-04), which is the point |
| Populated | Records resolved and visible to this viewer | Ledger in the work owner's billing order (D-12), each line carrying role, party, tier and any qualifier. Viewer-scoped: two viewers of one work legitimately see different ledgers |
| Partial | Some records visible, some suppressed by embargo or unresolved party | Visible records render. Suppressed ones are **absent with no count** (`02.01.05` D-04). Unresolved parties render as a placeholder line to the Producer only ("1 contributor needs identifying") — never to the public |
| Error | Ledger cannot be read | "Couldn't load credits" with retry. **Never renders a partial ledger as if it were whole** — a silently-truncated credit list is a false negative about who played on a record, which is the failure this domain exists to prevent |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The credit record should carry the split percentage, since capture happens at the same moment | Naive reading of "capture at source"; the split-sheet moment in `personas.md` | ❌ **REJECTED** — the domain's own charter says a session drummer has a credit and zero ownership. Splits are domain 09. Capturing them in the same *moment* (one UX flow) is right; storing them in the same *record* is the conflation the domain exists to prevent. The UX adjacency is a CX (02 ↔ 09), not a field. |
| DT-02 | An unconfirmed credit should be hidden until attested, so the graph is only ever "verified" | Purity argument — the differentiator is verification, so show only verified things | ❌ **REJECTED** — this kills the cold start and punishes the truth. Most true credits will never be confirmed (people move on, die, don't join). Hiding them recreates the empty-database failure. The answer is a **visible provenance tier**, not suppression: show the fact, show how well we know it. This is the whole reason `02.04.02` exists, and it is domain D-08. |
| DT-03 | A contributor must be able to hard-delete a credit naming them | Privacy intuition; GDPR erasure reflex | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED WITH CONSTRAINT** — a right-to-erasure request is real and statutory. But a credit is a *joint* fact: the Producer and three other players also assert it, and erasure would rewrite their history. Likely resolution is de-identification (the contribution survives, the personal link is severed), not deletion. This is a genuine legal/product collision — escalated to `/create-prd-security` and Q-01 below. |
| DT-04 | The ledger is an unordered set — order is a rendering preference | The record model as written: party + role + work, no position | ✅ **CONFIRMED AS A GAP; resolved by D-12.** A domain expert would have caught this instantly: **billing order is contractual**. "Featuring", primary vs featured artist, and the sequence of production credits are negotiated positions people take less money for, and DDEX RIN (`02.08`) carries a display sequence — an unordered ledger cannot emit a valid one. But order is **relative**, so it cannot live on an individual record: a credit does not know it is third. It is a property of the **ledger**, asserted by the work owner. It is also the one thing here that is *not a fact about contribution* — it is an editorial/contractual decision, so it is not attestable and must not feed the tier. Getting this wrong makes billing order look like evidence. |
| DT-05 | A credit captured at session close is a credit on the released record | The implicit assumption of capture-at-source, and the assumption every step of the happy path made before this pass | ✅ **CONFIRMED AS A GAP; resolved by D-09.** Parts get comped out, replaced, and re-cut months after the session the platform witnessed. A system that captures at close and never revisits publishes credits the liner notes do not have — **the product generates credit inflation by construction**, committing `personas.md`'s Musician worst-intentional behavior on the user's behalf. The two facts (played on the session / is on the master) are genuinely different, and the industry disagrees with itself about them: AFM says a player who tracked is owed; liner notes say if you're not on it you're not on it. The record keeps the contribution and qualifies the outcome. The one thing it must never do is let the Producer delete the player — that is the erasure vector wearing a workflow's clothes. |
| DT-06 | Deleting a project deletes its credits | The default assumption of every parent-child data model, and an open marker this pass inherited from the breadth pass | ❌ **REJECTED; resolved by D-08.** The credit asserts an event in the world. Deleting a project cannot un-happen the session, and the deleter is a Producer — so a delete would let one party destroy four other people's evidence of their own careers. That is the same joint-fact problem as DT-03, arriving through a mundane workflow instead of a legal request, which is exactly why nobody designs for it. Works are withdrawn; credits survive; visibility falls back to participants. The abandoned project is also the *high-value* case: the shelved album whose credits get argued about in six years is precisely the record that must not have been garbage-collected. |
| DT-07 | Only people hold credits; a band credit is shorthand for its members | The person-centric reflex, and an open marker this pass inherited from the breadth pass | ❌ **REJECTED; resolved by D-07.** `personas.md` is explicit that a persona is not always a person and that the Band is a first-class entity (domain 01, D-19). Liner notes carry both "Performed by The X" *and* the member list because they are two facts at two granularities. Derivation in either direction fabricates: fan-out credits the drummer who missed the session; roll-up bills the band for a session two members played on. Both are false records the system would generate itself. They are independent, and a band credit is frozen to its contribution-date lineup — the same principle as `02.06` D-04, applied to entities instead of vocabulary. |
| DT-08 | The Producer's assertion is a broker's assertion, uniformly | `personas.md` names the Producer the trust broker; `02.04.02` weights producer-asserted above self-asserted | ✅ **CONFIRMED AS AN EXPLOIT; resolved by D-05.** The tier model reads *origin* (session-captured) and *asserter role* (Producer) — and a Producer crediting **themselves** satisfies both while being, in substance, a self-claim. Their own credit laundres to a broker tier simply by sitting inside a session they ran. This is `personas.md`'s Producer worst-intentional behavior ("assigns themselves a larger split than agreed and pushes it through while contributors aren't paying attention") executed against the credit graph instead of the split sheet, and the tier model cannot see it unless **this record** carries asserter identity distinctly from asserter role. The fix belongs here, not in `02.04.02`: a derivation can only weigh what the record tells it. |
| DT-09 | The record should carry its evidence inline — audio, take, waveform — so the credit is self-proving | "Verified by presence" taken literally; the intuition that evidence should travel with the claim | ❌ **REJECTED** — three separate failures. (1) It duplicates `02.02.04` (attendance proof) and domain 07 (stems), which own that evidence and its lifecycle. (2) It makes every ledger read an audio fetch at fan-scale volume, on a Fan surface that is read-only and read-heavy. (3) It makes the record un-portable, which quietly answers `problem-statement.md` Q-02 the wrong way — an exportable credit (`02.08`) is what makes the lock-in **earned** rather than hostile (`02.01.04` DT-02). Evidence is bound by reference. The record points at proof; it does not carry it. |
| DT-10 | The record needs no time axis beyond "when it was created" | The record model as originally written — party, role, work, tier, origin, visibility, and no date at all | ✅ **CONFIRMED AS A GAP; resolved by D-04.** Two dates matter and they are not the same. **Contribution date** (when it was tracked) is what disputes actually argue about and what domain 10 needs as the recording's fixation date for neighbouring rights. **Assertion date** is when this platform recorded it. For a captured session the gap is ~0; for an import it is 32 years. **The gap is the thesis, expressed as a number on the record**: it is the difference between a fact witnessed and a fact remembered, which is precisely what `problem-statement.md` says every competitor cannot distinguish and what `02.04.02` needs to distinguish tiers by. A record with one timestamp cannot tell reconstruction from capture — and telling them apart is the entire product. |
| DT-11 | A ledger should be able to declare itself complete — the liner notes are done | Genuinely valuable if possible: it would let `02.08` emit with confidence and let a reader trust an absence | ❌ **REJECTED** — "complete" is an assertion of a **negative** (nobody else contributed) and no party can honestly make it. The Producer thinks they remembered everyone; the assistant who tuned the piano and the friend who sang the third harmony are not in the room to disagree. Worse, stamping it makes every later-arriving true credit look like an intrusion into a settled record — the system would treat correctness as vandalism. What *can* be asserted is that the Producer closed the session's roll (`02.02.03`) — a claim about the **capture act**, not about the world. That distinction is domain D-10 in miniature: the platform records who asserted what, and does not adjudicate truth. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **09 Rights & Ownership** ([09-rights-ownership/](../../09-rights-ownership/)) — **shared moment, different entity**. The split is captured in the same human moment (`personas.md` step 3 for both Musician and Producer) but is a different fact on a different entity (DT-01, D-02). The hardest boundary in the product; domain Q-06 flags the close prompt as the highest-risk intersection on the map. This feature's contribution to that boundary is D-02: the record has no field a split could hide in.
- Touches **01 Identity, Profiles & Organizations** ([01-identity-profiles-organizations/](../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/)) — **shared entity**. Every credit's party is an identity, an entity, or an unclaimed shell. D-07 makes 01's Band-as-entity model load-bearing here: a party is not always a person, and Q-04 (who attests for a band) is 01's governance question surfacing as a 02 tier problem.
- Touches **02.06 Role & Instrument Taxonomy** ([02.06-credit-role-instrument-taxonomy.md](../02.06-credit-role-instrument-taxonomy.md)) — **shared vocabulary**. Role is chosen from the shared taxonomy, never free text (D-01, `02.06` D-05). A pending alias never blocks a record (`02.06` D-03), so a record can be live with a role the vocabulary does not yet canonically hold.
- Touches **02.04.02 Provenance Tiers** ([../02.04-attestation-credit-confidence/02.04.02-provenance-tiers-credit-confidence.md](../02.04-attestation-credit-confidence/02.04.02-provenance-tiers-credit-confidence.md)) — **this record is the tier's entire input surface**. Two new obligations land here from Deep Think: D-05 (asserter identity, or the Producer self-credit exploit in DT-08 is invisible to derivation) and D-04 (the contribution/assertion gap, which is what separates capture from reconstruction — DT-10). A derivation can only weigh what the record carries.
- Touches **02.01.05 Visibility & Embargo** ([02.01.05-credit-visibility-embargo.md](./02.01.05-credit-visibility-embargo.md)) — **shared property, enforced here**. Visibility lives on the record and is evaluated at query (`02.01.05` D-02). Two consequences settled this pass: visibility is **never inherited across a merge** (resolving the race in sub-domain CX-01 Q5 / CX-02 Q5 at the record rather than in merge), and the Private control is what lets a contested credit leave the disputing party's page without letting a contest suppress the work's ledger (D-11).
- Touches **02.01.06 Correction & Amendment** ([02.01.06-credit-correction-amendment.md](./02.01.06-credit-correction-amendment.md)) — **state dependency**. The Superseded state exists solely to serve amendment (sub-domain CX-03). D-09 adds a case: removing a comped-out contributor's credit is an amendment requiring their agreement, and since it changes the party set it is closer to `02.01.06` D-03's dispute territory than to a correction.
- Touches **02.01.04 Identifier Resolution & Merge** ([02.01.04-identifier-resolution-duplicate-merge.md](./02.01.04-identifier-resolution-duplicate-merge.md)) — **bulk rewrite of this ledger**. Merge re-points every record naming either party (sub-domain CX-04). D-03's uniqueness makes merge outcomes deterministic: same (party, role, work) collapses and unions evidence; differing roles both survive.
- Touches **02.03.02 Claim Inbox** ([../02.03-claiming-cold-start-seeding/02.03.02-claim-inbox-suggested-claims.md](../02.03-claiming-cold-start-seeding/02.03.02-claim-inbox-suggested-claims.md)) — **destination for the Unacknowledged state**. D-10's gated records have to land somewhere, and the inbox is where a named party first learns someone asserted a credit in their name. Domain Q-05 (shells have no notification channel) applies with force: a gated record naming an unclaimed shell is invisible to the human it names, possibly forever.
- Touches **02.02 Session Capture** ([../02.02-session-capture/02.02-session-capture-index.md](../02.02-session-capture/02.02-session-capture-index.md)) — **trigger dependency**. Capture is this record's highest-value origin and the reason the assertion gap is ~0. Domain Q-04 (is there a DAW plugin?) is upstream of everything here: without reliable capture, every record in this ledger is a reconstruction and the tier model has nothing to distinguish.
- Touches **02.05 Credit Dispute Resolution** ([../02.05-credit-dispute-resolution.md](../02.05-credit-dispute-resolution.md)) — **state dependency**. Contested is a state of this record. D-11 settles what contest does to it (marks, zero-weights, never suppresses) — which matters because domain D-03 makes the dispute engine a cross-cut, and a shared engine must not assume it may hide the thing under contest.
- Touches **12 Release & Distribution** ([12-release-distribution/](../../12-release-distribution/)) — **trigger dependency, two of them**. The release event authoritatively lifts embargo (`02.01.05` D-03), and delivery is when D-09's not-in-final-master qualifier is set. Both make 12 harder to sequence far behind 02 than the MoSCoW ledger implies.
- Touches **07 Projects** ([07-projects-collaboration/](../../07-projects-collaboration/)) — **lifecycle dependency**. D-08 (withdraw, never delete) is a constraint this feature places **on 07's project deletion flow**: 07 cannot offer a delete that destroys credits. Domain Q-03 (who owns the `session` entity — 02 or 07?) sits directly under this.
- Touches **10 Royalties & Collections** ([10-royalties-collections/](../../10-royalties-collections/)) — **downstream consumer**. A performer credit on a recording is the input to neighbouring-rights registration, and D-04's contribution date is the fixation date that registration needs. Q-05 (does a comped-out contribution register?) is a real money question, not a display one.
- Touches **02.07 Union & Performer Session Reporting** ([../02.07-union-performer-session-reporting.md](../02.07-union-performer-session-reporting.md)) — **the reason D-09 retains comped-out records**. AFM terms pay the player who tracked regardless of the master. `02.07` is the domain's only Won't — but if this record ever purged comped-out contributions, `02.07` could never be built at all. A Won't that a Must can permanently foreclose is worth naming.
- Touches **02.08 Credit Export & DDEX RIN** ([../02.08-credit-export-ddex-rin.md](../02.08-credit-export-ddex-rin.md)) — **downstream consumer**. RIN needs role codes (`02.06`) and a display sequence (D-12) — an unordered ledger cannot emit a valid one. Domain Q-08 (re-emission after change) compounds here: D-09's qualifier is a *post-export* mutation, joining amendment, retraction and demotion on the list of things that stale a downstream consumer.
- Touches **24 Trust & Safety** ([24-trust-safety/](../../24-trust-safety/)) — **abuse surface**. D-10 closes the fabrication vector (asserting credits in a stranger's name); it does not close the volume vector (Q-06). Bulk self-assertion is individually honest and collectively fraudulent, and this feature deliberately does not act on it — because acting on volume would gate a credit on something other than evidence, contradicting domain D-08.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | One record per party **per role** — never one record with a role array | A drummer who also engineered has two facts with independent evidence, independent attestation, and independent dispute surfaces. Collapsing them makes provenance tier meaningless. |
| D-02 | Credit asserts contribution only — never ownership, never percentage | The domain's founding distinction (domain index D-01). Holds by construction, not by convention — the record has nowhere a split could hide. |
| D-03 | At most one record per **(party, role, work)**; multiple sessions accrete evidence to one record | A drummer on two sessions for one track is one fact witnessed twice, not two facts. One line per fact keeps a discography readable and makes merge outcomes deterministic. Complements D-01: same party + *different* role = two records; same party + same role = one. |
| D-04 | The record carries **contribution date** and **assertion date** separately; the gap between them is a provenance signal, not metadata | DT-10. ~0 for a captured session; 32 years for an import. The gap is the difference between a fact witnessed and a fact remembered — the thesis expressed as a number. A record with one timestamp cannot tell capture from reconstruction, which is the only thing this product does that competitors cannot. |
| D-05 | The **asserter is always recorded**, distinctly from the asserter's role — including (especially) when asserter == party | DT-08. A Producer crediting themselves satisfies both of `02.04.02`'s trust inputs (session-captured origin, trust-broker asserter) while being a self-claim in substance. Only this record can tell the derivation otherwise. |
| D-06 | A credit binds to a **recording, a composition, or both** — and which is never optional | Resolves sub-domain Q-03. A drummer credits the recording; a co-writer credits the work; a producer-writer credits both. These are two graphs with two identifier systems (ISRC / ISWC, `02.01.04`), two downstream registrations (neighbouring rights / publishing, domain 10), and they are not interchangeable. An unbound credit is unexportable (`02.08`) and unregistrable. |
| D-07 | **Bands hold credits directly.** Band credits and member credits are independent records; neither is derived from the other | DT-07. `personas.md` and D-19 make Band an entity, not a person and not a persona. Fan-out fabricates a credit for the member who missed the session; roll-up fabricates a band credit for a two-member session. Liner notes carry both because they are two facts. A band credit is frozen to its contribution-date lineup (`02.06` D-04's principle, applied to entities). |
| D-08 | Works are **withdrawn, never deleted**; credits survive a withdrawn work at full tier | DT-06. Resolves Q-02. The credit asserts an event; deleting a project cannot un-happen the session. A delete would let one Producer destroy four other people's evidence — DT-03's joint-fact problem arriving through a routine workflow. The abandoned project is the high-value case, not the disposable one. This is a constraint on domain 07's deletion flow, not only a rule here. |
| D-09 | A contribution that does not reach the final master **keeps its credit, qualified** — removal is an amendment, never a Producer-side delete. When otherwise publicly eligible, its public discography line renders **"not in final master"**; the qualifier neither widens visibility nor changes tier, ownership, reporting, rights, registration, royalty, or payment treatment. | DT-05. The session happened; the master differs. `02.07`/AFM needs the contribution; liner notes need the outcome. Retaining both is the only honest model, and it stops "comped out" becoming a one-click tool for erasing a person from a record they played on. Owner sees the trigger date; public viewers do not. Domain 10 still decides neighbouring-rights registration. |
| D-10 | A **self-asserted credit naming another party does not publish to that party's page** — it queues for acknowledgement | The fabrication gate. Domain D-08 settles existence and tier; it does not cover speaking in someone else's name. Without this, anyone can put an unconfirmed line on any stranger's public discography. The gate is deliberately narrow — session capture and external import both pass, so it costs the cold start and the growth loop nothing. |
| D-11 | **Contest marks; it never suppresses.** A contested record stays on the work's ledger, at zero weight in derived surfaces | Resolves the breadth pass's open marker on discography suppression. Suppression-on-filing makes a contest a censorship tool — one click to remove any inconvenient true credit. The named party's escape hatch already exists and is separate: `02.01.05` Private removes it from *their* page without touching the work's ledger. Two controls, no weapon. |
| D-12 | **Billing/display order is a property of the ledger**, asserted by the work owner — not of the individual record, and never attestable | DT-04. Order is relative: a credit cannot know it is third. It is also the only thing here that is not a fact about contribution — it is a contractual/editorial position, so it must not feed the tier. `02.08`/DDEX RIN needs a display sequence and an unordered ledger cannot emit a valid one. |
