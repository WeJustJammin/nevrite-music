# Feature: Credit Record & Contribution Ledger

> **Number**: 02.01.01
> **Parent**: [Credit Graph & Discography](./02.01-credit-graph-discography-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The atomic unit of the whole platform's thesis: one assertion that **one party, in one role, contributed to one work**. Everything else in domain 02 either creates this record (capture, claiming), qualifies it (attestation, provenance tier), renders it (discography, search), or fixes it (amendment, dispute). The ledger is the set of these records for a given work — the answer to "who made this."

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (own credits) | Sees every credit naming them across every work — captured, claimed, or imported — each with its provenance tier. Can propose a new self-asserted credit, accept an attestation request, set per-credit visibility, and propose an amendment. Cannot silently delete an attested credit (retraction is `02.04.03`, which leaves a trace). |
| Producer | Full (sessions they ran) | Creates records for everyone in the room as a byproduct of running the session (`02.02`). Sees the full ledger for their works including credits they did not create. Their assertion carries a higher provenance tier than a self-claim — they are the trust broker (`personas.md`). |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees credits that name their room ("Recorded at X"). Can assert the room's own credit on a session hosted there and can contest a false one, but has no visibility into who played what on a session they merely hosted unless the Producer grants it. |
| Fan | None | Never touches the record directly. Reads its published projection through `02.01.02` / `02.01.03`. Explicit absence: fans have no write path into the graph at any tier — self-assertion requires a professional identity link, or the graph becomes Discogs (crowd-edited and unattributable). |

## Behavior

A credit record binds: **party** (a resolved identity — person, band, or organization), **role** (from the taxonomy, `02.06`), **work** (a recording and/or a composition), **provenance tier** (how we know — `02.04.02`), **origin** (which mechanism created it), and **visibility** (`02.01.05`). Detail (take-level, gear used, session reference) hangs off it rather than living in it.

The record is deliberately **thin and non-negotiable about ownership**: a credit asserts contribution and asserts nothing about splits, royalties, or chain of title. A session drummer has a credit and zero ownership — collapsing these is the exact error the domain exists to avoid (domain index D-01). Splits live in domain 09.

### Happy Path

1. A session closes in `02.02`; the capture flow emits one draft credit record per contributor per role
2. System resolves each party to an identity (existing user, unclaimed shell, or new — `02.01.04`) and each role to the taxonomy (`02.06`)
3. Each named contributor is asked to confirm their own record; the Producer's assertion already stands behind it
4. On confirmation the record's provenance tier rises to session-captured + attested (`02.04.02`)
5. Record becomes visible per the session's embargo setting (`02.01.05`) and appears on every party's discography

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Contributor is not a WeJammin user | Record is created against an unclaimed identity shell; sits in the claim inbox (`02.03.02`) awaiting them | Producer sees "credited — unclaimed"; the human sees nothing until they join and claim |
| Contributor never confirms | Record persists at producer-asserted tier, not attested. It does **not** vanish — a true fact with weak evidence beats no fact | "Credited by <Producer> — unconfirmed" |
| Same party credited in two roles on one work | Two records, not one with an array. A drummer who also engineered has two distinct facts with distinct evidence | Two rows on the work's ledger |
| Party disputes a record naming them | Record enters contested state (`02.05`); stays visible with a contested marker rather than disappearing | "Contested" badge; discography suppression rules `[PENDING — Step 5]` |
| Work is deleted / project abandoned | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` — likely the credit survives the project, because the work happened. Needs a decision. | `[PENDING]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Draft | Capture emitted it; session not closed | Editable row in the session's roll call, no discography presence |
| Unconfirmed | Session closed, named party has not responded | Visible with provenance tier "asserted by <party>" |
| Attested | Named party confirmed | Verified marker |
| Embargoed | Work unreleased or NDA set | Visible only to session participants; hidden from public discography and search |
| Contested | Dispute filed (`02.05`) | Contested marker; downstream royalty/reputation effects held |
| Superseded | Amendment accepted (`02.01.06`) or merge collapsed it (`02.01.04`) | Replaced by successor; history retains the original |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The credit record should carry the split percentage, since capture happens at the same moment | Naive reading of "capture at source"; the split-sheet moment in `personas.md` | ❌ **REJECTED** — the domain's own charter says a session drummer has a credit and zero ownership. Splits are domain 09. Capturing them in the same *moment* (one UX flow) is right; storing them in the same *record* is the conflation the domain exists to prevent. The UX adjacency is a CX (02 ↔ 09), not a field. |
| DT-02 | An unconfirmed credit should be hidden until attested, so the graph is only ever "verified" | Purity argument — the differentiator is verification, so show only verified things | ❌ **REJECTED** — this kills the cold start and punishes the truth. Most true credits will never be confirmed (people move on, die, don't join). Hiding them recreates the empty-database failure. The answer is a **visible provenance tier**, not suppression: show the fact, show how well we know it. This is the whole reason `02.04.02` exists. |
| DT-03 | A contributor must be able to hard-delete a credit naming them | Privacy intuition; GDPR erasure reflex | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED WITH CONSTRAINT** — a right-to-erasure request is real and statutory. But a credit is a *joint* fact: the Producer and three other players also assert it, and erasure would rewrite their history. Likely resolution is de-identification (the contribution survives, the personal link is severed), not deletion. This is a genuine legal/product collision — escalated to `/create-prd-security` and Q-01 below. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | One record per party **per role** — never one record with a role array | A drummer who also engineered has two facts with independent evidence, independent attestation, and independent dispute surfaces. Collapsing them makes provenance tier meaningless. |
| D-02 | Credit asserts contribution only — never ownership, never percentage | The domain's founding distinction (domain index D-01). Enforced at the schema, not by convention. |
