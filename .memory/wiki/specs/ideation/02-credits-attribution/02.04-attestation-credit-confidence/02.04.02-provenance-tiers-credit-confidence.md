# Feature: Provenance Tiers & Credit Confidence

> **Number**: 02.04.02
> **Parent**: [Attestation & Credit Confidence](./02.04-attestation-credit-confidence-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The model that answers "how well do we know this?" for every credit in the graph, and the label that communicates it. It is the load-bearing feature of the entire domain: without it, WeJammin holds the same undifferentiated pile of assertions as Jaxsta, Muso.AI, Sound Credit and Discogs, and the fact that some of its credits were captured in the room is invisible and therefore worthless.

Absorbs the sweep's "Provenance Tier Labelling" candidate (sub-domain D-02) and the credit-weighting half of "Endorsements & Credit-Weighted Vouching" (sub-domain D-01).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only (sees own tiers) | Sees the tier on every credit of theirs and, critically, **what would raise it** — an unconfirmed line with a "ask the two people who were there" affordance is the tier model doing its real job, which is motivating attestation. Cannot set or argue their own tier; a self-settable confidence score is not a confidence score. |
| Producer | Read-only | Sees tiers across their sessions — a session where nobody confirmed is visibly weaker than one where everyone did, which is feedback on their own capture discipline. Their assertion is an *input* to the tier, never a lever on it. |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees tiers on the room's credits. A room whose discography is all imported reads as unverified next to one whose sessions were captured — commercially meaningful to them. |
| Fan | Read-only (simplified label) | Sees a plain signal — the equivalent of "verified" versus not — with no tier taxonomy, no evidence trail, no jargon. `personas.md`: fans "will not tolerate professional-tool complexity". The distinction still matters to them (a Discogs-tier rumour vs a confirmed fact), but the vocabulary must not. |

## Behavior

The tier is a **live derivation, not a stamp**. It re-computes when attestations arrive (`02.04.01`), when they are retracted (`02.04.03`), when corroboration lands late (`02.02.04`), and when ring detection demotes an edge (`02.04.04`). A credit attested in 2026 and retracted in 2027 is not "verified with an asterisk" — it is re-derived downward, and the discography line changes.

The inputs, roughly in ascending strength:

| Input | Strength | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Imported from external catalog | Lowest | Reconstruction — a stranger typed it into a database (`02.03` D-01) |
| Self-asserted | Low | The claimant is the interested party |
| Producer-asserted | Medium | Trust broker (`personas.md`), but unconfirmed and self-interested in their own sessions |
| Session-captured | High | The platform was present; the assertion is contemporaneous, not remembered |
| Attested by co-present parties | High | Independent humans who were there, accountable by name |
| Session-captured **and** attested | Highest | Contemporaneous *and* corroborated — the thing no competitor can retroactively manufacture |

The unresolved shape (sub-domain Q-01) is whether this is a discrete ladder or a continuous score, and it is a real fork:

- A **ladder** is legible ("verified" means something specific), explainable, and gameable at the boundaries — the adversary optimises to clear the next rung and stops.
- A **score** is honest about the underlying continuum and unexplainable to users, which makes it feel arbitrary and makes disputes about it unanswerable.

The provisional lean: a **ladder publicly, a score internally**. Users need a claim they can understand; traversal (`02.01.03`) and detection need gradation. But this must be decided, not drifted into — two representations of the same thing will disagree, and the disagreement will surface somewhere embarrassing.

### Happy Path

1. A credit exists with some set of evidence
2. System derives its tier from that evidence
3. Tier renders on the credit — publicly on discography, as an edge weight in traversal
4. Evidence changes (attestation, retraction, corroboration, demotion)
5. Tier re-derives; the visible label changes; downstream consumers see the new weight

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Credit has no evidence at all | Lowest tier. It exists and is honestly labelled — never hidden (sub-domain D-03) | "Unconfirmed" |
| Tier drops after being public | Retraction or demotion. The visible label changes on a page others may have cited or linked | Tier change; credited party notified |
| Everything is attested because everyone colludes | Tier reports high confidence in a fiction. **The tier model cannot detect this from evidence strength alone** (`02.02.04` DT-03) — it is a topology problem (`02.04.04`) | Nothing, until detection demotes |
| Imported credit is claimed by its true owner | Tier does not rise. Claiming verifies the person, not the fact (`02.03.02` D-01) | Still import-tier |
| Attester is themselves low-standing | Weight depends on sub-domain Q-02 — unresolved, and it makes reputation recursive | `[PENDING]` |
| A credit is legally established (court, contract) but has no platform evidence | The tier model has no input for external truth, and would label a court-established fact "unconfirmed" | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — a real gap |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Derived | Evidence present | Current tier label |
| Re-deriving | Evidence changed | Transient; should be invisible |
| Demoted | Ring detection or retraction | Lower tier; credited party notified |
| Disputed | Contest open (`02.05`) | Tier held; contested marker dominates the label |
| Simplified | Fan viewing | Plain verified/unverified signal only |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Tier is a property stamped on the credit when it is created | Simplest model, and the one every competitor implies | ❌ **REJECTED** — tier is a *current assessment of current evidence*, and evidence moves in both directions: attestations arrive late (`02.02.04` edge cases), get retracted (`02.04.03`), and get demoted by detection (`02.04.04`). A stamped tier would report a credit as verified after the only person who verified it took it back. Tier must be derived on read (or maintained), never assigned on write — and this has real cost, since it is read on every discography page and every traversal edge at fan-scale volume. |
| DT-02 | High confidence means the credit is true | The obvious reading, and the one the word "verified" invites in every user's head | ❌ **REJECTED — and this is the domain's most dangerous confusion.** The tier measures **how the platform knows**, not **whether it is so**. A session-captured attested credit is a fact four people confirmed on the day; it is not adjudicated truth, and a colluding room produces exactly the same signature (`02.02.04` DT-03). Publishing this as "verified" makes a claim the platform cannot support, and sub-domain Q-03 flags it as a liability surface rather than a copy choice. The honest label describes the evidence ("confirmed by 4 people present") rather than asserting the conclusion. |
| DT-03 | The tier's job is to inform readers | The stated purpose — it is a label on a page | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED but secondary.** Its larger job is to **motivate attestation**. A visible gap on your own discography, closable in one tap by someone you worked with, is the mechanism that gets credits confirmed — and every confirmation pulls a collaborator into the product (`02.01.02` DT-01). The tier is therefore the engine of both the differentiator and the growth loop, which means its UI is a growth surface, not a metadata display. Designing it as a passive badge would forfeit most of its value. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Tier is derived from current evidence, never stamped at creation | DT-01. Evidence moves both ways. |
| D-02 | Labels describe the evidence, not the conclusion | DT-02. "Confirmed by 4 people present" is defensible; "verified" is a claim the platform cannot support. |
| D-03 | Claiming and self-assertion never raise the tier | Otherwise imported credits launder into verified ones (`02.03.02` D-01). |
| D-04 | Fans see a simplified signal, professionals see the tier | `personas.md` Fan constraint. Same model, different vocabulary. |
| D-05 | The tier UI is a growth surface, not a badge | DT-03. The gap-closing affordance is the attestation engine and the acquisition loop. |
