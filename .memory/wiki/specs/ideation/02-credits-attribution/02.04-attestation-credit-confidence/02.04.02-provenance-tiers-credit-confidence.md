# Feature: Provenance Tiers & Credit Confidence

> **Number**: 02.04.02
> **Parent**: [Attestation & Credit Confidence](./02.04-attestation-credit-confidence-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The model that answers "how well do we know this?" for every credit in the graph, and the label that communicates it. It is the load-bearing feature of the entire domain: without it, WeJammin holds the same undifferentiated pile of assertions as Jaxsta, Muso.AI, Sound Credit and Discogs, and the fact that some of its credits were captured in the room is invisible and therefore worthless.

It exists for the Musician and Producer, who need their real work to be distinguishable from anyone's claim to it, and for the Fan, who needs to know whether "who played that solo" is a fact or a rumour. It is consumed by everything downstream that treats a credit as evidence — traversal (`02.01.03`), the marketplace (05), royalty registration (10).

Absorbs the sweep's "Provenance Tier Labelling" candidate (sub-domain D-02) and the credit-weighting half of "Endorsements & Credit-Weighted Vouching" (sub-domain D-01).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only (sees own tiers) | Sees the rung on every credit of theirs and, critically, **what would raise it** — an unconfirmed line with a "ask the two people who were there" affordance is the tier model doing its real job, which is motivating attestation (D-05). Sees which of their credits are structurally capped and why ("nobody else was in that session"), so the absence of a rung is never mistaken for a failure to act. Cannot set, appeal or argue their own rung; a self-settable confidence score is not a confidence score. Never sees the internal score, their own demotion state (`02.04.04` D-02), or any other party's rungs beyond what that party publishes. |
| Producer | Read-only | Sees rungs across their sessions — a session where nobody confirmed is visibly weaker than one where everyone did, which is feedback on their own capture discipline and the only honest scoreboard for it. Their assertion is an *input* to the rung, never a lever on it: a credit they authored cannot be lifted past rung 4 by their own confirmation (D-08), including on lines crediting themselves. |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees rungs on the room's credits. A room whose discography is all imported reads as unverified next to one whose sessions were captured — commercially meaningful to them (`02.01` D-03). Their room attestation (`02.04.01` D-05) raises the **score** of a session-logged credit but can never cross the rung-5 gate, because a room cannot witness who played what. |
| Fan | Read-only (plain-language evidence sentence) | Sees one sentence describing how the credit is known — "Confirmed by 3 people who were there", "Listed on Discogs" — with no rung taxonomy, no rung name, no evidence trail, no score, no attester identity, no timestamp, no session metadata. `personas.md`: fans "will not tolerate professional-tool complexity". The distinction still matters to them (a Discogs-tier rumour vs a confirmed fact — Discogs and AllMusic *are* fan products), but the vocabulary must not, and a badge would assert a conclusion the platform cannot support (DT-11). |

## Behavior

### The two axes (DT-04)

The tier is not one thing. It is two, answering two different questions, and conflating them is the source of sub-domain Q-01's apparent fork:

| Axis | Question it answers | Shape | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rung** (the ladder) | *How* do we know this? | Categorical, totally ordered, gated on evidence **kind** | Professionals see the rung; Fans see it rendered as a sentence (DT-11) |
| **Score** (the weight) | How much should this edge *count*? | Continuous, `0.0`–`1.0` | Nobody. Internal only — traversal (`02.01.03`), ranking, detection input |

They are not two encodings of one quantity, so they cannot "drift apart" — the failure this file previously feared. They are two functions over one evidence set. Both are latent in the corpus already: `02.01.02` renders a categorical label *and* offers an ordered "provenance tier floor" filter; `02.01.03` D-01 weights traversal edges continuously. The two-axis model is what those two peers are already assuming.

**The score never promotes across a rung.** No amount of attestation volume converts a rung-2 credit into a rung-5 one — rung 5 requires a *kind* of evidence (an independent co-present confirmation) that you either have or do not. This is what removes the "adversary optimises to clear the next rung and stops" hazard: the rungs are not grindable, because they are not thresholds on a number.

### The rungs

Totally ordered — a total order is required by `02.01.02`'s tier-floor filter, and the natural evidence lattice is only a partial order, so the order below is **imposed and must be ratified** (Q-01).

| # | Rung | Evidence-kind gate | Why it sits here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Imported** | Present in an external catalog; source-marked permanently (`02.03` D-01) | A stranger typed it into a database. Reconstruction — the failure `problem-statement.md` names. Can never rise by claiming (D-03). |
| 2 | **Self-asserted** | The credited party said so | The claimant is the interested party. |
| 3 | **Asserted by the session owner** | A Producer authored the credit; no independent confirmation | Trust broker (`personas.md`) — but self-interested in their own session's roll, and unconfirmed. |
| 4 | **Session-logged** | Rung 3 **and** the platform observed the session in real time and the roll was logged at the time (`02.02.01`) | Contemporaneous, so not reconstructed — but still one interested party. Capture evidences **the session**; it does not evidence **the contribution**. |
| 5 | **Confirmed** | ≥1 co-present party, who is neither the asserter nor the credited party, confirmed the contribution (`02.04.01`) | Independence is the scarce property (`02.02.04` DT-01: "the strongest honest signal is other humans co-signing"). Beats rung 4 even lagged: a producer's own roll entry is what Sound Credit already ships, and it was not enough. |
| 6 | **Captured and confirmed** | Rung 4 **and** ≥1 independent co-present confirmation with lag ≤ 7 days | Contemporaneous *and* corroborated — the thing no competitor can retroactively manufacture, and (DT-10) no user can either. |

### The score

The score orders credits *within* and *across* rungs and is what traversal actually uses. Inputs:

| Input | Effect on score | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Rung | Sets the band | The score never crosses a rung boundary — a rung-6 credit is never scored below a rung-5 one. |
| Independent confirmation count | Saturating, sharply diminishing (DT-09) | Proposed default: 1st confirmation = 60% of the attestation contribution; 3rd = 90%; ≥4th adds < 5% in total. Calibration → `/create-prd`. |
| Attestation lag (session → confirmation) | Decays the confirmation's weight (DT-06) | Proposed buckets: ≤ session close = 1.0 · ≤ 7d = 0.9 · ≤ 90d = 0.6 · > 90d = 0.3. **Lag, never age** (DT-07). Calibration → `/create-prd`. |
| Refusal by a roll member | Negative (DT-05) | Offsets the attestation contribution; floors at the rung's base. Never drives the score to zero — capture evidence is platform-observed and a human's refusal does not unobserve it. |
| "Don't know" | **Zero** | Not a weak confirm — an absence of evidence (CX-01). Contributes nothing in either direction. |
| Operator room attestation | Positive, small | Independent evidence about the **session**, not the contribution. Raises the score of a rung-4 credit; never crosses the rung-5 gate (`02.04.01` D-05). |
| Ring-detection demotion (`02.04.04`) | Negative multiplier, **never a cap** (DT-14) | Detection never writes the rung or the score directly (CX-03 Q1); it supplies one penalty term the derivation reads. |

### Derivation properties (all load-bearing)

1. **Derived, never stamped** (D-01). Rung and score are a pure function of the *current* evidence set. Evidence moves in both directions.
2. **Current, not historical, evidence** — the derivation reads *active* attestations. A naive count over all-time history would make retraction a no-op (CX-02 Q1). This is the single easiest severe bug in the feature.
3. **Reproducible over time** (DT-16). The derivation must answer *"what was this credit's rung at time T"*, not only "what is it now" — because downstream consumers (10, 02.08) acted on a past assessment and a dispute six years later argues about *then*. Follows from the append-only evidence log (`02.04.03` D-01, `02.01.06` D-01) — **but only if every input is historised, including the demotion score.** A mutable detection score makes every past rung irreproducible.
4. **Independence is per human, not per role** (DT-17). One human's evidence counts once regardless of how many personas they hold that day. A Producer who also owns the room cannot corroborate their own session; a Band entity's attestation inherits its members' identities.
5. **Monotone within each evidence kind.** Adding a confirmation never lowers the rung. Removing one never raises it.
6. **Settles once, does not flicker.** Concurrent confirmations at close (`02.02.03`) converge to one derivation. An observer sees rung 3 → rung 6, never 3→4→5→6.

### What the tier is not

The label must not read as doubt. A rung-1 credit is not a credit the platform disbelieves — it is one the platform did not witness. "We weren't there" and "this is questionable" are different statements, and only the first is one the platform can make. This is why D-02 exists, and it is the whole answer to the court-judgment gap (DT-13).

### Happy Path

1. A credit exists with some evidence set — usually a roll entry from `02.02.01` at rung 3 or 4
2. System derives rung and score from that evidence set, per human, over active attestations only
3. Rung renders on the credit — as a label on `02.01.02` for a professional viewer, as a plain sentence for a Fan (DT-11); score renders nowhere, and weights the edge in `02.01.03`
4. The credit's owner sees the gap and what closes it: "2 people were in that room — ask them" (`02.01.02` step 7)
5. Owner taps the nudge → `02.04.01` request → an independent co-present party confirms in one tap
6. Evidence set changes; derivation re-runs
7. The attester's own view reflects the change **synchronously** — they must never tap and see nothing
8. The credited party's discography line and the traversal weight follow (proposed budget: P95 < 60 s; ratified at `/create-prd-compile`). The lag is a real UX cost at exactly the motivational moment (CX-01 Q2)
9. Rung crosses from 3 → 5, or 4 → 6; the public line changes without the owner touching the page again, and the collaborator who confirmed is now in the product (`02.01.02` DT-01)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Credit has no evidence at all | Rung 2 (self-asserted). It exists and is honestly labelled — never hidden (sub-domain D-03, domain D-08) | "Self-asserted" + owner-only nudge |
| Party has zero credits | No tier exists. Absence, not rung 1 — an empty discography is not an unconfirmed one | Empty state (`02.01.02`); no label anywhere |
| Rung drops after being public | Retraction (`02.04.03`), demotion (`02.04.04`), or amendment invalidation (DT-08). The visible label changes on a page others may have cited or linked | Label change. Credited party notified **with a reason** for retraction/amendment; **silently** for demotion (CX-03 Q4 — deliberate asymmetry) |
| Everything is attested because everyone colludes | Rung reports rung 6 in a fiction. **The tier model cannot detect this from evidence strength alone** (`02.02.04` DT-03) — it is a topology problem (`02.04.04`). But the payoff is capped: volume saturates (DT-09) and the past is unbackfillable (DT-10), so a ring can manufacture a future, not a career | Nothing, until detection demotes |
| Imported credit is claimed by its true owner | Rung does not rise. Claiming verifies the person, not the fact (`02.03.02` D-01/DT-02) | Still rung 1, source-marked |
| Attester is themselves low-standing | **Default: flat.** Attester standing does not weight their attestation until Q-02 resolves. At launch nobody has standing, so a standing-weighted model derives from noise exactly when the tier must work; and it makes reputation recursive. The weighting rationale is never surfaced either way — publishing it would publish a standing ranking of professionals | The rung label only. Never a reason, never a weight |
| A credit is legally established (court, contract) but has no platform evidence | Rung stays low and is **correct** to do so — the platform did not witness it. Legal status is a **separate axis** owned outside domain 02 (DT-13), never a rung. The gap was in the *vocabulary*, not the model: rung 1's label must read "we have no evidence of our own", not "we doubt this" | Low rung + a legal-status marker sourced from the authority of record. No contradiction between them |
| Session owner confirms their own roll entry | Not independent. Rung stays at 4 (D-08) | No change; nudge still shown |
| The multi-hyphenate credits themselves on their own session (Producer who played bass) | They are asserter **and** credited party. Structurally capped at rung 4 — there is nobody independent to confirm. Honest, and permanent | "Nobody else was in that session" — an explanation, never a deficiency marker (Q-04) |
| Producer also owns the studio and attests the room fact | One human, two personas. Counts once (DT-17). Self-corroboration is not corroboration | No score change |
| Band entity holds the credit; a member attests it | Entity attestation inherits member identity. Not independent | No score change; touches domain-index Q-12 |
| Solo bedroom producer's entire catalog | Every line capped at rung 4, forever, however honest. A large population structurally cannot reach the top rung | Explained per line; escalated as Q-04 — it may be an adoption problem, not just an honest one |
| Credit predates the user's adoption | Capped below rung 6 — contemporaneity is unbackfillable (DT-10). Same reason import caps at rung 1 | Explained: "WeJammin wasn't there" |
| Amendment changes the role family after attestation | Attestations are invalidated unless the new claim is **entailed** by the attested one (DT-08). Rung drops; attesters are re-asked, not silently dropped | Owner sees the drop + a one-tap re-ask; attester sees one question |
| Amendment narrows within a family ("Drums" → "Drums — brushes") or widens | Entailed. Attestations carry; rung unchanged | Nothing |
| Attest a trivial credit, then amend it upward ("tambourine" → "produced by") | Blocked by the entailment rule. Without it this is a one-click path from a tambourine to a production credit at rung 6 | Attestation invalidated; rung drops to 3 |
| Two roll members attest contradictorily (confirm + refuse) | Both recorded. The refusal offsets (DT-05); the contradiction surfaces on the record view and a dispute is offered, never opened (`02.04.01` D-03) | Contradiction marker on `02.01.01`; nothing on the public page |
| A hostile roll member refuses everything | Floors at the rung base, never zero. Mass refusal is a pattern — `02.04.04` and 24's, same shape as mass retraction | Rung dips; no explanation, no attester named (`02.04.01` Q-01 open) |
| Attester deletes their account | If attestations die with the account, every credit they confirmed re-derives downward the day they leave — a departing user silently devalues everyone they vouched for. **A tier-side argument for survival (de-identified)** | Feeds `02.04.03` Q-01 |
| Demotion lands while a legitimate late attestation lands | **Not a race** (DT-14). Both are inputs to one derivation; both apply. The framing only exists if you think the tier is a stamp | Whatever the joint derivation yields |
| Demoted cluster attests harder and nothing moves | Would be a **probe oracle** — the adversary learns they are flagged, defeating `02.04.04` D-02. This is why demotion is a weight, never a cap (DT-14) | Score moves, ambiguously. No oracle |
| Sockpuppet ring fabricates a session and confirms it | Reaches rung 6. **The tier model has no defence against sockpuppets and does not claim one** — identity strength (domain 01) and topology (`02.04.04`) are the only defences. Cost is wall-clock: the session must run in real time, at the claimed time | Nothing |
| Attested credit's "performance" was AI-generated | Rung 6 in a fiction of a different kind. Four people confirm "Sarah played bass" and nobody in the room considers it a lie (DT-15). `02.10` D-05: absence of disclosure is not a claim of human origin — so the rung must not imply one | Rung 6 + `02.10`'s disclosure axis, if disclosed |
| Contest opened on the credit (`02.05`) | The contest **does not move the rung** — an objection is not evidence (D-02, domain D-08). Evidence arriving during a contest applies normally: **no freeze** (D-10) | Contested marker on the record view for participants; per `02.01.02` D-06, nothing on the public page. **`02.05` D-02 and `02.01.02` D-06 currently contradict each other on publication** — flagged, not resolved here |
| The confidence assessment cannot be produced (evidence set momentarily unreadable) | The line renders with **no confidence label at all**. Never a stale one, never a default: "unconfirmed" defames and "verified" lies | Label absent, and visually distinct from rung 1/2 (D-11) |
| Downstream consumer cached a rung that has since dropped (royalty registration, a booking that cited it) | The consumer holds a stale assessment and acted on it. Domain 02 supplies rung-at-time-T (DT-16) so the consumer can justify *then*; it does not unwind anything | Feeds `02.04.03` Q-03, `02.08` Q-01, domain-index Q-08 |
| 4 people tap confirm simultaneously at close | Converges to one derivation; settles once | Rung 3 → 6. No flicker, no intermediate rungs |
| Attester retracts an attestation already invalidated by amendment | No-op on the derivation — it was already not counting. Still recorded (`02.04.03` D-01) | Nothing |
| Scraper harvests rungs across the public graph | The assertion and its rung are publishable; the evidence is not (`02.01.02`). Rung diffing over time can infer that a retraction happened — accepted, low-value leak | Rate-limited at `02.01.02`'s endpoint |
| Graph below `02.04.04`'s density floor (early platform) | Detection off (`02.04.04` D-04) ⇒ the demotion term is absent, not zero-by-default. The tier runs unpenalised and says so honestly: it measures evidence, and early on there is less of it | Normal rungs |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Discography or record view requested; derivation in flight | Line skeleton **including the label slot** — the label never pops in after the line, or the page asserts an unconfirmed credit for a frame |
| Empty | Party has no credits | No tier anywhere. Absence ≠ rung 1 |
| Derived (Populated) | Evidence present | Current rung label (professional) or evidence sentence (Fan) |
| Re-deriving | Evidence changed | Transient and invisible; settles once, no intermediate rungs (CX-01 Q5) |
| Error | Derivation unavailable | **No label.** Visually distinct from any rung. Never stale, never defaulted (D-11) |
| Partial | Some inputs unavailable (e.g. detection offline, lag unknown) | Rung renders only if every gate it depends on is evaluable; otherwise Error. A rung derived from a partial input set is a confident lie |
| Demoted | Ring detection or retraction | Lower rung. Retraction: credited party notified with a reason. Demotion: **silent** (CX-03 Q4) |
| Capped | Asserter = credited party, or credit predates adoption | Rung with an explanation of the ceiling, never a deficiency marker |
| Contested | Contest open (`02.05`) | Rung continues to derive normally. Contested marker on the record view for participants only; nothing on the public page (`02.01.02` D-06) |
| Invalidated | Material amendment (DT-08) | Rung drops; owner offered a one-tap re-ask |
| Simplified | Fan viewing | One plain-language evidence sentence. No rung name, no count of anything but confirmations, no identities, no timestamps |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Tier is a property stamped on the credit when it is created | Simplest model, and the one every competitor implies | ❌ **REJECTED** — tier is a *current assessment of current evidence*, and evidence moves in both directions: attestations arrive late (`02.02.04` edge cases), get retracted (`02.04.03`), and get demoted by detection (`02.04.04`). A stamped tier would report a credit as verified after the only person who verified it took it back. The tier must always reflect the *current* evidence set, never a value fixed when the credit was created — and this is a load-bearing behavioural cost, since the assessment is consulted on every discography line and every traversal edge at fan-scale volume. |
| DT-02 | High confidence means the credit is true | The obvious reading, and the one the word "verified" invites in every user's head | ❌ **REJECTED — and this is the domain's most dangerous confusion.** The tier measures **how the platform knows**, not **whether it is so**. A rung-6 credit is a fact four people confirmed on the day; it is not adjudicated truth, and a colluding room produces exactly the same signature (`02.02.04` DT-03). Publishing this as "verified" makes a claim the platform cannot support, and sub-domain Q-03 flags it as a liability surface rather than a copy choice. The honest label describes the evidence ("confirmed by 4 people present") rather than asserting the conclusion. |
| DT-03 | The tier's job is to inform readers | The stated purpose — it is a label on a page | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED but secondary.** Its larger job is to **motivate attestation**. A visible gap on your own discography, closable in one tap by someone you worked with, is the mechanism that gets credits confirmed — and every confirmation pulls a collaborator into the product (`02.01.02` DT-01). The tier is therefore the engine of both the differentiator and the growth loop, which means its UI is a growth surface, not a metadata display. Designing it as a passive badge would forfeit most of its value. |
| DT-04 | Ladder or score is a real fork requiring a choice, and shipping both means two representations that drift and disagree | This file's own prior lean ("a ladder publicly, a score internally"), and sub-domain Q-01 | ❌ **REJECTED — the dichotomy is false, and this dissolves Q-01's central hazard.** They are not two encodings of one quantity; they answer different questions. The rung answers *how do we know this* — categorical, because "we were in the room" and "a stranger typed it into a database" are kinds, not quantities. The score answers *how much should this edge count in traversal* — continuous, because ranking needs gradation. Two functions over one evidence set cannot disagree; two encodings of one number inevitably do. Confirming evidence: both axes are already assumed by DEEP peers — `02.01.02` renders a categorical label **and** an ordered tier-floor filter, while `02.01.03` D-01 weights edges continuously. Neither peer noticed they were asking for different things. The residue of Q-01 is not "which one" but "ratify the two-axis model and the imposed rung order". |
| DT-05 | A refusal is not evidence and must not lower the tier, because a free unilateral act must not impose a reputational sanction | `02.01.02` D-06 reasoning, applied by analogy: a contest cannot lower a rung, so a refusal shouldn't either | ❌ **REJECTED — the analogy breaks on who may act.** Anyone can file a contest; only a roll member can refuse, because attesters are identified from the roll and never guessed (`02.04.01`). A refusal is a positive claim of contrary knowledge from a bounded, non-arbitrary population of people who were actually there. A tier that counts confirmations and ignores refusals only counts good news — which is precisely "Jaxsta with a trust badge". **But** it floors at the rung's base: refusal offsets the *attestation* contribution, and never erases *capture* evidence, because a human's disagreement does not unobserve what the platform observed. Mass refusal is then a pattern for `02.04.04`, exactly like mass retraction. |
| DT-06 | An attestation is an attestation, whenever it is given | The current input table's implicit assumption — it grades *capture* on contemporaneity but grades *attestation* not at all | ❌ **REJECTED, and it contradicts the thesis.** `problem-statement.md` says memory is "contested, lossy, and self-interested" and that reconstruction is the root failure. **An attestation given five years after the session IS reconstruction** — the exact thing the product exists to replace — and the model as written called it the same thing as a same-day tap. Attestation weight must decay with the **session → attestation lag**. `personas.md` gives the threshold its meaning: the Producer's pain is "chasing people who have moved on, for a project they've mentally closed" — the >90d case, and the reason it is the weakest bucket. |
| DT-07 | If lag decays evidence, then tiers should decay with age | The obvious generalisation of DT-06, and it is wrong in a way that would be catastrophic | ❌ **REJECTED — and getting this backwards would gut the graph.** A 1998 session-captured, same-day-attested credit is exactly as well-evidenced in 2026 as it was in 1998. The evidence did not get worse; nothing about it changed. Decaying by age would erode every veteran's catalog — precisely the population whose depth the graph needs most, and precisely the users `personas.md` describes as having "twenty years of work". **Decay is a function of lag, not of age.** The two look identical in a young graph, where every credit's age ≈ its lag, and will silently diverge the moment the platform is old enough for the error to be expensive.\n |
| DT-08 | One rung per credit line is sufficient | Every competitor's model, and the simplest thing that could work | ❌ **REJECTED — a credit is a bundle, not an assertion.** It asserts *who*, *what role* (`02.06` leaf), and *on what work*, and these are independently evidenced. Four people will confirm "Sarah played on this" while nobody confirmed "Sarah played *fretless bass*" — and the music industry's credit fights are overwhelmingly about **role granularity and billing**, not presence: "produced by" vs "co-produced" vs "additional production" is the canonical example, and it has ended friendships and produced litigation. A single rung over the whole line reports rung 6 on a role nobody confirmed. **The one-tap constraint (`02.04.01`) forbids asking two questions**, so the resolution is not per-attribute rungs but a carry rule: an attestation confirms the credit *as stated when it was shown*, and an amendment (`02.01.06`) preserves attestations **only if the new claim is entailed by the attested one** — strictly weaker or equal. Widening and intra-family narrowing carry; any strengthening or lateral change invalidates and re-asks. Without this rule there is a one-click path from "played tambourine" to "produced by" at rung 6, which is the highest-value laundering hole on the map. |
| DT-09 | More attestations mean a higher tier | Natural, and the model's implicit reading of "attested by co-present parties" | ❌ **REJECTED as linear, ✅ CONFIRMED as saturating — and the saturation is an anti-collusion measure that costs nothing.** `02.05` D-05 warns that counting "would let the bigger friend group win a factual question"; the same warning applies here. Epistemically, confirmations have sharply diminishing returns: 0→1 is the entire step (from *nobody* to *someone independent said so*); 4→5 is noise. Saturating the count therefore matches the evidence *and* destroys the ring's only cheap lever, since a ring's advantage is volume and volume buys nothing past the third confirmation. **Detection does not have to catch volume rings — the score already ignores them.** That materially shrinks `02.04.04`'s burden and, with it, its false-positive cost, which is the domain's hardest open question (domain-index Q-07). |
| DT-10 | The top rung can be manufactured by a determined adversary, like every other trust signal | Adversarial: the ring fabricates a session, logs a roll, and everyone taps confirm — rung 6 | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED for the future, ❌ REJECTED for the past — and the asymmetry is the product.** Rung 6 requires contemporaneity, so it **cannot be backfilled**: a ring can only accrue it going forward, in real time, one wall-clock session at a time. A career's worth of fabrication costs a career's worth of sessions. **Contemporaneity is a rate limit on fraud**, and it is the same mechanism as the thesis itself — "the thing no competitor can retroactively manufacture" turns out to be unmanufacturable by users too. It also earns the lock-in `problem-statement.md` Q-02 asks about: your credits export freely (`02.08`), but no other platform can vouch for what it did not witness. That is *earned*, not hostile. |
| DT-11 | The Fan's simplified signal is a badge — verified vs not | `02.04.02` D-04 as originally written, and this file's own prior Role Lens | ❌ **REJECTED — D-02 and D-04 collide here, and the badge is where the collision surfaces.** A binary **is** a conclusion: any two-valued signal reads as true/false to a lay reader regardless of the words printed on it, which is exactly the claim D-02 forbids and the liability surface sub-domain Q-03 names. But omitting it fails the Fan too — Discogs and AllMusic *are* fan products, and "is this real" is mainstream fan behaviour. The resolution is a third option nobody proposed: **a plain-language evidence sentence, not a badge.** "Confirmed by 3 people who were there." "Listed on Discogs." No taxonomy, no rung name, no jargon, no conclusion asserted — and no vocabulary to maintain when the rungs change. It carries a count and an evidence class, never identities, timestamps or session metadata (`02.01.02`'s scraper boundary holds). It satisfies D-02 and D-04 simultaneously, which the badge cannot. |
| DT-12 | A party-level aggregate ("94% of this artist's credits are verified") is a natural page summary | Any designer will propose it for `02.01.02`'s header within a week of seeing per-line rungs | ❌ **REJECTED — it is a reputation score smuggled in as a summary statistic.** Domain D-07 routed the endorsement/reputation *surface* out of domain 02 explicitly: 02 supplies the signal, never the score. | DT-13 | An externally-established fact (court judgment, signed contract) needs a tier input, or the model labels an adjudicated truth "unconfirmed" | This file's Q-03 — logged as "a real gap" | ❌ **REJECTED as a tier input, ✅ CONFIRMED as a vocabulary defect. Resolves Q-03.** A top rung for external truth fails three ways: the platform did not witness it (so the rung would lie about its own evidence); it would make the platform an adjudicator, contradicting domain D-10; and the document is submitted by the interested party, so it is self-assertion with a PDF — forgeable, and a laundering channel straight to the top rung. **Legal status is a separate axis**, owned by the authority of record (`02.05`'s adjudication outcome, domain 09, domain 24) and rendered *beside* the rung, never inside it. | DT-14 | Demotion racing a late attestation is a state conflict that needs a winner | The sub-domain CX file logs this as undecided (CX-03 Q5) | ❌ **REJECTED — it is a category error, and it is stamp-thinking leaking back in.** Two inputs to one pure derivation do not race; both are simply present. The question only forms if you imagine something being written. **The corollary is load-bearing: demotion is a weight, never a cap.** A cap makes new evidence a no-op for a flagged cluster — which is a **probe oracle**: attest, observe nothing move, conclude you are flagged. | DT-15 | Attestation distinguishes a human performance from an AI-generated one | Implicit in every "confirmed by people who were there" claim the product wants to make | ❌ **REJECTED — and it is a fully-corroborated fiction of a kind `02.04.04` cannot see.** Four people confirm "Sarah played bass"; it was a plugin; nobody in the room considers this a lie, so there is no collusion, no topology, no ring. | DT-16 | "What is this credit's tier" is the only query the model must answer | The obvious reading of a derived-on-read model | ❌ **REJECTED — "what was this credit's rung at time T" is equally required, and it is nearly free until one input spoils it.** Downstream consumers act on a past assessment: domain 10 registers a royalty at rung 5, `02.08` emits a RIN, a booking cites a credit. | DT-17 | Independence is a property of roles — a Producer's attestation is independent of a Musician's | The natural reading, and the one an account-type persona model would produce | ❌ **REJECTED — `personas.md`'s structural fact makes this a routine bug, not an exotic one.** "One human routinely occupies three of them in one day": the Producer who owns the studio and also played bass is asserter, credited party, and room-attester on one line. ## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Tier is derived from current evidence, never stamped at creation | DT-01. Evidence moves both ways. |
| D-02 | Labels describe the evidence, not the conclusion | DT-02. "Confirmed by 4 people present" is defensible; "verified" is a claim the platform cannot support. |
| D-03 | Claiming and self-assertion never raise the rung | Otherwise imported credits launder into verified ones (`02.03.02` D-01). |
| D-04 | Fans see a plain-language evidence sentence; professionals see the rung | DT-11. Same model, different vocabulary — and a sentence, not a badge, because a badge is a conclusion. |
| D-05 | The tier UI is a growth surface, not a badge | DT-03. The gap-closing affordance is the attestation engine and the acquisition loop. |
| D-06 | **Two axes**: a categorical rung (public, kind-gated, totally ordered) and a continuous score (internal, `0.0`–`1.0`). The score never promotes across a rung | DT-04. Not two encodings of one number — two functions over one evidence set. Kind-gating is what makes the rungs ungrindable. |
| D-07 | **Contemporaneity is graded by lag (session → attestation), never by age** | DT-06, DT-07. A five-year-late attestation is reconstruction; a 1998 same-day one is not. Decaying by age would gut every veteran's catalog. |
| D-08 | **Independence is per human, and an attestation only counts if the attester is neither the asserter nor the credited party** | DT-17. Otherwise the multi-hyphenate corroborates themselves in three hats, and a Producer taps their own roll entry to rung 5. |
| D-09 | **Attestation count saturates** — the 1st independent confirmation carries most of the weight; ≥4 adds almost nothing | DT-09. Matches the epistemics and destroys the ring's only cheap lever without any detection. |
| D-10 | **A contest neither moves nor freezes the rung** | An objection is not evidence (D-02, domain D-08). A freeze would be a stamp with extra steps and would make opening a dispute a way to lock a rung. |
| D-11 | **On derivation failure the label is absent, not defaulted** — and absence is visually distinct from the lowest rung | Never stale, never guessed. "Unconfirmed" defames and "verified" lies; a wrong label is worse than no label. |
| D-12 | **Demotion is a weight, never a cap** | DT-14. A cap is a probe oracle that teaches the adversary they are flagged, defeating `02.04.04` D-02, and permanently freezes the dominant false positive. |
| D-13 | **An amendment preserves attestations only if the new claim is entailed by the attested one**; anything else invalidates and re-asks | DT-08. Closes the tambourine → "produced by" laundering path. |
| D-14 | **No party-level aggregate tier**, ever | DT-12. It is a reputation score, and D-07 (domain) routed reputation out of domain 02. |
| D-15 | **Legal status is a separate axis rendered beside the rung, never a rung input** | DT-13. The platform does not adjudicate truth (domain D-10), and a submitted PDF is self-assertion with extra steps. |
| D-16 | **Attester standing does not weight attestations by default** (pending Q-02) | Recursive at launch and rich-get-richer later; and with D-09's saturation, weighting buys less than it costs. |
