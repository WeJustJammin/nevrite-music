## Overview

The machinery that turns an assertion into a verified fact, and the model that says how well any given credit is known. This is **the differentiator made concrete**: Jaxsta, Muso.AI, Sound Credit and Discogs all hold credits, and every one of them holds them at a single undifferentiated tier of "someone said so". `personas.md` names why WeJammin can do better — the Producer is a **trust broker**, and "their attestation is worth more than a self-claim, which is precisely what makes counter-attestation valuable."

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities in continuous interaction. Attestation produces the evidence; the tier model consumes it; retraction withdraws it and forces re-derivation; ring detection decides whether the evidence was ever honest. The tier is a live function of the other three, not a stamp.

| D-07 | **Demotion is score-only: the provenance rung is invariant to ring detection** | Owner ratification of DQ-06.1. `02.04.02`'s score-input table always filed the demotion multiplier inside the score ("Detection never writes the rung"), and every rung gate is a *kind* of evidence that a multiplier cannot remove. Several rows across this sub-domain and `02.01.02` nonetheless said the rung drops; all are corrected. A rung-moving detector would need a rung-side cut that exists nowhere, would make the kind-gated rung a threshold on a number, and would publish a probe oracle on the party `02.04.04` DT-02 says the detector is most likely to be wrong about. **Accepted cost**: a fully corroborated fiction keeps its top-rung public label indefinitely. | D-08 | **One rung, one meaning: every rung-gated consumer reads the same detection-invariant rung** | Owner ratification of DQ-06.2. No per-consumer contract and no second derived value — both commission machinery and both reintroduce a probe surface on the dominant false positive. Consequences recorded rather than assumed: `02.08`'s re-emission staleness trigger **drops demotion**; `02.08`'s low-tier RIN exclusion is unreachable by demotion (it fires on rungs 1–2); `06.02.02`'s rung ≥ 5 credential block does not move, so a fully corroborated fiction keeps a credential on a minors-facing paid-teaching page, indefinitely and invisibly; domain 10 retains its own registration-floor decision. If that outcome is later judged unacceptable, per-consumer contracts (`02.04.04` D-07's shape) are the better remedy. See `02.04.02` D-20, `02.04.04` D-09. |
