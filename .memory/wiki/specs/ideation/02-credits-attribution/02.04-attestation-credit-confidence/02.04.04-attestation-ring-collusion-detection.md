# Feature: Attestation-Ring & Collusion Detection

> **Number**: 02.04.04
> **Parent**: [Attestation & Credit Confidence](./02.04-attestation-credit-confidence-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

The defence against the one attack that defeats everything else in this domain: a group of people who agree to confirm each other's fabricated credits. Every other mechanism — session capture, attendance corroboration, counter-attestation, the tier model — reports full confidence in a fiction when the humans supplying the evidence are cooperating in the lie.

**Deep Think addition** — absent from the sweep's candidate list, and named directly in `personas.md` as the Musician's worst intentional behavior: *"games the verified-credit graph to manufacture reputation"*. It is the attack on the thesis itself.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | None | No visibility into detection, their own risk score, or why an edge was demoted. Publishing it would teach the adversary the detector's shape and defame the innocent — most flagged clusters will be tight-knit real scenes, not rings (DT-02). |
| Producer | None | Same, including for sessions they ran. A Producer told "your session looks collusive" is being accused on the basis of a statistical pattern, and the accusation would be wrong most of the time. |
| Operator | None | Same. Room-level corroboration is a detection input, not a detection surface. |
| Fan | None | Same. Fans see only the tier's simplified label (`02.04.02`), never why it moved. |

> All four `None` is deliberate and unusual (sub-domain D-04). This is an internal integrity system. Enforcement, appeals, and any human-facing consequence belong to domain 24 — which has the due-process machinery this feature must not reinvent.

## Behavior

The core insight, established in `02.02.04` DT-03: **fabrication at scale is a topology problem, not an evidence problem.** Five colluding people produce a session with a full roll call, mutual co-signs, complete attestation, and every signal green. No amount of stronger evidence collection detects them, because they are supplying the evidence. What distinguishes them is the *shape of their graph*, not the quality of any credit in it.

Signals that live in topology rather than in evidence. Each is a directional term — it pushes a cluster's ring-likeness *up*; none is a verdict on its own:

- **Reciprocity density** ↑ — real sessions produce asymmetric, sprawling attestation. Rings produce closed loops where the same small set vouches for each other repeatedly and for nobody else. Directionally: the higher the fraction of a party's attestation edges that are mutual and confined to the same small set, the more ring-like.
- **Insularity** ↑ — a real professional's graph leaks outward constantly (deps, one-off sessions, strangers). A ring's edges terminate inside itself. Directionally: the lower the ratio of outbound-to-strangers edges over total edges, the more ring-like.
- **Implausible productivity** ↑ — credit volume inconsistent with the physical reality of making records. Directionally: credits-per-unit-wall-clock above what a human making records can physically produce raises the term; the physical-reality ceiling is a calibrated value (deferred, below).
- **Convenient proximity** ↑ — new accounts whose edges connect them, in few hops (calibrated hop-count), to high-value parties they have no other trace of. Directionally: shorter hop-distance from a young account to a high-value party with no independent corroborating edge raises the term.
- **Temporal clustering** ↑ — a career's worth of attestation created in a short window. Directionally: the more a cluster's attestation timestamps concentrate into a narrow window relative to the span they claim to cover, the more ring-like.

### Scoring skeleton (directional; the numbers are one deferral, not a gap)

The score is a **weighted composite** of the directional terms above, evaluated **per attestation edge** (D-05 — never per cluster, so membership is never itself punished; mirrors `02.04.02`'s "the tier consumes a per-edge penalty term, never a cluster verdict"). It feeds `02.04.02` as a single negative multiplier on the edge (never a cap — `02.04.02` DT-14/D-12). The **demotion cut** is the score above which an edge's weight is multiplied down; below it, no penalty is applied.

**The multiplier moves the continuous score only. It never moves the rung** (D-08; `02.04.02` D-19). Detection is invisible on the public label by construction, not by policy: every rung gate in `02.04.02` is written as a *kind* of evidence, and a multiplier removes no evidence of any kind. What demotion does change is ranking — traversal weight (`02.01.03` D-05), search ordering, and the per-edge weighting of attested evidence in a dispute (`02.05` D-06). The demotion cut deferred in Q-04 is a **score-side** cut; no rung-side cut exists, is needed, or may be invented.

Three quantities are genuinely undecidable at ideation because publishing them arms the adversary (D-02, D-04) and because they require real graph-timing data to calibrate honestly (same posture as `02.04.02` Q-05):

1. **Per-signal thresholds** — the physical-productivity ceiling, the insularity ratio, the proximity hop-count and window.
2. **The signal weights** — how the five terms combine into one composite score.
3. **The demotion-cut value** and the **density-floor value** (D-04) below which the whole detector is off.

These are routed as **one consolidated deferral to `/create-prd-security`** (see Q-04) — not scattered across Step 5 questions and not left as a silent gap. A spec-writer inherits the directional skeleton now and the calibrated numbers from that single owner, and the values themselves stay unpublished by design.

### Versioned dispute-evidence interface

`02.04.04` supplies the following internal-only contract to `02.05 Credit Dispute Resolution`; it is never a user, reviewer, or public payload:

| Field | Type / invariant | Consumer rule |
|---|---|---|
| `contractVersion` | Literal `"v1"` | `02.05` accepts only `v1`; an unknown version is excluded from dispute confidence calculation and cannot create or escalate a case. Normal dispute evidence remains available. |
| `attestationEdgeId` | Opaque immutable identifier for one attestation edge | The multiplier applies only to this edge's attested-evidence contribution; it never identifies a witness, cluster, party, or credit as collusive. |
| `negativeMultiplier` | Internal derived decimal where `0 < value ≤ 1` | Multiplies this edge's contribution to dispute confidence. It is a weight, never a zeroing hard exclusion or a cap; its calibration and source signals remain unpublished. |
| `requiresNonTopologicalCorroboration` | Literal `true` | The collusion-detection signal is not sufficient to file, advance, adjudicate, or escalate a factual-dispute case. A transition into domain 24's due-process machinery that relies on this signal requires separately captured non-topological evidence. |

Absence of a `v1` record means no collusion-derived adjustment for that edge; it is never a positive trust signal. This constraint never blocks ordinary dispute filing or direct/witness resolution on other evidence. The payload omits raw scores, signal terms, cluster identifiers, witness scores, boolean ring flags, and recommendations for enforcement. Its presence is neither a verdict nor a case trigger.

The unavoidable difficulty, and the reason D-01 exists: **a tight real scene and a collusion ring have nearly the same topology.** Three people in a band who play on each other's records for a decade, in one city, attesting each other constantly, are a dense reciprocal insular cluster. They are also completely honest and are the platform's best users. A detector tuned to catch rings will catch them, and punishing them would be the worst possible error — it would tell exactly the users the product depends on that their real work looks fraudulent.

This asymmetry drives the whole design toward **demotion over enforcement**: quietly weight edges down rather than accuse anyone, and let domain 24 handle the cases where evidence beyond topology exists.

### Happy Path

1. Attestation and credit edges accumulate across the graph
2. Topology is analysed continuously — not per credit, since no individual credit looks wrong
3. Clusters with ring-shaped signatures are scored
4. High-scoring clusters have their edges' **internal score** multiplied down in `02.04.02`'s derivation, which moves traversal weight (`02.01.03`), search ordering and dispute weighting (`02.05`) — silently. **The rung does not move** (D-08)
5. Only clusters with corroborating non-topological evidence escalate to domain 24 for human review

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Inactive | Graph below density floor | Nothing; detection does not run |
| Monitoring | Normal operation | Nothing |
| Scored | Cluster exhibits signature | Nothing; internal score only |
| Demoted | Score above the (score-side) demotion cut | The edge's **internal score** is multiplied down in `02.04.02`'s derivation; traversal weight, search ordering and dispute weighting follow. **The rung does not move** (D-08) — no public label changes, no rung-gated consumer changes, and no explanation is given because there is nothing visible to explain |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Better evidence collection defeats fabricated credits | The natural conclusion from the rest of the domain — capture, corroborate, attest | ❌ **REJECTED** — and this rejection is why the feature exists at all. Every evidence mechanism in domain 02 is supplied *by the participants*. When the participants cooperate in the lie, the evidence is perfect and the fiction is fully corroborated. `02.02.04` DT-03 reaches the same conclusion from the attendance side. **Collusion is invisible from inside any single credit and only visible in the shape of the graph.** A credits platform that ships verification without topology analysis has shipped a laundering machine with a trust badge. |
| DT-02 | A collusion ring is topologically distinguishable from a real tight scene | The feature's founding assumption — otherwise it cannot work | ⚠️ **CONFIRMED WEAKLY, and this is the honest core of the feature.** They are *nearly identical*: three lifelong bandmates in one city, playing on each other's records, attesting constantly, with edges that rarely leave the cluster. That is a ring's signature and also the platform's best users. The distinguishing signals (implausible productivity, temporal clustering, convenient proximity to strangers) are weak and evadable. **The detector cannot be confidently right**, which is precisely why it demotes rather than accuses (D-01) — a system that cannot be sure must not punish. Anyone specifying this downstream must not "improve" it into an enforcement system. |
| DT-03 | Users should be told when they are flagged, so they can appeal | Due-process instinct, and a good one in general | ❌ **REJECTED here, ✅ CONFIRMED in domain 24.** Telling users teaches the adversary the detector's shape, and — given DT-02 — most notifications would be accusations of fraud against innocent tight-knit scenes. The resolution is the split: **topological suspicion demotes silently; only cases with independent evidence become 24 cases, where due process, notice and appeal apply properly.** Domain 02 must never be the thing that accuses anyone. It leaves a real gap (a wrongly-demoted user cannot appeal what they cannot see) and that gap is Q-02 — genuinely unresolved, not resolved-and-uncomfortable. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Demote silently; never accuse | DT-02. The detector cannot be confidently right, and a system that cannot be sure must not punish. |
| D-02 | Invisible to all four personas | Sub-domain D-04. Publishing suspicion defames; publishing the mechanism arms. |
| D-03 | Escalate to 24 only with non-topological evidence | Topology alone is suspicion, not proof. Domain 24 owns due process; domain 02 must not reinvent it badly. |
| D-04 | Off below a graph-density floor | Everything looks insular at 200 users. Running it early produces confident nonsense. |
| D-05 | **Demotion is per attestation edge, never per cluster** | Resolves Q-01. Mirrors `02.04.02`'s CX note ("the tier consumes a per-edge penalty term, never a cluster verdict"). A cluster-level penalty punishes membership — i.e. punishes a real tight scene for being a consistent band (DT-02). A per-edge penalty devalues only the specific edges the topology implicates, and the tier reads it as one negative multiplier term. |
| D-06 | **Detection runs on the attestation graph only; imported (rung-1) edges are excluded** | Resolves Q-03. Imported credits carry no attestation (`02.04.02` rung 1) and every topology signal here — reciprocity, insularity, temporal clustering — is defined over attestation edges. Feeding the full credit graph would dilute every signal with edges that were never vouched for. Imports are a claim-target for rings (they seed convenient proximity), but the *scored* graph is attestation-only. |
| D-07 | **`CollusionEvidenceConstraintV1` is the only dispute-facing detection contract** | Owner ratified CQ-03 Option B on 2026-07-19. `02.05` receives a per-attestation-edge negative multiplier plus literal `requiresNonTopologicalCorroboration: true`; no raw score, cluster/witness identity, boolean ring flag, trust score, hard exclusion, or topology-only escalation crosses the boundary. |
| D-08 | **Demotion is score-only: the provenance rung is invariant to detection** | Owner ratification of DQ-06.1, mirrored at `02.04.02` D-19. This file's own score-input contribution was always filed inside the score ("Detection never writes the rung"); the correction is that several rows here and elsewhere nonetheless said the tier drops. They do not. Every rung gate is a *kind* of evidence and a negative multiplier removes no evidence of any kind, so there is no rung-side cut — and none may be invented, since Q-04 defers only the score-side cut. A rung-moving detector would turn the kind-gated rung into a threshold on a number (reintroducing grindability), convert the public label into a probe oracle D-02 exists to prevent, and land maximum severity on DT-02's dominant false positive. **Accepted cost**: a fully corroborated fiction keeps its top-rung label, and its rung-gated credentials, indefinitely. The defence operates on ranking, search and dispute weighting only. |
| D-09 | **Every rung-gated consumer reads the same detection-invariant rung** — detection produces no per-consumer variant and no second derived value | Owner ratification of DQ-06.2, mirrored at `02.04.02` D-20. Per-consumer contracts and a shadow gate-rung both commission machinery and both reintroduce a probe surface on the party DT-02 says the detector is most likely to be wrong about. Two consequences to record: `02.08`'s re-emission staleness trigger must **drop demotion** (a demotion changes nothing a RIN carries), and `02.08`'s low-tier RIN exclusion is unreachable by demotion in any case because it fires on rungs 1–2. Domain 10 retains its own registration-floor decision. |
