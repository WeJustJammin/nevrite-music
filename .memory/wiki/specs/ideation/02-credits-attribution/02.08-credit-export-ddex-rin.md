# Feature: Credit Export & DDEX RIN Emission

> **Number**: 02.08
> **Parent**: [Credits & Attribution](./credits-attribution-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Getting the credit record **out** — as DDEX RIN (the industry's recording-information standard, purpose-built for exactly this data), as delivery metadata for a release, and as the user's own portable copy. Absorbs the sweep's "Contribution Ledger → DDEX RIN Emission" candidate; the ledger half was a near-duplicate of the credit record and folded into `02.01.01`, leaving emission as the real capability.

This feature is also the concrete answer to a question the ideation has open at the top level: `problem-statement.md` Q-02 asks whether WeJammin's lock-in is **earned or hostile** — "if a user leaves, what do they take?" Export is what makes the answer "everything, in a format the industry reads."

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full (own credits) | Exports their own credit history — for a PRO registration, a label, an accountant, or because they are leaving. Getting their data out must not be a support ticket or a dark pattern; it is the promise that makes the graph safe to invest in. |
| Producer | Full (own works) | The commercially important user. Delivering a record means delivering metadata, and `personas.md` names their switching trigger as "a client demanding delivery in a form they can't produce (proper metadata, signed splits, clearances)". This feature is that form. |
| Operator | Full (room's credits) | Exports the room's discography and session records — for their own archive, marketing, or accounting. |
| Fan | None | No export. The discography is readable (`02.01.02`); bulk extraction of the credit graph by non-professionals is a scraping surface, not a fan need. Explicit absence. |

## Behavior

Three distinct exports that are easy to conflate:

- **RIN emission** — the industry-standard package for a recording session's information. Strict, validated, and the thing a label or distributor can actually ingest.
- **Delivery metadata** — what accompanies a release (domain 12) to DSPs. Overlaps RIN, is not RIN.
- **Portability export** — the user's own data, complete and readable, on demand. A values commitment more than a feature.

Emission is gated on things the rest of the domain must have got right. RIN requires exact role codes (`02.06`) and resolved identifiers (`02.01.04`) — ISRC for the recording, ISWC for the work, IPI/ISNI for people. A graph full of unanchored parties and free-text roles cannot emit anything valid, which makes those two features hard preconditions rather than adjacent niceties.

**Embargo is a live leak path.** Export reads the graph directly, bypassing the discography page entirely (`02.01.05` DT-02). An unfiltered export of a Producer's catalog would hand a label a list of unreleased sessions and everyone on them. Export must be embargo-aware at the record.

The re-emission problem is the ugly one: once a RIN goes to a label, the platform has published a fact it no longer controls. Amendment (`02.01.06`), retraction (`02.04.03`) and ring demotion (`02.04.04`) all change credits after emission, and the downstream consumer holds a stale copy with no idea it is stale.

The part the platform **can** own is split cleanly from the part it cannot:

- **Stale detection (owned here, buildable now)** — every `Emitted` record stores, at emission time, the recipient, the emission timestamp, the exact set of credit IDs in scope, and a content version/hash of each of those credits. Stale detection is a comparison: current credit version ≠ emitted version.
- **Re-emission trigger (owned here, buildable now)** — amendment (`02.01.06`), retraction (`02.04.03`) and demotion (`02.04.04`) each fire a post-mutation event carrying the changed credit ID. The event handler scans `Emitted` records whose scope contains that ID; every match flips to `Stale` and the exporting user is notified in-app with a re-emit action targeting the same recipient. The trigger is deterministic and internal — no external dependency.
- **Downstream update contract (NOT owned here — `Q-01`)** — whether and how the external recipient (label/distributor) is told its copy is stale, and whether it accepts a re-send, is outside the platform's control and is the one genuinely unsolved piece deferred to `/create-prd`.

### Happy Path

1. User (usually Producer) needs to deliver a session's information
2. System validates: roles mapped to codes, parties and works anchored, embargo checked
3. Gaps are surfaced *before* generation — an invalid RIN rejected by a label is worse than a blocked one
4. Package generates; embargoed credits excluded unless the recipient is a participant
5. Delivered; the emission is recorded, so re-emission on later changes has a target

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Parties are unanchored (no IPI/ISNI) | RIN is invalid or degraded. The realistic default — most session players have no IPI | Pre-flight gap list; partial emission where the standard allows |
| Roles are unmappable to DDEX (`02.06` DT-01) | Platform vocabulary is legitimately ahead of the standard. Degrade to the nearest code and retain the true role internally | Mapping shown before emission |
| Embargoed credits in scope | Excluded by default; participant-scoped export may include them | Exclusion stated explicitly, not silently |
| Credit changes after emission (amend/retract/demote) | Mutation event fires; handler flips every `Emitted` record whose scope contains the changed credit ID to `Stale` and notifies the exporting user with a re-emit action for the same recipient. Whether the external consumer accepts a re-send is not the platform's call (`Q-01`) | Stale-copy banner on the affected emission record + "Re-emit to \<recipient\>" action |
| User exports to leave the platform | Works fully, without friction. Any degradation here converts earned lock-in into hostile lock-in | Complete export |
| Low-tier (import / self-asserted) credits in a **RIN** export | An import-tier credit emitted as RIN loses its provenance context — the receiving system has no tier concept and will treat it as fact. **Interim default (buildable now): low-tier credits are EXCLUDED from RIN emission**, appearing in the pre-flight gap list; the user may include them only via an explicit per-emission override that acknowledges the provenance loss. Portability export (`D-01`) is unaffected — the user's own copy always contains everything. The final product call on whether low-tier is ever RIN-eligible is `Q-02` | Excluded credits listed with reason "provenance tier not RIN-representable"; explicit "Include anyway (loses provenance)" override |
| Export volume as a scraping vector | Rate-limited and scoped to own data | Bounded |

### States

Canonical UI states first, then the emission-specific lifecycle they gate into.

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty | User has no exportable credits yet (new Musician, no sessions; Operator with no room discography) | "Nothing to export yet" + pointer to how credits accrue — never a blank pane |
| Loading | Pre-flight validation or package generation running | Progress indicator ("Validating N credits…" / "Generating RIN…"); generation is not instant at catalog scale |
| Not ready | Validation found gaps — unmapped roles (`02.06`), unanchored parties (`02.01.04`), or embargo conflicts | Gap list, one fix action per gap |
| Ready | Validation passes clean | Export offered with scope + format selector (RIN / delivery metadata / portability) |
| Degraded | Valid but lossy — unmapped roles or missing anchors the standard tolerates | Explicit statement of exactly what is lost, shown *before* the user commits |
| Emitted | Package generated and delivered | Record of what went where and when — this record is the re-emission target |
| Stale | A credit inside an `Emitted` record's scope changed (`02.01.06` amendment / `02.04.03` retraction / `02.04.04` demotion); detection = current credit version ≠ the version snapshotted at emission | Stale-copy warning + "Re-emit to \<recipient\>" action. Detection and the re-emit trigger are owned and buildable here; only the downstream consumer-update contract (whether the external recipient is told / accepts a re-send) is deferred to `Q-01` |
| Error | Generation or delivery failed *after* validation passed | Failure reason + retry; a partial/invalid package is never silently delivered |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Export weakens the moat — it lets users take the graph and leave | Standard platform reasoning; data is the lock-in | ❌ **REJECTED, and the inverse is true.** `problem-statement.md` DT-04 defers exactly this question and Q-02 asks it directly: is the lock-in earned or hostile? **The moat is not the data — it is being the place where the work happens** (D-18: consolidation is the precondition for provenance). Nobody can take that with a file. What they *can* take is their credit history, and letting them is what makes it safe to invest twenty years of career in the graph. Withholding export would make WeJammin a hostage-taker in exactly the market whose defining grievance is that their career data is trapped in other people's systems. Export is a feature of the thesis, not a leak in it. |
| DT-02 | RIN emission is a straightforward serialisation of the credit record | It looks like a format conversion | ❌ **REJECTED** — RIN demands exact role codes and resolved industry identifiers, and **the graph will not have them for most parties**. Session players overwhelmingly have no IPI/ISNI; DAW-captured works have no ISRC until a distributor mints one. So emission is gated on `02.01.04` and `02.06` being genuinely complete, not merely present. This reframes those two features from internal hygiene into commercial preconditions — and it means emission's real cost is upstream, where it will not be noticed by anyone specifying this file in isolation. |
| DT-03 | Provenance tier travels with the export | The tier is the differentiator, so surely it goes with the data | ❌ **REJECTED — and it is a genuine loss.** DDEX RIN has no concept of "confirmed by four people present versus typed into Discogs by a stranger". Everything emits flat, and the receiving system treats an import-tier rumour identically to a session-captured attested fact. **The platform's entire differentiator does not survive its own export.** Nothing here fixes that; it is a standards gap. It does suggest that the tier is a reason to *stay* rather than a thing to ship, and it raises a live question (Q-02) about whether low-tier credits should be exportable as RIN at all — emitting a rumour as though it were a record puts the platform's name on someone else's guess. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Full, frictionless portability export — always | DT-01. The moat is presence at the work, not hostage-holding. Any friction here converts earned lock-in into hostile. |
| D-02 | Validation gaps surface before generation | An invalid RIN rejected by a label costs the user a client relationship; a blocked export costs them ten minutes. |
| D-03 | Export is embargo-aware at the record | It bypasses the page entirely; a render-layer filter does not protect it. |
| D-04 | Degraded exports state what was lost | Silent lossy export means the user discovers the gap when the label rejects it. |
| D-05 | Stale detection + internal re-emit trigger are owned here and buildable now; `Emitted` records snapshot recipient, timestamp, credit-ID scope, and per-credit version. Mutation events (`02.01.06` / `02.04.03` / `02.04.04`) flip matching records to `Stale` and notify the exporter | Separates the solvable half (detect the drift, tell the user, offer re-emit) from the genuinely external half (`Q-01`: convincing the downstream consumer). No reason to leave the whole thing `[PENDING]` when most of it is deterministic and internal. |
| D-06 | Low-tier (import / self-asserted) credits are excluded from RIN emission by default, surfaced in the pre-flight gap list, includable only via explicit per-emission override; portability export (`D-01`) always includes everything | Conservative default protects the platform's name from being attached to an unattested guess (DT-03). Interim, pending the final product ruling in `Q-02` — but the export gate is buildable today rather than blocked. |

| Q-01 | **Downstream update contract only.** Stale detection and the internal re-emit trigger are now owned by this feature (see Behavior + `Stale` state). This is outside platform control. Still the domain's most-flagged problem (`02.01.06`, `02.04.03`, `02.04.04` all hit it). | User | `/create-prd` |
