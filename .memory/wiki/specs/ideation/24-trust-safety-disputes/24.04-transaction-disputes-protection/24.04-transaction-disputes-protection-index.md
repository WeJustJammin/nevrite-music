## Overview

What happens when a transaction goes wrong and two parties disagree: the claim, the structured
negotiation before adjudication, the programme that decides who eats the loss, and the bank-side path
when the buyer goes around the platform entirely. This is the money half of adjudication — where
[24.01](../24.01-reporting-moderation/24.01-reporting-moderation-index.md) handles "this content is
wrong", this sub-domain handles "this deal is wrong".

**Why a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities with one shared evidence spine and one shared
state machine. A claim (24.04.01) either resolves in mediation (24.04.02) or escalates to adjudication;
the outcome determines whether protection pays (24.04.03); and a chargeback (24.04.04) is the same
dispute arriving through the card network instead of the platform, defended with the same evidence.

**The music-specific shape**: a dispute here is not an order dispute. It spans five object types with
five different remedy sets — an order (gear can be returned), a booking (an unsold Saturday cannot),
a service (a mix "not as described" is subjective), a settlement (door money after a show), and a
split (which is not this sub-domain's at all — see D-02).

| D-01 | **Claims and mediation split** from the sweep's single "Disputes, Claims, Evidence Locker & Mediation" candidate | Three separable things were merged. The **evidence locker** serves every adjudication surface in the domain, not just transaction disputes, and was promoted to a domain-level feature ([24.09](../24.09-case-evidence-locker.md)). **Mediation** is a party-to-party flow with no adjudicator; **claims** is intake and state. Different actors, different lifecycles. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 classification |
| D-02 | **Split and credit disputes are NOT this sub-domain's** | A contested split is a rights adjudication (domain 09) and a contested credit is an attribution adjudication (domain 02). They share intake (24.01.01) and evidence (24.09) but not remedy: you cannot refund a songwriting share. Routing them here would put the product's core asset in a returns queue. | `/ideate-discover` Deep Think |
| D-03 | Escrow, holds, refunds and the ledger are the `Payments, Escrow & Payouts` cross-cut's | This sub-domain decides *who should be paid what*; Payments moves the money. The cross-cut already owns "escrow with defined release triggers, refunds, and a double-entry ledger the platform owns". | Domain map cross-cut table |
