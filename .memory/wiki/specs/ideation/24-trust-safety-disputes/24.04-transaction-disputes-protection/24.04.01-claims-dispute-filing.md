# Feature: Claims & Dispute Filing

> **Number**: 24.04.01
> **Parent**: [Transaction Disputes & Protection](./24.04-transaction-disputes-protection-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-18

## Overview

The front door for "this deal went wrong": a party opens a claim against a transaction, states what
happened, and the case acquires a state machine, a deadline and an evidence bundle. Its defining design
problem is that **a WeJammin transaction is not one thing**. An order, a booking, a service engagement
and a live settlement are four different objects with four different remedy sets — and the fifth thing
people will try to file, a contested split, is not this feature's at all (24.04 D-02).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Files from both chairs, sometimes in the same week: as a buyer (the amp arrived with a cracked tube), as a seller (the buyer says it was cracked and it wasn't), as a service provider (the client won't approve the mix or release escrow), as a band member (the venue never paid the settlement). Their claim is usually about money they need this month. |
| Producer | Full | Files service disputes where "not as described" has no objective test — but with one advantage no other platform can offer: the platform holds the session record showing what was delivered, when, and what was asked for. See DT-03. |
| Operator | Full | Files against no-shows and unpaid deposits; defends against cancellation disputes. **The remedy set breaks for this persona**: their inventory is time, and time cannot be returned (`personas.md`: an unsold Tuesday is gone forever). A refund is not a remedy for a lost Saturday — it is the *absence* of a remedy. |
| Fan | Full | Files on merch and tickets. The only persona for whom a refund is a complete remedy, and the highest volume of claims by count. Also the persona whose named anti-persona behaviour includes refund abuse. |

## Behavior

A claim binds to a transaction object and inherits that object's remedy set. Filing does **not** gather
evidence — it retrieves it from [24.09](../24.09-case-evidence-locker.md), because the evidence that
decides the case (condition photos, serial, delivery proof, the message thread, the session record) was
capturable only at listing, dispatch and delivery time (24.04 CX-05). A claim filed against a transaction
with no captured evidence is two people's memories, which is the failure `problem-statement.md` says the
entire product exists to prevent.

Every claim enters mediation first (24.04 CX-01); adjudication is the escape hatch. Filing windows differ
by object type and are constrained by statutory returns rights (the Tax/consumer-protection cross-cut) and
by the card networks' own clocks (24.04.04).

### Happy Path

1. A party opens a claim from the transaction object
2. The claim inherits the object's type, its remedy set and its filing window
3. The claimant states what happened and selects a desired remedy from that set
4. The evidence bundle is assembled automatically from what was captured at the time
5. The counterparty is notified; mediation opens (24.04.02)
6. Settled → the claim closes. Unsettled → escalation to adjudication
7. Adjudicated loss meeting the policy → protection (24.04.03)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Band no-shows a booked venue slot | The remedy set has no "return". Deposit forfeiture is the only lever, and it does not cover the lost night | `[PENDING]` — 24.04 Q-02 is unresolved and this is why |
| Mix delivered; client says "not what I asked for" | Subjective — but the brief, the revision thread and the delivered versions are all on-platform (DT-03) | Both parties see the same record |
| Counterparty ignores the claim | Timeout resolves **in the filer's favour** by default, or silence becomes a winning strategy | "No response — resolved in your favour" |
| Buyer files a claim *and* a chargeback | Must link to one case, or the seller pays twice (24.04 CX-03) | Seller: one case; the chargeback overrides the platform's clock |
| Claim filed against a band entity | Who may respond on the entity's behalf? A domain 01 mandate question, and a settlement is a binding act | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| Claimant tries to file a split dispute here | Routed to domain 09; it is not a transaction dispute and has no refund remedy | "Routed to the rights team" — the clock must not restart |
| Escrow holds money belonging to five people; two of them are disputing | A two-party dispute freezes the band's and the producer's money too (Payments cross-cut: one payment splits across many) | Third parties: notified, and correctly angry |
| No evidence was captured at listing time | The claim is unwinnable on facts | The honest answer, not a coin flip — see 24.03.03 Q-03 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Available | Transaction within its filing window | "Report a problem" on the transaction |
| Filed | Claim opened | Case ID, remedy options, deadline |
| In mediation | Counterparty notified | Proposal thread (24.04.02) |
| Settled | Agreement reached | Terms + execution |
| Escalated | Mediation failed — refusal, impasse, or the window elapsing on a counterparty who actually proposed ([24.04.02](./24.04.02-mediation-resolution-center.md) States). A timeout against **silence** is not this row: it resolves in the filer's favour per D-03 | "Under review" with a real deadline |
| Adjudicated | Decision reached | Reasoned outcome + remedy |
| Superseded | Chargeback filed on the same transaction | "This is now with your bank" |
| Expired | Filing window closed | No claim route |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A dispute is an order dispute — one flow, one remedy set | Every marketplace's dispute system | ❌ REJECTED — WeJammin has **four transaction types with incompatible remedies**, and a fifth thing users will try to file that is not a transaction at all. An *order* can be returned and refunded. A *booking* cannot — the Saturday is gone, and refunding the deposit does not restore the night the Operator could have sold (`personas.md` calls the inventory perishable and it is the persona's defining constraint). A *service* has no objective standard for "as described". A *live settlement* is an arithmetic dispute over door money after the fact. And a *split* has no monetary remedy at all (24.04 D-02). One claim form for all of these produces the right answer for gear and the wrong answer for everything else — and gear is not even the thesis. |
| DT-02 | Evidence is gathered when the claim is filed | The universal design | ❌ REJECTED — and this is the product's own argument, restated. `problem-statement.md`: "the facts of the work are never recorded... then reconstructed years later, from memory, usually at the exact moment they become contested and valuable." A dispute is *precisely* that moment. The condition photos, the serial, the delivery signature, the brief, the revision thread — all are capturable at listing, dispatch and delivery, and none at filing. **A dispute system that gathers evidence at filing is asking people to remember**, which is the thing the platform exists to stop doing. Filing must be retrieval (24.04 CX-05). |
| DT-03 | Creative-service disputes are irreducibly subjective, so the platform can only split the difference | The reason SoundBetter-style platforms adjudicate badly | ❌ REJECTED — **and this is the one place domain 24 touches the wedge directly.** For a service dispute, WeJammin holds the brief, the revision thread, the delivered versions with timestamps, the approval (or its absence), and the session provenance (D-18). "Not what I asked for" becomes checkable against what was actually asked for. It does not make taste objective — a client can still dislike a mix that matches the brief exactly — but it converts most of these disputes from *opinion vs opinion* into *record vs opinion*. **No other services marketplace can do this, because none of them are in the room** — which is the thesis, applied to adjudication. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A claim inherits its remedy set from the transaction type | DT-01. Four types, four remedy models. |
| D-02 | Filing retrieves evidence; it does not collect it | DT-02. This makes listing/dispatch schema a dispute control, which is a dependency on domains 13/17, not a domain 24 build. |
| D-03 | Counterparty silence resolves in the filer's favour | Otherwise not responding is the dominant strategy. |
