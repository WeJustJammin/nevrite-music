## Overview

The children that answer **"whose is this, and are they who they say they are?"** — an impersonator taking
deposits under a musician's name, a band account held hostage by a departed manager, and the estate of a
musician who has died while their catalogue keeps earning. These are the disputes the domain map explicitly
stripped from Identity and routed here, on the reasoning that they are *adjudication*, not ontology: domain
01 owns the entity model and the membership graph; this sub-domain owns the contested case.

**Why a sub-domain, not features**: three capabilities resolved by the same evidence against the same graph.
A departed manager who keeps posting as the band is simultaneously an impersonation report and an ownership
dispute; a deceased member's estate claim is an ownership dispute with a different evidentiary basis
(probate rather than mandate). All three ask domain 01's membership graph the same question and get an
answer the credential cannot give.

**The line with [24.03](../24.03-fraud-risk-operations/24.03-fraud-risk-operations-index.md)**: signals
there, claims here (24.03 D-01). "Someone took my account" is detected (ATO); "someone is pretending to be
me" and "this is my band's account" are *asserted*, with evidence, by a human.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | **Impersonation moved here** from the sweep's copyright/counterfeit grouping | The sweep paired "impersonation/counterfeit control". Counterfeit is a claim about an *object* resolved against a brand register and expert examination; impersonation is a claim about a *person* resolved against the identity and credit graph. Grouping them would give a fake-Rick-Rubin profile the same adjudication path as a fake SM58. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 classification |
| D-02 | **Deceased & Incapacitated Succession added** — not in the candidate list | Music's economics are **posthumous by design**: catalogues are the asset class and estates are major industry actors. A platform that holds the ownership record (D-10, D-18) and has no succession path becomes unusable to the estate at the exact moment the record is most valuable — which is the moment the thesis is finally proved. Also: a band with a deceased member cannot make decisions requiring unanimous consent, which is a governance deadlock domain 01's model must anticipate. | `/ideate-discover` Deep Think addition |
| D-03 | **Provenance is this sub-domain's anti-impersonation mechanism** | Verification badges scale to famous people. The modal victim here is a session drummer nobody would verify, whose name is used to take deposits. What an impersonator cannot fake is a **counter-attested credit graph** (`personas.md`: a Producer's attestation is worth more than a self-claim). See 24.07.01 DT-01. | `/ideate-discover` Deep Think |
| D-04 | Credential architecture, MFA and the recovery flow *mechanics* are `/create-prd-security`'s | Already routed by the domain map ("Authentication, Session & Account Recovery Architecture"). This sub-domain owns the **contested** recovery — where two humans both claim the account — not the forgot-password flow. | Domain map not-product routing |
