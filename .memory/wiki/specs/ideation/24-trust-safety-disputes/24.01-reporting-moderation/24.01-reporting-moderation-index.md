## Overview

The intake half of the adjudication machine: the single front door through which any user of any
of the 24 domains says "this is wrong", plus the automated pre-triage, the queue that routes work
to a human, and the priority lane for rights-holders. Every enforcement action in domain 24
originates here — this sub-domain decides *what gets looked at, by whom, and how fast*, and
[24.02](../24.02-enforcement-appeals-policy/24.02-enforcement-appeals-policy-index.md) decides
what happens next.

**Why a sub-domain, not a feature**: five distinct capabilities that interact tightly — a report
(24.01.01) and a classifier hit (24.01.02) both land in the same queue (24.01.03), a trusted
flagger (24.01.04) jumps that queue with a different SLA and legal status, and message-surface
signals (24.01.05) feed both the queue and the risk engine in
[24.03](../24.03-fraud-risk-operations/24.03-fraud-risk-operations-index.md).
