# Feature: Report Intake & Notice-and-Action Flow

> **Number**: 24.01.01
> **Parent**: [Reporting, Moderation & Notice-and-Action](./24.01-reporting-moderation-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The single front door for "this is wrong", reachable from every object in all 24 domains — a listing,
a profile, a message, a review, a gig, a stem, a credit claim. A reporter picks a reason from a
taxonomy bound to the policy library, attaches evidence, and receives a case ID and a confirmation of
receipt; the notice is then routed, decided and closed against a clock. This is the feature the DSA
mandates exist (Art 16: electronic, easy to access, user-friendly, with confirmation of receipt and a
timely, reasoned, non-arbitrary decision) — and it is the one child of domain 24 with no size carve-out.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Reports an impersonator taking deposits under their name, a producer who published a track crediting someone who didn't play on it, a scam DM offering a fake tour, a listing using their photo. Files from the object itself — never navigates to a "safety centre". Sees their own case list with status and the final reasoned decision. Because a Musician is often *also* the acting member of a band entity, they are the persona most likely to file — or be reported *through* — an entity (see Edge Cases: entity resolution). |
| Producer | Full | Reports a leaked unreleased mix, a contributor claiming a credit they didn't earn, a stem pack containing an uncleared sample that exposes everyone downstream, a client who is abusive in the project thread. Most likely persona to file a *factual* dispute rather than a policy violation — the taxonomy must not force them to mislabel it (DT-01), and the reason they select, not their role, decides the lane (DT-06). |
| Operator | Full | Reports a fraudulent booking enquiry, a fake listing cloning their room's photos and specs, a band that no-showed, a promoter running a ticketed event at their venue that they never agreed to. Files from a phone at a loading dock — the flow must survive one hand and bad signal (see States: Error, and the network-failure edge case). |
| Fan | Full | Reports harassment, a scalped ticket listing, counterfeit merch, an artist impersonator asking for money. Highest volume, lowest precision (D-13), and the only persona with no professional stake — the flow must be usable with zero domain vocabulary. Also the persona most likely to be the *subject* of a harassment case (`personas.md` anti-persona), which is why the anonymity split (D-02) is load-bearing for this persona above all. |

> **No persona has "None" access here, and that is deliberate.** Reporting is the one universal
> right in the product — every account holder, in every role, can file. The actor missing from this
> table is the **moderator/admin**, who is *not* one of the four personas (`personas.md` Q-02) and who
> acts on the case downstream in 24.01.03, not at intake. A **logged-out visitor** is not a persona
> and gets a reduced flow (see the permission edge case), not a "None" row.

## Behavior

Reporting is available from every reportable object via a consistent affordance. The reason taxonomy is
versioned and bound to [24.02.04 Policy Library](../24.02-enforcement-appeals-policy/24.02.04-policy-library-versioned-terms.md)
— every reason maps to a rule in force, so a decision can always cite one. Three reason families
**bypass the general moderation queue entirely**: CSAM (→ 24.08.01, preservation duty), self-harm
(→ 24.06.03, no sanction ever), and factual rights disputes (→ domains 02/09, not a policy question).

Legal notices (DMCA, counter-notice, trusted-flagger submissions) require an identified reporter and a
statement under penalty of perjury; safety reports permit anonymity to the target. That split is
load-bearing — a harassment victim reporting non-anonymously is a retaliation vector.

**Two invariants govern intake and everything below inherits them:**

1. **A submitted report is never lost.** From the moment the user taps Submit, the report either
   produces a case ID or is preserved as a retryable draft. A dropped report is a statutory breach of
   DSA Art 16, not a UI inconvenience — so intake never responds with an unrecoverable error, and
   rate-limiting throttles *priority*, never *acceptance* (DT-04).
2. **Severity comes from the reason code and the classifier, never from the count of reporters** (DT-03).
   Volume dedups; it does not escalate.

### Happy Path

1. User opens a reportable object and selects **Report**
2. System presents the reason taxonomy, scoped to what this object type can actually be reported for
   (a stem cannot be reported for "no-show"; a booking cannot be reported for "uncleared sample")
3. User selects a reason; system asks only for the evidence that reason requires (free-text ≤ 5,000
   chars for harassment; a claimed-work URL for a credit dispute; a rights identifier for DMCA)
4. User submits; system dedups against existing open cases on the same target + reason within a rolling
   30-day window
5. System issues a case ID within 2 s and a confirmation of receipt, and states the expected response
   window as a concrete date (Q-02 sets the number; the mechanism is fixed here)
6. Case routes to a queue (24.01.03) or bypasses it (CSAM / self-harm / factual dispute)
7. On decision, reporter receives the outcome; target receives a Statement of Reasons (24.02.03)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Retaliatory counter-report** — reported user reports their reporter back | Both cases proceed independently; the A→B/B→A linkage is recorded as a signal on each case, never as a defence or an auto-dismissal | Nothing — the reporter is not told their target counter-reported |
| **Brigading** — 400 fans report one artist's post | Dedups to one case with 400 reporter references; count is stored but is **not** an enforcement input (DT-03) | Each reporter gets a receipt; all receive the identical outcome |
| **Target deleted before review** | Case survives via the intake evidence snapshot ([24.09](../24.09-case-evidence-locker.md)); delete does not moot the notice | "This case is still open" |
| **Report filed against a band/entity, not a member** | The case attaches to the **entity**, but any sanction must resolve to the **acting member** — the account recorded as the actor on the *specific reported object* in the intake snapshot (the member who sent the message, published the credit, posted the listing). If the report targets a shared surface with no single actor (an entity bio, a co-owned profile photo), the case is held `awaiting-actor-resolution` and routed to 24.01.03 for a human to identify the actor against domain 01's membership graph *before* any sanction; sanctions are then scoped to the resolved actor or to the shared surface only, **never** cascaded to co-owners' individual accounts. A co-owner reporting their own entity is accepted, proceeds, and is flagged `self-conflict` for the reviewer | Reporter sees a normal receipt; the entity's innocent co-owners see nothing unless they are the resolved actor |
| **Reporter picks the wrong lane** (files a split dispute as "harassment") | Reviewer re-routes; the re-route preserves the original `receivedAt` timestamp so the reporter's statutory clock does not restart | "Your report was routed to the rights team" |
| **Intake-abuse spike** — one reporter files a flood to bury a target or exhaust review | Rate-limit is **per reporter identity, never per target** (throttling per target lets an abuser suppress reports about a target). A reporter may file **20 distinct reports per rolling 60 min** across all targets; submissions 21+ still receive a case ID and receipt (invariant 1 — never dropped) but are tagged `intake-abuse-suspect`, deprioritised, and the *reporter* is handed to 24.03 fraud/risk for account-side review. Coordinated multi-account floods are caught by dedup + device/account clustering in 24.03, not by rejecting reports | Reporter sees a normal receipt (throttling is invisible; visible throttling teaches abusers the ceiling) |
| **Concurrent duplicate submit** — user double-taps Submit, or two devices submit the same report | Each report draft carries a stable identity, so the system recognises the second submission as the same report and collapses both to one case; the duplicate returns the same case ID, not a new case | One receipt, one case ID |
| **Concurrent report + target-edit** — target edits/deletes the object in the same instant the report lands | The intake snapshot is taken at `receivedAt` and is immutable; the report captures the object *as reported*, regardless of an edit landing 200 ms later | Reporter's evidence reflects what they saw |
| **Empty / no reason selected** | Submit is disabled until a reason is chosen; the taxonomy is required, free-text is optional unless the chosen reason requires it | Inline "Select a reason to continue" |
| **Malicious input** — XSS/script in free-text, malformed rights ID, or a weaponised evidence upload (malware, 2 GB zip, polyglot file) | Free-text is stored as text and never rendered as HTML (escaped on every surface); evidence uploads are size-capped (≤ 25 MB/file, ≤ 5 files), validated against their declared type, scanned for malware in the background, and served in a sandboxed context that cannot execute — a failed scan quarantines the file but the report proceeds on its text | "That file couldn't be attached" — report still submits |
| **Permission — logged-out visitor reports** | Public objects accept an unauthenticated report but require a verified email challenge before the case is enqueued (anti-drive-by); the case exists immediately (invariant 1) but is `pending-reporter-verification` | "Confirm your email to complete this report" |
| **Permission — blocked/suspended user reports** | A user under sanction may still file (removing the reporting right from a suspended user is itself an abuse vector); their report is accepted and flagged `reporter-under-sanction` for reviewer context | Normal receipt |
| **Network failure mid-submit** | Draft (reason + free-text + evidence refs) is preserved on the device under the report's stable identity; on reconnect the same identity resubmits without creating a duplicate. A report the client believes failed but the server accepted resolves to one case, not two | "Saved — we'll finish sending when you're back online" |
| **Partial data** — reason + text submit, but evidence upload is still in flight or failed | Case is created immediately on the text (invariant 1); evidence attaches asynchronously and links to the open case by ID; a permanently failed upload leaves the case `evidence-incomplete` for the reviewer, it does not block the case | "Report received — attachment still uploading" |
| **Multi-hyphenate reporter in two roles at once** — the reporter is both the Producer who owns the session *and* a credited Musician on the disputed track | Routing keys off the **reason code + object type**, not the reporter's role(s) (DT-06), so holding two roles never changes the lane. If the two roles put the reporter on *both sides* (they own the entity being reported), the `self-conflict` flag applies but the report still proceeds | Normal receipt; no role-picker is ever shown |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Reason taxonomy being fetched/scoped to the object type | Skeleton reason list; the object stays visible behind it so the reporter keeps context |
| Empty | User has never reported | No case list; the report affordance lives on objects, not in a hub |
| Submitting | Report sent, case ID not yet returned | Optimistic receipt with a provisional spinner on the case ID — must not block on classification |
| Populated (Received) | Case created | "Received — we'll respond by {date}" with the case ID; entry appears in the reporter's case list |
| Under review | Assigned to a reviewer | Status only; never the reviewer's identity |
| Decided | Outcome reached | Reasoned decision; if the reporter is also the target, the SoR path (24.02.03) applies |
| Rerouted | Wrong lane detected at or after intake | "Routed to {team}" — original clock preserved |
| Error | Submission failed at the network layer | Retry affordance with the full draft preserved under the report's stable identity — a lost report is a statutory breach, not a dropped form |
| Partial | Case created but evidence still uploading or scan-quarantined | "Report received — attachment still uploading" / "attachment couldn't be added" with the case already live |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A generic marketplace/social report taxonomy is sufficient | Every incumbent's report flow | ❌ REJECTED — a music platform's reason list must include "credit theft", "uncleared sample", "this isn't my performance" and "unreleased material leaked". No generic taxonomy has them, and they are not policy violations at all — they are factual claims about provenance that route to domains 02/09. A taxonomy without them forces the platform's *signature* harm into an "Other" box. |
| DT-02 | All reports are moderation cases | Domain framing as one adjudication engine | ❌ REJECTED — the taxonomy must triage into at least three lanes: **policy violation** (24.02), **factual dispute** (domains 02/09/24.04), **welfare** (24.06.03). Handing a contested split to a content moderator produces a confidently wrong answer with a Statement of Reasons attached to it, which is worse than no answer because it is now evidence. This triage is the single highest-leverage decision in the domain and it is made at intake. |
| DT-03 | Report volume is a useful signal of severity | Standard queue-prioritisation heuristic | ❌ REJECTED — the Fan persona outnumbers professionals by orders of magnitude (D-13). Volume-as-signal hands any fanbase the power to suppress an artist, and music has a documented brigading culture. Severity must come from the reason code and the classifier, never from the count of reporters. |
| DT-04 | Under abuse, intake may reject reports (429/hard cap) to protect reviewers | Standard rate-limiting practice | ❌ REJECTED — a rejected report is a dropped statutory notice (DSA Art 16). The correct lever is *priority*, not *acceptance*: over-quota reports are still issued a case ID and receipt, then deprioritised and the *reporter* is sent to fraud review (24.03). Throttling must key on the reporter, never the target, or an abuser suppresses reports simply by reporting their target first. This is textually distinct from DT-03: DT-03 is about how volume feeds *severity*; DT-04 is about how volume feeds *admission*. |
| DT-05 | A report against a band entity can sanction the entity (and thus its members) directly | Naive "report the account that owns the object" model | ❌ REJECTED — bands are first-class entities with co-owners (`personas.md`, domain 01). Sanctioning the entity punishes innocent co-owners for one member's act. Sanction must resolve to the **acting member** recorded on the reported object's intake snapshot, or — when no single actor exists — be held for human actor-resolution against domain 01's membership graph and scoped to the shared surface only. |
| DT-06 | The reporter's role should influence which lane the report routes to | Role-Lens-driven design instinct | ❌ REJECTED — routing must key on **reason code + object type**, not the reporter's persona, because multi-hyphenates hold several roles simultaneously (`personas.md` structural fact) and a Producer-and-Musician on the same track has no single "role" to route by. A role-based router is undefined for the platform's central user. Confirmed by construction in the multi-hyphenate edge case. |
| DT-07 | Intake must be instrumented so a dropped report is detectable, not merely "shouldn't happen" | Invariant-1 hardening | ✅ CONFIRMED — every Submit carries a stable report identity and a device-persisted draft, and every submitted report is durably recorded before it is acknowledged. The pairing means the set of submitted reports and the set of created cases are reconcilable, so a gap is an alertable defect rather than an invisible statutory breach. This is the mechanism that makes "never lost" auditable rather than aspirational. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **[24.02.04 Policy Library](../24.02-enforcement-appeals-policy/24.02.04-policy-library-versioned-terms.md)** — the reason taxonomy is a *projection* of the versioned rulebook; an unbound reason cannot be cited in a decision. **Trigger dependency**: a policy-library version bump can add/retire reason codes, so intake must pin the taxonomy version onto each case.
- Touches **[24.09 Case Evidence Locker](../24.09-case-evidence-locker.md)** — the immutable snapshot is taken *at* `receivedAt`; a deleted/edited target does not moot the case. **Shared entity**: the case's evidence record originates here and is owned there.
- Touches **[24.06.03 Crisis & Welfare Escalation](../24.06-personal-safety-threat-response/24.06.03-crisis-welfare-escalation.md)** — the self-harm bypass is *defined* here (reason family) and *executed* there (no sanction ever). **Trigger dependency + state-race**: the bypass must fire before any queue enqueue so a welfare case is never sanctioned.
- Touches **[24.08.01 CSAM handling]** — CSAM reason family bypasses the queue into the preservation-duty path; intake must not expose or re-render the reported media. **Permission + notification**: no human-review UI may surface the payload at intake.
- Touches **[24.03 Fraud & Risk Operations](../24.03-fraud-risk-operations/24.03-fraud-risk-operations-index.md)** — over-quota reporters and multi-account brigading clusters are handed off here. **Trigger dependency**: intake emits the `intake-abuse-suspect` signal; 24.03 owns the account-side adjudication.
- Touches **Domain 01 (Identity/Entities)** — entity-to-acting-member resolution reads domain 01's membership graph. **Shared entity + permission**: the band-entity model and co-ownership roles are owned there; intake only *consumes* the graph to scope sanction.
- Touches **Domain 02 (Credits)** and **Domain 09 (Rights)** — the factual-dispute lane hands off to their adjudication, not to a moderator. **Trigger dependency**: intake classifies into their lane; they own the outcome and its notice. A collusion-associated credit case may enter Domain 24's due-process machinery only with separately captured non-topological corroboration; `02.04.04` topology and its internal `CollusionEvidenceConstraintV1` payload never independently create or advance a case.
- Consumed by the **Notifications & Alerts** cross-cut — receipt, status, reroute and decision notices. **Notification**: each state transition emits a reporter-facing (and, on decision, target-facing SoR) event.
- Consumed by the **Admin, Back-Office & Support** cross-cut — the case surfaces inside the operator/moderator shell, which this feature does **not** own (24.01 D-02). **Permission**: reviewer identity is never exposed to reporter or target.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Reporting is an affordance on objects, not a destination | Reports are filed in anger, in seconds, usually on a phone. A "Safety Centre" the user must navigate to is a report that never gets filed. |
| D-02 | Legal notices require identity; safety reports permit anonymity | DMCA requires a sworn identified statement; a harassment victim reporting under their own name is handing the abuser a retaliation target. One flow, two identity regimes. |
| D-03 | A submitted report is never rejected for volume — over-quota reports are admitted, deprioritised, and the reporter is sent to fraud review | Rejecting a report is a dropped statutory notice (DSA Art 16). The correct lever against intake abuse is priority + reporter-side review, not refusal. (DT-04) |
| D-04 | Rate-limiting keys on the **reporter**, never the **target** | A per-target throttle lets an abuser suppress all reports about a target simply by filing first. Per-reporter is the only safe axis. (DT-04) |
| D-05 | Routing keys on **reason code + object type**, never on the reporter's role | Multi-hyphenates hold several roles at once; a role-based router is undefined for the platform's central persona. (DT-06) |
| D-06 | Entity reports resolve sanction to the **acting member** (or the shared surface), never to co-owners | Sanctioning a band entity punishes innocent co-owners for one member's act. Resolve against the intake snapshot's actor, or hold for human resolution against domain 01's graph. (DT-05) |
| D-07 | Re-submitting a report yields the same case, and drafts are preserved on the device | Guarantees invariant 1 under double-taps, multi-device submits, and network loss, and makes a dropped report an *alertable* reconciliation gap rather than an invisible breach. (DT-07) |
