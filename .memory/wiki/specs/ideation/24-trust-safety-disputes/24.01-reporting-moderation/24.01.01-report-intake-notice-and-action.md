# Feature: Report Intake & Notice-and-Action Flow

> **Number**: 24.01.01
> **Parent**: [Reporting, Moderation & Notice-and-Action](./24.01-reporting-moderation-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The single front door for "this is wrong", reachable from every object in all 24 domains — a listing,
a profile, a message, a review, a gig, a stem, a credit claim. A reporter picks a reason from a
taxonomy bound to the policy library, attaches evidence, and receives a case ID and a confirmation of
receipt; the notice is then routed, decided and closed against a clock. This is the feature the DSA
mandates exist (Art 16: electronic, easy to access, user-friendly, with confirmation of receipt and a
timely, reasoned, non-arbitrary decision) — and it is the one child of domain 24 with no size carve-out.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Reports an impersonator taking deposits under their name, a producer who published a track crediting someone who didn't play on it, a scam DM offering a fake tour, a listing using their photo. Files from the object itself — never navigates to a "safety centre". Sees their case list with status and the final reasoned decision. |
| Producer | Full | Reports a leaked unreleased mix, a contributor claiming a credit they didn't earn, a stem pack containing an uncleared sample that exposes everyone downstream, a client who is abusive in the project thread. Most likely persona to file a *factual* dispute rather than a policy violation — the taxonomy must not force them to mislabel it. |
| Operator | Full | Reports a fraudulent booking enquiry, a fake listing cloning their room's photos and specs, a band that no-showed, a promoter running a ticketed event at their venue that they never agreed to. Files from a phone at a loading dock — the flow must survive one hand and bad signal. |
| Fan | Full | Reports harassment, a scalped ticket listing, counterfeit merch, an artist impersonator asking for money. Highest volume, lowest precision, and the only persona with no professional stake — the flow must be usable with zero domain vocabulary. Also the persona most likely to be the *subject* of a harassment case (`personas.md` anti-persona). |

## Behavior

Reporting is available from every reportable object via a consistent affordance. The reason taxonomy is
versioned and bound to [24.02.04 Policy Library](../24.02-enforcement-appeals-policy/24.02.04-policy-library-versioned-terms.md)
— every reason maps to a rule in force, so a decision can always cite one. Three reason families
**bypass the general moderation queue entirely**: CSAM (→ 24.08.01, preservation duty), self-harm
(→ 24.06.03, no sanction ever), and factual rights disputes (→ domains 02/09, not a policy question).

Legal notices (DMCA, counter-notice, trusted-flagger submissions) require an identified reporter and a
statement under penalty of perjury; safety reports permit anonymity to the target. That split is
load-bearing — a harassment victim reporting non-anonymously is a retaliation vector.

### Happy Path

1. User opens a reportable object and selects **Report**
2. System presents the reason taxonomy, scoped to what this object type can actually be reported for
3. User selects a reason; system asks only for the evidence that reason requires
4. User submits; system dedups against existing cases on the same target
5. System issues a case ID and confirmation of receipt, and states the expected response window
6. Case routes to a queue (24.01.03) or bypasses it (CSAM / self-harm / factual dispute)
7. On decision, reporter receives the outcome; target receives a Statement of Reasons (24.02.03)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Retaliatory report — reported user reports their reporter back | Both cases proceed independently; the linkage is recorded as a signal, never as a defence | Nothing — the reporter is not told their target counter-reported |
| Brigading — 400 fans report one artist's post | Dedups to one case with 400 reporters; volume is **not** an enforcement signal | Each reporter gets a receipt; all receive the same outcome |
| Target deleted before review | Case survives via the evidence snapshot ([24.09](../24.09-case-evidence-locker.md)); a delete does not moot the notice | "This case is still open" |
| Report filed against a band/entity rather than a member | Case attaches to the entity; enforcement must resolve to the *acting member*, not the entity's co-owners | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| Reporter picks the wrong lane (files a split dispute as "harassment") | Reviewer re-routes; the re-route must not restart the reporter's clock | "Your report was routed to the rights team" |
| Report volume spike (coordinated abuse of the intake itself) | Rate-limit per reporter, not per target — throttling per target lets an abuser suppress reports | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty | User has never reported | No case list; report affordance lives on objects, not in a hub |
| Submitting | Report sent | Optimistic receipt with case ID — must not block on classification |
| Received | Case created | "Received — we'll respond by {date}" with the case ID |
| Under review | Assigned to a reviewer | Status only; never the reviewer's identity |
| Decided | Outcome reached | Reasoned decision; if the reporter is also the target, the SoR path applies |
| Rerouted | Wrong lane at intake | "Routed to {team}" — clock preserved |
| Error | Submission failed | Retry with the draft preserved — a lost report is a statutory breach, not a dropped form |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A generic marketplace/social report taxonomy is sufficient | Every incumbent's report flow | ❌ REJECTED — a music platform's reason list must include "credit theft", "uncleared sample", "this isn't my performance" and "unreleased material leaked". No generic taxonomy has them, and they are not policy violations at all — they are factual claims about provenance that route to domains 02/09. A taxonomy without them forces the platform's *signature* harm into an "Other" box. |
| DT-02 | All reports are moderation cases | Domain framing as one adjudication engine | ❌ REJECTED — the taxonomy must triage into at least three lanes: **policy violation** (24.02), **factual dispute** (domains 02/09/24.04), **welfare** (24.06.03). Handing a contested split to a content moderator produces a confidently wrong answer with a Statement of Reasons attached to it, which is worse than no answer because it is now evidence. This triage is the single highest-leverage decision in the domain and it is made at intake. |
| DT-03 | Report volume is a useful signal of severity | Standard queue-prioritisation heuristic | ❌ REJECTED — the Fan persona outnumbers professionals by orders of magnitude (D-13). Volume-as-signal hands any fanbase the power to suppress an artist, and music has a documented brigading culture. Severity must come from the reason code and the classifier, never from the count of reporters. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **[24.02.04 Policy Library](../24.02-enforcement-appeals-policy/24.02.04-policy-library-versioned-terms.md)** — the reason taxonomy is a projection of the rulebook; an unbound reason cannot be cited in a decision
- Touches **[24.09 Case Evidence Locker](../24.09-case-evidence-locker.md)** — snapshot at intake, or a deleted/edited target moots the case
- Touches **[24.06.03 Crisis & Welfare Escalation](../24.06-personal-safety-threat-response/24.06.03-crisis-welfare-escalation.md)** — the self-harm bypass is defined here and executed there
- Touches **Domain 02 (Credits)** and **Domain 09 (Rights)** — the factual-dispute lane hands off to their adjudication, not to a moderator
- Consumed by the **Notifications & Alerts** cross-cut — receipt, status and decision notices
- Consumed by the **Admin, Back-Office & Support** cross-cut — the case surfaces inside the operator shell, which this feature does not own

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Reporting is an affordance on objects, not a destination | Reports are filed in anger, in seconds, usually on a phone. A "Safety Centre" the user must navigate to is a report that never gets filed. |
| D-02 | Legal notices require identity; safety reports permit anonymity | DMCA requires a sworn identified statement; a harassment victim reporting under their own name is handing the abuser a retaliation target. One flow, two identity regimes. |
