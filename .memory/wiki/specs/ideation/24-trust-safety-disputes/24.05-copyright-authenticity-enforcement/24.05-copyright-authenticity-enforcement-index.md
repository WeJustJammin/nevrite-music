## Overview

Enforcement of claims about **what a thing really is and who it belongs to** — copyright notices and the
repeat-infringer ladder that safe harbour depends on, acoustic matching, counterfeit and misrepresented
goods, and the forensic response when unreleased material escapes. It is the sub-domain where domain 24
touches the product's thesis most directly, because WeJammin is the first platform in this position to
hold a **positive provenance record** to weigh against a claim, rather than only the claim.

**Why a sub-domain, not a feature**: four capabilities that chain. A fingerprint match (24.05.02) becomes a
claim, a claim runs the notice/counter-notice cycle (24.05.01), a repeat pattern trips the rigid strike
ladder, and the same "is this what it claims to be" question governs physical goods (24.05.03) and
escaped masters (24.05.04). They share an evidence base and an adversary.

> **24.05.01 is `✅ Full` for all four including Fan** — not because fans are rights-holders, but because
> 512(g) counter-notice is a **statutory right** attaching to whoever uploaded the material. A fan whose
> gig video is claimed has the identical legal right as a label. The platform cannot withhold it, and the
> volume being negligible does not change the obligation.
>
> **24.05.03 is `✅ Full` for Musician and Operator** — the personas who list physical goods (D-05) and
> who therefore both make and receive authenticity claims.
