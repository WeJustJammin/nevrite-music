# Feature: Case Evidence Locker & Chain of Custody

> **Number**: 24.09
> **Parent**: [Trust, Safety & Disputes](./trust-safety-disputes-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The shared spine under every adjudication surface in domain 24: a case-scoped, immutable bundle of the
evidence **as it existed at the moment that mattered**, with a chain of custody. It sits directly under the
domain rather than inside a sub-domain because every one of them depends on it — moderation appeals, split
disputes, chargeback representments, DMCA counter-notices, ownership disputes, leak findings, and law-
enforcement productions all reach for the same bundle.

Its defining property is **snapshotting**. Referencing a live object loses the case: a seller who edits a
listing after the sale has destroyed the evidence unless it was captured at transaction time. This is the
product's own thesis (D-18) applied to disputes — the fact must be captured at the moment it is true,
because reconstruction at the moment of contest is exactly what does not work.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | Sees the bundle for **their own case, scoped to their own case** — the listing as it was, the thread, the delivery proof, the session record. Cannot add to it retroactively, which is the point and will occasionally feel unfair to someone who genuinely forgot to photograph the amp. |
| Producer | Read-only | Same, and the persona whose bundle is richest: the brief, the revision thread, the delivered versions, the approval, the session provenance. Their disputes are the ones the platform can actually adjudicate on facts (24.04.01 DT-03), and this is why. |
| Operator | Read-only | Same. Their bundles are the **thinnest** — a booking has no delivery signature and no item, so the evidence for "the band didn't turn up" is a hold record and a message thread, which is why their chargebacks are the hardest to defend (24.04.04 DT-03). |
| Fan | Read-only | Same, scoped to their own case. Sees an order snapshot and a thread; the simplest bundle in the product. |

> No persona has write access. **Evidence is written by the system at the moment of the event**, never by a
> party at the moment of the dispute — that is the entire design.

## Behavior

The locker assembles, per case, from sources it does not own: the `Audit Log & Provenance Ledger`
cross-cut (who did what, when, on what basis), the `Messaging & Contextual Inbox` cross-cut (the thread
bound to the object), media as it was at the time, the listing/booking/brief snapshot, the delivery proof,
and the executed contracts.

**Snapshots, not references.** A snapshot of the listing photo at transaction time is evidence; a link to
the current listing photo is a link to whatever the seller changed it to.

Retention is **not one clock**. CSAM preservation, chargeback windows, tax retention, open disputes, legal
holds and GDPR erasure all impose conflicting requirements on the same bundle. Legal hold outranks all of
them (24.08 CX-04); the `Privacy` cross-cut's "anonymise-and-retain, not DELETE CASCADE" is the reconciling
rule.

Both parties to a dispute see the same bundle (24.04.02) — asymmetric evidence is what makes people fight.

### Happy Path

1. A consequential event occurs — a listing publishes, an item ships, a mix is delivered, a message is sent
2. The system snapshots what is capturable **at that moment**, as a byproduct of the event
3. Later, a case opens (a claim, a report, a notice, a chargeback, a request)
4. The locker assembles the relevant snapshots into a case-scoped bundle with a chain of custody
5. Both parties and the adjudicator see the same bundle
6. The bundle survives the case, subject to whichever retention clock wins

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Seller edits the listing after the sale | The snapshot holds. **The single most common evidence-destruction move in marketplaces**, defeated by construction | Both parties see the listing as it was at purchase |
| Nothing was captured at listing time | The bundle is two people's memories, and the case is unwinnable on facts | The honest answer (24.03.03 Q-03) |
| A party deletes their account mid-case | The bundle survives — anonymise-and-retain | Case continues |
| GDPR erasure request over an open dispute's bundle | Retention wins while the dispute is open; the user is told it is retained, not why | Privacy cross-cut's conflict, in practice |
| Legal hold on a bundle | Overrides every other clock, silently | Nothing (24.08 CX-04) |
| A message thread moved off-platform mid-negotiation | The bundle has half the conversation, and the half it has may be misleading | The gap is visible in the timeline, which is better than pretending |
| Bundle grows to hundreds of GB (a tracked album session) | The `Media Handling` cross-cut flags multi-GB stem transfer as core; evidence retention on that is an economic problem | `[PENDING]` — see Q-02 |
| A leak finding names a collaborator (24.05.04) | The bundle is an accusation, and it will be contested by someone with a lot to lose | Chain of custody is what makes it defensible |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Accruing | Events happening | Nothing — capture is a byproduct, and invisible |
| Assembled | Case opened | Both parties: the same bundle, scoped to the case |
| Sealed | Case decided | The bundle as it was at decision |
| Held | Legal hold | Nothing user-facing |
| Retained | Case closed, clock running | Nothing |
| Purged | Every clock expired, no hold | Nothing |
| Incomplete | Nothing was captured at the time | Explicit — a bundle that is missing evidence must say so, not imply completeness |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The audit log **is** the evidence locker — build one thing | The tempting consolidation, and both are cross-cut-shaped | ❌ REJECTED — they are different objects with different properties. The `Audit Log & Provenance Ledger` cross-cut is a **system-wide, append-only stream of actions**: who did what, when, on what basis. The locker is a **case-scoped bundle of snapshots**: the listing photo as it was, the mix as delivered, the thread as it stood. The audit log records that the listing was edited; **it does not contain the old photo**. In a used-gear dispute, the old photo *is* the case. The locker reads the audit log and adds what the log structurally cannot hold — content at a point in time. Merging them produces either an audit log bloated with media or a locker with no evidence in it. |
| DT-02 | Evidence can be gathered when a case opens | The way every dispute system works | ❌ REJECTED — **this is the product's thesis, and it is the reason this feature sits at domain level rather than inside disputes.** `problem-statement.md`: "the facts of the work are never recorded... then reconstructed years later, from memory, usually at the exact moment they become contested and valuable." A case *is* that moment. The condition photo, the serial, the delivery signature, the brief, the approval, the session record — all are capturable as a byproduct of the event and none at filing. And it is not one sub-domain's problem: 24.03.03 DT-02 (returns), 24.04.01 DT-02 (claims) and 24.04.04 DT-01 (chargebacks) each reached this conclusion independently, from different directions. **Three independent derivations of the same principle is the signal that it belongs one level up.** |
| DT-03 | Retention is one policy — pick a number of years | The way it gets specified | ❌ REJECTED — **the same bundle is subject to several clocks running in opposite directions.** CSAM preservation demands retention (24.08.01); chargeback windows demand months; tax demands years; an open dispute demands indefinite; GDPR erasure demands deletion; a legal hold demands everything stays. The `Privacy` cross-cut names this collision explicitly and reaches the right answer — "delete my account" must mean anonymise-and-retain-financials, not DELETE CASCADE — and adds the crucial point that it is **far cheaper to decide at architecture time than under a live DSAR clock**. The locker is where every one of these clocks meets the same row, which makes it the concrete place that abstract decision becomes a schema. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Snapshots, never references | DT-02. A reference to a live object is a reference to whatever the other party changed it to. |
| D-02 | No party may write to a bundle | Evidence is written by the system at the event. A party adding evidence at dispute time is the reconstruction the product exists to prevent. |
| D-03 | Domain-level, not inside 24.04 | Three sub-domains derived the same principle independently (DT-02), and six reach for the same bundle. |
