# Feature: Crisis & Welfare Escalation

> **Number**: 24.06.03
> **Parent**: [Personal Safety & Threat Response](./24.06-personal-safety-threat-response-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-18
> **Origin**: Deep Think addition — not in the sweep's candidate list

## Overview

The lane for a report that a person is in danger from themselves: a self-harm disclosure in a message, a
farewell post, a concerned collaborator. It is defined primarily by what it must **not** do — it bypasses
the moderation queue, the enforcement ladder, the statement of reasons and the appeal, entirely. A crisis
is not a policy violation, and every platform that has processed one as content has been publicly and
correctly destroyed for it.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Both the most likely subject and the most likely reporter. Music's occupational profile — precarious income (`personas.md`: "a portfolio of small income streams"), isolation, rejection as a daily event, touring, substance exposure — carries documented elevated mental-health risk. They report a bandmate; they are reported by one. |
| Producer | Full | Reports what they see in a session thread. The persona who spends the most one-to-one time with people whose work is being judged, and who therefore sees the moment the rejection lands. |
| Operator | Full | Reports what happens in their room. Venue staff see people at 2am more often than anyone else on this platform. |
| Fan | Full | The persona bringing the volume, and — per D-11/D-13 — including minors, whose disclosures carry a different duty. Also reports concern about an artist they follow, from a parasocial distance that makes the report unverifiable and sincere at once. |

> **No persona is None, and that is the point.** Every persona is simultaneously a possible *reporter* (anyone can raise concern for anyone, from wherever they saw it) and a possible *subject* (crisis is not role-gated — a Producer at a mixing desk and a Fan in a comment thread are equally in scope). Access is uniform because gating a crisis lane by role is exactly the kind of "policy" logic this feature exists to refuse. The one party who conspicuously sees *less* than usual is the **Admin/reviewer** (the operator-as-moderator, `personas.md` Q-02): in every other 24.x lane they see a case, a severity, a queue position — here they see, by design, **no case and no record** (States: Closed). The absence of an admin surface is the safety feature.

## Behavior

The lane starts at intake: a self-harm reason code in the taxonomy (24.01.01) routes **out** of the
moderation queue before any classifier, any severity model, or any sanction machinery touches it.
Consequences:

- **No sanction, ever.** No removal, no strike, no restriction, no statement of reasons, no appeal.
- **Resources, localised.** Crisis line numbers are country-specific and a wrong number is worse than none.
- **The reporter gets something too** — a person who reports a friend's farewell post is not fine.
- **The reviewer needs support**, which is 24.01.03's exposure-controls problem at its most acute.

The hard boundary is **emergency-services contact**: high-liability, sometimes wrong, occasionally the
only thing that matters. It is deliberately left as an open question (24.06 Q-02) rather than answered by
an agent.

### Happy Path

1. A user or a bystander reports concern for someone's safety, from wherever they saw it
2. Intake routes to the crisis lane before anything else evaluates it
3. The subject is shown localised crisis resources — quietly, without accusation, without a case number
4. The reporter is acknowledged and given resources of their own
5. No enforcement action is taken, recorded, or implied at any point
6. The content stays up unless the subject removes it

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Self-harm disclosure that also violates a content policy | **The crisis lane wins.** Do not sanction a person in crisis for how they expressed it | Resources; no action |
| The subject is a minor (D-11/D-13) | A different duty applies — the `Safeguarding & Minor Protection` cross-cut, plus possible guardian involvement, which may itself be dangerous | `[PENDING]` — routed to Safeguarding |
| Malicious report weaponising the lane against someone | A crisis intervention aimed at an enemy is a harassment tactic ("swatting" has a softer cousin). But **verifying before responding costs time the real case does not have** | `[PENDING]` — see Q-02 |
| Report is about a public figure a fan has never met | Unverifiable, sincere, and high-volume by construction (D-13) | Resources to the reporter; the subject is not contacted on a parasocial hunch |
| Wrong-country crisis number shown | A dead number in a crisis is worse than nothing | Localisation is a correctness requirement here, not polish |
| Nobody reports; the signal is only in a private message | The lane depends on a human noticing. The alternative — scanning DMs for self-harm — collides head-on with 24.01.05 DT-01 | Nothing — the honest limit |
| The reviewer is the owner, alone, at 1am | `meta/constraints.md` describes no team. **This is the least supportable review any platform performs**, and here it has no rotation | `[PENDING]` — 24.01.03 Q-02 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Empty / Not applicable | No signal | Nothing — this lane has no standing UI; it exists only once a concern is raised |
| Submitting (Loading) | Reporter confirms concern | A quiet, non-alarming acknowledgement in progress — never a "report received, case #" confirmation, which would frame a crisis as a ticket |
| Reported | Concern raised | Reporter: acknowledgement + resources for themselves |
| Resources surfaced (Populated) | Subject identified | Subject: localised resources, quietly, no case, no accusation |
| Error — localisation failed | Subject's country/locale cannot be resolved | Fallback to a curated multi-region / international resource set (e.g. findahelpline.com-class list) rather than a wrong-country number; the flow **never** shows a dead local line and **never** blocks on the lookup |
| Escalated | Imminent-danger threshold | `[PENDING — Step 5 Must-depth / /create-prd-security]` — 24.06 Q-01/Q-02, deliberately unanswered at this level |
| Closed | No further action | Nothing recorded against the subject — the absence of a record **is** the design |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A self-harm report is a report, so it belongs in the moderation queue like the others | Consistency — and it is how this gets built by default | ❌ REJECTED, and this is the entire reason the feature exists as a separate node. Running a crisis through the enforcement machine means: a classifier scores it, a queue prioritises it, a reviewer applies a rule, the content is removed, a **statement of reasons** is issued to a person in crisis explaining which policy their farewell violated, and they are given an appeal route. Every step is defensible in isolation and the composite is monstrous. It is also the specific failure that has burned platform after platform in public. **The crisis lane must bypass everything, structurally — not by convention, and not by a reviewer's good judgment at 1am.** The routing rule belongs in 24.01.01's taxonomy from day one, and it is nearly free to put there and impossible to bolt on once the queue is the only path. |
| DT-02 | A music-industry platform does not need a crisis lane — this is a social-network problem | The scoping instinct, and it is wrong twice over | ❌ REJECTED — **the population is high-risk in both directions.** Professionals: music's occupational profile is precarious income, isolation, constant rejection, touring, and substance exposure; elevated mental-health risk in the industry is documented and widely discussed. Fans: D-11 makes them first-class users and D-13 acknowledges minors, which is a second high-risk population with a different duty. The volume will be low — perhaps a handful a year at launch scale — and **the cost of mishandling the first one is unbounded**, both morally and to the product. Low volume is an argument for a simple lane, not for no lane. |
| DT-03 | Detection should be automated — scan for self-harm signals and intervene proactively | The instinct that follows once you accept the lane | ❌ REJECTED for this pass, and the rejection is uncomfortable. Automated self-harm detection means scanning private messages, which collides head-on with 24.01.05 DT-01 (different lawful basis, different expectation) and with the Privacy cross-cut's regional-consent requirement. It also has a false-positive profile that is uniquely bad in this domain: **song lyrics.** A murder ballad, a breakup song, a lyric sheet about despair — this platform is *full* of exactly the language a self-harm classifier is trained on, which is 24.01.02 DT-01 arriving in the highest-stakes possible context. Proactive detection here would generate crisis interventions aimed at songwriters doing their job. **The honest position is that this lane depends on a human noticing, and that this is a real limit** rather than one to design around with a model. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The crisis lane bypasses classification, queue, ladder, SoR and appeal — structurally, not by convention | DT-01. The routing rule lives in 24.01.01's taxonomy and is nearly free to add on day one. |
| D-02 | No sanction is ever applied to a person in crisis, whatever else they did | DT-01. If the expression also broke a rule, the rule loses. |
| D-03 | No proactive detection | DT-03. It requires DM scanning, and its false-positive surface on a music platform is *lyrics*. |
