## Overview

The children where the harm is to **a human being**, not to content, money, or a rights record: harassment,
stalking and doxxing; the in-person meetings the platform arranges between strangers; and the welfare
escalations that must never touch the enforcement machine at all. This is the sub-domain the candidate
sweep almost entirely missed — it produced "Meetup Safety & Safe Exchange" and nothing else, with no
harassment feature of any kind on a platform whose own persona documentation names "harasses artists
through open contact surfaces" as a known behaviour (`personas.md`, Fan anti-persona).

**Why a sub-domain, not features**: three capabilities that interact along one axis — a stranger causing
real-world harm to a person. A meetup that goes wrong becomes a harassment case; a stalking case can
become a welfare escalation; a harassment campaign against a musician on tour is simultaneously a location
problem and a threat problem. They share a victim, an urgency profile, and a rule the rest of domain 24
does not have: **speed matters more than process.**

**The music-specific structural fact nobody else has**: a gig listing is a **public, timestamped,
geolocated commitment that a named human will be at a specific address at a specific hour**. Domains 17,
18, 19 and 21 broadcast it by design — that is their entire job. Reverb does not tell you where the seller
will physically be next Tuesday at 9pm. WeJammin does, on purpose, at scale. See 24.06.01 DT-01.
