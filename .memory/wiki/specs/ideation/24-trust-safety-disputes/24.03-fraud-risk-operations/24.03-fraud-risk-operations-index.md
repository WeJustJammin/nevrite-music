## Overview

The detection-driven half of domain 24: signals, scores, rules and graph analysis that identify bad
actors *before* anyone reports them. Where [24.01](../24.01-reporting-moderation/24.01-reporting-moderation-index.md)
and [24.04](../24.04-transaction-disputes-protection/24.04-transaction-disputes-protection-index.md) are
claim-driven (a human asserts something is wrong), this sub-domain is signal-driven — nobody files a
report about card testing, it just happens 4,000 times in an hour.

**Why a sub-domain, not a feature**: six capabilities sharing one substrate and feeding each other. The
rules engine (24.03.01) consumes device/graph fingerprints produced for ban-evasion detection (24.03.02);
return abuse (24.03.03), triangulation (24.03.04) and review rings (24.03.06) are all specialisations of
the same graph and the same score; screening (24.03.05) is a gate in the same pipeline with a different
legal regime attached.

**The music-specific trap this sub-domain must not fall into**: `personas.md` describes the multi-hyphenate
as the structural centre — one human who lists a cymbal, sells a mix, books a room and joins a band in a
single afternoon. **That behaviour is indistinguishable from account-farming under any generic velocity
rule.** A fraud system tuned on marketplace defaults will flag the platform's ideal user first. See
24.03.01 DT-02.
