## Overview

The feature D-15 named as the justification for this domain's existence: *show me who I can actually
play with.* Physics caps the playable radius at a few hundred miles, which turns a constraint into a
discovery product — a map of the humans within your reach, corrected over time by what actually
happened rather than what geography predicted. It exists because distance is only a proxy for latency,
and the proxy is often wrong in both directions.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: the discovery map, the correction loop that keeps the map
honest, and the reusable match predicate are distinct capabilities that feed each other. The map shows
a radius; the correction loop learns the real one from probe history and moves the map; the predicate
exports the map's judgment into other domains' searches without exporting the map itself.

**Boundary**: this sub-domain owns the *destination* — the map and the matching product. The
`playable-with?` predicate it defines is consumed as a cross-cut by Community (03), Opportunities (04),
Services (05) and Education (06). Same line the domain index draws with Stripe/Payments vs Gear
Marketplace: a cross-cut consumption does not dissolve the destination that defines it.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The map is domain-owned; the predicate is a cross-cut | The discovery experience is a destination. The boolean "can these two play?" is a mechanism four other domains need in their own search surfaces and would each reinvent badly. Owning the definition while exporting the answer is the correct split. | Agent, Node Classification Gate, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | Predicted radius and measured radius are separate features, not one | They have different data (geo/topology priors vs accumulated probe history), different lifecycles (static vs learning), and different failure modes (systematically wrong vs sparse). The correction loop is what turns a plausible map into a trustworthy one, and that is a capability, not a detail. **This is the sub-domain's weakest split** — logged as Q-02. | Agent, Node Classification Gate, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | Radius is a *set* of radii by instrument pair, not one circle | Follows from 08.01.02 DT-02 — a drummer/bassist lock needs far tighter sync than a vocalist over a bed. One circle would be simultaneously too strict and too loose. This makes the matching feature richer, not weaker, and strengthens rather than undermines D-15. | Agent, derived from 08.01.02 DT-02, 2026-07-16 |
