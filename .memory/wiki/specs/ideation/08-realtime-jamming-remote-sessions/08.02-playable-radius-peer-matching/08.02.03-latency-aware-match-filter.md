# Feature: Latency-Aware Match Filter

> **Number**: 08.02.03
> **Parent**: [Playable Radius & Peer Matching](./08.02-playable-radius-peer-matching-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The `playable-with?` predicate — domain 08's judgment, exported. Community (03), Opportunities (04),
Services (05) and Education (06) all have search surfaces where "can this person and I actually do this
in real time?" is a legitimate filter, and none of them can answer it. This feature owns the definition
and the contract; the consuming domains apply the answer inside their own searches. It is the mechanism
by which a domain that may never be a destination still earns its keep.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Turns on "only show people I can actually play with live" in any search across the platform — finding a jam partner in Community, answering a casting call in Opportunities, booking a lesson in Education. Sees when results were excluded and how many, never a silently shortened list. |
| Producer | Full | Filters service-provider and casting searches to people who can attend a *simultaneous* session, as opposed to those who can only deliver an overdub. This is the distinction that decides whether a remote tracking date is even possible, and it is currently invisible on every platform in the market. |
| Operator | Config | Configures whether their room is offered as a real-time-capable endpoint in searches, and against what instrument-pair thresholds. Cannot alter the measured values behind the predicate — only whether they participate. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. No fan search surface consumes this predicate; fan discovery in Community (03) and Fanbase (20) is affinity-based and physics-blind by design. |

## Behavior

The predicate is deliberately *not* a bare boolean. A bare boolean would be applied by four different
domains with four different assumptions and would silently rot as measurement data changes.

The contract it exports per pairing:

| Field | Why it exists |
|-------|--------------|
| Verdict | `playable` / `playable-with-ceiling` / `conductor-only` / `not-playable-live` / `unknown` — mirrors 08.01.02, never a simplified two-state |
| Tempo ceiling | The consuming domain may want to show it (Opportunities casting a fast punk session cares) |
| Confidence | From 08.02.02. Without it, consumers cannot distinguish "measured, no" from "guessed, no" |
| Instrument-pair scope | Per parent D-03 — the predicate is meaningless without knowing which pairing was asked about |
| Freshness stamp | Consumers cache. A cached predicate with no freshness is a lie with a long half-life |

**Consumers may not apply their own thresholds.** If Community decides 40 ms is fine and Services
decides 25 ms is fine, the platform has four different definitions of "playable" and the map (08.02.01)
contradicts all of them. Domain 08 owns the threshold; consumers own the presentation.

**`unknown` is a first-class value and must not collapse to `false`.** The difference between "we
measured and you cannot" and "we have never measured" is the difference between excluding someone
permanently and prompting them to measure. Collapsing them makes unmeasured users invisible forever —
a cold-start trap that would silently kill the feature at launch, when *every* user is unmeasured.

### Happy Path

1. Musician runs a search in a consuming domain (e.g. Community: "drummers, jazz, this week").
2. Musician enables the real-time filter.
3. Consuming domain requests the predicate for the viewer against each candidate, scoped to the relevant instrument pair.
4. Results render with verdict badges; excluded candidates are counted and the count is shown.
5. Musician can see the excluded set on demand — with the reason, and with the Overdub Mode route for those who failed only on latency.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Candidate never measured (`unknown`) | Included, badged as unknown. Never silently excluded. This is the cold-start protection. | "Not measured yet — invite them to check." |
| Filter excludes almost everything | Common at launch and in low-density areas. Must not render as an empty list with no explanation. | "142 people match, 3 are within real-time reach" + Overdub route for the other 139. |
| Predicate service unavailable | Fails **open** — the filter is disabled and says so. Fails-closed would silently hide everyone, which looks like an empty market rather than an outage. | "Real-time filter unavailable right now — showing everyone." |
| Consuming domain caches a stale predicate | Freshness stamp lets the consumer detect it. Policy for what they do is `[PENDING — Step 5]`. | `[PENDING]` |
| Predicate used as a location side channel | Real risk — varying one's own position to binary-search a target's location. See parent CX-03. | `[PENDING — /create-prd-security]` |
| Two consumers disagree because one applied its own threshold | Contract violation, not an edge case. Prevented by the contract, not handled at runtime. | N/A |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Off | Filter not enabled | Normal results, no latency badges. |
| Loading | Predicate resolving | Results render immediately; badges arrive after. The search must never block on this. |
| Applied | Predicate resolved | Verdict badges + exclusion count. |
| Partial | Some candidates unknown | Mixed badges; unknown candidates present and marked. |
| Degraded | Predicate service down | Filter disabled with an explanation. Fails open. |
| Empty | Nothing playable | Honest count + Overdub Mode route. |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The predicate must fail **open**, not closed — an outage that silently hides every candidate is indistinguishable from an empty market, and users will conclude the platform has no one on it rather than that a service is down. | Reasoning: fail-closed is the safe default for permissions, but this is a *quality* filter, not a permission. Inverting the usual instinct here is deliberate. Note this is the opposite of 08.01.01 D-02, where an unmeasured link fails *closed* — because there the false green costs a paid session, while here the false exclusion costs the platform its perceived liquidity. | ✅ CONFIRMED — and the contrast with 08.01.01 D-02 is the interesting part: the same domain needs opposite failure postures depending on whether the cost lands on a user's money or on the platform's credibility. |
| DT-02 | `unknown` must not collapse to `false`. At launch every user is unmeasured, so a predicate that treats unknown as not-playable makes the entire platform invisible to its own filter on day one. | Reasoning: cold-start analysis. The feature's worst day is its first day, and the naive boolean fails hardest exactly then. | ✅ CONFIRMED — `unknown` is first-class. This is the single highest-value detail in this feature file. |
| DT-03 | Since four domains consume it, the predicate is really a cross-cut and this feature should not exist as a node here. | Adversarial: the D-15 invocation test applied to this feature. | ❌ REJECTED — the *consumption* is a cross-cut and is recorded as one in the domain CX file. The *definition* — thresholds, verdict taxonomy, confidence model, instrument scoping — must be owned somewhere, and it can only be owned where the measurement and the musical thresholds live. A cross-cut with no owner becomes four divergent implementations, which is precisely the failure this feature prevents. Same structure the domain index defends with Stripe/Payments vs Gear Marketplace. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The predicate exports verdict + ceiling + confidence + instrument scope + freshness, never a bare boolean | A bare boolean cannot be presented honestly and rots invisibly in consumer caches. |
| D-02 | Consuming domains may not apply their own thresholds | Four definitions of "playable" is worse than none. Domain 08 owns the threshold; consumers own presentation. |
| D-03 | `unknown` is first-class and never collapses to `false` | Cold-start protection. See DT-02. |
| D-04 | The filter fails open | An outage must not masquerade as an empty market. See DT-01, and note the deliberate contrast with 08.01.01 D-02. |
