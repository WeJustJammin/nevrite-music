# Feature: Session Attendance Record & Provenance Capture

> **Number**: 08.05.04
> **Parent**: [Session Capture & Recall](./08.05-session-capture-recall-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

> **This is domain 08's link to the thesis (D-18).** Every other feature here is consolidation.
> This one is provenance — and it is the reason a domain that is otherwise infrastructure-hungry,
> owner-undirected, and MoSCoW-vulnerable earns a place in the product at all.

## Overview

Records who was actually in the session, for how long, and which track came from which endpoint — as a
byproduct of the session running. **A network session is the most instrumentable room that has ever
existed**: the platform *is* the transport, so it does not ask who played the bass part, it observed the
connection that carried it. Per `meta/problem-statement.md`, the root cause of music's most litigated
failure is "absence at the point of truth." Here, absence is structurally impossible.

The record is **evidence with a known ceiling**, not proof. What the transport observes is an
authenticated *endpoint* producing audio — not a human, and not liveness (DT-04). Naming that ceiling
precisely is what separates this from `02.02.04`'s rejected geolocation signal, which "only *looks*
strong, which is worse." The record's value comes from claiming exactly what it saw and nothing more.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Sees the record of their own participation — their track, presence span with every gap listed, audible-seconds — and **declares the one thing the system cannot know: what they played**, bound to `02.06`'s controlled vocabulary, pre-filled from their profile so the common case is one tap (D-09). Per `meta/personas.md` their success criterion is "credits are cited rather than claimed"; this is the mechanism that makes citation possible. Can dispute a record about themselves; cannot edit it, because an editable observation is a claim wearing better clothes. Sees only their own row's detail — another participant's audible-seconds is not their business (D-13). |
| Producer | Full | Per `meta/personas.md`, **the capture point** — the persona who knows who played what and has never had a tool that records it at the moment it is true. Sees every row, runs the end-of-session confirmation, and attests to the record, which is worth more than a self-claim precisely because it is theirs. **Except for their own row**: when the Producer is also playing — the multi-hyphenate default, not an edge case — their attestation of themselves is marked `self-attested` and carries no more weight than any self-claim (DT-05, D-11). Declares roles *about other people*, which is recorded as their assertion, attributed, never as observation. |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees that a session in their room happened and who attended, for their own booking and duty-of-care records (16/17). Not a party to the creative facts — who played what is not the room provider's business, and them having a say in it would be a category error. Consistent with `02.02.04`'s Operator scoping: the room corroborates that a session existed, never who played what. Where an Operator runs the session they are acting as Producer (domain 08 Role Matrix note). |
| Fan | None | Not visible, at any tier, ever. The eventual credit surfaces publicly via Credits (02) as a plain label; the record itself is a working document. Per `02.02.04`'s Fan scoping, exposing an evidence trail publicly would be surveillance data on working professionals — and per DT-08 this record is also incidentally a timesheet, which makes public exposure worse than merely useless. |

## Behavior

**The record is an observation, not a claim.** That distinction is the whole feature. Every existing
credits system — Jaxsta, Muso.AI, Sound Credit, the MLC — ingests assertions and tries to reconcile them,
because none of them were in the room. This one is in the room by construction, so it records what
happened rather than what someone remembers.

### What is observed, and with what certainty

| Fact | Recorded as | Certainty ceiling |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Which identity authenticated | Account identity on the connection | The **account**, not the human at it (DT-04) |
| When they joined and left | Presence segments, **transport clock** (D-14) | High — the transport is the witness |
| Which captured track is theirs | Endpoint↔track binding (08.05.01) | The **endpoint**, not the person |
| Whether they were audible | **Audible-seconds per segment**, never a boolean (D-12) | High for signal; says nothing about what made it |
| Who else was present | Same certainty, same moment | Same ceiling |
| Provenance grade | A live-room segment is `observed-playing`; an overdub is `delivered-by` by default and becomes `observed-playing` only for segments a live witness observed (CX-09) | `delivered-by` establishes delivery, not performance; a witnessed segment establishes observation, never human liveness or contribution |

**What is NOT observed, and must never be inferred:**

| Not a fact | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| That a human played it | The transport saw an endpoint. A file routed through a virtual audio device is indistinguishable from a performance (DT-04). |
| That they contributed to the work | Presence is not contribution. A player whose every take was discarded added nothing. |
| What instrument they played | The system heard audio, not an instrument. Declared against `02.06`, never detected. |
| Whether they wrote anything | Authorship is a legal fact, not an acoustic one. Domain 09 territory. |
| What the split should be | A negotiation. Never an inference from connection minutes (DT-09). |
| Which work this becomes | **The work does not exist yet** (DT-07). Sessions precede works by months. |

**The record is evidence; Credits (02) owns the conclusion.** An over-eager inference here manufactures
credits, which is precisely the fraud `meta/personas.md` names as the Musician's worst intentional
behaviour ("games the verified-credit graph to manufacture reputation"). A provenance system that can be
gamed by idling in a room is worse than none, because it launders a false claim into a "verified" one.

### Where the record goes (resolves Q-01)

Q-01 asked whether an observed attendance record translates into a credit and by what rule. **It does
not, and the rule already exists upstream.** Domain 02 states the principle three times independently
(`02.02.04` D-02, `02.04` D-03, `02.01.01` DT-02): *evidence sets the tier; it never gates the credit.*
This record is therefore **an input to `02.04.02` Provenance Tiers** — the strongest routinely-available
one, since `02.02.04` concedes every signal it collects is spoofable by a motivated adversary — and it
mints nothing by itself.

The bridge from presence to contribution has exactly three components, none of which 08 owns:

| Component | Source | What it establishes |
|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| Kept material | 08.05.03 highlights survived into a take | The playing mattered (parent CX-04) |
| Attestation | `02.04.01`, by the Producer | A trust broker vouched (except self-attested rows — D-11) |
| Declaration | The participant, against `02.06` | What they say they played |

**Presence alone contributes zero.** A row with attendance and no kept material, no attestation and no
declaration is a record that someone was connected — which is true, and worth exactly nothing as credit.
That is the design, not a gap. What remains genuinely open is the *shape* of the tier ladder that
consumes this, which is `02.04` Q-01 and belongs to the User at `/ideate-validate` — not to 08.

### Confirmation — does it block, and what happens to a non-confirmer?

Per `meta/personas.md`, "the moment that matters" is credit captured while everyone is present and
friendly. Not next week. Not when money appears. The Producer's documented worst accidental behaviour is
closing a session without capturing splits, and the personas file's instruction is explicit: **the design
must make the lazy path the correct path.**

**Confirmation blocks nothing** (D-05) — not session end, not upload, not the project handoff, not
payment. A blocking prompt at the end of a session is dismissed, and a dismissed prompt captures nothing.

**But "does not block" must not mean "easy to miss."** The resolution is three properties at once:

1. **Non-blocking** — every path forward remains open with the prompt untouched.
2. **Unavoidable by default** — it is the only thing on screen at the instant of session end, presented
   in-session while participants are still connected, never as a notification to be read later.
3. **One tap** — pre-filled with the observation plus the declarer's profile default instrument. The
   common case (a drummer who played drums) is a single confirmation; anything else is an edit.

**A non-confirming participant** gets: one in-session prompt at session end; one notification if they had
already left; **then nothing**. The record seals at `session_end + 24h` regardless (D-10). No third nag —
a work tool that nags about admin is a tool people mute, and a muted tool captures nothing, which forfeits
the wedge to protect a form. Their observation stands unchanged; only their *declaration* is missing, and
`unconfirmed ≠ invalid`.

### Happy Path

1. Participant joins the room. **Before the connection completes**, the room states that the session
   records attendance and whether capture is running. Joining is the act — there is no separate consent
   step, and there is no silent start (D-15).
2. Transport binds connection → authenticated identity → (once capture begins) track (08.05.01). A
   presence segment opens, timestamped on the **transport's clock**, not the endpoint's (D-14).
3. Session runs. The record accretes with nobody doing anything and nothing on screen. Segments open and
   close on every connect/disconnect, including transport failures (08.08).
4. Session ends — Producer stops capture, or the last participant disconnects, whichever is first.
5. Every still-connected participant sees a pre-filled summary of what was observed **about them only**:
   their track, presence span with gaps listed, audible-seconds.
6. Each declares what they played, bound to `02.06`'s vocabulary, pre-filled from profile. One tap.
   **They declare participation, not credit** — the work this becomes may not exist for months (DT-07).
7. Producer attests. Their own row, if they played, is marked `self-attested` (D-11).
8. Record seals at `session_end + 24h` and is offered to `02.04.02` as a tier input and `09.02` as split
   evidence. **No credit exists yet.** If the downstream handoff fails, the record survives here — it is
   the asset (parent CX-02).

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Participant present but contributed nothing | Recorded as present with no kept material, no declaration. **Must not become a credit.** The gap between presence and contribution is where credit fraud would live. | Record shows attendance; no contribution asserted. |
| Participant leaves before confirmation | The observation stands — it does not need their agreement to be true. Their *declaration* is missing. One notification, then seal at +24h. | Row marked `undeclared`. |
| Participant disputes the record about themselves | Dispute is attached; the observation is not edited. An editable observation is a claim. Routes to 24. | Dispute visible alongside; observation intact. |
| Someone idles in a room to manufacture a credit | The attack. Presence-only rows are worthless as credit by construction (D-02) — no kept material, no attestation, no declaration, no tier contribution. | Nothing. The record confers no credit by itself. |
| **Audio injected from a file via a virtual audio device** | **Undetectable at the transport, and the honest ceiling on the whole feature** (DT-04). A pre-recorded stem routed through Loopback/VB-Cable/BlackHole is bit-identical to a performance. Mitigation is claim wording, not detection: the record says "this endpoint produced this audio", never "this human played". | No indication — because the system genuinely does not know. |
| **Producer is also performing** (the multi-hyphenate default) | Their attestation of their own row is `self-attested` and carries self-claim weight only. `meta/personas.md`: the Producer's worst intentional behaviour is pushing a self-serving split through while contributors aren't paying attention — this is the mechanism that would enable it (DT-05). | Own row visibly marked `self-attested`; other rows unaffected. |
| **A dep plays on the booked player's credentials** | The most likely real-world false record, and it is *innocent* — a forwarded invite link is the normal way a substitute arrives (DT-06). The record is honestly wrong and cannot know it. The declaration step must therefore ask "who is playing?" rather than assume identity == invitee. | Declaration offers "I am someone else" → row becomes an attributed assertion, not an observation. |
| **Two humans on one endpoint** (drummer + bassist sharing a room and a laptop) | Real and common. One track, two humans — the 1:1 binding the feature depends on simply does not hold. The transport can only say the endpoint produced the track. Declaration allows naming co-present humans; those names are assertions at assertion tier, not observations. | "More than one of us played on this" → named co-performers, marked asserted. |
| **Participant joins, leaves, rejoins** (parent CX-02 escalated this here) | **One attendance with a gap list — never N attendances** (D-08). Segments are stored exactly as observed and never merged in the data; presentation coalesces to one span per identity per session with gaps visible. No gap-duration threshold is applied, because whether a gap "counts" is 02/09's judgment, and baking a threshold into an observation would make it a conclusion. | One attendance span; every gap listed with exact times. |
| Network drop vs deliberate leave | The transport cannot reliably distinguish them. Records the disconnect and its *observed cause only* — clean close vs timeout — and never guesses intent. See 08.08. | Gap annotated `clean` or `timeout`; no inference of why. |
| Participant connected but muted throughout | Present, audible-seconds = 0. Presence is real; audibility is not. | Attendance recorded; audible-seconds shown as 0. |
| Participant connected with no input device | **Not a player.** Bound to a track = played; on the roster = heard (parent R-02). Someone with no track is a listener (08.03.02) even if they were invited as a player. | Appears on the roster, not in this record. |
| Session runs without capture | Attendance observed; no track binding. A weaker record, honestly weaker. | Attendance only, labelled as such. |
| **Identity is a band entity / shared account** | The record binds to the **authenticated identity**, so it names an *account*, not a human — and a band account produces a record with no person↔track binding at all. Honestly labelled as such rather than silently treated as a person (D-16). Declaration lets a human name themselves; that is an assertion at assertion tier, never observation. Whether a shared band credential may join a session at all is domain 01's call (Q-03). | "This connection was a band account — name who played" → asserted, not observed. |
| Guest / unauthenticated join, if ever permitted | Attendance-only, no identity binding, labelled. In a network session every participant must authenticate to connect, so unlike `02.02` D-04 there are **no unclaimed shells** here (DT-11) — a guest path would be the sole exception and would forfeit the property. | "Unidentified participant" — no binding claimed. |
| Account credentials stolen; impostor plays | The record is honestly wrong and cannot know it. DT-04's ceiling made concrete. Only path is dispute (24) and account security. | Nothing at the time; dispute afterwards. |
| Producer never attests / leaves early | Record seals unattested. Attestation is not required for the record to exist — the observation was never theirs to authorise. | Sealed, `unattested`. Tier reflects the absence. |
| No Producer at all (a leaderless jam) | Legitimate and common. Observation-only record, no attestation, no one to run confirmation. Participants still declare for themselves. | Sealed with no attestation. Honest, weaker. |
| Solo overdub, no producer | Subject and attestor are the same person throughout — `self-attested` end to end. The weakest record the feature produces, and it must say so rather than inherit the network session's authority. | `self-attested` on the single row. |
| Session never formally ends (nobody stops capture) | Session end = last participant disconnects. The confirmation prompt cannot fire in-session for anyone (nobody is there) → all rows notified once, seal at +24h. | Notification only; no in-session moment. |
| Declaration arrives after the record sealed | **Addendum, never an edit.** The seal is what makes the record evidence; a record that can be revised after the fact is a claim. | "Added after sealing" — timestamped, attributed, visibly later. |
| Producer attests while someone is still declaring | Attestation snapshots what it attested to. Later declarations land as addenda outside the attestation's scope — an attestation must never silently expand to cover facts added after it was given. | Attestation shows exactly which rows it covered. |
| Participant declares an instrument they did not play | It is an assertion, it is theirs, and it is attributable. It gets assertion tier, not observation tier. The system heard audio, not an instrument — it was never in a position to contradict them. | Recorded as their declaration. |
| Participant declares a role for someone else | Not permitted. You declare about yourself. Only the Producer declares about others, and it is marked as their assertion (Role Lens). | Declaration UI scoped to own row. |
| Everyone confirms except one unreachable participant | Seals partial at +24h with the gap named. The observation of the absent participant is unaffected. | Sealed; named gap. |
| Track upload never arrives (08.05.01) | The binding was observed at session time; the audio is missing. Record: bound, unuploaded. The observation does not depend on the artifact existing. | "Bound — take not uploaded." |
| Participant refuses to upload their take (08.05.01 Q-02) | The attendance record stands regardless. Withholding a stem is leverage over the *audio*, never over the *record* — which is exactly why the record is the asset. | Attendance intact; take marked withheld. |
| Endpoint clock disagrees with transport clock | Transport clock wins, always (D-14). Otherwise an endpoint could claim a longer session than it had — and per DT-08 session duration has labour consequences, so this is a money surface, not a cosmetic one. | Times shown as observed by the platform. |
| Record subpoenaed / requested in a dispute (24) | The evidence trail's visibility beyond participants is `02.02.04` Q-02 and unresolved there. 08 supplies the record; it does not set disclosure policy. | Out of scope here — see Q-04. |
| Overdub session (no real-time room) | The record has delivery-certainty, not performance-certainty: it records the authenticated identity, bed version, delivery time, and pass arrival as `delivered-by`. It becomes `observed-playing` only for segments a live witness observed (CX-09); the grade is per-segment, so an unwitnessed segment remains `delivered-by`. | Record shows the provenance grade and any witness; it never presents delivery as proof of performance. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Opening a past record | Skeleton row per known participant. Never a spinner over an empty page — the participant list is known before the detail loads. |
| Empty | Session created, nobody has joined | No record. There is nothing to observe yet, and it must not pretend otherwise. |
| Accreting | Session running | **Nothing.** The record builds silently — the byproduct property depends on it being invisible. Any UI here competes with a person playing. |
| Pending confirmation | Session ended | Pre-filled summary of own row, one tap. Only thing on screen. In-session, at the natural end, not later. |
| Confirmed | All present participants declared | Sealed record offered to `02.04.02` and `09.02`. |
| Partial | Some rows undeclared at seal | Sealed with named gaps. Observation stands regardless. |
| Unattested | Sealed with no Producer attestation | Sealed, labelled. Tier reflects the absence rather than hiding it. |
| Self-attested | Attestor is also a subject | Row visibly marked. Weight equals a self-claim (D-11). |
| Attendance-only | No capture ran | Weaker record, honestly labelled. No track binding claimed. |
| Sealed + addendum | Declaration arrived after seal | Original record intact; addendum shown as later, attributed, timestamped. |
| Disputed | Someone contests their row | Dispute attached; observation intact. Routes to 24. |
| Error | Record cannot be retrieved | "Can't load this record — the session's takes are unaffected." The audio and the record are separate assets; an error here must never imply the takes are gone. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The record is an observation, immutable, never edited — disputes attach beside it | An editable observation is a claim. The certainty is the value. |
| D-02 | Presence never becomes credit automatically | Gameable-by-idling would launder false claims into verified ones. See DT-02, DT-09. |
| D-03 | Only what was observed is recorded; instrument and role are declared, never detected | The system heard audio, not an instrument. |
| D-04 | Confirmation happens at session end, pre-filled, one tap | "The moment that matters" per `meta/personas.md`. The lazy path must be the correct path. |
| D-05 | Confirmation does not block anything | A blocking prompt at the end of a session gets dismissed, and a dismissed prompt captures nothing. |
| D-06 | Evidence here; conclusion in Credits (02) | The instrument's precision is not its authority. |
| D-07 | **The claim is narrowed to the observation**: "this authenticated endpoint produced this audio during this window" — never "this human played this" | DT-04. The feature only survives `02.02.04` DT-01's audit if it refuses to overclaim. Detection is impossible; honest wording is the entire mitigation, and `02.04` Q-03 warns the label is a liability surface. |
| D-08 | Rejoins are **one attendance with a gap list**, never N attendances; segments stored as observed, never merged; **no gap-duration threshold** | Resolves parent CX-02's escalation. Someone whose wifi drops eight times is not eight contributors. A threshold deciding when a gap "counts" would be a conclusion, and 08 does not make conclusions — 02/09 judge it with the gap data in hand. |
| D-09 | The declaration binds to `02.06`'s controlled vocabulary, pre-filled from profile | Free text makes the graph unqueryable and the DDEX export invalid (`02.06`). Pre-fill is what buys the one-tap property D-04 depends on. |
| D-10 | Seal at `session_end + 24h`. One in-session prompt, one notification if absent, then nothing | One sleep is enough for the laptop that died, and short enough that "we'll do it later" cannot begin. A third nag makes a work tool a nuisance and a nuisance gets muted — which forfeits the wedge to protect a form. |
| D-11 | Rows where the attestor is also a subject are marked `self-attested` and carry self-claim weight | DT-05. The producer-performer is the default, not an edge case, and an unmarked self-attestation is exactly the mechanism `meta/personas.md` names as the Producer's worst intentional behaviour. |
| D-12 | Audibility is recorded as **audible-seconds per segment**, never a boolean | A boolean forces 08 to pick the threshold at which sound becomes participation — which is 02's judgment, not 08's. Storing the quantity lets the consumer set its own bar. The signal floor is an audio-engineering constant for `/create-prd-architecture`, not a product decision. |
| D-13 | A Musician sees only their own row's detail | Another participant's audible-seconds is not their business. Per DT-08 this data is also labour evidence, which makes lateral visibility worse than merely nosy. |
| D-14 | Timestamps come from the **transport's clock**, never the endpoint's | An endpoint that sets its own times can claim a longer session, and per DT-08 session duration has money attached. Distinct from 08.05.02's problem: that is sample alignment, this is wall-clock attribution. |
| D-15 | Recording state is disclosed **before the connection completes**; there is no silent start and no separate consent step | Joining a room labelled as recording is the act. This preserves the byproduct property (a per-session consent dialog destroys it) without making the product a surveillance tool. Whether disclosure is a sufficient *legal* basis is Q-02 — this decides the behavior, not the compliance question. |
| D-16 | A shared/band account produces an **account-level** record with no person↔track binding, honestly labelled | The person↔track binding is the feature; a shared credential does not have one. Labelling it weak is honest; silently treating an account as a person would put a fiction at the top of the tier ladder. |
