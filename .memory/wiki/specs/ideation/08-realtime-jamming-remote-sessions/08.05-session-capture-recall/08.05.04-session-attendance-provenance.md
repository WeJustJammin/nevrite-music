# Feature: Session Attendance Record & Provenance Capture

> **Number**: 08.05.04
> **Parent**: [Session Capture & Recall](./08.05-session-capture-recall-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

> **This is domain 08's link to the thesis (D-18).** Every other feature here is consolidation.
> This one is provenance — and it is the reason a domain that is otherwise infrastructure-hungry,
> owner-undirected, and MoSCoW-vulnerable earns a place in the product at all.

## Overview

Records who was actually in the session, for how long, and which track came from which human — as a
byproduct of the session running. **A network session is the most instrumentable room that has ever
existed**: the platform *is* the transport, so it does not ask who played the bass part, it observed the
connection that carried it. Per `meta/problem-statement.md`, the root cause of music's most litigated
failure is "absence at the point of truth." Here, absence is structurally impossible.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Sees the record of their own participation — what they played, on which track, for how long — and confirms it at session end while everyone is still present. Per `meta/personas.md`, their success criterion is "credits are cited rather than claimed"; this is the mechanism that makes citation possible. Can dispute a record about themselves; cannot edit it, because an editable observation is just a claim wearing better clothes. |
| Producer | Full | Per `meta/personas.md`, **the capture point** — the persona who knows who played what and has never had a tool that records it at the moment it is true. Runs the end-of-session confirmation, attests to the record, and hands it to Credits (02). Their attestation is worth more than a self-claim, which is exactly what makes it valuable. |
| Operator | Read-only | Sees that a session in their room happened and who attended, for their own booking and duty-of-care records. Not a party to the creative facts — who played what is not the room provider's business, and them having a say in it would be a category error. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. No fan path into this domain. The eventual credit surfaces publicly via Credits (02), which is where a fan would ever encounter this — the record itself is a working document. |

## Behavior

**The record is an observation, not a claim.** That distinction is the whole feature. Every existing
credits system — Jaxsta, Muso.AI, Sound Credit, the MLC — ingests assertions and tries to reconcile them,
because none of them were in the room. This one is in the room by construction, so it records what
happened rather than what someone remembers.

What is observed with certainty:

| Fact | Why it is certain |
|------|-------------------|
| Which identity connected | They authenticated |
| When they joined and left | The transport knows |
| Which captured track is theirs | Their endpoint produced it (08.05.01) |
| Whether they were audible | Signal present on their track |
| Who else was present | Same certainty, same moment |

**What is NOT observed, and must never be inferred:**

| Not a fact | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| That they contributed to the work | Presence is not contribution. A player whose every take was discarded added nothing. |
| What instrument they played | The system heard audio, not an instrument. Declared, not detected. |
| Whether they wrote anything | Authorship is a legal fact, not an acoustic one. Domain 09 territory. |
| What the split should be | A negotiation. Never an inference from connection minutes. |

**The record is evidence; Credits (02) owns the conclusion.** An over-eager inference here manufactures
credits, which is precisely the fraud `meta/personas.md` names as the Musician's worst intentional
behaviour ("games the verified-credit graph to manufacture reputation"). A provenance system that can be
gamed by idling in a room is worse than none, because it launders a false claim into a "verified" one.

**Confirmation happens at session end** — per `meta/personas.md`, "the moment that matters" is credit
captured while everyone is present and friendly. Not next week. Not when money appears. The Producer's
documented worst accidental behaviour is closing a session without capturing splits, and the personas
file's instruction is explicit: **the design must make the lazy path the correct path.** So the
confirmation is one screen at the natural end of the session, pre-filled with what was observed,
requiring a tap — not a form.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]`: whether confirmation blocks anything (it should not —
blocking makes people dismiss it), and how a non-confirming participant is handled.

### Happy Path

1. Session starts. Each participant's connection is bound to their identity and, once capture begins, to their track (08.05.01).
2. Session runs. The record accretes with nobody doing anything.
3. Session ends. Every participant sees a pre-filled summary of what was observed about *them* — track, duration, audible.
4. Each declares the one thing the system cannot know: what they played. One tap for the common case.
5. Producer attests to the record. It is sealed and offered to Credits (02) and Rights (09) as evidence.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Participant present but contributed nothing | Recorded as present with no kept material. **Must not become a credit.** The gap between presence and contribution is where credit fraud would live. | Record shows attendance; no contribution asserted. |
| Participant leaves before confirmation | The observation stands — it does not need their agreement to be true. Their *declaration* is missing. Unconfirmed ≠ invalid. | Record marked unconfirmed for that participant. |
| Participant disputes the record about themselves | Dispute is attached; the observation is not edited. An editable observation is a claim. | Dispute visible alongside. Routes to 24. |
| Someone idles in a room to manufacture a credit | The attack. Presence-only records must be worthless as credit for exactly this reason. | Nothing — the record confers no credit by itself. |
| Producer attests to a record that is wrong | Their attestation is worth more precisely because it is theirs; per `meta/personas.md` their worst intentional behaviour is pushing through a self-serving split. Attestation is signed and attributable, not anonymous. | Attributed attestation. |
| Session runs without capture | Attendance observed; no track binding. A weaker record, honestly weaker. | Attendance only, marked as such. |
| Identity is shared (a band account) | Breaks the person↔track binding the whole feature depends on. Real: `meta/personas.md` models bands as first-class entities. | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — see Q-03. |
| Overdub session (no real-time room) | Works identically. One participant, one track, same binding, same certainty. **This is why the feature survives even if the rooms are cut.** | Same record. |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Accreting | Session running | Nothing. The record builds silently — the byproduct property depends on it being invisible. |
| Pending confirmation | Session ended | Pre-filled summary, one tap. At the natural end, not later. |
| Confirmed | All declared | Sealed record, offered to Credits (02). |
| Partial | Some participants unconfirmed | Sealed with named gaps. Observation stands regardless. |
| Disputed | Someone contests | Dispute attached; observation intact. |
| Attendance-only | No capture | Weaker record, honestly labelled. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The record is an observation, immutable, never edited — disputes attach beside it | An editable observation is a claim. The certainty is the value. |
| D-02 | Presence never becomes credit automatically | Gameable-by-idling would launder false claims into verified ones. See DT-02. |
| D-03 | Only what was observed is recorded; instrument and role are declared, never detected | The system heard audio, not an instrument. |
| D-04 | Confirmation happens at session end, pre-filled, one tap | "The moment that matters" per `meta/personas.md`. The lazy path must be the correct path. |
| D-05 | Confirmation does not block anything | A blocking prompt at the end of a session gets dismissed, and a dismissed prompt captures nothing. |
| D-06 | Evidence here; conclusion in Credits (02) | The instrument's precision is not its authority. |
