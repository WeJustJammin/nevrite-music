# Feature: High-Fidelity Monitor Stream & Quality Contract

> **Number**: 08.03.01
> **Parent**: [Remote Monitoring & Session Attendance](./08.03-remote-monitoring-session-attendance-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

Sends what the room is hearing to people who are not in it, at the fidelity the work is being judged
in. Every general-purpose conferencing tool destroys exactly the thing under evaluation — a speech
codec's job is to throw away everything that is not a voice, which is most of a mix. This feature's
entire value is a *stated* quality guarantee: the network does not get to decide what your mix sounds
like to your client.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Producer | Full | Starts and stops the stream, chooses its source (main mix, a stem bus, a specific cue), and sets the quality contract. Sees the delivered quality per listener — not the intended quality, the actual one. This is their client relationship on the line, so they own the stream. |
| Musician | Read-only | Sees that a stream is live, what it is carrying, and who is on it. Cannot start, stop or reconfigure it — but per `meta/personas.md` this persona's fear is losing control of their work, so "a stream of my performance is going somewhere" is information they are owed, not a setting they configure. |
| Operator | Config | Declares their room's streaming capability as a saleable amenity — uplink headroom, whether the room can sustain a lossless stream, what the room's monitoring source is. Does not run other people's sessions. If they are running it, they are acting as Producer. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Rejected deliberately (parent D-04): a fan-facing "listen live" perk is a Fanbase (20) product with a different audience and rights posture, and admitting it here re-imports the listening-room boundary the domain map split out. |

## Behavior

The contract is the feature. A best-effort stream that silently degrades has destroyed its only reason
to exist while continuing to appear to work — and worse, it makes the client's judgment wrong in a way
neither party can see. A client who says "the top end is harsh" when the codec put it there has just
sent the Producer to fix a problem that does not exist.

What the contract governs:

| Dimension | Why stated |
|-----------|-----------|
| Sample rate / bit depth | The nominal promise; the thing being paid for |
| Codec / lossless | Lossy at any bitrate is a judgment risk on a mastering-adjacent decision |
| Channel format | Stereo vs mono vs multi — a mix decision cannot be judged folded down |
| Degradation policy | **The critical one**: what happens when bandwidth cannot sustain the contract |

**The degradation policy is a product decision, not a fallback.** Two coherent options, and they are
genuinely different products:

- **Hold quality, accept dropouts** — the listener hears silence rather than a lie. Correct for judging a mix.
- **Hold continuity, degrade quality** — the listener hears everything, imperfectly. Correct for following a take.

A system that picks one silently will be wrong for half its users half the time. The listener must know
which regime they are in, because it changes what their ears are entitled to conclude.

**The Producer can audition the stream as delivered** (resolves Q-02). The engineer in the room hears the
room; the stream carries a chosen bus, re-encoded to the contract, and travelled over a network — three
places it can diverge from what the engineer trusts. Without a monitor-as-sent path, the Producer is
blind to the exact divergence the client is reacting to, and the client's "the low end is gone" becomes
un-diagnosable. Auditioning is a Producer-side confidence path on their own outbound stream, distinct
from any listener's receive path — it is not a second listener seat and does not consume a roster grant.

### Happy Path

1. Producer selects a source and starts the stream.
2. System establishes the contract, verifying the uplink can sustain it before promising it.
3. Listeners on the roster (08.03.02) connect and see the contract they are receiving — stated, not implied.
4. Stream runs. Both ends see the *delivered* quality continuously, not the intended quality.
5. Producer stops the stream; listeners are told it ended rather than being dropped into ambiguous silence.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Uplink cannot sustain the contract | Per the degradation policy. Never a silent downgrade — a silently degraded stream is the one failure that makes the product actively harmful. | Either dropouts with a "quality held" badge, or a visible "degraded to {x}" badge. Never nothing. |
| One listener's downlink is inadequate, others fine | Per-listener degradation. That listener's badge changes; nobody else's experience moves. | Affected listener sees their own degradation; Producer sees which listener degraded. |
| Producer's own monitoring differs from the stream source | The engineer hears the room; the stream carries a bus, re-encoded and networked. Resolved (Q-02): the Producer can audition the stream as delivered on demand. | An "audition as sent" control on their own outbound stream; hearing what the client hears, not the room. |
| Non-Producer attempts to start/stop/reconfigure the stream | Denied — the stream is Producer-owned (Role Lens). Musicians get visibility, not control; a Musician who needs to run it must be acting as Producer. | Control affordances are absent, not merely disabled-with-error, for non-owners. |
| Listener revoked from the roster (08.03.02) mid-stream | The grant is the roster; revoking it terminates that listener's receive within the same beat, not at next reconnect. Their delivered audio stops. | Revoked listener sees an explicit "access ended" state; Producer sees the roster shrink. |
| Producer's source disconnects / room drops mid-stream | The stream cannot carry audio it is no longer receiving. Enters Error, not a frozen "Live" that implies sound is flowing. Listeners are told it is a fault. | Producer: source-lost error + retry. Listeners: "stream interrupted", never silent-but-green. |
| Listener joins mid-take | Joins live; no scrub-back. Whether a short catch-up buffer exists is `[PENDING — Step 5 Must-depth or /write-be-spec]`, and it interacts with the leak posture (parent Q-02). | Joins at the current moment. |
| Unreleased audio streamed to a compromised link | The leak surface. Watermarking and DRM-grade controls are parent Q-02, likely reclassified to security architecture. | `[PENDING — /create-prd-security]` |
| Stream continues after the session ends | Must not. An orphaned live stream from a room nobody is watching is a leak with no owner. | Auto-terminates with the session (D-04). |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Idle | No stream | "Not streaming" + start CTA (Producer only). |
| Verifying | Contract being established | "Checking your connection can hold {contract}" — before promising, not after. |
| Live (contract held) | Streaming at contract | Contract badge + delivered-quality indicator. |
| Live (degraded) | Bandwidth insufficient, continuity policy | Visible degradation badge naming the actual delivered quality. |
| Live (dropping) | Bandwidth insufficient, quality policy | "Quality held — you are missing audio" indicator. |
| Ended | Producer stopped, or session ended | Explicit end state. Never ambiguous silence. |
| Error | Stream failed | Error + retry; listeners told it is a fault, not a pause. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The quality contract is stated and verified before the stream starts | Promising fidelity the uplink cannot carry is worse than admitting it up front. |
| D-02 | Degradation is never silent — the delivered quality is always visible to both ends | See DT-02. This is the rule the whole feature hangs on. |
| D-03 | The degradation policy is a per-stream product choice, not a system default | See DT-01. Judging a mix and following a take want opposite behaviour. |
| D-04 | The stream terminates with the session | An orphaned live stream is a leak with no owner. |
| D-05 | The Producer can audition the stream as delivered; auditioning is Producer-only and consumes no roster grant | Resolves Q-02. See DT-04 — closes the diagnosis loop DT-02 opened for the listener but left open for the Producer. |
