## Overview

The inverse trade-off from the rest of the domain: loose latency, uncompromising audio quality. A
client, label, or bandmate listens to a session as it happens, in the fidelity the work is actually
being judged in — the problem Zoom's speech codec destroys precisely by doing its job well. This is the
one part of domain 08 with proven willingness to pay: Audiomovers Listento was acquired by Abbey
Road/Universal, and Source-Connect is the broadcast standard for remote sessions.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: the monitor stream, the roster controlling who hears it,
live feedback against it, and the question of whether the listener's playback can be trusted are
distinct capabilities that interact. The roster gates the stream; feedback is anchored to a moment in
the stream; playback context changes how much the feedback is worth.

**Boundary against the transport cross-cut**: transport carries the bytes. This sub-domain owns the
*quality contract* — what the listener is guaranteed to hear, who is allowed to hear it, and what their
opinion of it is worth. A one-to-many high-quality stream is also a materially easier infrastructure
ask than the sub-30 ms mesh the rest of the domain needs, which matters at MoSCoW.
