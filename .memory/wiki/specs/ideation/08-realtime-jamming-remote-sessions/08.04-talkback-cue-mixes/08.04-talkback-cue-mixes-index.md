## Overview

Reproduces the control room over a network. A studio's talkback and cue system is a century-old
solution to two problems that do not go away when the room becomes distributed: the engineer needs to
speak to performers without that speech landing in the take, and every performer needs a different
balance in their headphones. Without these, a network jam room is a conference call that people are
expected to play music on.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: talkback and cue mixes are distinct capabilities that
interact constantly and non-obviously. Talkback must duck the cue mix, must route to some performers and
not others, and must be excluded from capture — every one of those is an interaction, not a setting. The
third child (recall) is what makes the cue mix survive beyond a single room, which turns a per-session
config into something that follows a person.

**Boundary against the transport cross-cut**: transport carries audio. This sub-domain owns the routing
matrix, the ducking relationships, and the rule that talkback never reaches the recording.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Talkback is never recorded into the take | The oldest rule in the studio and the one a software team is most likely to break, because "record everything" is a sensible software default and a catastrophic audio one. Stated as a decision so it cannot be lost. | Agent, domain convention, 2026-07-16 |
| D-02 | The performer owns their own cue mix; the Producer can help but not override | "More me" is the most-requested thing in any tracking session and the reason every modern cue system is performer-controlled. An engineer adjusting someone's headphones from across a network, with latency, is worse than the problem it solves. | Agent, domain convention, 2026-07-16 |
| D-03 | Cue mix recall is a property of the **person**, not the room | A drummer's monitoring preferences are theirs and follow them between sessions and rooms. This is the consolidation thesis applied to a small thing: the platform already knows, so it should not ask again. | Agent, derived from D-18, 2026-07-16 |
| D-04 | The entire sub-domain depends on real-time rooms existing | Talkback and cue mixes are meaningless without a live multi-participant room. If the rooms are cut at MoSCoW, all three children are cut with them. Recorded so the dependency is not discovered late. | Agent, 2026-07-16 |
