## Overview

Turns the physics of network audio into a product judgment. The transport cross-cut moves packets and
emits raw telemetry; this sub-domain interprets that telemetry into the only questions a musician
actually asks — *can I play with this person, at this tempo, right now, and if not, what is eating my
milliseconds?* It also owns the consequence of the answer: when two players are separated by more
latency than an ensemble can absorb, something has to be the timing reference, and this sub-domain
decides what.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature**: three distinct capabilities that feed each other — the
measurement ledger produces the numbers; the playability verdict converts numbers into a go/no-go and
a tempo ceiling; the shared clock implements whatever timing model the verdict implies. Change the
measurement and the verdict moves; change the verdict and the clock model changes.

**Boundary against the transport cross-cut**: transport *produces* RTT, jitter and loss. This
sub-domain *interprets* them. If a change is about how packets travel, it is the cross-cut. If it is
about what a human is told and what they are allowed to do about it, it is here.
