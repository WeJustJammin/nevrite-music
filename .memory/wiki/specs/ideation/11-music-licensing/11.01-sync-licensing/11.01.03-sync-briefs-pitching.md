# Feature: Sync Briefs & Pitching

> **Number**: 11.01.03
> **Parent**: [11.01 Sync Licensing](./11.01-sync-licensing-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

A buyer posts what they need — scene, mood, reference, budget, deadline, usage scope — and rights holders
pitch works against it. It is the pull-side complement to search: search finds what exists now, a brief
catches what arrives next and what the matcher would never surface because the need is contextual ("must
not mention drinking", "needs to survive a dialogue duck at 0:30").

The consolidation dividend is direct: because WeJammin holds the chain of title (D-10), a pitch can carry
its work's *live* clearance state, so the buyer never shortlists a track that turns out to need four
consents under a three-week deadline. That is the one thing a generic job board cannot do, and it is why
this lives here rather than in domain 04 (DT-01).

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Browses open briefs filtered to what they could actually win; pitches own works with a note; sees pitch status (submitted / shortlisted / passed) and, critically, *why* passed when the buyer supplies a reason. Cannot see rival pitches. |
| Producer | Full | Same, for works they hold a master share in. More often the party who can pitch an alternate mix/instrumental against a brief's technical constraint — which is frequently what wins it. |
| Operator | None | No sync brief surface — a venue/studio operator sells time and space, not master/publishing rights, so they are neither buyer nor pitcher here. If an Operator also holds a master share in a work (multi-hyphenate), they act through the Producer lens, not this None row. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Briefs disclose unreleased campaigns, target references and budget bands — commercially confidential, and of no fan-facing value. |

> Buyer-side (post a brief, triage pitches, shortlist) is **not a persona**: it is the **professional licence
> buyer** counterparty profile in `meta/counterparties.md` (parent domain D-10, sub-domain D-04, resolving
> parent Q-01). Referenced, never restated here, and it adds no row to the table above.

## Behavior

Briefs reuse the platform's post → submit → triage mechanism, shared with domain 04 (Opportunities &
Casting) — an explicitly acknowledged cross-boundary reuse (parent domain rationale, D-16). What makes a
sync brief different is not the flow but the payload: a brief carries usage scope and a clearance
requirement, and pitches are auto-filtered against clearance state. A brief that says "must be one-stop,
airing in 3 weeks" should never show the buyer a pitch that needs four consents.

### Happy Path

1. Buyer posts a brief: scene description, reference track(s), facet constraints, usage scope (media/territory/term), budget band, deadline, clearance requirement
2. System runs the brief as a standing inverted query (the 11.01.02 matcher, reversed) and notifies owners of strong matches
3. Musician/Producer sees the brief, pitches a work with a short note; the pitch carries the work's live clearance state automatically
4. System filters out pitches that fail the brief's hard constraints before the buyer sees them, and tells the pitcher why
5. Buyer triages: shortlist / pass; shortlisted pitches move to hold (11.01.04)
6. Brief closes; every pitcher gets a terminal status — no silent expiry

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Owner pitches a work that fails a hard constraint (has vocals, brief says instrumental) | Rejected pre-triage with the specific constraint named | "Not eligible: this brief needs an instrumental. You have one for [other work]?" |
| Work's clearance state degrades between pitch and shortlist (a co-owner sets a category block) | Pitch is re-evaluated on state change, not only at submit; buyer is told before they build an edit around it | Buyer: "This pitch is no longer one-stop." Owner: "Your pitch was withdrawn — [co-owner] blocked this category." |
| Brief's budget is below every plausible rate card | Owners see the mismatch before spending effort pitching | "Budget is below your rate card for this usage" — pitch still allowed; the owner may take it |
| Buyer ghosts — never triages, brief just expires | Brief auto-closes at deadline; all pitches get a terminal "expired, not reviewed" status | Honest status, and it counts against the buyer's responsiveness record |
| Owner mass-pitches every work at every brief | Rate-limited per brief and per period; relevance floor enforced by the matcher | "This work isn't a plausible match" before submit, not a silent drop |
| Owner pitches the same work twice to one brief | Deduped — a second submission of the same work updates the existing pitch's note rather than creating a duplicate the buyer has to reconcile | "You've already pitched this work here — note updated" |
| Fan or Operator reaches the brief surface by direct link or shared URL | Blocked at the access boundary; briefs are not enumerable by non-rights-holders | "This isn't available on your account" — no brief detail, no confirmation the brief exists |
| Owner submits a pitch in the same moment the deadline passes / buyer closes the brief early | Submission is evaluated against brief state at commit; a pitch that lands after close is rejected, not silently queued | "This brief closed before your pitch landed" — never a pitch that appears submitted but was never received |
| Owner pitches a work whose ownership they only partially hold, without co-owner buy-in | Pitch is allowed but carries the true clearance state (not one-stop); it is not silently upgraded | "Pitched — but this needs [N] more consents to clear; the buyer sees that" |
| Brief is a fishing expedition to survey the market with no real budget | `[PENDING — /create-prd: owner deterrent policy — this is Q-03, answer once]` — buyer verification, posting cost, or accepted as noise; all three are product/values calls that no later stage resolves on its own | — |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Brief list or match run | Skeleton list |
| Empty | No open briefs match this owner's catalogue | "Nothing open you could win right now" — not a list of briefs they cannot win |
| Populated | Briefs available | Brief cards with deadline, budget band, clearance requirement, and an honest "your catalogue has N plausible matches" |
| Error | Matcher unavailable | Briefs browsable manually; matching degrades, discovery does not disappear |
| Partial | Pitch submitted, clearance recomputing | Pitch shows "clearance checking" — never asserts one-stop on stale data |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Sync briefs belong in domain 04 (Opportunities & Casting) — it is the same post/submit/triage mechanism, and duplicating it is a wrong cut | Adversarial: the strongest objection to this feature's placement, and the domain index pre-empts it | ❌ REJECTED — the *mechanism* is shared (recorded as a cross-cut) but the *payload* is inseparable from clearance and catalogue search. A brief whose pitches are not auto-filtered by clearance state is just a job post. Moving it to 04 would either drag the clearance engine across the boundary or ship a brief that cannot do the one thing that makes it valuable here. Mechanism reuse ≠ node relocation. |
| DT-02 | The brief is a standing inverted search — same matcher as 11.01.02, aimed at future arrivals | Reasoning from DT-02 in 11.01.02: supervisors brief in reference tracks; a brief is a reference query that has not been answered yet | ✅ CONFIRMED — this collapses two apparent features into one matcher with two directions, and means a newly-tagged work can win a brief posted last week. That is a real consolidation dividend: a work finished in a domain 07 session on Tuesday can match a brief on Tuesday. |
| DT-03 | Pitch status must be terminal for every pitcher, even when the buyer never engages | Reasoning from the Musician's pain: "re-proving themselves to each new client" and the wider thesis that unrecorded facts evaporate. An un-triaged pitch is an unrecorded fact about the owner's effort. | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it implies buyer responsiveness is a tracked, published property. Sync's incumbent norm is that pitches vanish; the platform's differentiator is that facts do not. Applying that to the buyer side is consistent, and will be unpopular with buyers. Flagged as a product decision (Q-02). |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Pitches are auto-filtered against the brief's hard constraints and clearance requirement before buyer triage | The buyer's scarce resource is attention under deadline; an unfiltered pitch list recreates the incumbent inbox |
| D-02 | Every pitch reaches a terminal status, including on buyer abandonment | Per DT-03 — consistent with the thesis that the platform records facts rather than letting them evaporate |

## Open Questions

| # | Question | Owner | Deferred To |
|---|----------|-------|-------------|
| Q-01 | **[OWNER]** Are briefs public, invite-only, or tiered by owner standing? Public briefs attract spam pitches; invite-only recreates the closed-network gatekeeping the platform exists to dissolve. **One bound is already fixed and is not part of the choice**: the Edge Cases row above holds that a Fan or Operator reaching a brief by direct link is "blocked at the access boundary; briefs are **not enumerable by non-rights-holders**" — so "public" can only ever mean *open to all rights holders*, never open to the web. The live choice is that reading versus invite-only versus tiered-by-standing, and it is a market-design call the platform's anti-gatekeeping thesis constrains but does not decide. | User | `/create-prd` |
| Q-02 | **[OWNER]** Should buyer responsiveness be a published property (DT-03)? It is honest and consistent with the thesis, and it will deter buyers. This is a product/values call, and DT-03 itself flags it as one rather than resolving it. **The record's existence is not in question — only its audience**: D-02 already commits that "every pitch reaches a terminal status, including on buyer abandonment", and the ghosting edge case already states "it counts against the buyer's responsiveness record". The owner decides whether that record is shown to the sell side, and to whom. | User | `/create-prd` |
| Q-03 | **[OWNER]** How is a fishing-expedition brief (no real budget, market survey) deterred — buyer verification, posting cost, or accepted as noise? All three options are product/values calls with no upstream answer: a posting cost is a monetisation decision on a project `meta/constraints.md` records as pre-revenue and depends on 11.01 index Q-03; buyer verification is an onboarding-friction decision against the professional licence buyer counterparty profile (`meta/counterparties.md`, D-10); accepting it as noise is the do-nothing branch. **This is the deterrent policy the "fishing expedition" edge case above is still marked `[PENDING]` for.** | User | `/create-prd` |
