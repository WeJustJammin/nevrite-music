## Overview

The supervisor-facing half of the domain: making the catalogue findable by people who search for music
by feeling rather than by name, and running the pitch → hold → licence negotiation that precedes a sync
placement. This is where a music supervisor with a scene to fill and a brand agency with a campaign meet
the works that WeJammin's split-at-creation ritual has already made clearable.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature** — 2+ interacting capabilities: tagging feeds search; search
feeds brief-matching; briefs produce pitches; pitches produce holds; holds constrain what search may
offer to the next buyer; and dual-licence coordination determines whether a hold on the master means
anything at all without the matching publishing side. A hold placed in one child silently rewrites the
result set of another.
