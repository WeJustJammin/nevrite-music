## Overview

Whether a rights holder's work may be used to train generative music models, and what happens if it is:
consent recorded per work and per share, corpora assembled from works whose owners said yes, and compensation
flowing back to the people whose work is in the weights. It is the newest licensing category in music and the
one where the default — silence, scraped, unpaid — is currently the industry's operating state.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature** — 2+ interacting capabilities: consent gates corpus inclusion; the
corpus is what makes a dataset deal possible; a dataset deal is what makes compensation exist; and a consent
withdrawn after a model has trained cannot un-train it, so consent state and corpus state can diverge
irreversibly. That divergence is not a lookup — it is the sub-domain's central problem.

> **The asymmetry in this matrix is the finding.** Rights holders have Full control over consent and are
> Read-only on everything downstream — they can say no, and they cannot shape the deal, price it, or refuse a
> specific buyer once they have said yes. That is honest about where the leverage sits and it is worth the
> owner seeing plainly, because it is the opposite of every other sub-domain in domain 11, where the owner
> negotiates.
>
> **The AI company — the buyer — is not a persona**, like every other buyer in this domain. Parent domain
> Q-01 is **resolved** (parent D-10 = global D-71) and no longer the reason to leave this actor undescribed:
> the buy side is now described by two counterparty profiles in `meta/counterparties.md`. Neither of them is
> this counterparty, though — D-10 authored a **professional licence buyer** (supervisor / brand / agency) and
> a **creator micro-licence buyer** (podcaster / streamer / small-business channel), and an AI lab acquiring a
> training corpus is neither. Here it matters less: the counterparty is an institution, not a person, and the
> relationship is a corpus deal, not a user flow. Recorded as Q-04 rather than assumed covered — D-10 is a
> deliberately **narrow** ratification and its non-closure list is what stops exactly this assumption.
