# Feature: Training-Use Compensation & Attribution

> **Number**: 11.07.03
> **Parent**: [11.07 AI Training Licensing](./11.07-ai-training-licensing-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Divides a dataset deal's money among the people whose work was in the corpus, and records that their work was
in it. The division is by manifest, not by influence — because attributing a generated output to specific
training works is an open research problem, and building a payment model on an unsolved problem would be
fabricating precision the platform does not have.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Reports | Sees what training use paid them, for which corpus, and on what basis. Cannot influence the basis. **Likely a very small number** — a corpus deal divided across thousands of works is pennies per work, and the platform should not dress that up. |
| Producer | Reports | Same. Stems may warrant a different rate than mixes — see Q-02. |
| Operator | None | No ownership stake in this capacity. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

## Behavior

The deal produces a number. The manifest (11.07.02) says what was in it and whose shares those works carried.
Compensation divides the first by the second (`#CX-02`). That is the whole mechanism, and its simplicity is
deliberate: every more sophisticated proposal in this space rests on a measurement nobody can make.

**Per-output attribution is explicitly out of scope** (`#R-01`). Paying a rights holder when a generated track
"resembles" or "was influenced by" their work is not computable today. Asserting otherwise would put a number
on an unsolved research question and pay people on the strength of it.

Division basis is open (Q-01): equal per work, weighted by duration, or weighted by some quality proxy. Every
option is arbitrary in a different direction, and the platform should say so rather than imply the maths is
principled.

### Happy Path

1. A dataset deal executes for a corpus with a pinned manifest
2. Deal value divides across the manifest's works by the agreed basis
3. Each work's share divides across its rights holders by their splits
4. Payment routes through the platform's normal disbursement (domain 10)
5. Rights holder sees: which corpus, which deal, what basis, what they got
6. The record persists — their work was in this corpus, licensed on this date, for this consideration

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Deal divides to pennies per work | **The likely outcome.** A five-figure corpus deal across 40,000 works is small change. | Show the real number. Do not aggregate it into something that looks bigger. |
| Splits changed since the manifest pinned | Follow the manifest or the current split? Consistency with 11.02.01 argues current. | 11.07.02 Q-04 — unresolved |
| A rights holder's payout is below the minimum disbursement | Accrues rather than vanishing; the debt is real even when tiny | Consistent with 11.03.03 D-01's treatment of owed-but-unpaid |
| Stems vs mixed tracks in one corpus | Stems are worth more as training data. Equal division under-pays the Producer who supplied them. | See Q-02 |
| Buyer never pays | An ordinary receivable; the manifest is unaffected | Domain 10 / 24 |
| A work is later found encumbered | Money already paid to parties who had no right to license it | 11.07.02 DT-03 — unrecoverable, and the payment is the least of it |
| Rights holder wants attribution but not money | Plausible and unsupported by a payment-shaped feature | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` |
| No deal ever closes | This feature has nothing to do. **The likely steady state**, per 11.07.01 DT-03. | — |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Division computing | Batch |
| Empty | No deals | **The expected state.** Not a defect. |
| Populated | Deal divided | Per-corpus, per-deal, with the basis stated |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Compensation should track influence — pay a rights holder when a generated output resembles their work | The intuitively fair model, and the one every artist advocacy proposal in this space asks for | ❌ REJECTED — **it is not computable.** Attributing a generative model's output to specific training examples is an open research problem, not an engineering task. Building payment on it would mean paying real money on a fabricated number and calling it attribution. The honest mechanism divides a **real deal** by a **real manifest**. If the research lands this becomes possible; today, asserting it would be exactly the kind of confident fabrication this pass is supposed to refuse. `#R-01` records the rejection at CX level. |
| DT-02 | The money is the point of this sub-domain | The sweep's framing, and the reason "AI Training Licensing" reads as a revenue feature | ❌ REJECTED — **third confirmation of the same inversion**, after 11.07.01 DT-02 (the record, not the revenue) and 11.07.02 DT-02 (the manifest, not the deal). The money here is pennies per work from a buyer market that barely exists. What is durable is the **statement**: this work was licensed for training, on this date, by these people, for this consideration. That is the fact the industry cannot currently produce for any work, and it is worth having whether or not the cheque is meaningful. **The pattern across all three features is that this sub-domain's value is evidentiary, not commercial** — which should reframe its MoSCoW entirely: 11.07.01 earns its place, 11.07.02 and 11.07.03 do not yet. |
| DT-03 | Equal-per-work division is neutral and therefore fair | It sounds principled | ❌ REJECTED — it is arbitrary, just quietly. A 30-second sketch and a fully-produced multi-track master are not equal training data; a stem set is worth several multiples of a mixed track. Equal division systematically under-pays the Producer who contributed the most valuable material — and per personas.md the Producer is the platform's provenance capture point, the persona it can least afford to treat carelessly. **There is no neutral basis.** Every option is a value judgement, and the platform should present it as one (Q-01) rather than hiding a policy inside a formula. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Division is by manifest, never by influence | Per DT-01 — per-output attribution is an unsolved research problem, and paying on it would fabricate precision |
| D-02 | The real number is shown, however small | Per the Role Lens — a corpus deal across thousands of works is pennies, and dressing it up is the kind of dishonesty that costs trust cheaply |
| D-03 | The division basis is presented as a value judgement, not a formula | Per DT-03 — there is no neutral basis, and hiding a policy inside arithmetic is how the persona gets rolled |
| D-04 | Sub-minimum payouts accrue rather than vanish | Consistent with 11.03.03 D-01 — a small debt is still a debt |
