## Overview

The licence itself: the structured grammar that says exactly what is permitted (media, territory, term,
exclusivity, usage), the certificate that serialises it, the means for a stranger to verify it is real, and
the lifecycle that governs how it is amended, extended, expires, or ends. Every other sub-domain in domain 11
produces one of these — sync, samples, covers, creator micro-licences and AI corpus deals are all just
different routes to the same instrument.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature** — 2+ interacting capabilities: the grammar defines the scope
vocabulary that policies (11.04), rate cards (11.03) and clearance (11.02) all key off; the certificate
serialises an instance of it; verification lets a third party check the certificate without trusting the
holder; and lifecycle mutates an instrument that other parties are relying on. An amendment rewrites what the
certificate says while verification is answering questions about it, and expiry changes an answer verification
already gave.
