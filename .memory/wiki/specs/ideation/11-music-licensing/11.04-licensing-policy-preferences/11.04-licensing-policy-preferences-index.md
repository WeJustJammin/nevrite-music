## Overview

Standing answers, given before anyone asks: what a rights holder will and will not license their work for,
which categories are refused at any price, when a request may be auto-approved without disturbing them, and
what happens when co-owners of one work answer those questions differently. It is the machinery that lets
the platform say yes without a human in the loop — and the machinery that lets one co-writer say no.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature** — 2+ interacting capabilities: a per-work policy sets auto-approve
thresholds; a category block overrides them at any price; a co-owner veto is a policy that binds parties other
than its author; and conflict resolution reconciles a work whose four owners set four contradictory policies.
The last one is not a nicety — **the work's effective policy is an aggregate that none of its owners authored**,
and computing it is a genuine interaction, not a lookup.

**This sub-domain was not in the sweep's candidate list.** It exists because of a gap found in the domain's own
rationale — see D-01.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | **Entire sub-domain added — not in the sweep's candidate list** | The domain rationale claims "clearance collapses from months of archaeology to a lookup plus a pre-set price". A lookup plus a pre-set price only works **if the price was pre-set** — and nothing in the 18 candidates covers pre-setting terms. The registry removes the *archaeology* (who owns what); it does not remove the *asking* (getting them to say yes). This sub-domain is the missing half of the domain's central claim. Found via 11.02.04 DT-01. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | Kept separate from 11.03 Rate Cards despite both being standing owner preferences | A rate card answers **how much**; a policy answers **whether at all**. They are not points on a scale — a tobacco block is a refusal at any price, and expressing refusal as a number implies a big enough offer should work. See 11.03.01 DT-01. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | Policy is evaluated **before** pricing and before clearance's consent routing | A forbidden use must never be priced, quoted, or routed for consent. Evaluation order is a correctness property, not an optimisation. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-04 | Conflict resolution (11.04.03) is a feature, not an implementation detail of 11.04.01 | The effective policy of a multi-owner work is an aggregate none of its owners authored. Someone has to be told their policy lost, and something has to decide. That is product, not plumbing. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-05 | **The buyer this sub-domain silently approves or refuses is a non-persona counterparty profile (`meta/counterparties.md`), not a Role Matrix column** | Parent domain D-10 (= global D-71). Both profiles are in play here: the **professional licence buyer** on the request side and the **creator micro-licence buyer** on the silently-governed side. 11.04.01's Role Lens **references** the professional profile; it is authored once in `meta/` and never copied.
