> **Last updated**: 2026-07-23

## Overview

Everything that involves making new music out of existing music: samples, interpolations, replays, remixes,
stem licensing and bootlegs. It is where the domain's most concrete claim lives — that clearance can collapse
from months of archaeology to an instant lookup against pre-set terms — because a sampled work whose owners
are already enumerated in the registry is a lookup, and one that is not is a phone call to a publisher who
will not return it.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature** — 2+ interacting capabilities: declaration identifies what was
borrowed; the programmatic path prices it instantly against standing terms; the negotiated path handles
everything the programmatic path cannot; revenue-share terms assign an ongoing split rather than a fee, which
writes into the *ownership* record rather than just producing a licence; and interpolation and remix are the
same machinery aimed at different legal objects. The paths share one declaration and fall through into one
another.
