# Feature: Sample Declaration & Source Identification

> **Number**: 11.05.01
> **Parent**: [11.05 Sample & Derivative Clearance](./11.05-sample-derivative-clearance-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Establishes what a work is made of: which sample, from which recording, of which composition, for how long,
and where in the track. It is the key that every downstream clearance path looks up — an unidentified source
cannot be priced, cleared, or paid — and it is the point where fingerprinting stops being sufficient, because
the most litigated borrowings are the ones no machine can hear.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Declares samples in their own works; reviews fingerprint suggestions; sees what they have inherited from a collaborator's contribution. Frequently does not know a declaration was needed — the flow must teach, not just collect. |
| Producer | Full | **The primary role.** Per personas.md they hold the stems and are "the person who *knows* who played what" — extended here to *what is in the mix*. They are also the only party who can identify a source that predates the session, and the one whose client delivery is worthless if this is wrong. |
| Operator | None | No stake in a work's composition in this capacity. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

## Behavior

A declaration names: **the source recording** (which master), **the source composition** (which song — often a
different owner set), **what was taken** (duration, which element, where), and **whether it is already cleared**.
The recording/composition distinction is not pedantry: it determines who gets asked, and a producer who clears
the master and not the publishing has cleared nothing.

Identification runs three ways, in descending reliability:

1. **On-platform lookup** — the source is a WeJammin work; the registry knows its owners exactly. This is the case the whole domain is built for.
2. **Fingerprint suggestion** — the source is a known commercial recording. Machine proposes, human confirms.
3. **Manual declaration** — the source is a record shop find, a friend's unreleased track, a library disc, or an interpolation with no audio in common. **The machine is blind and the human is the only mechanism.**

Per 11.02.03 D-01, fingerprinting **suggests**; humans **declare**. An auto-declared sample propagates
encumbrance downward through every derivative on the strength of a probabilistic match.

### Happy Path

1. During a session close or work upload, the system asks plainly: "Is anything in here from somewhere else? Samples, loops, a melody you played from another song?"
2. Fingerprint analysis runs in the background and proposes candidates with timestamps
3. Producer confirms, rejects, or adds a source the machine missed
4. For each source: what was taken, how long, already cleared?
5. Uncleared sources create an encumbrance (11.02.03) and offer a clearance route (11.05.02 or 11.05.03)
6. On-platform sources resolve to a known owner set instantly; off-platform sources get whatever the human knows

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Fingerprint proposes a false match | Human rejects; the rejection is recorded. **Never auto-declares.** | "We think we hear [track] at 0:42. Is that a sample?" — "No" is a first-class answer |
| Fingerprint misses an interpolation entirely | Expected — structurally blind (`#CX-03`). Declaration is the only route. | The prompt must ask about melodies played, not only audio used |
| Producer does not know what the sample is ("it's off a record I bought in 2009") | Partial declaration: "unidentified sample at 0:42, 4 bars". **Better than nothing** — it encumbers honestly and can be resolved later. | "Declare it as unidentified" must be an offered option, not a dead end |
| Source is on-platform | Owners resolve exactly; programmatic path opens; this is the case the domain exists for | "Cleared instantly — [terms]" |
| Source is a major-label recording | Off-platform; identification is fine, clearance is not — the platform cannot reach the owner | "We can identify this. We can't clear it — that's a phone call." Honest and useless, which is honest. |
| Declaring leaks an unreleased track to the sampled party | **Producers will not declare if declaring leaks.** May gate the whole sub-domain's adoption. | See parent Q-03 |
| Sample of a sample (chains) | Transitive; the chain must be walked and named | "Encumbered via [B] via [C]" — per 11.02.03 |
| Producer declares nothing at all | Work is not one-stop (11.02.03 D-02: silence is not cleanliness) | "No declaration — this can't be one-stop" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Fingerprint running | Background; declaration is never blocked on it |
| Empty | Nothing declared | "Nothing declared" — **not** "clean". Per 11.02.03 D-02. |
| Populated | Sources declared | Each: source, what was taken, where, clearance status, route |
| Error | Fingerprint failed | Manual declaration fully available. The machine is an assistant, not a dependency. |
| Partial | Declared but unidentified ("a record from 2009") | "Unidentified source — encumbered" — a legitimate, useful terminal state |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Declaration is a compliance chore and will be skipped, so it must be enforced with a hard gate before release | Enforcement instinct; and it is true that it will be skipped | ❌ REJECTED — a hard gate produces false declarations, not true ones. People blocked from releasing will click "no samples" and move on, and the platform will have manufactured a *documented lie* that is worse than silence: an attributable false statement. The lever is **value**, not enforcement — declaring must visibly buy you something (instant clearance, one-stop status, a sale) so that the lazy path is the correct path, per personas.md's Producer principle. Where declaration is genuinely absent, the honest state is "not one-stop", which costs the owner money without accusing them of anything. |
| DT-02 | The platform can identify most samples automatically — Content ID does it at YouTube scale | The obvious technical read | ❌ REJECTED — fingerprinting recognises **recordings**, and the clearance problem is about **works**. It is blind to: interpolations (no shared audio at all, `#CX-03`), heavily processed samples (pitched, chopped, reversed — the normal case in the genres that sample most), samples of unreleased or non-commercial sources, and library/vinyl material outside any reference database. Those blind spots are not the tail — **they are where the lawsuits are**, because the recognisable ones get cleared or caught early. The machine handles the easy half. |
| DT-03 | On-platform sources make this feature's value **compound with platform age**, and that is a cold-start problem nobody has stated | Reasoning from 11.01.05 DT-03, applied to samples: the same cold-start caveat surfaces from a second direction | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is worse here than in sync, because sampling skews heavily toward **catalogue** rather than new work. A producer samples a 1974 soul record, not last week's WeJammin session. The registry's coverage of what people actually sample will be near-zero for years, and no amount of split-at-creation fixes it — those records were made before the platform existed. **The programmatic clearance claim (the domain's most concrete differentiator) applies to a slice of sampling that is currently almost empty and grows only as WeJammin-native works become worth sampling.** This is the single strongest argument against 11.05.02 being a `must`, and it deserves the owner's attention rather than an agent's silent MoSCoW call. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Fingerprint suggests; human declares | Per 11.02.03 D-01 — a probabilistic match must not propagate encumbrance through a derivative tree unattended |
| D-02 | No hard gate on release for missing declarations | Per DT-01 — a gate manufactures documented lies, which are worse than honest silence |
| D-03 | "Unidentified source" is a first-class declaration | Honest partial information encumbers correctly and can be resolved later; forcing precision produces silence |
| D-04 | Recording and composition are declared separately | They have different owners; clearing one is clearing nothing |
