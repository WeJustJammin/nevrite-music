## Overview

What a licence costs and how the number is arrived at — standing rate cards that let a work be priced
without asking anyone, deal-specific quoting and counter-offers when it cannot, and most-favoured-nation
clauses that reach backwards and rewrite already-agreed prices when a co-owner negotiates a better one.
It serves every licence type in the domain: sync, samples, covers, creator micro-licences and AI corpus deals
all need a price before they can be an instrument.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature** — 2+ interacting capabilities: rate cards seed quotes; quotes
produce executed deals; executed deals are MFN's input, and MFN then reaches back and rewrites both the
quotes and (in effect) the rate cards it came from. MFN makes pricing **retroactively circular**, which is
precisely the interaction that disqualifies "pricing" from being a single leaf feature.

| D-01 | Candidate "Licence Quoting, Rate Cards & MFN" split into three features | Three distinct capabilities with a circular dependency (rate card → quote → executed deal → MFN → rewrites quote). Bundling them into one leaf would hide the retroactive rewrite, which is the only part with a hard correctness requirement. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | Placed at domain level rather than inside 11.01 Sync Licensing | Pricing serves sync, samples, covers, micro-licensing and AI corpus deals alike. Nesting it under sync would force four other sub-domains to reach sideways into it. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | Rate cards are **not** the same object as licensing policy (11.04) | Considered merging: both are standing owner preferences. Rejected — a rate card answers "how much", a policy answers "whether at all". A £5,000 rate card is an invitation; a tobacco block is a refusal at any price. Conflating them produces the worst possible UX: "set your price for tobacco ads: [never]". | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
