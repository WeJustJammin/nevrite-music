# Feature: Encumbrance & Uncleared-Sample Declaration

> **Number**: 11.02.03
> **Parent**: [11.02 Clearance & One-Stop Status](./11.02-clearance-one-stop-status-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

Records that a work contains something borrowed and not yet paid for — an uncleared sample, an
un-negotiated interpolation, a stem with a third-party plugin's licence restriction, a topline whose writer
was never enumerated — and propagates that taint to every work derived from it. It exists because
personas.md names this exact event as the Musician's worst accidental behaviour: *"uploads a stem containing
an uncleared sample and exposes every collaborator downstream."* A clearance model with no upstream taint
cannot represent the failure the persona doc says will happen.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | Declares known encumbrances on their own works; sees encumbrances they have inherited from someone else's contribution, and who declared them. Sees the blast radius: which of their works are affected. **The persona this feature protects** — the harm is accidental, so the design must catch it for them rather than punish them after. |
| Producer | Full | **The critical role.** They hold the stems and are the only party who knows what is actually in the mix. Declares on delivery and on session close. Also the party most exposed: they deliver to clients, and an encumbered delivery is their professional liability, not the sampler's. |
| Operator | None | No ownership stake in this capacity. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

## Behavior

An encumbrance is a **typed, scoped defect attached to a work**, and it flows downward through the
derivation graph. Its central property is that it **outranks a clean local computation**: a work whose own
splits are perfect and fully attested is still not clearable if it samples something uncleared. Clearance
must therefore walk *upstream* — "what is this made of?" — not merely across the local ownership set.

Declaration is the honest half of the ritual whose other half is completeness attestation (`#CX-05`):
"nobody is missing" and "nothing borrowed is unpaid" are the two facts only a human present at creation can
supply, both unfalsifiable at assert time, both catastrophic when false.

Resolution routes into 11.05 (sample clearance) — declaring the problem and fixing it are different features,
and conflating them means people avoid declaring because declaring starts a chore.

### Happy Path

1. During a session close or a work upload, the system asks directly: "Does this contain anything you didn't make? Samples, interpolations, loops, a topline from elsewhere?"
2. Audio fingerprinting (cross-cut, shared with 11.05.01) *suggests* possible matches — as prompts, never as automatic assertions
3. Producer/Musician declares: what, from where, cleared or not
4. System attaches the encumbrance, recomputes clearance for this work **and every descendant**
5. Every co-owner and every downstream derivative owner is notified — the notification set is exactly the exposed set
6. Owner is offered the resolution route (11.05.02 programmatic clearance if the source is on-platform; 11.05.03 negotiated if not)
7. Encumbrance clears on licence execution → clearance recomputes upward

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Declaration fan-out to descendants partially fails | **All-or-nothing, or fail toward "not clearable"** for anything unreachable. A partially-propagated encumbrance leaves some descendants falsely clearable — the worst outcome in the sub-domain. | Affected owners: "This work is now encumbered via [source]" |
| A collaborator declares an encumbrance on a work others co-own | Correct — the truth is the truth — but it reduces co-owners' asset value without their consent, and is a spite vector. Needs a contest path. | Co-owners: "[Name] declared an uncleared sample in [work]. [Contest]" |
| Fingerprint suggests a sample that isn't one (false positive) | **Never auto-declares.** A suggestion the human rejects, with the rejection recorded. | "We think we hear [track]. Is that a sample?" → "No" is a first-class answer |
| Encumbrance declared *after* a licence has issued | Licence stands — licences are irrevocable instruments (11.08). Creates a dispute (domain 24) and a platform liability question. | Buyer: "A defect was declared in a work you licensed" — and then the hard part, see parent Q-01 |
| Nobody declares anything — silence | **Silence must not mean clean.** A work with no declaration and no attestation is not one-stop. | "No sample declaration — this work can't be one-stop. [Declare]" |
| The sample source is itself encumbered | Taint is transitive; the graph walk must handle chains and cycles (A samples B samples A is possible with remixes) | "Encumbered via [B] via [C]" — the chain named, not just the nearest node |
| A stem carries a plugin/sample-pack licence restriction (Splice-style) | Genuinely an encumbrance and universally ignored in practice. `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5 deepening]` on whether this is in scope or over-reach. | — |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Fingerprint analysis running | Runs in background; declaration is never blocked on it |
| Empty | No declaration made | "Nothing declared" — **not** "clean". The distinction is the feature. |
| Populated | Encumbrance(s) attached | Each: type, source, status, resolution route, and the blast radius (N downstream works) |
| Error | Graph walk failed | Fail closed — descendants treated as encumbered until the walk succeeds |
| Partial | Declared, resolution in flight via 11.05 | "Clearing — [source], requested [date]" with the ETA from 11.05 |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Audio fingerprinting can detect samples automatically, so declaration should be automatic and the human step is friction | Content ID precedent; also the appealing "the platform just knows" story | ❌ REJECTED — twice over. (1) Fingerprinting catches recognisable recorded samples; it cannot hear an interpolation played by a session musician, a topline written by an uncredited friend, or a chord progression lifted from a voice note. The undetectable cases are the ones that produce lawsuits. (2) An auto-declared encumbrance lets a **false positive poison a catalogue with no human in the loop** — and encumbrance is a downward-propagating taint, so one bad match damages every derivative. Fingerprint suggests; human declares. |
| DT-02 | Encumbrance is really a domain 09 (Rights) concern — it is a defect in the ownership record | Boundary challenge: why does licensing own this? | ❌ REJECTED — an encumbrance is not an ownership fact. Domain 09 answers "who owns this work"; the answer can be complete, correct and unanimous while the work remains unlicensable because of what it is *made of*. Composition (`what is this made of?`) and ownership (`who owns it?`) are orthogonal graphs, and only licensing needs both traversed together. It sits here. |
| DT-03 | The declaring persona and the harmed persona are the same person, at different times | Direct reading of personas.md: the Musician's worst *accidental* behaviour is exposing collaborators via an undeclared sample; the Musician is also the one exposed when a collaborator does it to them | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it dictates the tone of the entire feature. This is not an enforcement surface aimed at a bad actor; it is a **safety net for a well-intentioned person who does not know they are creating a liability**. That means: ask directly and early (not buried in an advanced panel), never punish the declaration, make declaring cheaper than not declaring, and route straight to resolution. A feature designed as compliance policing would suppress the very declarations it needs. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Fingerprinting suggests; humans declare. No automatic encumbrance. | Per DT-01 — a false positive propagates downward through every derivative with nobody in the loop |
| D-02 | Silence is not cleanliness — an undeclared work is not one-stop | Silence-means-clean would make the whole feature opt-in for exactly the people least likely to opt in |
| D-03 | Declaration and resolution are separate features | Per Behavior — if declaring immediately starts a chore, people stop declaring, and the platform loses the fact it most needs |
| D-04 | The feature is a safety net, not an enforcement surface | Per DT-03 — the harm is accidental and the declarer is the victim's future self |
