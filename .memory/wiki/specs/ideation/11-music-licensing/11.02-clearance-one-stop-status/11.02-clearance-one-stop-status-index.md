## Overview

Answers one question the whole domain depends on: **can this work be licensed right now, by whom, and what
is in the way?** It projects the rights registry (domain 09) into a licensing verdict, collects the consents
that a "no" requires, tracks the encumbrances that poison a work from upstream, and gates issuance so the
platform never sells a licence it cannot honour.

**Why this is a sub-domain, not a feature** — 2+ interacting capabilities: computation reads the registry and
produces a verdict; attestation supplies the fact that computation cannot derive (that nobody is *missing*);
encumbrance declaration injects upstream taint that overrides an otherwise-clean computation; and consent
routing is the workflow that converts a "no" into a "yes" and writes back into the computation. Each one
rewrites the others' output.

**This sub-domain is where the thesis cashes out.** D-18 says provenance is the wedge; the wedge is only worth
money if provenance converts into clearability, and this is the converter. If this sub-domain is weak, WeJammin
is a credits database with a marketplace attached — the Jaxsta failure mode the problem statement names.

>
> **Operator is genuinely absent from this entire sub-domain**, not overlooked. Venue/studio operators sell
> time and space (personas.md); they do not hold recording or composition shares in that capacity. When a
> studio owner holds a master share, they are wearing the Producer lens. The explicit ❌ is the finding.
>
> **Fan is absent** for the same structural reason: no professional stake, no ownership, no clearance surface.
> The one place a fan-adjacent user touches licensing is creator micro-licensing (11.06), not here.
>
> The **licence buyer**, who consumes this sub-domain's output as their primary decision input, has **no
> persona** — see parent domain Q-01.

| D-01 | The clearance **gate** is merged into 11.02.01 rather than kept as a separate child | The gate is the enforcement face of the computed verdict, not an independent capability. | D-02 | Completeness attestation (11.02.02) added — **not in the sweep's candidate list** | Clearance computation can prove that known splits sum to 100%. It cannot prove nobody is *missing*. Without an attestation the one-stop flag is an unfalsifiable claim, and the domain's central promise is decorative. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-03 | Encumbrance declaration (11.02.03) added — **not in the sweep's candidate list** | personas.md names the exact failure as the Musician's worst accidental behaviour: "uploads a stem containing an uncleared sample and exposes every collaborator downstream." A clearance model with no upstream taint propagation cannot represent it. | Deep Think + personas.md, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-04 | Consent routing (11.02.04) added — **not in the sweep's candidate list** | The sweep listed the one-stop *flag* but not the workflow that turns a "no" into a "yes". Without it the flag is a read-only verdict and non-one-stop works are permanently dead, which throws away most of the catalogue. | Deep Think, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
