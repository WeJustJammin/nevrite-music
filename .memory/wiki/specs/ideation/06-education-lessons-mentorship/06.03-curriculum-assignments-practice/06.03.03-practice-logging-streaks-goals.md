# Feature: Practice Logging, Streaks & Goals

> **Number**: 06.03.03
> **Parent**: [Curriculum, Assignments & Practice](./06.03-curriculum-assignments-practice-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

## Overview

The record of what the student actually did between lessons. **This is the highest-frequency surface
in the entire product** — the domain rationale is explicit that "a student opens it daily where an
artist opens analytics weekly," and nothing across 24 domains has a stronger claim to a daily open.
Against the thesis (D-18), consolidation earns the daily reason to show up; this is the closest thing
on the map to one.

It is also the feature most likely to be built wrong, because the obvious build — a log, a streak, a
badge — is a self-report system aimed at children, and self-report systems decay.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Student**: logs practice (or has it logged for them — see Q-01), sets goals ("grade 5 by summer", "play this at 120bpm"), sees their own history and streak, notices they practised 20 minutes a day for a month, which is the whole point. **As Teacher**: sees what was *actually* practised — not what was assigned — and adjusts. The gap between assigned and practised is the single most useful diagnostic a teacher can have, and today they get it by asking a child who has an incentive to lie. |
| Producer | None | Not visible (see the sub-domain index note). |
| Operator | Reports | As an academy: **aggregate engagement only** — never a named student's daily log. An academy has a legitimate interest in whether their teaching produces practice; it has no legitimate interest in a specific child's diary. This line is deliberate (sub-domain index note). |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

## Behavior

A practice entry is: when, how long, on what, how it felt. The minimum viable entry is one tap. Every
field beyond the first is friction against a 12-year-old who wants to stop practising and go outside —
which means the design constraint is unusually harsh: **the log must cost less than the practice.**

Goals give the log a direction ("grade 5 by summer"; "this piece at tempo"), and streaks give it
feedback. Both are conventional. What is not conventional, and is the honest problem in this feature,
is that all of it is **self-reported by children**, and self-report decays. See DT-01.

**Decided (D-01)**: capture is a byproduct of the practice room (06.03.04), not a form. **Deferred to
the user (Q-01)**: whether capture goes further — *audio detection* ("we heard you play for 20
minutes") that removes even the one confirming tap. That is a product/ethics decision, not an
implementation one: it makes the log honest and effortless, and it points a microphone at a child in
their bedroom. It is not the agent's to make. The byproduct level below is complete and buildable
regardless of how Q-01 resolves; audio detection is an escalation on top of it.

### Happy Path

1. Student opens the practice room (06.03.04) to practise — **not to log**
2. The session is timed as a byproduct of practising, with the assignment (06.03.02) in front of them
3. They finish; the log entry is already 90% written from what the room observed
4. One tap confirms, optionally noting how it went
5. Streak advances; goal progress moves
6. Teacher sees the week's real pattern before the next lesson — 4 short sessions, all Sunday night, or nothing

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Student practises without opening the app | The base rate. Manual back-fill must be trivial and unshamed | Student: "Add practice" with a sane default. **No guilt, no broken-streak drama** |
| Student lies about practice time | Structurally unpreventable while self-reported, and the teacher can hear the truth in 30 seconds of the next lesson | Nothing. The product must not police a child; the instrument already does |
| Streak breaks | Per D-02 the streak is forgiving by construction: a lapse is absorbed by a freeze/grace allowance, not converted into a loss event. If streaks are off entirely (Q-03), there is nothing to break | Student: "Welcome back — pick up where you left off," never "you lost your 14-day streak." No counter reset to zero on screen, no loss-framed notification (DT-02) |
| Student practises 6 hours in one day, nothing else | Logged honestly; that is real (and often how it goes before a lesson) | Teacher: sees the pattern — genuinely useful |
| Teacher uses the log to admonish | The product hands a teacher a surveillance record of a child. **This is a foreseeable misuse of the feature's most valuable data.** Per D-06 the teacher's view is framed diagnostically, never evidentially — the gap is shown as "here's what happened this week," and the copy never renders a "did-not-practise" verdict | Teacher: assigned-vs-practised pattern with no compliance score, no red "missed" flags, no exportable "proof" artifact. The framing is a design constraint, not a preference (DT-03) |
| Parent uses the log to enforce practice | Same, sharper, and much more common. Whether a parent even *has* this view is unresolved (domain D-03) | `[PENDING]` — see Q-02 |
| Adult self-directed learner, no teacher | Fully supported and possibly the feature's **best** user — motivated, honest, no shame dynamics | Student: log + goals work standalone |
| Student has 2 teachers | One log, attributed per instrument | Both teachers see their own relevance |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | History fetch | Skeleton week strip |
| Empty | No practice logged | "Start your first session" — a beginning, never a rebuke |
| Populated | History exists | Week strip + streak + goal progress |
| Error | Log write failed | **Queued locally, retried** — practice happens in bedrooms and garages with bad wifi (Offline cross-cut) |
| Partial | Session timed but unconfirmed | "Was that 20 minutes of practice?" — one tap |
| Recovered | Streak lapsed then resumed | Per D-02, the lapse renders as a neutral gap in the week strip and a return prompt — "Good to see you back" — with no zeroed counter and no loss-framed alert. This state exists precisely so the lapse is a resume point, not a quit trigger (DT-02, the feature's biggest single risk) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Self-reported practice logging decays, and the domain's "daily habit surface" claim depends on it not decaying | Domain expertise: every practice-journal app, paper or digital, has been abandoned by every student who ever used one. The base rate of long-term adherence is close to zero | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is the most important finding in the sub-domain, because it **threatens the domain's central strategic claim**. The rationale says practice tracking is the product's highest-frequency habit surface; that is only true if students keep doing it, and history says they don't. The fix is structural, not motivational: **the log must be a byproduct of a tool they open to practise** (06.03.04), never a form they open to report. This is the same capture-at-source logic the platform's thesis rests on (`meta/problem-statement.md`: "no system is present at the moment of creation, so the record is always a reconstruction") — applied to practice instead of splits. A practice log filled in from memory on Sunday is a reconstruction, and it is worthless for the same reasons. **This confirmed hypothesis is the sole justification for 06.03.04 existing.** |
| DT-02 | Streaks and gamification will drive practice adherence | Duolingo's model, which is the reflexive comparison and will be proposed by someone | ❌ REJECTED as a default, on a domain-specific argument. Duolingo's user chose to learn a language; **a music student's most common profile is a child whose parent chose for them**, and who is already under practice pressure at home. Adding a streak to that adds a second nagging authority and a loss-frame: a broken streak reads as "you already failed, so why start again today." The population that quits instruments is exactly the population that responds worst to loss-framing. Streaks may be *offered* (the adult self-directed learner genuinely likes them and is the segment they fit); they must not be the spine, and forgiveness/freeze must exist. Recording this because "add streaks" is the single most predictable request against this feature. |
| DT-03 | The teacher's most valuable view is the **gap** between what was assigned and what was practised | Reasoning from the teacher's actual job — a teacher's whole diagnostic problem is that they see 30 minutes and infer the other 167 hours | ✅ CONFIRMED, and it is the feature's strongest teacher-side value. Today a teacher asks a child "did you practise?" and receives an answer with a known bias. The gap view — assigned bars 12–20 at 80bpm, practised 4×15min on the piece they already liked — turns a weekly guess into data. **Caveat**: it is one keystroke from a surveillance record handed to an adult with authority over a child. Framing must be diagnostic ("here's what happened") and never evidential ("here's the proof you didn't"). That is a design constraint, not a preference. |
| DT-04 | This is the domain's real bid for the platform's daily open, which makes it strategically larger than its MoSCoW instinct suggests | D-18 (consolidation wins the user, and needs a daily reason to show up); domain rationale's habit-surface claim; the ideation index's own note that the black-box-royalty hook is "a once-event, not a habit" | ✅ CONFIRMED, conditionally — and the condition is DT-01. Across 24 domains, almost nothing has a genuine daily open: gear browsing is episodic, gigs are weekly at best, royalties are quarterly, analytics is weekly. Practice is **daily by definition**. If the domain-map synthesis is right that the platform needs a daily habit and doesn't obviously have one, this feature is the strongest candidate on the map — but **only** if practice is captured as a byproduct rather than self-reported (DT-01), and only if it isn't gamified into a second source of pressure (DT-02). Both conditions are unmet by the obvious build, which is why this feature is the easiest one here to get wrong. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **06.03.04 Practice Room Tools** — **the dependency the feature's viability rests on** (DT-01). The log is a byproduct of the room, not a form beside it.
- Touches **06.03.02 Assignments** — the assignment is what's practised; the gap between them is the teacher's diagnostic (DT-03).
- Touches **06.03.05 Progress Reports** — practice volume is a report input, and a **poor proxy for progress** (hours ≠ improvement).
- Touches **06.01.04 Lesson Delivery** — the teacher reviews the week's pattern before/at the lesson.
- Consumes **Offline & Low-Connectivity Field Resilience** (global cross-cut) — practice happens in bedrooms, garages and practice rooms with no signal. A log that needs connectivity loses entries, and a lost entry is a broken streak, which per DT-02 is a quit trigger.
- Consumes **Privacy, Consent & Data Portability** (global cross-cut) — a minor's daily behavioural record is among the most sensitive data on the platform.
- Consumes **Safeguarding & Minor Protection** (global cross-cut) — who may see a child's daily activity is a safeguarding question, not a permissions preference.
- Consumes **Notifications & Alerts** (global cross-cut) — practice reminders, which are one design mistake away from nagging a child (DT-02).
- Consumes **Analytics Instrumentation & Per-Domain Reporting** (global cross-cut) — the Operator's aggregate view.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Practice is captured as a **byproduct of practising**, never as a form to fill in | Per DT-01 — the platform's own capture-at-source thesis, applied to practice. A reconstructed log is worthless and gets abandoned. |
| D-02 | Streaks are **optional and forgiving**, never the spine | Per DT-02 — loss-framing a child's hobby is a quit trigger, and this population is the one most vulnerable to it. |
| D-03 | Manual back-fill is trivial and **unshamed** | Practising without opening the app is the base rate, not a deviation. |
| D-04 | Entries are queued locally and survive offline | Per the Offline cross-cut — practice rooms have no signal, and a lost entry breaks a streak, which per D-02 costs a student. |
| D-05 | The Operator sees **aggregates only**, never a named student's log | The line between supervising a teacher and surveilling a child (sub-domain index note). |
| D-06 | The teacher's gap view is framed **diagnostically**, never evidentially | Per DT-03 — it is one keystroke from a surveillance record in the hands of an adult with authority over a child. |
