# Feature: Practice Room Tools

> **Number**: 06.03.04
> **Parent**: [Curriculum, Assignments & Practice](./06.03-curriculum-assignments-practice-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

> **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list. It exists because 06.03.03 DT-01
> concluded that self-reported practice logging decays, and the only structural fix is to make the log
> a byproduct of a tool the student opens *to practise*. Without this feature, practice tracking is a
> form, and the domain's daily-habit claim fails.

## Overview

The thing a student opens to practise: metronome, tuner, drone, a slow-downer/looper for the
recording they're learning from, the assignment in front of them, and a record button. The point is
not the tools themselves — free ones exist everywhere — it is that **practising inside them makes the
practice log honest, automatic and free** (06.03.03 D-01), and puts the assignment and the submission
in the same place as the practice.

This is the platform's capture-at-source thesis applied to practice: be present while the work
happens, and the record writes itself.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Student**: opens the practice room, sees this week's assignment, sets the metronome to the target tempo the teacher specified, tunes, loops the four hard bars at 70% speed, plays along, hits record when it's ready to send. The session logs itself. **As Teacher**: configures per-assignment — target tempo, loop points on a reference recording, the drone note. Sets up the room so the student's practice is *specified*, not guessed at. |
| Producer | None | Not visible — though see Q-01: a click and a tuner are not education-specific objects, and a Producer plainly uses both. That is the cross-cut question, not an access question. |
| Operator | None | Not visible. An academy has no role inside a student's practice session. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. |

## Behavior

The room is the assignment plus the tools needed to execute it, in one surface:

- **Metronome** — with the teacher's target tempo pre-set, and tempo ramping ("start at 60, +5 each pass")
- **Tuner** — mandatory before practice for most instruments; being out of tune is how bad practice starts
- **Drone** — a pitch reference for intonation practice; essential for strings, voice, winds, invisible to pianists
- **Slow-downer / looper** — play a reference recording at 70% without pitch shift, loop bars 12–20. This is how people actually learn parts by ear
- **Record** — submit to the teacher (06.03.02) from here, with no export step

Everything the student does is observable by the room, which is what makes the log free. That is also
precisely what makes it a privacy surface pointed at a child, which is why the observation is scoped
to the session and not to the microphone (D-03).

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5]`: which tools ship. The list above is a domain expert's default,
not a validated set, and it varies enormously by instrument — a drummer needs a click and a
slow-downer, a singer needs a drone and a piano, a pianist needs neither tuner nor drone.

### Happy Path

1. Student opens the practice room; this week's assignment is already there (06.03.02)
2. Tuner is up first — 15 seconds, and the practice isn't sabotaged from the start
3. Metronome is pre-set to the teacher's target tempo
4. Student loops bars 12–20 of the reference at 70%, plays along, ramps tempo up over 20 minutes
5. They record one take and send it
6. They close the app; the log entry is written and needs one tap, or nothing (06.03.03)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Student uses their own metronome/phone app | The base rate — they already own tools they like. **The room must not require its tools to be used** | Log still works from session time; the room degrades to assignment + record + timer |
| No microphone permission | Tuner and record die; metronome, drone and looper live | Graceful degradation, with a plain reason — never a permission wall on the whole room |
| Latency makes play-along unusable | Browser audio latency is real and instrument-dependent. A drummer will notice 40ms instantly; a singer may not | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — and it is the stack question below |
| Student practises acoustically away from the device | Extremely common (a piano across the room, a garage kit). Session time is still capturable; audio is not | Room works as a timer + assignment; no penalty |
| Reference recording is copyrighted | Slowing down and looping a commercial recording for private study is how everyone learns — and its legal footing varies by jurisdiction | Routed to domain 24 / DMCA. See DT-03 |
| Bad wifi | Room must work offline; the log queues | Offline cross-cut; tools are local |
| Student is a pianist | Tuner and drone are irrelevant; showing them is noise | Instrument-aware room composition |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Room open | Tools render immediately; assignment fills in |
| Empty | No assignment set | Tools alone — **the room is useful with no teacher at all** (see DT-02) |
| Populated | Assignment + tools | The full room |
| Error | Audio init failed | Tools that don't need audio still work; the reason is stated |
| Partial | Some permissions denied | Degraded room, no wall |
| Active | Session running | Timer, tempo, loop state — and the log accruing quietly |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A practice log needs a practice tool underneath it, or it is a form and it will be abandoned | 06.03.03 DT-01 — self-report decays; `meta/problem-statement.md`'s root cause: "no system is present at the moment of creation, so the record is always a reconstruction" | ✅ CONFIRMED — this is the feature's entire reason for existing, and it is the platform's own thesis with the nouns swapped. WeJammin's argument for why it can capture splits when Jaxsta cannot is that it is *in the room where the work happens*. Practice is work, and it happens in a room. A product that asks "how long did you practise?" is Jaxsta for children: asking someone to remember. A product the student opens *to practise* knows the answer without asking. The domain's daily-habit claim (06.03.03 DT-04) rests entirely on this feature existing. |
| DT-02 | The practice room needs a teacher to be worth anything | It sits in a sub-domain about the teaching loop; the assignment is what gives the room its purpose | ❌ REJECTED — and the rejection reveals something strategically interesting. The room is **fully usable with no teacher**: a self-taught guitarist learning a solo by ear wants a looper, a slow-downer and a tuner, and has no lessons at all. That makes this the domain's only **free, teacher-less, top-of-funnel surface** — a reason for an unaffiliated musician to open the app daily before they have ever bought anything. Given `meta/personas.md`'s Musician cannot "afford per-tool subscriptions across six products", a genuinely good free practice room is both a consolidation win and the cheapest acquisition surface in the domain. It also weakens the case for gating the room behind a lesson relationship. |
| DT-03 | This is small, so it is a MoSCoW `could` at best | Its apparent modesty — free metronome apps are a solved commodity | ❌ REJECTED, on a conditional. Individually the tools are commodities; **as the substrate of the log they are load-bearing** for the domain's strongest strategic claim (DT-01 + 06.03.03 DT-04). Cutting this feature does not cut a metronome — it silently converts practice tracking into a self-report form and forfeits the platform's only daily open. That said, the honest caution: a *good* looper/slow-downer is real audio DSP, and the ratified map already routes "heavy audio transcode/waveform/DSP" to `/create-prd-architecture` as a **hard constraint against the locked Astro + Workers + Supabase stack**. The metronome/tuner/drone are trivial; the slow-downer is not. That split may need to become a MoSCoW split. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **06.03.03 Practice Logging** — **the reason this exists** (DT-01). The room's session data is the log.
- Touches **06.03.02 Assignments** — the assignment renders in the room; the submission is recorded from it, with no export step.
- Touches **06.03.01 Curriculum** — the teacher's target tempo/loop points come from the unit.
- Consumes **Media Handling & Audio Playback** (global cross-cut) — playback, slow-down, loop. **Carries the map's hard stack warning on heavy audio DSP** (DT-03).
- Consumes **Offline & Low-Connectivity Field Resilience** (global cross-cut) — practice rooms and bedrooms have no signal; the tools must be local.
- Consumes **Accessibility** (global cross-cut) — a visual metronome and tuner are not a nicety here; deaf and hard-of-hearing musicians exist and practise.
- Touches **[08 Real-Time Jamming](../../08-realtime-jamming-remote-sessions/)** and **[07 Music Projects](../../07-music-projects-collaboration/)** — see Q-01: a tuner and a click are plainly wanted there too, which is the cross-cut question this feature does not resolve.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The room exists to make the log a **byproduct** | Per DT-01. Without it, practice tracking is a form and the domain's daily-habit claim is false. |
| D-02 | The room's tools are **optional**; the room degrades to assignment + timer + record | Per the first edge case — students already own tools they like, and requiring ours would lose the log entirely. |
| D-03 | Observation is scoped to the **session**, not to the microphone | The room can know "20 minutes of practice happened" without listening to a child's bedroom. Weakens the log's honesty; it is the right default given who the user is. Q-01 in 06.03.03 may reopen this. |
| D-04 | The room is **instrument-aware** — a pianist is not shown a tuner and a drone | Irrelevant tools are noise, and noise in a daily surface is what closes it. |
