## Overview

What happens in the 167 hours a week the student is **not** in a lesson. Learning an instrument
happens during practice; the lesson is a weekly course-correction. This sub-domain owns the loop that
carries a student between lessons: a plan of what to learn, an assignment of what to do this week,
the practice they actually do, the teacher's feedback on it, and the progress that emerges.

**Why this matters more than its apparent modesty**: the domain rationale identifies practice
tracking as **the highest-frequency habit surface in the entire product** — "a student opens it daily
where an artist opens analytics weekly." Nothing else in 24 domains has a daily open. Against the
thesis (D-18), consolidation is what wins the user by giving them a daily reason to show up; this
sub-domain is the strongest daily-reason candidate on the map. That is a strategic claim, and it is
argued rather than assumed in the domain index.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: five capabilities in a closed loop, each useless alone. A curriculum
with no assignments is a document; assignments with no practice log are homework nobody checks;
practice logs with no feedback are a diary; feedback with no plan is noise. The loop closes: lesson →
assignment → practice → evidence → feedback → next plan.

## Decision Log

| # | Decision | Context | Source |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | The sweep's single candidate "Curriculum, Assignments & Practice Tracking" is a **sub-domain of five features**, not one | Three interacting capabilities were bundled by the candidate name, and drilling found two more (practice tools, progress reports). The loop between them is the sub-domain's justification. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |
| D-02 | **Practice Room Tools added** — not in the candidate list | Deep Think. A practice *logger* without practice *tools* is a chore that asks a 12-year-old to do admin about their homework. The domain rationale's claim that practice tracking is the product's daily habit surface is only true if opening the app is how you practise — not how you report having practised. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 Deep Think |
| D-03 | **Progress Reports & Skill Tracking separated** from practice logging | Different reader, different cadence, different privacy class. A practice log is the student's own daily record; a progress report is a periodic artifact written *for someone else* — usually a parent, who is paying. Merging them would give the report's reader access to the diary. | `/ideate-discover` Step 3 Deep Think |
| D-04 | Assignment attachments carry a **third-party copyright surface** and are routed out | A teacher uploading a scan of a copyrighted method book is UGC infringement, identical in kind to any other. Domain 24 + the DMCA compliance line in `meta/constraints.md` own it; this sub-domain only flags where it enters. | 06.03.02 DT-04 |
