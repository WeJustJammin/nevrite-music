## Overview

Teaching and learning — lesson booking and packages, curriculum and practice tracking, teacher matching, courses, mentorship, and the safeguarding that working with minors demands.

**Why this is a top-level domain**: Proposed as a domain independently by four lenses. Deliberately numbered next to Services Marketplace because its whole rationale is the contrast, and the contrast must be legible: the model diverges materially — recurring rather than one-off, relationship rather than deliverable, progress-tracked rather than accepted, pack/subscription priced rather than escrowed, and minors-heavy, triggering background checks, guardian consent and safeguarding duties no other domain carries. Lessonface, TakeLessons, Tonebase and Fender Play are standalone businesses. It matters more than its apparent modesty: teaching is how a large share of working musicians actually pay rent, and practice tracking is the highest-frequency habit surface in the product — a student opens it daily where an artist opens analytics weekly. The platform's unfair advantage is credentialing: verified credits beat a bio and a headshot decisively. Consumes the Real-Time Rooms cross-cut for live lessons rather than owning transport.

**Interacting capabilities** (what justifies domain status):

- recurring lesson booking & packages
- teacher/student matching & trials
- curriculum, assignments & practice tracking
- course authoring & marketplace
- mentorship programmes
- safeguarding-gated delivery

> **Drilling note on the sixth capability**: "safeguarding-gated delivery" survives as a *delivery
> gate*, not as a node. The **mechanism** (vetting, guardian consent, age assurance, chaperone norms)
> is the ratified `Safeguarding & Minor Protection` cross-cut, which serves 7 domains. What is
> domain-owned is the enforcement point — a lesson room that fails closed (06.01.04 D-02). The other
> five capabilities each became a node. See D-05.
