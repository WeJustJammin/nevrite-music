# Feature: Group Lessons, Workshops & Masterclasses

> **Number**: 06.05
> **Parent**: [Education, Lessons & Mentorship](./education-lessons-mentorship-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-17

> **Deep Think addition** — not in the sweep's candidate list. The nine candidates describe a domain
> that is 100% one-to-one tuition plus asynchronous courses. That omits how an enormous share of music
> teaching actually happens: in groups. School ensembles, band coaching, rock-school band nights,
> weekend workshops, and the masterclass — a format so central to music pedagogy it has its own word.

## Overview

One teacher, many students, one slot. Group teaching is structurally different from 1:1 in every
dimension that matters: it is priced per head or per group, it has a roster and an attendance
register rather than a credit ledger, it has a minimum viable size (a two-person "band workshop" is a
refund), and — uniquely in this domain — **the other students are part of the product**. Nobody
learns to play in a band alone, which is why this format exists and why 1:1 cannot substitute for it.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Teacher/leader**: creates a group class or a one-off workshop, sets capacity, min/max size, price per head, and a schedule; manages the roster; takes a register; teaches to a room rather than a person. **As Student**: browses open groups, sees who else is in it and at what level (which is *the* deciding factor — a band workshop with the wrong people is worthless), joins, attends. |
| Producer | Full | **The masterclass is a Producer's format.** A one-off "how I record drums" workshop to 30 people is a thing Producers genuinely do, would never do as weekly 1:1 tuition, and monetises their credits (06.02.02) at their hourly worth. This is the second place in domain 06 the Producer is first-class — the other being course authoring (06.04.01). |
| Operator | Config | **The strongest Operator fit in the entire domain.** Groups need rooms with capacity — the Operator's inventory is "time and space", perishable and capacity-constrained (`meta/personas.md`). A rehearsal-space operator running Saturday band workshops is filling a Tuesday-afternoon problem with a product. They host, and may run the group themselves. |
| Fan | None | Not visible as a fan surface. A Fan joining a workshop is acting as a Musician (student) from that moment. *But see DT-03* — the masterclass is the one format where the fan/student boundary genuinely blurs, and this `None` may be wrong. |

## Behavior

A group is a **scheduled class with a roster and a capacity**, in one of three shapes that behave
differently enough to be worth naming:

| Shape | Cadence | Composition | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recurring group class** | Weekly, termly | Fixed cohort, level-matched | Members drift apart in ability; one drops and the group is unviable |
| **Workshop** | One-off, a few hours | Ad hoc, mixed | Under-subscribed |
| **Masterclass** | One-off, an event | One expert, many observers, few performers | Nothing much — it's the most robust format here |

The 06.01 credit ledger does **not** apply cleanly, which is the main reason this is a sibling of that
sub-domain rather than a feature inside it. A group has a **per-head price and a viability threshold**:
if only 2 of a minimum 5 sign up, the class doesn't run, and everyone is refunded — a cancellation
semantics 06.01.03 has no concept of, because a 1:1 lesson has no minimum size.

Level-1 resolution: group teaching ships as **one feature with three shapes** (recurring class,
workshop, masterclass) sharing one roster/viability/register model. Whether the recurring class is
different enough to be promoted to its own sub-domain is a structural call deferred to
`/ideate-validate` (Q-01) — it does not change the behavior specified below.

### Happy Path

1. Teacher (or Operator) creates a group: "Beginner band workshop, Saturdays 2pm, 5–8 players, £15/head, 8 weeks"
2. Sets required instruments/levels — a band needs a drummer, and this is the constraint no generic class has
3. Students browse and join; the roster fills; the viability threshold is met
4. Class runs; the leader takes a register
5. Recurring group repeats; the roster is stable
6. Cohort forms — and per DT-02, the cohort may become the most valuable thing the platform produced

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Under-subscribed (2 of min 5) | Class doesn't run; all refunded. **A semantic 06.01.03 has no equivalent for** | All: "Not enough players — refunded. Next date?" |
| Band workshop with no drummer | The music-specific failure. A "band workshop" of four guitarists is not a band workshop | Roster composition by instrument, not just headcount |
| Levels diverge in a recurring group | The chronic failure of group teaching — one student pulls ahead, one falls behind, and both leave. Level-1 stance: the leader can re-band the roster or split into two cohorts; the platform surfaces a divergence signal but does not auto-resolve it | Leader gets a "roster spread widening" nudge; the mechanism is a product decision — `[PENDING — Q-02, user]` |
| One member drops mid-term | Roster falls below the viability threshold **mid-flight**. Level-1 stance: a running group does **not** auto-cancel — the leader chooses to continue (absorb the gap), backfill the seat, or wind down with pro-rata refunds for remaining sessions | Leader: "Roster below minimum — continue, backfill, or wind down?" Remaining members: "Class continues" or pro-rata refund notice |
| Mixed minors and adults in one group | **Safeguarding is materially different from 1:1** — an adult in a group of minors is a different risk profile than an adult alone with one minor, with different (not lesser) controls | Safeguarding cross-cut. See DT-01 |
| Masterclass to 200 people, remote | Not a lesson room — a broadcast. Different transport entirely | Real-Time Rooms cross-cut, at a scale the lesson room never hits |
| Student attends 1 of 8 sessions | Per-head pricing means the seat was reserved for all 8; the group ran whether they came or not. Level-1 stance: **no make-ups, no per-session refund** — attendance is the student's, the seat is the class's. Recurring classes may offer leader-set make-up credits, but that is opt-in policy, not a platform guarantee | "You've missed 3 of 8 sessions. Seats are reserved per class — no automatic refund." Make-up policy shown only if the leader enabled one — otherwise `[PENDING — /write-be-spec: leader-configurable make-up policy]` |
| Recording a masterclass and selling it | The natural bridge into 06.04 (Course Marketplace) | Q-03 |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Group fetch | Skeleton roster |
| Empty | No groups | Student: "No group classes near you". Teacher: "Start a group" |
| Populated | Roster filling | Roster with spaces, composition, level mix |
| Error | Join failed | Retry; place held |
| Partial | Below viability | "3 of 5 — needs 2 more by Thursday to run" — honest and urgent |
| Viable | Threshold met | "Running — 6 players" |
| Cancelled | Threshold missed | Refunded, with alternatives offered |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Group teaching is a variant of 1:1 with a bigger roster, and belongs inside 06.01 | The apparently obvious structural read — it's a lesson with more people | ❌ REJECTED on three independent grounds. (1) **Economics**: per-head pricing with a **viability threshold** — a concept the credit ledger cannot express, since a 1:1 lesson has no minimum size and never cancels for under-subscription. (2) **Product**: the other students *are* the product; a band workshop with the wrong instruments fails regardless of the teacher. (3) **Safeguarding**: an adult with a group of minors is a *different* risk profile than an adult alone with one minor — not lesser, different, with different controls (chaperone norms and recording defaults designed for 1:1 don't transfer). Any one of these would be a stretch; together they make it a sibling. |
| DT-02 | Group teaching is a cold-start and cohort engine for the wider platform, not just an education format | `meta/personas.md`'s Musician is a member of multiple bands; the map's own Community rationale says "local scenes are the only tractable cold-start strategy: 200 users in one city feels alive, 20,000 scattered globally feels dead" | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is the most strategically interesting finding in this feature. A group class is **a room full of local musicians at a similar level who now know each other**. That is: a cohort for domain 03 (Community), latent bands for domain 01, future collaborators for domain 07, and a dense local graph in one city — which is precisely the cold-start asset the map says the platform needs and cannot buy. A weekly band workshop manufactures the local scene that Community can only hope to find. **No other node in domain 06 produces a network effect at all**; 1:1 tuition creates one relationship, a course creates none. |
| DT-03 | The masterclass blurs the fan/student boundary in a way the Role Lens's `None` may get wrong | `meta/personas.md`: the fan/hobbyist boundary "is porous"; a Producer's masterclass is watched by people who are there for the *person* | ✅ CONFIRMED as a real tension, ❌ unresolved. "Watch {famous producer} mix a record for two hours, 200 seats" is, honestly, **half a fan event**. Most attendees will never apply it; they came because they admire the person. That makes the masterclass adjacent to domain 20's ticketed listening parties and domain 19's ticketing — and the boundary the ratified map drew ("a scene listening session is a gathering (Community); a ticketed listening party is D2F monetization (Fanbase)") suggests a **ticketed masterclass may be a Fanbase product wearing an Education costume**. Raised as an open question rather than resolved, because it crosses a boundary the owner ratified. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Group teaching is a **sibling** of 06.01, not a child | Per DT-01 — viability thresholds, roster composition and group safeguarding are three independent breaks from the 1:1 model. |
| D-02 | A group has a **viability threshold**; below it, the class doesn't run and everyone is refunded | The defining commercial semantic, and one the credit ledger cannot express. |
| D-03 | Roster composition is **by instrument and level**, not headcount | A band workshop of four guitarists is not a band workshop. Music-specific and non-negotiable. |
| D-04 | Group safeguarding is **different in kind** from 1:1, not a relaxation of it | Per DT-01 — the intuition that "a group is safer" is how controls get quietly dropped. Different, not lesser. |
