# Feature: Verified Credentials & Credit-Backed Credibility

> **Number**: 06.02.02
> **Parent**: [Teacher Discovery, Profiles & Trials](./06.02-teacher-discovery-profiles-trials-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

**The domain's unfair advantage, isolated into one feature.** Every teacher marketplace in existence
ranks self-asserted claims: "toured internationally", "played with major artists", "20 years'
experience". None can check any of it. WeJammin can — a teacher's verified credits from domain 02 are
counter-attested by the people who were in the room, which makes "played on this record" a *fact* on
a teacher page rather than a boast. This feature is the evidence block: what is proven, by whom, and
how a student can check it.

Its defining property is that **the teacher cannot write it**. That is not a limitation; it is the
entire source of its value.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | **As Teacher**: cannot author or edit the credential block — can only *submit things for verification* (a degree, a diploma, a DBS check) and choose whether to surface an already-verified credit on their teaching page. Sees what evidence they have and where it's thin. **As Student**: reads the block, drills into any credit to see the counter-attesting party, and can check a claim rather than trust it. |
| Producer | Read-only | The **counter-attester**. Per `meta/personas.md`, the Producer is a trust broker whose "attestation is worth more than a self-claim". They do not act *in this feature* — they acted in domain 02, when they confirmed the credit in the room. This feature merely renders the consequence. Their presence in this table is the whole mechanism. |
| Operator | Config | As an academy: issues a **vouch** for their staff — itself an attestation with the academy's own reputation staked on it. Meaningful precisely because it is revocable and attributable. |
| Fan | Read-only | Sees the badges and the human-readable claims on the public page; the full evidence chain (who attested, when) may require an account. `[PENDING — Step 5]` on where that line sits. |

## Behavior

The credential block aggregates evidence of three distinct kinds, which must be visually distinct
because their trust properties are not comparable:

| Kind | Source | Trust property |
|---|---|---|
| **Verified credits** | Domain 02 — counter-attested by session participants | Strongest. Un-fakeable without collusion. Unique to this platform |
| **Safeguarding vetting** | Safeguarding cross-cut — DBS / state background check | Binary, current-or-expired, legally mandated. Not a ranking signal — a **gate** |
| **Qualifications** | Teacher-submitted, platform- or issuer-verified — degrees, diplomas, ABRSM grades | Middling. Verifiable in principle, expensive in practice |

Conflating these is the failure mode. A DBS check is not a credibility signal (it says nothing about
teaching), and a degree is not a credit. The block must resist collapsing into one "trust score",
which is the obvious and wrong design (DT-03).

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5]`: the mechanics of qualification verification. Checking an ABRSM
Grade 8 or a conservatoire degree against an issuer is an integration problem per-issuer, and the
honest position is that we do not yet know which issuers are checkable, at what cost, or whether the
answer is "manual review".

### Happy Path

1. Musician has verified credits in domain 02 from sessions they played — earned as a byproduct of work, not entered for this purpose
2. They enable their teaching facet (06.02.01)
3. The credential block populates **automatically** from existing credits — nothing to write
4. They add a DBS check and a diploma for verification
5. Vetting clears; the under-18 gate opens; the qualification is marked verified or self-asserted per what could be checked
6. A student reads "played bass on {record} — confirmed by {producer}" and can click through to the evidence

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Excellent teacher, zero verified credits | Block shows qualifications and academy vouches; **no penalty, no empty-state shaming** | Student: an honest, different evidence mix. Teacher: never "your profile is incomplete" for lacking credits they were never going to have (DT-02) |
| Teacher inflates a credit | The credit graph is counter-attested in domain 02 — an unconfirmed claim never becomes a verified credit. The attack surface is **collusion**, not assertion | Student: unconfirmed claims are visibly unconfirmed. Collusion detection is domain 02's problem, not solved here |
| DBS check expires | Under-18 gate closes; lessons with minors cannot start (06.01.04 fails closed); the block shows expired, not verified | Teacher: escalating renewal warnings well before expiry, because expiry cancels their lessons |
| Credit is disputed in domain 02 | Renders as disputed here — this feature never overrides its source | Student: the dispute is visible, not hidden |
| Teacher hides a credit | Permitted — surfacing is the teacher's choice; a session for an artist they'd rather not advertise is theirs to omit | Student: sees no gap; absence is not evidence |
| Credits are impressive but irrelevant (a legendary session drummer teaching beginner ukulele) | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — genuinely unresolved. Relevance-weighting credits against the instrument taught is where the wedge gets subtle and possibly self-defeating | `[PENDING]` |
| Academy vouch, then the teacher leaves badly | Vouch is revocable; revocation is itself a signal | `[PENDING — Step 5]` on whether revocation is visible or silent |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Block fetch | Skeleton evidence rows |
| Empty | No evidence of any kind | Teacher: "Add a qualification or a check". Student: no block at all — **not** an empty box that reads as an absence of trust |
| Populated | Evidence present | Three visually distinct groups: credits, vetting, qualifications |
| Error | Domain 02 unreachable | Vetting and qualifications still render; credits show "temporarily unavailable" — the safeguarding gate must never depend on the credit service being up |
| Partial | Some verified, some pending | Pending items labelled as pending, never as verified |
| Expired | Vetting lapsed | Prominent to the teacher (their lessons are about to fail), honest to the student |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Verified credits are a materially better teacher-credibility signal than a bio, and this is the domain's defensible advantage | Domain rationale: "The platform's unfair advantage is credentialing: verified credits beat a bio and a headshot decisively"; D-18 thesis | ✅ CONFIRMED — and structurally, this is the clearest instance of the thesis's flywheel anywhere on the map. Provenance captured in domain 02 (the wedge) directly monetizes in domain 06 (a consolidation surface). The credit was earned as a byproduct of a session; it is spent, years later, winning a student. **The teacher did no work to produce this advantage**, which is exactly what makes it un-copyable: a competitor cannot retroactively manufacture attestations from rooms it wasn't in. |
| DT-02 | Therefore credits should be the dominant ranking signal in teacher discovery | Follows naturally from DT-01 — if credits are the advantage, rank on them | ❌ REJECTED, and this is the most important rejection in the sub-domain. **Playing ability and teaching ability are weakly correlated, and every working musician knows it.** The best teacher of a 7-year-old beginner is often a patient, unglamorous professional teacher with no discography; the most-credited session player may be a terrible teacher. Ranking on credits would systematically demote the domain's best supply, insult the teachers who make it work, and mislead the buyer. Credits are **one evidence source, presented honestly** — not the ranking function. The wedge's job here is to make claims checkable, not to decide who's good. |
| DT-03 | The three evidence kinds should collapse into a single trust score for comparability | Obvious product instinct; every marketplace does it; students want to compare | ❌ REJECTED — it would average a **legal gate** (DBS) with a **credibility signal** (credits) with a **qualification**. That produces a number where a teacher could offset expired child-protection vetting with an impressive discography, which is both absurd and dangerous. Vetting is binary and is a gate on booking, not a contributor to a score. Recording this because "add a trust score" is the single most likely future request against this feature. |
| DT-04 | This feature also fixes the *reverse* problem: the platform can tell a student whether a teacher is still an active professional | Reasoning from the credit graph's shape — credits are timestamped, so they show recency, not just volume | ✅ CONFIRMED, with a caution. "Still gigging and recording" is a real signal students value and no competitor can produce. But it cuts against DT-02's segment: a teacher who stopped playing to teach full-time is often the *better* teacher, and a recency signal quietly punishes exactly that. Surfaced as a fact ("last credited session: 2019"), never as a demerit. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The credential block is **not authorable by the teacher** | Its un-authorability is its value. A teacher may submit for verification and choose what to surface; they may never write the block. |
| D-02 | The three evidence kinds stay **visually and semantically distinct**; no composite trust score | Per DT-03 — a score lets a discography offset expired child-protection vetting. |
| D-03 | Absence of credits is **never** rendered as an absence of trust | Per DT-02 — the domain's best supply often has no discography. An empty-state nudge here would insult and demote the teachers who make the domain work. |
| D-04 | This feature is a **read-only consumer** of domain 02; disputes and collusion are domain 02's problem | Prevents two sources of truth for a credit, which would be catastrophic for the platform's most valuable asset. |
