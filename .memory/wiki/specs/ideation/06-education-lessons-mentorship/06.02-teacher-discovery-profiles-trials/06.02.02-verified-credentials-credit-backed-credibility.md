# Feature: Verified Credentials & Credit-Backed Credibility

> **Number**: 06.02.02
> **Parent**: [Teacher Discovery, Profiles & Trials](./06.02-teacher-discovery-profiles-trials-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

**The domain's unfair advantage, isolated into one feature.** Every teacher marketplace in existence
ranks self-asserted claims: "toured internationally", "played with major artists", "20 years'
experience". None can check any of it. WeJammin can — a teacher's verified credits from domain 02 are
counter-attested by the people who were in the room, which makes "played on this record" a *fact* on
a teacher page rather than a boast. This feature is the evidence block: what is proven, by whom, and
how a student can check it.

Its defining property is that **the teacher cannot write it**. That is not a limitation; it is the
entire source of its value.

**The block's contract with its reader** — the sentence every decision below is accountable to:

> *Everything in this block is a dated statement of fact that someone other than the teacher is
> accountable for. Nothing in it is an opinion, a recommendation, a score, or a safety assurance.*

Three of this file's findings (DT-06, DT-07, DT-16) are cases where an obvious design silently
breaches that contract while appearing to honour it. That is the characteristic failure mode of this
feature: not fabrication, but **quiet promotion** — evidence of one thing rendered in a grammar that
asserts another.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Read-only | **As Teacher**: cannot author or edit the block. Can (a) *submit* things for verification — a qualification, a DBS/background check; (b) *suppress* an individual already-verified credit from the teaching facet (D-05), which removes it from this page only and never from their discography; (c) see a private "what's thin" view listing evidence they could add. Cannot reword, caption, reorder, or annotate any item (DT-09). **As Student**: reads the block; each credit renders as a claim with its counter-attestation scope stated ("confirmed by an independent party who was in the session"); signed in, may open the evidence chain to see *who* confirmed *what* and *when*. |
| Producer | Read-only | The **counter-attester**. Per `meta/personas.md`, the Producer is a trust broker whose "attestation is worth more than a self-claim". This feature renders the consequence. **Their name appears only inside the account-gated evidence chain, never on the public page** (DT-06): they attested a *contribution*, not a teacher, and a public byline on a stranger's tuition page converts their attestation into a recommendation they never gave. A Fan who books becomes a Musician (Student) and the chain opens. |

## Behavior

The block aggregates evidence of **four** kinds, which must stay visually and semantically distinct
because their trust properties are not comparable. Conflating them is the failure mode (DT-03).

| Kind | Source | Who is accountable | Trust property |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Verified credits** | Domain 02, rung ≥ 5 only (D-06) | An independent co-present party | Strongest. Un-fakeable without collusion. Unique to this platform |
| **Safeguarding vetting** | Safeguarding cross-cut — DBS / state background check | A statutory body, **as of one date** | Binary, dated, jurisdiction-bound, legally mandated. Not a credibility signal — a **gate** |
| **Qualifications** | Teacher-submitted; issuer-checked, document-reviewed, or self-stated (three distinct labels) | The issuer, the platform's reviewer, or nobody | Middling, and only when issuer-checked. Verifiable in principle, expensive in practice |
| **Academy vouch** | An Operator with an active roster relationship | The academy, by name | An **opinion**, not a fact — the only one in the block. Meaningful because it is attributable and revocable |

A DBS check says nothing about teaching. A degree is not a credit. A vouch is somebody's opinion. The
block must resist collapsing into one "trust score", which is the obvious and wrong design (DT-03) —
and must equally resist collapsing into one *badge*, which is a trust score with one bit (DT-16).

### Only rung ≥ 5 is evidence (D-06)

Domain 02 grades every credit on a six-rung evidence ladder (02.04.02). The teaching page surfaces
**rung 5 (Confirmed) and rung 6 (Captured and confirmed)** as evidence, and nothing below:

| Rung | 02.04.02's meaning | Surfaces in this block? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Imported | A stranger typed it into a catalog | No |
| 2 Self-asserted | The teacher said so | No — this block exists to not do that |
| 3 Asserted by session owner | One interested party said so, unconfirmed | No |
| 4 Session-logged | Platform watched the session; the roll is still one party's | No — "capture evidences the **session**; it does not evidence the **contribution**" (02.04.02) |
| 5 Confirmed | ≥1 independent co-present party confirmed the contribution | **Yes** |
| 6 Captured and confirmed | Rung 4 **and** independent confirmation with lag ≤ 7 days | **Yes** |

Rungs 1–4 are not hidden — they live on the person's discography (02.01.02), honestly labelled, one
click away, and the block always links there when a discography exists (D-07). They are simply not
*evidence*, and the block's contract is that everything inside it is. Rung 4 is the closest call and
still fails: the platform observed that a session happened, not that this person played bass on it.

The block never renders rung numbers, tier labels, or domain 02's internal score — the score "renders
nowhere" by 02.04.02's own rule, and a rung label is professional vocabulary a parent has no map for.
Each surfaced credit renders as a sentence naming what was confirmed and by what kind of party.

### The rung is invariant to ring detection (D-14)

Domain 02 grades collusion risk on a **continuous internal score** that is a separate axis from the
rung (02.04.02 D-06). Ring detection (02.04.04) moves that score and **never writes the rung** —
02.04.02's score-input table has always filed the demotion multiplier inside the score with the
annotation "Detection never writes the rung", and 02.04.02 D-19/D-20 with 02.04.04 D-08/D-09 now
state it as the rule for the whole tree: every rung gate is a *kind* of evidence, a negative
multiplier removes no evidence of any kind, and every rung-gated consumer reads the same
detection-invariant rung. This block is one of those consumers and D-04 makes it a read-only one, so
it reads that rung and gains no detection-aware variant of its own. Two consequences:

**A demoted credit does not leave the block.** It was never the rung that moved. Retraction
(02.04.03), record deletion and record privacy still remove credits, because those are changes to
the evidence itself. A suspicion is not.

**The accepted cost, stated plainly**: a fully corroborated fiction — a credit manufactured by a
collusion ring that domain 02's detector has flagged — keeps its place in this block's rung ≥ 5
evidence, on a minors-facing paid-teaching page, **indefinitely and invisibly**. The platform's
defence against it is real, but it operates entirely in domain 02's ranking, search and dispute
weighting. It never operates on this page, and nothing here signals it.

This is the ratified answer, not an oversight, and the reasons live upstream. A rung-moving detector
would need a rung-side cut value that exists nowhere (02.04.04 Q-04 defers only the score-side cut),
would turn the kind-gated rung into a threshold on a number, and would convert the public label into
a **probe oracle** — the adversary attests, watches the label move, and learns the detector's shape
(02.04.02 D-12/DT-14; 02.04.04 D-02). It would also land maximum severity on the false positive
02.04.04 DT-02 calls dominant: a real tight-knit scene is *nearly identical* to a ring, "the detector
cannot be confidently right", and what a rung-moving version buys here is a wrongly-suspected honest
teacher silently losing the evidence block that wins them students, with no notice and no appeal. If
the accepted cost above is later judged unacceptable, the remedy named upstream is a **per-consumer
contract** — this feature declaring its own detection-aware gate under 02.04.04 D-07's already-
ratified shape — never a second rung and never a second derived value. This feature needs no new
logic to hold the rule today.

### Relevance is grouping, never weighting (D-09, resolves Q-01)

A legendary session drummer teaching beginner ukulele is the sub-domain's sharpest display problem.
Credits are grouped by instrument/role using domain 02's taxonomy (02.06):

- Groups are ordered: **instruments the teacher teaches first**, then everything else, each headed by
  the instrument name.
- **Empty groups do not render.** A ukulele teacher with no ukulele credits sees and shows no ukulele
  group, and never a "Ukulele: 0" — D-03 forbids rendering absence as an absence of trust.
- Nothing is filtered out, weighted, hidden, or scored. The drummer's 40 confirmed drum credits render
  under "Drums". The reader draws their own inference.

Relevance is expressed as **structure and labelling**, not as a function. This is the only resolution
consistent with DT-02 (credits must not rank) and D-03 (absence is not a demerit) simultaneously: a
weighting function would have to encode an opinion about whether drum credits predict ukulele teaching,
and the platform does not have one and should not pretend to.

### Qualifications have three labels, and only one of them is "verified" (D-10)

The **mechanics** of checking an issuer are an integration problem per issuer and belong to
`/create-prd` (Q-02). The **behavior** does not, and is specified here:

| Label | Established by | What it asserts | Renders as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Issuer-checked** | Confirmed directly against the awarding body's record | The body says this person holds this award | "ABRSM Grade 8 Piano — confirmed with ABRSM, 4 Feb 2026" |
| **Document reviewed** | A human read a submitted document; it exists, is legible, and names this identity | A document exists. **Not** that it is genuine | "Diploma, {institution} — document reviewed, 4 Feb 2026" |
| **Self-stated** | The teacher typed it | Nothing beyond the teacher's word | "Diploma, {institution} — stated by the teacher" |

A reviewed document is **not** a verified qualification (DT-15). Review confirms a piece of paper, not
an award; a reviewer cannot detect a competent forgery and the platform must not imply they can. Only
an issuer check yields "confirmed". Where no issuer is checkable, the honest ceiling is "document
reviewed", and where nothing is submitted, "self-stated" — which is permitted and rendered plainly.

Labels never read as doubt, borrowing 02.04.02's rule directly: "self-stated" means *we did not check
this*, not *we disbelieve this*. `[Not verified]` is a defamatory framing of the same fact.

**Identity match is a hard gate**: the name on a submitted qualification must match the domain-01 legal
name or an alias in that identity's recorded alias history (Canonical Data cross-cut). A mismatch is
rejected with the reason stated; it is not silently accepted or silently dropped.

### Vetting renders as a dated statement, never a tick (D-11, DT-07)

The vetting item states: **the check performed, its jurisdiction, its date, and one plain sentence of
what it does and does not mean.** For example:

> *Enhanced DBS check (England & Wales) — 12 March 2026. This confirms no relevant convictions were
> recorded against this person on that date. It is not an assessment of their conduct, and it is not a
> WeJammin endorsement.*

No green tick. No shield. No word "safe". The reasoning is DT-07: a DBS certificate is a **snapshot**,
not a status, and a tick is status grammar.

Because a UK DBS certificate carries **no legal expiry**, "expiry" here is a platform-set renewal
policy, not a legal fact:

- **Renewal interval**: proposed 3 years from the certificate date (common sector practice); ratified at
  `/create-prd-security`.
- **Continuous status** where the teacher subscribes to the UK DBS Update Service, which permits
  ongoing status checks and is the only mechanism that converts the snapshot into a status. Opt-in and
  UK-only.
- **Renewal warnings** at 90, 30 and 7 days before the renewal date, and on the day. The 90-day lead is
  sized to enhanced-check processing time (commonly 2–8 weeks, longer at peaks) — a shorter lead
  guarantees a gap in which the teacher's booked lessons fail closed through no fault of theirs.

### The vouch

An academy vouch requires an **active roster relationship** (06.10) and **independence** (DT-13): a
vouch from an organisation the teacher controls is self-vouching laundered through an entity and does
not render, applying 02.04.02 DT-17's principle ("independence is per human, not per role") to the
Operator lens. The vouch is attributed by name, dated, and revocable. Revocation is silent (D-08).

### Happy Path

1. Musician has confirmed credits in domain 02 from sessions they actually played — earned as a
   byproduct of work years ago, never entered for this purpose. They did nothing to produce this
   advantage; that is what makes it un-copyable (DT-01).
2. They enable their teaching facet (06.02.01).
3. The block **populates automatically** — every rung ≥ 5 credit, grouped by instrument, taught
   instruments first. The teacher writes nothing.
4. The system tells them, unprompted, what it just published: "12 confirmed credits are now on your
   teaching page." This is the wedge visibly paying them (CX-02).
5. Any credit on an explicit-flagged record is held back from the teaching facet pending the teacher's
   opt-in (D-12) and listed in their private view with the reason.
6. Teacher submits an enhanced DBS certificate and a diploma for verification.
7. Vetting clears → the under-18 age-range field on 06.02.01 unlocks, the minor search pool opens
   (06.02.03 D-03), and the vetting statement renders with its date and jurisdiction.
8. The diploma's issuer is checkable → renders "confirmed with {issuer}, {date}". Had it not been, it
   would render "document reviewed" or "self-stated" — visibly, without apology.
9. A parent reads: "Played bass on {record} — confirmed by an independent party who was in the
   session", grouped under Bass, above the teacher's own words about why they teach.
10. Signed in, they open the evidence chain on that credit and see who confirmed it, when, and that
    what was confirmed is a contribution — not a recommendation of this person as a teacher (DT-06).
11. They book a trial (06.02.04) — having checked a claim rather than trusted one.

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Block fetch | Skeleton evidence rows **including each row's label slot** — the label never pops in after the claim, or the page asserts an unlabelled credit for a frame (02.04.02's Loading rule) |
| Empty | No evidence of any kind | Student: **no block at all** — not an empty box, which reads as an absence of trust (D-03). Teacher: private "Add a qualification or a check" |
| Populated | Evidence present | Up to four visually distinct groups: credits (grouped by instrument, taught first), vetting statement, qualifications with per-item labels, vouch attributed by name |
| Partial | Some kinds present, others absent or pending | Only what exists renders. Pending items render **nowhere** (pending is not a weak yes); absent kinds leave no placeholder and no gap |
| Credits-unavailable | Domain 02 unreachable, or rung not derivable | Vetting, qualifications and vouch render normally. Credits: "temporarily unavailable" — never cached, never defaulted, and distinct from having none |
| Vetting-unknown | Safeguarding cross-cut unreachable | No vetting item; the under-18 gate treats it as absent and fails closed. Never renders as cleared |
| Lapsed | Renewal interval passed, or Update Service reports a change | Teacher: prominent — their booked lessons die with it. Student: the date and its lapse, stated plainly |
| Error | The block itself cannot render | The profile (06.02.01) renders without it. **A block that cannot load must never block the page** — degrading to profile-only is correct; blocking publication is not (CX-02) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Verified credits are a materially better teacher-credibility signal than a bio, and this is the domain's defensible advantage | Domain rationale: "The platform's unfair advantage is credentialing: verified credits beat a bio and a headshot decisively"; D-18 thesis | ✅ CONFIRMED — and structurally, this is the clearest instance of the thesis's flywheel anywhere on the map. Provenance captured in domain 02 (the wedge) directly monetizes in domain 06 (a consolidation surface). The credit was earned as a byproduct of a session; it is spent, years later, winning a student. **The teacher did no work to produce this advantage**, which is exactly what makes it un-copyable: a competitor cannot retroactively manufacture attestations from rooms it wasn't in. |
| DT-02 | Therefore credits should be the dominant ranking signal in teacher discovery | Follows naturally from DT-01 — if credits are the advantage, rank on them | ❌ REJECTED, and this is the most important rejection in the sub-domain. **Playing ability and teaching ability are weakly correlated, and every working musician knows it.** The best teacher of a 7-year-old beginner is often a patient, unglamorous professional teacher with no discography; the most-credited session player may be a terrible teacher. Ranking on credits would systematically demote the domain's best supply, insult the teachers who make it work, and mislead the buyer. Credits are **one evidence source, presented honestly** — not the ranking function. The wedge's job here is to make claims checkable, not to decide who's good. |
| DT-03 | The three evidence kinds should collapse into a single trust score for comparability | Obvious product instinct; every marketplace does it; students want to compare | ❌ REJECTED — it would average a **legal gate** (DBS) with a **credibility signal** (credits) with a **qualification**. That produces a number where a teacher could offset expired child-protection vetting with an impressive discography, which is both absurd and dangerous. Vetting is binary and is a gate on booking, not a contributor to a score. Recording this because "add a trust score" is the single most likely future request against this feature. |
| DT-04 | This feature also fixes the *reverse* problem: the platform can tell a student whether a teacher is still an active professional | Reasoning from the credit graph's shape — credits are timestamped, so they show recency, not just volume | ✅ CONFIRMED, with a caution. "Still gigging and recording" is a real signal students value and no competitor can produce. But it cuts against DT-02's segment: a teacher who stopped playing to teach full-time is often the *better* teacher, and a recency signal quietly punishes exactly that. Surfaced as a fact ("last credited session: 2019"), never as a demerit. |
| DT-05 | The block should surface the teacher's credits — i.e. their discography, as domain 02 holds it | The naive read of "credits back the teacher", and what every "import your credits" integration does | ❌ REJECTED — **the block is not a discography.** Domain 02 holds credits at six evidence rungs (02.04.02), and rungs 1–4 are things the teacher or one interested party asserted. Surfacing them would put self-assertion back into the one surface whose entire proposition is that self-assertion cannot reach it — the feature would fail at exactly the thing it exists to do, while looking richer. Only rung ≥ 5 (independent co-present confirmation) qualifies. Rung 4 is the closest call and still fails on 02.04.02's own words: "capture evidences the session; it does not evidence the contribution." The discography remains one click away, complete and honestly labelled, so nothing is concealed — the distinction is between a **record** (all rungs) and **evidence** (rung ≥ 5), and this feature is the second. |
| DT-06 | A domain-02 attestation can be rendered on a teaching page with its meaning intact | Implicit in the original design — the credit is verified, so show it and show who verified it | ❌ REJECTED as stated — **the context shift changes the claim.** A producer confirmed, in domain 02, that *Sarah played bass on this record*. Rendered with their name on Sarah's minors-facing tuition page beside a booking button, that same attestation is read by a parent as *this producer vouches for Sarah as a teacher* — which is not what they attested, not what they consented to, and not something they were asked. This is context collapse, and it manufactures a character reference from a factual confirmation without anyone lying. Two consequences: (1) the public page renders the attestation's **scope**, never the attester's name as a byline — "confirmed by an independent party who was in the session"; (2) the named evidence chain sits behind an account gate, which domain 02's publication rule ("the assertion and its rung are publishable; the evidence is not") independently requires. Two arguments — third-party consent scope, and the source domain's own rule — arriving at the same gate. This also substantially answers Q-03. |
| DT-07 | Vetting can be rendered as a verified badge alongside the other evidence | Universal marketplace convention — every platform with background checks ships a green tick | ❌ REJECTED, and this is the most dangerous design in the file. Three compounding errors in one tick. **(a) A tick is status grammar; the fact is a snapshot.** A UK DBS certificate has no legal expiry and reports convictions *as of its issue date* — a teacher checked in 2026 and convicted in 2027 is still "ticked", and the platform cannot know. Only the DBS Update Service converts the snapshot into a status, and it is opt-in and UK-only. **(b) A tick asserts safety; the check does not.** A DBS check says "no relevant convictions were recorded on this date". It does not say this person is safe with children. Every safeguarding professional knows this; no parent does. **(c) Co-location defeats the visual separation DT-03 relies on.** DT-03 rejects the composite *score* — but a tick sitting beside credits and a vouch produces a composite in the reader's head anyway, and you cannot fix a semantic conflation with a visual separator when the reader is a non-expert parent under time pressure with a booking button in view. Resolution: a **dated statement naming the check, its jurisdiction and its date, with one plain sentence of what it does and does not mean.** No tick, no shield, no "safe". The date *is* the honesty — it renders a stale fact as a dated fact rather than as a current status. |
| DT-08 | The block's evidence kinds cover what a music teacher's credibility actually rests on | Implicit in the file's original three-kind model | ❌ REJECTED — **the block has no evidence of teaching ability at all, and a domain expert would name the gap in one breath.** In real music education, a teacher's reputation is built almost entirely on *their students' outcomes*: "three of my students got into the Royal Academy", "my student passed Grade 8 with distinction". That is the currency, and it is what a teacher would expect to put on their page. It is also the only thing that answers DT-02's objection — credits evidence *playing*; student outcomes evidence *teaching* — and the platform can verify it: exam entries and results are on-platform (06.09), and the domain index's Q-08 already concludes an exam grade is "the only honest external measure" the domain has. Nobody connected that finding to teacher credibility. **But it cannot simply be added**, for three independent reasons: the students are mostly children, and a child's exam result is not their teacher's marketing asset; publishing it needs the student's or guardian's consent, freely refusable; and small numbers identify — a teacher with 2 students publishing "100% pass rate" has published a specific child's result. If it is built, the only defensible shape is **aggregate, consented, and k-anonymous** (proposed floor: 5 consenting students; ratified at `/create-prd-security`) — "12 students entered ABRSM exams in 3 years; 11 passed", never a name, never an individual result. Whether it is built at all is a scope decision the owner makes: Q-04. |
| DT-09 | The teacher should be able to caption each credit to explain its pedagogical relevance | A real and sympathetic need — "I played bass on this, and it's why I teach groove the way I do" answers DT-02's non-sequitur directly, in the teacher's own voice | ❌ REJECTED **inside the block**, ✅ ALLOWED **beside it**. The need is legitimate; the location is fatal. A caption field is teacher-authored text inside the one block whose value is that teacher-authored text cannot reach it — and it is a laundering wedge: "I played bass on this [and I was the musical director]" places an unverified claim inside a container the reader has been taught to trust. Since a caption reads as verified precisely because of where it sits, it is the D-01 breach that looks most like a feature. Resolution: the teacher says all of this in 06.02.01's **self-authored** bucket, where their voice belongs and is labelled as their voice, rendered adjacent to the block. Same words, honest frame, and it uses the CX-02 seam that already exists. This is why the sweep's bundled "Profile *with* Verified Credentials" candidate had to be split (index D-01) — pressure to re-merge them will keep arriving disguised as small features. |
| DT-10 | Vetting is a comparable signal across teachers | Implicit in rendering it as one item in one block for every teacher | ❌ REJECTED — **vetting is not portable across jurisdictions, and remote lessons cross jurisdictions by default.** A UK enhanced DBS, a US state background check and a German *Führungszeugnis* have different scopes, different disclosure rules and different meanings; some jurisdictions have no equivalent scheme at all. Rendering them all as "background checked" flattens incomparable facts into a false equivalence — precisely DT-03's collapse, arriving through geography instead of scoring. And the domain makes this the normal case, not the exotic one: remote lessons are a core mode (06.02.01), so a parent in London routinely evaluates a teacher in another country. Resolution: **the check and its jurisdiction are always named**; there is no generic vetting item. The harder half is not this feature's to resolve — whether a teacher in a jurisdiction with no scheme may teach minors remotely at all is a gate the Safeguarding cross-cut sets, and it is unresolved (Q-06). This feature can only refuse to disguise it. |
| DT-11 | Silent vouch revocation (D-08) is safe because it creates no negative signal | Follows from the anti-weaponisation argument, and 02.04.03's precedent | ✅ CONFIRMED **as a rule**, ❌ REJECTED **as complete** — silence is right for the common case and creates a hole in the dangerous one. If an academy's only tool for expressing "we no longer want this person teaching here" is revoking a vouch, then an academy revoking because of a **safeguarding concern** expresses that concern as a badge quietly disappearing — and, worse, plausibly believes it has discharged its duty by doing so. The mechanism becomes a trap for the conscientious operator. This is the failure mode nobody designs for: not a missing feature, but a **correct feature absorbing a signal it cannot carry**. Resolution: revocation forks at the moment of action — "withdrawing your endorsement" stays here and stays silent; "raising a safeguarding concern" leaves this feature entirely for the Safeguarding report flow, with its own escalation and its own ability to suspend. Conflating them is DT-03's collapse once more (a gate expressed as a credibility signal), and the fork must be unskippable. |
| DT-12 | Auto-population from domain 02 is unambiguously good — it is the flywheel | DT-01, and CX-02's "the teacher does nothing — that is the flywheel" | ✅ CONFIRMED **with one carve-out that only appears at the 02 × 06 × Safeguarding intersection.** Auto-population means a teacher's credits publish themselves onto a page whose primary reader is a parent choosing a teacher for a 9-year-old (06.02.01 DT-03). Some records are explicit — adult titles, adult artwork — and the industry has flagged them as such for forty years (the PA marker; carried in the standard metadata domain 02 already emits at 02.08). So the default behaviour of the feature's best property is to publish adult content onto a children's-lessons page, chosen by nobody. The flywheel is preserved for the ~95% case; the teacher is asked once about the rest. |
| DT-13 | An academy vouch is independent evidence by construction, because it comes from a different entity | The org/person distinction the platform already models (domain 01) | ❌ REJECTED — **entity boundaries are not independence boundaries.** A sole-member academy vouching for its only teacher is self-vouching laundered through an organisation, and one-teacher "academies" are the common case in this market, not an edge case. Domain 02 already settled the principle in a different vocabulary: 02.04.02 DT-17, "independence is per human, not per role — a Band entity's attestation inherits its members' identities." The same rule must apply to the Operator lens, or the block's one opinion-bearing item is trivially self-issued. Resolution: a vouch from an organisation the teacher controls does not render. This is the multi-hyphenate hazard in Operator clothing, and it is worth noting that the platform's central structural fact keeps generating this exact class of bug — one human wearing two hats to corroborate themselves. |
| DT-14 | Domain 02's retraction weaponisation model covers this feature's exposure | 02.04.03 models retraction-as-leverage thoroughly and this feature is a read-only consumer | ❌ REJECTED — **it covers the mechanism and misses a motive.** 02.04.03 models retraction as *payment* leverage in a producer↔musician relationship, citing `personas.md`'s "withholds stems as leverage in a payment dispute". Domain 06 introduces a relationship domain 02 never saw: **teacher↔teacher competition for the same students in the same city**. A rival who once attested a competitor's credit now holds a button that removes evidence from that competitor's income-generating page, with no payment dispute anywhere in sight. The retraction is indistinguishable from an honest one at the single-instance level (02.04.03's own admission), and 02.04.04's pattern detection is tuned for a payment-shaped signature. Not resolvable here — this feature only observes the credit leave the block — but it is a **finding that must travel back to domain 02**, and it is exactly the kind of thing a read-only consumer is uniquely placed to notice: the consumer knows the motive, the source domain knows the mechanism. Escalated in Cross-Cut Notes and Q-07. |
| DT-15 | A human reviewing a submitted document verifies the qualification | The natural fallback when no issuer integration exists, and what "manual review" is assumed to mean | ❌ REJECTED — **review verifies a document, not an award.** A reviewer confirms that a piece of paper exists, is legible, and names this identity. They cannot detect a competent forgery, they have no access to the issuer's record, and a platform that renders their output as "verified" has asserted something nobody checked — in a market where the word carries legal weight (02.04.02 Q-03 flags exactly this). This is an honesty ceiling, not a UI nicety: it is what makes Q-02's answer safe to defer — whichever issuers turn out to be checkable, the unchecked ones degrade to a weaker true claim rather than to a stronger false one. |
| DT-16 | If not a score (DT-03), the block could carry a summary badge — "Verified Teacher" — or a headline credit count | The pragmatic middle ground a product manager reaches for the moment DT-03 lands; it feels like a compromise rather than a reversal | ❌ REJECTED — both are DT-03 wearing a costume. **A badge is a trust score with one bit**: it must decide what it includes, and every answer is wrong — include vetting and a discography can offset child-protection lapse (DT-03's exact absurdity, now hidden inside a word); exclude vetting and "Verified Teacher" means something no reader will guess. **A count is a trust score with one number**: "40 confirmed credits" re-ranks teachers in the reader's head on precisely the axis DT-02 forbids, rewards volume (a session player accumulates counts; a dedicated teacher never will), and re-creates the folk score domain 02 deliberately refuses to render — 02.04.02 keeps its internal score hidden for this reason, and a count on the teaching page reconstructs one from the outside. Show the credits; never the tally, never the summary. Logged because DT-03's rejection does not survive on its own — it survives only if its cheaper disguises are rejected by name, and these two are what will actually be proposed. |
