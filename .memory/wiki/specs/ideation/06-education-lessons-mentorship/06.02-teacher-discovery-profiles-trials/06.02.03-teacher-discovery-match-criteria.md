# Feature: Teacher Discovery & Match Criteria

> **Number**: 06.02.03
> **Parent**: [Teacher Discovery, Profiles & Trials](./06.02-teacher-discovery-profiles-trials-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

How a student gets from "I want to learn bass" to a shortlist of three teachers worth a trial. The
generic search machinery is consumed from the `Search & Discovery` cross-cut; what this feature owns
is the part that is irreducibly about music teaching — **what the criteria are**, and **how they are
weighted**. Those are product decisions with real consequences: get the weights wrong and the domain
promotes glamorous session players over the patient beginner-teachers who are its actual supply.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Student**: filters on instrument, level, age range, mode (remote / in-person radius), price, availability that fits their week, language, exam board, genre. Sees why each result was matched, and books a trial from the result. **As Teacher**: tunes what they are matched for (levels, ages, radius, whether they take beginners), sees how they rank and — more usefully — why they don't. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. A Producer looking for a player uses Opportunities (04) or Services (05); teacher search is a different intent with different criteria and must not be conflated — a Producer hiring the best bassist and a parent finding a patient beginner-teacher want opposite results from the same pool of people. |
| Operator | Read-only | As an academy: sees how their teachers rank and where they lose. Cannot manipulate ranking (paid placement is Q-02 on the parent index, unresolved and values-laden in a minors market). |
| Fan | Read-only | Public search is open and indexable — "guitar teacher near me" with no account. Acting on a result makes them a Musician (Student). |

## Behavior

The criteria fall into three groups with different logic:

- **Hard filters** — instrument, mode, age range, level. A teacher who doesn't take beginners is not a "lower-ranked" result for a beginner; they are **not a result**. Getting this wrong wastes the student's trial.
- **Soft ranking** — availability fit, price, reviews, evidence, proximity, response rate
- **Gates** — safeguarding vetting for under-18 students (a gate, never a ranking factor — see 06.02.02 DT-03)

Two criteria are genuinely music-specific and absent from generic search:

1. **In-person radius is asymmetric.** The teacher's travel willingness, the student's travel willingness, and "at my studio" are three different radii, not one. A weekly lesson at 30 minutes' drive is sustainable; the same drive for a one-off is irrelevant. Distance tolerance is a function of recurrence — which no generic geo filter models.
2. **Level is not a scalar.** "Intermediate" means nothing across instruments and traditions. A grade-5 classical pianist and a self-taught rock pianist of equal ability filter differently, and the exam-board taxonomy (06.09) is the only rigorous handle available — and only for the traditions that have one.

### Happy Path

1. Student states intent: instrument, rough level, remote or in-person, budget, when they're free
2. System applies hard filters, ranks the remainder, and shows why each matched
3. Student compares 3–5 teachers on evidence, price and availability
4. Student books a trial (06.02.04) directly from the result — no intermediate enquiry step
5. Trial outcome feeds back into ranking quality

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| No teachers for that instrument in that city | Falls back to remote teachers rather than an empty page — the cold-start reality for anything but guitar and piano | Student: "No {instrument} teachers near {city} — these 6 teach remotely" |
| Nobody at all (cold start) | Waitlist/notify, and honesty about supply | Student: "We'll tell you when a {instrument} teacher joins near you" |
| Student is a minor | Results are restricted to vetted teachers **before ranking**, not filtered after | Student/guardian: the restriction is stated, not silent |
| Student wants a specific famous teacher | Direct navigation, no matching involved | Straight to 06.02.01 |
| Teacher matched but has no availability | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — genuinely unresolved. Demoting a fully-booked great teacher is right for the student and hostile to the teacher (their reward for being good is invisibility) | `[PENDING]` |
| Query is a genre, not an instrument ("I want to learn jazz") | Genre is a soft signal across instruments | Student: prompted for instrument, genre retained as a ranking input |
| Student's budget is below every local teacher | Shown honestly with remote alternatives, never an empty page implying they can't learn | Student: price reality + remote/group (06.05) alternatives |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Query submitted | Skeleton result cards |
| Empty | No matches after hard filters | Progressive fallback: remote → wider radius → waitlist. Never a bare "no results" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is the `Search & Discovery` cross-cut and should not be a feature | The cross-cut list explicitly serves 8+ domains with search, and duplicating it is exactly what the Node Classification Gate forbids | ❌ REJECTED, with a boundary. The cross-cut owns indexing, querying, faceting and relevance **machinery**. It cannot own **what the facets are** or **how they're weighted** — those are per-domain product decisions, and here they are load-bearing (which evidence counts, whether credits dominate, whether vetting gates or ranks). The machinery is consumed; the criteria and weights are Education's. Same seam the ratified map draws for every other domain that consumes this cross-cut. |
| DT-02 | Recurrence changes the geography, and no generic geo filter models it | Reasoning from the domain's own structure — a lesson is a **weekly standing commitment** (06.01.01 DT-01), not a transaction | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it is a real, music-specific criterion nobody listed. A 40-minute drive is fine for a one-off masterclass and unthinkable every Tuesday for a year, in the rain, with a cello. Distance tolerance is a function of recurrence and instrument portability. The `Geo` cross-cut supplies distance; **what distance means** is Education's, and it is different here than in any other domain that consumes that cross-cut. |
| DT-03 | The availability filter is the highest-value criterion and is being under-weighted by treating it as one facet among many | Reasoning from the parent persona: a working musician's student is often *also* a working person with a fixed weekly window | ✅ CONFIRMED — for a weekly commitment, "can you do Tuesdays at 5" is frequently a **hard filter in disguise**, not a preference. A brilliant teacher who cannot do the student's only free slot is not a match at any ranking. This inverts the usual marketplace assumption that price and quality dominate: in recurring tuition, **schedule fit is often first**, which also means 06.01.01's availability data quality directly determines this feature's usefulness. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Consumes **Search & Discovery** (global cross-cut) — indexing, querying, faceting, relevance machinery. This feature owns criteria and weights only (DT-01).
- Consumes **Geo, Location & Map Discovery** (global cross-cut) — distance. **What distance means here is recurrence-dependent and unlike every other consumer** (DT-02).
- Consumes **Safeguarding & Minor Protection** (global cross-cut) — the vetted-only pool for minors, applied before ranking.
- Consumes **Promoted Placement & Advertising** (global cross-cut) — *if* Q-02 on the parent index resolves to yes. Unresolved and values-laden in a minors-facing market.
- Consumes **Reviews, Ratings & Portable Reputation** (global cross-cut) — a ranking signal.
- Touches **06.02.01 Teacher & Tuition Profile** — supplies the structured fields filtered on.
- Touches **06.02.02 Verified Credentials** — supplies a signal that must **not** dominate (06.02.02 DT-02).
- Touches **06.01.01 Slot Booking** — availability fit is often a hard filter (DT-03), so stale availability data destroys this feature.
- Touches **[06.09 Exam Board Alignment](../06.09-exam-board-alignment.md)** — the only rigorous "level" taxonomy available, for the traditions that have one.
- Touches **[04 Opportunities & Casting](../../04-opportunities-casting/)** — deliberately **separate**: finding a player and finding a teacher are different intents over the same people.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Criteria and weights are **Education-owned**; the search machinery is cross-cut | Per DT-01 — the weights are the product decision, and here they decide which teachers the domain promotes. |
| D-02 | Level/age/instrument/mode are **hard filters**, not ranking inputs | A teacher who doesn't take beginners must not appear for a beginner at any rank. A wasted trial is expensive for both sides. |
| D-03 | Safeguarding vetting **gates** the pool for minors; it never ranks | Per 06.02.02 DT-03 — a gate that ranks is a gate that can be outweighed. |
| D-04 | Empty results always degrade progressively (remote → wider → waitlist) | Cold start is guaranteed outside guitar/piano/major cities; a bare "no results" page teaches the student the platform is empty. |
