# Feature: Teacher Discovery & Match Criteria

> **Number**: 06.02.03
> **Parent**: [Teacher Discovery, Profiles & Trials](./06.02-teacher-discovery-profiles-trials-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

How a student gets from "I want to learn bass" to a shortlist of three teachers worth a trial. The
generic search machinery is consumed from the `Search & Discovery` cross-cut (CX-M16); what this
feature owns is the part that is irreducibly about music teaching — **what the criteria are**, and
**how they are weighted**. Those are product decisions with real consequences: get the weights wrong
and the domain promotes glamorous session players over the patient beginner-teachers who are its
actual supply.

The feature's real job is narrower than "find the best teacher", and admitting that is what makes it
designable. **Teaching fit is not computable from anything the platform holds** — rapport between a
9-year-old and an adult is not a facet, and domain Q-08 records that the domain has no measure of
musicianship at all. So the ranking function does not pick a winner. It produces **3–5 candidates
worth 30 minutes**, and the trial (06.02.04) is the evaluation function (DT-06). Every weight below
is chosen to serve precision-at-3 for trial-worthiness, not ranking accuracy.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Student**: states an intent (instrument, level, student age band, mode, budget, weekly window, language, optionally exam board and genre) and receives a ranked shortlist of 3–5 bookable teachers, each with a plain-language reason it matched and a trial CTA that books without an enquiry step (CX-04). Adjusts any criterion and re-ranks; sees exactly what was relaxed when nothing matched; sees fully-booked high-match teachers in a separate, labelled partition rather than not at all (D-08). **As Teacher**: tunes what they are matched *for* — instruments, level ranges, age ranges accepted, modes, radius and travel willingness, languages, exam boards, whether they take absolute beginners, and which slots are series-claimable. Sees their **own controllable inputs** and where each is costing them (accept rate, median response time, window coverage, per-facet conversion, page completeness) and the published weights those inputs feed — but never a comparative leaderboard position and never another teacher's numbers (D-10). The same human does both: a bass teacher searching for a piano teacher is normal, and their own page is excluded from their own results (DT-12). |
| Producer | None | Not visible, and the absence is deliberate. A Producer looking for a player uses Opportunities (04); teacher search is a different intent over the same pool of people and must not be conflated — a Producer hiring the best bassist and a parent finding a patient beginner-teacher want *opposite* results, and 06.02.02 DT-02 requires those two rankings to disagree. One search with a mode switch would force one ranking function to serve two contradictory jobs (`06.02-...-cx.md#R-01`). A Producer who wants lessons for themselves is lensed as **Musician (Student)** from that moment, per domain D-02. |
| Operator | Read-only | As an academy: sees how each of their listed teachers ranks **per facet** (this teacher ranks well for Grade 1–3 piano and poorly for adult jazz), and which controllable input is costing them — the same numbers the teacher sees, for the staff they list. Cannot alter ranking, cannot buy position, cannot suppress a rival's result. Their genuine lever is operational: fix accept rates, publish availability, fill the window coverage gaps. Paid placement (CX-M25) is Q-01 — unresolved and values-laden in a minors-facing market. |
| Fan | Read-only | Public search is open and indexable with no account — "guitar teacher near me" is the domain's highest-volume acquisition query and its searcher is, by definition, not a user yet (06.02.01 DT-02). A signed-out searcher gets the full intake, the same hard filters (including the minor gate, which fires on the **declared** age band — DT-09) and the same ranked shortlist, with availability shown as **shape** ("Tue/Thu evenings") rather than an actionable slot grid, per 06.01.01's signed-out rule. Booking anything — including a trial — makes them a Musician (Student); that is the exact conversion boundary. |

## Behavior

The criteria fall into three groups with different logic. Conflating them is the failure mode.

- **Hard filters** — a miss means **not a result**, not a lower rank. A teacher who doesn't take
  beginners is not a demoted result for a beginner; they are absent. Getting this wrong spends the
  student's trial, which is the domain's scarcest resource.
- **Soft ranking** — applied only *within* the bookable pool, to order it.
- **Gates** — safeguarding vetting for under-18 students. A gate, never a ranking factor (06.02.02
  DT-03: a gate that contributes to a score is a gate a good discography can outweigh).

Two criteria are genuinely music-specific and absent from generic search:

1. **In-person radius is asymmetric, and distance tolerance is a function of recurrence.** The
   teacher's travel willingness, the student's travel willingness, and "at my studio" are three
   different radii, not one. A weekly lesson at 30 minutes' drive is sustainable; the same drive for
   a one-off is irrelevant (DT-02). The origin is not even fixed — for the school-age market it is a
   school at 16:00 and a home at 10:00 on Saturday (DT-08).
2. **Level is not a scalar.** "Intermediate" means nothing across instruments and traditions, and the
   person answering the question is the person least able to answer it (DT-04, DT-05).

### Intake — what is asked, and in what order

One question at a time; each answer narrows the next. All of it works signed-out.

| # | Field | Required | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instrument | Yes | Taxonomy-bound (CX-M17) | Free-text and genre queries route here first (DT-13) |
| 2 | Student age band | **Yes** | `under-11` · `11–17` · `18–24` · `25–64` · `65+` | Load-bearing twice: it is a hard filter against the teacher's declared accepted ranges *and* it fires the safeguarding gate signed-out (DT-09). Not optional, ever |
| 3 | Level | Yes | Proxy questions, not a self-rating (DT-05) | "What can you play right now?" · "Do you read music?" · "How long have you played?" · exam grade if the tradition has one (06.09) |
| 4 | Mode | Yes | `remote` · `teacher's room` · `student's home` · `academy` — multi-select | Drives whether S5 contributes at all |
| 5 | Weekly window | Yes | Weekday × time bands | DT-03 — the highest-weighted soft signal, and the one students under-report the softness of (DT-14) |
| 6 | Budget | Yes | Per-lesson range, viewer currency (CX-M20) | S6, weighted deliberately low (DT-10) |
| 7 | Language | Defaults to locale | Multi-select | Hard filter |
| 8 | Exam board | No | 06.09 taxonomy | High-intent when present; near-meaningless outside the graded-exam traditions (06.09) |
| 9 | Genre | No | Soft | Never a hard filter — a genre is not an instrument (DT-13) |

### Hard filters — pool membership

| Filter | Source | Semantics |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument | 06.02.01 structured fields; CX-M17 taxonomy | Exact match. Taxonomy-adjacent substitution (keyboard ≈ piano) happens **only** at relaxation step 9, and is labelled |
| Student age band | Intake (declared) vs teacher's declared accepted ranges | Miss = absent |
| Level | Intake, with **±1 band tolerance** (D-06) | Teacher's accepted level range must intersect `[stated−1, stated+1]` |
| Mode | Intake vs teacher's declared modes | A remote-only teacher never appears in an in-person-only search |
| Language | Intake vs teacher's declared teaching languages | |
| Bookability | 06.01.01 + 06.01.02 | A teacher with no live rate card or no published availability is **not a result**. 06.02.01 D-03 already blocks publishing without both; this is the search-side restatement — an unbookable page wastes the student's only click |
| **Vetting gate** | CX-M31 | If age band is `under-11` or `11–17`: **vetted-only pool, applied before ranking** (D-03). Not a filter the student can turn off, and not shown as a toggle |

### Soft ranking — the weights

> These are **platform defaults, owner-overridable at `/create-prd`**, stated as numbers because "credits
> should not dominate" is a sentiment and not implementable. The *ordering* is argued from the domain
> and is the load-bearing part; the exact integers are a starting position. Signals are scored 0–1 and
> multiplied by the weight; the total is 0–100 and is **published** (DT-10 sibling — see D-10).

| # | Signal | Weight | Range | Why this number |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Schedule fit** — does the teacher's open slot land inside the student's stated window | **30** | 15–40 | DT-03. For a weekly standing commitment the slot is the binding constraint. A brilliant teacher at the wrong hour is not a match at any rank, and this is the highest single weight because tuition inverts the usual marketplace assumption that price and quality dominate |
| S2 | **Reliability** — accept rate + median time-to-respond (06.01.01), no-show rate (06.01.03) | **20** | 10–30 | 06.01.01's only countermeasure to phantom availability ("a teacher who never accepts ranks down"). A declined request costs the student a week of a decision they are already procrastinating |
| S3 | **Per-facet trial→series conversion** (CX-05) | **15** | 5–25 | The only outcome signal in the domain that measures **teaching** rather than **playing**. Per `(instrument, level band, age band)`, never aggregate (DT-11). Neutral prior below 5 trials on that facet |
| S4 | **Reviews** (CX-M08) | **12** | 5–20 | A real signal, and a gameable one. Weighted below the behavioural signals that aren't |
| S5 | **Recurrence-weighted proximity** (DT-02, DT-08) | **10** | 0–25 | In-person only — contributes **0** for a remote search. Scored on journey-at-lesson-time from the stated origin, not home-to-home straight-line distance |
| S6 | **Price fit** vs stated budget | **5** | 0–20 | Deliberately low. Under-budget is **not** better than at-budget — a teacher priced at 40% of the local rate is a signal, not a bargain, and DT-10 records why the platform declines to industrialise the market's backwards price instinct for beginners |
| S7 | **Evidence** — verified credits + qualifications (06.02.02) | **5** | **0–8 (capped)** | Per 06.02.02 DT-02 and D-07. This is the numeric encoding of "must not dominate": the domain's best supply often has no discography |
| S8 | **Page completeness** — intro video, "what a first lesson looks like" | **3** | 0–10 | 06.02.01 DT-02 — the reader is a stranger from a search engine deciding whether to hand this adult their child |
| — | **Openness (count of free slots)** | **0** | **fixed** | Never a signal (D-08, DT-07). Ranking by how much spare capacity a teacher has promotes, by construction, the teachers nobody wants |

**The evidence cap (D-07)**: S7 may never exceed the lowest-weighted behavioural signal (S2, S3) and
is hard-capped at 8 regardless of owner override. An impressive discography must not be able to
reorder a result above a teacher who answers their messages and converts their trials.

**Explainability is a requirement, not a nicety.** Every result renders its top two contributing
signals in plain language ("Tuesdays 17:00 free · 12 min from Kingsdown Primary"), and every result
the student sees can be traced to criteria the student typed. A ranking a student cannot interrogate
is a ranking they will not trust with their child's Tuesday.

### Result partitions — never blended

The pool is split, not sorted, because these three groups answer different questions (D-08):

| Partition | Membership | Rendering |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Bookable, fits your window** | Passes every hard filter; has an open series-claimable slot inside the stated window | Ranked by the weights above. Capped at 20; target set is 3–5. Trial CTA live |
| **2 — Bookable, different hours** | Passes every hard filter; has open slots, none inside the stated window | Separate labelled block, ranked by the same weights with S1 recomputed against their actual openings. Shown because a student's stated window is softer than they said (DT-14): "Amy has Thursdays 18:00 — you asked for Tuesdays" |
| **3 — Fully booked** | Passes every hard filter; **zero** open series-claimable slots (06.01.01 `Full`) | Separate labelled block, never mixed into 1 or 2, never counted in the result total. No trial CTA. Shows the teacher's next `Completed` series date if one exists. This is how a `Full` teacher stays "discoverable-but-honest, not invisible" (06.01.01's own cross-cut note to this feature) without any waitlist machinery — which is new scope, not a deferral (06.01.01 Q-01) |

### Relaxation ladder — what gets given up, in order

Fires when partition 1 is empty or thin. Each step is applied one at a time, stated explicitly, and
reversible with one click. **Never silent.**

| Step | Relax | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Genre | Already soft — drop it from ranking entirely |
| 2 | Time — ±2h around the stated window, same weekday | The student named a school-night hour; the hour either side is usually available to them |
| 3 | Time — other weekdays | Promotes partition 2 into partition 1 |
| 4 | Price +20% | Stated before it is applied, in the viewer's currency |
| 5 | Radius — tolerance ×2 (recurrence-weighted, not straight-line ×2) | DT-02 |
| 6 | Mode — in-person → remote | The cold-start reality for anything but guitar and piano |
| 7 | **Group lessons (06.05)** | Cheaper, and for a beginner child frequently *better*. Uses the domain's own supply to answer a query 1:1 cannot fill |
| 8 | **Courses (06.04)** | The only supply in the domain with no local dependency and no teacher availability (domain Q-03) — in a genuine cold start it may be the only honest answer |
| 9 | Taxonomy-adjacent instrument (CX-M17), labelled | "No ukulele teachers near you — these 4 teach guitar and take ukulele beginners" |
| 10 | Waitlist / notify-me | The floor. Never a bare "no results" |

**Never relaxed, at any step**: the safeguarding vetting gate (D-03), the student age band, and the
level band beyond ±1. Relaxing a gate to fill a page is how a product produces an incident.

### Availability freshness

The result card's availability is a **hint**; 06.01.01's calendar is authoritative at hold time.
Availability facets must reflect a calendar write within **60 seconds (P95)**. A card that offers
"next Tue 17:00" and a booking path that finds it gone falls back to 06.01.01's own race handling
(the loser sees the teacher's 3 nearest alternative slots, pre-filtered to the same weekday/time
band) — this feature never asserts a slot, it reports one.

### Happy Path

1. Student opens search signed-out from a Google result and answers the intake one question at a
   time: `bass` → `under-11` → proxy level questions resolve to `absolute beginner` → `teacher's room
   or student's home` → `weekdays after 16:00` → `£25–35` → `English`.
2. System fires the **vetting gate first**: the declared age band is `under-11`, so the pool is
   restricted to teachers with current vetting **before any ranking runs**, and says so on the page —
   the restriction is stated, not silent.
3. System applies the remaining hard filters (instrument, age range accepted, level ±1 band, mode,
   language, bookability) and produces a pool of 9.
4. System ranks the 9 on the published weights, partitions them, and renders **4 bookable results**
   (partition 1), 2 in partition 2 ("different hours"), and 1 in partition 3 ("fully booked").
5. Each card states its top two reasons in plain language, the rate in the viewer's currency, next
   availability as a **shape** (signed-out), a vetting badge, and the evidence mix from 06.02.02 —
   credits where they exist, qualifications and academy vouches where they don't, with no implicit
   demerit for their absence (06.02.02 D-03).
6. Student compares 3 teachers, opens two profiles (06.02.01), and books a **trial directly from the
   result card** — no enquiry step (CX-04). Booking creates the account; from this moment they are
   lensed Musician (Student).
7. Trial is delivered (06.01.04) and converts, or doesn't. The trial→series outcome writes back to
   S3 **on that teacher's `(bass, beginner, under-11)` facet** (CX-05, DT-11) — which is the loop
   that makes this a sub-domain rather than four features.

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **No teachers for that instrument in that city** | Relaxation ladder runs to step 6 — remote — rather than an empty page. The cold-start reality for anything but guitar and piano | Student: "No {instrument} teachers within {tolerance} of {city} — these 6 teach remotely" + the step that was relaxed, reversible |
| **Nobody at all, at any step** | Ladder exhausts to step 10 | Student: "We'll tell you when a {instrument} teacher joins near {city}" — plus the honest count of what the platform *does* have ("3 bass courses" — 06.04) |
| **Student is a minor** | Vetted-only pool applied **before ranking**, never as a post-filter. Firing after ranking would leak the existence and position of unvetted teachers through result counts | Student/guardian: the restriction is stated on the page, not silent. No toggle exists to turn it off |
| **Signed-out searcher is a 14-year-old searching for themselves** | The declared age band gates the pool (DT-09). Declaration is not assurance — CX-M31 owns assurance at booking. Defence in depth, the same two-gate pattern as 06.01.01 (booking) and 06.01.04 (join) | Student: the vetted-only pool, stated. At booking: the full consent + assurance flow |
| **A guardian searches on behalf of a child but answers the age question as themselves** | The intake asks "how old is **the student**", never "how old are you" — the wording is the countermeasure, and this row is why it is not a preference | Guardian: an unambiguous question. The domain's largest buyer is not the student (06.02.01 DT-03) |
| **Student wants a specific named teacher** | Direct navigation, no matching involved | Straight to 06.02.01 |
| **Teacher matched but has zero open slots** (`Full`) | **Partition 3.** Not demoted, not hidden, not blended, not counted in the result total, no trial CTA (D-08). Demoting them punishes being good; hiding them lies about supply | Student: "Fully booked — no open slots" + their next `Completed` series date if one exists. Teacher: nothing punitive; being full is the domain's most valuable state (06.01.01) |
| **Teacher has slots, but none in the student's window** | **Partition 2**, ranked with S1 recomputed against their real openings | Student: "Amy has Thursdays 18:00 — you asked for Tuesdays after 16:00" |
| **Query is a genre, not an instrument ("I want to learn jazz")** | Genre is never a hard filter. Intake routes to instrument first, retaining genre as a soft signal | Student: "Which instrument? Jazz — we'll keep that in mind" — never an empty result for a well-formed intent |
| **Query is a goal, not an instrument ("I want to write songs" / "I want to produce")** | Routes out of the domain rather than fabricating a match. Songwriting and production tuition are 06.06 (mentorship) and 06.04 (courses) territory | Student: routed, with the reason stated. Not a zero-result page for a legitimate ambition |
| **Student's budget is below every local teacher** | Ladder steps 4 → 6 → 7 → 8. Never an empty page implying they cannot learn | Student: the local price reality stated plainly, then remote (6), group (7) and course (8) alternatives at their budget |
| **Student's budget is far above the local rate** | No effect. S6 scores fit, not magnitude — a teacher is not ranked up for being expensive, and the platform's fee interest in the opposite is exactly why this row is explicit (DT-10) | Student: nothing. This row exists so the implementer knows S6 is symmetric |
| **Student self-rates "intermediate" and is a beginner** | The ±1 band tolerance (D-06) absorbs the systematic error; the proxy questions (DT-05) reduce it at source. A teacher accepting "beginner–Grade 5" covers the mis-statement | Student: nothing. The error is designed for, not corrected in public — telling an adult their self-assessment is wrong before they have played a note is not a first impression the domain survives |
| **Adult self-taught player who reads no music but plays well** | The proxy questions produce a level *profile*, not a point (DT-04) — "plays fluently, reads nothing" is a real and common shape that a scalar destroys | Student: a level captured as what they can do, never a dropdown between "beginner" and "advanced" |
| **Student states an exam grade in a tradition the teacher doesn't use** | Exam board is a high-intent soft signal, not a hard filter — a Grade 5 pianist is legible to a teacher who never entered a candidate. Boards are not commensurable across each other either (06.09 DT-02), so a grade is never converted between boards | Student: matched; the grade renders as the board's own claim ("ABRSM Grade 5"), never normalised to a platform-wide number |
| **Teacher declares 11 instruments and every level to appear in every search** | Hard filters are **self-asserted** (06.02.01 D-04 permits assertion and forbids the platform adjudicating ability), so breadth is free at the filter and **not free at the rank**: S3 is per-facet (DT-11). Declaring bagpipes and never converting a bagpipe student sinks them in bagpipe results only, and touches nothing else | Teacher: their own per-facet numbers, which name the problem without an accusation. Student: nothing |
| **New teacher with no history at all** | Neutral prior on S3 below 5 trials on that facet — the platform median for the facet, never zero. A zero prior means no new teacher ever ranks, which is a supply death spiral in a domain with a guaranteed cold start | Student: a "new to WeJammin" label — an honest fact, not a demerit. Teacher: ranked, not buried |
| **Teacher games the ranking** | Mostly a feature, not a bug (DT-10). Every gameable signal except S4 is one where gaming it *is* improving: respond faster, accept more, cover more of the week, complete your page, convert your trials. S4 (reviews) is the exception and is why it sits below the behavioural signals. S7 is un-gameable by construction (06.02.02 — the teacher cannot author it) | Teacher: the weights, published |
| **Teacher accepts every request to lift S2, then cancels** | Caught on the other side: S2 includes no-show and late-cancellation rate from 06.01.03, so accept-then-cancel is worse than declining. The two halves of S2 are adversarial to each other by design | Teacher: "Your accept rate is high and your cancellation rate is too — students are being sent elsewhere" |
| **Two students book the last trial slot of the same teacher from a result card simultaneously** | 06.01.01's atomic hold serializes; this feature asserts nothing. The card's availability was a hint | Loser: 06.01.01's handling — "That slot was taken while you were booking" + 3 nearest alternatives on the same weekday/time band |
| **Card says "next Tue 17:00", teacher blocked it 20 seconds ago** | Within the 60s freshness budget. Booking re-validates against 06.01.01 and fails cleanly | Student: the same alternative-slots fallback. Never a card that silently 404s |
| **Search index is stale by minutes (backlog, replay)** | Availability facets degrade to shape ("Tue/Thu evenings") rather than asserting a specific slot. Hard filters and gates are **never** served from a stale index — the vetting gate re-evaluates at render | Student: fewer promises, same results. Nobody sees a stale vetting badge |
| **Teacher's vetting expires between indexing and render** | The gate re-evaluates at render, not at index time. A minor's pool never contains a teacher whose check lapsed this morning | Student: the teacher is simply absent. Teacher: 06.02.02's escalating expiry warnings fired weeks earlier — this is the consequence they were warned about |
| **Teacher's vetting expires while a minor's result page is open** | The page is a snapshot; the booking path fails closed at CX-M31 | Student/guardian: at booking, the specific blocking condition and the action that clears it — never a generic "unavailable" |
| **The multi-hyphenate: a teacher searches for a teacher** | Normal and unremarkable. Their own profile is excluded from their own results (DT-12); nothing else changes. Two people who teach each other is 06.01.01's guitar-for-piano swap and needs no special case here | Musician: their own page never appears in their own search. Everything else is a normal result |
| **A student searches and their current teacher matches** | Shown, ranked normally, with no special badge. Hiding an existing teacher assumes the relationship is exclusive; labelling it assumes it is in trouble. Both are the platform having an opinion about a relationship it cannot see | Student: a normal result |
| **A teacher's own student appears as a teacher in their search** | Correct and permitted — an advanced student teaching beginners is the domain's most common supply origin. No special case | Both: normal results. The platform does not police who may teach whom |
| **Student searches from a device whose location is wrong / VPN'd** | Proximity (S5) contributes 10 of 100 and only for in-person searches, so a wrong origin degrades the ranking rather than breaking it. The stated origin is editable and shown on every distance claim | Student: "12 min from {stated origin}" — a wrong origin is visible and correctable, not silent |
| **Student is between two cities / commutes** | The origin is a *place plus a time* (DT-08), and more than one may be stated: "from school Tue–Thu, from home Saturdays". Both are scored | Student: distance stated per origin, never averaged into a meaningless number |
| **Two searches, same criteria, different results minutes apart** | Expected — availability, reliability and conversion all move. The ranking is not stable and must not pretend to be | Student: no ranking numbers, no "#1" — positions are not a promise. This row is why the UI never shows a rank integer |
| **Result set is enormous (guitar, remote, adult, any time, any budget)** | Ranked list caps at 20. The answer to a large pool is not pagination to 200 — a student comparing 200 teachers is a student who books none. The intake asks for one more constraint | Student: 20 results + "narrow this down — what time of week actually works?" |
| **Student refuses to state a window ("any time works")** | S1 (30) has nothing to score against and redistributes proportionally across S2–S8. Permitted, and the result is measurably worse | Student: results, plus an honest nudge — "telling us when you're free changes these results more than anything else" |
| **Malicious: scraping the teacher graph via unauthenticated search** | Public search is deliberately open (Fan, CX-M23) and the credit graph is the asset a competitor most wants (06.02.02 Q-03). Rate limiting is CX-M16's / `/create-prd-security`'s, not this feature's — but the *product* decision is recorded: the shortlist is capped at 20 and the full evidence chain is not in the result card, so the search surface is a poor scraping vector by shape | Attacker: 20 cards, no evidence chain, no bulk enumeration |
| **Malicious: teacher enumerates competitors' rankings to reverse-engineer weights** | The weights are **published** (D-10) — there is nothing to reverse-engineer. What is withheld is other teachers' input values and any comparative position | Teacher: their own numbers and the public weights. Never a rival's |
| **Malicious: a teacher lists at an implausibly low rate to farm S6** | S6 is weighted 5 and scores *fit*, not cheapness — undercutting the local rate by 60% buys a fraction of one signal and signals distress to a parent reading the card. The market punishes this better than the ranking can | Student: the rate, in context of the local range |
| **Malicious: mass fake accounts to farm S3 (conversion) with sockpuppet trials** | Out of scope here, exactly as in 06.02.04: sockpuppets are Identity (01) + Trust & Safety (24). Recorded because S3 is the highest-value signal to forge and someone will try | Routed out of the domain, with the per-facet shape limiting the blast radius to one facet |
| **Student books a trial, it goes badly, they walk** | No rating is asked (06.02.04 D-04 — silence is the feature). The non-conversion still feeds S3 as an outcome, because conversion is *inferred*, not solicited | Student: nothing. This is the whole point of the "no explanation owed" rule |
| **A teacher is excellent and converts nobody, because they are honest about not being a fit** | A real and unrewarded behaviour: the best teachers refer students on. S3 punishes it, and this feature cannot distinguish "bad teacher" from "honest teacher" from conversion data alone | Teacher: nothing today. Logged as Q-04 — the honest limit of the domain's only teaching signal |
| **Network failure mid-search** | CX-M16's failure UI + the last cached result set if any, labelled as cached with its age | Student: "Showing results from 2 minutes ago" + retry — never a blank page that loses their intake answers |
| **Intake abandoned halfway (the common case, signed-out)** | Answers persist locally and are restored. An intake that a stranger from Google must re-answer is an intake they leave | Student: their answers, still there |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Intake** | No criteria stated yet | One question at a time, not a 9-field form. The instrument question first, because it is the only one everybody can answer |
| **Loading** | Query submitted | Skeleton result cards. The stated criteria stay visible and editable throughout — a student who cannot see what they asked for cannot tell why the answer is wrong |
| **Populated** | Partition 1 non-empty | Ranked cards: top-two match reasons, rate in viewer currency, next availability (shape when signed-out, slots when signed-in), vetting badge, evidence mix (06.02.02), trial CTA. Partitions 2 and 3 below, labelled, never blended |
| **Thin** | Partition 1 has 1–2 results | Results **plus** the next relaxation step offered explicitly: "Only 2 teachers match. Widen to Thursdays?" — a thin page is a decision the student should be making, not a silent outcome |
| **Empty** | Partition 1 empty after hard filters | Relaxation ladder, one step at a time, each stating what it gave up and reversible in one click. Never a bare "no results" (D-04) |
| **Exhausted** | Ladder ran to step 10 | Waitlist/notify + an honest statement of what the platform *does* have for this instrument (group, courses). The supply reality, stated — a student told the truth returns; one shown an empty page does not |
| **Partial** | Some filters unsatisfiable, results exist under relaxation | "No teacher matches all your filters — here are the closest, and what we relaxed" with the relaxed criteria named individually and each restorable |
| **Restricted** | Declared age band is under-18 | Vetted-only pool, applied pre-ranking, with the restriction stated on the page. No toggle, no count of what was excluded |
| **Stale** | Index lag beyond the 60s budget | Availability degrades to shape; hard filters and the vetting gate still evaluate live. No stale badge is ever rendered |
| **Error** | CX-M16 unavailable | Cross-cut's failure UI + last cached results labelled with their age, + retry. Intake answers preserved |
| **Full-only** | Every match is in partition 3 | **A distinct state, not Empty.** "3 teachers match you exactly — all are fully booked." The honest answer to a real market condition, and the clearest evidence for 06.01.01 Q-01's register-interest scope |
