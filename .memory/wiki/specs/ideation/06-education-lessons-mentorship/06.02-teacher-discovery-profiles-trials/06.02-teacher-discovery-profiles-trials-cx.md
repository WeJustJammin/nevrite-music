## Cross-Cut Details

### CX-01: Verified Credentials ↔ Discovery & Match

**Relationship**: The most consequential seam in the sub-domain, and it is a **deliberate
restraint**. Credentials are the platform's unfair advantage (06.02.02 DT-01); the obvious move is to
rank on them; that move is wrong (06.02.02 DT-02). Credits demonstrate *playing* ability, and the
domain's most valuable supply — patient professional teachers of beginners — often has none. Ranking
on credits would systematically demote the people who make the domain work, in favour of session
players who may be poor teachers, while telling the buyer something true but irrelevant.

The resolution: credentials are **displayed prominently** and **weighted modestly**. The wedge's job
is to make claims checkable, not to decide who is good.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician (as Student)**: sees evidence on every result; is not steered by it
- **Musician (as Teacher)**: is not punished in ranking for lacking a discography — the explicit intent of 06.02.02 D-03
- **Operator**: sees where their teachers rank; cannot buy past it (parent Q-02 unresolved)
- **Producer**: not affected — they don't use teacher search
- **Fan**: sees the same public ranking as anyone

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: None — 06.02.02 is a read-only projection of domain 02; matching reads it. No writer contention.
2. **Trigger chain**: Credit confirmed in domain 02 → credential block updates → match signal changes. Async; staleness here is harmless (a credit appearing a day late costs nothing). **Exception**: vetting status must **not** be eventually-consistent — an expired check must gate the minor pool immediately, which is why vetting gates rather than ranks.
3. **Permission intersection**: Yes, and it is the important one. Vetting is a **gate** on the minor pool (06.02.03 D-03); credits are a **signal**. A gate that contributes to a score can be outweighed by a good discography — the absurdity 06.02.02 DT-03 rejects.
4. **Notification fan-out**: Expiring vetting notifies the teacher hard (their pool eligibility and their booked lessons both die with it).
5. **State transition conflict**: A credit disputed in domain 02 while a student is mid-decision. Resolution: display the dispute; never silently re-rank underneath a reader.

### CX-02: Teacher Profile ↔ Verified Credentials

**Relationship**: The credential block renders inside the profile page but has an **inverted write
model** — the teacher authors everything around it and nothing in it. This is why the sweep's
candidate 08 ("Teacher & Tuition Profile *with* Verified Credentials") was split into two files
(index D-01): one is authored content whose value is the teacher's voice, the other is evidence whose
value is that the teacher's voice cannot reach it.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician (as Teacher)**: writes the profile; submits to the credential block; may choose what to *surface*, never what it *says*
- **Musician (as Student)**: reads both, and the visual distinction between "they said" and "we verified" is the page's most important affordance
- **Producer**: sees the person's credits in a hiring context — the tuition facet does not render for them (06.02.01 D-01: facet, not profile)
- **Operator**: adds a vouch — the only externally-authored item in the block, and it carries the academy's reputation
- **Fan**: reads both publicly; the evidence chain may be account-gated (06.02.02 Q-03)

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: None — disjoint write sets by design. The profile owns self-authored + structured; the block owns nothing (it projects domain 02 + Safeguarding).
2. **Trigger chain**: Enabling the teaching facet → block auto-populates from existing credits. **The teacher does nothing** — that is the flywheel (06.02.02 DT-01). Failure of domain 02 degrades to profile-only; it must never block publishing.
3. **Permission intersection**: The under-18 age-range field on the profile is gated by vetting status in the block — a profile field whose editability depends on evidence the profile cannot write.
4. **Notification fan-out**: A newly-confirmed credit in domain 02 surfacing on the teaching page is a pleasant, unprompted event worth telling the teacher about — it is the wedge visibly paying them.
5. **State transition conflict**: Teacher disables the teaching facet while a credit dispute is open — the block unpublishes with the page; the dispute continues in domain 02, unaffected.

### CX-03: Teacher Profile ↔ Discovery & Match

**Relationship**: The profile's **structured** bucket (instruments, modes, languages, exam boards,
levels, age ranges) exists precisely because matching needs taxonomy-bound values. Free text is
unfilterable. This is why 06.02.01 splits content by authorship model rather than by page section —
the split is functional, not editorial.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician (as Teacher)**: what they tick is what they're found for; the ranking-diagnosis view ("why don't I rank") is really a view onto this data's completeness
- **Musician (as Student)**: filters on it, and the hard filters (06.02.03 D-02) are all sourced here
- **Operator**: house presentation standards effectively mean enforcing structured completeness
- **Producer / Fan**: read-only

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: 06.02.01 owns the fields; 06.02.03 reads them. Single writer.
2. **Trigger chain**: Profile publish → index → matchable. Async is fine, but the lag must be visible to the teacher, who will otherwise conclude the platform is broken when their new page doesn't appear.
3. **Permission intersection**: The under-18 age range is vetting-gated (CX-06), so a hard filter's value is itself gated by the Safeguarding cross-cut.
4. **Notification fan-out**: None.
5. **State transition conflict**: Teacher narrows their levels while a student's search is open. Stale results are acceptable here — the cost is a wasted click, not money.

### CX-04: Discovery & Match ↔ Trials

**Relationship**: The trial CTA is on the result card. No enquiry step, no "message this teacher to
discuss" — that step is where every competitor's funnel leaks, because it converts a decision into a
correspondence. The student picks a teacher and books a slot in one action.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician (as Student)**: books from the result; may trial several teachers in parallel (06.02.04's first edge case — this is legitimate)
- **Musician (as Teacher)**: sees an inbound trial labelled as a trial, so expectations are calibrated
- **Operator**: house trial policy makes all their staff's cards consistent
- **Producer / Fan**: not affected

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: None — matching reads trial availability; 06.02.04 owns the cap.
2. **Trigger chain**: Book from card → occurrence created in 06.01.01 → one-per-pair cap enforced. Sync; the cap must be evaluated **before** the slot is held or a farmer holds slots they can't book.
3. **Permission intersection**: A minor's result pool is vetted-only (06.02.03 D-03), so every trial reachable from it is already gated.
4. **Notification fan-out**: Teacher notified of an inbound trial with its trial label attached.
5. **State transition conflict**: Two students booking the last trial slot — resolved by 06.01.01's slot serialization; nothing trial-specific.

### CX-06: Verified Credentials ↔ Trials

**Relationship**: The Safeguarding gate applies to a trial identically to any lesson (06.02.04 D-05).
Recorded as its own CX rather than folded into CX-01 because "it's only a trial, and it's free" is
exactly the reasoning that produces a safeguarding incident, and the sub-domain should carry an
explicit artifact saying no.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician (as Teacher)**: cannot offer trials to under-18s without current vetting — the same gate, no discount
- **Musician (as Student) / guardian**: same consent flow as any lesson
- **Operator**: a house free-trial policy cannot lower the gate for staff
- **Producer / Fan**: not affected

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: None — both read vetting status from the Safeguarding cross-cut.
2. **Trigger chain**: Trial booking → vetting check → allow/deny. Fails closed, as at 06.01.04.
3. **Permission intersection**: The whole entry. Vetting gates trials, minor search results, profile age ranges and lesson joins — four enforcement points, one owner (the cross-cut).
4. **Notification fan-out**: Expiring vetting must warn before it silently removes the teacher from the minor pool and kills their trial CTA.
5. **State transition conflict**: Vetting expires between trial booking and delivery — same as 06.01.04's first edge case, and the reason CX-07 in the 06.01 CX file flags double-gating as unresolved.
