# Feature: Teacher & Tuition Profile

> **Number**: 06.02.01
> **Parent**: [Teacher Discovery, Profiles & Trials](./06.02-teacher-discovery-profiles-trials-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The teaching facet of a musician's identity: what they teach, to whom, at what level, how, and why a
student should pick them. This is **not a second profile** — domain 01 owns the person. This is the
tuition-specific layer that appears when that same person is being considered as a teacher, and it is
the public, SEO-indexed page that most students' journey into the platform actually starts on.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Teacher**: authors the tuition facet — instruments and levels taught, genres, teaching philosophy, student age ranges accepted, modes (remote / their room / student's home / academy), languages, exam boards prepared for, a short intro video, and what a first lesson looks like. Chooses what to show; cannot fabricate the credential block (06.02.02). **As Student**: reads it to decide, compares it against alternatives, sees availability and rate inline so the decision needs one page not three. |
| Producer | Read-only | Sees the person's **credits**, not the tuition facet, when considering them for a session — the tuition layer is irrelevant to hiring a player and would be noise. Reinforces D-03: this is a facet that renders in context, not a separate profile. |
| Operator | Config | As an academy: curates which of their teachers are publicly listed, attaches the academy's vouch, sets house presentation standards. Cannot author a teacher's own words. |
| Fan | Read-only | The public teacher page is indexable and readable with no account — "guitar teacher near me" is the domain's highest-volume acquisition path, and its reader has no account by definition. Sees everything public; the moment they book a trial they are acting as a Musician (Student). |

## Behavior

The tuition facet renders on top of the domain-01 identity. Its content is deliberately split by
authorship model:

- **Self-authored** — philosophy, intro video, what a first lesson is like, genres, levels, age ranges
- **Structured** — instruments, modes, languages, exam boards (taxonomy-bound so matching can filter on them)
- **Not authorable** — the credential block (06.02.02), reviews (Reviews cross-cut), reliability signals

That third bucket is the whole point. Every competing teacher marketplace is 100% bucket one, which is
why every teacher on them claims to have toured internationally.

The page also has a **live** half — current availability (06.01.01) and rate card (06.01.02) render
inline, because a teacher page that requires a second click to learn the price is a page that gets
abandoned.

### Happy Path

1. Musician enables the teaching facet on their existing identity — no second account, no re-registration
2. System pre-fills what it already knows: instruments, verified credits, location, languages
3. Teacher adds tuition-specific structure — levels, age ranges, modes, exam boards
4. Teacher writes their philosophy and records a short intro
5. Publishing requires a rate card (06.01.02) and availability (06.01.01) to exist — an unbookable page is worse than no page
6. Page goes live, is indexed, and enters the match pool

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Teacher publishes with no rate card or availability | Blocked — publishing an unbookable teacher wastes the student's click and the teacher's ranking | Teacher: checklist of what's missing, each linking to the fix |
| Teacher claims levels/instruments their credits don't support | Allowed — self-assertion is permitted, but sits visibly beside the credential block that either backs it or doesn't. The platform does not adjudicate teaching ability | Student: claims and evidence side by side; the gap is legible without an accusation |
| Teacher accepts under-18s but has no current vetting | Age-range field is gated by the Safeguarding cross-cut — it cannot be set without vetting | Teacher: "Add your DBS check to teach under-18s" |
| Teacher has no verified credits at all (a great teacher who never recorded) | Page publishes fine; the credential block shows qualifications and academy vouches instead | Student: sees a different, honest evidence mix — **not** an implicit demerit (see 06.02.02 DT-02) |
| Person disables teaching | Facet unpublishes; identity, credits and everything else are untouched | Teacher: "Your teaching page is hidden. Your profile is unaffected" |
| Teacher is also a student of another teacher | Both are true simultaneously and neither is hidden — the multi-hyphenate fact | Nothing special. This is normal and the UI must not treat it as a contradiction |
| Profile scraped for a competitor's directory | `Public SEO Surfaces & Embeds` + scraper defence cross-cuts | `[PENDING — /create-prd-security]` |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A teacher profile is a distinct profile from a musician profile and should live in domain 06 | The candidate name ("Teacher & Tuition Profile") implies a profile object; every competitor ships exactly this | ❌ REJECTED — it is a **facet**, and getting this wrong breaks the platform's central structural fact. `meta/personas.md`: "one human simultaneously holds several professional roles… any UX forcing 'are you an artist OR a producer OR a seller?' fails them immediately." A separate teacher profile forces exactly that choice, splits the person's credits across two identities, and destroys the credentialing advantage — the credits that make them credible as a teacher are the credits they earned as a player. Domain 01 owns the entity; this is a facet rendered in context. |
| DT-02 | The page's primary reader is a logged-in student browsing the platform | Naive product read — a marketplace's profile serves its users | ❌ REJECTED — its primary reader is a **stranger from a search engine** with no account, and probably a parent rather than the student. This is why Fan appears in the Role Lens at all, why the page must be complete and indexable while logged-out, and why "what a first lesson looks like" matters more than any platform-native affordance. The page is an acquisition surface first and a product surface second — the reverse of the assumption. |
| DT-03 | The parent, not the student, is the actual decision-maker for the domain's largest market segment | Domain expertise: music teaching "is overwhelmingly a minors market" (Safeguarding cross-cut rationale) — a 9-year-old does not choose their piano teacher or pay for lessons | ✅ CONFIRMED — with an uncomfortable consequence. The page's highest-value reader is a **persona that does not exist in the ratified set** (D-19). A parent evaluating a teacher wants: is this person safe with my child, are they patient with beginners, is there parking. The verified-credit wedge — "played on three records you'd know" — is aimed at the *adult self-funding student*, and may be near-worthless to the parent segment that is most of the market. **This is the sharpest strategic finding in the domain** and it is escalated to the domain index (Q-02) rather than resolved here: it questions whether the domain's stated unfair advantage actually addresses its largest buyer. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The tuition profile is a **facet** of the domain-01 identity, never a separate profile | Per DT-01 — a separate teacher profile contradicts the platform's central structural fact (the multi-hyphenate) and splits the credits that make the teacher credible. |
| D-02 | Content is split by **authorship model**: self-authored, structured, and not-authorable | The not-authorable bucket is the only thing a competitor cannot copy. Making the split explicit in the design keeps it from eroding into "another bio field". |
| D-03 | Publishing requires a rate card and availability | An unbookable teacher page wastes the reader's only click; the domain's funnel is too thin to spend it. |
| D-04 | Self-assertion is permitted and shown **beside** evidence, not adjudicated | The platform verifies facts (credits, checks), not teaching ability. A great teacher with no credits must not be implicitly demeaned — see 06.02.02 DT-02. |
