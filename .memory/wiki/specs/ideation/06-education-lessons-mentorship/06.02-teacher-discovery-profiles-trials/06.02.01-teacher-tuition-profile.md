# Feature: Teacher & Tuition Profile

> **Number**: 06.02.01
> **Parent**: [Teacher Discovery, Profiles & Trials](./06.02-teacher-discovery-profiles-trials-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The teaching facet of a musician's identity: what they teach, to whom, at what level, how, and why a
student should pick them. This is **not a second profile** — domain 01 owns the person. This is the
tuition-specific layer that appears when that same person is being considered as a teacher, and it is
the public, SEO-indexed page that most students' journey into the platform actually starts on.

It solves two problems at once, for two different readers. For the **teacher**, it ends the
Linktree-and-a-PDF regime: one page that carries their proof of work, their rate, and their calendar,
without re-proving themselves to each new enquiry. For the **buyer** — usually a stranger from a search
engine, frequently a parent (DT-03) — it is a page where the claims are checkable, which no competitor's
teacher page is.

It is also the domain's structural admission that a teacher is a whole musician. The credits that make
them credible as a teacher are the credits they earned as a *player*, in domain 02, as a byproduct of
work they did for other reasons. That is the thesis (D-18) paying out on a consolidation surface.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Teacher**: authors the tuition facet — instruments × levels taught, genres, teaching philosophy, age ranges accepted, modes (remote / their room / student's home / academy), languages, exam boards prepared for, an intro video, and what a first lesson looks like. Sets **intake status** (open / closed / from-a-date / waitlist — DT-08), distinct from calendar availability. Chooses, **per credit**, which verified credits surface on the teaching page (DT-06) — but can never author what a credit *says* (06.02.02 D-01). Sets location disclosure to district-level only; the precise address is never theirs to publish here (DT-04). **As Student**: reads it to decide, compares against alternatives, sees rate and next availability inline so the decision needs one page, not three. |
| Producer | Read-only | Sees the person's **credits**, not the tuition facet, when considering them for a session — arriving from a hiring context (04/05) composes the identity page around credits and the tuition facet does not render (DT-05). This is D-03/DT-01 made visible: a facet renders *in context*, it is not a separate profile. The Producer's real act in this feature happened elsewhere — their domain-02 counter-attestation is what fills the credential block they never see here. |
| Operator | Config | As an academy: curates which of their teachers appear on the **academy's own roster page**, attaches the academy's vouch (revocable, attributable), and sets house presentation standards (minimum structured completeness, house intro-video format, house rate bands). **Cannot author a teacher's own words, cannot unpublish a teacher's facet, and cannot make a departing teacher's page disappear** (DT-07). Does **not** see: the drill-through evidence chain (account-gated — D-09), the exact teaching address, or any contact channel. The moment they book a trial they are acting as a Musician (Student). |

## Behavior

The tuition facet renders on top of the domain-01 identity. Four things define its behavior: an
**authorship split**, an **intake status**, a **location-precision rule**, and an **audience-appropriate
composition** rule. The rest is presentation.

### 1. Content is split by authorship model — not by page section

| Bucket | Fields | Why it is in this bucket |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-authored** (free text / media) | Teaching philosophy, what a first lesson looks like, intro video, genre notes, a short bio-for-teaching distinct from the identity bio | The teacher's voice is the value. Unfilterable by design. |
| **Structured** (taxonomy-bound) | Instruments × levels taught, modes, languages, exam boards, age ranges accepted, travel radius, intake status | Matching (06.02.03) can only filter on taxonomy-bound values. This bucket exists *because* 06.02.03 needs it (CX-03), not for editorial reasons. |
| **Not authorable** | Credential block (06.02.02), reviews (CX-M08), reliability signals (response rate, no-show history from 06.01.03), academy vouch | The only content a competitor cannot copy. A teacher may choose whether a given item **surfaces**; never what it **says**. |

That third bucket is the whole point. Every competing teacher marketplace is 100% bucket one, which is
why every teacher on them has toured internationally.

**The split is enforced at the field level, not the page level.** A single visual row may combine a
self-authored claim ("I specialise in jazz improvisation") with a not-authorable evidence item
("played on {record} — confirmed by {producer}"), and the two must remain visually distinguishable as
"they said" versus "we verified". That distinction is the page's most important affordance (CX-02).

### 2. Intake status is a first-class field, distinct from calendar availability (DT-08)

06.01.01 owns *when* the teacher is free (a recurring rule). This feature owns *whether they want new
students at all* — a different question with a different answer. A teacher can have an empty Tuesday
and still not be taking students: they are holding it for a returning pupil, they are at emotional
capacity, or it is August and they take on in September.

| Intake status | Meaning | Page effect | Match effect (06.02.03) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `open` | Taking new students now | Trial/booking CTA live | Ranked normally |
| `from-date` | "Taking students from {date}" — the single most common real-world teacher status | CTA becomes "Book from {date}"; bookings allowed for occurrences on/after {date} | Ranked, with the date shown on the card |
| `waitlist` | Full, but wants to be found | CTA becomes "Join waitlist"; no bookable slot | Ranked, labelled — gives 06.02.03 Q-02 a handle from the supply side |
| `closed` | Not taking students; page stays for existing students and for search presence | No CTA; page renders | Excluded from results, **not** unpublished |

**Dormancy**: if no lesson has been delivered and the teacher has not logged in for **90 days** (one
academic term — the domain's natural unit), intake auto-transitions `open` → `waitlist`, and at **180
days** → `closed`. The page never unpublishes itself. Rationale: an indexed page outlives the teacher's
engagement, and a student booking a trial with someone who quit two years ago is the domain's worst
first impression (06.02.04 Q-04) delivered by a page we chose to keep ranking. Auto-transition is
honest; auto-deletion would destroy a teacher's SEO presence over a holiday. Per domain D-04, the
teacher is **told** ("your page is now on waitlist — one tap to reopen"), never scored, streaked, or
nagged.

### 3. Location precision — district, never a point (DT-04)

Music teaching's "their room" is overwhelmingly a **home**. The page must never publish, to a
logged-out stranger, the home address of a person who teaches children.

- **Pre-booking, public**: district / town name + a stated travel radius ("teaches at their room in
  {district}; will travel up to 8 miles"). **No map pin. No offset circle.** An offset circle is not
  anonymisation — its centre is the address, and centres are recoverable by triangulating a listing
  across queries. A point that is "roughly right" is a point.
- **At confirmed booking**: the precise address is disclosed to the booked student only, through the
  booking record — never through the page.
- **Academy mode**: the academy's address is a business address and renders precisely. The distinction
  is *whose* address it is, not whether teaching happens there.
- **Remote-only teachers**: no location field renders at all beyond timezone (CX-M20).

### 4. Composition is audience-appropriate — the facet model's cost (DT-05)

DT-01 established the facet and did not price it. The price: one identity page serves a Producer casting
a session, a buyer on the gear marketplace, and a parent choosing a piano teacher for an eight-year-old.
Those readers must not get the same page.

**Rule**: arrival context determines primary composition. Arriving from teacher search, a teacher page
link, or an SEO landing → the tuition facet is the page's primary content and is the addressable thing.
Arriving from a hiring context (04/05) → credits are primary and the tuition facet does not render at
all. The identity header (domain 01) is constant across every composition; everything below it is
contextual.

This does not resolve **Q-01** (own URL vs tab) — that is a URL-shape decision for
`/create-prd-design-system`. It resolves the **behavior** the URL shape must satisfy, which is the part
that belongs to ideation.

### 5. The live half

Current availability (06.01.01) and the rate card (06.01.02) render **inline**, in the reader's currency
and timezone (CX-M20), with the teacher's source currency visible. A teacher page that requires a second
click to learn the price is a page that gets abandoned, and the domain's funnel is too thin to spend a
click on arithmetic.

### 6. What is public, what is gated, what never renders (resolves the scraping question — D-09)

The defence against a competitor scraping the credit graph is **compositional**, not merely a rate
limit: the scrapeable half is deliberately the half we want indexed.

| Tier | Content |
|---|---|
| **Public, indexed** (no account) | Identity header; all self-authored content; all structured fields; the credential block's **claims**, each as a plain labelled statement and never a badge, tick, shield or count (06.02.02 D-02) — "played bass on {record}", and vetting as the dated statement naming the check and its jurisdiction that 06.02.02 D-11 requires ("Enhanced DBS check (England & Wales) — 12 March 2026"), never "DBS checked, current"; reviews; rate; next-availability summary; district; intake status; trial CTA |
| **Account-gated** | The drill-through **evidence chain** — who attested, when, in which session. The claim is the acquisition asset and must be indexed; the chain is the graph a competitor wants, and it is worth an account to see. **06.02.02 Q-03 owns the final line**; this is the profile's position and its rationale |
| **Booked-party only** | Precise teaching address; contact channel (via CX-M06, never a raw address) |
| **Never rendered** | Any student's identity or count (they are frequently minors — a public "teaches 14 students" is an association claim about children); teacher DOB; the teacher's own address as a point, at any tier |

### Happy Path

1. Musician opens their identity (domain 01) and enables the teaching facet — no second account, no re-registration, one toggle
2. System pre-fills what it already knows from the existing identity and credit graph: instruments, verified credits, base location district, languages, timezone. **The teacher writes nothing to get their strongest evidence** — the credential block populates itself (06.02.02 DT-01, CX-02)
3. Teacher adds the tuition-specific structured fields — levels per instrument, age ranges, modes, travel radius, exam boards, languages taught
4. Teacher sets age ranges. Selecting any range including under-18s opens the Safeguarding gate (CX-M31): the field cannot be saved without current vetting, and the system offers the vetting flow inline rather than rejecting the save
5. Teacher writes their philosophy and "what a first lesson looks like", and records or uploads an intro video (CX-M10). Each field saves on blur; drafts persist locally through a failed save
6. Teacher reviews the auto-populated credential block and chooses, **per credit**, which surface on the teaching page (DT-06). Default: all surface. Hiding one is silent and costs nothing
7. Teacher sets intake status — defaulting to `open`
8. Teacher hits publish. System runs the publish checklist: rate card (06.01.02) exists · availability rule (06.01.01) exists · at least one instrument × level · age range set · vetting current if under-18s selected. Each unmet item links to its fix
9. Page goes live, is submitted for indexing (CX-M23), and enters the match pool (06.02.03). The teacher is told the indexing lag exists and roughly how long it is — otherwise they conclude the platform is broken when their page isn't on Google by teatime (CX-03)
10. A stranger searches "{instrument} teacher {town}", lands on the page logged-out, reads a claim they can check, and books a trial (06.02.04) — the only action the page's most important reader can take (CX-07)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Teacher publishes with no rate card or availability | Blocked at the publish gate — an unbookable teacher page wastes the student's only click and the teacher's ranking | Teacher: checklist of exactly what's missing, each row linking to the fix. Never "profile incomplete" |
| Teacher claims levels/instruments their credits don't support | Allowed — self-assertion is permitted, and sits visibly beside the credential block that either backs it or doesn't. The platform verifies facts, not teaching ability | Student: claims and evidence side by side; the gap is legible without an accusation |
| Teacher accepts under-18s with no current vetting | Age-range field is gated by CX-M31 — it cannot be **set**, not merely not enforced | Teacher: "Add your DBS check to teach under-18s" + inline vetting flow |
| **Vetting expires while the page is published with an under-18 range** | The age range **retracts automatically** to 18+; the page stays published; existing lessons are handled by 06.01.04's fail-closed gate, not here. The profile never silently keeps advertising something the gate will refuse | Teacher: escalating warnings before expiry (their bookings die with it), then "your under-18 range is paused". Student: the teacher stops appearing in minor searches — no public shaming, no "expired" badge to a stranger |
| **Teacher narrows their age range while teaching existing minor students** | The field is **intake**, not entitlement. Narrowing stops new matches; it does not cancel or invalidate existing series (06.01.01) | Teacher: "This stops new under-18 enquiries. Your current students are unaffected" |
| Teacher has no verified credits at all (a great teacher who never recorded) | Page publishes fine; the credential block shows qualifications and academy vouches instead | Student: a different, honest evidence mix — **not** an implicit demerit (06.02.02 DT-02/D-03) |
| Person disables teaching | Facet unpublishes; identity, credits, gear listings and everything else untouched | Teacher: "Your teaching page is hidden. Your profile is unaffected" |
| **Academy delists a teacher from its roster** | The academy's **roster** loses the teacher and the **vouch** stops rendering. The teacher's own tuition facet is **untouched and stays published** (DT-07) | Teacher: "You've been removed from {academy}'s roster. Your teaching page is still live." Academy: cannot escalate further |
| **Academy revokes a vouch after a bad exit** | Vouch stops rendering. The page does **not** announce a revocation | Student: no gap, no accusation. Rendering "vouch revoked" would let an academy defame a departing teacher on the teacher's own page — the same hostage dynamic DT-07 rejects. (Whether revocation is visible *elsewhere* is 06.02.02's call) |
| Teacher is also a student of another teacher | Both are true simultaneously and neither is hidden — the multi-hyphenate fact | Nothing special. This is normal and the UI must not treat it as a contradiction |
| **Parent lands on a teacher page whose identity is dominated by the person's explicit-content band** | Composition rule (DT-05) puts the tuition facet first; the person's artist output does not render in a tuition composition unless the teacher surfaces it. Per-credit surfacing (DT-06) lets the teacher keep a credit off this page without hiding it from their identity | Teacher: control, silently exercised. Parent: a page about teaching |
| **Teacher's home address leaks via another domain** (gear listing pickup point, venue check-in) | **Unresolved cross-domain hazard, escalated.** This feature's district-only rule (DT-04) is defeated by any other facet publishing a point on the same underlying address entity, which domain 01 owns. Location precision is an **identity-level policy**, not a per-facet preference | Q-05 — escalated, not fabricated. This feature cannot fix another domain's disclosure |
| **Teacher writes a phone number / WhatsApp / email into their philosophy** | Detected at save and **blocked with an explanation**, not silently stripped. Two harms, one of them not commercial: it disintermediates the platform, **and** it opens an unlogged, unmonitored adult↔child channel outside every safeguarding control the domain has | Teacher: "Contact details can't go in your page — students message you through WeJammin (CX-M06). Here's why for under-18s." Silent stripping would make them retype it and conclude we're broken |
| Teacher writes something discriminatory or unsafe ("I don't teach girls") | Moderation is domain 24's; the page is a UGC surface like any other | Routed to 24 — not adjudicated here |
| **Intro video contains a copyrighted cover** ("here's me playing {song}") | **The most obvious intro video is an infringing public performance that the platform hosts and monetises.** No mechanism exists here to fix it. This is the third independent arrival at the domain's largest gap (index Q-02 — licensed repertoire) — from a route nobody listed | Q-04 — escalated. Honest gap over a fabricated licence |
| **Teacher is themselves under 18** (a 17-year-old Grade 8 pianist teaching a 9-year-old) | **Genuinely unhandled and real** — music has many teenage teachers. A minor cannot hold vetting in the usual form; a minor teaching a minor inverts every assumption in CX-M31; an adult student booking a minor teacher is a separate hazard. The profile currently cannot express it | Q-03 — escalated to Safeguarding. Not fabricated |
| Teacher dies or the account is memorialised | Domain 01 owns memorialisation. The tuition consequence is specifiable and must not be left to a 410: intake goes `closed` immediately; the page must not accept a trial booking | Routed to 01 with the intake requirement attached |
| Teacher deletes their account with students mid-pack | Erasure (CX-M18) collides with prepaid credits (06.01.02) — the same shape as index Q-07 (an academy closing on outstanding credits). The page's part is trivial (unpublish); the money's part is not, and is not this feature's | Routed to CX-M18 + 06.01.02; flagged because the page is where the user performs the act |
| Domain 02 unreachable at publish time | Publish proceeds. Credential block degrades to "temporarily unavailable"; **the wedge failing must never block a teacher from publishing** (CX-02) | Teacher: publish succeeds. Student: everything but the credits |
| Save fails mid-philosophy (network drop) | Draft retained locally; retry on reconnect; the teacher's written text is never lost | Inline error + "saved locally, retrying" — never a cleared textarea |
| **Teacher edits published page while a student is reading it** | Stale read is acceptable — the cost is a wasted click, not money (CX-03). No live re-render underneath a reader mid-decision | Student: nothing. Never a page mutating while it's being read |
| **Teacher edits the same field on phone and laptop concurrently** | Per-field last-write-wins. Free-text fields (philosophy) are the only ones where this loses work, and they warn | Teacher: "This was changed on another device" on the affected field only |
| **Teacher raises their rate while a student is mid-decision on the trial CTA** | The rate shown at booking is the rate charged for that booking; a mid-decision change does not retro-price a slot the student is holding | 06.01.02 owns rate mechanics; the page must not display a rate it will not honour |
| Two teachers with the same name | Entity resolution (CX-M17); the credential block is the disambiguator a competitor can't offer | Student: distinct evidence, not distinct names |
| Someone impersonates a known player to teach | Identity's problem (01) — and the credential block is the structural defence: attestations cannot be self-minted (06.02.02) | Student: the impostor's block is empty. Absence of credits is not a demerit (06.02.02 D-03), so **impersonation is deterred by the block's un-authorability, not by its emptiness** |
| Rate is in a currency the reader doesn't use | Converted for the reader with the source rate and currency visible (CX-M20) | Student: "£35/hr (≈ $44)" — never a converted figure passing as the real one |
| Profile scraped for a competitor's directory | Composition is the primary defence (D-09) — claims are public because we want them indexed; the evidence chain is gated because it is the asset. The **mechanism** (rate limiting, bot detection) is architecture | Q-06 — routed to `/create-prd-security` with the composition decision already made |
| Logged-out stranger reads a page and wants to book | The only action available, and the domain's steepest funnel step: account creation, plus guardian consent if the student is their child (CX-07, and it turns on Q-02) | The trial CTA (06.02.04) owns this. Flagged here because this page is where the drop-off happens |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Page fetch | Skeleton with the identity header (domain 01) resolved first — the person's name and face are the cheapest and most reassuring bytes on the page |
| Empty | Teaching facet not enabled | Musician: "Do you teach? Turn on your teaching page." Shown in their own identity settings only — never to a reader, and never as an absence on a public page |
| Draft | Facet enabled, publish checklist unmet | Teacher-only preview + the checklist. Not indexed, not matchable, not linkable |
| Populated | Published, intake `open` | Full public page: identity header, tuition facet, credential claims, reviews, rate, next availability, trial CTA |
| Error | Save failed | Inline error on the affected field; draft retained locally; **never lose a teacher's written philosophy** |
| Partial | Published but thin (no intro video, short philosophy, few structured fields) | Live. Private, dismissible prompt naming **specific missing items and their concrete effect** ("no intro video — you won't appear in video-preview results"). **Never a percentage, never a score** (DT-09) |
| Degraded | Domain 02 unreachable | Everything renders except credits, which show "temporarily unavailable". The page stays bookable — the wedge is not on the critical path |
| Books-closed | Intake `closed` / `waitlist` / `from-date` | Page renders in full; the CTA changes rather than disappears ("Join waitlist" / "Book from {date}"). Existing students unaffected. **The page is never taken down for being full** (DT-08) |
| Dormant | 90 days no delivered lesson and no login | Intake auto-moves to `waitlist`; page stays live and indexed. Teacher: one-tap reopen |
| Hidden | Teacher disables the facet | Facet unpublishes; identity remains. Existing students and their series (06.01.01) are unaffected. Removed from the index; the URL resolves to the identity, never a 404 |
| Restricted | Reader is a minor / minor's guardian, teacher unvetted | The page renders (it is public), but no bookable action exists, and the restriction is **stated** rather than silently absent (06.02.03 D-03's principle, applied at the profile) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A teacher profile is a distinct profile from a musician profile and should live in domain 06 | The candidate name ("Teacher & Tuition Profile") implies a profile object; every competitor ships exactly this | ❌ REJECTED — it is a **facet**, and getting this wrong breaks the platform's central structural fact. `meta/personas.md`: "one human simultaneously holds several professional roles… any UX forcing 'are you an artist OR a producer OR a seller?' fails them immediately." A separate teacher profile forces exactly that choice, splits the person's credits across two identities, and destroys the credentialing advantage — the credits that make them credible as a teacher are the credits they earned as a player. | DT-02 | The page's primary reader is a logged-in student browsing the platform | Naive product read — a marketplace's profile serves its users | ❌ REJECTED — its primary reader is a **stranger from a search engine** with no account, and probably a parent rather than the student. | DT-03 | The parent, not the student, is the actual decision-maker for the domain's largest market segment | Domain expertise: music teaching "is overwhelmingly a minors market" (Safeguarding cross-cut rationale) — a 9-year-old does not choose their piano teacher or pay for lessons | ✅ CONFIRMED — with an uncomfortable consequence. | DT-04 | The page can show a map pin like every other marketplace, because location is how in-person teaching is found | Every directory does this; 06.02.03 needs geo; the `Geo, Location & Map Discovery` cross-cut supplies pins | ❌ REJECTED, and this is a music-specific fact the generic cross-cut cannot know: **"their room" is a home**. Peripatetic teachers and home studios are the norm, not the exception, and the page publishes to a logged-out stranger. A pin on a teacher page is the home address of an adult who teaches children, published to anyone, for SEO. The offset-circle mitigation every marketplace reaches for does not work either — a circle's centre *is* the address, recoverable by triangulating one listing across queries. **Resolution**: district + stated travel radius pre-booking; precise address disclosed to the booked student through the booking record only; academy addresses render precisely because they are business addresses. The distinction is whose address it is, not whether teaching happens there. Nobody in this sub-domain raised address disclosure — 06.02.03 DT-02 reasoned hard about *distance* and never about *disclosure*. |
| DT-05 | DT-01's facet model is free — one identity page serves every reader | Follows from DT-01's confirmation; the multi-hyphenate model says one person, one page | ❌ REJECTED — the facet model has a **cost DT-01 never priced**, and it lands exactly on this feature's most valuable reader. One identity page must serve a Producer casting a session, a gear buyer, and a parent choosing a piano teacher for an eight-year-old. Those readers cannot get the same page: a parent who lands on a page whose loudest content is the person's touring band, their gear listings, and an "available for sessions" CTA has not been served, and the multi-hyphenate fact — the platform's crown jewel — is what did it to them. **Resolution**: arrival context determines composition. Teaching context → tuition facet primary; hiring context → credits primary, tuition facet absent. The identity header is the only constant. This is the intersection where domain 01's entity model and domain 06's audience genuinely break against each other, and it is the real content of Q-01 (which is stated as a URL question but is actually a composition question). |
| DT-06 | The credit wedge is at worst *neutral* for the parent segment — irrelevant, but harmless | The moderate reading of DT-03: if a parent doesn't care about discographies, credits are simply wasted page space | ❌ REJECTED — it can be **actively negative**, which is worse than DT-03 concluded. The credits are real, and real credits are made of real records: a death-metal album, an explicit single, a tour with an artist a given parent dislikes. The platform's unique asset, auto-populated (06.02.02 DT-01) and un-authorable by design (06.02.02 D-01), can auto-publish to a parent exactly the fact that loses their child's lesson — and the teacher didn't write it and, in the naive design, can't do anything about it. **Consequence**: the "may choose whether to surface" clause in 06.02.02 D-01 is not a courtesy, it is **load-bearing**, and it must be **per credit**, not an all-or-nothing block toggle. All-or-nothing forces the teacher to choose between a page with an unhelpful credit and a page with no evidence at all — which hands the parent segment's teachers a reason to have no wedge. Hiding must be silent and costless (06.02.02 D-03's principle: absence is not evidence). |
| DT-07 | An academy delisting a teacher and a teacher disabling their own facet are the same unpublish event | The `[SURFACE]` draft of this file said exactly that: "Hidden — facet disabled **or academy delisted** → 410" | ❌ REJECTED — a real defect, and it inverts the domain's own decision. 06.10's Role Matrix says the teacher's "identity stays theirs". If an academy can unpublish a person's teaching page, then an academy **holds the teacher's public presence hostage** — and restrictive covenants and student-poaching clauses are standard practice in real music schools, which means this would be used, deliberately, the week a teacher resigned. The academy owns its **roster** and its **vouch**, both of which it may withdraw. It never owns the person's facet. Corollary: vouch revocation renders as *absence*, never as an announcement, or the academy defames a departing teacher on the teacher's own page by a second route. |
| DT-08 | Calendar availability (06.01.01) is sufficient to express whether a teacher is taking students | Reasonable seam: 06.01.01 owns availability as a rule, so "can I book?" is answered there | ❌ REJECTED — they are different questions with different answers, and conflating them models the teaching year wrong. **Teaching is termly.** "Taking students from September" is the single most common teacher status in the real world and it is expressible in neither an empty calendar nor a full one. Worse, a free slot does not mean an open door: teachers hold slots for returning pupils, and a teacher at emotional capacity with a free Tuesday is *not* available. **Resolution**: intake status (`open` / `from-date` / `waitlist` / `closed`) is a profile field, distinct from the calendar. This also hands 06.02.03 **Q-02** ("how are fully-booked teachers treated — demoted or waitlisted?") a supply-side handle: a teacher declaring `waitlist` has *chosen* to be found while full, so ranking them is no longer hostile — the question stops being the platform's to guess. And it gives the dormant-page problem an honest answer that isn't deletion. |
| DT-09 | A profile completeness score, shown to the teacher, would raise supply quality — every marketplace ships one | Standard supply-side lever; the `[SURFACE]` draft already had "a private completeness nudge showing the ranking cost" | ❌ REJECTED **as a score**; confirmed as a **specific checklist**. A percentage necessarily averages the self-authored bucket with the not-authorable one — so a teacher with no discography is permanently sub-100%, and the product has quietly told them their evidence is deficient. That is precisely what 06.02.02 D-03 forbids ("absence of credits is never rendered as an absence of trust"), arriving through a progress bar instead of an empty state. It also runs into domain **D-04** (no motivation-by-scaffolding, no completion-as-measure) — recorded against students, and the reasoning holds for a teacher whose income depends on the number. **Resolution**: name the missing item and its concrete effect ("no intro video — you won't appear in video-preview results"). Dismissible. Never a number, never a ranking, never a nag. |
| DT-10 | The tuition facet should be auto-enabled for musicians whose credits look teaching-shaped, to attack the domain's cold start | The domain has a genuine cold-start problem (index Q-03), the facet is one toggle, and pre-filling works (Happy Path step 2) | ❌ REJECTED — it fixes cold start by lying. A published teaching page for someone who does not teach is ghost supply: it ranks, it takes a trial booking, and it no-shows (06.02.04 Q-04, the platform's worst first impression). And the safeguarding failure is worse than the product one — auto-enabling a page means defaulting an age-range field for a person who has never been vetted and never asked to teach children. **Pre-filling is not publishing.** The pre-fill (step 2) is the right idea and it stays; the toggle stays the person's. Recorded because "just turn it on for everyone with credits" is a cheap, plausible growth suggestion this feature will receive. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Touches **[01 Identity, Profiles & Organizations](../../01-identity-profiles-organizations/)** — **owns the entity, the profile and the address**. This is a facet on it (D-03, DT-01). Three specific dependencies: (a) the identity header renders in every composition; (b) **composition-by-arrival-context is an identity-page capability this feature requires and does not own** (DT-05); (c) **location precision is an identity-level policy, not a per-facet preference** — this feature's district-only rule (DT-04) is defeated by any other facet publishing a point on the same address (Q-05). The single most important boundary in this sub-domain, and now the one with the most outbound requirements.
- Touches **[02 Credits & Attribution](../../02-credits-attribution/)** — supplies the credential block via 06.02.02. **Per-credit surfacing control (DT-06) is a requirement this feature places on that block.** Read-only; never blocks publish when unreachable (CX-02).
- Consumes **CX-M23 Public SEO Surfaces & Embeds** — the page is a primary indexed acquisition surface (DT-02). Indexing lag must be visible to the teacher (CX-03). Unpublish must resolve to the identity, never a 404, or the domain burns its own SEO equity.
- Consumes **CX-M16 Search & Discovery** — the structured bucket exists because 06.02.03 filters on it (CX-03). **Intake status is a new field this feature requires the index to carry** (DT-08).
- Consumes **CX-M31 Safeguarding & Minor Protection** — the under-18 age-range field is vetting-gated at **set** time, not enforcement time; expiry auto-retracts the field. Two escalations back to this cross-cut: the **minor teacher** (Q-03) and how the vetting statement composes for a logged-out parent — a statement, per 06.02.02 D-11, never a badge.
- Consumes **CX-M13 Geo, Location & Map Discovery** — supplies distance. **This feature is the cross-cut's most restrictive consumer**: it needs district-level geo *without* a point, which is not what a map cross-cut is built to do (DT-04). 06.02.03 DT-02 already found that "what distance means" is Education-specific; DT-04 adds that *what may be shown* is too.
- Consumes **CX-M08 Reviews, Ratings & Portable Reputation** — reviews render here, owned there. Reputation is portable across the person's facets, not per-facet — a consequence of DT-01 that cuts both ways (a bad gear-sale review follows them onto a teaching page; that is the multi-hyphenate's honest cost).
- Consumes **CX-M10 Media Handling & Audio Playback** — the intro video. **Copyright exposure escalated (Q-04)**: the most natural intro video is an infringing cover, hosted and monetised by the platform.
- Consumes **CX-M20 Localization, Currency & Timezone** — languages taught; rate in the reader's currency with the source rate visible; timezone for remote-only teachers.
- Consumes **CX-M06 Messaging & Contextual Inbox** — the only sanctioned contact channel. Load-bearing for safeguarding, not just for disintermediation: contact details in free text create an unlogged adult↔child channel.
- Consumes **CX-M18 Privacy, Consent & Data Portability** — account erasure collides with prepaid credits (06.01.02); the page is where the user performs the act but not where the money lives.
- Consumes **CX-M17 Canonical Data, Taxonomy & Entity Resolution** — instruments, levels, exam boards and languages are taxonomy-bound; same-name teachers are an entity-resolution problem.
- Touches **06.01.01 Lesson Slot Booking** — availability renders inline; publishing requires an availability rule. **Boundary sharpened (DT-08)**: 06.01.01 owns *when*, this feature owns *whether* (intake). They are not the same field and must not collapse.
- Touches **06.01.02 Packages, Credits & Rate Cards** — rate card renders inline; publishing requires one; the page must never display a rate the booking will not honour.
- Touches **06.02.02 Verified Credentials** — renders inside this page, not authorable by it (CX-02). This feature requires **per-credit** surfacing from it (DT-06). **Rendering grammar is 06.02.02's, not this page's**: the profile owns the tier a credential is published at (D-09), never the form it takes. Vetting is a dated statement naming the check and its jurisdiction (06.02.02 D-11); no evidence kind is ever a badge, tick, shield, summary label or count (06.02.02 D-02).
- Touches **06.02.03 Discovery & Match Criteria** — supplies every hard filter. **DT-08 gives its Q-02 a supply-side handle**: a teacher who declares `waitlist` has chosen to be found while full.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The tuition profile is a **facet** of the domain-01 identity, never a separate profile | Per DT-01 — a separate teacher profile contradicts the platform's central structural fact (the multi-hyphenate) and splits the credits that make the teacher credible. |
| D-02 | Content is split by **authorship model** — self-authored, structured, not-authorable — enforced at field level, not page level | The not-authorable bucket is the only thing a competitor cannot copy. Making the split explicit keeps it from eroding into "another bio field". A row may mix buckets; "they said" and "we verified" must stay visually distinct (CX-02). |
| D-03 | Publishing requires a rate card and an availability rule | An unbookable teacher page wastes the reader's only click; the domain's funnel is too thin to spend it. |
| D-04 | Self-assertion is permitted and shown **beside** evidence, not adjudicated | The platform verifies facts (credits, checks), not teaching ability. A great teacher with no credits must not be implicitly demeaned — 06.02.02 D-03. |
| D-05 | **Intake status is a profile field, distinct from calendar availability** | Per DT-08 — teaching is termly, and "taking students from September" is expressible in no calendar. Also gives 06.02.03 Q-02 a supply-side handle and the dormant page an answer that isn't deletion. |
| D-06 | **Location is district + travel radius pre-booking. No pin, no offset circle. Precise address only to a booked student, via the booking record** | Per DT-04 — "their room" is a home, the reader is a logged-out stranger, and the teacher works with children. An offset circle is not anonymisation; its centre is the address. Academy addresses are business addresses and render precisely. |
| D-07 | **Composition is determined by arrival context**; the identity header is the only constant | Per DT-05 — a parent and a Producer cannot get the same page. Resolves the *behavior* Q-01's URL shape must satisfy. |
| D-08 | **Credit surfacing is the teacher's choice, per credit, silently, at no cost** | Per DT-06 — the wedge can auto-publish the fact that loses a parent's booking. All-or-nothing forces a choice between an unhelpful credit and no evidence. Hiding is not adjudicated and leaves no gap. |
| D-09 | **Claims are public and indexed; the evidence chain is account-gated.** Scraper defence is compositional first | The claim is the acquisition asset (DT-02) and must be crawlable; the chain is the graph a competitor wants. 06.02.02 Q-03 owns the final line — this is the profile's position and rationale. |
| D-10 | **An academy never unpublishes a teacher's facet.** It curates its roster and may withdraw its vouch; revocation renders as absence, never as an announcement | Per DT-07 — otherwise an academy holds a departing teacher's public presence hostage, which restrictive covenants make a certainty, not a risk. Corrects a defect in the `[SURFACE]` draft. |
| D-11 | **No completeness score.** Named missing items with concrete effects, dismissible | Per DT-09 — a percentage averages the authored bucket with the un-authorable one, quietly telling a teacher with no discography they are deficient (06.02.02 D-03) and importing motivation-by-scaffolding (domain D-04) through a progress bar. |
| D-12 | **The facet is never auto-enabled.** Pre-fill yes; publish is always the person's act | Per DT-10 — auto-publishing creates ghost supply that no-shows, and defaults an age-range field for someone never vetted and never asking to teach children. |
| D-13 | **Contact details in free text are blocked at save with an explanation, not silently stripped** | Disintermediation is the lesser harm. The greater one: an unlogged, unmonitored adult↔child channel outside every safeguarding control the domain has. Silent stripping teaches the teacher we're broken and doesn't teach them why. |
| D-14 | **Vetting expiry auto-retracts the under-18 age range; it never unpublishes the page** | The profile must not keep advertising what the gate (06.01.04) will refuse. But a lapsed check is an administrative fact, not a public accusation — the teacher is warned hard, the stranger sees no badge of shame. |
