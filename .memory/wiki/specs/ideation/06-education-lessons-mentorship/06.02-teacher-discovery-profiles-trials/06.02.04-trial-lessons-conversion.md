# Feature: Trial Lessons & Conversion

> **Number**: 06.02.04
> **Parent**: [Teacher Discovery, Profiles & Trials](./06.02-teacher-discovery-profiles-trials-index.md)
> **Status**: [PARTIAL]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The single lesson that decides a multi-year relationship. A trial is a discounted or free first
lesson, capped to one per teacher–student pair, whose only real job is conversion into a recurring
series (06.01.01). It exists as its own feature because its economics and abuse surface are nothing
like a normal lesson: it is priced below cost deliberately, it is the domain's primary acquisition
mechanic, and free-first-lesson is the most farmable offer on the platform.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Teacher**: chooses whether to offer trials and at what price (free / reduced / full), caps how many they'll give per month (their time is finite and trials pay badly), sees trial→series conversion — the number that tells them whether their page is attracting the wrong students. **As Student**: books one trial per teacher, may trial 2–3 teachers before choosing, converts to a series with the credit applied, or walks away with no obligation and no awkward conversation. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. |
| Operator | Config | As an academy: sets a house trial policy (all staff offer a free 20-minute taster) and sees conversion across teachers — the metric that identifies which staff to keep. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Booking a trial is the act that makes someone a Student — this is the exact conversion boundary between the two personas. |

## Behavior

A trial is a normal occurrence (06.01.01) with three differences: a trial price, a **one-per-pair
cap**, and a conversion moment attached to its end. The conversion moment matters most — the decision
to commit to a year of Tuesdays is made in the ten minutes *after* a good first lesson, and if the
product's answer is "email me and we'll sort it out", that is where the domain leaks its funnel.

**No-obligation must be real and visible.** The reason students avoid trials is the awkwardness of
saying no to a musician who was kind to them for 30 minutes. The platform's job is to absorb that
awkwardness — walking away should be a silent default, not a conversation.

**The trial's outcome is inferred, not asked.** Conversion within the decay window (7 days) reads as
"good fit"; a walk reads as "undetermined" — never as "bad teacher". Asking a student who just chose
not to buy to rate the fit contradicts D-04 (walking away owes no message and no rating) and taxes
exactly the moment the platform is trying to keep frictionless. Explicit outcome data would be
cleaner for the funnel dashboard, but the honest, no-obligation walk is worth more than the datapoint
(DT-04). The conversion event itself is the signal.

### Happy Path

1. Student finds a teacher (06.02.03) and books a trial from the result card
2. Trial is booked as a single occurrence at the trial price; one-per-pair cap is enforced silently
3. Lesson is delivered (06.01.04) — same room, same safeguarding gate, no reduced protections
4. In-room, at the end, the teacher can propose a standing slot and the student can accept **there**
5. Student converts: the trial fee applies to the pack, and the series starts next week
6. Or: the student walks, no message required, no explanation owed

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Student books trials with 5 teachers at once | Permitted and normal — comparison is the point; teachers see "trial" and price accordingly | Teacher: labelled as a trial, so their expectations are calibrated |
| Serial free-trial farming (free lessons forever, never converting) | One-per-pair cap contains it per teacher; **cross-teacher farming is the real hole** — 40 teachers is 40 free lessons. Level-1 lean: no hard platform-wide cap (it punishes the many genuine comparison shoppers to stop the few); instead a soft signal — after ~5 un-converted free trials the account stops seeing *free* trial offers but can still book *reduced/full* first lessons (DT-05). Final policy is the owner's economics call | Farmer: free-trial CTAs quietly stop appearing; genuine shopper never notices. `[PENDING — Q-01, owner decision]` |
| Sockpuppet accounts to re-trial one teacher | Identity (01) + Trust & Safety (24) problem, not solvable here | Routed out of the domain |
| Trial goes badly | Student walks; **no rating prompt**, no explanation, no message | Student: nothing. Silence is the feature |
| Student converts, then quits after two lessons | Normal churn; nothing special | — |
| Teacher no-shows a free trial | Costs the student nothing financially and costs the platform a first impression — the worst-value failure available. The reliability signal (06.01.04 / domain 24) must fire hardest here, not softest, precisely because no money changed hands. Level-1 lean: one-tap priority rebook with another teacher offering a trial, plus a hard reliability hit on the no-show. A cash credit is meaningless when the lesson was free | Student: "Sorry — {teacher} didn't show. Here are 3 teachers free this week." `[PENDING — /write-be-spec]` on the exact reliability penalty weighting |
| Minor's trial | Full safeguarding gate applies — **a trial is not a reduced-protection lesson** | Guardian: same consent flow as any lesson |
| Teacher offers a free trial then pressures for commitment in-room | A real, quiet harm. The conversion CTA must be student-initiated | Student: the walk-away path is always present and never requires a message |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Trial availability check | Skeleton on the result card |
| Empty | Teacher offers no trials | "This teacher doesn't offer trials — book a first lesson at {rate}". Not an error |
| Populated | Trial available | "Free 30-min trial · next Tue 17:00" on the card |
| Error | Booking failed | Retry, slot held |
| Partial | Trial delivered, not yet converted | Student: "Book a regular slot with {teacher}" — one nudge at ~48h, CTA persists but decays to silent after 7 days; never re-fires |
| Consumed | Trial used for this pair | Trial CTA replaced with the standard rate. Silent, not a rebuke |
| Converted | Series started | Trial fee credited against the pack |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A trial is just a discounted first lesson and needs no separate feature | The gate's own test — "Password Reset (one flow, even with edge cases) → FEATURE" suggests this might not even merit that | ✅ CONFIRMED as a feature (not promoted to sub-domain, not merged away). It stays separate from 06.01 for one reason: its **abuse surface and conversion mechanics are unlike any other lesson**. A free lesson is the most farmable object in the domain, the one-per-pair cap is an invariant no other occurrence has, and conversion is the funnel's narrowest point. Those don't belong in the booking sub-domain's credit-ledger logic. |
| DT-02 | Conversion happens after the trial, via a follow-up email or a prompt in the app | Standard marketplace funnel thinking — nurture the lead | ❌ REJECTED — the decision is made **in the room, in the last two minutes**, while the student is still warm and the teacher is right there. This is the same capture-at-source principle the platform's thesis rests on (`meta/problem-statement.md`: "no system is present at the moment of creation, so the record is always a reconstruction") applied to a commercial decision instead of a credit. Deferring conversion to an email reconstructs a decision that was live and is now cold — and the teacher will do it off-platform by text, which is worse. **The conversion CTA belongs inside 06.01.04's room.** |
| DT-03 | Free trials should be encouraged platform-wide to accelerate the domain's cold start | Growth reasoning — free trials are the standard supply-side unlock, and the domain has a genuine cold-start problem | ❌ REJECTED as a platform default. It burns the domain's supply on exactly the wrong people. A working musician's teaching hour is their **rent** — `meta/personas.md` says they live on "a portfolio of small income streams" — and a mandated free trial taxes the poorest persona on the platform to subsidise acquisition. Worse, free-trial farmers are attracted in proportion to how free it is. The trial price stays the **teacher's** decision (with an academy-level house policy as the Operator's option), and the platform may recommend but never mandate. |
| DT-04 | The trial's outcome should be captured explicitly — the student rates the fit after the lesson — because a conversion signal alone can't tell "loved it, can't afford it" from "wasn't for me" | Standard marketplace instrumentation — richer outcome data improves matching and teacher feedback | ❌ REJECTED. Asking a student who just chose not to buy to rate the teacher directly contradicts D-04 (the walk owes no message and no rating) and re-introduces the exact awkwardness the feature exists to absorb. The confounding of "can't afford" vs "wasn't for me" is real but is the cheaper harm — worth less than a genuinely silent, obligation-free walk. **Outcome is inferred from the conversion event**; the funnel dashboard loses one dimension of resolution and keeps the trust that makes people trial at all. Resolves Q-02. |
| DT-05 | Cross-teacher free-trial farming must be stopped with a hard platform-wide trial cap | The one-per-pair cap only contains farming per teacher; a determined farmer hits 40 teachers for 40 free lessons | ❌ REJECTED as a *hard* cap. Comparison shopping across 2–3 teachers is not just legitimate, it is the feature's whole point (a student *should* trial before committing to a year). A hard cap punishes the many honest shoppers to deter the few farmers. **Preferred: a soft, silent throttle** — after roughly 5 un-converted *free* trials an account simply stops being shown *free* trial offers while still being able to book reduced/full first lessons. The farmer's supply of free lessons dries up; the genuine shopper, who converts, never approaches the threshold and never notices. Sockpuppet re-entry remains a domain 01 / 24 problem, not solvable here. The exact threshold is the owner's economics call (Q-01). |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The conversion moment lives **in the room**, at the end of the trial | Per DT-02 — the decision is made warm and dies cold; deferring it to email hands it to an off-platform text message. |
| D-02 | Trial price is the **teacher's** decision; the platform never mandates free | Per DT-03 — a mandated free trial taxes the platform's poorest persona to buy growth. |
| D-03 | One trial per teacher–student **pair**, capped silently | Cross-teacher comparison is legitimate and must not be punished; per-pair repetition is farming. |
| D-04 | Walking away requires **no message and no rating** | The awkwardness of saying no is why students don't trial. Absorbing it is the feature. |
| D-05 | A trial carries **full** safeguarding protections | A discounted lesson is not a discounted duty of care. Stated explicitly because "it's only a trial" is exactly the reasoning that produces an incident. |
| D-06 | Trial outcome is **inferred from conversion**, never rated explicitly | Per DT-04 — an explicit fit-rating asks a favour of someone who just walked, contradicting the no-obligation walk (D-04). |
| D-07 | The post-trial nudge fires **once at ~48h**, decays to silent after **7 days**, and never re-fires | Per the Notifications cross-cut — a nudge that repeats becomes the awkward chase the walk-away path exists to avoid. |
