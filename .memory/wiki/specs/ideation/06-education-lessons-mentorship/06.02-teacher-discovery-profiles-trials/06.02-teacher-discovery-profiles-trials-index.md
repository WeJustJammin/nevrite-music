## Overview

How a student finds a teacher and decides to commit. This sub-domain carries **the domain's unfair
advantage**: everywhere else, a teacher is a bio, a headshot and a self-asserted claim to have
"toured internationally" — here, a teacher's verified credits from domain 02 make the claim checkable.
The domain rationale states it plainly: "verified credits beat a bio and a headshot decisively." That
is the only thing in this sub-domain a competitor cannot copy, so the structure puts it in its own
feature rather than burying it as a profile field.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: four capabilities in one loop. The profile supplies what matching ranks
on; credentials are the highest-weighted signal in that ranking; matching produces a shortlist a trial
converts; and trial outcomes feed back into ranking. Cut any one out and the other three lose their
input.
