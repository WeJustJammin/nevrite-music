# Feature: Lesson Packages, Credits & Rate Cards

> **Number**: 06.01.02
> **Parent**: [Lesson Booking, Packages & Delivery](./06.01-lesson-booking-packages-delivery-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

How tuition is priced and paid for. Teachers publish a rate card (per-instrument, per-duration,
per-mode) and sell **packages** — "10 lessons for £350" — which the student draws down as credits,
one per delivered occurrence. This is the single sharpest divergence from Services Marketplace (05):
a service is escrowed once against a deliverable and released on acceptance, whereas a lesson pack is
money paid up front against a *relationship*, spent gradually, and governed by expiry and refund
rules that a one-off escrow has no concept of.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Teacher**: publishes a rate card (£/30-45-60 min, per instrument, remote vs in-person, travel surcharge), defines packages with a discount and an expiry, offers sliding-scale or concession rates, sees credits outstanding per student and the liability they represent. **As Student**: sees the rate card before booking, buys a pack, watches a credit balance fall as lessons are delivered, sees expiry dates and what happens if the pack lapses. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. A Producer engaging a musician for a session uses Services (05) escrow, not lesson credits — the two must never share a wallet. |
| Operator | Config | As an academy: sets house rates or a rate floor/ceiling teachers work within, sets the revenue share, sees aggregate credit liability across all their teachers (the number that matters if a teacher leaves mid-pack). |
| Fan | None | Not visible — no fan-facing entry point. |

## Behavior

A rate card is per-teacher and dimensioned by the things that actually change a lesson's price:
instrument, duration, mode (remote / teacher's room / student's home), and level. A package bundles
N credits at a discount with an expiry window. Purchase moves money once; delivery draws credits one
at a time. **The credit ledger is the sub-domain's shared state** — 06.01.01 reserves against it,
06.01.03 burns or restores against it, and 06.01.04's delivery record is what proves a draw was
earned.

The unresolved half is what a credit legally *is* — see Q-01 on the parent index. If credits are
refundable stored value, the platform is holding a liability; if they are non-refundable expiring
entitlements, the consumer-protection surface is real and jurisdictional. Everything below is
written to survive either answer.

### Happy Path

1. Teacher publishes a rate card and one or more packages
2. Student picks a package on the teacher's profile and pays via the Payments cross-cut
3. System credits the student's balance **with that teacher** and confirms
4. Student books occurrences (06.01.01); each confirmed occurrence **reserves** one credit
5. Lesson is delivered (06.01.04); the reserved credit is **consumed** and the teacher's earning accrues
6. Balance nears zero; system prompts a top-up before the standing slot is at risk

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Student books more occurrences than credits held | Booking allowed up to balance; beyond it requires a top-up or an explicit pay-per-lesson | Student: "You have 2 credits — booking 4 lessons needs a top-up" with the shortfall priced |
| Pack expires with credits unspent | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5]` — depends entirely on Q-01 (money vs entitlement). Consumer law in several jurisdictions restricts forfeiting prepaid value. | Student: expiry warnings at 30/7/1 days, minimum |
| Teacher raises rates mid-pack | Existing credits honour the rate paid — a credit is a lesson, not a sum of money | Student: unaffected. Teacher: "Rate change applies to new packs only" |
| Teacher stops teaching / leaves the platform with credits outstanding | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5]` — this is the scenario Q-02 (escrow vs pay-on-purchase) exists to answer. If money was released on purchase, the platform cannot make the student whole from the teacher's balance. | `[PENDING]` |
| Student wants to move credits to a different teacher | Rejected by default — credits are teacher-scoped because the price is teacher-specific | Student: "Credits are with {teacher}" + refund path per policy |
| Academy teacher leaves and takes students | Credit liability sits with the academy, not the individual, when the pack was sold under an academy rate card | Operator: liability report; Student: continuity of credits under a reassigned teacher |
| Chargeback on a partly-spent pack | `[PENDING — /create-prd]` — payments/disputes surface, routed to the Payments cross-cut and domain 24 | `[PENDING]` |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | Lesson packs can reuse the Services Marketplace (05) escrow model | The two domains are adjacent by design and both involve a musician being paid | ❌ REJECTED — decisively, and this is the strongest structural evidence for the domain's independent existence (it is exactly the contrast the domain rationale says "must be legible"). Escrow releases against **acceptance of a deliverable**. A lesson has no deliverable and no acceptance event — nobody "accepts" a piano lesson. Forcing tuition through escrow yields either a hostage release step nobody performs or an auto-release that makes escrow decorative. Packs need a **credit ledger with expiry**, which escrow does not have. |
| DT-02 | Prepaid lesson credits are a financial-regulatory question, not a UX one | Domain expertise: prepaid stored value is regulated in several jurisdictions; music schools have been caught by exactly this on closure | ✅ CONFIRMED — and escalated. If credits are refundable stored value the platform holds a liability on its balance sheet and may touch e-money/prepaid rules; if they are expiring entitlements, consumer-protection law limits forfeiture. Either way this is a `/create-prd-security` compliance input, and `meta/constraints.md` currently has no line for it. Raised as Q-01 on the parent index. |
| DT-03 | Credits should be platform-wide (spendable with any teacher), because that's better for students | Agent's own first instinct — a portable wallet is obviously friendlier | ❌ REJECTED — it cannot price. Teacher A charges £30 and teacher B £70; a portable "credit" would either be a currency (making the platform a bank, per DT-02) or would silently transfer value between teachers. Credits are teacher-scoped. The genuinely hard case this leaves — an academy where students rotate between teachers — is handled by scoping the pack to the **academy** rather than the individual, which is why the academy rate card exists. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Consumes **Payments, Escrow & Payouts** (global cross-cut) — pack purchase, teacher payout, refunds. Note this feature deliberately uses the *payout* half and **not** the escrow half (DT-01).
- Consumes **Subscriptions & Entitlements** (global cross-cut) — a monthly "4 lessons/month" plan is a subscription; the entitlement engine is not rebuilt here. The seam: the cross-cut owns billing and entitlement grants; this feature owns what a lesson credit *means* and when it burns.
- Consumes **Tax & Fiscal Compliance** (global cross-cut) — tuition VAT treatment is jurisdictionally weird (education is VAT-exempt in some regimes, which the generic marketplace tax engine will not assume).
- Consumes **Localization, Currency & Timezone** (global cross-cut) — cross-border remote tuition is normal, so a rate card is multi-currency.
- Touches **06.01.01 Slot Booking** — confirmation reserves a credit.
- Touches **06.01.03 Cancellation Policy** — the policy decides whether a credit burns or returns; this feature owns the ledger it acts on.
- Touches **[05 Services Marketplace](../../05-services-marketplace/)** — the deliberate contrast (DT-01). One human may hold service escrow and lesson credits simultaneously; the wallets must not merge.
- Touches **[23 Career, Finance & Business](../../23-career-finance-business/)** — tuition is a large share of a working musician's income and must appear in their income picture.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Credits are **teacher-scoped** (or academy-scoped), never platform-wide | Per DT-03 — a portable credit cannot price against heterogeneous rate cards without becoming currency. |
| D-02 | A credit represents **a lesson at the rate paid**, not a sum of money | Makes mid-pack rate rises fair automatically and avoids re-pricing a balance the student already bought. |
| D-03 | Tuition does **not** use escrow | Per DT-01 — there is no deliverable and no acceptance event to release against. |
