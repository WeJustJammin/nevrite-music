## Overview

The commercial and operational core of 1:1 tuition: how a lesson gets booked, how it gets paid
for in advance as a pack of credits rather than per-transaction, what happens when somebody
cancels, and what actually occurs in the room. This is where Education diverges hardest from
Services Marketplace (05) — a service engagement is escrowed once against a deliverable, whereas
a lesson relationship is a recurring series drawing down a pre-purchased credit balance under a
cancellation policy the teacher sets and the platform enforces.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: four capabilities that are meaningless apart. A recurring series
generates occurrences; each occurrence consumes a package credit; the cancellation policy decides
whether a missed occurrence burns that credit or returns it as a make-up; and the delivery record
is the evidence that decides which of those happened. Change the cancellation policy and the
credit ledger changes; change the pack structure and the booking flow changes.

| D-01 | Booking, packages, cancellation and delivery are ONE sub-domain, not four sibling features | They share one ledger. A cancellation is only meaningful as a credit event; a credit is only meaningful against a scheduled occurrence; the delivery record is the evidence that settles both. Splitting them puts the credit ledger's invariants across four files with no owner. | Node Classification Gate, `/ideate-discover` Step 3 |

## Open Questions

| # | Question | Owner | Deferred To |
|---|----------|-------|-------------|
| Q-01 | Are lesson credits **money** (refundable, a stored-value liability, possibly regulated) or **entitlements** (non-refundable, expiring)? This is a financial-regulatory question, not a UX one — stored value can trigger e-money rules in some jurisdictions. | User | `/create-prd-security` (compliance) |
| Q-02 | Does the platform hold pack money in escrow and release per-lesson, or pay the teacher on purchase? Escrow protects the student against a teacher who vanishes mid-pack; paying up front is what teachers actually want. | User | MoSCoW / `/create-prd` |
| Q-03 | **[OWNER]** Can a lesson be delivered **in person** (teacher's home, student's home, an academy room)? Everything here assumes it can, which drags in address exchange, travel time between slots, and — with minors — a physical-meeting safeguarding surface far heavier than a recorded video room. A scope call only the owner takes: no later stage resolves it, because `/create-prd-security` can design the safeguarding controls for a physical adult↔minor meeting but cannot decide whether that surface exists. **This is the canonical sub-domain instance of [domain Q-06](../education-lessons-mentorship-index.md)**, which carries the full cost statement and is already `[OWNER]` at `/create-prd`; it is also re-stated by each child that pays part of the cost (06.01.01 Q-03, 06.01.03 Q-06, 06.01.04 Q-03) — **one decision, five ids**. **Deferral target re-pointed** from the completed `/ideate-discover` Step 5 run to `/create-prd`, matching domain Q-06. | User | `/create-prd` |
