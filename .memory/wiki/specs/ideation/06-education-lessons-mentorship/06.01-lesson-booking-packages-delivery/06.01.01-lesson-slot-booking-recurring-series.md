# Feature: Lesson Slot Booking & Recurring Series

> **Number**: 06.01.01
> **Parent**: [Lesson Booking, Packages & Delivery](./06.01-lesson-booking-packages-delivery-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

How a lesson gets onto two calendars. A teacher publishes teaching availability; a student books
a one-off or — far more commonly — a recurring series ("Tuesdays 5pm, term time"). The recurring
series, not the single booking, is the real primitive here: tuition is a standing appointment that
persists for years, and the product's job is to make that standing appointment survive holidays,
tours, term boundaries and the fact that working musicians' calendars are wrecked by gigs.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Teacher**: publishes recurring teaching availability (day/time blocks, per-instrument, in-person vs remote), sets slot length and buffer, blocks out tour dates, accepts or declines requests, proposes a reschedule. **As Student**: browses a teacher's open slots, requests a one-off or a recurring series, reschedules an occurrence, sees the whole series on one calendar. The same person does both — a teacher's own lesson with their mentor appears in the same calendar as the lessons they teach. |
| Producer | Read-only | Sees a lesson block as a busy period on a shared studio calendar when a teacher also engineers out of that room — enough to avoid double-booking the space, never the student's identity or what is being taught. |
| Operator | Config | As an academy or a studio renting rooms to teachers: allocates rooms to lesson blocks, sets opening hours and term calendar that bound what teachers can publish, sees room occupancy. Does not book on the student's behalf. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Tuition is not a fan surface — a Fan who wants lessons is acting as a Musician (student) and is lensed as one from that moment. No fan-facing entry point exists. |

## Behavior

A teacher's availability is a **rule**, not a list of slots: "Tuesdays and Thursdays, 16:00–20:00,
30/45/60-min slots, 15-min buffer, remote or at my studio, term-time only." The system materializes
bookable occurrences from the rule. A student booking a recurring series claims the same slot in the
rule indefinitely (or for a fixed count), which removes that slot from everyone else's view for the
life of the series. Each materialized occurrence is what a credit is spent against (06.01.02) and
what a cancellation acts on (06.01.03).

The hard part is that a recurring series must tolerate exceptions without dissolving. Skipping one
week is an exception to a series, not a cancellation of it — a distinction the UI must make obvious
because getting it wrong loses a teacher their standing slot.

### Happy Path

1. Teacher publishes a recurring availability rule with slot length, buffer, mode and term bounds
2. Student opens the teacher's profile, sees materialized open slots in the student's own timezone
3. Student requests "Tuesdays 17:00, weekly, ongoing" and selects a package to draw against
4. System places a hold on the whole series and notifies the teacher
5. Teacher accepts; the series is confirmed, all future occurrences appear on both calendars
6. System schedules reminders and confirms the first occurrence's credit reservation

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Two students request the same slot within seconds | First confirmed request wins the slot; the loser's hold is released | Loser: "That slot was taken while you were booking" with the teacher's nearest alternatives pre-filtered |
| Teacher books a tour/session over an existing recurring series | System blocks silent overwrite — the series occurrences must be explicitly cancelled or rescheduled first | Teacher: conflict list showing every affected occurrence and its student, with bulk reschedule/cancel actions |
| Series crosses a DST boundary | Occurrence anchors to the teacher's local wall-clock time; the student's displayed time shifts | Student: an explicit "this lesson moves to 16:00 your time from 27 Oct" notice, not a silent shift |
| Student in a different country books a remote series | Times render per viewer via the Localization cross-cut; the underlying anchor is unambiguous | Both: each sees their own local time plus the counterpart's, side by side |
| Teacher goes on tour for 6 weeks | Series is suspended, not cancelled — the slot is reserved and no credits are consumed | Student: "Your Tuesday slot is held until 12 Sep" — series resumes automatically |
| Term ends / academy closes | Occurrences stop at the term bound set by the Operator; the series does not silently roll into a holiday | Both: "This series ends 18 Dec — re-book for spring term" prompt |
| Minor student books an adult teacher | Booking is gated by the Safeguarding cross-cut before any hold is placed | Student/guardian: consent + vetting gate, not a booking form |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Calendar opened | Skeleton week grid with the current week's shape |
| Empty | Teacher has published no availability | Teacher: "Publish your teaching hours" CTA. Student: "This teacher isn't taking bookings — ask to join their waitlist" |
| Populated | Availability rule materialized | Week/month grid of open slots in the viewer's timezone |
| Error | Booking request failed | "Couldn't confirm — the slot is still held for 5 minutes" with retry, hold timer visible |
| Partial | Series confirmed but some occurrences conflict | Series shown with conflicted occurrences flagged amber and a resolve action |
| Pending | Requested, teacher has not responded | Student: "Awaiting {teacher} — expires in 48h", slot held meanwhile |
| Suspended | Teacher blocked a date range | Series greyed with a resume date |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The primitive is a single booking, and recurrence is a convenience layer on top | Naive reading of the candidate name "1:1 Lesson Booking" | ❌ REJECTED — inverted. Real tuition is a standing weekly slot held for years; the one-off is the exception (a trial, or a masterclass). Building single-first makes recurrence a bolt-on, and every hard problem here (exceptions, term bounds, suspension, slot ownership) lives in recurrence. The series is the entity; the occurrence is derived. |
| DT-02 | This duplicates the `Availability, Scheduling & Reservation` cross-cut and should not exist | Cross-cut list — that mechanism explicitly serves education-lessons-mentorship | ❌ REJECTED, with a boundary drawn. The cross-cut owns generic slots, holds and conflict detection. It does not own: a **series** that survives exceptions, **slot ownership** (this Tuesday 5pm belongs to Amy until she gives it up — a venue booking has no equivalent), or **term bounds**. Those are tuition semantics. The seam is recorded in the sub-domain CX. |
| DT-03 | A working musician teaching between gigs will wreck their students' standing slots constantly, and this is a domain expert's first objection | Persona: Musician's calendar is dominated by sessions and gigs; a teacher is the same person | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it makes "suspend series without losing the slot" a first-class state rather than an edge case. It also creates the strongest CX in the domain: a gig booked in 17 or a session in 07 must *see* teaching commitments, or the platform will cause the double-booking it exists to prevent. |

## Cross-Cut Notes

- Consumes **Availability, Scheduling & Reservation** (global cross-cut) — slot materialization, holds, conflict detection. This feature layers series/ownership/term semantics on top.
- Consumes **Localization, Currency & Timezone** (global cross-cut) — a remote lesson has two timezones and a DST boundary that shifts one of them; this is the domain's most common support complaint.
- Consumes **Notifications & Alerts** (global cross-cut) — reminders, request expiry, reschedule proposals.
- Consumes **Safeguarding & Minor Protection** (global cross-cut) — a minor↔adult booking is gated before a hold exists.
- Touches **06.01.02 Packages, Credits & Rate Cards** — an occurrence reserves a credit at confirmation, not at delivery.
- Touches **06.01.03 Cancellation, Make-Up & No-Show** — the policy acts on occurrences this feature materializes.
- Touches **[17 Live Booking & Settlement](../../17-live-booking-settlement/)** and **[07 Music Projects](../../07-music-projects-collaboration/)** — the teacher is a gigging musician; a gig must be visible against teaching commitments (see DT-03).
- Touches **[16 Venues, Studios & Spaces](../../16-venues-studios-spaces/)** — an in-person lesson consumes a room the Operator owns.

> For detailed cross-cut analysis, see [06.01 CX](./06.01-lesson-booking-packages-delivery-cx.md) and the [domain CX](../education-lessons-mentorship-cx.md).

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | The **series** is the entity; the occurrence is materialized from it | Per DT-01. Determines the data model downstream: cancelling an occurrence must not orphan the series, and the student's claim on a recurring slot must outlive any single lesson. |
| D-02 | Teaching availability is a **rule**, not a hand-placed slot list | A teacher who must hand-place slots every week will stop, and their calendar goes stale — the exact failure mode that kills every teacher-marketplace calendar. |
