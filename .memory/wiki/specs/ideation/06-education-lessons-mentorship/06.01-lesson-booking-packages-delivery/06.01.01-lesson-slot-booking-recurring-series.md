# Feature: Lesson Slot Booking & Recurring Series

> **Number**: 06.01.01
> **Parent**: [Lesson Booking, Packages & Delivery](./06.01-lesson-booking-packages-delivery-index.md)
> **Status**: [DEEP]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

How a lesson gets onto two calendars. A teacher publishes teaching availability; a student books
a one-off or — far more commonly — a recurring series ("Tuesdays 5pm, term time"). The recurring
series, not the single booking, is the real primitive here: tuition is a standing appointment that
persists for years, and the product's job is to make that standing appointment survive holidays,
tours, term boundaries and the fact that working musicians' calendars are wrecked by gigs.

The problem it solves is **slot tenure**. Every competitor models a lesson as an event; teachers and
students experience it as a *tenancy* — "Tuesday 5pm is Amy's" — and every hard behaviour in this
file (suspension, exceptions, term bounds, the annual re-sort, who loses the slot and why) is a
consequence of taking that tenancy seriously. Get it wrong and a teacher loses their book; get it
right and the standing slot is the switching cost that keeps the domain's habit surface alive.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Teacher**: publishes recurring teaching availability rules (weekday × time window, per-instrument, per-mode, slot length, buffer, lead time, term bounds); marks slots as series-claimable or one-off-only (DT-05); blocks out tour/session dates; accepts, declines or counter-proposes a series request within 48h; suspends a series without releasing the slot; bulk re-slots their whole book at the September shuffle (DT-07); sees, per slot, who holds it and their paid-through date. **As Student**: browses a teacher's open slots in their own timezone; requests a one-off, a trial (06.02.04) or a recurring series; skips a single occurrence without losing the slot; proposes a swap; sees the whole series and its paid-through horizon on one calendar. The same human does both — a teacher's own lesson with their mentor lands on the same calendar as the lessons they teach, and the system must detect when those collide (DT-10). |
| Producer | Read-only | Sees a lesson block as an opaque busy period on a **shared studio calendar** when a teacher also engineers out of that room — start, end, room, and the word "Lesson". Never the student's identity, age, instrument, or the fact that a minor is in the building. Enough to avoid double-booking the space and nothing more; the redaction is a safeguarding requirement (CX-M31), not a courtesy. Producers have no booking, accept, or reschedule action here. |
| Operator | Config | As an academy or a studio renting rooms to teachers: allocates rooms to lesson blocks; publishes the **term calendar** and opening hours that bound what their teachers may materialize; sets the re-enrolment checkpoint (default off — DT-06); sees room occupancy and per-teacher slot utilisation; reassigns a departing teacher's series to another teacher with per-student acceptance (never silently). Cannot book on a student's behalf, cannot see a student's series with a teacher outside their academy, and cannot evict a series to free a room without the teacher's action. |
| Fan | None | Not visible. Tuition is not a fan surface — a Fan who requests a lesson is acting as a Musician (student) and is lensed as one from that moment, exactly as booking a trial does (06.02.04). No fan-facing entry point exists, and the teacher's public page (06.02.01) shows availability as *shape* ("Tue/Thu evenings") without an actionable slot grid until the viewer has an account. |

## Behavior

A teacher's availability is a **rule**, not a hand-placed list of slots: "Tuesdays and Thursdays,
16:00–20:00, 30/45/60-min slots, 15-min buffer, remote or at my studio, term-time only, 24h minimum
lead time." The system materializes bookable occurrences from the rule. A student booking a
recurring series claims the same slot in that rule for the life of the series, which removes it from
everyone else's view. Each materialized occurrence is what a credit is reserved against (06.01.02),
what a cancellation acts on (06.01.03), and what a lesson room is opened for (06.01.04).

Three invariants govern everything below:

1. **The series holds the slot; occurrences hold the credits.** An ongoing series is unbounded, so it
   cannot reserve credits for all of its occurrences — only for those inside a rolling reservation
   horizon (DT-04). This is the correction that makes an "ongoing" series coherent at all.
2. **Tenure binds to payment, not to attendance.** A student who pays and doesn't turn up keeps their
   slot; a student who stops paying loses it after a stated grace (D-03, DT-08). Attendance is
   06.01.03's business, not the calendar's.
3. **An exception never dissolves the series.** Skipping one week, a holiday, a six-week tour and a
   term break are all *suspensions of occurrences*, not terminations of tenancy. The UI must make
   this obvious, because getting it wrong is how a teacher loses their standing book.

### Recurrence patterns supported

`weekly` · `fortnightly` (with an explicit anchor week) · `every-4-weeks`. All weekday-anchored.
Monthly-by-date is **not** offered (DT-14). Series length is `ongoing` (default) or `fixed-count(N)`.

### Tunable defaults

> Platform defaults, all owner-overridable at `/create-prd`; the per-teacher ones are also settable
> on the availability rule. Stated as numbers because "a reasonable window" is not implementable.

| Knob | Default | Range | Owner | Why this number |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Materialization horizon | 12 weeks ahead, or the end of the published term if later | 4–52 wks | Platform | Long enough for a parent to plan a term; short enough that a rule change doesn't rewrite a year of calendar |
| Reservation horizon (credits) | 28 days | 7–90 d | Platform | Roughly one pack of 4 weekly lessons — matches how packs are actually sold (06.01.02) |
| Series request hold | 48h, then auto-release | 12–96 h | Platform | A teacher checking their phone daily still clears it; longer and the slot is dead inventory |
| Transaction hold (single booking) | 5 min | 2–15 min | Platform | Consistent with CX-M05's hold-on-checkout; 06.01.02 owns the separate 15-min *payment* hold |
| Minimum lead time — new series | 24h | 0–168 h | Teacher | Teachers plan material; a series starting in 20 minutes is a mis-click |
| Minimum lead time — released one-off | 2h | 0–48 h | Teacher | Backfill only has value if it's bookable late |
| Inter-slot buffer | 15 min | 0–60 min | Teacher | Instrument changeover + notes |
| Travel buffer (in-person, student's home) | 30 min each side | 0–180 min | Teacher | Journey time is the teacher's cost; see D-09 |
| At-Risk grace after balance hits 0 | 2 occurrences **or** 14 days, whichever comes first | 0–8 occ | Teacher | Two missed top-ups is a real lapse; one is a busy week |
| DST-shift notice | 7 days before the shift | 1–28 d | Platform | Enough to re-plan a school night, not so early it's forgotten |
| Re-enrolment window (academy only) | Opens T-21d, closes T-7d before term end | — | Operator | Mirrors how academies actually re-enrol; default **off** (DT-06) |
| Reschedule / swap proposal expiry | 48h | 12–96 h | Platform | Same rationale as the request hold |
| Series request rate limit | 5 pending requests per student per teacher per 7 days | — | Platform | Blunts request-spam without touching a legitimate 2–3-teacher trial search (06.02.04) |

### Slot classes

| Class | Series-claimable? | One-off bookable? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `open` | Yes | Yes | The default teaching slot |
| `one-off-only` | **No** | Yes | The flex hour a fully-booked teacher keeps free so 06.01.03's make-ups have somewhere to land (DT-05) |
| `released` | No | Yes | A single occurrence freed by a cancellation — the tenancy still belongs to its holder (D-08) |
| `blocked` | No | No | Tour, gig, holiday, or an Operator closure |

### Happy Path

1. Teacher opens their teaching calendar and publishes an availability rule: `Tue + Thu, 16:00–20:00, 60-min slots, 15-min buffer, remote + my studio, term-time (academy calendar #4), 24h lead`.
2. System materializes `open` occurrences from the rule out to the materialization horizon (12 weeks / end of published term) and renders them on the teacher's grid in their local wall-clock time.
3. Student opens the teacher's profile (06.02.01), sees the materialized open slots **in the student's own timezone**, each labelled with the teacher's local time beside it.
4. Student selects `Tue 17:00`, chooses `weekly · ongoing`, and picks the pack to draw against (06.01.02). System shows: the first 4 occurrences by date, the credit cost of those 4, the balance after, and the cancellation policy that will pin to the series (06.01.03 D-02).
5. Student confirms. System places a **5-minute transaction hold** on the slot, validates that the teacher has a live rate card (06.01.02 Empty blocks booking) and that the Safeguarding gate passes for this pairing (CX-M31), then converts the hold to a **pending series request** and notifies the teacher. The request expires in 48h.
6. Teacher accepts. System confirms the series: the slot's class flips to claimed for the life of the series, it disappears from every other student's view, and occurrences appear on both calendars out to the materialization horizon.
7. System reserves credits **only for the occurrences inside the 28-day reservation horizon** (4 credits), capped by available balance. Occurrences beyond it are `scheduled · unreserved` and reserve as the horizon rolls forward.
8. System schedules reminders (CX-M07), writes the tenancy grant to the audit log (CX-M19), and shows both parties the paid-through date: "Reserved through 11 Aug · 6 credits remaining".

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Two students request the same slot within seconds** | CX-M05's atomic hold serializes it; first hold wins. The loser's request never becomes pending | Loser: "That slot was taken while you were booking" + the teacher's 3 nearest alternative slots pre-filtered to the same weekday/time band |
| **Two students hold the same slot; the first lets the 48h request expire** | Slot returns to `open`. The second student is **not** auto-promoted (D-08) — they were told no and must re-request | Second student: "Tue 17:00 is free again" push if they opted in at rejection time; otherwise nothing |
| **Student's balance hits 0 between confirmation and reservation** | Series still confirms — tenancy is not conditional on the ledger (invariant 2). The occurrence enters `scheduled · unreserved` and the series enters **At Risk** | Student: "Your Tuesday slot is held — top up by 30 Jul to keep it." Teacher: "Amy's balance is empty — slot held until 30 Jul" |
| **At-Risk grace elapses (2 occurrences / 14 days, no top-up)** | Series → **Lapsed**. Slot class returns to `open` and re-enters the market. Tenancy release is audit-logged (CX-M19) as an evidential event, because it is the kind of thing that gets disputed | Student: "Your Tuesday 17:00 has been released" — sent at T-7d, T-1d **and** at release, never only at release. Teacher: "Tue 17:00 is open again" |
| **Student tops up during the grace window** | Reservation resumes at the next occurrence; series → Confirmed. No re-request, no re-acceptance, tenancy never broke | Student: "Tuesday 17:00 is secure — reserved through 8 Sep" |
| **Teacher books a tour/session (17/07) over an existing series** | System blocks silent overwrite. The conflicting occurrences must be explicitly suspended, rescheduled or cancelled first; the gig cannot confirm until they are | Teacher: conflict list — every affected occurrence, its student, its date, with bulk suspend/reschedule/cancel. Blast radius stated up front: "This tour blocks 18 lessons across 6 students" |
| **Teacher accepts a series request at the same instant they accept a gig covering that slot** | Cross-domain race. Whichever write serializes first wins; the loser fails with the winner shown by name, never a generic conflict | Teacher: "Can't accept — you confirmed 'Sesh at Konk' for Tue 17:00 twelve seconds ago" |
| **Teacher goes on tour for 6 weeks** | Series **suspended**, not cancelled. Slot stays claimed, occurrences in the window are removed, **no credits are consumed or burned** (this is not a teacher cancellation under 06.01.03 — nothing was scheduled to cancel), and reservation resumes on the resume date | Student: "Your Tuesday slot is held until 12 Sep — no credits used." Teacher: one action, not 6 cancellations |
| **A cancellation arrives for an occurrence inside a suspended window** | Idempotent **no-op**. The occurrence does not exist; no ledger effect, no error toast, no no-show. Resolves the sub-domain CX-04 Q5 race | Student: "That lesson is already suspended — nothing to cancel, no credit used" |
| **Term ends / academy closes for the holiday** | Occurrences stop at the term bound; the **series persists and the slot stays claimed** across the break (DT-06). When the next term is published, occurrences materialize automatically into the same slot | Both: "No lessons 19 Dec – 5 Jan (Christmas). Your Tuesday 17:00 resumes 6 Jan." **Never** "re-book for spring" |
| **Academy has the re-enrolment checkpoint ON and the student misses the window** | Series → Lapsed at term end, slot released. This is the one case where a term boundary *can* end a tenancy, and only because an Operator deliberately configured it | Student: re-enrol prompt at T-21d, T-14d, T-7d; at close, "Your slot was released — Tue 17:00 is now open to others". Operator: unre-enrolled roster |
| **Next term's calendar isn't published yet** | Materialization stops at the last published bound. The series does **not** end and the slot does **not** release — it simply has no occurrences past that date | Student: "Lessons scheduled to 18 Dec. Spring dates aren't published yet — your slot is held." Operator: "3 teachers, 41 series waiting on the spring calendar" |
| **Series crosses a DST boundary** | The occurrence anchors to the **teacher's** local wall-clock time; the student's displayed time shifts | Student: "This lesson moves to 16:00 your time from 27 Oct" at T-7d — never a silent shift |
| **UK teacher ↔ US student across the asymmetric DST window** | UK clocks change the last Sunday in October, the US the first Sunday in November — a ~1-week window where the gap is 1h off (and ~3 weeks each March). The anchor rule handles it correctly; the *notice* is what's missing without this row | Student: an explicit notice for **each** shift, i.e. two notices a week apart in autumn, not one |
| **Teacher in London ↔ student in Sydney** | The gap moves by 2h across the year as the hemispheres shift in opposite directions | Both: the counterpart's local time is rendered on every occurrence, always, not only near a shift |
| **Teacher relocates country mid-series** | Wall-clock anchor would silently move every student by up to 8h. Blocked: relocation is an explicit **series migration** requiring per-student acceptance; unaccepted series suspend rather than move | Teacher: "Moving to Los Angeles shifts 7 students by 8 hours. Propose new times." Student: "Your teacher has moved — Tue 17:00 becomes Wed 01:00 your time. Accept or pick a new slot" |
| **Student in a different country books a remote series** | Times render per viewer via CX-M20; the anchor is unambiguous | Both: own local time primary, counterpart's local time secondary, side by side, on every occurrence |
| **Minor student books an adult teacher** | Gated by CX-M31 **before** any hold is placed. No hold, no pending request, no notification to the teacher — the booking form itself does not proceed | Student/guardian: the consent + vetting gate with the specific action that clears it, never a generic "unavailable" |
| **Student turns 18 mid-series** | Safeguarding posture changes for future occurrences. The gate re-evaluates; guardian delegated authority (domain D-03) lapses on the student's own timetable, not the calendar's | Student: "You now manage your own bookings." Guardian: "Your access to Amy's lessons ends 4 Sep" — before, not after |
| **Teacher's vetting expires in 30 days with live minor series** | New minor bookings are blocked at T-0; **existing** series are not cancelled — 06.01.04's join gate is the enforcement point (defence in depth, CX-07). Warn early because a lapsed check silently kills a book | Teacher: T-30/T-7/T-1 renewal prompts naming the count at risk ("11 lessons across 4 students") |
| **Teacher publishes availability but has no rate card** | Booking fails closed at hold time — 06.01.02's Empty state blocks the calendar entirely | Student: "This teacher hasn't published rates yet." Teacher: "Set your rates — your calendar is visible but unbookable" |
| **Teacher deletes an availability rule that has live series on it** | Blocked. The rule cannot be deleted while it materializes claimed slots; it can be *closed to new claims* instantly, which stops new bookings without touching existing tenancies | Teacher: "3 students hold slots in this rule. Close it to new bookings, or move them first" + the bulk re-slot action |
| **Teacher shrinks a rule's window (16:00–20:00 → 16:00–18:00) over a claimed 19:00 slot** | Same treatment as deletion: the claimed slot survives as an orphaned tenancy outside the rule and is flagged, rather than being destroyed by an edit to a settings form | Teacher: "Amy's 19:00 is outside your new hours. Keep it as an exception, or reschedule her" |
| **Teacher blocks a date range while a student is mid-booking inside it** | The 5-min transaction hold wins — it was placed first. The block applies to everything else in the range | Teacher: "Blocked 12–19 Aug. One booking completed in that window (Amy, Tue 17:00) — suspend it?" |
| **Two occurrences of the same series materialize twice** (retry, replayed job) | Materialization is idempotent on `(series, local date, slot)`. A duplicate is a no-op, not a second occurrence and not a second credit reservation | Nobody sees anything. This row exists so the implementer knows it is a requirement |
| **Network dies between confirm and the teacher's notification** | The request is durable at hold-conversion, not at notification. Retry delivers the notification; the request's 48h clock runs from conversion, not from delivery | Student: "Requested — awaiting {teacher}". Teacher on reconnect: the request, with the correct remaining time |
| **Student submits the same confirm twice (double-tap, retry)** | Idempotency on the request; the second submit returns the first result | Student: one request, one confirmation. Never two series on one slot |
| **Student requests 4 series with 4 different teachers for the same Tue 17:00** | Allowed while pending — they are shopping (06.02.04's 2–3-teacher trial pattern). On the **second acceptance**, the conflict surfaces and the student must drop one before it confirms | Student: "You already hold Tue 17:00 with Sam. Keep Sam, or switch to Alex?" |
| **The multi-hyphenate: teacher books their own lesson at a time they publish as teaching availability** | Detected and blocked at hold time. The platform's central structural fact bites here first (DT-10) | Musician: "You teach Tuesdays 16:00–20:00. Booking your own lesson at 17:00 blocks a teaching slot — block the hour, or pick another time?" |
| **A and B teach each other** (guitar-for-piano swap) | Two independent series, reciprocal, no special case. Access is by relationship to the specific lesson (domain D-02) | Both: two series on one calendar, one labelled "teaching", one "learning" |
| **Sibling back-to-back: one guardian, two students, 17:00 + 17:30** | Bookable as a linked pair. Cancelling one does **not** auto-cancel the other — the parent is still making the journey (DT-12) | Guardian: "Cancel Ella's 17:00 only, or both? Jack's 17:30 is unaffected" — stated, never assumed |
| **The sibling pair is booked with a travel buffer between them** | Rejected at booking: an in-person back-to-back at the same address needs **no** travel buffer. The buffer is per-journey, not per-slot | Teacher: "Same address — no travel time needed between these two" |
| **Student wants 30 min → 60 min mid-series (series resize)** | Requires the adjacent 30 min to be free. If it is, resize in place, tenancy preserved. If it is not, it is a reschedule, not a resize — and the system must say which | Student: "17:00–18:00 isn't free — Ben has 17:30. Move to Thursday 18:00 for 60 min?" |
| **Two students agree to swap slots permanently** | Both parties **and** the teacher accept within 48h; both tenancies transfer atomically. Neither slot touches the open market at any point — a swap that briefly opens a prime slot to the market is a race a stranger wins | All three: one proposal, one accept each, one atomic result |
| **September shuffle: every student's school timetable changed** | Bulk re-slot mode — the teacher proposes a whole new grid and each student accepts their new slot. Tenancies hold until accepted; unaccepted ones suspend rather than release (DT-07) | Teacher: one board, drag 14 students, one send. Student: "Amy proposes Thu 17:00 from 8 Sep. Accept, or ask for another time?" |
| **A student never accepts their shuffle proposal** | Their tenancy suspends at the old slot's last occurrence. It does **not** release for 28 days — the slot is theirs, they've simply gone quiet | Teacher: "2 of 14 haven't responded — their slots are held until 6 Oct." Student: escalating reminders at T-14/T-7/T-1 |
| **Public holiday the teacher forgot to block** | The platform surfaces the teacher's locale's public holidays as **suggested** block-outs at rule-publish time and 14 days ahead. It does not auto-block — plenty of teachers teach on bank holidays | Teacher: "25 Aug is a bank holiday. 6 lessons scheduled — block the day?" |
| **In-person series and the Operator revokes the room** | The room is a dependency, not the tenancy. Occurrences flag `room-pending`; the teacher may re-room or switch the occurrences to remote, and only the teacher may. The Operator cannot evict a series | Teacher: "Room 3 is unavailable 12–26 Sep. Move to Room 1, or run these 4 lessons remote?" Student: nothing until the teacher decides — a student told their room vanished before the teacher has fixed it is pure alarm |
| **Operator changes the term calendar over existing occurrences** | Occurrences inside a newly-closed window suspend; occurrences inside a newly-opened window materialize. Tenancies survive both. The Operator sees the blast radius **before** committing | Operator: "Closing 12–19 Feb suspends 87 lessons across 11 teachers. Confirm?" |
| **Departing academy teacher with 14 live series** | Series are reassigned only with per-student acceptance. Credit liability sits with the academy (06.01.02). An unaccepted reassignment suspends; it never silently hands a child to a different adult | Student/guardian: "Amy has left. Ben can take your Tuesday 17:00 — accept, or end here?" (with the safeguarding gate re-run for the new pairing) |
| **Malicious: student squats a scarce prime slot and never attends** | No countermeasure needed, deliberately. Tenure binds to payment (D-03), so a squatter pays the teacher full rate to hold an hour the teacher then doesn't work. That is not an attack; it is the teacher's best-case Tuesday (DT-13) | Teacher: nothing. The slot is paid |
| **Malicious: bot mass-requests every slot on a popular teacher to deny them** | Rate limit: 5 pending requests per student per teacher per 7 days; pending requests hold slots for 48h max. A bot at scale is an account-abuse problem, escalated to domain 24, not a calendar problem | Attacker: rate-limit error. Teacher: unaffected — requests never became holds |
| **Malicious: teacher publishes availability they never intend to honour, to look in demand and rank better in 06.02.03** | The calendar cannot detect intent. What it does supply is the signal: accept rate and time-to-respond per teacher, fed to 06.02.03's ranking and to 06.01.03's reliability signal. A teacher who never accepts ranks down | Student: nothing directly. Teacher: "You've declined 9 of 10 requests — students are being sent to teachers who accept" |
| **Malicious: student cancels a stranger's occurrence via a guessed identifier** | Authorization is per-occurrence via CX-M09; a student may act only on occurrences of a series they hold, a guardian only within delegated authority | Attacker: 404, never 403 — a 403 confirms the occurrence exists and leaks a real teacher's real calendar |
| **Student's timezone is wrong / device clock is skewed** | The anchor is server-resolved from the teacher's IANA zone; the client never computes the truth. A skewed client renders a wrong local label, which the counterpart-time line makes visible | Student: "17:00 your time · 17:00 {teacher}'s time" — a mismatch a student can see and report beats one they can't |
| **A trial (06.02.04) is requested on a series-claimable slot** | Allowed — a trial materializes an occurrence but **never claims tenancy**. The slot stays `open`, and converting the trial to a series is a separate claim | Student: "This is a one-off trial — it doesn't reserve Tuesdays yet." At conversion: "Keep Tuesday 17:00 as your weekly slot?" |
| **Group lesson (06.05) on the same slot** | The occurrence has capacity > 1; tenancy is per-participant against a shared occurrence. The slot is claimed while the group runs, not by any one student | Teacher: "Tue 18:00 — Group: 4 of 6." Student: their seat, not the room |
| **Fixed-count series reaches its last occurrence** | Series → Completed. Slot returns to `open` at the last occurrence, **not** before | Student: "Last lesson 18 Aug. Continue weekly?" at T-14d — so the answer arrives before the slot is gone. Teacher: "Amy's block ends 18 Aug — Tue 17:00 reopens" |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Loading** | Calendar opened | Skeleton week grid with the current week's shape and the viewer's timezone label already correct — the one thing that must not pop |
| **Empty (no rule)** | Teacher has published no availability | Teacher: "Publish your teaching hours" CTA. Student: "This teacher isn't taking bookings" |
| **Empty (no rate card)** | Rule published, 06.01.02 has no rate card | Teacher: "Set your rates — your calendar is visible but unbookable". Student: "This teacher hasn't published rates yet" |
| **Full** | Rule published, every `open` slot claimed by a series | **Distinct from Empty, and the domain's most valuable state.** Student: "{Teacher} has no open slots" + the teacher's next `Completed` series date if any, + nearest matching teachers (06.02.03). Teacher: "You're fully booked — 14 slots, 0 open" |
| **Populated** | Rule materialized, open slots exist | Week/month grid in the viewer's timezone, counterpart time on every slot, past slots dimmed rather than hidden |
| **Pending** | Requested; teacher hasn't responded | Student: "Awaiting {teacher} — expires in 47h", slot held meanwhile. Teacher: the request with accept / decline / counter-propose |
| **Confirmed** | Teacher accepted | Series on both calendars to the materialization horizon; paid-through date shown |
| **At Risk** | Balance 0, next occurrence unreserved | Student: amber series + "Top up by 30 Jul to keep Tuesday 17:00" with the exact release date. Teacher: "Amy's balance is empty — held until 30 Jul" |
| **Suspended** | Teacher blocked a range / term break / unaccepted proposal | Series greyed, slot still claimed, **explicit resume date**, and "no credits used" stated — because "suspended" reads as "cancelled" to a parent who is owed nothing but reassurance |
| **Lapsed** | At-Risk grace elapsed, or a missed re-enrolment window | Student: "Tuesday 17:00 was released on 30 Jul" + a re-request path if it's still free. Teacher: slot back in the market |
| **Completed** | Fixed-count series hit its last occurrence | Both: series archived, history intact, "Continue weekly?" offered at T-14d |
| **Conflicted (Partial)** | Some occurrences clash (gig, room loss, term change) | Series shown whole with the conflicted occurrences amber, the count stated ("4 of 12 need attention"), and a resolve action per occurrence and in bulk |
| **Error** | Booking request failed | "Couldn't confirm — the slot is still held for 4:31" with a live hold timer and retry. The timer is the whole message: a student who doesn't know the slot is still theirs re-books elsewhere |
| **Gated** | Safeguarding gate failed pre-hold | The specific blocking condition and the specific action that clears it. Never a generic "unavailable" (06.01.04 D-02) |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | The primitive is a single booking, and recurrence is a convenience layer on top | Naive reading of the candidate name "1:1 Lesson Booking" | ❌ REJECTED — inverted. Real tuition is a standing weekly slot held for years; the one-off is the exception (a trial, or a masterclass). Building single-first makes recurrence a bolt-on, and every hard problem here (exceptions, term bounds, suspension, slot ownership) lives in recurrence. The series is the entity; the occurrence is derived. |
| DT-02 | This duplicates the `Availability, Scheduling & Reservation` cross-cut and should not exist | Cross-cut list — that mechanism explicitly serves education-lessons-mentorship | ❌ REJECTED, with a boundary drawn. CX-M05 owns generic slots, holds, atomic decrement, race prevention and external calendar sync. It does not own: a **series** that survives exceptions, **slot tenancy** (this Tuesday 5pm belongs to Amy until she gives it up — a venue booking has no equivalent), or **term bounds**. Those are tuition semantics. The seam is recorded in the sub-domain CX. |
| DT-03 | A working musician teaching between gigs will wreck their students' standing slots constantly, and this is a domain expert's first objection | Persona: Musician's calendar is dominated by sessions and gigs; a teacher is the same person | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it makes "suspend series without losing the slot" a first-class state rather than an edge case. It also creates the strongest CX in the domain: a gig booked in 17 or a session in 07 must *see* teaching commitments, or the platform will cause the double-booking it exists to prevent. |
| DT-04 | An ongoing series reserves a credit for every occurrence it will ever have — as the `[SURFACE]` happy path's "hold on the whole series" implied, and as sub-domain CX-01 ("confirming an occurrence reserves a credit") reads at face value | Reasoning from CX-01 + the `[SURFACE]` happy path step 4 | ❌ REJECTED — it is incoherent. "Ongoing" is unbounded; reserving its credits needs an infinite balance, so under this reading no student could ever book the domain's *primary* booking shape. **The series holds the slot; only occurrences inside a 28-day rolling reservation horizon hold credits.** This is not a contradiction of CX-01's fail-closed rule (which governs a *booking action*, and still holds): it separates tenancy from ledger. ## Cross-Cut Notes

**Consumed global cross-cuts**

- **CX-M05 Availability, Scheduling & Reservation** — slot materialization, holds, atomic decrement, race prevention, blackout dates, lead times, buffers, and external calendar sync (a working musician lives in Google Calendar; a teaching calendar they must check separately is a teaching calendar they don't check). This feature layers series / tenancy / term semantics on top and owns none of the mechanism (DT-02).
- **CX-M20 Localization, Currency & Timezone** — the teacher-local wall-clock anchor, per-viewer rendering, and the asymmetric UK/US and northern/southern DST windows. The domain's most common support complaint, and the one place a silent correctness bug (relocation, DT-11) is worse than an error.
- **CX-M07 Notifications & Alerts** — reminders, 48h request expiry, DST-shift notice at T-7d, At-Risk warnings at T-7d/T-1d/release, re-enrolment prompts, shuffle proposals. Every tenancy-threatening event must fire **before** it happens, never only at it.
- **CX-M31 Safeguarding & Minor Protection** — the minor↔adult gate fires **before any hold exists**, so a blocked pairing never reaches the teacher as a notification. Booking-time gate; 06.01.04 holds the join-time gate. Which is authoritative is CX-07's open question, routed to the cross-cut's owner.
- **CX-M09 Roles, Permissions & Delegated Authority** — a guardian books, reschedules and cancels within delegated authority over a minor's account (domain D-03); authorization is per-occurrence, and a non-holder gets 404 not 403 (a 403 leaks a real teacher's real calendar).
- **CX-M19 Audit Log & Provenance Ledger** — every **tenancy** change (grant, suspend, resume, lapse, transfer, swap, reassign) is an evidential event, not a status flip. A released slot is disputed as often as a burned credit, and by an angrier person.

**Sub-domain siblings**

- **06.01.02 Packages, Credits & Rate Cards** — *shared entity, corrected seam*: an occurrence reserves a credit at confirmation **only inside the 28-day reservation horizon** (DT-04), not for the whole series; 06.01.02 owns the ledger, this feature only reserves. A `released` one-off is sold at rate-card price to a student with no pack. **This note supersedes the face-value reading of sub-domain CX-01 and answers CX-06's `[PARTIAL]`** — a zero balance suspends within a stated grace, then releases (D-03).
- **06.01.03 Cancellation, Make-Up & No-Show** — *trigger dependency, and the sub-domain's largest unowned gap (DT-05)*: 06.01.03 grants make-up entitlements and this feature must supply somewhere for them to land, which a fully-booked teacher does not have. Resolved here by the `one-off-only` slot class. **Ownership seam for Step 6**: 06.01.01 owns the slot class; 06.01.03 owns the entitlement that spends it. Also: a cancellation against an occurrence in a suspended window is an idempotent no-op — **this resolves sub-domain CX-04's Q5**, flagged there for Step 5.
- **06.01.04 Lesson Delivery & Session Record** — *trigger dependency*: this feature materializes the occurrence the room opens for; 06.01.04's join gate is the second safeguarding gate (CX-07). A teacher's vetting expiring mid-series stops **new** minor bookings here but does not cancel existing series — 06.01.04 fails closed at join.

**Domain 06 siblings**

- **06.02.01 Teacher & Tuition Profile** — *permission*: availability renders inline on the public page as *shape* only ("Tue/Thu evenings") for signed-out viewers and Fans; an actionable slot grid requires an account. The **Full** state (every slot claimed) is this feature's most valuable output to the profile, and is not the same as "no availability".
- **06.02.03 Discovery & Match Criteria** — *shared entity*: openness is a search facet, and this feature supplies accept-rate and time-to-respond, which is the only available countermeasure to phantom availability (DT-14 sibling case in the edge table). A `Full` teacher must be discoverable-but-honest, not invisible.
- **06.02.04 Trial Lessons & Conversion** — *boundary*: a trial materializes an occurrence but **never claims tenancy**; the slot stays `open` and conversion is a separate claim. Aligns with 06.02 CX-04 (trial booked from the result card, no enquiry step) and 06.02.04 D-05 (a trial carries full safeguarding protection).
- **06.03 Curriculum, Assignments & Practice** — *trigger dependency*: the occurrence is the hook an assignment hangs off; a suspended series produces no occurrences and therefore no assignments, which must not read to a student as "you're behind" (domain D-04 — no motivation-by-scaffolding).
- **06.05 Group Lessons, Workshops & Masterclasses** — *shared entity*: a group occurrence occupies one teacher slot with capacity > 1; tenancy is per-seat against a shared occurrence, not per-room.
- **06.10 Academy & Multi-Teacher Operations** — *permission + trigger dependency*: the Operator owns the term calendar that bounds materialization, the room allocation an in-person occurrence depends on, and the re-enrolment checkpoint (default off, DT-06) — the only mechanism that lets a term boundary end a tenancy. A departing teacher's series reassign only with per-student acceptance and a re-run safeguarding gate.

**Other domains**

- **[17 Live Booking & Settlement](../../17-live-booking-settlement/)** and **[07 Music Projects](../../07-music-projects-collaboration/)** — *state race, the domain's strongest external CX (DT-03)*: the teacher is a gigging musician, and a gig or session confirmed over a teaching slot must be blocked with its blast radius named, not silently overwritten. This is bidirectional and cross-domain, which is why Q-02 (one calendar or many) is architecture-shaped.
- **[16 Venues, Studios & Spaces](../../16-venues-studios-spaces/)** — *shared entity*: an in-person lesson consumes a room the Operator owns. The room is a **dependency of the occurrence, not of the tenancy** — losing the room re-rooms or goes remote; it never evicts a series. Note 06.01 CX R-05 already rejects the room-hire transaction being a line item on the student's price.
- **[24 Trust, Safety & Disputes](../../24-trust-safety-disputes/)** — *escalation*: mass-requesting is account abuse, not a calendar problem; a contested tenancy release escalates here with the CX-M19 audit trail attached.
- **[23 Career, Finance & Business](../../23-career-finance-business/)** — *shared entity*: confirmed series are a working musician's **forward book** — the most predictable income in their portfolio, and the number that tells them whether they can turn down a session.

Also answers sub-domain **CX-06** (`[PARTIAL]` — does a zero balance suspend or release?): both, in that order, with a stated date. |
| D-04 | **The series holds the slot; only occurrences inside a 28-day rolling reservation horizon hold credits** | Per DT-04. | D-05 | A term boundary **suspends**; it never terminates. Re-enrolment is an Operator-configurable gate, **default off**, and is the only mechanism by which a term end may release a tenancy | Per DT-06. Corrects a real defect in the `[SURFACE]` pass. Ending series each term returns every standing slot to the market three times a year — the failure D-01 exists to prevent — while still honouring the academy's genuine commitment checkpoint. |
| D-06 | The availability rule carries a **`one-off-only` slot class** — publishable, bookable as a single lesson or a make-up, never series-claimable | Per DT-05. Encodes the flex hour real teachers already keep. |
| D-07 | Occurrences anchor to the **teacher's** local wall-clock time. Relocation is an explicit **series migration** with per-student acceptance; touring is a block-out, not a migration | Per DT-11. The anchor is correct under DST and silently catastrophic under relocation — an 8h shift that throws no error is worse than one that does. |
| D-08 | A cancelled occurrence returns as a **`released` one-off** at rate-card price. No waitlist auto-promotion | Per DT-09. | D-09 | Travel buffers are per-**journey**, not per-slot | Per DT-12. Two siblings at one address need no gap between them; a cross-town in-person lesson needs 30 minutes each side. A per-slot buffer gets both cases wrong and costs a teacher an hour of unsellable time per sibling pair. |
