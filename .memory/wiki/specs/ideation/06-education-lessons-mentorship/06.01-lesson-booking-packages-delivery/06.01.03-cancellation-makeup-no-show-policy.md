# Feature: Cancellation, Make-Up & No-Show Policy

> **Number**: 06.01.03
> **Parent**: [Lesson Booking, Packages & Delivery](./06.01-lesson-booking-packages-delivery-index.md)
> **Status**: [SURFACE]
> **Last updated**: 2026-07-16

## Overview

The rule that decides who eats the cost of a lesson that didn't happen. A teacher authors a policy
(notice window, make-up entitlement, no-show treatment); the platform enforces it automatically
against the credit ledger. This exists as its own feature because it is the single most
relationship-damaging conversation in tuition — "you cancelled at 4pm, I'd already turned down a
session for that slot" — and the entire point of encoding it is that the platform, not the teacher,
delivers the bad news.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As Teacher**: authors the policy (e.g. "24h notice, 1 make-up per term, no-show burns the credit"), sees it enforced without having to ask, can override in a specific instance with one tap (goodwill is a feature, not a bug), sees the make-up ledger per student. **As Student**: sees the policy *before* booking and again at cancellation time with the consequence stated in credits, cancels or requests a make-up, sees make-ups remaining. |
| Producer | None | Not visible. |
| Operator | Config | As an academy: sets a **floor policy** individual teachers cannot undercut (a school's reputation is set by its worst teacher's cancellation behaviour), and handles escalated disputes between a teacher and a parent. |
| Fan | None | Not visible — no fan-facing entry point. |

## Behavior

The policy is a small set of rules evaluated against an occurrence's scheduled start time:

- **Notice window** — cancel with more than N hours' notice: credit returns to balance
- **Late cancel** — inside the window: credit burns, or converts to a make-up entitlement per policy
- **No-show** — nobody attended and no cancellation: credit burns (subject to the evidence question below)
- **Teacher cancels** — credit always returns, and the platform should make this asymmetry explicit rather than quiet
- **Make-up entitlement** — a capped right to reschedule a late-cancelled lesson without burning a credit

Evaluation is automatic at the moment of cancellation and at a grace period after the scheduled
start. The teacher override always exists, in the student's favour only.

### Happy Path

1. Teacher authors a policy when publishing their rate card; it is attached to the series at booking
2. Student sees the policy at booking and accepts it as part of confirming
3. Student cancels Tuesday's lesson on Sunday — outside the 24h window
4. System evaluates: credit returns to balance, occurrence released, slot reopened
5. Both parties are notified with the ledger effect stated plainly ("1 credit returned — 8 remaining")

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Student cancels 2h before | Inside window: credit burns or converts to a make-up per the policy | Student: consequence shown **before** confirming ("This will use your last make-up") — never after |
| Teacher cancels late | Credit always returns; no make-up cap consumed; teacher's reliability signal is affected | Student: "{Teacher} cancelled — credit returned." Teacher: sees the reliability consequence |
| Nobody joins the room | Grace period elapses, then the no-show rule fires | Both: "Marked as no-show at 17:15 — 1 credit used. Dispute this" |
| **Both** parties no-show | `[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5]` — the symmetric case is genuinely unclear and the naive rule (burn the credit) is wrong when the teacher was equally absent | `[PENDING]` |
| Student was in the room, teacher wasn't | Attendance evidence from the Real-Time Rooms cross-cut decides it — this is why delivery must record join events (see 06.01.04) | Student: auto-resolved in their favour, no dispute needed |
| Illness / bereavement | Teacher override, one tap, in the student's favour | Teacher: "Waive policy this time" always present |
| Student disputes a no-show | Escalates to domain 24 (Trust, Safety & Disputes) with the session record as evidence | Both: dispute thread with the join log attached |
| Policy changed mid-series | The policy in force is the one attached at booking, not the teacher's current one | Student: unaffected. Teacher: "Applies to new bookings" |
| Minor's guardian cancels | Guardian acts with delegated authority over the student's account (see domain D-03) | Guardian: same flow, same consequences |

### States

| State | Trigger | What User Sees |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Loading | Policy fetch at cancel time | Skeleton with the occurrence's time fixed |
| Empty | Teacher authored no policy | Platform **default** policy applies and is labelled as such — a teacher with no policy must not mean "no rules" |
| Populated | Policy attached to series | Plain-language policy card + make-up counter |
| Error | Cancellation failed to record | "Not cancelled — your lesson is still booked" (fail closed; a silently-failed cancel is worse than none) |
| Partial | Cancelled, ledger effect pending | "Cancelled — credit return processing" |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | This is a settings screen and belongs folded into the rate card | Its apparent modesty — "a notice window" is one number | ❌ REJECTED — it is a **state machine over the credit ledger with a dispute path**, and it is where the money and the relationship both break. Folding it into a rate card hides an automatic financial consequence inside a pricing form. The evidence it needs (join logs) also crosses to 06.01.04, which a settings field cannot express. |
| DT-02 | A no-show can be determined from the room join log alone | Real-Time Rooms cross-cut records joins, so absence looks trivially detectable | ❌ REJECTED for the in-person case, which the sub-domain's Q-03 says is in scope. There is no join log for a lesson at the teacher's house — the platform has **no evidence** and must fall back on assertion + dispute. This means the no-show rule needs two implementations, not one, and a remote-only design silently assumes away half the market. |
| DT-03 | The policy's real function is emotional, not financial — it exists so the teacher never has to chase a student for money | Domain expertise: private teachers overwhelmingly under-enforce their own policies because enforcement damages the relationship they depend on | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it sets the design constraint: enforcement must be **automatic and attributed to the platform**, with the teacher's override in the student's favour always one tap away. A policy the teacher must manually invoke reproduces the exact problem it was meant to solve. This is the same "make the lazy path the correct path" principle `meta/personas.md` identifies for the Producer's split capture. |

- Touches **06.01.02 Packages, Credits & Rate Cards** — this feature is the primary writer to that credit ledger.
- Touches **06.01.01 Slot Booking** — cancellation releases the occurrence and reopens the slot without dissolving the series.

- Touches **Reviews, Ratings & Portable Reputation** (global cross-cut) — teacher-side late cancellation is a reliability signal; whether it publicly surfaces is Q-02 below.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | Enforcement is **automatic and platform-attributed**; the override is teacher-initiated and student-favouring only | Per DT-03 — the feature's purpose is removing the teacher from the enforcement conversation. |
| D-02 | The policy in force is the one **attached at booking time**, not the teacher's current policy | A retroactively-changed policy is a trust catastrophe and probably unenforceable anyway. |
| D-03 | A teacher with no policy gets the **platform default**, labelled as such | "No policy" must never mean "no rules" — the ambiguity always resolves against the student at the worst moment. |
| D-04 | Consequences are shown **before** confirming a cancellation, priced in credits | Per DT-03 — surprise is the thing that damages the relationship. |
