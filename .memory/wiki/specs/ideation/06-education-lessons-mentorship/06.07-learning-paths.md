## Overview

A curated, ordered route through the platform's teaching content — "learn to play blues guitar" as a
sequence of courses, exercises and milestones, followed **without a teacher**. This is the
self-directed learner's spine: the person who wants to learn, has no teacher, and does not know what
to do next. That last part is the real problem — the internet has infinite music instruction and no
order, which is why a beginner can watch 400 free videos and still not play.

## Role Lens

| Role | Access | What They See/Do |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Musician | Full | **As learner**: picks a path, follows it, sees what's next and what's behind, practises against it (06.03.04), progresses without needing to decide what to do each day — which is the whole value. **As author/teacher**: may curate a path over their own and others' courses. |
| Producer | Full | As author: a production path ("from bedroom to a finished mix") is a Producer's curation, and a natural extension of course authoring (06.04.01). |
| Operator | Config | As academy: publishes a house path aligned to their syllabus — the self-service version of their curriculum. |
| Fan | Full | **A path is buyable and followable with no teacher and no professional stake.** Same porous boundary as 06.04 — a fan who wants to learn their favourite artist's style follows a path. |

## Behavior

A path is an ordered sequence of **units drawn from things that already exist**: courses (06.04),
exercises, repertoire, and milestones. It has prerequisites, a rough duration, and a completion
notion. Critically it is **not new content** — it is curation over existing content, which is why it
is a feature and not a sub-domain.

The relationship to a curriculum (06.03.01) is the thing to get right: a curriculum is a **teacher's
plan for a specific student**, adapted continuously and meaningless without them. A path is a
**published route for strangers**, identical for everyone, and works with no teacher at all. Same
shape, opposite semantics — the same distinction 06.03.01 DT-01 drew between a curriculum and a
course, for the same reason.

`[PENDING — /ideate-discover Step 5]`: whether paths are platform-curated, author-curated, or both.
The answer decides whether this is an editorial function (with staff cost) or a marketplace one (with
a quality problem).

### Happy Path

1. Learner states a goal: "play blues guitar", "produce a track start to finish"
2. Picks a path; sees the route, roughly how long, what they'll be able to do
3. Works through units — courses, exercises, repertoire — in order
4. Practises against each via the practice room (06.03.04)
5. Hits milestones; sees the route behind them, which is the motivation
6. Finishes — or, far more often, doesn't, which is normal (06.04.03 D-02)

### Edge Cases / Failure Modes

| Scenario | What Happens | User Sees |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Path references a course the learner must buy | The path is partly an upsell. Honest disclosure or it is a bait-and-switch | Learner: cost of the whole path visible **up front**, not discovered at unit 4 |
| Path references a withdrawn course | Broken route | `[PENDING — Step 5]` — path maintenance is a real ongoing cost nobody budgets for |
| Learner is already past unit 1–5 | Skipping must be trivial; a path that forces a competent player through beginner units loses them immediately | Self-placement, no gatekeeping — the platform cannot assess them anyway (06.03.05 DT-02) |
| Learner abandons at unit 2 | The base rate. Not a failure UI | Quiet, decaying resume prompt |
| Learner also has a teacher | Path and teacher's curriculum may **contradict** — genuinely awkward | `[PENDING — Step 5]`. A teacher whose student is following a competing path is a real dynamic |
| Path has no content behind it | Cold start: paths are curation, and there is nothing to curate on day one | **A path cannot exist before the courses it sequences.** See DT-03 |
| Author curates a path entirely of their own paid courses | Legitimate, and it is also a sales funnel wearing a pedagogy costume | Disclosure |

## Deep Think Annotations

| # | Hypothesis | Source | Outcome |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|
| DT-01 | A learning path is the same object as a curriculum (06.03.01) and should be merged with it | Structurally identical: both are ordered sequences of units with prerequisites | ❌ REJECTED, on the same axis 06.03.01 DT-01 used to separate curriculum from course — and the fact that the **same distinction recurs a third time** suggests it is a domain-level law rather than a coincidence. The axis is: *is this authored for one known person, adapted continuously, and meaningless without its author* (curriculum, mentorship) *or authored once for strangers, identical for everyone, and consumed without its author* (course, path)? A path is the second. A curriculum that doesn't adapt is negligence; a path that adapts per learner is broken. Same shape, opposite semantics, third instance. |
| DT-02 | The path's real value is **removing the decision**, not the content | Domain expertise: the internet has infinite free music instruction. The bottleneck for a self-directed beginner has never been access to information — it is that they don't know what to do next, so they do nothing, or they do 400 disconnected YouTube videos | ✅ CONFIRMED — and it reframes the feature. A path is not a content product; it is a **decision-removal product**. That means its quality is entirely in the sequencing, not the material, and it means the honest comparison isn't "our videos vs YouTube's videos" (which we lose) but "an order vs no order" (which YouTube structurally cannot provide, because it optimises for the next click, not the next skill). It also means a *good* path over *free* content would be valuable — which is a strange and possibly important business insight this file is not the place to resolve. |
| DT-03 | Paths are a launch feature | They look cheap — curation, no new content, a list of things that already exist | ❌ REJECTED — **a path is curation, and there is nothing to curate on day one**. It is structurally downstream of 06.04's catalogue: with 4 courses there is no path, only a list. This is the same cold-start failure 06.04.02 DT-03 identified for catalog subscriptions, arriving from a different direction, and it makes this feature's MoSCoW near-mechanical: it cannot precede the content it sequences. It also carries an unbudgeted ongoing cost — paths **rot** as referenced courses are withdrawn or updated (06.04.01 Q-03), and someone must maintain them forever. |

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Context |
|---|----------|---------|
| D-01 | A path is **curation over existing content**, never new content | It is why this is a feature rather than a sub-domain, and why it is downstream of 06.04. |
| D-02 | Self-placement is free; **no gatekeeping between units** | A path that forces a competent player through beginner units loses them in one click — and per 06.03.05 DT-02 the platform cannot assess ability anyway, so it has no standing to gate. |
| D-03 | "Playbooks" **merged away** — not a distinct thing | The sweep's third noun. A "playbook" ("how to run your first session") is either a **course** with a non-linear shape (06.04) or **onboarding content** (the Onboarding cross-cut). Neither is a learning path, and neither needs a node here. Creating one to honour the candidate name would be inventing structure to hit a count. |
| D-04 | Total path cost is disclosed **up front** | A path that reveals a paywall at unit 4 is a bait-and-switch, and it is the natural failure mode of author-curated paths. |
