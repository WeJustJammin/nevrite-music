## Overview

Teaching that scales past the hour. A 1:1 lesson is a musician selling time — capped, unleveraged,
and the reason teaching plateaus as an income. A course is the same knowledge authored once and sold
repeatedly: it decouples a teacher's earnings from their calendar. Tonebase, Fender Play, Pickup Music
and a thousand individual Patreon-and-Teachable operations exist because that decoupling is real money.

**Why this is a sub-domain**: three capabilities that only work as a chain. Authoring produces the
product; the catalog prices and sells it; consumption delivers it and reports back what learners
actually finish — which is the only signal telling the author what to make next. Cut the catalog and
you have a filing cabinet; cut consumption and you have a shop selling something nobody can use.

> **Why this Role Matrix is the domain's widest — and why that is a finding, not an accident.** Every
> other sub-domain in domain 06 is Musician-only with a narrow Operator config. This one activates
> **all four personas**, because a course is a *product*, not a relationship:
> - The **Producer** becomes a first-class author here and nowhere else in the domain. `meta/personas.md`
>   describes them as a trust broker whose attestation carries weight; a mixing course sold on the back
>   of verified credits is that reputation monetised directly. A Producer would never take on a weekly
>   beginner, and would absolutely film "how I mix drums".
> - The **Fan** can buy a course outright — no lessons, no teacher, no relationship. `meta/personas.md`
>   states the fan/hobbyist boundary "is porous"; a fan buying their favourite producer's course is
>   that porousness generating revenue.
>
> This makes 06.04 the domain's **only genuinely cross-persona surface**, and — per 06.04.02 DT-02 —
> arguably its best standalone commercial bet, since it needs no lesson relationship to exist.

## Decision Log

|---|----------|---------|--------|
| D-01 | Courses stay in **Education**; they are not routed to Digital Goods (14) | Applies the ratified map's own principle verbatim: "Digital music sales to fans stay in Fanbase and reuse Digital Goods' download/library delivery — split by destination." A course is instructional content for learners; Digital Goods is "creative raw materials sold to makers" (samples, plugins). Split by destination; share the delivery layer. | Ratified map's stated splitting principle (D-16) |
| D-02 | A course is **not** a curriculum (06.03.01) | Near-identical structure, opposite semantics: a course is authored once and identical for every buyer; a curriculum is adapted per student and meaningless without the teacher. They may share a unit primitive. | 06.03.01 DT-01 |
| D-03 | Video/media delivery is consumed from the **Media Handling & Audio Playback** cross-cut | It already serves 6+ domains. **Carries the map's hard stack warning**: heavy transcode is routed to `/create-prd-architecture` as a constraint against the locked Astro + Workers + Supabase stack. A course platform is a video platform, and this is not a small note. | Cross-cut list, ratified 2026-07-16 (D-16) |
| D-04 | Course purchase consumes **Payments** and **Subscriptions & Entitlements**; a course entitlement is **not** a lesson credit | Two unrelated ledgers that both look like "prepaid learning". A lesson credit is teacher-scoped and burns per occurrence (06.01.02); a course entitlement is perpetual (or subscription-bound) access to a product. Merging them recreates exactly the nullable-column mess D-14 rejected for the marketplaces. | Cross-cut list; D-14's reasoning applied within a domain |
