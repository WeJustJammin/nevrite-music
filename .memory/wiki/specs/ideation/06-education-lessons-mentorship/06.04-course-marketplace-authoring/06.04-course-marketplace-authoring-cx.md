## Cross-Cut Details

### CX-01: Authoring ↔ Catalog & Pricing

**Relationship**: The credits block travels with the course into the catalog and is what converts a
stranger. This is the domain's wedge (06.02.02 DT-01) applied to a product rather than a
relationship — and, importantly, **at full strength**. 06.02.02 DT-02 had to reject credits as a
teacher-ranking signal because playing ability ≠ teaching ability; that objection largely evaporates
here, because a course buyer is explicitly buying "show me how you got that sound", where the credit
*is* the qualification.

It is also the best answer the sub-domain has to index Q-04 (why not YouTube): YouTube cannot verify
that the person teaching you actually made the record.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician (as author)**: does nothing to produce their own differentiator — it renders from domain 02 automatically (06.04.01 D-02)
- **Producer (as author)**: the persona this most benefits, and the reason they are a first-class actor here and nowhere else in domain 06
- **Musician / Fan (as buyer)**: buys on checkable evidence rather than a claim
- **Operator**: house courses carry the academy's own vouch alongside the author's credits

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: None — the credits block is a read-only projection of domain 02 (06.02.02 D-04). No writer contention; the same single-source rule that protects the credit graph.
2. **Trigger chain**: Credit confirmed in domain 02 → renders on the course. Async; staleness is harmless here (unlike vetting in 06.02, which must gate immediately).
3. **Permission intersection**: None — no safeguarding gate on authoring or buying a video, which is exactly why 06.04.02 DT-02 argues this sub-domain is independently shippable.
4. **Notification fan-out**: None required.
5. **State transition conflict**: A credit disputed in domain 02 while a course is listed on it — display the dispute; never silently re-rank a live listing under a buyer.

### CX-02: Catalog & Pricing ↔ Consumption

**Relationship**: Purchase grants an entitlement; consumption checks it. The entitlement's semantics
are the load-bearing part and they are **deliberately unlike a lesson credit** (index D-04, 06.04.02
DT-01): product-scoped not teacher-scoped, perpetual not expiring, never burning, governed by no
cancellation policy. The two ledgers coexist in one domain and must not merge — this is D-14's
marketplace argument recurring at sub-domain scale.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician / Fan (as buyer)**: owns the course; it is in their library permanently, including after the author withdraws it (06.04.01 D-03)
- **Producer / Musician (as author)**: paid on sale; cannot revoke a buyer's access
- **Operator**: academy bundles are where this ledger touches 06.01's credit ledger — the unresolved bridge (index Q-02)

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: 06.04.02 owns entitlements; 06.04.03 reads them. Single writer.
2. **Trigger chain**: Purchase → entitlement → access. Sync at grant. Payment failure grants nothing and charges nothing.
3. **Permission intersection**: The entitlement **is** the permission. Withdrawal of the course does not revoke it (06.04.01 D-03) — a sale is a sale, not a subscription to the author's goodwill.
4. **Notification fan-out**: Purchase confirmation; course updates to existing buyers, pending the versioning question (06.04.01 Q-03).
5. **State transition conflict**: A refund granted while the learner is mid-lesson. Access must end cleanly, and the refund policy itself is unresolved (CX-06).

### CX-03: Consumption ↔ Authoring (the drop-off loop)

**Relationship**: The author's only feedback. A course author is blind after publishing — no room, no
student, no reaction — and "68% quit at lesson 3, minute 4" is the single most actionable signal
available to any actor in this sub-domain.

The constraint is the same one this domain keeps rediscovering: **diagnosis, not target**. Drop-off
measures engagement; per 06.04.03 DT-02 engagement is not the goal, and an author optimising drop-off
makes lessons shorter and more entertaining — turning instruction into content.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician / Producer (as author)**: sees where learners quit; re-shoots the section
- **Musician / Fan (as learner)**: their abandonment is data. Aggregated, not individually surfaced to the author — the learner who quit at lesson 3 should not feel watched
- **Operator**: cohort completion across house courses

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: None — consumption emits progress; analytics aggregates. Read-side.
2. **Trigger chain**: Progress events → aggregation → author's view. Async; staleness is fine.
3. **Permission intersection**: The author sees **aggregate** drop-off. Whether they see a named learner's progress is unresolved and should default to no — the same line 06.03's sub-domain index draws between supervising a teacher and surveilling a student.
4. **Notification fan-out**: None. An alert to the author when a specific learner quits would be surveillance dressed as engagement.
5. **State transition conflict**: A course updated mid-consumption re-bases the drop-off data against a new structure (06.04.01 Q-03).

### CX-04: Authoring ↔ Consumption (the practice room)

**Relationship**: An authored practice task ("bars 12–20, 80bpm") hands the learner a practice room
with the tempo set, the reference loopable at 70%, and a record button (06.04.03 DT-01). This is the
sub-domain's second differentiator, and it **composes** with the first: credits make a stranger buy
the course; the room makes them able to use it.

Notable structurally: this seam reaches **out of the sub-domain** into 06.03.04 — a feature that was
added by Deep Think and justified entirely by the *teacher-led* loop. 06.03.04 DT-02 found the room
works with no teacher; this is where that finding pays off, in a sub-domain with no teacher in it.

**Role scoping**:
- **Musician / Producer (as author)**: specifies practice rather than describing it
- **Musician / Fan (as learner)**: practises in the same surface they're learning in — no metronome hunt, no third app, no exit
- **Operator**: not affected

**Synthesis questions answered**:
1. **Shared state conflict**: None — the course supplies configuration; the room consumes it.
2. **Trigger chain**: Lesson → practice task → room opens pre-configured. Sync, in-surface. **The room must degrade** (06.03.04 D-02) — a learner with their own metronome must not be blocked.
3. **Permission intersection**: None — no safeguarding gate on a solo practice room. (If course practice writes to the practice log, per 06.04.03 Q-01, a minor's log gains a new writer, and the Privacy cross-cut applies.)
4. **Notification fan-out**: None. There is no teacher to notify — which is the point.
5. **State transition conflict**: None.

### CX-06: Consumption ↔ Catalog & Pricing (the refund problem)

**Relationship**: **RESOLVED — 06.04.02 D-06.** Consumption depth supplies a bounded change-of-mind rule:
within 14 calendar days and before 20% material consumption, refund is eligible; after that threshold,
no change-of-mind refund applies. A substantiated delivery defect, misrepresentation, or mandatory law
overrides the threshold. This treats consumption as a practical boundary without pretending it measures
learning quality.

**Role scoping**: buyer requests under D-06; author receives a reversible entitlement outcome; payment/dispute
controls handle defects and mandatory-law overrides without reopening the consumption threshold.

**Synthesis questions**: Deferred — Medium confidence.
