# Propagation Batch 8 — Transaction Dispute Limits

> **Date**: 2026-08-02
> **Status**: Applied
> **Authority**: User delegated full decision autonomy until ideation completes
> **Decision**: D-83

## Decisions

1. A loss of perishable time has no restorative remedy. V1 may enforce only a recorded
   cancellation/no-show amount or deposit/escrow allocation; it never assesses speculative loss.
2. Entity evidence and non-binding messages require a recorded `communicate` mandate. Binding settlement
   requires an independent `settle` mandate. No member is implied to be an agent.
3. Evidence-backed adjudication is described accurately in a case but is not an external promise of
   a verdict, payment, or outcome.
