# Product

## Register

product

## Users

WeJammin serves working music people whose identities, work, and income span multiple roles at once. The primary users are multi-hyphenate musicians, producers, engineers, bands, and venue or studio operators. Fans are first-class users with a simpler consumer-facing surface, but the v1 product is professional-first.

Users move between studios, venues, loading docks, home offices, and phones. Their recurring job is to run music work without splitting identity, projects, services, credits, rights, and evidence across disconnected tools. On any screen, the primary task must remain obvious even when the underlying record is complex.

## Product Purpose

WeJammin is the platform where music work happens and where the record of who did what, and who owns what, survives. Consolidation gives people a daily reason to use the platform. Provenance makes the resulting record durable, credible, and difficult to recreate elsewhere.

Success means credits, splits, permissions, and ownership are captured as a byproduct of real workflows rather than reconstructed years later. The product must never overclaim certainty. Self-asserted, counterparty-confirmed, disputed, unavailable, and verified facts remain visibly distinct wherever trust depends on them.

The platform is also its own first-party content management system. Authorized operators can manage typed content, approved blocks, templates, navigation, media, publication workflows, and settings without arbitrary plugins, themes, scripts, CSS, or expressions. Variables belong in governed tokens or settings rather than scattered hard-coded values, while protected provenance and transactional rules remain code-owned.

## Brand Personality

**Credible, human, exact.**

WeJammin should feel like serious music work handled by people who understand the room. Its voice is plainspoken, calm, and accountable: confident enough to make a decision, human enough to acknowledge uncertainty, and precise enough that professionals can trust the consequences. The emotional result should be dignity, orientation, and earned confidence rather than hype.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards made from interchangeable card grids, oversized metrics, and empty whitespace.
- Dark-by-default music products with neon gradients, glassmorphism, nightclub clichés, or waveform decoration unrelated to the task.
- Streaming-service clones that reduce professional identity to popularity, followers, and passive consumption.
- LinkedIn-style corporate blandness, performative networking, and status theatre.
- Gamified profile-completion meters that treat missing third-party evidence as a personal failure.
- Decorative verification badges or visual treatments that make asserted content look attested.
- WordPress-like extensibility through arbitrary themes, plugins, scripts, CSS, or executable content.

## Design Principles

1. **Make provenance legible.** A stranger must understand the status and source of a material fact without learning internal terminology or reading a legend.
2. **Serve the task, not the dashboard.** Prefer direct workflows, predictable controls, and purpose-built views over decorative containers and vanity metrics.
3. **Be honest about state.** Loading, unavailable, pending, disputed, blocked, and absent are different facts and must never collapse into one another.
4. **Respect multi-role identity.** Never force a person into one primary professional role; let context and evidence shape the view without hiding the rest of the record.
5. **Govern flexibility.** Put legitimate variables into typed tokens and settings, but protect accessibility, provenance, permissions, and transactional invariants from editorial override.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is the minimum release bar across public, authenticated, administrative, and PWA surfaces. All workflows must support keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic structure, screen readers, reflow and zoom, sufficient target sizes, and non-color status cues. Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and no essential meaning may depend on animation, audio, color, hover, or pointer precision.

CMS publication gates must validate heading order, link purpose, required text alternatives, document language, broken references, and policy metadata. Automated checks supplement human review. Blocks that cannot meet the accessibility contract leave the approved registry rather than shifting remediation to content authors or end users.
